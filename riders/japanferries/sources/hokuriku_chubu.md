# hokuriku_chubu — per-route ferry passenger sources

Region: Niigata, Toyama, Ishikawa, Nagano (北陸信越運輸局) + Fukui, Gifu, Shizuoka, Aichi, Mie (中部運輸局). Researched 2026-09-15.

## Result in brief

- **17 routes with at least one per-route figure**, plus an all-Sado-routes total. Figures for 2023 or later exist for: the 5 Toba municipal routes (+ circuit sailing), Atami–Hatsushima, Atami–Oshima, Ito–Oshima, Suruga Bay Ferry (Shimizu–Toi), Ogi–Naoetsu, Awashima, and the Sado all-route total. Hegura stops at FY2020 (route suspended from the Jan 2024 quake). Ise Bay Ferry only FY2009, Sakushima only FY2018.
- **Biggest gaps:** Niigata–Ryotsu on its own (car ferry / jetfoil split), all Meitetsu Kaijo Kankosen routes (Morozaki / Kowa / Irago – Himakajima – Shinojima), Ise Bay Ferry after FY2009, Tsu Airport Line (Tsu–Centrair), recent Sakushima.

## Definitions and oddities

- **Children counted as half** in the Toba tables, Awashima (hence .5 values), Ise Bay Ferry, and every MLIT bureau total.
- **Oct–Sep years.** The 二次評価 and 自己評価 sheets follow the national 離島航路 subsidy year (October of the previous year to September). In the CSV, `fiscal_year` is the year the period ends and `year_basis` is `Oct-Sep` — a value outside the FY/CY pair. For the Toba and Shima evaluation sheets the Oct–Sep period is inferred from the scheme, not printed on the page.
- **Atami figures** are boardings + landings at the mainland port, calendar year; that equals both directions on the route. Ito–Hatsushima ended in April 2019. 東海汽船 is Tokyo-based, so the Oshima rows may duplicate the Kanto file.
- **Sado all-route totals** are doubled from the report's one-way figures (輸送人員合計 ÷ 2). The doubled 2019 value, 1,466,682, sits one person off the 1,466,681 that 新潟日報 quotes. The totals include 新潟–両津 (car ferry + jetfoil) and 直江津–小木, and exclude cruise passengers. With Ogi–Naoetsu at 60–75k, Niigata–Ryotsu is roughly the rest, but the year bases differ, so nothing was subtracted.
- **Ogi–Naoetsu 2025**: the sheet gives 63,074 and says +6,458 (111.4%) on the previous year, which does not fit the 74,194 published a year earlier. Left as published.
- **Toba**: the 統計要覧 FY2021 total is misprinted as 4,856,666 (routes sum to 485,666.5). The evaluation-sheet route values (thousands, Oct–Sep) differ a little from the 統計要覧 FY values; both are in the CSV.
- **あご湾定期船 FY2023–24** values are 観光客等 only, a subset — flagged SUBSET in notes, not usable for thickness.
- **Wikipedia-only rows** (flagged in notes): Toba FY2015–17 (table's route values sum exactly to its totals; cited PDF is 404), Hegura FY2016–19, Awashima FY2017 (産経), Suruga Bay Ferry FY2025 (朝日). asahi.com, sankei.com and web.archive.org cannot be fetched from here.
- **No passenger-km** was found for any route. Route km is filled only where a source gives it (Toba–Kamishima 14.9, Wagu–Kashikojima 6.7, Isshiki–Sakushima 13, Wajima–Hegura 50, Toba–Irago 23.2).
- The 駿河湾フェリー homepage carries a notice titled 「９月以降の運休のお知らせ」 (not opened); FY2025 was reported as a record low.
- The WebSearch budget (200 calls) ran out partway through, so municipal yearbooks for 南知多町, 西尾市, 津市, 輪島市 and 佐渡市 were never searched for.
