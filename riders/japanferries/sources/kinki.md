# Kinki — ferry passenger sources

Region: Osaka, Hyogo, Kyoto, Shiga, Nara, Wakayama. Researched 2026-09-15.
The session's web search budget ran out partway through, so a few leads below
were never searched; they are marked as such.

## Result

16 routes have at least one annual figure (55 CSV rows, including 6 rows that
flag a download or have no annual total):

- Hyogo: Himeji–Bozejima (FY2015, FY2017–FY2024), Himeji–Ieshima (2 operators,
  FY2015), Ieshima–Bozejima (FY2014), Numashima–Habu and Numashima–Sumoto
  (FY2013), Kobe–Kansai Airport Bay Shuttle (FY2020–FY2024)
- Osaka: the 8 municipal crossing ferries (渡船), daily averages as of FY2025
- Wakayama: Nankai Ferry Wakayama–Tokushima (FY2015–FY2024)
- Shiga: Okishima boat (2015–2021, non-residents only)

## Best sources

1. **令和6年度 和歌山県公共交通機関等資料集**, p.64 (PDF p.69) —
   https://www.pref.wakayama.lg.jp/prefg/020500/book_d/fil/R6siryou.pdf
   Nankai Ferry FY2003 and FY2010–FY2024: vehicles, non-vehicle passengers and
   total. Every row sums. The R3 edition (…/R3siryou.pdf) has the same table.
2. **Himeji island-route subcommittee (姫路市地域公共交通会議離島航路分科会)** —
   https://www.city.himeji.lg.jp/sangyo/0000033752.html (links to the FY2021–FY2026
   sessions). Each year's 姫路港～坊勢島航路 生活交通確保維持改善計画 gives
   ridership for the last ~8 fiscal years. The FY2026 plan (06R8kaizenkeikaku.pdf)
   covers FY2017–FY2024 and adds a section split and passenger-km for the subsidy
   year (Oct 2024–Sep 2025: 211,945 riders, 4,608,963 人キロ, average trip 21.7 km
   on a 22.1 km route). This is the only national-subsidy (国庫補助) island route
   in the region.
3. **Kobe transport bureau (神戸運輸監理部) island studies** — per-operator
   ridership for all five Ieshima routes, FY2005–FY2015:
   - H29.3 report (000021529.pdf), p.4: FY2005–FY2015 for the four Himeji routes
   - H28.3 report (000021526.pdf), p.9–10: FY2005–FY2014 including 坊勢渡船
   - H27.3 近畿圏の離島航路 report (000021519.pdf), p.15–18 and p.33–35:
     Numashima FY2003–FY2013 and Ieshima FY2005–FY2011
   Only the latest year from each was entered (FY2015 cut-off), except where
   nothing newer exists.
4. **こうべ未来都市機構 事業概要** (Kobe city council papers on city-owned
   companies). Each edition's 主要事業の推移 table gives 3 years of Bay Shuttle
   年間乗船客数. R7 edition (documents/79677) covers FY2022–24; R5 edition
   (documents/61432) covers FY2020–22. The FY2019 figure would be in the R3 or R2
   edition, which I did not locate.
5. **Osaka city 渡船場 pages** (city.osaka.lg.jp/kensetsu/page/00000112xx) give
   1日平均 per crossing. Annual figures are in 大阪市統計書 table 11-11 (Excel,
   flagged for download).
6. **第2期滋賀県離島振興計画** p.5 — Okishima boat riders excluding islanders,
   2013–2021.

## Definitions and oddities

- **Nankai Ferry** 利用者合計 = non-vehicle passengers (bus passengers included)
  + number of vehicles. Each vehicle counts as one person, so extra car
  occupants are missed. The operator's widely quoted "旅客数" is this total.
- **Okishima** counts exclude islanders and are labelled 年 (not 年度); the
  平成31年 label suggests a fiscal year might be meant. They are probably
  per-visitor counts, not boardings.
- **Osaka 渡船** figures are published as daily averages "令和7年度現在". The CSV
  annual value is that average × 365, computed here. An earlier version of two
  pages said 令和6年度現在 with slightly different values.
- **Numashima** FY2013: children counted as 0.5; commuter-pass riders estimated
  from pass sales.
- **Himeji–Bozejima**: FY2017–FY2019 predate the April 2020 merger of 坊勢汽船 and
  輝観光 and appear to be the two combined. FY2020 and FY2021 were revised
  slightly between plan editions (207,578 → 207,610; 212,642 → 212,638); both are
  in the CSV as separate source rows. Some sailings skip 男鹿島.
- **Ieshima study charts**: values are bar labels. The H27.3 report swaps the
  FY2010 labels of 輝観光 and 坊勢渡船 relative to the later reports (pre-2015, not
  entered).
- Kobe bureau figures come from 運航事業者各社資料 and cover both directions.
