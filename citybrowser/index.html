Show on map
All three hidden layers are bubbles for a place that already has one. Metro areas duplicate their city — "New York", "New York metropolitan area" and "New York–Jersey City–Newark urban area" are three bubbles for one place. Counties and rural districts are the area a town sits in, not the town: Los Angeles County, Savar Upazila, Bogor Regency. Districts of cities are one piece of a city — wards, arrondissements, boroughs.
Basemap
Vector tiles, so they stay sharp at every zoom. They need a network connection; with none, or with the basemap off, the bubbles still draw over bare water.
Urban centre matches
Matches GHS was not sure about — an urban centre with no city claiming it, or one whose population is far from the city it is named after. Reviewing does not mark a city as curated.
