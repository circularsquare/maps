Religion Dot Map

Every dot is 1,000 people, the same number for every group, so two colours can be compared by counting. Rings mark a body that is present but too small to reach one dot, or that reported congregations without a membership count — a ring says only "here", never how many.

Dots are placed at random within small census units, not at anyone's address. Those units are built to a few thousand people each, so this places people about where they live without using any population data — but it says nothing about where inside a unit a group lives.
