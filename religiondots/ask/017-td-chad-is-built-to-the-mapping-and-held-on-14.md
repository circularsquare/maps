# 017 — td: Chad is built to the mapping and held on 14: Christians are attacked in Lac and Logone Oriental

Summary: Chad is built but held: armed groups attack Christians in Lac and Logone Oriental. Draw it at its 22 regions as built, treat it like Burkina Faso and Mali, or leave it off? Recommended: draw it.

*Filed 2026-09-14 by session `f95259a4-td`. Anita's call; nothing is waiting on it.*

I built Chad up to the step before registration and stopped. The census table, the 2009
boundaries and the mapping are on disk and checked; nothing is in `countries.py` and nothing is
on the map. My own call would be to draw it at the 22 régions exactly as INSEED printed them,

## Why it is yours rather than mine

AGENT_BRIEF §3's first bar: whether publishing where a group lives affects its safety. Armed
groups in two Chadian régions are reported to pick victims partly by religion, and Burkina Faso
and Mali were held for you on the same question today, so the three may be easiest to rule on
together.

## The detail

**The source.** INSEED's RGPH2 2009 structure volume, Tableau 5.07: six answers (animist,
Catholic, Muslim, Protestant, other, no religion) as one-decimal shares for 22 régions, beside
each région's population; Tableau 5.06 gives the national counts. It is the office's own printed
table at the office's own tier, about 500,000 people a unit. It was on INSEED's website until
the site was rebuilt and is now reachable through the Wayback Machine. Chad is not in UNSD's
table 28. `sources/td.md` has the checks.

**The evidence that made me stop.**

- USCIRF, *Freedom of Religion or Belief in the Sahelian Countries of Burkina Faso, Cameroon,
  and Chad* (October 2025): from 2020, JAS-Boko Haram and ISWAP "prioritized victimizing civilian
  communities, including Christians" in Lac Province, and in 2025 they "and violent Fulani herder
  groups continue to target religious communities in Chad".
- US State Department, *2023 Report on International Religious Freedom: Chad*: "armed groups in
  the Logone Oriental region attacked a number of Christian communities and killed 17
  individuals, including Pastor Doumro Tadingao Gaston and 12 congregants attending a prayer
  meeting". Religious leaders tie the tension to "mostly Muslim herders and mostly Christian
  farmers".

**What the map would show in those two places.**

| région | people, 2009 | the group at risk, as it would be drawn | dots at 1:1,000 |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Lac | 433,790 | Christians 3,928 (0.9%), animists 872 (0.2%) | about 4 and 1 |
| Logone Oriental | 779,339 | Christians are the majority (90.0%); Muslims 8.6% | Muslims about 67 |

Dots inside a région are placed by population density (Kontur), not by where any religious
group lives, so nothing locates a community more finely than the région. The north/south
divide is in every atlas of Chad, and the figures are seventeen years old.

**Why I would draw it.** §14 rule 2 (no finer than the state's own publication) is met by
construction, and §14.2's reflect-or-reveal test comes out as reflect: Lac's Christians are four
dots in a région of 433,790, and in Logone Oriental the Christians are the région. Guinea (§9dh)
was considered on similar grounds and not escalated, and Nigeria (`ask/answered/010-ng`) was
drawn at 37 states on your ruling. What differs from Guinea, and why I did not decide it alone:
a named armed group in one of these régions is reported to single out Christians.

**How Chad differs from the two held today.** Burkina Faso would be drawn at 45 provinces,
about 310,000 people each, with its Christian and animist minorities inside the insurgency
provinces. Mali's Christians and animists are concentrated in three central cercles where there
have been identity massacres. In Chad the Christian and animist concentration is the whole
south, where Christians are the majority, and the région with the insurgency is 98.8% Muslim.

**Three ways you could rule.**

1. Draw Chad at the 22 régions as built.
2. Wait, and give Chad whatever answer Burkina Faso and Mali get.
3. Leave Chad off the map.
