*Filed 2026-09-15 by session `d743fc47`. Anita's call; nothing is waiting on it.*

## What I did

Left Algeria as its builder drew it, with no presence rings for Jews or Ibadis. The rest of the
Maghreb (Morocco next, then Tunisia and Libya) is built the same way unless you rule otherwise.

## What it costs to reverse

Nothing to undo. Adding rings later means a non-survey source per community, a rings entry per
country, and the build tail.

## Why it is yours rather than mine

Your Maghreb ruling (`ask/RULINGS.md`, 2026-09-15) left the construction open, and named rings for
Jews and Ibadis as part of it. Whether the map marks where a very small minority lives, and from what
kind of source, is a display question about who is shown.

## The detail

From the Algeria build (`sources/dz.md`; `runlog.md` 2026-09-15):

- **Jews.** Arab Barometer wave V had three "Jewish" answers. All three were among six interviews
  where the religion answer contradicts the branch or church given to the next question, so they were
  dropped. No Jewish answer survives.
- **Ibadis.** Seven Ibadi answers in waves V to VII, none of them from Ghardaïa, the M'zab valley
  where Algeria's Ibadi community is known to live. The survey cannot place them.
- **So a ring would come from outside the survey**: a community's own count or a published estimate,
  at a place rather than a share of a wilaya.
- **The same question comes up next** for Morocco's Jews, Tunisia's Jews on Djerba and Libya's Ibadis
  in the Nafusa mountains.

Options:

1. **Leave rings off across the Maghreb**, and draw only what the surveys measure.
2. **Draw rings where a named non-survey source gives a count for a place**, marked as such, and say
   so in each country's note.
