About

Every city in the world, sized by population. Each settlement appears once it reaches 5,000 people; colour runs dark to bright with population.

The timeline is warped so recent centuries get more of the track than ancient millennia: the first half of it covers everything up to 1400, then 1400–1900, then 1900–today.

Historical populations from Stadestér, which compiles Chandler, Bairoch/Buringh, de Vries, Populstat and others. Figures before 1800 are estimates and often disagree between sources. The modern era comes from UN WUP 2025.

Coastlines from Natural Earth, map built with MapLibre GL.
