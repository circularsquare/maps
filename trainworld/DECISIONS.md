## 2026-10-27
- **The ground curve fades out over the tail's own 300-700 km window, and its normaliser stays the plain curve** (T-1603, Anita's option 1 of T-1508): past 700 km only the tail carries trips, with no new constant; a closed world (no tail) keeps the plain ground. The normaliser is unchanged because trips under 300 km are the same either way and the pipeline's rows and packs need no rebuild; the cost is that a place's ground departures now total a little under 0.107 per resident (the share that went past the fade is dropped, not given to nearer towns), which is what the double count needed.
- **A move of the draw anchor shifts the drawn pieces rather than laying them again** (T-1170, my call, under Anita's "slight lie" rule): the clock worker keeps every edge's and line's points in float64 mercator (exact at any anchor) and moves the cached stroke pieces by the anchor shift in float32, so a segment can sit up to half a float32 step (at its distance from the old anchor) off its exact place: measured 3-5 cm near the view on Japan, never past 12.5 cm since a move longer than 2^22 local units (about 3,000 km) is laid exactly, as is every 8th move so the rounding cannot add up. Station boxes are exact. `?patch=check` lays every move exactly; `?patch=shiftcheck` logs the error. notes/T-1170.md.
- **The Lines and Stations tabs list the world a task after it arrives; a line chip's symbol is measured in idle time** (T-1021, my call): `listWorld` follows `world` by a `setTimeout(0)`, a new symbol shows as plain text until measured (a frame or two), the chip's CSS zoom is read after the paint, and the track's zones (`followTrack`) are worked out in a task right after the state's. Each was part of one 700-900 ms task when a big save loads; none of them changes what is shown a moment later. notes/T-1001.md "T-1021".
- **Planned lines and the blueprint are priced only when a panel shows them, a moment after the last edit** (T-1171, my call): the Build tab's quote and a planned line's Construct price are clock queries, asked 250 ms after the world last changed (at once for the C key), one answer per world version. The figure shown can lag an edit by about 300 ms; a state no longer pays for the trials (1.3 s an edit on the real New York save with 12 planned lines). notes/T-1171.md.
- **Region money shares are worked out in idle time** (T-1021, my call): each line's and the upkeep's split between regions is posted to the clock worker from `requestIdleCallback` (at most 2 s late) rather than inside the state's task; the ledger's region split can use the last network's shares for up to an hour tick after an edit. It was about 200 ms of a big save's first state. notes/T-1001.md "T-1021".
- **The market's pass goes through destination zones west to east, a window at a time** (T-1545, my call): zones by their centres' x; a station's route costs are held from its first read to its last and its trips to its assignment. On all of Japan 197 stations are read at once and 1,954 touched (the commutes' trips home make the long spans), against 562 and 6,508 in zone order and more in a Hilbert order. Searched strategies are kept to their station's assignment within 128 MB (`WINDOW_STRATEGY_BYTES`), those wanted soonest first, the rest searched again (698 of 8,877 searches on Japan). Figures move by summing order only (at worst about 1e-6 of a line or station, rail a day within 1e-7). The lean's 8,192-station gate (`LEAN_OD_BYTES`, T-1503) stays although its table is gone: lifting it costs a walk per destination.
- **The network canvas has no MSAA at dpr 3 and up** (T-955, my call): the stroke shader rounds only its caps, so a line's long side edges rely on MSAA and stair-step without it (visible at dpr 1 to 3 magnified); stations, trains and crowd outlines look the same either way. At dpr 3 a step is a third of a CSS px, and the 4x buffers there are about 80 MB on a phone, so `overlay.ts` asks for `antialias: dpr < 3`. The stencil stays (track through station boxes). Analytic side edges in STROKE_FS would let it go everywhere but would bead at the segment joints.
- **A bus runs at 70% of the car's local street speed, at most 18 km/h** (T-1577, Anita's yes to the idea; the factor and the rest my call): min(18, 0.7 x the car's density-based street speed in the zone), so 18 below about 10,000 homes and jobs a km2 and 11.55 in the densest; no extra floor, since the car's own floor is 16.5. Taken from the zone's pack in the clock worker (the track model has no density), kept equal to demand's by a test. Running cost stays $9 a bus-km, so a slow bus costs less an hour ($104 at 11.55 km/h against $162). A bus line is marked in demand by its bus cars, not by having a bus-only stop, so a short bus between two stations pays the boarding cost too. notes/T-1466.md.
- **Far out, bubbles come before lines and stations for the whole overview** (T-1550, my call): the threshold is zoom 8.5, the one where the zone bubbles replace the local ones (`OVERVIEW_ZOOM`), not a separate number: at 6.5, 7.5 and 8.5 lines cross the New York bubble in all three, and stations are not drawn before zoom 9. With a city selected the inter-city bubbles show only up to 8.5 too, so the rule is "while `cityOverview`". Closer in T-1515's network-first order stands.
- **The fare scale shows in Japan's terms, stored in India's** (T-1554, my call): the panel divides a stored scale by Japan's default (6.1, `incomeScale("JP")`), so Japan reads 1.00x, India 0.16x (the brief said about 0.17x, from a baseline of 6.0), the US 1.90x; typed values go back by the same factor. Nothing saved or sent to demand changes: `FARE_BASELINE` stays India, the curve stays in India's dollars, the fare scale in baseline units. Stepping by 0.05 shown needs finer storage than a tenth, so a player's scale is kept to a hundredth (saved tenths read back the same). The heading names the country ("Fares in Japan") on a line of its own above the stepper, so no name is cut.
- **Crowding colours gain steps above 9 minutes** (T-1553, my call): crimson at 20, plum 25, purple 35, indigo 50, and red-orange at 12, since long lines' figures fall in 9 to 20 and the real save's highest is 23 (notes/T-1553.md). The ramp up to 9 is Anita's, unchanged; the grades for text (6, 9, 15) are unchanged.
- **The solve's results carry each station's transfers** (T-1530, my call): every period result has the transfers in per route node and kind of day (the sims' `transfers_in`, scaled as the query scales its pairs), so the station bubbles' split shows with the figures; a solve's final still asks the transfer pairs and takes their answer, so finals are the query's to the bit. About 140 kB more a period result on the real save, a few ms a population; asking the workers instead waited one or more rounds (40 s on a load) and got nothing after an edit.
- **A worker's first figures estimate its other periods from its first** (T-1538, my call, over T-1206's coarse level and bigger commute chunks): in a rough solve and in the free-flow preview after an edit, each population's other periods are its first period's free flow scaled by the periods' trips (everyday purpose by purpose), on their own graphs; the preview's periods are solved after it and the rough solve is never reused, so finals are unchanged. The coarse level would need a second copy of the zone in every worker for a 15-20% shorter period; the pool is at most 3, so dropping a worker's second period saves 35-40%.
- **A cached demand result is found by the save's network inputs before the network** (T-1539, my call): a SHA-256 of the save's track bytes and the header fields the snapshot reads, the result holding the snapshot that load worked out; the arriving network is compared with it. A game opened from the menu also fits the camera to the save's built stations and shows only their realms as soon as the save is read, since the zoomed-out bubbles need the camera there.
- **A game load shows the map at the zone in view's first figures** (T-1533, my call, replacing T-1051's "drawn once, when the card goes"): the shade, outlines and bubbles come in one step when a cached result, a rough solve or an estimate gives the zone in view figures, behind the card; the card and the held clock still wait for the final (T-347 unchanged). Anita asked for the city in view's demand within about 5 s, and the blank map hid figures that were in for a minute or more.
- **A rough solve of the zone in view goes first** (T-1533, my call): while a whole solve waits for packs, the zone in view alone, no market, as an estimate never final; it holds the other packs' opens back until its free-flow round, which can put the final a few seconds later on a game like the real save (5 zones), traded for the zone in view's figures 4-8 times sooner. City packs open one at a time and a game's market packs go to the workers after its city packs, since composing China's 370 blocked a worker for a minute.
- **A loose-end ring means track you can see** (T-1534, agent's call, completing T-1485's "only on drawn track"): the rings show at the loose ends of built track and active blueprints, not a switched-off blueprint (drawn grey and faint) and not a piece under 4 px long on screen at the current zoom (`MIN_STUB_PX`; the length is summed along the piece, so it comes back as the map zooms in). The old filter tested `net.edgePts`, which holds every edge, so it passed all of them.
- **The market's rides reserve a line per track component; the local layers' do not** (T-1511, my call): past the 6 cheapest lines a market zone's rides take up to 2 more, the cheapest line of each track component the 6 miss (`RIDE_TRACK_MAX`). Applied to the local layers too it moved New York's rail trips +3,820 and China's +29,000 with nothing the market reads changed, so it stays the market's. Market passes on China about 1.1-1.3x, New York and Tokyo unchanged (notes/T-1511.md).

## 2026-10-26
- **The pointer is off the map over anything laid on it** (T-1522, my call 2026-10-26): the loose-end rings take the pointer from the window and count it on the map only over the map itself (MapLibre's canvas container), so over the view tabs or any control inside the map's box they go, as when it leaves the map.
- **A terminal draws a buffer stop only where the track ends** (T-1527, my call 2026-10-26): the dark bar is at the platform end with no track beyond it, found as a station with one track leaving it (flag 512, the -axis end, where the track stops at the node); a terminal with track on both sides has no bar, since there is no buffer stop to show, and its sidings already mark both ends. The hover on the bar follows.
- **Station ridership circles are one disc shaded by the transfers' share, with a faint edge** (T-1531, my calls 2026-10-26, replacing T-1448's nested discs): the colour runs from the entries blue to the transfers green, evened in OKLab as the bubble ramp is, linear in the share (no skew), the end colours kept (light #2456e0 and #169a50, dark #8aa6ff and #52d68e). Origins and Transfers are the two ends. Edge 1 px at half strength over a 2.5 px casing at 30% (was 1.75 px at 95% over 3.5 px at 55%); fill 20% light, 26% dark. The Total hover's total row gets a dot in the circle's own colour. The mode mix's light-map grey (#aaaaaa, T-1450) belongs to the local bubbles and is untouched.
- **The font is Nunito and not a setting; related lines start on** (T-1524, T-1529, my calls 2026-10-26): a stored `font` is dropped on load with the other retired settings and the Zen Maru Gothic fallback for kana and kanji stays. The related lines toggle was never stored (a signal kept while the page is open), so its default simply went to on and no old choice needed carrying over.
- **Clicks and hovers pick lines, stations and trains before demand bubbles** (T-1515, Anita 2026-10-26, reversing T-097's "Clicks: trains and stations first, then a bubble, then the rest of the network", which the code had become bubbles first, and T-486's "a click never acts on something drawn beneath another clickable thing" for these): a small bubble's reach past its disc (`MIN_PICK_PX`, `bubblePad`) hid the lines and bus routes behind it. With the select tool, a rail or bus line, station or train within its own reach now wins, for the click, the bubble tag, the cursor and the track tag alike; a bubble keeps its wider reach where none of those is near. Track and junctions without a line still give way to a bubble.
- **A trip's fare curve extra is shared between the lines it rides by km** (T-1516, my call 2026-10-26): it was all put on the line the trip first boards, so under a tapering curve (Anita's China save: $0.05 a km to 20 km, then $0.031) a feeder bus into a long rail trip took the whole trip's discount and its fares went below zero (Bus 13 in Shenzhen, "-$312k"). Now each line takes the base share of its boarding and the curve's fare a km on its own km, which is never below zero; the network's total is unchanged. Splitting the whole fare by km (the bus would lose most of its base) was the other choice; boarding plus km keeps the rule players already see.
- **A zone's access list reserves slots by track, not by line** (T-1506, my call 2026-10-26): past its 8 best stations a list keeps the best station of each track component none of them is on, up to 10. A slot per unlisted line (the proposal) cost Tokyo 1.28x the market's pass time for +30 longer boardings and added nothing on China, since a line on linked track is reached by changing trains. notes/T-1506.md.
- **Loose-end marks show near the pointer, as accent rings** (T-1485, my call 2026-10-26): Anita's "floating station bubbles" with the track and station tools were the loose-end marks, white with a dark rim like a station, at every loose end in view, many at the ends of faint unserved or switched-off track that does not show at city zoom. Now a hollow accent ring within 160 px of the pointer (all of them on a touch screen), and only on track the network draws. Not reproduced: a loose end with no drawn track at all; the drawn-track rule covers it if it exists.
- **A line's past-24-hour running cost is held at the service its fares were solved for** (T-1492, my call 2026-10-26): both figures are the rate in force times 24 hours, and fares can only move when demand lands, so the running cost now uses the trains and cars the shown demand result solved (`LineDemand.tph`, `cars`; `todayService` in game/today.ts) rather than an estimate of the new fares; a line with no figures yet, or a cached result, uses its trains now.
- **A zone in view without its bubbles says "Loading data…" and asks again** (T-1493, my call 2026-10-26): the gap Anita saw was the bubbles being asked for and built after the zone's figures were in (up to seconds while the workers solve), which no loading state covered; the status tag now covers it, and an ask that ends with nothing is retried from 1 s doubling to 8 s, since a dropped answer (a worker replaced, a zone not yet in the result) was otherwise asked again only at the next result.
- **Station ridership circles are nested discs over the bubbles** (T-1448, T-1449, my calls 2026-10-26): of three looks tried on the real save (nested discs, a pie, a two-colour ring; artifacts/trainworld/t1448/look-*.png), the nested one reads best and stays calmest: a blue disc for all riders with a green inner disc of the transfers' share of the area, translucent, each edge on a thin casing of the land so it holds against the purple bubbles. The ring was loud, the pie's wedge hard to read. Total is entries plus transfers (each rider counted once, where they board), so blue is trips starting there, not starting or ending. The circles moved off the network canvas to a 2D canvas over the bubbles; their hover and clicks come before the bubbles, but the network under them keeps its own. The ridership row sits second at the map's upper left, between the bubbles and the track rows; folding a row keeps its choice in force. One circle per complex always; the old setting is dropped from storage.
- **One rider is drawn from the boarding account's shares, and the explorer link sets the purpose filter** (T-1436, my calls 2026-10-26): no per-rider or per-stop-pair figure exists, so the rider's stops come from the period's boardings and the line's alighting shares, their purpose from the stretch's riders by purpose; the two assumptions are that who is aboard got on in proportion to what boarded and stayed through the shares, and that purpose is independent of the stops. Opening the explorer sets the map's purpose filter to the rider's purpose (the explorer reads that filter, and "all" would show whichever purpose has most trips between the stations) and its period to the rider's; the filter stays set when she comes back to the train.
- **Station ink is #8f8b85 in dark, new bubbles and light-mode grey** (T-1432, T-1441, T-1450, my calls 2026-10-26): the station box rim, terminal end bars, upgrade marks and selection outline use their own `stationInk` (the map's #2b2b2b in light, #8f8b85 in dark): bright enough to read on the dark land and the terminal bars, not the map ink's near white, which made the boxes loud. The mode mix's even-share grey is 170 on the light map (190 in dark), the one theme-dependent bubble colour; the shade is part of the scheme key so built discs repaint on a switch. Starting any new game (the sheet's Start, a world-map start too) sets Destinations on, replacing T-801's Homes for world-map starts; the setting stays per browser, so opening a saved game keeps what was stored. A first visit with no saved or account games opens on the new game sheet, with Back to the one-button menu.
- **The trouble list is a corner button, not a dock tab** (T-1433, my call 2026-10-26): the map's lower right above the attribution button, where nothing else sits and it never competes with the top bar or panels; a tab would hide it from anyone on another tab, and a list some players will not treat as real trouble should cost nothing shut. Its cuts keep the real NYC save at 20 rows, not 96: only the map's orange and red capacity tags (90% and up), one row per kind by a station; riders left behind from 3% of those wanting the line; full trains only at a quarter of trains and with fewer left behind (a line leaving riders behind is one row, its full trains in the hover). Reversals sit second, after broken lines, since they are usually an accident and cheap to fix.
- **Imported names are tidied by shape, in the sim's load** (T-1442, T-330, my call 2026-10-26): a save has no "imported" flag, so `track/names.rs` runs on every `load` and in `realsave`, and changes only a name with the untidy shape: a station name with a part in capitals (two words, or one of five letters), a line name that is a real feed's key, a space and the route, with no symbol. Titles keep initialisms (HBLR, NJT, PATH) and drop a trailing "STATION"; a mixed-case "Seoul Station" or "Battersea Power Station" stays, since cutting those makes a wrong name. A player's own name in capitals ("PARIS") would be re-cased too; accepted. Lines lose the feed word ("subway 6X" is "6X", "lirr Babylon Branch" "Babylon Branch"); a chip with no symbol shows a code of up to five capitals or digits whole, else the first letter.
