# T-737: all of Japan's rail network at 4x (stress test, 2026-10-19)

Anita: "stress test: try loading in like all of japan network and running it at 4x speed and see if
its laggy."

## Plain answer

- **Playing and panning do not lag.** At 240x (the "4x" button) and 960x, panning and zooming over
  Tokyo, Osaka, Kansai and the whole country at zoom 6 runs at 55-60 fps, 1-7.4 ms of main thread a
  frame, 0.3-1% of frames over 50 ms (single hitches of 100-300 ms during long pans at zoom 6-8, mostly
  MapLibre's tile work). The clock worker is 1-9% of a core without riders, 20-25% with them.
- **Loading takes about 8 s** (was 10 s; fixed below): the clock worker derives the track (709 lines,
  10,320 nodes) and the first state costs the main thread one 360-500 ms task.
- **Demand is where it breaks.** On the whole network demand never gives a figure: every solving
  worker runs out of WASM memory about a minute in. On Japan's seven cities alone (no line joins
  them) the first solve takes 12 minutes with 2.5 GB in each worker. New York's real save, for
  comparison: final in about 50 s.

## The saves

`../artifacts/trainworld/t737-japan.save` (whole country) and `t737-japan-cities.save` (the seven
city packs plus 10 km), both sandbox. Built by realnet's new `n02` adapter (`pipeline/realnet_n02.py`):

    python pipeline/realnet.py net jpn      # or jpn7
    python pipeline/realnet.py geom jpn
    data/work/target/release/realsave.exe --city jpn
    python pipeline/realnet.py gamesave jpn # then the header's mode set to sandbox

- Source: MLIT KSJ N02-24 (every line's track and stations, 576 operator lines), frequencies from
  japanriders' passing counts (MLIT 通過人員, people a day past a section; 384 of the 576 lines have
  one): peak trains an hour each way = 6% of the day over the train's seats, 0.5-24; midday 55%,
  first hour 30%; lines without a count take a default by operator kind. Run times from track
  length at a speed by kind (Shinkansen 160 km/h, JR 48, private 42, subway 32, tram 16).
- Per line, the track is a graph of stations and junctions; routes are its longest station paths in
  turn, a branch carried on to the nearest station of its trunk, stubs cut back, parallel pieces whose
  stops are all served dropped. Whole country: 709 lines, 10,171 stations (of N02's 10,235), 73,308
  trips a day, fleet 21,499 cars. Seven cities: 491 lines, 5,896 stations.
- Rough on purpose, like the other realnet saves: each line has its own double track (no shared
  trunks), the track model reports 1,911 geometry issues and 62 flat crossings it does not check on
  load. Cash is irrelevant (sandbox; the network would cost US$1.6 trillion).

## Measurements

`checks/t737-japan.mjs` (now a standing check), headless Chrome 1400x900, RX 6600, 2 solving workers
(the target machine's), my own Vite. Outputs `../artifacts/trainworld/t737/japan-*.json`.

**Load** (import to the lines on the map): 10.3 s before, 7.7 s after the fix; the clock worker's
"loaded" 7.4-7.9 s before, 5.7 s after. Main thread: the first state 360-500 ms (one long task of
500-700 ms). Clock worker profile before the fix: `load` 6.3 s, of which the second load in
`masksForLoaded` about 3 s; the derive with water masks is about 4.9 s in WASM (native `realsave`
derives the same network in 0.42 s without masks).

**Demand, whole country.** Every zone is reached (45 city and prefecture zones plus 46 market packs
opened, 93 loads); each solving worker opens them in 16 s (233 MB). Then:
1. Every worker refused the network: "invalid fare or segment distances". Seven two-stop lines whose
   stations are closer than their platforms (N02 has such pairs) get a negative track distance from
   `line_stop_s`, and `set_fares` rejects the whole network. Demand then never finished, silently,
   for 25 minutes (the error only in the console). Fixed (below).
2. After that, every worker dies with `RuntimeError: unreachable` about 60 s in: out of WASM memory.
   A population's skims hold station x station tables (`Skims.r`, `station_exp`, the everyday OD),
   over the whole cluster's stations, and the whole country is one cluster: 10,320^2 x 4 B = 426 MB
   per table. Native (no 4 GB cap), Tokyo's pack alone on the whole network (`calib`, one crowding
   round): **243 s and 3.28 GB peak**. The same with the network cut to Tokyo's box plus 20 km (2,658
   stations, 224 line pieces): **107 s and 0.50 GB**, the same figures (operator boardings 11,032,799
   against 11,032,801). Tokyo's own census network (Kanto only): 77 s.

**Demand, seven cities** (5,992 stations): one solve with 31 zones; first figures and final at 680-735 s
(New York real save: about 50 s). Worker WASM memory at final 2,659 and 2,458 MB; the renderer
process 6 GB private. Profile of a solving worker over the solve (735 s): 69% in the crowding
rounds (`crowd_with_external`, each a full pass), 23% in the free-flow pass, packs opening 13 s,
`set_network` 12 s. The Tokyo cluster carries several zone populations (Tokyo and the Kanto
prefectures), each searching the whole cluster's network for its destinations.

**Playing** (whole country without riders, seven cities with):

| | fps | main ms a frame | frames over 50 ms | worst frame | clock worker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole country, 240x | 56.6-60 | 1.0-5.7 | 0-5 of ~500 a scene | 250 ms (zoom 6 pan) | 1-9% |
| Whole country, 960x | 57.2-60 | 1.3-6.9 | 0-5 | 167 ms | 1-9% |
| Seven cities, 240x | 55.4-60 | 2.3-6.8 | 0-12 | 200 ms | 20-25% |
| Seven cities, 960x | 58.8-60 | 1.6-7.4 | 0-2 | 117 ms | 20-25% |

Over the moving scenes 4.6-5.5 ms of main thread a frame, 0.3% of frames over 50 ms. The hourly
`money` message costs the main thread 3-16 ms, the trips hour 0-1 ms. A `state` arriving mid-play
(a zone joining) 130-200 ms. Main-thread profiles of the pans: MapLibre's render about 70-80% of the
busy time, our overlay draw and picking about 10%; `widgets.tsx` reading `currentCSSZoom` in a hook,
140 ms over a 10 s pan (a forced style read on every render; small).

## What changed

- `workers/demandClient.ts` `packService`: a hop's fare distance is clamped at 0, so two stops closer
  than their platforms no longer make every worker refuse the network. The track model's negative
  distance itself is T-760.
- `workers/clock.worker.ts` `masksBeforeLoad` (with `TrackApi.save_points` in `sim/src/track/wasm.rs`):
  before a load, the water masks of the zones the save's nodes and curve points fall in are fetched,
  so the network is derived once instead of twice; `masksForLoaded` still catches a zone only the
  drawn curves reach. Whole country 10.3 to 7.7 s; every save that meets a zone whose mask is not in
  yet gains the same one derive. Smoke passes.
- `pipeline/realnet.py`: the `n02` adapter, cities `jpn` and `jpn7`, `boundary` as False or a list of
  packs, no station merge loop when `merge_m` is 0, per-line shapes into `geom`.
- `checks/t737-japan.mjs`, the standing check.
