# T-1285 The world's coarse water mask

Found in T-1275 (notes/T-1277.md): open sea outside every pack's water mask was land, so track
crossed the Atlantic at ground level at land prices. Done 2026-10-26 in tree t1285.

## What was built

- **Data**: `pipeline/world_water.py build` writes `world.water` (format in the script and
  `cost::WorldWater`): the sea from OSM's coastline water polygons (the same
  `data/raw/osmdata/water-polygons-split-4326.fgb` the packs' sea comes from) and Natural Earth
  1:10m lakes (`maps/data/ne_10m_lakes/`, public domain) for the Caspian, the Great Lakes and the
  like. Each 30-degree tile is drawn with PIL at 4 x 4 subcells a cell, islands after the water, and
  a cell is water when at least 8 of its 16 subcells are. Rows are run-length coded with LEB128 gaps.
  The terrain files were no help: they cover only the zones' boxes and GEDTM30 has no water class,
  so the open sea between zones is in none of them.
- **Sizes**: 1/60 degree (1.85 km north-south): 0.58 MB, 443,060 run boundaries, 66.2% of cells
  water, 6 minutes on one core. 1/120 degree (0.93 km): 1.34 MB, 1,004,562 boundaries, 9 minutes.
  Kept 1/60: inside packs their own 25 m masks decide anyway, and the checks pass at 1/60; 1/120
  is one flag away (`--per-deg 120`, columns fit u16 up to 1/182) if coasts between zones ever
  matter more.
- **Sim**: `WaterMask::covers` (a raster says land or water here); `MaskSet { rasters, world }`
  answers water where any pack raster does, else, where no raster covers the point, what the world
  mask says. `WorldWater` reads lon/lat through the network's frame. `TrackApi::add_world_water`
  installs it and reworks every edge once. Kept across loads, frame changes and `clear_water`.
- **App**: the clock worker fetches `packs/world.water` once at start; previews, edits and loads
  wait for it (`worldWaterIntoModel`); a 404 leaves the old behaviour. Vite serves it from
  `data/terrain/world.water` (`TW_WORLD_WATER` overrides) and a build copies it to `dist/packs/`.

## Left as it is

- Rivers and small lakes outside every zone are not in the world mask (zones now tile the built
  countries, and their packs carry their own inland water).
- Before a zone's mask arrives, the world mask decides inside its box; edits wait for the zone's
  mask anyway (zones.md 6), so nothing is built on the coarse coast where a pack exists.
