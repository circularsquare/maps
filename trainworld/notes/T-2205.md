## What was wrong

Classified the 128/181 close pairs: nearly all were apart after B (at 1.0 to 2.0 of their spacing)
and made close by the snap and routing. Two kinds stood out: two straight pieces leaving one key node
the same way, drawn on top of each other (the 4 and the B/D from 161 St, Graham Av and Central Av, 170
St twice); and stations next to a key node on pieces 45 degrees apart. No push along the pieces (the
spread) can part either.

## The separation (layout.ts `separate`)

The drawing as drawn is put into variables that keep it octilinear by construction: x, y of each
piece's ends, one distance per station along its leg, a bent piece's legs fixed in direction (so the
bend is `a + l0 d0` with `l0 = cross(z - a, d1) / cross(d0, d1)`, linear in the ends). Every
constraint is then a linear row. Port clashes between straight pieces are broken first by giving one
a zero-length first leg 45 degrees off, which the spacing terms lengthen.

Tried, in order:
1. LSQR with penalties (direction 100, spacing 10, anchor 1) on full x, y per station: directions
   broke by up to 17 degrees, close pairs stuck near 100; the penalty compromise leaves violations
   where a push moves a big rigid region.
2. Same in the reduced variables: better (74 New York) but still a plateau, and weaker anchors made
   it blow up (LSQR unconverged).
3. Sweeps of projections (Gauss-Seidel, position-based-dynamics style) on the rows: fast (0.2 s) and
   converging. Two fixes made it work: a station pushed across a straight piece moves both of the
   piece's ends alike (else straightness fought every push), and the gap round a bend (two stations
   either side of a bend could meet at it, the pill overlaps of adjacent stations). The draw after it
   keeps only order (a tenth of an even share), since its half-share clamp moved 100 Tokyo stations by
   up to 0.6 hops and undid the work.
4. Stability: projections drift (nothing pulls back), so the rebuild check moved 0.4 to 1.9 hops.
   Hildreth's method (dual coordinate ascent; the row-action method for the nearest point of a
   polyhedron, Hildreth 1957, see Censor and Zenios, Parallel Optimization, ch. 6) from the last
   drawing gives the nearest feasible drawing, continuous in its start, but still drifted where a few
   terms cannot all be met (the dual grows without bound). Capping each term's correction at 0.3 of a
   hop fixed that: 0.024 / 0.022. A first layout keeps the projections (freer, closer to zero pairs);
   a rebuild uses capped Hildreth from the last drawing (`lastPlaces`, which now carries the drawn
   places beside the B ones), unless under 90% of the graph was in the last drawing.

Background: Nöllenburg and Wolff's MIP and LOOM (Bast, Brosi, Storandt, octilinear grid graphs) make
spacing a hard constraint by construction; both are bigger rebuilds than this task. The separation is
the same idea (hard octilinearity, spacing as constraints) done as a projection on top of the
existing pipeline.

Not taken: fewer B rounds (20) saves about 0.5 s but the rebuild moved 0.04 to 0.06 hops.

## Hooks (draw.ts, T-2206)

Shinjuku's two hooks were the Toei Shinjuku line, which turns at the station and crossed none of the
four axes, and a stop 1.8 pitches past the pill's end with no line between (two pills, the far one a
dot). Three changes: an axis may pass up to 2 pitches to one side (cost a pitch of pill a pitch) when
none through the place crosses every stopping line; a line beside the axis within 1.25 pitches counts
as under the pill's edge (was 0.5); stops up to 4 pitches apart share a pill when no ribbon crosses
the axis between them (`crossesAny`, ribbons parallel to the axis ignored).
