## T-2999 numbers and sources

- Length: Citadis X05 comes as 24 m, 32-37 m and 43-45 m (product sheet above; https://www.railway-technology.com/projects/citadis-x05-light-rail-vehicles/). The game's 2 cars is 40 m. Kept.
- Upkeep: game ground rail US$350 real a km a day (US$128k a year); street tram 0.5x (US$64k), off-street 1x. Tampere's tram maintenance alliance about EUR 2.5M a year, EUR 100-150k a km (https://tampereenratikka.fi/en/?p=4249), so street track is about half of real. Proposal: street 0.5x to 1.0x. It barely changes play: a 10 km line every 5 minutes with 2 cars runs about 4,300 tram-km a day, US$3.5M a game day in running costs, against US$175k of street upkeep (US$350k at 1.0x) and US$105k for 21 stops. Trams earn because 240 places run at US$8 a tram-km against a bus's 53 at US$6, a ratio that is real (Tours: tram EUR 8, BRT EUR 6 a km run, https://www.adtc-grenoble.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Revue-Transport-janv-fev-2017-BHNS-tram.pdf). If she wants trams harder, the levers are the tram car's running cost (US$2 a car-km) or the stop's upkeep (US$50 a day against a station's 500).
