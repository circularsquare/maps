# T-700: Shanghai's sharp walking divide

Anita, playtest 2026-10-19 (notes/playtest-2026-10-19.md): around Middle Henan Rd and Nine-Zigzag
Bridge, destinations west of a line walk about 25%, those east of it about 0%, "they won't walk to a
place like 5 minutes away".

## Cause: a bug in the zone-level walk factor round water (T-275)

A walk the whole way costs `straight distance between the two cells x walk_factor(zone of one,
zone of the other)` (everyday.rs `no_rail` and `trip_inputs`; the subzone pairs in the rail pass;
kernel.rs `set_walk_fine` for commutes). The factor is one number per zone pair, made offline by
`sim/src/bin/pack_walk.rs`: from the zone's representative cell (nearest its weighted centre), the
walk to every cell of the other zone over the straight line, weighted by people and jobs, with a
cell the search cannot reach counted at the cap, `exp(255/64) = 53.75`. Both directions averaged.

A 2 km zone that straddles a river nobody can walk across therefore gets a factor that is a blend of
1 (its cells on the representative's bank) and 53.75 (those on the far bank), and that blend is
applied to every walk into, out of and inside it, same bank or not.

In Shanghai the zone column from x = 1,133 m east of the pack origin (about 121.4856 E, one block
east of Middle Henan Rd) to 3,133 m spans the Huangpu: zones 590, 704 (the Bund and Lujiazui), 702
(the Old City, Nine-Zigzag Bridge, Yu Garden) and 705. Zone 704's representative cell is in
Lujiazui (x 2,069), so every Puxi cell is cut off from it. The factors:

| | to 820 (Middle Henan Rd) | to 704 (Bund) | to 702 (Old City) |
|---|---|---|---|
| from 704 | 48.2 | 23.1 (itself) | 40.6 |
| inside 702 | | | 14.5 |

Walks from cell 17118 on Middle Henan Rd (zone 820), plain page shares against a dense-district
drive with free parking (`notes/t700_cells.py`):

| cell | lon | zone | straight m | factor | counted as km | walk share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17114 | 121.4846 | 820 | 284 | 1.00 | 0.28 | 83% |
| 16322 | 121.4857 | 704 | 324 | 48.18 | 15.6 | 0% (over 10 km: not offered) |
| 16297 | 121.4878 | 704 | 525 | 48.18 | 25.3 | 0% |
| 16299 | 121.4867 | 702 | 568 | 6.83 | 3.9 | 7% |

The real walks are straight: `pack_walk --probe` from Middle Henan Rd to the Bund (628 m) and to
Nine-Zigzag Bridge (1,239 m) both find the straight line on the walking barrier itself. So the
barrier raster, the bank cells snap to, merged cells and no-car shares are all fine; the divide is
the zone edge, and only the aggregation into one factor per zone pair is wrong. That is a bug, not
a rule: T-275's rule is that walks go round water, and these walks cross none.

Not part of the bug: walks across the Huangpu are cut off (no foot bridge in the centre, ferries
are not modelled). That is the rule doing what it says.

## How far it reaches

`notes/t700_scope.py` over the 110 packs with routes (log:
`artifacts/trainworld/t700/walk_zone_scope.log`): share of people plus jobs in zones whose factor to
themselves is over 2. Median 0.3%; the worst are river and harbour centres: Rotterdam 8.2%, Antwerp
8.2%, Shanghai 4.7%, Hamburg 3.9%, Newcastle 3.8%, Springfield (spf, Massachusetts) 3.1%, Lagos 3.0%, Cologne
2.8%, Budapest 2.7%, London 1.6%, New York 0.9%. These are often the busiest destinations, and their
neighbours inherit the blend too (Shanghai: 1,577 of 42,369 window pairs over 5, 3,367 over 1.2).

## Fix: walk factors per bank (made 2026-10-19)

- `routes::walk_tables` (sim/src/demand/routes.rs; `pack_walk` runs it): each zone's cells split
  into at most `WALK_PARTS` = 4 parts. Part 0 is the cells the zone's representative reaches by a
  walk of at most 1.5 x the straight line + 500 m (searched 2.5 squares out); the next part starts
  from the representative of the cells left; cells left after 4 parts join the part whose
  representative walks to them shortest. Factors are then searched from each part's
  representative to each part of the window's zones, both directions averaged, as before.
  `--parts 1` gives exactly the old table.
- Pack: `walk_part` (u8 per cell) and `routes.walk_zone.parts = true`; `walk_zone` is laid out per
  zone, per part, then the window as before, then each window zone's parts. A pack without the
  flag reads exactly as before (one part a zone; `Routes::set_walk_zone`).
- Sim lookups by cell (`Routes::walk_factor_cells`, `Gravity::walk_factor_cells`): everyday
  `no_rail` and `trip_inputs`, kernel `set_walk_fine`, api `commute_walk_bands`, trip.rs's walk
  row. The rail pass's subzone pairs take each subzone's busiest part (`EverydayApi::sz_part`,
  people-weighted), not a blend. Zone-centre walks (`walk_cost_at` without cell samples) use part 0.
- Cells centred in the river (Shanghai: 510 cells, 0.53% of people and jobs; 16323 has none) can
  reach both banks and join the first part that reaches them. Left as is.
- Checks: `cargo test` `a_zone_astride_a_river_keeps_each_bank_apart` (a synthetic river through a
  zone column: one-bank pairs under x1.1, across over x5; with `pmax` 1, today's blend, the same
  pair is over x5); `checks/t700_walk_banks.py` (every pack: under 0.5% of people and jobs in a part
  walking to itself at over x5; Shanghai: Middle Henan Rd to the Bund under x1.2, the Bund to
  Lujiazui over x5). It FAILs on the backup in `data/work/pre-T700/` (3.5%, x48.18) and PASSes on
  the rebuilt Shanghai (0.00%, x1.00, x53.75).

## Last step: rebuild the other packs' walk factors (orchestrator, when T-613 hands back)

Back up the packs' .json and .bin to `trainworld/data/work/pre-T700/` first (Shanghai's is there
already), then from the maps root, with `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=3`:

    ..\venv\Scripts\python.exe trainworld/pipeline/routes.py --stage walk --unparted

Shanghai took 47 s in all (7 s of search), so 109 packs about an hour and a half at most, one core.

`--unparted` picks every pack with current routes whose walk factors have no parts (`--list` only
names them); it is safe to rerun and skips packs done. Then
`..\venv\Scripts\python.exe trainworld/checks/t700_walk_banks.py` should PASS every pack.
