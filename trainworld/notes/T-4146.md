## What was built

- **The worker's word** (workers/clockWordStations.ts `stationWord`). Edge ids change under these edits: a station on
  an edge splits it (the old id keeps the first half, a new id the rest, and lines over it take both), removing a
  station joins the two pieces at its node under one of the ids (T-1299), and removing track past a station keeps a
  stub under the old id. So the word speaks only in edges the page draws (`posted.net`):
  - removeEdge: the edge (and a crossover's other diagonal) hidden; each line whose path used it, its edges after the
    apply that are drawn and untouched (a rerouted line shows its new way, a broken one just loses the piece).
  - removeStation: the station hidden; each line stopping there, its edges after the apply, plus the other half of the
    joined pair beside the half the join kept.
  - Placing or changing a station needs no word: the page draws its box, the track under it is drawn as it was, and
    the base's word carries the charge.
- **Shown at the click** (map/stationAhead.ts for the guesses; game/pending.ts `ahead(show).send(() => edit(op))`):
  - Station tool, edge and node spots: the ghost's box as the station, kept through every spot tried (`tries`), the
    box dropped once a second spot is tried, the entry dropped when none is taken. Only when the ghost was not red or
    held (the old SVG marker's rule; that marker is gone). BRT: the box beside the road. Open ground: the box and its
    own piece of track (the Show's `track`).
  - Deletes (delete tool, panels' Remove, Shift skip or after the confirmation): a station or track, built or
    blueprint, hidden; a line the station ended loses that stretch at once (`edgesWithoutEnd`), other lines from the
    word. The selection clears at the click and comes back on a refusal.
  - Station panel: platform length (the figure, and the box redrawn at the new length along the drawn track), one or
    both tracks, a one-track station's way, upgrades (one station or a complex), through `shown` (`shownStation`);
    cash from the price shown for upgrades and both tracks, from the word's charge for platforms.
  - Passages (game/links.ts): added or removed in the panel's list at the click (a "links" record); cash from
    `passagePrice` when both stations are built.
- docs/ui-building.md: the station tool's click and removal at the click rewritten.
- Changelog editstations-1 (performance): "placing or deleting a station, deleting track and changing a station in its
  panel show up at the click".
