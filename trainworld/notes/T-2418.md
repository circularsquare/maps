Agent carwater, 2026-11-04. Investigation and proposal; nothing in app/, sim/, pipeline/ or data/ changed.

## The short version

- **Liberty and Governors Islands are on New York's main land part** (part 0), so no layer treats them as islands.
  Liberty Island (0.065 km2) is under the parts step's 0.1 km2 islet size and is closed as water; its three cells (126
  jobs, and the Statue of Liberty's 4.4M visits a year) sit on water just off it and take the nearest land, New Jersey.
  Governors Island (0.75 km2) is joined by the Brooklyn-Battery Tunnel: the parts step makes land of every pixel under
  a drivable way, and the tunnel runs under the island's east edge, so its strip touches the island.
- **Both fixes are pipeline only** (`pipeline/routes.py`, then a parts rerun). No kernel path lacks the rule: the trips
  to both islands are local (the everyday layer's attractions and the commutes), which T-2214 already handles once
  the cells are on their own part. Only a selection's far-end bubbles (T-2256) would still show car and walk.
- **Car times across water.** Local drives are routed over OSM roads per pair of 2 km zones (within 12 km) and per
  pair of 8 km supercells beyond, so they already go round lakes and bays. Trips between packs (regional commutes and
  outings across zone lines, and the market's longer trips) take the market's routed drive per pair of places, round
  the water on the 1/60 degree lattice. **Toronto - Buffalo is routed round Lake Ontario already**: 170 km of road
  for 101 km straight (1.68x; the QEW is about 160 km). The brief's "one number per pack" is the world's single
  scale (1.3 / 1.210) on top of routed factors; docs/demand-worldwide.md's "per-pack routed factor" reads wrongly.
- **Where a car still crosses big water cheaply**: (1) every zone pair of two places takes the place centres' ratio,
  so zones across the widest water get too short a road and zones at the water's end too long a one (Port Hope area
  to the Niagara/Orleans shore 102 km of road for about 300 by land; Hamilton to Buffalo 140 for about 105); (2) the market
  lattice has a car ferry on any water within 60 km of land, real or not (138 place pairs, 515,000 trips and commutes
  a day: Detroit - Windsor-Essex over Lake St Clair, the Gulf of Kutch, Lake Baikal, the Bicol bays); (3) Northern
  Ireland counts as Great Britain, so Belfast - Dublin pays the Channel and has no routed drive.
- **Proposal** (a demand rule change, for Anita before it is built): per market zone and partner place, the zone's
  own routed detour to the partner's centre over the centres', read from the searches market_drives.py already runs;
  and the lattice's ferry layer dropped, as T-2207 dropped car ferries locally. Section 3.

## 1. Liberty Island and Governors Island

**The pack.** `nyc` has 39 land parts. Every cell near both islands is part 0 (`islands.py`, `liberty9.py`):

| Place | res-9 cells | Jobs, people | Part | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liberty Island | 3 (centres on water 150-270 m off the island) | 126 jobs, 0 people | 0 | island is 26 px of 50 m (0.065 km2), under `MIN_LAND_PX` 40: closed as water, so each cell takes the nearest land, label 1 |
| Governors Island | 5 or so (centres on the island) | about 190 jobs, 3 people | 0 | island is its own 300 px label (0.75 km2) before roads; with the road pixels it is label 1 |
| Ellis Island | | | 0 | joined to Liberty State Park by a service bridge (`access=private`); correct under T-2213's rule |

**Liberty's attraction.** `attractions.json` has the Statue of Liberty National Monument at -74.0431, 40.6942 (in the
water between Liberty and Ellis); `everyday::attraction_sites` gives it the nearest cell, merged cell 18036, Liberty's
(515 m). Its 4.44M visits a year are about 12,000 trips a day to a cell on the mainland's part, so they drive. Governors
Island National Monument (625,653 a year, about 1,700 a day) goes to merged cell 17997 on Governors, part 0.

**Governors' join.** The road-opened pixels round the island (`labels.py`, from `data/work/parts/px/nyc`) lie along
the island's east shore at -74.0112 to -74.0153, 40.690 to 40.693. Overpass (`overpass_gov.py`): the Brooklyn-Battery
Tunnel (ways 5681925 and 413749473, `highway=motorway`, `tunnel=yes`, `layer=-2`) runs from Battery Park to Red Hook
through exactly those points, past the island's ventilation building. Its burnt strip is 8-connected to the island's
land, joining it to Manhattan and Brooklyn. The island's own roads are `highway=pedestrian` and private service
ways, none of which leaves it.

**Which layers.** Both islands' trips are local: the everyday layer's attraction purpose (`attraction_sites`, one
cell per attraction; attractions never cross a zone line, `cross.rs` `CROSS`) and the commutes to their jobs. T-2214
already gives those no car or walk between parts. So the cause is the part data, not a kernel path. After the data fix
a selection on either island would still show car and walk at its far ends (`flows`' far cells, T-2256).

### The fixes (not made: maps-5c gives the go)

**Fix 1, islets that are a cell's land** (Liberty). In `routes.land_labels` (parts only; the walk raster keeps its own
closing): an islet under 0.1 km2 stays land when it is some cell's land (the label under the cell's centre, or the
nearest land to a centre on water, taken before the closing) and it lies 150 m or more from other land. About 15
lines; `land_labels` needs the cells' positions and the origin.
- Measured on 15 packs (`islets.py`, `islets.log`): islets 150 m+ from other land that are a cell's land, per pack:
  New York 28 (80 people, 142 jobs; Liberty Island's 126), Stockholm 19 (88, 235: Fjaderholmarna), Venice 10 (9, 214:
  San Servolo, San Lazzaro, San Clemente), Helsinki 9 (20, 2), Amsterdam 6 (204, 41), Kolkata 5 (119, 24), Toronto 8
  (7, 4); Hong Kong, Athens, Copenhagen, Seattle, Tokyo, Istanbul, Vancouver 0-2 each with under 35 people. The
  150 m floor drops the mask's slivers beside a shore (Kolkata's Hooghly bank pixels with 1,695 jobs at 100 m,
  Amsterdam's dock pixels at 112 m). Without the floor New York would split 55 islets, many of them slivers.
- Run: back up the packs' `part` state to `data/work/pre-T2418/`, then `python pipeline/parts_run.py --all --redo
  --workers 4` (11 minutes in T-2213, no downloads: it reads the kept road pixels), which re-merges packs where a
  merged cell would span two parts. Checks: `checks/t2213_islands.py` with Liberty Island, Fjaderholmarna and San
  Servolo added as islands, `checks/t2213_packs_load.py`, smoke. No sim change.
- Risk: low. It only splits off small car-free land holding a cell; the worst case is a mask sliver 150 m from shore
  whose one cell turns grey or rail-only. Amsterdam's 6 (204 people) are worth a look on the map after the run.
- It writes `data/packs`, so it waits for the join now running.

**Fix 2, tunnels join land at their ends only** (Governors). A drivable way tagged `tunnel` (not "no") is not burnt
onto the water; instead the land nearest each end of its chain (tunnel ways sharing an end point are one chain), within
200 m, is joined (a union over labels before `land_parts`). Bridges stay burnt.
- Needs the tunnel tag, which the cached road pixels do not keep: a new `way_flags` bit (8), `WAYS_RULE` 2418, the
  tunnels' chain ends saved beside the pixels (`data/work/parts/px/<pack>/<extract>.tunnels.npy`) so `--redo` keeps
  working, and every extract read again: T-2213's world run took 1 h 42 min on 4 readers and 5 downloads (67 GB,
  each deleted once read), plus the 11-minute fold. New York alone (6 extracts) is about 20 minutes and could go
  first, the rest with the next world parts run.
- Risk: medium. Hong Kong Island, Haneda and the Aqua-Line's Umihotaru are joined only by tunnels; a tunnel mapped in
  pieces that do not share end points, or a chain end over 200 m out in the water, would split them. It can only
  split land, never join more, and `t2213_islands.py` already checks Hong Kong Island and Haneda; add the Aqua-Line,
  the Chesapeake Bay Bridge-Tunnel's islands, Hampton Roads, Peberholm (Oresund) and Busan - Geoje, and read
  `t2213_interior.py`'s list again.
- Cousin left out: a bridge passing high over an island with no ramp joins it the same way. None seen; a P3 if one is.

## 2. Car time across water, layer by layer

### Local trips (inside one pack): routed, round the water

`kernel.rs` `drive_factor` -> `routes.rs` `drive_factor`: the road is the straight line times a factor per pair of
2 km zones within 6 squares (`drive_near`) or per pair of supercells beyond (`drive_far`, 4 x 4 squares, 8 x 8 in
countryside packs), times the world's one scale 1.3 / 1.210. The factors are Dijkstra over the pack's OSM roads
(`routes.py` `drive_tables`), commuter-weighted, so a lake, bay or strait is crossed only where a bridge or tunnel
is. Measured (`local_far.py`): far factors across Puget Sound, the Bay, the Chesapeake, Tokyo Bay, the Bosphorus and
Hong Kong's harbour all follow the real crossings (Hong Kong's 1.47-1.83). The leaks are small:
- a supercell is routed from its heaviest zone, so a zone across water from it takes that zone's factor. People in
  zones 3 km+ of water from their supercell's routing zone (`super_split.py`, a coarse 1/60 degree test that also
  catches shorelines): Baltimore 1.1%, Toronto 0.9%, Seattle 0.4%, Istanbul 0.3%, New York 0.2%, Hong Kong, the Bay
  and Tokyo 0.1% or less.
- a near pair's road is at most 50 km (`NEAR_LIMIT_M`), for zones under 12 km apart: rare.
- an unreachable pair falls back to 1.3, but such pairs are on two land parts, where T-2214 has no car.

### Trips across zone lines and the market: routed per pair of places

Regional commutes between zones (`intercity.rs` 4007, 4027), everyday outings across zone lines (carried by the
regional layer; `cross.rs` only draws them and prices their walk at straight x 1.3) and the market's longer trips
(3984) all price the car as the market's rule (15 minutes plus 1.25 x the straight line at 85 km/h) at the zone
pair's own distance, times the place pair's two routed ratios from `market-drives.bin` (`rt` minutes, `rk` road
km). The ratios come from one least-cost path between the places' centres on the 1/60 degree lattice
(`market_drives.py`): GRIP4 road speeds, water impassable beyond 12 km from a shore and a 6 km/h swim nearer (for
bridges the mask misses), Wikidata fixed links as land, and a car ferry layer on water within 60 km of land.

**Toronto - Buffalo.** `tor` and `buf` are two places with an entry: `rt` 1.225, `rk` 1.346. Road 170 km for 101 km
between the centres (1.68x), about 2 h 08 of driving against the rule's 1 h 44. Round the lake, as the QEW goes.
The lake's other pairs (`region_pairs.py`): Toronto - St Catharines 1.74x, Toronto - Rochester 1.58x, Kingston -
Rochester 2.61x, Buffalo - Kingston 2.06x. Elsewhere: Istanbul - Bursa 1.66x, Baltimore - Delmarva (`sus`) 1.94x,
Seattle - Port Angeles 2.23x, Shenzhen - Zhongshan 1.35x. Tokyo Bay, Puget Sound's sides, the
Bay Area and Hong Kong's harbour are inside one pack each (the local tables above).

**Where the car still crosses big water cheaply.**

1. *One ratio for every zone pair of two places.* The centres' ratio is right for the centres and wrong in both
   directions elsewhere (`zone_smear.py`, against a land-only path on the lattice scaled to the routed road at the
   centres; zone pairs weighted by people x people):

   | Place pair | model road / land-aware road: 10%, median, 90% | worst short | worst long |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Toronto - Buffalo | 0.80, 1.01, 1.29 | Port Hope area - Niagara/Orleans shore 102 km for about 300 | Hamilton - Buffalo 140 for 104 |
   | Toronto - St Catharines | 0.83, 1.07, 1.39 | Scarborough - Niagara-on-the-Lake 89 for 147 | Grimsby edge 5 for 1-3 |
   | Baltimore - Delmarva | 0.76, 1.08, 1.29 | Kent Island area pairs (lattice gap, see below) | |
   | Bursa - Istanbul | 0.88, 1.01, 1.19 | Mudanya side - Istanbul west 115 for 226 | Bursa - Kocaeli side 81 for 54 |

   So a player's coastal line from Toronto to Buffalo competes with a fair car at the core; the car is too slow for
   the zones near the lake's west end (helping rail) and too quick for the few across its widest part.
2. *Phantom car ferries.* The lattice's ferry layer lets a car cross any water within 60 km of land for 30 minutes
   plus 35 km/h and a fare. 138 place pairs use it, 515,000 trips and commutes a day (`ferry_noent.py`). Some are
   real car ferries (Helsinki - Tallinn, Dalian - Yantai, Algeciras - Ceuta, Frederikshavn - Gothenburg,
   Ghogha - Hazira); many are not: Detroit - Windsor-Essex over Lake St Clair (47,600 a day, road 0.62 of the
   rule's), Bhuj - the Jamnagar side over the Gulf of Kutch (38,800), Irkutsk - Ulan-Ude over Lake Baikal, five
   Bicol pairs over Lagonoy Gulf and San Miguel Bay (`iri`, `lay`, `lgp`, `nay`, `ctt`), the Tomini Gulf in Sulawesi. Locally T-2207 ignores car ferries
   (Anita, 2026-11-03); the market still has them.
3. *Pairs with no routed entry* on one landmass with 10 km+ of water between: 107 pairs, 82,000 a day. Nearly all
   are the Channel and the Tsugaru Strait by design (the rule plus the crossing's fare and minutes). The exception is
   a bug: `crossing_cost` (intercity.rs 1446) and `market_drives.py` 401 take country "GB" as Great Britain, so
   Northern Ireland's places pay the Channel ($150 and 60 minutes) to the Republic's and have no routed drive:
   Belfast - Dublin 8,000 trips and commutes a day, and `bel`-`bod` 6,100, `bel`-`mis` 5,600, `dub`-`ner` 4,200,
   `mis`-`ner` 3,200.
4. *How many trips there are* (every layer's gravity) is by straight distance, so water never thins the trips
   across it (T-2207's Vashon, 3 times the real ferry's riders). A separate rule, a new factor; not proposed here.

A lattice gap: Baltimore - Delmarva's worst pairs show the land-only path missing a short crossing (Kent Narrows,
under the lattice's 1.85 km cell). The market's own path has the 6 km/h swim for that; the proposal below reads the
market's own searches, so it keeps the swim.

## 3. Proposal: the car's road round water, zone by zone

For Anita before it is built (a demand rule change, docs/demand-worldwide.md "Routed drives" changes).

**A. Each zone's own detour to the other place.** `market_drives.py` already runs one search per place over its
window and reads the cost at the other places' centres. Read it also at each zone of every partner place: zone i's
minutes and road km to place B's centre over the rule's at that distance, divided by the same ratio for A's centre.
Store per (zone, partner place) two bytes (`round(64 ln)`, minutes and km) only where either is 10% or more off 1.
The zone pair's car is then the rule at its distance x the place pair's `rt`, `rk` x zone i's factor towards B x
zone j's towards A. The place pair's corridor split stays at the centres.
- Accuracy (the same `zone_smear.py`, the separable product against the land-aware path): Toronto - Buffalo 0.97,
  1.00, 1.03 (10%, median, 90%); Toronto - St Catharines 0.95, 1.00, 1.04; Baltimore - Delmarva 0.95, 1.00, 1.05;
  Bursa - Istanbul 0.99, 1.00, 1.01.
- Cost: no new search, a lookup per zone of each partner. A recipe change re-routes every place: T-2111 routed 869
  places in 28 minutes on 3 cores, so the world's 3,390 in about 2 hours on 3 (40 minutes on 8). The zone files'
  positions are read alongside market-rows.json.
- Size: 872,701 market zones; the 2,579 routed place pairs under 400 km with 10 km+ of water between hold 826,000
  zone ends, 3.3 MB if every one were stored; kept only where 10% off, about 1 MB, plus far pairs: a new
  `market-drive-zones.bin` of 1-5 MB beside `market-drives.bin` (2.7 MB), loaded with it.
- Layers: regional commutes and outings across zone lines and the market's longer trips (intercity.rs's zone pair
  car at 3984, 4007, 4027, and the trip explorer's). Not local trips, which are routed per zone pair already.
- Worldwide: one rule from the same lattice everywhere; no new source.

**B. No phantom car ferries.** Drop the lattice's ferry layer (`FERRY_REACH_KM` to 0) in the same rebuild, so a car
goes round or swims a gap under 12 km, as T-2207's local rule ignores car ferries. Helsinki - Tallinn, Dalian -
Yantai and the other real ferry pairs then drive the long way round (or have no car where none goes, T-2216's
rail-only rule), and the player's ferry is their only short way. The other choice keeps ferries only where OSM maps a
car ferry (`route=ferry`, `motor_vehicle=yes`): truer, but a new data step and the opposite of the local rule.
Anita picks.

**C. Northern Ireland is not Great Britain** (a bug, no design): Great Britain is "GB" off Ireland's landmass, in
`crossing_cost` and `market_drives.py`'s matching skip; reroute those places' pairs. Small; its own task.

**Not proposed**: per-zone far factors in the local tables (the supercell leak is about 1% of people at most); water
in the gravity (a new factor, Anita's call).
