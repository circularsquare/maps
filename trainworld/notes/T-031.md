# T-031 capacity display: junction inspector and the round trip split

Markers at 75% and up came with T-025. Finished 2026-10-09 by the gameplay agent (batch 4):
`T-batch4-junction.png`, `T-batch4-roundtrip.png`.

## Queries (`sim/src/track/wasm.rs`)

The inspectors ask the clock worker when they open and after every edit (`query` / `answer`
messages, `queryClock` in `workers/clockClient.ts`), rather than every state carrying them.

- `TrackApi.junction_info(node)`: the node's edge ends (each with the direction to the track 1 km
  out, for the diagram) and the moves running lines make through it: (in end, out end), the line
  directions on it, trains an hour per level, and from the capacity pass's junction resource the
  move owns, the delay per train, the utilisation and the moves that load it (the ones it
  crosses). A move that crosses nothing, or any move at a flying junction, has no resource and
  shows no wait. Reads `Capacity` through its public fields only (`res`, `users_of`, `rho`,
  `delay`), so T-050's incremental pass is untouched.
- `TrackApi.line_delays(line, level)`: each resource the line waits at with the delay per round
  trip (both directions counted), largest first.
- `TrackApi.line_dwell_split(line)`: dwell over a round trip and the two turnarounds (the UI
  computes the same from `ClockLine.dwell` / `turnaround`; kept for tests).

## The junction inspector

Flat or flying with the toggle as before, then a 112 px diagram (each track end as a grey stub,
spread to at least 30 degrees apart because a branch leaves almost along the main line; each move a
curve through the middle in its first line's colour, thicker if it crosses another), then the
moves: line chips, "W 18th St to Madison Av" (each end named after the first station along it,
else a compass direction), trains an hour now, the wait per train, and "Crosses ..." under it;
and the busiest crossing's utilisation.

## The round trip

Round trip at the level in force, then running (the rest), dwell at stops (2 x intermediate
stops x dwell), turning at the ends (2 x turnaround), and waiting at busy track (the capacity
holds, SPEC 6.2) with up to three places named: "At <station>" for platforms and termini, "At the
junction near <station>", "At the crossing near <station>", "On the track near <station>",
merging both directions.

## Checks

`junction_inspector_and_delay_split` (cargo test): a Y with two lines at 15 and 18 trains an hour
gives three ends, four moves, one crossing pair seen from both sides, a wait on the crossing
move, and a round trip whose parts add up. In the app: the three-line test network (two lines on
a trunk, one branching off through a flat junction) shows 24 and 12 trains an hour on the moves,
+1m48s on the two that cross, 90% at the busiest crossing.
