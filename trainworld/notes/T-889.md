# T-889 Loading a big game faster

Brief: Japan's test save (t737-japan.save) loaded in 54 s after T-884, Anita's world save
(world-map-day73) in 76-81 s; about 40 market packs opened one after another at about 1 s each,
downloads took about 20 s, and a 6 s main-thread block remained. Cut both without changing a figure.

Method: headless Chrome, a temporary profile per run, the save imported into a slot and picked from the
start menu (as `checks/t884-loadbar.mjs` does), `?debug=1` for `twDemand.start.loads` (each pack's
request, download and opening), long tasks, and temporary `console.info` timings in the clock and demand
workers (removed). Dev server on 6130; the machine shared with other agents; the packs were rewritten by
T-850b's cell merge at 00:29-00:32, before every run here, so all before and after runs are on the same
data. Times are from the pick.

## What the time went on

Japan, before (two runs alike):

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Clock worker: decode, water masks, track derive (4.8 s), state, snapshot | 0.2-7.9 s |
| Main thread takes the state (1.7 s block); demand sees the network | 8.2-10.6 s |
| Downloads (46 city packs, 45 market packs) | 10.6-16.5 s, stretched by the block below |
| Main thread placing stations and track (`placeAt`, 5.1-5.4 s block) | 11.1-16.5 s |
| Market packs open, one at a time in each solving worker, 0.7-0.9 s each | 16.5-52.2 s |
| City packs open, queued behind them | 52.2-58.9 s |
| Solve sent | 63.3 s |

The solve then took about 750 s: the card waits for the zone in view's final (`holdDone`), and Ishikawa is
in the 44-zone cluster that is all of Japan, solved ahead at about 180 s a round (T-780, T-782). The card
went at 817 s. The brief's 54 s was a card that went at the first estimate; it does not reproduce today.

World save, before: downloads 3.4-4.9 s, markets open 4.6-35.5 s (39 of them, one by one), cities to
40.9 s, solve sent 41 s, card 69.4 s, final 82.1 s.

Why each market pack took so long: every `open` composed the market again from every pack held
(`MarketPacks.market`, `recompose`), 0.7 s for one of Japan's packs and about the same for 45; adding a
pack (parsing its JSON) is 10-25 ms. And a worker took `open`s one by one.

The 5 s block: `placeAt` for every station and track edge (game/regionMoney.ts, game/listScope.ts) walked
whole prefecture coastlines in `distanceTo` (4.2 s) and scanned every zone's box in `zonesTouching`
(1.4 s).

## What changed

1. **A worker opens the `open`s waiting for it together** (`openQueued` in demand.worker.ts): one
   composition for them all. Opens are batched only up to the next `close` (a count of closes orders
   them), and bare opens apart from the rest.
2. **The page holds market packs until none is still downloading** (`postMarkets`): on a slow network
   they would otherwise land a few at a time and each batch compose again (the world save composed twice,
   1.4 s and 3.6 s, before this).
3. **A save's packs open while its network is worked out** (`TrackApi.save_stations`, the clock
   worker's `stationsAhead`, `DemandClient.stationsAhead`): the save's built stations (as `nodes_info`
   lists them) are read before the 5-8 s of track derive, and the zones they reach and those zones'
   markets download and open as prefetched zones. The real network then finds them open. A zone opened
   ahead that the network does not reach is never solved (prefetched); a market it does not want is closed
   with a console warning (none seen).
4. **`distanceTo` through a grid of each outline's edges** (cityReach.ts): the inside test reads only
   the edges in the place's row of cells, the nearest edge searches rings of cells outwards; ties go to
   the first edge in outline order, as the full walk takes them. Checked to the bit against the walk on
   459,499 points over all 757 zones (random, on and beside vertices, far outside): 0 differences, 4.6x
   faster on that mix. `around` in zones.ts reads boxes widened once.
5. **The bar**: a market pack's expected opening is a share of one composition (1.5 s for the load's
   markets) plus 20 ms per MB, not 450 ms each. A game load's bar job is kept while its network is on its
   way (it was dropped when the camera's move to the save's view passed over dead space, and the new job
   started from 0: the world save's bar fell 9 points once markets opened ahead), and demand does not
   count as idle then (the card disappeared for 0.4 s in one run).
6. `?perfOff=openAhead` and `?perfOff=batchOpen` turn 3 and 1-2 off (perfFlags.ts).

## After

Japan (pick to solve sent, 3 runs): 12.2, 12.5, 13.2 s. Downloads 0.6-1.4 s; every pack open in each
solving worker by 12-13 s (cities about 7 s of it, the market's one composition 1.5 s). No main-thread
block over 2 s (the clock state's arrival, 1.5-2.0 s, T-759). Card 750 s against 817 s, all of it
the solve now.

World save (pick to card, final): before 69.4 s, 82.1 s (and 66.4 s, 78.6 s with both perfOff flags);
after 38.6, 42.6, 42.9 s card and 50.4-55.2 s final (solve sent at 8.9-11.1 s, from 41 s). The rest is
the solve (about 30 s to the card), which these changes do not touch.

Real New York save (t884 check): 27.4-27.8 s card (T-884's 29-31 s).
