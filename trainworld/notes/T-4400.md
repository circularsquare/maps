Agent showmodes, 2026-11-27, tree `.tw-trees/showmodes` from master 916e0fbff.

kept), five icon buttons in the Lines tab's Type order and icons: Train, High speed, Tram, Bus, Ferry (hovers reuse

## Reading the code first

The map hides things in four independent places, and each needed the new kinds:

1. **Lines and their vehicles**: the hidden line set (`hiddenNums` in main.ts -> `hidden.lines`) already removed a
   line's strokes (alpha 0 in the line colour texture), its trains (train shader), its bus or ferry route and stops
   (bus layer), its picks, and its place in the schematic (`offOf`). Hidden kinds just add lines there, by the line's
   kind (`modeOf` in game/vehicle.ts: high speed is the trains', as the Lines tab and transit split already count it).
2. **Track**: track strokes carry their kind in flags (F_HSR, F_TRAM, F_BUSWAY). A `u_mode_hide` bit mask in the
   stroke vertex shader drops them (not what is being placed); `pickSkip` on the track set, the track tool's snaps
   (`edgeOff`/`nodeOff` in tools.ts), busway snapping, junction picks and loose-end marks skip them too.
   Line strokes cannot be hidden by track kind: their F_HSR/F_TRAM flags are look only (set only for a fast line on HSR
   track), so lines go by their own kind. Lie: a shown line can run over hidden track (DECISIONS).
3. **Stations**: a station's kinds are its own (port ferry, BRT bus, tram stop tram, else its track's train/HSR) plus
   every line stopping there; hidden only when all are hidden (`stationModes` in lineVisibility.ts). In the run, with
   Train hidden the three subway stations the bus stops at stayed and the two train-only ones went.
4. **Things placed by position, not by line or station**: grade marks (junction, crossing, ramp: no id in their data,
   so the renderer finds the edges passing within 1.5 local units of each and drops a mark whose every edge is of a
   hidden kind), capacity gauges (their nearest track node), reversal signs (all their lines hidden, or their
   station), station names, ridership circles, station faces and transfer bubbles. Several of these also stayed for
   hidden levels and hidden lines before; all now go through the same `stationOff`/`nodeOff` test, so levels and the
   Lines tab's hiding are covered too.

The DOM placers (names, gauges, signs) run on MapLibre frames, which `overlay.invalidate()` does not cause; the hidden
effect in main.ts now also calls `map.triggerRepaint()`. Without it the gauges stayed until the map moved (seen in the
run, fixed, confirmed).

## Levels: the even fade

Ruled out from the code, not tried in the run. All track is drawn, then all lines, in buffer order, never by level.
An opaque colour mixed toward the land would paint over any shown track or line a hidden one crosses (a hidden tunnel
crossing a shown street-level line would cut it), and over water it is a land-coloured band that reads as a causeway.
Making it work needs a draw pass of its own (hidden first, or an offscreen layer composited at low alpha), not a
small change. So hidden levels are removed: `levelShown` is 0 on a hidden level (LEVEL_FADE gone); strokes, station
boxes, cars and marks wholly on one are not drawn; a ramp still fades out toward it. Screenshot: subway at -1 with
bends, hidden, nothing left (artifacts/trainworld/review/showmodes.png, right half).
