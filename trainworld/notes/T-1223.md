## What is in

- `workers/demandLod.ts`: the rules (`planFine`, `firstFine`, the budget, `?lod=`).
- `demandClient.ts`: the fine set (priorities from the view, the selection and the edits a new
  network makes), its checks, a level change as a reopen through `fetchCity` with the pack's
  `level`, `Solve.coarse`, `flush` sending a solve when a covered zone's level changed, the cache key
  (":coarse" on a coarse zone's pack hash), `roughZones` and `refiningZone`.
- `demand.worker.ts`: opens a pack with `DemandApi.at_level`; a zone open at another level is
  dropped and opened again, after the solve in hand if it covers it (`reopenLater`); the aux worker
  opens every zone fine.
- The save's `demandFine` (clock worker header, sent with `stationsAhead`, kept current by
  `reportDemandFine`).
- `memoryCheck.ts`: judged at the least a game can take (its biggest zone fine, the rest coarse), and
  the 4 GB test on the WASM part alone (the 0.7 GB JS heap of a worker no longer counts against it).
- Stars and goals never from a rough zone's figures (clockClient).
- Stage 4: the status tag "Working out Guangzhou in detail" (hover "These figures are rough until
  Guangzhou is worked out in detail."), the split's hover "(rough while Guangzhou is worked out in
  detail)", the rough line in a rough zone's city view opened from elsewhere.
