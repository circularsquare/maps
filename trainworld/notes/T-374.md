# T-374: riders arriving by ride in the station inspector and the trip explorer (2026-10-15)

Interface: docs/ui-inspectors.md (Commuters, the trip explorer), SPEC 5.3.

## What is counted

`DemandApi::home_access_modes` (T-371) counts the local commute's home end only: `st.sub.acc_home`
by whether the entry is a walk (`Subzones::walk`) or a ride (`Subzones::ride`). It does not include
the regional layer's rides (T-373, `intercity.rs`) or everyday trips' (T-389, `everyday.rs`
`pass`), which keep no per-station tally by access mode. The inspector's split therefore covers
commutes, the same people as its "Live nearby" row.

## Choices

- **Where it shows**: under the Commuters rows (`AccessSplit`, `StationRiders.tsx`), not under
  Today's boardings. Today's purpose split is shares of live counts since midnight; the walk and
  ride figures are the model's commutes a day, so they sit with the other model figures there.
  `DemandApi::station_riders_day` now returns `walked` and `rode` after `n_home`, `n_work`
  (same unit and day weighting as the cell riders; `views_add_up` checks they sum to the home
  riders).
- **Style**: the purpose split's bar and key; walk colour and drive colour from `MODE_COLOURS`
  (the mode bar's); a walk and a car icon (`Icon.walk`, `Icon.ride`) with hovers instead of words.
- **Station hover left alone**: it shows the circle's riders (boardings plus alightings from
  `DemandView`), a synchronous read with no per-station mode split, and a second line would
  crowd it. T-390 holds the follow-up.
- **Trip explorer**: `trip` packs, per station pair, whether the access entry is a ride and the
  perceived minutes beyond the drive's (`PAIR_LEN` 14). The access row reads "Drive to" with the
  drive minutes; its hover adds the ride's fixed cost and the station's parking or drop-off. The
  door to door line gains a drive part, and the map's access leg hover says Drive. The walk hovers
  now say 6 km/h, not "the fast walk".

## Real save (Hicksville complex, weekday)

Live nearby 9k commuters a day: 8% walked (763), 92% rode. Matches T-371's 92% at Hicksville.

The trip explorer's "Counts as" hover said walks count double; the model's `w_walk` has been 1.5
since T-371, so it now says 1.5 times.
