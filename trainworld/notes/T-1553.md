# T-1553: crowding dots on long lines

Anita: dots on long inter-city lines read all red or all green. The dot is the average wait for a
train with room (min) at the platforms where trains come full (`CrowdDots`, `crowdColour`), per
demand level, from the boarding account. Measured 2026-10-27 with `artifacts/trainworld/t1553/measure.mjs`
(the figure per line and level exactly as `CrowdDots` computes it; weekday; raw rows in
`artifacts/trainworld/t1553/measure-*.json`, `summ.mjs` tabulates).

## Reading

- Nothing is near the old scale's top (25+): the real save's highest figure is 23, and a sparse line
  tops out at 15-20. The data sits in 9-20, where the old scale had orange at 9 and red at 15, so a
  line that fills read orange-red whatever its degree, and the rest read green.
- So the range worth steps is 9 to about 30 min (30 is a rider turned away). New stops: orange 9,
  red-orange 12, red 15, crimson 20, plum 25, purple 35, indigo 50 (`CROWD_STOPS`). The ramp below 9 is
  unchanged: figures there are rare on long lines and Anita's own steps (T-692) stay.
- Not done: the cliff between "no train leaves full" (0) and one that does (several minutes) is the
  account's, not the scale's.
