# T-2214: islands I2, local demand between land parts

Design: notes/T-2207.md 3, 6. Agent t2214, 2026-11-03. The rule is in docs/demand-mode.md 4.3 "Between two
land parts".

## What it is

- `pack`: `DemandApi::new` reads the pack's `part` (T-2213); `kernel::Parts::of` keeps it only where the cells are on
  two parts or more (`Params::parts`), with each zone's cells by part as slots. `--set parts=0` drops it (checks).
- Access: a station's part is its nearest cell's (`Access::st_part`); walks, bus corridors and rides reach only
  stations on the cell's part; ride-only groups split by part; `Subzones::part` (every subzone is on one part),
  `Subzones::apart`.
- No rail: `Gravity::no_rail_by_slot` gives walking with no rail per slot (between slots of one part only, the
  fine walk's cell pairs on one part) and the commutes between parts (`unr_home`, `unr_work`), summing only pairs
  with a zone off the main part (`Parts::mixed`).
- `mode_choice`: a lane whose subzones are apart prices the car on a missing bridge (straight x `road_detour`), takes
  `Params::bound_share` (the ratio curve's 3-4x fade alone), skips the walking rule and T-1782's fade, takes no
  walkers, and counts its rail in `ModeOut::unr_lost_by_sub`/`_to_sub` (`rail_unr`). The everyday pass the same per
  pair (`EverydayApi::apart`, `unrs`, `unr_lost`); `no_rail` is 0 between parts. The trip explorer (`pair_choice`,
  `everyday_choice`, cell walks on the cell's part).
- Totals: no-way trips = those between parts less the rail among them, in every total, in no mode; the cap's turned
  away riders between parts stay home (commutes: `SubOut::unr_lost_*` scaled as the walkers; everyday: per purpose).

## WASM outputs (for T-2215)

- `DemandApi.summary(q)` and `EverydayApi.summary(q)`: a 14th number, index 13, the period's trips no way serves
  (driving is now trips - rail - walk - this). The worker's sums over parts (`demand.worker.ts` 1362: `i >= 10`)
  already add it; the city summary's everyday merge (line 1969, `[1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 11, 12]`) needs 13 added.
- `DemandApi.sub_sums(_day)`: six blocks of `n_subzones` (was four): rail home/work, walkers taken home/work, rail
  between parts home/work. `cell_modes(_day)` takes the six (its drive leaves no-way trips out).
- `DemandApi.cell_unreach_day(sums, day)`: two cell blocks, no-way commuters at the home end and the work end.
- `EverydayApi.cell_unreach_day(kind, day)`: two cell blocks (home end, activity end); `kind` -1 every purpose.
  `cell_modes*` keep six blocks, drive leaving no-way trips out.
- `DemandApi.flow_rail(_day)`: four blocks (was three; the fourth the selection's rail between parts at its own end);
  `flows(_day)` takes them and appends one number after its far cells: the selection's no-way trips (near end only;
  the far cells still split them as walk and drive: T-2256).
- `DemandApi.info()`: a 6th number, the day's commutes between parts.
- Trip explorer head: `HEAD` 48 (was 47, `demandProtocol.ts` updated); slot 47 is 1 when the two cells are on two
  land parts; then slots 17/37 (walk) and 19/39 (drive) are 0 and the rest beside rail (18/38) is no way.
