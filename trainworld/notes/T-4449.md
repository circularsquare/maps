# T-4449 Retime costs less for each line it redoes (agent retime, 2026-11-27)

Stage C5 of T-4267. Tree retime. Measured with T-4441's Node driver on the Asia save (edge 1482, 4 clicks), a busy machine, so
absolute ms move by a third between runs; the shares hold.

## Where retime's time went (T-4441: 48 ms, passing_waits 35, lead_in 10)

Finer timers (a throwaway build, never landed) showed the cost was in building profiles, not the waits:
182 profiles built per redone cluster, 28 of the ~37 ms. Of each build, the part that only reads the run's holds (spread holds'
crawl speed, stands inside hops) took 30 ms of it; the standing times and the hop copying took about 10. That holds work is the
same for every timetable of a run (base, every variant), yet was redone for each.

## What changed (sim/src/track: profile.rs, service.rs, timing.rs)

- profile.rs: `run_with` split into `plan_holds` (what the holds do: standing added per stop, zones and stands per hop, and each
  held hop laid from its start) and `run_planned` (the timetable's standing times and the phases). `PlanMemo` keeps a spread hold's
  result and a hop's result by their inputs, so a lead-in variant whose holds differ in one hop plans only that hop.
- service.rs: `hold_plan`, `profile_with_plan`; `run_profile` and `profile_with` keep their signatures.
- timing.rs: `Built` keeps plans and memos per run for one retime, and each run's departures (`around`, `day_deps` per slot at once,
  a HashSet for the place dedup instead of a list search), shared by the three passes; `cluster`'s `ok` takes a list of departures
  so a merge's wait list is built once; the `which` fill is linear; the headway cap is read once per wait point.
