  (no account; `query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&outSR=4326&f=geojson`), features edited **2026-09-29**, saved as

## Design (decided; to build)
1. **Occupied land is nobody's**: a `world_plan` key, say `LEFT_OUT = {"UKR": (RAW / "isw_ukr_control_2026-09-29.geojson",
   0.5)}`: in `gb_units` and `state_units`, a unit of that country with over half its area inside the file's polygons is
   dropped (as `de_facto` returning None); a unit with less stays whole, so no ISW edge is drawn. Crimea's units are
   taken out first (point 2).
2. **Crimea is Russia's**: units of UKR whose ADM1 is Crimea or Sevastopol become units of RUS (iso RUS, their UKR
   shapeIDs kept). Russia is planned on `plan_units` (state.pkl), so either the state gains them (a `DE_FACTO_TAKE =
   {"RUS": {"UKR": ["Autonomous Republic of Crimea", "Sevastopol"]}}` read wherever a country's units are read), or the
   UKR zones step zones them as their own group and writes those rows with `countries ["RUS"]`, `iso2 "RU"`. The
   first is the general rule (a unit is the country holding it); try it, the second if the state cannot be rebuilt
   safely. Expected 2-3 zones (floor 0.5M, cap 1.5M; Sevastopol, Simferopol, the north and east), tz Europe/Simferopol
   (UTC+3, Moscow time), Russia's ru-west realm line. Extract: the index picks one (Geofabrik's Crimean extract or
   `europe/ukraine`).
3. **don** stays held for good (reason: inside the line of control, T-2344); `setbuilt.py kyi,kha,ode --unhold`.
4. **Rule `UKR`**: floor 0.5M, cap 1.5M, `group_level` 1 (councils by old raion, as Korea and Malaysia), `contiguous`;
   density floor decided on the dry run (start at the default 20; Polissia and the south may want 10, as Turkey);
   `fua_whole` 0.5M if the dry run cuts a city of 0.5-1M (the only metro rows are kyi kha don ode, so Dnipro, Lviv,
   Zaporizhzhia and Kryvyi Rih are zones). Zaporizhzhia city is 29 km from the line and stays.
5. Names: zones after their lead city in Latin (zone-names.json); realm `ukraine` ("Ukraine") in realms.json.
