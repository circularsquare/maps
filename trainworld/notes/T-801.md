# T-801: grey city bubbles undone

Anita, 2026-10-21: "lets actually undo the gray bubble thing". T-729's design is notes/T-728.md
"Cities not yet opened".

## What changed
- Every zone loads for the view on pan and draws its ordinary bubbles (zoomed out, its one city
  bubble in mode colours). `map/greyCities.ts`, the "grey" disc layer, `greyCity` in the shade table,
  the click-to-open path and `?opened=all` are gone.
- The save keeps `opened` (start zone, then zones reached), read by nothing; old saves load as before.
- A station reaching a zone loads it for the solves as before T-729 (the reach rule never depended on
  the list).
- A zone's market pack opens only when the zone is reached, a station is within 30 km of its box, or
  it is selected (a zone selection, or a bubble selection in it; `marketZones` in
  `workers/demandClient.ts`); never for viewing or being beside the view.
- A start on the world map turns Homes on, and the demand buttons work zoomed out (8.5 and below)
  even over dead space (`zoomedOut` in `map/intercityView.ts`). With no market result yet the
  zoomed-out bubbles are local trips alone, and a zone selected then keeps the overview drawn.
- `checks/t729-grey.mjs` replaced by `checks/t801-no-grey.mjs`; `t728-newgame.mjs` expects the start
  zone first in `opened` (T-778's stale expectation).

## Pan measure (2026-10-21)

`checks/t301-pan.mjs` on a `?new=1` New York game (three solving workers and the aux worker, Homes
on, zoom 10.5, Washington to Toronto and back, 5 s a stop, REST=15000), port 6141. Outputs
`artifacts/trainworld/t801/pan-{before,all,after}.json`.

| | T-729 (New York only) | every zone, markets as before | T-801 (every zone, no viewing markets) |
|---|---|---|---|
| pack and market loads | 4 (2 market) | 56 (25 market, 31 bare) | 31 (0 market, 31 bare) |
| reopenings | 2 | 28 | 17 |
| most packs open at once | 1 | 5 (13 markets) | 5 (0 markets) |
| start WASM memory, solving / aux | 125 / 125 / 109 / 37 MB | the same | 8 / 1 / 1 / 37 MB |
| end WASM memory, solving / aux | 125 / 125 / 109 / 37 MB | 311 / 311 / 298 / 56 MB | 8 / 1 / 1 / 56 MB |

The 125 MB was New York's market pack, opened for the view. The aux worker's growth (37 to 56 MB) is
the bare zones, as before T-729.

## What a viewed zone no station reaches no longer shows
- Zoomed out, its bubble has no inter-region trips (the hover says "0 inter-region trips"), and the
  other zones' bubbles count none of its regional trips: only zones whose market is open contribute.
- Selecting it (or a bubble in it) opens its market and re-solves (about 9 s for New York with no
  station); the inspector then has its figures, and the map's partner bubbles grow from zero over a
  game day (the existing catch-up, T-156), since the solve before had no market.
- On a start on the world map nothing is solved (no zone in view), so a zone selected there shows
  "Calculating demand." in its inspector and no partner bubbles until the player goes to a zone.
