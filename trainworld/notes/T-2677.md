# T-2677: parking by income at the 0.5 power (2026-11-06, agent parking, tree `parking`)

Anita (2026-11-06): "yeah can do parking same way, be consistent". T-2278 set paid parking's income factor to
(GDP per head / $35,000)^0.3 below $35,000, worked out as Colliers' price slope (1.0) minus the fares' power of
income (0.7). T-2670 made that power 0.5, so the same rule gives 0.5.

Anita, on these numbers (2026-11-06): "yes can land 0.5".

## The change

- `sim/src/demand/kernel.rs`: `PARK_INCOME_E = PARK_PRICE_E - MONEY_E` (1.0 - 0.5), with `MONEY_E` the fares'
  power. The sim has no copy of the fares' rule (the app sends minutes a dollar), so `MONEY_E` is a constant
  that a test, `kernel::tests::money_power_is_the_fares`, reads against `export const FARE_E` in
  `app/src/game/income.ts`: changing one without the other fails `cargo test --features demand --lib`.
- Docs: demand-mode.md (Parking), demand-worldwide.md (Parking, and the fares entry's "car's money" line),
  demand-plain.md (parking bullets).
- The regional market is untouched: its car reads each zone's `site_park`, baked into the market packs by
  `pipeline/intercity.py park_by_cell` with no income factor (T-2278 left it so: "the market's longer trips keep
  their own parking"). So no market corridor moves.
- New York (and every country from $35,000 a head: the US, Japan, Korea, western Europe) has factor 1 before
  and after; the real save is unchanged by construction. Not run.

Factors, old -> new: India 0.46 -> 0.28, Vietnam 0.56 -> 0.38, Mexico 0.76 -> 0.63, China 0.76 -> 0.63,
Turkey 0.83 -> 0.73, Poland 0.94 -> 0.90.

## Is 0.5 right? Parking in dollars against Colliers

The densest centre's paid space (the 50-minute cap) in dollars, at each country's minutes a dollar now, against
Colliers 2009 monthly CBD rates over 42 one-way trips (notes/T-2278.md):

| City | Colliers, a one-way trip | Game at 0.3 (today on master) | Game at 0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mumbai | $0.60 | $0.91 (50 x 0.46 / 25.4) | $0.55 |
| Mexico City | $2.79 | $3.38 (50 x 0.76 / 11.2) | $2.81 |
| New York Midtown | $13.00 | $11.36 | $11.36 |

Parking is counted in minutes, so T-2670 did not move it (riders saw the same 23 minutes in Mumbai's centre
before and after); it changed what those minutes are worth in dollars. Before T-2670 (51 minutes a dollar)
they were $0.45, a little under Colliers' $0.60; at 25 minutes a dollar they are $0.91, 1.5 times it. The
0.5 power brings the dollars back to Colliers ($0.55), and that is a real cut in minutes (23 -> 14), which is
the rail drop above.
Against fares at 0.5: Mumbai's densest parking is $0.55 a trip against a $0.45 base fare (1.2x); New York's
$11.36 against $2.50 (4.5x). Real: Mumbai $0.60 against a metro fare of about $0.23, New York $13 against $2.90.
