# T-4545: move one train with its range and reasons (stage 3 of 18, T-4511)

Agent shift3, 2026-11-28, tree shift3. Sim only; nothing a player sees, no save change, no strings, no changelog item, no WASM
function (stage 13 adds the readout, stage 14 the arrows). Nothing in the game calls the new edit, so a game with no moves runs
as before.

## What is built
- **`Op::MoveTrain { line, moves }`** (world.rs): sets a line's whole list of moves (`Service::set_moves`); its inverse is the
  same op with the list it found, so undo and redo are exact. A service edit like `Op::Nudge`: nothing to settle or place; the
  line is noted changed (`nudge::lines_of`, `op_lines`, `step_is_service`).
- **Edit lead / era** (`Service::note_changes`): a line whose moves changed since `before_edit` is treated as a changed
  pattern: trains running carry on, an era at the edit keeps the moves from before (T-4544 made eras hold them), a departure
  before the edit runs as it did. So the wasm `run` path (edit lead, `before_edit`, `note_changes`) needs no new code.
- **`Trip` carries its move** (`moved: f32`) and `ready` (when its train is ready at the far end with the move undone). A
  moved trip's `end` is `max(ready, draw + journey + min(close_stand, 60 s))`: moved later it stands less at the far end,
  down to the 60 s; moved earlier it stands longer and is ready when it was. With no move `ready == end`, same arithmetic as before.
- **`hand_over` from unmoved times**: trains are paired by the departures' unmoved times and `ready`; only the result (`end`,
  `next`, both read from the real draws) shows the move. So a move never brings a train into service or sends one away,
  and nothing changes for a trip with no move (`at()` is `draw`, `f.max(end)` is `f`).
- **`held(l, s)`**: `Service::move_holds` = the weekday slots with an own or with-partner move that matches a departure;
  `spacing::place` and `seed_split` skip those rows as they skip an offset's (the others are placed around them). The offset
  itself stays in `Network`; a move holds without an offset ("derived, nothing new stored"). Weekend rows: stage 7.
- **`track/move_train.rs`**: `move_range(line, run, dep)` and `move_op(line, run, dep, by)`, for the train identified as the app
  will (run and the pattern's departure, `Trip::dep`).
  - Rule 1: later, the train must reach the far end `TURN_MIN_S.min(close_stand)` before its next departure (`Trip::next`, as
    that trip is drawn); earlier, the train that runs it (the trip whose `next` is this draw) must have stood that long at the
    first stop. Worked out on variant 0 (the slot's timetable). A train with no next (leaves service) or no previous (enters)
    has no limit from rule 1.
  - Rule 2: at least `max(60 s, longest User::occ of the line's trains that way)` from the line's trains before and after, as they
    run (moved or not), so the order never changes.
  - Rule 3: a running, non-bus, non-ferry, non-circle line, and a departure that exists; else `Why::NoTimetable` / `NoTrain`.
  - The reasons carry what the strings will name: `Leaves { node, at }`, `Arrives { node, at }`, `Close { at }`, `Far`.
  - A step is clamped to the range as the nudge is; a step with no room left is refused with `Refused { limit, why }`. A train
    past a limit because its neighbours moved is allowed to stay and to move back (range always holds the current move).
  - The move is stored as `Move { run, at: dep, secs, kind: Own }`; stage 5 replaces `at` with the key plus `lead`.

## Numbers (corridor fixture of hours_tests, 3 rows)
- Count line (turnaround 180 s): range -119.96 / +120.00 s; the ends refuse with `Arrives` (first stop) and `Leaves` (last stop).
- Headway line, 5 min headway: range about +-168 s, both ends `Close` (the neighbour's 132 s of platform occupancy is the bound).

## Tests (`move_train/tests.rs`, 4 new)
- count line: both ends refuse with their reasons; the day's trains and their `next` links are identical at both ends; the moved
  train stood 60 s at the first stop and makes its next departure; 20 undo steps back to the bit.
- headway line: neighbours keep 60 s and the order; no train enters or leaves service at either end.
- a moved train holds its row through the line's next edit (the control, the same edit on a world without the move, does not place
  that fixture's row either, so the test only shows the row is held and `move_holds` says so).
- the era at the edit: a train that left before it is as it was, one after it moves; the era holds the earlier moves.

## Lies and limits
- `move_op` doesn't know the game time: a move of a train that left before the edit takes effect on none of today's trains
  (the era keeps them as they ran) and the key matches no later departure until stage 5. The app (stage 14) offers moves only
  for trains not yet left.
- A moved train's own wait inside `retime` is stage 4's; waits from moved departures stage 9's.
- `move_range` scans every resource for the line's `occ` (a few ms on a big save at most: once per click).
