Rule now: docs/ui-diagram.md "The rubber sheet", the map's last bullet. Code: `sheetLines.ts` `sheetMap` (`trackKm`,
`wholeShare`, `nearT`, the borders block).

## What changed

1. **Water everywhere again**: T-2609's drop-out (water only where the nearest land is a zone a line passes through) is
   gone, on every map. The far-land squeeze (`freeKm`) and the cut stay.
2. **Borders near track**: the hops' tracks are marked on a ground grid over the carried lattice (cells at least 250 m,
   at most about 4 million), chamfer distances taken (3 a side step, 4 a diagonal: within 8% of the straight-line
   distance), and each cell of the drawn grid marked near when its ground point is within 50 km of a track. A border edge
   of the drawn grid is drawn when it is between two zones, one of them a zone a line passes through (T-2609's rule),
   and either cell is near.
3. **Whole outlines**: per zone, the share of its edges with other zones (coasts not counted) that rule 2 draws; a zone a
   line passes through with more than 30% gets its whole outline, against every neighbour. A zone no line passes through
   never gets a whole outline (only its edges near track with a zone that has a line): the first try let such zones go
   whole too, which added New York's Lehigh, Lackawanna and Albany outlines (border length 4,577 -> 6,069 pitches).
4. Open borders end where the near cells end; the fit keeps each end. Stations are kept on their side as in T-2629.

Any map, city or country: on New York's map nearly all land is within 50 km of a track, so its look is unchanged.
