# T-3043: a conventional line missing from the real-rail layer near Long'an

Agent railgap, 2026-11-10. Investigation only: no pipeline or app change, no rebuild. Scripts and raw output are in the
agent's scratchpad (`local.py`, `nk.py`, `gap.py`, `world.py`, `world2.py`, `world2.json`); none worth keeping.

## Short answer

- The line is in OSM and continuous. It is 南昆线 (Nankun Line, ref 0082), `railway=rail usage=main`, electrified, 120 km/h,
  operator China Railway Nanning group.
- The layer hides it because noritetsu's line model has no line over it. The model builds a line only between 12306
  passenger stations; the last one on 南昆线 going east is Pingguo (107.56E). East of it there is no 12306 station until
  Nanning, so 87 km of named, tagged, connected main line (Pingguo to the Nanning approach, through Long'an) is in no line.
  Its tiles carry `n=1` ("no line runs over"), and the game's real-network filter drops `n=1`.
- This is not the realgraph build. realgraph.py reads the same `ways.json` and drops the same ways; it inherits the gap.
- The same cause hides about 32,000 km of track worldwide that carries the name of a line the model does have (20,000 km
  in China). A second, separate rule hides another 43,000 km that a line does run over.

## 1. How the layer is built

Three stages, none of them realgraph first:

1. `noritetsu/extract.py` keeps every OSM way with `railway` in rail, light_rail, subway, tram, monorail, funicular,
   narrow_gauge, preserved (not disused, abandoned, construction or proposed).
2. A per-region line model (`cn_register.py` for China, then `build_model.py`) makes lines and writes
   `dist/data/<cc>/ways.json`: way id -> the lines running over it. China's register unit is the national line by name
   (南昆线 is a line, 南昆客专线 the high-speed one beside it). Passenger stations come from 12306's ticketing list
   (3,404 stations); a line is the graph of ways carrying its name, cut into sections between its passenger stations.
3. `noritetsu/build_tiles.py` makes `dist/data/<cc>.pmtiles`. It drops yards, industrial and military track unless a
   passenger route relation runs over it, drops islands that touch no line, and marks `n=1` on every way no line in
   `ways.json` runs over (`mark_no_line`, Anita 2026-10-04: freight shown in passenger blue misled). Ways stay in the
   tile with `n=1`.
4. The game (`app/src/map/realNetwork.ts`, `SHOWN`) draws a way only when `n != 1` and it is metro, or rail with `r == 0`
   (usage=main, or urban kinds) or `p == 1` (a passenger route relation runs over it).
5. `pipeline/realgraph.py` (the snapping graph) keeps `shown = wid in on_line`, then drops rail of rank 1 without a route
   relation; gap fillers add back under 3 km groups. Same inputs, so same hole.

## 3. Why it was dropped, exactly

1. `cn_register.py` builds sections between passenger stations only. `extend_ends` carries a line on only to a passenger
   station it stops *short* of, and `join_pieces` joins pieces of one line. Nothing carries a line's named track on past
   its last station when no station follows (a tail).
2. So the 87 km east of Pingguo is in no section; `ways.json` omits it.
3. `build_tiles.mark_no_line` sets `n=1` on it (it is not an island: it joins 南昆线 at Pingguo and the Nanning
   junctions, so `drop_islands` keeps it).
4. `realNetwork.ts` `SHOWN` has `n != 1`, so it is not drawn; realgraph leaves it out of the routing graph too.

It is not a tag filter (usage=main is the best rank), not a gap in OSM, and not a service or construction tag.

## 5. Fixes and costs

**Fix 1: carry named track on past a line's last station (register).** In `cn_register.py` (and the same step in the
other registers) add a tail section: from a line's end station along ways bearing its name or ref, up to the junction
where that track meets another line's. The line then owns the ways. Cost: `build_model --region cn` 6 min, `build_tiles`
cn 6.5 min (392 s in the last run), `realgraph cn` seconds, plus `check_model`; about 15 min of one core, no new
download. Size: cn.pmtiles unchanged to within noise (the ways are already in the tile, only `n` flips), `cn.rgr` 6.6 MB
to about 7.1 MB. Adds wrongly: the model's `km` and station-to-station sections would gain a dead-end tail past the last
station, so 南昆线's panel would show a line running to a junction with no stop; a freight spur sharing a line's name
would ride along; 唐包线, 临哈线 and the like would be drawn whole, and any route-less usage=main branch bearing a line's
ref. Needs a rule for other registers (Korea's, NZ's).

**Fix 2: show same-name orphans (tile build).** In `mark_no_line`, leave `n=0` on an attached way whose name or `ref`
equals a model line's name or ref, is rail rank 0 and is not freight-tagged; and make realgraph's `shown` read the same
widened set. No register change, no line gains sections. Cost: `build_tiles` for the regions with matches (cn 6.5 min, us
about 5 min, mx, ca, au, nz, fr, kr small, 15-20 min in all, single core each, can run in parallel on 4 cores), realgraph
for the same regions about 2 min. Size: +20,000 km in China is +8% of its tiles' lines but the ways are already in
the file, so the tile size barely moves; `.rgr` +4% overall. Adds wrongly: the freight lines above (+3,000 km China
and Mexico "Linea A" 1,829 km if not named out), accidental generic-name matches ("Main Line" in the US), and nothing of
yards or sidings (those have `service` or rank 2 and never reach tiles). To limit: skip the register's FREIGHT_LINES, skip generic names ("Main Line"), and for China require a `ref`.

**Fix 3 (app only, no data rebuild): draw `n=1` rank-0 rail faintly.** The tile already holds the ways. Add a second
filter in `realNetwork.ts` for `n == 1`, `k == rail`, `r == 0`, drawn as other-rail grey at low opacity, matching what
noritetsu's own map does (its design is "subtle grey, almost invisible"). Cost: no data rebuild; in China 56,272 km
of n=1 rail are in the tiles (cn log, 2026-10-09), most of it freight; draw cost is the issue (T-2748 notes 44 layers at
zoom 6 already cost a frame), so cap at zoom 8 and up. Not snappable: realgraph still lacks it. Adds wrongly:
all main-line freight, which the layer excluded on purpose (Anita 2026-10-04).

**Fix 4 for H2 (separate, small):** drop the `r`/`p` clause in `SHOWN` so any rail with `n == 0` draws, and drop
realgraph's "rank 1 and not on a route is not shown" rule. A way in a line is passenger track by the model's own
evidence. Cost: app edit; realgraph all regions about 5 min at 2 cores (T-727e). Adds +43,400 km (+4%). Wrongly adds: a
usage=branch way the model puts in a line over freight-only track (small: those lines need two passenger stations).

## Recommendation

Do Fix 1 for China only now (the case the player hit, passenger lines in a country with a fine register) and Fix 4
everywhere (a few lines of filter, 5 min of realgraph). Leave Fix 2 and 3 for Anita: they decide whether the layer
shows freight, and that is the call she made on 2026-10-04. Fix 2 is the generalisation of Fix 1 if she wants one rule for
every country; its cost is about 20 min of rebuild.
