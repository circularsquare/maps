# T-409: the market's pairs split by track network (2026-10-15)

Rules: docs/demand.md "Clusters" and "The market opens without its zone pairs"; the choice in
DECISIONS.md.

## What was built

- **Rust** (`intercity.rs`): the pack's data (sites, zones, ride zones, commutes, derived block,
  site keys) sits behind `Rc`, and `IntercityApi::beside()` makes another market on the same pack
  with no network: a part. Each part is set on one cluster's network, so its pairs are those
  between zones its own stations serve. `set_access` also drops pairs (and commutes between zones)
  whose zones' access stations share no component of the rail graph: no path, no rail trip, so
  the figures are unchanged (a skipped pair carried exactly 0). `zone_boxes()` gives each 6 km
  zone's site box; `selection_rail_day` a selection's rail numbers alone, for adding parts.
- **Clusters** (`demandClusters.ts`): one node per 6 km market zone instead of one market node,
  joined to the stations within 30 km of its sites. The cluster's `marketKey` (its zones) keeps
  its part from solve to solve, so T-124's kept periods and T-326's kept clusters work per part.
  Registry zones were tried first: their boxes overlap and run to 110 km, and the real save plus a
  Boston line merged through Providence's box (a Boston edit 21 s).
- **Worker**: a part per market cluster (`assignParts`: same key, else a part whose key is gone,
  else `beside`; leftovers freed, the opened instance kept). The market's figures in every result
  are all parts' (`marketFigures`): rail added, trips, pairs and corridor trips once; worst loads
  the largest part's. A selection adds every part's rail to the first part's answer; a station
  asks its own cluster's part. `?perfOff=split` joins the market's zones as one node.

## Measured

`checks/t409-worker-check.mjs` (real worker in Node, market + Boston + Philadelphia packs, a 4-stop
line in each city, no track between): with both lines the plan has two clusters, and every
period's line loads (seg, board, alight, crush), Boston's and Philadelphia's summaries and the
market's parts equal a network holding that line alone to the bit; market rail is the two parts'
sum exactly, its trips once. Worker time (5 periods, 3 rounds): Boston edit 0.17-0.18 s split
(Philadelphia kept) against 0.21-0.22 s with `oneMarket` (one cluster).
