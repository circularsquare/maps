Stage 1 (design and measure), 2026-10-16. Nothing built; no pack, registry or config touched.
Anita's rule (2026-10-16): "literally every demand figure should be tied to a job or a population
count in some way." Jobs are the target; floor area was the fallback idea and she prefers jobs.
Why it came up: notes/T-451.md (Tokyo's terminals draw no more errands than residential shop streets).

Scripts (read only on the packs, numpy): `t453_common.py`, `t453_tyo.py`, `t453_lon.py`,

`data/raw/tier0/japan/ec2021_9-1B.xlsx` (economic census 2021 table 9-1B, 7.0 MB).

## 3. The rule (draft for docs/demand-worldwide.md "Everyday destinations")

- **Everyday destinations are jobs in the customer-facing sectors**, everywhere. Errands: retail
  and personal services; social: food, accommodation, arts and recreation, as LODES's CNS07 + CNS19
  and CNS17 + CNS18 (the table above). Social keeps 46% population, 46% these jobs, 8% parks.
- **Where a country publishes them by small area** (Japan's mesh, England's BRES by LSOA,
  Canada's CT of work), a cell gets its jobs x its unit's sector share. A sector mixed with
  non-customer work (Japan's I with wholesale) is split by the finest table that separates it
  (Japan: municipality, 9-1B).
- **Elsewhere (fallback D)**: each 2 km zone draws the geometric mean of its share of the pack's
  jobs and its share of the purpose's OSM places, spread over its cells by those OSM places. Where a
  country publishes sector totals for larger units (France's communes, China's districts if found),
  the units' totals replace the jobs share.
- Why it may not carry over: rounded or suppressed small-area counts; a sector table that mixes
  wholesale or head offices into retail; the fallback still rests on OSM's coverage of shops.

Findings:
- Sector jobs move errands onto the terminals (Tokyo 3.9% to 6.2%, London's West End 4.3% to
  6.0%) and London's leisure onto the West End (6.3% to 11.9%). Tokyo's leisure moves off the
  terminals (7.6% to 5.8%): eating places are spread through the residential wards more than OSM
  mapping suggests.
- **It does not change T-451's centre share** (34.7% to 34.3%, PT 16%): it moves trips between
  places inside the centre, as T-451 found for the half-jobs blend. The gap to PT is the distance
  curve and the home-based-only outings, for calibration (T-450).
- Total jobs alone (A, C) send errands into office districts (the City 9-12% against 2.7%) and
  are no closer than OSM counts. D, needing both jobs and shops, is the best fallback in both
  cities (18-23% misplaced against 22-30% for OSM counts today), a modest gain.

in Shimane, leisure up to 32% in Tokachi and 30% in Okhotsk: roadside malls and resort hotels
their OSM lacks), which would have left Japan a patchwork of sources. So a hook may name the pack
its source was judged on (`japan.SECTOR_JUDGED`, Tokyo), and other packs run the level test only.
