# T-2756: Tokyo's bubbles took minutes in a long-open window

Agent tokyoload, 2026-11-07. Anita on 8800 at de29a14c, a window open a long while, a busy machine (a join on 5 cores,
another agent on 3, an R2 upload filling her uplink): the tag sat on "Loading data…" (hover "Loading the bubbles for
Tokyo") for over 4 minutes, after an earlier stall at about 90%; leaving Tokyo and coming back started the wait again;
a reload loaded the whole game in about 40 s.

Script: `long.mjs` in the session scratchpad (`tokyoload/`), logs and JSON in `artifacts/trainworld/tokyoload/`. Her
Tokyo save (`artifacts/trainworld/anita-2026-11-03e/tokyo-day-414.save`) imported into a fresh profile, Homes on,
Shinjuku; a probe leaves for Osaka and comes back and times the tag. Chrome pinned to TW_CORES cores; `NOWORKERS=1`
lets the page size its own pool (3 solving workers, as on her 16-thread machine) instead of `?demandWorkers`.
`SLOW=<kbit/s>,<ms>` sends every non-localhost request through one throttled link; `RESTART=1` restarts every
worker as `memCheck` does (`debugRestart`).

## What it is not

- **Not today's commit.** de29a14c on a fresh page: bubbles 8-10 s into the load, 0.3-0.5 s after a return.
- **Not the network.** On 8800 the packs are local; with the internet throttled to 1 Mbit/s and 300 ms (and
  150 kbit/s, 600 ms) the times are the same. Only terrain and the basemap come from outside.
- **No buildup over a 25-minute scripted session** (239 pans, zooms, bubble clicks, end switches, city switches): no
  queued or unanswered questions, pool whole, every probe 0.3 s. The workers' WASM grew from 431 to 820 MB against a
  need of about 540 MB.

## The cause

That growth is what restarts them: `memCheck` (T-1205) restarts an idle worker once its WASM is 1.15 times its need
and 384 MB above it (820 MB was 281 MB above). The replacements reopen every pack and solve the whole game again, and
until that solve's final every question waits (`refreshing`: the new workers hold no earlier result). The zone in
view keeps its bubbles, but a zone left and come back to asks the pool for its cells and legs, so it waits for the
whole re-solve, with the tag on "Loading data…" and a time-based bar, which fits all of her account. A reload ends
it because the reload hits the demand cache. Her console would say `demand: worker N restarted to give back memory`.

Forced restart, then Osaka and back, the tag's "Loading data…" after the return (Chrome pinned, 3 solving workers):

| | 3 cores | 2 cores |
|---|---|---|
| before (de29a14c) | 37 s | 34 s |
| after | 0.5 s, then "Trips in 20 cities being recomputed…" until 28 s | 0.5 s, then the same until 27 s |

On her machine, with 8 of 16 cores taken by other jobs, the same re-solve plausibly takes minutes.

## The fix

- `demandClient.cityCells`: the focus zone's cells are kept per zone and frame (with the nearby zones' `otherCells`,
  dropped when the zone closes or re-levels), so a return asks no worker.
- `commuterView`: the last 6 zones left keep their final legs and built bubbles; a return to one takes them when the
  result shown is the same (version, day, purpose, legs' source), else shows them, rebuilt in the zone's own bubble
  worker, until the result shown's arrive (as nearby zones keep their last, T-1533).
- `StatusTag`: no hover for the bubbles wait (Anita's TODO, "the word bubbles is ambiguous"); a hover line
  "Recalculating after freeing up memory" while a memory restart's re-solve runs (`memoryResolve`, also set by a pool
  shrink), and "Your internet connection seems slow" when a city-data download has been open over 10 s or terrain has
  been downloading 10 s past its tag (the terrain branch is untested in a page: the throttled runs never showed the
  terrain tag).

Left (T-2757): everything else still waits for the re-solve after a memory restart (a selection's far end, a click on
a city or station, nearby zones' bubbles). Restarting one worker at a time, or answering from the result shown, would
end it.
