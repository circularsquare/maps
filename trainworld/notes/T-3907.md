## What is in the game now

| Rule | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Plain bus floor (moving speed) | 25 km/h (was 30) | `streets::BUS_LANE_KMH` |
| BRT floor | plain floor + 10 = 35 | `params::BRT_LANE_BONUS_KMH`, `streets::lane_kmh` |
| Trunk road or motorway | at most 1.4x the line's own street speed at the place, never under the same road without the trunk | `streets::BUS_TRUNK_MAX_X`, `streets::bus_fast_kmh` |
| In town (BRT pays there) | where a plain bus moves under BRT's floor: 0.85 x car street speed < 35 (car under about 41 km/h); trunk roads there count | `streets::brt_in_town`, `bus::Pace::town_m` (100 m steps) |
| BRT build | US$5M a road-km in town, paid at once on a constructed line, in the blueprint (Construct row "BRT", kind 16) on a planned one | `params::BRT_COST_PER_KM`, `Network::bus_cost` |
| BRT upkeep | street tram track's, US$210 a road-km a day (US$21k a game day), in place of the plain US$30, in town only | `params::UPKEEP_BRT_KM = UPKEEP_GROUND_KM * STREET_TRAM_UPKEEP` |
| Switch | one undoable `Op::Line` (`set_bus_brt`); off gives nothing back; undo and redo move no money (every undo does that) | `wasm/bus.rs` |
| Save | trailing `LBR1` (count, then line indexes), save level 34 | `save.rs` |
| Look | a BRT route drawn 1.4x as wide (not in Traffic, where width is riders) | `busLayer.ts` `BRT_WIDTH` |

## Left for after landing

- busspeed (T-3901) adds `Pace::speed_runs` and a speed list in `bus_quote`: its `street_bus_kmh(c, trunk)` is the
  plain line's; for a BRT line's redraw it should call `street_bus_kmh_at(c, trunk, floor)` with the pace's floor
  (`lane`, 0 meaning `BUS_LANE_KMH`). The trunk cap already flows into it.
- The in-town colouring of the bus tool's road hint (Anita's second ask, 11-21) is not built: the hint is a MapLibre
  vector-tile layer on the main thread, and the street grids live in the sim in the clock worker. The cheapest honest
  way I see: a sim call giving the in-town zone squares of the loaded grids, merged into polygons on the main thread,
  and the hint split into two layers by a `within` filter. A few hours, and it touches basemap.ts, which busspeed also
  edits.
- Out-of-town BRT is free and does nothing; the cost depends on the street grids, which a game's load fetches for its
  bus routes before the network is priced, so a loaded game's price is stable.
- Fixed on the way: the Construct list's bus route hover priced a km at the old US$1M (`BUS_ROUTE_M_PER_KM`); it now
  reads the sim's price.
