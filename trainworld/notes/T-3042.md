# T-3042 flat junction drawn once in the stop list

2026-11-16, agent bug-e. `TrackApi::line_marks` (sim/src/track/wasm/service.rs) walked every node of the run, so a junction a looped route passes twice (a balloon's junction, before and after the loop) gave a mark per pass, in two different gaps of the stop list. Now a junction node is marked once, at its first pass; a flat crossing where both pieces are on the route (a route crossing itself) is also marked once, at its first pass. The first pass is the upper one in the list.
Test: `a_junction_passed_twice_is_one_mark` (wasm/tests.rs, balloon fixture); it failed with 12 numbers (two marks) before the change. The real save has no flat junctions on a line, so there is no browser picture.
