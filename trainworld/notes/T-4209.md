# T-4209: the loading time left never jumps up (agent timeleft, 2026-11-24)

Rule: the card shows a time left only once the solve's own time is known: remembered from this slot's last load (`was`),
told by the workers' units (`unitsLeft`), no solve needed, or a cached-result load. Until then the card shows the step and the
percent only. Code: workers/demandClient.ts, the `solveTold` flag (3 lines) and the `job.left` line right after the `target` block.
Why: before the solve is measured its time is the model's guess (`prior`); on asianita (Use more cores off, 1 solving worker)
the guess gave 3 min, then 2 min, then at 135 s the units figure said 8 min. The units figure starts high and counts down faster
than the clock, never up, so it is safe to show. Not changed: nextLeft, the estimate, the load. T-1654's "always a figure" is
dropped for the early part of a first load.

Per-second screen (scratch: timeleft/base-asia.json, fix-asia.json, base-nyc/fix-nyc; driver rec.mjs, fresh profile, 2 cores, 1 worker).
asianita before: 57 s "3 min", 84 s "2 min", 135 s "8 min" (jump up 6 min), then 7,6,5,...,40 s, final at 436 s.
asianita after: no time until 138 s ("Loading city data" 8%, "Calculating trips" 14%), then 7 min, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 min, 50 s ... 20 s; final 523 s.
No rise at all inside the load (the "10 -> 420" a script would see is the earlier new-game card before the Open a file reload).
NYC new game: "About 10 s left" from 20.3 s to final at 22.4 s, unchanged.
