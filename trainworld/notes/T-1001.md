# T-1001: track placement stuck or laggy (Anita, 2026-10-23)

Report: start placed, then no ghost and the second click placed nothing until a refresh, after
switching to High speed and back. Later: "might have just been really laggy ... about 1 fps".

## Not reproduced (headless Chrome, dev 6180 and 8800, 2026-10-23)

Stuck state: start then High speed and back mid placement and between placements, keep placing on
and off, Caps Lock flips, a type switch while a segment edit is in flight, Quad and High speed
switched back and forth, fast toggles, the snap switch on with a real route found or searching.
No page errors; the tool, the preview queue and the snap state were never left set. 3,000 random
previews and 169 route edits (high speed and design speeds mixed): no worker "Internal" answer
(no Rust panic). Ids that do not exist are refused cleanly, so a panic could not be forced.

Lag, time per pointer move with a start set (move handler p50 / preview round trip p50, frames):

| setup | move ms | preview ms | frames |
|---|---|---|---|
| new game NYC z13, double (8800 and 6180) | 0.5 | 2.3 | 16.7 ms |
| same, high speed | 0.6 | 3.2 | 16.8 ms |
| real NYC save (109 lines), high speed | 0.6-0.7 | 4.3-5.1 | 16.5 ms |
| real NYC save, a click every 20 moves | 0.6 | 4.5 (max 510 just after an edit) | 16.6 ms, 107-188 ms after edits |
| Tokyo real network sandbox (133 lines, clock running) | 0.8 | 6.4 | 17.2 ms |
| Chengdu z11; NYC area z8.5 high speed; z10 with snap | 0.4-1.4 | 2-3 | 16.7 ms |
| 60 High speed toggles first | 0.4 | 2.1 | 16.7 ms |

Two things seen but not 1 fps: the first preview of a page shows a 0.4-0.6 s frame gap (once);
just after loading the real save, demand's main-thread work (`corridorSums`, the demand client's
message handler) freezes frames for 2-6 s until the solve settles.

Scripts (scratchpad, not kept): repro (UI clicks), fuzz (previews and edits), snap, prof (CDP CPU
profile and trace, time per move).

## Defensive fixes built

- `setTrackType` (game/ui.ts) in one `batch`: the tool never previews a mix of old and new type.
- tools.ts `segmentClick` and `takeBackSegment`: `placing` is cleared in a `finally`, so a failed
  edit never leaves later clicks ignored.
- clockClient.ts: a preview callback that throws still sends the preview waiting behind it.
- realRoute.worker.ts: a route search that throws answers "off", so a click waiting on it becomes an
  ordinary segment instead of waiting for ever.
