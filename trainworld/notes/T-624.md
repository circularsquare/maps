# T-624: join constructed pieces under a platform

Rule (DECISIONS 2026-10-17): a station within half a platform of a plain joint joins the pieces
there when both are constructed (or both blueprint, switched alike) and have the same track count
(`EdgeData::tracks`; quad track is two double edges, so it joins pair by pair). Unlike track, or
constructed beside blueprint, still refuses ("the end of a piece of track").

## The change

`TrackApi::free_joint` (sim/src/track/wasm.rs) refused any node with a constructed edge end
(`built_ports > 0`). It now takes `built_ok`: `reshaped` (dragging a blueprint corner moves the
joint with it) passes false, since constructed track never reshapes; `unjoin_near` passes true and
already compared `built`, `off` and `tracks` of the two pieces. It also stops at a crossover piece.

## Per-edge stores checked

Everything below is rebuilt from `EdgeData` by `Network::set_edge` and `derive`, or from the line
path, so joining is the same `Op::Edge` replace, `Op::Edge`/`Op::Node` delete and `Op::Line` path
rewrite that blueprint joins already used (one `Op::Batch`, one undo step).

- `net.rs` `edge_*` columns: `a b tracks built off pis thru crossover` are the `EdgeData` fields
  (joined edge: `built` and `tracks` kept, `off` equal on both pieces); `len pieces vert water cost
  time` are derived from the shape.
- Cost and money: no stored per-edge payment. `built_cost()` is derived from shape, `charge` of an
  edit is `max(built_after - built_before, 0)`. Same shape and levels give the same cost, so no
  charge and no refund (tested: cash and `built_cost` unchanged, and after undo).
- Levels and ramps: joint kept as a corner where levels differ (T-622); heights checked every 49 m.
- Curve radii and speed limits: a corner's radius is in `Pi`; the joined fit's radii are set back to
  the pieces' where the join would change them. Speeds follow from radius and level.
- Lines (`line_path`, `edge_lines`): paths rewritten onto the one edge in travel order; `stops`
  untouched. `edge_lines` is rebuilt by derive.
- Capacity (`svc.cap.res` per edge or node): rebuilt for the touched edges by `update_scoped`.
- Trains: not stored. A train is a departure of a line at a time, and its place is the distance
  along the line's path (`profile::state_at`), not an edge and offset. The path keeps its length, so
  every train stays where it was. `before_edit` / `note_changes` record a shift only if a trip
  length changed; here none does (tested: no eras, no shifts, same trips, same positions).
- App: the clock worker keeps `edgeDrawn` per edge id and drops the ids in `dirty_edges()`; the
  removed piece is among them.
- Undo: `Op::Batch` inverse restores both pieces, the joint node and the line paths.

## Tests (sim/src/track/wasm.rs)

- `constructed_pieces_of_one_kind_are_merged_under_a_platform`: single and double, with ramps.
- `a_station_across_a_built_joint_keeps_the_line_its_trains_and_the_money`: running line over built
  double track in two pieces (flat and with a ramp either side), station across the joint.
- `a_joint_that_cannot_be_merged_says_so`: built beside blueprint (both ways), single beside
  double (blueprint and built) still refused.
