# T-3999: the station tool picks what it places (agent stationkinds, 2026-11-21)

Anita, 11-21, after playing T-3378's BRT stations: they should snap more to placed bus routes; hovering open ground
with the station tool must not mean a BRT station ("this would confuse people ... who dont play with buses"); a
choice in the station tool between a train/tram station and a bus station; ports without a ferry route. Later the
same day: stations on open ground again, "placing first point then placing second endpoint (just orientation, for
station length we still use the specified station length)", removed earlier by T-1820.

## What changed

- **Kind picker** (`ui/ToolBar.tsx` StationBar, `game/ui.ts` `stationKind`, saved as `build.stationKind`): three icon
  buttons at the front of the station bar, train (Train or tram station, the default), bus (BRT station), anchor
  (Port), names as hovers. Station length and the one/both tracks buttons show for the train kind only. On a phone
  the kind is one button opening the three above the bar (the track type's pattern) and the "Station length" label
  is left out: with three buttons inline the bar was 411 px wide in a 372 px box and scrolled the tracks buttons off.
- **BRT station** (`map/stationTool.ts` `brtSpot`, `busRouteNear`): only with the bus kind; never snaps to track.
  Snaps beside the nearest bus line's route (no ferry's, from `lineStats[].bus.routeLL`) within 80 screen px of
  ground, clamped 50-400 m (146 m at zoom 15 at 40° latitude, 73 m at 16, 293 m at 14); else the nearest road a bus
  may use within 50 m as before. A pointer within 8 m of another road, with the route more than 8 m further, takes
  that road. First tried 20 m: in Manhattan (cross streets ~80 m apart) a pointer 105 m off 2nd Ave mid-block was
  within 20 m of E 34th St and the station went there, so half of every block lost the snap.
- **Port** (`map/stationPort.ts`; `addPort` edit op in `workers/protocol.ts`, `clockEdits.ts`, BUILD_OPS in
  `game/world.ts`): the ferry tool's water worker (`tk.ferry.water.port`, `portReachM`) finds the landing and berth;
  ghost, tags and refusals are the ferry tool's strings. `TrackApi::add_port` was already in the WASM interface, so
  no Rust change.
- **Open ground** (`map/stationFree.ts`, ported from the T-1820 diff, a945e323): first click one platform end (a ring
  and "Click to start a station here"), the platform then points toward the pointer at the set length with its own
  straight piece 10 m longer each end (`add_free_station`, checked by `preview_free_station` as the ghost moves), tag
  "Click to set the station's direction", "220 m of new track, $106M", the help lines. Second click places. Reads
  today's signals: track type (tram: tram platform length and route kind 3; high speed), level, quad. Esc, another
  tool or kind cancels; by touch the first tap is the end and the next places toward it (bar shows cancel).
- `tools.notOnTrack` retired (en, context, every locale and sources file); nothing refuses open ground now.
- `checks/t2430-station-tag.mjs`: its open-ground assert now wants the hint, not the refusal.
