# T-1185 (with T-1189's China part): China's zones on the township census, replanned by T-1140's rule

Code and dry runs 2026-10-25, tree t1185 (built on tree t1191's uncommitted planner: its
`world_plan.py` and three docs copied in at the start, since `tw/t1191` had no commits to merge;
land t1191 first, then this tree's diff is only its own). Nothing in `data/packs`, `data/work`, the
registry, market rows or the plan was written. The rebuild runs later, from the main tree (section 5).
Pictures and logs: `artifacts/trainworld/t1185/`; `t1185-china.png` in the maps root.

## For Anita

China's 660 countryside zones get the same people source as its cities since yesterday: the 2020
census by township, spread inside each township by GHSL's housing volume. On five zones checked it
puts every township right (wrong-township share from 9-25% to 0-0.2%) and takes the false blocks of
150,000-300,000 people a km² out of the zone towns (Xinxiang's centre 305k to 39k). In all, the
zones hold 27M fewer people than GHSL put there (986M against 1,013M): the migrants GHSL still
counts in their home counties, most of all in the north-east (the census 0.79-0.81 of GHSL) and in
Guangdong's, Shanxi's and Jiangxi's countryside (0.88-0.89); the census counts them in the cities,
which gained 22M at T-1184.

In the same rebuild the zones are regrouped by the compact-zones rule (T-1140) with T-1191's
newer rules: 660 zones become 638, median 1.62M people, 80 of them exactly as now. 82 end up under
the 1M floor, 40 of those beside a city that is their main travel partner (Beijing's Miyun,
Nanning's Yongning, Quanzhou's Luojiang): those would disappear if the cities grew, which is a
metro rebuild for later. One call of mine: the "two cities never join by shape" rule counts a city
from 200,000 people in China rather than 100,000, or 140 zones would stay under the floor.

The rebuild: about 9 hours on 12 cores, overnight.

## 1. The code

- `pipeline/world_plan.py`: `ZONE_RULES["CHN"]` names the `china` module (as the US's names
  `usa`), so every Chinese zone row the planner writes takes `china.plan_hooks`: the township
  hook, nothing else (zones keep the zone curve and the tier 0 jobs proxy). Rows already in the plan
  have no module; they get it from the replan, which rewrites every Chinese zone row.
- `world_plan.py zones --countries CN --replan` (`plan_zones(..., replan=True)`, T-1189): the
  country's built zone rows are regrouped too, their packs no longer counted as packs (so their
  units are free and nothing is cut by them); a new zone with exactly an old row's units keeps its
  id, order, status, run record and gate relaxations; `splice` takes such rows whatever their status.
- `ZONE_RULES["CHN"]["city_pop"]` 200,000 (`city_units` takes it; the default stays `CITY_POP`
  100,000): section 3.
- `pipeline/china.py`: docstrings only. docs/zones.md, docs/data-register.md: the module, `--replan`,
  China's city threshold.
- `notes/t1185/`: the rebuild kit (section 5).

## 3. The replan dry run

`dry.py` (scratch): `plan_zones(["CHN"], out, replan=True)` on copies of the main tree's plan and
registry taken 2026-10-25 00:00 (T-1184's join running; the 84 metro rows gated or parked, so none
grows), about 20 minutes on one core; the result is deterministic (two runs identical). 2,844
counties: 409 in metro rows, 2,418 free, none dead space.

| | Zones | Median | Under 1M (under 0.5M) | Hull ratio under 0.7 | Rows unchanged | Rings left |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Now | 660 | 1.48M | 1 (0) | 294 | | 57 |
| T-1140's design (repair: only the 363 failing rows regrouped) | 608 | 1.70M | 57 | | 123 | 4 |
| Full replan, T-1191's rules as they are (city 100,000) | 681 | 1.47M | 140 (28) | 154 | 76 | 5 |
| **Full replan, city 200,000 in China (built)** | **638** | **1.62M** | **82 (13)** | **144** | **80** | **4** |
| Full replan, no two-cities rule | 629 | 1.66M | 72 (13) | 142 | 80 | 4 |

**Why a full replan, not T-1140's repair**: every Chinese zone is rebuilt anyway for the census,
so keeping passing rows saves no build; a full replan is the planner's own rule, with nothing kept
from the old smallest-neighbour plan.

**The two-cities rule** (T-1191: zones each holding a GHSL urban centre of 100,000 never join by
shape alone): 821 of China's 2,418 free counties hold one, so with a 1M floor it stopped most shape
joins and left 140 zones under the floor, most of them ordinary county towns 50-100 km apart. At
200,000 (a fifth of China's floor, as 100,000 is of the 0.5M elsewhere) 375 counties count, and the
result is near the rule without it (82 under the floor against 72).

By province (now → replanned, under 1M after): Henan 52 → 49 (7), Shandong 50 → 48 (3), Jingjinji
48 → 43 (2), Sichuan 46 → 38 (2), Hunan 41 → 39 (3), Guangdong 33 → 36 (5), Jiangsu 30 → 32 (8),
Zhejiang 20 → 23 (8), Xinjiang 16 → 17 (6), Qinghai 3 → 6 (4), Tibet 2 → 5 (4: the 100,000 km²
relaxation splits the plateau). Full table: `compare_final.txt`.

Rings left (as T-1087 found, one-county rings round a metro): Bozhou District round Honghuagang,
Huangzhong round Xining, Yunyang round Shiyan, Xiangfu round Kaifeng.

**Under the floor** (82): 40 have a built metro or pack as their biggest gravity partner
(`partners_c200.txt`); the rest are the western big-area zones (Qiemo 0.12M, Ngamring 0.21M, Tingri
0.22M, Alxa Left 0.23M, Yushu 0.45M) and county towns whose neighbours are neither partners nor
compact joins (Daye 0.73M, Shenmu 0.81M, Sihong 0.85M).

**Names**: `names.py` carries a hand-set name (zone-names.json, T-897's rules) to a new zone when
each holds the other's origin: 174 of the 558 new zones take one (Muye's zone stays "Xinxiang",
Zhifu's "Yantai"); 50 hand-set names are not carried and 384 new zones show the planner's county
name until someone looks (`names_final.txt`).

## 5. The rebuild (one run of T-1185 and T-1189's China part)

**Before**: t1191 and t1185 landed on master; T-1184 done (its join and its 15 parked rows'
rejoin); Korea's build (T-1238) done, so China and the plan are free; 15 GB free on C:. Everything
from the maps root, `venv\Scripts\python.exe -I` for Python.

**What changes**: every Chinese zone row (660 now, 638 after: 80 the same units, 558 new, 580
removed), rebuilt with the township census and the bounded GHSL; the join of all 27 Chinese solve
units, carrying T-1193 (seams.py's pairs, rerun after the builds) and T-1175's cross-line draw (the
join's default). Metro rows, sha, Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan are not rebuilt; their market packs are
rewritten.

**Steps**:
1. Tiles: `trainworld\notes\t1185\tiles.py` (moves the 14 tiles from `data/raw/t1185_ghsl/`,
   checks all 24 the zones touch; seconds).
2. Kit: `trainworld\notes\t1150\run\setup_sim.py refresh` (the sim copy at HEAD), then `... check`.
3. Backup: `trainworld\notes\t1150\run\backup.py T1185 pre CN,HK,MO,TW` (registry, market rows,
   names, aliases, plan, realms, outlines, joint files, every such pack; about 3.5 GB). Also copy
   `trainworld\data\work\worldplan\extents.geojson` to `data\work\pre-T1185\`.
4. `trainworld\notes\t1185\retire.py save` (old zone ids, units, extents, origins).
5. Replan: `trainworld\pipeline\world_run.py step world_plan zones --countries CN --replan >
   artifacts\trainworld\t1185-replan.log 2>&1` (about 20 minutes, one core; takes the plan lock
   only to write). Expect 638 zones, 80 unchanged, 4 rings
   left, "375 units with a city of 200,000"; the log's zone list against
   `artifacts/trainworld/t1185/dry_final.log` (ids may differ if another country took some since).
6. `trainworld\notes\t1185\retire.py go --dry`, then `retire.py go`: unregisters the 660 old ids,
   sets the 80 kept rows planned, moves the 580 removed ids' packs and caches to
   `data/work/pre-T1185/removed/` and every zone's signal cache to `pre-T1185/signals/`.
7. `trainworld\notes\t1185\stale.py --cmd`, then `trainworld\notes\t1185\market_fix.cmd` started
   hidden (`notes\t1150\run\start_hidden.ps1`; about 15 minutes): no market pack may name an old
   zone's place, or each region's first gate parks its rows (T-1184's first start, T-1231). Then
   `stale.py` again: 0.
8. `trainworld\notes\t1185\relax.py --procs 4` (read), then `relax.py --set --procs 4`: pop_vs_plan
   relaxed on the zones whose census lies outside 0.7-1.5 of the plan (the dry run: 28, from
   Xingning, Huai'an and Fengrun at 0.56 to Lianchi 1.53 and Fucheng 1.55; 149 more only warn);
   about 20 minutes.
9. `trainworld\notes\t1185\split.py 3 6181`: runs A (south and south-west, about 180 rows), B (east,
   152), C (centre, 147), D (north and west, 143), with disjoint extracts, and E (16 rows whose
   extracts fall in two groups). Start A-D at once, each with `start_hidden.ps1 run_<x>.cmd`
   (3 jobs each, 12 cores, ports 6181-6184, logs `artifacts/trainworld/t1185-<x>.log`); write the
   batch ids, PIDs and logs into this note. When all four end, E (6185).
10. `world_run.py status`: 638 gated, the parked with causes; restart a run the same way (built rows
   only gate again).
11. Seams (T-1193): `trainworld\pipeline\seams.py --dry`, then `seams.py` (3 minutes): the seamless
   pairs among the new zone ids into `borders.json`.
12. Join: `backup.py T1185 prejoin CN,HK,MO,TW`, then `notes\t1150\run\join.cmd t1185 12` started
   hidden (log `artifacts/trainworld/t1185-join.log`). Every solve unit holds new zones, so all 27
   re-solve; T-1175's cross-line draw and the seamless factors are in by default. If a unit's
   re-gate fails, read the cause (T-1184's join hit `other_packs_unchanged` on `everyday_kept_*`,
   fixed in pack_gate 2026-10-24).
13. After: `world_run.py step market_zones hon mcu jiw` if their units were not re-solved;
   `trainworld\pipeline\zone_successors.py` (the 580 removed ids, T-1053); `notes\t1185\names.py`,
   then `names.py --write`; smoke and `checks/t1053-zone-ids.mjs`; Guangzhou-Foshan's commuters
   across (T-1193: 1.3-1.5M). Then delete `data/work/pre-T1185/` and the kit's `bk` once clean.

**Hours at 12 cores**: steps 1-8 about 1.2 h; runs A-D about 3.5 h (the 660 zones' build steps
took 15.1 machine hours in T-897, so about 1.3 h on 12; the gates about 35 s a row, about 1.5 h in
each run, and the extracts about 30 minutes; T-897's 171-row run took 3.5 h); E about 40 minutes
(its 16 extracts again); seams and the join 3-4 h (T-1184's China join ran past 3.5 h on 6 jobs);
step 13 about 30 minutes. **About 9 hours.** Memory: the bounding adds about 1.2 GB to the biggest
windows (Lhasa's, Wensu's; notes/T-1150.md 5), which the zones' first builds already held.
