# T-3824 Schematic ferries drawn after the water (agent coastround, 2026-11-20)

Anita, 11-20: "now that we're drawing ferries, it might be nice to draw ferries after drawing water so that their
ports actually line up closer to the coastline". Goal: ports on the drawn coast, routes over drawn water.

No layout change was needed: the thin lines (T-3360) are already made after the layout, in the worker, from the
drawing that holds the water (`thinLines` gets `d`, whose `d.water` is set when the water is on).

## What is in the game now (`thin.ts` `overWater`, `ferryWater.ts`)

With the water on, per ferry line:

- The drawn water on a grid: the sea (even-odd), the bands and the fillets (T-3833), half a pitch a cell (coarser for a
  long route, at most 500 cells a side), over the carried route's box and 12 pitches round it.
- Each stop that is no station moves onto the drawn coast: where its route (carried, toward the next stop; the last
  toward the one before) first meets the drawn water; a port already on water, or whose route never meets it, takes the
  coast within 6 pitches; else it stays. A port at a station (within 400 m, as before) starts at its pill.
- Stop to stop, the cheapest way over the grid (A*, steps of a side or a corner, land 12 times dearer), so a ferry
  leaves a pill for the water the shortest way and then keeps to water.
- Fitted at 0, 45 and 90 degrees within 3 pitches of that way (was 4 of the carried route), cut at every stop so the
  line passes through each port, a run kept only where it crosses no more land than the way found did. Corners and
  dashes as before. Water off: as before.

Cost: the thin lines on the New York save with four ferries (Staten Island, East River, Soundview, South Brooklyn):
5.5 ms with the water off, 110 ms with it on (each ferry puts the drawn water on its own grid). Runs once after a
layout, in the worker.
