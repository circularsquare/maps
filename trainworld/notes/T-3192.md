# T-3192: "Statically used varyings do not fit within packing limits"

A player on the site's chat (live v0.2.1): the game stopped at startup with "Die Seite ist beim Start hängen
geblieben (link: Statically used varyings do not fit within packing limits. (see GLSL ES Specification
1.0.17, p111))", "after allowing html5 graphics and webgl".

## Cause

The message is Firefox's own link check (dom/canvas/WebGLShaderValidator.cpp, `CanLinkTo`, which runs ANGLE's
`sh::CheckVariablesWithinPackingLimits` with the context's MAX_VARYING_VECTORS). Firefox with fingerprinting
resistance (`privacy.resistFingerprinting`: LibreWolf, Mullvad Browser, Tor Browser, hardened Firefox; also
`webgl.min_capability_mode`) caps that at **8 for WebGL2 too** (WebGLContextValidate.cpp, kCommonMaxVaryingVectors
= 8), below WebGL2's guaranteed 15. "Allowing html5 graphics" is RFP's canvas-extraction prompt; LibreWolf ships
WebGL off. The station program (and the bridge program, which shares its fragment shader) packed to 12 vectors:
the platform's 13-point centre line (T-254) went to the fragment shader as 7 flat vec4 varyings.

Reproduced exactly with Playwright's Firefox 151 and `privacy.resistFingerprinting: true` (the page reports
MAX_VARYING_VECTORS 8, MAX_VERTEX_TEXTURE_IMAGE_UNITS 8, MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE 2048, MAX_RENDERBUFFER_SIZE 2048, vertex
attribs 16, uniform vectors 256 vertex / 224 fragment): the same startup screen, in English,
`artifacts/trainworld/t3192-firefox-rfp-before.png`.

## Programs and their packed varyings

Vectors as the A.7 algorithm packs the varyings both shaders statically use, plus used gl_FragCoord /
gl_PointCoord / gl_FrontFacing (what Firefox counts). `node trainworld/checks/t3192-varyings.mjs` prints this.

| Program | Varyings | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| renderer STATION_VS + STATION_FS | 15 -> 10 | **12** | 6 |
| renderer BRIDGE_VS + STATION_FS | 15 -> 10 | **12** | 6 |
| renderer STROKE_VS + STROKE_FS | 11 | 7 | 7 |
| renderer TRAIN_VS + TRAIN_FS | 12 | 5 | 5 |
| buildingFloors QUAD_VS + SHOP_FS, + CELL_FS | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| busLayer SEG, STOP | 5 | 3 | 3 |
| demandOverlay DISC | 5 | 3 | 3 |
| gradeMarks | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| busLayer ARROW | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| demandOverlay LINE | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| parkingLots | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| buildingFloors FILL, EDGE, SEL; demandOverlay CUT; oneWayMarks; renderer SEL; strokeCache; transferBubbles | 1-3 | 1 | 1 |
| renderer PROBE (transform feedback), shadeLayer | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Only the station and bridge programs were over 8; none was over 15, so ordinary WebGL2 browsers never hit this.
Stroke at 7 is the next nearest. The budget is 8 because that is a limit real players run with; WebGL1's
minimum is the same number. No program uses gl_FragCoord with varyings beyond SEL_FS (no varyings).

## Fix

- The station fragment shader works out the centre line itself (`centrePoint` in rendererShaders.ts): it reads
  u_stline and projects each point exactly as the vertex shader does, from two flat varyings (`v_at`: the node and
  the platform's middle on screen; `v_inst`: the station's index, -1 for a straight line `v_size.x` either side,
  which rooms and bridges are). Same arithmetic, so the shape is unchanged; 13 texel reads and projections per
  station fragment more, which the per-fragment polyFrame already roughly matched in cost. The bridge program's
  u_stline sampler is pointed at the stations' unit 11 once (it never reads it).
- Decorative layers degrade: `compileOptional` (map/gl.ts) returns null and logs "<layer> not drawn: ..." where the
  browser refuses a program; transfer bubbles, one-way chevrons, grade marks, car parks and building floors (all five
  of its programs or none) then draw nothing and the game carries on (their hit-test data is kept). Essential and
  left to stop startup as before: stroke, station, bridge, train, selection outline, stroke cache, buses (the
  player's bus lines), the demand overlay, the realm shade.
- Found on the way, also RFP-only: the stroke cache's renderbuffers and textures are the canvas's size, and RFP caps
  MAX_RENDERBUFFER_SIZE and MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE at 2048, under a 1440p or HiDPI canvas: allocation failed and the
  framebuffers were incomplete. The renderer now skips the cache (draws strokes each frame, as `strokeCache` off)
  when the view is larger than either limit (`cacheMax`).
- Vertex texture units: TRAIN_VS samples 10 textures and RFP reports MAX_VERTEX_TEXTURE_IMAGE_UNITS 8, but Firefox
  does not enforce it at link or draw: trains draw in the RFP run.

## Checks

- `checks/t3192-varyings.mjs`: FAIL before (2 over 8), PASS after; added to smoke's Node list.
- Firefox RFP, after: new game and the real save load and draw lines, stations, platforms and trains at zoom 12.5,
  15.5, 17.6 (`artifacts/trainworld/t3192-firefox-rfp-after-real-z15.5.png`), no WebGL warnings past the
  canvas-extraction notices.
- Chrome before/after screenshots (`artifacts/trainworld/t3192-{before,after}-{real,new}-{light,dark}-z*.png`, 16
  pairs): the real save's 8 pairs are pixel-identical; the new game's differ only where the demand card and a
  street label moved with timing, in a view with no stations.
- Typecheck clean; smoke on 6118 all PASS.
