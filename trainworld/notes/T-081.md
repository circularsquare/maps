Done 2026-10-09 by the gameplay agent (batch 5).

- One constant, `ECONOMY = 100.0` in `sim/src/track/params.rs`. `TrackApi.running_cost` multiplies

  `TrackApi.money_params` sends it as the third number; the clock worker passes it on as

- Real figures stay real where the player sees a price: the fare ($1.50 a ride + $0.10 a km), the
  US$6 a car-km in the Money tab's note, US$2.5M a car, and all construction.

  Track, stations and trains cost what they would in real life." `money(0)` reads "$0" (it read

## How it reads (headless)

- The real New York save (6,245 cars): a day at its schedule shows fares $1.71B against running
  trains $2.36B; three game hours settled $448M of fares and $452M of running costs.
- A test network of two 5-6 km tunnel lines at 16 trains an hour, 10-car trains, 74k and 25k
  riders a day: Line 2 earns $5.1M a day and costs $17.3M to run. Lines with few stops and long
  trains at high frequency lose money at any scale; the payback in about a week holds for a busy
  line with trains sized to its riders (T-028's 5 km test line: $141M a day in fares against $17M
  to run at 100x, so a US$1.5B line pays back in about 12 days).
