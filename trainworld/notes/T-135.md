longer-trip counts, with a 0.5/day cutoff and a 400-zone cap. Small distant origins could be
invisible or omitted. Boston work selections still correctly queried incoming trips (`ends`).

total, using the existing exact place rows. Registered cities use their city centres, Trenton
uses its weighted endpoint. Screen radii are `10 + 28*sqrt(total/max)` pixels before the size
slider. Labels show city and daily total; hover states direction and rail count. At closer zooms,
all positive zones are retained, sized relative to the busiest zone with a 2.5px radius floor.
Trip totals, mode mix, workers and simulation assignments are unchanged. Hover and click keep
the existing topmost-layer priority and parent selection retention.
