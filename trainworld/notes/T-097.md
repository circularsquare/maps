## How

- **Levels**: a res-9 cell's parent at resolution r is its H3 id with the digits r+1..15 set to 7
  (bits below 3 x (15 - r)), so grouping is one BigInt OR per cell and level, 55-60 ms once per
  city in the worker, with each group's member cells kept for selections. 202,337 cells make
  about 30k res-8, 4.7k res-7 and 780 res-6 bubbles.
- **Size**: radius = sqrt(commuters / density / pi), in local units; density 25,000 a km² for homes,
  80,000 for jobs (`BUBBLE_DENSITY`). A selection's far end is sized among itself: the biggest has
  0.6 of its level's mean hexagon area (`RES_AREA_M2`).
- **Colour**: `modeMix(train, walk, drive)`: the three colours weighted by share, scaled so the
  brightest channel matches the primaries'. Drawn at 80% opacity with a white 1 px edge; selected
  bubbles get a dark 2 px outline.
- **Hit testing** is in local units against the disc list of the level on screen, last drawn
  first: 0.014 ms at zoom 11.2, 0.43 ms at zoom 14 (198k res-9 bubbles).
- **Selection**: groups' member cells, unioned; the near end re-summed on the main thread
  (thousands of cells, under 2 ms), the far end from `flows(end, cells)` (T-098) summed into all
  levels in the bubble worker. Asked again when the demand result changes. Far-end places: the
  40 biggest res-7 groups named by the nearest station within 3 km, merged by name, top 8 listed.
- **Long press**: mousedown in the select tool with bubbles on starts a 0.4 s timer; moving over
  5 px first cancels it (a normal pan). When it fires, map panning is off, the cursor a crosshair
  and a box follows the pointer; on release the bubbles whose centres are in the box are selected.
