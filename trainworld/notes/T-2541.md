## The measures

Scripts in the session scratchpad `diagstraits/` (`m.mjs`: layouts cached per label, `lay.mjs`, `timing.mjs`):
- **Hops across water**: ends on different land masses (`massAt`), or 2 km of sea on the track. Drawn length against
  the real length at the local drawn scale (the median drawn over real length of the land hops within 8 km).
- **Sea pairs**: stations within 3 km of the sea, pairs within 40 km whose straight way has a 2 km and 30% run of sea
  (T-2540's "sea between stations"): drawn distance over real distance at the two stations' local drawn scales. Under
  0.5 is squeezed (the water's own threshold for cutting a strait).
- **Regional scale**: the similarity fit (drawn over real) of a region's stations, over the Kanto core's (within 12 km
  of Tokyo). Boso: within 45 km of Tateyama east of Hama-Kanaya; Izu: within 45 km of Ito south of Atami.
- **Per-island IoU**: t2169-water.mjs (T-2540).
- T-2520's parallel measure and T-2534's crossings kept outside dense places, on six graphs (New York, Tokyo, the
  Northeast, Toronto, the T-2534 test save, all Japan; the game captures have no sea, see below).

Noise: master laid out twice (as is, and with one more LSQR iteration a round), the tree likewise.

## Looked outward

T-2540's notes (JR maps keep the four islands apart with plain gaps the Seikan, Kanmon and Seto lines cross; the Tokyo
Metro map draws Tokyo Bay as one flat shape; Bast, Brosi and Storandt treat coastlines as obstacles) point the same way:
the outline is the layout's to hold, and the water only follows. Here the water's own threshold (a strait is cut where
two stations are drawn nearer than half their real distance at their scales) is what the pairs keep clear of.

(`diagram-saves.mjs`: `openSave(..).frameSpec`, and `ground(g)` for the mercator frame and box), and every diagram

the `-game` checks lay out without the sea until diagram-game.mjs is run again.
