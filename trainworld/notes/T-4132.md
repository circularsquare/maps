# T-4132 Re-time only the lines whose timing moved (agent retime, 2026-11-23)

## What changed

- `timing::retime` (sim/src/track/timing.rs) works lines out in clusters: the lines joined through a wait at a passing station or a following loss (owners, users and the plan's two lines). A cluster's variants are worked out from its own lines alone (lead_in, passing_waits, floor and following each read only the line and the other users of its resources), so each cluster keeps a key of everything it reads and the state it left its lines' variants in (`Service::timing_keys`). A cluster is worked out again only when its key or its lines' variants moved; the rest keep theirs, and their profiles stay in the memo.
- The key: each resource's users, delays, wait point and plan (`Plan::key_into`: points, spacing, headway cap), then per line its weekday pattern, dwell, turnaround, stand at the far end, spare time and split per slot, loop and one-way, and per run its free run, path, profiles and holds. More than master's single key read (it left out plans, stands, spare time and profiles' shapes, which any change elsewhere hid).
- The resources are read in the order of what they hold, not their index: any edit renumbers them, and their order moved the key (the big cluster of the Asia save was redone on most edits). It also decides the last bits of a slower train's wait where two waits sit at one stop: against master's index order, 53 of 261 checked retimes on the Asia save timed a line otherwise, every one by at most 2.1e-14 s in a variant's stop hold, with every profile duration and pattern the same (check mode `order`).
- Fallback: a load, a time-zone table, the game's hours (reslot) and the first retime have no stored keys, so every cluster is worked out (`Service::forget_timing` does the same for tests). A rebuilt line starts with no variants, which the state check catches.
- Stage 2, the station tool's preview: `TrackWorld::trial_with` (station previews, the trains-to-buy price, the link check) no longer times trains in the trial's edit or its rollback (`Service::timing_held`): what it inspects reads only the means (`cars_needed`: trains; links: `line_broken`), and nothing in a commit's update, settle or departure placing reads variants. Each line the trial rebuilds hands its variants to the stash first; after the rollback they go back on the lines that have none, and one retime keeps every cluster whose inputs the rollback left as they were. That one retime runs after the phases are put back, so the variants match them (master timed the rollback before `set_phases`).
- Cost of the bounded retime itself: the keys and states of every cluster each time, about 1.5-2 ms natively on the Asia save (20 clusters); profiles of clusters that stand stay in the memo in place (no rehash).
- Check mode, native only (`timing/check.rs`): `TW_RETIME_CHECK=1` runs a full retime from scratch beside every bounded one and panics on any line whose variants, patterns or timing differ; `skip` leaves the first moved cluster standing (the check must fail); `order` compares with master's resource order and counts. `TW_RETIME=full` redoes every cluster whenever one moved (master's behaviour), to time both in one binary. `edit_fuzz` prints per-op retime figures with `TW_RETIME_STATS=1` and the totals at the end.
