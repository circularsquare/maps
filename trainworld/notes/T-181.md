## 3. Borders outside the US (documentation)

SPEC section 4 and docs/demand.md 4.4 item 1: national borders get a factor, weaker inside the
Schengen area than across other national borders; first-level regions get factor 1 by default;
values fitted when the first such pack is built. The US states are the exception because LODES
measured their effect (T-159, T-162). A pack spanning Schengen and non-Schengen borders would need
a factor per region pair, which the pack format does not have yet (one `border_factor` a pack).
