# T-3893: t671-ghost-hover "near track ends" (21 of 84)

Agent checkfix, 2026-11-20. Nothing fixed: the game is the thing that is off, and the fix is more than small.

## Cause

At a track end the ghost's first frame comes from the page's layout guess (`map/stationSnap.ts` `layoutIssues`,
which joins pieces through plain joints with its own `joinThrough`/`same`), and the worker then confirms. For
12 new track ends the worker's answer on a platform of 200 m (edges are ~154 m, so the platform must be joined
through the next piece):

- chains whose three pieces are all clear: worker accepts, guess agrees;
- chains whose middle piece has a flat crossing with existing track (4 chains seen across 3 runs, each with one
  crossing on the middle piece at 26 m or 127 m): the worker refuses both ends with PlatformJoint (and
  PlatformTooLong at the end node), the guess says fine. The ghost shows green, then red.

All 21 corrections are ok -> bad colour changes on those chains (ends 1756/1759/1772 in the check's run), and
the "first candidate was not the worker's spot" count of 46 is the same thing.

Why the worker will not join: `unjoin_near` (sim/src/track/wasm/edits.rs) only joins pieces with equal `ramp`
(T-3027) and `limit` (T-3405), among others. A piece that crossed existing track flat went through every ramp
try, and "the last try's answer stands", so its stored ramp differs from its neighbours'. Strongly supported
(perfect correlation with a flat crossing, ramp is the only join condition the page cannot see) but not shown
directly: the world's edges carry no ramp.

So this is the T-702 "refusals the page cannot see" case, made common by T-3027 (the check passed with 0-1 of
112-336 end hovers on 10-19, before pieces carried a ramp). On 8800 a player sees the ghost turn red a moment
after it appeared when hovering a loose end of blueprint whose neighbouring piece flat-crosses other track.

## Fix options (not done)

1. Pass each edge's `ramp` (and `limit`, same join rule) to the page like `edge_limits` (Rust `TrackApi` getter,
   WASM rebuild, `stateRows.ts`, `protocol.ts`, `clockClient.ts`) and compare them in `same()` in `stationSnap.ts`.
   Player-visible (less flicker), about 1-2 hours.
2. Page-only guess: treat a piece with a flat crossing as not joinable. Cheaper, but a guess on top of a guess.
3. Leave the game, loosen the check (count only wrong spots, not green-to-red). Hides a real flicker.

Probe used: scratch copy of the check's first stage printing the worker's checkStation on each new end plus
`crossingsOn` per piece (scratchpad checkfix/probe.mjs).

## Done (same day)

Option 1. `TrackApi::edge_ramps` (sim/src/track/wasm/limits.rs) -> `stateRows.ts` -> `ClockEdge.ramp` -> world edge `ramp`;
`stationSnap.ts` `same()` now also compares limit, ramp and kind, as `unjoin_near` does. The ramp hypothesis held:
t671-ghost-hover near track ends went 21 of 84 -> 0 of 84 (overlaps 0 of 40). agent_cargo test 698 passed,
typecheck clean, t3134-single-ghost passes. Changelog item checkfix-1 (track, fix).
