# T-3657: free fares inflate leaderboard points

Agent profitpts, 2026-11-18. Tree `.tw-trees/profitpts`, Vite 6182, play.mjs game, 3 cores. Scratch: the session
scratchpad's `profitpts/` folder (`probe.js` and `probe-page.js` the probe, `runall.mjs` the runner, `summ.py` the
table, `out-*.json` one per network, `ff-nyc-*.json` the free-fare runs, `shares/` the downloaded shares).

Anita's rule, 2026-11-18: each line's points scale with its fare income over its running cost, capped at 1 ("if fares
cover 50% of expenses they should count as 50% points"); a trip's points split over its legs, each leg taking its line's
factor.

## Built

`game/points.ts`: `lineCover`, each running line's cover by line number: weekday fare income (`incomeByDay[0]`) over
the day's running cost at the trains and cars its riders were solved with (`LineDemand.tph`/`cars`, else the line's),
oneway lines one way, times `lineRunFactor`; at most 1, 1 with no running cost; a twin and its line share the pair's.
`linePoints` is `fullPoints` (the old figure) times the cover, so the clock worker's ledgers, `pointsPerDay`,
`pointsScore`, the Cities tab, the leaderboard, Community and the board send all take it. The weekday cover scales every
kind of day (the score is the weekday's; the ledgers' other days take the same).

Two holes closed on the way: `pointsScore` fell back to the past 24 hours when no region had points above 0, so a
network set all free showed (and kept) its old score for a game day; it now falls back only when no solve has
placed any region. And `figures()` (account.ts) never sent a 0 for a running network, so an all-free game would have
kept its old board figure; it now sends 0 when the riders earn points before cover (`pointsBeforeCover`). Checked on
NYC all free: score 0 shown while the 24-hour ledger still held 447,262,408; before the change, the clock run a game day
had the ledger's 24 hours at 447,262,408, the covered weekday figure exactly.

A trains and cars edit (Caracas: line 0's headway tripled, line 1 two cars more), after its final: the probe reading
the world as it is now gives the clock's running cost to the dollar (4,160,375) and the game's cover on every line, and
the game's score is the probe's (11,227,009). The 0 send was checked by reading `figures()`, not by a recorded PUT.

Checks: `npm run check`; smoke PASSED (29 checks, against Vite 6182); t3465-forecast-score 4 PASS; i18n 2 stale keys a
language (these two).

Left alone: achievements and goals (none reads points). Scores already on the boards stay until each game next sends
a whole final, so a game never opened again keeps its old figure, and a tab still on the old build sends old-rule
points; shares keep the points they were shared with.

Strings: `cities.pointsRule` and `leaderboard.pointsRule` gain a second line, "A line whose fares cover half its
running cost gets half its points". Docs: SPEC 6 points, `docs/track-and-service.md` "Points", wiki points-and-goals.
