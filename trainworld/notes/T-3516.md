# T-3516 Snap beside button off the phone bar

Tree: .tw-trees/snapbar (branch tw/snapbar), one CSS change in app/src/ui/style.css, plus changelog c136 (track, fix).

## Cause
Under 600 px wide the track bar is one row with `overflow-x: auto`. Its buttons add up to about 502 px (level, type, three track counts, ramp, four snap buttons, target speed) against 330 px inside the bar at 360. Before: Snap to roads (x 342-378), Snap beside (381-417) and the speed button (421-457) were past the bar's right edge (351), and `elementFromPoint` hit something else (the info button / the map) there.

## Change
In the phone block, under 540 px: the track bar wraps, and the snap buttons (`.build-toggles`) take a full row of their own, order 5, under level / type / counts / ramp / speed. The tool notes and the level/type/speed popups sit 40 px higher (`#main:has(.track-bar)`) to clear the taller bar. Buttons stay 36 x 36 like the rest. Station bar and others untouched. Desktop untouched (the rule only exists under 540 px).

## Measured (headless Chrome, touch, nyc sandbox; each button's centre hit-tested)
Before at 360 regular: 3 buttons past the edge. After, regular and tram, at 360, 390 and 414: no button outside the bar, none covered, all at least 36 x 36, bar scrollWidth equals clientWidth (no sideways scroll). Bar is 85 px tall instead of 45.

Screenshots: artifacts/trainworld/review/t3516.png (before | after, 360), t3516_desktop.png (desktop after; a pixel diff against before showed only the clock and animations since the rule cannot apply at 1400 px).

Typecheck clean; smoke on port 6188 (result in the report).

Not changed: 540 px was picked because the row needs about 514 px; between 541 and 600 the one row fits.
