# T-3052 Rebuild built track with new properties (design)

Anita, 2026-11-10: select built track, press an "edit" button, place blueprints that demolish the line and rebuild it
at another height or track type; on construct it rebuilds at once, stations too, and the same lines run over it.
Cause: a player's "changing height of track after its already built is really annoying".

## Recommendation in one paragraph

Do not demolish and recreate. Change the built pieces **in place**: the same edge and node ids, so stations (name,
platform, upgrades), lines (stops and path) and everything keyed by id stay without any rebuilding code. One edit
changes the level (and the node levels) and the kind of the picked built pieces; the track model checks it as it
checks any edit, and it is charged the way a construct is. The Select tool's bar gets the level and Regular / High speed
buttons for a built pick, as choices, plus a **Rebuild** button that shows the price and asks before it spends.
A drawn preview of the new shape is a second stage.

## 1. How it fits what exists

- Select tool: a pick is blueprint or built, never both (ui-building.md "The Select tool"). A built pick today gets only
  Remove and Clear; level, Regular / High speed, Disable and Double up are blueprint-only (`s.built` in
  `ui/Blueprint.tsx` `BlueprintBar`; `levelPicked`, `hsrPicked` in `game/blueprintPick.ts` return on `p.built`).
- Rust: `TrackApi::set_edges_level` and `set_edges_hsr` (`sim/src/track/wasm/links.rs`) already do the whole job for
  blueprint (corners, node levels, ramps to untouched neighbours, no new flat crossing, grouped trial). They skip
  built pieces by a plain `built` test, and `movable` refuses a built platform node. That is the only thing between
  us and a built edit.
- **A blueprint cannot sit over built track.** Two alignments on top of each other are `CrossKind::Overlap`, refused
  (`net.rs`). So "ordinary blueprints plus a list of built pieces to remove" would need the removal to happen first,
  which is exactly the demolish we want to avoid (new ids, lines broken, stations gone). A new kind of blueprint
  (a pending spec stored on the built edge, drawn as blueprint, applied on Construct) works but touches the save,
  both renderers, the quote and Disable. Not worth it for a two-property change.
- Money and undo: `apply_paid` charges `max(0, built_after - built_before)` and clears undo for a final edit
  (`world.rs`). An in-place edit gets a correct price with no new pricing code.

## 2. What the player can change

- **Level** (-3 to +3): yes, stage 1. Same rules as the blueprint button: corners and nodes whose track is all picked
  move, a node with unpicked track keeps its level and the picked piece ramps to it (200 m a level), refusals leave
  that piece as it was and are counted ("9 of 12 pieces ...").
- **Track type**: Regular and High speed, double track only (as `set_edges_hsr`). Curvature: nothing is refused and
  nothing is redrawn. A kind changes the speed cap (160 / 320) and the price (1.5x); a curve's speed is the lowest of
  its radius, the cap and the train (track-and-service.md "Curve speed"), so a bendy line converted to High speed
  stays as slow as its curves. The quote's hover can say so ("Curves still limit speed").
- Left out on purpose: single / double / quad (changes the number of tracks, ports and platforms), rail vs tram
  (families do not join), station length. Double up already exists for the express pair.
- Cheap extra, stage 2: a station alone (picked with no track) changes level with its own track. See 3.

## 3. Stations

In the pick, a station moves with the track: its node, both platform edges and corners go to the new level in the same
batch, so the id, name, platform length, upgrades, passages and buildings all stay (nothing is deleted).
Change in `movable`: a built platform node is movable when its station is in the pick. A station not in the pick keeps
its level and the track beside it ramps to it, which the platform rules (`PlatformNotLevel`, ramps outside the
reach) accept or refuse. Station price follows its level (75 at grade, 125 at -1), so it is part of the charge.
A box pick takes the stations inside it, so a run through stations works as one drag.

## 4. Lines and trains

- Lines are lists of stops and a path of edge ids; ids do not change, so every line keeps its stops and runs on.
  Nothing is "rebuilt for" a line. This also answers the broken-junction worry: a junction with unedited track is a
  node whose level stays, the edited edge ramps to it, and if there is no room the model refuses that piece and the
  message counts it. A line never ends up disconnected by this edit. (Demolish and rebuild would have.)
- Trains are timetable-driven, not bodies on the track (`profile::state_at`): an edit takes effect at `edit_at`,
  `note_changes` recomputes run times (a ramp adds length, a kind changes the cap), trains already running keep their
  places. No special handling. Any extra cars a longer round trip needs are bought as for any edit (`trains_to_buy`).

## 5. Money

- Code gives for free: pay the **increase** in built value (new price minus old, floored at 0). Going cheaper costs
  nothing and refunds nothing. Same prices as everywhere: levels, terrain, water, road crossings, stations, kind 1.5x.
- Strict reading of "demolish and rebuild": the full new price, no refund of the old. A small override in the
  quote function (charge = built value of the changed pieces after). **Question for Anita**: net difference
  (recommended: the complaint is annoyance, not a wish to charge twice) or full price.
- The player sees, before spending: the Rebuild button's hover (length, price) and a confirm (price, pieces,
  stations) that also names the lines that change if any (`lineEffects` exists). A short wallet gives the existing
  money-short notice. Construct waits for terrain, water and road tiles and is refused when they did not load
  (T-2613); Rebuild must do the same.

## 6. Undo and saves

- Undo: final, clears history, like Construct. Reason: it spends money, and undo that restores old track free while
  spending nothing back is easy to misread. The confirm is the safeguard. (Undo would not allow free building: it
  only ever restores what was there. It can be added later.)
- Saves: no change in stages 1 and 2. Only existing fields (`level` on pis and nodes, `kind` on edges) change.

  https://code.theleruby.com/cpp/openttd-patchpack/source/raw/b5a029db465b0a75d575d71060ac6ce2b34ab7fb/src/rail.h

- Transport Fever 2: the track modification tool upgrades or electrifies in place (player reports); modular station
  parts must be bulldozed and rebuilt; mods exist for bulk upgrades because the stock tool is per piece.

  https://www.train-fever.com/forums/topic/upgrading-track-to-high-speed . Not checked against official docs.

## As built (T-3059, 2026-11-10)

Anita's answers (2026-11-10) override the above: **full new price** (no "pay the increase"), the first cut is the Select
bar with the level and Regular / High speed choices, a Rebuild button whose hover shows the price, and a confirm; the drawn
preview stays T-3060; **final, no undo**. New: pieces the rebuild would refuse are drawn differently on the map before the
player confirms, and the hover and confirm count them.

- **Rust**: `sim/src/track/wasm/rebuild.rs`. `plan_edges(ids, level, hsr, built_ok, picked_stations)` is the old
  `set_edges_level` body generalised (`set_edges_level` is now a thin caller; blueprint behaviour unchanged): level and
  type together, built track allowed, and a built station node is movable when its station is in the pick. It returns
  the ops, the edges it would refuse (not a count) and the last issues. Trials use the new `TrackWorld::trial_light`
  (no services, so a pick over lines is cheap). `rebuild_quote(edges, stations, level, kind)` returns
  `[price US$M, changed edges, charged stations, refused n, ...refused ids, ...changed ids]`; `rebuild_pieces` applies
  it as one final edit through `TrackWorld::apply_priced`, which sets the charge to the **full price of the changed
  edges and of the picked stations whose level or type changed** (`Network::station_build_price`), plus the trains the
  lines need, as any edit. Level 99 (outside -3..3) and kind 0 keep that property. Blueprint ids in the pick are ignored.
  An edge with no corners has no level of its own; its nodes' levels are its profile, so the edges at a moved node count
  as changed. Tests: `wasm/rebuild_tests.rs` (7).
- **Not charged**: junctions and flat crossings at the changed nodes, station upgrades. A quote shows no trains the
  longer round trips may need; the edit pays them and a short purse is refused with the usual notice.
- **App**: `game/rebuild.ts` (choices `rebuildLevel` and `rebuildKind`, the quote signal, hover, confirm, `rebuildPicked`),
  `ui/Blueprint.tsx` (built pick: the level buttons and Regular / High speed choose, **Rebuild** spends),
  `workers/` op `rebuild` and query `rebuildQuote` (the op waits for terrain like Construct), `map/renderer.ts`
  `setRefused` and `map/blueprintBox.ts` (the refused pieces get a red band over the pick's, `shades.fullTrack`),
  `game/blueprintPick.ts` (`canBeHsr` no longer excludes built track).
- **Strings**: `blueprint.rebuild`, `rebuild.*` in en and pass 1 of the other ten languages (not reviewed).
- **Check**: `checks/t3059-rebuild.mjs`. Docs: ui-building.md "Rebuild", track-and-service.md 6.4, SPEC, code-map,
  wiki levels-and-tunnels.
- **Deliberately not done**: the "Curves still limit speed" line on High speed's hover (needs the picked pieces' curve
  limits; `TrackEdge.vminKmh` has them if wanted); a built click in plain Select still makes no pick (box or blueprint
  tool picks built track).
- **T-3051 hint**: `rebuildPicked` counts a successful level change through `noteHeight`, so single-piece rebuilds count.

## As built, round 2 (T-3087, T-3088, 2026-11-10)

**T-3087, the cause.** Anita rebuilt a Diemen-Weesp run (pieces at 0 with bridges at +1) to -1: a bunch of pieces
were refused, pieces over water stayed at 0/+1 and land pieces went to -1 with ramps at every joint. The planner
tried the whole pick as one batch, and on any refusal fell back to groups of pieces joined through moving nodes,
which for a run is one group: the one bad piece (for her probably a ramp with no room by the station or junction
at the pick's end) failed the whole group, so **every** piece fell to the "alone, ends kept" pass. That left each
inner node at its old level, so every piece ramped down from its joint, and the ramps of pieces whose joint was by
the water passed through ground level over it (`GroundOverWater`) and were refused, keeping their old 0 and +1.
Reproduced by `one_refused_piece_does_not_leave_the_rest_ramping_at_every_node` (a run drawn a km at a time over
four waters, a constructed tunnel crossing one piece: 7 of 11 refused before, 1 after).

**The fix** (`wasm/rebuild.rs` `plan_edges`, shared with blueprint `set_edges_level`, so the blueprint level buttons
are fixed too): try the whole pick; on a refusal, `culprits` maps the problems to picked pieces (the piece an issue
names, the picked pieces at a node it names, the picked pieces beside an unpicked piece it names, the later of two
picked pieces that cross, either side of a new flat crossing), drops them from the pick and tries again, so the rest
still moves whole and ramps only to them. Problems that name no piece fall back to the old group-by-group pass. The
dropped pieces with corners are then tried alone with their ends kept, as before.

**T-3088, rail and tram.** `rebuild_quote` / `rebuild_pieces` kind 3 = tram (kind 3 only; street tram is not offered,
since its shape follows streets and is drawn by the street snap). Decisions (DECISIONS.md 2026-11-10):
- **Families never join**, so a change of family takes whole stretches. `prune` drops a piece that would meet the
  other family at a node, repeatedly; one whose clash is with track outside the pick (or a crossover, single track,
  anything left in place) is refused for its own sake (`Plan::root`), one that only meets a refused piece is not.
  The quote's header has a fifth number, the root count; the root ids come first. Only roots are drawn red, so a
  partial pick shows red where it meets rail, and one tight curve shows red on the curve, not over the whole network.
  When nothing changes and the planner has no issue of its own, the issue is `TrackFamily` at the first clash.
- **Stations are kept and fitted, not refused**: a station whose track changes family gets its platform clamped
  into the new kind's range (rail to tram: cut to 80 m; tram to rail: out to 60 m, which may hit the platform
  rules and be refused). A train's cars already follow the shortest platform (`build_line`), so a tram line from
  rail runs 4 sections. Every station on track that changes family is charged (rebuilt as the other kind), picked
  or not.
- **Lines follow the track**: no refusal needed for mixed lines, since a line's path is connected and families are
  whole along it. `family_ops` clears `fast` on lines becoming tram (a fast tram line is refused by `set_line`) and
  swaps dwell and turnaround to the new kind's defaults where they were the old kind's (30/180 to 15/120 and back);
  a player's own settings are kept. Trams are bought as for any edit (`trains_to_buy`, the tram fleet); the rail
  cars the line had stay in the fleet.
- **Curves**: tram to rail with a curve under rail's minimum radius is refused on that piece (`RadiusTooSmall`), and
  the rest of its stretch with it (it cannot become rail alone).
- Crossovers have no tram form, so rail with crossovers cannot become tram until they are removed (refused red).
- **App**: `pickedState.tram` counts tram pieces; a built pick's type group has Regular, High speed and **Tram**
  (`toolBar.tram`); `rebuildTo` says "tram track" (`blueprintPick.toTram`, new). Rebuild is no longer greyed when
  nothing can change: it then asks nothing and the edit's refusal says why.
- **Checks**: Rust `rebuild_tests.rs` (13 now: the T-3087 run with bridges, the tunnel-crossing case, a station left
  out, rail to tram and back with a station and a line, a partial pick, a tram curve too tight for trains);
  `checks/t3059-rebuild.mjs` adds the tram rebuild through the bar and back, and a run across the East River and
  Roosevelt Island drawn at 0 with a bridge at +1 rebuilt to -1 (all pieces at -1, none at 0).
