# T-3368: track and lines "disappear" when zoomed in (player Elliot, mobile)

Feedback 1794509588-9140c806, 2026-11-12, Chrome 156, build mwduazen. Text only, no screenshot. Investigation, no app edits.

## What was tried (zoomgone, own Vite on 6151)

Playwright Chrome (ANGLE d3d11), desktop 1400x900 and phone emulation (390x844, deviceScaleFactor 3, touch, isMobile).
Map max zoom is 22 (MapLibre default; mapView.ts sets none).

- New game, 3-station straight line, zoom 13 to 22 over the track: track and line stay drawn at every zoom on both. Desktop and mobile alike.
- New game, 5-station bent line, jumps to real edge vertices (edgePts), zoom 16-22, mobile: present everywhere.
- Real save (T-007-real-nyc.save), 8 real edge vertices, zoom 16-22, mobile: present everywhere.
- Unbuilt blueprint track (track tool open), mobile, zoom 15-22: present.
- Pixel counts of the line colour per shot agree with the eyeballed shots. One apparent miss (a spot on leg 2) was my assumption that the route is straight; the routed track was elsewhere.

## What does happen

Track is drawn true to scale from about zoom 19 (laneState in renderer.ts: 4 m between double-track centres grows past LANE_GROW_PX, then the strokes are metres wide). On a 390 px phone:
- zoom 19: line band about 40 css px.
- zoom 20: about 100 px a track.
- zoom 21-22: one stroke wider than the screen. At zoom 22 the view is solid line colour (new game: all green; real save: stripes of teal and pink). Nothing on screen reads as track any more.

So "disappears" is most likely this: past about zoom 20 the track is a screen-filling block, and a player placing track by eye loses the shape. No culling, no maxzoom on a layer, no precision loss was found (positions are anchor-relative, strokes are highp, see T-953 comment in rendererShaders.ts).

## Not reproducible here

A real phone GPU (Mali/Adreno/Apple) may differ from desktop ANGLE: fp16 mediump in some fragment path, or the DPR cap (cappedPixelRatio) on an 8 GB phone. Elliot's phone model is not in the report. If the wall explanation is wrong, the next step is a reply asking his phone and what zoom (not contacting him from here).

## Proposed fix (if wanted)

Cap the zoom where track stops growing, or cap the stroke width:
1. Cap `maxZoom` of the map near 20 on touch devices (mapView.ts, one line; hours: 0.2). Loses close-in detail on stations.
2. Better: clamp the true-to-scale stroke width in px (max about 60-80 css px per track) in renderer.ts where u_width and laneState are computed, so track and station boxes keep a readable size at zoom 21-22. Touches renderer.ts laneState, u_width uniform, and the station box scale (stationGeometry.ts uses the same px per metre): about 2-4 hours, because stations and trains are also drawn in metres and would need the same clamp.
Collision: option 2 touches stationGeometry.ts and the stroke path of renderer.ts (in-flight). Option 1 touches only mapView.ts, no collision.
Recommendation: ask Elliot's phone and zoom (or accept option 1) before option 2.
