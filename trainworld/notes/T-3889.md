# T-3889: tram track joins regular and high-speed track

Agent tramrail, 2026-11-20. Brief's decisions (Anita 11-20): tram track may meet rail at a node; trams may run on rail
capped at 80 km/h; train lines never path onto tram track; a line with a tram stop is a tram line.

## What changed

**The track model** (`sim/src/track/net/`)
- `Issue::TrackFamily` is gone: `check_node` no longer refuses a node where tram and rail meet.
- `Network::node_kind` at a mixed node is its first rail edge's kind: a junction there holds rail's 120 s conflict.
- No station where tram and rail meet (`Issue::StationFamily` in `check_node`): otherwise drawing rail on from a
  tram stop at a track end, or deleting one side, would silently flip the station's kind and with it a line's kind,
  and nothing would re-check the line (advisor's catch). `tram_stop(n)`: a station on tram track.
- Junction price: `cost::junction_cost(level, flying, wet)` takes no kind, so a tram/rail junction is priced as any
  junction. Nothing changed there (DECISIONS 2026-11-20).

**Line kind and routing**
- `line_tram(l)` = any stop is a tram stop (`stops_tram`). Before: the first stop's track.
- Routing: `find_path_side(.., tram)` skips tram edges unless `tram`. Every entry point takes the flag from the whole
  line's stops: `route_hops`/`route`/`route_hops_plain`/`route_legs` compute it from the stops they are given;
  `*_t` variants take it; `take_legs`, `loop_back` take it; `back_run_of` uses the stops or a path already on tram
  track (a part-built stretch). `HopMemo` (wasm.rs) is now a struct whose key carries the flag, and whose `tram`
  floor carries a line's kind into routes of part of its stops (`sort_stops` builds from an empty list, legs.rs
  routes a skeleton, `turns` routes a subset); set at creation (`HopMemo::of`) or by `turns`, `unturn_branch`,
  `legs::{flatten, ordered, insert, sorted}`; `legs::insert` puts the floor back on exit (`stop_tiers` shares one memo over every station tried). `straighten_legs` now takes a memo per line.
- The brief's "or its route uses tram track" adds no case: a line without a tram stop never routes onto tram track.
- `check_route`: a path on tram track with no tram stop is `Issue::TramTrack { line }`, so a train line can't be
  left on tram track by any edit (dropping the only tram stop is refused; a rebuild under it is refused).
- `find_path` (no line) allows tram track for a hop to or from a tram stop (tests, bench only).
- Speed: `line_top` returns 80 km/h (`TRAM_V_CAP_KMH`, new const in params.rs) for a tram line, so trams run at 80
  on rail and high-speed track; slower track (street tram 60) and limits still apply. service.rs and profile.rs untouched.
- Kind flip by a stop edit (`Network::fit_kind`, called from `TrackApi::run` for an `Op::Line`, so not on undo): adding a line's first tram stop or taking its last turns
  `fast` off (to tram), swaps dwell/turnaround where they were the old kind's defaults (30/180 <-> 15/120), and
  cars over 4 become 2. Undo puts the old data back untouched.
  `set_line` refuses `fast` with any tram stop (was: first stop).

**Refusals** (`wasm/lines.rs`)
- `no_route`: a train line's stop edit that has no route and whose stops are joined only through tram track is
  refused with `TramTrack`; used by `set_line_with_trains`, `set_stops`, `order_stops`.
- `add_stop`, `one_way_stop`: the "nowhere to put it" return 2 becomes a `TramTrack` refusal when
  `train_kept_off` (train line, rail station, joined only over tram track).
- `order_fault` leaves that case to `order_stops`' refusal.
- `stop_tiers`: tier 9 for such a station; the app (`stopMarkers.ts`, StopTier "tram") draws it as "cut" and its
  hover and click show the TramTrack text.
- New `TrackApi::stops_tram(stops)` for the app's `addLine` (tram defaults when any stop is a tram stop).

**Kind change** (`wasm/rebuild.rs`, both rebuild of built track and `set_edges_kind` for blueprint)
- No more whole-stretch rule: `prune` lost its family part, `beyond` its `families` gate (level changes across a
  family change ramp beyond stations as any).
- A plan that changes family is still tried whole with `family_ops`; blamed pieces are refused and the rest tried
  again; no group-by-group or solo fallback for a family change (those trials would leave out the station and line
  ops). `culprits` blames `TramTrack` on the picked pieces under that line, `StationFamily` on those at the station.
- `family_ops`: a station's platform is clamped by its kind after the change (`tram_after`: all its track tram);
  each line on changed track gets an `Op::Line` (so the edit validates it), its kind before (`line_tram`) and after
  (its stops after the change) deciding fast and dwell/turnaround.

**App**
- `map/tools.ts`: the family snap filter, `familyClash` and its red tag are gone: with either kind picked, all track
  and stations are snap targets. Snap beside still keeps to the picked family (it lays parallel track, not a join).
- `game/issues.ts`: `TrackFamily` -> `TramTrack`.
- `workers/clock.worker.ts` (hourturn's file; two one-line edits): addLine's tram test is `a.stops_tram(stops)`,
  copyLine's kind is `a.line_tram(line)`.
- Strings: `issues.trackFamily`, `tools.noTramToTrain`, `tools.noTrainToTram` removed from en.json, every
  messages/ and sources/ file and context.json (checks/i18n.mjs faults keys missing from en.json); `issues.tramTrack` added.

## Left as is, possible follow-ups (no ids)

- Crossovers between tram and rail track beside each other are still refused (`crossover_op` keeps to a family).
- Snap beside keeps to the picked family.
- Route choice for a tram line is by `edge_time`, which is line-blind: a tram line prefers rail track as if it ran
  at 160, though it runs at 80. Harmless where the tram track is the only way.
- Demand: `tram_stops` marks a rail station served only by tram lines as a tram stop (no boarding cost) - a rail
  station only tram-trains stop at. Left so.
- A line made by the Lines tab's New line button starts with no stops, so it gets train defaults until its first
  tram stop flips it (now with tram dwell, turnaround and 2 cars by the set_line rule).
