# T-909: trips of about one person, grouped into chunks of at least about 10 (2026-10-22)

Anita: "its quite hard to see the trips that are like 1 person. would it be possible to just group
all trips into at least 10 people? like all origin-destination pairs have at least 10 people? it
would shift people around but is there a way to do it equitably?"

Built: option 2 (display only, 10 trips as the hover counts, every level), `game/farChunks.ts`,
used by `map/commuterView.ts` and the explorer in `map/tripView.ts`; option 1 at 1 a day filed as
T-921. On the real save, a Brooklyn home cell at zoom 11.6: 5,325 far bubbles (5,126 under one
trip) became 119, the smallest 10.06, the total 5,307.5 both ways (`artifacts/trainworld/od10/`
far-before.png, far-after.png).

## What she sees, and at what level the pairs are

- **Where "1 person" shows**: a bubble selection's far-end bubbles (sized among themselves, the
  biggest a hexagon's area, so a 1-trip bubble is a speck beside a 50-trip one), their lines to the
  selection (faded by drawn size), their hovers ("0 trips a day to here") and the trip explorer
  opened from one (its count is the far bubble's, T-586).
- **The far end is not the model's pairs.** `DemandApi::flows_day` builds it from the gravity's
  2 km zone trips from the selection, spread over every cell of each far zone by its jobs (or
  people), with rail from the far subzones; the smallest far cells holding the last 1% are cut.
  The model's commute pairs are 2 km zone pairs (subzone pairs inside), per period; T-874 deals
  those under 0.1 trips a period. Nothing a player sees is a zone pair: what shows is the
  selection's cells against every far cell, summed into the bubble level on screen (res 9 to 6).
- **How small**: on the real New York network (commutes only; everyday purposes add to it), 300
  single res-9 home cells chosen by trips: a cell sends a median 696 trip legs a day to a far end
  of 19,886 cells; at res 9 every one of its far bubbles holds under 1 leg (median 0.01), at res 7
  (zoomed out, 5 km² bubbles) 93% hold under 1 and all under 10. A res-7 area selected (about 49
  cells, a median 14,600 legs): 87% of its res-9 far bubbles hold under 1 leg, 99% under 10.

So making every model pair carry 10 people would not change what she sees: a selected cell is
about a fortieth of its 2 km zone and a far zone is about 40 cells, so a 10-person zone pair still
shows as 40 specks of a few hundredths of a trip. Only grouping at the far end itself does.

## Option 1: deal the model's commute zone pairs in chunks of C people a day

T-874's dealing with the chunk in people a day (`c = C x period share`, so every period deals the
same pairs), 16 km blocks. Native `calib`, the real New York network (`nyc_real.json`), a weekday
with three crowding rounds, against every pair solved; two runs at a time on a shared machine, so
times are rough. Home cells: commute rail by home cell.

| chunk | trips dealt | zone pairs (AM) | solve | station worst, >=1000 / >=100 / >=10 entries | segment worst (>=1000) | home cell rail worst (>=100 rail) | home cell share worst (>=1000 legs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| every pair | | 4.25M | 43.9 s | | | | |
| today (0.1 a period) | 0.8% | 1.80M | 34.3 s | 0.02% / 0.08% / 0.4% | 4.1% | 0.03% | 0.00 points |
| 1 a day | 4.3% | 0.99M | 29.6 s | 0.26% / 0.76% / 1.3% | 1.7% | 0.26% | 0.04 points |
| 3 a day | 9.4% | 0.67M | 26.6 s | 0.70% / 2.0% / 4.9% | 1.4% | 1.95% | 0.21 points |
| 10 a day | 19.0% | 0.43M | 21.8 s | 1.99% / 5.5% / 16% | 2.1% | 11.8% | 0.99 points |

Commute rail in total moves under 0.01% at every chunk (the deal keeps each origin zone's trips
with each block). The segment column is the crowding rounds' noise (T-874: 1-5% at any setting).

Countryside, Romania's `aad`, two made-up lines through Hunedoara county towns (Brad, Deva, Simeria,
Orastie, Hunedoara; Petrosani, Vulcan, Lupeni; 2 trains an hour at the peak): 535 commuters ride a
day, stations 44-155 entries. At 10 a day the 155-entry station moves 1.5%, a 44-entry one 5%;
3 a day 0.5% and 1.5%. The solve is about a millisecond a period at any chunk.

Ceiling tests (T-636's harness with its zone pairs dealt the same way; the harness predates T-854,
so only the change counts): commute rail of rail + car moves under 0.001 points at every chunk in
New York and London, A and B (New York A 93.2031% every pair, 93.2031% at 10 a day; London A
88.7531%, 88.7539%). The deal moves trips between zone pairs of the same origin at the same block,
which are at about the same distance, so the ceilings cannot tell. Both pass as before.

Reading: at 10 a day the effect is not small (stations of 100-1,000 entries up to 5.5%, a home
cell's rail up to 12%), and it would not make the far end readable. At 1 a day it is small
(stations under 0.8%) and takes another 14% off the solve beyond today's; at 3 a day another 22%.
That is a performance option, not an answer to her question.

## Option 2: group the far end for the display only

The selection's far end (every purpose, after the workers' answers are merged) dealt before it is
summed into bubbles: far cells holding under C trips a day (as the hover counts) are walked along a
Hilbert curve over the city's cells and dealt their total in draws of `total / floor(total / C)`
(so each at least C), systematic from a fixed start; each drawn cell carries the trips, by mode
and purpose, since the draw before it, the first also those after the last. Cells of C or more
keep their own trips. Totals are exact by mode and purpose; a drawn cell is chosen with
probability proportional to its trips; the curve keeps each stretch's trips within a step of where
they were. Demand, stations and every figure outside the selection's far end are untouched.

Measured on the same 300 cells and 300 res-7 areas (Python on the far ends `flows` returns):

| | today: res-9 far bubbles (under 10 trips) | C = 10: bubbles, smallest | trips moved, median / 90% |
|---|---|---|---|
| one res-9 cell (696 trips) | 19,412 (all) | 68, 10.0 | 2.8 km / 13 km |
| a res-7 area (14,600 trips) | 19,340 (19,168) | 1,131, 10.0 | 0.7 km / 3.1 km |

At res 7 (zoomed out) the single cell's far end is 54 bubbles of about 10 instead of 1,920 specks.
Every far bubble at every level holds at least 10 trips. The cost is a sort of the far cells
(up to about 80k) per selection, in the main thread or the bubble worker.

What changes for a player: a selected bubble's far end shows where its people go as groups of 10:
a cell sending 3 people to a suburb shows either nothing there or 10 people a few km off. The trip
explorer opened from a far bubble counts the bubble's (dealt) trips, so its count and the hover
agree (T-586); its routes and mode split are the pair's own. The hover over a far bubble states
trips the model spread more thinly.
