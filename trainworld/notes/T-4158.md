## Pick

**Radius 500 m, same lines (option a500).** It takes about 29% off Detroit's solve and 18% off Belgrade's (peak
memory 32% and 17% less), the median line moves 1.5-2% and 94% of lines stay within 10%, and it never makes a free
change between two lines' stops, so T-4079's bus changes are untouched. Its cost: rides of a stop or two inside a
group are gone, which shows on short lines with close stops and on Auto lines (their stops sit 400 m apart, so any
radius of 400 m or more pairs them up): the 30 Auto test lines lose 1.2% of riders, about a third of what T-3924 gained.

## The switches (in the tree, nothing changes until a default does)

`Params` (kernel.rs), read into `busmerge::Rule` for the populations and the market alike:

- `bus_merge_m` (300 today): every two places of a group within this.
- `bus_merge_share` (1 today): a place joins a run when it shares at least this part of its lines with every place
  already in it (shared lines over all lines of either). 1 is today's same-lines rule; 0.5 lets a place with one line
  more or less join. 0.5, 0.34 and 0 give the same groups within 1% (nearly every mismatch is `{A}` beside `{A, B}`),
  so there is no useful middle setting.
- `bus_merge_corridor` (0 today): a place a line stops at between two places of one group joins it (lines along one
  street that stop at different places), so the line visits the group once instead of leaving and coming back.

Tests: `busmerge/tests.rs` `runs_with_most_lines_shared`, `corridor_takes_a_stop_between`.
**Landing a500**: `MERGE_M` = 500 (kernel default follows it); drop `bus_merge_corridor` (it does nothing measurable)
and `bus_merge_share` unless she picks it; docs `demand-kernel.md` (the merge, "within 300 m"), `demand-plain.md`
("under 300 m apart"), DECISIONS "Buses" (replace the T-4094 entry); changelog (performance) once measured in game.

## What each option gives up, and the shape that shows it worst

- **What the bus fixes were for.** T-3924 made every drawn stop a demand stop ("if people are seeing bus stops the bus
  stops have to be real"), which raised a 3.7 km line's riders 26% and the 30 test lines' 3.6%; T-4079 kept bus
  changes on foot to 150 m so dense networks stop looking like a web. Every option keeps each stop's own walk-in area
  and shows every figure on the real stops; what goes is a ride between two stops of one group and the exact ride
  time across it (riders board mid-group).
- **Wider radius (a)**: more short rides vanish and the mid-group lie grows to 250 m a side. Worst shape: short lines
  with close stops where most rides are a stop or three (Belgrade 66, 3.3 km with 16 stops: -29% at 500 m; 44 and 34
  from Topčidersko brdo -22 to -26%), and every Auto line once the radius reaches 400 m.
- **Share (b)**: a crossing or joining line's stop is folded into another line's group, so changing between them is
  free up to the radius, which T-4079 allows only to 150 m. Worst shape: a trunk where branches join and leave, or a
  bus grid with crossings every few hundred metres (Belgrade 704 Zemun polje - Zeleni venac +34% and 84 Nova Galenika
  -29% at b + c 500, both through Zeleni venac).
- **Corridor (c)**: almost nothing to merge in either save. Worst shape: lines along one street that alternate stops.
