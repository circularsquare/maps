Design only, read-only (master 9f6ac302b). Task: TASKS.md T-4220. Background: notes/T-4204-options.md, notes/T-2188.md,
notes/T-3616.md ("An edit made during the whole solve", options 1-4), notes/T-1734.md, notes/T-2605.md, notes/T-3925.md.
Gemini review: `artifacts/trainworld/gemini/t4220-brief.md`, `t4220-design.md` (the draft it read), `t4220-review.md`.

## For Anita

- **What changes.** Today an edit made while trips are being calculated for the whole map (after a new line, a long
  line's stop, a fare change, or the quiet refresh two minutes after you stop editing) waits for that calculation: on
  your Asia save 100-250 s before its lines move. With this, a second worker beside the main one recalculates only
  the cities the edit touched, against the figures on screen, and the edited lines show in about the time a lone edit
  takes (8-40 s on Asia), plus 3-6 s the first time (15 s or so if Tokyo has to open). Edits made while one of these
  runs share the next one, never one calculation per click. The gain is for big joined-up saves like your Asia one: on
  New York the whole calculation is short (73 s) and the main pool has two workers to the side one's one, so it
  mostly won't start there, and on Asia an edit in Tokyo may be turned down for memory.
- **The lie.** Until the running calculation ends and your edit is recalculated on top of it, the edited cities and
  their lines are worked out against the map as it was when that calculation began. During the quiet refresh that is
  off by what the refresh moves (on Asia 0.003% of all rail trips, one line at most 1.4%). During an intercity
  change's calculation, that change's own effect on those lines is missing until then (a new express's longer trips,
  say). "Calculating trips" stays up until then, as today.
- **How long.** The rest of the running calculation plus the edit's own: at most about 4.5 minutes on Asia with one
  worker, about 2 minutes on New York.
- **When it can't help** (today's wait stays): fare edits, an edit that changes the longer trips (a stop on a run of 20
  minutes or more, a line switched on or off, a line's time end to end moved 3 minutes or more), edits touching more
  than 5 cities, an edit that joins or splits two parts of the map, phones, a game still loading, memory too short for the second worker, and whenever the
  side calculation would not come clearly sooner than the main one.
- **Waits for your yes** before it is built (a big task's design).
- **Stages**: 9, about 12-14 hours (most about an hour, two about two): a snapshot of the figures on screen, read-only
  stand-ins for the cities not touched, the side calculation (two stages), a check that it matches the main
  calculation, starting and batching it, showing and retiring its figures, several workers, then measuring on New York
  and your Asia save.

## What the code does today

**What a running whole solve holds** (demand.worker.ts `run`, 1939-3248):
- At each cluster's set-up (`setUp` 2304, `setUpPops` 2238-2303) every population is put on the new network: in JS
  `held` is deleted and `disturbed` (523-528) drops `cityOn`, `heldOn`, `trail` and, for the market, `marketOn`,
  `marketNet`, `marketFare`. In Rust `set_network` keeps a period only where its inputs are unchanged bit for bit
  (api.rs 1589-1598; `same_periods` 574-577 is 0 when the layout differs). The market is set on the whole cluster's
  view (2040), which every edit since its last whole solve changed, so after a stop edit it loses all five periods at
  set-up, after a train edit those whose trains an hour moved (intercity/setup.rs 562-575). The idle refresh does this
  too: it re-sets the market on the current network.
- Then each period: round 0 `solve(q)` replaces each population's `Period` (api.rs 1780-1806), rounds average into it
  in place (`crowd_in` 1872-1921), the cap at the end. `keepTrail` restarts each population's trail of that period at
  round 0 (193-200). Only the zone in view's cluster goes period by period (`byPeriod` 3017-3064); the rest go round by
  round over all periods (3065-3093). On Asia the one big cluster is all of it.
- So from a cluster's set-up until the solve ends, the state on screen exists only in periods not yet reached, and not
  at all for the market after a stop edit.

**Paused, cancelled, or a local solve beside it on one worker?**
- It yields between population passes (`step` 2509-2510, `localSolve` 2837-2838): a message is handled there, and a
  newer job in the single `pending` slot (91, handler 1123) makes the solve stale. There is no resume.
- Cancel: yes (T-3925: `firm` 2333 lets a newer network through while `restarts` 2346-2353 holds, under 40%), but the
  cut solve has already destroyed the shown state of what it set up, so the next solve has no local stage: `editOf`
  needs `marketOn` for the market (2190-2191) and `cityOn` with a full solved mask for each city (2194).
- A local solve beside it on one worker: no. A population object holds one network context (Rust `svc`, `sz`, `per`)
  and the touched ones would need two; `figures` (2605-2677), `purposeParts` (1857) and `comeRaw` read every
  population of the cluster from Rust, and the frozen ones are mid-solve. T-1734 and T-3616 option 2 priced a Rust
  stash-and-restore plus composing each figure kind at 12-18 h, with real exactness risk.
- Anything asked of the busy worker waits for the pass in hand. A market pass on Asia is about 9 s (derived: T-3599
  saw one market pass scaled to 213 populations read 2,000 s a unit), so a design that asks it at edit time has that
  floor.

**What an edit's quick result needs** (`localSolve` 2776-2883, `figures`): the touched populations on the new network
and solved; the others' loads per round as last solved (`trail`; else the ended loads and `capRatio` at 2808-2815,
the fallback that once moved Japan lines 6-16%); their boardings (free flow and last round) for the cap and `arrive`;
their contributions to each figure (segment loads, boardings, alightings, crush, fare and base extras, transfers by
kind of day, purpose blocks including the market's commute, everyday and lean blocks); the market read through its
last-solved route nodes carried to the new network (`marketOn`, `remapKept` 498-521, as a city edit reads it at 2892);
a T-1735 window's delta where the shown local result had one (2896-2923). All of it but the touched populations' own
solve is read-only data about the state on screen.

**How edits queue** (batching mostly exists): the page holds one network for the main pool, newest wins (`waiting`,
`flush` 2250-2283); the worker holds one job (`pending`). `firm` (2330-2337) holds a network behind an edit's solve
except in the local result's gap and the T-3616 chain (`quickHold` 2357-2364). A station no line stops at starts no
solve (T-2410, demandClient 705-709), so clicking to add stations costs solves only once a line stops there. Missing
is a quick calculation for the one network held, or for a whole solve's own network when it has no local stage.

## The pick: a side calculation against a snapshot of the state on screen

The main pool's solves run exactly as today, so its figures, queries and memory are unchanged. A second worker (the
focus pool of T-2045 and T-2605, which already solves a zone beside a whole solve) gets the frozen state as plain
arrays, solves only the touched populations on the newest network with the same `localSolve`, and its result is shown
until the main pool's own result for that network or a newer one lands.

1. **Shown populations are tracked** (`shownIntact`). Cleared whenever a population is set on a network (`disturbed`:
   a local stage's and a refine's own set-ups too); set for a cluster's populations at `solved(c)`, for a local stage's
   fresh populations once all their periods are sent and `whole` had not come (else another worker went straight to the
   whole solve and the result is never shown; a city edit's `endHere` included), and for a T-1609 refine's.
2. **Snapshot, sent before the first set-up.** A non-barrier solve (an edit's whole solve, or the refresh), after its
   local stage and the wait for `whole` (2949-2954) and before the first `setUp` (3005), copies for every shown
   population of every cluster, dirty or not: kind, zone, `popScales`, its fare settings (`marketFare`, what the main
   pool tests an edit's fares against), its shown view (`cityOn`, a `PopView` with its
   network arrays, so the side job can test `sameView`; one per zone, shared by its commutes and everyday layer), for
   the market `marketOn` and the network ids it maps to, and per period it solves: `trail` (every round), `boardsOf`
   free and rounds, `seg`, `board`, `alight`, `load_of_crush`, `fare_extra`, `base_extra`, `transfers_in`,
   `kind_totals`, `part_loads` and `purpose_count` (everyday), `commute_loads`, `everyday_loads`, `lean_loads`
   (market), its `heldQ` (the period signatures it holds) and its pass times (`passByRound`); also the zones' boxes,
   each zone's level (coarse or fine) and whether it has an everyday layer, the market zones (as T-3373 B's `zones` message), the job's clustering flags
   (`whole`, `oneMarket`, `wide`), and the T-1735 window delta per period, kept per market part from the local result
   that showed it (`windowShown`) until that part is set up, its cluster's result is shown without one, or a final lands
   (a whole solve cut by T-3925 before it set up that cluster leaves the window on screen into the next job). Copies only: never the worker's own `trail` or `boardsOf` arrays, which
   `keepTrails` puts back for kept periods (2305, 2331). Posted to the page, transferred, before any set-up, so nothing
   is asked of the busy worker later. A cluster's entries are sent again after its `solved(c)` or its refine in a multi-cluster
   solve (its result is then on screen). A restarted solve (T-3925) sends only what is still shown; the page merges by
   population and period (two solving workers send their periods apart) and drops every period sent at or before a
   final's version when that final is shown. Sent only when the solve message says `snap` (a focus
   pool could run here, memory not short).
3. **Read-only stand-ins.** A `ShownPop` answers the reader calls from the snapshot's arrays (`seg(q)` ...
   `solved_mask()`, `purpose_count()`), so `localSolve`, `figures`, `purposeParts`, `xferDays` and `sumAll` run on a
   cluster whose frozen members are stand-ins. Their `instanceof` tests become a `kindOf`. `figures` reads each zone's
   summary and purposes from the cluster's populations (`zonePops`: its own, else its stand-ins), not the worker's own
   `cities` and `everyday` maps, which a frozen zone is not in on the focus worker. `figures` gets a mode for a
   market stand-in: `purposeParts` with the market's blocks (the "longer" key), no `marketFigures` (its corridors are
   not in the snapshot; the page keeps the shown market figures for a result with `market: false`). The side job enters
   through `run()`, whose closures these are.
4. **The side job** (`kind: "aside"`, focus worker). Build the newest network's clusters from the snapshot's boxes,
   market zones and flags (the T-3373 B path 1998-2014 already clusters zones a worker does not hold), set views
   (`reachView`, `clusterView`), give each frozen member its stand-in and the market stand-in its shown route nodes
   carried to the newest network (`nodeMap`), and take as fresh every population whose new view is not `sameView` its
   shown one. **Kept periods as the main pool keeps them**: a period where every population's snapshot `heldQ` is the
   new cluster's `psig[q]` (or it plus `LOCAL`) is not solved, as `keptOf` (2128) has it; `psig` holds the layout and
   that period's run times and trains (demandClusters.ts 450-470), so a train edit at one level re-solves only its
   periods (`PERIOD_ROW` [0,0,0,1,2], sim/src/track/params.rs 582: a medium-level edit, period 3 alone). Without this
   the cold side worker would solve all five, about five times the main pool's work for such an edit (Bunkyo's 41 s
   would be about 190 s). In a kept period every population, the touched ones too, is its stand-in. The touched zones
   open in the focus worker at the level the snapshot's solve held them (refused when `levelPosted` differs now; T-2605
   opens fine on purpose, this must not) and stay open for the next side job on the same snapshot. `localSolve` on the stand-ins' trails in the
   periods to solve, capped with their boardings; `figures` adds the stand-ins' arrays and the window delta. Each
   period's result goes as it ends (T-2624), as `result` with `cluster: { cities: fresh, market: false, local: true,
   aside: true }`. The fresh zones' bubble sums go only where it solved every period of the zone (`sub_sums` covers the
   periods a population solved); else they stay as on screen.
5. **Starting and batching** (page). A side job is wanted when a whole solve or the refresh runs and the newest
   network has no main-pool result coming soon: (a) it is held behind that solve (`firm`, not `quickHold`), or (b) it is
   that solve's own network and the solve has no local stage (`localPlans` all 0: a solve started again by T-3925's
   cut, or an intercity edit solved whole at once). It starts once the snapshot from every solving worker is in, after
   `ASIDE_QUIET_MS` (1 s) with no newer edit, if the focus pool may run, the expected memory fits (below), and it would
   win: its expected time (the fresh populations' pass times in the periods to solve, from the snapshot, as `localExp`
   and `passPrior` time a local stage, plus the pool's start and pack opening) under `ASIDE_GAIN` (0.7, first guess) of
   the main pool's time to the edit's lines (`unitsLeft` of the running solve, demandClient 2349, plus its local stage's
   estimate). One focus worker solves every period the main pool spreads over its workers, so with two or more solving
   workers (New York's real save, or "Use more cores") it is that many times slower and this rule turns it down. One at a
   time; when it ends, the newest network, if newer than its own, starts the next. Fresh is always taken against the
   snapshot, so each side job covers every edit since the state on screen. While the pool serves a side job it is not
   ended by an edit (today every edit ends it: demandClient 4306); T-2605's edit trigger (`refineAside` 3664) goes, and
   its relevel aside waits while a side job holds the pool.
6. **Showing and retiring** (page). Its own publish path, not `publishCluster`: it keeps `linesSettled` (5963, so its
   lines read as worked out) and drops `cityVersion` (questions keep going to the main pool), `lodShown` (the level is
   the main pool's), `unsettled`, `localShownAt`, `wholeGo`, `demandStep` and `demandSolving` (5949-5981, the main
   solve's bookkeeping). Its merge
   puts in the fresh zones' figures, every line that stops in a fresh zone or that its network added or changed, their
   stations and purpose entries, and drops lines its network removed (`withZone` 3972-4000 only fills lines missing,
   right for T-2605's zone-alone figures, wrong here). Kept over any older result (generalising `asideOver` and
   `refineOver` 3680-3690); retired, and the pool ended, when the main pool publishes a result of the side job's network
   or a newer one.
7. **Refused** (today's wait): an intercity edit (as `editOf` classes one: the main pool's local stage would open a
   market window, which a side job cannot); the fares moved against what stays frozen (the market part, else an untouched city), by the
   main pool's own test (`faresMoved`): any fare edit, a line's own multiple too; fresh zones over `ASIDE_MAX_ZONES` (5;
   T-2188's Asia train edits touched 1-5); a cluster or market key of the newest network not in the snapshot (an edit
   that joins or splits clusters: in the main pool `assignParts` 1918 gives it a new part with no `marketOn`); a frozen
   member without a full trail (never the ended-loads fallback); the zones the main pool's solve of the network covers
   not the snapshot's (one opened or closed since; a cluster kept apart by T-1607 is not refused, stage 3); `?split=1`; a barrier solve; a small or address-limited device; memory: the fresh
   zones' `zoneGB` (demandLod.ts 35) plus the snapshot plus a worker's `MEM_WORKER` past `GROW_ROOM` of the budget.
   `?perfOff=editAside` turns it off.

**Why this over the others.**
- **Hold until the running final, then locally on top** (today past 40%; T-3616 option 4 tunes the gap): the edit
  waits the rest of the whole solve, up to 243 s on Asia.
- **Cut and restart** (T-3925 under 40%): the restarted solve has no local stage, so the lines wait a whole solve.
- **Cut cleanly in the main worker** (the refresh period by period, each period committed, the one in progress
  finished or put back): the market's periods are dropped when the refresh sets it up (above), so after a cut there is
  no market to solve locally on. Keeping it needs a Rust move-aside of the market's whole network context, and an
  intercity whole solve would still restart from period 0 at each cut, so steady editing never lands it (T-1734).
- **Stand-ins inside the main worker** (Gemini's alternative: keep the snapshot there, cut the refresh at any point
  and the intercity solve under 40%, and run the edit's local stage with stand-ins for the populations the cut left
  half-solved): no second worker, no pack opening, no zone cap, phones too. But the main pool's populations are also
  what answers every question (station panel, trip planner, bubbles, the market's legs: `query` 1307-1600) and what
  the next local solve and T-124's kept periods start from. After such a cut, the frozen ones hold half a refresh
  until a whole solve completes, so questions would read wrong state or wait minutes, and each later solve would have
  to know which populations are stand-ins. An intercity solve past 40% would still wait, and the busy worker answers
  only after its pass in hand (up to about 9 s on Asia). The side worker keeps all of that out of the main pool.
- **Pause and solve locally in the main worker** (T-1734, T-3616 option 2): Rust stash or twin populations and
  per-figure composition, 12-18 h, exactness risk, every pause lengthens the whole solve.
- **T-2605's zone-alone aside as it is**: no market and no other zones, so lines read low (Salt Lake City 50,400
  against 67,800); it never shows lines.

## The lie, in detail

From the side job's result until the main pool's own result of that network or a newer one (the rest of the whole
solve plus the main pool's local stage on top: up to about 243 + 41 s on Asia with one worker, about 73 + 60 s on New
York):
- **The edited lines and the fresh zones' figures and bubbles** are solved against the base on screen when the whole
  solve began. During a refresh, off by the drift it clears (T-2188: Asia +0.003% of rail trips, lines up to 1.4%; New
  York 0.002%, lines up to 0.2%). During an intercity edit's whole solve, that edit's own whole effect on them is
  missing (a new express's longer-trip riders beyond its window, T-1760).
- **Once the older final lands meanwhile**, the rest of the map moves to it while the side job's zones and the lines
  that stop in them keep its figures; a line through touched and untouched zones carries the old base's riders from
  the untouched ones.
- **Totals** (rail trips a day, the market's figures) stay as on screen until the main pool's result, as for any local
  result. The market stand-in reads the route nodes the main pool's own local solve would (`marketOn` carried job by
  job), so a stop dropped and then put back has no market riders until a whole solve, as today.
- **Bubbles** of the touched zones stay as on screen when the edit kept some periods (a one-level train edit), until
  the main pool's result.
- **Questions** about the fresh zones still go to the main pool and wait for its result (T-2757), so a station panel
  can disagree with the line figures for that time.

## Time and memory (estimates; stages 1 and 9 measure)

  and views on the 2,700-station network (well under a second, guessed); then the local solve, the same work as the

## Stage 1: the snapshot (agent aside1, 2026-11-24, tree aside1)

Built, no behaviour change: the snapshot is only stored and logged. `?perfOff=editAside` sends none.

- **Worker** (`demand.worker.ts`): `shownIntact` (WeakMap population -> the cluster it was shown in, zones and market
  key) and `windowShown` (market part -> its shown window delta and that network's ids), both dropped in `disturbed`.
  `snapPops` copies every shown population's periods it holds (never its own `trail` or `boardsOf` arrays; WASM getters
  are `&self` copies) and transfers them; `sendShown` posts `shown` before the first `setUp` of a solve with `snap`
  (none with the barrier, none when no cluster is set up whole: a city edit's `endHere`, a refine alone), then again
  for each non-last cluster after its result (a whole solve's or a refine's). Over `SNAP_MAX_BYTES` (120 MB) it sends
  `over` and nothing after.
- **Page** (`demandShown.ts` `ShownStore`, `demandClient.ts` `shownSnap`): merges by population (`kind:key`) and period,
  each period with the version it came from and its own `on` (view or market nodes); a final shown drops every period
  of its version or before (`dropTo` in `publish`); a game load or an over-cap snapshot clears it. `snap` is set on a
  solve without the barrier when `snapOn`: no `?perfOff=editAside` or `focus`, not a small or address-limited device,
  `memShort` false. Dev log once every solving worker's is in: `T4220 snapshot v7: 9 populations, 5 periods, 10 MB,
