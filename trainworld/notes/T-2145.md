The figure is the city zone's train share of its trips within (rail / (rail + walk + drive), what the Within bar draws) with
the city's real network save (`data/packs/real/<id>.save`) loaded into a `?new=1&opened=all&demandWorkers=1&paused=1` game,
read at the average day's demand final. `checks/t2145-real-share.mjs` reruns it (json in `../artifacts/trainworld/t2143/`)
and prints the rows of `REAL_SHARE` in `app/src/game/realShare.ts`.
