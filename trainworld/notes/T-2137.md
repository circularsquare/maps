# T-2137: Delhi on the emulated phone

2026-11-04, agent phone. Measurement only; no code changed. Method as in T-2122.md (`phone.mjs`, copied to
`notes/T-2137/phone.mjs`; `notes/T-2137/lines.json` holds five Delhi lines on real metro stations: Yellow-like
12 stops at level -2, Blue-like 9 at -3, Violet-like 6 at 2, Red-like 7 at 3, Gurgaon-Qutub 6 at -1).
Zone id `ned` ("New Delhi", registry box 76.66-77.99 E, 28.01-29.24 N). Run against 8800 (build 877, production
build), headless Chrome, 412 x 915 touch viewport, 1 solving worker (the cores ledger granted 3). JSON, logs and
the exported saves: `artifacts/trainworld/phone/ned-phone.*` and `ned-iphone.*`.

Renderer private bytes, peak per step, MB (limit 2048). "Load" is the five-line save loaded in a fresh page.

| Device | Open (zone loaded) | 1 line | 3 lines | 5 lines | Load |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Android-like (deviceMemory 4, 8 threads) | 744 | 1151 | 1009 | 1026 | **1218** |
| iPhone-like (no deviceMemory, 4 threads, iPhone user agent) | 776 | 1167 | 1026 | 1056 | 1168 |

Rail trips a day, the same in both runs: 549,060 / 1,333,002 / 1,885,711 (the load the same as five lines).
First solve after each build 5.9, 2.9, 3.6 s; the load 8.7 s.

Demand workers at the Android-like peak: the solving worker's WASM 208 MB (163 MB at the load), the aux
worker's 71 MB; the other workers' JS heaps 10-20 MB each (demand solving 17-18 MB, clock 23-43 MB,
maplibre 7-40 MB); the page's JS heap 130-225 MB (about 200 MB of ArrayBuffers at the peak: the corridor
table, T-2136). The GPU process is separate (about 400 MB, not in the renderer figure). No page crash, no
console error line.

Worst step: loading the five-line save, 1.22 GB (Android-like); the first line is next at 1.15-1.17 GB. About 0.8 GB
under the 2 GB cap in every step. Jakarta's figures after T-2122 were 1.27 / 1.12 / 1.10 / 1.29 GB, with the
solving worker's WASM at 190 MB: Delhi sits at the same level, the load slightly lower.

Not done: Jakarta on a real phone (brief: skipped). The emulation cannot show Safari's own accounting
(WebKit counts the tab's whole process, GPU memory included on some versions): the renderer figure plus the
GPU process would be about 1.6 GB, still under 2 GB.
