# T-3592 board changelog: drag items into and out of groups

Branch tw/groupdrag (worktree .tw-trees/groupdrag), not committed. Edits: board/board.js, board/board.css, tools/changelog.py, serve.py.

- Endpoint: `{op:"move", id, before, under}`; `under` = a group's id, null for top level, absent keeps the parent. serve.py passes it on (one line), so it needs a restart and lands by hand. The old serve.py still works (move_item keeps path as its third argument).
- `changelog.move_item(id, before, path=NEXT, under=KEEP)`; `before` must sit in the item's new parent. A group can't go under a group (400). Changing parent bumps rev; going into a group clears the highlight, and the item becomes hidden when the group is hidden (a hidden item in a shown group stays hidden).
- CLI: `move <id> [--before <id>] [--under <group>|none]`.
- Board: on a group row the top quarter drops above the group (top level), the rest puts the item first in the group (row lit with an outline and tint). On a child row it drops among that group's children (indented line). On a top-level row it drops at top level (a child dragged there leaves its group). Sections still keep apart: a drop beside a row of another section does nothing; a group row always accepts (the item takes the group's section). Dragging a group over a child counts as below that group.
