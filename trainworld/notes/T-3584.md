# T-3584: weekend lines sharing track are paced from their weekend headways (agent weekpace, 2026-11-18)

## What changed

`TrackWorld::settle_weekend` (world.rs) now runs `pace_merges` on the weekend service after the weekend settle, the
same loop the weekday settle has (`PACE_ROUNDS`, a settle between). The weekend pacing is its own pair of fields,
`pace_rest` (the `Network::line_pace` of the weekends) and `pace_in_rest` (what each merge was paced from), swapped
into `net.line_pace` / `pace_in` for the duration, as the weekend counts, headways, rates and service already are.
They start as a copy of the weekdays' when the weekend service is built fresh (the first weekend settle, or after a
load), and stay with `svc_rest` after. A load keeps the saved weekend rates and the first edit that settles weekends
works the pacing out again, as it does the service. (T-3735 moved `pace_rest` into `Network::line_pace_rest` and
saves it in `LWP1`, so the weekend departures read the same pacing after a load.)

Weekdays are not touched: the weekday pacing and `pace_in` are put back to the bit, and with no line other on
weekends `settle_weekend` returns before any of it.
