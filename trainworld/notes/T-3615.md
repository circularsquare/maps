# T-3615: single-side station boxes with a real width

Agent sidebox, 2026-11-17, tree `.tw-trees/sidebox` (branch tw/sidebox). Anita's pad, 2026-11-18, quoted in TASKS.md.

## What changed

The one-track station shape (ST_ONE: a one way station on double track; ST_ONE_MID: a station on single track
with no passing loop; rail and tram alike) is now sized from its own full box's half width `hw` (fixed on the
ground, with the same on-screen floor full boxes have from zoom 14 to 15, tram boxes already at TRAM_BOX 0.7):

- the bar at the platform ends: `max(0.3 x 2hw, track span)`
- across the bump: `0.5 x 2hw + track span` (never less than the ends)
- the bump: the middle half of the platform (`ONE_BULGE_K` 0.18 -> 0.5 of the half length each way)

"Track span" is the track the bar covers, rim included, as STROKE_VS draws it (renderer.ts `cover`): single track
either side of its line (`max(line x SINGLE_WIDTH eased, lane width)`); double track the whole stroke until its two
tracks part, then from the centre line out to its own track's outer edge (`gap/2 + lane/2`), as before. Tram track
takes TRAM_WIDTH. The bar's inner edge is the track's inner edge, so the box grows towards the platform side only.

Code: `stationGeometry.ts` (`OneCover`, `ONE_END`, `ONE_BUMP`, `oneBar`, `ONE_BULGE_K`, `stationShape`),
`rendererShaders.ts` (STATION_VS one-track branch; `u_one`/`u_one_mid` now vec4: rail inner/outer, tram inner/outer;
TRAM_WIDTH, SINGLE_WIDTH, DOUBLE_WIDTH exported), `renderer.ts` (the cover per frame, the uniforms). The CPU twin
(selection outline, demand cut-out, picking, placement ghost) goes through `oneBar`, so it matches the shader.
oneWayMarks.ts and busLayer.ts untouched.

## Measured (New York, 1400x900, dpr 1)

- Zoom 18.5, rail: full box 140 px wide; one way bar 41 px at the ends (0.29), 90 px at the bump (70 + its track's
  20); bump 311 of a 622 px platform. Tram no-passing stop: 27 px ends, 60 px bump against a 98 px tram box.
- Zoom 15 (boxes at their screen floor): the track span is wider than 0.3 of a box, so the ends are the track and
  its rim as before; the bump sticks out one box half width (6.3 px rail, 4.4 tram; was 7.5 and 5.3), twice as long.
- No gap between the rim and the track's outer edge; on double track the bar starts at the centre line, clear of
  the other track (light blue band between the lanes is cut at the centre, as before).
