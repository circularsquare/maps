# T-938: driving a little faster, owning a car a little dearer

Anita (TODO.md, 2026-10-22): "bring car speed back up a little? also raise car ownership cost a bit?"

## What changed

`Params` defaults in `sim/src/demand/kernel.rs`:

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Dense streets and roads (`road_city_kmh`) | 15 km/h | 16.5 |
| Open-country local streets (`road_town_kmh`) | 40 | 44 |
| Open-country main roads (`road_far_kmh`) | 80 | 88 |
| Open highway in a long drive's middle (`road_open_kmh`) | 100 | 110 |
| Ownership a car trip (`car_own_usd_trip`) | $1.50 | $2.00 |
| Ownership a km of road (`car_own_usd_km`) | $0.10 | $0.13 |

Every local drive is 10% quicker (each speed x 1.1, so every drive's minutes / 1.1: 3 km inside
Manhattan 13 to 12 minutes, 60 km into a dense centre 71 to 65). Ownership is about 30% dearer: a
typical trip $2.20-4.00 becomes $2.90-5.25. The drive to a station pays both, as before. The
regional market's car (85 km/h on 1.25x the line, `intercity.rs` `drive_min`) and its no-ownership
rule are unchanged.

## "Back up"

No drive speed was ever cut. DECISIONS and ARCHIVE show the speeds fitted once on New York's census
car commutes (T-090: 40/80 km/h falling to 15) and raised once (T-370, 2026-10-14: a long drive's
middle at 100 km/h instead of 80, Anita finding 80 pessimistic). What did make drives slower was
T-275 (2026-10-13): drives follow the routed road length, round water (Hoboken to Midtown 5.0 to
7.5 km of road), at the same speeds; and T-699's explorer now shows the drive's average speed
(17 km/h SoHo to Times Square). So I took the brief's fallback, every speed +10%.

Net effect: driving feels quicker and costs more, rail and walking gain a little (short trips walk
more, since the car's fixed cost rose by $0.50 = 1.5 perceived minutes).
