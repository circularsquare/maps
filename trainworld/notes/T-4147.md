# T-4147 Stop cuts, line groups, bus and ferry edits shown at the click (agent editstops, 2026-11-24)

## What changed

- **Group panel stop edits** (`dropStop` of an end stop, `endLineAt`): map/lineToolAhead.ts `cutBack` draws the line's strokes cut back at the click, from the drawn network's own stretch numbers (map/lineAhead.ts `edgesInStretches`, `edgesWithoutEnd`). Only with a network that holds the world's stops, no loop, no other stop edit out. Probe on NYC and Asia: the guessed edge set equals the final network's, all four cases (end, last, endLineAt early and late). A middle stop dropped keeps every stroke (no guess; its circle is the panel's own).
- **Line groups** (`groupMembers`, `groupName`, `groupLook`): game/groupAhead.ts works out each line's chain of groups as sim/src/track/wasm/lines.rs does (target chain, `set_group_members`, the lone-group tidy), and gives it as `fields` `line:<num>` `chain`. game/lineGroups.ts `groupChain` reads it through `shown`, so the Lines tab, group panel, schedule panel and diagram take it with no other change. A new group's number is the next free one, as `new_line_group` picks. Cleared by the rows.
- **Bus and ferry routes** (`busRoute`, `addBusLine`, `ferryRoute`, `addFerryLine`): map/busAhead.ts turns the op's route into longitude and latitude; `Show.buses` (game/pending.ts) is a part that clears with the rows (the bus layer reads the world, not the network). map/busLayer.ts `setAhead` draws the route in place of the line's, with no stops until the rows (the sim places them); a new line is drawn blueprint under number 1,000,000. Probe on Asia: at the click 1 override, route of the same length as the world's, no stops; after, none left, 84 stop numbers back.
- **Bus panel** (spacing, Manual, BRT) and **ferry boat size**: `fields` through `shown` (ui/inspector/BusInspector.tsx), cleared by the rows. Spacing, Manual and BRT had a local `asked` that was dropped at the answer; it is gone, so there is no flicker back to the old value between the answer and the rows.
- **Bench**: tools/edit_bench.mjs has `--only order,groups,bus,ferry`: orderStops, dropStop (end and middle), endLineAt, group merge, name, look, split, bus spacing, route, Manual, copy, ferry boat; on the save's own lines, each put back by undo. A panel guess counts as shown at the next animation frame after its entry was made.

## Not done, and why

- **Stop order** (orderStops, sortStops, a reorder by setStops): the click cannot know the new path between stops, which the track model routes. It shows with the worker's word (about 100-300 ms on Asia); the panel's rows and stop circles were already at the click. A hover-time read as for a stop click would need the drop place known before the drop.
- **Copy line money**: the copy's price is the trains of the new-line default headways, which only the model's apply works out exactly (the rule in game/pending.ts `priced`: a price at the click only when it is the charge). The word's charge moves the cash about 50-300 ms later. A copy of a bus line is not drawn ahead either.
- A manual bus stop's click (`busHandStop`), BRT lanes and a bus's stops after a spacing change: stops are the sim's.
- Ferry before-numbers were not run on the old build.
