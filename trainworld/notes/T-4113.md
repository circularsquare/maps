# T-4113 Bounded edits, part A: what an edit touched, and regions (agent bounded, 2026-11-23)

**Summary for Anita**
- Each edit now carries what it touched, and the world is split into regions of at most 64 stations (about 120 on your Asia save). The worker's rows step redoes only the touched records and the regions they're in, and keeps everything else from last time.
- Work per edit on the Asia save (records the rows step reads again): a stop reorder went from 8,598 track pieces, 8,779 nodes and 222 lines to 0, 0 and 13. Construct all went from all of them to 300, 306 and 24, and placing a station to about 210, 215 and 27.
- Biggest single find: after a stop edit on one long line, the track model said 219 of 221 lines had changed, but only 2 lines' rows really had (5 phase tables). Each line now has a stamp, so a line is listed only when what the worker reads of it moved: 219 down to 22, 17 of them bus and ferry lines.
- Time: over the 14 edits measured on the Asia save, the rows step went from 2.1 s to 1.6 s in all (-23%). The clearest single actions were a stop reorder, rows 256 down to 50 ms (about 16% off its 1.3 s click-to-map wait), and a station, 290-450 down to 165-215 ms (about 9% off 1.7-2.2 s). Single runs swing 2-3x on this machine. The work counts above are the firm number.
- Nothing a player sees changes, except that it comes sooner.
- Advisor called once, for the region design.

## What was built

- **Touched set (Rust, `sim/src/track/wasm/touched.rs`).** `take_touched()` lists every edge and node the edits since the last call added, removed or changed: the union of each applied edit's `Dirty` (undo and redo too, previews not), `[u32::MAX]` after a new or loaded world. One state can follow several edits, hence the union.
- **Line stamps (Rust, same file).** `take_changed_lines` and `peek_changed_lines` drop a noted line whose stamp is as at the last take. The stamp is a hash of exactly the getters the worker reads for a line (heavy parts, its own calls, `line_phases`), so equal stamps mean equal rows. Bus and ferry lines always pass, since their rows read more. Stamping 219 lines costs about 17 ms in Node, against the 70-90 ms the re-reads cost. `set_line_stamps(false)` turns it off (`?patch=walk`). changed_tests.rs: a station rename now lists no line, since no line row holds a station name; a dropped stop lists its line only.
- **Regions (`app/src/workers/regions.ts`).** A quadtree over the model plane. A cell splits exactly when it has more than `REGION_CAP` stations and is wider than about 1 km (stacked platforms can pass the cap at that floor). So the regions are a function of the station set: an edit reshapes only where stations came or went, and an undo gives the same regions back (checked against a fresh build after every fuzz step). Every node and edge belongs to one region (an edge to its a end's). A region is dirty from a change in it until a read takes it.
- **Bounded rows (`app/src/workers/stateRows.ts`).** `begin` takes the touched set and the stamped line list, and reads the changed lines' heavy parts first. Track whose lines' paths moved is read again at both ends, also in the next read, since a platform's ways are ordered by the previous read's lines. It then takes the dirty regions. `edges` and `nodes` visit only records in scope (touched, in a dirty region, new, or whose set membership moved: one-way, joins, limits, ramps, fares, upgrades and the rest are diffed against the last read); all others keep their row with no work. Name folding over complexes is skipped when no station row is new and the links and buildings are unchanged. A bus or ferry row is read again only where its route or stops reach a taken region, or the model lists it. Bus stops' nodes are kept with the bus row. A repricing for water or terrain (every line listed) still visits every record, because it moves edge prices nobody touched.
- **Focus first.** A rows state (`sendRows`: stop clicks, renames, periods) passes the view's rectangle. When more than 3,000 records are dirty, it takes the regions holding the edit's own records and those in view, and leaves the rest dirty. The full state that follows takes them before the network is built, so nothing is dropped. In practice an edit dirties only its own regions, so this matters for the "everything" fallback, which becomes focus first. A rows state also no longer builds the drawn pieces, line samples and phase tables it used to throw away.
- **What stays global:** a touched line's whole row (timing, capacity, stops), the money record, the cost sums, capacity tags, crossings and full track (bulk calls under 1 ms each in Node, left whole).

| cap | regions (non-empty) | most stations in one | station placed: regions / records redone | Construct all: regions / records |

| 32 | 436 (261) | 32 | 1 / 25 | 3 / 259 |
| 64 | 217 (121) | 64 | 1 / 25 | 3 / 495 |

| 256 | 55 (29) | 254 | 1 / 2,372 | 3 / 5,329 |
| 512 | 40 (19) | 504 | 1 / 2,372 | 3 / 8,814 |

Read time in Node stays 20-45 ms at every cap: the floor is the bulk calls and the stamps, not the region. I picked 64. It gives the same records per edit as 32 with half the regions, about the count of zones on the save, and a region's few hundred records cost a few ms in the worker, inside a 0.1 s piece even at the 2-5x slowdown seen while trips are calculated. Parts C and E may want their own cap: it is one constant (`REGION_CAP`).

## Found, for later stages

- **The rows floor is the bulk calls** (`edges_info`, `nodes_info`, the sets, `line_upkeep_periods`: about 10 ms in Node, 2-5x in the worker), plus the stamps for a big list. Next: `*_info` for a list of ids from Rust, and stamping from internal state instead of the getters.
- **Why 219 lines are noted:** a settle reschedules every line sharing capacity with the edit (`svc.update` notes `rescheduled` whatever moved). The stamps absorb it. Noting only what moved, in `service.rs`, would also save the stamps' 17 ms.
- **Repricing** on every camera move with new ground marks every line (`take_repriced`), so those reads visit everything. Line rows hold no price, so lines could be spared, but that is the model's call (T-1382). Left as is.
- Bus and ferry lines are noted on almost every edit (14-17 of 17).
