# T-1763 cull overlay stations and trains to the view (c3, 2026-11-16): not done

Looked at renderer.ts (station draws at the layerDraw calls, trains at drawTrains). Both draw their whole buffer
(n.stationCount, tripCount) in buffer order, which is id/trip order, not spatial. Culling needs either a buffer
reordered by a spatial key (Hilbert) with per-block boxes like strokes' visibleRanges, or a per-frame index buffer.
Stations: their buffer index is shared with textures and picking (station number), so reordering touches those;
trains have per-trip textures (u_sel_trip, stretch, lane). Saves about 1.5 ms a frame on Japan at zoom 15 (T-1644).
Needs its own design and a shader pass; left open.
