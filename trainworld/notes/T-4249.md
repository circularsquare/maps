# T-4249 Every bus line rebuilt for each station edit (agent busrebuild, 2026-11-25)

Tree busrebuild (master d1bb22aae). Not committed. Her words, 11-25: "the game reworking the whole network after each
edit is really the target here ... isolating the changes and cutting out the impacts on the broader map."

**For Anita.** On your Asia save every station edit, stop click or line edit rebuilt all 25 bus lines and all 6 lines
with a way back of their own, wherever it was made, and every edit sent the trains' trips again whether any train's
run changed or not. Now an edit rebuilds only the bus lines it can reach (stations within 200 m of a route, routes
crossing those, routes crossing a bus route changed) and the ways back it really moves, and an edit that changes no
line's service sends no trips. On your save the edit itself takes 25-58 ms less (17-58%), 0-6 lines rebuilt instead
of 31-37. That is a small part of the 2-5 s a station takes to show up there: most of the rest is elsewhere (below).

## The cause, from the code

- **Bus lines.** `TrackWorld::lines_touched` (world.rs), the lines an edit rebuilds, added every bus line
  (`bus_lines()`) whenever the edit touched any station or set any line (`!t.stations.is_empty() || !t.lines.is_empty()`):
  a station anywhere, a stop click on a rail line, a rail line's cars. A bus plan (`bus::plan_by`) reads only the
  stations within 200 m of its route (`reach`), the routes crossing it (and, through `theirs`, the stations those
  reach), the kept stops within 50 m and the busways under it; nothing recorded where an edit was.
- **Ways back.** `lines_touched` also added `back_lines()` on every edit ("an edit rebuilds them whatever it
  touches"), because a way back of its own is found by a search (`back_run_of`) and is not on `lines_on_edge`.
- **What a rebuild costs beyond itself.** A rebuilt line is recomputed (its phase tables re-read), its train-by-train
  timetables are dropped (its whole retime cluster is worked out again), its departures are placed again
  (`place_departures(direct)`) and it seeds the settle's scope.
- **Trips.** The clock worker sent the trips and the boarding steps after every edit but a track piece, a blueprint
  removal or a rename (T-1780), whatever the edit changed.

## The fix

- `Touch` records where an edit changed what a bus plan reads, at the setter (by `lines_touched` a removed station or
  an old route is gone): `station_places` (a station made, removed, constructed or made a BRT station, `set_station`;
  a blueprint station's node moved, `set_node`; with whether it was constructed before or after) and `bus_routes`
  (a bus route as it was and as it is, where its `BusRoute` or the line's existence changed, `set_line`).
- `bus::lines_touched`: the bus lines within `STATION_REACH_M` (+1 m) of a station place (a constructed line: of a
  constructed station's only, as `reach` reads), every bus line crossing one of those (the `STATION_GAP_M` rule in
  `reach` lets a station near B alone take B's stop at a station A shares, so A stops at the crossing; a test), those
  crossing a changed route, those within `KEEP_M` of a kept stop changed (T-4080's rule, moved here from
  `Network::bus_lines_near`), every one on a busway edit (as before).
- `TrackWorld::back_moved`: a way back line is rebuilt where a rebuild would give it other runs: its forward stops
  (`fwd_stops_of`, T-3348's platforms), its way back (`back_run_of`) and whether its loop runs once (`runs_once`)
  compared with the service's, or where the edit touched track its way back runs over, or the line is broken, not ok or
  partly built. The search costs about 0.1-0.4 ms a line on her save. `note_lines` (every hover check and rollback)
  still notes every way back without the search.
- `TrackWorld::rebuild_all` (`TrackApi::set_rebuild_all`): every edit rebuilds every bus line and way back, as before;
  the tests and the timing compare with it in one build.
- Trips: `TrackApi::take_service_moved` (set by `done` when an edit rebuilt, rescheduled, re-timed, re-shared or
  moved for demand any line; sticky across one player action's several edits). The clock worker takes it before the
  apply (dropping what previews moved and put back) and after; no move: no trips, no boarding steps.
- A cousin fixed: a blueprint station whose node was dragged placed no bus stops again (`set_node` noted no station).
- Docs: track-and-service.md (6.5 trips; the bus stops paragraph; the way back in the one-way and the per-direction
  stops sections).
- World.rs lines touched (settlestop's file): the `rebuild_all` field and its constructor, `lines_touched`,
  `back_moved` (new), `note_lines`' call, `commit_with`'s call, `merge_derived` (which also dropped a second batch's
  `busways` flag before). Nothing in the settle.
