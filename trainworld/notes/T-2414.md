# T-2414: "Not enough space for bridge ramps" where there is space

Anita (2026-11-04): "im still getting some "not enough space for bridge ramps" when theres clearly
enough space for bridge ramps. possibly cuz we're trying to like ramp down and then ramp up again,
rather than staying ramped up between two water crossings. we should be a bit omre persistent here
and try to get more cases to work so this feels less finicky"

## Where the refusals came from

`bridge::bridge_in` (sim/src/track/bridge.rs) placed every ramp of a segment with one shared shift:
when a curve kept one ramp off its place, all ramps moved out together, so the ramp down after one
crossing and the ramp up before the next ran into each other, and the whole segment was refused
(crossings closer than 458 m of land were already merged into one bridge; the failures were curves
with 420-520 m between). The bigger source was ends: a ramp needs 215 m of land (200 m ramp, 14.5 m
shore) between the water and the segment's end, and both a click on land near the water (an island,
a bank) and the next segment from that click (its start, existing track at ground) were refused.

## What changed

- `bridge::lay`: ramps laid one at a time, each moved back on its own (25 m steps, 400 m); a ramp up
  with no room after the previous ramp down drops that ramp down, so the bridge stays up over the
  land between (priced as viaduct; `land_kept_up_is_priced_as_viaduct`).
- A new end (a free click) with no room for its ramp stays up at +1 on land, as it already did over
  water (T-1269).
- `raise_end` (draw.rs): a route from or to a dead end of blueprint ground track with no room puts
  that end at +1 in the same edit, its piece ramping up as near it as fits, what it has up already
  staying up (so a bridge just before carries on). A piece too short for the ramp whose far end is
  another such dead end goes up whole. Not on quad.
- What still refuses names the end: station, junction (or track branched off), built track.

## Counts (before -> after)

Cases: `sim/src/track/wasm/water_gap_tests.rs`.

- Synthetic sweep (`close_crossings_islands_and_banks_build`): 33 of 91 refused -> 5 of 96 (the
  totals differ because a refused first segment skips its second). The 5 left are real: water
  60-180 m from a station (3), 20 m from a junction, 80 m from built track; each named.
- New York's real water (`nyc_water_sweep`, the NYC pack's mask in a world-frame game, 14 lines:
  Roosevelt Island, Randalls and Wards, Newtown Creek, Harlem River, East River, Flushing Creek,
  Jamaica Bay by Broad Channel, Spuyten Duyvil, Kill Van Kull; each straight, a click on every
  strip of land under 600 m then on, clicks 60/120/180 m short of each water then on straight and
  turning, segments ending 60/120/180 m past each): 127 of 143 refused -> 0 of 159. The cases
  probe the near-water spots on purpose, so "before" is high.
- In the game (new game, edit API as clicks; scratchpad shot.mjs): Manhattan -> Roosevelt Island ->
  Queens, Manhattan -> Randalls Island -> Astoria, across Newtown Creek with a click by the bank,
  Gowanus: 3 of 4 chains refused before, 0 after (artifacts/trainworld/ramps/after-*.png).

## Left

- A refusal remains where the water is within 215 m of a station, a junction or built track at the
  segment's end: those are not moved (a platform is level; a junction or built track is the
  player's). Quad track does not raise an existing end.
- Long snapped routes (`bridge_long`) are unchanged apart from a route's own start or end now
  staying up; Lake Hamana stays T-755.
