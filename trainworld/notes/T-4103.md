# T-4103: a lighter phone load, figures unchanged (agent phoneload, 2026-11-23)

Design: notes/T-4086.md. Tree `.tw-trees/phoneload`, from master 0ec0d3df.

## What changed

On a phone or tablet (`phoneLoad()` in `workers/deviceMemory.ts`, which is `mobileDevice()` as the brief said; the design
named `tabLimited()`, which leaves out Android Chrome; one function, so either is a one-line change):

1. **No aux worker.** `demandClient.ts`: a pool of one solving worker on a phone (`auxShared`; never with `?split=1`) spawns
   no aux worker. Every question for the aux worker goes to `auxAt` (worker 0 there, `computeN` elsewhere): cells,
   cellModes, the bare zones' `bare`, `bareFlows`, `bareSquares`, `tripLengths`, `trip`, `zoneSquares`; bare opens and closes;
   `holdersOf` without the duplicate; a replacement worker 0 reopens the bare packs. `demand.worker.ts`: `init.bareToo`
   lets a solving worker open bare zones (with `groups`, one group of all its periods) and answer `trip` for a zone it
   does not solve from its bare copy. A phone pool never grows past one (`growTo` stops at `poolN`), so the shrink and
   grow paths that move the aux worker are not reached.
2. **No refine on a pan.** `heldZones`: on a phone the settled view keeps a fine zone fine (and touched, so it does not
   go coarse after 10 minutes of looking at it) but never makes a coarse one fine. A selection or an edit in a coarse
   zone still refines it.
3. **The jump spike, named and cut (every device).** A far jump moves the draw anchor (T-307, 100 km at zoom 10+); the
   clock worker then posts the network whole. On the shift path (T-1170: 7 moves of 8, under 2^22 local units) the
   stroke pieces were already shifted copies, but `lineBands` and `corridors.besideSamples` key their caches on the
   edges' point arrays, which the shift replaces, so every edge's bands and beside-samples were worked out again:
   about 500 of the 600 MB the clock worker allocated in the 40 s after a Tokyo to Sendai jump (allocation sampling
   with collected objects, scratch `alloc-sum.mjs`). `shiftNetCache` now tells `lineBands.shiftKept` and
   `corridors.shiftBeside` the old and new arrays, and the kept results (metres along and across edges) carry over;
   `lineBands` hands a kept result a `rule` on the call's own points. Every 8th move (and any beyond 2^22) is still
   laid exactly and works everything out again.

Docs: `docs/demand-runtime.md` (the pool, bare zones, levels of detail item 1), `docs/world.md` (the anchor).

- **Settled**: S 60-100 MB lower (about 6%), R 35-70 (3-6%). The aux worker's WASM (49-65) and its JS go; the solving
  worker's WASM grows 24 MB on both saves (the cells and bubbles questions now allocate in it, at its high-water mark).
- **Load peak**: S unchanged within the spread (before 1,468-1,531, after 1,412-1,551); the load peak is the solve's,
  which this does not touch (T-3160). R 248 MB lower (16%): before, the zone in view loads coarse and is refined right
  after the load (a second solve, 160 s, that set R's peak); on a phone it stays coarse.
- **A far jump**: the in-play peak 150-250 MB lower (about 10-13% of it): no refine (about 140 MB, T-4086) and the bands
  and corridors carried over. With trips off (no demand at all) the clock worker allocated 605 MB in the 40 s after the
  jump before and 95 MB after (-84%). The first jump of the measured sessions with trips on was an exact re-lay (an 8th
  move), which still costs what it did; the second and third were shifts (+111, +133 against +134, +394).
- **What is left of the spike**: the page's buffers (the whole network posted again, 21 MB, and its upload: page buffers
  87 -> 155), `trainGuard.update` on the page (80-100 MB allocated, the trips in the new view) and the clock worker's
  money tick (`lagged`, `regionLines`, about 300 MB allocated per hour that ends, not the jump's).

## Figures

- S: rail trips 17,427,676 before and after, every run. The phone's no-refine changes nothing on S at load (its view
  zone is fine).
- R: 17,427,676 before (after the post-load refine of the zone in view) against 17,427,762 on a phone (that zone left
  coarse): 86 trips, 0.0005%. That is the designed lie (DECISIONS 2026-11-23).
- The shared worker's bare zones: `t325-bare-check.mjs` run with one worker that is the aux worker too (scratch
  `phoneload/bare-check.mjs`, `SHARED=1`): every figure identical to the bit (summaries, parts, everyday legs, every
  purpose, commuters by mode, 12 selections' far ends and longer trips). The unchanged check passes as before.
- Cells and bubbles of a solved zone: the solving worker's own copy at level 1 (coarse) and 0 against the aux worker's
  level 0 copy, New York, weekday and weekend (scratch `phoneload/coarse-check.mjs`): identical to the bit (54,258
  cells).
- Bands after a shift: `?patch=shiftcheck` reports the line strokes' count off a fresh build after a shift (the track
  strokes' count already was before this change: shifted copies drop points the fresh build keeps, by float32
  rounding), so positions are not compared there. `t2261-graph-anchor` (the diagram's graph the same after the anchor
  moved twice) and `t1799-bands` pass on the tree's dev server.

## Not done

- Real iPhone: none; Chrome's emulation only (Safari's JS garbage and collection differ).
- The exact re-lay every 8th move still recomputes everything; the page's network upload after a jump is untouched.
- The solve's working space (T-3160) is what sets the load peak on S.
- Advisor called once before building: it caught that dropping the view from the held zones would let a watched fine
  zone go coarse after 10 minutes (hence "keeps a fine zone fine"), that heap snapshots would miss garbage (hence
  allocation sampling), and asked for the coarse-level bubble check.
