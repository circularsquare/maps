# T-3625 Stations toggle

Settings > Show > Stations (`display.stations`, default on, stored with the other display settings).
Off: renderer draws no station boxes/dots, bridges, floors, car parks, hall crowds or bus stops at stations
(`DrawOptions.showStations`, renderer `stationsOff`); `stationAt`, `lotAt`, `selectedStationAt`, `upgradeAt`
return nothing so click and hover skip them; station names off too (main.ts labels.setVisible). Lines, trains, demand bubbles stay.
Station tool: stations are drawn while it is the active tool (plainer than flipping the setting back); other tools leave them hidden.
Settings search alias key settingsSearch.aliasStations. Strings: settingsTab.stations "Stations" in 13 languages.
Checked: tsc, i18n.mjs clean, screenshots in artifacts/trainworld/T-3625_*.png (real save, Manhattan z15).
Not covered: a selected station stays selected (inspector open) but unseen; flicker on toggle not examined.
