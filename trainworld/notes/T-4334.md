## 1. Where a mode shows today, and what the gear does

### 1.1 The rule

Every place a mode shows is one of two kinds:

- **Places that let you build or set up a mode before you have one.** The gear decides these.
  `modeShown(m) = gear has m ticked || the game has some of m`.
- **Places that show what the game already has.** Line panels, the Money tab's rows, the Lines gear, the diagram, the
  mode bars and the map are of this kind. Most already show a mode only when there is some, and the gear leaves them
  alone. One of them, Settings' Show modes row, lists every mode today and changes to "the modes this game has".

Train is always shown, and so is high speed: it is a track type and train type within rail, and was not on her list.
It can join the gear later with one row.

### 1.2 What "has some" means

The game has some of a mode when its world holds any piece of it, built or blueprint, switched off included:

| Mode | Counts |
|---|---|
| Tram | tram track (street or off-street), a tram stop, a tram line |
| Bus | a bus line (BRT included), a BRT station, a busway |
| Ferry | a ferry line, a port |
| Monorail | monorail track, a monorail station, a monorail line |
| Cable car | a cable car line, a cable car station |

Blueprints count, so a half-drawn cable car never strands its own tool. Kept bus stop points (T-4080) don't count:
they are forgotten at load once no bus line is near. One pass over the world gives the set, kept per world as
`lineVisibility.ts` `trackModes` (line 89) and `stationModes` (line 108) already are. Those two already sort track
and stations into modes and can be reused.

### 1.5 Places that keep naming modes, on purpose

- **The changelog** (ui/Changelog.tsx, ui/updates.ts): a record of what was added. It is also how a player learns the
  gear and a new mode exist. It keeps "added cable cars".
- **The wiki**: a separate site the game doesn't link to. Each mode gets its page as any mechanic does.
- **The side switch question** (`sideSwitch.ask`, "Switch trains, trams and buses to the left?"): it names what
  keeps to a side, including anything built later. Wording variants per mode mix would be four per side for little gain.
- **Settings, Real network, All's hover** (`settingsTab.realAllTip`, names trams): it describes the reference layer's
  OSM content, not modes the player builds.
- **"Show transit by mode"** (ui/SettingsTab.tsx:115-120): a general setting that names no mode.

### 1.6 Loaded, shared and viewed games

- **A loaded game with a mode the gear hides**: that mode shows everywhere, as if ticked, because the game has some.
  Its gear box stays unticked, with the hover "Shown while this game has some". The gear is never changed by a load.
- **A shared save opened as your own game**: the same.
- **Viewing someone's network** (Community, View): the viewed world decides "has some" for what is drawn and listed.
  The build tools are not used in a view.
- **The last one removed**: when the gear hides a mode and you remove its last piece, it leaves the build menus. Undo
  brings the piece back and the mode with it. Its tool, if it is on, stays on until you leave it (next section).
- **Older builds**: the gear writes nothing into saves, so it never stops a save opening anywhere. Saves with monorail
  or cable cars are refused by builds older than those modes, as any newer save is (T-4014).

### 1.7 Edge cases

- A tool whose mode stops being shown while it is on (you untick Bus with the bus tool on, or delete your last cable
  car with the cable tool on) stays on until you leave it; then its button goes. Nothing vanishes under the pointer.
- Unticking a mode never removes, switches off or hides anything that exists; the map is the Show modes row's job.
- The Select tool picking built track sets the drawing type to the pick's (blueprintPick.ts:349). A pick of busway
  means the game has bus, so the Busway type is shown then.
- Two browsers keep two gears; a game opened in each follows that browser's.

### 1.8 The gear itself

- **Where**: the Build tab's row under the 2 by 2 tool grid, after Bus, Ferry and later Cable car, at the row's end;
  alone in the row when all three are unticked. The same icon and popover as the Lines tab's gear
  (ui/LinesGear.tsx:16-73: Escape and a click away close it).
- **What**: a heading and one checkbox per mode with its icon in the mode's colour: Tram, Bus, Ferry, Monorail, Cable
  car. A box unticked for a mode the game has keeps its row, with the hover "Shown while this game has some".
- **Defaults**: Tram, Bus and Ferry ticked, Monorail and Cable car unticked, for new browsers and returning ones alike,
  so nobody's game changes when it lands.
- **Kept**: in ui.ts's store with the build settings (`saved.build.modes`, a box per mode; a missing one takes its
  default). No setting syncs through the account today, and this one doesn't start that.

## 2. Monorail (T-4335)

Her shape: a train that can't connect to train track, only at or above grade, a bit dearer at grade and a bit cheaper
elevated, steeper slopes allowed, top speed about 80-100 km/h, maybe tighter curve limits.

### 2.2 How it fits the track model

The busway (T-3378) is the model: a track kind in a family of its own.

- **Sim**: `TrackKind::Monorail = 5` (sim/src/track/params.rs:277-287; edge flags bits 8-10 hold up to 7, so it fits),
  family 3, and a `TRACK_SPECS` row (params.rs:360-470) with the values above: `v_cap_kmh` 80, `min_radius` 20,
  `level_mult` [refused x3, 0.6, 0.75, 1.125, 1.5], `terrain_mult` 0.5, `water_mult` 1, `road_crossings` true,
  `ramps_over` true, `conflict_s` 150, `platform` (40, 120), rail's section, margin, dwell and turnaround, and a car
  spec (US$3M, rail's running costs, `riders_scale` 1, fleet 7, so `FLEETS` in wasm.rs:318 goes 7 to 8 and the
  `VEHICLE` table in edits.rs:180 gains a train).
- **New rules in the sim**: a level below 0 refused for monorail (a new `Issue`; no kind refuses a level today); flat
  crossings with monorail on either side refused, where today tram and train may cross on the flat
  (capacity.rs:774-781). With the ramp switch on (T-1615), track drawn across other track at its level ramps over where
  that fits, so most crossings take care of themselves.
- **Joins**: nothing refuses a join between families today except at a busway. Since T-3889 rail and tram join, and
  validate.rs:159-162 refuses only a node where a busway meets other track (`BuswayJoin`). That check becomes a spec
  field, `joins_own_only` (true for busway and monorail), so a node where such a kind meets any other is refused. With
  joins refused, the train-line checks that bar only families 1 and 2 (`edge_barred`, net/edit.rs:40-43;
  `check_route`) never meet monorail track from rail, and a line on monorail track is a monorail line by its stations.
- **Code that reads `family != 0` as tram**: the street lead-ins (wasm/draw.rs:236) become tram and busway only;
  `ramp_floor` (params.rs:509-512) becomes a spec field (rail's 160 m, tram's and busway's 100 m, monorail's 135 m); the
  Select bar's retyping (`kind_ok`, wasm/rebuild.rs) refuses monorail both ways, as it does busway. Automatic flyovers
  (wasm/flyover.rs:205, 275) stay rail only: none on monorail, where a flying junction is rare. Fine as they are:
  `crossing_trams` (net/derive.rs:295) and the flat-crossing capacity code, since monorail has no flat crossings;
  upgrades (upgrades.rs:88) and crossovers' price and kind (cost.rs:739, wasm/crossings.rs:334), which already take a
  non-rail kind's own level table and kind; `node_kind`, twin.rs and perdir.rs, which compare families.
- **Lines**: a line on monorail track is a monorail line, as a line on tram track is a tram line (`st.tram`); one
  train type, no high-speed choice. Its vehicle noun stays "train", so none of the 109 strings that switch on the
  vehicle need a new branch.
- **Stations**: a train station on monorail track, or placed alone with the station bar's Monorail station kind,
  which lays its own piece of monorail track (as a tram stop does on open ground). Its platform stepper runs 40-120 m
  (2-6 cars), a setting of its own beside the train and tram ones (ToolBar.tsx `StationBar` has only those two),
  starting at 80 m. Auto over monorail track gives a monorail station (stationAuto.ts:45 tells only tram apart today).
- **Track tool**: a fifth track type, Monorail, its target max speed 80 by default (as high speed's is 320); quad not
  offered (as busway). While Monorail is the type, the level buttons -1 to -3 are greyed (both layouts of
  `LevelButtons`), with the refusal as their hover ("Monorail runs at ground level or above"); a level below 0 picked
  when switching to Monorail moves to ground, and the level keys for -1 to -3 do nothing.
- **Select bar**: no rebuild between monorail and other kinds; families can't join, so a rebuild would strand
  stations and lines.
- **Real network**: monorail track snaps to real monorails only, and train track stops snapping to them (today it does,
  docs/ui-building.md:655). The real-network sandbox starts already import OSM monorail routes (pipeline/realnet.py:278-281)
  but on train track; moving them to monorail track is a later task (stage M8). Under Passenger the layer leaves real
  monorails out (realNetwork.ts `PASSENGER`, realStations.ts `NOT_PASSENGER`), so while monorail track snaps it draws
  them, as tram snapping adds tram lines (`PASSENGER_TRAM`): a third `SnapMode`, `monorail`, with a FOLLOW row of
  monorail only (game/realFollow.ts), and train track's row drops monorail.

### 2.3 Once the track rework (T-3444) lands

Nothing in the rework's design changes. Its bundle carries a kind (notes/T-3444-design/01-model.md:58-59, 07-saves.md
"Bundle" flags), and monorail is one more value of it. Every track being a single track with its own way matches
beams. Crossovers, laid as short single tracks, are beam switches on monorail, at monorail's 150 s. If monorail lands
first, which is likely while the rework is paused, the rework's conversion carries the kind through, and its kind
list in 01-model.md gains Monorail.

### 2.4 Demand

Nothing new. A monorail line is a train line in every demand rule: the 2+2 minute boarding cost, train crowding, each
minute aboard counting 1. A figure diff on the real NYC save should show no change at all, since it has no monorail.

### 2.5 Saves

- Track kind 5 in edge flags, the header's `fleetMonorail` (absent for none) and `newHeadway.monorail`.
- The save level goes up one (`LEVEL` in sim/src/track/save.rs:135 and `SAVE_LEVEL` in workers/saveFile.ts:31, now
  42); older builds refuse these saves (T-4014).
- In the browser: the Monorail track type, station kind and platform length in the build settings. No line shape of
  its own: it takes the train's (1.9).
- Panic recovery keeps every fleet by name, outside the save (`Kept` in workers/clockPanic.ts:37-40, restored at
  141-145), and would set a fleet it doesn't list to 0. Stage M3 replaces that with one WASM `fleets()` and
  `set_fleets()` over `FLEETS`, so no new fleet needs listing there. The header's field lists (sharedSave.ts `FIELDS`,
  saveFile.ts `HEADER_FIELDS`) are typed by `SaveHeader` and fail the typecheck when a field is left out.

## 3. Cable cars (T-4336)

Her shape: Mi Teleferico style; straight spans only, waypoints allowed; very cheap to build; at or above grade; no
slope limit; very short headways; small low-capacity cars drawn as little squircles; no terrain yet.

### 3.1 Rail facts, decided

| Fact | Real | Game | Source |

### 3.2 How it fits: a line kind like ferries

The track graph is the wrong home. A cable car turns hundreds of cabins an hour at each end, and the track model's
rule is 15 turns an hour per platform (30 with a terminal). Every capacity rule would need an exception. The ferry
line model fits almost as it is (docs/track-and-service.md 6.9, sim/src/track/ferry.rs): a line with no track,
stations of their own kind with walking access, passages and complexes, a route of points each with a kind (stop or
waypoint) and a top speed, out and back, no capacity holds.

- **Sim**: a cable line is a ferry route (`line_ferry`, `FerryRoute`) whose medium is air. The twenty-odd places that
  know a route line by `line_bus` or `line_ferry` (`route_built`, `one_way_default`, the save's hand and manual
  blocks, `check_line`, paths, twins, legs, the snapshot's rail test) then cover it unchanged; only the boat's places
  (its size and fleet in `fleet_kind`, `set_ferry_boat`, the boat block) ask the medium. Air's legs are straight lines
  between stations and waypoints, so no water search. Speeds come from the cabin: 6 m/s on the rope, plus 40 s a station
  and 60 s at the ends. Acceleration becomes the medium's, passed to `profile`: 0.15 m/s² on water (`BOAT_A` today),
  1.0 in the air. An angle station is a slow stretch that `ferry::limits` lays through each waypoint of an air route
  (30 m at 2 m/s, about 13 s against running through), so `profile` still stops only where riders board. Cable car
  stations are trackless stations at level 0 (as ports, `Network::is_port`) with no berth: an air route's stop point
  lies at its station. Several cable lines may share one; any station may join it in a complex or by a passage. A
  cable station can't stand on water.
- **Water**: a leg over more than 1 km of unbroken water is refused by the sim, sampling each straight leg against its
  water mask as `bridged_water_through` does for track (T-1930), so edits and loads are checked alike. The ferry tool's
  water probe (`FerryWater.probe`) answers for one point only, so the tool shows the sim's refusal instead.
- **Fleet**: cabins, a fleet of their own (`FLEETS` one more again, in `fleets()`, 2.5); quote rows for stations,
  cabins, waypoints and the route's metres, as ferries have. Redrawing a built line's route (`set_ferry_route`, free
  on water) charges the rope and angle stations it adds; what it takes away refunds nothing, as removed built track
  doesn't.
- **App**: a cable line's row is a bus row with `cable` set, as a ferry's is a bus row with `ferry` set, so the bus
  panel (ui/inspector/BusInspector.tsx) is its panel, its station list as the ferry's port list.
- **Headways**: a floor of 12 s for cable lines. Today's floor is 60 s for every line (game/world.ts:525
  `HEADWAY_MIN_S`), plus and minus step whole minutes (`nextHeadway`) and a typed value rounds to 5 s
  (`headwayFromMinutes`), none of which reach 12 s. A cable line's headway is typed and shown in seconds, to the second,
  and steps 12, 15, 20, 30, 45 and 60 s, then as today; the floor and steps go by line kind. The sim has no floor of its
  own. A cable line's schedule shows each period's interval and no departure lists, and its timetable chart is left
  out, as a bus line's is.
- **Waiting**: a cable station holds a BRT station's waiting riders (`BRT_STATION_WAITING`, about 670, some 13 minutes
  of cabins at 3,000 an hour), not a port's 5,000: `LineSvc::waiting_cap` (track/service.rs:171) gives every stop of a
  ferry route `WAITING_CAP`.
- **The boarding account** works per departure. A cable line has 30-40 times a train line's departures (7,200 a
  direction a day at 12 s). Stage C4 measures it on a 10-line test. If it costs more than a tenth of today's account
  time, the account takes a line's cabins a minute at a time: five cabins as one departure of 50 riders. No player
  could tell, and that lie goes in DECISIONS.md if used.
- **Drawing cabins** (her note on call 3): they are trains to the renderer, which already draws every train as GPU
  instances placed from keyframes (map/renderer.ts), so a cabin costs what a train car does and needs no draw of its
  own. Picked: cabins are drawn only from zoom 14 in (trains from 10, `TRAINS_ZOOM`), where cabins 12 s (72 m) apart
  are about 10 px apart; further out the rope alone shows the line. A 3 km line at 12 s keeps about 100 cabins moving,
  ten lines about 1,000. The clock worker's hour of runs for the page grows by 600 a line; stage C5 measures that
  message.

### 3.3 Once the track rework lands

Cable cars don't touch track, so the rework changes nothing for them. Its station record needs to carry cable car
stations as it carries ports (trackless, no platform): one more row in its conversion list. Its line waypoints
(04-stops.md:132) are free, with no dwell or price; a cable car's waypoints cost time and money, and stay the cable
line's own.

### 3.4 Demand

Nothing new in the kernel. A cable line is a line like any other, as a ferry is (docs/demand-kernel.md, "Ferries"):
its stations are walked to as stations, its hop times are the cabins' run with the station times, and its cabins are
their riders in cars (10 at crush is a twenty-fourth of a car). It needs no new kernel flag: there's no fare row or fan
group to tell apart, and a cabin's crush load (10) is far from a bus's (80), so `bus_line` never takes it for a bus.
The rules assigned to it:
- **Boarding cost**: none, as on a ferry: the cabin is boarded at ground level where you walk in (trains and trams
  pay 2+2 minutes). It goes by the flag ferries use: cable lines join `pierLines`, which demandClient.ts:918 sets
  from `l.ferry` alone today.
- **Crowding**: as a train's, by its riders, as boats are.
- **Waits**: half the headway, so 6 s at 12 s, with no floor (buses have 2 minutes, T-4224, for their unreliability).
- **Each minute aboard**: 1. No cable car fans (call 2).
