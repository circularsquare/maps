# T-3196: Construct gone, no new lines, trains stopped (two Japanese players, live v0.2.1)

## The reports

Both through the feedback panel, kind "error", build `mwb4nsa9` (v0.2.1), no screenshot, no failure text.

- Kanto, 2026-11-11 06:59 UTC, Chrome 157, 16 GB, signed in: construction and adding new lines stopped working;
  is there a limit on the number of lines; rarely, people pile up at stations while every train stops.
- Rinkaru, 2026-11-11 02:21 UTC, Safari 26, not signed in: the Construct button for track and stations no
  longer comes back.

Kanto's account games (copied read-only to the agent's scratchpad, not kept in the repo): "Tokyo" went from
day 103 to day 123 with no construction at all while cash rose from 440 to 17,958 US$M (19 lines, 219
stations throughout), then built 4,832 US$M on day 124 between the 06:54 and 06:58 cloud saves, and the
report came at 06:59. Stuck, unstuck (a reload), stuck again: a state that lives in the page, not the save.
The 06:58 save opens on master with no error, answers the blueprint quote and adds a 20th line.

## Cause

The clock worker handles its messages one at a time, and Construct waits in that queue for the ground under
the blueprint (`groundForBlueprint`: the world's water, the terrain index and blocks, the zones' water masks,
the basemap's zoom 14 road tiles). The road tile fetch (`roadFeed.ts`) and the pack range fetch under every
water mask and street speed (`rangeFetch.ts`) had no time limit. A request the network never answers (a
dropped mobile or Wi-Fi connection, a laptop waking on another network) then held Construct for ever, and
behind it every later message: the blueprint quote (so Construct stays greyed), new lines, edits, and the
next hour's trips (so every train stops while riders keep arriving). Only a reload cleared it. Downloads
with a limit (60 s) still stopped the trains for up to a minute at a time, which fits "rarely".

Reproduced on master with the zoom 14 tiles held unanswered (scratchpad `hang.mjs`): Construct unanswered
after 90 s, the quote and `addLine` unanswered after 20 s each, and the trains' hour stuck at 32 while the
clock reached 58 at 100x. Live v0.2.1 has the same code (`groundForBlueprint`, `roadFeed.ts`,
`rangeFetch.ts` are unchanged since f5a2b979).

Ruled out: a line, station or id limit (none: ids are u32, `?synth=1&lines=500` runs 500 lines, Kanto's save
takes a 20th); the panic path (a model left down after more than 3 panics a minute or 8 in all also greys
Construct and refuses edits, but it says so: "The train simulation stopped working. Reload the page...").

## Fix

- Every clock worker download has a time limit: road tiles 20 s (`TILE_TIMEOUT_MS`), range fetches 60 s, the
  street speed head 60 s (the rest already had `GROUND_TIMEOUT_MS`).
- Construct waits at most 20 s in all for its ground (`GROUND_WAIT_MS`), then is refused with the words it
  already had ("The roads here did not load. Try again." or the terrain one); the downloads go on for the
  next press.
- While the queue waits on a download with the model at rest (an edit's or a ghost's water masks,
  Construct's ground: `parkedOn`), a new hour's trips are sent at once (`tripsNow`), so trains never stop for
  a download.
