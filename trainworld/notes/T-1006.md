# T-1006 Regions shaded by the zones' outlines; floating buttons; a blank start menu

Anita, 2026-10-23: the region shade missed Shanghai's coast and islands (Natural Earth and
geoBoundaries shapes against the basemap's coast), the eye buttons looked creepy in numbers, and the
start menu's world map showed every city as a red bubble. Built 2026-10-23.

## What changed

- **Shade**: `boundaryGeometry.outsideZones` cuts the shown zones (`zoneShown`) out of one world
  polygon, from the coarse outlines (`outlines.json`, read once and shared with the outline layer).
  Each zone ring is turned anticlockwise, shared directed edges cancel (the coarse file keeps shared
  runs vertex for vertex, T-799), the rest are chained into rings (sharpest right turn at a vertex
  with several ways out), clockwise rings (gaps the zones enclose) are dropped, and an outer ring
  inside a kept one is dropped. The holes go clockwise after the world ring. Same `inactive` colours;
  it draws over the outside shade, so a hidden place is both (light 28% in all, dark 64%).
- **realms.ts** no longer fetches `realms.geojson` (167 KB gzipped saved; `vite.config.ts` stops
  copying it). Button point and box come from the registry (zone nearest the mean of the realm's
  zone centres; the box centre put Spain's button on the Canaries). A station's realm: the zone
  whose coarse outline holds it, else the nearest zone centre. A split country's zone with no
  registry `realm` (new rows before the join) is always shown. `zoneFocus` skips `placeAt` when no
  shown zone's box is within `ZONE_REACH_M` of the view's centre.
- **Buttons**: `Icon.expand` (four arrows to the corners) replaces the eye; no box, `--map-ink` at 70%,
  full and heavier on hover; tag "<name>\nClick to show the cities there" (some names are plural, so
  no "its"). Always the icon alone, so the old zoom-3 switch went.
- **Start menu**: `intercityView.render` draws nothing while `choosing` and holds `cityOverview`, so
  the local bubbles and airport marks stay off too.

## T-1025: stray triangles, and the shade's own layer (2026-10-23)

Anita saw a long grey triangle from Shandong over Korea to Kyushu. Two causes, both general:

1. **The cut was not a valid polygon.** The coarse outlines overlap: neighbours from different
   sources cross (Zhoushan over Ningbo), and 558 of the 5,217 rings cross or touch themselves
   (Douglas-Peucker per ring, T-800; Tokyo's islands are little bow-ties). Cancelling shared edges
   left holes that crossed each other (344 crossings for Korea + Japan + China east). Earcut, given a
   hole that crosses another, bridges it from a point inside the other hole to that hole's far side.
2. **Even a valid cut breaks in MapLibre's tiles.** geojson-vt rounds every vertex to the tile grid
   (150 m at zoom 5, 5 km at zoom 0), and parts of the outline a few metres apart come out crossing;
   simplification makes it worse. Tried and dropped: one ring with slits for the holes (tolerance 0:
   earcut still drew a 0.4° triangle at Weihai in tile 5/26/12 with all China shown); the triangles as
   one GeoJSON MultiPolygon (rounding turns thin triangles inside out, and the fill bucket's
   `classifyRings` then takes one for a hole of the triangle before it); one feature per triangle
   (robust, but geojson-vt took 0.35 s a change for three realms and 2.3 s for all, in the worker).

Now: `outsideZones` computes the union exactly (shared edges cancel; the rest are split where they
cross or touch, on the integer grid; an edge is kept where the zones' total winding is 0 on one side
and not on the other, so the kept edges always close), joins the holes to the world ring by vertical
slits (`keyhole`; earcut's own hole joining took 12 s for all realms), and earcut cuts that ring into
triangles in Mercator. `shadeLayer.ts` draws them as a custom layer, positions as two floats taken
relative to the view's centre, so edges hold still at zoom 16. A self-crossing ring winding a loop
backwards over another zone cancels it: a sliver a few metres across is shaded there.

Check: `checks/t1025-shade.mjs` (every realm alone and five groups up to all: 0 points of a shown zone
shaded beyond 10 m of an edge, none shaded twice, open land and sea shaded once). Screenshots
`artifacts/trainworld/t1025-{before,after}-*.png` (the before build shows a band across Shandong and a
triangle over southern Kyushu at the same view).
