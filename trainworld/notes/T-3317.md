# T-3317: track and train bugs from feedback 11-12 (diagnosis)

Agent t3317, 2026-11-12, read-only on app/sim/data. Live is v0.3.2 = branch `tw/release-031`, cut from 3a442a6e. It
has T-3137 (lanes), T-3166 (quad HSR), T-3078 (HSR platform fix) and the loop rules (d8412b34); it does **not** have
T-3202 (trains no longer pass through each other on single track), T-3286 (platform lengthening), T-3297 or T-3296.
Several reports are about things already fixed on master and waiting for a publish.

## 1. Stations over 4 cars refused on high-speed track (Ben, Big Man)
Already fixed and live. Cause was `stateRows.ts` copying the raw `edge_kind` (1 = high speed) into the state's `kind`,
which the app reads as tram (T-3078, 51fd1c61, archived). In `tw/release-031`; Anita told both players on 11-10 and Big
Man confirmed on 11-11 that quad on HSR works too (T-3166). No regression found; nothing to do. Reach was every HSR player
for about 2 hours on 11-10.

## 2. Two opposite one-way singles meeting a double (anon, Japanese)
Not reproduced in the app, no save in the thread. Sim tests in `scratchpad/t3317/sim` (a copy of sim/ with scratch tests
`zz_*` appended to `lanes_tests.rs`):
- Double with singles drawn from its end afterwards (both hands): joins, ways and the move table are right
  (S1 joins left, S2 right, one runs in, one out, moves match). Setting the ways opposite moves the joins.
- Singles first (drawn separately or meeting at one node), double drawn afterwards: all three single ends keep
  `join [0,0]`. `end_joins` (sim/src/track/wasm/draw.rs) returns early when `!r.double` and only gives existing single
  ends a join where the route drawn leaves double track on the other side. So a double drawn at a node of singles
  leaves them unjoined: unset ways run both ways, and a set way is honoured in routing (`find_path` E to W: none) but
  the end has no join to draw it on a track, so the map moves a train across lanes with no crossover.
- The same family as 5174140f (anon, 10:47: "from each track of a double only one single can be extended, the second
  can't"): the second single from the same end meets `RadiusTooSmall` because the node's heading is fixed by the first.
Cause (likely, drawing side): missing joins when the double comes after the singles. Fix: let `end_joins` take the
`theirs` branch when the route itself is double and the node has single ends (give each the track beside it by lateral
order), plus a Rust test in `lanes_tests.rs`, then a browser look. 3-5 h. Needs her save or a repro in her words to be
sure; filed as T-3344 [P2]. Reach: players who build single pairs then upgrade, mostly Japan (left-hand), a handful.
Collision: quad2 and dbl3 do not touch draw.rs or lanes.rs (dbl3: rebuild.rs, links.rs; quad2: net.rs, world.rs, save.rs,
lines.rs). 10:50 report "trains on single track pass each other" is T-3202, on master, not live.

## 3. Local leaves a passing station before the express (F_Nickey_44, 11-12, also "pushes away the express")
Cause by reading (not reproduced): `overtake::Plan::sweep` plans a wait per slow train, but `service.rs::profile_for`
adds the plan's **averaged** wait to every train's dwell (`run.stop_holds`). A local whose express comes sooner or later
than the average leaves early or late; "pushes away the express" is the display guard holding the express behind it.
Also: the wait is dropped when it would pass the local's headway minus the spacing or one faster headway plus the lead
(T-3238, live), and each direction gets its own best express offset, so one phase cannot suit both. Fix: carry the
per-train waits (`trains_at` already returns them) into the timetable as per-departure holds, 10-16 h, touching
overtake.rs, service.rs, spacing.rs and the departures placement. Reach: passing-track users (Japan mostly), cosmetic
and in the figures' favour, not a stuck state. File **P3**, with T-3264 and T-3219 (filed as T-3343). No collision with
quad2/dbl3 now; T-3333's later stage (alternate trains per pair) also edits spacing.rs, so do this after it.

## 4. "Why isn't a loop line recognised" (anon, Chinese)
No bug. Anita decided (T-3140, 2026-11-10: "assume people do not want to loop") that a ring closes only on a right
click on its first stop ("Right click to close the loop" tag); live has this. The player probably built a ring and
expected it to close. Option, hers to decide: a hint or an "Close loop" button in the line panel when the last stop lies
on a ring of track back to the first. 2-3 h, P3, touches the line panel and `stopMarkers`; no collision.

## 5. loopo: lengthening a platform refused (account, zh-Hans, v mwczes3h)
Fixed on master by T-3286 (b6467f46: `lengthen_op` joins the pieces under the new reach), not live yet (v0.3.2 predates
it). Anita answered "yes, will fix". The part "or extend in the other direction not blocked by slopes" is a wish: the
platform grows evenly both ways from its node, so a ramp or junction on one side refuses it; shifting the node's
platform to the free side is a new feature (asymmetric platforms, about 6-10 h, P3, touches station placement and
saves). The T-3286 fix goes out with the next publish.
