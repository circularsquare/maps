graph 11.5 s, with the final at 11.4 s. Selecting a zone after the final (Osaka, coarse): graph in 0.3-0.4 s.

1. **The graph waits for a final for every zone, the zone in view too.** `tripLengths` asks the workers about the
   round shown with no wait; during a solve with the barrier every worker is already past it, so the answer is
   empty and the graph comes only with the zone's cluster's final or the solve's. It never asks the focus pool
   either. Asked of the next round with `wait` (as T-327's view questions are) it is answered at 32.7 s for Seoul
   and, from the focus pool, 18.1 s for Tokyo: 39 s and 54 s sooner on 2 workers.
2. **The selected zone's split itself waits for its cluster's final** (71 s): while the focus pool shows the zone
   in view, the cluster solved ahead (T-402) is put in only at its final, so its other zones show nothing
   from its rounds.
3. **Order** (which cluster goes ahead) matters only when the selected zone is in another cluster than the zone in
   view (the world save: Amsterdam in the second group, 11.5 s). On the Tokyo save the selected zone shares the
   zone in view's cluster, so no cluster order changes anything.
