## What changed

1. **Show all countries** removed (checkbox, its signal, its string). The list is what it showed by default: the
   reference country first, then every country with a built station, by name. The picker still offers the Money
   tab's country when it has no station built (as before).
2. **Money tab Fare heading**: Advanced, undo and the zoom pair sit in one centred inline-flex (`.h .right.fare-head`,
   gap 6 px), so all four have the same top and bottom (measured 240-265 at UI scale 0.9) and "Fare" keeps its
   baseline with the Advanced text. Minus and plus stay joined as one group, as in the panel's own header and
   everywhere else in the game.
3. **Amount boxes** in the panel's fare rows (base fare, rates, steps, transfer; not the countries' multiples): min
   width 64 px (was 56), figure right-aligned (they were centred), tabular digits (Nunito has them; the stepper already
   asked for them); the typing box matches. Measured in Nunito 14 px: seven characters fit ("+$55.50" and "+$0.138"
   54 px, "+¥1,500", "+₩3,250", "+₹1,250", "+€0.081" 54 px; box content is 64 - 8 padding = 56). Eight ("+$125.00",
   "+₩13,000", 62 px) widen that one box, which is rare. Checked with USD, EUR, JPY, KRW, INR on the staircase.
4. **Gap under "Fares by country"**: nothing else ever sits there. It was the column heads' row ("Multiple", "Base
   fare"), held at the fare rows' 28 px minimum with the heads pushed to its bottom and an empty first cell. Shrinking
   that row to its text only took heading-to-rule from 38 to 33 px and the empty line stayed, so the heading is now
   that row's first cell (bold, text colour, the list 14 px below the rows above as a heading was): heading to
   "United States" is one row pitch (25 px at UI scale 0.9), the rule under the whole heads row. A visible call she
   can reverse (back to a separate heading over a heads row of text height). The 8 px margin above the checkbox went
   with the checkbox.
5. **Steps in the rows** (the panel only; the Money tab's three rows always read as rates, so typing 0.4 into its
   "0 - [20] km" can't turn the row into "0 km" with no box): a stretch of 0.5 km or less that is not the last reads
   as one row "10 km" with the jump
   "+$2.50", no "per km". Threshold: Add point and the panel's drags make steps exactly `NODE_GAP_KM` (0.1 km)
   wide, but a sideways drag snaps to 1/200 of the view, which is 0.5 km at a 100 km view, so a riser dragged there
   can be 0.25 or 0.5 km; 0.5 km is under 3 px on the panel's graph at that zoom. The last stretch never reads as a
   step, since its rate carries on forever. The jump steps by the base fare's step ($0.25) from the shown figure,
   types, never goes below $0 (minus at $0 shakes); its width is kept and every later point moves by the same amount
   (the rows' usual rule, through `fromRows`). The stretch after a step reads from the step's distance ("10 - 40 km",
   "Past 70 km", also in the rate's hover). Typing the end of the stretch before a step moves the whole step with
   it, width kept (before, that box could only move the step's foot left, since its top had no box). The step row
   has no hover: it would repeat the row. Its bin stays in the bins' column (an unseen "per km" keeps its place, so
   the narrow layout lines up too). In the panel, typing an end within 0.5 km of the point before turns that row into
   a step and its box goes; undo, a drag, or the box of the row before (which moves the step) gets it back.
6. "Use the simple curve" -> "Use simple curve" (also the yes button of its question, same key).
7. Found on the way: Esc in a typed value's box in the panel closed the whole panel (the dialog's Esc handler ran
   after the box's). Now Esc there only cancels the typing.

Not changed: at a 480 px wide window the "Add point" / "Use simple curve" line is cut at the right (it was before,
a little more with the old longer label).

Docs: ui-panels.md "Advanced fares" rewritten for 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.
