# T-1847: a first line in Ceuta takes seconds to settle

Anita (2026-10-30): a first line in Ceuta, nothing else built in Europe, "took more than a few seconds
to calculate demand. We're prioritizing the currently viewed, right?"

Investigation only; no code changed (temporary timing marks in `demand.worker.ts` were removed).
Measured on my own Vite (port 6189), 2 demand workers (Anita's machine gets 3), headless Chrome,
a 3-station line of 2.4 km. Times are from the line's last edit (addLine). Script (scratchpad, not kept):
`ceuta.mjs`, with `ZONE=<id>` and `SAVE=<path>`.

## Answers

| case | first figures for the new line | viewed zone's own figures final | whole solve done |
|---|---|---|---|
| fresh game, Ceuta (ceu) | 0.6 s | 4.1-4.3 s | 4.1-4.3 s |
| fresh game, Melilla (mea, the same size and not rebuilt) | 0.6 s | 4.4-4.6 s | 4.4-4.6 s |
| Tokyo real-network save loaded (2 workers, 117-132 s to load), then the Ceuta line | 0.03 s | about 1-6 s | 109-123 s |

1. **It is not Ceuta-specific.** Melilla (same pack size, market pack 2.8 KB against Ceuta's 8.9 KB,
   no rebuild) takes the same 4.4 s. The rebuilt pack and its neighbours (Cadiz's market pack is the only
   other one it opens, 0.1 s to download, 0.7 s to open on the workers) cost nothing visible.
2. **Fresh game, where the 4 s goes.** The line is added at 0. Zone and market packs open 0.5-1.3 s
   later (packs download in 0.1 s, the workers open them in 0.7 s). Then 2.6-3.1 s is the
   corridor table: the first time a worker produces figures with a market part, `corridorTable()` in
   `demand.worker.ts` asks the sim for the world's corridors, 7.9 million place pairs. `corridor_pairs` takes 0.2-0.5 s,
   `corridor_modes` 2.2-2.4 s (two `pair_mode_rule` calls per pair), then the loop that keeps the 1.9 million pairs that have trips. Every worker
   does it for itself, all at once, once per page load. The solve itself is milliseconds
   (period solves 0.5-5 ms; the preview goes out 16 ms after the plan). Everything else is under 0.2 s.
3. **In a real save the table is made while the save loads**, inside the load's minutes, so the Ceuta line does not pay it
   (confirmed: in the Tokyo run both workers log the table before the line). What she sees there is
   the other thing below.
4. **Is the viewed zone first? Yes.** In the worker the zone in view's cluster is first in the order
   (`dirty.unshift` on `job.first`), solved through all four rounds in about 30 ms, and its results sent as a cluster result
   (the preview 16 ms after the plan); the other clusters follow. In the Tokyo run the line's early
   figures showed at once (the edit's own quick answer) and the Ceuta cluster's final landed within about 1-6 s
   (the worker finished it about 1 s after the pack opened; my sampling on the page was coarse once the page
   got busy). The status tag's own rule counts the viewed zone as final once its cluster is, so
   `viewFinal` is true throughout.
5. **What she most likely saw with a big network elsewhere:** the tag "Updating demand" and the clock
   capped at 4x for the whole solve, 109-123 s with Tokyo and 2 workers (about 75 s with 3). Opening the Ceuta
   and Cadiz market packs recomposes the market, so every cluster is dirty again and the whole Tokyo
   network is solved again (`pre-plan v3 clusters 2 dirty 2`), though Tokyo and Ceuta share no rider.
   Ceuta's own figures are done early; the tag and the cap stay up until Tokyo has been solved again.

## Is that expected?

The 3 s corridor table in a fresh game is a one-time cost per page load that nobody designed to be on the first line's
path; it is not a regression of the rebuild. The long "Updating demand" with a large network elsewhere is how the
solve works today (a market composition change dirties every cluster), not a Ceuta problem: any first line in a new region of a
loaded game does it.

## Fixes (none made: neither is small and obvious)

- **Corridor table (about 2.6 s of the 4.1 s).** `corridor_modes` computes both modes for all 7.9 M pairs but the
  table keeps only the 1.9 M with trips or commutes. Computing modes for those rows alone would cut
  `corridorTable` from about 2.6 s to about 1 s, but `ownModes` in `demandClient.ts` reads the full-size array
  (63 MB, sent to the page once), so the interface and the main thread's reader change with it (a WASM interface
  change: its own tree, figures to be checked equal with `calib --cells --exact`). Alternatives: build the table in
  idle time once the first market pack opens (no idle time on a first line), or cache the table in IndexedDB beside the demand cache.
- **Whole re-solve of unconnected networks.** A market pack opening in a region with no track to the existing
  clusters should leave their results alone (same idea as T-1609/T-1733's local solves, applied to a cluster
  whose market part's input did not change). A design task, not a patch.
- A cheap relief for the tag only: not showing "Updating demand" and the 4x cap while only clusters other than the
  viewed zone's solve is a product decision (the cap protects the workers' solve), so it is left to Anita.
