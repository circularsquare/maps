## What a country run changes, and what each did to a reload

- **Routed drives** (`market-drives.bin`, rebuilt at every join): their SHA is in every market pack's hash
  (`fetchMarket`), so every save's kept result missed on "pack market:...": a full load. Now a stand-in.
- **Market packs** (`market_zones` rewrites them all): the same. Now a stand-in.
- **Registry, market rows, zone names, corridor file** (app/src/game, in DEMAND_KEY): already a stand-in (T-4202).
  `register --trips` writes local trips into the registry only, not into city packs.
- **City packs** of the run's own rows (and a joint group's): still a full load for a save covering them; their cells
  could be renumbered under the kept bubbles and splits.
- **Which zones' markets are open** (a final written while a city off the network was selected keeps its market):
  missed on "zones" before; now a stand-in, with the miss reason "markets" when `?perfOff=standIn`.

## The rule now (workers/demandCache.ts `noStandIn`)

A kept result stands in when its key differs only in the demand code's version, the worker count or the market
(which market packs, their bytes, the drives in their hashes). Format, flags, the zones, their city packs and their
levels must match. `sameKey` now tells "markets" from "zones". Market places are append only (world_run.py
`unregister`, market_zones.py), so a kept corridor book names this build's places by index.

## What can go wrong under a stand-in, and what was done

- **Places added since** (every country run adds some): the kept book's per-place arrays (totals, own, apart) are
  shorter than this build's place list; `marketPairs` (game/corridorFigures.ts) would read past the end: NaN totals
  for every new zone. `fromStored` now pads a short book with zeros (one padded copy per book, the days still share it),
  so new places read no trips until the fresh final. The other readers (`withinOf`, achievements' `corridorSums`,
  CityInspector's `elsewhere`) already guarded.
- **Corridor rows** of a changed market: the workers' book has another key, so rows asked for the kept book come back
  none (`rowsFor`), and only the rows kept with the result (`stored.rows`) show; a place's trip list waits for the fresh
  final. Rows and the kept rail figures never mix two tables (equal keys mean the same table).
- **Markets differing**: the shown figures include (or lack) one market's trips for the minutes of the solve; the
  cache's log entry carries the kept markets, and `covers` reads only the fresh solve.
- After the stand-in, `cachedStandIn` makes the fresh final write over it (T-4202's path, unchanged).
