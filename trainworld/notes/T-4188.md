# T-4188: a Community save that never loads on a phone (agent phoneload, 2026-11-24)

From T-4184: Community save jwxidghd did not finish loading in 10 minutes on an emulated iPhone.

## The save

Community share jwxidghd, rev 1 ("Japan, Taiwan, The Philippines"), a Tokyo start: 42 zones open (Japan from Hokkaido
to Okinawa, Busan, Taiwan, Manila; 4 fine), 370 lines of which 92 are bus lines, 1,858 stations, game day 1,784. 557 KB
as shared; 1.5 MB of header (1.2 MB of it `regions`), 510 KB of network. Downloaded to scratch, deleted after.

## The cause: one load runs the whole network's settle 20 times

Not memory, a stall or the emulation: the clock worker is busy the whole time, on desktop as on the phone (Chrome's
phone emulation does not slow the CPU). A CPU trace of the clock worker in its first 60 s: 33 s in `TrackApi.load`
(`openNetwork`), 20.5 s in `add_pack_streets` (`ensureStreets`).

1. **`load(net)`**: 21-28 s in the game (dev build, desktop), 16.5 s natively (`edit_bench`), of which the settle of
   train counts (`settle_unpaced`) is 14.8 s: all 80 rounds (`SETTLE_ROUNDS`), about 150-185 ms a round over 370 lines.
2. **Then the street speeds under the bus lines, one zone at a time** (`masksForLoaded` -> `ensureStreets` for the 19
   zones the 92 bus routes meet). Each zone's `add_pack_streets` calls `rework_buses`, which runs
   `settle_train_counts` over every line (settle, up to 3 pacing rounds, the weekend's settle) however few buses it
   retimed: 34-58 s a zone in the game, 19 of them.

Desktop, current code (dev build, emulated phone and desktop do the same work), import to network open:

| step | s |
|---|---|
| water masks (47 zones) | 2.2 |
| `load(net)` | 21.4 |
| street speeds: tyo (48 bus lines), osa 21, mai 5, smg 5, fuk 3, sag, ngs, oky, kgs, ymg, ..., yil 12, zhb, tcg, gnm, szo, ngn, nig | 34.6-51.8 each, 762 in all |
| network open | **783 s (13 min)** after the import; the map and lines come in at 801 s |

A production build on desktop (3 demand workers, busy machine) had no network after 15 min; T-4184's phone run stopped
at 10 min. A real phone's cores are slower than this machine's, so its load would be longer still.

### Why one settle is so long here: it never holds still

Natively, in the load's settle: round 1 moves 156 lines' rates (the save's rates are not this build's settled ones),
and from round 19 on 10-13 lines keep trading well under 1% of their rates every round, never repeating a state in 80
rounds (rates and round trips hashed), so the settle always runs to its cap. The lines (round 76-79): 押上線、浅草線
(8.31 -> 8.22 -> 8.25 tph), 埼京線, 各駅停車, 東横線(特急Fライナー)..., 横浜線, 本線、浅草線、押上線(快速), 通勤準急,
横須賀線、総武快速..., 成田エクスプレス and two 普通: Tokyo trunks sharing full track. Every edit pays it too:
`edit_bench` on this save, natively, a headway set 32-35 s, back to counts 4.8-6.1 s, train counts 5.8 s, undo 1.5-1.7 s.

## A fix, tried, not landed: the street speeds in before the load

A prototype (`artifacts/trainworld/t4188/instrumented-and-prototype.patch`, the `PHONELOAD prototype` hunk in
`masksBeforeLoad`): the street speeds of every zone the save's track meets, into the empty model before `load(net)`,
so the load's one settle already has them and `masksForLoaded`'s `ensureStreets` finds nothing to add:

| | current | prototype |
|---|---|---|
| streets before the load | - | 47 zones, 1.6-2.3 s |
| `load(net)` | 21.4 s | 14.6-15.0 s |
| street speeds after the load | 19 settles, 762 s | none |
| import to network open, desktop | 783 s | 19.7 s |
| import to network open, emulated iPhone | over 10 min (T-4184) | 17.7 s |
| import to trips calculated, emulated iPhone | - | 400 s |

Not landed because figures can move: today the load settles with no street speeds and then once per bus zone with
them; the prototype settles once, with them. On a network whose settle holds still that should end at the save's own
rates (T-2499's aim, in `masksForLoaded`: "the load's network is the save's alone (its buses timed on them)"), arguably closer than
today's, but it is not shown to the bit, and on this save (never still) the end state differs. To land it:

- read the bus routes' points from the save (a `TrackApi.save_bus_points`, as `save_points`) and fetch only those
  zones' streets (19 here, not 47: each is a few MB downloaded and held in the clock worker);
- compare a bus-heavy real save's figures after the load (rail trips, line rates) before and after.

## Memory once it loads (prototype, after the network is in, trips still calculating)

| context | desktop (MB) | emulated iPhone (MB) |
|---|---|---|
| page | JS 100-130 + buffers 325-341 | JS 131 + buffers 324 |
| clock worker | JS 124 + buffers 276-278 + WASM 366 | JS 126 + buffers 286 + WASM 380 |
| demand workers | 334 + 334 WASM, one at 1,262 with the market | one: JS 21 + buffers 39 + WASM 561 |
| demandSums worker | | JS 147 + buffers 28 |

With the prototype the save loads on the emulated iPhone: network open 17.7 s after the import, the clock started at
386 s, trips calculated at 400 s (17,287,850 rail trips a day; desktop, with the market, 888 s and 17,577,706, a busy
machine). At the final the phone's contexts hold about 2.1 GB (page 182 + 403, clock 130 + 289 + 380, demand
22 + 72 + 561, demandSums 63 + 2), above the 1.6-2.0 GB tabs at which T-3160's iPhones crashed (not the tab's own
total: this driver did not sample processes). So on a real phone this save's next wall is memory: the page's and clock
worker's buffers, then the solving worker's WASM.

## Left (for new tasks)

- The load fix above (small: one WASM reader, one ordering change; a figures check on a bus save).
- The settle that never holds still on networks like this one: 80 rounds a settle, every edit 5-35 s natively. Stopping
  early (a tolerance) breaks the settle's to-the-bit rule (T-3233); a design call.
- The page's and clock worker's buffers (about 325 and 278 MB here): T-4184's drawing state (`netCache`, the bands'
  `memo` and `orders`, `posted`), which scale with a player's track, are the likely holders; not snapshotted here.
