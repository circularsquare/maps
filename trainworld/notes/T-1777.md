# T-1777: track ghosts stuck on the screen

Anita, 2026-10-29: two small squares sit at one screen spot after zooming out, whatever the pan
(screenshot over Singapore in the New York save).

## What they were

The select tool's drag squares (`endMarkers.ts`, class `pi-mark`) at the two ends of a blueprint
piece. On the globe (Settings > Map > Globe, zoom under 12) a point on the far side projects to NaN
(`mercToCss`). `translate(NaNpx, NaNpx)` is refused by the browser, so the element kept the transform
of the last frame its point was on the near side; and NaN passed the "off the screen" test
(`x < -8 || ...` is false for NaN), so the mark stayed shown. Two ends of one piece, a few px apart:
the "2 squares".

Repro (headless, real save, globe): a blueprint piece in Manhattan, the select tool, the pointer by
it, the wheel zooms out, the view jumps to Singapore at zoom 10.5, then pans 180 x 90 px. Before:
2 `pi-mark`s at `translate(12px, 401px)` and `translate(18px, 397px)` through the pan. After: none.
The flat map never showed it (every projection is finite there).

## The owner

`map/screenPlace.ts` `placeAt(el, x, y, dx, dy, round)`: every DOM mark placed through the overlay's
projection goes through it; a non-finite point hides the element (visibility, so the callers' own
display switching is untouched) and a finite one shows and moves it, writing only what changed.
`onScreen` is the matching off-screen test that is false for NaN. Placers that already switch
visibility themselves check for NaN in place.

The brief's wider design (request ids, ending events) was audited and not needed: every placer is
re-run on each MapLibre frame (`overlay.afterMapFrame`), so a camera move re-places it, and each
async preview already drops a stale answer by its own key.
