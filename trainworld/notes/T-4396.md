# T-4396: A second try for a one-worker load out of memory

Agent `oomretry`, 2026-11-27.

## What happened before

A load that ran out of memory ends in one of two ways (`app/src/game/oomRetry.ts`, `loading.ts`, `demandClient.ts`):
1. **Tab death** (iOS kills the tab or Chrome's renderer crashes; `sessionStorage` keeps `tw-load-started`): on the next
   page load, `main.ts` calls `lastLoadDied()` then `retryDiedLoad()`. When `ran <= 1` (`d.workers ?? poolSize()`),
   `retryWorkers()` returned `null` ("it ran 1 solving worker"), so `main.ts` called `loadCannotFinish(outOfMemoryText(), true)`
   with `game = true`. On `LoadingScreen.tsx`, `{!game && ...}` hid "Play without intercity trips", leaving the player
   with only "Try again" (which reloaded into the same crash) and "Submit feedback".
2. **In-page worker OOM** (`demandClient.ts` `workerDied`): with `computeN > 1`, `memoryShort` halves workers; with
   `computeN === 1`, the worker is replaced once like a panic, and a second OOM calls `fail()` -> `loadFailed()`.
   `retryLoadsInPage` checked `retryWorkers(d)` (which returned `null` for `ran <= 1`), so it fell through to
   `LoadingScreen.tsx` (`game = false`, offering "Play without intercity trips" if `!marketOff()`, else "Play without
   demand", never less detail) or `DemandStopped.tsx` mid-game.

## The change

- **Automatic retry** (chosen over a button on the OOM screen): matches T-3933's fewer-cores retry, handles both tab
  deaths and in-page OOMs uniformly, and avoids trapping players on the OOM screen when `game = true`. Because it flips
  the real Settings toggles (`dropMarket()` -> `intercityTrips: false`, or `setDisplay("coarseDemand", true)`), the
  player can turn intercity trips back on or less detail back off in Settings (or the start menu's gear) at any time.
- **Step order** (`app/src/game/oomRetry.ts` `retryPlan`):
  1. `ran > 1`: unchanged T-3933 path (halves workers via `rememberPoolCap`, including for a Cores slider pick).
  2. `ran <= 1` and `!marketOff()`: retries once with `dropMarket()` (`intercityTrips = false`), kind `"intercity"`.
  3. `ran <= 1`, `marketOff()`, and `!coarseDemand() && lodMode(true) === "coarse"`: retries once with
     `setDisplay("coarseDemand", true)`, kind `"detail"`.
  4. Otherwise (already retried, or both intercity trips off and less detail on, or no slot): returns `null`, ending on
     today's out-of-memory screen with its text unchanged.
  Settings are only mutated in `retryDiedLoad()` on the reloaded page (never in `retryLoadsInPage()` before reload), so
  an in-page reload does not see `marketOff()` already flipped and double-step into `detail`, and a failed save with no
  slot leaves settings untouched.
- **Early-death slot fix** (`app/src/game/loading.ts` `startGameWait`): recorded `choice.slot` into `mark.slot`
  immediately when opening a saved slot, rather than waiting for the clock worker's `slots` message (`clockClient.ts:2024`).
  Without this, a saved slot dying 2 s into `"demand game"` had no `slot` on its flag and could not retry.
- **Notice** (`app/src/ui/RetryNote.tsx`, `app/src/game/loading.ts`): `loadRetried` is now `signal<RetryKind | null>(null)`
  (`"cores" | "intercity" | "detail"`). `RetryNote` shows one short line at the bottom of the map (`z-index: 8`, above
  the loading card) from the start of the retried load until 45 s after ready (or until closed). `diedLoadReport` also
  distinguishes the three retry kinds.

## What "demand game" allocates in the first 2-11 s on Tokyo (read-only)

In `demandClient.ts:2956`, `noteLoad("demand " + stageInHand(parts), ...)` logs `"demand game"` while `parts.game < 1`,
i.e. until `submitNetwork` sets `job.netAt` (when the clock worker finishes building the network). Before `netAt`, the
tab allocates concurrently:
1. **Main thread**: MapLibre (`createMap`), basemap tiles, city boundaries, realm overlay, `coarseOutlines`, and the
   `shade` / `outlineTopo` workers.
2. **Clock worker** (`sim_bg.wasm` instance #1): reads, decompresses, and parses the save from IndexedDB, builds the
   track graph, schedules, capacity, and boarding structures, and posts `stationsAhead` early.
3. **Demand pool** (`demandClient.ts` `constructor` + `stationsAhead`, lines 1218-1219, 1332-1375):
   - On a 4 GB Windows PC (`mobileDevice() === false`, `smallDevice() === true`), `phoneLoad()` is `false` so
     `auxShared` is `false`: it spawns **2 demand workers** (1 solving + 1 aux, `sim_bg.wasm` instances #2 and #3).
     On Android Chrome 4 GB (`mobileDevice() === true`), `auxShared` is `true` (1 demand worker).
   - `startFocus()` is skipped (`smallDevice()` is true).
   - On desktop 4 GB, `intercityTrips` defaults to `true` and `coarseDemand` to `false`. As soon as `stationsAhead`
     arrives (still inside `"demand game"`), `demandClient` downloads and opens **Tokyo (`tyo`, 70.3M local trips)** at
     fine LOD (`level = 0`) in **both** demand workers (`fetchCity("tyo", "full")` copies the pack buffer per worker),
     and downloads/opens the market packs for `tyo` and its neighbouring Japan zones (`fetchMarket`, line 1365).
   - On a 4 GB PC whose browser is already near 3.3 GB, those simultaneous WASM heaps + fine `tyo` pack + market packs
     + clock worker save load hit the limit 2 s after the pick, before `submitNetwork` ever arrives.

## Verification

- `npm run check --prefix trainworld/app`: clean (`tsc --noEmit`, exit 0).
- Game run on `:6110` (`tyo`, `?demandWorkers=1`, real saved slot `slot-e6b98571-4551-479f-ba9f-59686eb6e245`, 58.5 s):
  1. 1-worker died load (`intercityTrips: true, coarseDemand: false`) -> reloaded automatically with `marketOff() === true`,
     `intercityTrips: false, coarseDemand: false`, notice `"This game ran out of memory, so it loaded again without intercity trips."`.
  2. Second death (`retry: "intercity"`) -> ended on today's OOM screen (`"Ran out of memory. Close other tabs and try again."`),
     no notice, no third load.
  3. 1-worker died load (`intercityTrips: false, coarseDemand: false`) -> reloaded automatically with `coarseDemand: true`,
     notice `"This game ran out of memory, so it loaded again in less detail."`.
  4. 1-worker died load (`intercityTrips: false, coarseDemand: true`) -> ended on today's OOM screen, no retry.
  5. In-page OOM via `tw.failDemand` on 1 worker during load -> saved, reloaded automatically, came back with `marketOff() === true`
     and `"This game ran out of memory, so it loaded again without intercity trips."`.
