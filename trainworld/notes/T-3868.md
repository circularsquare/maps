# T-3868: big save stuck calculating trips after memory restarts

Agent bigload, 2026-11-20. Her save `artifacts/trainworld/saves/na-day-491.save` (174 lines, 2,240 stations, 129 zones
reached, Mexico City, New York and Philadelphia fine), my Vite 6157, headless Chrome on 4 pinned cores
(`play.mjs --cores 4 --query debug=1&demandWorkers=auto`, so the page's own pool rule runs), while gapfill's join held
8 cores, as on her machine. Traces: a 1 s sampler of the status tag (text, hover, bar width), the loading card, the
solve in hand and each worker's memory, and of each worker's `work` message
(scratch `bigload/trace.jsonl`, `summ.py`, `worksumm.py`, `sampler.js`).

## What happens on this save

- **One solving worker.** 16 threads give 3, and `capAhead` cuts them to 1 before the packs open ("about 1250 MB a
  solving worker by the save's zones" against T-3373's 1.4 GB share). Worker 0 then holds 1,319 MB of WASM (need
  1,443), the aux worker 353-385 MB.
- **A whole solve takes 3.5-5.5 minutes**: the load's 236-245 s, an edit's whole stage 20 units of 8.7-9.7 s (200-210 s;
  311 s in a later run with the machine busier), a cached load's re-solve 243-325 s. An edit's local stage (Mexico City
  alone) takes 4.7-6.9 s.

## Causes

1. **"Calculating trips again after freeing memory" with no memory freed.** The phrase is the hover's line while
   `memoryResolve` is set, which `shrink` set on every pool cut, including `capAhead`'s at every big save's load. It
   stays set until a full final lands. Opening her save from its slot (cached result: the card goes at 22 s), the
   tag said "Calculating trips here…", then "Calculating trips in 129 cities…", both with that hover, for the whole
   4-minute re-solve; an edit before that final replaces the solve and keeps the hover on. No real memory restart
   (T-1205) happened in four sessions of 15-25 minutes, edits and zooms included: worker 0's WASM 1,319 MB against a
   need of 1,320-1,464 MB, where a restart needs the WASM 15% and 384 MB over the need (need at most 935 MB). With one
   worker a restart would only follow a large close (zones going coarse after 10 minutes left alone took the need
   from 1,455 to 1,320 MB on phl, far from it). Her own report (T-3781's diagnosis) would show whether her pool had
   more than one worker (for example "Use more cores" on, which skips the share), where cuts and growths do restart.
2. **"Fills, then restarts."** Each solve had its own bar from 0. An edit made while an edit's whole solve runs is held
   (T-1734) until that solve's final, then solved whole again: bar 94% -> 0%, about 3.5 minutes more each time. Building
   in Mexico City every couple of minutes, the bar fills and restarts without end, and the load's re-solve never lands.
   The load's phases do the same: the card's bar (98%), then "Calculating trips here…" from 0, then "in 129 cities" from 0.
3. **The 90% stall: the bar measures the wrong thing.**
   - A whole solve under the barrier (a load, a cached load's re-solve, a memory re-solve) is measured by rounds
     published, and on one worker all four rounds land in the last 1.2 s (roundAt 398.5, 398.8, 399.1, 399.7 s for a
     solve sent at 73.8 s). Before: 0% for 130 s, then 50% (the zone in view's cluster) for 110 s, then done.
   - The load model's estimate (`solveStage`, used by the card and `solveLeft`) expects round 0 at about 40% of the
     solve; past that its time left is 0.2 x the time so far, so the share closes in on 1/1.2: the bar sat at 75-84%
     for the last 150 s of a 325 s solve. This is the "fills to 90% and stalls" shape, and it gets worse the slower the
     machine runs against the model (her 8 busy cores).
   - An edit's solve is measured by units done, and units differ ten times in length: after a restart, 60 units, the
     first 18 at about 9 s each, the last 42 in 5 s together, so the bar stood at 31% when the final landed.
   - The time-based fallback (`timeShare`, the share of the last such wait) caps that wait at 120 s and the bar at 95%,
     so any longer wait sits at 95%.
   - The hover's own time left for an edit's solve (`editLeft`, T-3599) is good on this save: "About 5 min" said, 300 s
     taken; "About 7 min" with a held edit, 400 s taken; "About 3 min", 195-205 s.
4. **Zooming out.** Zoomed out, no zone is in view, so the tag shows nothing while a whole solve runs with no edit
   behind it (`viewRecomputing` is false; T-1851 ties the tag to the zone in view). The work goes on: back in, the bar
   was where it would have been (2% -> 11% after 30 s out; 20% -> 46% for an edit's solve). Where the bar was time
   based (`timeShare`), hiding the tag restarted its clock, so it came back at 0. Level changes on a view (a zone
   going fine 2 s after it is in view, Boston here) did not restart a solve in flight; they wait for it.
5. **After a real restart** the tag shows nothing for the 40 s the replacement reopens its packs, then the re-solve
   (190-220 s) by units, the bar reaching 31%.

## What I changed (tree bigload)

- `demandClient.ts` `shrink`: `memoryResolve` only when a game already solved is cut (no load in hand, no cached
  result shown, a final shown); a whole game's load clears it. The hover line now shows only after a real restart or
  a cut of a solved game.
- `StatusTag.tsx`: the solve's bar follows the time left the hover shows: (time since trips started being calculated)
  / (that + time left), kept from going back (`keepPlace`), at most 97%, the same through a held edit's solve (a
  break of under 1 s counts as the same wait) and while the tag is hidden zoomed out. Where no time left is known
  (a fresh worker's first units) it falls back to units done as before.

## Options (not built)

- **A. Two solving workers on saves this size** (T-3373's share at 2 x 1.6 GB = 3.2 GB of an 8 GB budget, about 40%):
  a whole solve's 5 periods split 3 + 2, about 60% of the time (an edit's whole stage about 125 s instead of 205 s;
  the load about 150 s instead of 240 s; not measured), for about 1.6 GB more memory. Or the share at 20% only lets
  New York's real save keep 3 (DECISIONS 2026-11-16); this save needs 40%.
- **B. A real estimate for barrier solves**: let barrier solves send `work` as edits' do (the line in `receive` drops
  them) so `editLeft` times them by units with each population's last pass, and drop the round model there. About
  1-2 hours; it would fix the cached load's crawl (75-84% for 150 s) and give the hover a time left during a load's
  re-solve, which it has none of now.
- **C. Held edits**: an edit made during an edit's whole solve now waits for it and then solves whole again, so two
  edits 100 s apart cost 400 s, three about 600 s. Sending the newer network at once (dropping the running whole
  solve) would end at about 205 s after the last edit; it gives up T-1734's guarantee that some final lands while
  the player keeps building. A middle way: drop the running whole solve only while it is under half done.
- **D. Tag zoomed out**: show "Calculating trips in 129 cities…" (string reused) with its bar while a whole solve
  runs and no zone is in view, so zooming out never looks like lost work. Small (under an hour), but it changes
  T-1851's rule that the tag is about the zone in view.
