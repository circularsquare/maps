# T-4231: Apply to all lines misses some lines' weekend percent

## Cause

`Network::set_weekend` (`sim/src/track/net/edit.rs:403`) records each edited line in `Touch::weekend` (not `Touch::lines` or `Touch::schedules`), so a weekend-only edit settles only weekends and leaves weekdays untouched.

In `TrackWorld::commit_with` (`sim/src/track/world.rs`):
- `note_lines` (`world.rs:2221`) called `self.svc.note_changed(&lines)` only for `self.lines_touched(t, der)`, which does not include `t.weekend`.
- `settle_weekend` (`world.rs:1194-1196`) only called `self.svc.note_changed(&moved)` for lines whose `line_tph_rest` changed.
- `place_weekend` (`world.rs:543-550`) only called `self.svc.note_changed(&moved)` for lines whose `line_phase_rest` or `line_split_rest` changed.

So any line whose weekend setting (`line_weekend`) changed — share percent or manual flag — without changing its weekend rates or phases was never passed to `Service::note_changed`, and therefore omitted from `TrackApi::take_changed_lines()` (`sim/src/track/wasm/snapshot.rs:25`). That covers:
- lines with small fleets where `ceil(n * 0.7) == n` at every level (all counts <= 3),
- lines capped by track capacity whose weekend rate stayed at the weekday cap,
- lines switching between manual counts and a percent when the counts match,
- planned/broken lines with no running service,
- and the same lines on `undo`/`redo` or single-line weekend edits.

Before T-4113 (`df23c836d`, landed 2026-11-23, after 0.4.5 shipped 2026-11-20), `StateRows.lines` (`app/src/workers/stateRows.ts:754`) re-read `weekendOf(a, id)` for every line on every state. With `StateRows.bounded = true`, lines omitted from `take_changed_lines()` kept their previous `own.weekend` (100% / empty), leaving 61 of 109 running lines on the NYC save stale after "Apply to all lines" at 70%.

## Fix

- `sim/src/track/world.rs` (`TrackWorld::note_lines`, line 2226): also call `self.svc.note_changed(&t.weekend)`. `TrackApi::line_stamp` (`sim/src/track/wasm/touched.rs:81`) already hashes `line_weekend` and `line_row_weekend`, so `take_changed_lines()` passes every line whose weekend setting changed (apply-all, single-line share/counts/headways, twins, undo/redo) and still drops no-op/rolled-back lines. Weekdays and demand (`svc.demand`) are untouched.
- `sim/src/track/wasm/changed_tests.rs`: added `weekend_edits_list_lines_whose_rates_do_not_move` covering `set_weekend_share_all`, `undo`, `set_line_weekend_share`, and `set_line_weekend_counts` on a 3/2/1-train line whose 70% counts equal its weekday counts.
