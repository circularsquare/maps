## T-4081 how it works today
The place picker sends `city` (a city id, country_xx or region_x). The server includes a share in a place only when its `Cities` map has points > 0 there. `Cities` is the sharer's account game's points per city (top 64), copied at share time. So a save with track in Detroit but no points there at that moment (trips still calculating, a bus-heavy save, no fares yet) is missed, as is a city outside the top 64.

## Plan
The client already knows the zones holding stations: the slot summary's `cities` (clock.worker.ts, stations per city, built ones). Share sends `zones` (up to 64 ids) in the meta. Server stores `Zones` on the share (index.json, omitempty), and a place matches a share when it has points there OR any zone in the place. Biggest still ranks by points only. The card's city falls back to a zone in the place.
Old shares have no zones, so they behave as before until their owner shares again. A backfill from the stored drawing would need city outlines on the server; not built. Needs a server deploy (the game side sends `zones` and an old server ignores it).
