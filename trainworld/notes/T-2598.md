## What is in the establishment file (2025)
Columns: Bairros SP/Fortaleza/RJ code, CNAE 2.0 class and subclass, Distritos SP, Qtd Vinculos CLT / Ativos / Estatutarios, Municipio (IBGE 6-digit
code), Natureza Juridica, Regiao Adm DF, Tamanho Estabelecimento, Tipo Estab, UF, IBGE Subsetor, **CEP Estab** (8 digits, leading zero dropped).
So the grain is the establishment's CEP, with jobs, sector and public/private. No coordinates, no address. The worker (vinculos) files add pay,
occupation, hours, but are not needed for a jobs map.
Every row of the 5 Recife-area and 2 Goias municipios had a CEP.

## Geography: CEP, joined to points by CNEFE 2022

  5 health, 6 other purpose, ...), DSC_ESTABELECIMENTO. Also `Agregados_por_CEP/` (a 9 MB national zip, counts per CEP, no coordinates).
  CNEFE has no job counts: it says where non-residential addresses are (Recife: 72,760 other-purpose, 1,689 schools, 1,519 health).
- Join test, Recife: RAIS jobs on CEPs that also occur in Recife's CNEFE: 83.2% of 767,681 active jobs. The rest are single-user "CEP unico"
  codes (big buildings, agencies) and CEPs of neighbouring municipalities' records; those could be put at the municipio's centroid of its non-dwelling
  addresses, or at the CEP's address points found via any municipio's CNEFE file.
- Within a municipio a CEP covers a street segment or a neighbourhood (rural and small towns: one CEP for the whole town), so a CEP point is
  coarser than OSM but far finer than the municipio. Recife has 5,593 CEPs with jobs, Goiania 10,904.

So RAIS formal jobs are about 60-70% of the proxy total, consistent with informality of around 40% (see below). The totals agree in size, so RAIS
would mainly change where the jobs sit, not how many.
Head-office effect, as LODES (demand-worldwide.md "Job locations from registers"): jobs are counted where the establishment is registered, and the
largest CEPs hold a lot: Recife's top 3 CEPs 50810900, 50050000, 50030230 hold 46,821 / 45,302 / 33,117 jobs (6%, 6%, 4%); Goiania's top CEP 74643010
holds 73,644 (10%). Camaragibe's top CEP (54590000, the whole town centre) 21%. These lumps are partly real (a state government compound, a
hospital) and the pull cap (20x) and `PLACE_JOBS_CAP` already tame lumps. Not checked: the exact CEPs' buildings.
Would it beat OSM jobs? OSM in Brazilian cities beyond Sao Paulo is patchy for shops and offices; RAIS counts every formal establishment with its
size, so it replaces a guess about job count by place with the real count, at CEP grain. Not measured against OSM at a point level here (no pipeline code
allowed). The proxy's weakness is outside the centres, where OSM tags are thin, so RAIS should help most in mid-size cities and the interior.

## Informal work
RAIS holds formal jobs only (signed work card, public servants, registered firms). IBGE PNAD Continua, Q4 2025: 37.6% of employed are informal in
Brazil; Maranhao 57.3%, Santa Catarina 25.7%, Bahia about 52% (Q2 2025); Pernambuco and Goias not found (SIDRA table 4095 has every UF).
Effect on a city's map: informal work (street trade, domestic work, small shops, home-based, construction day work) is spread nearer homes and
in markets and periferias, so a RAIS-only map overweights the formal centre and offices (central Recife, Brasilia, Goiania's government
compound) and underweights poor outskirts and the Northeast, where the informal share is highest. Fix: scale RAIS to the proxy total and mix it in
the way T-537 mixes the population share, with an informal share by state (SIDRA 4095), the informal part spread by population/GHSL like
the existing 20-30% population part. The same method as Mumbai/Lagos.
