# T-047 trains wiggle as they move

Done 2026-10-09. Anita saw trains wiggle while moving and guessed antialiasing.

## Measuring

In-page probe over CDP (headless Chrome, real GPU, 1280x800, dpr 1): one train on Broadway near
its sharpest bend, the network copied with only that train in it so nothing else crosses the
window; 120 consecutive frames at 1 game second each (60x speed), each drawn twice (trains on
and off) and read back with `readPixels`, the difference being the train. Per frame: the
rendered heading (second moments of the train's pixels), sideways offset of its centroid from
the true position, and the area of its dark core. Old and new shaders A/B in one page through a
temporary switch (removed after).

Ruled out: MSAA was on (`antialias: true`, 4 samples); overlay and map canvases had the same
pixel ratio; position was steady (sideways step 0.02-0.03 px a frame at zoom 11-12).

## Cause

Two things, both in the train shader:

1. **The white rim switched to the coloured core with a hard edge**, decided once per pixel, so
   MSAA did not smooth it. As the train slid sub-pixel each frame, the core's outline stepped a
   pixel at a time: its area flickered 1.2-1.4 px² a frame at zoom 11-12 (of 60-85 px²). This is
   the wiggle at normal zooms.
2. **The heading came from the 50 m track sample under the train's centre**, so on curves it
   turned in steps each time the train crossed a sample. Visible zoomed in: at zoom 14 the
   frame-to-frame change in turning rate (second difference of heading) had p95 1.45°, max 2.1°.

Also: the quad was exactly the train's size, so the shader's soft edge was cut off at the long
sides and left to 4x MSAA.

## Fix and result

Rim blended into the core over a pixel; heading along the chord between points half a train
length either side of the centre (as the bogies sit); quad 1 px larger all round so every edge
is antialiased in the shader; train fragment shader at highp.

| Zoom | Core area flicker, px² a frame (mean) | Turning jitter p95 / max (°) |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | 1.21 -> 0.68 | 2.45 / 3.33 -> 1.90 / 3.01 |
| 12 | 1.38 -> 0.64 | 3.06 / 3.94 -> 2.74 / 3.12 |
| 14 | 2.12 -> 0.94 | 1.45 / 2.11 -> 0.51 / 0.61 |

At zoom 11-12 the train is about 18 x 8 px and its pixel-measured heading has a noise floor, so
the turning numbers there say little either way. Cost: GPU 0.70 ms median at 10k trains on
screen (0.56 ms with trains hidden), same as before within noise.

Not a cause here but looks similar: with the window unfocused the game draws at 10 fps (SPEC 7),
which reads as stutter.
