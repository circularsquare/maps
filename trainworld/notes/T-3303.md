# T-3303 Fare exploit (player report, Twitter, 2026-11-12)

A player on the live game (v0.3.1) set a base fare of about 90 billion; riders said to be already on board paid
it, and their money reached about 200 quintillion.

## Mechanism (reproduced)

After any edit, the demand pool first solves the touched zones locally (T-1733) and shows that result ahead of the
whole solve. A local result does not solve the regional market: each line's longer trips in it are as last solved,
at the fares of that solve. The page stamped the whole line with the local result's own fares (`daysOf`,
`solvedFares`, T-2266), so after a fare edit every longer trip on a line paid the new fare though it chose the old
one. The local riders on that line answer the new fare at once (they drop to nothing at a huge fare); the longer
trips do not, until the whole solve's final replaces everything. At 100x game speed that window is one to two
game days. The same holds for any cluster result without the market and for an edit's early figures for new lines
(`publishNewLines`).

So the payout is the line's longer trips times the typed fare times the economy (100) for the window. On the real
New York save: 11,386 longer trips a day of 23.5 million riders; a base of US$90 billion pays about 1e17 a game hour.

Fare code is the same on v0.3.1 (71009033) and master: `git diff 71009033 master` over fareCurve.ts,
fareInEffect.ts, money.ts, demandClient.ts and demandCache.ts shows only T-3239's `fareFactors` (the market's
weights), which changes nothing here.

## Runs

Headless Chrome, 2 demand workers, clock 100x from the edit. Income is the highest kind of day's, a game hour.

On the small Manhattan network the longer trips are 1.3e-5 a day per line (four km lines carry almost none), so the
spike is tiny in riders and only large through the fare. The local result's longer riders were bit for bit the
ones shown before the edit (1.344349726650762e-05 on line 0 at x3.6e6, x3.6e10 and x3.6e14), which is what gave it
away. The cache reload path is not it: a cached view's lines carry their `solvedFares` already.

Ruled out on the way: the demand cache (stamped), the clock worker's settling (it only pays the page's income
table), offline catch-up, the sim (no fare money in the track model), the transfer fare, the fare estimate (display
only), demand's mode choice at a huge fare (riders go to 1e-31).

## Fix

`game/fareInEffect.ts` `takesMarketless`: a line from a result without the market replaces a shown line only when
both were solved at the same fares (compared field by field, key order ignored); a line with no shown figures takes
it. Used in `publishCluster`'s merge (a local result or a cluster without the market) and `publishNewLines`. A held
line keeps its old figures and old fares until the whole solve's final, which is what the Money tab's wording
already said ("a line's income keeps the old fares until riders solved with the new ones appear"). Its zones'
views still update from the local result. `perfOff=fareHold` turns it off, for the check.

After a fare edit the lines' riders now change at the whole final only, not at the local result: a second or two
later for a small network, about 30 s on the real save.

Check: `checks/t3303-local-fares.mjs` (new game, three Manhattan lines, x300 under FARE_MAX). It asserts that the
fare's solve showed a local result and that no line is priced at the new fares before that solve's final. With
`perfOff=fareHold`: FAIL, 181 samples with 21 lines priced at the new fares on the old view. Fixed: PASS.

`checks/t2266-fare-lag.mjs` (real save, 10x then 5x) before and after: without the fix the old view showed every
line at 10x for 26 s with income 2.30x (the longer trips at 10x); with it, income stays at 1.00x until a solve's
final. Its "the 10x curve was never used" FAILs both ways: the 5x edit, made while the 10x solve runs, waits for
it, so the 10x solve's own final shows (its riders solved at 10x, priced at 10x; no over-pay). Filed as T-3322.

FARE_MAX (US$1,000 a node, landed in cfc1e5ff) bounds the old behaviour to 400 times the start's base on the longer
trips; on the real save that is about 1.1e9 a game day extra for the window, on an intercity network far more.
