# T-3924: count every Auto bus stop for demand (agent autostops, 2026-11-21)

**Outcome: every Auto stop counts for demand (Anita, 11-21: "lets count every auto bus stop. i think if people are
seeing bus stops the bus stops have to be real. we will have to try to find optimizations elsewhere.").** The cost,
held for her call first: 30 Auto lines of 10 km on the real NYC save make demand's solve about 50% slower and its
peak memory about 53% higher (natively; in the browser each solving worker's WASM memory rose 25-32%).

## Why the 2 km rule was there

T-1445 (the bus design, notes/T-1445.md "Why demand should not see every street stop"): solve cost. Demand prices
homes in groups that share their nearest station and searches once towards every station; a stop every 400 m "would
split the subzones along every bus route and add 25 stations for a 10 km line; a city with 30 bus lines would double
or triple the solve". Its second reason, bus stops crowding rail out of a cell's 6 nearest stations, has been handled
since by `BUS_SLOTS` (a cell adds at most 2 bus stops beside its stations). T-2947 then made Auto the stepper's
"400 m" without comparing riders; T-3920 found the gap. A line set to 600 m or more, or stops placed by hand, already
pays this cost today (every stop is a demand stop there).

## The change (in the tree)

`sim/src/track/bus.rs`:
- `plan_by`: on Auto every point of the 400 m street grid is a demand stop (`KIND_MIDDLE`), so `Plan.street` is always
  empty. Stops stay where they were (stations, ends, crossings, the grid); covers run half way to the next stop. A
  route over 400 km widens its grid to length / 1,000 so stop ids fit (`SPACED_MAX_STOPS`; Auto had no cap).
  `DEMAND_STOP_M` and `STATION_PAIR_GAP_M` are gone.
- `run_profile`: a stop with no dwell of its own stands 20 s inside its hop on Auto too (as the street stops did and
  Spaced stops do), capped at 40% of the hop. Hop times are unchanged (`auto_time`), so round trips, bus counts and
  money do not move (the 30 lines' round trips sum to 161,009 s before and after).
- Untouched: `Plan.street`, `profile()`'s signature, the app's street-stop drawing (now never fed), `Pace`, lanes, BRT.

Tests rewritten: `bus/tests.rs` (`plan_on_an_empty_network`, the 400 m spacing match, the stand inside a hop) and
`wasm/bus_tests.rs` (the feeder's middles, stands at plain stops). Both suites pass (714 and 932).

Tool: `sim/src/bin/demand_mem.rs --snapshot FILE` solves a game's network as its workers got it, bus stretches
included (captured from the page with scratch `snap.js`: wrap the workers' `postMessage`, `twDemand.resubmit()`).

Docs: SPEC.md (bus lines), docs/track-and-service.md (stops, run), code-map.md, demand-worldwide.md (bus
corridors), ui-map.md, ui-inspectors.md, ui-building.md (no more street stops that light or select their line: a
click on any stop opens its stop panel). No wiki page stated the 2 km rule. Changelog item autostops-1 (demand).

The browser's wall times swing more than the change (the plain real save's first load took 585 s); the native runs
are the cost figure. The bench sets the stations, lines, times, trains, cars, fares and bus stretches, not the
complexes, span, fare zones, tram or ferry setters; the browser evidence is memory only.

Per added demand stop: about 50 ms of native solve (48-57 ms over the two runs) and 0.4 MB of peak heap. Auto adds about
2 stops a km (618 over 300 km), so 10% on this network arrives near 60 km of Auto bus. The 30 test lines gain only
3.6% riders because they are straight lines through rail stations, mostly fed at those.

**Real saves** (counted on load with the new rule, no solve): Anita's North America save (`saves/na-day-491.save`,
175 lines) has 4 bus lines, 1 on Auto, 14.2 km, 36 stops: about 25 more demand stops, about +2% by the rate above. A
player's save (`t3481/player-bangkok-tokyo-newyork-day459.save`) has 7 bus lines, all Auto, 162 km, 413 stops: about
320 more, about half the fixture's, so very roughly +25% on its zones' solve (not measured; its networks differ).

## The call

Asked with options (keep 2 km and fix the panel; every second stop; count every stop; plus a kernel task). Anita chose
counting every stop and finding the speed elsewhere. Not tested here, for the batch checker: `t2952-crossing-stops`,
`t2992-bus-transfers`, two Auto lines on one street (a merge and a connector at every stop; busway's shared lanes,
T-3378). Busway's frontier figures for the 400 m bus (`tools/frontier.py`) move with this.
