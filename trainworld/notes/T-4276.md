# T-4276: timetable for a whole line group

A player (11-24): "Currently, the timetable only displays one route at a time. I'd like to be able to display all the
routes in a group together."

## What was there

The group view already had a group timetable (T-3346 stage 1, the chart icon "Timetable chart" in its Stops heading).
Its rows were the longest line's stops only: another line's trains showed only at stations it shares with that line,
its own stations (a branch) were missing, and a line sharing fewer than two stations was left out. On the real NYC
save with 4, 5, 6 and 6X grouped, the table had the 5's 36 stops; the 4's Jerome branch, the 6 and 6X's Pelham branch
and the 6's local stops in Manhattan were not in it. That is the "one route at a time".

## What changed

- The group's table lists every station its lines stop at, each once (a complex counts as the station), in the group
  view's order: `groupRows`, the row merge factored out of `game/groupLayout.ts` (the diagram calls it too,
  unchanged): the longest line's stops, each other line's own stations a block where it leaves and rejoins, a line
  sharing none below. The 4-line group has 81 rows (36 before).
- Every line of the group gets columns, whatever it shares.
- "|" only where a train really runs through: a station its line passes (`passed`), or one between two of its stops
  where another line stopping at both stops too (an express beside a local). Before, every row between a train's
  first and last showed "|", which with branches would mark another branch's stations. DECISIONS (Map and UI) has the lie.
- A column's times start at its first row (its rule and hover still the table's height); with 80+ rows the blank
  cells above were most of the DOM.
- The line panel's own timetable is unchanged (`lineRows`: its stops, as before).
- Files: `app/src/ui/inspector/ttRows.ts` (new: the rows per look), `TrainTable.tsx` (sources and columns from the
  rows), `game/groupLayout.ts` (`groupRows` exported), `ui/style.css` (looks b and c), docs ui-panels "A group's",
  SPEC, ui-inspectors, code-map.

## The sheet

`artifacts/trainworld/t4276-group-timetable-sheet.png`: the group of 4, 5, 6 and 6X on the real NYC save (it has no
groups: grouped with `groupMembers`, the op the Lines tab sends on a drop), panel at 1400 x 900, other lines off.
Each look as it opens and scrolled to 125 St.

- **Now**: the longest line's stops only.
- **A** (built): one table, every station once, every train a column in time order under its bullet.
- **B**: a band per line: its letter, then its own stops and trains; bands one under another. Columns picked per band,
  so each band is full at its own zoom; no bullets over the columns.
- **C**: every station once, a row under it per line stopping there (the line's letter at the row's end); columns
  picked per line.

Judged on 2 lines (the two A lines, `t4276-a-2lines.png`), 4 (the sheet) and 6 (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 6X,
`t4276-a-6lines.png`, `t4276-c-6lines.png`):

- A reads like the line's own timetable and a printed one: each train straight down its column, the lines
  interleaving at shared stations, express trains "|" at local stops. Best at 2-4 lines on a shared trunk, which is
  what groups are for. Weak spot: with 6 lines on many branches, a branch's rows show only the columns that fit
  among all lines', so at the opening zoom (an hour across) some of a branch's trains wait for a zoom in.
- B at the panel's height (44% of the window) shows the first line's band and little else: the other lines are
  below the fold, which is the "one route at a time" again. Stations repeat per band.
- C keeps every line's trains at any zoom and names each time's line by its row, but each station becomes a block
  of up to one row per line (the 6-line group about 200 rows), and following one train means hopping rows.

Built A. Advisor called once before building (on the framing): it pointed out the existing group table, the false
"|" on branches, and per-lane picking for b and c; all taken.

## Switching

`groupLook` in `ui/inspector/ttRows.ts` (signal, "a"): set "b" or "c". B and C differ from A only in the row order and
lanes (`groupTable`), with in `TrainTable.tsx` `pickLanes`, `Lane`, the rules and `lanes` branches, and the
`.tt-body.lanes`/`.tt-rule`/`.tt-lane` CSS. Once she picks, delete the other looks' branches.
