# T-3829 Show fast and slow roads when drawing a bus route

Anita's pad, 11-20: "we should highlight fast speed roads and low speed roads differently". Open-source issue #27
(tmkttmkt) asks the same, and also asks for routing by time rather than distance (its part B): not done here, display only.

## The speed classes

The game has two: **fast** roads are OpenMapTiles `trunk` and `motorway` (ramps included: the tiles class a ramp as
its road), which `RoadGraph.fastEdge` marks `K_TRUNK`, so a bus counts the car at 88 km/h at least there (bus 79).
Every other road takes its speed from the place's density (bus 30-40 km/h), not its class. So every look is a
two-way split; primary/secondary are not graded against minor, since the sim doesn't. Expression: `fastRoadExpr` in
`app/src/map/busRoads.ts`, beside `busRoadFilter`.

(Side fix: docs/track-and-service.md "Trunk roads" said only `trunk`; the code has always counted motorways too.)

## The looks (temporary switch)

All three are built, behind `?busroads=0|a|b|c` (0 the look before) and `twBusRoadLook("a")` in the page console,
which repaints live. Default is **b**. Code: `BUS_LOOKS`, `BUS_LOOK_PICK`, `hintPaint` in `app/src/map/basemap.ts`.

- **A**: fast roads blue (#3a4fb0, dark #95a8f5) at 0.85 opacity, 1.2x width; streets the teal as before.
  Reads, but thin blue lines sit close to the blue subway lines, and a hue on a 1 px line is weak.
- **B (pick)**: one teal; fast roads 2x width at 0.85 (dark 0.7), others 0.8x at 0.38 (dark 0.26). The fast network
  reads at once at every zoom (at the city zoom it is a clear skeleton), streets stay visible as "a bus can go here",
  and it adds no colour to clash with lines. No legend needed: wide reads as major road.
- **C**: fast roads hollow (teal casing 3.8x, land-coloured core 1.8x), streets as before. Classic highway mark, but
  subtle at city zoom where the hint is ~1 px.

One-way arrows (Settings, bus one-way) follow the per-road width (`mul`), so they stay 1.2x the stroke on fast roads
and on streets; checked at zoom 15.4 in light and dark. The shade switch with the tool on repaints (sheet's dark
panel was taken by switching live). Tram and rail hints unchanged.
