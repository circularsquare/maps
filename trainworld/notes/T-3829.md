# T-3829 Show fast and slow roads when drawing a bus route

Anita's pad, 11-20: "we should highlight fast speed roads and low speed roads differently". Open-source issue #27
(tmkttmkt) asks the same, and also asks for routing by time rather than distance (its part B): not done here, display only.

## The speed classes

The game has two: **fast** roads are OpenMapTiles `trunk` and `motorway` (ramps included: the tiles class a ramp as
its road), which `RoadGraph.fastEdge` marks `K_TRUNK`, so a bus counts the car at 88 km/h at least there (bus 79).
Every other road takes its speed from the place's density (bus 30-40 km/h), not its class. So the hint is a two-way
split; primary/secondary are not graded against minor, since the sim doesn't. Expression: `fastRoadExpr` in
`app/src/map/busRoads.ts`, beside `busRoadFilter`.

(Side fix: docs/track-and-service.md "Trunk roads" said only `trunk`; the code has always counted motorways too.)

  at city zoom and 7 (CIEDE2000) from the platform periwinkle #8085f0. Now drawn #a0b4f4-ish: 60 from the land

  which is a thick line beside a thin one. Light stays as she saw it: drawn #5667b8, 8 from #1f6fd1, but darker,
  more violet and far thinner than any line; the palette's blues leave no hue gap for it anyway.
- One-way arrows: a second chevron image in the fast blue (`ONEWAY_ARROW_FAST`), chosen per road, so arrows are the
  road's own colour; sized 1.2x the stroke on each road (`mul`).
