One agent, two stages, one report each. Stage 1 first.

## T-671 stage 1, placing a station
Two rules for the station tool (`map/tools.ts`, `map/pick.ts`, `stationGeometry.ts` for the ghost):
1. "when placing a station... if we're hovering over an area with multiple tracks on it and some of
   them have stations and some of them dont, prioritize the track that doesnt already have a station...
   also prioritize blueprinted track over actually constructed track." When several tracks are under
   the pointer, the candidate order is: tracks without a station before tracks with one, then
   blueprint before constructed, then whatever order picking uses now. Test where two tracks lie
   within a few pixels (the real save has plenty).
2. "when trying to place a station near the end of the track instead of just saying like you cant
   place here cuz its off the end of the track, just snap the station to the end (we already snap if
   we hover the endpoint but we should make it so that we snap if it's ineligible in general)." When
   the pointer's spot is refused only because the platform would run off the end of the track, the
   ghost and the click use the end spot (as hovering the endpoint does today). Other refusals
   (water, a crossing, an existing station) keep refusing in the words `issues.ts` already has.

Done: a node check beside `t133-picking-check` for rule 1 if picking is testable that way; the
ghost shown in a screenshot for rule 2; typecheck; `ui-building.md` states both rules.

## T-673 stage 2, drawing touches
All in the map's drawing code, each its own small change:
1. **Round line caps** instead of square, "especially in line + traffic thickness mode". Line ends in
   `renderer.ts` (the track and line shaders). Check the thick traffic-width lines, a line's end at
   a terminus and where a line stops mid-track; casing too. Cost on a 10k-train synthetic network
   must not rise noticeably (`?synth=1`).
2. **Remove the seat indicators** on train cars: "the little seat indicators on train cars, they look
   weird". The tick marks on the train gauge (the seats mark; the hover's seats figure stays).
3. **Demand colour in more steps.** "have the demand indicator color thing gradate a bit more
   continuously from green to deep red rather than only having 3 settings". Find where the demand
   level colour (the 3 settings: probably the station use circles or the demand status tag) is chosen
   and make it a continuous ramp green, yellow, orange to deep red by the value; keep the hover's
   number, keep both themes legible (`shades.ts`).
4. **Flat junction icon colour by elevation**: "for the flat junction icon lets color it based on the
   track elevation its happening on." The flat junction and crossing marks (`gradeMarks.ts`,
   `markData.ts`) take the colour the elevation rows already use (`trackTagRows.ts`).
5. **Switch for the flat intersection marks**: "ability to turn off flat intersection visibility": a
   checkbox in Settings beside the other display settings (`game/ui.ts`, `SettingsTab.tsx`), saved
   with the other settings.
