## What changed

**State.** `BusRoute::brt_bp` (sim/src/track/bus.rs): on a constructed route only, its BRT lanes are in the
blueprint. `brt` keeps meaning "runs as BRT" (speed, fares, lane room, upkeep, which stations it stops at), so every
reader of `is_brt` stays right; a pending line has `brt` false. A planned route's BRT is still `brt` on the planned
line, built with it. `brt_any()` (either) and `lanes_built()` (`brt && built`) are the two helpers.

- `set_bus_brt(on)` (wasm/bus.rs): on a constructed line sets `brt_bp`, a free undoable `run`; on a planned one sets
  `brt` as before. Off takes a blueprint out (free `run`) or takes built lanes away (`run_free`, nothing back).
- `construct_ops` (wasm/links.rs): a constructed route with `brt_bp` gets `brt` and loses `brt_bp`, so Construct on
  the line, Construct blueprints and C build it, and `line_build_cost` / the panel's quote price it.
- Lane order (net/store.rs `brt_order`): pending lines are among the planned ones (partition on `lanes_built`, the
  constructed count `brt_lanes().1` likewise), so kind 16 (`brt_lane_m(false) - brt_lane_m(true)`) prices them and
  `brt_room`, `brt_upkeep_m` never see them. `brt_key` hashes `brt` and `brt_bp`.
- `line_parts` (wasm/links.rs): a constructed bus route gives no stations, so the BRT row's Construct (`construct_line`)
  builds and prices the lanes alone, never a blueprint station built beside the route since (brtstations' bus
  stations will be the usual case).
- clock.worker.ts: a paid construct re-snapshots demand, since building the lanes flips the line's fare multiple
  (T-3915) even where no time changes (a route with no road in town); that used to ride on the `busBrt` op.
- `copy_line`: the planned copy takes `brt_any()` as its own BRT. `set_bus_route` carries `brt_bp`.
- Save: trailing `LBQ1` (after `LBR1` and brtstations' `LBT1`), level 36, the constructed lines with `brt_bp`. Old saves: `LBR1` lines load
  built, as before.
- Map geometry: `Network::brt_marks(l)` / WASM `bus_lane_marks`: the route's road in town as runs
  `[kind, points, x, y, ...]`, kind 0 new lanes in the blueprint, 1 in the blueprint on a constructed BRT line's lanes
  (free), 2 constructed. Against the constructed lanes only, the same comparison the line's Construct price makes, so
  picture and price agree (two planned lines overlapping each draw their shared stretch as new). Built from
  `bus::lanes::marks` over `each_town_step` (the steps `town_steps` uses, now with their road points).
- regionMoney.ts needed no change: it books the lanes where `brt` turns on, which is now Construct.

**Panel** (ui/inspector/BusInspector.tsx `BrtRow`, nothing else in the panel): the checkbox became an upgrade button
(`btn icon upgrade`, the station upgrades' classes: outlined off, dashed in the blueprint, filled built; a road with a
dashed lane line and a bus as its icon) and "BRT" beside it; while a constructed line's lanes are in the blueprint,
"Construct for $X" (the line's quote) at the row's right. Hover: name, the old BRT hover, the shared line, "Costs $X"
until built, the station upgrades' click line.

**Map** (map/busLayer.ts): lane segments after the transfers and connectors (out of the picking), `FLAG_LANE`, drawn
in a pass 5 after the bodies, through the stencil as the corridor outline is (whole across segment joints; with no
stencil a segment's band is cut out of its own middle only). From zoom 11, not in the Traffic or Level views.
- Constructed: 1 px dashed (4 on, 3 off) `brtLane` white, 1 px inside the line's colour each side, once the colour is
  5 px wide (about zoom 14 up: at 12 and 13 the line is too thin and none shows, `built-z12-*.png`, `built-z13-*.png`).
  Three placements were shot (`var-{a,c}-{light,dark}-z{14,16}.png`; side by side, light and dark:
