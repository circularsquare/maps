# T-2841: Spanish (es)

One Spanish for all players: neutral, understood in Spain and Latin America. Address the player as `tú`
(imperatives: "Elige", "Construye"). No voseo, no vosotros. Avoid regionalisms; where Spain and Latin America differ
(coche/carro, ordenador/computadora, coste/costo), use the word both read without a blink or one the game's UI makes
clear. Numbers are formatted in code (Intl es: 1.234,5 and compact "763 mil", "1,2 M").
