level stepper (-3 to +3), and Remove. Add corner buttons occupy each interval, including the
straight edge with no corners. They insert the interval midpoint with auto radius and the
rounded mean level of its neighbors; the player then drags the map square into the desired
shape. The last corner can be removed, returning to a straight alignment. Constructed track
has none of these controls and the worker continues to reject shape edits to it.

All actions reuse edgePis/previewEdge and existing geometry validation/undo. No save or worker
protocol changes. Shape controls disable while an edit is pending; fetched inspector shapes
reset on edge/version change, and the map editor ignores older shape-query responses.

actual browser clicks and pointer drag: insert midpoint, drag into a 1796 m radius curve,
raise only that corner, insert the next corner in order, remove first, exact undo/redo, remove
