# T-4017 Busway price and station refusal

## Price (no change)
Same ground, same 1.2 km, off-road tram (snap to streets off) against busway, New York sandbox at Fort Washington (-73.94, 40.835), US$M by terrain class and level (blueprint quote rows):

| stretch | metres | tram | busway |
|---|---|---|---|
| ground, class 0 | 272 | 10.20 | 6.80 |
| ground, class 1 | 318 | 14.92 | 10.94 |
| ground, class 2 | 207 | 12.42 | 9.84 |
| level +1, class 1 | 139 | 7.57 | 5.48 |
| level +1, class 2 | 90 | 6.06 | 4.71 |
| level +1 over water | 136 | 31.63 | 29.59 |
| total | 1,162 | 82.8 | 67.4 |

Busway base is 2/3 of tram's at ground (6.80 / 272 m = US$25M a km). The terrain surcharge per metre is the same for both (class 1: 11.93 tram flat part + 2.99 = 14.92; busway 7.95 + 2.99 = 10.94). Water and levels follow the same table. Busway ignores road crossings, as tram does. On flat open ground (Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens) a 1.2 km busway is US$29.1M. So the batch checker's US$35M a km was terrain and a bridge, priced as tram track would be on that path. Nothing changed.

## Station refusal
Station tool over a busway already refuses with "Stations can't go on busways. Place a BRT station beside one." (BuswayStation). Where the busway was on a ramp, "The platform would be on a ramp." was appended (PlatformNotLevel). Fix in sim `draw.rs` (`station_ops`): when a station is refused for the busway, the platform-geometry reasons (ramp, too long, joint, junction, station, crossing) are dropped, so only the busway reason shows. I kept the refusal rather than skipping the busway like tram track, since a click that silently does nothing is worse than a refusal that names the busway and says what to place.

In game (own Vite 6171, play.mjs): click on a busway ramp stretch before: "...Platforms are 60 m to 400 m long. The platform would be on a ramp."; after: "Stations can't go on busways. Place a BRT station beside one. Platforms are 60 m to 400 m long."

No string added or changed.
