Calls by the orchestrator (maps-cb) from her words; built by agent dbl3 in `.tw-trees/dbl3`.

## Quad

The `Quad` refusal and its detection (`quad_pair`, `edges_near`) are gone. Each picked double piece goes single on
its own: quad picked whole becomes two singles side by side, one pair picked leaves single beside double. A station on
a pair becomes a single-track station (twin stations keep their shared name; each is on its own pair). The ends of a
quad stretch, where the pairs meet as a junction, become a junction of single track. What still refuses: a crossover
between the pairs (the `Crossover` reason, since a crossover joins tracks beside each other and keeps double). T-3333's
two-pair lines had not landed when this was built, so there was nothing to check there; a line that runs over both
pairs just routes over single track.

When it comes up: rarely. Someone who drew quad and then decides it was overkill, or who picks a whole area with a
box to thin it out and catches a quad stretch in it. Picking one pair only is the more likely case: a quad stretch
whose express pair carries little.

## Passing tracks

The `Passing` refusal is gone. A station with passing tracks stays a double-track passing place: for each picked
double piece that ends at it, `passing_cuts` splits the piece at the platform's reach (half the platform at a through
station, all of it at a dead end) plus `PASSING_RUN_OUT`, 20 m (the room a junction keeps past a platform), with
`Op::Split` in the same edit. The part at the station keeps double, the rest goes single. So a 200 m through station
keeps 120 m of double each side of its node. Pieces at a passing station at both ends get both splits; the single part
is the middle one. A piece with under 10 m of single left after that, one that will not split there (`split_plan`
refuses a split on a ramp), and one whose level or type changes in the same rebuild stay double whole and are not
counted as refused. This works whether or not the station itself is inside the pick, and whether or not the track
beyond the station is picked: the station always keeps double along its platform.

The split edits are free (counted with the piece they split), keep lines on their path (`Op::Split` moves them),
and undo with the rest on blueprint. Ids for the splits are handed out before the trials and given back after the
quote or edit (`Plan::fresh`).

When it comes up: whenever a player who bought passing tracks on a double line wants to save money (the free direction)
by thinning a quiet line to single: the stations with passing tracks are exactly where trains should meet.

## Checks

5, the two at the station 120 m double, the station still `node_tracks` 2 with its upgrade).
