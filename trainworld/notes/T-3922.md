# T-3922 Station upgrades cost upkeep

Anita, 11-21: "probably start charging maintenance cost for station upgrades. i think in long term if they dont, i
tjuts incentivizes mindless spamming."

## Rule

Everything bought on a station pays `upgrades::UPKEEP_SHARE` = 0.2% (first 0.1%; waiting rooms 0.1% since T-4342) of its price a game day: terminal, park and
ride, waiting room, passing tracks, passages and station buildings (the whole "Station upgrades" row, so a building
is not a free waiting room). Price at today's prices (`upgrades::price`, level, water, platform, track kind).

Why 0.1%: the game's own upkeep over build price, a game day:

| Thing | Price | Upkeep/day | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Port | US$10M | US$10k | 0.100% |
| Ground double track, 1 km | US$37.5M | US$35k | 0.093% |
| Bored tunnel track, 1 km | US$120M | US$130k | 0.108% |
| Ground station (200 m) | US$75M | US$50k | 0.067% |
| Station at -1 (200 m) | US$125M | US$200k | 0.16% |
| Tram stop | US$2M | US$5k | 0.25% |
| BRT in town, 1 road-km | US$5M | US$21k | 0.42% |

0.1% is the port's exactly and close to ground track's. A ground terminal (priced as 1 km of ground track) comes to
US$37.5k a day, against US$35k for that km of track. The constant is the game ratio directly (game upkeep over real
price), not a real figure times `ECONOMY`, since prices are real and upkeep a game day's.

Resulting figures: waiting room US$5k/day, park and ride US$20k, terminal US$37.5k on the ground, passing tracks at a
200 m ground station US$22.5k, passage US$10k-100k, building US$2 a m².

## Measured on saves

- `T-007-real-nyc.save`: 1,756 stations, no upgrades, passages or buildings: nothing changes.
- `artifacts/trainworld/t3600-asianita-1852.save`: 2,647 stations; 10 terminals (US$525M), 73 park and ride
  (US$1,460M), 22 waiting rooms (US$110M), 36 passing tracks (US$1,159M), 0 passages, 4 buildings (about US$316M).
  New upkeep US$3.57M a day (141 stations carry it), against 7-day averages of fares US$22,291M, running US$4,841M
  and upkeep US$1,030M a day. +0.35% upkeep, 0.016% of fares: no risk of a loss, and on a network this rich it barely
  deters spam (park and ride at every station would be about US$53M a day, 0.2% of fares). Stiffer ratios already in
  the game: underground station 0.16% (US$200k on US$125M at -1), tram stop 0.25%, BRT 0.42%. Not raised without
  Anita.

## Where it counts

- Sim: `Network::upkept_upgrades_value` (totals) and `upgrade_upkeep_by_node` (per station: own upgrades, half of
  each constructed passage, an even share of each building over its constructed members; an empty building to the
  network's rest). `TrackApi::upkeep` adds them to the stations' figure (a port's own to the ports'), index 9 the
  upgrades alone; `upkeep_shares` gives each station's to the lines stopping there. `station_upgrade_upkeep` hands
  the per-station figure to the app (node rows, `StationInput.upgradeUpkeep`), which uses it in `nodeUpkeepOf`
  (region split, stretch money, station panel's Upkeep row, Build tab's projected upkeep). `money_params[31]` is the
  share. No save change: derived from upgrades already saved.
- Blueprint projection: a blueprint station's own upgrades count; a passage to a blueprint station does not until
  both are built (small understatement).
- `profitEstimate.ts` (leaderboard profit) sums track, stations and buses, never ports: a port's waiting room is left
  out there as the port is (pre-existing, not changed).
