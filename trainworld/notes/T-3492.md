# T-3492: give back idle solving workers' memory (agent idlemem, 2026-11-17)

Outcome: **measured, not built.** A restart does not reliably give the memory back, so no new restart rule. One
fix built on the way: a question asked while restarted workers reopen now waits for the re-solve and is answered
(it came back empty before).

Tree `.tw-trees/idlemem` (branch tw/idlemem), own Vite on 6182, headless Chrome pinned to 4 claimed cores, the
all-China save (`t1156-all-china.save`), weekday, 3 solving workers forced (`?demandWorkers=3`, as T-3472) unless
said. Scripts, logs and JSON: `artifacts/trainworld/t3492/` (`measure.mjs` the forced restart, `measure2.mjs` a
restart with the page hidden, `measure3.mjs` the GC'd per-target samples and the question check, `diff.mjs`).
Chrome MB are Windows private bytes over the check's Chrome, three readings a few seconds apart.

## What a restart gives back

T-3472 counted a worker's WASM minus its heap at rest (about 240 MB a worker, 0.7 GB in all) as what a restart
frees. But the replacement reopens everything and solves the game again from nothing, and its WASM grows to that
cold solve's peak plus the allocator's steps. The cold solve costs more than the warm ones the pool has been doing,
and more the more zones are fine:

| Zones fine when restarted | WASM a solving worker before | After the restart | Chrome before | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (camera left where the load put it, after 10 min) | 1,236 | 1,140 | 7,070-7,213 | 6,491-6,672 |
| 1 (camera on a station, as a player's) | 1,236 | 1,268, 1,268, 1,204 | 7,259-7,329 | 6,992-7,015 |
| 90 (right after the load) | 1,236 | 1,364, 1,364, 1,300 | | |

- The aux worker: 289 to 257 with 0 fine; left alone otherwise.
- The cold solve's heap peak over the heap at rest: +101 MB with 0 fine, +237 with 1 fine, against +107 for every
  warm solve before it (the load's last solve included), so nothing the pool sees before a restart predicts it.
- With 1 fine the WASM went up 32 MB in all, while Chrome fell about 270 MB; that fall is not told apart from the
  idle swing (7,082 to 7,308 within one run with no restart). With a forced GC in every target (`measure3`, before a
  restart only: the after-sample of that run was lost), each solving worker's JS is about 30 MB and its buffers 118,
  so what a restart could win outside the WASM looks small, but it was not measured after one.
- Default pool: since T-3373 A, all-China on an 8 GB machine runs 1 solving worker: 1,268 MB, an estimated 49 MB to
  give back at best.

## What a restart costs

- A whole cold re-solve: 111-128 s on 4 cores, against 50-73 s for an edit's solve on warm workers. An edit made 3 s
  into a restart took 107 s. The edit after the restart's final was no slower (54-68 s against 52-73).
- Every question waits for it (T-2757).
- **Seen once: demand stopped after a restart.** In one of seven all-China restarts (`long1`), the three replacements
  stayed at 33 MB of WASM, nothing answered, and the page had logged about 2,700 `net::ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES`.
  Another check (t1174) was loading from the same dev server at the time; `client.status` and when the errors began
  were not captured, and it was not reproduced. A likely path: each replacement fetches all 541 items at once
  (`reopen`'s `Promise.all`), three at a time, and a failed reopen stops the pool. T-3472 saw the same Chrome error
  with no restart, while flying between cities.

## What was tried

- T-1205's rule never fires on all-China: its need estimate (heap at rest x 1.38 = 1,401 MB) is above the 1,236 a
  worker holds. The 1.38 is stale for today's allocator (fresh workers are about 1.18 x rest) but `needOf` also sizes
  the pool, so it was left alone.
- Built and measured, then taken out: a restart once the page had been hidden 3 minutes (nobody asking or editing),
  for the workers whose WASM was 32 MB above an estimate of a fresh worker (heap at rest plus the most any warm solve
  added, plus 72 MB), when together 192 MB. It fired as meant, but the estimate holds only with no zone fine; with
  the camera on a station the replacements came out above the old workers. Headless Chrome also never hides a page
  (a tab behind another, a minimized window both stay "visible"), so the hidden state was emulated.
- A shadow worker solving beside the old one, swapped in when ready, was ruled out without building: it holds two
  copies (+1.1 GB a worker for two minutes), and a worker cannot solve its share alone (the workers trade period
  loads, T-1607, and keep their market parts alike, `freshParts`).

What would give the memory back: a cold solve that peaks no higher than a warm one (solve clusters one after another
in a replacement, as the load does), or less held per worker at all (T-3373 B, T-3493 for the peaks).

## Built: a question during a restart waits

`demandClient.ts`: while a replaced worker is still reopening (`reopening()`), `viaRound` and `tripLengths` hold the
question until the solve that follows is sent (`respawnWaits`, released in `dispatch`, when no solve follows, and on
`fail`); `target` then asks of that solve's final as before (T-1205's `refreshing`). Before, `refreshing` was set
only once every replacement had reopened, so a question in that window went to the old version, the new workers had
nothing, and it came back empty.

- Before (forced restart, a station asked 5 s in): no answer, 329 ms.
- After: answered once the re-solve's final was out, 175 s later; the answer equals the idle one to float rounding
  (427 numbers differ, at most 1.2 parts in a million, from the periods being summed in another order), as does every
  question after the restart. Two idle asks a few seconds apart were identical.
- This does not fix T-2757 (questions still wait for the whole re-solve, minutes on a big game); it turns an empty
  answer into a wait. Not checked in the panels: whether a view that got the empty answer asked again by itself.
