### Why the test conversion is not mechanical (tried 2026-11-20)
First try: made `tests::line(w, stops, tph)` build whole trains (rate x round trip, rounded). 36 sim tests failed (plus `demand::api::tram_tests::a_tram_carries_at_least_the_bus_it_replaces`, not through this helper, so probably tramerr's or a stale baseline: check on an unedited tree). Causes:
- A rate-only line has an exact rate; a counted line has a rate of trains x 3600 / round trip, so literal expectations (`8.0 * occ / 3600`, `[10.0, 6.0, 3.0]`, "5 an hour") need the settled `line_tph` as the expected value. Fixed this way in capacity tests.
- Counts come from the round trip when the line is made; adding a second line on the same track lengthens the first's round trip, so its rate drops. `set_rate` again after all lines exist.
- A line with 1 train on single track takes the lone-train allowance (`occ_at`), so "5 an hour on 2 km" became 2 trains.
- Counted lines in a world where several lines share track came out with a lower tph than counts x 3600 / round trip (seven_express: counts [27, 20], tph 10.25 and 4.7), so "the shared track is busy, rho > 0.8" cannot be had by asking for 20/h. Not understood; needs a look at how `TrainCounts` sets tph when no settle has run.
- `tiny_reversal_does_not_couple_independent_through_directions` asks for 0.001 trains an hour; one train on a 3 km line is about 5/h. The subject (a rare fractional-rate reversal) goes with the path; decide whether to drop or redesign.
- `a_frequency_line_is_given_trains_on_load` (tests.rs) tests the legacy flag-0 load and must keep a rate-only line.
- Other failures not examined: boarding (6), overtake, spacing `lines_on_shared_track_leave_apart`, tests.rs (a_circle..., a_line_reversing..., a_line_runs_with_times_and_fleet, junction_delay_matches_the_spec_example, merge_waits..., schedule_change_dirties..., station_links_save_and_load (bytes), sparse_and_reversing..., the_middle_of_a_long_line..., whole_line_counts... expects None after undo), timing (2).

## fixedrate2, 2026-11-21

### The half-rate result is the terminus, not a sharing bug
`seven_express_and_local_on_one_double_track`, counted (probe in scratch, removed): the limit is the Terminus
resource at each dead end, rho 1.000, not the shared section (rho 0.506). Both lines turn at n[0] and n[20] with no
terminal upgrade, so one turn at a time: `pooled_turn_s(180 s turnaround, 60 s TERMINUS_MARGIN_S, 1 place)` = 240 s
a train, 3600 / 240 = 15 trains an hour for both lines together. Settled: 10.254 + 4.746 = 15.000 (high), 9.837 +
5.163 = 15.000 (medium). The local alone already sits at 15.0, not the 20.4 its 22 trains would run (no sharing
involved). The rate-only test only looked busy because rate lines are never cut: it ran 40/h through that terminus,
rho 2.67, a state no game can reach.
- With `terminals_at_dead_ends` (2 places, 120 s a turn, 30/h) the shared section becomes the limit:
  15.295 + 13.209 an hour at high, (15.295 x 110.95 + 13.209 x 144.07) / 3600 = 1.000; the express loses 990 of its
  1003 s gain behind the local (medium: section 0.936, 955 of 1003 s).
- The split between the two is as documented (track-and-service.md "Capacity limits", DECISIONS 2026-11-18 T-3482):
  order of adding does not matter (10.254/4.746 local first, 10.235/4.765 express first); dropping the local to 5
  trains lifts the express to 11.46; giving the local 40 again leaves both where they are at high (a held line that
  already runs keeps its rate until a line beside it moves, as decided), while at medium, where the track was not full,
  the local rises to 17.6.
- For conversions: a dead-end fixture asking more than 15/h at one end needs `terminals_at_dead_ends` or a lower ask;
  a capped line's rate is not counts x 3600 / round trip, so assert on the settled `line_tph`.

### Found on the way: count and headway lines draw expresses through locals (timing tests parked)
On T-2228's 180 km line (`overtake::tests::shinkansen`, passing tracks at B to F, 10/10), the timetable has 24
express-through-local passes between passing stations in 4 h when both lines are count or headway lines, 0 when
both are fixed-rate (94 good passes). Probe (scratch, removed):
- not the rates: fixed-rate lines at 5.99, 5.999854, 6.0 and 6.01 an hour all give 0 bad; headway lines at exactly
  6.0 give 24.
- not the continuous delays: rounding count lines' delays as fixed-rate ones (`capacity::solve_one`) gave identical
  tph (6.0), round trips (7199 / 5135 s) and holds, still 24 bad. Departures differ: fixed-rate local +65 s,
  express +156 s past the hour (91 s apart); headway lines local +165 s, express +356 s (with continuous delays
  +165 / +181 s).
- the cause: `Service::lattice`'s `off_at` returns 0 for a fixed-rate line (its return run leaves at the same
  phase) and for count lines `(trip_len + split x spare) % headway` (T-2607, T-2972). Forcing 0 for count lines
  too: 0 bad, 94 good. All 24 are on run 0: spacing places run 0 around run 1's derived phase. This is the open
  item in notes/T-3346.md ("the two directions can't both have the best", 30/10 floor). T-3346's Rust done-when
  only ever ran on fixed-rate lines; `t2233-passing-map` checks drawn trains, where the guard holds the express
  back, and leave times only at passing stations, so it could not see this.
- The gate was tried on `every_local_waits_for_its_express` at 10/10 only. `both_rush_stretches_are_timed_on_their_own`
  and `a_moved_morning_hour_is_timed_too` also fail on headway lines (bad passes); cause not checked.
  `headway_locals_make_their_next_departure` is different: it switches both lines to headways itself, so it runs
  count lines in both forms; it fails only when started from headway lines (10/30: 10 locals stand longer than a
  headway, 15 leave service), against the [6, 6, 0] trains a fixed-rate start leaves it; cause not checked.
- So those four stay on fixed-rate lines (`overtake::tests::shinkansen_rates`, assertions unchanged). That keeps `LineData.trains: None` alive, so the
  Option and capacity's `continuous` rounding cannot go until this is fixed (a behaviour task) or the tests are
  weakened. Orchestrator's call.
