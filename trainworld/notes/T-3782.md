# T-3782: stops the relation leaves out, and Fukuoka's reversals (agent realjapan)

All in `pipeline/realauto.py` `tidy_stops` (called from `cut`), counted per city in the extract (`reordered`, `stops_added`).

1. Missing stations: JR Tokaido Main Line's OSM relations skip Nagoya, Gifu and Kiyosu. A train or subway relation gets a passenger stop (railway=stop/halt on its own ways) added in track order when: the stop's network/operator matches the relation's or its master's name/network/operator (a stop with its own network that differs, e.g. the Chuo line's Nagoya stop, is refused); no listed stop within 400 m with the same name or 150 m of any other; inside the pack; strictly between the relation's first and last stop; no planned/disused/construction stop; relation not named express/rapid/limited/semi/RE/ICE; train relations need listed stops under 5 km apart on average; at most 3 stops or 15% of those listed. A stop the route master's other direction was given is added with no limit. Not for tram, light rail or monorail (shared junction track: Hong Kong light rail lines stopped running with extra stops), and no end extensions (the Sangi Line gained a reversal).
   Tried and rejected: 50% allowed (Santiago's Ruta Roja/Verde express routes got 8 stops each); no kind limit.
2. Fukuoka's reversals, cause: OSM lists a shuttle as one relation out and back (JR Kagoshima Main Line Hakata - Omuta ends with a second Minami Fukuoka, Nishitetsu Kaizuka Line lists Kaizuka and Shingu twice, Heisei Chikuho Itoda Line, Hita-Hikosan Line), so the route turned at the far end and ran back. Fix: a chain that retraces itself (points at 10-40% match those at 90-60%, within 200 m) is read as its first half, stops sorted along it, a station listed again later is listed once, and stops over 300 m off the chain (depot stops) go, unless a terminus within 1.5 km at the chain's end. A closed ring (circle line, Hong Kong's circular light rail) is left alone. The sorting threshold is now 1.1 (T-3777's 2.5 left these).
