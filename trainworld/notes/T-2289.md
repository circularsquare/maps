# T-2289: the network diagram at the intercity scale

Rule in docs/ui-diagram.md ("The layout": A, Lonely hops); figures and reasoning here.

Anita (notes/todo-2026-11-03i.md): "condensing the areas between cities that are just like long lonely thin
tracks. i think the map looks like it could be a little bit more compressed int he intercity scale in many cases."

## Why the countryside was drawn so long

Two causes, the second the bigger:
- A evens each hop (`1000 m x (length / 1000 m)^0.3`), so a rural hop of 4 km is 1.5 city hops, and a line of
  thirty of them is forty-five: a stretch between two towns can be longer than a city.
- **A does not converge on a country.** Its 10 rounds of 400 LSQR iterations move a place about as far as the
  hops it can feel; on all Japan (9,106 complexes) the drawing stayed near the real map's shape and scale,
  Tokyo a small knot in a long country (`artifacts/trainworld/diagram/jpn-whole-base.png`). Shortening the
  lonely hops' targets alone (0.4) barely moved it: the longest stretch went 1,111 to 1,031 pitches. With 40
  rounds the same targets gave 515.

## The change

- **Lonely hops**: `cityParts` marks a station as in a city when 3 others lie within 2.5 km in reality (a city:
  such stations within 2.5 km of each other). A hop is lonely when its lines are all of one colour, both its
  stations have two hops or fewer, it leaves a city or joins two, and one end is alone (no station within 4 km
  whose lines share no colour with its own). Its target is 0.4 of its evened length, at least 4 pitches, after
  the pitch is set.
- **A's rounds** grow with the graph: 10 up to 2,000 complexes, rising evenly to 40 at 8,000. New York, Tokyo
  and the Northeast keep 10.

The "alone" and "two hops" terms came from the checks: without "alone", New York's parallel Long Island
branches (Nassau Boulevard and West Hempstead, 2.9 km apart) were drawn on the wrong side of each other, 25
near pairs against 10 (18 with it); without "two hops", all Japan had 6 pill overlaps against 4, Ebina and
Atsugi among them.

## Tried and dropped

- **A started from a tree of target lengths** (each station its parent plus the real direction at the hop's
  target, shortest paths by target): all Japan folded onto itself, a ball of lines.
- **A started from the real places scaled** by the targets' total over the real lengths: compact (3,070 x 4,141)
  and the stretches met, but the cities came out cramped (city block 443, 10 pill overlaps); at the scale's
  square root, 4 overlaps but two stretches over the block.
- **A started from the graph with every two-hop chain contracted** to one hop, solved, and each chain's real
  shape laid onto its solved chord: 60 pill overlaps and 33 close pairs where chains swung.
- A minimum of 5 pitches for a lonely hop: no fewer overlaps (7).
