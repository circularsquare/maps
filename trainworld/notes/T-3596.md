# T-3596 one-way arrows quieter when zoomed out

Chevrons (one-way train lines, one-way track, the track ghost; all one class, `OneWayChevrons` in `app/src/map/oneWayMarks.ts`) now:
- draw from zoom 13 (`CHEVRON_ZOOM`, was 12);
- keep at least 300 CSS px apart (`CHEVRON_GAP_PX`, was 150), via a new `gapPx` argument of `oneWayEvery`.
Bus and ferry arrows are untouched (settled; they still use `ONE_WAY_GAP_PX` 150 and zoom 13).
Because thinning keeps every 2^n-th mark, doubling the gap is exactly one more halving.
Review shot: artifacts/trainworld/review/t3596.png (Caracas save, zooms 12.5, 13.5, 16, before | after).
At 13.5 the 420 m pair now shows none (300 px is longer than the pair); at 16 it keeps about half.
