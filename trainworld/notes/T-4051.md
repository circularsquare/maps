- **Two paths, in both builds.** A zone-open solve is either an edit's whole solve (short: every round worked out, all

  (`showEstimates || newZones || hold || ...`): T-2793's `newZones` makes it long when a zone in the solve has no
  figures shown yet, so whether the zone-alone rough solve's figures were in when the whole solve was sent decides it.
  That race is the likely reason her waits are "not super consistent". Which condition fired in v0.4.5 1 (its rough
  solve had landed 8 s before) is not pinned down.
- Like for like, the short path costs the same on both builds (154 against 147 CPU-s). The long path was seen on both
  but timed on master only at 1 core, so no like-for-like number for it. **Nothing seen makes master slower at opening
  a zone than v0.4.5.**
- The load itself was slower on master (solve 346 s against 198 s; T-4041's +40% plus a busier machine).

## What I changed (tree zoneopen)

- `demand.worker.ts`: at a solve's last round, the bubble sums of zones on screen go ahead of the results as before,
  the others' right after them, before any await (the same state, so the same numbers). The final no longer waits
  for zones nobody is looking at. Both paths: an edit's whole solve (all rounds sent at the end) and the barrier
  solve's last round when no unit of it follows. A question about an off-screen zone in between is answered once
  its sums are kept. Under `?split=1` a worker holding nothing keeps the old order.
- `demandClient.ts` `jobProgress`: while the workers can't time their units, a zone's solve moves its bar by the model
  only to a quarter of the solve (`BLIND_SOLVE`), so the units take over from there instead of finding the bar already
  at its end. Game loads (the card) keep the model as before.
- `demandClient.ts`: a zone's load bar expects its market packs to take as long as the last batch opened after the
  load did (download to open in every worker), not 1.5 s, so it moves through that stretch instead of sitting at 5%.
- Typecheck clean. (Measured since: 11-24 sections below.) At the time: the core ledger stayed full from 15:40 to 17:40, and a
  1-core game is too slow to time. Expected: the short path's tail (18 of 82 s, about 20% of opening Medellin) mostly
  goes, and on the long path the bar moves through the solve instead of sitting at 91%. Needed: one run on 4+ cores
  of the Medellin open with and without the changes (wall time, the bar's samples), and bubbles after a pan.
- After the change, on the 1-core game, a pan to Mexico City gave no bubbles within 20 s: the tag said "Calculating
  trips here…" (a new solve for the zone's level), so this is probably the 1-core re-solve, but it was not followed up.
- `t1493-bubbles-or-loading.mjs` (bubbles arrive on zone jumps) was started on the change and given up: 1-3 cores,
  27 minutes for its first two stops.

## The slow path fixed (agent zoneslow, 11-24)

**Cause, pinned.** After a load, the aux worker opens the zones built track crosses bare one at a time (T-1047,
`openCrossed`, about 33 zones in 15 s on na-day-491, nearest the view first). The new line's track makes Medellin such
a zone, but it queues behind the ones still left; its stations reach it 1-2 s later, so it is fetched for every worker
and never opened bare. The whole solve then finds a zone with no view (`unshown`), so `newZones` makes it a barrier
solve. When the chain had ended before the track landed, `mee` opened bare 1-2 s before its full fetch and the open
went short. So the slow path needs an edit soon after a load (or while the chain runs for another reason); a later edit
never met it.

**Fix** (`demandClient.ts`, `fetchCity` and `bareFirst`, 14 lines): when a zone downloaded to open in every worker has
no figures shown (no view, not open bare, no `bareKept`), a final result is shown and no game load holds, the same
bytes go to the aux worker as a bare open first. The aux worker takes opens one at a time in order, and the solve waits
for every worker's open, so the bare figures and the zone's view are always in before the solve is sent. The bare
copy closes once a shown solve covers the zone (`sweep`, as for any zone viewed and then reached). The load log gets a
bare entry for it. No worker change. Doc: docs/demand-runtime.md (the "station built within reach" bullet).
