# T-4347: time left from the start of a load

Anita 11-25: Belgrade bus save sat at 6% with no time left.

## Cause (code)
workers/demandClient.ts `jobProgress` set the card's time left only when `solveTold` (a remembered wait for the slot, or a worker's
unit ended; T-4209). On a save solved on 3 workers the first unit takes minutes, so the card showed a bar and no time. Her diagnostics
(load at 28%): v2 whole solve, 9 zones, 3 workers, no unit ended in the first 75 s. The stage was the demand solve. Before the network
is in (the game stage, a few seconds) there is also no figure; left as is.

## Change
Dropped the `solveTold` gate. Until units tell, the time left is the solve model on `prior` (SOLVE_FIT by stops and zones, times this browser's
last solve pace, `loadPace().solve`) through `solveStage`; the workers' units replace it when they can. The bar is unchanged: still capped at half
until `bounded` (T-4264). No new strings. No default pace constant added: the existing model already scales by stops and zones, and on a
first-ever load pace is 1.

## Run (Belgrade save, 2 cores, Use more cores off, card sampled every ~16-30 s; s from first sample, load ended ~1235 s)
First figure at 19 s: 13 min (real left ~19 min), 12 min at 85 s (1.6x low then ~0.9 at 100 s after a step to 17 min as the first units
ended), 16 min at 134 s, 14 at 216 s, 13 at 249 s, 10 at 390 s, 17 at 515 s (step up when a later round began; real ~12 min), 14 at 704 s,
9 at 829 s, 7 at 962 s, 4 at 1086 s, 2 at 1211 s (real ~0.5). Bar 0-23% at 249 s, 62% at 735 s.
Weak spots: the estimate steps up once (9 -> 17 min at 484-515 s) and runs high by 1.3-2x in the last third; blank for the first <15 s (game stage).

## DECISIONS (suggested, loading)
2026-11-25: the load card shows a time left from its start, on the solve model scaled by this browser's pace, until the workers' units give a pace; the bar stays under half meanwhile. T-4347 (replaces the T-4209 rule of no figure before a unit ends).
