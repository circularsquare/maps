# T-1479 Loading: a % and the time left (covers T-890); T-1480 the Brazil circle

## T-1480

A hidden realm's circle is a DOM mark placed by `map.project`, hidden on the globe's far side by
`hiddenOnGlobe` (map/live.ts). That asked the overlay's camera (`behindHorizon`), which is a frame old
and, like the shaders, hides nothing once MapLibre's globe is under 20% (zoom about 11.8 to 12). But
`map.project` stays the pure globe until the blend reaches 0, so there the antipode projected onto the
middle of the screen: from Wuhan (30.6 N, 114.3 E) that is Bolivia and Brazil's circle. Headless repro: a new
Wuhan game on the globe showed Brazil at zoom 11.8, 11.9 and 12 (6 of 42 views). Fix: `hiddenOnGlobe`
asks MapLibre's own camera transform (`isLocationOccluded`, what MapLibre's markers use), the same one
`map.project` uses at that moment; it fixes the airport, attraction, city label and zone-loading marks
too. Standing check `checks/t1480-antipode.mjs` (0 of 42 after; near realms still circled zoomed out).

## T-1479 method

Headless Chrome on my Vite (6141), a fresh profile per save, the save imported into a slot then picked
from the start menu, the card and `twDemand.client.barParts` sampled every 250 ms (scratchpad
`t1479/measure.mjs`, `analyze.mjs`; JSON and card shots in `artifacts/trainworld/t1479/`). The machine
was shared with other agents and grew busier during the session (the real New York save's solve 59 s,
then 77, 87): read ratios, not seconds.

## What changed

- **Solve model** (`SOLVE_FIT`, demandClient.ts): a game load's solve 34 s + 148 ms a zone + 7.88 ms
  times stops squared (fits the three saves above to the second; the world save, solved ahead, comes
  out 84 s against 63-97 s). Times this browser's last game solve against the model (`Pace.solve`).
- **Rounds timed apart** (`solveStage`): the first round, then the later ones at this browser's last
  ratio (`Pace.later`, 0.5 with none) until one lands, then at their own mean; a round running late
  stretches the ones after it.
- **Time left** (`jobProgress`, `nextLeft`/`leftText` in loadBar.ts): each stage's expected time less
  its time so far, a late stage guessed on at a share of its time so far; shown once the network is in
  (from the start with the slot remembered); counts down, rises only at 0.5 s a second while the estimate
  is over a fifth above it. The card shows "42%" and "2 min left" under the bar.
- The slot's remembered stage times (T-1195) are used as before.
