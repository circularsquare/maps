# T-3614 new zones map

Tool: `trainworld/tools/release/new_zones_map.py` (usage at its top). Run from the maps root:

    venv\Scripts\python.exe trainworld/tools/release/new_zones_map.py --version v0.4.0

- Before = live game's outlines.json: the `tw-data` meta of https://anita.garden/trainsworld/ names the data version
  (2026-11-13 for v0.3.5); the file is plain JSON on R2 (the tool also un-gzips by magic bytes). Cached as
  `data/work/outlines-<host>_trainworld_<version>.json` (gitignored). `--before` takes a path or URL.
- After = data/packs/outlines.json. New = id not in before; grown = kept zone that gained over 0.002 deg2.
- Clusters: single linkage on boxes, 9 degrees (about 1000 km). Biggest = main map, up to 3 more as insets if over
  1.5% of new area and not already inside the main frame. Each map: LAEA centred on its cluster, 10% padding.
- v0.4.0: 4045 zones before, 4135 now: 91 new, 6 grown. One cluster (Central America from Guatemala/Belize through
  Panama, and Venezuela), main map only, no insets. Left out as under 1.5%: 3 pieces near Turkey, 1 UK, 1 Portugal
  (grown zones, 0.1% together).
- Tested the multi-cluster layout with a synthetic before (zones dropped in Europe, Korea, Ontario, Andes): main map
  plus three insets laid out fine.
- Not handled: clusters straddling the antimeridian (frame is centred by circular mean, but not tested).
- Publish step in docs/orchestrator.md is the orchestrator's.
- Land-coloured holes in the image (Guatemala City area, Barquisimeto) are parked zones (anu, scd, tsx, clt from T-3399; T-3517) and the unregistered lel: their packs sit on disk but are not in outlines.json or the registry, so they are not in the game. outlines.json is the only source of zones; the tool prints "cannot draw zone X" for an empty or invalid outline.
