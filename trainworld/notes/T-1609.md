# T-1609: the local refine (T-1206 stage 3), 2026-10-28

Design: notes/T-1206.md section 4 ("Local refine"). Stages 2 and 4: notes/T-1223.md.

## The mechanism (for T-1650 too)

All in `workers/demand.worker.ts`, no new WASM function: the existing `solve`,
`crowd_with_external` and `cap_with_external` already take another population's loads.

- **Which clusters.** In a solve without the barrier (an edit's, or the one `flush` sends when a zone
  changed level), a dirty cluster is solved locally (`localOf`) when its signature (`sig`: network,
  zones, rounds, fares) is the one its other populations hold solved, its only unsolved populations
  are city layers (commutes, everyday; never a market part), they are at most half of its
  populations, and every other population holds each of this worker's periods from a solve of the
  same `psig` (or from an earlier local refine). Only a level change produces that: a new zone,
  station or line changes `sig`. `?perfOff=refine` turns it off (job `noLocal`).
- **Frozen loads.** Per period: the other populations' segment loads, boardings and alightings on
  the whole network's route nodes, as they stand (the last solve's, after its capacity fit), times
  `capRatio`, the last whole solve's loads before the fit over those after it, kept sparse per
  cluster `sig` and period (only route nodes the fit changed; a few KB). So the fresh populations
  crowd on what the whole solve's last round crowded on.
- **The solve.** The fresh populations alone, each period: free flow, then `rounds` crowding rounds,
  each on frozen + their own loads of the round before (the populations of one cluster step together
  from one total, as in `step`); then the capacity fit on frozen + own with every population's
  riders coming to the platforms (`comeOf`, the others' from their last solve). The others are
  untouched.
- **Splice.** The figures are then read from every population as after any edit (`figures` over
  the clusters), so lines and stations carry the fresh zone's new riders plus the others' old ones,
  and the network's totals change by the zone's difference. `plan.solving` names only the fresh
  zones. The fresh populations' periods are marked `psig + "~local"`, so a later local refine may
  build on them but an edit's solve never keeps them as a whole solve's (T-124): the next edit that
  touches the cluster solves it whole.
- **The bubbles' sums.** A solve whose every solved cluster is a local refine sends the last solve's
  subzone sums for every zone it did not solve (`keptSums`): working all 680 Chinese zones' out again
  was 5.4-5.9 s of a 10 s refine.
- **The lie.** Until that next whole solve, the other zones' riders do not react to the refined
  zone's change of crowding on shared lines (and the zone's own riders crowd on the others' riders
  as they were).
