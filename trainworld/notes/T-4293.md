## Answers

1. **Noise, not master.** Like with like (three samples per action, quiet cores, DEV builds, the same bench), master at
   80cff39c2 and at 800843cf0 match or beat the 11-24 baseline on almost every row (table below). Tonight's alarms came
   from agents' tree runs, which take one sample per action (`--runs 1` in a tree), and that one sample is the first edit
   of its kind in the game: a cold build, often 1.5-3x the later ones. The tree's diff set it against the baseline's
   median of three, mostly warm samples, from a build 25 landings old. Build kind is not it: the trees' runs and mine
   were all on DEV Vites (the bench reads /src/ modules; T-4139 leaves dist builds out), and my DEV master runs match the
   baseline. Load is not it: the slow tree runs had their cores 1-5% busy before the game.
2. **No landing to blame.** followcut2's 910 / 1,607 ms (station / Construct) were on the code of 800843cf0, which in my
   run gave first samples of 338 / 742 and medians of 108 / 372. Fixed instead: the tree diff compares like with like
   (tools/edit_bench.mjs `compare`, below). Filed: T-4294 (stop clicks that sent no edit in two of my three runs).
3. **pagestage3 is not worse** than master on its four flagged "shows" rows: station 14 (master 10-12), one way on 1
   (1), rebuild 2 (1-2). Double up shows 44 against 24-32: its own named steps on a double up's word add up to under
   3 ms (probe below), so the ~10 ms is where the worker's word lands in the 16.7 ms frame, not its code. Everything
   else is equal or faster, except the click run's later clicks (346 against 103-108), which is its base (3d899ba02,
   before T-4281 stages 1 and 2), not its change. Fit to land from the timing side. Its run was stamped NOISY (5 other
   cores claimed elsewhere; its own cores 7% busy before, 40% over the game).

## How far one sample moves on master's own code

Six samples per row over my two master runs (plus the second stations `--steps` logs and does not count): station built
102-503, Construct 243-742, track piece 301-537, undo 98-180, stop reorder 106-202. Tonight's tree runs, one sample each:
station 129-910, Construct 258-1,607, undo 105-612, reorder 152-538. So a tree's lone sample cannot show a change under
about 2-3x on these rows; the baseline's three samples show only part of the spread too.

Why the first sample runs high, from the steps:
- The worker's network build for the first station after the game opened took 38 and 146 ms ("buffers") against 13-47
  for the next five; the first Construct 279 and 606 ms against 109-199. Cold code and caches, every run.
- The wait from an edit's apply to its follow-up's start (`fromApply`, clockFollowUp.ts) was 234 ms for run 1's first
  station against 13-55 for the others; a track piece's is 230-330 every time (held for the pointer's rest, REST_MS
  220, T-2404, by design).
- One station in run 1 (an uncounted second one) spent 75 ms in the rows' samples (`sSamples`) and was built at 503.
