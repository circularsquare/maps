# T-1502: bus money shares by route points

regionMoney.ts `busKmByRegion`: a bus route's road km now go by its own points: the route (flat x, y with its longitude/latitude twin) is cut into pieces of about 200 m ground distance, each to the region at its middle (placeAt), scaled so the pieces add to the road length. A route with no points keeps T-1464's stop-to-stop halves. Used by bus upkeep shares and new-bus-line charges. Rail and ferry shares unchanged. Doc: track-and-service.md (region ledgers). Typecheck and smoke pass; not measured against the real save's bus routes.
