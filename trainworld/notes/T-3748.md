# T-3748 Income short of the fare curve on bent curves

Agent farefix, 2026-11-19, tree farefix (Vite 6128). App only; no sim change.

## Cause

The regional market's curve extra (and its base extra, T-3578) came back in curve dollars and income added it as
if charged. Each zone's population is given the curve times its country's scale (`scaledKnots`), so its
`fare_extra` is charged US$; the market is given the curve as set (`set_fare_curve(curveKnots)` at scale 1, its
weight `marketFareWeight`), so `IntercityApi::fare_extra` / `base_extra` (`fare_x_usd(rn_fare_x, fare_w)`) are
in curve dollars. The demand worker summed both into one `fareX` per route node (`figures`), and `money.ts`
`incomeFor` added `fareX` unscaled while it takes the base and rate at the stops' scales. So every market trip's
extra was 1/scale of what the curve charges. The scale is 1 in the start country of a game with `scales["@"]` and
no own scale (no error there), but not on an older save (no `"@"`, the US at 11.6), with an own scale, or at a
foreign stop. The error goes either way:
- scale over 1, extra positive (steps, a flat first stretch, a curve steeper later): income short;
- scale over 1, extra negative (a curve that flattens with distance, past its first stretch): income over;
- scale under 1 (a country cheaper than the start's): the reverse of each.
So after the fix some games' income from trips between cities falls (fixture below: Philly on the bent curve).

`fareS` / `baseS` are fixed when the solve is packed; `money.ts` re-reads stop scales when zones load, so a stop
whose zone appears in between is charged at the old one until the next solve (cross-border stops only).

Other suspects ruled out on fixtures: the per-km first-stretch rate (`firstStretch`), a pair's extra at its
strategy's mean km (exact on single routes; on route splits it is E[f(km)] against f(E[km]), not seen here),
`cap_loads` cutting the extra by both `fit.seg` and `spread` (the real save comes out within $0.0001 a trip,
over, not under).

## Fix

One rule, the one income already uses: a stretch pays its starting stop's scale, a base its boarding stop's.
- `game/money.ts` `stopFareScales(stop, fares)`: a stop's base and rate multipliers (country scale, or the
  rounded default's ratios, T-1662); `scalesOf` now uses it.
- `workers/demandClient.ts` `packService`: `net.fareS` / `net.baseS`, per route node the multiplier of the stop
  it leaves (run 1's k-th node leaves stop n-1-k), only where any is not 1. Derived from the service, so the
  demand cache key and every solve input are unchanged.
- `workers/demand.worker.ts` `sumAll(..., market)`: the market population's values times that per node; the
  cities' are added as before (same float order, so unchanged to the bit).
- Docs: `docs/track-and-service.md` "Fares and the ledgers"; wiki `fares.md` (the line that said trips between
  cities use the curve "not a country's scale").
