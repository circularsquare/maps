## The rule (DECISIONS 2026-11-11, T-3216, T-3226)

1. A site whose name matches `NOT_CAMPUS` (open, distance and correspondence universities in
   English, Indonesian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Dutch, German and Turkish; atomic, nuclear and
   research centres, national laboratories) is left out.
2. An institution is a counted Wikidata id, or for an id with no count its smallest counted
   institution by P361, P749, P466 or P137 (`wikidata_parents`, cached in
   `data/raw/tier0/wikidata_parents.json` under the same `wikidata-cache` lock as the counts).
3. Its count is cut to 14 (e^(2 x 1.32)) times the curve of its sites in the pack together, and to
   100,000, then split over those sites by area. That share is the site's weight and its cap.
4. A site with no count weighs its area at the counted ones' median students a m² (as before) and
   is capped at 5.4 (e^(1.28 x 1.32), the curve's 90th percentile) times the curve and 50,000.
5. The national total (population x tertiary rate) goes over the sites by weight, none past its
   cap, a capped site's excess to the others (`campus_fill`); what no site can take goes near
   home by adults, as schools' do. None of the six test packs needed that.

## What picks it up

The same as T-3214: a pack's next `education_osm`, in its row's rebuild or through
`merge_cells.py <ids> --through "tier0 {id} --education"`. Unlike T-3214 this needs no new OSM
read (the counts, caps and names work on the existing caches), only the Wikidata parents query
(about 0.2 s a batch of 200 ids). So the counts can be refreshed on every shipped tier 0 pack
without extracts. The step and merge took 2-3 s a pack here (Delhi and Buenos Aires included,
Wikidata already cached); with merge_cells' undo and the step's own process call it about 15 s,
so about 14 hours on one core for all 3,344, under 2 hours on 8, then `cross_everyday` for the
kept shares. The first pass also asks Wikidata for each pack's parents (a few seconds a pack).
