# T-1546: rail against the plane at 4-5 hours (2026-10-27)

Analysis only, no game code changed. From T-1541 and T-1521 round 3: on the every-zone corridor runs
London - Edinburgh is 8% rail of rail + air (45% published), Paris - Marseille 33% (73-82%), Tokyo -
Fukuoka 34% (11%, the other way).

Rulings that stand: one world plane fare ($100 + 5c a km), every mode weighs money alike, money at
0.4 on longer trips, no car upkeep, rides reach 15 km.

## The causes, per pair

**The plane as built** (`intercity.rs` `air_cost`): the place's people-weighted mean drive to each
airport (`drive_min`: 15 minutes plus the straight line x 1.25 at 85 km/h, plain minutes), 90 minutes at
the airports, a flat 25 minute wait, the flight at 750 km/h plus 30 minutes. Rail: access and egress
weighted (walks 1.5x, rides 2.66x the drive + 25), the planned wait (10 minutes at 2 trains an hour) and
the ride in plain minutes, the fare at the rider's weight. Both are scored in one logit at the car's
damped scale (`mode_beta`); there is no mode constant and no nest beyond the plane-or-car split.

**London - Edinburgh: the plane's time at the airports is short.** The station's own zones, London's
real network and a stand-in metro at Edinburgh (middle income group, 2.59 minutes a dollar):

| | access + egress | wait + ride | time | money | perceived |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rail (King's Cross zone) | 50 + 38 | 291 (ride 260, wait 10, the change to the train) | 379 | $99 = 256 | 611 |
| Plane | 66 (drives at 85 km/h) | 90 airports + 25 wait + 72 flying | 253 | $126 = 327 | 580 |

Published airport times (Monmousseau et al. 2021, door-to-door air times from Uber data, taken from
airline recommendations): 90 minutes at the departure airport and 45 at the arrival one in Europe
(Amsterdam, Paris), 100-125 and 35-65 at US airports. The model gives 90 for both ends together, so
the plane is about 45 minutes too quick at the airports. Its flight is about 10 minutes quick too
(531 km: 72 minutes against the 80-85 minute scheduled blocks London - Edinburgh), which I leave: the
45 is the published figure. Rail's frequency is right (LNER 2 an hour; the test line runs 2 midday). With 135 minutes at the airports the station zone goes 23% to 48% rail and the whole pair
14% to 33%.
The rest of the gap is London's spread: from every zone the plane costs the same 66 plain minutes of
drive, while rail pays London's network to King's Cross (46-50 perceived plus the change) at every
zone. Counting each zone's own airport drive does not help London (14% to 10%): its five airports ring
the city, nearer most zones than King's Cross is.

**Paris - Marseille: mostly the test, then the same airport time.** T-1521's run has no city
networks: Paris zones past 15 km of Gare de Lyon get no rail. With Paris's real network and a stand-in
metro at Marseille it is 60% (34% lone). The station zones already take 84-89% rail; what stays low is
Marseille's place beyond the stand-in metro (Aix, 25-40 km out: 27-33%), which a real Aix TGV station
would serve. With 135 airport minutes: 74% (published 73-82%).

**Tokyo - Fukuoka: the fares, then the airport drive.** Station zones (3.55 minutes a dollar):

| | time | money | perceived |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rail | 397 (access 44, egress 37, wait + ride 316) | $119 = 423 | 777 |
| Plane | 298 (drives 84, airports 90, wait 25, flying 100) | $143 = 509 | 807 |

The game's starting Nozomi fare is $119 against ¥23,810 ($159, Ekitan), 0.75x; the world plane fare
is $143 against JAL's average domestic ¥15,700 ($105), 1.36x (T-1521). So in the game rail is $24
cheaper than the plane, in life $30-50 dearer. At the real Nozomi fare (rail's fare x 1.34) Tokyo -
Fukuoka goes to 4% rail; that is the whole inversion. Smaller parts, all toward the plane in life:
the place mean drive to Haneda is 47 minutes and to Fukuoka airport 37, against 26-40 and 19-25 from the
zones that travel most (each zone's own drive: 37% to 29%); flights run about every 20 minutes (the
model's 25 minute wait assumes one every 2 hours: 10 minutes gives 31%); and Japanese airports are
quick (ANA and JAL close security 20 minutes before a domestic departure, bags 30).

Rail frequency is not a cause anywhere: the test lines run 2 (London), 3 (Paris, more than the
real 1 to Marseille) and 6 (the Nozomi, more than the real 2-3 to Hakata) trains an hour midday,
worth at most 5-7 minutes.

Other variants measured (networked London - Edinburgh / Paris - Marseille / Tokyo - Fukuoka):
the plane's access, airports and wait all counted 1.5x: 60 / 83 / 76%; the airport drive per zone
counted as a ride (2.66x + 25 an end): 80 / 89 / 90%, the plane gone from every pair under 650 km;
rail's ride counted 0.8x: 37 / 72 / 61%; the plane's wait at 10 minutes: 10 / 54 / 31%; rail's fare
at the real Nozomi's (Japan only): Tokyo - Fukuoka 4%, with 135 airport minutes 9%.

What it says:
- **No change to the plane's minutes moves all three the right way.** Every one that lifts London -
  Edinburgh lifts Tokyo - Fukuoka too; the only ones that lower Tokyo - Fukuoka (each zone's own drive,
  a shorter wait) lower London - Edinburgh. They differ in their fares (Japan's rail at 0.75 of real
  and its plane at 1.36, the UK's rail at 1.1 and its plane about right) and in their airports.
- **Japan's inversion is the starting fare.** At the real Nozomi fare Tokyo - Fukuoka is 4% built and
  9% with 135 airport minutes, against 11%. A player who sets JR's fares gets about the real share.
  Tokyo - Osaka then shows the car at 43% (25% published), since the same 1.34 is too little there
  (the real Nozomi is 1.8x the game's to Osaka).
- **135 minutes costs the plane on the shorter pairs**, where it is already low: Madrid - Barcelona
  7% to 3% of all (18% published, about 17% lately), Tokyo - Osaka 2% to 0% (11%), the Northeast 5%
  to 1% (5%; there the US car takes the rest, T-1542).

## Proposal

1. **Minutes at the airports 135, was 90** (`AIR_TERMINAL_MIN`; also `marketRule.ts`'s copy):
   Monmousseau et al.'s European figure, 90 before departure and 45 from landing to the kerb, the
   low end of their US airports' 135-190. One world number, no new parameter. London - Edinburgh 14%
   to 33%, Paris - Marseille 60% to 74% (networked; 8% to 19% and 34% to 43% on lone lines); error
   1.77 to 1.68 networked, 1.80 to 1.40 on T-1521's cases. Tokyo - Fukuoka goes 37% to 56% at the
   starting fares, the wrong way, and 4% to 9% at JR's fares.
2. **Nothing more in the model for Japan.** Its gap is fares: the plane's is ruled, rail's is the
   player's, and the starting curve sits at 0.75 of the Nozomi (T-1521's table: Tokyo - Shin-Osaka
   0.56). Whether a new game in Japan should start nearer JR's fares is Anita's call (below).
3. **Not proposed: each zone's own airport drive.** A consistency fix (the plane would cost a far
   suburb what it does), but it moves London the wrong way (its airports ring the city), takes the
   Northeast's plane from 5% to 13%, and costs a plane per zone pair.

The rest of Paris - Marseille is the test (one station a city); of London - Edinburgh, London's
network trip to King's Cross against a flat airport drive, which nothing above fixes without
overshooting the shorter pairs.

## How it was measured

Scripts in `notes/T-1546/`: `t1546_copy.py` copies master's committed sim crate (git archive of HEAD,
c5bc407e) to a scratch folder with `t1546_test.rs` (T-1541's corridor run, every period, three
crowding rounds, the built fares; then the midday zone pairs of each focus corridor dumped with what
the choice needs); `t1546_run.py` writes the rows (main tree's rows plus T-1521's `fw`, `fg`, `cu`)
and runs the cases; `t1546_eval.py` rebuilds the shares under variants (`table`, `parts`, `rings`).
Dumps and `eval_cache.json` in `../artifacts/trainworld/T-1546/` (about 0.5 GB, the Northeast's 300
MB; delete once T-1546 is settled). Runs: 1-2 minutes a case, Japan 35 minutes.

Sources: Monmousseau, Delahaye, Marzuoli, Feron, "Analyzing and comparing door-to-door travel times
for air transportation using aggregated Uber data" (arXiv 2101.08852, Tables I-II); ANA and JAL
Haneda domestic security cut-off 20 minutes (ana.co.jp, jal.co.jp); Madrid - Barcelona rail 83.1% of
rail + air (railwaypro.com, 2025); fares as T-1521's table.
