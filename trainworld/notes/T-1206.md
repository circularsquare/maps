# T-1206: level of detail for demand (design, 2026-10-24)

Anita, 2026-10-24: "a fairly aggressive sort of LOD where we calculate coarsely, and refine if the
player goes and actually looks at an area", as the general answer for big networks. This is the
design only: no code or data changed. Memory background: notes/T-1199.md (where a worker's memory
goes), notes/T-1156.md (the China network). Figures marked "est." are worked out from those notes,
not measured; stage 0 below measures them before anything is built.

## For Anita

- **What coarse means** (measured and built, T-1222, section 9). A zone nobody is looking at keeps
  the same model with fewer samples: one everyday destination sample per 64 trips instead of per 8,
  1 to 8 a cell rather than 2 to 16, and small commute pairs dealt in bigger chunks. Every zone's
  number of trips stays the same (within 0.05%). What moves is how they split between train, car
  and walking, by sampling noise. Periods, purposes, crowding, the zone's engine and the longer
  trips between cities stay as they are.
- **No lean, measured.** Over 18 zones (4 Chinese metros, 9 towns, 4 countryside zones, each with
  a test metro of four lines, and New York's real network) and 8 draws each, coarse rail is +0.05%
  against fine on average, +0.14% in the metros, +0.13% for the Chinese zones' total. The first
  recipe (at most 4 samples a cell) leaned metros +0.5% and was dropped for 8.
- **Memory.** A zone holds 25-35% less natively (Shanghai 24.6 to 16.4 MB, Wuhan 9.5 to 6.4, a
  town 6.1 to 4.5, New York 80 to 60), its everyday layer opens in half the time, and its solve
  is 10-50% shorter.
- **When a zone goes fine.** When you look at it (the zone in view for 2 seconds), click in it
  (a bubble, a station, the zone), or build in it. It goes coarse again once you have not looked
  at it, clicked in it or built in it for 10 minutes, whatever the memory, and sooner when memory
  runs short (the zone you looked at least recently first).
- **No direction in the noise** (Anita, 2026-10-24). Coarse must keep on average how much
  ridership there is. Stage 0 picks the recipe partly on a measured bias: each coarse zone's rail
  trips against its fine solve's, averaged over many zones and seeds, must come out near zero.
  Stage 5 checks it again on the China network.
- **Small games barely change.** A game opens with every zone fine that fits, and only zones left
  alone for 10 minutes go coarse. On your Paris and Shanghai save that is Paris while you work in
  Shanghai, or the other way round: about 0.1-0.2 GB. What helps that save is T-1204 (about 1.2 GB
  less at load).
- **The China network** (169 zones): about 1.5-1.8 GB a solving worker against 2.4-2.6 GB today
  (est. from section 9; T-1204 gives the first 0.4 GB), the load's pack opening shorter, an
  edit's solve a few seconds shorter (the longer trips' searches are most of it, T-782). On that
  network's high-speed lines a zone has few stations, so its samples (which coarse halves) are
  more of it than its pairs: about 20-30% less a zone, not the 45% first estimated. All 745
  Chinese zones would need about 4 GB a worker (est.): stage 6.
- **What you would see.** Looking at a coarse zone shows its rough figures at once. The status tag
  says "Working out Wuhan in detail" with the small spinner, and the figures change in one step
  3-10 seconds later. No grey: grey keeps meaning "no figures yet".

Anita's answers, 2026-10-24 (DECISIONS.md): approved as designed, except that zones left alone go
coarse even when memory is fine, and that coarse must not bias ridership. Coarse figures are not
marked in the Cities list.

## 1. Why the samples, and not blocks or periods

A reached zone costs each solving worker about 12 MB on average on the China network (2.4-2.6 GB a
worker, less the 450 MB market, over 169 zones), much more for a metro (Paris and Shanghai: 185-285
MB a worker for the two). Of that, by T-361's breakdown of New York and T-397's measurements:

| part | scales with | share of a zone (est.) |
|---|---|---|
| everyday destination samples, their pair indices (per network), their probabilities (per period held) | samples | 55-65% |
| the commute pass: subzone pairs, the S x S station table per period, access | subzone pairs, stations | 15-20% |
| the everyday layer's own copy of the commute engine (T-361 item 3) | the pack | 5-10% |
| pack data, cells, zones, gravity table | cells and zones | 10-15% |

So samples are the lever. The others looked at and set aside:

- **4 km blocks** (T-852). Measured there: peak memory moves -15% to +2% (the decay table triples).
  It saves opening time, not memory, and blocks have to be built by the pipeline (gravity
  re-solved). Packs that get blocks get them for everyone; not a runtime level.
- **Fewer periods.** Each solving worker holds only its own periods, so their memory is already
  divided between workers. Cutting them would leave the boarding account, the money's hours and
  the line panel's crowding without figures for the periods skipped. Kept.
- **Fewer purposes.** Airports, medical and attractions already take 2 samples a cell. Errands and
  social could share one draw in a coarse zone (same 0.7 km halving), which halves the floor's
  samples in thin country. Kept as an option for stage 0 to measure, not in the first recipe.
- **Fewer crowding rounds.** T-434 already stops each population's rounds once its loads settle.
  Little left to gain.

## 2. The two levels

| | fine (today) | coarse (T-1222, section 9) |
|---|---|---|
| everyday samples a cell and purpose | trips / 8, 2 to 16 (airport, medical, attractions 2) | trips / 64, 1 to 8 (those three 1) |
| commute pairs dealt in chunks (T-874) | pairs under 0.1 trips a period | pairs under 1 trip a period |
| everyday layer's engine | its own copy beside the commutes' | the same (sharing it is 4-8% of a zone and changes the everyday subzones, so not a level, T-361) |
| cells, subzones, access, periods, purposes, crowding, capacity fit | as today | as today |
| the market (longer trips between cities) | as today (T-1204 shrinks it) | as today |

Both rules keep every budget exact: each home cell's everyday trips per purpose, each zone pair's
commutes, each 8 x 8 block's chunked trips. The samples keep their seeds and stratified offsets and
their count depends only on the cell's budget, so a zone's figures at a level are the same on every
load. Stage 0 picks the numbers (64, the floor, the chunk)
by measuring, as T-397 picked trips / 8.

Unreached zones and bare zones (T-325: viewed, no station) are untouched. A bare zone is always
viewed, so it is fine anyway, and the auxiliary worker holds it as today.

## 3. When a zone refines and coarsens

The page keeps a **fine set** (`demandClient`), recomputed when the view settles, on a click, on an
edit and when a solve's memory is reported. Priority, highest first:

1. the zone in view (the top bar's), once it has been in view for 2 s, so a pan across the map
   refines nothing;
2. the selected zone: a zone or bubble selection, a station clicked (from the map or a panel), a
   trip explorer pair's two ends;
3. zones the player edited in the last 15 minutes of wall time: track, a station, a line's stops,
   trains or fare zone (the zone of the edited piece, as `stationZone` places it);
4. other zones on screen at zoom 9 or more;
5. every other reached zone, the most recently in 1-4 first.

**Budget.** Each solving worker's memory for zones: first every reached zone at coarse, then zones
made fine in priority order while the total fits. Costs per zone come from the same local trips a
day that `memoryCheck.ts` (T-1197) uses, with a coarse rate beside the fine one, corrected by the
workers' reported WASM after each solve. The budget is about 1.5 GB a worker on a device reporting 8
GB (`deviceMemory`), less below, and never past T-1197's 3.5 GB line.

**At load** the fine set is the save's (`demandFine`, section 4). A save without one starts with
every zone fine that fits the budget, in priority order (the zone in view first).

**Coarsening** (Anita: zones left alone run coarse even when memory is fine):
- a zone in 4 or 5 that has been in none of 1-3 for 10 minutes of wall time goes coarse, whatever
  the memory;
- over budget, the lowest-priority fine zone goes at once;
- never one in 1-3, and never one made fine in the last 5 minutes (no flapping while the player
  looks between two cities).

Each coarsening is a level change (below), so the zone's cluster is solved again at the next whole
solve. Zones that go coarse together in one check are changed together, so a long session pays one
re-solve for them, not one each.
WASM never shrinks, so coarsening bounds the peak (the next refine reuses the space) rather than
lowering it. T-1205's worker restart is what gives memory back.

**A level change is a close and reopen** in every solving worker: the samples are drawn at opening.
The pack comes from the browser's cache, as T-301's reopening does.

## 4. Consistent figures across the seam

**One model, two sample densities.** A coarse zone is not a different model, so there is no seam
in the rules: a line from a fine zone into a coarse one carries both populations' riders, summed and
crowded together as now, and the coarse side is noisier but not biased. The trip explorer works out
its two places exactly at any level, as now.

**A refine moves only the refined zone's own figures** (by its sampling noise) and its neighbours'
through crowding on shared lines. The trips counted never move; train, car and walking shares do.

**No bias** (Anita, 2026-10-24: "preserve on average how much ridership there is... don't wanna
directionally bias stuff"). Exact budgets keep the trips, but the mode choice is not linear, so
fewer samples could still lean rail up or down (T-852's 4 km blocks leaned everyday rail +30-40% on
rural lines before the decay was averaged over squares). Stage 0 measures, per candidate recipe,
each zone's rail trips coarse against fine over many zones (metros, towns, countryside; China and
New York's real network) and several sample seeds, and the recipe must pass:
- **bias**: the mean of (coarse rail / fine rail - 1) over zones and seeds within ±0.5%, and the
  same over the network's total rail;
- **noise**: a zone's train share within 1 point, no station of 1,000+ boardings a day past 10%.
A recipe that fails the bias test is not loosened into passing by a correction factor; it is
dropped for one with more samples (or the merged-purposes option, which removes the floor's
unevenness rather than adding noise). Stage 5 checks both again on the China network with the
fine set moving as a player would move it.

**Local refine.** On a big network one cluster (T-326) can hold every zone: high speed joins them.
Solving the whole cluster again for a refine would cost an edit's solve (29-31 s on China). Instead
the refined zone's population is solved alone against the cluster's loads frozen from the last
solve, the way each population already reads the cluster's summed loads every round and
`cap_with_external` already caps one population against them. Its figures replace its coarse
ones; the lines it touches get its new contribution; the network's totals change by its difference.
The next whole solve (any edit) solves it with the rest as usual. Between the two, the other zones'
figures do not see the refined zone's crowding change. That is a few riders on shared lines, and it
is the same order as the "before your last change" figures the game already shows between solves.

**The top bar.** It shows the zone in view, which is fine 2 s after arriving plus the local refine.
Until then it shows the coarse figures, the status tag says "Working out Wuhan in detail", and the
split's hover ends "(rough while Wuhan is worked out in detail)".

**Points and money.** They come from the solve's figures at whatever level, a game hour at a time,
over the past 24 hours (T-684). A refine changes one zone's hours from then on, so points move by
that zone's difference spread over a day: no jump. Coarse noise has no direction, so it neither adds
nor takes points on average.

**Stars** (T-685) come only from a fine zone's final figures, since a star is never taken back and a
coarse zone could cross 10% by noise. (Agent's call.)

**Saves and reloads.** The fine set goes in the save's header (`demandFine`: zone ids) and a reload
starts from it (within the budget), so a reloaded game shows the same figures to the bit. T-328's
cache key gains each zone's level.

## 5. What the player sees

- Looking at a coarse zone: its bubbles, city inspector and top bar show the coarse figures at once,
  and change in one step when the refine lands. No grey, no loading bar: those stay for zones with
  no figures (T-343, T-421).
- The status tag (where "Updating demand" shows) says "Working out Wuhan in detail", the demand
  side of what T-1200's busy tag does for the clock worker. One zone at a time: the latest looked
  at first.
- A clicked bubble or station in a coarse zone: the panel's lines that wait show the 8 px ring
  (`.spin`) as they do today, and fill when the refine lands. The far ends and trip purposes need
  the fine samples, so they wait; the commuters by mode do not.
- Coarse figures are not marked in the Cities list (Anita). The one remaining mark is a muted line
  in the city inspector of a coarse zone opened without going there: "Rough figures. Go there to
  work them out in detail." Stage 4 shows it to Anita, and it goes if she would rather have none.

## 7. What it breaks or complicates

- **Same network, same figures.** Today a network's figures are the same to the bit however it was
  loaded. With levels they depend on the fine set too. The save and cache carry it (section 4); the
  checks pin it (`?lod=fine` forces every zone fine, `?lod=coarse` every zone coarse).
- **Bare zones (T-325).** Untouched: a bare zone is viewed, so fine, and its bit-equal check against
  the zone solved with no station still holds. A coarse zone whose last station is deleted becomes
  bare, which opens fine in the auxiliary worker as any bare zone does.
- **Clusters (T-326).** A population's key gains its level, so a whole solve re-solves only the
  clusters whose zones changed level. The local refine is new: a population solved against frozen
  loads outside a whole solve, its result spliced into the shown one (the same splice as an edit's
  cluster results, T-393, at the scale of one population).
- **Kept periods (T-124).** A level change is a new population: its periods are solved afresh.
  Other zones' kept periods stand.
- **The boarding account.** Reads demand's period loads by route node: five periods are kept at
  both levels, so nothing changes.
- **The demand cache (T-328).** Key with levels; a reload restores the fine set, so it still hits.
- **Memory check (T-1197).** Its formula needs the coarse rate, or China-sized saves stay refused
  that would now fit.
- **Selections (T-098, T-285).** A selection's far ends and trip purposes read the samples. They
  are answered after the refine, never from coarse samples, so a panel never shows coarse far ends.
- **Checks.** A game opens with every zone fine that fits, and zones go coarse only after 10
  minutes left alone, so smoke, t301, t325, t326, t328 and the native dumps hold as they are while
  they run under 10 minutes; any longer check pins `?lod=fine`. New: a check that loads a big network with `?lod=coarse`, refines a zone by
  viewing it, and compares its figures with `?lod=fine`; and one that a reload restores the fine set.

## 9. Stages 0 and 1, measured and built (T-1222, 2026-10-24)

**How.** `everyday::lod_tests::t1222_probe` (ignored; `TW_LOD_PACKS`, `TW_LOD_RECIPES` and the
rest in its comment) opens a zone's commutes and everyday layer at a recipe, sets a network, solves
the five periods as the worker does (free flow, three crowding rounds on both layers' loads, the
capacity fit), and writes a JSON line: memory by part (the counting allocator, `alloc.rs`; the
held parts found by dropping them one by one), times, and the day's trips, rail and boardings by
station and line. Zones: Shanghai, Guangzhou, Chengdu, Wuhan; nine towns (Jianli, Tongcheng,
Daiyue, Leizhou, Yongchuan, Nanbu, Huangdao, Kaiping, Sanhe); four countryside zones (Pei,
Suining, Ju, Zhucheng), "town" meaning a quarter or more of the people in cells over 5,000/km²;
their packs copied from data/ before T-1184's rebuild reached them. Each Chinese zone gets a test
metro (four lines crossing at its busiest 2 km square, 5-25 km each way by its people, a station
every 1.2-1.5 km, 12/8/4 trains an hour): the real China network gives a zone only a few
high-speed stations, too few riders to see a lean. New York on its real network
(`nyc_real_track.json`). Each recipe at 8 draws (3 for New York): `Sampling::salt` mixes every
sample's seed and `Params::chunk_salt` the chunks' start; draw 0 is the game's. Outputs and the
analysis: `../artifacts/trainworld/t1222/` (`an2.txt`), `notes/t1222/analyse.py`. Native, one core
each, three at once; times are rough.

**Fine against itself.** One draw of fine is not the truth: between fine draws a zone's rail moves
0.1-0.3% (sd) in a metro, 0.3-1% in a town and up to 2.3% in a countryside zone (Pei). So the bias
is each zone's mean over a recipe's draws against its mean over fine's draws, then averaged over
zones (se from both sets of draws).
