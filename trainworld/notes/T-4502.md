# T-4502: a selected city shows no trips to other places while its market opens (agent marketcalc, 2026-11-27)

Seen in T-4501: in a new game (New York, no stations, one worker), Philadelphia, the first city selected, showed
"To other places 0" and "No intercity trips are modeled here yet." until its market pack opened and the next
calculation landed. Tree marketcalc.

## For Anita

- **Changed.** While the figures shown have no trips between zones yet and they are on their way, a selected city's
  panel says "Calculating trips" instead of "To other places 0". The status tag waits on it the same way. On a new
  New York game with one worker, Philadelphia showed "Calculating trips" for 6.4 s after the click, then
  "To other places 2.15M" (top destinations New York 774k, Reading 284k, Allentown 244k). The 0 never showed.
- **Not waited for:** a city picked once trips between zones are in the figures shows them at once, even while its
  own market pack still opens (below).
- **Not fixed (T-4533):** in a game with no station yet, a city picked with the map zoomed out too far for zones to
  load still shows "To other places 0": nothing is needed there, so no market pack opens and nothing is calculated.

## The cause, from the code

- The panel (`ui/inspector/CityInspector.tsx`) shows the market's figures whenever `intercityDemand` is not null.
  A result solved with no market pack open (a new game with no station: the first solves have none) has
  `NO_CORRIDORS`, a book with no key; `rowsOf` answers `NO_ROWS` for it, so `placeFigures` gives no places and the
  panel reads "no trips" where it should read "not in yet". The same holds while a result's book has not come
  (`corridorsOf` asks `corridorsAgain` and answers `NO_CORRIDORS`).
- A city selection wants its zone's market pack (`marketZones`, T-801): it downloads, opens, the market is
  composed, `covers` sees an open market the shown solve lacks and a new solve goes; its result has the book.
- Once any market is composed its rows hold every place's longer trips and commutes (the world's rows,
  docs/demand-market.md), so only the first market is worth waiting for. Waiting for the selected zone's own pack
  (the literal goal) would hold the panel for a whole re-solve on a big save: T-4501 measured Boston, with no pack of
  its own open, answered in 0.26 s during an edit's whole solve on the real New York save; a new solve with its pack
  shows only at its final. Its own pack changes little: rail there needs a station within 30 km, which would have
  opened the pack already, and everyday outings across a zone line are built only between served zones.

## The change

- `game/demand.ts`: `marketComing` (written by the demand client: a wanted market pack is downloading, opening or
  open, and demand is not idle, since an idle client sends no solve) and `marketDue` (`marketComing` and not
  `marketIn` of the shown day: the same "market in" test the goals and achievements use).
- `workers/demandClient.ts` `noteMarkets`, at the end of `ensure` and `closeFull` (where `marketWanted` and the
  market items in `fetching` change).
- `ui/inspector/CityInspector.tsx`: with `marketDue` the market counts as not shown, so To other places and the
  destinations wait ("Calculating trips", reused); a zone with its own figures keeps Trips within and its graph.
- `ui/StatusTag.tsx` `clickWait`: a selected city also waits while `marketDue`.
- Docs: docs/ui-inspectors.md (the city view's waits). DECISIONS.md Map and UI.
- Left alone: the map's bubbles draw none while there is no market (they state no 0), the trip graph draws the
  local trips alone as it does with no market, and a trip's panel waits for its own market answer.
