- No national DENUE file: `denue_00_csv.zip` and `denue_15_csv.zip` answer a 2 KB HTML page with HTTP 200. `mexico.fetch`
  rejects anything under 1 MB or not starting with a zip header. The download page: https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/descarga/?ti=6
- INEGI serves one connection at about 200 KB/s; `mexico.py fetch` takes six at once (about 1.8 MB/s, 15 minutes for all).
- Encodings differ by state: some AGEB files are UTF-8 with a BOM, others Latin-1; DENUE is Latin-1. Only code and
  number columns are read, as Latin-1, with the BOM stripped from the first header.
- ITER lists every locality, those of one and two dwellings too; its `9998`/`9999` rows repeat them as roll-ups, so
  they are left out. Every one of ITER's 189,432 localities has a point (`LONGITUD`/`LATITUD` as degrees, minutes,
  seconds).
- 331 AGEB rows (1.08M people) have no urban AGEB polygon: their localities are not urban in `00a`, so they count
  as rural localities at ITER's figure. Urban AGEB polygons 63,982 (99,260,887) plus rural localities 184,521
  (26,753,137) = ITER's 126,014,024 exactly; every municipio's rest is 0.

## Method
- Urban AGEBs by representative point in the outline, people over their cells by GHSL (England's cell_units).
- Rural localities (point in the outline): each takes the cells, not in an urban AGEB, nearer it than any other
  locality and within 2 km (`RURAL_KM`); GHSL inside; a locality with none goes to its nearest free cell.
  `cell_points` gives every locality a cell at its point.
- A municipio's rest (0 with these files) over its free cells by its GHSL share inside the outline.
- Jobs: DENUE's units binned once into res-9 cells (`mx_denue_cells.npz`, 228,402 cells); a row takes the cells it
  has, cells of its outline it lacks go to its nearest cell. Band sizes: 0-5 people 2, 6-10 8, 11-30 18, 31-50 40,
  51-100 72, 101-250 160, 251+ 900. Censos Economicos 2019 (press release 305/20): 6,373,169 establishments,
  36,038,272 people; units of 0-10 people 37.2% of them (2.2 a unit), 251 and more 32.1% in 0.2% of units (about
  900 a unit). DENUE 2026-05 at these sizes: micro 13.8M (census 13.4M), 11-250 12.6M (11.1M), 251+ 11.9M (11.6M),
  38.3M in all (6,138,075 units).
- The gap: World Bank 2025 jobs a resident 0.455 (tier 0's) less DENUE's 38.3M / 126.0M = 0.304, so 0.151 a
  resident (19.0M jobs nationally: farm work, street trade, domestic work) goes over each row's people by tier 0's
  proxy (`weight`). Each row's jobs a resident is tier 0's on average, more where DENUE is dense.
