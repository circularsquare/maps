# T-2747: a sparse longer-trips answer for a bubble selection

Agent sparse, 2026-11-07, in its own tree (`.tw-trees/sparse`). Design and the before profile: notes/T-2746.md
"Why icSel is slow".

## What changed

- **Rust** (`sim/src/demand/intercity/selection.rs`): `selection` collects into `Selection` (the head dense, the far
  zones in `ByGid`, a map from global zone to its slot), adding every entry in the same order as the dense array did.
  `selection_all_day` and `selection_rail_day` answer it packed: matched share, 8 per place, the commutes 4 per place,
  the trips with places that have no zone (2), then the far-zone count and per zone (gid ascending, all-zero zones
  left out) gid, x, y, place and 38 numbers (`SEL_Z`: longer trips 4, commutes 4, the 5 `E_CROSS` kinds 6 each).
  `station_flows_day` and `no_station_flows` the same way (6 per place, then gid, x, y, place, starts, ends).
  `selection_flows`/`selection_flows_day`/`station_flows` (not exported to WASM; tests) still give the old dense
  layout (`Selection::dense`); tests read the exported answers through `unpack_selection`/`unpack_rail`/`unpack_station`.
  `Market::gid_xy` is `write_zone_xy` for one zone.
- **App**: `demandSums.ts` `addIntercity` merges two packed answers by gid (a new array), `sumParts` folds with it,
  `selectionFlows`/`stationFlows` walk the listed zones (`intercityHead`, `intercityRow`, `SEL_Z`).
  `demand.worker.ts` `icSel` merges each market part's rail with `addIntercity`; `icStation` sends the binding's
  array without a `Float64Array.from` copy. `checks/t1177-cross-everyday.mjs` and `t1374-touristy.mjs` read the new layout.
- **One departure from the design**: each listed zone carries its own x, y and place instead of the page reading every
  zone's once. Three numbers per listed zone (a few thousand zones a click) against a 15-30 MB table, a new message
  and a cache on the page and in the sums worker; no gain to measure.

## Identity

- `t1204_signature` (the five-pack composition, 147 hashed outputs, the exported answers unpacked to the dense
  layout): `a6a2d9bb9913821c` before and after, to the bit. A rail answer's zone x, y and place were zero in the
  dense layout (rail-only), so `unpack_rail` zeroes them.
- In the browser (`clicks.mjs` with an identity step: 8 bubble selections, each end's `intercitySelection` asked
  straight of the client, and 12 stations' `intercityStation`, all output serialised; `cmp.mjs`):
  NYC real save 1,552,698 numbers all identical; NA day 383, 5,889,266 numbers all identical (nothing within float
  noise either: every number is bit-equal). Each entry is added in the same order as before, and a zone missing
  from one answer is the dense array's 0 (`x + 0 = x`).
