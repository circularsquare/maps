# T-1144: Kwai Chung missing from Hong Kong

## Cause

Hong Kong is one pack, `hon` (plan row 109, one unit `HKG-ADM0`). Kwai Chung and Tsing Yi, the
whole Kwai Tsing district (about 0.5M people), lay outside `data/work/hon.outline.geojson`, so no
cell was made there. Not water: the pack's own water mask has Kwai Fong, Kwai Hing and Lai King as
land; no other pack's outline covers them either.

The outline comes from `osm_units.py`. Hong Kong has no geoBoundaries, so its plan unit is the
territory whole from Natural Earth 1:10m. That outline lacks Tsing Yi, the container terminals and
most of the reclaimed shore. `match` picked the 18 OSM districts (admin level 6) as "several
relations lying mostly inside the unit", which needs half a relation's land inside the unit.
Kwai Tsing had 0.44 (its Tsing Yi half and the terminals are sea to Natural Earth); the other 17
had 0.71-1.00. The leftover of the unit that no picked district covered, Natural Earth's Kwai
Chung, was about 1% of the unit, under the 10% at which the unit's own polygon fills a gap, so
it was dropped as well.

## Fix

`pipeline/osm_units.py`: a unit of kind "Natural Earth admin 0" (a territory whole: Hong Kong,
Macau) takes a relation when a quarter of its land is inside (`TERRITORY_INSIDE = 0.25`), not
half. Such a unit has no sibling unit a relation could belong to instead, and a neighbouring
country's relation lies mostly outside. Every other unit is unchanged. Rule in
docs/adding-a-city.md 3, "Outline".

Checked without touching data/ (outlines written to `artifacts/trainworld/t1144/`, script
`outline_stage.py` in the agent's scratchpad, patching `build_city.WORK` and `osm_units.BOXES`):
- old code: 17 districts, 1,086 km2, byte-for-byte today's outline (`before/`);
- new code: 18 districts, Kwai Tsing r7351646 among them, 1,109 km2, the old outline plus 23 km2,
  nothing lost (`after/`);
- Macau with the new code: the same 21 km2 outline (it now picks the level-6 relations rather than
  one level-3 one; the outline is the same). mcu needs no rebuild.
- `build_city hon` in a scratch tree (`artifacts/trainworld/t1144/tree`, `TW_RUN_ROOT`): the
  res-9 cells reach Kwai Fong, Kwai Hing, Lai King and Tsing Yi (5,979 cells, 7.30M people; today's
  res-9 pack 5,761 cells, 6.79M). It stopped at the gravity step, as the tree had no sim copy (tree since deleted).
  The rise is Kwai Tsing (census 2021: 0.50M) and T-1150's population rule, which hon predates.
