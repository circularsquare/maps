# T-4342 Station improvement upkeep against real maintenance

Anita, 11-25: "a couple people complaining about the station improvement maintenance change making their rural lines unprofitable. could we briefly look into whether the improvement maintenance costs are realistic? if they are too high we can lower them. if they are too low relative to irl we can discuss". Research only: nothing was run in the game, nothing built or changed.

## What changed and where it is

T-3922 (2026-11-21, 0.4.6) made every station upgrade, passage and station building pay upkeep. It started at 0.1% of price a game day (809039d2d) and Anita raised it to 0.2% the same day (e778ce68a). Code: `upgrades::UPKEEP_SHARE = 0.002` in sim/src/track/upgrades.rs. DECISIONS.md 2026-11-21 still says 0.1%: it needs the 0.2% fix when this task lands. Notes: notes/T-3922.md (also still says 0.1%).

The upgrade list is: terminal, park and ride, waiting room, passing tracks (the "Station upgrades" row), plus passages and station buildings. There are no elevators or platform screen doors in the game; passages stand in for the walkway side of that.

## How game days compare with real ones

A game day counts 100 real days for money (`ECONOMY` = 100, research/upkeep-france.md). So a game figure per day divided by 100 times 365 is the real figure per year, and real per year times 0.274 (100/365) is the game figure per day. Prices (construction) are real, so 0.2% of price a game day is 0.73% of price a real year. Everything below uses that.

## Verdicts

| Improvement | Game per day | Real range per day | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terminal | US$75k | US$38-77k (2 track-km, maintenance to maintenance plus renewal) | about right, at the top |
| Passing tracks | US$45k | US$23-46k (1.2 track-km) | about right, at the top |
| Park and ride | US$40k | US$36-130k for the ~1,300 spaces US$20M buys (US$15k a space all in); US$5-20k for a rural 200-space lot | about right to a little low for what the price buys; high for a rural-sized lot |
| Waiting room | US$10k | US$0.8-1.9k (shelter), US$3-7k (small hall) | too high for what a waiting room is; low only if US$5M is read as a 2,500 m2 hall |
| Passage | US$56k | US$22-300k, mid-range by walkway count, low by the 2-4% rule | about right to low, discuss |
| Station building | US$4 a m2 | US$11-22 a m2 | too low, 3-5 times, discuss |

The game as a whole sits under real on purpose (T-867: running cost and track upkeep about a quarter to half of real so fares can cover them). At 0.73% of price a year it is under the 1-4% a year real buildings and lots take. That is why the big-ticket items come out low and only the small cheap one (the waiting room, whose price is far larger than a real one) comes out high.

## One rural example, from the constants

US, ground, double track, 3 stations 6 km apart (12 km), 2-car trains, one train an hour each way for 16 hours. All figures game units a day. I worked it from the constants; nothing was run in the game.

- Running: 12 km x 32 trips = 384 train-km x (US$4 + 2 x US$2) x 100 = US$307k
- Track upkeep: 12 km x US$35k = US$420k
- Stations: 3 x US$50k = US$150k
- Fixed total without upgrades: US$877k
- Fares: a 8 km trip pays US$2.50 + 8 x US$0.20 = US$4.10, times 100 = US$410 a rider. Break-even 2,140 riders a day.
- Typical upgrades, a park and ride and a waiting room at each of the 3 stations: 3 x (US$40k + US$10k) = US$150k, 17% on top of the fixed total. Break-even 2,505 riders, 365 more.
- The park and ride is US$120k of that 150k. One park and ride costs 80% of the station it sits at.

So a rural line is unprofitable at rural ridership before any upgrade (track and stations are 65% of the cost), and the improvement upkeep adds about a sixth. Trains an hour is the player's lever: at one train every 2 hours running falls to US$154k and the upgrades are 21% of the cost.

## Recommendation

1. **Waiting room: lower to 0.1% of price, US$5k a day** (US$18k a real year, inside the shelter-to-small-hall range, top end). Saves US$5k a day a station, which is 1% of the example's costs: it is correct, not a fix for rural lines. Needs a per-upgrade share in upgrades.rs (a small change) or the waiting room price set at its 0.2% equivalent; DECISIONS.md and docs/track-and-service.md 6.4 text follow. Hover reads "Upkeep $5k per day", already built from the price.
2. **Everything else stays at 0.2%.** Terminal and passing tracks sit at the top of the real range; park and ride is at the low end for the lot its price implies.
3. **Rural lines: the real-world data gives no reason to cut the park and ride rate.** If she still wants rural relief, the honest fix is the size of the lot, not the rate: US$20M buys about 1,300 spaces (a city hub), real rural lots are 50-200 spaces at US$0.5-3M. A cheaper small park and ride (say US$5M, US$10k a day, 300 spaces) would match a rural lot. That changes what players can build and the price list, so it is hers to decide. Dropping all upgrades back to 0.1% (the original T-3922 rate) would save US$75k a day in the example (8.5% of its costs) but is below real: 0.37% of price a year against 1-4%.
4. **For discussion, too low against real:** buildings (US$4 a m2 against US$11-22) and passages by the 2-4% rule. I would leave them: they are rare, and raising them goes against her "mindless spamming" aim only if spam happens there.
