# T-4377 touch drag to group

Cause: LinesTab.tsx pointerDown returned at once for pointerType "touch" (the drag was desktop-only), so rows never dragged on a phone.
Fix: on touch the drag starts after a 350 ms press-and-hold with under 10 px of movement; a swipe before that aborts (the list scrolls; pointercancel also aborts). Once dragging, a non-passive touchmove preventDefault stops the list scrolling, contextmenu is suppressed, and the same ghost/marks/dropLine run as on a mouse. Desktop path unchanged.
Check: emulated phone (420x800, CDP touch events, NYC sandbox, two lines): swipe starts no drag; hold + drag onto the second row made a group of both. Screenshot: scratchpad touchgroup/drag.png.
