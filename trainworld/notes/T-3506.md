## What counts as an enclave

A zone (any pack, city or countryside) is an enclave when **one other pack holds at least 90% of its border with
packs**. Coast, dead space and land of another country with no pack don't count, the same as in the no-ring rule's
wrap test (docs/zones.md "No ring zones"). So a zone between one zone and the sea, a border or dead space is listed; the
sea, border or dead space is then a reason in its call, not a filter. A zone inside a zone of another country (Macau in
Zhuhai) is listed too and kept.

Scan: every registered pack's `boundary.geojson`, read 2026-11-16 22:56 (4,100 packs), each border split into the packs
within 0.4 km of it, then the rest into sea (the zone's own sea in `packs/outlines.json`, for the 163 zones where a
pack holds 80% or more; Natural Earth's coast for the rest, which only labels groups) and land. Read-only on everything.

## Rules for the call, first match wins

1. **Another country around it**: keep. Sovereignty is a real line (Macau, Hong Kong, Singapore, Ceuta, Melilla,
   Brunei and Miri, Tierra del Fuego and Río Gallegos beside Punta Arenas).
2. **The encloser is one plan unit and the enclave is at the floor or over**: keep, nothing can move at that unit
   level. Prague in Central Bohemia, Samarinda in Kutai Kartanegara, Dublin in Mid-East, Gdańsk (Trójmiejski) in
   Wejherowo. Central Bohemia is 1.47M already, so no merge.
3. **Geography leaves one neighbour**: island halves, peninsula tips, coastal strips, the edge of covered land.
   Keep, as a group (the "coast" and "edge" tables).
4. **Under the floor and a district of the encloser's own city or prefecture**: merge. Arnavutkoy (an Istanbul
   district), Yuhuan (a county of Taizhou prefecture), Suchetgarh and R.S. Pura (Jammu district tehsils). Zones that
   are their own first or second level unit keep their place even under the floor: Chalkidiki (its own regional unit,
   and Greece's rows predate the floor), Kilis (its own province), Taganrog (its own urban okrug), Tongyeong (its own
   si).
5. **A ring of several units around a city at the floor or over, no coast or border to explain it**: shift, by moves
   only (plan_edit cannot make a row). Şanlıurfa, Diyarbakır, Perth, La Plata, Bucaramanga.

## Why these slipped past the no-ring rule (not followed up)

The wrap test runs while zones merge. Rows built before it (Turkey's province centres), remnants and FUA-whole steps
after it, and metros built by hand don't meet it. A standing check after each join (this scan, about 25 minutes)
would catch new ones; worth adding to the morning checks next to split_zones.py if enclaves come back.

## Calls made (for DECISIONS.md once shipped)
