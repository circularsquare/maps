## What changed

- `demand.worker.ts`: a barrier solve (a load's, a cached load's re-solve, a memory re-solve) makes a `Work` like an
  edit's. Units are one cluster's round of a period; planned for every group up front, those a worker holds already
  (`reuse`) or has no side of are taken back after `setUp`, as the edit path does. The group solved ahead (the zone in
  view's) counts as the view's units, else all units do. Units with no earlier pass are sized by populations at 0.9 per
  round (`sizeB`): on this save a load's rounds took 50, 46, 46 and 43 s per cluster, where the edits' 0.75 gave a time
  left 1.4 times too short.
- `demandClient.ts`: the `work` handler no longer drops barrier solves. `unitsLeft` (`editLeft` over the workers' parts)
  replaces the load model's time left and share where it can tell: the loading card's solve stage and `solveLeft`
  (the zones' hover/bars). Until a unit has run (set_network is 24 s here) the old model stands in.
- `StatusTag.tsx`: the tag "Calculating trips in N cities..." during a load's solve gets the hover's time left
  ("About 2 min left"), the strings of an edit's. No new string.
- `noteEditSolve` still skips barrier solves, so a load never moves the edits' pace prior.

## Found, not fixed

- A card load right after a cached load (`trace_cold3_staleshares.jsonl`: cache miss) sat at 91-98% for 230 s: the
  remembered stage times (`was`) came from the cached load, whose demand stage was seconds. The card's shares are
  fixed from that, so the solve's share is tiny whatever its estimate. Every deploy (a cache miss) after a cached load
  would meet it. Fix would be to not remember a cached load's stages as `was`, or to re-derive the shares from
  `unitsLeft`. Not in this task.
- "Calculating trips here..." (the focus pool's solve of the zone in view, 40-60 s here) has a bar at 0 and no hover.
- The first 90 s of a load's solve have no unit time (set_network 24 s, then a blind unit): the old model stands in.
  Priors exist after the first load, so a second load in the same page session has them from the start.
