# T-133 — map picking follows drawing order

Implemented 2026-10-10. Demand bubbles draw on a canvas above the rail network and now receive
click priority there. Ordinary select clicks choose a city commuter bubble before a train,
station or track underneath. Selection near/far bubbles, station catchments and longer-trip
discs retain their current parent selection when clicked; invisible whole-city bubbles cannot
be selected through a selection's replacement discs.

Within a disc set, `discAt` already walks back to front, matching the renderer's instance order.
Near discs beat far discs, and commuter/catchment discs beat longer-trip discs. Bubble box
selection starts only on a visible city or selected-near bubble. Construction tools retain
their deliberate station/track snapping and selection editing handles retain their priority.

Network picking now walks trips, stations, line strokes, junctions and track strokes back to
front within their layer, retaining the existing 7 px hit tolerance. A selected line's final
highlight pass takes priority over ordinary line strokes and repeat clicks still select its
track. Height/speed display modes do not pick the line strokes they hide.

Capacity delay tags are DOM targets underneath the demand canvas. Their click handler now asks
the shared demand picker at the actual pointer first, and covered tags suppress their own
tooltip. Demand hover also listens on these DOM targets so the visually higher bubble supplies
the hover text. Exposed junction tags retain their link; construction tools keep their map
interaction instead of being overridden by a tag's junction selection.
