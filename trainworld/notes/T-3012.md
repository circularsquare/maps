# T-3012 Mansoura's zone is in pieces (stage 1: diagnosis and the check)

Anita, 2026-11-10: "mansoura is disconnected..? which is not that urgent of an issue in itself but i thought we prevented
this, we should try to prevent it in general". Agent splitzone. Read-only on game data; scripts in the agent's scratchpad.

## Cause, shown on Mansoura (zone `elm`, "El Mansura", 23 units, 8.7M people, 3,266 km2)

`elm` is a **metro row**, not a countryside zone, so the `contiguous` rule never saw it. Its units come from the metros stage,
`world_plan.assign`: every free unit with at least half its people (`UNIT_SHARE` 0.5) inside the metro's area joins, and the
area is GHSL's urban centre 5167 plus FUA 4710 (one 2,035 km2 polygon of 6.1M people). Nothing in `assign` asks whether the
units touch.

The units' shares in that area: members at 0.51 to 1.00 on both sides of the Delta; the two units standing between the halves
fall just under: Aga 0.36, Sinbillawin 0.46 (Tamy Al-Amdid 0.28, Minya Al-Nasr 0.26). Those, out of the metro, were zoned on
their own by the countryside step (`aad` Aga, `slw` Sinbillawin) and now sit in the gap. Result: two pieces, 11.9 km apart
(1,535 km2 with Mit Ghamr, Zifta, Santa, Quwisna, Shibin al-Kum, Dyarb Nigm; 1,731 km2 with the Mansoura units, Talkha, Dikirnis,
Kafr Sad, Shirbin, and Dumyat Al-Gadida). The boundary file has 4 polygons (two touching pairs).

What did not do it:
- `contiguous` (zone_groups) applies to countryside groups only; metro rows are outside it. The only check on a finished
  zone is a log line in `plan_zones` (about line 2832, "parts over 10 km from its main part") that does not stop anything and
  is not written for metro rows.
- ABSORB (T-2965) did not touch `elm`'s units (checked: none of its 23 units has a new part). It does make far pieces elsewhere:
  `absorb` keeps each Zemam-Out piece within 40 km of a unit but the piece need not touch the unit, so 6 units grew new parts
  (Abu Simbel 3 to 7 parts, 63 km out; Aswan, Tushaka, Paris Paris, Abshaway). Upper Egypt, unit level; zones there are probably fine
  or show in the check.
- Metro growth: `Dumyat Al-Gadida` (a T-1191 remnant, 44,000 people) was added after and touches Kafr Sad, so it is joined.
  The belt step (`metro_belts`) did not run (no commute table).
- Outline simplification is not involved: the pack's own `elm.boundary.geojson` already has the two pieces (4 polygons, 11.9 km apart).

The same `assign` rule applies to every metro in every country, so the cousins of Mansoura are other FUA-shaped metros whose
area has a notch (see the check's results).

## The check

`checks/split_zones.py`, output `../artifacts/trainworld/split-zones.json`. Reads each registered zone's
`data/packs/<id>.boundary.geojson` (not outlines.json: its rings include the sea off the coast and join across water).
See the file's docstring for the four kinds (tiny, seam, water, foreign) it ignores and `land`, which it reports.

- 895 zones have more than one polygon part cluster; most are islands (2,018 water-separated pieces), seams under 500 m
  (1,580) and slivers under 1 km2 (3,948), all ignored; 10 pieces are foreign-country exclaves, ignored.
- **254 zones have a land piece** (353 pieces): India 63, China 33, Indonesia 25, Russia 20, Canada 14, Philippines 10, Brazil 7,
  Mexico 7, Spain 6, Malaysia 6, Australia 6, Germany 5, Ukraine 4, Egypt 3 and 25 other countries with 3 or fewer.
- Most are small: biggest piece 1-5 km2 in 79 zones, 5-20 in 56, 20-100 in 62, 100-1,000 in 40, over 1,000 in 17.
- **65 are material** (a piece of 20 km2 or more, 2 km or more from the rest): IN 12, RU 9, CN 8, CA 7, ID 3, UA 3, SA 2, ES 2, MY 2,
  AU 2, and 1 each in BR BY CO KW EG VN OM CL MX AE PH DE US IS PL. 54 are countryside zones (2,169 units), 11 metro rows
  (341 units: Mansoura, Hanoi, Makhachkala, Valencia, Dubai and others). Egypt's only one is Mansoura; Cairo has three
  Nile slivers 0.26 km off its main piece (seams), which is not T-3011 (the Cairo border east of the Nile is where the
  kism polygons run, not a split).
- Worst: Golmud (China, 38,538 km2 piece, 139 km off; probably Tanggula, a real exclave of the Golmud unit, which the check
  cannot tell from a split), Khabarovsk (24,242 km2, 29 km, Komsomolsk and Birobidzhan between), Al Taif (13,701 km2),
  Santarem (12,019), Barysaw (10,924 km2, Minsk's zone cuts it), Uzhursky (9,261), Popayan (5,134), Winnipeg (5,128),
  Bar_Elahmadi (4,159, Kuwait City between), Mansoura (1,480 km2, 12 km, Aga and Sinbillawin between).
- Cause by comparing each row's units before the pack cut: of the 65, 54 have units that are already apart (zone_groups
  joined them across water or by a far-reaching rule, or a metro's `assign` picked them: Mansoura), 8 have connected units cut
  apart by a hand-built pack or neighbouring metro (Bar_Elahmadi by Kuwait City, Kashgar's Peyziwat, Thoothukkudi), and 3 have
  units missing from the state (Oman, US Tennessee, Iceland: not classified). The first figure includes a unit's own exclave
  (Golmud's), which the check lists as a split.
- 48 of the 65 have another zone standing in the gap, 17 have only water or dead space.

## Fix proposed (not applied)

1. **Stop it in the planner** (no rebuild; a few hours of code, in world_plan.py once the join is done):
   - `assign` (metro rows): after the units are picked, find the pieces (units touching within 500 m, or across at most
     `WATER_GAP_KM`). Bridge: free units touching two pieces with a share of at least 0.25 join the metro, the biggest share first
     (Mansoura: Sinbillawin 0.46 alone bridges it, 0.65M more people, 9.4M in all). No bridge: the piece without the metro's
     densest point goes back to the free units for the countryside step.
   - `plan_zones`, where the log line "parts over 10 km from its main part" is now (line 2832): the same test on every zone row's
     units; a stray piece's units go to the neighbour row sharing the longest border (kept within `cap`), else form a zone of their own
     when they hold the floor, else dead space; a unit with several parts of its own is left alone (a real exclave).
   - Pack cuts (`g.difference(cut)`): a zone a hand-built pack cuts in two gets the same mend.
   - `checks/split_zones.py` runs after every plan and after the join; exit 1 means look.
2. **Mend the 65 material rows now**: edit only those rows' unit lists (`metro_edits`/`splice`, not `zones --replan`, which
   regroups whole countries: India's 12 rows would put hundreds of rows in play), then `world_run.py run --rows` on them and
   the rows that receive units (about 1 receiving row each), so about 65-130 rows.

### Rebuild cost (from the plan's recorded step times)
Zone rows: median 78 s, mean 161 s (the 54 affected: 145 min in all). Metro rows: mean 304 s (the 11 affected: 56 min). So about
3.4 core-hours for the 65 rows, 5-7 with their receiving neighbours, plus gates (about 1-1.5 cores per region's chunk) and one
join per affected country (25 countries; the join takes all its cores). At 4 cores about 2-3 hours, and the join cannot overlap
another join or any pack write: after the Israel and Palestine join ends. Countries: IN 12, RU 9, CN 8, CA 7, ID 3, UA 3, SA 2,
ES 2, MY 2, AU 2, 15 others one each. Big metro rows (Hanoi, Valencia, Dubai) are the dearest; the 189 zones with only small
pieces (under 20 km2 or under 2 km off) stay as they are.

Not done: the same-country exclave rule in the check (needs unit data: a piece that is one unit's own part), so Golmud and a few
like it will keep showing until the check reads the plan's units.

## Stage 2 (2026-11-10, code only, world_plan.py; no live plan, pack or terrain written)

over other units' or packs' land; a unit's own exclaves stay with it), `bridge_metro` called from `assign(..., bridge=True)`, and
