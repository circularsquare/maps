## What changed

- `demandClient.ts` `firm` / `restarts` / `wholeRuns`: once an edit's whole solve has started (`whole` sent, or no
  worker has a local stage), a newer network that differs from its own (`waiting.svc !== cur.svc`: an edit, never a
  zone opened or re-levelled, which re-dispatch the same service) is dispatched at once while
  - time since the solve was sent < `RESTART_SHARE` (0.4) of (that + `unitsLeft`, the hover's time left), and
  - time since `restartFrom` (the first of the edits solved one after another without a final) < that same total.
  With no time left known it holds as before. `?perfOff=restart` restores the T-1734 hold.
- The worker already stops between population passes when a newer network comes (`pending` -> `stale`), as for
  barrier solves and the old `?perfOff=firm` path: no worker is killed, memory and warm caches stay.
- `dispatch` carries `restartFrom` across restarts, marks the cut solve (`cutAfter`, ms it ran, in `twDemand.log`)
  and bumps `solveRestarted` (`game/demand.ts`); `StatusTag.tsx` then starts the bar's wait and the hover's
  count-down from 0.
- Docs: demand-runtime.md "An edit's solve runs to its end", ui-panels.md (the hover's time left). DECISIONS
  2026-11-21. Check: `checks/t3925-restart-solve.mjs` (about 3.5 minutes, Node, one worker).
