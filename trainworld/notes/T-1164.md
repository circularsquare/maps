# T-1164: walking (and driving) across water

## For Anita

- **Now.** A walk treats water as a wall, except where OpenStreetMap has a bridge a person may walk
  on (footways, paths, streets; not motorways, and trunk roads only with `foot=yes`); a tunnel never
  counts. Drives follow OSM's roads, motorway bridges and tunnels included, so a car crosses wherever
  any road does, often the long way round: a drive is cut off for 0.01% of nearby zone pairs even in
  Hong Kong and Venice, a walk for 20% and 14%.
- **Proposed change.** Let a walk cross water at a cost: each metre over water counts as 3 metres of
  walking (as if crossing at 2 km/h; it stands for the bus through the tunnel, the bridge just out of
  reach, the ferry, the bike). The same for the walk to a station and for walking the whole way.
  Drives stay as they are: they already use the real crossings, and a crossing where no road goes
  would put cars on open water (car ferries are T-280, parked).
- **What it does.** Small. Walking the whole way (no rail) rises by 0.3 to 1.8 points: Hong Kong's
  everyday walking 68.4% to 70.2%, Venice 38.1% to 39.2%, Stockholm 38.1% to 38.6%, New York 37.1% to
  37.6%, London and Seoul a few tenths. On New York's real network rail trips fall 0.17% (PATH 1.5%,
  since some of its short trips now walk). Solve time is unchanged.
- **Examples** (walking metres): Central to Tsim Sha Tsui across the harbour, 1.3 km straight: cut
  off now, 2.9 km. Fondamenta Nove to Murano, 1.9 km: cut off, 3.0 km. Greenpoint to East 23rd St,
  1.6 km: cut off, 3.1 km. Long Island City to East 34th St, 1.2 km: 4.4 km round by the Queensboro
  Bridge now, 2.8 km. Hoboken to 14th St across the Hudson, 1.7 km: cut off, 4.0 km, so the Hudson
  stays out of a station's reach (3 km), as T-275 wanted. Wide water stays shut; rivers and canals
  under about half a kilometre open.
- **Rebuild.** The walk to a station is searched in the game, so it changes with the code at once.
  The walk tables for walking the whole way are baked into each pack and need `routes.py --stage
  walk` rerun: 5 to 60 seconds a pack, about 3 to 6 hours of one core for all 1,749 packs (a data
  batch). A pack not yet rerun keeps its old whole-way walks, which is harmless.
- **Your call.** The cost per metre of water: 2, 3 or 5. I'd pick 3. At 2 the Hudson opens for
  walking (Hoboken to 14th St 3.0 km) and PATH loses 4%; at 5 most crossings over 500 m stay shut.

## How walks are blocked now

- The barrier raster (`walk_row`, `walk_x`, 50 m pixels, `pipeline/routes.py`): water mask pixels,
  less OSM ways a person may walk on that cross as bridges. Tunnels are not bridges, so a
  cross-harbour tunnel or a motorway-only crossing (Essingeleden, the Western Harbour Crossing) is
  water to a walker.
- The walk to a station (`routes::WalkGrid::field`, in the game): a search over the raster within
  3 km of each station, eight neighbours, never onto a barrier or across its corner; a cell it does
  not reach has no access. Shared by the commute and everyday populations, the access subzones and
  the trip explorer; `app/src/game/walks.ts` copies it for the map's walking minutes.
- Walking the whole way (`walk_zone`, `walk_part`, baked by `pack_walk` through `routes.py --stage
  walk`): each 2 km zone's cells split by bank into up to 4 parts, and the walk factor between parts
  of zones within 2 squares from the same search; a pair cut off takes the byte cap (53 times the
  straight line), so nobody walks it.
- Transfers between stations stay straight x 1.3, unaffected.

## The experiment

A copy of the sim (scratch) with one change in `WalkGrid::field`: a step onto water, or a diagonal
past a water corner, costs K/10 of a land step instead of being refused (env `WALK_WATER_K`; 0 is
today's rule), and the search's bucket ring grows from 256 to 2048 so the larger steps fit. Pack
copies took walk tables recomputed by the copy's `pack_walk`; `calib` gave the no-rail walking
shares (`--info-only`) and New York's weekday on its real network. K0 recomputed rather than the
shipped tables is the baseline: the shipped ones differ from a fresh run by 0.1 to 0.7 points of
cut-off pairs (packs whose weights changed after their walk stage).

## Building it

- `sim/src/demand/routes.rs` `WalkGrid::field`: the water step at `WATER_COST` (3) times a land
  step, the bucket ring above the largest step (512 for 3x); `app/src/game/walks.ts` the same (the two change together).
  `walk_tables` needs nothing more; its bank split finds fewer parts.
- Rerun `routes.py --stage walk` over every pack (the data agent's batch; the pack format is
  unchanged, only the numbers in `walk_zone` and `walk_part`).
- Docs: demand-mode.md "Routed walking and driving" and "Walking to a station", demand-plain.md's
  walking lines, demand-worldwide.md's routed walks entry.
- No change to drives.
