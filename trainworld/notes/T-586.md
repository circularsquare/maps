# T-586 (with T-201, T-628): the bubble hover and the trip explorer agree

## What was wrong

The far-end bubble's hover sums the selection's trips to that bubble under the purpose filter and
the day shown (the selection's far end, `flows`, summed over the bubble's cells), while the
explorer showed one commute between two cells in one period (the clock's). Anita's New York case:
Union Sq's hover mostly walking, the explorer 61% train in the evening peak.

## What the explorer does now

- **Headline from the view** (`tripMix` in game/trip.ts): opened from a far-end bubble, the
  selection's far end (`flows(end, selection cells)`, the same answer the far bubbles are drawn
  from) summed over every cell of the clicked bubble, for the purpose filter (all purposes: the
  whole far end; else `byPurpose`), on the day shown. So the bar, the rows and the count are the
  hover's sums. From the trip tool: the start's cell as homes, the end's cell as the far end.
  When the view counts none between them, the headline is the representative trip's chances.
- **Representative trip**: the purpose with most of the mix (all purposes) or the filter's;
  commutes through `DemandApi.trip`, everyday purposes through the new `EverydayApi.trip`
  (T-201), school and university by the kind with more trips between the two areas (HEAD slot
  34). Period: the busiest of that purpose (`busiestPeriod`, from the selection's timing; the
  period shares are the purpose's own, the same for every pair) until a period row is clicked.
- **Walk row** (T-628): shown when walking the whole way takes under 30 min (HEAD slot 33, the
  cells' straight line x walk detour / walk speed x the water factor).
- T-558's walkers rail takes: the headline is the views' own numbers, so they count the same by
  construction. The everyday representative's walk is the cells' no-rail share x (1 - rail), the
  views' rule; the commute's is the zone pair's no-rail walk x (1 - rail), as `mode_choice`.
