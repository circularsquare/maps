# T-3883: long high-speed ramps still run at 160 (agent rampbug, 2026-11-20)

## Cause
Not T-3027's ramp rule: every ramp in the cases tried stored 400 m and its own limit was 320. The 160 came from
**phantom curves at the ramps' ends**. A bridge (and a ramp over track, both `bridge::build`) adds a vertex at
each end of each ramp, on the straight, with no radius, quantised to the millimetre grid. On a route that is not
exactly axis-aligned the rounding leaves its legs meeting at about 1e-6 to 1e-4 rad, so the fit gives it the
automatic radius (1,796 m = 160 km/h) on an arc a few millimetres long. Then:
- trains: `profile::widen` spreads every limit over a train's length, so each ramp end held trains at 160 for
  about 200 m (four times per crossing: ramp up's two ends, ramp down's two). A 400 m ramp is mostly inside
  those windows, so trains ran it at about 160.
- Speed map mode: on built track with a running line it draws the fastest train's speed (T-1271), so 160
  there; the Track inspector's Slowest (`edges_info` vmin) said 160 for the whole piece.
- The drawing preview and the ghost's colours sample midpoints and never land on a millimetre arc, so they
  said 320. That is why the label rose with ramp length while the built track did not.
Over land with clicked level changes no vertex is added (the ramp spreads inside the leg), so the T-3027
checker's 319 on land was right. This is the same bug as T-3421 (player: "high speed autoramps over water are
lowkey 160km/h"); its tests used axis-aligned routes or asserted on preview speeds, so it never reproduced.
Second candidate checked and ruled out for these cases: `route_op`'s fallback to 200 m ramps (a real 160 that
T-3027 decided) did not trigger for one river or two bands 300 or 600 m apart (`route_ramp_m` stayed 400).

## Fix
`Piece::v_curve` (geom.rs): an arc turning less than `MIN_TURN` = 3e-4 rad (0.017 degrees, params.rs) has no
speed limit. `geom::limits` and `run_time` read it, so trains, vmin, the speed map, line stretch speeds and
ghost all agree. The curve labels (`vertex_rows` for previews, `edge_pis` for the Track inspector's corner
list) share `vertex_kmh`, which gives such a vertex the cap. The fit is untouched (shapes, lengths, costs).
Docs: track-and-service.md, curve speed.

Threshold from three real saves (turn of every arc, by decade, count):
| save | 1e-9..1e-5 | 1e-5..1e-4 | 1e-4..1e-3 | 1e-3.. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| north-america-day-491 | 2,296 | 94 | 15 | 6,934 |
| asianita-day-557 | 2,176 | 122 | 6 | 8,925 |
| bangkok-tokyo-nyc-els-459 | 200 | 37 | 4 | 4,796 |
On high-speed track the automatic-radius (1,796 m) arcs go up to 1.03e-4 (0.18 m long); above that are set
radii (7,183 m, 320 anyway). Ordinary and tram track have automatic arcs from 1.75e-4 to 5.6e-4, which change
nothing (their cap is 160 or less). Edges whose slowest curve rises: NA 342 at 1e-4, 344 at 3e-4 and at 1e-3
(320 high-speed); asianita 387, 388, 388 (369); bangkok 7. So 3e-4 and 1e-3 free the same pieces. The lie: a polyline of vertices under 2 m apart each turning under 3e-4 would no longer limit; no
tool draws that (traces step 25 m).

## Before / after
- Sim, high-speed at 320, 8 km on a slant across 200 m of water: piece slowest 160 -> 320; a line over it
  (stations 6 km apart): run limits had four 200 m windows at 160 -> none under 320; top speed reached 205 -> 219.
- Game on :6188 (sandbox nyc, scratch `probe.js`, `probe2.js`), high-speed at 320 drawn by pointer across the
  Hudson on a slant (9.5 km, a bridge, stations at the ends, 6/4/2 high-speed trains): ghost slowest 320 both
  times; piece Slowest 160 -> 320; line top speed 219 -> 259 km/h, average 140 -> 150, round trip 14m12s ->
  13m39s. Speed map mode at Weehawken: `artifacts/trainworld/t3883-before.png` (the stretch around the ramp
  on the NJ shore in the slower colour) and `t3883-after.png` (one colour throughout).
- Existing saves (edit_bench TW_LINES, round trip at the high level): north-america-day-491 29 of 175 lines
  changed, 22 faster by 32,167 s in all (Floride 38,711 -> 32,370 s, Crescent 45,048 -> 40,434, Pacific
  43,696 -> 39,980), 7 slower by 0.2 to 74 s, all of it delay (faster trains on shared track); asianita-day-557
  25 of 221 changed, Shanghai Guangzhou 60,614 -> 52,949 s, 1 slower by 0.9 s (delay).

## Still 160 by rule (T-3027, not this bug)
- A flyover's crossing leg keeps 200 m ramps, so 160 on high-speed track.
- A route whose 400 m ramps are refused (a platform or crossing on the ramp, no room for a bridge's ramps)
  falls back to 200 m ramps, 160, stored with the piece.
