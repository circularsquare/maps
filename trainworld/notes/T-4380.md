# T-4380 and T-4382: a bus line's first stop, and BRT stations side by side

Both are in the stop plan (`sim/src/track/bus.rs`, `plan_by`), so each is one rule, not a patch per screen.

## T-4380 first stop removable
- Why it was fixed: `bus_stop_edit` refused the first and last stop, and `hand_stops` always put `first` in. Archive T-2937 only
  made a line's ends drawn as end stops; rail's ends rule lives in `check_route`, which a bus line never reaches (`check_line`
  returns early for bus), so nothing for rail changes and validate.rs is untouched.
- New state `BusRoute::first_off` (by-hand lines only, never a loop). `StopRule::Hand` carries it. Taking out the start sets it
  and drops the hand points that made that stop; a click on the route within 50 m of where the start's stop was (its station's
  place if it was one) clears it. Refused with two stops left, so a line always has two. A redraw keeps it while the route starts
  at the same point; Auto or a spacing makes it inert (the by-hand stops come back with it, as `hand_kept` does).
- Save: new trailing block `LAF1` after `LAP1`, save level 41 -> 42 (`SAVE_LEVEL` in saveFile.ts too), BLOCKS 45. Another tree adding a
  block will clash on that number.
- The bus runs the whole route from its first point (Anita 11-27): `build_bus` (service.rs) works the trip out with the route's end as a
  point that is no stop (`lead`), keeps the stops' own times, so the first stop is reached after the lead-in and the way back goes on to
  the start. Round trip time is unchanged (a by-hand hop's stand is inside its time). Under Manual a stop still serves 400 m each way, so
  the stretch before the first stop is driven but no stop serves it (as any gap wider than 800 m).
- Review fixes: `hand_stops` puts the start back when no stop is placed (the two-stop minimum on every path: redraw, Auto and back,
  out-and-back removal), a removal that would leave no placed stop is refused, a click on another station's place near the removed
  start adds that station (not the start), and no gap check against a removed start (Ctrl+click works there).
- App: `busStops.ts` makes the first marker a removable stop (not for loops, not at two stops); `ClockBus.firstOff` (protocol.ts,
  clockDemand.ts, wasm `bus_first_off`). No new strings: the hover tag reuses `stopMarkers.remove` and `add`.

## T-4382 BRT stations side by side
- Cause: `reach()` kept only the nearer of two stations within `STATION_GAP_M` (100 m) along the road, whatever kind; the Manual
  tool (`hand_stops`, `HAND_GAP_M` 50 m, and `pick()` refusing a click within 200 m of any station stop) had the same gate.
- Now two stations merge only when neither is a BRT station (or it is one station's own two passes). `hand_stops` lets two
  different stations be stops however close; `pick()` blocks a click only for the station it would be the stop of.
- Demand does not merge two consecutive stops of one line (`merge_stations` skips adjacent pairs), so riders board at each of two close
  stations the line stops at.
- Existing BRT lines on Auto gain stops where two BRT stations or a BRT and train station were within 100 m, on load.

## Checks
- `agent_cargo.py test`: all pass (new: `a_by_hand_lines_first_stop_comes_out_and_goes_back`, `a_brt_line_stops_at_each_of_two_close_brt_stations`;
  one old assertion changed: a click on the first end no longer does nothing). Typecheck passes.
- Game run (new NYC game, sandbox, ops through `tw.client.edit`): a constructed BRT line with BRT stations 40 m and 150 m apart stops
  at all three on Auto and by hand; first stop taken out -> 5 stops from 6 with firstOff, put back with a click at the start,
  taken out again.
