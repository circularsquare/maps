# T-513: "other" for homes without a car

2026-10-16. Rule: docs/demand.md 4.3 "Homes without a car"; caveats: docs/demand-worldwide.md
"Driving alternative and homes without a car"; DECISIONS 2026-10-16 (T-513).

## What was built

- **Choice** (`Params::nocar_choice`, kernel lanes in `mode_choice`, `trip::pair_choice`, everyday
  `pass`): the no-car share of a home's trips picks among rail, walking the whole way (15 perceived
  min a road km) and other (road km x 5 + 8 x 1.37 + `other_asc`), one logit, scale damped on
  other's minutes. Fixed: `other_kmh` 12, `other_wait_min` 8. Fitted: `other_asc`, default
  `kernel::OTHER_ASC` = 10; `set_extra("other_asc", x)`; `calib.py run --set other_asc=..` passes
  it through (calib now calls `refresh_walk` whenever `--set` is given, so other's no-rail total
  follows the parameters). Car owners unchanged.
- **Test**: `pair_choice_reproduces_mode_choice` now also compares, per origin, what rail takes
  from other (`ModeOut::other_lost_by_sub` against `PairChoice::other_lost`) and asserts other is
  in play. Dropping other's term from the lanes fails it (worst origin off by 0.65); restored:
  worst 1.5e-6.
- **The figure apart**: trips by other per period = trips by other with no rail (commutes:
  `Gravity::other_trips_by_zone` over zone pairs at pack open; everyday: per destination sample
  in `add_totals`) less no-car rail trips x other's share of their non-rail choice (a logit's,
  the same with or without rail). `summary(q)[10]` on both layers; the worker sums index 10 with
  the others; nothing displays it, so every bar, split and hover counts other as driving (drive
  is trips less rail and walk everywhere). Per cell it is not kept: showing other apart in
  bubbles needs the per-cell version (T-514).
- **Cost**: New York's pack opens 0.2-0.3 s slower natively (6,407 zones: an all-pairs pass
  without logits, plus logits within about 25 km, read from a 50 m table, `OtherShare`), and
  the everyday layer about 0.2 s. Solve time unchanged.
