# T-4546: retime's room and floor for moved trains (stage 4 of 18, T-4511)

Agent shift4, 2026-11-28, tree shift4. Sim only (`track/timing.rs`); nothing a player sees, no save change, no strings, no changelog item.
A game with no moves runs as before (`train_lost` returns at once for a line with none).

## What is built
- **`train_lost`** (per run, per slot, aligned with `day_deps`): the seconds of a train's stand at the far end that moves take: its own
  move later, plus the move earlier of the departure its train takes next (the other run's departure nearest where the train is ready
  with no move, `run.prof` duration + turnaround + its share of the spare). Cached per run in `Built::lost`.
- **`lead_in` and `following`**: each train's room is the line's room minus its lost stand (a train's own loss, and the trains clustered
  with it, are cut to what is left). The lost stand (in steps) is a fourth part of `cluster_follow`'s key, and a variant is built with
  the slack its key leaves; `cluster_follow`'s `ok` makes a merge of unlike lost stands the last resort. With no moves the fourth part is
  0 everywhere: same keys, same clusters.
- **`floor`**: after the run-level check, a per-train one for a line with moves: a train on its own variant whose stand
  (close stand + spare share - its variant's extra duration - lost) is under `TURN_MIN_S` - 1 loses its pattern entry and runs the slot's
  timetable (variant 0); the rest of the run keeps its variants (renumbered by the next clustering, same timetables). This covers
  `passing_waits` as well, which has no room of its own.

## Test
`a_local_moved_later_keeps_its_runs_other_timetables` (timing/tests.rs), the 180 km line with passing tracks, locals/expresses at 30/10,
20/10 and 30/30 min: the local on its own timetable that can move furthest later moves to its range end (182 s at 30/10); every other
departure keeps the same timetable (compared by phases, not variant number) and the moved one makes its next departure. Control with the
per-train floor off: the moved local ends 424 s after its next departure.

## Lies and limits
- **A move is of one day's departure until T-4547** (`at` is game time): `retime` times the reference day (day 10) only, so a move on any
  other day changes nothing in the variants yet. The test moves a train of day 10. Stage 5's key (time of day plus `lead`) lands every
  day on it.
- Moves of a train **earlier** are counted for the train before it (its stand shrinks): paired by nearest unmoved departure, not by
  `hand_over`'s own matching; a rough pairing where the line's two directions drift.
- A move **later** of the next departure and a move **earlier** of the train's own give room back; not counted (conservative).
- A moved train dropped to the slot's timetable loses its lead-in / passing waits (its own stand is all it has); the range of stage 3 is
  worked out on variant 0 anyway.
- The range (`move_range`) is still variant 0's: it does not read this stage's per-train result, so on a line with variants the range end
  can be a hair past what the train's own timetable would give; the train then runs the slot's.
