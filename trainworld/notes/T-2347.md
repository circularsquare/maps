## What the flag does

Behind `?capInside=1` (perfFlags.ts `capInside`, off in play): in the Traffic view every line stroke with a red mark
(track at capacity, `a_full.x < 0`, or a crowded stretch, `a_full.x > 0`, at its T-2236 strength) gets a red stripe
down the middle of its own colour, 60% of the colour's width, mixed 60% toward the red (`INSIDE_SHARE`,
`INSIDE_MIX` in map/rendererShaders.ts). The stripe is drawn by the line's own colour stroke, so it has that line's
draw order: no line's mark over a neighbour's band. Passes 1-3 (the red band outside the casing, its rim, and
T-2184's red edges over neighbouring bands) are skipped, and the line is not darkened (`FULL_DARKEN`).
`?capInside=2` is the same stripe in the old dashes (16 px period, 60% drawn), in case "continuous" meant one
unbroken dashed line rather than solid.

Read of "continuous": solid, no dashes. The second flag is there so she can compare.

Known look: on a red or orange line the stripe barely shows (the outside band had a casing rim for that, T-1284).
