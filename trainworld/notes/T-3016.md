## Why the start-tree idea buys little

`turnPath` is already A* over arc states with a straight-line bound, so it expands only 24 to 2047 states (0.2-2.4 ms
here; 4 ms p90 at Lisbon 2 km). A start tree (Dijkstra without the bound, kept between moves) would expand more
states for the first end, then answer later ends by lookup. It would work: the costs do not depend on the end except
through the end's own turn onto its segment, the end snap alternatives (`ends` list), the end penalty `pen` and the
cap `capM`, which are all added when an arc entering the end's nodes is popped, and `doubles()` reads the tree's own
parent chain. The end would be answered by scanning the settled arcs into its two nodes and growing the tree while the
frontier is under the best total. But the most it can save is the 0.2-4 ms search, about 3% of the update. Not built.
Same for A* tweaks, moving the search to a worker (adds a round trip about as long as the search) and caching the sim
fit for unchanged prefixes (fit is 0.1-2 ms).

## Ranked proposal

| # | Change | Effort | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Search on the next tick after each move instead of after a 60 ms rest (`REST_MS` 60 to 0). Workers already drop stale asks (`latest` seq in the router, `previewStale` in the clock worker) | one constant | 75-82 | 17-32 (done) |
| 2 | Skip the sim price until the pointer rests: not worth it, the price is 1-4 ms | - | - | - |
| 3 | Draw the street line at once, refine after: already done while no ghost is up; with #1 the fitted ghost lands in 3-10 ms anyway | - | - | - |
| 4 | Start tree / A* / worker for the search | medium | 0.2-4 | saves under 3 ms |

What is left after #1 is the frame wait (6-12 ms, one vsync) and about 10 ms of search, fit and price.

## T-3035: the sharp turn instead of a long way round

Anita (Colerain St and Nobles Lane, Pittsburgh): the snap went round a ~600 m block loop rather than take a slow
sharp turn. Cause: `turnPath` refused any turn over `BACK_DEG` (135 degrees) outright. Now a turn between 135 and
165 degrees (`HARD_DEG`, `streetSnap.ts`) costs `TURN_M` (200 m) rising to twice that at 165, and over 165 is
refused. Kept: no turn back at a chained start (that check stays at 135), no U-turn within `BACK_M` of street
(`doubles` now refuses a reversal over 165 rather than 135, so a 150 degree turn can be followed by the street it
leads onto, a median crossing is still near 180). Code: `RoadGraph.turnPath` (`hardTurn`), `BusRoads.tramLeg`,
`streetSnap.ts`.

Test near her spot: 300 random street pairs (150-900 m apart) within 500 m of Colerain St, the old rule against the
new: 62 differ; typically the old route is a loop (833 m to 416 m, 746 to 354, 2393 to 1912, 1993 to 965) and three
pairs the old rule refused with "No road path found" now route. The ghost fitted and priced (tag, no "streets tight")
for the pairs shot; the full result is in the report. Before and after shots: `artifacts/trainworld/t3035-before-*.png`
and `t3035-after-*.png`.
