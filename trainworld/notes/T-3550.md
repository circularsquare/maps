## What changed (`sim/src/track/profile.rs`)

- `crawl_speed`: a hold's crawl speed by a secant in 1/v instead of bisection in v. The bracket is exact: at
  the train's top speed over the zone (from the profile before it) nothing changes, and at
  `len / (time over the zone + delay)` the zone alone adds the delay. The first try is that second bound; the
  second solves a braking-penalty model (`len / v - t_in + c (v_in - v)^2`, c fitted from the first try); then
  secant, kept in the bracket, halving every third try if the bracket has not halved. 3-4 tries a hold, to within
  `CRAWL_TOL_S` = 0.01 s of the delay (bisection stopped at 0.1 s for point holds, 0.05 s for spread ones).
- The 0.5 m/s test (does the crawl take the delay at all, else a stand) is only run when the same bound says
  it might fail (`len / 0.5 - t_in < delay + 1`); otherwise its answer is known. The tests that choose a stand
  over a crawl are otherwise as before.
- `Stretch`: the last stretch of a hop (last standstill to the hop's end) kept with each interval's entering
  and leaving speed² and time. A try with a zone or a stand works out only the pieces it changes and the
  intervals whose envelope they move (forward until it meets the old one, braking likewise), so a try costs a
  few intervals instead of the whole hop (about 37 intervals). A test checks these against whole hops to 1e-9.
- `intervals` takes the hop's breakpoints by binary search instead of scanning the line's ~160 (exact; alone
  it saved little).
- `segment`'s per-interval body moved to `cross` (same arithmetic, bit-identical).

Advisor consulted twice: first on the cost split (it pointed at the point-hold loop and asked for exact savings
first and bit-identical stand tests); the stretch retiming came after the secant alone gave only 2.2x.
