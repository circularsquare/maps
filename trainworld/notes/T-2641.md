# T-2641: the regional market's coarser far ends (built and measured, 2026-11-06)

Agent farends, tree `.tw-trees/farends`. Stage 1 of T-2621 (notes/T-2621.md section 4, option c2), built into the crate as
prototyped there: `solved_pairs` (sim/src/demand/intercity/api.rs) deals a zone's trips with a far place in a chunk of
`MARKET_CHUNK x clamp(d / 50 km, 1, 16)`, d the distance to the place's first zone (`FAR_CHUNK_KM`, `FAR_CHUNK_MAX`), and
place pairs at least 250 km apart (`FAR_DEAL_KM`) are dealt once over both places' zones (the served origin zones' runs
follow each other, the start fixed by the two places' ids). `IntercityApi::set_far_chunks` (wasm), job flag
`nearChunks`, `?perfOff=farChunks` for the old dealing. Docs: regional.md 5.1, demand-worldwide.md. The only change to
the WASM interface is the new method `set_far_chunks(on)`.

## Sampled-pair tests

`sampled_pairs_keep_each_zones_trips_with_each_place` is kept as it was, for the chunk at every distance (`far` off).
`far_chunk_pairs_keep_each_place_pairs_trips` (tyo, gnm, osa): fewer pairs than the chunk at every distance, none
that is not a pair, the same every time, a far pair carries whole chunks, a zone's trips with a place under the
distance exact to within a chunk, and each far place pair's trips within 5% (plus two largest chunks) of the exact
ones: an origin zone's run is off by up to a chunk, one way or the other, and these add as independent errors, so a
small place pair is off by more than a big one.

## Known edge

A far place pair's run depends on the served origin zones before it (an unserved origin zone's trips are not dealt, to
avoid pricing every zone pair of far places), so a new station that serves a new origin zone changes the far pairs
dealt to later origin zones of the same place pair: unrelated far lines can move by sampling noise (tens of riders) on
an edit. Not measured on an edit; the totals hold. If it shows, deal over every origin zone of the place (costs the
trips of every zone pair of the far places once per network).
