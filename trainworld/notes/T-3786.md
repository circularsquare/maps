## Causes tested

- **4 MB cap**: no. 454 stations at day 199 is 61 KB; a 500-day game would be well under 1 MB.
- **Refused upload (level or block)**: no. `pck35q3ttygg` r33 was written by v0.4.2 at 16:27 today and checked
  intact; the account has no quarantine.
- **Safari clearing local storage alone**: does not explain it. Eviction writes no tombstone, and the account copy
  would still be listed.
- **A delete**: yes. Every client DELETE starts at a delete confirm (`deleteVersions` from the start menu or saves
  panel, or deleting the game being played); owed deletes can be sent later, so a tombstone's time is at or after the
  click. The tombstone then deletes unedited copies in the player's other browsers on their next settle
  (`settleSlot`).
- **A gap worth a look, not yet tied to this case**: a card's delete removes every version of the card
  (`groupHeads`), but the "type the station count" gate (100 stations and up, `DeleteAsk`) counts only the top
  version's stations, and "Delete this version" has a plain confirm. A big game sitting under a card whose top is a
  small version can go with one click. File as T-3787 only if the backup index shows the lost game was in such a card.
