# T-2163, T-2235: the network diagram's clicks, camera, picture and loading bar

Rules in docs/ui-diagram.md ("The button and key M"); figures and calls here. Agent t2163, 2026-11-03.

## As built

- **Picking** (`draw.ts` `pickAt`, `names.ts` `nameAt`): pills first (within their half width plus
  3 px), then names (their placed box at the zoom's level), then ribbons (half a stroke plus 3 px).
  The drawing now carries `lineOf` (each drawn line's graph line), `chainAt` (per ribbon point, the
  chain index of the last node passed, so a hover knows its stretch) and `passageOf`.
- **Focus** (`draw.ts` `DiagramFocus`): the selection's lines full, the rest at 0.45 width and half
  opacity (the map's MUTE), redrawn over with their casings so they sit on top. Stations off them fade
  (outline 0.35, the white over the background at 0.35, so dark pills recede too); their names 0.35.
  The selected station's outline is the accent colour, 1.5 times as thick. The line under the pointer
  is drawn full over the rest.
- **Tags**: a station's is `complexTip` (map/networkTips.ts, new): the diagram's name, the bullets of
  every line stopping at any member, and the ridership circle's riders. Not the platforms' waiting rows
  (`stationTip`'s), which are per station: on the first version the Times Sq tag listed only the 1. A
  line's is the map's first rows (bullet and name, status, riders on the stretch, purpose).
- **The camera**: opening centres on the selection (for a line, its stop nearest the map's centre) or
  the complex nearest the map's centre; going back moves the map only if the diagram was panned, zoomed
  or reselected, so toggling M without touching anything leaves the map where it was.
- **The canvas is re-appended to the map's box each time it opens**: the attraction marks and other
  z-index 3 layers are added after it at load and drew over the diagram (the museum icons in the first
  screenshots). The guide card is z-index 3 too, so it is under the diagram while it is up.
- **Loading bar** (T-2235): the worker posts progress each percent on a first layout: A to 0.2, B to
  0.7, routing the rest of the layout's 0.85, names (per level) the last 0.15. On New York the bar
  went 0 to 98 in 51 steps over 3.5-4.4 s, evenly enough.
- **Picture**: New York 7,516 x 6,882 px at a pitch of 9.9 px, 4.2 MB, names at 12 px
  (`artifacts/trainworld/t2163/nyc-diagram.png`, a crop `nyc-diagram-crop.png`).
