# T-4555: select a train in the timetable and chart (T-4511 stage 13)

## As built

- `app/src/ui/inspector/trainPick.ts`: the switch, `MOVES_SHOWN` (`?moves` in the address; T-4559 makes it true), the
  picked train (`pickedTrain`, a signal: line number, run, departure as drawn) and `pickedOf`, which finds it in a new
  answer.
- `TrainTable.tsx`: a click (a press let go within 4 px, or a tap) on a column or its bullet picks the train; a click
  anywhere else in the table or chart lets it go (not the scrollbars, not a drag). The picked column is drawn over the
  drawn ones it lands on at every zoom (`Raised`, shared with the hover), tinted in its line's ink, its bullet in front.
  Switching the direction lets the pick go; a picked train of the other way (a click on a dimmed chart train, or later
  the train view's entry) turns the table round to it, and the table scrolls to it only if it is out of view.
- `MareyChart.tsx`: `picked` drawn lit as on a hover (no fade); a click hit-tests as the hover does and hands the train
  to `onPick` (another line's train with no column, or a pass dot, lets the pick go). Pointer cursor over a train.
- `SchedulePanel.tsx`: Esc lets a picked train go first, then closes; closing lets it go.
- With the switch off nothing changes for players (the hover's code is shared but draws the same).

## How it is kept

By line, run and departure as drawn (`line_trips`'s, moves included), found again in each answer as the nearest
departure of that line and run, accepted while within half the gap to its neighbour on that side: the sim's rule for
which departure a move goes to (`moves::secs`). Not by the move's key (the plan said so): an Offset step changes the
key as well (`Op::Nudge` rewrites stored keys), so the app needs the nearest match either way, and a move keeps its
line's order with at least a minute to the next train (rule 2), so the train moved is always the nearest to where it
was. When the arrows come (T-4556) the app also knows the step it sent. A train out of the table's day (an hour
passing) or gone (a count step leaving none that near) is let go.

## What is left: a sim change

The plan's stage 13 also put each trip's move, kind and key in the readout. The brief kept this task to app/ (shift6 is
in the sim), so that went to T-4597, which T-4556 now waits on:
- `line_trips` (sim/src/track/wasm/service.rs) gives 4 numbers a trip; it needs the seconds moved (`Trip::moved`), the
  kind (`MoveKind` of the move that matched) and the unmoved departure (`Trip::dep`), which `move_op` and
  `move_range` take to name a train. TrainTable's `useColumns` and MareyChart's `tripsOf` read the wider rows.
- An export that moves a train (`move_op`, after T-4548's partners) and answers the refusal (`Why`, limit) for the knock
  and its hover.
