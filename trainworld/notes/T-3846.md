# T-3846: quieter timetable related lines

Rule (Anita 2026-11-20): a line is related to the timetable's line when it runs on the same track at any point, or
shares at least 3 stops (a complex counted once) that are at least 40% of either line's stop count. `isRelated` and
`relatedCommon` in TrainTable.tsx, fed by the `lineRelated` groups (`shared` = some track meeting; stops = distinct
complexes where both stop). A line still needs two shared rows to get columns (as before). Bullets and times 50%,
past 25%, lit 50% (style.css `.tt-bullet.rel`, `.tt-col.rel span`). The line panel's related lines are unchanged.
