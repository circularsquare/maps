# T-3248 Leaderboards show current scores, not best

Agent boards, 2026-11-11. Anita: "perhaps we should have the leaderboard scores show like *current* (or most
recent) score rather than max ... if people dont log on for a while they'll be stale but thats fine we can clear
them out occasionally after huge nerfs".

## How scores reached the boards before

- The page (`app/src/game/account.ts` `sendScores`) sends the playing game's figures at most every 2 min while
  signed in: `points` (the past 24 game hours, all cities) and up to 64 cities' points. Nothing best-ever on the client.
- The server (`shonei-server/trainworld.go` `handleScore`) **overwrites** the game's entry in
  `trainworld/scores/<account>.json` with each PUT, stamped `At` (unix seconds). So a game's stored figures were
  already its most recent ones; no per-game maximum was ever kept.
- The only max was `indexGames`: a person's row on each board (world `points`, `points_<city>`, `country_<cc>`) was
  the highest over all their stored games (up to 20). A game they stopped playing kept its pre-nerf figures
  forever and held their row up. That is the stale score she described.
- Community's Biggest: each share's points are copied from the game's figures **when it is shared** and fixed
  with that drawing; Biggest ranks one share per person, their highest. Not changed (see open points).

## What changed

The brief's reading ("best of their games' current scores") is exactly the old behaviour, because per-game
figures were already current. So the change is across games: on each board, a person's row is the game they
**sent most recently** that has points on that board.

- World board: their most recently sent game.
- City board: their most recently sent game with points in that city.
- Country board: same, by the game's sum over that country's cities.
- Two games sent in the same second: the higher one (map order never shows).
- Consequence: starting a new game in a smaller city lowers their world row until they play the big one again
  (any send from it makes it the latest again). Their old city's board keeps the old game, since no newer game
  scores there.

Server: `indexGames(f, since)` picks the latest per board; `twPick`. Tests:
`trainworld_current_test.go` (`TestTwBoardsShowCurrentScores`, `TestTwSameSecondKeepsHigher`,
`TestTwBoardsCutoff`, `TestTwBoardsCutoffNeedsKey`); `TestTwScoreJoinAndBoards` and `TestTwCountryBoard` now pin
the clock and expect the latest game. `go test ./...` passes. Commit b382e82 in shonei-server, not pushed or deployed.

## Migration

Nothing to migrate: no stored maxima exist. The score files already hold each game's last figures and send
time, and the board index is rebuilt from them at start. At deploy every row becomes the person's most recently
sent game, at once. A person whose latest game is smaller than an older one drops on the world board.

## Clearing stale rows

A cutoff, non-destructive: `<TRAINWORLD_DIR>/boards.json` `{"since": <unix>}`. Games last sent before it count on
no board; nothing is deleted, a game played again is back on its next send, and setting 0 brings all back.
Routes (inbox key): `GET /trainworld/inbox/boards[?since=]` gives `since`, `people` (listed people with a row) and
`would` (how many keep one under a trial cutoff); `POST {"since"}` sets it (future refused). Doc:
shonei-server `docs/trainworld-api.md` "The boards' cutoff".

Inbox page (`tools/feedback-inbox/index.html`, localhost:8800/twfeedback/): a new **Leaderboards** tab with
"Hide games last sent before [date]", Set cutoff, Clear cutoff, the current cutoff and how many players have a
row, and a preview line when a date is picked. Checked against a local build of the server
(scratchpad check, Playwright): preview, a future date refused, set, clear.

## Client text

No player-facing string said "best". Nothing changed in the game's text. Only a code comment in
`ui/Leaderboard.tsx`. Inbox page labels (hers, not players'): "Leaderboards", "Hide games last sent before",
"Set cutoff", "Clear cutoff", "No cutoff. N players have a row.", "Cutoff: <date>. N players have a row.",
"With this cutoff N of M would keep a row."
