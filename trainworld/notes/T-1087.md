# T-1087: no ring zones (design, 2026-10-23)

Anita (2026-10-23): "i think ideally we dont have ring cities. the point of splitting the world into
areas is to keep together areas that mostly commute within themselves. if we have a ring area around a
city, thats like the opposite of what we're trying to do. we should be thinking kinda like voronoi."
This replaces T-1005's "leave metro rings alone". A design, measured on dry runs; nothing in
`pipeline/`, `data/` or the plan changed.

## Summary

**The rule.** The planner keeps growing small countryside zones up to the floor, with three changes:

1. **Join the nearest, not the smallest.** A zone under the floor joins the neighbour whose centre
   (the mean position of its people) is nearest its own, among those that keep both under the cap. Zones
   then gather round their own towns, like Voronoi cells, at today's sizes. If no neighbour fits under
   the cap it joins the smallest, as today, or stays as it is once it holds 80% of the floor.
2. **The wrap test counts metros, and is never overridden.** A join that would make a zone wrap a
   metro or another zone (T-1005's test: half of it inside the zone's convex hull, the zone holding half
   its border) is refused. A zone whose every join would wrap stays as it is if it holds 80% of the
   floor; a smaller one joins anyway and step 3 takes the ring apart.
3. **Cut what is left.** A zone that still wraps something is cut in two across it: its units by
   direction from the wrapped city, split where the two halves hold about the same people and each
   half is in one piece. Each half then regroups by steps 1 and 2. A single unit wrapping a zone takes
   it when both fit under the cap (Wrocławski with Wrocław).

Metros keep their extents (GHSL commuting areas). A ring round a metro becomes wedges, each belonging
to the next town out. The floor, caps, contiguity, no exclaves and grouping within a region stay as
they are.

## Details

### 1. The ring test

As T-1005: zone A wraps B when at least 0.5 of B's area lies inside A's convex hull and A holds at
least 0.5 of B's border with other zones, metros and built packs (coast, dead space and other
countries do not count). New here: B may be a metro row or a built pack, and the planner applies the
test to them. Shapes are the units dissolved (500 m simplified, Mollweide), so a few pairs differ from
T-1005's pack outlines.

A commuting test was measured but not used as the rule (it needs data most countries lack): ACS
2016-2020 county-to-county flows (`data/raw/acsflows/table1.xlsx`, every US county), shares of a
zone's resident workers working in it. The sharper figure is among **county leavers** (workers whose
job is outside their home county), since most US workers work in their own county whatever the zones.

### 2. Variants tried (US)

All from today's free counties (the plan's US zone rows), GHSL 2025 people, floor 0.5M, cap 1.5M,
states as regions, metro rows and the 13 built packs fixed. "Leavers kept": county leavers whose job
is in their own zone. "All kept": every worker in zones.

| Variant | Zones | Median | Rings (metro / zone) | Under 0.5M | Over 1.5M | Leavers kept | All kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The plan today | 191 | 0.79M | 13 / 5 | 5 | 1 | 42% | 85% |
| Today's rule, metros in the wrap test | 192 | 0.77M | 10 / 7 | 6 | 0 | 40% | 84% |
| Join the strongest pull (people x commute curve), wrap test | 164 | 0.89M | 10 / 0 | 6 | 3 | 53% | 88% |
| Strongest pull, strict wrap test | 172 | 0.89M | 0 / 4 | 16 | 4 | 54% | 88% |
| Seeds at towns (GHSL urban centres and local peaks), grown to 1M, strict, cut | 168 | 0.91M | 0 / 1 | 10 | 3 | 55% | 88% |
| **Nearest centre, strict, cut (the rule)** | **179** | **0.80M** | **1 / 0** | **10** | **1** | **53%** | **88%** |
| Ring repair only, on today's plan | 189 | 0.79M | 1 / 1 | 7 | 1 | 43% | 85% |
| The rule, metros grown by counties sending 25% into them | 174 | 0.78M | 1 / 0 | 15 | 2 | 57% | 89% |

What this says:
- The wrap guard alone does not stop rings round metros: when every join wraps, today's guard takes
  the first anyway. Refusing those joins ("strict") is what removes them, at the cost of a few zones
  left under the floor; the cut deals with the rest.
- How a small zone picks its neighbour matters more than seeds. Joining the smallest neighbour (today)
  strings leftover counties together, which is how the rings form. Joining by gravity pull favours big
  neighbours, so zones grow (median 0.89M) and some break the cap. Joining the nearest centre keeps
  today's sizes and keeps 53% of leavers in their zone, where today's rule keeps 42%.
- Explicit seeds (GHSL urban centres and the biggest county among its neighbours, grown by the commute
  curve) give about the same result with bigger zones and more parameters (seed size, growth cap). The
  nearest-centre rule is Voronoi-like without a seed list: a zone's centre sits where its people are,
  so it drifts to its town.

What is left with the rule (US):
- One ring: St. Mary's (0.86M, 42 counties: Virginia's Northern Neck and Middle Peninsula with St.
  Mary's MD) round Richmond, a strip between Washington, Richmond and Virginia Beach that the cut
  cannot halve into two pieces that each hold together. Wave 4: a look by hand at its replan, or
  question 1's metro growth.
- Under 0.5M: Hawaii's islands (the dry run lacks the planner's archipelago rule; the plan joins Hawaii
  and Maui), Barnstable, Nantucket, Atlantic NJ 0.44M and Washington MD 0.44M (as today), Cameron TX
  0.42M (Cameron and Willacy beside McAllen), Delaware IN 0.50M.
- Over 1.5M: Greenville SC 1.69M (12 counties); today's plan has Caddo Parish at 1.60M.
- By wave (zones before and after, county leavers kept): pilot 17 to 16, 46% to 54%; South 52 to 51,
  45% to 55%; Midwest 55 to 47, 42% to 56%; West 37 to 38, 32% to 36%; Northeast 30 to 27, 43% to 55%.

### 3. China

Units: geoBoundaries ADM3 (`CHN_ADM3.t897e.pkl`), provinces as regions, floor 1M, cap 2.5M, the 84
metro rows fixed (their pack outlines). No commuting data, so the ring test only.

| Variant | Zones | Median | Rings (metro / zone) | Over 2.5M | Rows unchanged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shipped today | 660 | 1.48M | 16 / 47 | 10 | 660 |
| Today's rule, metros in the wrap test | 645 | 1.54M | 5 / 4 | 12 | 310 |
| **The rule** | **601** | **1.70M** | **4 / 1** | **9** | **104** |
| Ring repair only | 639 | 1.54M | 8 / 3 | 10 | 537 |

The rule makes China's zones bigger (median 1.48M to 1.70M): nearest-centre joins leave fewer
stranded small zones than smallest-first. Rings left: single counties round their city's metro row
(Bozhou round Zunyi's Honghuagang, Yunyang round Shiyan, Weishi round Kaifeng, Huangzhong round
Xining) and Zaoyang round Xiangyang's districts. Each is a county that is the whole ring: only
growing the metro (question 1) or a finer unit would remove it.

Ring repair only (T-1089): 123 shipped rows change, by solve region Sichuan 10, Jiangsu 8, Inner
Mongolia 8, Henan 8, Guizhou 8, Heilongjiang 7, Guangxi 7 and 1-6 in 19 more; 11 rings stay (8 round
metros: Kaifeng, Zunyi, Datong, Shiyan, Xining, Jilin, Fuzhou, Ma'anshan), where a half cannot regroup
without wrapping again.

### 5. Cost

- **The planner change (T-1088)**: `world_plan.zone_groups` takes `choose="near"`, metros and built
  packs in the wrap test, strict refusal with the 80% rule, and the ring cut (`notes/t1087/rule.py`
  `floor_merge`, `cut_ring`, `cut_rounds` are the logic). A replan regroups only planned rows, so the
  US West and Northeast replan without touching built rows. Units under 100,000 keep the longest-border
  join and islands the archipelago rule (the dry run simplified both).
- **US built rows**: ring repair 15 rows (about 13 new), builds of minutes on AWS, the gates, and the
  joins of south-atlantic, west-south-central (shipped) and east-north-central, west-north-central
  (wave 2's, free if done before its join). Names into `zone-names.json` again for the new rows.
  T-1053 (saves naming removed zones) must land first for the shipped ones.
- **China**: 123 rows (about 102 new), 2-3 hours of builds at 4 cores or one AWS run; the joins are
  T-1000's reruns of every region, so no extra joins. Supersedes T-1005 part 2's Chinese list (75 rows),
  which it contains in spirit (the same cut and merge, plus metro rings).
- **Europe**: about 49 rows into about 32 in Poland, Germany and Britain (overlapping T-1005 part 2's
  merges), plus Prague's and Dublin's finer units; the DE, PL, GB, CZ and IE joins, which T-1000 reruns
  anyway. Other countries not measured: run `other_run.py <ISO> repair` on each in the batch.
- **Order**: T-1088 before wave 3's plan (wave 3 is next); the repairs in the fix batch with T-1000,
  T-903 and T-1002 (T-1005 part 2 becomes T-1089).
