# T-4314 Station reach and trains' fill by what changed (agent reachfill, 2026-11-27)

**For Anita.** After an edit the renderer no longer compares every station's platform for the delete/select tool's
hit-test reach, and the demand layers no longer allocate and copy every line row of the trains' fill array. When new
trips land, only lines whose train gauges actually changed are refilled and uploaded. Nothing visible changes except
timing.

## What each pass rebuilt before, and what a missed patch looks like

- **Station reach** (`app/src/map/renderer.ts` `stationReachNow`): on every new network, `reachNow` walked all
  `stationCount` stations (2,698 on Asia), matched each id in `was.stationIds`, and compared 32 int32 words
  (`stationShape`, `stationAxis`, `stationLine`) per station (2-4 ms on Asia). `NetPatch` in `map/network.ts` named
  strokes, edges, `stretches` and `lanes`, not stations, even though `network.ts` already computed `stDirty`, `copyRun`
  runs, and whether `oneStation` recomputed a station's cached platform `c`.
  - *Missed patch*: a new station's reach stays `0` or a resized/re-curved station keeps its old reach, so on a flat
    camera `stationAt` skips it in its bounding-box pre-check and the player cannot hover or click it with the delete
    or select tool.
- **Trains' fill** (`app/src/map/demandLayers.ts` `buildLayers`, `map/trainSlots.ts` `putSlots`, `map/renderer.ts`
  `setLayers`): on every edit, `buildLayers` allocated a flat `Float32Array(nSamples * 2)` (3.34 MB on Asia), filled it
  with `-1`, and copied every unchanged line row from `was.fill` (2-4 ms on Asia). When trips landed, `numsKept` was
  keyed on `WeakMap<DemandView, ...>`, so a new `DemandView` made fresh `LineNums` objects for every line;
  `was.nums.get(line) === nums.get(line)` failed for all 242 lines, refilling all ~417k samples and uploading all
  3.34 MB (30 ms on Asia). `NetPatch.stretches` already named the lines whose per-sample stretches changed.
  - *Missed patch*: a line's trains show stale crowding colours or stay striped (`PENDING`) until a whole rebuild.

## What changed

- `app/src/map/network.ts`: `NetPatch.stations?: { runs: Int32Array | null; changed: number[] }`. When `stDirty` is
  available, `was.st === st` emits `runs: null` and `changed` = only station indices where `oneStation` actually
  recomputed `c`; when stations were added/removed (`was.st !== st`), `copyRun` records `(to, from, count)` triples in
  `runs` and `changed` records the remaining indices where `oneStation` ran.
- `app/src/map/renderer.ts`: `stationReachStep` patches `reachOf` from `n.patch.stations` (reusing `prev.r` with zero
  allocation when `runs === null && changed.length === 0`, copying `runs` via `Float32Array.set` otherwise, and
  recomputing only `changed` indices). `halfWidth` tracks its attaining index `maxHwAt` for O(changed) exact updates
  (scanning `stationShape` only if that single station was removed/changed). `setNetwork` keeps `this.reachOf` chained
  across edits once asked for, so it stays patched between hovers. `?patch=check` compares both `r` and `halfWidth`
  against a fresh `stationReachWhole(n)` (`T-4314 check`). Planted fault switch: `reachTest.drop` (`?reachTest=drop`).
- `app/src/map/demandLayers.ts`, `app/src/map/rendererParts.ts`, `app/src/map/trainSlots.ts`, `app/src/map/renderer.ts`:
  - `NetworkLayers.fill` is now a per-row `ReadonlyMap<number, Float32Array>` keyed by `rowKey` instead of one flat
    packed array, eliminating the 3.34 MB allocation and unchanged-row copies on every edit.
  - `SlotSrc`, `SlotTex`, and `SlotNames` in `trainSlots.ts` support `rowMap` (with `fillVal: -1` for blank fill),
    reading each changed key's `Float32Array` directly in `stage` with zero copy and O(1) identity checks in `differs`.
  - `numsKept` is a single `PerRecord` keyed by line id with `[l, st, dm, pm, factor, period, purpose, key]`, so
    untouched clusters' lines keep their `LineNums` object across new `DemandView`s; `sameGauges` compares `g0`/`g1`
    bit-for-bit so even newly built `LineDemand` objects with unchanged gauges keep their fill row.
  - `?patch=check` builds a fresh whole per-row map and compares every row bit-for-bit (`T-4314 check`).
