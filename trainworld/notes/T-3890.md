# T-3890: bus stop panel and stop connectors

Agent busstop, 2026-11-20. Anita (11-20): "make bus stops clickable and show a view sorta like we show for all the
other stations ... if bus stops connect but dont actually like visually connect or overlap we can draw a little
connector between them sorta like how we draw connecting halwlays between train stations."

## What changed

- **Clicking a stop** demand sees (an end, a station's, a crossing's, a plain one) selects it: `busStopHit` in
  `map/pick.ts`. A train station's stop selects the station (its panel). A street stop (drawn only, id -1) still
  selects its line. `main.ts` `NET_FIRST` has `busStop`, so a stop is picked over a demand bubble as lines and
  stations are. The selection is `{ kind: "busStop", stop, at }` (it was `{ lines, stop }`, T-2949's shared stop).
  Stops are placed again on every edit (ids are positional), so the panel's module effect finds the stop again by
  id, else the stop nearest `at` within 60 m, switching to the station's panel when it became a station's stop.
- **The place**: `busPlaces(w)` in `game/busLinks.ts`, memoised per world. Union of bus stops (at `stopPlace`) and
  train stations within 100 m, never two stops of one line, as the kernel's `merge_stations` (which skips only
  consecutive stops of a line; the app skips the whole line, a loop case only). Walks: every pair of groups within
  300 m (demand's `xfer_walk_m`, straight line) where the other group has a line this one has not.
- **Panel** (`ui/inspector/BusStopInspector.tsx`): title (existing name: "Bus stop near X" / "Bus stop") with Show
  on the map; grey sub "2 lines stop here" and an "At X" button to a merged train station; Blueprint while planned;
  Lines with boardings today (reuses the station panel's `BoardingsToday` and `Waiting`, now exported); Change on
  foot (at most 8, metres via `length()`, not minutes: the kernel's walk is 1.2 m/s x 1.3 and Closest stations'
  6 km/h x 1.3, so minutes would disagree with one of them); "Build a station here" (`pickTool("station")`, Build
  tab, camera to the stop). Stations still go only on track (T-1820); not changed.
- **Connectors** (`map/busLayer.ts`, `FLAG_LINK`, passes 3 rim and 4 white after the routes, before the stops):
  white, the stations' rim (`stationInk`), blueprint blue rim and fill while either line is planned, white 0.75 of
  a ring's inner radius each side, from zoom 13. Within a place by shortest joins between plain stops of different
  lines (none on one spot); between places a walk apart from their nearest stops, unless a bus line stops at both
  (its route joins them already; else every crosstown route with two crossings 150 m apart is painted white;
  the panel still lists the walk). The dashed transfer stays for a
  stop to its train station, and for a plain stop to another line's station stop within 100 m (`busStopLinks` now
  only those). `BusLayer.transfers` keeps its meaning; `links` and `walks` count the connectors.
- **Hover**: `Hot.stops` (stop ids); hovering a stop lights its place's marks (rim 30% to white, `HOT_MIX`, as a
  hovered line) and its connectors (rim 60%, as hovered station bridges). The selected stop's place stays lit
  (`hotStops` in main.ts, in the renderer's cache key). The stop tag lists the other bus lines at the place, and
  the resting list leaves those lines out (it repeated them). A train station's stop now shows the station's tag
  (the click opens the station), its lines in the resting list.
- Units checked: demand gets city pack metres (`worldToCity`, ground), so the app's ground 100 m and 300 m match
  the kernel's `station_merge_m` and `xfer_walk_m`; t2992 agrees (106 m apart is no merge).
