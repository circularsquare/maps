# T-4223: closing a loop line refused as "Can't loop back here"

Anita, 11-24: a right click to close a loop is refused about 10% of the time for her (a retry usually works); some
players tried for days. Her lead: a loop with "a super long segment without any stations that kinda stuck out" would
not close; with more stations along that segment it did.

## Causes

The refusal comes from `StopMarkers.loopAt` (app/src/map/stopMarkers.ts): a right click acts only when the marker's
tier is close, open, turn or unturn (or any stop of a closed line); anything else showed `stopMarkers.noLoop`.

1. **A long stretch with no stop before the first stop never closed** (her case, permanent for that line shape).
   Tier 5 (close) needs `close_path` (sim/src/track/wasm.rs) to route the closed stops as a circle. The closing
   hop goes through `route_memo` / `route_hops_plain_t`, which take the hop that carries on round only when it is
   at most `CARRY_SLACK_S` (300 s) slower than the plain hop (`Network::carry_worth`). The plain hop may turn back
   at the last stop for `REVERSAL_SEARCH_S` (600 s) and go back over the line's own track. So when the way round
   from the last stop to the first took more than 10 minutes of track time longer than the line's own way back, the
   plain hop turned back, the closed route had a reversal, and close was never offered. The line tool's insertion
   (`insert_stop`) puts stops so the long stop-less stretch ends up exactly there, between the last stop and the
   first (in a probe, a line set with its stops in another order came back reordered that way). Stops added along the
   stretch shorten the closing hop under the threshold, which is why that fixed it for her.
   Evidence: new test `a_long_stretch_without_stops_still_closes` (route_tests.rs): a ring of six corners with
   stations at three neighbouring ones; radius 3 km closes, radius 30 km gave tier 0 at both ends before the fix.
2. **A right click just after a change to the line was refused** (her intermittent 10%). After every world change
   for the line being edited (each stop click brings a rows state and then a full state, an undo, ground landing)
   the markers showed the page's estimate (`stopTiers` in stopMarkers.ts) until the worker's `stop_tiers` answer
   came back, and the estimate never gives a stop a loop tier: the first and last stops read "on", so a right click
   in that window said "Can't loop back here". Closing a loop puts the last stop next to the first, so the right
   click often comes right after the last click. On a big network `stop_tiers` takes 15-100 s natively on a long
   line (notes/T-4116.md, open as T-4045), so the window lasts that long after every click: a player there could
   try for days.

## Fix

- Rust: `Network::carry_worth` takes the path so far; a plain closing hop that turns back on the way more often than
  the carry (where it leaves the last stop, or at a track end) loses to the carry however long, since it makes no
  loop. `CARRY_SLACK_S` still keeps a balloon's closing from running a long way round to avoid its turn at the first
  stop. Both closing-hop routers use it (wasm.rs `route_memo`, paths.rs `route_hops_plain_t`). Lines whose stored
  route has such a turned-back closing hop would carry on round at their next reroute; that shape could not be made
  by a right click (close was never offered for it).
- Page (stopMarkers.ts): the right click acts only on the worker's tiers for the line's stops as they are, with no
  stop click of the line still out (`tiersFresh`); one made before waits and is made when the answer lands
  (`loopWait`, `loopWaited`, from the answer and from `settleClick`). The worker's last tiers stand whole while the
  stops are the ones they were for (another state of the same edit, ground landing), so the markers and the hover
  ("Right click to close loop") no longer flicker to the estimate in between.
- A refusal says why (`noLoopWhy`): on the first or last stop of an open line, the track would make trains reverse;
  on another station of an open line, only the ends close a loop; else the old words.

## Strings (en.json)

- new `stopMarkers.noLoopEnd`: "Trains would have to reverse to close a loop" ("Trams ..." on a tram line)
- new `stopMarkers.noLoopMiddle`: "Only the first or last stop closes a loop"
- `stopMarkers.noLoop` "Can't loop back here" stays for the rest (a closed line's non-stop, a line under two stops)

- Game run on a new New York sandbox game (port 6186), two octagons of track with stations at three neighbouring
  corners: short (sides 1.7 km) and long (sides 15 km, six stop-less sides from the last stop round to the first).
  20 right clicks with the mouse in a row on each (first and last stop in turn, undo between): 40 of 40 closed, in
  22-91 ms. 20 right clicks on the long one made at the moment the line changed (at the world an undo brings, or
  with the click that adds the last stop back): 20 of 20 closed, 15 of them while the end showed the estimate's
  "on", which the old code refused; no refusal tag shown. A middle stop's right click: "Only the first or last stop

## Not done

- `stop_tiers` itself stays slow on big networks (T-4045): a right click now waits for it rather than being refused.
- A long stop-less hop in the middle of a line still turns back at the stop before it when that is over 10 minutes
  quicker (`REVERSAL_SEARCH_S`); the next station's marker says trains reverse there. The insertion order usually
  puts such a stretch at the closing hop instead, where it now closes.
