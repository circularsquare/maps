# T-2734: the line cross-section

Anita, 2026-11-07: a cross-section of the whole line at the bottom of the line view, on real ground
elevation, track coloured by level (level +1 at 5 m above ground, each level 5 m apart), crossing lines
as dots in their colour above, at or below, stations, and a hover that zooms in at a scale that does
not depend on the line's length.

## Stage 1: feasibility (done)

**Ground elevation.** No pack or zone file holds it: `data/terrain/<id>.terrain` holds only the four
terrain classes (flat, hills, hills, mountains, 2 bits a cell, `game/terrain.ts`), and the sim knows
only the classes and track heights relative to the ground. The page does already load real heights:
the hillshade's Terrain Tiles (AWS Open Data, Terrarium PNG, `elevation = R*256 + G + B/256 - 32768`,
`map/basemap.ts`), fetched and cached by `demBytes` in `map/demFlat.ts` (400 tiles). They are a
surface model in places (T-1881: towers and SRTM speckle in dense centres), bare earth in the US
(3DEP); good enough for a profile.

- Resolution: the source goes to z15; real detail ends near z12-13 outside the US (SRTM 30 m).
  A z12 pixel is 38 m at the equator, 31 m at Tokyo, 29 m at New York.
- Sampling a polyline: pick a zoom, fetch the tiles the line crosses, decode each once
  (`createImageBitmap` with no colour conversion, `getImageData`, 256 x 256 floats), read bilinear.
- Cost: the overview's zoom is picked so a pixel is about the line's length / 600, so any line needs
  about 3-10 tiles (a 20 km city line at z12, a 1000 km line at z7). The lens reads z12: 1-2 tiles at a
  time, fetched as the pointer moves along (about 8 km of line a tile in Japan). Tiles are ~50-150 KB.
- No ground (offline, a failed tile): the ground there is drawn at 0 m and the hover says the
  ground is unknown.
- Sea: Terrarium has bathymetry under the sea; ground is clamped at 0 m so a bridge over a bay is
  over water, not over a trench (the lie: land below sea level reads as sea level).

**Track geometry and levels.** The clock worker holds the track model: `line_path` (edges with
direction, first stop to last) and `edge_render` (x, y, height in m every 100 m or less, plus every
height breakpoint). Track height is relative to the ground, `level x LEVEL_H` with the sim's
`LEVEL_H = 8 m` and 200 m ramps per level. Levels run -3 to +3; their colours are `LEVEL_COLOURS` in
`game/palette.ts`, the one source for the drawn track, the Build tab's level buttons and the Height key:

| level | colour |
|---|---|
| +3 | #4a2c9a indigo |
| +2 | #2f6fd0 blue |
| +1 | #16a39a blue-green |
| 0 | #58b04a green |
| -1 | #f0b928 yellow |
| -2 | #ee7a1a orange |
| -3 | #c2351c red-orange |

So Anita's "yellow for -1" is the game's colour already. The profile draws a level-n stretch at
n x 5 m from the ground as she asked, i.e. the sim's height x 5/8 (a ramp keeps its shape). The sim's
own 8 m is left alone; the 5 m is the drawing's only (`PROFILE_LEVEL_M`).

**Stations**: `line_stops` (node ids, so names from the save's stations by num) and `line_stop_s`
(run 0's distance of each stop, scaled onto the drawn length).

**Crossing lines**: no sim call lists grade-separated crossings (`crossings_info` has only flat ones),
so the worker finds them from geometry: every other rail line's path edges not on this line's path,
pre-filtered by their end nodes against the line's box, `edge_render` for the rest, segment
intersection against the line through a 250 m grid; intersections within 3 m of the other edge's ends
are junctions, not crossings. Each gives the distance along this line, the other edge's height there,
and the lines on that edge. One dot per crossing edge, in the colour of the first line using it, the
hover naming all of them.

**No sim or WASM change**: a new clock query, `lineProfile`, in JS on existing calls.

## The plan

**Data**: `workers/lineProfile.ts` (clock worker) answers one Float64Array: points (s, lng, lat,
height m), stops (s, node), crossings (s, height m, line ids). Asked again on the line or network
version. `game/groundHeights.ts` (page) samples the Terrain Tiles at a zoom, cached by tile.

**Drawing** (`ui/inspector/LineProfile.tsx`): an SVG strip at the foot of the line inspector,
after Line improvements, under its own heading.
- **Overview**: the panel's width, 72 px tall. Along the line at true scale (the whole line fits the
  width). Heights: true scale would be unreadable (a 20 km line over 30 m of relief at 330 px is
  0.5 px of relief; a 500 km line over 1000 m of mountains is 5 px of relief, its track's levels a
  hundredth of a pixel), so heights are exaggerated to fill the strip, the range at least 40 m, and
  the hover states it ("Heights drawn 40 times taller"). Ground a filled area; track a 2 px line on top
  coloured by level; crossings 4 px dots in their line's colour at their height; stations white dots
  with the dark ring as in the stops list.
- **Lens**: while the pointer is over the overview, a box above it (absolutely placed, so nothing
  moves) shows 2 km of line centred on the pointer, at one fixed scale for every line: 2 km over the
  lens width (about 6 m a pixel along) and 1 m a pixel up (so a level is 5 px and -3 is 15 px under the
  ground), 110 px tall, centred on the track's height at the pointer. A bracket on the overview marks
  the 2 km. Station names written in the lens; crossing dots 6 px.
- **Hover** (the game's tag, on the overview, following the pointer): distance from the first stop,
  ground height, track level and its height from the ground; any station within 150 m of the pointer;
  any crossing within 150 m ("5 crosses 10 m above"). The exaggeration in the last line.

## Stage 2: build (done)

Built as planned; the spec is docs/ui-inspectors.md "The cross section". Changes from the plan:
- **Low priority** (Anita, 2026-11-07, "mostly flavor"): asked only once the page is idle; the worker's
  `lineProfile` is async, 6 ms slices with `setTimeout(0)` between, so other messages are answered first,
  and gives up if the track model is replaced; tiles decode in idle callbacks; the strip stays empty until
  the line and its overview tiles are in.
- **Crossings**: the real saves' parallel lines weave across each other's centre lines along shared
  corridors, so the first build drew 86 dots on Newark's 7.9 km NLR and 768 on the 675 km Tohoku
  Shinkansen. Now track meeting at under ~12 degrees is not a crossing, and crossings at one height within
  60 m (a line's several tracks), or of a same line within 400 m, merge: 12 and 67 dots.
- The close-up is the hover itself (a popover in the tag's style, opaque), so the pointer's tag does not
  cover it; a stop or crossing within 4 px on the strip takes the centre.
- Every app SVG is 16 px by the global `svg` rule in style.css: the drawings set their size and
  `stroke: none` inline.
