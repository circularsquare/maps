# T-3532 Buenos Aires to Montevideo drive too short (bamvd, 2026-11-17)

Read-only investigation; nothing changed. Scripts in the scratchpad (bamvd_b.py reads market-drives.bin, bamvd_c.py
re-runs the router's search on a window round the Plata and traces the path).

## How the game works out the drive

Rows `bue` (Buenos Aires) and `moe` (Montevideo) are both on market landmass `road` 1, so the pair gets a routed drive
(docs/regional.md 5.1, pipeline/market_drives.py, data/packs/market-drives.bin, sim `routed_car`). Method: a
least-cost path on a 1/60 degree lattice, cell speed from GRIP4 road density (110/85/55/20 km/h) and slope; water within
12 km of a shore is swum at 6 km/h, farther water is blocked; a car ferry only where OSM maps one (90 min + 30 km/h,
$80 + 50c/km shared by two); Wikidata fixed links burnt in as land. Drive = 24 + 1.252 x path minutes, road km = 1.107 x
path km. Stored as ratios to the rule (15 min + 1.25 x great circle at 85 km/h). Only the Channel and Tsugaru add a
crossing fare; there is no border-crossing cost or wait anywhere (no tolls at borders, regional.md).

## The game's figure for this pair

- Great circle between the market centres: 218 km. The rule alone: 207 min.
- Routed: ratio 1.65 on the rule: **341 min (5 h 41), 323 road km**, no ferry. Car money cost about $24 per person
  (323 km x 15c, halved for two), plus parking 12.4/2 minutes.
- Other pairs from Buenos Aires: Salto-side sbv 318 min, Maldonado-side mld 421 min.

## Why it is short

Tracing the path (scratchpad bamvd_c.py, same cells as the router): it starts in Buenos Aires's northern suburbs, goes
north-east over the roadless lower Parana delta (35 km at 20 km/h, 106 min), then east near 34.17 S across the head of
the Plata / Uruguay mouth into Uruguay, then along Uruguay's coast at 110 km/h. (It does not use the Zarate bridges.)
It touches zero water cells of the game's mask. Against Natural Earth's coast (ne_10m_land): 18 of its 155 cells (about
33 km) are sea that the mask has as land; the rest is delta and river that Natural Earth also calls land (channels
narrower than a 1.85 km cell, no road). So two causes: missing estuary water in the mask, and sub-cell delta channels
crossed as ordinary roadless land. 735 cells in the window are sea in Natural Earth and land in the mask.

Test (in memory only, nothing written): burn Natural Earth's sea into the mask and re-search. New drive **442 min
(7 h 22), 400 land km x 1.107 = 443 road km**, no ferry (before: 341 min, 323 km). Time is then close to the real 7 h;
km is still about 25% short, so some delta is still crossed. A forced waypoint at Fray Bentos on the old mask gives
424 min and 591 km (the real road), so the remaining gap is the delta again.

The OSM ferries are in the window (BA-Colonia and a BA-Montevideo line) but never chosen: a ferry costs 168 raster min
base plus 2.2 per km, so any land path beats it.

## Reality

- By road round through Gualeguaychu and Fray Bentos: BA to Gualeguaychu about 230 km (2.5 h), Fray Bentos to Montevideo
  about 316 km (about 4 h), whole trip roughly 580 km, about 7 h plus the border (the brief's figure; the one planner
  total found, 1,023 km / 12 h, looks wrong and is ignored).
  https://www.iprofesional.com/turismo/440529-como-cruzar-a-uruguay-en-auto-desde-buenos-aires-pasos-y-costos
  https://www.distanciasentre.com/uy/tiempo-de-viaje-desde-montevideo-a-buenos-aires-argentina-via-fray-bentos-gualeguaychu/TiempoHistoria/16534.aspx
- Ferry: aggregators give the fastest Buquebus sailing BA-Montevideo about 2 h 30 (typical 3 h 20) for foot passengers,
  and several say cars are not carried on that route now (unconfirmed). Colonia times (1 h 15 + 2 h 30 driving) and
  Colonia car carriage could not be confirmed by search; the brief's figures stand unverified.
  https://www.ferryscanner.com/en/ferry-routes/ferry-buenos-aires-montevideo
  https://www.omio.co.uk/ferries/buenos-aires/montevideo-gqd1t
- Border formalities: iProfesional gives Gualeguaychu to Fray Bentos about 50 min and bridge toll about USD 10-12; Argentine
  press reports 30-40 min waits in Dec 2025 and up to 3 h on peak weekends (undated stories report longer). The game
  has none.
  https://www.iprofesional.com/turismo/440529-como-cruzar-a-uruguay-en-auto-desde-buenos-aires-pasos-y-costos

Game 5 h 41 / 323 km against real about 7 h / 580 km: the game is about 80 min and 250 km short. A car via Colonia
(BA-Colonia ferry plus 2.5 h drive) would be about 4.5-5 h door to door, which is where the complaint may come from
if the player was counting ferries.

## Fixes (propose, not built; any demand change waits for Anita)

1. **Burn the missing estuary water into the mask** (Natural Earth sea is a free source; JRC Global Surface Water for
   wide rivers). Measured here: 341 to 442 min for this pair. Pairs touched: not counted (a router rerun to a scratch
   table and a diff would count them); the window test found 735 cells in a 9 x 6.5 degree box, so expect AR-UY pairs
   (114 in the table) plus Plata-side Argentine pairs, and mask holes elsewhere. Needs a drives rebuild only.
2. **Treat the delta as slower than 20 km/h or blocked where GRIP shows no road across a mapped channel** (a river
   crossing rule: no cell run longer than N km without a GRIP road/bridge). Would fix the remaining 148 km. Wider
   effect (every river delta), unmeasured.
3. **Ferry speed**: OSM ferries run at 30 km/h in the game (Buquebus about 45-50); with 90 min fixed a BA-Montevideo
   ferry prices at about 490 min against real about 3 h 15 with check-in. Raising speed does not fix this pair, since
   the land route wins, but matters once 1 is in. Affects every ferry pair; size 20-60 min on a 100-200 km ferry.

Which reality the car should match (the road round, or ferry plus Colonia) is Anita's call: the ferry options are about
4.5-5 h door to door, shorter than the game's 5 h 41, so the complaint "too short" fits the road reading.

Recommendation: option 1, then re-measure; add 2 only if BA-Montevideo is still below about 6.5 h.
