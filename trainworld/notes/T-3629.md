# T-3629 track under a station

Repro (checks/lib/play.mjs, scratch repro): a single track 300 m, a 60 m siding 12 m north of it, a 10-car station at the middle. The siding lies wholly under the station box; Select and Delete both took the station on every click.
Only track that does not touch the station node is affected: the platform stretch is the station's own (delete refuses it, "A station needs this track").
Fix: pick.ts, a click on the already selected station picks track under the pointer not incident to its node (first click still the station, so double clicks on a station are unchanged); tools.ts deletePick, the same track (not incident) wins over the station box.
Checked: the siding picked on the second click, track panel opens, Remove track and the delete tool both remove it, the station stays; typecheck passes.
Not done: track incident to the station node but wholly under the box stays reachable only by removing or moving the station.
