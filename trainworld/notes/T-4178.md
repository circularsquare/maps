# T-4178: reversals mid-line turn like a line's end

Anita, 11-24: "people frequently build switchbacks/reversals and are confused why they reduce capacity ... could we just make this consistent? and maybe not show the little yellow icon if its not the limiting factor in throughput (which i think it should almost never be)". Her call: DECISIONS.md, Lines and scheduling, 2026-11-24.

## What was inconsistent

- A turn at a station holds one place for max(turnaround + 60 s, 240 s) (T-3107), the same at a line's end and partway.
- But each run (direction) of a line turns at a reversal partway, so a reversal took two turns a round trip against one at each end: 7.5 trains an hour at a plain station instead of 15.
- And the two runs were two streams in the Kingman delay, so a line alone waited at its own reversal (249 s a train in the fixture below) where it waits nothing at its end.
- The same two-streams wait hit the stretch a switchback runs out and back over (both runs pass each direction of it).

## What changed

- `capacity::build_nodes`: a line's turns at one node are divided by how many it makes a round trip, so a reversal counts half a turn each way. Line ends (one turn) are unchanged.
- `capacity::solve_one`: a line's own users are one stream on terminus, section and single-track resources (single track already was, T-3526), so a line alone waits for nothing until the spot is full, as at its end.
- `Capacity::turn_limits` (+ `TrackApi::turn_limits`, `WorldView.turnLimits`): a turn partway limits its line when at some worked level it is 90% full or more (`TURN_LIMIT_RHO`, the map's "near full") and the line's tightest spot by 0.1 trains an hour (`TURN_LIMIT_MARGIN_TPH`), so a tie with the line's ends doesn't count. Only those places get the map icon, the stop list's yellow icon and an issues list row; each hover now gives the limit.
- `line_limits_lone` now goes through `each_limit`, which `turn_limits` shares.

## What a switchback still pays (not the reversal)

The stretch a switchback runs out and back over carries every train twice in each direction. Double track at 160 km/h holds a train 144 s, so 288 s a train there: 12.5 an hour, under the turn's 15. So at default settings the stem, not the reversal, is the limit, and the panel says "Track near <station> is full". The turn only limits when the line is slower (80 km/h: 2 x 107 s = 214 s, 16.8 an hour) and its ends have terminals, or its turnaround is longer than 3 minutes. That is why the icon will almost never show, as she expected.
