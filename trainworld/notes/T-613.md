## Retrospective (so far)
Wall time: first runner session (previous agent) 3 batches; this session about 6 h: gate rerun 12 min,
Russia batch 2 h (downloads 880 MB and 655 MB, read 12 and 6 min), Stockholm debugging 2 h 20 (90 minutes of it
a wait that should not have happened), the resumed 27-row run 1.5 h before I stopped it. Tokens: the orchestrator's measure.

- **A step that hangs silently costs its full limit and then a retry.** Stockholm's `osm_units.build` ran 3 x 90
  min. `TW_FAULT_S` found the line in 3 minutes; set it on every unattended run. Proposal: the runner gets a
  per-step "no log growth for 20 minutes" check, or dumps the stack itself before the limit kill, and does not
  retry a step that timed out (the retry repeats it).
- **A step's own timeout does not tell the runner the batch is doomed.** After T-700's change every row of the
  resumed run parked on the same merge error, 15 rows in 80 minutes of downloads and builds. Proposal: stop the
  run when 3 rows in a row park on the same reason text, and print it.
- **The sim copy and the pipeline scripts must come from the same commit.** The first `.part` error (routes.py new,
  pack_walk old) and the second (merge_cells old, pack_walk new) were one cause: pipeline `.py` files are read
  from the live tree, the sim from a copy. Proposal: the runner's step list checks that the TW_SIM_DIR copy
  matches HEAD's sim, or changes to pack fields come with their merge rule in one commit.
- **Wake-ups.** Monitors that expire at 30 minutes cost a re-read of the context each; one background `until`
  loop on the log's `exit` line was the right way.
- **Not worth its cost:** the Russia region (13 more rows, 650 MB to 880 MB each) before Anita decided on it.
- **Regions are one or two rows**: reading dominates (2 to 10 min a region), as in T-541; T-719 covers it.
