## How a station showed in two fare areas

A station holds one code (`StationData::fare`, area << 8 | zone), so painting a lone station into area B already
took it out of area A, silently. What reads as "in two":
- **Two areas over one place with the same name** (most likely her case, not reproduced from her session: "Port of Spain fare zones" twice). Painting with the
  second moved stations out of the first with no word, and both names read the same in the panel and the bar.
- **A complex whose members were painted apart and then joined** (a player link up to 1 km, or a station moved
  near another). The panel, the tint and the number show the first painted member's zone; demand charges each
  demand station (stations within 100 m) its first member's code. Probed: fixture stations 4 and 5 (a complex, one
  demand station) at 257 and 513 charge exactly as both at 257; a linked complex 6 (257) and 9 (513), 640 m apart,
  shows area 1 but 9 is charged area 2.
- Painting order can't split a complex: a click or loop paints whole complexes.
