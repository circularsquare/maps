the market's country-keyed fare factors).

## The rule as built

- `Fares.byCity` (true, else absent: by country) and `Fares.cityScales` (by zone id, stored as a country's own
  scale is). Header JSON only: no save level; an old save loads by country.
- "A city" is the zone a station is in (`stationZone`), the Cities list's unit, each demand population's, and the
  market place's `zone`.
- One resolver, `faresIn(f, city)` (game/fareDefault.ts): by city, the fares with only the `@`/`*` keys plus the
  city's own multiple under its country's key; by country, `f` itself. `cityFareScales`, `cityScale` wrap it. So every
  country rule (default, rounded default, relative storage) applies to a city unchanged, and a city with no own
  multiple charges its country's default.
- Readers moved to it: `stopFareScales`/`stopScales` (income, region and stretch money, `packService`'s per-node
  scales), the demand worker's per-population scale, rounded default and `popScales` (so zone prices follow the city
  too), `perDollar`, the market (`marketFareFactors` -> Rust `set_fare_factors_by(byCountry, byZone)`, a place's own
  factor standing where its country's would), `profitEstimate` (a line's first stop's city), the trip explorer's
  market fare (`MarketTrip`, the start place's city), the Money tab and Advanced fares.
- `demandFares()` strips the list not in use (by country: `cityScales`; by city: the countries' own keys), so an edit
  to the unused list starts no solve; the cluster signature adds the city list by city.
- Clock ops `fareCityScale {city, scale|null}` and `fareByCity {on}` (WIDE in editCap, in the fare edit routing);
  `loadFares` and the `fares` op carry both fields (`cityFares`).
- Multiples by city read against the reference country's default fares; city rows have no anchor.
