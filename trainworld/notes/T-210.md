# T-210: the game's own tag everywhere, no browser tooltips

Anita, 2026-10-12: "when mousing over the links between stations... it's like a default google
chrome tooltip and not our own. we should have our own responsive tooltip for everything and not
use the browser one".

## How it works

`app/src/ui/tip.ts` (`startTips()`, called once from main.ts): one tag element on the page, class
`tool-tag ui-tag`, the same look as the map's tags (demandHover.ts, the track tag). A capture-phase
`pointermove`/`pointerover` listener on the document finds the nearest ancestor of the target with
`data-tip` and shows its text at once, 14 px right of and 16 px below the pointer, following it,
flipped left or up where it would leave the window. A "\n" in the text starts a new line. A
MutationObserver on the hovered element refreshes the text if it changes under a still pointer (the
clock's day). Pressing, scrolling, a key, the pointer leaving the page or the window losing focus
hides it; touch pointers are ignored. The map canvas's own tags are untouched.

As a net for markup the game does not write, a `title` met under the pointer is moved to
`data-tip` at once, before the browser's delay would show its tooltip. Today that is only
MapLibre's attribution button (which lies under the top bar, out of reach anyway).
