## What was built

A rider takes the dearer line's train unless the service's next train comes soon enough to make up what it
costs more. With evenly spaced trains that gap comes in steps of the combined headway; it is taken as spread
evenly from half the combined headway (`lo`) to the service's headway minus that (`hi`): the steps smoothed.

- Share: the dearer line keeps its trains-an-hour share while dearer by up to `lo`, none from `hi`, a straight
  line between; the middle is today's cliff point (half the cheaper line's headway).
- Cost: riders pay the extra or the gap, whichever is less: `d_S + H/2 + f/F * E[min(x, gap)]`. Continuous,
  rising with the extra, never above today's (taking every train, or none of the dearer's), equal to it at both
  ends. The service goes to `pool_cost` as one line, (cost minus the half spread of the trains taken, trains an
  hour taken), so mixing with independent lines at the station works as before. Three or more lines fold in one
  at a time.
- Planned waits between cities (capped headways): the gaps are centred where taking the line's trains costs what
  the service so far does, so the cost stays continuous there too (today's rule jumped by up to a few minutes).
- Outside the fade band the arithmetic is today's to the bit (`df/sf`, trains-an-hour weights); `Search::pin`
  holds each line's riders' weight in its entry.
- Numbers: two lines every 10 minutes, one dearer by 2.5 keeps its half, by 5 a quarter, from 7.5 none. Every 6
  (A) beside every 10 (B): A keeps its 10/16 while dearer by up to 1.9 minutes, none from 8.1.
- Cost to calculating time: a few float operations per evenly spaced class at a station; nothing else changes.

Code: `sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs` (`merge_even`, `pool_lines`, `Search::pin`). Docs:
docs/demand-kernel.md 4 (the rule and the lie), docs/demand-worldwide.md "Which line riders take",
docs/demand-plain.md, wiki/waiting-and-changing.md (read/ rebuilt).

### 1. How a rider inside B's stretch chooses between boarding A and boarding B

In the reverse strategy search ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs)), each boarding option at a station is offered with a perceived cost `d` from boarding to the destination (`Search::offer_line`, [search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2545-L2599)). That `d` is the sum of crowded ride time (`set_crowding`, [search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L510-L518)), distance fare, any service-span penalty, and any left-behind wait (`g.rn_left`, [search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2180), [L2208-L2211](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2208-L2211)). A line joins the station's pool if `d < cut`, where `cut` is the smallest `d_i + wait_spread(f_i)` among lines already joined ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2550-L2575)).

How riders split between A and B inside `Search::pool_lines` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2271-L2285)) depends on whether A and B share platforms and stops:

- **Case A: A and B share the same platform, next-stop platform, and stop pattern (within `EVEN_RIDE_MIN = 0.5` min, rail/tram only).**
  `even_classes` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L239-L275)) groups them into one `rn_even` class. For a rider whose whole trip is inside B's stretch, both lines alight at the same stop after the same number of stops (`alight` matches in `Search::merge_even`, [search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2296-L2359)), so they merge into one evenly spaced service:
  - Sorted cheapest `d` first (uncrowded B, then crowded A), A joins if `d_A < d_B + wait_spread(f_B, wait_f) / 2.0` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2347)), i.e., if A's extra perceived cost is less than half of B's headway (times the station's wait factor).
  - If A joins, riders split **purely by frequency (trains per hour)**: `f_A / (f_A + f_B)` and `f_B / (f_A + f_B)` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2281)). Crowding has zero effect on the split below that threshold.
  - If `d_A >= d_B + wait_spread(f_B, wait_f) / 2.0`, A does not join (`pmap = NONE`, [search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2351)) and gets **0%** of those riders in that round.
  - This cliff is explicitly noted as "The lie" in [demand-kernel.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/docs/demand-kernel.md#L154-L159).

- **Case B: A and B use different platforms, different stops (e.g. express/local), or are buses/ferries.**
  `merge_even` returns `false`, and `pool_cost` / `pool_two` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L3195-L3371)) splits them as independent arrivals with a **mix of generalised time (including crowding and left-behind wait) and frequency**:
  - `FIRST_ARRIVAL_SHARE = 0.75` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L3160)) take whichever line minimizes `d_i + U(0, h_i * wait_f)` (earliest perceived arrival).
  - The other `0.25` take the first departure that can still arrive by `end = min(d_i + h_i * wait_f)` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L3353-L3361)).
  - Here, A's share falls smoothly as `d_A - d_B` grows, reaching 0% when `d_A >= d_B + h_B * wait_f` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L3341-L3342)).
  - When `d_A == d_B` (free-flow round 0), for `f_A >= f_B`, both groups give B `f_B / (2 * f_A)` and A `1 - f_B / (2 * f_A)` (from `s2` and `l2` at [search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L3352-L3356)).

Assignment then splits station flow using those stored shares (`DestWalk::run`, [assign.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/assign.rs#L456-L477), reading `put_shares` at [assign.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/assign.rs#L426-L445)).

### 2. Does A's crowding (or B's emptiness) change the choice? Can riders skip full trains? Is there a queue for room?

- **How crowding feeds back (rounds 1 to 3):**
  Round 0 is free-flow (`fac = 1.0`, `rn_left = 0.0`, [api.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/api.rs#L1792-L1793)). Up to 3 crowding rounds follow ([DECISIONS.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/DECISIONS.md#L88), `crowd_in` at [api.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/api.rs#L1872-L1900)). Crowding increases `d_A` in two ways:
  1. **Ride-time multiplier (`crowd_factors`, [assign.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/assign.rs#L540-L588)):** Above `crowd_start = 0.7` of rated load ([kernel.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel.rs#L377), or seats if higher; `0.85` for buses), `fac = 1 + 2 * x * (1 + x) / 2` where `x = (hour - start) / (rated - start)` (`crowd_alpha = 2.0`, [kernel.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel.rs#L375)). That is `3.0x` at 100% rated, `7.0x` at 130% rated, and `10.78x` at 150% crush. `set_crowding` ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L510-L518)) multiplies each segment's `rn_ride` by `fac`.
  2. **Left-behind wait (`left_behind`, [stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L38-L129)):** Added to `g.rn_left` ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L144-L147), [api.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/api.rs#L1887)), capped at `LEFT_WAIT_CAP_MIN = 60.0` min ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L9)). It is **0 unless a train actually hits crush load (150% of rated, `net.line_crush[l]`, [stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L62), [L98-L101](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L98-L101))**.
  - **B's emptiness** gives no bonus below 70% rated: `x` is clamped at `0.0` ([assign.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/assign.rs#L563)), so B at 60% has `fac = 1.0` and `rn_left = 0.0`, identical to an empty train.

- **Can a rider let a full train go and wait for the next useful one?**
  - **In route choice (rounds 1 to 3):** Yes, if `d_A` rises enough relative to `d_B`, riders drop A and wait for B (`d_A - d_B >= h_B * wait_f / 2` in `merge_even`; smoothly up to `h_B * wait_f` in `pool_two`). However, because crowding multiplies `rn_ride`, on a short trip (say 1 to 2 minutes of ride time inside B's stretch) even heavy crowding on A may not exceed `h_B / 2`, so under `merge_even` nobody lets A go.
  - **At the platform / in the queues:** No. `left_behind` ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L55-L57)), `capacity_fit` ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L217-L219)), and the boarding account ([boarding.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/track/boarding.rs#L10-L11)) simulate each line and direction alone. A rider assigned to A who cannot board A waits for the next train of line A, never B.

- **Is there a queue for room, and does it only apply at the capacity limit?**
  - Yes, both queues only trigger at **crush load (150% of rated, 240 riders/car, [boarding.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/track/boarding.rs#L31-L33))**:
    1. `left_behind` ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L38-L129)) prices extra wait into crowding rounds only when trains fill to crush. (Because arrivals follow a peaked in-period shape with default `peak_hour_factor = 1.25`, [kernel.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel.rs#L374), a line whose period mean is around 125% to 130% rated can touch 150% crush mid-period and get some `left_behind` wait.)
    2. `capacity_fit` ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L201-L283)) runs once **after** the last crowding round and caps each line's boardings to those who can board within `WAIT_FOR_ROOM_S = 1800` s (30 minutes, [boarding.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/track/boarding.rs#L51)) at crush load. Excess riders are not moved to B; `cap_loads` ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L289-L364)) drops them from rail to car/walk ([demand-kernel.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/docs/demand-kernel.md#L466-L468)).

### 3. Is the reported result what the rules should give, or is something conceptually wrong?

It is **partly what the rules say, and partly three artifacts of how the rules are approximated**:

1. **Expected under the rules:** Riders going past B's end must take A, so A starts much fuller than B. If riders inside B's stretch take the first train that comes (or are on a short ride where crowding's extra perceived minutes are smaller than waiting for B), A stays more crowded than B.
2. **Artifact 1 (`merge_even` step cliff, [search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2347)):** On shared track/platforms, A keeps 100% of its `f_A / (f_A + f_B)` share until `d_A - d_B >= h_B * wait_f / 2`, then drops abruptly to 0%. That causes two bad behaviors: (a) on short trips inside B's stretch, A can sit well over 120% to 130% without losing a single inside-B rider to B; (b) when `d_A - d_B` hovers near `h_B / 2`, A flips between 0% and its full frequency share across rounds.
3. **Artifact 2 (MSA keeps free-flow weight, [assign.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/assign.rs#L591-L593), [api.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/api.rs#L1894-L1900)):** With 3 crowding rounds (`round = 0, 1, 2, 3`), MSA gives each pass weight `1/4`. Even if **every** inside-B rider refuses A in rounds 1, 2, and 3, **25%** of the free-flow pass (where A was empty and took its full frequency share) stays baked into A's final load. Worse, `SETTLED = 0.03` ([stalls.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/stalls.rs#L153), [api.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/api.rs#L1878)) checks total load movement across the whole network; on a large map, if this corridor's shift in round 1 is under 3% of network loads, rounds 2 and 3 are skipped and free-flow keeps **50%** of the final load.
4. **Artifact 3 (no platform spillover below or at crush):** Between 100% and 150% rated there is no left-behind penalty, and at 150% crush `capacity_fit` sends overflow off rail rather than onto B.

#### Worked example
Take **A = 10 tph** (`h_A = 6` min), **B = 6 tph** (`h_B = 10` min), **2-car trains** (320 rated, 480 crush per train, [boarding.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/track/boarding.rs#L31-L33)):
- Rated hourly capacity: **A = 3,200/hr** (crush 4,800/hr), **B = 1,920/hr** (crush 2,880/hr). Total = 5,120/hr.
- Demand (treated here as busiest-hour rates for clean arithmetic): **3,000/hr** going past B's end (must take A) + **2,000/hr** inside B's stretch (can take A or B). Total = 5,000/hr (97.7% of combined rated capacity).
- Assume shared track/platforms (`merge_even`, `wait_f = 1.0`), so the cliff is `h_B / 2 = 5.0` min.

**Round 0 (free-flow, `fac = 1.0`):**
- Inside-B riders split by frequency: A gets `10/16 = 62.5%` = 1,250/hr; B gets `6/16 = 37.5%` = 750/hr.
- Loads: **A = 4,250/hr (132.8% rated)**, **B = 750/hr (39.1% rated)**.
  *(On independent tracks/platforms via `pool_two`, A gets `1 - 6/20 = 70%` = 1,400/hr -> A = 4,400 = 137.5% rated, B = 600 = 31.3% rated.)*
- Crowding factor after round 0: A has `x = (1.328 - 0.7)/0.3 = 2.094`, so `fac_A = 1 + 2.094 * 3.094 = 7.48` (and `left_behind = 0`, since 4,250 < 4,800 crush). B has `fac_B = 1.0`.

Now see how the final numbers after 3 crowding rounds depend on the ride length inside B's stretch:

- **Case 1: Very short inside-B ride (e.g. 0.7 min, 1 stop).**
  `d_A - d_B = 0.7 * (7.48 - 1.0) = 4.54 < 5.0` min. A stays under the `merge_even` cliff in every round, so **0 riders** shift to B.
  - Final: **A = 132.8% rated**, **B = 39.1% rated**.

- **Case 2: Short inside-B ride (e.g. 1.5 min).**
  - Round 1: `d_A - d_B = 1.5 * 6.48 = 9.7 >= 5.0` -> Pass 1 puts 0% on A (3,000), 100% on B (2,000). MSA avg: **A = 3,625 (113.3%, `fac = 4.52`)**, **B = 1,375 (71.6%, `fac = 1.06`)**.
    *(If `settled` skips rounds 2 and 3 on a large network, it stops right here: A = 113.3%, B = 71.6%.)*
  - Round 2: `d_A - d_B = 1.5 * (4.52 - 1.06) = 5.19 >= 5.0` -> Pass 2 again puts 0% on A, 100% on B. MSA avg: **A = 3,417 (106.8%, `fac = 3.74`)**, **B = 1,583 (82.5%, `fac = 1.59`)**.
  - Round 3: `d_A - d_B = 1.5 * (3.74 - 1.59) = 3.22 < 5.0` -> **A snaps back** to taking 62.5% (1,250)! MSA avg: **A = 3,625 (113.3% rated)**, **B = 1,375 (71.6% rated)**.

- **Case 3: Longer inside-B ride (e.g. 3.0 min), all 3 crowding rounds run.**
  Rounds 1, 2, and 3 all send 0% of inside-B riders to A and 100% to B.
  - Even with 100% diversion in every crowding round, MSA's 25% free-flow weight leaves **A = 3,312.5 (103.5% rated)** and **B = 1,687.5 (87.9% rated)**.
  - *(If instead 3,000/hr and 2,000/hr are period-mean rates with default `peak_hour_factor = 1.25`, busiest-hour through-riders alone are 3,750/hr = 117.2% rated on A; round 0 puts A at 166% rated, and 3 rounds of 100% diversion still leave A at **129.4% rated** via MSA's 25% free-flow share.)*

### 4. Smallest rule change, compute cost, and whether it is Anita's call

The smallest targeted change depends on which artifact is the main culprit, and two small changes stand out:

1. **Smooth the `merge_even` cliff ([search.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs#L2296-L2359)):**
   Right now, inside `merge_even`, a dearer line keeps its full `f_A / (f_A + f_B)` share until `d_A - d_B >= h_B * wait_f / 2`, then drops to 0%. Replacing that step with a linear (or smooth) fade of the dearer line's share from `f_A / (f_A + f_B)` at `d_A == d_B` down to `0` at `d_A - d_B = h_B * wait_f / 2` (or `h_B * wait_f`) lets crowding on A shift inside-B riders onto B gradually instead of either doing nothing or oscillating across rounds.
   - **Calculating time cost:** Effectively **0%** (a few extra floating-point ops inside `merge_even`, which only runs at multi-line shared-track stations; no extra searches, mode-choice passes, or crowding rounds).
   - **Limitation:** By itself, this does not remove MSA's 25% (or 50% if `settled` fires) free-flow hangover, nor does it help if A's through-riders alone exceed 130% or if B's stretch is so short that `d_A - d_B` is tiny.

2. **Down-weight or drop round 0 (free-flow) in MSA ([assign.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/demand/kernel/assign.rs#L591-L593)):**
   Giving free-flow weight 0 once crowding rounds run (or heavier weights on later rounds, e.g., `w = 2 / (r + 1)` or averaging rounds 1 to 3) stops round 0's uncrowded split from pinning 25% of inside-B riders onto a crowded A.
   - **Calculating time cost:** **0%** (one arithmetic change in `msa`).
   - **Caveat / unsure:** Changing MSA weights affects convergence and oscillation across every crowded line in the game, and I have not tested its swings on the real NYC save (note [DECISIONS.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/DECISIONS.md#L88), where Anita rejected 2 rounds for +19% line swings on NJT NEC).

3. **Is this a demand rule change (Anita's call under [trainworld/CLAUDE.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/CLAUDE.md#L24-L31))?**
   - **Yes.** Smoothing `merge_even` changes the T-4050/T-4041 boarding rule that Anita approved on 2026-11-22 ([demand-kernel.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/docs/demand-kernel.md#L139-L160), [DECISIONS.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/DECISIONS.md#L89-L90)), and any change to crowding feedback (such as adding a boarding crowding penalty or letting riders skip trains below crush) is a demand rule change ([CLAUDE.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/CLAUDE.md#L26-L27), [L189-L191](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/CLAUDE.md#L189-L191)).
   - Even a pure solver tweak like changing MSA weights would move crowded lines' riders by well over 1% on the real NYC save, which is also Anita's call under [CLAUDE.md](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/CLAUDE.md#L200-L202).
