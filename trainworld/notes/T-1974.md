**The market step is the largest single item**: register, heal and the join's market together are
20-57 minutes a country (19-31%), and they grow with the world, not with the country. On
2026-10-22 a gate's register took 33 s (T-962); 200-430 s by 2026-10-24; 410-561 s now.

**China**, for scale. The 638-row rebuild (T-1185, 10 cores, four runs side by side) took about 10 h:
runs A-D 2.8 h (A's critical path: builds 1.2 h, read 0.6, gate 0.5, register 0.3), reruns for 108
parks 3.2 h (gate-only reruns F, G, H spent 82-85% of their time in register; asia/sea's read 1 h),
join 4.05 h (solve 2.4, market 0.5, re-gate 0.5, outlines 0.25). China's 11-row origin rebuild
(T-1765): run 2.03 h, of which **heal 0.88 h and register 0.84 h (85%)**, builds 10 min; then a
2.3 h join (solve 1.14 h for 8 units).

**Reruns** add 30-100% to a country's wall: Vietnam run 2 and 2b (40 min) and an origin fix (run 20 +
join 65); Thailand run 2, run 3 and join 2 (about 105 min); Australia run 2 (18) and run 3 with its
own join (39). Each rerun pays the market fixed cost once or twice and each extra join the full
outlines step, so speeding those up shrinks reruns most.

### Why the market step is slow (profile, read-only, 2 cores)

`register.py --market` calls `market_zones.main(ids)`, which for any id list:
- `airports_by_zone(reg)` for every registered zone: **17 s**;
- `Field()`: 1.6 s;
- `Groups(reg)`: lists and parses every `data/work/*.joint_commutes.json` (116 files, **610 MB**)
  and every `*.cross_everyday.json` (71 files, 290 MB), each twice (once in `joint_files` /
  `unit_files` for the solved sets, once for the pairs), and loops over every pair in Python. The
  listing alone: **62 s**; `Groups(reg)` in all **366 s** (5.7 GB at its peak; 1,969 packs with
  commutes, 1,301 with everyday pairs), for a register that then writes one pack's file.
- So a one-row register is about 385 s of fixed Python (airports, field, Groups) out of its 410-560 s.
- then `build()` per named id (2-4 s each), `market_pack` (5 s for 27 rows), and with `--trips`
  `zone_summary` over every pack.

Files of retired rows stay (T-1185's 27 old China units hold only pairs of removed ids), so this
grows with every country and every replan.

### Smaller findings

- **Network fetches inside builds.** Australia's run lost 8 builds (3 at 263-281 s, 5 at 36-125 s)
  to `tier0.tertiary_share`'s World Bank request reset by the server while 9 builds asked at once; the
  retries rebuilt from the cached cells. About 19 machine-minutes and ~5 min of wall; a worse outage
  parks rows. Every new country hits this, since the per-country indicator caches are empty.
- Big rural zones' gravity: grs (107,000 km2, 17,111 squares) spent 199 s of its 435 s build in the
  Furness solve; routes' `pack_walk` is one thread (T-970).
- The `registry` lock covers the whole market step, so two countries' runs at once wait on each
  other's registers.
- A rerun's join re-solves the whole unit: Vietnam's 10-row origin fix re-solved all 93 packs (17 min).

## 3. Recommendation

1. **Before the next country: #1 (T-1976) and #2's pool (T-1977).** Both small, both pure speed with a
   byte-for-byte check, and together they halve a small country's wall and every rerun. #1 also stops
   the registry lock serialising two countries' runs.
2. **Alongside Cambodia and Malaysia: #4 (T-1979)**, small, and #6's measurement (T-1981) during their
   join, at no cost.
3. **Before Mexico: #3 (T-1978)**, once #1 is in; Mexico's 120-row region is where it pays.
4. **Before Indonesia, India, Brazil or the US/Europe replan: #5 (T-1980)** and the AWS build flow for
   rows in the hundreds.
5. Run joins at night with 12 cores where Anita allows; by day the 10-core cap costs little, since most
   of a country's wall is serial.

For T-1975 (process, not compute): reruns are where the hours go, so whatever catches a park before
the run (the dry run's checks: far origins, multipart units, old caches, gate_relax carried on a
replan) saves more wall than anything above; and one join for several countries at once saves a whole
outlines and market step each.

- T-1981 CPU poll (T-1738's KH,MY,BN join 20261102-141037, `--jobs 10`, a 30 s sample in artifacts/trainworld/t1738-join-cpu.log): the joint solve scales only at its start. Cambodia's unit (17 packs, 72 fixed, 202 s) ran joint_gravity at 5-9.2 cores for about 70 s, then 1-1.6 cores for the last ~2 min; Malaysia's (20 packs, 87 s) peaked at 7.6. So about half a unit's wall is a one-core tail and 12-15 threads would gain little; the join's 29.4 min was groups 2.0, solves 5.2, merges and re-gates 2.7, register --market 12.8 (the first after T-1976, every row once), zone_outlines 3.8 (T-1977, 10 jobs), realms 1.8. Runs beside it: Cambodia 17 rows 19 min (download 1.8, read 0.7, gate 8.0), Malaysia and Brunei 30 rows 23 min (download 0.7, read 2.8, gate 8.9), at 4 and 6 jobs at once. Other agents' Chrome and rustc took 2-5 cores during the join.
