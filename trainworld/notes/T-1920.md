# T-1920: deal a selection's far rail only over cells within reach

Agent reach, 2026-11-04, tree `.tw-trees/reach`.

## What the mismatch is

T-1902 found trip tool cell pairs whose headline counts some train riders while the two cells' own
trip offers none. Every cell that gets commuters by rail is already in a subzone, and a cell is in
one only with a station within the walk's 3 km bound (`walk_cutoff_m`; demand-kernel.md step 3 says
4 km, which is stale). So no cell "beyond any station's reach" gets rail. The real cause is a pair
limit: a subzone pair is offered rail when the two subzones' walks (logsums over their cells, so
near the near cells' walks) are within `pair_walk_max` (80 perceived min), while the cell pair's
own walks can be over it. A subzone spans a 15 real minute band, so a far cell in it can walk up to
22 perceived minutes more than the subzone does.

## The rule

`DemandApi::flows` (bubbles' far end, the explorer's headline) is the only view that knows both
ends. It now deals each far subzone's rail over its cells the selection reaches (`far_reach`): the
far cell's cheapest walk (`Subzones::walk_c`, the explorer's own cell walk; a home end's cheapest ride
where the two zones are far enough apart for one) plus the selection's cheapest end within
`pair_walk_max`. A far subzone with no cell in reach keeps its rail on all its cells by weight.
`cellModes` and `stationRiders` have no other end and are unchanged. The walking rule is not
applied: it needs the station pairs' skims per far cell, which `flows` does not have.

## Numbers (real New York save, `?demandWorkers=2`, 150 home cell -> work cell pairs 2-25 km apart)

The T-1902 probe's 71 pairs were not saved; these are 150 pairs drawn the same way (seed 1920), the
same pairs before and after.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| pairs with train offered in some period | 124 | 124 |
| train counted, none offered | 22 (0.008 a day in all) | 17 (0.008) |
| of which walks too long | 18 | 13 |
| walking rule (`walkFurther`) | 3 | 3 |
| no route | 1 | 1 |

None of these is near a whole trip (the largest 0.004 a day), so the explorer's bar read 0% train
before too. The 13 left are home cells whose own walk is the long one: no cell of the far subzone
is within reach of them, so the subzone keeps its by-jobs dealing. T-1902's lifted route covers them.

Far zone totals for single home cells (each zone's work rail, before -> after): home cell 2314 zone 258
17.305 -> 17.305, zone 261 10.036 -> 10.036; 6203 zone 258 51.132 -> 51.132, zone 261 28.265 -> 28.265;
26139 zone 258 9.232 -> 9.232; 23812 zone 258 4.499 -> 4.499. The selections' rail totals did not
change at all (worst relative change 0).

On the nyc pack with the real network (`nyc_real_track.json`, five periods free flow; a one-off test,
removed): 360 selections (200 home cells, 100 work cells, 30 home and 30 work bubbles of 1.5 km):
far rail 233,128, of which 92.8 moved (0.04%); 149 in far subzones with no cell in reach. Worst far
zone change 1e-7 relative. Bubbles' biggest far zone, e.g. zone 258: 164.722 -> 164.722,
4,057.608 -> 4,057.608, 323.323 -> 323.323.

Piles: the largest per-cell gain is 0.37 a day (0.342 -> 0.714, a cell 25 perceived min from its
station); the largest ratio for a cell with half a rider or more is x1.14 (0.913 -> 1.043); the biggest
rise in one cell's share of its subzone's far rail (subzones of a rider or more) is 26% -> 52%, of 1.3
riders over 6 cells. Nothing a player could see.

Cost: `walk_c` is 4 bytes a cell (211 kB on New York's 54,245 cells, per subzone set: the commutes'
and the everyday layer's), 0.36 ms on top of 21 ms of subzones per rebuild; `far_reach` takes 0.41 ms a
`flows` call. Load to demand final on the real save: 12 s before and after (2 workers). The page's
JS heap read 381 then 423 MB, which is noise between runs (the WASM memory is in the workers).
