# T-3935: real networks miss lines OSM maps only as track

Agent keihan, 2026-11-21, tree `.tw-trees/keihan`. Finding: notes/T-3632.md "Stage 3".

## What changed

- `pipeline/realtracks.py` (new): the lines a pack lacks because OSM maps them only as track. Rules in its docstring.
  Track and stations come from N02 in Japan (noritetsu's Japan extract has no `infra.pkl`), else noritetsu's
  `infra.pkl` relations (`route=railway` / `tracks`; Hong Kong, Singapore, Taiwan, Korea have none). Each line's
  station paths (realnet_n02's `_routes`), cut to the pack with realauto's inside and spacing rules, are checked hop by
  hop against OSM: a hop is covered where its points lie on track a passenger route relation runs over (every
  relation of the extract, dropped ones too: the nearest track, or any track near that is the line's own by name; for
  infrastructure relations any track near). Stretches of uncovered hops (a lone hop of either kind takes its
  neighbours', a hop with no OSM track near is a hard break) become route relations in `osm_<id>.json`, extended one
  hop at each end so they reach their junction. A station at the end of a spur of 1.5 km or less off the path (the
  Yoro Line's Ogaki, a switchback) is stopped at from the junction. A hop whose two stations the line's other paths
  also join, through stations between, over at most 1.5 times its length is a bypass and never added (the Shin-Tarui
  Line, Arao - Sekigahara, beside the Tokaido Line through Tarui). N02 stations become the OSM stop of their name
  within 600 m (kept-route stops first, then station nodes, then lowest id), so the Keihan Main Line shares
  Chushojima with the Uji Line and Hirakatashi with the Katano Line; a station OSM has no stop of is left out.
  Name and kind from the OSM track's `name:en` and `railway` (else noritetsu's N02 line name), colour from
  noritetsu's line. Trains: realnet's default for the kind; a train line with stations over 2 km apart on average
  every 30 minutes (`RURAL_INTERVAL`).
- `realauto.py`: `cut` adds them where the city's `realauto.json` entry has `"tracks": true` (osa, ngo) and always
  reports them (`track_lines`, `track_refused`, `track_partly_covered`); `tracklines [ids]` is the dry run (writes
  only `$REALNET_OUT/tracklines.json`). `shared_path`: in a worktree, noritetsu's and N02's data are the main tree's
  (also used by `realcolour.py`, whose noritetsu colours were silently empty in a tree build).
- Docs: code-map.md (realauto entry), architecture.md (real networks), data-register.md (Japan).

## Why the N02 trim's null is not the line

`n02_trim.json`'s `京阪電気鉄道|京阪本線|n02:701|n02:696` is Chushojima (N02 index 701) to Fushimi-Momoyama (696):
N02 maps the main line's Chushojima twice (690 and 701), so the stress save's line graph had a 1.2 km stub, which
T-1625's game runs found too thin (its notes list it among the 0.1-5 km stubs). The `jpn` stress save still has
Yodoyabashi - Sanjo. realtracks does not read the trim file, and maps both copies to one OSM stop, so the stub adds
nothing. Left as is.

- Trains on three rural lines: the 30-minute rule takes stations over 2 km apart on average, and these fall just
