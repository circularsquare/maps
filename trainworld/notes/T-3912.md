# T-3911 and T-3912 (agent ferryends)

## T-3911 train line stop editing took ferry ports

Ports are stations (platform > 0, a berth, no track). Only a rail line's first stop could be one (a port is joined to
nothing, so a second stop found no route), but `stop_tiers` called a port "route" on a line with no stops and
`check_route` accepted a one-stop line on a port.

- `sim/src/track/net.rs`: `Issue::PortStop { line }`.
- `net/validate.rs` `check_route`: any port among a rail line's stops is `PortStop` (covers saves and raw edits).
- `wasm/lines.rs`: `set_stops`, `add_stop`, `one_way_stop` refuse early with `PortStop`; `stop_tiers` tier 10 for a
  port on a rail line (`port_clash`). Ferry lines: `ferry::check` already refuses a non-port (all stops must be ports).
- App: `StopTier` "port" (`map/stopMarkers.ts`): drawn as a cut marker, hover and click show `issues.portStop`
  ("Ferry ports are for ferry lines."), the same tag path as the tram refusal; `game/issues.ts` `PortStop`.
- Test: `ferry_tests.rs` `a_train_line_refuses_a_ferry_port_as_a_stop`. `agent_cargo.py test` 705 + 912 pass; wasm built.

## T-3912 extend a ferry route from its other end

No gesture existed. `FerryDraw.head` (new ports before the first) was in the ferry and bus tools but nothing set it
true. Now a shift click on the other end of an open route (`otherEnd()`, two ports or more, not a loop) flips it
(`extendFrom()`): the preview leg, new ports (`add` already unshifted), Backspace and the end circle follow
`endIndex()`. Waypoints waiting after the last port are dropped by the switch; `appending()` is false while head is set
so a waypoint goes on the leg where it adds the least. The other end's hover tag gets "Shift click to extend the route
from here". Buses: same `head` field, but the bus tool's preview legs are road searches with their own head cases
(busDraw.ts 360-420, 1074-1100); not done, left as is.
