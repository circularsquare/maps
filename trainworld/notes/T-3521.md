# T-3521: weekend train counts per line (agent weekend, 2026-11-17)

Built from GabrielMoraez's PR 13 (circularsquare/trainsworld #13, closed), reshaped to Anita's design. Co-author for
the commit: `gabs <moraes.madm@gmail.com>` (GitHub login GabrielMoraez, the name and email on the PR's commits).

## What it is now

- **One weekend.** Saturday and Sunday (a country's own weekend) and public holidays run a line's weekend trains; a
  superholiday's days (any of its away, going-out, coming-back days) and the weekend days a country works run the
  weekday trains. `rowOfIn` (game/holidayCalendar.ts) and `Boarding::kind_row` agree: account kinds 1 and 2
  (Saturday, Sunday, so a public holiday too) run the weekend trains, every other kind the weekday ones. The PR's
  third set (holiday counts), its `HOLIDAY_ACCT` and `Boarding::rows` are gone.
- **Per line: a percent or counts set by hand** (`LineData::weekend`, `Weekend { share, counts }`). Default 100%, no
  counts. At a percent a count line's weekend counts are its weekday counts times the percent, rounded up; a headway
  line's headway is divided by it and rounded down to a whole minute (`weekend_headway`), its count from the weekday
  round trip, clamped to the track as the weekdays' (`clamp_auto`) and, under 100%, never more than the weekday
  count (a full track trims weekday headway counts, `trim_auto`; without the cap a 50% weekend bought more trains
  than the weekday on the test line). A weekday edit moves the weekends at once.
- **A headway line's weekends run the weekend headway** (fix by weekend2 after the checker's find): the
  weekend settle and `mirror_weekend` swap the weekend headways into `line_auto` (`weekend_auto`,
  `swap_in_weekend_autos`) beside the counts and rates, since `running_trains`, `Service::spare` and
  `pattern_tph` read it. Before, the weekday headway stayed in: over 100% it capped the trains at
  the weekday rate; under 100% the smaller fleet ran flat out (trip / count: line 7 of the real
  save at 70% ran 1004/1339/2008 s for 1260/1680/2520). A line whose weekend headway differs
  counts as one with weekends of its own even at its weekday count (one train at 15 min, 70%).
  Counts set by hand on a headway line run as a count line's (no headway on weekends). Counts set by hand stay put and keep
  the percent for the panel (shown greyed); picking a percent drops them. No copy-on-first-edit (the PR's `pin_rest`
  is gone).
- **Edits**: `set_line_weekend_share`, `set_line_weekend_counts`, `set_weekend_share_all` (one `Op::Batch` of
  `Op::Weekend`, twins skipped as they follow their lines). All undoable and service edits (`step_is_service`, the
  clock worker's lead).
- **Settle** (`TrackWorld::settle_weekend`, after the weekdays' settle in `settle_train_counts`, and alone for a
  weekend-only edit): the weekday `settle_counts` run on the weekend counts and rates (swapped into `line_trains`,
  `line_tph`, `settle_kept`), on a **weekend service kept between edits** (`svc_rest`): each edit's
  `update_scoped` is repeated on it with the weekend rates (`mirror_weekend`). Without it, every edit rebuilt every
  line whose weekend rates differ (London at 70%: 121 lines, about 430 ms an edit). It is not saved: a load keeps the
  save's weekend rates to the bit (`weekend_as_saved`) and the first edit that settles weekends builds it again
  (clone of the weekday service plus that rebuild). A weekend-only edit touches `Touch::weekend`, not `schedules`:
  no weekday rebuild, no weekday settle, so no demand solve (svc.demand stays empty: `demand_moved` is only asked
  in the weekday branch).
- Zero cost: `weekend_own` returns at once when no line is other than the default (`any_weekend`), `lattice()`
  returns the weekday pattern without `rest_solved`, `set_cal` notes no line then, the boarding key hashes weekend
  rates only with `rest_solved`.
- **Fleet**: most trains of either kind of day (`cars_needed`, weekend counts worked out once per call).
- **Running cost** per kind of day: `running_cost` 0-2 weekdays, 3-5 weekends; the clock worker's `costHour` picks by
  the line's `rowOf`.
- **Save**: trailing `LWE1` after `LTW1`, save level 21: count, per line index, percent, 1 + three counts where set by
  hand (else 0); then 1 where weekends were settled, then rates per rated line (f32 bits) and kept lines as `LRT1`.
  Written only when a line is other than the default, so every other save is byte for byte as master's.
  (A new tag, not the PR's `LDT1`, so a save from his fork is not misread.)

## Measurements (native `edit_bench`, master d607c0b2 against this tree on it, two other 3-core jobs running)

All native (release `edit_bench`), not in the browser's WASM, which runs slower.

| Save | master edits (mean of 11, 2 runs) | weekends default | every line at 70% | Apply 70% to all | reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-007-real-nyc.save (109 lines) | 5.3-6.4 ms | 5.5-6.3 ms | 8.4-9.5 ms | 12-13 ms | 89-105 ms in every case |
| London, packs/real/lon.save (171 lines) | 12.6-14.4 ms | 13.1-14.5 ms | 20.6-20.8 ms | 2.4-2.5 s | 1.98-1.99 s master, 1.80-1.92 s default, 2.02-2.09 s at 70% |

- Weekends default: `lines_info` after load, after the 11 edits, and the per-line table are byte for byte master's
  on both saves (`TW_DUMP`, `TW_LINES`, compared by hash).
- At 70% on every line: the weekday `lines_info` after the 11 edits is still byte for byte master's on both saves
  (the weekend settle leaves the weekday figures to the bit). The first version (clone and rebuild per edit) took
  430-490 ms an edit in London; the kept weekend service brought it to about 20 ms.
- The first weekend edit after loading a save with weekends pays one rebuild of every line whose weekend rates differ
  (an estimate from the first version's per-edit cost: about 0.4 s natively in London; not measured on its own).
- `rework_buses` (a pack's streets arriving, as an area opens) drops the weekend service; the next edit rebuilds it.
- Memory: the kept weekend service is a second `Service`. London's `edit_bench` peaks at 32 MB with weekends default,
  51 MB at 70% (native; about 19 MB more).
- Every line at 70% runs, summed over lines, 85% of the weekday trains an hour at high in London (563 against 664)
  and 82% in New York (402 against 489): many weekday fleets are past what their track takes (held at the ends),
  so 70% of the trains still fills more than 70% of the track.
- A quad-track line's twin follows a weekend edit as an `Op::Weekend` (`twin_ops`), not a whole-line edit, so the
  edit still settles only the weekends.

## Calls (DECISIONS.md)

- Superholiday days run the weekday trains, a public holiday the weekend's; a day that is both runs the weekday's
  (rush first, as `kindOfIn`).
- Under 100% a headway line's weekend count is capped at its weekday count.
- The weekend service is kept between edits, not saved; a load keeps the saved rates.
- A percent is typed as any whole number from 10 to 200; the stepper moves 5% a click.

## Lies

- Weekend trains run their slot's weekday timetable, so their run times are the weekday holds' where the
  weekend service's would differ (T-3735 placed weekend departures on the weekend headway, pacing, spare time,
  split and phases: notes/T-3735.md; T-3584 paced them, notes/T-3584.md).
- A superholiday day with no superholiday riders reads the Sunday account (`Boarding::kind`), which runs the weekend
  trains, while the trains drawn are the weekday ones.
- Riders' arrival masks (`line_masks`) come from the line's real calendar, used for every kind's account.
