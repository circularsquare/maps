# T-4233: update t3303 fare checks after quick city edits (agent t3303fix, 2026-11-25)

## Why both t3303 checks failed on master

1. `checks/t3303-local-fares.mjs`:
   - Since T-3512 (and T-2188's quick city edits), a fare edit is an intercity edit, and in a one-city game (Manhattan is 100% of its cluster, >= 35%) it skips the local stage and runs whole with per-period steps (`publishStep`). The old check asserted `localShown >= 1`, which was `0`.
   - The old check built a 3rd line and edited fares as soon as `view().lines.size > 2` (mid-solve, before line 3's final). With T-3925, line 3's own final at the old fares landed inside the fare edit's window, raising income from `5.768e6` to `6.691e6` (`1.160x`, failing the `1.05x` bound even though `priced` stayed `0`).
   - It did not verify that figures settled to the full solve's at the new fares.

2. `checks/t3303-fare-spike.mjs`:
   - Its cash-gain bound computed `(v0.income * secs * 100) / 3600 * 1.1 + 5e6`, assuming 100x clock speed is 100 game seconds per wall second (comment: "a game hour is 36 s"). In `game/clock.ts`, `SPEEDS[0] = 60` game seconds per wall second, so 100x is 6,000 game seconds per wall second (60x faster than the comment assumed). That produced a false FAIL (`1.35e+7 against 6.62e+6` with `peak 1.000x`).

## Changes

- `trainworld/checks/t3303-local-fares.mjs`:
  - Wait for `demandFinal` after building all 3 Manhattan lines so `v0.income` is the settled 3-line baseline at the old fares.
  - Check `out.local + out.steps >= 1` (counting `steps.shown.length` on `s.clusterRes`) so the check confirms pre-final steps or local results ran.
  - Check `peak` across all samples (`<= 1.05x`), `early.length === 0`, and `gained <= 2 * v0.income * hours + 5e6`.
  - Add final settlement check: all shown lines priced at the new fares (`21/21`), riders `< 1%` of before, income `< 1%` of before.
- `trainworld/checks/t3303-fare-spike.mjs`:
  - Measure elapsed game hours from `tw.clock.now()` (`hours = (out.end.g - mark.g) / 3600`) and bound cash gained by `2 * v0.income * hours + 5e6`, matching `t3303-local-fares.mjs`.

## Runs (Vite on 6173, 1 core / 1 demandWorker)

- Before (master):
  - `t3303-local-fares.mjs`: FAIL (`localShown 0`, peak `1.160x`).
  - `t3303-fare-spike.mjs`: FAIL (cash gained `1.35e+7` vs bogus wall-time bound `6.62e+6`; peak `1.000x`).
- After:
  - `t3303-local-fares.mjs`: PASS (`localShown 0, stepsShown 3`, early `0`, peak `1.000x`, cash `3.23e+7 <= 1.41e+8`, settled `21/21`, riders `9.30e-31`, income `4.46e-27`).
  - `t3303-local-fares.mjs perfOff=fareHold`: FAIL as expected (`stepsShown 3`, 24 early samples, peak `240.103x`, cash `3.61e+9` vs `1.43e+8`).
  - `t3303-fare-spike.mjs`: PASS (`FARE_MAX 1000`, peak `1.000x`, cash `7.07e+6 <= 1.76e+8` over `15.2` game hours, riders `7.17e-31`).
