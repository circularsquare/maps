# T-1608: stop a market search once its origins are settled (2026-10-28)

**Outcome: built and exact, but worth about 6% of each search's nodes, so not landed.** The
patch is `T-1608/early-stop.patch` (against 2a397b76; it still has a TEMP counter, `T1608`).

## What was built

- `Search::run` takes a `Need` (stations to settle, whether their planned first waits are read)
  and stops once every needed station in the destination's part of the graph is settled and,
  where planned waits are read, no later offer can change them. The reasoning, in the patch's
  `entry_bound`: offers come at the cost they are popped at, so later ones are at least the
  queue's least `D` less the station's largest tolerance penalty `S`. With `F W(F)` never falling
  as `F` grows (true for `board_cost` and `planned_board_cost`), a departure costing at least the
  pool's cost never lowers it, so the pool is final once `D - S >= c`. And no pool costs less
  than its cheapest departure plus `transfer_min`, so where that is at least the plain cost the
  planned cost is never read. 0.01 min of room for rounding.
- `pass_window` builds, per destination station, a bitset of the stations whose costs its zones'
  pairs read plus the work and activity ends whose trips home walk its strategy (s^2/8 bytes:
  13 MB on Japan's 10,320 stations).
- Test `an_early_stopped_search_gives_the_whole_strategy` (8 stations, hourly and
  span-penalised lines, every destination and every set of up to 3 needed stations: costs, planned
  waits and loads identical to the bit) passed, as did `a_streamed_pass_is_the_pass_to_the_bit`.

## Measured (native `t1545_dump`, `t780jp` captured as `checks/t761-plain-net.mjs`, period 1)

| build | solve | figures |
|---|---|---|
| master 2a397b76 | 51.1 s | |
| early stop | 46.4 s, 47.6 s with the counter (-7 to -9%, shared machine) | every dumped figure identical |

Counter: 8,394 searches, 8,028 of them read by longer trips (planned waits). On average a
destination needs 3,555 stations, and the last of them settles after 94.0% of the nodes the whole
search settles (236.0M of 236.0M settled at the stop vs 251M in the graphs); the planned-wait
condition adds nothing measurable. So almost every destination has a longer trip starting near the
far end of Japan's network, and an exact stop on settled origins cannot get near halving the
searches. The 7-9% is mostly noise around the 4% the nodes suggest; not worth 13 MB a pass and
the code.

## What could still cut the searches

- Cheaper search per node (profiling `Search::run`, `entry_costs`, `trip_km`): untried.
- One search per destination zone instead of per station: not exact.
- T-1607 (spreading a period's market over idle workers) stays the bigger, exact lever.
