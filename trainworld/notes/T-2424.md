# T-2424, T-2423, T-2421: cars round water (agent waterroute, 2026-11-04)

Design and findings behind it: notes/T-2418.md. Anita's go (2026-11-04): "yeah lets do road detours. i think we
should be able to get good mielage with not a crazy amoutn of extra resources spent. ah perhaps we keep the osm car
ferries and just make ferries quite expensive too."

## What changed

- `pipeline/market_drives.py` (recipe v9): every place is searched from (before, one end of each pair); each search
  is read also at the partner places' market zones (centres from `<id>.market.json` as the market weights them,
  cached in `data/work/market_drives/zones.json`), giving each zone its own (minutes, road km) ratio towards the
  partner over the centres'. Kept for pairs up to 400 km, where 5% or more off (64 ln >= 3) and the zone's or the
  centres' straight line crosses 5 km of lattice water. Written as a trailing section of `market-drives.bin` (same
  `TWD1` header: an older WASM reads the rest and ignores it), sparse (zone, qt, qk) or dense (every zone, qt, qk)
  per (place, partner) group, whichever is smaller. Path sums over the whole search tree by pointer doubling
  (`path_sums`), replacing the per-target walk.
- Car ferries: only cells under OSM `route=ferry` lines tagged `motor_vehicle`/`motorcar` yes, designated or
  permissive (6,521 lines, 123,599 lattice cells), fetched from QLever's OSM planet into
  `data/raw/market_drives/car_ferries.tsv` (7 s; Overpass gave 502/504 for world, 30 degree and 10 degree queries
  that afternoon). The old layer (any water within 60 km of land) is gone. Price 90 min + 30 km/h, $80 + $0.50/km.
- A place centre on lattice water routes from its nearest land cell (Windsor-Essex's sat in Lake St Clair and was
  swum to: Detroit - Windsor-Essex rt 4.1, now 1.7 on the scratch run).
- Northern Ireland: `great_britain()` (GB less lon < -5.4 and lat < 55.4) in `crossing_cost` and the pipeline's
  `crossing`; NI pairs route by road on Ireland's landmass.
- `sim/src/demand/intercity/drives.rs`: decodes the zone section (`Drives::zone`), new ferry constants;
  `intercity.rs` `Market::zone_ratios` applied to the market's zone pairs (`set_served`), the commutes and
  everyday outings between zones, and the trip explorer (`market_trip.rs`); the corridor split keeps the centres'.
- T-2421: `routes.land_labels(bar, opened, cells)` keeps an islet of 0.02 km2 or more holding a cell, 150 m or more
  from other land (`ISLET_KEEP_M`, `ISLET_KEEP_PX`); `parts_run.py` and `pack_parts` pass the cells.
- Checks: `checks/t2213_islands.py` (Liberty, San Servolo, Fjaderholmarna, IJmuiden's Forteiland, Amsterdam),
  `checks/t2421-liberty.mjs` (tree), `notes/T-2424/zone_check.py` and `ferry_pairs.py`.

## Sizing (scratch runs)

- 34 places round Lake Ontario, Detroit, Ireland, Kutch, the Gulf of Finland and the Chesapeake: zone detours at
  10% everywhere were 46% of zones even at 200-400 km (road quality, not only water); a 30-place random sample put
  the world at about 40 MB with every pair, 7 MB under 400 km. The water condition barely changes watery regions.
- Threshold against carwater's test (zone pairs, km ratio, model / land-aware path, 10%, median, 90%):

  | kept where off by | Toronto - Buffalo | Toronto - St Catharines |

  | none kept (the centres') | 0.80 1.01 1.29 | 0.83 1.07 1.39 |
  | 10% | 0.94 1.00 1.08 | 0.93 1.00 1.09 |
  | 8% | 0.95 1.00 1.07 | 0.93 0.99 1.06 |

- Limits of the separable product: Baltimore - Delmarva 0.52 0.98 1.03 (pairs across the bay far from the
  bridge get the product of two detours, not their own); Detroit - Windsor-Essex 0.72 0.97 1.24; Bhuj - the
  Jamnagar side 0.67 0.81 1.02, where the reference land path cuts across the Little Rann (land, no roads) and the
  routed path goes round on roads, so the reference is the one off.

## Results

- The table (16:03): 679,457 pairs, 2,507 with a car ferry (the old any-water layer: 9,960), 2,683,704 zone detours
  in 45,700 groups (10,035 dense), 11.51 MB (old 2.81; gzipped 6.66 against 1.71). At `--zone-q 6` (10%) it would be
  8.13 MB with Toronto - Buffalo at 0.94-1.08. Build: about 11 core-hours (28 min on 4, then 77 min on 7), against
