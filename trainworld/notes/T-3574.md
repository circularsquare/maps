# T-3574 and T-3575 Terminal, passing tracks and crossovers for tram

Anita, 2026-11-17: "yeah lets do the terminal" (T-3574); "my gut is yes if its how trains work" (T-3575).

## As built (agent tramterm, tree `.tw-trees/tramterm`)

- Station panel (`ui/inspector/StationUpgrades.tsx`): a tram stop shows Terminal and Passing tracks (and the existing
  Waiting room, Passage). Park and ride stays rail's. The complex list's terminal button shows for tram stops too.
- Prices (`track/upgrades.rs`): `price` and `cost` take a `TrackKind`; a tram stop's km is at the tram kind's level
  table, rail and high speed as before. `Network::node_price_kind` picks the kind (high speed if any of its track is,
  else its first edge's). Off-street tram at ground has the same 0.5 as rail, so the terminal is US$37.5M, the same as
  rail; on a street US$7.5M; at +2 US$82.5M (rail 112.5 at +2 is not the same table).
- Turning 15 to 30 an hour needed no change: `capacity::turn_places` reads the terminal bit for any station.
- Crossovers: `Network::set_edge` drops tram's refusal (high speed's stays); `crossover_op` gives a tram crossover the
  tram kind and pairs only tracks of the same family; `cost::crossover_cost` prices it at the kind's table, used by
  `Network::edge_price`. At ground off street it equals rail's (US$18.75M, both diagonals).
- Player-drawn crossing track: it needed no change. A tram edge between two tram doubles was never refused; only the
  flagged crossover edge (made by `add_crossover`, a check and save path, no player tool) was. The check shows the
  same layout on tram and rail behaves alike.
- Docs: track-and-service.md (upgrades, crossovers, prices), ui-building.md (Crossovers), ui-inspectors.md.
- Changelog c113, c114. No strings added or changed; the hovers still say "trains" at tram stops (follow-up: tram
  wording means touching all twelve languages).

## Repro for a checker

1. `cargo test --release --lib tram_` and `--lib passing_tracks_price` in `trainworld/sim` (prices, 15 to 30, crossover
   kind, price and save round trip); full suites `cargo test --release` and `cargo test --release --features demand --lib`.
2. `node trainworld/checks/t3574-tram-terminal.mjs http://localhost:<port>/` on a Vite of the tree (about 2 minutes).
   Reads the tram stop panel, terminal hover ($37.5M), builds it, a rail station's four upgrades and $37.5M, then on
   tram and on rail: two double tracks, a stop on each, no line before crossing track, crossing track drawn by pointer,
   a line over both stops runs; last, `previewCrossover` on a tram quad and a rail quad (both $18.75M).
3. By hand: Track tool, Tram, Double, draw 2 km, place two stops, Construct, press a stop: Terminal and Passing tracks
   are in the row. Try to break it: a tram stop on single track (one-track stop has neither), a stop on street tram
   (price 7.5), water, a station with tram and rail beside, undo, save and load with a tram terminal, passing tracks
   on tram (needs double track), a tram quad crossover then Rebuild, set single, change type to rail or high speed
   (crossover pieces are refused as before).
