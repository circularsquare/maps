# T-537: generated config and a generic OSM outline (pipeline stage 5)

2026-10-16. Design: notes/T-535.md. The rules are in docs/adding-a-city.md 3 "A planned city".

## What it is

- `pipeline/plan_config.py`: a world plan row to a `CITIES` config; `build_city.py` adds every
  planned row (515) after the hand-made entries, which keep their ids (no clash today).
- `pipeline/osm_units.py`: the outline from the OSM admin relations matching the row's units.
- `world_plan.py` rows now carry `iso2` (Natural Earth); a rerun changed nothing else.
  `world_plan.py row <pack> <out.json>` plans one pack's metro as if the pack did not exist.
- Two small hooks: tier0 resolves a callable `job_blend`, places.py a callable `latin_names`.

**The done criterion (within 5%) is met on the shared area and missed over the whole packs**, and
the whole difference is the plan's extent, not this stage: Ikorodu (0.77M on the 1 km grid) and
Epe (0.24M) lie outside GHSL's 2015 functional urban area for Lagos (0.1% and 0.7% of their people
inside it), so T-536's half-the-people rule leaves them out, and Ewekoro (57% inside) comes in.
The hand pack's jobs outside the shared area are 7.0% of its total, the new pack's 1.6%. Followed
up as T-556.

## What nigeria.py did that the generic outline does differently

- Units by state membership (admin_level 4 containing a unit's point) plus named extras: replaced
  by matching each plan unit to its best OSM relation at any level.
- Clipped to the coastline, slivers dropped, holes filled: kept as rules. Matching itself is done on
  land less inland water too: on the coastline-clipped shapes Lagos Island and Lagos Mainland
  matched nothing (0.03 and 0.47), since OSM's Lagos Island relation is 161 km2 of mostly lagoon
  and geoBoundaries' polygon stops at the shore. The outline keeps the lagoon, as nigeria.py did.
- Nothing nigeria.py did is lost. New: a cut along existing packs, and outline pieces with nobody
  in them dropped (one 0.08 km2 sliver here).

## Costs

The Nigeria extract: download 2 minutes, admin relations 3 minutes, inland water 7 minutes,
features 8.5 minutes (on two cores); the build itself then about 1 minute. The .pbf was deleted;
its caches (`data/raw/osm/africa_nigeria.*`, 124 MB) are kept.
