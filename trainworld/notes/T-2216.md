# T-2216 Ground-only solve for island pairs: design (islanddesign, 2026-11-20)

Design only; nothing built. Earlier design: notes/T-2207.md 1, 3, 6. Scripts (read-only, scratch, not kept):
`islanddesign/rows.py`, `pairs.py`, `sado.py`, `zoneparts.py`, `bound.py`, `top.py`, `roadcheck.py`. Advisor called once
before settling: it pointed out that simply dropping `has_alt` gives a ferry nobody (checked: 0.000), and split the
zone parts off into step 2.

## For Anita, in short

- Trips between places with no road or flight between them (Vancouver - Victoria, Singapore - Batam, Tenerife - Gran
  Canaria) get worked out once a line joins them, with your ferry or train as their only way. Today none ride, and
  the city panel and bubbles count them as car trips. After this they show "No route" even before any line is built.
- **Cut-off (my pick, A)**: transit is compared with driving a bridge that isn't there (the usual long drive: 15 min
  plus 1.25 x the straight line at 85 km/h, fuel, parking). All ride up to 3 times its cost, none from 4 times. It's
  the rule Sado's and Salamis's local trips have had since 2026-11-03, so there's no new number.
- By hand, for riders near the ports (stage 1 measures it): the default boat costs 2.3-2.6 times that drive, so all
  ride. Vancouver - Victoria keeps every rider up to about a $48 fare, none past $114; Seattle - Victoria $64 and
  $142 (the real Clipper: about $100-150).
- **B**: the same cut-off as a smooth curve ("stay home" at 3.5x). Nearly the same numbers, one more constant.
  **C**: a tighter 2-3x. The default boat would carry 36-65%, nearer real ferries, but islands would get a rule of
  their own.
- Trip counts ignore water, so Vancouver - Victoria has 103,000 longer trips a day against BC Ferries' 62,000 on all
  routes; port reach and full boats cut it. Cost: nothing until a line crosses water, at most 1.1% after; under 3 MB.
- **Step 2, yours to approve**: 16.3M people live on islands inside another island's or the mainland's area (Sado
  inside Niigata, many in Indonesia and the Philippines). They drive across today. After step 2: "No route" until a
  line reaches them.

## How it works

### Where "fixed, never solved" is decided today

- `PairRule::has_alt` (`sim/src/demand/intercity.rs`): `car || plane`. `r.car` is `p.road == q.road`, the two
  places' world landmass (`pair_ways`, same file). A plane needs both places 250 km or more apart and with airports.
- `IntercityApi::solvable` and `solved_pairs` (`intercity/setup.rs`, `intercity/api.rs` lines ~399 and ~426) leave
  out every zone pair whose place pair fails `has_alt`. Those trips stay in the totals and are solved by no mode.
- The trip explorer's answer (`intercity/market_trip.rs`) gives T-3632's `noAlt` code for them. The panel shows "No
  road or flight joins these two places, so for now none of their trips go by transit." The main thread's copy
  (`app/src/game/marketRule.ts` `corridorOf`) already prices the imagined drive (`refMin`, "the car's as though a
  road crossed the water").
- **Commutes and outings between zones are not left out.** The `cpairs`/`epairs` loops in `set_served` don't check
  landmass. A commute between two landmasses is solved today on the ratio curve against the imagined drive
  (`pass.rs` ~556, `ratio_share(rail_c, cp.car)`). The rest is counted as car. That is why T-3435's Vancouver -
  Nanaimo ferry got 424 riders, all of them commuters. These are T-2207's "631,000 commutes" (now 661,700 a day). They
  are solved already, but with a car that can't exist.

### What rail is compared with when there is no car or plane

The logit's other choice is missing. If `has_alt` were simply dropped, the no-car, no-plane branch of `PairRule::alt`
would give `rest: 1` at the imagined drive's cost, an ordinary logit against a car that can't go. The ferry would
then get almost nobody. With the market's damped beta, the default boat's share comes out 0.000-0.001 on every
example below. That would not fix T-3431, so the rule has to be the one Anita asked for: a point past which nobody
goes.

**Option A (recommended): the local island rule, `Params::bound_share`.** For a zone pair on two landmasses with no
plane, each income group's rail share is `bound_share(rail_c, alt.car)`. `alt.car` is the imagined drive at the
group's minutes a dollar, which `alt()` already returns and which the damping already uses. The share is 1 up to
`ratio_fade_lo` (3) times that drive, falls linearly to 0 at `ratio_fade_hi` (4), and the rest is "No route". It
reuses the ratio curve's own fade, the numbers Anita approved for local trips (DECISIONS 2026-11-03, T-2214). It adds
no factor: a mode is unavailable (Biogeme's availability conditions, MATSim's link modes; notes/T-2207.md 3).
Commutes and outings between zones on two landmasses swap `ratio_share` for `bound_share` and lose the car. Outings
lose their walkers too, which is what the local layer does between parts.

**Option B: a smooth cut-off.** The market's own logit against a "stay home" choice priced at 3.5 times the imagined
drive. At the damped beta it gives the same plateau (0.97 at 3 times, 0.02 at 4 times for a 100-minute drive) with a
soft edge. It adds the 3.5 and breaks the match with the local rule. Not recommended.

**Option C: a tighter fade, 2-3 times.** The same rule with lower numbers, for islands only or for both layers.
Today's default boat would take 36-65% of the example crossings instead of all of them, and a 50 km/h boat
(T-3435's proposal) would take all. This pulls the volume nearer to real ferries, given the gravity's blindness to
water. Two island rules would be a complication; one changed rule changes Anita's 2026-11-03 call.

**The examples, by hand** (`bound.py`: one zone pair at the ports, one income group. Money is at about 1.5
perceived minutes a dollar and the fare at 1.75 a curve dollar. The sim weighs both at the group's one weight, so
this is rough, and Vancouver - Victoria at 2.64 is only 12% under 3. Stage 1 measures it properly. Assumed: a 40-minute walk or feeder at both ends together, the default boat
at 32 km/h every 20 minutes, a starting fare of about $2.50 + $0.20 a km; perceived minutes):

| Crossing | Imagined drive | Ferry at default (on board, fare) | Ratio | A share | A: fare before any loss | A: fare at none | C share | Plain logit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seattle - Victoria | 137 | 350 (251, $29) | 2.55 | 1 | $64 | $142 | 0.45 | 0.000 |
| Vancouver - Victoria | 115 | 302 (211, $24) | 2.64 | 1 | $48 | $114 | 0.36 | 0.000 |
| Tenerife - Gran Canaria | 109 | 257 (172, $20) | 2.35 | 1 | $61 | $123 | 0.65 | 0.000 |
| Singapore - Batam | 51 | 121 (57, $8) | 2.35 | 1 | $28 | $57 | 0.65 | 0.001 |
| Sado (Ryotsu) - Niigata | 79 | 200 (125, $16) | 2.54 | 1 | $36 | $82 | 0.46 | 0.000 |

At 50 km/h every ratio is 1.8-2.0. The fare limit is the point Anita asked for ("a certain point at which transit is
just not taken even if its the only option"). Under A, fares between the starting fare and the limit cost no riders.
That is an island monopoly, the same as Sado's local trips today. A rider pushed off a full boat stays home, as
between local parts.

**With no network** the solve doesn't change, but what is shown does. Today the city inspector's inter-city bar
and the bubbles count these trips as **car**: `CityInspector.tsx` takes car as all minus rail, walk and plane, and
`lm.none` there is only the local layer's no-way trips (T-2214). Unserved commutes and outings between two
landmasses also count as car. After step 1 they all show as "No route". The sim must then give each place its
no-way trips, the longer trips' rest where the pair has no car or plane, beside `air`, and the app must show them
(stage 3). The old done-when said the same: "unreachable with no network". **With a line across** they ride, up to
the cut-off.

The 1,487,000 counts only pairs under 250 km, because it comes from the rows' `near` lists. Pairs 250 km or more apart
on two landmasses where one end has no airport fall under the same rule. They aren't counted here, since the rows
don't list far pairs' trips. Stage 1 counts them from the sim.

### Market zones on islands inside another place (step 2)

T-2213 wrote each 6 km market zone's land parts into every `data/packs/<id>.market.json` (`zone_parts`, `site_part`
and the pack's `parts` table: people, jobs, open, world landmass). The sim doesn't read them yet (no match in
`sim/src`); its `ZoneIn` just ignores the fields. So no data run is needed, only the reader. One check before step
2: the published copies on R2 must carry the fields too.

The rule, as T-2213 and T-2214 defined it: a zone's part is its main part (`zone_parts[z][0]`). Two zones are joined
when they are on the same part of one pack, or on two open parts on one world landmass. A zone pair is **apart**
when its places are on two landmasses (step 1) or its zones are not joined. An apart pair has no car, keeps a plane
where the place pair has one, and otherwise takes the bound.

Scan of all 4,292 zone files (`zoneparts.py`): 5.90 billion people in market zones, **16.3M in zones off their
pack's main land**. The largest: Patikul 982,000, Basanti 657,000 and Pathar Pratima 434,000
(Sundarbans islands), Muna 437,000, Halmahera Selatan 433,000, Flores Timur 424,000, Kota Tarakan 420,000, Breves
384,000 (Marajo); Sado (51,000) and Hawaii's are among them. 1.9M live on a part that isn't their zone's
main one, and they keep their zone's answer. That is the lie of taking each zone's main part, as against T-2214's
per-cell slots, and it is small enough at 6 km. Their trips with their own mainland and its landmass are solved with
a car today. After step 2 they are "No route" until a line reaches them. That is a visible change in Indonesia and
the Philippines, so it goes to Anita. Another lie: a Sado zone's flights still start with the place's drive to
Niigata airport, over the water.

This step also does T-2256's regional half (commutes and outings across zone lines between two parts), with the same
reader and the same swap. T-2256 would keep only the selection's far-end bubbles.

### Cost

- **Solve time.** Pairs are only built between zones that stations on one track network serve. With no line across
  water, nothing changes. Step 1 should solve the real save to the bit, since none of its lines should join two
  landmasses (stage 1 checks it). Step 2 should move it only by Shelter and Fishers Islands. A save that bridges every island pair adds at most their share
  of the longer trips' pairs, 1.1%, each priced as an ordinary pair. The bound costs no more than the logit.
- **Memory.** Step 1: nothing per pair. One more figure per period for the corridors' "No route" part, if the
  corridor tables don't already leave it as the rest. Step 2: one u16 part and an open flag per market zone, 1.39M
  zones in the world, so under 3 MB with every place open and a few hundred KB in a usual game. Reading `zone_parts`
  adds a little to a market pack's open.

### Would a player see something wrong?

- A ferry to an island fills, often to the boats' room. That reads as a busy island ferry, as Vashon's did in T-2214
  (3 times the real riders). The water-blind gravity (notes/T-2207.md 5, "Too many island trips") is the cause. It's
  a separate question, so it isn't fixed here. A water term in the gravity would be a new factor.
- Under A, a player can raise an island ferry's fare to about 2-3 times the starting fare before losing anyone. Real
  long ferries charge about that (Clipper $100-150, Jumbo 1,990 yen for 116 km).
- Nothing changes for pairs that have a car or a plane.
