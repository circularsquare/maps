# T-154: compact intercity demand display

Anita asked to make the longer-trip bubbles less dominant and the focused-city connection list
denser. The bubble radius scale is halved at both detailed and regional zooms. Area remains
proportional to trips, so this reduces every bubble's displayed area to one quarter without
changing relative values. The detailed busiest-zone cap therefore falls from 0.6 to 0.15 of a
6 km zone's area, and the regional maximum falls from 38 px to 19 px before the size slider.

The city panel shows each connection as train trips / all trips, uses a 5 px bar, and removes the
visible percentage legend. The full train/other counts and percentages remain in the row tooltip
and bar accessibility label.

## Check

The combined production WASM/TypeScript/Vite build passed after the concurrent station renderer
landed. Served browser review confirmed five compact rows with `3/133k trips`-style values, 5 px
bars, no visible percentage legend, and expanded train/other counts and percentages retained in
the accessible row labels. The reduced map scale is shared by the detailed and regional renderers;
their constants preserve square-root sizing and therefore area proportionality.
