## How a solve reaches the line panels today

Worker (`workers/demand.worker.ts`), one job per network:

1. `editOf` (about line 1967): a cluster the edit touched gets a local solve when its market part and untouched cities
   still hold their last solve, unless the touched populations took 35% or more of its passes (`LOCAL_SHARE_MAX`).
2. `localSolve` (about 2502): the touched populations only, **period after period** (`for (const q of mine)`), each
   through its four rounds on the others' loads as each round was priced (`trail`), then the capacity fit; the market
   window's pairs after the rounds. Only when all periods are done is one result per period sent, marked
   `cluster.local` (about 2652).
3. It waits for `whole` (the page sends it 8 s after the local results, `EDIT_SETTLE_MS`), then the whole solve:
   `setUp` puts every population of the cluster on the new network, which drops its `trail` and held state
   (`disturbed`, about 440; the comment at about 2666: no later edit can be solved locally until the whole solve ends).
   The whole solve goes **round after round over all periods** (`for round { for q }`, about 2711), so no period is
   final before the last round. The zone in view's cluster may send a free-flow preview (round 0) for new lines
   (`sendPreview`, T-1533); clusters solved before the last go as cluster results; the final as the job's results.

Page (`workers/demandClient.ts`):

4. `receive` (about 3907) keys cluster results by cities (local apart) and publishes a round only once **every period**
   of it is in (`res.every(Boolean)`).
5. `publishCluster` (5415) merges the cluster's lines, stations and zones into `demandByDay` (`game/demand.ts`; the
   panels and the map read `demandView`, `cityDemandViews`), keeping a line shown at other fares (`takesMarketless`,
   T-3303); `linesSettled` clears the lines' "calculating..." label; a local result starts `wholeGo` after 8 s and lets
   a held network go (T-3616). Round 0 only reaches new lines (`publishNewLines`, T-324).
6. `firm` (2140) holds any newer network on the page from the whole solve's start to its final (T-1734); `flush`
   (2070) sends it then. While held, `refineAside` (3395) starts a one-worker focus pool (`demandFocus.ts`) that
   solves the zone in view alone on the newest network; `publishRefine` (3656) shows its zone figures and bubbles, and
   only lines with no figures at all (`withZone`, T-2793).

## Ways to show lines in seconds

### A. Period by period from the main pool's own solve (recommended)

The local solve sends each period's result as it finishes, morning peak first; the page shows lines and the edited
zone with the solved periods' change in and the rest as shown. Where there is no local stage (skipped, as on the
New York save; impossible, as after a whole solve cut short), the edited cluster's whole solve goes period after
period instead of round after round and sends each period's final as it lands, shown the same way.

- Memory: none. CPU: none extra if the loop order changes no figure (periods share no rider); a few more result
  messages and page merges (main thread: measure, each merge of a big save's lines is likely 0.1-0.3 s).
- When: one period of the local solve is 6-8 s on NA's New York, under 2 s on Seoul; one period of the New York save's
  whole solve about a third of its 62 s.
- How close: each period shown is the local (or final) figure for that period. The unsolved periods stay as shown, or
  get the solved period's change in proportion (same demand level at least); which is closer is for stage 1 to
  measure. A player sees the edited line's riders move in up to five steps over 30-50 s, then a small step at the
  final (under 1% per line on these runs).
- What could go wrong: mixing periods across a stops change (the shown periods are on the old route nodes; carry by
  line, as `lineLoadsFor` does, not by node); `takesMarketless` and the window's market delta per period; bubbles stay
  at the local result (their sums are per round), so the zone's figure and its bubbles disagree for half a minute;
  the whole solve's period order must change no figure, the T-1538 preview, T-1543 parts and T-1607 market-apart
  periods included (check by hash).
- Rules it bends: T-232/T-324 (only finals replace finals) further than T-1733 already does; record the lie in
  DECISIONS.md. T-2793 untouched (no zone-alone figures go onto lines).

### B. Aside zone solved on the shown network too (the task's recorded recommendation)

The T-2605 focus worker solves the edited zone alone on the shown network and on the new one; lines show their
shown riders plus the difference.

- Measured on NA, New York alone, Line 6 34/30/8 against 35/31/9: Line 6 moves about 1,300 riders where the main
  pool's change is about 17,000, and Line 6a about 7,800 where the final's is about 16,400. Alone, New York's lines
  carry none of the market's or other zones' riders, so more trains relieve no crowding: a train edit on a busy line
  reads about ten times too small. One sample, but the cause is general to big zones.
- Each aside took 30-39 s for its four rounds (open 2-3 s, round 0 at 4-6 s), so old and new together are no faster
  than the local result; it slowed the main pool's local solve from 42 to 49 s on 4 cores. Memory: one more worker
  with the zone fine (102 MB of WASM at its last report, not a peak; T-2495 put a focus worker at about 0.1 GB). None
  on phones or tablets (no focus pool there). It would revise T-2793.
- Kept only as a fallback; not planned.

### C. Aside crowding on the main pool's loads

B, but the main worker sends the touched cluster's frozen loads (its `trail`) to the aside, which solves the touched
zones on them period by period. Close to the local result, less the market window's change. It is the only one that
helps an edit held behind a whole solve, since the main worker is busy then. Costs B's memory plus the trail's copy
(not measured), one more core, and the most code (trail transfer, its route nodes carried to the new network, the
window left out): 6-10 h. Later, if held edits still matter once A and T-3925 are in.

## Recommended: A, in stages

**Stage 1. Local results period by period** (2-3 h). Worker: `localSolve` sends a period's result as soon as that
period is done (and its window share), morning peak first; the last one as today, so `wholeGo`, T-3616's chain and
the "calculating..." label behave as now (the label clears at the last). Page: `receive` publishes a local cluster
result per period; a merge puts each solved period's change per line (and per station, and the touched zones'
figures) onto the shown day; unsolved periods as shown, or scaled, both tried. Measured at the **first step**, since a
line's whole change is only 0.4-1.0% of its riders (so "within 1% of the final" holds before the edit too), on NA
(Line 6 trains +1, a station on Line 6) and asianita (Line 1a +1). Done when:
- the lines in view first move within 12 s of the edit (Seoul within 4 s);
- the first step shows at least half of the edited line's final change (Line 6: 9,000 of 17,900);
- no other line in view moves at any step by more than its final change plus 0.3% of its riders (today's local result
  already overshoots: E +7,748 against +574 final, line 4 -4,597 against -67; with five steps that could show five
  times);
- the final is the same to the bit with the stage's `?perfOff=` flag on, and no merge is a long task over 200 ms.
The same two numbers (share shown, wobble) pick "as shown" or "scaled" for the unsolved periods.

**Stage 2. Whole solve period by period where there is no local stage** (3-4 h). The edited cluster's (the zone in
view's first) whole solve after an edit runs period after period and sends each period's final; the page merges them
as in stage 1. Covers the New York save (local skipped) and any edit with no local stage (a re-levelled zone, a solve
cut short). Stop rule: if the period-first order changes any figure (hash of every line's riders, boardings and loads,
and the totals, against round-first in the same game), the stage stops and reports why rather than accepting a
drift. Timing by paired runs in one game, flag on and off alternately (the same Line 6 edit took 228 s here and 296 s
in the survey, so single runs cannot judge "no later"). Done when the New York save's lines in view first move within
25 s of a line 1 edit (from 62 s), its final is within the paired runs' spread, and every figure is the same.

**New York misses "seconds".** The New York save gets about 20-25 s from stage 2, not seconds: one period of its
whole cluster is the floor of anything exact here, and its city is three quarters of the cluster, so there is little
to freeze. Closing it takes cheaper crowding rounds (T-3513) or one period split over both workers (as T-1543 does
for the free-flow round), each a task of its own.

**Stage 3, only if wanted after measuring**: C for edits held behind a whole solve past T-3925's cut-off.

Gain: lines in view move in 3-12 s on the big saves' cities (from 10-51 s), about 20 s on the New York save (from
62 s), at no memory. The main risk: stage 2's loop order changing a figure or memory peak (skims per period), and on
the player's side, more visible steps in a line's riders.

## T-3925 (a newer edit restarts a half-done calculation)

- They fight here (read from the code, not measured with T-3925): a whole solve puts the edited cluster's populations
  on its network at its start and drops their trail, so a restarted solve gets no local stage, and its lines wait for
  the restarted whole final (about 210-230 s on NA). Today's held path showed them at 136 s in my one run (an edit
  about 60 s into the whole solve: the rest of that solve, 107 s, then its own 27 s local solve). An edit early in a
  whole solve would wait about as long either way; one later, nearer T-3925's half-way cut-off, comes out worse after a
  restart. The totals come sooner either way (T-3925's goal: about 340 s after the first edit against 560).
- Stage 2 removes the conflict without touching T-3925's logic: a restarted solve is a solve with no local stage, so
  its cluster in view goes period by period, and the first period's final would show at about a fifth of the whole
  (derived: about 46 s on NA, the 249-population cluster solved exactly for that period).
- Order: T-3925 lands first (being built). Stage 1 touches only the local stage and the page's merge; stage 2 then
  starts from T-3925's code and measures two edits 60 s apart on NA (the second's lines within about 50 s of it).
  Both tasks touch `firm`, `flush` and `wholeGo` in `demandClient.ts`: stage 1 leaves them as they are; stage 2 adds
  only the per-period sends. For T-3925's agent: measure the second edit's lines in view as well as the final, and
  expect them later than today for an edit well into a whole solve, until stage 2.
- Keeping the trail through a cut-short whole solve (double-buffered, swapped when a period completes) would give the
  restarted solve a real local stage, but the frozen populations' own figures are gone too, so the local result would
  have to be made from loads alone: more code than stage 2 for the same gap. Not planned.
