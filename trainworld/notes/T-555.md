2026-10-16. The rule is in docs/adding-a-city.md 3 "A planned city", "Country keys"; what was left
out and why in DECISIONS (two T-555 lines).

## What changed

- `plan_hooks(row)` in england, france, germany, brazil, canada (keys) and china, india, korea,
  nigeria (`{}`, docstring saying why). Japan has none: no planned rows.
- `plan_config.config` = `global_config` then `with_country_keys`. `planned()` leaves the merge to
  `build_city.Cities` (CITIES), which merges on a config's first lookup: country modules import
  build_city, so importing one while build_city loads (nocar.py importing england first, say)
  found it half loaded.
- france: NUTS-3 to département by name against geo.api.gouv.fr (101 of 101 match). germany:
  NUTS-3 to Kreis by VG250's `NUTS` column (400 of 400). brazil: tracts of the plan municipalities'
  states, kept by representative point in the outline; Brasilia's unit gives 5,418 tracts, 2,817,381
  people, the census's DF total exactly. canada: `ct_sectors` without the Ontario filter.
- nocar.py and everyday_rates.py take a planned pack's country from its row; England's LSOA cache
  by pack id, not London's.
