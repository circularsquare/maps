# T-4144 Every kind of edit shows at once, planned together (agent editplan, 2026-11-23)

**Summary for Anita**
- Every edit goes through one of six paths in the worker. Almost all of them take the slowest path, which rebuilds the whole game's lists and drawn network. So their wait depends on the size of the save, not on the edit. On your Asia save that is 0.5-1.5 s for anything from a colour change to deleting a station. On New York it is 0.15-1.0 s.
- The worst waits on Asia, before anything is shown: deleting a station on a line, 0.7-1.5 s. A new line from the Lines tab, 0.6-2.2 s. Copying a line, 1.1-1.9 s. Placing a station, 0.8-1.5 s; the ghost box stays where you clicked, but the station itself is only drawn after that. Construct, 0.7-0.9 s (once 5.5 s). Deleting built track, 0.65-1.2 s. A line's colour or settings, 0.55-1.0 s. Already at the click: stop circles, a line's new path (T-4114, landed today), a deleted line, blueprint track removed, train counts and headways in the panel, and the track ghost.
- The plan has two halves. One makes every edit draw itself at the click. The other makes the checked version arrive in about a third of the time for every edit, including the ones nobody draws ahead. There is a short shared stage first (3-4 h), then five streams that can run at the same time on different files.
- Two things you would see differently need your call. Something you placed can disappear again with the red flash and a notice, where today the refusal comes before anything shows. And money comes off at the click, from the price shown, and goes back if the edit is refused.
- Advisor called once, on this plan. It changed the "while trips calculate" figures (that condition could not be reached here) and the base: no new stamp, the worker's message order is enough.

## 2. The shared mechanism

What exists today, five separate pieces: the track tool's placed stand-ins (renderer `addPlaced`, dropped by the next network), the station tool's ghost marker (`addPending`/`dropPending`), the track hider for blueprint removal (`hideEdge`), the stop markers' pending tier (T-3159), and the panels' pending signals (`pendingTrains`, `pendingCars`, `pendingHeadway`, `pendingWeekend`). T-4114 added a sixth, `lineAhead`: a line drawn on a new set of edges until the network holding its edit.

**Can lineclick's version be the base as it stands? Not quite.** It is the right shape for lines, and the worker's word (`linePath`, sent right after apply) is the right idea for everything. Four things first:
1. It is keyed by line, not by edit. A refusal drops only an unanswered guess, so a refused later edit to the same line cannot be told from an earlier one that was answered.
2. The shader hides at most four lines (`u_hide_lines` is a vec4; `buildAhead` stops at 4). A station delete or a Construct touching five lines would draw both stroke sets for the fifth.
3. It draws only line strokes. Stations, track pieces (built, hidden, level, track count) and colours need the same kind of override.
4. Its word covers the line-path ops only.

**The base (stage 0):**
- **The worker's word.** Right after every accepted apply the worker sends one `applied` message for that seq, with whatever the edit kind can say cheaply. Line paths are lineclick's `lineEdges`, generalised to any lines the edit changed. Later, the pieces and stations it touched. It is built in `workers/clockWord.ts`, which clock.worker.ts calls once, with one file per kind so streams don't share one.
- **No new stamp on states.** Message order already says what a state holds. Every state the worker sends after an edit's word was built after that apply: `later()` starts any state begun before an edit again, and `standAside` sends its rows before the next edit is applied. So an entry is armed by its word. Its map part clears with the first network taken after the word, its panel part with the first rows or full state after it. That is lineclick's rule, written down once in docs/architecture.md and checked in dev builds.
- **A pending list on the page** (`game/pending.ts`). An entry holds the edit's seq, what it draws (line edges and look, hidden edges, edges drawn as built, level, track count, a station box, a line colour) and what the panels show (fields by line or station, the cash). It is made at the click from what the page can tell, armed by the worker's word (which replaces the guess when they differ), cleared as above, and dropped by seq when that seq is refused. A guess never answered goes after 15 s, as lineclick's does. Edits that depended on a refused one are refused by the worker and dropped by their own seq.
- **Renderer overrides for any number of records.** A per-line texture replaces the vec4: hidden, drawn ahead, colour override. A per-edge texture adds hidden, drawn as built, level and track count. Station stand-ins are drawn like the placed track stand-ins, over the cached layer (T-4136's `ghostPass`). Streams then write data and never touch renderer.ts or the shaders.
- **Panels read through one helper**, `shown(record, field)`, which gives the pending value or the world's. The four pending signals move onto it.
- **Undo** (stage 0b, same agent after stage 0): undo names the seq it undoes, so it never undoes an earlier edit when the newest is still pending and then refused (T-4097 section 3). When nothing has been edited since, it shows at the click from the network and world the page held before that edit.

## One path (T-4170, agent editunify, 11-24)

Every edit shown at its click now goes through one hook point, the same way for every stream.

- **The hook: `pendingSent` in game/pending.ts**, which clockClient's `edit` calls once for every edit it sends, with its seq.
  It is the one place every edit passes (the panels, the tools, `tw.client.edit` from probes, undo), and the only place an
  entry gets a seq, so an edit has at most one entry by construction. Before, there were two hook points: `ahead().send()`
  (lines, stations) and T-4148's `aheadEdit` inside game/world.ts `edit()`. A caller that wrapped an op the world.ts hook
  also guessed got two entries, the outer one never keyed and drawn for 15 s; the check's self-test reproduces that.
- **What the entry shows** is read there from the op (game/editGuess.ts, one part per kind of edit, as the worker's word
  has one file per kind): lines in map/lineToolAhead.ts (a stop click's path, a deleted line, a colour, the line panel's
  switches), stations and deletes in map/stationAhead.ts (station or track deleted, platform length, one or both tracks,
  a one-track way, upgrades), Construct and the select tool in game/pieceAhead.ts (moved there from world.ts unchanged),
  passages in game/links.ts. An undo's comes from the network before the edit it takes back, at the same hook.
- **A caller adds only what the op cannot say**, with `ahead(show)` at the click and `.send(() => edit(op))`; it joins the
  same entry: the station tool's ghost box (kept through every spot tried), a BRT box, a free station's box and track, a
  blueprint drag's preview strokes, and a price the panel quoted (upgrades, both tracks, a passage). Callers that had
  built their guess by hand (line panel switches, colour, Delete line, the delete tool, the station panel, passages) now
  just call `edit()`.
- **Arming**: the word or the answer arms the entry, and a part it has nothing for is done at once. Before, a panel
  edit's entry (fields only) kept its map part until a network came, so after an edit answered by rows alone (a rename)
  or a service edit it stayed until some later edit. An empty list no longer counts as something drawn.
- **Refusal and undo** are the base's, unchanged, now for every edit alike: a refused seq's entry goes in the frame the
  answer lands (one notice, from `edit()` or a quiet caller's own); an undo names the edit it takes back.
- **Gone**: the line tool's per-line unkeyed guesses (`guessLine`, `dropLineGuess`, `showAhead`, `dropAhead`, with their
  own replace-by-word and drop-on-refusal rules), world.ts's `aheadEdit`, and the T-2763 hider (`renderer.hideEdge`, the
  `u_hide` uniform), which nothing called since T-4146.
- **Worker side**: already one path (workers/clockWord.ts runs each kind's part; one `applied` per accepted apply, an
  empty word from the side branches). Unchanged.
- **Left on its own path**: the track tool's placed stand-in (T-1407, `addPlaced` and `keepPreviewAsPlaced`), drawn
  after the early answer and dropped by the next network. Drawing it at the click through `ahead()` is stream E's piece
  (T-4097 section 3 piece 2). The T-229 pending signals (train counts, headways, weekends) stay where the base left them.
- **Found**: a copied line drawn ahead is drawn on its source's edges in its source's colour, exactly over the source's
  strokes, so the click's frame looks unchanged; the copy shows as its own band only with the network
  (artifacts/trainworld/t4170/nyc-copy-click.png against nyc-copy-final.png in the tree). The ahead model holds it (1
  line, 1 colour at the click). Drawing it beside its source needs the line bands' layout (map/lineBands.ts, editnetwork's).
- No player strings changed. No changelog item.
