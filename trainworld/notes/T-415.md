# T-415: a level's service figures stay put when another level is edited

## Cause

`TrackWorld::settle_train_counts` (sim/src/track/world.rs) solves every fleet line's trains an
hour from its train counts with a damped Newton loop. It stopped on the largest error over all
three levels, and each pass stepped every level. So an edit at the high level ran passes that
also stepped the medium and low levels, already inside the 1e-4 tolerance: the step was tiny but
moved their f32 trains an hour in the last bits, and with them the capacity holds and hop times
of lines sharing track (lines 4 and 48 on the New York save). Demand (T-124) then saw changed
periods and re-solved them. Later edits looked fine only because the first one had walked the
other levels to a float fixed point. Not the legacy save's timetable conversion: line 0 already
had a fleet when the first measured edit ran.

## Fix

Levels share no capacity (`capacity::solve_one` works level by level), so the loop now decides
per pass which levels are outside tolerance and probes and steps only those. A level inside
tolerance keeps its trains an hour to the bit, so its round trips and hop times are recomputed
from identical inputs and come out identical. Test:
`a_count_edit_at_one_level_leaves_the_others_to_the_bit` (two fleets sharing track; four high
edits; medium and low trains an hour, hop times and round trips equal to the bit; fails on the
old loop).

## Measured

`checks/t124-periods.mjs`, 3 workers, own Vite 6115. Edit to final, seconds:

| | high edits (first, second, third) | every level |
|---|---|---|
| T-124, reuse on | 20.8, 11.4, 11.7 | 22.2, 22.0 |
| T-415, reuse on | 11.5, 11.7, 11.5 | 23.3, 23.2 |
| T-415, reuse off | 21.7, 21.7, 21.4 | 22.5, 22.5 |

The first edit now keeps the medium and low periods (`periods` shows 3 kept, 2 solved, like the
later ones). Hashes at all seven points equal on and off, and equal T-124's runs; the save 7,597,260.8
rail trips a day. Evidence: `../../artifacts/trainworld/t415/`.
