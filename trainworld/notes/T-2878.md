# T-2878: the market window starts from the market part's tables (A2 of notes/T-2830.md)

Agent marketwindow, 2026-11-09, tree `.tw-trees/marketwindow`. Outputs: `../artifacts/trainworld/t2878/`.

## What it does

The 13 s was `set_served` for the window (`solved_pairs` walks every served zone against every zone, and the far
deal needs all of them), not the access lists. A window's pairs are the whole market's with an end in the window, so
`set_network_ll_beside(part, ...)` takes them from `part` (`set_served_from` in `intercity/setup.rs`) when
`same_pairs_as` holds: same zones served, chunk settings and market, the part has no window of its own, and the same
zones joined by track (`same_joins`). A pair's trips, car and band read only the market, so they don't depend on the
network. Anything else deals from nothing as before.

`part` is only borrowed during the call. No field became shared and nothing is kept, so there is no lasting copy.

`same_joins` compares which zones share a component, not the component numbers. Dropping a stop can renumber
components without changing what is joined (the dropLong capture below).

**Still slow:** an edit that really changes which zones are served or joined pays the full rebuild, as today. The far
deal runs over served origin zones in order, so a change there moves other pairs; patching only those would be a
much larger change.
