# T-3801 Edits stuck behind panel polling

Anita, 2026-11-19: "might be worth noting the rename being queued behind stuff for the future. i could imagine like
we build some future feature and it fails like this. so we should be aware of it when adding the next thing that
might fail in the same way. also can check to see if there are any current parallel failures".

## The shape of the problem

The clock worker runs its messages one at a time, in order (`messages` promise chain in `clock.worker.ts`). An edit
waits for everything posted before it and for whatever the worker runs on its own clock. This has been patched three
times for one asker each: T-1815 (panel reads held while previews are asked), T-2766 (reads held 0.5 s after a stop
click), T-3793 (`today` paced in `today.ts`). The general fix is in `ClockClient.query`: one rule for every panel read
that repeats. The rule is in docs/architecture.md "Workers" ("Edits never queue behind polling").

## The rule as built

- `POLL_READS` in `clockClient.ts`: today, linesPeriods, linePeriods, lineAccount, lineHeld, lineDelays, lineWaits,
  lineMarks, lineTrips, stationDeps, lineRelated, linePairs, passingSpots, upgradeCosts, upgradeCost, tracksCost,
  platformCost, junction, flyover, blueprintQuote, blueprintPieces, lineCost. Tool reads (stop tiers, quotes for a
  drawing, removal, one-ways, edge PIs) and lineProfile stay outside: they are asked once, by a player waiting.
- One already-answered poll question in the worker at a time; after each answer, the next waits 2x the worker's own
  time for it (the answer now carries `ms`), at most 3 s. A question never answered before goes at once (a panel just
  opened). `soon` skips the rest and goes first among the waiting ones, but still after a read in flight (today.ts:
  the panel opening, a stop edit's new count; passed through T-2766's held reads). An ask of a question already
  waiting joins it.
- Keys that move with the clock never rest: lineTrips (the Marey window's start, the train table's day) and
  stationDeps (the board's day) get new keys about once a game day or when the view moves.
- Edits, previews and tool reads are never held on the page.
- Waiting reads of the same question share one answer (each caller its own copy for the reads new to the dedup).
- today.ts's own pacing (T-3793) is gone: it would rest twice.
- Tried first and dropped: a rest per question with up to 100 ms of reads queued. Read time barely moved (241 -> 227
  ms/s on the Lines tab): six linesPeriods and today each took their own third of the worker.

## Before and after

Old and new alternated in one loaded game (`__T3801old` flag, since removed), each running the same 8 edits (rename,
rename back, headway, headway back, stop out, stop in, short track, undo) 1 s apart with the clock running. The
backlog is the read time an edit posted at that instant would wait behind, sampled every 10 ms; it and the reads in
flight are the proof. Edit waits (post to the worker starting the edit) are one run, n=16 an arm, and swing between
runs (the Lines tab's old median was 300 in one and 1,507 in the next); at 1 s gaps most of what is left is the
previous edit's full state. No A/B row for the line inspector or the Build tab.

## Not fixed here (bigger than a poll)

- An edit's held full state (network, trips, boarding): 0.6-2.7 s on her save; the next edit within that time waits for
  it. The biggest cause of waits at 1 s gaps.
- The hour turning (`epoch`): 0.14-1.1 s every game hour, 3.75 s apart at speed 2.
- One `today` answer: `past_day` 70-250 ms, 0.5-0.8 s under load. Splitting it means an answer that can mix the state
  before and after an edit: a design call.
- The snapshot timer and the next hour's boarding step: 0.2-0.5 s each, sometimes 1 s.
- Not measured: undo and redo of a rename, `unbindAll` (T-3793's slow-path leads), the junction panel.
