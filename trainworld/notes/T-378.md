## What was built

- **Kernel** (`intercity.rs`): each place's pack carries its airports and, per airport, the places
  it flies to directly; its row the drive to each airport (people-weighted over its zones), the
  direct flights, and its parking. When the market is built every pair of places gets its car
  (one landmass) and its plane (far, both with airports: `air_min`, the best pair of airports), and
  from them the plane's share of the trips not by rail and the weight of every non-rail mode
  (`PairRule`). A served pair's rail share is `sum / (rest + sum)` against its zones' car time (the
  plane's with no car), so a far pair across a sea is now solved where track joins it. New answers:
  `corridor_modes()` (per corridor, plane and car shares of the rest), `elsewhere(q)` (per place,
  trips with places that have no zone), and a selection's last two numbers (the same for it).
- **Pipeline** (`market_zones.py`): airports from OurAirports within 150 km of the zone's centre
  with 1M FR24 passengers a year (FR24 matched by position, so Berlin's BER is FR24's SXF), direct
  flights from FR24 routes of 50,000 a year; `--air` patches only the airports into every existing
  pack (used here, so Tokyo's pack, being rebuilt by another agent, kept its sites). Only the choice
  of airports and the direct flags leave the FR24 file.
- **App**: the worker sends the corridors' shares and each place's elsewhere with a period's result;
  the client splits every corridor's and selection place's trips not by rail into plane and car
  (commutes between zones drive). The selection's and the city inspector's "Inter-region trips"
  show All, then train, car and plane columns under icons (`Icon.train`, `Icon.ride`, a new
  `Icon.plane`), each number with its hover, and an Elsewhere row sorted among the places. The
  bubbles' hovers add the trips by plane; their colours are unchanged (blue train, red the rest).

## The rail-air share curve

`scratchpad` script, the market's own costs: rail door to door with 33 + 20 minutes of access, 17
perceived minutes of waiting (hourly), the in-train time and $1.50 + 10 cents a rail km; air at the
great circle (rail km / 1.15) with 20 km drives at both ends; share of rail in rail plus air.

| Hours on the train | 2 | 2.5 | 3 | 3.5 | 4 | 4.5 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Design (90 min at airports, $60 base) | 99% | 97% | 94% | 89% | 80% | 65% | 47% | 17% |
| Chosen (60 min, $50 base) | 94% | 89% | 79% | 64% | 46% | 29% | 17% | 4% |

(200 km/h on average; 160 and 250 km/h give the same to a point.) The studies (Steer Davies Gleave
2006, Coogan 2012): about 90% at 2 hours, 70% at 3, half near 4, a fifth at 5. One logit at 0.025
a minute cannot be as flat as the studies' curve; the chosen point puts 3 and 4 hours between the
two readings. The kernel test `air_flies_far_pairs_by_place_and_rail_takes_its_share_by_the_curve`
runs it on a 787 km line: 3 hours on the train 93% of rail and air, 6 hours under 30%.

## A fast line on real pairs

A test network in the game could not be built: a new game and the real save both hold about $6B,
and St Pancras to Gare du Nord through the tunnel quotes $35B. So the kernel ran it natively on the
real packs (`real_far_lines_share_rail_with_air`, ignored test): one line, two stations, no
feeders (walks and rides only), every 30 minutes, $1.50 + 10 cents a rail km, free flow, midday.

| Pair, hours on the train | Corridor: rail / car / plane | Rail of rail and plane | Stations' own zones: rail of rail and plane |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| London - Paris, 2.27 h (Eurostar) | 31% / 42% / 27% | 53% | 86% |
| London - Paris, 3 h | 24% / 46% / 30% | 45% | 68% |
| London - Paris, 4 h | 16% / 51% / 33% | 32% | 32% |
| New York - Washington, 2.75 h | 11% / 59% / 30% | 28% | 89% |
| New York - Washington, 3.5 h | 8% / 61% / 31% | 21% | 71% |
| New York - Washington, 4.5 h | 4% / 64% / 32% | 12% | 36% |

Where the line serves both ends the share follows the curve (68-71% at 3-3.5 hours); over the whole
corridor it is lower because one station per city reaches only the zones within a ride of it.
