# T-4294 Stop clicks in Edit stops that sent no edit (agent stopclick, 2026-11-25)

## Cause: the bench was editing the Q's stops, not the 2's

edit_bench opens line 2 from the Lines tab by pressing the row it finds by the name "2" (checks/lib/actions.mjs
`openLine`). The list sorts by riders and its rows have no key (ui/LinesTab.tsx `lineRow`), so when it sorts again
Preact keeps each `li` where it is and puts another line in it. On New York's real save the 2 and the Q are neighbours
(789k and 776k riders/day, rows 8 and 9 with trips final). After the bench's earlier edits trips are calculated again,
the two can swap, and the row the bench had found for "2" held the Q by the press. `openLine` waited only for some panel
with text, so Edit stops went on for the Q.

On the Q, the 2's 96 St (station 62, the mid stop the bench presses) is on track the Q cannot reach: in her save the 2 runs
on track of its own (108 of the 109 rail lines have no stop on it). A press there picks that marker, tier "cut", and
`StopMarkers.activate` (map/stopMarkers.ts) shows "96 St is not on track this line can reach. Draw track from the line to
it first." and asks `stopGap`; the gap's frame moves the camera (map/gapFlash.ts, fitBounds). That is the logged "saw
view:, input:pointerdown, query:, view:" with no edit, six times over.

Evidence (game run on the real save, my Vite, play.mjs):
- Both failing runs logged the camera at [-73.9548, 40.6319] zoom 15 after every press. `stopGap` for the Q and station 62
  frames exactly that centre; the B's is [-73.9549, 40.6320], every other line's is elsewhere (the A's [-73.9034, 40.853]).
- The row element that held "2" held "West Hempstead Branch" once the list was sorted by name, and a press on it opened
  West Hempstead Branch.
- Edit stops on the A, a press on station 62: posts `query:stopGap` then `view:`, no edit, and the tag above.
- The ledger has no stop click rows for 5 NYC runs since 11-24 (the 11-24 baseline b7b9e4ee, gpuslots, benchwhy's master
  and pagestage3 runs, T-4291-rowsdelta) and has them for the rest; no landing lines up with them. The baseline's run is
  T-4234's: the same press, after which the camera was far south of 96 St (the gap's frame).

## Could a player hit it?

Not as a stop click: on the line being edited, a click on its own stop takes it out or adds it every time; these presses
were on a station the open line cannot reach, and the game said so. The list part is a player's too, narrowly: a click
begun on a row while the list sorts again opens the line that row holds by then. Filed as T-4306.

## Fix (harness and bench only; no game code)

- checks/lib/actions.mjs `openLine`: waits until the open panel is the named line's (its title), pressing the row again
  up to three times, and otherwise throws naming the panel that is open. The panel read it returns carries `again` (the
  presses that opened another line). Every check that uses it gets this.
- tools/edit_bench.mjs, stop section: says when openLine had to press again; waits for Edit stops to be on line C.line
  (`tools.stops`' line) and otherwise says so and skips the presses; before each press reads what the markers hold there
  (`STOP_WHY`: their line, the station a press there picks and its tier, the mid stop's tier and its marker's distance)
  and prints it when the press sends no edit; "saw" names each question (`query:stopGap`).
