## b. Zone borders: one long zig and one long zag

Each traced border between two zones was fitted with runs at 0, 45 and 90 degrees straying at most 6 pitches, and less
near a station (a pitch clear of it): a border at 20-30 degrees came out as a staircase. Now the fit may stray 24
pitches (`borderTol`), and keeps a way (one run, or one straight and one diagonal) only where:

- no station lies between the traced border and the way (the polygon they make, even-odd), so **every station stays on
  its side**;
- the way passes no nearer a station than a pitch, or than the traced border did;
- the way runs over the land of the zones that border meets (samples half a cell apart, a cell and a half from its ends),
  so it never crosses water or cuts a third zone's corner.
