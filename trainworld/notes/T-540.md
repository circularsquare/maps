# T-540: the runner (pipeline stage 8), with T-579 and T-580

2026-10-16. Design: notes/T-535.md (stage 8).

## Stage A: T-579, a pack with no OSM parks

`enrich_parks.py` failed on Aboh (`abo`, a planned Nigerian row, T-539): OSM has no park areas
there, so the geometry lists were empty Python lists, which shapely's `STRtree.query` takes as a
float array, and past that the 95% coverage check raised on a total draw of 0. Now the queries take
object arrays, and the coverage check runs only when there is draw to place. Such a pack keeps a
zero `park_draw`, which the kernel already reads as no parks (`everyday.rs`, `sum > 0` filter).

Test: Aboh built from its row into `data/work/t540/a/` (T-539's recipe, sim/ from HEAD): 95 s,
`found 0, kept 0`, `park_draw` all zero, `read_pack` OK on cells, commute, gravity and water.

## Stage B: the runner

`pipeline/world_run.py`; how to run it and what each phase does is docs/adding-a-city.md "Running
the pipeline". Choices beyond the brief, and why:

- **Status values** `planned`, `built`, `shipped`, `parked` (plan_config `ROW_STATES`,
  `is_plan_row`). Every script that read "planned" alone (build_city's `CITIES`, nocar's country,
  world_plan's rerun) now takes any of them, so a shipped row keeps its config; a world_plan rerun
  keeps a row's status and `run` record while its units are unchanged.
- **Gate after registration with the market**: the browser check opens the pack in the game, which
  needs a registry entry, a market place and a `.market.json`. A parked pack is taken out again;
  its place stays in `market.places` unused (ids are positions), and the rows are rebuilt.
- **Gating is per region, not per city**: the runner's Vite reads the registry once (dev-nowatch),
  so it starts after the region's rows are registered; one Vite and one rates test a region.
- **After a joint solve the group is gated again** (no browser, no rates: gravity is all that
  changed) against the region's snapshot; a FAIL restores every member's packs, res-9 copies and
  joint files from a backup taken just before, and parks the group's new packs.
- **T-580 by rule**: a new pack within 50 km of a pack with a Northeast `market` id parks before
  its gate, with the reason; US cities are paused, so moving the Northeast onto `--packs` waits
  for a row that needs it.
- **--jobs** is cores in all: one city at a time per 3 cores, each step with that many threads
  (`OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS`, routes' `--workers`).
- **Clean up per region**, once its rows are joined (disk is the limit), not at the batch's end.
  Only caches a run made go (`data/work/world_run/made.json`), so England's caches from London's
  builds stay.
- **Steps in their own process** (`world_run.py step <script> args`, `python -I`): a crash parks
  one row, memory is freed, and ctrl-c (the steps are in their own process group) stops only the
  runner, after the city under way. A failed build step runs once more after a minute.
- **Scratch trees** (`--root`): every pipeline module's paths into data/packs, data/work, app/
  and the plan, and the tree root itself, are rewritten as each module loads (`Redirect`, an
  import hook; function defaults too); osm_units' `PACKS` stays real (the packs an outline is cut
  along). The pipeline scripts themselves did not change for it. `build_city`, `routes`,
  `market_zones` and `zones` now honour `TW_CARGO_TARGET_DIR` as intercity did, and
  `market_zones.py --rows` rebuilds only the market rows.
- **Not in the runner**: `zones.py`'s non-city zones (the plan makes none yet, T-535 decision 5).
- **A step time limit** (`STEP_LIMIT_S`: build 90 minutes, routes 60, gate 90, joint solve 120,
  others 30; four times that for a row over 15M people): the step's process tree is stopped and
  the row parks with the log's last lines, so one stuck build cannot hold a night's batch.

### Found and fixed on the way

- **osm_units stalled on the Netherlands**: Rotterdam's box has 66,387 OSM inland water polygons
  (2.3M vertices); their union took 2 minutes and each admin unit's difference with it far longer
  (killed at 22 minutes). The match now leaves out inland water under 0.1 km2 (`WET_MIN_KM2`;
  294 polygons keep 514 of 665 km2) and clips the water to each shape's box first. Rotterdam's
  outline in 51 s; Owerri's, Aba's and Aboh's units come out identical to before.
- **pack_gate's rates** stopped at the first pack whose everyday layer panics, so every later
  pack read "gave no line"; the packs after it now run again one at a time, and a panic is the
  pack's reason ("the everyday layer does not open: ... empty university inputs").
- **pack_gate's other_packs_unchanged** failed every joint solve: re-merging a member rewrites its
  header's `merged.source` stamp (the res-9 pack's hashes, which hold the new gravity). That stamp
  is now its own hash, which a member of the gated pack's group may change, like the gravity. And
  the re-merge recomputes header shares in another order (Rotterdam's school
  `location_match_share` 0.9999999818245351 to ...352), so the header is now hashed with its
  figures to 9 significant digits.
- **Redirect** first rewrote paths twice (the scratch tree is inside data/work); a path already
  in the scratch tree is now left alone.

## Retrospective (stage C)

1. Backup, sim/ copy, dry run: 5 minutes. 2. Batch: 28 minutes unattended (above). 3. Checking
the result (gate files, files written, registry and plan against the backup, joint reports): 10
minutes, and that is where the fault showed. 4. every-pack-opens and smoke together: 12 minutes
(72 zones at about 10 s). Tokens: the orchestrator's measure.

- **Worth its cost**: the backup (cheap, and London was rewritten as planned); the region table in
  the report. The read of a whole-country extract (Portugal 5.2 of 12.9 minutes) is the biggest
  phase; builds are only 11 of 28 minutes, so more `--jobs` would save little. Reading the next
  region's extract while this one gates would save more (T-592).
- **Not caught by the gate**: a joint solve's cross figure. Proposed T-591: the re-gate after a
  joint solve reads the group's joint report and fails a border pair whose two countries differ
  from the registry's `marketRow.country` (or are equal there but read as a national border).
- **every-pack-opens** at 72 zones is 12 minutes and grows by about 10 s a zone; past a few hundred
  zones, running it on the batch's new zones and their group members only, with smoke for the
  rest, is enough (the gate's browser check already opens each new pack).
- **The browser check serves the main tree's app**, so an app/ edit mid-batch (tonight T-585's WASM
  at 01:21) can change what it opens. Tonight's gates finished before that; for an overnight batch,
  say in its brief which agents may rebuild app/ while it runs.
- **Before the first real batch (T-541)**: T-590 first (else every row next to London, Paris or
  Toronto parks by the guard), then re-solve London and Portsmouth: `merge_cells lon por --undo`,
  `intercity --group auto por --no-market`, `merge_cells lon por`, `pack_gate lon por --no-rust`,
  `register lon por --market --trips` (about 2 minutes).
