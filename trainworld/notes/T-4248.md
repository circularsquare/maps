### Core coordinate choice

The cleanest model is to keep every **node's** stored `x` canonical in `[-PI * R, PI * R)` (longitude in `[-180, 180)`), and **unwrap** each edge's vertices sequentially starting from `node_a` so every leg takes the shortest east-west delta (`dx` wrapped into `[-PI * R, PI * R)`). That means an edge from `179.9` to `-179.9` has `node_a` at `+179.9`, `node_b` stored canonically at `-179.9`, and its derived `Piece` geometry unwrapped from `+179.9` to `+180.1`. (Storing unwrapped `x` on nodes themselves would drift unboundedly if someone built a ring around the Earth.)

Below is every area that breaks today, the simplest fix, and a rough estimate in hours of agent work.

  - **Drawing tool routing in sim**: [draw.rs](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/sim/src/track/wasm/draw.rs#L153-L350) `route_op_as` resolves `ra`, `rb`, lead-ins, tangent arcs, bridges, and `beside` offsets in raw plane `(x, y)`.
- **Simplest fix**
  - In `merc_inside`, check latitude only (`y.abs() <= ymax`), and update `issues.outsideWorld` text.
  - Add a small helper in `frame.rs` (`WORLD_W = 2.0 * PI * EARTH_R`, `wrap_x(x)`, `unwrap_dx(x, ref_x)`). Use `unwrap_dx` in `edge_vertices`, `route_op_as` (unwrap `rb` and `pis` relative to `ra`), `derive_node`, and `find_crossings`.

  - In `cost.rs`, wrap `lon` in `ClassGrid::at`; wrap `x` into `[-W/2, W/2)` in `MaskSet::is_water`; in `GeoMask::new`, unwrap corners relative to `pack_lon` (and store up to two canonical x-intervals if a pack crosses 180).
  - In `roads.rs` and `roadFeed.ts`, step along unwrapped chords and map tile column via `tx.rem_euclid(TILES)` while keeping the local tile origin `ox = tx_unwrapped * EXTENT`.
- **Rough size**: **6 to 10 hours** (most of the care is in `cross.rs`, `draw.rs` lead-ins/bridges/flyovers, and Rust tests).

### 3. Drawing on the MapLibre map (world copies, 179.9 to -179.9)

- **Simplest fix**
  - **In `gl.ts` and `rendererDraw.ts` (and the other WebGL layers)**: on the flat map (`!cam.globe`), determine which world copies `k in {-1, 0, +1}` overlap the viewport (from `viewCentreMerc(cam)` and viewport span; in normal play away from 180 only `k = 0` is visible, so zero extra cost).   - **For 2D / DOM placers (`mercToCss`, `overlay.toScreen`)**: on the flat map, pick the world copy `k in {-1, 0, +1}` that places `mx + k` closest to `viewCentreMerc(cam)[0]`.
- **Rough size**: **8 to 12 hours** (many overlay layers to touch, plus stroke caching / culling in `strokeLod.ts` and `rendererDraw.ts` so drawing 2 copies when straddling 180 does not fight the stroke cache).

### Hard or risky parts

- **MapLibre flat-map world copies + StrokeCache + Float32 precision (Stage 3)**: This is the trickiest part. Every WebGL layer (`rendererDraw.ts`, `busLayer.ts`, `demandOverlay.ts`, `gradeMarks.ts`, `buildingFloors.ts`, `parkingLots.ts`, `shadeLayer.ts`) and DOM placer (`stationLabels.ts`, `screenPlace.ts`) currently assumes one world copy. Worse, if the draw anchor sits at `+180` and a point is stored at `-179.9` without unwrapping to the anchor's copy, its Float32 offset is ~40,000,000 m (~4 m precision loss). Keeping single-point buffers canonical and rendering visible copies `k in {-1, 0, +1}` works cleanly **only** if the shader or `localCamera` avoids losing precision (shifting `a.merc[0]` by `±1` in `float64` before `mul4` preserves camera precision, while individual vertices far from the anchor still have Float32 rounding unless shifted per-copy or kept near the anchor). This needs careful design before coding.
- **Crossings grid (`sim/src/track/cross.rs`)**: Because `2 * PI * EARTH_R` is not an integer multiple of `CELL = 1000.0`, wrapping cell indices mod `floor(W/1000)` is off by ~173.6 m at the seam. Instead, wrap the chunk coordinates in metres into `[-W/2, W/2)` before dividing by `CELL`, and split any chunk that straddles `±W/2` into both halves.
- **Hidden `x`-comparison sites**: Even after fixing the main files above, there are many secondary features (e.g., `blueprintBox.ts` drag-box selection, `facilityPlace.ts` waiting rooms/car parks, `buildingNet.ts` station buildings, `realSnap.ts`, `intercityView.ts`, `locate.ts` camera fitting) that were not read in full here and may compare `x` or `lng` directly without wrapping. Expect a short audit pass during Stage 3 to catch those stragglers.
