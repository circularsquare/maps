# T-2064 The real save's flat crossings (stage 1, 2026-11-03, agent t2064)

## Cause

`T-007-real-nyc.save` predates `EdgeData.sep`. It was imported 2026-10-09 (T-007), converted to train
counts 2026-10-24 (T-1146) and never regenerated: T-1751 (2026-10-28) set `sep` on every edge of the seven
shipped saves (`data/packs/real/*.save`, made by `realsave`), and its notes say the smoke save is "a separate
file that no step here touches". Read directly (gunzip, the TWT4 network parsed, then loaded in the track
WASM in Node with the frame conversion the clock worker does):

| save | lines | edges | edges with `sep` | node levels | flat crossings | junction pairs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `T-007-real-nyc.save` | 109 | 1,647 | 0 | -3 to +3, one per line | 340 | 0 |
| `data/packs/real/nyc.save` (T-1305 shared) | 111 | 1,548 | 1,548 | all -1 | 0 | 0 |

The crossing test does its job: `find_crossings` skips a pair only when both edges carry `sep`, and here no
edge does. T-007's importer put each line on one of six levels, so any two lines on the same level that meet
make a flat crossing. A sandbox game started from New York's real network today starts from the shipped
`nyc.save` and has no flat crossings; the 340 are only in T-007 and in games made from it.

## Which of the 340 are real

None is a real diamond crossing of two separate lines. By the railways of the two lines
(`artifacts/trainworld/t2064/t007.json`, every crossing with its edges, lines and nearest station):

| kind | crossings | in reality |
|---|---|---|
| two railways (subway x LIRR 70, x Metro-North 15, x PATH 7, x NJ Transit 5; PATH x NJ Transit 13; light rail x NJ Transit 12; others 4) | 126 | grade separated: different structures (the 6 and the LIRR at 59 St, the A and NJ Transit at Penn Station, PATH under Hoboken) |
| subway x subway, two lines | 79 | grade separated: different tunnels or levels (the L under Broadway at Union Square, the 6 and the B, D, F and M at Broadway-Lafayette, DeKalb, Atlantic Av); A x D (6) share Central Park West, whose junctions are flying |
| one railway's lines (LIRR 91, NJ Transit 32, Metro-North 6) | 129 | lines that really share track, each given its own double track by the import, crossing where those tracks swap sides: 86 near a real junction (Harold by Woodside, Jamaica, Mott Haven, Secaucus, Hoboken, Harrison), 43 in plain shared corridors (the Main Line at Forest Hills, the 63 St tunnel, the Penn Station approach) |
| a line with itself | 6 | none (a tangled hop: the N at Canal St, PATH at Hoboken) |

Some moves at those real junctions are at grade (Jamaica's interlockings, parts of Harold and Mott Haven).
But a crossing between two separate per-line tracks is not that move, and its place is wherever the
importer's tracks swap sides, not the junction. The rule the shipped saves already follow (DECISIONS
2026-10-29, T-1305 stage 2; T-1751) is that imported track never makes a flat crossing and imported
junctions are flying, since the sources cannot tell a flat junction from a flyover. So all 340 go.

## Fix (proposal): mark every edge of the save separated

`notes/T-2064-mark-sep.mjs <in> <out>` rewrites a save with bit 7 set on every edge's flags; header, nodes,
lines, levels and every other byte unchanged. No sim, app or pipeline change, and no re-import: a re-import
from `realnet.py` would make a different network (T-1303's routed hops, other names), and the checks pin
T-007's 109 lines and 1,756 stations.

Staged copy: `artifacts/trainworld/t2064/T-007-real-nyc-sep.save`. Checked in the track WASM against the
original: header identical, nothing unread, 1,756 nodes, 1,647 edges, all 109 lines with the same stops and
path, all ok; flat crossings 340 -> 0.

To apply (from the maps root, not while a smoke or a check is reading the save):

    node trainworld/notes/T-2064-mark-sep.mjs trainworld/T-007-real-nyc.save trainworld/T-007-real-nyc.save

It touches only `trainworld/T-007-real-nyc.save` (tracked in git; nothing under `data/`). The shipped
`data/packs/real/*.save` need nothing. Games already in IndexedDB that were made from the old file keep
their crossings until reloaded from the new file (saves are Anita's; no migration).

## What changes for the player (in T-007 and games from it)

- No crossing marks or waits at the 340 points: at zoom 14 and in, no train stands short of Union Square or
  Broadway-Lafayette for another line.
- Run times: the capacity pass's crossing holds go (13,779 s a round trip, summed over the lines at the high
  level), so round trips are 2.5% shorter in all; 81 of 109 lines change, most the N (-12%), G (-12%), 2
  (-10%), 5 (-10%), D (-8%), Q (-8%), PATH (-7.5%). Train counts stay as saved (672 at the high level), so
  these lines run that much more often.
- Riders: see the measurement below.

## Measurements

Rail trips a weekday, demand final, one demand worker, own Vite (`artifacts/trainworld/t2064/rides.json`):
11,297,375 -> 11,590,441 (+2.6%), both round 3, 4,498.6 km and 1,756 stations, no page errors, about
115 s to a final each. Lines that lost the most holds gain most: the 2 700k -> 881k, D 792k -> 951k, N 653k ->
767k, 7 921k -> 1,032k, L 785k -> 861k; some parallel lines lose riders to them: the 3 741k -> 685k, B 631k ->
597k, R 1,033k -> 997k, 1 1,252k -> 1,213k. (T-1751 saw +1.9% for New York's old per-line shipped save.)
smoke's `realRail` figure moves with it, and `smoke-figures.json` is rewritten on the next smoke.

## Other saves from before T-1751

The same reading finds `data/work/realnet/jpn7_real.save` (2026-10-19, 491 lines, the T-2093 ridership
check's network) with 0 `sep` edges and 44 flat crossings, and `artifacts/trainworld/t737-japan-trains.save`
(807 lines) with 0 and 60. `realsave` has set `sep` on every edge since T-1751, so these are old files, not a
bug in the importer: T-2165.
