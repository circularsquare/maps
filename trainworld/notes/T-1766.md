# T-1766: attraction visits from a budget (2026-10-29)

Run: `venv/Scripts/python.exe trainworld/pipeline/attractions_json.py [--report]` from the maps root. Offline from its
caches: Wikidata in `artifacts/trainworld/t263/wikidata.json`, people within 25 km in `artifacts/trainworld/t263/pop25.json`,
sitelinks, page views and the World Bank's tables in `data/work/t1766/`.

## The rule

A place with no Wikidata figure gets its share of two pools, by its page views (English plus its country's own
editions, `LANG_OF`; September 2026 times 12):

- **Residents**: 6 outings a person a year (`OUTINGS`) for the people within 25 km, shared among the listed places within
  25 km by views^1.3, with 0.03 views a resident (`UNLISTED`) standing for the places not on the list.
- **Tourists**: one world pool, 0.6 listed places seen (`SEEN`) per trip, trips = international arrivals (World Bank
  ST.INT.ARVL, 2019) plus one domestic trip a resident a year (`DOMESTIC`, World Bank population 2019), shared among all
  places by views^0.5.

Capped at 25M. A Wikidata figure overrides the budget for its place (1,273 rows); the figures are the check.

## What was tried (share of the 1,273 figures within 3x of the budget)

| form | within 3x | why not |
|---|---|---|
| tourists by country pool (views^0.8) | 57-60% | Italy's 6,000 listed places thin its pool (x0.3), China's 350 and India's 430 swamp theirs (Taj Mahal 52M) |
| country arrivals by English views + domestic by own-language views | 60% | the same thinning |
| world pool, views^0.5, absolute unlisted weight | 73-74% | a city with one or two listed places pours all its outings into them (Oriental Pearl Tower 25M, White Horse Temple 10M) |
| world pool, unlisted weight per resident (shipped) | 68% | West Lake against the Oriental Pearl Tower: both Chinese, similar views, can't both be right |

Wikipedia views per visitor run fairly even across countries once the local edition is counted, except China, where
Wikipedia is blocked: Chinese places with a figure come out at 0.43 of it, the rest of the world 0.9-1.5.

## Page views

The Wikimedia REST and action APIs allow a client that names no contact 10 requests a minute, and the action API gives
views for about 10 titles a request: hours for 31,000 articles. The monthly dump
(`pageviews-202609-user.bz2`, 6.3 GB) is read as it downloads, 25 minutes, never kept on disk; the article totals land
in `data/work/t1766/pageviews.json`.
