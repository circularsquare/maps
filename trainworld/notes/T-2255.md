# T-2255: the diagram on the game's own graphs

Rules in docs/ui-diagram.md ("The layout"); figures and reasoning here.

## Why the checks and the game differed

Two separate causes:
- **The graph.** The game's graph (the clock worker, from the network as drawn on the map) is not the one
  `diagram-saves.mjs` builds from the same save: places differ by a few metres (another frame origin and
  float32 local coordinates) and New York has a complex fewer (854 against 855). So the checks laid out
  a different graph.
- **The engine.** Node 22 (V8 12.4) and Chrome 156 give different last bits for `Math.atan2`, `sin`,
  `cos`, `tan`, `exp`, `log`, `pow`, `atan`, `acos`, `cbrt` (200,000 random inputs each); `sqrt` and
  `hypot` agree. The layout used `atan2` and `**`, so even the same graph laid out differently (B up to
  4 hops, the drawing up to 8).

The pipeline is chaotic: any change in the last bit, or a move of a micrometre, gives a different drawing.
Rounding at stage boundaries cannot fix that (the differences grow within a stage too), so the choice was:
- `diagram/fmath.ts`: atan2 and powers from `+ - * /` and sqrt, used by layout.ts, draw.ts and names.ts
  (and `Math.hypot` replaced by `sqrt(x*x + y*y)`, whose last bit is the engine's own too). Node and
  Chrome now lay out and draw a graph to the same bits (checked: 854 places and 4,520 pill numbers
  identical). This also makes every player's browser agree.
- `checks/diagram-game.mjs` captures the game's graph (and the measured name widths, the worker's times
  and a hash of its places and pills) from headless Chrome, about 17 s a city; t2160, t2161 and t2162 lay
  out `nyc-game` and `tyo-game` too, check Node's result against the page's hash, and take the page
  worker's times. Capturing the layout alone would not have been enough: the fixes need a Node loop that
  reproduces the game.

The game's graph depends on the camera's draw anchor when the network was built (float32 coordinates
relative to it), so a session that builds it from another anchor gets another sample. Follow-up.

## The folds (doubling back) and why

Since every graph is just one sample of the chaos, figures were taken over the four graphs (the checks'
and the game's New York and Tokyo) and 3 perturbed copies of each (every node moved 1e-6 m * k), 16
layouts, then confirmed on 24 more (k = 10 to 15). On the base (HEAD with fmath.ts), stage by stage:
- folds mostly first appeared in the snap (9 of 20): B turned a line 30 to 87 degrees, the snap 140 to
  175. Snap directions and hold terms (weight 30 each) overrode B's turn term, which only covered lines B
  had turned more than 45 degrees past their real turn; a piece whose chord swung during the rounds was
  snapped to the far side and its hops dragged round (63 Dr-Rego Park: B 47, snap 160).
- some in A (5): A's pushes reversed short real hops (5 to 16 reversed edges in New York after A).
- some in routing (5) and one in the separation: a bent piece's leg shrank to nothing, so it left its end
  another way (Tokyo 東神奈川).

What changed (layout.ts):
- The snap: the lines "turned more than 45 degrees past their real turn" are found again each round from
  the current places, not only from B's, and drawn back at weight 20 (was 10).
- The separation: a pass whose drawing folds a line its start did not is done again with both empty legs
  of those bent pieces kept 2 pitches (at most twice a pass). Ports are read past legs of no length.

Tried: unsnapping pieces whose direction must be flipped to run the real way (no change); B's directions
limited to within 90 degrees of the real one (no change or worse); a term keeping each hop's real way
in A and B (fewer folds, more hooks and close pairs); the turn term at 10, 30 or 60 (10 leaves 14 folds,
30 and 60 trade folds for hooks); weight 100 for a fold in routing (no change). **No pushes in A** gave
0 folds in all 40 layouts and the fewest hooks, but packs the middle: interchanges unnamed at 14 px rose
from 16 to 35 over 16 layouts (Times Sq itself, Hunters Point Av, 飯田橋). **Pushes only in A's last 3
rounds** gave 0 folds and the best names (15) but a hook at New York Penn in 5 of 16, the game's New
York among them. Kept: A as it was.
