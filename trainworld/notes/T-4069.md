# T-4069 Ctrl+click places a bus stop without snapping

## How a manual stop is placed (before)
Line tool, by-hand bus line, stop editing: `map/busStops.ts` (`pick`) turns the pointer into a route point, `edit busHandStop` -> sim `set_bus_stop` -> `bus::hand_stops`.
UI rules (the ghost already showed the snapped spot; the sim repeated the station rule):
- route within 8 px (ROUTE_SLOP_PX) of the pointer, else no ghost, no click
- none if within 200 m (STATION_REACH_M) of a station's stop, or 50 m (HAND_GAP_M) of a hand stop
- near a station the route can stop at (within 200 m, ground, of the route's nearest point to it): snaps to that point, tag names the station
- else within BUS_XFER_M (100 m) of another bus line's stop that is itself within 100 m of the route: snaps to the route's nearest point to it, tag names the line
- else the route point under the pointer
Sim: a hand point within 200 m along the road of a kept station becomes that station's stop; a stop within 50 m of the previous one or an end is dropped.

## Now
- Ctrl or Cmd (same `noSnap` as the track tool, `tools.ts`) skips both snaps and the 200 m station refusal; 50 m gap stays. Pointer still has to be within 8 px of the route, and the stop lies on the route's road (so "exactly at the pointer" means the nearest point of the route).
- The sim cannot take the station off a point by itself (it snaps within 200 m), so new `BusRoute::exact`, wasm `set_bus_stop_exact`, save block `LBE1` (level 39, after `LBO1`). Redraw keeps it through `onto`; removal drops it.
- Demand (busLinks) still joins a stop within 100 m of another line's stop; Ctrl does not change that. The tag names that line.
- Ghost: always the landing spot. Holding or releasing Ctrl re-hovers at the stored pointer (`noSnapFrom`).
- Tag row `stopMarkers.addExact`, only where the stop is snapped, not on touch.
- Merge note: LEVEL 38 -> 39 and BLOCKS 41 -> 42 will conflict with any other branch that adds a block.
