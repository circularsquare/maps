**Answer: doable.** About **11-16 hours** for the version that shows the player one line, in three stages; the first
stage alone (about 5-7 h) already runs the trains on both pairs.

## How quad works today

- **Pairs**: quad track is two ordinary double pieces 8 m apart (T-255); doubling up adds the second pair as
  blueprint with no stations and no crossovers (T-708, T-1688, T-1689). Lines change pairs only where the pairs meet
  (the quad stretch's ends, a junction) or where the player drew crossing track.
- **Stations**: one per click, on the pair clicked (T-1687). A station level with one on the other pair takes its name
  (`twin_at`) and the two are one complex (platforms within 50 m). Demand counts snapshot stations within 100 m as one
  demand station (demand-kernel.md 4.4), so twins 8 m apart are already one stop to riders.
- **Which pair a line's trains take**: a line's stops are nodes, each on one pair, and its path is one fixed list of
  `(edge, dir)` found through them. Every train of the line runs that one path. So a line uses the pair its stops are
  on, and quad helps only by putting different lines on different pairs (locals on one, expresses on the other).
- **Passing**: within a pair only at stations with the passing tracks upgrade (T-2231, double track only); quad itself
  passes nothing.

**T-3317's report** ("upgraded to quad, trains don't seem to use all four tracks") is this wish, not a bug: doubling up
adds the second pair with no stations on it, and nothing moves a line onto it. Worth a line in the reply to that player
once T-3333 is in.

## When a line can use both pairs

Proposed test, run by the sim on every edit (`line_twin_ok`):
- Every stop has a **twin**: a station of the same complex on another track beside it at the same spot (the `twin_at`
  rule: within half its platform of the spot beside, 4-15 m off), with the same platform length or longer, serving the
  same ways (T-3332's one-track stations).
- A path exists through the twins in order (`route_hops`), with the same turn-backs as the line's (ends at the twin end
  stations, any partway reversal at a twin). This settles **ends and turnbacks**: each pair's trains turn at their own
  end station, holding its platforms as any line's end does.
- **Short stretches off quad**: the twin path may share track with the line's own path (a double stretch where the
  quad pairs meet, a junction at a quad end). Capacity counts both there, so a shared stretch shows up as a limit in
  the line's figures. Stricter is possible: refuse when the paths share any piece (call 2).
- Rail lines only, trams included (tram quad exists); no bus or ferry lines.
- Loops: a circle on quad works if its twins make a circle too; nothing special.

## How the trains are shared

- **Alternate trains**: odd departures on one pair, even on the other, in both directions. Each pair runs half the
  line's trains at twice its headway, departing half a headway after the other pair's (`spacing.rs` pins the phase;
  today it offsets only lines that share track, and the two pairs share nothing).
- Not useful: **one pair per direction**. Each pair already carries both directions on its own lanes, and running one
  direction on both tracks of a pair is wrong-line running, which the lane model does not have (T-3137).
- **Capacity and frequency**: each pair has its own platforms, sections and turnarounds, so the line's ceiling
  roughly doubles where both pairs are clear. A headway line's "most that fit at the track's limit" is the two pairs'
  limits added; a set train count is split as evenly as it goes (7 trains: 4 and 3, the timetable taking the odd one
  where it fits).

## Three ways to build it

1. **One line, two routes inside it** (the cleanest for the player): `LineSvc` carries a second pair of runs and every
   reader knows which departures use which. That is about 176 places in 24 sim files reading `runs` plus the app's
   train drawing, Marey and line profile, and the departure lattice has no simple "odd departure" today (stretches,
   levels, dropped departures). About 25-35 h, with a long tail. Not recommended now.
2. **A hidden twin line kept in step** (recommended): the toggle makes a second line in the sim on the twin stops,
   marked `twin_of` the parent; every edit to the parent rewrites the twin in the same edit (as T-3137's
   `way_reroutes` does): stops, path, trains (half), cars, train kind, speed limit, dwell, colour, phase. Capacity,
   timetables, boarding, money, drawing trains and demand all work unchanged because the twin is an ordinary line. The
   app then hides the twin: the Lines tab lists the parent only, the line panel adds the twin's riders, trains, profit
   and limits into the parent's, the map draws its trains and stroke in the parent's colour, and a click on its train
   opens the parent.
3. **Two visible lines in a group** (cheapest): option 2 without the hiding. The player sees "Line 1" and its twin as
   two rows in one line group. Simple, but she asked for one line with a toggle.

Option 2's first stage is option 3, so it can be stopped there if the hiding turns out not to matter.

### Demand

No change expected. The twin's stops merge into the same demand stations as the parent's, and the kernel's
common-line strategy combines lines with the same next stops (a wait is half the combined headway), so riders see one
service at the full frequency, and loads split between the two by frequency. Check with a kernel test (two lines on the
same stops at headway 2h waits as one at h) and the ceiling tests once.

### Line panel and toggle

- In the line panel's service rows, a switch with her words: "Run trains on two pairs of tracks". Shown only while
  the line passes the test, or while it is on.
- While on, the train counts, headways, riders, profit and capacity alerts are the two pairs added; the Schedule's
  headway is the combined one; the Marey chart draws both.
- **An edit that breaks the test**: the switch stays on and the line runs on one pair until it passes again, with one
  line in the panel naming why ("Mill St has no platform on the other pair") (call 3). The other choice is to turn the
  switch off by itself.
- Saves: the parent's flag in a small trailing block (as `LSL1`), and the twin saved as a line with its link, or
  rebuilt on load from the flag (agent's call; rebuilt is smaller and cannot drift).

### Interactions

- **Left-hand traffic (T-3297)**: none. Both pairs are double track whose lanes follow the running side; paths are
  `(edge, dir)`, so a flip moves the twin with the parent (the flip's reroute applies to both).
- **T-3332**: a one-track station's twin must be one-track the same way, else the line does not pass the test.
- **Doubling up**: the new pair has no stations, so the line passes the test only once the player has clicked twins
  onto it (T-1687). A later nicety: an "Add the missing platforms" button beside the switch while only twins are
  missing. Not in the estimate.
- **Passing tracks**: per station and pair; unaffected.

## As built (agent onestop, 2026-11-12, `.tw-trees/quad2`, option 2)

Anita's answers: option 2 (one line to the player); when an edit breaks it the switch stays on, the line runs on one
pair and a notice says why. Orchestrator: the other pair's route may share track where the pairs meet.

- **Sim** (`track/twin.rs`, `world.rs` `twin_ops` and `pin_twin_phases`, `wasm/twin.rs`): `LineData::twin` is
  `Twin::None`, `Parent { split }` or `Of(parent)`. `station_beside(n)`: the nearest station at `n`'s level and family,
  parallel (heading sine under 0.1), node 4-16 m away. `twin_plan(l)`: those stations for every stop (none a stop of
  the line), `route_hops` through them, `check_route` (new: `check_line`'s body on given stops and path), the same
  number of stop reversals, built where the line is; else a `Why` (stop, hop, route, unbuilt, not rail).
  `twin_ops` runs in `apply_rerouted` for every checked edit after the way reroutes, as a second batch in the same
  edit (undo and redo restore it whole, never recomputing): a parent that can split halves its counts (rounding up),
  rates and doubles its headways and gets its twin (an id from `spare_line_ids`); a split parent that cannot doubles
  back and loses its twin; a twin out of date is rewritten (its tph kept, and a headway line's counts, which the
  settle owns); an orphan twin goes. `pin_twin_phases` sets each twin's phase to its parent's plus half a headway
  after `place_departures`. `set_train_counts` / `set_auto_headway` on a split parent take whole-line figures.
  Save: `LTW1`, level 17. Cost: nothing while no line has the switch (one `all` over `line_twin`); with some, per
  parent per edit a scan of the stations per stop and a route search.
- **App**: `ClockLine.pairs/twinOf` (`line_pairs`, `line_twin_of`), `LineInput.twinOf`, `LineStats.pairs/twin/twinOf`,
  `WorldView.twins`. The twin stays in `save.lines` (fares, money and demand sums need it: an earlier try that dropped
  it would have stopped its fares), and `listedLines` leaves it out of the Lines tab, search, stations tab, trouble
  list, city counts and station labels; `linesAt` gives the line at the twin's stations. Selecting the twin selects
  the line (an effect in `Inspector.tsx`). `today` folds the twin's past day into the line's (`foldTwins`, the stops
  index by index), `runningToday` and `upkeepToday` add the twin's, `pairStats` gives the panel and the Lines tab the
  two pairs' trains and the combined headway. The switch (`LinePairs` in `LineInspector.tsx`) asks `linePairs`
  (`line_pairs_ok`, `line_pairs_why`) and shows only where it can or while on.
- **Not done** (T-3335): the Marey chart, the line's held trains and its delay places read the line's own pair only; the
  stations panel of a twin's station counts no boardings (the line's are counted at its own stations); a pending
  line's riders before its account (demand's `ridersPerDay`) read the line's pair only. The busiest-line achievement
  adds the twin's riders. A tram line uses the same switch ("trains" in its wording).
- **Running side flip** (T-3297): `flip_right_hand` runs `twin_ops` after its reroutes, so a flipped game keeps its
  lines on both pairs.
- **Checks**: `sim/src/track/wasm/twin_tests.rs` (4 tests: switch and split, edits that break and mend it, undo,
  remove line; headways and saves; built line waiting for the other pair; a flip), `checks/t3333-pairs.mjs` (15 checks
  in a browser, pictures `artifacts/trainworld/t3333-*.png`).
