# T-3900 downloaded schematic image in a blank frame

Cause: `picture()` in app/src/diagram/view.ts framed the image on every ribbon and every station name of the laid-out
network. Lines left out by the mode buttons or hidden on the map (`d.off`, `d.offNode`) stayed in that box, though
`paint` and `paintNames` skip them. So a player with most lines hidden got the few drawn lines tiny, in the frame of
the whole network. Visible bus and ferry lines (`d.thin`) were not in the box at all (could clip).

Fix: the box counts only drawn ribbons, drawn thin lines and drawn names. A perfectly straight line (zero height) used
to return null, so nothing downloaded; the pad now gives it a frame. Caps unchanged (16000 px side, 120e6 px area,
24 px margin, names from a pitch of at most 10 px).

Repro: big240 fixture, hide lines through `import('/src/game/lineVisibility.ts')` `setLinesHidden`, download.
Sizes (px), one line shown each, before -> after: wide 7661x6655 -> 1626x160; tall 7661x6655 -> 754x2025;
small 7661x6655 -> 987x459. Whole real networks (NYC save 10131x9166, northeast 11107x8630, big240 7661x6655) were
already framed right.

Left as is: city names (the grey ones) are not in the box, so one at the edge can be cut off.
Not reproduced: a phone-sized window (the picture takes nothing from the window).
