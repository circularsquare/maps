# T-003 notes: New York boundary

**Choice: the 2023 New York-Newark-Jersey City MSA (22 counties) plus Fairfield County CT,
Dutchess County NY and Orange County NY. 25 counties, 21,736,575 people in 2020.**

The MSA is the core: the five boroughs, Long Island (Nassau, Suffolk), Westchester, Rockland,
Putnam, and twelve New Jersey counties down to Ocean, Hunterdon and Sussex. Three counties from
the combined area (CSA) are added because a rail player building New York would expect them and
each ends a real commuter line: Fairfield (the New Haven Line through Greenwich, Stamford and
Norwalk; OMB puts it in its own Bridgeport-Stamford-Danbury MSA), Dutchess
(Hudson and Harlem lines to Poughkeepsie and Wassaic) and Orange (Port Jervis Line, Newburgh).
Dutchess and Orange form the Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie-Newburgh MSA, which has never been part of
the New York MSA. Left out: Mercer NJ and everything south of it (Trenton is between New York and
Philadelphia, which becomes its own city in milestone 2), Pike PA (dropped from the MSA in 2023,
no rail), Ulster and Sullivan NY (rural, no commuter rail), New Haven CT (its own city or a
town later; the New Haven Line's far end is a station, not a reason to take the county).

Geometry: Census cartographic boundary file `cb_2020_us_county_500k` (shoreline-clipped,
1:500k), dissolved with shapely, coordinates rounded to 6 decimals, written to
`data/packs/nyc.boundary.geojson` (a MultiPolygon of 19 parts, mostly islands, 0.10 MB). 2020
counties rather than 2023 because Connecticut replaced its counties with planning regions in 2022
and the 2020 blocks and LODES 8 both still use the old ones (Fairfield = 09001). The county list
lives in `pipeline/build_city.py` (`CITIES["nyc"]`); the 2023 delineation file used to check it
is `data/raw/list1_2023.xlsx`.

Populations (2020 Census): the 22 MSA counties 20,081,935 (the published 2020 MSA figure
20,140,470 less Pike PA's 58,535); Fairfield 957,419; Dutchess 295,911; Orange 401,310.
