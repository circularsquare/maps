# T-4251 Bus and BRT stations on busways (busways, 11-25)

Player (inbox 1795559519-be009c2d, 11-24): "when creating a BRT line, cant connect BRT stations. Also cant place stations in BRT tracks". Anita on the scratch pad: stations on busways should be allowed.

## Cause (two app bugs, no sim change)
1. The station tool's snap treated a busway (edge kind 4) as train track (only kinds 2 and 3 are tram). With Auto, the pointer on a busway picked "rail", and the sim refused it: "Stations can't go on busways." Fix: station snapping skips busway edges (`tools.ts` buswayEdgeSet), so the pointer is over open ground, Auto goes on to `roadUnder` and picks the bus kind, and the BRT station stands 10 m beside the busway.
2. The BRT station's road finder (`StreetSnap.busRoads`) was the tram road graph, with no busways in it, so with the BRT kind a busway more than 50 m from a street had no road to stand beside. Fix: its own BusRoads with the busways added (`buswayLines.ts`, moved out of busDraw.ts and shared). Tram routing keeps no busways.

Rail kind picked by hand on a busway now starts a free station (open ground) instead of the refusal; the sim rule stays (no rail station on a busway).

## Check
typecheck clean. NYC new game, busway drawn in Central Park, Auto + click on the busway: "E Drive added as a blueprint station", 1 station, no refusal (before the fix: the refusal text).

## Second complaint: design question, not built
A bus line stops only at a BRT station when the line is BRT (`bus.rs reach`: `!brt && node_road.is_some()` skips it). The draw tool has no BRT switch: a new line is plain until upgraded on its panel, so while drawing, its stops never meet the BRT stations beside the route. Probably what the player saw. Not a bug in the rules. Recommendation: no change to the rules; if she wants it, show BRT stations the route passes as "BRT stations (upgrade to BRT to stop here)" in the draw tag, or add a BRT switch to the draw tool. Her call.
