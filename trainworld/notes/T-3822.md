# T-3822 Choice of routes shows as a plus in effective time

Player report (11-20, board shot `artifacts/trainworld/board/20261119-210750-0f0e.png`): an inter-city trip,
evening peak, Singapore/Johor, K1 Komuter Selatan Bukit Panjang to Kulai: "Walk 33.6 + wait 3.2 × 1.03 + ride
27.7 + fare $14.71 × 1.3 + choice of routes 26.2 = 110 min".

## Cause

Two different causes, one per panel. For local trips no term is missing from `routeTerms` (case a ruled out for
the cases swept). For inter-city trips one term is missing, the planned first wait (below), but it takes minutes
off, so it is not what made the plus.

**Inter-city (MarketTrip.tsx), the reported one: the figure is an average, not a logsum.** `market_trip_w`'s
rail cost (head slot 6) is `rc / rc_w`: each 6 km zone pair's logsum, weighted by **all** its trips (`tg`, every
income group), over the zone pairs rail can serve. The station pairs and routes are weighted by **rail riders**
(`rt = tg × frac`). Riders gather where rail is good, the trips do not, so the mean sits far above the riders'
routes; where the riders take long routes the other way it sits far below. The hover's last term was this spread
of where people start and end, labelled choice of routes.

NYC real save, NYC to whole places, all periods (probe `market_run.js`):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New Haven, midday | 223.7 | 160.1 | +63.5 | 162.2 | 163.5 |

The route sums agree with the riders' station pair costs; only the figure is off. Control (`zone_run.js`): one
near cell against single far 6 km zones (50 cases, so no averaging across zone pairs): the last term is −18.6 to
+2.2, the local trips' size and kind. So the tens of minutes come from the averaging. Within a zone pair the
market also costs the first boarding at the planned wait (`entry_r`, T-1430) while the legs carry half the
headway: Newark Penn to one New Haven zone (gid 672665, midday), one station pair carrying 99.9% of the riders,
eff 176.1 against the pair's 194.5. The first ride (line 93) waits 33.6 min (headway 67.1); planned, min(33.6, 5 +
67.1/6, 30) = 16.2, so 17.4 min less, about 17.9 felt at the wait weight: most of the 18.4 gap. That is a real
market term the route's equation does not show (follow-up below). The control is US only; the player's trip
crosses the Johor border, and a rail border term is ruled out only by reading `market_trip_w` (the rail cost has
none; crossings are the car's), not by a test.

Second save, `T-125-northeast.save` (also NYC-centred): local 744 period trips, 135 plus (75 over 1 min, mean
+2.2), routes against pair cost within 0.05, figure never above pair cost; inter-city NYC to New Haven +64 to +118,
Hartford down to −261. Same picture. No Asian or fare zone city was run.

**Local trips (TripInspector.tsx): the strategy is an expected cost, not a minimum.** Rail is a logsum over station
pairs, each pair's cost its strategy cost (`join_line`: the frequency-weighted mean of the useful lines' costs plus
the combined wait). The routes listed are walked through that strategy, each with the combined wait. So the main
(most used) route can be cheaper than its own pair's cost, when other trains that come first are slower. Sweep on
the real save (`local_run.js`, 150 station-pair trips offset up to 600 m, every period, 731 period trips; and
`local_far_run.js`, home ends up to 3 km out, 672, 53 with a drive to the station):

- 112 / 731 (92 / 672) show a plus, 62 (59) over 1 min, mean +2.3 (+2.8), worst +17.2 (Ramsey Route 17 to
  Hillside Facility, night).
- Where the main pair's routes are all listed (485 / 423), their share-weighted mean sum equals the pair's cost
  (access + egress + strategy + base fare) within 0.02 (0.04) min, drive access included: `routeTerms` misses
  nothing.
- The figure is never above the main pair's cost (max 0.0): the logsum part is never a plus.
- Example: 40 St-Lowery St to Wyandanch, evening peak: eff 104.70, main route 98.76, its pair's cost 105.87.

Not covered: a fare zone city (T-3784's free transfers), high-speed lines; the routes' consistency check is the
place to look if one turns up.

## Fix (app only, no demand number changes)

- `game/trip.ts`: `pairCost(t, pair)` (access + egress + strategy + base fare) and `routeVehicle(r)`
  (`roomVehicle` now shares its helper).
- Local hover (`TripInspector.tsx trainEquation`): the old last term split in two, the sum still the row:
  **first train to come** = pair cost − route sum (either sign), **choice of routes** = figure − pair cost (never
  a plus). Each from 0.05 min, each with its line. After (40 St-Lowery St to Wyandanch, night): "Walk 3.2 × 1.5 + wait 9 + ride 33.2 + walk between
  stations 1 × 1.39 + change 2 + fare $6.79 × 4.4 + first train to come 0.9 − choice of routes 0.7 = 80 min".
- Inter-city hover (`MarketTrip.tsx trainEquation`): the route's own equation ending at its sum, then "The sum is
  this route's own. The effective time is averaged over the 6 km areas at the two ends, by their trips." (the
  second sentence the car hover's line with a subject). No pooled term: an average over zone pairs has no split with a known sign. After (NYC to New Haven, midday):
  "Walk 50 + wait 13.9 + ride 66.2 + walk between stations 3.5 × 1.39 + changes 2 × 2 + fare $11.93 × 1.78 = 160
  min" under a row reading 225 min.
- docs/ui-inspectors.md: both hovers.

The market's Time row (31 min in the shot, legs 13 + 31 + 20) is intended: its hover reads "Station to station:
wait 3.2 + ride 27.7 = 31", since the market knows only felt minutes for the walks (`stationTimes`), so not a
second bug. The legs' 13 and 20 are felt minutes, not real ones.

No changelog item: the change is a hover's wording and terms only (trainworld CLAUDE.md, Anita 2026-11-18).

## Strings

- new `tripText.termFirstToCome`: "first {vehicle} to come" (train, tram, bus, boat, ride)
- new `tripInspector.firstToCome`: "People board the first {vehicle} to come, so some ride other routes from the
  same stations."
- new `marketTrip.routeOwn`: "The sum is this route's own. The effective time is averaged over the 6 km areas at
  the two ends, by their trips."
- The three new keys have no translations yet (the other languages show the English).
- removed from the inter-city hover: the "choice of routes" term and "Choice of routes is the time saved by having
  several ways to go." (both still in the local hover)
