## The change (`workers/demandClient.ts`, `refineAside`)

While a whole solve without the barrier runs, if the zone in view goes fine (the solve covers it coarse) or an edit
that touches it is held behind that solve, a focus pool of one worker (T-2045's, now outside loads too) opens the zone
fine and solves it alone with the newest network, through every round. Each round shows as the zone's figures and
bubbles (`publishRefine`); lines only where the shown result has none for them (new, or their stops changed, T-324's
rule). Those stay over any solve that covers the zone coarse or is of an older network (`asideOver`, `refineOver`,
`shownCoarse`: the whole final included), and the pool ends once a main-pool solve of that network shows the zone, or
at a newer edit. The main pool's solves are untouched. Not on a small device (no focus pool there): as before.
`?perfOff=refineAside` is the old path.

Lines already shown keep their figures: from the zone alone its two lines read 50,383 a day against 67,805 (new1, the
first version, which put them in), lower whatever the edit did; follow-up T-2624.
