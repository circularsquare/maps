# T-4501: a selected city takes over a minute on one core (agent cityload, 2026-11-27)

Her words, 11-27 19:16 on 8800: "is it expected that the selected city sometimes still takes > a minute to load? im
experiencing that. on using oen core." A repeat: notes/repeats.md "The loading bar sits at 90% for minutes". No
diagnostics download had arrived by the report. Tree cityload.

## For Anita

- **Where a minute comes from.** On one trip worker, a city you select while a game is loading gets its first figures
  only when the whole load's calculation finishes its first round: on the real New York save, 53 s after the click
  (round 0 of a 147 s calculation); on your Asia save that round is roughly the first third of a 5-6 minute load. Only
  the city in view had a shortcut (a second worker that calculates it alone).
- **Changed.** That second worker now takes the selected city once the city in view has its first figures: New Haven,
  selected as the New York save loaded, showed its trips 23 s after the click instead of 53 s. The city in view's first
  figures come as before (12 s).
- **Outside a load** I found nothing that slow on the New York save: a selected city with figures filled in within
  1.3 s during a whole recalculation, a city with no station opened in 1.7 s. Your Asia save was not run.

## Why the earlier fixes don't cover it (from the code)

- T-4263 removed the pool cut after a load's first final and its whole re-solve; T-4204 the re-solve after a planned
  restart; T-4265 lets a city's bubbles answer from the figures on screen during an edit's whole solve; T-4359 ends a
  mid-game city load at its packs when the map has figures for it; T-4264 and the bar fixes before it changed the bars,
  not the wait. Every one of them is about a wait where some figures are already on screen, or after the load.
- During a game load nothing is on screen to answer from: a selected city's figures can only come from a solve of it.
  The main pool solves it at its cluster's round 0 (T-2453: `selectedRound`, or the solve's own round 0), and on a
  joined-up save the cluster is most of the game, so on one worker that is a whole solve's first round. The focus pool
  (T-2045), the one way a zone gets figures in its own time, took only the zone in view (`focusSoon`: `frefine ??
  activeCity`). T-2453's item 4 (the focus pool for the selected zone) was proposed and never built.
- T-2453 also orders the main pool's queues for the selected zone only for a selection made before the load's solve is
  sent; a city selected after waits for the order already set.

## Following a selected city on one worker (code, then the runs)

A city selection (`selection` kind city, the Cities list, a city mark zoomed out, the city panel) does three things in
`demandClient.ts`: `ensure` opens its market pack in the solving workers (T-801), `lodSoon` makes it fine
(priority 2), and `selectedFirst`/`firstZones` put its pack second in the queues. Its city pack opens only if it is
viewed or reached. The panel (`CityInspector`) shows "Calculating trips" while the zone is in `zoneLoads` (fetched, no
figures) or its corridor rows (`rowsOf`, asked of the solving workers by the shown book's key) are not in.

Runs, my Vite 6123, `?demandWorkers=1&debug=1` (one solving worker and the aux, as Cores 1), 2 cores claimed:

| case | wait from the click |
|---|---|
| new game (New York, no stations), select Philadelphia, its market pack the first | panel at once, but "To other places 0" (T-4502) |
| same, then Boston (market pack opens, recompose, a new solve) | "Calculating trips" 0.25-3.1 s |
| real NYC save loading, New Haven selected as the load's solve started (before) | trips within 37 s, other places 43 s; main round 0 at 60 s from its send, final 147 s |
| real NYC save, an edit's whole solve running (transfer fare), Hartford (figures shown) | 1.3 s |

| real NYC save loading, New Haven selected at the load's start (after the change) | 22.8 s (trips within 48.3 s, other places 48.6 s; main round 0 at 78.2 s, 52.7 s after the click) |

After the change: New York's focus solve sent at 26.5 s, its round 0 at 38.3 s (11.8 s; 11.1 s before); New Haven's pack
downloaded 38.35-38.64 s, opened in the focus worker at 47.6 s (9 s waiting for that worker to end a New York population
of its round 1), its rounds 0-3 at 48.3-49.5 s. The focus pool retired at 78.7 s, just after the main pool's round 0.

Outside a load the waits I could find are short on this save: rows for the shown book are answered at the solving
worker's next break (`ROWS_WAIT_MS` 20 s, after which a city's rows count as failed until a newer result: not seen);
a market pack opened by a selection recomposes the market (3-15 s on big markets, T-3373-B-plan all-China 13-15 s).

## The change

- `workers/demandClient.ts` `focusTarget`: the zone the focus pool solves: `frefine` (T-2605); else the zone in view
  until the pool has shown it a round; then the city view's zone (`selectedZone`) while the load's solve covers it and
  neither pool has shown it a round, through every round of its own; not while the main pool solves its cluster ahead
  (T-2459, where the card waits for the zone in view's last focus round), nor with a cached result shown, nor before the
  network. `focusSoon` uses it, and runs on a selection change and after every focus round. `?perfOff=focusSelected`
  turns it off (`perfFlags.ts`).
- Figures: the main pool's solves are untouched, so every final is the same; during the load the selected zone shows the
  focus pool's figures (the same lie as the zone in view's: no market, its own riders only on shared lines).
- The trade: the zone in view keeps its first focus round, then takes the main pool's rounds. On the run, the focus pool
  retired at the main pool's round 0 (78.7 s); before, New York's focus rounds 1-3 came at 77, 115 and 152 s, now its
  rounds come from the main pool (126, 155 s in the before run). The card waits for the zone in view's own final either way.
- Docs: docs/demand-runtime.md "The focus pool". DECISIONS.md Saves and performance.
