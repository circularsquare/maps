# T-3411 one platform on one-way single track (bugs1, 2026-11-16)

Fixed what I could reproduce; the cause may be a different case than hers, say so if it is.
On a one-way single track (set by hand, or inferred from a join) a station in the middle already takes one platform
(placing with the one-track toggle, and the panel's switch, both worked in a new game). What was refused (OneTrackHere)
was a station at the END of single track: `one_fits` (sim/src/track/wasm/onetrack.rs) required a through node (two ends).
Now the end of single track (one end, single) takes it; the end of double track stays refused. Test:
`at_the_end_of_single_track` (onetrack_tests.rs); check: checks/t3379-single-fixes.mjs.
A station on inferred one-way track that the old rule made two-way (T-3386) also looked like "can't be one platform"
to a player, since the one-way flag vanished; that is fixed by T-3386.
Not reproduced: any refusal in the middle of a run. If she means something else (a line through it, a built station), ask for the save.
