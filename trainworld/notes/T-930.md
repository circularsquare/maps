# T-930 and T-3656: the join's re-gate checks only what a joint solve writes

T-930 (from notes/T-928.md section 4): the GB join spent 9.8 of its 31 minutes re-gating every figure of every
member. T-3656 (notes/T-3455.md "What ran (nightfix3)"): Malaysia's join failed because Kuala Terengganu (kur, built
2026-11-02) scored 0.92 on `water_points_in_water` against the Malaysia water cache rebuilt 2026-11-13, and the
whole group parked. Both are the same fault: the re-gate re-ran checks a joint solve cannot change, against files
beside the pack that can be newer than its build.

## What a joint solve writes

`world_run.solve` per unit: `merge_cells --undo` (members back to res-9), `intercity --solve` (bin/joint_gravity.rs:
the gravity arrays `zone_row`, `zone_col`, `zone_home`, `zone_work` and the header's `gravity` block, plus the
unit's `<name>.joint_report.json` and joint commutes; then `cross_everyday.run_unit`: the `everyday_kept_*` arrays),
`merge_cells` again (the gravity carried to the merged cells, the merge stamp). That is the set `GRAVITY_ONLY` plus
`header:gravity` and `header:merged_source` that `other_packs_unchanged` already allowed members to change; every
past re-gate passing `other_packs_unchanged` (Malaysia, GB) shows members' other content comes out of a solve
unchanged.

## Checks, kept and dropped

Rule: a check stays when its figure reads something the solve writes.

| Check | Reads | Re-gate |
|---|---|---|
| `joint_borders` | the joint report, header gravity.joint | kept |
| `read_pack` | the whole pack; `check_gravity` tests the new factors against row and column sums; its `check_water` uses fixed test points on the pack's own mask, not the cache | kept whole |
| `mean_commute_km` | header gravity `mean_trip_km` | kept |
| `other_packs_unchanged` | every other registered pack against the snapshot taken before the solve | kept |
| `own_pack_unchanged` (new) | the member itself against that snapshot, only its gravity allowed to change | added |
| `fleet_range`, `gate_relax` | the checks above | kept |
| `water_points_in_water` | the pack's mask and the extract's OSM water cache | dropped: the cache is outside the pack (T-3656) |
| `stations_on_land` | the mask and the station file | dropped |
| `pop_vs_plan`, `pop_vs_fua`, `pop_vs_ghsl` | `pop`, the outline, the plan's people, GHS-FUA, GHSL | dropped |
| `near_main_piece_pop_share`, `area_km2` | the outline | dropped |
| `jobs_per_resident`, `top_cell_jobs*`, `top_zone_jobs_share` | `jobs`, `zone` | dropped |
| `top_school_cell_pupils`, `largest_hospital_beds` | school arrays, the medical audit | dropped |
| `drive_factor_median`, `road_snap_median_m` | `drive_near`, the routes header | dropped |
| `osm_land_share`, `osm_top_place_jobs_share` | the tier 0 signals, the build log | dropped |
| rates (`school_rate`, ...), browser | `everyday_kept_*` (a solve output) | never ran in the re-gate (`--no-rust`, no `--browser`); left so |

A dropped check is sound only while the member is unchanged outside its gravity, so `own_pack_unchanged` holds it to
the snapshot. Before, a member was covered only by its siblings' `other_packs_unchanged`, and a one-member unit
by nothing. A member missing from the snapshot (registered after it) gets the whole gate. The plan row is still
read, so a `gate_relax` on a kept check still counts.

The rates are a real gap, not a new one: `cross_everyday` changes `everyday_kept_*` in the solve and the rates test
reads them, but the re-gate has run `--no-rust` since before this task (the cargo test costs minutes). Not added.

## Code

- `checks/pack_gate.py`: `--joint --since <snapshot>` (`run_joint`, `joint_figures`, `joint_checks_of`); one
  SKIP line `own_build` names what is not re-run. `changed_items` split out of `changed_since`, used for the own
  check. Caching, same results: `read_json` (the 11 MB plan, the 2.7 MB registry and the 11 MB snapshot,
  read once a pack before, now again only when their size or time changes); `report_packs` (each joint report's
  pack set kept, the body read only on a match: the 228 reports are 62 MB, 1.5 s a pack); `pack_stats` (one
  `os.scandir` of data/packs instead of a stat of each pack's three files, 2.5 s a pack gated).
- `docs/adding-a-city-by-hand.md` 8: the station file is no longer read by the join's re-gate.

The dropped checks rely on nothing changing a member between its last full gate and the snapshot before its
solve. The rule for data runs already says so (nothing else writes data/packs during a join), and the stale-stamp
fault at the top of `solve` refuses a merged pack changed after its merge. A side effect worth knowing: a join
no longer applies today's thresholds, gate code or caches to its old members; a threshold change reaches shipped
packs through a figures gate of its own (`pack_gate.py --all` or the light pass), not through joins.

Proposed DECISIONS line (2026-11-18, T-930): the join re-gates only what a joint solve writes and holds each member
unchanged otherwise, so a newer cache or a tightened threshold never parks a group; old packs meet new rules
through a gate of their own.

Follow-up for a task: the rates (`school_rate`, `university_rate`, `everyday_purposes`) read `everyday_kept_*`,
which `cross_everyday` rewrites in every solve, and no gate checks them after a join (`--no-rust` since before
T-930). A `--joint` with the runner's built test binary (`TW_EVERYDAY_EXE`) would cover it for seconds a pack.
- `pipeline/world_run.py` `solve`: the re-gate passes `--joint` instead of `--no-rust`.
- `docs/world-run.md` "Joint gravity": the rule.

## Measured (offline, read-only on data/packs; scratch harness, regate scratchpad)

Harness (`gate_harness.py`): pack_gate from this tree with the world plan swapped for a copy without kur's
`gate_relax` (as it was in the night) and `write_stations` refused, so nothing is written into data/.
Snapshot taken by the same harness just before. dayrun (T-3671) ran meanwhile, so the machine was busy.

| Group | Full re-gate (`--no-rust`, as before) | `--joint` | Night's join |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malaysia, 20 members (buk-my) | 262 s (old code), 262 s (new code) | 31 s | 190 s (2026-11-18 06:35-06:39) |
| GB, 62 members (lon-gb) | 261 s (new code) | 51 s | 404 s (2026-11-17 01:30-01:37) |

Of the `--joint` time, about 14-16 s is hashing every pack once (as the full gate does) and 0.3-0.5 s a member after
(read_pack 0.05-0.4 s, comparing 4,132 packs 0.25 s, joint report 0.17 s).

- **Kuala Terengganu**: full gate without the relax: `kur PARK; FAIL water_points_in_water 0.9211` (38 points, dry
  at 103.339 4.912, 103.336 4.921, 103.334 4.922), the night's fault reproduced, the Malaysia water cache on disk.
  `--joint`: `kur SHIP` (read_pack, own_pack_unchanged, other_packs_unchanged, mean_commute_km 6.364 all PASS). Every
  Malaysian and GB member SHIPs with `--joint`.
- The new caching changes no result: the full gate on Malaysia with old and new code gives the same status and
  value for every check of all 20 packs (`compare.py`), `other_packs_unchanged` aside: dayrun rebuilt elm and cai
  between the two runs, which both modes report (FAIL `elm: airport_trips, boundary, ...`).
- The fallback: pnt left out of a fresh snapshot gets the whole gate (16 water points, SHIP) while kur in the same
  run gets the `--joint` checks (all PASS, joint_borders through `report_packs`).
- `own_pack_unchanged` on scratch copies of kur (`own_test.py`, both stat paths): nothing changed, `zone_row` with
  the gravity block, `zone_col` pass; one byte of `jobs` fails `jobs`; the header's totals fail `header`.
- `--joint` still catches a change to another pack: kur and pnt against the Malaysia snapshot after dayrun's
  rebuild of elm and cai both PARK on `other_packs_unchanged` (elm and cai: airport_trips, boundary, ...). GB's
  full run met the same (23 members FAIL on elm alone, dayrun's, nothing else failing).

For a join: Malaysia's re-gate 3.2 min becomes about half a minute, GB's 6.7 min (9.8 in T-928's join) under a
minute. Each unit's snapshot (8-16 s) is unchanged.
