# T-3913: the bus tool shows what is in town (agent townshade, tree `.tw-trees/townshade`, 2026-11-21)

Anita, 11-21: "could we color what is in town and out of town, with a slightly different color for streets in town and
out of town?" In town is BRT's rule (T-3907, `streets::brt_in_town`): a plain bus there moves under BRT's 35 km/h
floor, which is where the car's local streets run under about 41 km/h, so homes and jobs over about 570 a km² of land.
That takes in nearly all of a metro's suburbs; out of town is open country, big parks, marshes and airports, and
anywhere no pack's street speeds are loaded.

**What landed tints the ground, not the streets.** Colouring the streets themselves came out wrong by up to a tile
(below), so every look is a tint under the hint; the streets keep their teal and the trunk roads their blue.

## How it works

- **Sim** (`streets.rs`): `StreetGrid::town_outline(before)` takes a grid's 2 km zone squares that are in town, joins
  those touching side to side into polygons (outline first, then holes; squares meeting only at a corner stay
  apart), corners only, in longitude and latitude. A square whose centre an earlier grid covers is left to that grid,
  as `Streets::car_kmh_at` takes the first grid. WASM: `last_town_outline()` for the grid just added. Test:
  `the_town_outline_merges_squares_into_polygons_with_holes`.
- **Worker** (`clockGround.ts`): after each `add_pack_streets`, posts `{ kind: "town", id, outline }`; a panic's new
  model posts `townClear`. Street speeds come with the view's masks, so the view's packs always send theirs.
- **Page** (`map/townArea.ts`): keeps each pack's polygons (NYC with Philadelphia loaded: about 65 polygons, 2,100
  corners). `basemap.ts` `setTownFill` draws them from a GeoJSON source while the bus tool is on, 300 ms after a burst
  of packs; no relayout of the basemap's tiles.
- **Looks** (`TOWN_LOOKS`, `?town=a|b|c|none`, kept in localStorage `twTownLook`):
  - **A (landed)**: the town tinted the hint's teal (#1a9e94 at 0.14, dark #3fd0c4 at 0.12), under water and roads.
  - **B**: out of town tinted khaki (#9a8a3a at 0.2, dark #c8b468 at 0.14): a world polygon right over the land, then
    the town covered in the land's own colour (`bus_town_mask`, read from the background layer), both under imagery,
    parks and everything else. In a city nothing changes; only the country is marked.
  - **C**: both.

Sheet: artifacts/trainworld/T-3913/T-3913-sheet.png. Columns current, A, B, C; rows zoom 12, 13.2 and 15, light then
dark; the real NYC game over Livingston and Northfield NJ, where a park and golf courses make a few squares out of
town. Pack seam checked at Trenton (nyc and phl packs, look C): no overlap and no visible seam; Trenton's greyer
ground there is the basemap's own.

## Why not colour the streets (tried, taken out)

The first A and C split the street layer with MapLibre's `within` (streets out of town olive, or faded). `within`
takes a line only when all of it is inside, and the basemap's tiles merge each kind of road into one line across the
tile, so one out of town square turns every street of that kind in the tile out of town:

- zoom 12: all of Belleville, Kearny and Harrison (one z12 tile, x 1204) came out olive though all of it is in town.
  MapLibre's own `within` run on short lines there against the same polygons said in town (scratch `within_test.mjs`),
  so the polygons were right and the merged lines were the cause.
- zoom 13.2 and 15 over Northfield: olive streets a few km into town where the tint (the squares themselves) shows town.
- Cost: every street in every tile pays for every polygon corner. Relaying out the basemap after a colour change,
  headless on 3 cores: Manhattan z13 2.2 s plain against 3.8 s; Queens z12 4.6 s against 8.5 s; Orange z14 0.6 s
  against 0.84 s.

Exact street colours would need a road source of our own with the streets cut at the square edges (from the bus
tool's own decoded road tiles, `busRoads.ts`): about 3-4 hours, not done.
