## Why every bar froze near 90% (from the code; Gemini's read checked)

- Every bar moved on a time guess with a cap: `timeShare` (to 98% of a stage), `solveStage`'s round model, the
  units' `el / (el + left)` (to 99%), the status tag's `timeShare` (to 95% in 3 s, or the last such wait), its
  `roundShare` (to 95-97%). `keepPlace` never let the bar back, so any wait the guess missed parked the bar at its
  cap. `shownShare` stretched the top tenth of the model over half the bar: a model at 98-99% showed 83-91%.
- The status tag's waits on data and bubbles (`!demandView`, `bubblesWaiting`) never looked at a running solve:
  their bar was the 3-second guess even when a whole recalculation, counted unit by unit, was what they waited on.
- The units' time left mixed this solve's pace with the passes' prior half and half after one unit (T-3599's
  `PACE_WEIGHT_UNITS`), so a time that looked measured could still be a guess.

## The rule and where it lives

- `game/loadBarCore.ts` `barPlace(bar, guess, bounded)`: a guess shows as the model to the power 1.7 with its 90% at
  half (`guessShown`, `GUESS_TOP` 0.5) and goes no further; a bounded share runs from the bar's place to the top
  (`keepPlace`, never back, 99% until done). `shownShare` and `SHOWN_AT_90` are gone.
- `game/editLeft.ts` `editLeftOf`: `editLeft`'s time left plus whether this solve's units bound it: every unit still
  to run is of a stage with a unit ended on that worker, and the unit in hand is within its time at this solve's own
  pace (no prior). Not bounded: before any unit ended, a unit past its time, the stretch after the last unit, a held
  edit's part (`StatusTag`).
- Counted as guesses (half at most): the load model's stages, remembered loads, the round model, rounds published
  (`demandStep`; on one worker every round lands in the last seconds), units done (`workShare`; units differ 10x),
  the last such wait. Counted as bounded: the workers' own-pace time left, terrain downloads finished.
- The card (`jobProgress`): bounded once the solve's units bound it and the data stage is done. Zone bars
  (`zoneProgress`): bounded on the running solve's units once the zone is open and in it; a zone of the load in hand
  takes the card's bar as is (it was placed twice before). `solveLeft` carries `bounded` for the status tag.
- `ui/StatusTag.tsx`: one place (`placeFor`) per kind of wait, reset when the kind changes, the tag has been gone a
  second or a newer edit restarts the solve. Data, bubbles and a click wait take a zone's bar, else the running
  solve's share, else the guess.
- T-4051's `BLIND_SOLVE` (a blind solve's model capped at 25%) is gone: the general cap covers it.
