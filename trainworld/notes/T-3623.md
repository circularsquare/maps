# T-3623 Cairo's place point sits in the desert (2026-11-18, agent cairo)

## Cause

Two things together, measured on the Egypt extract's cached features and `cai`'s files (outline written
2026-11-12, place list 2026-11-10):

1. **The Cairo node is in a unit no pack has.** The node (place=city, capital, population 9,120,350, at 31.23573,
   30.04439, Tahrir) lies in geoBoundaries EGY ADM2 unit "Qasr Al-Nile" (37247803B52731964573716, 1.2 km2:
   Tahrir, Garden City, the riverside downtown). No world plan row (metro or zone) has that unit, and it is not in
   the plan's dropped list or the EGY zone merges; its neighbours Abdin, Bulaq, Zamalik and Sayyida Zainab are all
   cai's. So cai's outline has a hole about 0.9 km east-west by 1.4 km north-south there: the node is 219 m from
   the east part, 660 m from the Gezira island part; the nearest pack cell is 287 m off, 9,512 people within 500 m,
   55,935 within 1 km. It is not the Nile itself (the earlier guess). places.py dropped every point outside the outline.
2. **An area's label is its box centre.** OSM also maps Cairo as a governorate-sized place=city area (no
   population). places.py placed it at its bounding box centre (31.5645, 30.0346), 37 km east in the desert, and
   only twinned same-name rows within 2 km (DEDUP_M), so it stayed as "Cairo", city, population 0.

The other Egyptian gap nodes the new rule keeps (below) are in no ADM2 unit at all: true river gaps (Nile islands
and banks the units leave out).

## Rule (in the tree's places.py)

- **Gaps** (`Gaps`, GAP_M 500): a place node outside the outline is kept when it lies inside the outline closed by
  500 m (buffered out then in, in metres: a hole or inlet under about 1 km across) and inside no other pack's
  outline. "Other packs" are every boundary file in data/packs, read through `osm_units.pack_boxes` as
  `cut_packs` does, so packs built earlier in the same world run count before they are registered. The read box is
  padded to match. 500 m is the smallest tested value that catches Cairo (250 m doesn't fill its hole).
- **Folds**: an area holding a node of its own OSM name (any kind) is that node's place. When this pack keeps the
  node, the area's other names and population merge into the node row; when another pack's outline holds the node,
  the area is dropped; when no pack holds it, the area stays (the place's only row). A folded area still twins
  later same-name areas within 2 km (OSM's second outline of a village). Only the extract path has area shapes; the
  Overpass path (13 cached US, UK and Canada packs) keeps the 2 km rule alone.
- `build(city, out_dir=None)`: a scratch folder for the output, so checks don't write data/packs.
- docs/code-map.md's places.py line says so.

How the rule was picked (scratch `classify.py`, 20 packs): of 1,880 place nodes within 2 km outside an outline,
1,842 are inside a neighbouring pack (zone edges; the neighbour already lists them), 38 in no pack. A plain
distance would keep Lagos's edge villages (32 nodes in Ogun state, 44-1,997 m out, where no pack exists yet) while
Cairo is 219 m out, so distance doesn't separate them; the closing does (none of Lagos's in it at 500 m). 21 nodes
in a 500 m closing sit inside a neighbouring pack's outline (hamlets on wiggly zone edges), so the other-pack test
keeps them out of two lists.

## World count

A dry count over every pack isn't possible from disk: 12 extracts are cached. The sample rate is 26 of 345 packs
(7.5%), by region Egypt 11/75, China 9/53, India 6/192, US/UK/Canada 0/13; at 7.5% of 4,133 packs about 300 would
change, most by a hamlet or a folded duplicate. As a proxy for the Cairo symptom itself (scratch `proxy.py`, every

naming mismatches (Lodz/Łódź, P'yŏngyang/Pyongyang, Chinese district names), not lost nodes.
