## Real findings
1. T-4237 (+T-2188): cumulative drift in clusters with no market part. demand.worker.ts:2941 (holdNet on e.fresh after a local solve) moves the touched city's baseline to the local network; the next city edit reads kept = that city (2205-2211), so two 2-minute run shifts (or two edits each under the 20-minute / 3-minute tests) never add up to an intercity edit. With a market part the baseline stays at the last whole solve (holdNet only in whole path, ~2348), so it only affects market-less clusters. The 120 s refresh limits the drift. Also found independently by Gemini (#4).
2. T-2188: refreshNow (demandClient.ts, refreshNow) returns without re-arming when published<ROUNDS, waiting, hold or failed at the 120 s mark; localEdits stays > 0 and no timer runs until the next solveDone. Player: city edit, 120 s later a network is held (cache/zone open) -> no refresh until another edit.
3. T-2188: writeCache (demandClient.ts:5642) runs on an endLocal final; the saved cache then holds the local-only figures as final and a reload has localEdits 0, so no refresh. Mechanism confirmed; effect is the same drift the design accepts. Gemini #1.
4. T-4214 C (minor): deletePick (toolDelete.ts:32) finds drawn station boxes only; a station placed a moment ago is found by stationUnder on its platform stretch/node but a click on its box off the track picks the track or nothing until the network lands. Gemini #5, partly covered by stationUnder.
5. T-4153 (unconfirmed, low): cache.samples keyed by slot (network.ts:2309); after a line is removed the next line takes its slot and alikeSamples (2296-2305) compares only path, edge, firstStroke and knots, not line id or bus, so lane (bus 0 vs rail 1) could copy across. Needs two lines with the same path prefix, one a bus. Gemini #8, not reproduced.
