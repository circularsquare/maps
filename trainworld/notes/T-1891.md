# T-1891: "Demand ran out of memory" on a new New York game with one line

Anita, 2026-10-31: the alert on localhost:8800 (build of 157c2a6a) in a brand new NYC game after one
Manhattan line, Destinations and Traffic on; Chrome at 16 GB in Task Manager (19 processes, other
tabs included). The same save loaded on the live site (5f232d9b) went fine.

## What the alert meant

`loadFailed` (game/loading.ts) chose "memory" when the failure text matched
`/unreachable|memory|allocat/`. A dead demand worker's text is always
`<error> (<last_panic>), <N> MB of WASM memory` (demandClient `receive`, kind "dead"), so **every**
second worker death showed "Demand ran out of memory", whatever its cause; any Rust panic also
aborts as `unreachable`. So the alert does not show that memory ran out: most likely a demand
worker panicked twice (the first is replaced, T-1174; a second before the replacement has seen a
solve through stops the pool).

Fix: only a failed allocation counts as memory now (`out of memory` from `last_panic`'s
FAILED_ALLOC, V8's `could not allocate`, `allocation failed`); anything else shows "Demand stopped"
with the error text in its hover. Checked with two `debugPanic`s on the dev server: "Demand stopped",
hover naming the panic and its source line.

## Memory measured (no leak found)

Headless Chrome, a new game from the start menu in New York, a five-station line Lower Manhattan to
the Upper East Side (`buildLine`), clock at the top speed, Destinations/Homes and Line/Traffic
switched every 15 s. Private bytes of the profile's processes; demand WASM from
`twDemand.client.mem`.

| run | renderer (all workers) | demand WASM per worker | over the run |
|---|---|---|---|
| dev, 4 min (2 game days) | 1.28 GB loaded, 1.9 after the line, 2.16 at the end | 3 x 255 MB + aux 65 | flat |
| 8800 (157c2a6a prod), 3 min | 1.92 GB | same | flat |
| dev, clock at 20000x, 57 game days | 2.2 GB | same | flat |
| dev, 1,200 track previews | 1.77 GB | same | flat |
| dev, 40 headway edits, 20 solves | 2.3-2.75 GB | 255 MB, heap 119 MB | flat |
| dev, 40 jumps over the north east at zoom 11 | 2.3-2.9 GB, GPU 0.4 GB | same | flat |

The renderer's ~0.5 GB jump when the line is built (before demand grows) is the clock worker: masks
and terrain grids of the zones near the line. Nothing grows with time, edits, previews or panning.
The three commits between the live build and 8800 (c5a11543, 695750aa, 157c2a6a) touch no demand
code (no file under workers/ or sim/src/demand); their Rust changes are track prices and
`preview_ground`. No bisect was run: nothing differed to bisect.
