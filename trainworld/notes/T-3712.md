# T-3712: branching off a ramp (agent rampend, 2026-11-18)

Repro by pointer (play.mjs, sandbox New York, scratchpad rampend/fix1.js + br2.js): level-0 track 300 m, then level +1 track
from its end (699 m, ramps over its first stretch). A branch pressed 550 m and 650 m along was refused with "Too close to the
end of a piece of track, or on a ramp..." (350, 450, 800, 950 m worked: flat parts).

Why it stays refused: a node has one level (`NodeData.level`), so a split mid-ramp has no height. `split_plan` refuses it
(`geom::on_ramp` +-1 m). Sliding the branch to flat track would put it up to a ramp length (hundreds of metres) from the click,
so the route end is refused and says so instead.

Built: `Issue::SplitRamp` (net.rs), returned from `route_op_as` (draw.rs `on_ramp_end`) for an End::Edge on a ramp after
`split_room` has moved it; text `issues.splitRamp`. The station path is unchanged (it already says PlatformNotLevel).
`issues.splitPoint` keeps "or on a ramp" for the remaining callers (add point, crossovers).

Test: `a_branch_off_a_ramp_says_ramp` (wasm/tests.rs). Screenshot: artifacts/trainworld/t3712_ramp_refusal.png.
