# T-4441 Where a click's train work goes (agent trainparts, 2026-11-27)

Stage C0 of T-4267. Measurement only: no speed change, nothing landed. Her Asia save (asianita-day568: 242 lines, 8,746 edges),
the track model's own wasm (agent build, no LTO) run in Node, four stations placed on edge 1482 of the Tokaido trunk 0.3 s apart.
Each click goes through `add_station` as the worker's does (`commit_local`, then `pay_owed` when the click before still owes
trains), and the last click's work is paid by `settle_owed`. Timers sit around each part (instrumentation.patch, never lands).

## What the run showed

- **The bundle on this edge is 3 lines**, not 150: the sharing closure (`settle_scope`) holds 3 of 239 lines for every click.
  The settle goes over 239 lines each round (`sc.ids`) but moves 3. The 150-line reach in T-4267 was edge 1818; edge 1482 is
  a small bundle. Not measured here: 1818 (budget: 1 run). If C7's size is to be settled, one more run of the same driver on 1818.
- **The settle rounds are small.** Steps C1 aimed at: all of `settle_train_counts` is 4.2 ms on later clicks (weekday rounds 2.6, weekend 1.5;
  3 weekday rounds + 1 weekend round; `bottlenecks` 0.3, classify 0.2 across the rounds). C1 alone would save at most about 3 ms of 150.
- **What costs is the work per changed line**, repeated for every click of the run (each later click pays the one before's work whole,
  so 4 clicks do the 3-line cluster's timetable 4 times):
  1. `retime` 48 ms: 1 cluster of 20 clusters / 46 lines is redone (3 lines); `passing_waits` 35 ms and `lead_in` 10 ms are that cluster's own work.
     Keys, lattices and states for all clusters are 2 ms. So C5 (keep the cluster keys) saves about 2 ms; the cost is the redone cluster, which a key cache does not touch.
  2. Capacity `update_scoped` twice (weekdays, then the weekend service `mirror_weekend`): 33 + 29 ms. `Capacity::update` 40 of it (2 calls), line profiles 15 (6 line updates), rebuilding 3 lines 5.
  3. `place_weekend` 20 ms (departures `place_departures` 2).
  4. `before_edit` 7 + `note_changes` 5 (the two C4 parts; over 1 ms each).
- Not over 2 ms: `lines_touched` 1.2, `demand_view` 0.7, `auto_counts` 1.6, `cars_needed` 0.8, `car_value` 0.2, `ahead` and `line_rates_all` 0.1-0.5, the closure 0.1, `pin_twin_phases` 0.
- First owed work after a load is 200 ms, not 150: it builds the weekend service once (`stc.weekend` 60 ms; later clicks 1.5).

## What it means for the stages (for the orchestrator)

- A1 (a run of clicks pays its owed work once) is worth 3 of every 4 clicks' work; by itself it takes this click run from 4 pays to 1.
- C1 (settle rounds over the scope) is not worth its 1.5 h: 3 ms. The biggest parts are not in the stage list as stated: retime's redone cluster (48), the capacity
  update done twice (62 with the weekend), `place_weekend` (20). C5 as written (keys) saves 2 of the 48.
- Worth a stage of its own: what `passing_waits` costs per redone line (about 12 ms a line here), and whether the weekend service has to be updated and placed on an edit
  that changes no weekend row (`mirror_weekend` 29 + `place_weekend` 20 = 49 ms, a third of the work). Not decided here; the second is a possible lie to consider.
- C2 (capacity update by touched records): `Capacity::update` 20 ms a call for 3 lines on 6 touched edges' worth is loops over the save; worth it.
- C7 only matters on a wide bundle (edge 1818); on 1482 it has nothing to do.
