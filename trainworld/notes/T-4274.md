# T-4274 The trains' tables in slots of their own on the GPU (agent trainslots, 2026-11-25)

**For Anita.** After stage 2 of T-4262 the drawn track and lines stayed put on the GPU after an edit, but the trains'
tables (where each line's trains run, when they stop and how they bend) were still kept in line order, so deleting a
line moved every line after it: 20 MB sent per deleted line on your Asia save. Now each line keeps its own place in those
tables and an edit writes only the lines it changed; a deleted line's place goes to the next line that fits. On your Asia
save the trains' tables sent 0.5 MB for a station placed and 1.4 MB for a deleted line (whole edits: 1.6 and 7.0 MB, the
GPU's wait 5-8 ms; before: 1.5 and 20.2 MB, 4-32 ms). The zoomed-out map no longer starts over after an edit either: the
simplified lines you see zoomed out are carried through it, only the changed part worked out again. The map and the
trains draw the same, pixel for pixel. Memory: about 3 MB more on the GPU on your Asia save.

## Why the tables moved (the code, before)

The train shader reaches every table through three indexes: a line row's first sample (`u_lines`.x), a profile's first
phase (`u_meta`.x) and a sample's first bend point (the `bendAt` code). The clock worker packs the tables end to end
in line order, so a line that changes length (or goes) changes every later line's index and values' place; the renderer's
kept textures (T-4262 `keepTexture`) compared them position by position, found every row after the change different and
sent them all. Since only the indexes place a line, the fix is to give each line its own place on the GPU and rewrite
just the three index tables; the network's arrays stay packed for every reader on the CPU (picking, the train guard,
crowds). Unlike the strokes (paint order, T-190) nothing here is drawn in order, so a slot can go anywhere.

## What changed

- `map/trainSlots.ts`: `planSlots` keeps each key's slot while it fits, frees the slots of keys gone or grown (joined
  with free runs beside them), places the rest best fit in the free runs, else past the last; laid out afresh (packed)
  only past the tables' room. Keys: a line's run 0 (id * 2), its return run's own way back (id * 2 + 1, T-3250), a line
  for phases. `putSlots` writes a key where its slot moved since that texture's last write, was blanked, or its values
  differ from the last write's (compared line by line, old packed slice against new, through each layout); exact texel
  spans, at most three `texSubImage2D` a slot. `bendAt` codes are shifted to their line's bend slot (0 past 2^24, as
  `bendTables` does).
- Room: phase slots get an eighth more (a stop more or less changes a line's phase count on most edits); sample and bend
  slots none (a line's samples change count only when its path does, and then they are all written anyway). The tables
  keep an eighth past the last slot and at least the largest slot and an eighth: on your Asia save line 33 alone holds
  67,891 of 317,343 phases, and deleting it grew lines 69 and 112 into its place, so its undo needed 67,891 free texels in
  one piece (the measured run below laid the phases out afresh there; the recorded rows replayed with the room: no fresh
  layout in two rounds of the six edits).
- renderer.ts: `setNetwork` plans the three layouts and puts samples, bendAt, bends, phases and stretch through them,
  the line table and profile meta rewritten (`slottedLineTable`, `slottedMeta`, kept textures as before); lanes
  (`putLane`), the fill (`setLayers`), the levels (`putLevels`) and `setService` through the same layouts. `freshFill`
  blanks only the lines written or moved since the fill was. `?perfOff=keepGl` lays out afresh and makes the textures
  anew each time, as before. `renderer.texSent` counts bytes sent per texture.
- Simplified copies (strokeLod.ts, rendererStrokes.ts `carryLod`): `patchSignificance` copies a set's significance over
  each kept run from a run start of both sets (`runBreak`: a break that does not depend on where the run began, and the
  old set's Infinity) to the last run start inside it, and works out only between (a run's tolerances depend only on its
  segments, and the runs from a run start only on the segments from there). `patchSimplified` makes the copy the view
  shows from the last one: merged segments over the copied ranges copied, the rest made; written into the copy's slots
  (copies are now slotted, a sixteenth of room: a quarter cost 5.7 MB for two zoom bands on Asia), the start of a kept
  run that continues an `a_full` run from before it written again. Other copies are dropped and made again from the
  carried significance when drawn (no significance pass). An edit that made more than a quarter of a set (and 20,000
  segments) drops them as before. Their widths and marks are rewritten through the slots (`writeSegs`).
