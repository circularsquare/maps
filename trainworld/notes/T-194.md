## How the network tag works

`map/networkTips.ts`, called from `trackTip` in `map/trackHover.ts`, so it is the track tag: the
same 0.1 s rest, the same pick order, the same element. A train or station `pick` hit gets its tag;
otherwise a point within 18 px of a drawn platform (where crowd bands lie over the lines) gets
that station's; otherwise, with nothing of the network under it, a demand circle containing the
point (the one whose centre is relatively nearest) gets its count. The circle's position and
radius are recomputed from the demand result as `demandLayers.ts` and the renderer's shader draw
them (`stationHaloRadius(zoom) * stationScale(riders)`, at the mean position of a complex's
constructed stations), so no renderer state is read beyond public accessors. The tag refreshes
each game minute while shown.

Follow-up for T-199 (crowd bands lighter, from zoom 14), done 2026-10-12: `CROWD_REACH_PX` (now
10 px) lives in renderer.ts beside the band's sizes, and `networkTips.ts` tests the renderer's
`crowdsShown` (crowds on, riders known, zoom 14 and in) instead of `stationStyle.boxes`.
