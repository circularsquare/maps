# T-1204, T-1220: the market's pair tables and its composing time (2026-10-24)

Docs: docs/demand-market.md ("What is kept per pair"), docs/demand-runtime.md. Before: notes/T-1199.md
(where a worker's memory went), notes/T-1216.md (the first bubble click's wait).

## What changed

`sim/src/demand/intercity.rs`:
- **Pair rules for open places only.** A composition kept a `PairRule` (48 bytes) for each of the
  6.6 million ordered pairs of the world's 2,567 places and worked them all out again at every
  composition. Now it keeps each open place's row and column (`Rules`), and any other pair's rule
  is worked out when asked (`pair_rule`), which only `corridor_modes` and the trip explorer
  between two places that are not open do.
- **The corridors' figures once a worker.** Each place pair's longer trips and commutes a day, the
  departures to places with no zone and the totals depend on the places alone when the world has
  rows, so `MarketPacks` keeps them (`Corridors`) from its first composition for every later one.
  They are worked out without the pair rules (the border and income factors per pair as
  `pair_trip_rule` has them, the border's general part once a place). The corridor index is a
  formula (`tri`), no table.
- **Each period's rail by corridor for the open places' corridors alone** (`open_corridor`):
  every period held two dense tables of 3.3 million corridors (53 MB) it filled only for the
  pairs of open places.
- `corridor_modes` works out only the modes part of a pair's rule (`pair_mode_rule`): 1.2 to 0.5 s
  natively.

`app/src/workers/demand.worker.ts`: the corridor table (the pairs with trips, sent to the page) is
made once for every market of the same corridors (`corridors_id`), not once a market part: tens of
MB of JS a part, and as many again to make.
