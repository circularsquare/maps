# T-2409 How far behind demand is, and the clock (2026-11-04, agent solveend)

Design: notes/T-2386.md "Proposed design". Anita's answers (2026-11-04), verbatim: "yes lets stop at
4x. lets also do 1734, and "how wrong" estimate if you find that theres a way we could do it in a
useful way. maybe say "time capped" instead of clock capped. and maybe "calculation 1 day behind"
instead of trips."

## Built (tree t1734, with T-1734 and T-2410)

- **The cap at 4x, never lower**, now also while any new line anywhere has no riders yet
  ("constructing...", `linesPending` kind "build") and a solve runs (`lineFigures.ts`). Before, the
  cap lifted once the zone in view was final, so panning away let a new line run at 16x and up,
  costing and earning nothing.
- **Behind**: the page keeps each edit's network with the game minute it came (`demandClient.ts`
  `uncovered`); a final drops every network up to the one it solved, so with T-1734 a final of an
  older network leaves the edits since. `demandBehindFrom` is the oldest left.
- **The tag** "Calculation 1 day behind" / "Calculation N days behind" (`StatusTag.tsx`) once a whole
  game day has passed since that edit while some figures in play still miss it: a line reading
  "constructing..." or "loading...", or the zone in view recomputing.
- Strings: "Clock capped at 4x while updating demand" -> "Time capped at 4x while updating demand";
  the speed button's "16x (capped at 4x while updating demand)" -> "16x (time capped at 4x while
  updating demand)".

## The "how wrong" estimate: what was kept

T-2386 proposed three measures: behind, missing and long. Kept: behind, shown only while something
is missing. Not built: "long" (lines edited since the last whole solve whose longer trips matter),
because T-1735's window, landed since T-2386's design was written, already re-solves the market's
pairs near an edit of 20 minutes or more in the local result, which was the case "long" stood for.
After a local result, lines are within about 2% of the whole solve (T-1733, T-2410's runs: worst
1.2% on China), so a tag counting days behind there would tell the player only that a slow solve is
running, not that anything shown is off. The tag therefore says how far behind the figures are only
while some of them are visibly incomplete; otherwise it stays quiet however far behind the whole
solve is.
