# T-139 — Overlapping demand bubbles and metropolitan focus

2026-10-11, Codex.

Anita's screenshots show a 26/day longer-trip circle larger than a 583-commuter circle near
Old Bridge, NJ, while inspecting a Midtown destination. These are separate models, spatial
grains and units: the commuter far end counts workers from local home/work gravity, whereas
the regional market generates a small number of longer non-work journeys. Its display scale
normalizes the busiest zone to 0.6 of a 6 km zone's area; commuters use fixed population
density scaling. Thus circle size was not comparable across the two simultaneous layers.
The difference in numbers is expected; the oversized overlap made it confusing.

## Changes

- Hide longer-trip zones in the focused market at all zooms, matching the existing regional
  overview exclusion. Longer trips inside that market remain in the inspector; other-city/town
  circles remain on the existing relative scale. Clarify that distinction in Settings and the
  selected-area inspector. Commuter hover now explicitly says commuters and describes their
  relationship to the selected home/work area.
- Export exact market membership with each zone's immutable x/y metadata in selection/station
  queries. No centroid containment or nearest-city guesses. Selection rows are 1+8*M header,
  4*N zone counts, then 3*N x/y/market metadata; station rows are 6*M header, 2*N counts then
  3*N metadata. Worker aggregation sums only counts/weighted place centroids, not zone metadata.
  Counts, routing, fares and saves are unchanged by this display work.
- City outlines are solid 0.8 px strokes at regional zooms. The focused city's two 0.75 px
  strokes remain visible up close, at offsets +0.45 px inside (opacity .18) and -0.45 px outside
  (opacity .48). [MapLibre's polygon offset convention](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-style-spec/layers/#line-offset)
  specifies positive inset, negative outset.
- Shade the basemap outside the focused pack at .075 opacity. Inverse geometry retains all
  islands and restores shading within excluded enclaves. Labels, network and demand stay above
  it; no click targets. Focus follows activeCity, which existing code pins during a bubble/
  station selection. Style reload recreates layers; stale asynchronous footprints cannot win
  over a newer focus, and cleanup invalidates outstanding loads.
- "City" here is the metropolitan pack. Old Bridge and much of northern NJ belong to NYC;
  those longer-trip circles should disappear, rather than treating the state line as a city edge.
