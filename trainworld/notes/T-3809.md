## What fails without waypoints

Scratch probe (`ferry/route.ts` in Node, the game's own tiles; scripts in the agent's scratchpad, `probe.mjs`,
`wayprobe.mjs`). 40 legs tried. Archipelagos route well: Turku to Nagu, Korpo, Houtskar and Mariehamn, Stockholm to
Vaxholm, Grinda, Moja, Sandhamn and Turku, Tsawwassen through Active Pass, Hong Kong's outlying islands, Split to
Hvar and Vis, the Cyclades, Onomichi to Setoda, the Kiel Canal, the Bosphorus to Varna. All within 1.0-1.5 times
the straight line, and where they go round it is real geography (Sai Kung to Tap Mun, 3.95x, is the way by sea).
Two real causes of "No water path found":

- **The search's box** (track-and-service.md 6.8): a leg is searched within its two ends plus 35% of their distance,
  then plus their distance (at least 3 km). Where the water path leaves that, the leg is `no-water-path`:
  Ma Liu Shui to Sai Kung (7.5 km straight, about 50 km by sea round the Sai Kung peninsula), Odda to Rosendal in the
  Hardangerfjord (34 km straight, about 90 by sea). Coves either side of a peninsula, fjord arms, and so on: this is
  what "unable to path through some archipelagos" most likely was.
- **400 km** (`MAX_LEG_M`): Piraeus to Rhodes (439 km straight), Manila to Cebu (575 km) are `too-long` ("or its too
  long or smth").
- A third, not reproducible in Node: tiles that fail to load also say "No water path found" in the game.

Waypoints fix both: each leg between two points is searched on its own. Ma Liu Shui, Tolo Channel's mouth, east of
the peninsula, Sai Kung: 16.9 + 14.9 + 19.1 km. Odda, the Sorfjord's mouth (6.66, 60.38), off Utne (6.60, 60.44),
Rosendal: 35.9 + 8.9 + 65.5 km. Piraeus to Rhodes through one waypoint off Paros: 194 + 279 km.
Widening the second box (a third try at three times the distance) would also find Ma Liu Shui to Sai Kung without
a waypoint, at the cost of slower refusals for waters that truly do not meet: left as a follow-up.

## The tool

Shift was unused in the ferry tool (MapLibre's box zoom is off outside Select), so a shift click on water puts a
waypoint. Design (ui-building.md "Drawing a ferry line", DECISIONS 2026-11-19):

- `ways[k]`: the waypoints between `ports[k]` and `ports[k + 1]`, kept beside the port list so the loop logic
  (closed, loop back, open) is untouched; every port edit keeps them (a port taken out partway joins its two legs'
  waypoints; opening a loop rotates them; a line that loops back runs its way out's waypoints backward home).
- Where a waypoint goes: the leg, and the place among its waypoints, where it adds the least straight distance. One
  rule for new lines, edits and loops; never after the last port (the sim needs ports at the ends).
- On land: refused, on the click and on a drop (the drag puts it back). The water worker answers in order, so the
  drop's check can wait for route searches; the tool is not held meanwhile, and Make line waits for it.
- Free: no price, no stop or dwell; saved as the route's kind 2 points, which the sim and the save (LFR3) already
  had. No Rust or save change. `begin` reads them back, so Edit route opens with them and an unchanged edit searches
  nothing. The panel's port list keeps a leg's waypoints while its two ports stay side by side.

## Strings

New (en.json, context.json):

- ferryDraw.shiftWay "Shift click to add a waypoint" (tag row, until the line has one)
- ferryDraw.addWay "Add waypoint" (tag, Shift held over water)
- ferryDraw.dragMove "Drag to move" (tag over a waypoint; reused word for word from the bus tool)
- ferryDraw.tagWayPorts "Place two ports first" (tag, Shift held, under two ports)
- ferryDraw.tagWayWater "Place waypoints on water" (tag, Shift held over land)
- ferryDraw.sayWayPorts "Place two ports before adding a waypoint." (notice)
- ferryDraw.sayWayWater "Place waypoints on water." (notice, a shift click or a drop on land)
- help.addFerryWay "Add a waypoint to a ferry route" (controls list, beside the reused "Shift click")

Over a waypoint the tag also reuses "Click to delete". Changelog: ferrywp-1, area track (no buses area exists).
