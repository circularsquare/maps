## What the dial turns

The terms that draw the network off its real scale, each a ratio of a hop's target to its real length, go to the
dial's power d:
- A's evening of hop lengths: a target over its real length is `(length / 1000 m)^-0.7` at 1, so the target is
  `1000 m x (length / 1000 m)^(1 - 0.7 d)` (the power 0.3 at 1, 0.44 at 0.8, 1 at 0);
- the lonely hops' (T-2289) and local hops' (T-2491) share, 0.4 at 1 (0.48 at 0.8);
- the big sparse regions' floor (T-2550), 0.25 at 1 (0.33 at 0.8).

Left alone: B's octilinear pull, the snap, the routing, the separation, pill spacing and the room for bundles, the water's
terms (hops across water at twice their real length at the local scale, bays at 1.25) and the blocks' gaps. So a
schematic stays a schematic, and dense cores still open up at 0: Manhattan is drawn at about 2.7 times its real scale
at every setting, since the room its pills and bundles need sets it, not the evening. What changes is how small the
sparse land is drawn beside the cores.

d = 1 is bit for bit master's first layout (`dialled(1)` returns the tuned options themselves: `pow` is not called and
the power is 0.3 exactly); the d = 1 figures below equal T-2550's tree figures where both measured them.

## The choice: 0.8

Anita leans a little less distorted than today and loves today's look. At 0.8 the drawing keeps today's character (the
cores as large on screen, the same schematic close up: `nyc-open.png`, `jpn-kanto-dark.png`) while the sparse land
comes about a quarter closer to the core's scale (Hokkaido 0.15 -> 0.18, northern Tohoku 0.14 -> 0.18, Aomori 0.20 ->
0.24, Long Island's far end 0.06 -> 0.09) and Kyushu's and Shikoku's shapes match better (.57 -> .65, .59 -> .70).
It had the best layout figures of the settings measured (parallel, Manhattan, crossings kept, overlaps). 0.75 draws a
little closer to geography still, with more doublings in all Japan (16, likely chaos). At 0.5 Japan reads most like
Japan, but Tokyo and Osaka shrink to small knots in a long country and New York's subway to a small knot among its
commuter lines, which is a different look; 0.5 also splits one river band on all Japan. Below 0.5 the commuter
railways fill the picture and close pairs rise (8, then 19).
