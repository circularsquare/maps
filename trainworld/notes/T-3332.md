# T-3332: one-track stations (scope)

Agent onestop, 2026-11-12, read-only. Anita: "i suppose since we're adding quad stations we should also allow single
stations... they snap to just one track of a double track (only one way stop) or on a one-track line they just dont
allow passing lol. is that doable?"

**Answer: doable.** About **11-16 hours** of agent time in four stages, after dbltrack (T-3296/T-3321) and lht (T-3297)
land, since all three touch capacity, `net.rs` and the station drawing. Context read in `.tw-trees/lht` and
`.tw-trees/dbltrack`; nothing touched.

## How it works today

- **A station is a node** splitting the piece it is on (`StationData`: platform length, name, upgrades). Its
  platform tracks are the piece's tracks: on double track one platform track per direction (`capacity::build_nodes`:
  `track = side_in`, the side the train arrives from), on single track one shared platform track (`track = 0` when
  `active_node_tracks == 1`).
- **Double track** is one piece with two lanes inside it (T-3137, `lanes.rs`). A path entry `edge << 1 | dir` is the
  lane on double track, so "one track of a double" and "one direction" are the same thing in the sim. The running side
  (`right_hand`, T-3297) says which physical lane carries which way.
- **Quad** is two double pieces 8 m apart. A station clicked on quad goes on the pair clicked (T-1687,
  `stationSnap.ts` `orderOverlapping`); a station level with one on the other pair takes its name (`twin_at`).
- **Single track.** A station on it has one shared platform resource and no stored passing loop, as the brief says,
  **but it is drawn as a passing loop**: since T-3212 two-way single track splits into two tracks through every
  station, trains stand on the two sides and meet there on screen (T-3202's guard). Capacity also treats a station as
  a place trains can meet: single-track sections are cut at every node with a platform (`build_sections`, the
  `node_platform == 0` test). The timetable does not schedule the meets yet (T-3219). One-way single track and single
  track joining one lane of a double already draw unsplit. **So the "one-track line" half falls short**: today every
  station on two-way single track is a passing place. A station where trains cannot pass is new, though small.
- **Lines** have one stop list; the return run is the path backwards with the same stops reversed (`build_run`,
  `match_stops`). Both runs stop at every stop. The one existing "served one way" idea is a one-way loop's stops
  (`line_stop_ways`, T-3122), which drives the map's half dots, the stop list and the hover's chevrons.
- **Demand** sees each line's stops forward then reversed (`route_nodes`, `packService`), both runs with the same stops.
  A one-way loop's run 1 gets `span` 0 everywhere, which turns its boarding off (`rn_tph` 0).
- **Placing**: the station bar has the platform length only. The track bar's count group has the 1-line, 2-line and
  4-line icons (`Icon.tracks1/2/4`); T-3321 puts the same 1-line / 2-line icons in the Select bar.

## Design

One idea covers both halves of her message: a **one-track station**, chosen with the same 1-line / 2-line icons.

- On **double (or quad) track**: the platform is on one lane only. Trains running that way stop; trains the other way
  run past on the other lane as at a plain node.
- On **two-way single track**: the station has no passing loop. Drawn as plain single track (no T-3212 split), the
  single-track section runs through it in capacity, and the guard does not let trains meet there.

### Sim

- **Stored**: one bit "one track" on the station plus one bit for the served way along the node's heading (save node
  flag bits 9 and 10; bit 8 is `named`; older saves read 0, both tracks). Store the **way**, not the physical side:
  lines, the timetable, capacity and demand all read directions, and the lane follows from the running side.
- **Placement**: `add_station` / `preview_station` take the lane (a point beside the centre line, as `set_route_join`
  does for single track, NaN for none). Allowed on a through node of double or quad track (two double ends) and on
  two-way single track; refused at a junction, a track end, and on one-way single (there it means nothing). Price: as
  a single-track station (`SINGLE_TRACK_STATION`, 0.6x; a 200 m ground station US$75M against US$125M).
- **Runs**: `build_run` marks a stop the run passes the wrong way as a **pass** (`Run.pass` per stop, kept in
  `stop_idx` so every per-stop array stays aligned with the line's stops). The profile drops passes from its stops and
  gives them a time from `profile::time_at`; their dwell is 0. The nine files reading `stop_idx` (capacity,
  overtake, boarding, money, related, wasm service) check `pass` where they count a dwell or a boarding.
- **Capacity**: the served lane gets the platform resource; the other lane's trains are through traffic
  (`PASSING_S` today goes on the platform; it should go nowhere). Turning there holds the one platform
  (`turn_places` 1). On single track without passing, `build_sections` joins the sections through the station.
- **Lines** (`check_line`): a line whose runs all pass the station the wrong way is refused ("Trains stop here only
  going the other way."). A line that ends there must arrive on the platform's lane; it then leaves on the other lane
  (the game's turns have no drawn crossover anyway). A two-way line stops one way and passes the other (call 1).
- **`line_stop_ways`** answers for every line, not only one-way loops, so the half dots and chevrons come for free.
- **Upgrades**: passing tracks and terminal not offered on a one-track station (passing tracks would be one more track
  for the served way only; later if asked). Park and ride, waiting room and passage as usual.
- **Changing it**: `set_station_tracks(node, one, way)`: blueprint free and undoable; built, see call 3. Lines that
  stop there are rechecked; a line refused by the change refuses the edit, listed by name, as one-way track does.

### Demand

- A route node can be a pass: no boarding, no alighting, no change there, and the ride goes through it
  (`Network::rn_pass` or a third `span` value; `build_graph` skips its board and alight edges). The snapshot sends
  each run's passes (`packService`), and the run's hop time into a pass is the time to pass the node, dwell 0.
- Riders wanting the other direction there walk to another station or ride on and change, as route choice already
  does. No new demand factor. Run both ceiling tests once (nothing should move without such stations).

### App

- **Station bar**: the 1-line / 2-line icons beside the platform length, 2 by default, remembered. With 1 on, on
  double or quad track the platform goes on the lane nearer the pointer once the two tracks part on screen (T-3137 C3's
  `joinPoint`, about zoom 16.7); zoomed further out, on the side of the centre line the pointer is on, the ghost
  showing the one platform. On quad that picks one of four tracks. On single track it makes the no-passing station.
- **Drawing**: one platform box on its lane (`stationGeometry.ts`, platform crowds, waiting room annex, car park);
  single track without passing drawn unsplit (T-3212's path for one-way single); the guard treats it as plain single.
- **Station panel**: the same two icons to switch, and "Reverse direction" (an existing string) on a one-track
  station on double track.
- **Line panel and schedule**: the stop list's half dot (already there for loops), the Schedule table and the Marey
  chart showing the pass one way.

### Interactions

- **Left-hand traffic (lht, T-3297)**: the way is stored, the lane derived, so a left-hand game puts the platform on
  the left lane with no extra code. lht's stage 3 flips a game that has track: the flip should turn the stored way
  round so the platform stays on its track (as joins stay), and list the station in the flip's sites, since lines
  then stop there the other way. Agent's call.
- **Double to single (dbl2, T-3321)**: a one-track station on double track rebuilt to single becomes a no-passing
  single-track station (one platform, the same physical track). **Single to double (T-3296)**: a no-passing station
  doubled becomes an ordinary both-tracks station, as stations there do now.
- **Quad and doubling up**: no change. A one-track station on one pair takes nothing from the other pair; doubling up
  passes it with a plain joint like any station.
- **Lengthening platforms (T-3286)**: the same along the track, refused where it would reach single track or a
  junction on double.
- **Joins (T-3137)**: a single branch joining one lane would make the station's node a junction, which a one-track
  station is not allowed on; track drawn from its node later is refused like a crossing under a platform (agent's
  call; the other choice is to turn it back into a both-tracks station).
- **T-3333** (a line on both quad pairs): a stop's twin must serve the same ways.

### Real cases

- **Paris Metro line 10's Auteuil loop**: Eglise d'Auteuil, Michel-Ange-Auteuil and Porte d'Auteuil are served only
  toward Boulogne, Chardon-Lagache, Mirabeau and Javel only toward Gare d'Austerlitz. Each is a station on one track.
- **Paris line 7bis**: Danube on the one-way loop, served in one direction.
- **Britain**: Pilning (one platform in use since its footbridge went, trains one way) and Reddish South (one train a
  week, one way) are one-direction stops on double track.
- **Trams on one-way street pairs** (Portland Streetcar's 10th and 11th Avenues): each stop served one way.
- **Single track with no loop**: a halt on a branch, in Japan a bosen-eki (one platform, no passing loop), common on
  rural lines.

## Stages

1. **Sim** (4-6 h): the station bits and save, placement with a lane, passes in runs and profiles, capacity (served
   lane, through traffic, turn, sections through a no-passing station), `check_line`, `line_stop_ways`,
   `set_station_tracks`, rebuild and flip hooks, tests both running sides.
2. **Demand** (2-3 h): passes in the kernel and the snapshot, a kernel test, both ceiling tests.
3. **App** (4-6 h): station bar icons and lane snap, ghost, drawing one platform and the unsplit single station,
   guard, station panel switch, schedule and Marey; a check with pictures.
4. **Docs, wiki, strings** (1 h): track-and-service.md "Stations", the wiki's stations page, DECISIONS.

**11-16 h.** Checked against: T-3137 C1+C2 (estimated 3.5-4.5 agent-days, built in about 8 h on 11-10/11-11) and C3+C4
(estimated 2.5-3, about 2 h); T-3212 (station split drawing, about 6-8 h from proposal to landing, mock review
included); T-3094 one-way single track (a few hours, sim and panel). This is about one T-3094 in the sim plus one T-3212
in the app, with a small demand change on top.
