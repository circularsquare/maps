# T-1809 Group view layout, second version (agent t1809, 2026-10-29)

Anita's playtest of the group view (a Tokyo group: three branches in, an express stretch, branches
out): lines forced straight, shared stations as long bars over wide gaps, every stop a row, a row
per line in the header, the bullets above the diagram, the Y's last branch floating, and the
bottom branches' stops interleaved. The rule as built is in docs/ui-inspectors.md "The group view";
this note holds how it works and the calls.

## How the layout works (`game/groupLayout.ts`)

1. **Rows**, one place each. The spine (most stops) first; each other line matched by place, turned
   round if needed, its own places put in as one block: between the rows it leaves and rejoins, or
   for a branch at an end, beside the shared row it leaves at, unless the block is longer than the
   rows already on that side, in which case it goes at the very end (or top) so the shorter bar runs
   beside it. This fixes both bugs: a branch never breaks (its bar runs on from where it leaves),
   and a branch's stops are one block.
2. **Together**, per pair of lines: from a stop both make to the next unless each makes stops the
   other does not in between (skip-stop: apart). The pair's together stretches name their ends
   (meet, part), and a stretch where only one of them stops between names its two ends (express
   stops beside a local). Termini and stops where lines outside the group stop are named too.
3. **Order** per row: clusters of together lines, contiguous; every arrangement (up to 5,040) costed
   as: within a cluster, for each pair, their distance in the order times the rows ahead they stay
   alike (together and the same stop or not, up to 60); across clusters, distance times the rows
   ahead they will be alike, discounted by how far; plus 8 a crossing against the row before; plus
   a tie-break by input order. The line that differs or leaves soonest ends up outside; the order
   changes where the likeness gained pays for the crossing (usually at a meeting).
4. **x**: each cluster at PITCH 7 (5 px bars, 2 px gap), clusters 24 apart; each cluster's centre
   wants its members' x on the row before; weighted least squares under the order and gaps (pool
   adjacent violators). So a line stays straight until it meets or leaves, a meeting bends to the
   middle, a parting pushes apart just enough.
5. **y**: a named row 18 px (taller with more than two crossing bars, as related lines), a tick row
   7, and extra room where a bend needs it (0.85 px down per px across). Bars are S-curves between
   rows where x changes. Bullets above the first stop and below the last, spread where several end
   at one place, moved out where another line runs through.

## Calls (DECISIONS.md)

- Copy line: a button in the Stops header opens a row of the group's bullets; a click copies that
  line. The line's own bullets (at its ends) open its panel, as the removed rows did.
- Branches are stacked, not side by side: a branch's block of rows comes after (or before) the
  other's, so the names read one branch then the other (her point 7). The cost is a bare bar beside
  the other branch's block (short in the default view, since most of its rows are 7 px ticks).
- Both ends of a stretch one line skips are named, not only the next stop: an express's stops all
  get names that way, which is what reads.
- A row every line passes stays as a bare 7 px row (a ghost there adds the stop), so a stop taken
  out can be clicked back in the default view.
- Floating bullets sit below the panel's sticky title, side by side where lines run close.

## T-1817 refinements (Anita's playtest, 2026-10-29)

- Ticks 4 px tall (2 px white, 1 px outline in `--net-ink`), rows 8 px; crossing bars 2 px and only
  across the row's own lines, 10 px past them (the "intermediate" she named; the diagonal between
  two crossings of one line was not built).
- Edit hovers carry the line's bullet: `tipBullet(letter, colour)` in ui/tip.ts puts a token in the
  `data-tip` text that the tag draws as a small chip.
- Tick rows zip: after naming, each unnamed row moves up into the first unnamed row after its lines'
  previous places that holds only lines apart from them (checked against the first analysis's
  together flags at both rows), then together is worked out again. So two tails of ticks share
  rows; named rows stay put, so a branch's named stops still read in order.
- The order is swept down, up, down (cost against the neighbouring rows' orders): the tie at the
  top of two branches (where no line is alike with another yet) took the input order before, and a
  later meeting could not undo it for the crossing it cost. Synthetic check "a line between two it
  shares with".
- Group pills: `legibleOn` (palette.ts), luminance capped at 0.45 on light, floored at 0.14 on dark,
  mixed toward black or white; text centred by its ink box (`inkShift`, ui/bullet.ts).
