# T-1418 marks coming apart from the map under load; T-1392 station circles off their line

## T-1418 (2026-10-26)

Probes (scratch, not kept): the real save, basemap layers hidden on a grey ground, a magenta MapLibre
circle 40 px west of an isolated station (Riverdale); fast CDP mouse drags; CDP screencast frames;
the station's white pixels against the circle, minus the offset at rest.

| Run | Moving frames | Max offset |
|---|---|---|
| 6x CPU throttle, z13 | 15 | 2.7 px (a train in the window) |
| 6x, Chrome pinned to one core, z13 | 13 | 0.8 px |
| 6x, pinned, z10 (globe) | 10 | trains in the window; crops show the dot on its line |
| 3x, z11 (globe) | 78 | 4.7 px, all trains crossing the window (checked by eye) |
| 6x, pinned, DPR 2 | 25 | 1.9 px |
| 6x, pinned, after the fix | 20 | 0.8 px |

The network canvas (stations, lines, trains) is drawn in MapLibre's own frame from the camera layer
(T-015, T-075) and stays glued under every load tried. What did lag: the platform faces
(`map/faces.ts`) drew on their own requestAnimationFrame with `map.project`, which runs before
MapLibre applies the frame's camera, so during a pan they were one frame behind; at 6x a frame is
100-250 ms, so the faces trailed by up to 86 px. Measured by hooking `map.project` from faces.ts
against the camera of each MapLibre `render` (Times Square, z15.5, clock running, 6x):

| | MapLibre frames with faces | Frames off by > 1 px | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 81 | 35 | 54 px | 86 px |
| after | 63 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Fix: faces draw from `Overlay.afterMapFrame`, and their own loop skips a frame MapLibre has queued
(`Overlay.mapFrameQueued`, shared with the overlay's clock loop). Also `sizeCanvasLike` (gl.ts) now
copies MapLibre's canvas buffer and CSS size: MapLibre rounds the buffer and styles the canvas at
buffer / ratio (1203.2 px for a 1203 px container at 1.25), where ours was styled at the whole px,
a stretch of up to 0.2 px across the screen.

Left as designed (T-1207): station names, capacity tags and attraction marks hold still through a
zoom (and a globe pan below zoom 12) between full placements, at most 100 ms.

Screenshots: `artifacts/trainworld/t1418/` (faces-pan-before/after.png, station-pan-6x-pinned.png,
frames/ and faces/ hold the screencast frames).
