# T-1610: clicked zone waits for its refine, 2026-11-23 (agent zonerefine)

Result: the wait no longer happens on master. No code changed; the task can be closed.

## What was measured

Tokyo save (data/packs/real/tyo.save, 5 zones: tyo, tcg, gnm, yns, szo), my Vite 6148, 3 cores, the machine busy
(cores.py 8-14 of 14 claimed). Every station in the save is in zone tyo, so for a station click tyo was made coarse
by hand (client.fine / lodApply in the page, view in Shizuoka so tyo was not in view); the other zones were coarse too.
The zone in view was kept from settling (2 s rule) by patching heldZones in the page, so the bubbles could be found
before the click; the click itself then makes tyo held, which starts its refine, as for a player.
Times are from the click (act.press) to the answer of the query the overlay or panel asks, taken by wrapping the
client's methods in the page.

| click | tyo coarse, auto mode (refine starts at the click) | tyo fine (baseline) | coarse setting on (T-4117) |
|---|---|---|---|
| station (panel: `transfers`) | 44 ms | 28 ms | 36 ms |
| bubble, far end (`flows`) | 1,028 ms | 670 ms | 1,316 ms |
| bubble, trips between zones (`intercitySelection`) | 1,404 ms | 791 ms | 1,764 ms |

The refine of tyo (a whole zone, this busy machine) took about 100 s from the click to the figures landing (status tag
"Calculating trips here..." and `refiningZone` set for all of it). The clicked bubble's far end was drawn 1.0-1.4 s
after the click, so the click does not wait for the refine: 1.0 s of a 100 s refine is about 1% of what a wait would
be, and the answer is the coarse samples' at once. The map's bubbles and the bubble sums are asked again when the refine
opens the zone fine (about 5.5 s after the click) and again when its figures land.

With the setting on (every zone coarse, mode "coarse") nothing refines on a click, so there is nothing to wait for;
the clicked zone stays coarse and answers from the coarse samples (the table's last column).

## Why it went

T-1206 section 5 planned that far ends and purposes "wait for the refine, never from coarse samples". As built
(T-1223, T-1609, T-2605) the queries go to the solve in hand (viaRound/target), which has the coarse zone's samples
open; the refine is a separate reopen plus a local solve (refineAside) and its answers replace the shown ones when it
lands. Nothing in the click path (commuterView farWaiting, StationInspector, tripView) reads roughZones or refiningZone.

## Not covered

clicked zone's refine; not part of this task and not measured.
