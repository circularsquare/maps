# T-2775: market check around a join (agent c1, 2026-11-16)

New `checks/market_check.py` (in worktree branch tw/c1): reads the registry and every registered `data/packs/<id>.market.json`
(4,045 files, about 2 minutes, read only). Exit 1 and a line per problem for: a registered zone with no market file or a file
for a zone not in the registry, a place index that differs from the registry's, parallel arrays of different lengths, commute or
outing rows naming a home zone, place or zone index that does not exist ("invalid commute between zones"), zones that differ from
the file's own sites. `--cells` also works the zones out from the pack's cells (what T-2702's thl/tug failure was: 159 against 158);
`--only id,id` limits that. `--save f` writes each pack's zone, site and row counts and `--against f` lists the packs a join changed.
Rows added to docs/adding-a-country.md (tools table) and docs/code-map.md.

First run on today's data: one problem, shz: its market file says place 7423, the registry 2598, and its zones from cells are 84 against
the file's 82. Not touched (a run or join may be rewriting it; `stale.py`-style fix: `world_run.py step market_zones shz`).
