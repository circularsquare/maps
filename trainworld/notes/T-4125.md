## What changed (app only; no Rust, no worker change)

- `map/snapIndex.ts`: the node grid now comes in two sets, track nodes (as before) and junctions (three tracks or more), and `nearestNode(w, x, y, set)` finds the nearest by widening the reach (256 local units, x4) until the nearest candidate is within it; past 65,536 or with no grid yet it scans all nodes as before. Ties go to the first in the world's order, as the scan.
- `map/capacityMarkers.ts`: `tramSpot` and `nearestJunction` call it.
- `game/world.ts`: `stopsOf` names a bus stop from the named stations in nearby 2,048 m squares (a grid per stations list, kept while no station changed), reach 1,000 ground m times the Mercator scale times 1.1; `lineIndicesAt(w)`, the lines stopping at each station as indices.
- `ui/inspector/LineInspector.tsx`: `transferLines` takes its candidates from `lineIndicesAt`, in the save's order, then the same filter and sort.
