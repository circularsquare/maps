# T-2186: trains fill to 150% (2026-11-03)

## The rule as built

- A 20 m car: 44 seats, rated 160 (100%), crush 240 (150%) (`RATED_PER_CAR`, `CRUSH_PER_CAR` in sim/src/demand/api.rs,
  sim/src/track/boarding.rs, app/src/game/boarding.ts). A bus is half a car's seats and rated load, 80, and full at
  80 (demand's `line_crush` for cars 0.5 is 0.5 x 160; the account's `BUS_CRUSH` 80).
- The queue (`capacity_fit`), the wait for a train with room (`left_behind`) and the boarding account fill to crush.
- Crowding (`crowd_factors`) counts against the rated load (`Network::line_rated`): x = (load - 112) / 48 a car,
  1 + x(1 + x), unclamped: a minute aboard counts 1 at 70% (112), 3 at 100%, 5.4 at 120%, 7 at 130%, 10.8 at 150%.
  Same curve as before up to 100%; past it the same formula runs on (continuous, rising, no new factor).
- What the player sees: the train's gauge on the map full at 100%, then a second band (the dark shade 65% of the way
  to the warning red) filling from the back again, whole at 150% with the red rim. The train inspector's bar runs to
  crush with ticks at the seats and at 100%, accent to 100% and orange (`--warn`) past it (red sat too close to the pink theme's accent in the dark shade), state "Crowded" past 100%. Average load
  (line panel and track hover) counts against 160 and may pass 100%, at most 150%. Crowding dots unchanged.

**Why the gain is a quarter of T-2147's.** T-2147's flag moved the crowding start with the ceiling (to 168 a car, 105%,
and 3 at 150%); this rule keeps it at 112 with 3 at 100%. With the same trains nobody is turned away now on
Anita's Seoul lines: the crowding price holds them at 120-130% of rated, short of the 150% ceiling, so the ceiling
seldom binds and the riders it freed mostly stay put off by the crowding. A gentler line past 100% (straight on at
the slope it has there: 5.0 at 120%, 8 at 150%) would change little, as the lines settle at 120-130%, where the two
differ by under 15%.

**Done when.** A crowded line needs fewer trains to stop turning riders away (Seoul: 274k turned away to none, same
trains). The rail figures move by about a quarter of T-2147's, for the reason above. Starting the crowding at 100%
(T-2147's proposal) is what gives T-2147's figures; that is Anita's call.
