## Demand cache
No manual bump exists to make. The cache key's demand part (`DEMAND_KEY`, workers/demandCache.ts, T-3926) is a SHA-256 of the demand WASM and the demand modules' source, so changing access.rs changes the WASM and every old cached result is dropped on the next build.
