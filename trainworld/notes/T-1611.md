## What changed (every figure the same to the bit)

1. `cpair_of` and `epair_of` gone: `cpairs` and `epairs` are in commute and outing order, so the
   index of one is a binary search (`IntercityApi::cpair_of`, `epair_of`), read only by the bubbles'
   and the trip explorer's queries. -298 MB.
2. `Gravity::set_walk_fine` shrinks `wf_*` to their length: grown a zone's row at a time they held
   40.9 million entries' room for 28.6 million. -141 MB.
3. A period's market corridor figures held sparse (`Sparse`: the nonzero entries, averaged across
   rounds and scaled as the dense table, read with a binary search). -161 MB.
4. `DemandApi::new` drops the pack's own gravity block (`City::gravity`) once the zones and gravity
   are built from it (its cells' zone and the zones' factors, a copy of what `z` and `gr` hold).
   -58 MB.
5. The everyday layer's per-purpose `live` cell lists shrunk to their length. -34 MB.

## Left, and why not now

What still holds the most: the coarse zones' packs and everyday layers (about 1.6 GB, the same
pack data a fine zone holds: a coarser level would have to drop parts of the model there, such as
the cell-to-cell walks, which changes their figures), and the market's composition (0.7 GB, which
grows with the packs open, the worker-dealing redesign T-1560's ground). The refine's skims cache
(250 MB, two station x station tables over all of China) is exact as it is; one table fewer would
search again when two graphs alternate.
