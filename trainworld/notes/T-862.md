# T-862 Holidays

## Stage 1 (done 2026-10-21)

- Calendar: `pipeline/holidays_json.py` writes `app/src/game/holidays.json` from the Python
  `holidays` package (0.83, MIT; installed into the maps venv): 250 countries, national public
  holidays only, no "observed" substitute days, English names. 239 kB raw, loaded as its own chunk
  after the top bar first renders (`loadHolidays`). Kosovo (XK, 2 zones) is not in the package and
  has none.
- Game years take the holidays of the real non-leap year with the same January 1 weekday
  (Mon 2029, Tue 2030, Wed 2031, Thu 2026, Fri 2027, Sat 2033, Sun 2034), so Thanksgiving stays a
  Thursday and Easter moves between game years; the cycle is seven game years.
- Day 1 moved from January 1 to Monday January 27 (`DAY1_OF_YEAR` in game/days.ts): no country with
  registered zones has a holiday that day and only one in its week. Year 0 is real 2031.
- Marks: `holidaysNear` (game/holidays.ts) lists holidays in countries the player has stations in
  (a station's zone's country and local time), spanning their country's local day, converted to
  the local time in view. The sky draws each as a pink ribbon along its top with a tick at its
  start; the sky's hover lists them ("Tomorrow: Thanksgiving Day, United States"); on the day itself
  a pink dot sits before the date and the date's hover names it.
## Stage 2 (T-872, built 2026-10-21)

Built as designed below, with three simplifications:
- No account key change in `boarding.rs`: accounts are already kept per kind of day, so a holiday
  only changes which one `kind_at(t, line)` reads.
- Running cost is not split by country: it does not depend on the kind of day. `localGroups` keeps
  one group per local time with a `parts` list per country for fares and rides; no line filter
  in `running_cost_in`.
- The worker resends holidays (`syncHolidays`) before each read of the account by day (the hour's
  trips, today/past_day) and after each state build; it sends only when the lists change.
The view's country is the one under `viewCentre` (set on move end).
Check: `checks/t872-holidays.mjs` (real save, Thanksgiving day 305: riders 14.90M and fares equal
Saturday's, Wednesday 19.70M; ledger fares 2070 US$M Thursday and Saturday, 2871/2869 Wednesday and
Friday).

## Stage 2 design: a holiday runs as a weekend day in its country (T-872)

Where the kind of day is read today (always `day_kind_at` / `dayKindOf` on the day number alone):

1. **Boarding account, sim** (`sim/src/track/boarding.rs`): `kind_at(t)` with `t` in the line's
   local time, five call sites (trip loads, the hour's account, transfers' feeders, the platform
   tally, a train's load). Every one has the line id at hand.
2. **Money, clock worker** (`workers/clock.worker.ts` `localGroups`, `game/moneyTiming.ts`
   `between`): lines are grouped by local time (`running_shifts`, `line_shift`); each group's fares,
   rides and running cost per hour read `dayKindOf(day)`.
3. **The view's "Today"** (`game/clock.ts` `currentDay`): the demand views' kind of day.
4. **Line inspector** (`LineInspector.tsx`): the line's level and kind now.

The demand solve itself needs nothing: it already has a weekday and a weekend result per region.

Design:

- **A line's country** is its first stop's zone's country, the same rule as its local time
  (`Network::line_shift`). The clock worker knows both (it imports cities and localTime).
- **Sim** (`boarding.rs`, `wasm.rs`, about 60 lines with tests): `Boarding` keeps
  `holiday_days: HashMap<u32 line, Vec<i64>>`, the line's holiday days (local game days) in a
  window, set by `TrackApi::set_line_holidays(packed)` (line id, count, days...). `kind_at(t)`
  becomes `kind_at(t, line)`: 1 if the day is in the line's list, else `day_kind_at(t)`. The account
  key hashes the line's days so a changed list redoes it. Nothing about calendars or countries in
  Rust: the worker sends days.
- **Clock worker** (about 50 lines): imports holidays.json (one more chunk in the worker), and on
  each network change and each new game day sends every line's holiday days from yesterday to 8
  days ahead. Money groups split by (shift, country) instead of shift; `between` and `lagged` take
  a `kindOf(day)` per group. A group's running cost needs `running_cost_in(level, shift)` narrowed
  to the group's lines: add a line filter (a list of ids) to that call, or sum `line` costs on the
  worker side (regionLines already has per-line costs).
- **Main thread** (about 15 lines): `currentDay` is 1 on a holiday in the view's zone's country;
  the line inspector reads the line's country.
- **Checks**: `cargo test` (a holiday hour reads the weekend account; a line without a list reads
  the day number's kind), `checks/t488-money-days.mjs` (whole days still settle), smoke.
- **Cost**: a focused agent, roughly 300-500k tokens. The sim part is in `track/`, not the demand
  code T-854 is editing, but `wasm.rs` is shared: keep the edit to one method.
- Open after it: whether a holiday's eve (Christmas Eve, a Friday before a long weekend) should be
  anything but a weekday. Not proposed.
