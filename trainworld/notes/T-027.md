## What the shell does

- **Top bar**: city, mode split (train first, at 18 px), cash, day, time, demand level name,
  pause and three speeds. The clock is its own component, so a game minute re-renders one node.
- **Dock**: tabs build, lines, stations, money, city and a gear for settings. The tab area is 36%
  of the dock, fixed, and scrolls; the inspector sits under it. Checked: the inspector's top is
  at 360 px on all six tabs.
- **Inspector**: blank by default (T-048). Line: name with rename in place, colour
  swatch (steps through the palette until there is a picker), schedule rows high/medium/low
  under "Demand" with steppers, headway and trains, the current demand level in bold, running
  cost, riders, fare stepper, stops with each stop's time from the start of the line next to its
  name and boardings, round trip. Station: level, platform length, lines with boardings here.
  Train: towards, next stop, distance along the line (updates each game minute). Track,
  junction, cell, zone: placeholders saying what will show.
- **Map**: the demo lines through the overlay renderer: white casing, selected line wider and on
  top, station dots (bigger at transfers), capsule trains in a darker line colour, as many as
  the current demand level needs (rebuilt when the level turns or a stepper changes it).
  Station names as DOM labels above the overlay. Click picks station, then train, then track
  (its line); hover shows a pointer; empty click or Escape clears.
- **Build toolbar** in the map's corner: track and station tools (no action yet), level -3..+3,
  and the selected level's name, price and multiplier (SPEC 6.4, with +3 1.8x, +2 1.3x).
  Build tab: double or single track, flat or flying junctions, the water rule.
- **Settings (T-037)**: track colour by line or height (with a level key), show trains, station
  names, capacity markers, map labels, theme pink/blue/green (renamed in T-049). All wired except capacity
  markers, which have nothing to show until T-031. Remembered in localStorage, guarded.
- **Workers**: `clock.worker.ts` loads the WASM and answers `init`; edits are refused. The
  protocol types (edit ops, network/keyframes/lines/capacity messages, demand messages) are
  stubs for T-025/T-026 and assume no particular keyframe layout beyond today's renderer.
- `?debug=1`: readout (draw ms, MapLibre ms and frames/s, GPU ms, frame interval, clock, worker)
  and `window.tw` probes. Off by default.

## Checks (headless Chrome, real GPU, Chrome on 2 cores)

- Type check and build pass; bundle 1,103 kB (306 kB gzipped), +52 kB / +19 kB gzipped over the
  spike for Preact, signals and the shell.
- No element has a transition or animation; text uses 12, 14 and 18 px and weights 400 and 700.
- Themes switch the tokens; map labels off hides all 16 visible basemap symbol layers; station
  names toggle; a stepper edit rebuilds trains; rename, swatch, Escape, every inspector kind work.
- T-015 lag probe in the new app while dragging: 0 frames off (demo and 10k synthetic).
- CPU, % of one core: playing 15-26%, paused 0-3%, dragging 47-79%. The drag figure swung that
  much between identical runs while other agents were building; the spike measured 37% on a
  quieter machine. Not settled: follow-up T-045.

## Known rough edges

- Station names do not collide with basemap place names (T-032), so "Journal Sq" can sit by
  Positron's "Journal Square".
- The demo's trains run at constant speed out and back without stopping (spike keyframes).
- Fonts load from Google Fonts; offline the UI falls back to the system font.
