## What was built

- **Pipeline** (`pipeline/market_zones.py [ids]`, about 40 s for all 20 zones): per zone a market
  pack `data/packs/<id>.market.json` (sites in 600 m squares, each 6 km zone's ground and tail
  normalisers over the world field at its centre, each site's r5 cell people, parking, commutes);
  the Northeast's sites and commutes are taken unchanged from `data/packs/intercity.json` (the
  joint commute gravity's file, still written by `intercity.py`, never read by the game now), in the
  market frame; every other zone's are made from its own pack, in its own frame. Then
  `bin/market_pack.rs` (1.1 s) composes all packs with the kernel's code and writes each place's
  registry row (`marketRow`) and the trips of every place pair under 250 km; the script merges them
  into the registry (`place` for every zone, `market.rule`, places 14-20 appended). `market` keeps
  its old meaning (the joint gravity's Northeast, -1 elsewhere), so the joint gravity's choice of
  packs is unchanged. `outside.py` and the derived block are gone.
- **Kernel** (`intercity.rs`, rewritten around a shared `Market`): a composition of the world (rule,
  rows) and the packs open; places near (< 250 km) pair 6 km zone to 6 km zone, far ones at their
  centres' distance spread by each zone's departures and attraction; pairs built only between
  served zones on one track component and one landmass. `MarketPacks` holds parsed packs in the
  worker and composes them; `set_network_ll` takes stations as lon/lat; `selection_all_day`
  answers all modes over any periods with no solve; `zone_boxes` gives world zone ids and degree
  boxes; answers lay out every 6 km zone of the world (5,894). An old single pack opens as a closed
  world (fixtures, and the joint gravity's check of what it writes).
- **Each place is its own group of sites** for access and rides: with groups by frame, the 2 km
  ride zones of the Northeast's group had their grid origin at the westmost open pack, so New York's
  loads differed (9,082.7 against 9,080.5 on a segment) by which Northeast packs were open; now a
  place's figures never depend on which other packs are open (t149: 0 difference).
- **App**: a zone's market pack is a load item (`market:<id>`) opened in the solving workers with
  the zone (viewed, beside, reached, or a station within 30 km of its box) and closed as the zone
  closes; the worker recomposes on each open or close, and a part keeps its composition while its
  cluster's key lasts. Clusters key on world zone ids. Every zone has bubbles, the city overview
  and the inspector's places (`place` in place of `market`).

## Decisions (DECISIONS.md)

- A far pair's trips split over 6 km zones by each end's departures over the normalisers (ground and
  tail apart) and attraction (ground and tail apart) at the places' centre distance: the zones of a
  far pair add up to the places' figure exactly, so the registry row, the corridor and the solved
  pairs agree.
- A pair with no served path (no station at an end, no track between, or no car: another landmass)
  is shown as any unserved pair is: all its trips, none by train, red in the bubble's mode mix; no
  special mark until T-378 adds air.
- "Inter-region trips" keeps its name and hover: it is still visits, shopping and business between
  places. Its places now include every zone of the world.
