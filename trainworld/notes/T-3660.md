## Cause

Both units are in geoBoundaries EGY ADM2 (365 features, no duplicate shapeIDs) and not in `state.pkl` (363 EGY units,
every one of them in the file). Keyed by shapeID throughout: the file has 21 duplicated names, two "North Coast" among
them (37247803B38142631960117 is ale's; 37247803B36627997079635 is the lost one).

- Each lost unit's representative point lies in no EGY ADM1 polygon, and neither overlaps any other ADM2 unit by 1%.
  `country_units` takes the chosen level's units into the first-level unit holding their point; before T-2695 (commit
  8ca54120, 2026-11-07) a unit whose point fell outside every simplified first-level unit was in no unit at all.
- The plan state was built before that rule and has never been rebuilt (`--fresh` recomputes every country); T-2695 and
  T-2702 patched the state and the rows country by country for the large holes (ARG, BRA, MEX in the state; CN, KR, VN,
  MY, BN rows only), and T-2897 left Egypt's two under the 10,000 line. Readers of the state (`state_units` for builds of
  `plan_units` countries, `plan_zones`' state path for `plan_units` countries and countries with no zone rule) never
  applied the rule.
- T-2897's "North Coast (3,550), Port Suez (153)" do not match these units (225 and 409 outside packs by
  `checks/state_gaps.py`, which keys by shapeID); unexplained (its kit's gaps.py is gone).

## Fix (tree, `pipeline/world_plan.py`)

- `gb_file_units(iso, adm)`: a geoBoundaries file as fetched, on the de facto lines, far parts apart, no gap fill (Canada's
  takes over an hour) and no cache write.
- `state_lost(st, iso, have)`: the units of the state's level the state lacks that the state's units leave half of
  (country_units' T-2695 test), by id and geometry, never by name; a unit mostly on another country's Natural Earth land
  and under a tenth on its own is skipped (de_facto's test: Venezuela's Zona en Reclamacion, 142,622 km2, which Guyana
  holds and zones).
- `state_units` (builds) and `plan_zones`' state path (zones) both add them, after ABSORB: with them in ABSORB's list its
  Voronoi ties (STRtree order) moved 22 km2 of desert from Kharga (ghy) to Paris Paris and swapped slivers between four
  Asyut units; after it, no existing unit's geometry changes (`check_state.py`: 349 -> 351 units, 0 changed). In
  plan_zones a rescued unit takes the parents of the unit it shares the longest border with, and its people from GHSL.
- Cost per build: see "Timing" below.
- Not changed: `checks/split_zones.py` and `checks/zone_new_origins.py` read state.pkl themselves (exclave kinds,
  origins); a rescued unit is missing there until the state is rebuilt. The metro planner (`main`, cached state) also
  still plans on the old units; `--fresh` would rebuild every country.
- ABSORB's Voronoi ties go by STRtree order, so any change to Egypt's unit list (a fresh read, a new unit) moves desert
  slivers between neighbours; harmless today, worth sorting `near` if Egypt is ever re-read whole.

Checks: `state_units("EGY")` holds both (North Coast 11.02 km2, 225 GHSL people; Port Suez Police Department 5.57 km2,
563, 409 outside packs). Dry zones (`dry_zones.py EGY`, the tree's code, written to scratch): 351 units, the two free; with
every Egyptian zone built they make no zone of their own (under the floor, "no zone beside it") and stay dead, as Halayib.
