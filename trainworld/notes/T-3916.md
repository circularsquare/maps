# T-3916 Trips home lose their loads at drive-to-only stations

homeload, 2026-11-21.

## Cause

A commute's trips home are the home -> work station pairs turned around (`DemandApi::pass`), so a
trip home's destination is the station its trip out started from. The assignment walks only the
destinations the strategy search reached (`assign_inner` skips a destination with no slot; its
`debug_assert` is off in release and agent builds), and the commute searched only
`egress_stations`: the walk entries of subzones with jobs. A station reached only by ride entries
(people drive to it) was never searched, so the trips home towards it were counted as rail trips,
entries and exits, but never ridden: no boardings at the work end, no alightings at home, no load
on the line. Everyday trips already marked home ends with walks and rides (T-389), and the market
has its own list; only the commute missed it.

Entries and exits cannot show this: `DestAssign::take` counts them before the slot check. Only
boardings, alightings and line riders do.

## Fix

`kernel::egress_stations` (kernel/search.rs) now marks every access entry, walk and ride, of
subzones with homes, as well as the walks of subzones with jobs. Searching more destinations
changes nothing else (each destination's search is independent), so where every station was
already searched the figures are the same to the bit. Not in the functions tramerr (T-3909) is
changing.

## Evidence

Rust: `trips_home_end_at_stations_people_drive_to` (api/tests.rs), a two-stop line from a station
16 km east (4 km past the last cell, nobody walks to it, homes drive to it) to the centre. Before
the fix, period 0: 7,842 boardings out, 0 alightings back. After: alightings / boardings at that
station = TO_HOME / TO_WORK in every period.

In the game, a new NYC sandbox game with one line, Battery Park (-74.0135, 40.7035) to a station
in the Lower Bay (-74.05, 40.50), about 6 km from any shore:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| rail trips a day | 14,936 | 14,936 |
| Bay line riders a day | 11,293 | 14,936 |
| of them work | 3,608 | 7,251 |
| bay station work boardings / alightings | 3,608 / 0 | 3,608 / 3,643 |

The missing 3,643 riders are exactly the work trips home. Rail trips a day do not move: they come
from mode choice, before the assignment.

The "before" column is this tree with the old condition put back and its WASM rebuilt, not a
master build.

Real save (T-007-real-nyc.save), final round, before and after: rail trips 12,886,431 both, every
line's riders the same, 4 of 1,756 stations off by 1 boarding or alighting (two pairs at the
network's edge: Bridgeport / Derby-Shelton, New Hamburg / Poughkeepsie; not looked into). The low
work alighting ratios at Ramsey Route 17 (0.35) and Glen Rock (0.48) did not move, so they are not
this bug. The round-0 view showed stations like Radburn with far fewer work alightings than
boardings; not looked into, gone by the final either way.

Reach: stations no subzone with jobs walks to, but homes drive to: ferry ports out on the water,
lines built out into empty country. Ordinary park-and-ride stations, which some jobs walk to, were
never affected. Bus and tram stops reached only by a drive not measured. Calculation time not
measured; the wider list can add searches where a population drives to stations of a big network
it does not walk to.

New work, not filed: `assign_inner` drops flow towards an unsearched station silently in release
(its `debug_assert` is the only guard), and entries and exits are counted before that check, so
an "every rail trip is one entry and one exit" assertion cannot catch a loss like this one.
