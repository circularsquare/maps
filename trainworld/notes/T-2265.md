# T-2265: no train slower than walking, in real minutes

Anita, 2026-11-03: "\"only a little quicker than walking, so fewer take the train\" we should be
comparing actual train time to actual walking time, not effective train time to effective walking
time. we should not take into account fare or crowding. this check should only be to rule out
ridiculous train trips that are literally longer time than wlaking".

## What changed

T-1782's fade (rail's share scaled from none at walking's perceived cost to all of it at 30% under)
is gone, and T-1059's per-station-pair walking rule now counts real minutes instead of straight
metres: `Skims::walk` holds each station pair's real minutes (half the accepted headway at each
boarding, rides, walks between stations and platforms at the change's pace; no crowding, fare,
left-behind wait, span or fixed change minutes), stored as metres walked at walking's pace (76.9 m a
minute). A pair is refused when the way to its first station (a walk's metres, a drive's real
minutes), those minutes and the walk from its last take longer than walking the whole way, within
walking's 10 km reach. A trip with any quicker pair keeps its whole share. T-1059's old test is
inside the new one (walks longer than the whole walk take longer too).

which has no buildings and is in the US).
