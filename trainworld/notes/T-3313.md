## Causes

- **The clock worker's queue held by a download that never answered (T-3196, fixed in v0.3).** Rinkalk and Kanto
  are the two reports T-3196 was opened for. Kurimeshi fits the same stall: the page draws trains from the
  clock worker's hour of trips, and while the queue was held no new hour came, so every train on the map
  finished the trip it had and was gone at its terminal. The count raise was most likely the edit stuck
  behind a held Construct or ghost; a count edit itself downloads nothing. The Rust test
  `a_raised_count_keeps_every_train_running` (transition_tests.rs) rules out the sim: raising 3 to 4, 3 to
  6, 4 to 12, 6 to 40 and 2 to 3 on double and on single track keeps at least as many trains drawn two to
  four hours on and no train leaving service. Passes on master.
- **A demand download that never answered (new here, fixed).** The demand pool waits for every pack a solve
  needs to open in every worker, and the pack fetch (`demandClient.fetchPackFile`), the market rows and
  drives, zone boundaries and place lists had no time limit. A request the network drops without an error
  holds every solve behind it: riders, shares and the money they make stop changing until a reload, and a
  new station's riders stay at 0. Fits "values don't update" (both) and "station riders stay at 0".
  Reproduced on master: `checks/t3313-pack-stall.mjs` builds a line after holding the first request for
  nyc.bin, nyc.market.json and market-drives.bin; no riders within 120 s. Fixed: no riders after 59 s.
- **Money and costs about 150x on another device: the currency, by design (T-2798, T-2860).** The money's
  currency is a per-browser setting; a player who never chose starts on the currency of the first browser
  language with a region. A browser whose languages are `ja` alone (common on desktop Chrome) or `ja, en-US`
  starts on US dollars; one with `ja-JP` (an iPhone, a Mac) starts on yen at 150 to the dollar. Cash and every
  price scale alike, so nothing is dearer. Signing in does not carry the setting.
- **mizushin's "same device, figures don't update" (v0.3.1): not settled.** Station names change only by the
  player, so the likeliest reading is that the account copy did not reach this device (a tab left open on
  device A keeps its own game by design, "Autosave is off here because this game was opened on another
  device"; a slot with an unsent change splits into two versions of one card). A cloud call that never
  answered held every later sync in this browser (`cloud.ts` had no time limit); now fixed with the rest. A
  stalled demand pool would also stop transit share. Reading mizushin's account games would settle it; the
  agent's attempt to list them on the server was refused by the permission check.
- **qwas, "data couldn't be loaded" (v0.2, Safari 16).** In Japanese this is the loading card's
  `loadingScreen.demandTimeout` ("Demand figures did not load after {time}"), the watchdog after 3 quiet
  minutes. Either a held download (the fix above) or a slow device. Safari 16.0 to 16.3 cannot run the game
  at all (WASM SIMD needs 16.4) and would instead say the simulation could not start.

## Ruled out

- A line, station or id limit (T-3196 checked: none).
- The sim dropping trains on a count raise (the Rust test above).
- A deadlock between the clock worker's message queue and its account queue: every account call from the
  message queue is fire and forget (`void`), and the one that waits (`deletePlaying`) has a 5 s race.

## Fix

- `workers/patientFetch.ts`: `fetch` that gives up after 45 s without headers or without a byte of the body
  (the clock restarts at each chunk, so a big pack on a slow line still loads); the account's calls 120 s, as
  an upload counts toward the wait for headers. Used by the demand packs, market rows and drives, zone
  boundaries, place lists, the walking barrier's head (`walks.ts`), the real-network graphs, real-network
  starts, coarse outlines, the bus route tool's road tiles and its road list, the clock worker's account sync
  (`cloud.ts`) and the page's account calls (`account.ts`). The callers' own retries take over after it (packs: 0.5, 1.5, 4 s, then the
  zone is left out and tried again after 10, 30, 60 s).
- Feedback reports carry `state` and `currency` (`game/diagnostics.ts`): minutes the page has been open; the
  clock worker's oldest unanswered edit or read with its age and how many wait, the last hour of trips' age,
  its last error; the demand pool's status, newest solve (age, sent, rounds, final), the view shown, a
  network waiting, the last step, the oldest download in flight, replacements and the last worker trouble.
  Example during a held pack: "demand: status ready; solve v1 came 23s ago, sent 23s, rounds 4, final yes;
  shown v1 final; network waiting 20s; step 19s ago; oldest download 20s; workers 3, replaced 0".
