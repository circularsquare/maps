# T-2464: zone borders decided in the drawing

Rule: docs/ui-diagram.md "Zone borders". Code: `app/src/diagram/borders.ts` (made in `workers/diagram.worker.ts` after
the names), painted in `diagram/draw.ts`. Check: `checks/t2464-borders.mjs`. Replaces T-2168's warped real outlines
(notes/T-2168.md), which Anita found invisible in most places, wavy, uneven and not closing round their zones
(2026-11-04).

## How it works

A grid of one pitch over the drawing. The region: cells within 2 pitches of a line's casing or a pill, closed by 4
(grown and shrunk with 5/7 chamfer steps), every enclosed gap filled (a flood of the outside from the grid's edge,
tiles never touched flooded whole). Labels: a spread from the cells under each pill through the region, nearest first
(Dial's buckets, steps 5 and 7), each cell the zone of the pill it was reached from. Boundaries: unit edges between
differently labelled cells, chained between the corners where three labels meet; each chain fitted greedily with
one-or-two-leg octilinear ways (farthest point first by doubling and halving), each point's tolerance its clearance
less 0.3 pitch (between zones the clearance includes the region's edge, from a third spread), and no way allowed that
moves a pill's end or middle to its other side. Corners rounded like the lines' (radius 2 pitches).

## Tried on the way

- **Labels by the nearest line piece** (the brief's suggestion: each hop's halves its two stations' zones): where
  prefectures interleave inside a block (Shiga's stations inside Osaka's block) the enclaves grew tentacles along each
  half-hop and borders ran beside lines in the gaps of bundles. Labels by the nearest pill through the region give
  compact enclaves and cross each hop near its middle anyway.
- **Only small holes filled** (up to 1,500 pitches square): bigger loops left inner outlines that doubled every line in
  Queens and Brooklyn like casings. All holes are filled now.
- **Cell 0.5 pitch**: 2.5-3 s for Japan; 0.75 then 1 pitch looked the same at 7 px a pitch once the tolerance floor
  rose to 0.7 pitch. At 1 pitch a local stop's pill can hold no cell middle, so each pill also seeds the cells its ends
  are in (27 Japanese stations were on the wrong side before).
- **Tolerance 3 pitches between zones**: borders through wide filled gaps came out as staircases; 12 lets them run
  straight, the clearance from lines, pills and the region's edge keeping them off all three.

## Looking outward

- The Tube map's fare zones (notes/T-2168.md "Looking outward") are the model: bands drawn from which zone each
  station is in, boundary stations on the line between them. Regional fare zone maps such as Zurich's ZVV one draw zones
  as numbered areas round groups of stations on a schematic network, which is closest to this (from memory, not
  looked up in this task).
- Octilinear region drawing in the literature (the EuroVis octi-maps work linked in notes/T-2168.md) treats regions as
  an afterthought to the lines; nothing found that fits Voronoi-like regions to octilinear runs, so the greedy fit
  here is our own.
