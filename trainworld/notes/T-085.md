# T-085 dock and drawing polish

Done 2026-10-09 by the gameplay agent (batch 5), from Anita's feedback relayed mid-batch.
`T-batch5-dock.png` (dock dragged to 440 px, the divider moved up), `T-batch5-draw.png` (the tag).

- Dock (`ui/Dock.tsx`): an 8 px handle on the dock's right edge sets its width (280-560 px, at most
  45% of the window), a handle on the line between the tab area and the inspector sets the tab
  area's height (at least 110 px, leaving the inspector at least 160). Both through CSS variables
  (`--dock-w`, `--pane-h`) and remembered with the other UI settings (`dockLayout` in
  `game/ui.ts`, localStorage). Switching tabs still never moves the inspector. The map is told to
  resize when the width changes. No transitions.
- The Build tab's notes are gone. The construct button reads "Construct blueprints" and buttons no
  longer wrap (`white-space: nowrap` on text buttons).
- Drawing tag: "5.1 km, cost 1.10x, $507M": `km()` and `moneyShort()` (three figures) in
  `game/format.ts`; the multiplier is the track's price over its length at the base price
  (US$90M a km, single track 0.6x), so level and water averaged over the stretch, computed in the
  preview (`preview_out` in `wasm.rs`, the fifth number of the preview layout).
- Picking: trains before stations (`map/pick.ts`). A train standing at a station now takes the
  click; the station is still picked when no train is within 7 px, or from the Stations tab.
