# T-4210 Track ghost answers slow while the pointer moves (agent ghostspeed, 2026-11-24)

## Her words

"its kinda wiggly. like if i move my mouse the change sorta seems to propagate from my mouse to the origin point" (11-24).
On T-4208 on 8800: "we are not respecting the tangent / curvature until the placement actually fully catches up, the new
click just is a straight line from the last placed point" and "after a click, i see it stamped for a while, then it
disappears for about half a second, then it comes back".

## What changed

- **A run's next segment is laid on the page by the track model's own code.** `ghost_tangent` (sim/src/track/wasm.rs)
  is `route_op_as`'s tangent route (`tangent_arc`, `design_radii`, the fit, the preview's speeds and circle) with
  nothing of the network; the page loads the sim WASM on the track tool's first use (map/ghostLocal.ts: about 50 ms to
  import, 23 ms to start, 1.1 MB of memory) and calls it synchronously. It covers a start that is the bare end the run
  placed last (a click on open ground; `Placed.tangent`), an end on open ground at the same build level, single or
  double rail track (not quad, tram, busway), no snap or beside spot. There the ghost between answers is that route
  (`Tools.localAs`, in place of T-4136's `bend`), and so are the ghost before the run's first answer (`drawStandIn`),
  the ghost from the click while its answer is awaited (`drawOnward`), the stamp when a click beat its answer, and the
  heading the next start takes (the route's end heading, the worker's own). Rust test
  `the_ghost_tangent_is_the_previewed_route`: preview_route and ghost_tangent agree to 1e-6 on every value (points,
  vertices, speeds, end heading, circle) at 60/160/250 km/h, up to 160 degrees off the heading, past the cap.
- **The answer is still the check.** Each answer for the newest spot is held against the laid route (`sameLine`, 2 px
  on screen); one that differs (water, a ramp, other track) turns laying off for that start, which bends again.
- **The stamp stays until the network holding it is drawn.** The click's stand-in is now an edit drawn ahead
  (`ahead({ track })`, game/pending.ts), keyed to the click's edit, so it goes in the frame the network that holds the
  edit is set, never with an earlier edit's network (the renderer's own stand-ins went with any network). A click that
  beat its answer with nothing laid swaps it for the worker's held edges at the answer (`Ahead.set`). T-4208's
  `PlacedMark`/`dropPlacedSince` are gone.
- Not changed: starts on track (a station, a branch) still bend the last answer (T-4136), from where the worker's route
  leaves by `nearer_dir`/`lead_in`.
- edit_bench reads the track piece's stand-in from the drawn-ahead frame too. play.mjs refuses `--base` on a dev Vite.

## The "about 1 s" and "New York could not load" (T-4208's runs)

A test-page quirk, not the game: `play.mjs start ... --base trainworld` on an agent Vite opens the game at /trainworld/,
whose relative `packs/` URLs a dev Vite does not serve (it serves /packs only) and answers with the page. So every pack,
boundary and water mask "loaded" as HTML: "New York could not load" (and Tokyo), and each ghost waited on a water mask
that failed (two tries a second apart) and was dropped as stale by the next pointer move. Measured that way on NYC: 3 of
87 asks answered in 4 s, 84 dropped, the answered ones about 1 s apart. 8800 serves /trainworld/packs (200, geojson), so
her game never sees it. play.mjs now refuses `--base` off 8800.
