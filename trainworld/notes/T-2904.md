# T-2904: faster buses

Anita, 2026-11-09: 24 km/h feels slow; about 90% of car speed if no stops; 18 km/h for frequent stops not
sacred. Then: bus lanes, Chinese and Korean buses as the target; and highway speed on highways.

## The rule

`streets.rs` holds the constants, `bus::Pace` the sums. A hop = the car's time over its road at 90% of the
car's speed, the moving speed never under `BUS_LANE_KMH` (30), plus `BUS_STOP_S` (28 s). The car: first and
last 2 km local streets, next 8 km each end main roads, the rest open highway at 110 (`Params` in demand,
equal by test under the `demand` feature). Automatic stops: all local streets, one stop cost per 400 m.
A stretch on a trunk road counts the car at 88 at the least, at any density and on any hop
(`CAR_TRUNK_KMH`; 88 rather than 110 since a trunk road still has junctions).

## What else changed

- Trunk marks: `K_TRUNK` (4) in each route point's kind, saved with it; `busRoads`' road class weight
  `TRUNK`, `RoadGraph` edge kind 3, `RoadPath.fast`, `thinFlagged`, `busDraw`. A route from an old save has no
  marks until it is redrawn, and runs by the car model as before. No save format change.
- Speed display: `BUS_SPEED_STOPS` gains green at 60 and blue at 90; ferries keep the old scale
  (`FERRY_SPEED_STOPS`).
- `Pace::top` and `BUS_V` are gone; a bus `Run`'s limit is its own fastest phase.
- Motorways: the route tool already lets a bus use one (`busRoad` in `busRoads.ts`, T-2469); they do not get
  the trunk speed in this task. Adding them is one line (`emotor[e] === 3` to 1 or 3, with `K_TRUNK`).
