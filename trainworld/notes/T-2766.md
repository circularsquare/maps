# T-2766: stop clicks wait for the previous stop edit's whole state

The line tool's stop clicks queue (`Tools.queueStop`), each waiting for the last one's answer. The worker answered a
stop edit only after its whole follow-up (state with the network buffers, trips, boarding), and after each new world
the panels asked about a dozen reads (today, linesPeriods, linePeriods, lineDelays, lineWaits, lineHeld, lineMarks,
passingSpots, upgradeCosts, lineProfile, lineRelated), 0.2 to 0.7 s of the worker's on New York's real save, which
sat in the worker's queue ahead of the next click. So the n-th quick click waited for every earlier click's state and
reads, and a refusal for money came that late too (Anita's late bonk).

## Changed

- `rows` stop clicks (`map/tools.ts` `toggle` passes `edit(..., "rows")`): the worker applies the edit, posts a state
  of the rows alone (`ClockState.rows`: world and money, no network, no trips, `sendRows` in the clock worker) and
  answers. The client takes it as a world without touching the renderer (`takeStateNow` skips `onState`).
- The follow-up (network buffers, trips, boarding hour, demand's snapshot, autosave) is held as a stop click's
  (`Held.stops`, `STOP_REST_MS` 500 after the last stop click, 5 s at most); the next stop click joins it, so a run of
  clicks builds one full state. The line tool's reads (`STOP_QUERIES`) and demand's money results and the clock
  (`STOP_KINDS`) pass the hold; anything else runs it first.
- The client holds every read but the line tool's (`STOP_READS`) while a stop click is out and for 500 ms after the
  last answer (`holdsRead` in `clockClient.ts`), the same reads asked once.
- Dev only: `client.debugCash(cash, fleet)` sets the cash and, with `fleet`, owns only the cars in use (the check's
  refusal for want of money).

The lie: during a run of stop clicks the stops, line stats, money and markers are current, but the drawn line, its
trains, the boarding counts and the panels' account figures stay as they were until 0.5 s after the last click (plus
the full state's own time).

## Measured

`checks/t2766-stopwait.mjs` on the real save, line 2 (49 stops, put on a headway so stops cost trains), five stops
clicked off and back on, Chrome pinned to 3 cores, two demand workers. Times in ms from the click. The box's load
moved every figure a lot between runs (other agents' checks), so both columns give the spread over runs: "before" is
the old path (two runs under load, one quiet, the quiet one with the toggle's `"rows"` taken out for the run).

| | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| slow click, marker and list row | 145 to 1050, typically 150 to 450 | 50 to 500, typically 60 to 250 |
| 5 quick clicks 150 ms apart, edit sent after the click | quiet 0 to 160; loaded up to 485, 1050, 1620, 2090 for clicks 2 to 5 | 0 to 135 (behind the last click's rows only) |
| the same, marker and list row | quiet 128 to 458; loaded off 306, 1123, 1735, 2159, 2581 and 257 to 4318 | 60 to 200, 362 and 410 at worst under load |
| states for the 5 quick clicks | 5 full | 5 rows + 1 full, after the last click's answer |
| refusal for money, 3rd quick click | 210 (quiet) | 5 to 28 |
| worker per click | 45 to 900 (buffers 20 to 550) | 9 to 180 (apply 4 to 113, rows 4 to 170) |

The worst cases after are one rows build in the worker taking 170 to 210 ms (`sNodes` or `sLines` in
`stateRows`, a few ms otherwise; seen only on a loaded box), which the next click then waits behind. What is left of
the usual 60 to 120 ms is the main thread's own work on each new world (a profile over five clicks, per
click): the panels' re-render about 20, the demand layers rebuilt (`demandViews.ts` `rebuild`) about 15, taking the
state about 10, the region money shares (`regionMoney.ts`) about 9. Getting the marker into the first frame would mean
drawing it at the click before the world (an optimistic tier in `stopMarkers.ts`) or skipping the layer rebuild for a
rows world: T-3159.

## T-3159 (stopfast)

The marker is drawn at the click (StopMarkers.guessClick) and the rows world skips the demand layer rebuild (worldIsRows). Marker latency 55-110 ms before, 2-5 ms after (3 cores). The list row still shows with the world, 60-160 ms on a busy box: the observer only sees DOM changes once the main thread is free, and the world's task is still the panels' render. A guessed row was left out because the rows line up with per-stop figures by index.

## T-3232 (stoprow)

The list row is guessed on the DOM at the click (`ui/inspector/stopRowGuess.ts`): a removed stop's rows get `guess-out` (hidden), an added stop on the line's route (the panel's `passed` stations give the gap) gets a placeholder `li.adding` before the next row as drawn, a station off the route none. The panel's layout effect on the world version clears and re-applies the guesses still out; a refusal removes its guess. Row latency 83-445 ms before, 2-12 ms after (3 cores, real save, line 2); the refusal puts the row back (checked). The panel's own render (RENDER=1 in the check) is 5 ms when only the day's figures changed and 10-30 ms after a world; not memoed, because `stopRow` closes over the whole panel and the per-stop figures line up by index, and the row no longer waits for it.
