# T-4117: coarse demand as a setting (agent coarse, 2026-11-23)

Design and earlier measurements: notes/T-4086.md. Rules as built: docs/demand-runtime.md "Levels of detail" (last
paragraph), docs/ui-panels.md Performance.

## What changed

- **Setting** `display.coarseDemand` (game/ui.ts), default `mobileDevice()`, stored with the other display settings, so a
  player's choice is remembered per browser. Checkbox "Calculate trips in less detail" in Settings, Performance (after
  Calculate intercity trips) and in the start menu's settings panel (ui/SettingsTab.tsx `CoarseDemandCheck`, StartMenu.tsx).
- **Levels** (workers/demandLod.ts `lodMode(coarse)`, workers/demandClient.ts): the mode is read live: `?lod=` still wins
  and is fixed; else the setting gives "coarse" (every zone, the zone in view included) or "auto" (T-1223's rules).
  `lodCheck` in coarse mode only reopens zones at the wrong level (`lodApply`), behind the same guards (a load's card,
  the edit note settling). `lodSwitched` runs when the setting changes: to auto it makes the fine set afresh
  (`initFine`, the save's `demandFine` if this game has made none), then the next check applies.
- **Memory estimate** (game/memoryCheck.ts `memoryNeed`): the biggest zone counts coarse when the setting is on, so the
  pool cap and the load refusal see the smaller game.
- **Stars and goals** count coarse figures everywhere: the region star and goals now come from `goalsDue`
  (game/goalRules.ts, with STAR_SHARE and GOAL_SHARES moved there and re-exported from points.ts), which reads no level;
  the city first (achievements.ts) no longer skips `roughZones`. `roughZones` stays for the edit note and the refine tag.
- Check: `checks/t4117-coarse-goals.mjs` (Node, a few seconds): coarse-shown regions earn star and goals, non-final earns
  nothing, earned is not repeated, and clockClient/achievements/goalRules read no `roughZones`. PASS (7).

No sim/ change, so no cargo run.

## Measured (my Vite 6164, 4 demand workers, the shipped Tokyo save `data/packs/real/tyo.save`, 5 zones reached)

The machine was busy throughout: `cores.py total` read 11-14 claimed of a cap of 14 over every window (never over).

| | detailed (desktop default) | less detail | saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| load to final (wall, from reload) | 123.5, 137.2 s | 96.1, 103.9 s | about 23% (30 of 130 s) |
| +1 train on the Keihin-Tohoku Line to final | 88.9, 87.6 s | 68.5, 75.5 s | about 18% (16 of 88 s) |

T-4086's phone pool (one or two workers) saved a third to two thirds; with four workers the saving is smaller, since
the part that coarse shrinks is shared among more workers and the rest of the solve is not.

Figures, the same network (after the train edit), detailed against the setting switched on mid-game:
- Rail trips a day 16,244,901 -> 16,243,879 (-0.006%); all trips 900,348,502 -> 900,338,530.
- Lines with 10,000+ riders (113): median 0.8%, 90th percentile 3.4%, worst Tamagawa Line 36,708 -> 34,278 (-6.6%), Daishi
  Line +6.0%, Kodomonokuni Line +5.2%.
- Stations with 5,000+ boardings (1,384): median 2.0%, 90th percentile 5.8%, worst Higashi-Nagasaki 11,094 -> 13,083
  (+17.9%), Funabashi-Keibajo +15.1%, Shinanomachi -14.4%.
- Stars and goals the same (Tokyo star and 20% goal).
- A load with the setting on: rail 16,240,631 against 16,241,589 detailed (-0.006%).

Switching mid-game (what the player sees): figures stay; the status tag says "Calculating trips in 5 cities…" (on,
all five zones reopened coarse within 2 s, new figures 44 s later) or "Calculating trips here…" (off: the zone in view
is refined first, the rest follow; 75 s). Switching back off gave the detailed figures back to the rider
(16,244,901).
