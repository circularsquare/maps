# T-4564 Long-distance rail gets few riders after an update

Agent longrail, 2026-11-28. Tree longrail. No code changed. Advisor not called (no design decision made here).

## For Anita

**No recent change cut long-distance trips.** The long-trip rule (longhaul.json, the market rule, the tail past 700 km,
the plane) and India's place rows are unchanged since 11-15, and with enough trains a single long line gets a big share.
The few riders come from the network: long lines with a train every 2-3 hours fill up, and a full train's crowding
outweighs any fare cut. The players' "after some update" has no single cause; see "What may have changed for them".

**One real problem since v0.4.7 (T-2188):** a change of a line's trains counts as a "city edit". Its quick result is final,
but the trips across zone lines (long trips and commutes between zones) stay at their old figures. They catch up only in
the quiet refresh, 2 minutes after the last edit and only while the clock runs (or "Recalculate trips while paused" is
on). So adding trains to a long line shows no change while the game is paused, which is how a player tries
schedules. Measured below: 5x the trains showed 20,662 rides, to the decimal, until the refresh gave 75,054.

Proposed fix (not built, for her yes): count a train-count edit as an intercity edit when it moves trains an hour (past
the views' 5% tolerance) on a line with a run of 20 minutes or more, the same bar a stop edit already has
(`WINDOW_EDIT_MIN`). Such edits then solve whole: on her Asia save 165-300 s instead of 8-41 s, but only for long
lines. It goes in demand.worker.ts (`intercity`, about line 2993, and `editOf` about line 744), which the realgaps
tree (T-4543) holds now, so it waits for that to land. About 1-2 hours with an edit_bench run.

## What changed since 11-15, and what it does to long lines

From git log (sim/src/demand, pipeline/longhaul.json, market rows), the changes that move figures and could touch long rail:

- T-2188 (11-24, v0.4.7): the freeze above. The only change found that hides what a player does to a long line.
- T-4041 (11-22, v0.4.6): riders take the line that gets them to their stop first (three quarters) or the first train that
  can still make it. On one line it changes nothing (one line costs half its headway, as before); long trips' first wait
  is costed at the planned wait only where that is cheaper (`entry_costs`). Where long lines share stops with shorter,
  more frequent ones (this player's 30 HSR lines), it moves riders between lines, not off rail. Plausibly the India
  report's "after some update"; not checked without the save.
- T-2216 stages 1-3 (11-20/21, v0.4.5): islands and zones on two lands; could matter for Hong Kong Island, Mumbai's
  Salsette or Kochi. Not checked here; the Mumbai pairs keep their car, so Mumbai is not cut off.
- T-3888, T-3910 (11-20): 2 min boarding at each end, walking changes: minutes on a long trip.
- T-3426 (11-16): feeder lines always help long rail (raises it).
- Data: the registry changes since 11-15 that touch India are Muzaffarpur's split (11-22) and the 11-26 region redraws,
  which are not published yet.
- "Calculate trips in less detail" (T-4117) coarsens cells within a zone (within about 1% of fine); "intercity trips off"
  is the phone default, and this player is on desktop with demand final.

Stored real-save totals moved little: NYC 12.89M (v0.4.5) to 12.67M (v0.4.6), Tokyo flat, Belgrade 1.50M to 1.40M (v0.4.6)
to 1.33M (v0.4.7) (perf-ledger.jsonl).
