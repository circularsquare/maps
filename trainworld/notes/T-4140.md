# T-4140: trips between Madison and La Crosse

Agent madison, 2026-11-23. Investigation only, no code changed. A player in Madison, Wisconsin saw no
trips to La Crosse, in the same zone.

## Answer

The trips exist: about 160 a day each way between Madison and La Crosse, all by car on a new game
(the market's longer trips, 170 km apart). They count in the Madison bubbles' totals and in the
zone's Trips within (T-3509), but a selected bubble never draws them: the far end leaves out every
6 km zone of the zone in view (T-139, from before T-3509 made those trips count).

## The zone

Dane County (`dac`, tier T-723, 1.27M people) is 19 Wisconsin counties: Dane, La Crosse, Jefferson,
Sauk, Columbia, Grant, Monroe, Green, Vernon, Trempealeau, Juneau, Iowa, Adams, Jackson, Richland,
Lafayette, Crawford, Marquette, Buffalo (pipeline/world_plan.json; La Crosse County joined Dane as
"nearest centre, partner 0.63"). Madison centre to La Crosse centre: 171 km straight line.

## Which layer makes Madison - La Crosse trips

- **Local layer** (the zone's own commutes and everyday outings): almost none. Everyday outings taper
  out over 40-80 km; the US commute curve d^-0.8 exp(-d/30 km) is tiny at 170 km. Measured: 0 trips
  from Madison homes to La Crosse, 10.7 a day from La Crosse homes to Madison jobs.
- **The market's longer trips** (ground curve, onset 25-50 km, max(d,25)^-2.37; the US keeps the
  worldwide rule, no pull): yes. Since T-3509 the market's trips between two 6 km zones of one zone
  count as that zone's trips. T-1000's long commutes are 50-150 km, so La Crosse (171 km) is outside them.

## Where they show and where they don't

- **Counted**: a Madison bubble's count under Homes, Destinations and Total (T-3503's regional legs,
  T-3509's own-zone part), the selection inspector's "Inter-city trips" purpose row (by its rule, the
  all-purpose legs beyond every purpose's; not measured), and the zone's
  Trips within, train share and goals.
- **Not drawn**: a bubble selection's far end. The local far end (`flows`) has only the local layer
  (0-11 a day to La Crosse, grouped into chunks of 10 or more). The inter-city far end
  (`intercitySelection`) does return La Crosse's 6 km zones (place 5890, the zone's own), but
  `map/intercityView.ts` draws only `z.place !== current.own` (up close) and `p.place !== current.own`
  (zoomed to 9.5 and below). ui-demand-views.md "Inter-city trips": "Only zones other than the focused
  one are drawn; same-zone counts stay in the inspector [T-139]". The inspector no longer lists trips
  by place (T-1668), so they are nowhere by place.
- **Trip explorer**: opened from a far-end bubble only, so not reachable for this pair.

## Is the rule right?

The demand is the worldwide rule doing what it means to: 170 km apart, about 160 a day each way
between a 400k city and a 90k one. It is not removed by the zone: T-3509 made the market count trips
within one zone. The gap is display only: T-139 hid same-zone far zones when the market's same-zone
trips were counted nowhere and the local far end covered the zone; with zones of 19 counties (T-723
tier zones), the far parts of one zone are now drawn by neither far end.

## Built (tree .tw-trees/madison, 2026-11-23)

Why T-139 hid them (notes/archive/t0001-0499/T-139.md): overlap and scale. A 26-a-day longer-trip
circle near Old Bridge drew larger than a 583-commuter local circle beside a Midtown selection, two
layers on two scales. T-1246 has since put far zones on the local far end's scale (twice the radius).

- `map/intercityView.ts`: up close, a selection's far 6 km zones of its own zone are drawn when 50 km
  (`OWN_FAR_M`) or more from the selection's busiest cell (`nearPointOf`, the trip explorer's near
  point), past where local everyday outings end (they taper by the market's 25-50 km onset); zoomed
  out the own zone is still never a labelled bubble. Clicking one opens the trip explorer as for
  any far zone.
- Docs: ui-demand-views.md "Inter-city trips", regional.md "Seeing them". DECISIONS.md Map and UI.
  Changelog madison-1. No new strings (the far disc's hover and the explorer's text are the
  existing ones).
