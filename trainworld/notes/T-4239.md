# T-4239: train counts that never settle on shared track (agent settlestop, 2026-11-24)

Status: built in tree settlestop; Gemini's review (artifacts/trainworld/gemini/t4239-review.md) checked and fixed by
agent settlefix (master 6b5df61c6 merged in), not landed. Anita 11-24: "we need to run to fewer rounds ... we
definitely dont need to do to bit exact."

## The cause, in the code

`TrackWorld::settle_counts` (sim/src/track/world.rs) is a fixed-point iteration: each round every line's shortfall
(trains its fleet runs minus rate x round trip / 3600) gives a Newton step on its rate, `Capacity::fill` cuts the
rates to the full track, and the service update works the round trips out again from the new rates. It stops when a
round changes nothing, when no line is off its fleet, on an exact repeat of the whole state (T-3233, T-4194), or at 80.

Where expresses and locals share track the round trip is not continuous in the rates: the passing-track timetable
(`overtake::Plan::outcome`, kind 13 waits, kind 11 following loss) and the timed following loss pick the best of 24
relative phases over a cycle of both lines' departures, so a hair's change of rate moves a local's wait or an
express's loss by a step. No rate then fits the fleet: below the step the fleet has trains to spare, above it falls
short, and the Newton step circles across it. On T-3931's test line (one local, one express, passing stations, 10
and 12 trains) the local's round trip is 9,204-9,254 s at 3.835-3.890 tph and 9,376-9,386 s at 3.900-3.912 tph (a
1.7% step); on jwxidghd (T-4194's trace) the lines that never stop step their round trips 0.4-3% while their rates
move 0.2-1.25% (line 19, Toyoko Line F Liner: 8.0144 tph -> 6,204 s, 8.0224 -> 6,294 s). So it is oscillation
across a jump, not a slow drift.

T-1283 already settles such a line, but only within 0.05 trains of its fleet (0.25 on headways since T-4194): the
step is a share of the round trip, so the shortfall it makes is a share of the fleet (1.5% of 14 trains is 0.2). The
circling lines in the load's late rounds (shortfall at the turns, share of fleet): 19 0.19 trains 1.35%; 7 0.16 1.14%;
16 0.15 1.06%; 198 0.09 0.40%; 9 0.06 0.44%; 44 0.05 0.37%; 251 0.06 2.97% (2 trains). All count lines, all past
0.05, so the catch never fired; each sharing group circles with its own period and the 7/198 pair never repeats, so
the whole state never repeats and every settle ran 80 rounds. NYC has no such steps: its settles end in 1 round.

## The rule (world.rs `settle_counts`, `Swing`, `hold_circles`)

A second, wider catch beside T-1283's, keyed on the symptom rather than the source (any discrete step in a round trip).
The rule as reviewed and fixed by settlefix (2026-11-24, section "Review" below):

- per line, level and side (fleet short, trains to spare) a turn of the shortfall counts toward a circle when it is at
  least 80% (`SWING_SAME`) of the first turn to that side since the count began; a smaller one begins the count again
  there. A circle comes back to each side at the size it left. A converging swing shrinks, and fails this within a
  turn or two unless it shrinks by under about a tenth a turn there (0.8 per two turns), too slowly to settle before
  the cap anyway;
- a turn counts only when it or the turn before it is past T-1283's bound (0.05 trains, 0.25 on headways): a swing
  inside that bound on both sides is T-1283's;
- counting runs while the shortfall is within 3% of the line's trains (`SWING_SHARE`; under one train on headways,
  `SWING_SPARE_MAX`, as `trim_auto` drops whole spare trains); further out it starts again;
- the third counted turn to a side (`SWING_TURNS`) holds the line there: a count line on the side its fleet falls
  short, a headway line on the side with trains to spare (where `auto_counts` buys it no train at the next settle).
  It stays held while its shortfall stays on that side within the bound; pushed to the other side it is stepped and
  needs another counted turn back;
- lines sharing a capacity resource are held one at a time, the widest share of its trains first, then by line id
  (`hold_circles`), and a hold frees the lines sharing its track held before it, each once a settle: a circle that
  was a neighbour's doing then settles, and holds end;
- a held line stays so between settles while its rate, round trip and trains are as they were (`settle_kept`,
  saved). Since settlefix also its headway (an edit changing it drops the entry), its pace at a merge
  (`pace_merges` drops it) and, where its hours mix rows, its other slots' rates (`Network::kept_key`).

T-1283's own catch (0.05 / 0.25 trains, halving, the side with trains to spare) is unchanged. The cap of 80 rounds
stays as the backstop: a circle wider than 3% of the line's trains still runs to it.

**Why the side the fleet falls short.** On the other side the round trip has the plan's shorter waits, which the
trains' own timetables (`timing`) need more than: their stand at the far end goes under `TURN_MIN_S`, the run falls
back to the averaged timetable, and an express is drawn through a local. T-3931's test shows it: on master its 10/12
case circles with period 5 and lands on round 80's state, which happens to be on the short side; stopping at rounds
76, 77 or 79 instead gives 47, 53 and 51 bad passes in 4 h (rounds 78 and 80: none). Settled on the spare side its
local had 53 bad passes; on the short side none. (Telling the timetable about a count line's spare trains, a spare
time like a headway line's, halved them, 53 to 24; not pursued, see below.) **Shown for a circling local only.** No
case here has an express that circles: on 9/6 an earlier version of the rule (one catch, no T-1283 bound) kept a
converging express 0.0125 trains short and the timetable had 34 bad passes, but its local also sat at another rate
(4.563 against 4.549 tph); on 10/12 the cap's states with the express short were bad too, but so was its local's
side. Whether a kept-short express draws worse than master's phase luck is not measured.

**The lie.** A count line held this way runs a rate its fleet makes only on the lower round trip: up to 3% of its
trains more an hour than its fleet runs on the round trip it is drawn with. A headway line held this way runs a little
under its headway, with under one train to spare (as T-1283's catch already did within 0.25 trains). Worst on a line with few trains whose round trip steps
by nearly 3% (a local stopping at passing stations behind an express). Today's answer was no truer: round 80 lands
anywhere on the circle (T-4194: a reload moved 23 lines up to 1.32%).

## Review (Gemini's adversarial read, checked by settlefix against the code)

- **1(a) a line still converging: real.** The count compared each turn with the last turn to that side and only
  started again past 3%, so a swing shrinking by under 20% a cycle, or one slow turn and then far smaller ones, was
  held up to 3% over its fleet. In T-4194's trace of the jwxidghd load (master), settlestop's rule would hold line 0
  (22 trains, short of its fleet by 0.030, 0.013, 0.008, 0.002 ... trains, then on full track with trains to spare) at
  round 14 and 204 (1 train, shortfalls under 0.002) at round 21. Fixed: counted against the first turn of the count
  with a new count on a shrink, T-1283's band left to it, the hold only at a counted turn (rule above). Tests
  `swing_tests`: a swing shrinking 15% a turn there is not held; a steady circle is held on its third return; the
  trace's 16, 19 and 7 are held at rounds 7, 7 and 30; line 0 is not.
- **1(c) one line held while its partner moves: the ratchet is real but bounded; both held at once is real.** A held
  line pushed to the spare side is stepped up to its fleet on that round trip and held again on its next counted turn
  back: it ends at its fleet's rate on its shorter round trip, within the 3% bound, which is the stated lie; left so.
  Two lines held in one round, one of whose circle was the other's doing, stayed held. Fixed (`hold_circles`): lines
  sharing a resource are held one at a time (widest share first, then id) and a hold frees the lines sharing its track
  held before it, once each a settle, so holds end. Test `two_lines_circling_each_other_are_held_one_at_a_time`; on
  T-3931's 10/12 case the express now asserts it settles exactly once the local is held (it did before too).
- **1(b) an express held by a neighbour's swing: real, within the lie.** While the local circles wider than 3% the
  express's swing has no end either; held where its fleet falls short it is under 3% off, where master's cap left it
  anywhere on its swing. `pace_merges` reading round trip over trains and a mixed row sharing its rate by what each
  place runs are how every line off its fleet is read (master's cap, T-1283's side). No change beyond 1(c)'s freeing.
- **4 a save loading differently: pre-T-4239 saves real but no regression; a headway line held short then buying a
  train real.** An old save's line that stopped at the cap is stepped at load and held: master moves the same lines
  at reload too (T-4194: 23 lines up to 1.32%), and a save made after this reloads unchanged (jwxidghd above). A
  headway line held where its fleet falls short wants the train its longer round trip needs, and `auto_counts` buys
  it at the next settle (a load or any edit), as only the last of the settle's rounds refuses. Fixed: a headway line is
  held on the side with trains to spare, under one train. Test `a_saved_line_held_on_its_circle_loads_as_it_was`
  (counts; no small network circles a headway line).
- **3 order dependence: real, not new, narrowed.** Where a circling line ends depends on where its circle started, as
  master's cap did (it lands wherever the phase puts it); the hold now lands at a turn back at the size it left, near
  the fleet's rate on the shorter round trip from any start. Holds are ordered by share and id, nothing hashed. Since
  T-4193 an open takes the zones' street speeds before the network. No change.
- **1(d), 2 the kept entry missing inputs: real (pre-existing, widened).** The entry (rate, round trip, trains)
  stood after a headway edit that kept the trains (confirmed: `a_kept_line_given_another_headway_runs_it` fails
  without the fix, the line stays at 6 tph against the new 5.81), a pace change at a merge, and, where hours mix rows,
  another slot's rate. Fixed where they change: `commit_with` drops the entries of a line whose headway changed,
  `pace_merges` of a line whose pace moved, and `Network::kept_key` adds the other slots' rates to a mixed slot's
  entry (an old save's mixed-row entry then settles once at load). A band check on the shortfall was not used: it
  passes small pace changes, and `reshare_full`'s entries reach 0.5 trains. Not a new save field: the tuple is the
  save format and the undo's `LineRates`.

## Tried and dropped

- **A tolerance on rate change** (end a round that moves no rate or round trip by more than 1e-4 of it): cut the
  tail below, but left held lines short of exact, so every later settle moved them and re-woke circling neighbours
  (edits 10-11 rounds instead of 4-5), and 3 Rust tests failed (`a_new_line_on_full_track_gets_its_share_at_the_edit`,
  a tram test, a merc save test).
- **A line the fill pins doesn't hold its level open**: same trouble, edits 20-35 rounds.
- settlefix, replaying T-4194's trace of the jwxidghd load (`artifacts/trainworld/t4194/trace-jwx2.tsv`, all 16
  settles, without the rounds' feedback): counting each turn against the last one to its side and starting again on a
  20% shrink held 7 only at round 30 and 198, 0 and 204 never; counting from the first turn of the count (the rule)
  holds 16 and 19 at 7, 44 at 9, 198 at 9, 9 at 11, 251 at 12, 7 at 30, and not 0, 8 or 204. Waiting for two small
  turns in a row before starting again held 7 at 24 but 44 and 251 at 15. Four turns instead of three: 7 at 34, 198 at
  54. Starting again on a turn inside T-1283's bound on both sides (rather than leaving it uncounted): 7 at 43.

## What is left

- A line whose circle is wider than 3% of its trains (a step bigger than that, or a small fleet) still circles to
  the cap, and keeps its whole network's settle running with it.

- Of the load's 32 rounds about 15 are held lines on full track creeping to their shares (linearly, a little over half
  the way a round, from 1e-4 to the last bit), kept stepping by a line alone on single track past full whose step the
  fill takes back every round (T-3526, line 355 here). Cutting it costs more later (above).
- A settle that re-paces (`pace_merges`, up to 3 times a settle; on this save it changes every time, T-3233's
  "pacing never settles") re-catches the circling lines each time: the 98 rounds of a bus rework, which now runs
  only when a zone's streets arrive after the load. Hysteresis in the pacing is T-3233's open item.
- A line held because a neighbour's circle moves it (an express beside a local circling wider than 3%, a row's
  borrowed hour beside its own slot) stays held while that circle runs; it is freed only by a hold of a line sharing
  its track. Within the stated lie.
- The timetable's trouble with a count line off its fleet (an express drawn through a local) exists on master at 3 of
  the 5 states T-3931's 10/12 circle visits; telling `Service::spare` a count line's spare trains halved it on the
  spare side. A timing task, not filed here (no id given).
