## Where a Japan load's time goes

Instrumented cold loads of `artifacts/trainworld/t737-japan-trains.save` (a scratch timer around
each population's call in `demand.worker.ts`, reverted), per period and round, in the browser:
the market's pass 105-120 s for a daytime period (55-63 s at night), the 88 city populations
together 30-40 s. So the market is about 75% of every round, and it never settles: each round's
loads move far more than T-434's 1%.

Natively (`t1545_dump` on `t780jp`, timers in `pass_window`, reverted): one daytime free-flow pass
85-108 s on a busy machine, of which the destination searches 55-68 s (8,179, plus 215-698 again),
the longer trips' logsums and spreading 20-24 s, the walks 6-8 s, the commutes 2 s.

The pool deals five periods over three workers 2, 2, 1, so the worker holding periods 0 and 3 sets
the time and the third worker idles about half of every round.

## Crowding matters a lot to Japan's market

A scratch log of the market's own riders' crowding (`fac - 1` weighted by its loads) read 3-19 at
round 1 and 1-12 at round 2; each crowded pass moved the market's loads 9-60% and its longer
trips' rail 5-33% (period 2: 176k to 118k). So the market cannot skip crowding (variant "none"
below), and averaging fewer passes changes the intercity lines visibly.

## What would get to 500 s

1. **Share the slow worker's load** (biggest, no lie): five periods on three workers leave one
   worker idle half of each round. Solving a period's crowding rounds in parts across workers, as
   T-1543 does for the free-flow preview, or dealing the market of a period to the idle worker with
   the cluster's loads sent each round, would bring the bottleneck from two periods to about 5/3.
2. **Cheaper searches**: two-thirds of a market pass is 8,000-odd destination searches over the
   whole of Japan's graph, and WASM runs them about twice as slow as native. A search could stop
   once every origin station its zones read is settled (exact, but the planned-wait entry costs
   read offers made after a station settles, so it needs care), or search per destination zone.
3. One crowding pass for the market ("one" above) reaches about 600 s at 3 workers, but only with
   the intercity lines moving 25-35%; Anita would have to accept that.
