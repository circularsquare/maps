# T-3703: schedules for buses and ferries like trains

Anita, 2026-11-18: "some ppl on discord are complaining about schedule feature not being enabled for
buses / ferries also. we should get that working. we can leave out the marey diagram / timetable part
for now if thats harder to add but yeah want to bring buses and ferries to be more consistent with
trains." The fleet half of her ask (edits to a bus line's count or headway not recalculating) was
T-3704.

## What differed before this task

Line panel (bus and ferry: `BusInspector.tsx`; rail: `LineInspector.tsx`):

1. Schedule heading buttons. Rail: the timetable chart toggle and the "Detailed schedule" button
   (`ScheduleButtons`). Bus and ferry: none, so no way into the schedule panel.
2. Track limits. Rail: a headway shorter than the track allows is red, steppers stop at the cap,
   held messages (`lineHeld`, `trainCap`). Bus and ferry: no track, so no limits; nothing turns red.
3. Tutorial marks sit on a train line's cells only.

Schedule panel (`SchedulePanel.tsx`, rail only: `charted` excluded bus lines):

4. Weekdays and Weekends sections (T-3521): the weekend percent, weekend counts or headways, Apply to
   all lines. Bus and ferry: not shown, though the sim already ran weekends for them (a bus line has
   `LineData::weekend`, and Apply to all lines already changed bus and ferry weekends unseen).
5. Default headways for new lines (T-3543, T-3588), one set per kind: regular, tram, high speed.
   Bus and ferry: no kind (`lineKind` null), so no "Set as default" / "Use default".
6. Departures board (`TrackApi.station_departures`) and timetable chart (`TrainTable`, `MareyChart`):
   read the sim's trip timetables, which skip bus lines (`!l.is_bus()`).
7. Group chart (group view): rail lines only.

New lines (`clock.worker.ts` addLine / addBusLine / addFerryLine):

8. A new rail line takes its kind's default headways when set, else starts on counts (0 trains). A new
   bus or ferry line starts on Headway at its tool's headways (`NEW_LINE`: bus 8/15/30 min, ferry
   20/30/60 min).
9. A new rail line takes the weekend percent last set with Apply to all lines (`newWeekendShare`);
   a new bus or ferry line started at 100%.

Elsewhere:

10. The client's cash pre-check for a headway edit (`headwayTrains`) returns null for a bus line, so
    the worker decides (no limits to read).
11. `pace_merges` skips bus and ferry lines (no merges).

## What this task changes

- 1: the bus and ferry panel gets the "Detailed schedule" button (same component); no chart toggle.
- 4: the schedule panel opens for a running bus or ferry line: the same `Schedule` with Weekdays and
  Weekends, plus default headways. No board or chart (6), so the panel is the left column alone.
- 5 and 8: "bus" and "ferry" kinds for default headways ("Headways for new bus lines", "... ferry
  lines"). With none set the tool's headways are shown as the default, since those are what a new bus
  or ferry line gets. A new bus or ferry line takes its kind's default when one is set.
- 9: new bus and ferry lines take the weekend percent too.

- Also: `lineCountry` (game/holidays.ts) found no country for a bus line starting at a street stop (it looked
  among stations only), so the panel's "today" row could disagree with the clock worker, which places a line
  by its first stop's point. It now looks up the bus stop too (cached by stop id). Measured on a new NYC game: a
  bus line whose first stop is a street end (not among the stations, so null before) now gives "US".

Where: `SchedulePanel.tsx` (`scheduled` opens the panel, `charted` still gates board, chart and group
chart; `BusBody`; `Frame` `narrow`), `LineInspector.tsx` `ScheduleButtons` (no bus early return;
`MareyButton` hides itself), `BusInspector.tsx` (passes it as `copy`), `schedule.tsx` `DefaultHeadways`,
`newHeadway.ts` (`lineKind` bus and ferry), `newLine.ts` `headwaysFor` (one fallback for panel and
worker), `saveFile.ts` `NEW_HEADWAY_KINDS` (load) and `sharedSave.ts` (shared saves), `clock.worker.ts`
`newLineWeekends`, `style.css` `.sched-panel.narrow` (360 px).

## Left as they are

- 2: limits. Buses and boats use no track; a limit would be a new rule.
- 6, 7: the departures board and timetable chart. The sim keeps no trip timetable for bus lines
  (`station_departures` and the chart's trips skip them); adding one is its own task.
- 8: a new bus or ferry line still starts on Headway with service (a rail line starts with none).
- 3, 10, 11: no change needed.
