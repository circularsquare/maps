# T-3360 Schematic buses and ferries (with T-3785 hidden lines)

Agent schembus, 2026-11-19, tree schembus. Rules now in docs/ui-diagram.md "Buses and ferries".

## What was built

- **Thin overlay** (`diagram/thin.ts`): after a region is laid out, the page (`thinInputs`, view.ts) sends the layout
  worker each bus and ferry line of that region in the region's graph metres. The worker carries the route through the
  carry's map (`carryMap`), moves each stop to its place (a station stop to the nearest point of its pill, a ferry port
  within 400 m of a drawn station to that station's pill, any other stop to its own carried place), blends the moves
  along the route, fits octilinear runs between station stops (`fitOcti`, 2 pitches, ferry 4) and rounds the corners.
  The rail graph never sees them, so the rail layout cannot change. A bus-only change asks only for this pass.
- **Drawn** under the rail lines (whose casings cut them): 0.3 stroke, ferry dashed 1.6/1.1 pitches, stops off stations
  as small white dots with the ink outline, no names. Muted while something else is picked; click selects the line,
  hover shows its bullet and name.
- **Bus-only networks** (`diagram/busGraph.ts`, clock.worker.ts `postDiagram`): bus and ferry lines with no stop within
  0.05 degrees of any rail region's stations' box form their own graph (stops as nodes, a station's one per complex),
  split by `regionGroups`, laid out like rail, drawn thin with dot stops and no names. Tried with three Philadelphia
  buses beside the NYC save: its own region, octilinear, unlabelled (artifacts/trainworld/t3360-philly.png).
- **Mode buttons** (ui/DiagramButton.tsx `ModeButtons`): train, tram, bus, ferry icons (the existing `Icon.*`), shown
  for the kinds the network has when it has two or more; kept as `anitabuilder-diagram-modes`.
- **T-3785**: a line hidden on the map (hide mode, Settings' buses and ferries switch; `map/visibility.ts hidden.lines`,
  so a hidden line's twin too) is not drawn on the schematic; stations whose every stopping line is hidden lose their
  pill and name and cannot be picked. Same mechanism as the mode buttons: paint time, the layout keeps their room.
  Picture save follows it. Shares (diagram/shared.ts, the server's frozen v1 format) carry neither thin lines nor the
  filters: a later task if wanted.

## Decision for DECISIONS.md (the tree may not edit it; my edit of the main tree's was refused)

- 2026-11-19 (agent schembus, T-3360, T-3785): on the schematic, buses and ferries are fitted after the rail layout through
  its carried map and never enter the rail graph, so a rail layout is the same with or without them; only buses and
  ferries with no stop within 0.05 degrees of any rail region's stations' box are laid out themselves, as a network of
  their own. The mode buttons and lines hidden on the map are left out at paint time (not drawn, nor the pills and names
  of stations only they stop at), keeping the room the layout gave them, so a toggle is instant rather than a new layout
  (up to about 20 s on New York). notes/T-3360.md.

## Why A

At the start pitch the thin line is 1.6 px: visible but clearly second to every rail line. B (1 px, no stops) nearly
vanishes on the light background and gives no hint where it stops; C's medium line with coloured dots starts to compete
with the trams and rail local stops. A's white stops match the local-stop look players already know.

## For the report

- Painted under the rail lines (their casings cut them): a bus along a rail corridor mostly disappears, as the 42 St
  crosstown does under the 7 and the shuttle. Painting them over the rail is a one-line swap if she prefers.
- The mode buttons show only when a game has two or more kinds of line.
- The saved picture's box comes from the rail lines and names, so a thin line past them is cropped.
- No sim change, so no Rust tests.

## Known limits

- Names were placed without the thin lines, so a thin line can run under a name (the ferry under "Whitehall St-South
  Ferry").
- A bus stop near but not at another line's stop stays a separate dot; a bus-only network's lines meet only where they
  share a stop.
- Hidden lines keep their room: a bundle keeps its width with lines missing.
