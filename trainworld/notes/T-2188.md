## What changed

- **City edit or intercity edit** (demand.worker.ts, `ends`). An edit is intercity when, against the network the market
  part last solved on (so changes add up until a whole solve): it changes what the market's trips pay (the fares, a
  line's own multiple or distance fare, a station's fare zone: `faresMoved`), switches a line on or off at some level,
  adds or drops a stop on a run of 20 minutes or more (`stopMinutes`), or moves a line's run time end to end by 3
  minutes or more in some period (`shift`, `RUN_SHIFT_MIN`). Anything else is a city edit. An edit whose cluster was
  last solved with its market set apart (T-1607, the first edit after some loads) goes whole as before.
- **A city edit ends at its local result.** Each worker says so in `plan.final`; when every solving worker has, the page
  sends `endLocal` (demandClient.ts) instead of `whole`. The edited clusters keep their local result: `held` = the new
  sig, each population's locally solved periods marked `psig + LOCAL`, the market read through its old route nodes
  (`marketOn`) as the local solve read it. The final goes at once; untouched zones keep their bubbles' sums. Its local
  solve opens no T-1735 window (the result must be what the populations hold). A city edit touching no population past
  `sameView`'s tolerance (more trains on a line already at its most trains an hour) ends at once with nothing solved;
  before, it had no local stage and went to the whole solve.
- **Run-time blips no longer make a city line intercity.** A train-count edit moves run times here and there (dwell,
  trains on shared track): on the Asia save 0.2-1.5 minutes, up to 17% on single hops, also on neighbouring lines. The
  T-1735 window rule counts any hop past 1%, so 2 of 6 train edits on Asia opened a window and went whole before the
  `RUN_SHIFT_MIN` test.
- **The idle refresh** (`REFRESH_IDLE_MS` 120 s after the last edit, `refresh` on the solve): the same network solved
  again, every cluster holding a `LOCAL` period solved whole in those periods (T-124 keeps the rest). Quiet: no status, no
  clock cap. Armed only by a city edit that ended locally (`localEdits`), so T-1609 refines alone never start one; cleared
  by any solve that leaves no local result anywhere (`done.localHeld`). Waits while the game is paused with "Recalculate
  trips while paused" off. An edit during it follows T-3925 (under 40%: stops it, the edit's own solve is then whole with
  no local stage; past it: waits, the refresh then shown as a whole solve).
- **T-3512's skip only for intercity edits.** A city edit keeps its local solve however much of its cluster it touches
  (New York is 70% of the real save's): its local result is its final, so the local solve no longer puts the final
  later.
- **A whole solve's period steps hold lines priced at other fares** (`publishStep`, as local steps did): found by
  t3303 (below), on master too.
- `?perfOff=localEnd` (every edit goes on to the whole solve, as before), `?perfOff=refresh` (no idle refresh).

## Why 120 s

Its whole solve is 4 minutes on her Asia save on one worker (243 s measured), and an edit made in its first 40% waits
for a whole solve (T-3925, T-4220 open), so it should start in a real pause, not between two edits of a line being
built (T-4204's edits came 40-60 s apart). Two minutes is past that rhythm, and the drift it clears is small (below),
so a longer wait costs little. A count of edits was not used: it would put a 4-minute wait on every Nth edit, the wait
this task removes.

## The shape that shows it worst

A city edit's lie is everything frozen: the cities it did not touch and the market. It shows most where many frozen
riders share the edited line or its neighbours, and where they would move: a busy trunk line through several zones (an
edit to one zone's feeder changes the trunk's crowding, which the other zones' riders do not answer), and lines whose
riders are largely the market's (commuter rail, intercity trains), whose trains an hour an edit moves by knock-on, as
lines 148 and 159 on Asia. A world of many middling cities joined into one cluster (her Asia save) is that shape; a
one-city world is not (its city is most of its cluster: T-3512 then solves it whole anyway).
