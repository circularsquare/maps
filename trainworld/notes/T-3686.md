# T-3686: the focused city's trips on a big save

Agent focusload, 2026-11-19, tree `.tw-trees/focusload`, Vite 6160. Anita's TODO: "loading focused city demand still
feels kinda too long... am waiting like >3 min in my big asia save. why does this need to scale with save size at all,
anyways?" (uncommitted in TODO.md, so written after the focus pool of T-2045/T-2533 landed).

## What was wrong

**A half-loaded network reached demand as the loaded game's.** While the clock worker opens a save it awaits water
masks, street speeds and terrain. When a zone's street speeds landed during that time and the save had bus lines,
`ensureStreets` sent a state and scheduled a snapshot. The snapshot's timer fired at the next await, which on Anita's
save is `masksForLoaded` holding the empty track while it fetches one missed mask, so demand got a network of 0 lines
(seen directly in a run with the timing forced: `timer snapshot lines 0 stations 0`). Demand takes the first network
after a load as the game's (`takeGameLoadHold`), so:

- the focus pool solved the city in view on that network: Tokyo showed "Transit share 0.0%, Transit trips 0/day"
  behind the card, and kept it, since the real network then found the zone already in `focusZones`. Its real trips
  came only with the whole solve, 184-399 s into the load;
- the load's fine set was made twice, and after the card a level change (`rab:0, ath:1`) re-solved all 123 zones
  once more (60-70 s with 3 workers), ending on slightly different figures (2,057,204,306 trips a day against
  2,057,198,677);
- the street speeds of every zone the track meets (214 here, 8 fetches each, about 11% of a pack's bytes) went out
  at once beside the demand packs: `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES` in all 4 master batch runs (none in 5 fix runs), the focus worker dying
  with it twice ("demand: the focus pool stopped (focus worker 0: undefined)"), and the masks taking 6-22 s.

Unforced, a network reached demand before "clock loaded" in 6 of 8 master loads of her save (asiaA, B, C, m2, m3, w1m;
not D, m1); in 4 of those (A, C, m2, w1m) the city in view had no real trips until the whole solve, 184-399 s in.
Whether it happens depends on when the street speeds land, so it is a race, more likely with slow or contended
downloads.

## What changed (app/src/workers/clock.worker.ts)

- `midLoad`: from `loadGame`'s start to its `restart`, the clock worker sends no state and no snapshot (`sendState`
  and `scheduleSnapshot` return); `restart` sends both for the loaded game, as before.
- `ensureMasks(ids, streets)`: a load's masks (`masksBeforeLoad`, `masksForLoaded`) fetch no street speeds. The
  loaded network's bus routes still wait for theirs (`masksForLoaded`), and the view's and edits' masks still bring
  their zones' (bus timing reads only the zones a route crosses, `bus.rs`).

## Left, with sizes (not built)

1. **The card waits for every city** (her T-2049, 2026-11-02: "loading card should stay up until whole world
   finishes"). On this save the city in view has its first trips at 27-28 s and its last round at about 77 s; the
   card stays to 208-235 s with 3 workers and 320 s with the 1 her machine gets. This is the part that scales with the save; it is her
   call.
2. **The fine set trimmed 5 minutes into a game.** A saved fine set over the budget is kept for `FINE_HOLD_MS` and
   then trimmed, which re-solves every zone: fixG (3 cores, 2 workers) `sha:1` 240 s; w1f (1 worker) `sha:1` 13 s,
   since only 5 units were solved again (`T2729 v3 units 0+5 of 0+5`); master's race runs `rab:0, ath:1` 63-73 s. Seen
   after the card on loads over 5 minutes; "5 minutes into play" on a shorter load is read from the code (`planFine`,
   `recent`), not seen. Trimming the saved set at load would save that solve but changes which zones the first
   final has fine: a demand call.
3. **State before snapshot** in `restart`: demand waits 3.3-11 s for the renderer's buffers of the whole network.
   The order can't flip as it is, since `packService` reads the world for per-stop fare scales (T-3748); with those
   in the snapshot it could go first.
4. **The second network load** for masks `save_points` misses (1 zone here): 1.9-9 s.
5. **Pack priorities**: the zone in view's pack asks `priority: "high"` (T-1533), which is fetch's default, so the
   other packs are not behind it; they could ask "low". Not measured.

## Memory (for T-3757)

Master fetched and installed the street grids of every zone the track meets (214 on this save) at each load; now only
the bus routes' zones at load, the rest as they are viewed or edited. The grids are small next to demand's packs; the
bigger effect was the flood of fetches (`ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES`, a focus worker lost).
