# T-2873: buses backtracking

Cause: OpenMapTiles simplifies each road on its own, so a road running straight through a junction
loses the junction's vertex. The roads meeting there share no vertex, and the graph had no junction.
On Donhwamun-ro the side street ended 0.53 m from the road's line, and the 55 m leg took 360 m round
the block. Fix: `joinRoads` (`map/roadGraph.ts`) adds the vertices back, per tile, once, as it loads.

## Gap

Road ends with no vertex under them, distance to the nearest other road, 54 tiles in six cities: a peak
under 1 unit (0.5 m), almost none from 2 units on, then the free dead ends. Hence `JUNCTION_GAP` = 2.

## Against OSM (centre tile each, junction = a node on two or more bus-class ways)

| City | Junctions in the graph, before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Seoul | 51.0% | 98.2% |
| London | 71.9% | 99.7% |
| Sao Paulo | 69.5% | 99.5% |

Of 759 points the join added in those tiles, 7 had no OSM junction within 8 m.

The first version added vertices before clipping to the tile. That moved a segment's cut on the tile
edge, and 4 seam ends stopped meeting their neighbours, one on a main road in east London. Clipping
first took seam dead ends back to the old count.

Random pairs in the centre of 6 cities, 1500 legs of 150-700 m, 1200 of 2-5 km. Snapped as the game
snaps. "Round the block" is road length at least 1.6 times the straight line and 100 m more, or no path.

Path search, ms per leg, old then new: short legs mean 0.145, 0.156, p95 0.270, 0.287, max 4.05, 3.33;
long legs mean 1.107, 0.740, p95 5.61, 1.56, max 20.9, 19.9. `joinRoads` takes 2-4 ms a tile, once.
No long leg got more than 30 m longer; of the short ones 3 of 9000 did, from a stop landing on the
other of two equally near roads.

Of 24 Tokyo short legs still over the ratio, drawn and read by eye, most follow the real layout (rail
lines, parks, blocks with no through street). Two in the Nishi-Shinjuku grid weave oddly; stacked
roads there are a guess, not checked. The other cities' leftovers were not read.
