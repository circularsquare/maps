# T-2180: the edit note down both ways, and "18 cities" for one Tokyo line (2026-11-03)

Anita: "i added trains to a line and riders and money went down. then i reverted the change and riders
and money went down again." And one local Tokyo line's edit showing "Trips in 18 cities being
recomputed".

Scripts (scratchpad, not kept): `repro.mjs` (the real Tokyo save `data/packs/real/tyo.save` imported,
sandbox, the weekday shown, one demand worker, `?perfOff=focus`; a line's trains doubled, then undo;
every demand view, the money parts and the worker messages logged) and `repro2.mjs` (a Tokyo city game
with six lines, three out to the zone's edges so Gunma and Shizuoka are reached; a line's trains +2 at
each level, then undo). Outputs in `../artifacts/trainworld/t2180/` (`repro1`, `fine`, `real-lod`,
`city-old`, `city-new-run`, .log and .json).

## Part 1: the causes, measured

| Run | Edit | Undo | Edit + undo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real save, `?lod=fine` (no level changes), 大江戸線 trains 50/25/3 doubled | +46,837 riders, −$22.6M profit (running +$25.0M, fares +$2.4M) | −46,942 riders, +$22.6M | −105 riders (0.0003% of 31.7M), −$2k |
| Real save, levels automatic, view elsewhere: all 5 zones (Tokyo too) went coarse between the edit and the undo | +46,837 | −58,886 | −12,049 |
| City game, levels automatic, before the fix (`?perfOff=lodHold`) | +1,549 riders, −$4.54M | −1,549, +$4.54M | 0 |
| City game, the fix, clock running, a level check forced into the edit's settle after the zones' ten minutes | +1,549, −$4.54M | −1,549, +$4.54M | 0 (no level change during or 90 s after) |

What this rules in and out:

- **Zones changing level of detail between two settled states (found, the cause that can flip a
  sign).** A fine zone ten minutes out of view goes coarse at the next 30 s check
  (docs/demand-runtime.md "Levels of detail"), whatever else is going on. When that change landed
  while an edit was settling, its solve was the edit's "after", so the note added the level change to
  the edit. On the real save five zones going coarse moved riders by −11,944, a quarter of what
  doubling 大江戸線's trains adds. Adding two trains to a line moves a few thousand, so a
  coarse change landing with it turns the note's +2k into −10k, and the undo's own (legitimate) fall
  follows: riders down both ways. A level change mid-edit was likely in her session: every zone made
  fine at load times out at the same moment, ten minutes in, and a whole solve of a big Tokyo network
  takes minutes, so the chance one lands inside a settle is high. How much it moves riders depends on
  the zone: it is Tokyo's own level that counts. On the real save with Tokyo in view, the four
  prefectures going coarse moved riders by 0 and fares by $9 (`real-lod`); Gunma and Shizuoka in the
  city game, 0 too. So this explains a drop only where Tokyo itself changes level: the player looking
  at another zone (or zoomed out past 7.5) for ten minutes, then editing a Tokyo line from the Lines
  tab. With Tokyo in view, as she most likely was, it moves nothing, so it is probably not her case.
- **The crowding solve's path dependence (ruled out at this size).** Edit then undo with levels fixed
  comes back within 105 riders of 31.7M and $2k: an undo is honest when nothing else changes. T-1736's
  0.16% was after 17 lines in a row on all of Japan.
- **A baseline that was not final, or a cached result (ruled out).** The note's base is the load's
  whole-solve final (version 2 here); the local result shown mid-edit (same version, +46,750) never
  counts, since demand is still updating then.
- **The long-trip market (not involved).** A city line's train count leaves the market frozen
  (T-1735's window needs a 20-minute changed run); the whole solve re-solves it with the cluster.
- **Money not reversed by undo (ruled out).** The note's profit is fares minus running cost and
  upkeep a day: no purchase or refund is in it. Running cost came back within $7k of $4.15B on the real
  save, exactly in the city game. Profit falling when trains are added is real: on the real save
  doubling 大江戸線 cost $25.0M a day more to run against $2.4M more fares.
- **The day shown** switches base off (a switch of day makes no note); the clock running changed
  nothing in the city game.
- **The note never showing (found, minor).** The note forgets edits that have not settled in 5
  minutes (`GIVE_UP_MS`). Tokyo's real save on one demand worker took 361 s and 352 s, so no note at
  all; on her machine's five workers it is about 2-3 minutes. T-2189.

- **Trains an hour not coming back (ruled out on Tokyo).** +2 trains at each level on each of the 25
  busiest lines of the real save, then undo (`tph.log`): no other line's trains an hour moved, and
  every line came back exactly. (Tokyo's real track is grade-separated; a player's flat crossings
  could move other lines, T-1764's Tohoku case, but undo restores the network.)
- **Riders are boardings (a real effect that reads as wrong).** The note counts boardings with
  transfers, the riders fares are paid on (T-2125). Doubling 大江戸線 put +123,516 on it, while 54 other
  lines lost 76,679 (山手線 −16,056, 丸ノ内線 −11,194): riders move to it from parallel lines and change
  trains less. Net +46,837 here, but a line that saves trips a change can lower the total boardings
  while trips rise, and the note then says riders went down for a better service.

Her exact sequence (down, then down again on undo) was not reproduced: with Tokyo in view, every
pair here came back to within 105 riders. Without her save the candidates are the boardings effect
on the edit and, for money, the size of the edit: profit falls when trains are added (running cost)
and rises when they go, so profit down on the undo needs another change mixed in, which the level
hold now keeps out.

## Part 2: the fix

- While edits settle (`editsSettling`, game/demand.ts, set by game/editNote.ts) the demand client runs
  no level-of-detail check, and each check meanwhile marks every fine zone as just looked at, so a
  zone's ten minutes left alone start after the note (workers/demandClient.ts `lodCheck`). The held
  changes go at the next check after the settle, and land silently in the next base.
- If the zones shown coarse (`roughZones`) still differ between the two states (a level change already
  under way at the first edit), there is no note: the figures are at other levels of detail.
- `?perfOff=lodHold` gives the old behaviour.

The lie: a zone edited while it is coarse (stops changed from the Lines tab while looking elsewhere)
is solved coarse for the edit and refined after the note, a few minutes later than before; and a zone
the budget would drop stays fine until the settle ends (at most 5 minutes). DECISIONS.md.

## Part 3: the 18 cities

The status tag's "Trips in N cities being recomputed" counts the zones of every cluster an edit's
whole solve covers. A cluster is everything a line, a station complex, a city's 30 km reach or the
regional market's 6 km zones joins (docs/demand-runtime.md "Clusters"): a Tokyo network that reaches
out to the prefectures is one cluster. After an edit:

1. The local solve (T-1733) re-solves only the zones whose own view changed. The touch rule already
   does what the brief asks: a train change on a line inside Tokyo touched Tokyo alone ("T1733 v5
   local solve of 1 zones (tyo) of 3 after an edit: 109 ms", city game; the market stays frozen for
   a city line, T-1735). The tag says "Trips here being recomputed" for this step.
2. 8 s after the local results are shown, the whole solve re-solves every zone of the cluster (every
   population, every period the edit changed), because the other zones' riders crowd the same
   trains and the market rides across the cluster. This is the step that says 18 cities.

So the 18 is the whole solve, as designed in T-1733, not the touch rule. Measured: on the real Tokyo
save (five zones) the local result was within 83 riders of the whole solve (31,741,628 against
31,741,711) for doubling 大江戸線, and on one worker the local solve took about 160 s and the whole one
183 s, since Tokyo itself is most of the cluster's work; in the city game 0.1 s and about 8 s.
Ending a city line's edit at its local solve is T-1734's open question (Anita 2026-10-29: keep T-1733's
behaviour for now), with T-1733's bound that drift grows edit by edit (17 lines in a row: 4 of 455
lines past 5%), so it is not a quick fix: T-2188.
