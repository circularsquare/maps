# T-3823 Intercity hover shows the full first wait

## Fix (app only, no demand number changes)

- `game/trip.ts plannedFirstWait(route)`: a market route's first ride has its wait cut to the planned one,
  min(half, 5 + headway/6, 30), the sim's `planned_board_cost` (kernel/search.rs, T-1430). The half headway is
  `waitMin - fullWaitMin` (the sim's leg is `headway/2 + left - span`); the perceived wait is cut by the same ratio
  (as `routeTerms` already treats `waitPerceived - fullWait` as the plain wait). The leg gets `plannedFrom` (the half
  it was) so its hover words itself. Applied only when it cuts 0.05 min or more.
- `MarketTrip.tsx`: `t.routes` mapped through it once at the top, so the route rows, Time, the equation and the other
  routes' hovers all read the planned wait. `railPerceived` and every demand figure are untouched.
- `tripText.rideTip` uses `tripText.rideTipPlanned` for such a leg.
- Not done in Rust: the sim's legs are shared with local trips, which keep the half headway.
- Mirrors the sim's constants (5, 30, a sixth); if T-1430's constants change, change `PLANNED_*` in trip.ts.
