## What was built in stage 1 (the letter design since replaced by stage 2)

- `app/src/map/logoNetwork.ts`: the word as a network. Each letter is one or two strokes; each
  stroke is a line (its own palette colour) with a station at each end and, on some, one in the
  middle. Where strokes meet (the a, d) their boxes overlap and draw as one white shape, as a
  complex's stations do in the game. The i's dot is a lone square box. Timetables are built here:
  trains start from rest, run up to 14 m/s at 0.9 m/s², brake into each stop, wait 12 s, turn at
  the ends; each line's round trip divides an hour, so the clock wraps there without a jump.
- `app/src/ui/StartLogo.tsx`: a canvas with its own WebGL2 context and its own `NetworkRenderer`,
  a flat camera centred on the drawing (as overlay.ts's complex snapshot), drawn every frame.
  Unmounting (a game picked) cancels the loop and drops the context.
- `StartMenu.tsx`: the logo above the card, kept mounted between the menu and the new game sheet;
  the heading is now "Welcome to trainsworld!" (T-1568). `style.css`: the menu is a centred column.

## Choices and why

- **The real renderer, a made-up network.** The game's renderer draws `NetworkBuffers`, which the
  clock worker builds from the sim. The logo builds those buffers itself, as `synth.ts` does for the
  stress network, so the line strokes, casing, station boxes (platform shape from the game's own
  `platformShape`), the line through the boxes, interchange shapes and trains in cars are the
  game's drawing code, not an imitation. No pack, WASM or worker is needed.
- **Zoom 15, scaled by pixel ratio.** Everything is laid out in design px at zoom 15 (where boxes
  and cars show). To fit a screen the whole drawing is drawn at a pixel ratio of dpr x scale, so
  lines, boxes and trains keep their proportions. Scale is at most 1.25 (about 830 px wide).
- **Two rows on narrow screens**: "trains" over "world" when one row would fall under 0.8 scale, so
  a phone gets letters about as big as a desktop's.
- **Letterforms**: our own lowercase rounded monoline: straight runs with quarter turns, a
  double-storey a (arch plus bowl, two lines), a square-cornered s, a diagonal w, a closed-loop o.
  Nothing taken from NIMBY Rails' logo beyond the idea of letters made of lines.
- **Platforms 40 m, two car trains**, so a train fits its platform and the boxes stay small next
  to a 64 px x-height (real 150 m platforms would cover most of each letter).
- **A plain panel behind the logo** (the card's background and rule): over the bare world map its
  country names showed between the lines. Flat, no shadow.
- **Pace**: 5 game seconds a second. Reduced motion asked for: one still frame.
