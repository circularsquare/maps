# T-1506: a slot in the access list for unlinked track (2026-10-26)

From T-1504's cause 3 (notes/T-1504.md). A market zone end's access list kept its 8 best stations;
in a city of many metro lines those were all metro stations, so an inter-city station on track not
linked to the metro was in no zone's list. Now (`access_list` in `sim/src/demand/intercity.rs`) a
list keeps its 8 best, then up to 10 in all, the best station of each track component none of the 8
is on. All six lists (longer trips both ends, commutes' and outings' ends) are built by it, and the
trip explorer (`market_trip.rs`) reads the same lists, so it agrees with the solve.

## What was measured

Each save loaded on the tree's Vite in headless Chrome (weekday view after demand final). "Longer"
is the weekday's line boardings of the market's inter-city trips (T-1373's figure). The market's
pass time is the sum, over periods and rounds, of the time the workers spent in the market's
`solve` and `crowd_with_external` (a temporary timer in demand.worker.ts, removed). Another job held
8 cores throughout, so timings carry a few percent of noise.

## The rule chosen, and the one dropped

The brief proposed a slot per line not yet listed. Built first as two tiers (track first, then
lines, cap 10), it gave Tokyo 1.28x the market's pass time (34.4 to 44.0 s) for +30 longer
boardings (1,636 to 1,666) and New York +52, and on China nothing beyond the track tier (7,621 both
ways, every pair identical). A line on linked track is reached by changing trains, so its slot buys
little; the track tier alone is the fix, at no measurable cost. Dropped the line tier.

## Is 10 enough

On China, 445 of 6,295 home-end lists gained a track slot; 51 still leave some unlinked track out at
10 (zones near two or more unlinked high-speed lines and a metro). Tokyo and New York: no list
leaves track out (Tokyo's network is all linked; New York has 9 lists with a slot).

## What still limits China

Beijing - Shanghai is 7.4% served against 84% when T-1504 linked the stations: a zone's weights only
hold the stations its homes walk to (6 nearest a 600 m site) or ride to (the 6 cheapest lines from
its 2 km zone), and in Shanghai the high-speed line is rarely among the 6 cheapest lines, so most of
the city's zones never see its station at all. The access list cannot give a slot to a station that
is not in its weights. T-1511 is that step (rides reserving a line per track component), with its
cost on the local layers too.
