# T-1617 the dark meridian seam (done 2026-10-28)

Anita's screenshot (`artifacts/trainworld/t1617/anita-seam-tsushima.png`, flat map, a Nagasaki
sandbox) shows a thin dark line at about 128.43 E. It runs over sea and over Korean land and stays
after "Updating demand" ends.

Cause: the realm shade (`map/shadeLayer.ts`, the custom layer drawing `outsideZones`). Its
triangulation has a long straight edge at x = 128.4328, from 45.67 N to 27.11 N: the west side of
the box round the Japan zones, with a fan of very thin earcut slivers from its corner at 45.67 N.
In exact arithmetic nothing overlaps (2000x2000 rasterisation: no point covered twice), but on her
GPU the slivers meet a pixel apart. The core of her line is two or three coats of shade darker than
the sea, with a pixel with none on each side. The longitude is not a tile edge at zoom 6-9, and the
outside-city and zone-loading shades were clean under repeated setData.

Fix: the shade layer draws through the stencil (test equal 0, increment on pass), so a pixel is
shaded at most once whatever the triangles do. MapLibre's own stencil is cleared before and after
(`painter.frameRenderContext.clearStencil()`), which also makes its next tile-clipped layer draw
its masks again. Check: the shade with every triangle sent twice now matches the shade sent once,
on the flat map and on the globe (`artifacts/trainworld/t1617/t1617-after-*-shade-{once,twice}.png`).

Headless Chrome never showed the seam itself: not on the globe sweeps (any zoom, theme, pitch, the
real save, 14 realms shown, Terrain), and not on the all-Japan save with Anita's settings on the real
GPU.
