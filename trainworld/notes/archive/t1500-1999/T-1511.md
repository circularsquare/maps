# T-1511: rides reserve a line per track component (2026-10-26)

From T-1506's "What still limits China" (notes/T-1506.md). `kernel::access_options` kept, from each
place, the cheapest station of each of the 6 cheapest lines (`ride_lines`). In Shanghai the unlinked
high-speed line was rarely among the 6, so most zones never had its station in their weights and
T-1506's access-list slot could not reach it. Now, with `track` given (each station's track
component and whether a train line stops there), a place's rides add up to `RIDE_TRACK_MAX` (2) more:
the cheapest line of each component none of the first 6 is on, its station on a line with trains.

Only the market passes `track` (`set_access` in `intercity.rs`, both ends); the local layers'
`build_subzones` passes `None`. `on_train` and `station_components` moved from `intercity.rs` to
`kernel.rs` so both could use them.

## Reading it

The reservation does what T-1506 asked: Beijing - Shanghai goes from 7.4% to 48.5% served (T-1504's
linking test reached 84%, since a linked station is reached by the metro, not a ride of up to 15 km).
Rail barely moves, because the newly served zones reach the high-speed station by long rides (the
pair's perceived rail cost rises from 1,288 to 1,503 as they join the average, against the car's
1,102), and on this save rail is already dear for T-1504's causes 1 and 2 (fares, 160 km/h service).
So the gain shows once T-1507 fixes the save or fares weigh less; the reservation removes the cap.

## Local layers: left out

Applied everywhere, New York's rail trips moved +3,820 (local rides gaining other railroads' stations)
and China's +29,000, with no change to anything the market reads, and the local layers would build the
rail graph a second time per pack for the components. The local layers' rides choose among lines a
local rider changes between anyway, so the reservation is the market's only.
