# T-1530 Station bubbles: transfers at once, a new line's stations with its first figures

Anita, 2026-10-27: "when turning on station bubbles it takes a long time to load transfers. ideally
this is something we precompute, at least roughly, unless its super expensive. also after setting up
a new line it really takes quite a while to show station ridership."

Tree t1530, on master after T-1538 and T-1539. Spec: docs/ui-demand-views.md "Station ridership".

## Where the time went (before)

Origins and Transfers (and Total's shading) need each station's transfers. `game/stationUse.ts`
asked them of the solving workers for every view the page showed: `complexTransfers` over every
station, which the workers answer only while every one of their periods holds the round asked about.
Working the answer out is cheap (a pass over the transfer edges, a few ms); the time was the wait:

- **During a solve** (a load, or any solve with its estimates shown) the question goes to the next
  round, and each worker answers on reaching it: the split came one or more rounds after the figures.
- **After an edit** the shown view keeps the old solve's version (T-232, T-324's early figures), which
  the workers have left, so the question got nothing back, and the new line's stations had no
  Origins or Transfers figure until the edit's final.
- **Turned on after a final** it was already quick (0.1 s): idle workers answer at once.

## What changed

- `transfers_in(q)` on `DemandApi`, `EverydayApi` and `IntercityApi` (kernel `transfers_in`): riders
  boarding at each route node after changing lines in period q, `transfer_pairs` with every node
  marked, summed by the boarding node. Cargo test `transfers_in_sums_the_pairs_by_boarding_node`.
- The worker's period results carry `xferIn`: per kind of day and route node of the whole network,
  each population's `transfers_in` times the factor its `transfers_day` scales its pairs by
  (commutes by work's, the market by the longer trips', the everyday layer by its rail riders' mean
  factor over its purposes). 7 days x the route nodes, about 140 kB a period result on the real save.
- The day views (`demandSums.ts`) add each station's `transfers` beside its boardings; packed to the
  page, kept in the demand cache (an older cached result has none and asks the workers as before).
- `stationUse.ts` sets the split from the view at once. For a solve's final (not a cached result, and
  not a view carrying a new line's early figures, whose solve the workers have left) it still asks the
  transfer pairs and replaces the split with their answer, so final figures are the query's as before.
  `?perfOff=splitAhead` turns the carried split off.

Cost: the extra pass per population per period is a few ms against seconds of solve; no slower
solve was measurable in the runs below (load figures 10-12 s both ways, smoke's final 161-163 s on a
busy machine).

## Accuracy

The real save's final, average day (the default shown day): the carried transfers against the
query's, every one of 1,756 stations equal to the rider (rounded), sum 11,027,057 both. An earlier
version that scaled weekday transfers by each node's boardings was 1-3% off at the big stations,
hence the per-population factors.

## Measured

`checks/t1530-station-bubbles.mjs` on this tree's Vite, back to back, before with
`QUERY=perfOff=splitAhead` (`../artifacts/trainworld/t1530/before.json`, `after.json`). Busy machine.

| case | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| real save picked, bubbles on: Total's first figures | 11.4 s | 10.4 s |
| the same: Origins' first split | 49.4 s | 10.4 s (with the figures) |
| bubbles turned on after the final, Origins / Transfers | 0.12 / 0.00 s | 0.00 / 0.01 s |
| new line on the real save: Total | 10.7 s | 11.2 s |
| the same: Origins | 54.2 s (the edit's final) | 11.2 s (with Total) |
| new line, new game: Total / Origins | 0.95 / 5.4 s | 1.0 / 1.0 s |
| second line, new game: Total / Origins | 0.67 / 1.0 s | 0.66 / 0.66 s |

A first run before (with a different load) gave 9.1 s figures and 43.2 s split on the load, 11.2 s
and 64.2 s on the real save's new line.

The new line's first figures themselves (about 11 s on the real save here) are T-1538's free-flow
preview: the edit's network reaches the workers about 0.5 s after the line is built, then set-up
(about 0.65 s a worker) and one period's free flow on New York's whole cluster take the other 10 s. Splitting that period over the workers
is T-1543; nothing more to take here.
