# T-1607: a period's market on the idle worker (2026-10-28)

**Outcome: all of Japan's cold load 11% faster on 3 solving workers, figures identical; no gain on
2 workers, where it is off.** The quarter the task hoped for is out of reach for an exact split by
whole populations (below).

## What was built

In a solve with the barrier (a load, a zone opened) a period's market may be solved by another
worker than its cities (`marketsOfWorkers` in `demandClient.ts`; with 3 workers the evening's goes
to the worker holding only the evening peak). Each side solves its populations from the same summed
loads; after every round it posts its loads (3 x route nodes) through the page to the other side,
which adds them cities first, then the market (one population, so the bits are a single worker's).
Both send the period's figures as `result` parts; `addParts` adds them and works `arrive` out again
from the parts' boardings (`come`). A worker drops any period of a population it does not solve
(`IntercityApi::forget_period`, new). Such a solve re-solves every market cluster and keeps no
shared period; the next solve without the barrier re-solves market clusters once (`sharedParts`).
Spec: docs/demand-runtime.md, "A market set apart".

## Why not a quarter

Only a whole population can move with the figures exact to the bit: two parts of one population's
pass (or several populations moved together) add their floats in another order. With the cities
staying where they are, the markets are 94-96 s units against periods of 120-126 s, so some worker
ends with a whole period plus a market: about 216 s against 246 s by the cost table, -12%, which is
what was measured. Moving cities as well (one worker solving only markets) would reach about 192 s
by the table, but puts three periods' cities on one worker (more memory there, against the brief)
and leaves the midday-and-night worker at 205-220 s as measured, so about -15% in practice. On 2
workers the best move (the night's market) measured nothing, so `SHARE_GAIN` (11%) turns it off
there and on 4 workers.

## Cost after a load

The first solve after a load without the barrier (an edit) re-solves the evening period whole even
when its trains did not change, since its two sides were held apart.
