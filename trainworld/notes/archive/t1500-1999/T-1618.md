Anita: 30-50% of her GPU on the all-Japan save; target under ~10%.

## Setup

Headless Chrome on the real GPU (ANGLE d3d11, AMD Radeon RX 6600, the machine's only adapter), 1920x1080
at pixel ratio 1 (her display is 1920x1080 at 60 Hz), map canvas 1603x994. `t737-japan-trains.save`
(709 lines, 10,320 stations), master 85732f70, demand final after 1,090 s with 2 demand workers. Globe and
Terrain on (her conditions), light shade, Japan in view at zoom 4.6 unless the row says Tokyo (zoom 11).

- **GPU %**: the Chrome GPU process's 3D engine use, 6 one-second samples of the Windows counter
  `\GPU Engine(pid_*engtype_3D)\Utilization Percentage` (`typeperf`): the Task Manager figure.
- **frames/s**: animation frames with any rAF callback; MapLibre renders (`_render`); overlay draws.
- **GPU ms**: timer queries (EXT_disjoint_timer_query) per MapLibre render and per overlay draw.
- The overlay canvas has 4x MSAA and a stencil; MapLibre's and the demand canvas have neither.

Scripts: `notes/T-1618/hold.mjs` (loads the save once, keeps Chrome open on CDP 9338),
`measure.mjs <gpu pid>` (the whole table in one run, ~5 min), `probe.mjs` (stroke counts and cuts).
Raw log: `artifacts/trainworld/t1618/measure-run1.log`.

The pan rows' frame rates wander (11-56 MapLibre renders/s) because the GPU is the bottleneck: the
overlay's 12-13 ms per draw sets the pace. Compare them by GPU % and GPU ms, not frame counts.

### Where the overlay's 11 ms goes (probe.mjs, clock paused, forced draws)

The track strokes and the line strokes are 860,041 segments each. Each is drawn twice (casing, then
fill), so one draw is 3.44M instances of a 4-vertex strip (5.2M at zoom 15, where the lane passes add
more), at every zoom.

| view | instances | overlay GPU ms | without line strokes | without track strokes | viewport 1x1 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Japan, zoom 4.6 | 3.44M | 11.3 | 7.5 | 8.0 | 10.4 |
| Kanto, zoom 8 | 3.44M | 12.2 | 11.0 | 10.5 | 13.8 |
| Tokyo, zoom 11 | 3.47M | 11.2 | 10.6 | 11.2 | 12.4 |
| street, zoom 15 | 5.21M | 16.1 | 10.1 | 9.9 | 16.1 |

The cost stays the same on a 1x1 px viewport, so it is vertex work: all 3.4M instances go through the
vertex shader on every draw, whatever is on screen. Fill rate, MSAA and pixel ratio hardly matter (pixel
ratio 2 adds 4%).

## What it means

- **The page does not redraw when nothing moves.** With the clock paused and the map still, nothing is
  drawn (0 MapLibre renders, 0 overlay draws, 2.6% GPU). One rAF loop still runs every frame: the
  top bar's sky (`ui/SkyBanner.tsx`), which returns early when nothing moved. That costs CPU wake-ups
  only, no GPU.
- **While the clock runs, the overlay redraws all 1.7M stroke segments every frame (56/s), even
  though only the trains move.** That is 70 of the 77 points of GPU use. The trains themselves, the
  basemap, Terrain, station names, capacity tags, bubbles and the demand canvas are each under 1-2
  points. MapLibre does not redraw while the camera is still.
- The clock's speed changes nothing: 1x and fastest both redraw at the full frame rate.
- Zoomed out to Japan at 1x, the fastest train moves about 2 px a second (100 m/s x 60 / ~2.6 km per px),
  so 56 redraws a second is about 50 more than the picture needs.
- While panning, the same 12-13 ms stroke draw runs on every MapLibre frame. MapLibre's own draw is
  0.7-3 ms, about 1 ms of it Terrain.

## Fixes, ranked by gain

1. **T-1638 [P2] Keep the strokes in a texture between camera moves.** Draw track and line strokes (and
   whatever else only changes with the camera, network, selection or hover) into an offscreen texture
   (multisampled, then resolved). Clock-only frames then copy the texture and draw the trains and
   platforms over it. Gain: clock running with the map still goes from 77% to about 8%, which is the
   measured figure with strokes off. This is the state Anita is in most of the time.
2. **T-1639 [P2] Fewer stroke segments zoomed out and off screen.** 1.7M segments are drawn at every
   zoom. Zoomed out, most are far below a pixel: simplify them per zoom band (at zoom 4.6 one px is
   ~2.6 km), and cull chunks that are off screen when zoomed in. Gain: panning and zooming, which
   T-1638 does not help (75% now, overlay 12-13 ms per frame), and every redraw T-1638 still has to do.
   Probably 5-10x fewer instances zoomed out.
3. **T-1640 [P3] Redraw as often as trains visibly move.** While the camera is still, draw only when the
   fastest possible train (about 100 m/s x clock speed) would have moved about half a pixel at the
   current zoom: about 5 fps at Japan zoom at 1x, the full rate zoomed in. On its own: 77% to about 7%
   at Japan zoom and 1x, nothing zoomed in. After T-1638 it saves most of the remaining ~8%. P3 because
   T-1638 already takes most of the gain; it also needs a check that money and clock figures still
   move smoothly ("live feel").
4. **T-1641 [P3] The sky banner's rAF loop runs while paused.** It costs no GPU, but it wakes the page
   56 times a second while nothing changes. Draw it when the clock's minute, the view or the theme
   changes. Small gain: CPU and battery while paused.

Not worth a task: Terrain (about 1 ms of a MapLibre frame, 0 while still), the basemap, names, tags,
bubbles, globe or flat, and pixel ratio. Each is a point or two at most.

## T-1640: redraw only when something visible moves (2026-10-28)

What drew while hidden: `Overlay.scheduleNext` asked only whether the hour had trips, not whether any
showed, so with the map still and the clock running the whole overlay was redrawn 60 times a second
below zoom 10 (trains hidden): the stroke texture copied twice, the stations, the clear, and
`renderer.sync`. Trains were not drawn, MapLibre did not repaint (0 renders/s), the stations and the
cache copy were the cost (7.9% GPU against 3.0% paused).

Now `renderer.stillWaitMs` says how long the picture can wait with the camera still, and the overlay
plans its next frame from it (one timer, the game minute still turned on its own). Same setup as above
(`PORT=6159 CDP=9340`, `measure.mjs` rows `T1640 ...`, the throttle toggled off in the same load,
`t1640-check.mjs`). Raw: scratchpad t1640 (`table-part.md`, `check.log`).

| paused, Japan z4.6 | 3.0 | 0 |

| Japan z4.6, fastest | 8.7 / 4.7 | 60 / 2 (one is a stroke redraw that also happens unthrottled) |

Trains show from zoom 10, where the fastest train moves about 100 px a second at 1x, so the
