# T-1742: the seven real networks against published rail figures (2026-10-28)

Written answer only; no demand code or constant changed. Anita's question after T-1625 (0.48 of MLIT's
passenger-km for all of Japan): is the low figure Japan alone, or everywhere?

**Answer: not Japan alone, and not low everywhere.** Against published figures the game's rail trips a
weekday run from about 0.4-0.5 (Tokyo) to about 2.5 (New York). Tokyo is the one clearly low metro.
Toronto, London and Paris are near 1.0-1.2, Seoul is 1.5 high, New York 2.4-2.7 high, Sendai 0.8.

## How the game's figures were taken

Each `data/packs/real/<id>.save` was imported into a `?new=1&opened=all&paused=1` game on Vite 6195 (1 demand
worker), waited to the strict demand final of the weekday (day 0; 14 s Sendai, 28 s Toronto, 80 s Seoul, 197 s
Tokyo; New York, Paris and London a few minutes each), then dumped (`../artifacts/trainworld/t1742/<id>.json`,
scripts `notes/T-1742/run_real.mjs` (`CITY=nyc OUT=<file> node run_real.mjs` against a Vite on 6195, modelled on
notes/T-1625/run_japan.mjs) and `notes/T-1742/groups.py` (sums by operator group)).

- **Rail trips** = `view.railTripsPerDay`: journeys on the player's rail, a journey counted once however many
  lines it uses. This is the figure to set against operators' *entries* (MTA rides, gate entries, validations).
- **Line riders** = Σ `ridersPerDay`: boardings onto lines, transfers included. It is 1.8-2.0 per journey in
  every city but Sendai (1.25). Real networks run about 1.4 (NUMBAT: 7.12M line boarders over 5.17M entries,
  London TfL). The saves cut branches, patterns and pieces into separate lines, so a rider changing between
  two pieces of one real line counts twice. Line riders are therefore only comparable with a published
  *line* boarding count, and then run high. The headline uses journeys.
- **pkm** = Σ over lines of segment load x segment km (weekday, all periods). The game reports no pkm directly.
- Populations are the packs' `totals.pop`.

## What the pattern suggests

Hypotheses with their evidence; nothing here proposes a change.

1. **The game's rail volume follows each metro's network and population, not the real habit of using rail.**
   Journeys per resident in the game fall in a narrow band for the big metros (0.41 Tokyo, 0.50 Seoul, 0.56 New York,
   0.68 London, 0.73 Paris), where the published figures range 0.21 to 1.07:

   | | Game per resident | Published per resident | Ratio |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Tokyo | 0.41 | 0.86-1.07 | 0.4-0.5 |
   | London | 0.68 | 0.70 | 1.0 |
   | Paris | 0.73 | 0.62 | 1.2 |
   | Seoul | 0.50 | 0.33 | 1.5 |
   | New York | 0.56 | 0.21-0.24 | 2.4-2.7 |
   | Toronto | 0.21 | 0.21 | 1.0 |
   | Sendai | 0.19 | 0.25 | 0.8 |

   The ratio falls as real rail use per resident rises (Tokyo, London, Paris, Seoul, New York in order), and
   it is a matter of where on that line a place sits. The model has no country or culture term for rail, on purpose
   (Anita, 2026-10-17), and its rail share is set where a good train is everywhere (the T-636 ceilings, rail's share
   of rail plus car: A at least 85%). New York, whose real rail use is low for the size of its network, comes out
   high; Tokyo, the real maximum, comes out at about the same game level as the others, so low. This is read off the
   numbers above; the demand code was not opened to confirm which rules produce the flatness.
   Toronto and Sendai are the small networks and sit near 1 because their game volumes are small too (0.2 a resident).
   Size does not order the ratios (Tokyo 39.8M and New York 22.0M are at the two ends; Seoul 26.0M is in the middle).
2. **Distance is a second, smaller effect in Japan.** T-1625 found the conventional lines low on passenger-km
   (0.41) and trips together. The game's journeys run 11-15 km a trip in every big metro (pkm over rail trips: New York
   14.1, London 13.6, Paris 11.0, Seoul 14.9, Tokyo 13.1), and a JR line boarding is 8.6 km, a private railway one 5.7 km.
   Real Japanese commuter-rail trips are longer than the other cities' (JR East Kanto's 107.5bn pkm in FY2017 is a
   per-passenger figure well above a subway ride), and that does not show in the game. Not quantified: no JR East
   passenger count to divide by was found.
3. **Commuter rail is the part most above published in New York and the part most below in Tokyo.** New York's pkm
   against NTD runs 2.1 for the subway and 3.0-3.3 for the railroads; Tokyo's subways are 0.75 and its JR lines lowest.
   That is Japan's 0.41 conventional against 0.65 subway, turned round. It fits hypothesis 1: where a commuter
   network exists the model fills it by the global rules, and in Tokyo, where it is the real majority mode, it gets
   no extra weight for that.

If a cause looks obvious it is hypothesis 1, stated as: rail volume is set by network coverage and the global
mode rules, with no term for how much people in a place use rail. A place-specific rail propensity is the
"constant per country" Anita asked to be proposed first; this note does not propose one. Per T-1625 and her note
that she will mostly leave it loose, the finding to carry is that the game's rail trips are within 0.8-1.5 of
published figures for London, Paris, Seoul, Toronto and Sendai, 0.4-0.5 for Tokyo and about 2.5 for New York.

## Limits

- Dates differ (2023-2025 published, Tokyo 2015 and 2019 pre-telework, so Tokyo's published side is high by perhaps
  10%); the game's population is 2020s data. Weekday-to-average conversions are the operators' own where given, else
  the 1.14 above.
- Published sums are entries or validations (journeys plus changes between separately gated systems), a little above
  journeys, so ratios are a touch low; the exception is Paris, where missing validations push them high.
- Game rail trips include trips on every line in the save (SIR, NJT light rail, Toronto streetcars). Where the published
  sum lacks a mode the ratio is high by that mode's share; for New York about 5-8%, for Toronto streetcars are in both.
- The game's trips come from one demand solve per save in its packs with every zone opened; no player-built rail added.
- pkm: the game reports none directly; published pkm found only for New York (NTD, derived) and JR East. No pkm ratio
  is given for the other five. Reading NTD through a search summary is lower confidence than the other figures.
