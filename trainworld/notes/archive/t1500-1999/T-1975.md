## 1. Pitfalls that happened

Sources: notes/T-1191 4a (Korea), T-1239 (Canada), T-1738 (Vietnam, Thailand, Singapore, origins), T-1857
(Australia), T-1185 (China rebuild), T-1004 (US), T-723/779/786/788 (Europe), ARCHIVE and DECISIONS.
"Cost" is wall time lost and extra agent tokens where the notes or transcripts show them.

**Origins**
- A zone's origin was the densest pixel anywhere in the zone, so the gate's test line ran through desert,
  hills or another country. 35+ rows: China's Altay and 11 city zones, Thailand 5, Vietnam 11, Canada 5,
  Europe 3. Several were first relaxed as "browser: no school trip" before anyone looked at the line
  (knt, mge, lao, ksg, aty). Cost: Thailand's 75-minute join done twice; Vietnam, Canada, Europe and China
  rebuilt and rejoined (about 9 h of runs and joins); one agent 22.3M tokens. Fixed in the planner for new
  plans (T-1762). Fix here: `checks/zone_origins.py` on the dry run (playbook 3), and a rule: never relax a
  browser park without opening the line picture.

**Outlines and zone planning**
- Scattered units: geoBoundaries' "Unincorporated NT" put Uluru and Groote Eylandt specks in Darwin's zone,
  seen only on a picture after the join. Cost: a rebuild and a whole join (39 min, outlines 15). Fix: a
  dry-run check for zones with parts over 100 km apart (T-1982).
- Tiny lone zones park on their own values (top_zone_jobs_share, short commutes, park coverage): Vietnam's
  Muong Nhe and Con Dao, Australia's Whyalla, Canada's Iles-de-la-Madeleine (still parked), Korea's
  Tongyeong. 4 countries, each fixed by adding the country to `ALONE_MIN` after the park. Fix: a default
  for every country (T-1984); until then the playbook adds it before the build.
- The 1.5M cap or GHS-FUA cut real cities: Adelaide split in two, Sydney missing Campbelltown, Mission sent
  to Vancouver. Hand patches each time (Australia 10 min plus a picture; Canada about an hour of rule work).
  Fix: the cap never splits a GHS-FUA over 1M (T-1984); until then the preflight list says what to look for.
- Outline geometry faults: Daegu's GEOS error (a day parked, 4.1M tokens for T-1262), Kwai Chung dropped
  (T-1144), Portugal's three still parked (T-787). Fixed as found; the picture catches the rest.
- Canada's `plan_units` read geoBoundaries afresh: 10 builds stuck 2 h at the step limit. Fixed (T-1239).

**Census joins (population hooks)**
- Late finds of district tables meant second builds (Shanghai, Mumbai) and renamed units needed hand maps
  (Korea). Planned rows avoid most of it: a hook is either ready before the run or left out. The US module
  was tested on two rows in scratch first (T-1016) and built clean; Australia's was not and also built
  clean, but its census-vs-GHSL line was read only after the run. Fix: a new hook is tried on one metro and
  one zone before the run (playbook 1).

**Gate parks**
- drive_factor_median over 2.0 in hills, deltas and fjords: China 210 rows over two builds, Vietnam 7,
  Europe about 18, Canada 2, the US several, Chinese city zones 1. Every relax costs a script edit, a
  gate-only rerun (10-60 min) and one or two agent wakes (0.3-0.8M). The largest value relaxed is 2.85;
  no zone parked on it was ever kept parked. Fix: zones fail only above 3.0 (T-1985).
- The relax pass is routine but each agent wrote its own `setbuilt.py` (8 kits). Fix: one standing tool
  (T-1983) and a decision table (playbook 6).
- Replans dropped relaxations: 108 Chinese parks in T-1185. Fixed (T-1307).

**Joins and market heal**
- Market packs kept naming the places of rows that left the registry, so `register --market` panicked and
  whole runs parked: Vietnam's run 2 (8 rows), China's 11-row run (10 parked after 3 h), T-1184/1185
  (a pre-step). T-1743's heal reaches 400 km and missed 3 in China. Fix: heal every registered market pack
  naming the place, whatever the distance (T-1986); check with `tools/country/stale.py` after every run.
- Old per-id caches of reused ids: Canada 48 files (3 parks), Vietnam 210 files (2 parks), Hong Kong's
  first rebuild. Fixed (T-1743) except `data/terrain/<id>.terrain` (Australia's lnc) (T-1986).
- Several joins per country: Canada 5, Australia 2, Thailand 2, China's reruns 2. Each join pays about
  20 minutes of outlines and realms on top of its solve. Fix: one relax run, then one join (playbook 6, 7).
- A join on 2 cores timed out at 7,200 s (Saanich). Fix: a join claims at least 6 cores.

**Other writers during a run**
- A terrain job rewrote every zone's `data/terrain` during Australia's gate: 2 parks, an 18-minute rerun.
  A walk rerun parked China's Henan. Stale demand WASM in the main tree: Saanich failed its browser check
  3 runs running (T-1377). Another session's Vite on the run's port: 37 US rows parked at once. Fix:
  preflight items (smoke on your own Vite, port free, nobody writing packs or terrain), and CLAUDE.md's
  join rule names `data/terrain` too (T-1987).
- Machine limits: GDAL reads failing on Windows' commit limit (Canada BC twice, the US Midwest; T-1080
  open), a 100-row region gate past the 20-minute quiet timer (China C), the asia/sea read past its step
  limit twice. Fix: playbook notes (fewer jobs for reads, `TW_QUIET_S=3600` for regions over 100 rows).

**Checks skipped**
- The outline picture before the build (Australia), the line picture in the load check (Chiang Mai), smoke
  before the run (Canada's WASM). Each one cost a rerun. Fix: all three are preflight or "done when" items.

**Token waste**
- Polling: the T-1762 agent (Sonnet) woke every 12 minutes for 11 hours, 44 times, each re-caching about
  300k tokens: 17M of its 22.3M. The rule (one background wait on the process) exists in CLAUDE.md; it
  was not mechanical enough. Fix: the playbook gives the exact wait command.
- Waking at a big context: Canada's agent woke 17 times at 250-420k (7.25M of 16.1M), China's T-1185 run
  agent 14 times (4.4M of 10.3M), Europe's T-786 and T-788 supervisors 13 and 18 times (about 4.9M each).
  Every wake costs 1.25x the context. Fix: fewer stages (above), a short doc, and hand back before a long
  wait when the context is past about 200k (playbook 9).
- Reading: the doc is 60k characters, the last country's notes 10-30k, the data register 46k; agents
  read most of it whole, then carry it in every later call. Fix: a 200-line playbook with links; read one
  register row by grep.
- Kits rewritten per country: dry.py, cmpplan.py, setbuilt.py, backup.py, audit.py, start_hidden.ps1,
  stalecache.py, commutes.py in t1018, t1238, t1239, t1738, t1757, t1762, t1857, hh-t1185b. Fix: T-1983.
- Retrospectives say "tokens: not counted here" (4 of 4 recent ones) and city-costs.md stops at Sao
  Paulo. Fix: the table below goes into city-costs.md (T-1987).

## 2. Tokens per country

The table (weighted tokens, wakes and peak context of each country's agents) moved to notes/city-costs.md
"Per country" (T-1987).

What it says:
- A clean country on global sources costs about 6M (Vietnam, Thailand), 0.06-0.09M a row; with a new
  module about 6M (Australia). T-535 predicted about 0.2M a row; that holds where nothing goes wrong.
- Faults, not size, decide the cost: Canada 16M for 50 rows, the origin reruns 22M for 35 rows, Daegu 4M
  for one row.
- Wakes are 25-77% of every agent that waited on runs. Output is never over 0.6M; the rest is the context
  read back on every call.

Biggest savings, in order:
1. One background wait per stage, no polling: would have saved about 15M on T-1762 alone.
2. Fewer stages: the preflight list and the gate and planner defaults turn a typical "run, relax, rerun,
   join, fix, rerun, rejoin" into "run, relax run, join". Each stage avoided saves 1-3 wakes (0.4-1.2M)
   and 15-20 minutes of outlines.
3. A smaller context: the 200-line playbook, summary-printing tools, hand back past about 200k. A wake at
   120k costs 0.15M against 0.45M at 360k.
4. A standing kit (T-1983): no script writing per country.

Target for a global-sources country of about 100 rows: 2-3M weighted, one agent, about 4 wakes.

## 3. What the new playbook changes

- One procedure for a country (world plan rows), in order, each step with a "done when". The hand-made
  city path, the runner's internals and the inputs table move to reference docs (section 4).
- Source choice by rule: global sources (archetype D) unless a population hook can be made in an hour
  from an open small-area census with a pattern to copy (Canada's DAs, Australia's SA1s); jobs, flows and
  surveys are later P3 tasks, never part of the first pass.
- A preflight checklist on the dry run: origins, scattered units, lone tiny zones, cut cities, borders,
  reused ids, machine state (smoke, port, disk, cores, nobody writing packs or terrain).
- Launch detached with batch, PIDs, log, liveness and restart written down first, and the exact
  background wait command.
- A park decision table: which checks may be relaxed and with what reason, which need a fix instead
  (browser parks: look at the line), what is a code fault to hand back.
- One relax run, then one join.
- Stop and hand back rules, including the context size.
- A 10-line retrospective with a fixed shape; the orchestrator adds tokens and wakes.
- Model: with this playbook a global-sources country is a Sonnet task (my suggestion for the brief); a new
  module or zone rule stays Opus.
