# T-1887, T-1890: water in the track tag, terrain and water as added surcharges

## Prices per km, double track, US$M

Base 90 a km, times the level's multiplier (ground 0.5, +1 and -1 1.0, -2 1.1), times the terrain
factor at levels -1 and up.

Before (one factor at every level from -1 up: 1, 1.25, 1.6, 2.5):

| Class | Ground (0) | Viaduct (+1) | Cut-and-cover (-1) | Tunnel (-2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flat | 45 | 90 | 90 | 99 |
| Hills (low, 1.25) | 56.25 | 112.5 | 112.5 | 99 |
| Hills (high, 1.6) | 72 | 144 | 144 | 99 |
| Mountains (2.5) | 112.5 | 225 | 225 | 99 |

T-1887 first gave ground level its own mountain factor of 6 (270 a km at grade, viaduct 225). Anita
found 0.5x times 6x confusing; T-1890 makes terrain and water additive instead (her numbers):
multiplier = level (0.5 ground, 1.0 at +1 and -1, 1.1 at -2) + terrain (+0.4 low hills, +1.0 high
hills, +2.5 mountains; none in bored tunnels) + water (+2, any level).

After T-1890:

| Class | Ground (0) | Viaduct (+1) | Cut-and-cover (-1) | Tunnel (-2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flat | 45 | 90 | 90 | 99 |
| Hills (low, +0.4) | 81 | 126 | 126 | 99 |
| Hills (high, +1.0) | 135 | 180 | 180 | 99 |
| Mountains (+2.5) | 270 | 315 | 315 | 99 |
| Over water (+2) | bridge | 270 (was 180) | 270 | 279 (was 198) |

Ordering after: flat ground < viaduct < tunnel; low hills ground < tunnel < viaduct; high hills tunnel < ground < viaduct; mountains tunnel < ground < viaduct. Ground
can never pass a viaduct with an added surcharge (0.5 against 1.0), which Anita's list accepts.
