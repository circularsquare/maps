# T-1733: the local solve after an edit (T-1650 stage 1), 2026-10-28

Design: notes/T-1650.md sections 2-4 (approved 2026-10-28). Built on T-1609's local refine
(notes/T-1609.md). Anita: the constructing label clears zone by zone, intercity lines included.

## The mechanism

All in `workers/demand.worker.ts` and `demandClient.ts`; no WASM change.

- **What a population holds.** `cityOn`: the view (network within the city's reach) each city
  population last solved every one of its periods on, untouched since; `marketOn`: a market part's
  route nodes on the current network. Set when a cluster's solve ends (`solved`), dropped when a
  population is set on a network again (`disturbed`, from `setUpPops`) or freed.
- **Carrying route nodes across networks.** A solve message carries the network's line and station
  ids (`solve.ids`). Each solve starts by mapping the last network's route nodes to the new one's by
  line id and stop station (`nodeMap`; a stop added has none, one removed is dropped) and remapping
  `marketOn`, `capRatio`, `sharedMkt` and `sharedTrail` through it (`remapKept`). City views need
  no mapping: a city whose view is unchanged reads its state through the new view.
- **Touched.** In a solve without the barrier, a dirty cluster is solved locally first (`editOf`)
  when its market part (if any) holds every one of this worker's periods (or, a shared period
  (T-1607), the market's loads the other worker sent, `sharedMkt`). Touched: a city population
  with no `cityOn` or whose view differs (`sameView`: complex numbers compared as groupings, run
  times within 1%, trains an hour and `span` within 5%, since they move by float noise and by
  knock-on changes on far lines when a line is built).
- **The trail.** Every population keeps, per period and round, its loads as the next round was
  priced on (`trail`, kept in `step`; the last round's before the cap); the cities' side of a shared
  period keeps the market's per round as the other worker sent them (`sharedTrail`).
- **The local solve.** T-1609's `localSolve` over the touched set: they are set on the new
  network and solved through their rounds, round r crowding on the rest's loads as round r of their
  last solve was priced on (the trail; the market part read through its old route nodes) plus their
  own, so with nothing changed it repeats the whole solve. Without a full trail it falls back to the
  rest's final loads times `capRatio`. The result is
  sent per period as a cluster result with `local: true` and `cities` the touched zones; the page
  splices it as any cluster result (lines, stations, the touched zones' views; the market's own
  figures stay) and clears the constructing label (`linesSettled`) for the cluster's lines.
- **Then the whole solve.** Each worker waits for the page's `whole` before solving the cluster
  whole as before: the page sends it 8 s (`EDIT_SETTLE_MS`) after the local result is shown, or at
  once when a worker's `plan` says it solves nothing locally. Until then a new edit is solved
  locally again (the whole solve would set every population on the new network, after which nothing
  can be solved locally until it ends). Since T-1734 an edit while the whole solve runs waits for it
  (notes/T-1734.md).

- **Markets set apart (T-1607).** With 3 workers a load solves the evening's market on another
  worker than its cities, and the next solve without the barrier used to re-solve every such
  cluster whole (all of Japan: 15 minutes). Now the cities' side keeps the market's last loads of
  that period (`sharedMkt`, from the load's last round), a local solve crowds on them, and a solve
  that only refines (levels) keeps the split: the two sides send that period's results as parts,
  as the load did (`cq`, `mq`). An edit's whole solve joins the period back as before.
- **Levels after a long load** (a fix to T-1223): the load's own time no longer counts as a zone
  left alone. All of Japan's 17-minute load made 44 of 45 zones coarse the moment it ended, a
  re-solve of everything that the first edit then cancelled. A level change of most of a cluster
  is now a local refine too (no half-the-cluster limit), solving the cities against the frozen
  market.

## The lies (until the whole solve lands)

- The market (longer trips and commutes between zones) keeps its old riders and routes: a new line
  carries only the touched cities' riders (on China's new metro lines 1.5-2% under the whole solve).
- The untouched cities do not answer the touched zones' crowding (T-1609's lie).
- The network's totals (the top bar's network figures) and the bubbles' sums wait for the whole
  solve, as after any edit; the touched zones' own figures and their lines' and stations' riders
  are the local result's.
- A shared period's market loads count as longer trips in the purpose split of the local result.

## The line error, found and fixed (second agent, 2026-10-28 evening)

**Cause.** The local solve crowded the touched populations, every round, on the others' *final*
loads, while the whole solve prices round r on everyone's loads after round r-1. Three crowding
rounds are far from settled (Tokyo's loads still move 12-15% in round 3), so the averaged result
depends on the path: starting from free flow against a converged rest is another path. Where the
rest is small (the Tokyo-only save: 2% of Tokyo's loads) the paths agree; on all of Japan the
frozen market is half of Tokyo's loads, and lines swapped riders by 6-16%. Ruled out by
measurement first: T-434's early stop (no touched population settled early, every round moved
over 1%), T-1607's split periods (2 and 3 workers gave identical figures), the service-changed line
(not 316), the route-node mapping (387 lines equal to the bit far from the edit).

**Fix.** Every population keeps its loads per period and round as each round was priced on
(`trail`, about 10 MB a worker on all of Japan, 5 MB on all of China); the cities' side of a
shared period keeps the market's per round (`sharedTrail`). A local solve crowds round r on the
rest's round r-1, so with nothing changed it repeats the whole solve. Without a full trail it falls
back to the final loads times `capRatio`.

**Speed.** Trains an hour (and `span`) now count as unchanged within 5% (run times still 1%): the
Tokyo line's knock-on 2.7% on an Osaka line made Osaka and Shiga a third of the local solve.
