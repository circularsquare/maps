# T-510: merging lone sparse cells across parents

2026-10-16. Rule: docs/demand.md 4.1. Code: `pipeline/merge_cells.py` (`_join_lone`, `REACH`);
`read_pack.py`'s bound on a merged parent's centre went from 500 to 900 m. Backups of every pack,
market file and the registry before the re-merge: `data/work/pre-T510/`.

## The rule

After T-044 and T-466, a cell under 1,250 trip ends that neither merged (alone among its parent's
sparse children, or alone in a sparse parent's zone, or in a group left unmerged on water) is lone.
It joins the touching merged cell of its zone with the fewest trip ends: for a joiner of weight w
at distance d from a group of weight W, the trip-end-weighted displacement added is 2dwW/(W+w),
least for the lightest group. One pass: a cell joins a group as it was, never another joiner, so a
group grows by at most one ring. Lone cells that touch only other lone cells merge among themselves
under the busiest one's index. No trip-end cap; a grown group whose farthest member would be over
700 m from its centre (about a merged parent's own reach: T-044 groups go to 695-713 m) sheds its
farthest joiner until it fits, and a new group over 700 m stays apart. As before, a group whose
centre would leave its zone square or stand on water stays as it was.

Group sizes in Tokyo (merged units; p50 / p90 / p99 / max):

| | cells | trip ends | reach m |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-466 | 4 / 7 / 7 / 7 | 520 / 2,132 / 3,843 / 7,425 | 380 / 525 / 616 / 695 |
| T-510 uncapped | 4 / 7 / 9 / 13 | 568 / 2,293 / 4,203 / 7,425 | 407 / 600 / 813 / 1,280 |
| T-510 (700 m) | 4 / 7 / 8 / 11 | 560 / 2,288 / 4,199 / 7,425 | 406 / 579 / 677 / 700 |

A merged parent's centre can now sit up to about two res-9 cells (713 m in New York) from its
index's centre, since a joiner from outside pulls it; bubbles group by index and sit at their
cells' weighted centre, so nothing draws at the index.

The rule barely moves riders: rail within 0.1%, 0.0-1.3% of busy stations past 5% (T-466's own step
was 0.4-2.9%), the worst station 7-13% on stations of about 1,100-2,600 boardings. The cap changes
next to nothing in the measures; it is kept for the cells' size, not for these numbers. Cells fall
another 10-17% after T-466; memory 2-5 MB.

In Tokyo, of 10,046 lone cells (at 1,250; T-466's 2,555 were counted at 357), 6,332 join a merged
neighbour and 961 make 419 groups of their own; 849 are left because a join would spread too wide,
244 because the centre would stand on water, and the remaining 1,660 touch no sparse cell of their
zone.
