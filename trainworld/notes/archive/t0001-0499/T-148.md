# T-148 search

Anita (2026-10-12): "a search bar for finding a place (a station we've placed, or a line name, or a
city, or maybe a neighborhood / address)", low priority.

## What was built

- `app/src/game/search.ts`: matching and ranking. `app/src/ui/Search.tsx`: the box, keys, and
  `goTo` (camera and selection). Mounted from `main.ts` (`startSearch`), styles at the end of
  `ui/style.css`.
- Searches the save's stations, its lines (name, or the badge's symbol exactly) and the registry's
  cities. Names are compared lower case, without accents or punctuation; a match is ranked exact,
  then starts with, then a word starts with, then contains, then contains ignoring spaces. Equal
  matches go cities, lines, stations (cities are few and the biggest places, so "new" lists New
  York and New Haven first), then by size (station: lines then boardings; line: riders a day),
  then name. Eight results at most; "Nothing found" when none.
- Stations in one complex (T-146) that share a name are one result. Picking it selects the member
  with the most lines and turns on complex view, the same setting as the inspector's Station /
  Complex switch, so the inspector shows the complex. A same-named station outside the complex is
  its own result; when two same-named results are in different cities the row adds the city.
  Since T-183 same-named results in one city get their own lines, or a compass direction
  (docs/interface.md, Search).
  Since T-182 (Anita, 2026-10-12) the pick no longer turns complex view on: it selects the member
  with the most lines and leaves the Station / Complex setting as the player had it.
- Picking moves the camera at once (`jumpTo`, `fitBounds` with no duration): a station to zoom 14
  (or closer if the map already is), a line to its stops' bounds (padding 60, at most zoom 15), a
  city to its registry centre and zoom, the same view as the City pane's City centre button. The
  viewed city is set to the one picked (a station selection would otherwise keep focus where it
  was, main.ts's moveend). A city pick clears the selection.
- With a build tool on (track, station, delete, editing a line's stops) a pick only moves the
  camera; the tool, a half-drawn route and the selection stay, so the player can carry on there.
- Keys: `/` or Ctrl+K (Cmd+K) from anywhere but a text field or the confirmation dialog; up and
  down move through the results, Enter picks, Escape clears and leaves the box. The box shows a
  muted `/` while empty and unfocused. No clash: the game's keys are 1-3, Esc, Space, Ctrl+Z/Y and
  the line tool's Enter/Backspace.

Browser check (real NYC save, `scratchpad/t148-browser.mjs`, port 5817): "Whiteh" lists the
two-platform Whitehall St-South Ferry/South Ferry first, the lone same-named platform second and
WHITE HOUSE third; clicking the first selects station 800 with complex view on at zoom 14;
Ctrl+K "subway 2" + Enter selects the line and fits it; "bost" + Enter jumps to Boston and makes
it the viewed city; "zzqx" says Nothing found; with the station tool on, a pick moves the camera
and keeps the tool. No page errors. Screenshots `../../artifacts/trainworld/T-148-*.png` (idle,
results, station, line, city, nothing, narrow-1000, narrow-900).

## Neighbourhoods and addresses: options (follow-up T-177)

**Built (T-177, 2026-10-12): option 2.** `pipeline/places.py` → `data/packs/<id>.places.json`;
counts nyc 3,794, phl 3,149, bos 1,384, wdc 4,473, bal 3,891, pvd 652, nhv 233, hfd 434, spf 387
(18,397, about 730 KB). Overpass gave a 504 on the first try for bos, wdc, pvd and spf and
answered on a retry. Browser check (own Vite, real NYC save): no place list fetched before the
box is focused, all nine on focus; "williamsburg", "south philly", "back bay", "georgetown" each
list the expected place first; a station placed at zoom 11.5 in Queens was named "Elmhurst".
Screenshots `../../artifacts/trainworld/T-177-*.png`. The options below are the research.

Probed on the real NYC save: OpenFreeMap's Positron tiles (OpenMapTiles schema, source
`openmaptiles`, layer `place`) carry `suburb` at zoom 11 (the five boroughs, NJ townships),
`quarter` and `neighbourhood` only in zoom-14 tiles (at 14.5 over Midtown: 43 neighbourhoods,
Kips Bay, Sunnyside, Greenpoint, SoHo, Dumbo, ...; 3 quarters, Williamsburg, Long Island City,
Midtown). `housenumber` has numbers with no street, so addresses are not in the tiles at all.

1. **Loaded tiles only** (`map.querySourceFeatures("openmaptiles", {sourceLayer: "place"})`). No
   new requests, a few lines of code. But it finds only what is in the tiles already loaded,
   which at a regional zoom means suburbs and towns, and neighbourhoods only around where the
   player has been at zoom 14. A search that finds a place only if you are already near it is
   confusing. Possible as an extra "on the map here" group, not as the neighbourhood search.
2. **A place list built by the pipeline** (recommended). OSM `place=suburb|quarter|neighbourhood`
   (plus village/hamlet, which is how much of Long Island and NJ is tagged) inside each pack's
   footprint, written into the pack or a small `places.json` beside it (name, kind, lng/lat;
   New York is a few thousand rows, tens of KB). Global data, same for every city, no runtime
   requests, works offline, searched by the same code as stations. Source options: an Overpass
   query per pack at build time (one request per city per build, User-Agent set), or a Geofabrik
   state extract read with pyosmium. Neighbourhoods only, no addresses.
3. **A geocoder at runtime**, the only route to addresses. Each sends what the player types to
   a third party, so it needs Anita's say-so:
   - Photon (photon.komoot.io): OSM-based, built for search-as-you-type, no key, fair use, no
     guarantees; can be biased to the viewed city with `lat`/`lon`.
   - Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): its usage policy forbids autocomplete and allows 1
     request a second; it would have to be search on Enter only.
   - Hosted services with keys and free tiers (MapTiler, Geoapify, Stadia, LocationIQ): an
     account and a key in the client.
   - Self-hosting Photon or Pelias: a server to run, far more than the game needs.
   Addresses add little for a rail game (a neighbourhood or a station is what the player looks
   for), so the suggestion is option 2 and no geocoder unless she wants addresses.
