# T-455: are sparse outskirts cells merged? (Chichibu, 2026-10-16)

**Answer: Anita is right.** The small fringe bubbles around Chichibu that sit at the same spacing
as the town are unmerged res-9 cells. Nothing is broken: the merge ran on `tyo` and the pack is
unchanged since, and the bubbles draw merged cells correctly. The cells stay at res 9 because the
T-044 rule merges a res-8 parent only when the **parent's** seven children together make under
1,000 trip ends, and a sparse cell on the valley edge usually shares its parent with one or two
town cells that push the parent over.

`data/packs/tyo.json` and `.bin` were written 2026-10-15 19:13 by `merge_cells.py` and their
sha256 still matches `data/work/res9/tyo.merged.json`, so no later build (Ibaraki, T-440's market
rebuild, Japan stage 3 so far) rewrote it. Header: 141,609 -> 103,987 cells, threshold 1,000.

themselves and 242 have under 100 residents: these are the bubbles she saw.

(T-453 stage 2), 85% at 5,000.

## The browser

`bubbleLevel` (`map/demandOverlay.ts`) groups bubbles by res-8 parent at zoom 11.8-13.6 and shows
single cells only from 13.6. So at zoom 12-13 every bubble is one res-8 parent whether merged or
not, and merged and unmerged areas look the same by design. From 13.6 a merged parent stays one
bubble while unmerged parents split into seven, including their sparse fringe children.
The worker's res-8 grouping clears the resolution field, so a merged cell groups with nothing
else and draws one bubble at its trip-end-weighted centre: correct.

## What would change it

Nothing to fix in `merge_cells.py` or the app. T-453 stage 2's 2,500 threshold merges about a
third more of these cells. The rest are sparse children of busy parents, which a res-8 merge can
never take; merging only a parent's sparse children into one cell (leaving its busy children at
res 9) would, but that is a rule change, not a bug, and is not filed. Scripts used were one-off
and are not kept.
