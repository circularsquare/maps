# T-458: the Sao Paulo metropolitan region from tier 0, census tracts and the OD 2017 survey

2026-10-16. Sao Paulo (`sao`) built by `pipeline/tier0.py` with Brazil inputs from
`pipeline/brazil.py`: IBGE Census 2022 people by census tract (the `population` hook), jobs from
the Metro's Pesquisa Origem-Destino 2017 (workers by zone of their first job, the `jobs` hook),
work from home and both curve parameters from the same survey's microdata. Registered,
`market: -1`, `utcOffset` -3. Place names are OSM's Portuguese `name`.

Scripts: `pipeline/brazil.py` (units, outline, population and jobs hooks, survey tables),
`notes/t458_survey.py` (the survey's home-based trips by purpose), `notes/t458_everyday.py` (the
game's everyday rule redone in numpy on the pack, against the survey), `checks/city-browser.mjs sao`.
Logs and images in `../artifacts/trainworld/T-458-*`.

## Footprint

The 39 municipalities of the Regiao Metropolitana de Sao Paulo, whole (the survey's own area):
20,731,920 people in 2022, about 7,935 km2, one polygon, no holes, box 47.21-45.70 W,
24.06-23.18 S. Inland: the sea stays outside the outline (the Serra do Mar). No other pack nearby.

## Decisions (DECISIONS.md 2026-10-16)

- **Jobs from the survey, not the proxy.** Workers by zone of their first job (`CD_ATIVI` 1-2,
  `ZONATRA1` 1-517, person factor `FE_PESS`): 9,137,342 in 2017, times 0.9957 (census 2022 people
  over the survey's 20,821,671) = 9.10M jobs, 0.439 a resident. Second jobs left out.
- **Work from home 9.1%**: workers whose first job is at home (`TRAB1_RE` 1). Workers with no fixed
  workplace stay in their reported job zone.
- **Curve on home-to-job straight-line distances** of workers who leave home (survey coordinates,
  so no road factor; mean 8.49 km, median 5.79). Fitted on all nine bands it went to
  d^-2.3 exp(-d/145 km) (KL 0.069): the survey's 27% under 2 km against 13% at 2-4 km cannot be
  matched by 2 km zones, whose neighbours all sit at 2 or 2.8 km. With 0-4 km as one band:
  **d^-1.8 exp(-d/19.0 km)**, KL 0.008, model mean 8.5 km (survey 8.5), median 4.5 (survey 5.8).
- **GRU O&D 0.75** by judgement (a hub); Congonhas from Wikidata (9.04M boardings, 2022).
- Test line: Metro Line 1, Tucuruvi to Jabaquara, 23 stations through Se.

## The survey against the game (T-451's question)

`notes/t458_survey.py` (survey) and `notes/t458_everyday.py` (the game's rule in numpy on the
res-9 pack; its means agree with the Rust test). Survey: weekday home-based trips (from home to
the purpose), straight-line between the exact points. Game: one outing per resident-purpose,
2 km zone grid distances (own zone 1.04 km, so the game's 0-2 km band is only same-zone trips).
Centre: the Se and Pinheiros subprefectures (12 districts: the old centre, Paulista, Faria Lima;
0.71M residents); rest of Sao Paulo 10.71M; the other 38 municipalities 9.31M.

| Purpose | Mean km game / survey | 0-2 / 2-5 / 5-10 / 10-15 / 15+ km, game | survey | Centre stays, game / survey | Rest of SP to centre | Other municipalities to centre |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Errands (shopping, personal business) | 6.5 / 4.8 | 5 / 43 / 30 / 17 / 6 | 46 / 25 / 15 / 6 / 8 | 52% / 73% | 12.1% / 10.9% | 2.7% / 3.2% |
| Social (leisure, visits, meals) | 10.8 / 4.9 | 2 / 23 / 26 / 26 / 24 | 44 / 27 / 14 / 8 / 8 | 43% / 65% | **17.7% / 9.0%** | **8.6% / 2.2%** |
| Medical | 9.4 / 7.7 | 3 / 28 / 28 / 24 / 17 | 25 / 27 / 21 / 12 / 15 | 46% / 62% | 16.0% / 20.2% | 4.9% / 8.7% |
| Work (commute) | 8.5 / 8.5 (workers) | fitted | | | | |

- **Errands**: the centre share is right (12% against 11% from the rest of the city), the trips
  about 35% too long, mostly because the survey's many walking trips under 2 km (46%, a quarter of
  errands on foot) sit in the game's own-zone and neighbour-zone bands.
- **Social outings run twice the survey's length** (10.8 against 4.9 km) and send twice the
  survey's share to the centre from the rest of the city, four times from the other
  municipalities. Tokyo (T-451) showed the same direction. The 6 km social scale is New York
  judgement (T-138); the survey points to a much shorter one (not fitted here).
- **Medical** is close (9.4 against 7.7 km) and if anything sends too few to the centre's hospitals.
- **Rates**: the survey's weekday home-based rates are far below New York's: errands 0.090 a
  resident (game 0.300), social 0.037 (0.200), medical 0.043 (0.040), school 0.336 (game 0.156:
  the survey counts every student trip, adults included). Non-home-based trips (work to lunch,
  shop to shop) are left out of these rates; with them shopping and meals rise, though not by
  three times. Evidence for T-450's per-pack rates when calibration starts.
- **School trips**: game 4.85 km against the survey's 2.3 (49% on foot): OSM's 3,449 schools for
  3.6M pupils hold 1,050 each, so school trips gather and run long (Mumbai's pattern, milder).

Not worth its cost: two Overpass attempts for Line 1's stations (504, 500) before reading them
from the extract; a 10k-character IBGE directory page read through curl's stdout; the first and
second builds (World Bank timeout; the degenerate curve, which a fit on the survey bands before
the build would have shown). The context per call stayed near 200k weighted reads, so each step
cost about 27k: lean outputs matter more than step count.

Proposals:
1. **tier0.worldbank retries** (or a longer timeout): a 60 s read timeout failed a build after 3
   minutes of work.
2. **fit_curve_bands merges bands under the zone grain itself** (first edge below 2 x ZONE_M
   joins the next), so no country module has to: Mumbai's 0-1 and 2-5 km had the same problem.
3. **A station-list helper** (`places.py --stations <id> <name regex>`, from the feature cache)
   for test lines: the points cache drops `railway=station` nodes, and Overpass is unreliable.
4. **Household travel surveys first**: for any metro, look for an OD survey's microdata before
   census tables; one file gave jobs, work from home, the curve and the everyday check.
5. **Brief**: right and complete; naming the survey and the comparison saved the search.
