# T-189, T-190, T-191: station and train shapes, no interlining, thinner lines

Done 2026-10-12. Design as built: docs/interface.md ("Line widths", "Stations on the map",
"Trains", "Lines sharing track are drawn over each other"); decisions in DECISIONS 2026-10-12.
Since changed by T-204/T-205 (same day): boxes from zoom 15 with a width fixed on the ground
(`StationStyle.boxLinePx/boxRingPx`), nothing but lines below zoom 11 (`STATIONS_ZOOM`), thinner
far-out lines. The zoom and width figures below are T-189's.

## Where things are

- `map/stationGeometry.ts`: `platformShape` (where a platform lies on its track: middle, half axis,
  half the turn), `waysFrom` (a point part way along an edge, for the ghost), `stationShape` (the
  CPU twin of the shader: dot or band on screen), `stationCoords` / `stationDistance` /
  `insideStation` (picking), `stationPath` (the ghost's SVG), `SHAPE_GLSL` (the arc and box
  distance shared by the station shader, the train shader and the demand cut-out),
  `STATION_BOX_ZOOM` = 13, `PLATFORM_HALF_M` = 6, `SEL_OUTLINE_PX` = 2, `SEL_GLOW_PX` = 6.
- `map/network.ts`: per station `stationAxis` (half the platform at its middle) and the new
  `stationShape` (middle from the node, half turn, true half width), built in the clock worker.
  The platform follows the edge with the most lines (ties: lowest id) and carries on along the
  way most nearly opposite it; one edge only = dead end, platform from the node along it.
  T-062's `layoutBundles`, `offset` and `sampleOff` are gone; `Strokes.info` keeps the stretch,
  its slot fields are now 0, 1, 1.
- `map/renderer.ts`: `STATION_VS/FS` lay the quad out in the platform's frame at its middle and
  measure the distance from the arc band; `u_pass` draws the selected ones after the others;
  `stationShapeOf`, `stationAt` (selected first), `selectedShapes`, `selectedStationAt`,
  `onSelectionDrawn`, `stationStyle`. `TRAIN_VS/FS`: a pill's curvature from the circle through
  its back, middle and front, clamped to a half angle of 1 rad; cars stay straight. Line widths
  x0.6.
- `map/demandOverlay.ts`: `cut()` clears the disc canvas over the selected stations
  (`blendFunc(ZERO, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA)`), fully to the outline and fading over the glow;
  `map/demandViews.ts` wires it and gives clicks over a selected station to the network;
  `map/demandHover.ts` `hoverAbove` keeps bubble tags off it.
- `map/demandLayers.ts`: no offsets; a station's width is the widest line through it.
- `map/tools.ts`: the placement ghost is an SVG path of the platform as it will be drawn
  (`.station-ghost` in style.css).

## Frame cost

The T-180 method's frame gaps and the renderer's own timers, clock at speed 2 over Midtown, 6 s a
run, 1400x900, other agents busy:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| draw, CPU ms a frame (z13, z15, z13, z15, z16.5) | 0.39, 0.31, 0.32, 0.38, 0.35 | 0.42, 0.35, 0.47, 0.44, 0.47 |
| draw, GPU ms (median) | 0.73, 0.62, 0.71, 0.63, 0.61 | 0.80, 0.81, 0.76, 0.78, 0.88 |
| frame gap, average ms | 16.7 all | 16.7-16.8 |

About 0.1 ms more CPU and 0.1-0.25 ms more GPU a frame (the arc distance per pixel, the extra
selected pass), well inside a 16.7 ms frame; no visible change in frame rate.

## Limits

- Trains keep their former width, so at zoom 13-15 a pill is about twice as wide as a plain line.
- Lines on shared track hide each other; only the top one is clickable there.
- A platform is one circular arc: a platform half on a straight and half on a curve is drawn as
  the arc through its middle and both ends, off the track by a metre or two.
