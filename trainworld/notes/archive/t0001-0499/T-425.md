# T-425: fast enrichment on long coastlines

2026-10-15. Greater Tokyo's outline has 204,365 vertices (41 parts, one of 188k), so an unprepared
`covers()` costs about 60 ms and an overlay with the whole outline about 26 ms.

## Found and changed (pipeline/ only)

| Step | Test against the outline | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `enrich_medical.facilities` | `boundary.covers(pt)` for every facility (20k+ on Tokyo) | `shapely.prepare(boundary)` first |
| `enrich_parks.main` | `intersection(ring, whole outline)` for each park, 26 ms | each ring is intersected with the outline clipped to its box plus 1 km (`clip_by_rect`, about 4 ms) |
| `enrich_parks.fetch_tiles` | `boundary.intersects(tile)` | prepared (a few dozen calls) |
| `enrich_airports.airports_in` | `covers` for every scheduled airport worldwide | prepared (0.5 s, mostly the CSV) |
| `enrich_universities.institutions`, `education_sources.public_schools`, `canada.schools`, `canada.universities` | `covers` per IPEDS, NCES or Ontario row (thousands) | prepared; not run here, no data cached, a prepared geometry gives the same answer by construction |
| `places.build` | `contains_xy(foot, ...)` per place on the simplified outline | prepared |

Already fine: `tier0.inside`, `education_osm`, `airports` and `signals` use prepared `contains_xy`
or `contains` on arrays; `osm_features` and the STRtree merges in medical and parks are indexed.

The 1 km margin matters: a clip exactly at the ring's box left the cut edges against the ring and
97 of London's 44,968 audit floats differed in the 16th digit; with the margin all bytes are equal.

## Times (each step alone, on a copy of the pack in a scratch folder, 3 to 4 cores busy)

| Pack, step | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| tyo medical | 1,028 s | 11.7 s |
| tyo parks | 742 s (boundary already prepared by T-420; unprepared it was 35 minutes) | 119 s |
| tyo airports_in | 0.7 s | 0.5 s |
| lon medical | 125 s | 6.1 s |
| lon parks | 25 s | 15.5 s |
| lon airports_in | 0.5 s | 0.4 s |

Tokyo's enrichment is now about 2 minutes instead of 29 (or 50 unprepared). What is left in parks is
the rectangle clip, 4 ms for each of 24.8k parks; a pre-tiled outline would cut it further.
