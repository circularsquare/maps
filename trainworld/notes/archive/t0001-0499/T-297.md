# T-297: a faster joint commute gravity

2026-10-15. Rule: docs/zones.md section 4 ("The balance"). Code: `sim/src/bin/joint_gravity.rs`
(`balance`, `least_squares`, `Grid`; test `anderson_matches_long_furness`).

## What changed

- **Anderson mixing** of the last 12 Furness steps (on log col; rows follow exactly), with a restart
  when the residual doubles. Plain Furness crawls along a few slow modes (the level of one pack or
  region against its weakly joined neighbours); mixing removes them.
- **Tolerance 1e-8** on every column (rows exact), measured by the solve and re-measured by `--check`.
- **Groups**: packs joined by any pair within the 150 km cut are balanced together, others apart
  (the Northeast is one group; a Japan would be another).
- **Sums**: threads take blocks of 16 zones as they free up (New York's zones cost far more than a
  small pack's), and each pack's zones sit in its own grid of 10 km buckets, so a zone scans only
  other packs' buckets inside the cut, not a hash map of every bucket in a 300 km square. The pair
  count is unchanged (353,642,070 across packs on the Japan-sized case, both ways).
- Measured and not kept: over-relaxation (omega 1.8, no mixing) was still at 4.8e-4 after 150 steps;
  warm start from the packs' factors (not needed at these times, and would make results depend on
  history); a looser tolerance for small zones (every zone reaches 1e-8 anyway).

So today's factors are up to 3.5% from the solution although its residual is 1.6e-4: a residual
fixes a pack's level against its neighbours only to about the residual over the share of its
commutes that cross (a few percent for New York), so the slow modes keep percent-sized errors.
Fitting each pack's own scale removes most of it in Boston and Washington (under 1e-3 left) but not
in Philadelphia (2.4e-2), whose regions balance against each other weakly. The differences are all
today's unconverged slow modes: the new solve at 1e-5 and at 1e-8 and the reference agree to 7e-5
and 2e-7. Totals barely move (commuters within their pack 17,903,116 today, 17,903,125 after;
across 1,505,680 and 1,505,671); pack pairs move up to 2% (Philadelphia - Washington 1,542 to 1,568,
Baltimore - Philadelphia 17,425 to 17,128). Market pairs kept: 172,099 to 172,055.

The brief asked for factors within 2e-4 of today's; that cannot hold, since today's are not within
2e-4 of the solution. Rerunning `pipeline/intercity.py` with the new bin moves each pack's factors
by up to 3.5% and the commute figures by up to 2% a pack pair, toward the converged answer.
