# T-394: pack reach and the ride (2026-10-15)

Question: `workers/cityReach.ts` opens a zone's pack for a station within 4.5 km of its outline. Since
T-371/T-389 homes ride up to 15 km (`ride_max_m`) to a station, so does a station 5-15 km outside an
unopened zone lose that zone's riders?

## Result: no. The reach stays 4.5 km.

A pack's trips run between two stations of its own pack: the home end may ride, but the work or
activity end walks (`walk_cutoff_m` 3 km). Riders therefore need a station within a walk of the
pack's job and activity cells, which lie inside the outline (cells at most ~0.5 km outside), and
that station already opens the pack. Commutes between zones are the regional market's (6 km market
zones, no pack needed, its rides already start 17 km out), so a pack adds nothing for a station that
is only a ride away.

## Experiment (notes/T-394-reach-ab.mjs)

New game viewed in Boston; a test line of three tunnelled stations from 6.2 km east of Worcester's
outline (past the old reach) to Back Bay, 10 trains an hour, constructed; final solve (round 3).
Run once with `REACH_M` 15,500 and once with 4,500 (everything else identical):

| | solve covers | station boardings a day | home commuters | ridden to it | Worcester rail trips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| reach 4,500 | Boston | 6,319 | 3,043 | 2,980 | (not open) |
| reach 15,500 | Boston, Worcester | 6,319 | 3,043 | 2,980 | 0 |

Identical, and Worcester, open in every worker and not bare, carried no rail trips. An earlier run
with the station 9.9 km out gave the same (5,895 / 3,312 / 3,308 both ways). Real save smoke with
reach 15,500: 7.60 M rail trips a day, as with 4,500 (7,597,261 before); browser demand final 42-43 s
both ways (the extra packs of that save were already open). So widening costs a pack download and
open (seconds in the Chrome workers for a big pack) for no change in any number.

## Changed

Only comments and docs: `cityReach.ts` header said "the walk's 4 km bound" (the walk is 3 km since
T-371) and now says why the ride does not extend the reach; `docs/demand.md` "Which cities are
open" and `docs/architecture.md` state the same. REACH_M stays 4,500 (3 km walk plus margin), so
the T-325 bare-zone rule is untouched.

A rider test for the opposite case (a zone whose homes ride to a station that serves a job end near
it) needs no new check: such a station is inside the 4.5 km already.
