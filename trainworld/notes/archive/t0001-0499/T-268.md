# T-268: working tables for the intercity review

The review itself is `research/intercity-review.md`. This file keeps the numbers behind it and how
they were made, so the next agent can redo them. 2026-10-13.

## How the model numbers were made

All from `data/packs/intercity.json` as built today (229,885 sites, 2,913 zones, formula
"T-128 v2"). Scripts were scratch (not kept in the tree); what they did:

- **Corridor totals**: decoded `derived.corridor_trips` (f64 bit patterns) from the pack. Daily
  total 690,583 trips (both directions), 0.015 a resident on 46.6M people.
- **Distance bands and core-to-core**: recomputed every zone pair in Python with the same formula
  as `intercity.rs` (`decay_km`, `pair_trips`, `MIN_TRIPS`), great circles through the pack's
  frame. The recomputed total matches the derived block. "Core" = both zones' centres within
  20 km of the place's main centre (Midtown, Philadelphia City Hall, Trenton, Boston, the White
  House, Baltimore, Providence, New Haven, Hartford, Springfield).
- **Rail share by hand**: `intercity.rs`'s logit for one centre-to-centre trip, assuming 1 km of
  fast walk at each end (weight 2), the kernel's boarding cost (`board_cost`: half headway, 5 min
  at 1.37 then 0.4, plus the 5-minute boarding penalty), today's best regular train time, the
  start fares ($1.50 + $0.10/km on 1.15 x the great circle, 3 perceived minutes a dollar), drive
  15 min + 1.25 d at 85 km/h, beta 0.025. Access weights taken as 1 (best case).
- **Cross-pack commuting**: LODES 2023 OD (JT00, main + aux, all twelve states in `data/raw/`),
  S000 jobs summed by home pack and work pack using `build_city.CITIES` county lists, Mercer
  (34021) as its own place. Jobs, not daily trips: LODES places remote and multi-site workers at
  the reporting office and overstates long links; ACS county-to-county flows would be the check.
