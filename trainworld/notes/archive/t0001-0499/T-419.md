# T-419: a worker's set-up state

2026-10-16. `intercity.rs` (the market's pairs), `everyday.rs` (`Pair`). Results identical to the
bit.

## Measured (counting allocator, native, New York real save, one worker, all three layers)

T-439's scratch driver on copies of the packs. The figures in the task line (`edge_pair` 28 MB,
pairs 20 MB) predate T-397: New York's everyday layer now holds 1.65M samples.

| item | MB |
|---|---:|
| everyday `edge_pair` (1 u32 a sample) | 6.3 |
| everyday pairs (472,493 x 24 bytes) | 12.0 |
| everyday set-up kept, all | 20.1 |
| market pairs (379,120 x 40 bytes) | 14.5 |
| market `pair_index` (HashMap, 458,752 slots) | about 6 |
| market access lists, cpairs, served | 1.6 |
| market set-up kept, all | 22.2 |

`edge_pair` stays: selections and cell modes read it after set-up, and rebuilding it on demand
would mean the pair index again (about 150 ms). The everyday pair list's spare room is kept on
purpose (T-398).

## What changed

- Market: `pair_index` is gone. The pairs come two by two, (lower zone, higher zone) and then
  reversed, in the order of (lower, higher), so `pair_at` finds one by binary search over the
  even entries (only the selection reads it). `pair_at_finds_every_pair` checks it against a
  linear search.
- Market `Pair`: zones and corridor as u32, 32 bytes not 40.
- Everyday `Pair`: the purpose in the top 4 bits of the home subzone, 20 bytes not 24.
