# T-457 Seoul Capital Area (`seo`)

Tier 0 with South Korea's inputs from `pipeline/korea.py`. Low-fidelity first pass (Anita 2026-10-15).

## Footprint

Seoul (25 gu), Incheon (8 gu, Ganghwa-gun; Ongjin-gun left out: its islands reach 200 km west to
Baengnyeong off North Korea's coast, 20,497 people) and Gyeonggi (31 si and gun) whole. Districts at
the finest level the population table has: the non-autonomous gu of Suwon, Seongnam, Anyang, Ansan,
Goyang and Yongin replace their si. OSM relations from the Geofabrik `asia/south-korea` extract,
matched by Korean name inside their province, clipped to land by the OSM coastline.

## Sources

- Population: Ministry of the Interior and Safety, resident registration by si-gun-gu, November 2020
  (the census month), https://jumin.mois.go.kr/statMonth.do, CSV by POST to `downloadCsv.do` (open,
  no key; `korea.py fetch seo`). 26,015,029 registered (Seoul 9,679,771; Incheon less Ongjin
  2,921,810; Gyeonggi 13,413,459). Registered foreigners are not in it. KOSIS's 2020 census table by
  si-gun-gu needs an API key or the interactive site, so it was not used.
- Jobs: tier 0's proxy. The Census on Establishments by si-gun-gu is on KOSIS only.
- Curve: Statistics Korea with SK Telecom, mobile data linked to registers, June 2023 (press release
  2023-12-20, via Newsis https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20231220_0002565890): capital-area commuters'
  average commuting distance 20.4 km (national 18.4), average commute time 83 minutes both ways. The
  distance is read as one way along the route (at 41 minutes a way, 20 km is about 30 km/h), so
  15.7 km straight-line at a 1.3 road factor; `"curve": {"mean_km": 15.7}` at tier 0's power 1.4.
- Work from home: tier 0 default 0 (Korea's telework share is small; no source checked).
- Airports: tier 0 (Wikidata): Incheon 37.0M boardings (2025), O&D 0.85 by judgement; Gimpo 12.3M
  (Wikidata's 2022 figure).
- Line 2 reference: Seoul Metro, 1,964,128 riders a day on Line 2 in 2024 (Seoul Metro's 2024
  transport statistics via Financial News, https://www.fnnews.com/news/202502190600513949); all of
  lines 1-8 6,605,250 a day.

## Line 2 sanity check

The test line is Line 2 from City Hall east and south round to Sadang, 26 of the loop's 43
stations (the whole loop costs 8,848 against a new game's 6,000), alone in an empty network: 1.06M
riders a day (work 507k, shopping 277k, social 193k, school 56k, medical 21k). Seoul Metro reports
1.96M a day for the whole of Line 2 in 2024, with a full network feeding it. Half the published
figure on 60% of the stations, with no transfers in, is the right order; nothing calibrated.

## Retrospective

About 30 minutes of wall time, 2.47M weighted tokens (128 calls) before this write-up.

1. Reading the playbook and china.py as the template: 0.15M. Worth it; china.py was the right shape.
2. Population source: 0.25M, five web calls. KOSIS is closed without a key; the MOIS form was found
   by reading its page's HTML, then one POST per province.
3. Extract read (3 + 1 minutes) ran meanwhile.
4. korea.py and the districts: 0.5M, three failed runs. Incheon's July 2026 gu (in OSM, not in the
   2020 table) cost two runs of the 5-minute multipolygon read before the admin areas were cached.
5. Build 7 minutes (one wake after the wait, 0.19M), routes 5, merge, market, checks: 0.9M.
6. The browser check failed once: a 43-station line is more than a new game's money.

Proposals:
- Playbook (added): a test line costs about 200 a station, so at most about 29 stations.
- A shared helper for "OSM admin units by name inside a parent, cached" (china.py and korea.py
  both read the multipolygon layer; a cache of the extract's admin areas in `osm_features` would
  save the 5-minute read for every country module).
- `token_usage.py --timeline` prints one line per call: a `--summary` flag would keep a
  retrospective's own cost down.
