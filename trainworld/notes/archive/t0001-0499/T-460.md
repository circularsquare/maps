# T-460: the land-use cap as tier 0's default

2026-10-16. `LAND_CAP_KM2` 1.0 and `LAND_SPREAD_DEG` 0.003 in `pipeline/tier0.py` apply to every tier 0
build unless the config sets `land_cap_km2` or `land_spread_deg` (0 turns one off). The values are
T-459's, kept: the first pair measured passed, so no neighbours were tried. Stage 1 of the
unattended city pipeline (notes/T-535.md).

Scripts: `notes/t460_measure.py` (London and Toronto), `notes/t460_lagos_check.py` (Lagos).
Output: `../artifacts/trainworld/T-460-measure.json`.

## The measure

Half the L1 distance between two shares of jobs by 2 km zone ("misplaced", as notes/t360_jobs.py).
Both cities on the tier 0 proxy (`jobs_from`, blend 0.3/0.5/0.2) against their reference: London
BRES 2024 by LSOA, spread inside each LSOA by the proxy before the change (fixed, the same truth
before and after); Toronto the real pack's jobs (StatCan place of work). The "after" signals are the
cached ones with their OSM columns rebuilt from capped, spread land rows (new cells added); with
no cap the rebuilt columns equal the cache's (London exactly; Toronto's cached rows come from an
older Ontario extract, same land total to the unit). The land areas come from Geofabrik's England
and Ontario extracts of 2026-10-16, the Ontario cache remade for this and deleted after.

| | London before | London after | Toronto before | Toronto after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jobs misplaced, 2 km zones | 26.06% | 25.99% | 18.83% | 18.01% |
| London, BRES re-spread by the new proxy | 26.06% | 25.81% | | |
| Largest cell's share of jobs | 0.529% | 0.530% | 1.016% | 1.018% |
| Largest place's share of the OSM signal | 0.29% | 0.10% | 0.52% | 0.13% |
| Land units (5,000 m² each) | 51,850 | 49,252 | 84,557 | 80,232 |

Neither city gets worse (London 0.07 points better, Toronto 0.8 better; the bar was no worse than
0.5). Few areas reach the cap there: the largest is 3.0 km² in London's box and 4.0 km² in
Toronto's, 5% of the land units go. Lagos is the case it is for (23 km² refinery).

(Toronto's "before" differs from T-360's stored 18.05% on the same blend; the t287 signals or the
real pack have changed since. The comparison here is like for like.)

## Lagos

Rebuilt with no cap keys in its config (the two keys are gone from `build_city.py`), into a scratch
folder from a fresh Nigeria extract: the OSM rows (6,020, the four old columns), every array in
`lag.tier0_signals.npz` and `lag.cells.bin` are byte-identical to the current ones. The pack's
later steps read those cells, so the pack is the same. Nothing in data/packs or data/work changed.

## The build's line

`tier0.osm_signal_line`, printed once per build (format and fields in docs/adding-a-city.md):
`osm_signal: errand=.. leisure=.. job=.. land=.. land_share=.. top_place=.. top_place_jobs_share=..
top_cell=lat,lon top_cell_jobs_share=.. top_place_name=..`. New caches carry `ref` (OSM id) and
`name` columns on land rows, so a place is one area; an older cache gives single rows (`lat,lon`).
Lagos today: land 71%, largest place 0.42% of jobs, largest cell 0.29%.

A cache keeps the settings it was made with, so a pack with an old cache ignores a changed default
until its `osm_jobs_<id>.csv.gz` is deleted. Existing packs were not rebuilt.

## Cost

About 12 minutes of machine time at one core in all: Ontario's extract (1 GB, features 2 minutes), then
Nigeria's (711 MB) and the 6.5 minute Lagos scratch build. Both extracts, caches and the pickled
land areas were deleted afterwards.
