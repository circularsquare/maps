# T-287: the tier 0 adapter (stage 1)

2026-10-14. `pipeline/tier0.py`: a city pack from global sources only, as research/world-sampler.md
section 2 planned, checked by building Toronto from tier 0 into a scratch folder
(`data/work/t287/`, city id `t0tor`; never data/packs/) and setting it against the real pack
(StatCan census inputs, T-277) and the census itself. No new city, no registry change.

Scripts: `notes/t287_build.py` (the scratch build; `--finish POW KM` re-solves with another curve),
`notes/t287_compare.py [--blends]` (every table below), `notes/t287_yonge.py` (the test line as a
`realnet` network). Logs and the full comparison JSON: `../artifacts/trainworld/T-287-build.log`,
`T-287-compare.log`, `T-287-compare.json`, `T-287-yonge-*.log`. Build time with everything cached:
about 2 minutes (the first run adds about 5 minutes reading schools out of the OSM extract).

## Decisions made

- **Jobs proxy: GHSL non-residential volume weighted by height.** Volume alone put a third of the
  real jobs in the centre (46k within 1 km of King and Bay against 158k): by volume, suburban
  warehouses and plants outweigh office towers. Weighting each cell's volume by its mean
  non-residential height (volume over surface) to the power 1.5 fixes it (199k, 383k within 3 km
  against 354k). The power was picked on Toronto (in-sample); London (T-288) is the first
  out-of-sample check. The blend 50% height-weighted volume, 30% OSM job places, 20% population
  was set before looking; the best Toronto blend (40/30/30) is only 0.6 points better at 2 km zones.
- **Population from GHSL, not WorldPop**: one product family for population and buildings, tiles
  per 1,000 km, CC BY. WorldPop is the fallback if GHSL proves poor somewhere.
- **The curve fallback keeps power 1** and fits only the length (`tier0.fit_curve`). On Toronto it
  did badly (below); the better fallback is to borrow a fitted city's curve.
- **Schools by the square root of site area** (site area varies more than enrolment does: a
  secondary school's playing fields), points at the median site.
- **Pupils = population x share aged 0-14 x 0.9** (judgement; Toronto: 982k against Ontario's
  1,042k public-school pupils).

visible difference here.

## What this says for Shanghai and Mumbai

- **Population is the safe input**: within a few percent per municipality once scaled to district
  totals, worse only where building outran the 2020 epoch. For fast-growing cities use the 2025
  epoch (a config key) and the newest district totals.
- **Jobs land in the right municipalities (4% misplaced) and the right centre** with the height
  weighting, but at 2 km zones a fifth of the jobs are in the wrong place: industrial land
  overdraws (Brampton +40%), small suburban office parks underdraw. Fine for a sampler; trust the
  pattern, not a given neighbourhood. Shanghai's Pudong towers suit the height rule; Mumbai's
  informal work does not show in buildings, so raise the population share there.
- **The curve matters more than the inputs.** Tier 0 inputs cost about 4 points of misplaced
  commutes (6.9% to 11.3%); a wrong curve costs 5-10 points more. Fitting only the length to a
  published median (here 2016's 10.5 km, measured against 2021's lockdown flows) gave a curve far
  too flat (exp(-d/95 km)), worse than borrowing the US curve. Use a distance distribution where
  one exists (Mumbai's census bands) and fit both parameters; with a single figure, borrow the
  most similar fitted city's curve and check its mean against the figure.
- **Universities are the weakest input**: OSM campus tags rarely carry Wikidata ids (4 of 63
  sites here), so most are sized by area, and multi-campus institutions are guesswork.

## Left out (not easy, or not tier 0's)

- OSM water (T-041's): tier 0 takes a `water_source` from the config.
- Boundaries from geoBoundaries or NUTS: tier 0 reads a GeoJSON from the config.
- WorldPop age grids for children and adults: national shares instead.
- Building floor area for school sizes (would need every building inside each school site).
- UNESCO enrolment rates: 0.9 by judgement.
- A connecting share for hub airports: judgement per airport in the config.
- Institutions (prisons, care homes): none taken off.
- Regions and border factors: one region.
- Routed walks and drives on the scratch pack (`routes.py` works on any pack; not run here).
