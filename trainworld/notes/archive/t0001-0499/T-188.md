# T-188: riders left behind feed back into demand

Done 2026-10-13 (batch 21). T-164's phase 2. The design as built is docs/demand.md 4.4 step 7
("Riders left behind"); the choices are in DECISIONS 2026-10-13.

## What changed

- `sim/src/demand/kernel.rs`: `left_behind` (the boarding account's rule on one period's trains,
  per route node the average extra real minutes a boarder waits for full trains, capped at
  `LEFT_WAIT_CAP_MIN` = 60), `round_costs` (crowding factors and left-behind waits from the frozen
  loads), `Graph::rn_left` (added to the boarding at `w_wait` in `skims_extend`; part of the
  skims cache key), `Params::left_behind` (default on; `realnet --set left_behind=0` turns it off).
- `crowd_with_external` in `api.rs`, `everyday.rs` and `intercity.rs` takes the segment loads,
  then the boardings and alightings per route node (`3 x` route nodes); the segment loads alone
  still work and price crowding only (older tests and `t174-market-check.mjs`). `DemandApi::crowd`
  passes its own three.
- `app/src/workers/demand.worker.ts`: the round's frozen vector is every population's segment
  loads, boardings and alightings.
- `sim/src/demand/trip.rs`: the trip explorer's wait includes the line's extra wait at that
  boarding.
- `sim/src/track/boarding.rs`: `shape_g` and `shape_cum` are public (demand uses the account's
  shapes).

## Why the effect is small on New York

The crowding penalty already holds most lines below crush at the busiest hour, and riders arrive
fastest mid-period, so only lines near crush at the busiest hour leave anyone behind, mostly for
part of the peak. Where the account showed the most riders left behind (LIRR branches into
Manhattan, T-164) is where riders now move. A player's undersized line is where it bites.
