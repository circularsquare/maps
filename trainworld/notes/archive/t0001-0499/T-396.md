# T-396: the market's cluster join and opening (2026-10-15)

Rules now in docs/demand.md "Clusters" and "Which cities are open"; the choice in DECISIONS.md.

## The join

The MARKET node in `demandClusters.ts` joined every station within 200 km of any market zone's
centre. It now joins, only when the market is open, the stations within 30 km of a market zone's
registry box (`marketReaches`): the market's sites are those zones' cells, all inside the boxes,
and its rides reach about 17 km. The 200 km was an access reach, not the near-pair cutoff, so the
250 km cutoff plays no part here.

What it does not do, and why: decision 6 wants the market's pairs split by the track networks
their ends reach. The market is one `IntercityApi` population and a population is solved over
its whole cluster graph, so two networks with no track between them that both lie in its reach
must still share a cluster for exact figures. Splitting needs the kernel to solve each network's
served zones as its own pair set (or one instance per network, about 45 MB a worker each, with
the unserved pairs' totals added once), which is sim/ work: T-409, a requirement on T-377. Today
it matters only inside the Northeast, the only market; Toronto, London, Paris, Shanghai and Mumbai
are outside it. A pair with no station near one end is already fixed offline in the kernel
(T-174); a market no station reaches is a cluster with no lines, solved once and kept.

## Opening

The client (`demandClient.ts` `wantsMarket`) opens the market once a market zone is viewed,
beside the view or reached, or a station lies in its reach, and keeps it open. A solve sent
without it is sent again when it opens, and the demand cache keys a result by whether it covered
the market. The focus (`activeCity`) now starts at the zone in view rather than New York, which on
a Toronto reload had opened New York's pack and so the market.
