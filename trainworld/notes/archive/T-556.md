# T-556: towns grown up to a metro

2026-10-16. The rule is in docs/adding-a-city.md 1 ("Metros", "Order", "Over 40M people");
code `satellites()`, `keep_order()` and `split()` in `pipeline/world_plan.py`.

## What the Lagos gap was

Not growth since 2015. GHSL's UCDB R2024A has Ikorodu as its own urban centre (0.86M in 2025,
303 km2), 2.2 km from Lagos's, and GHS-FUA R2019A gives it its own FUA (0.87M in 2015), 1 km
from Lagos's. Ikorodu LGA had 0% of its people in Lagos's area (centre plus FUA). Epe is a
separate centre (0.14M) 30 km out; 1% of Epe LGA's people are within 15 km of Lagos's centre and
13% within 20 km.

## The rule

A smaller urban centre (under 1M, outside the US, its centroid in no metro's area) whose edge is
within 3 km of a metro's own centres joins the nearest such metro's area. A metro's own centres:
its merged centres; in Europe, the centres whose centroid is in its FUA. 143 centres join 75
metros. Only the centre joins, not its FUA (the variants below).

Measured on the cached units (people and area of all planned rows, existing packs taken):

| Rule | Europe | Elsewhere | Lagos test |

| Centres within 3 km, with the FUA around each | 202.8M | 1884.7M | 98.1% |

| **Centres within 3 km (chosen)** | **201.7M, 377k km2** | **1866.6M, 1823k km2** | **98.1%** |
| Every metro's centres grown by 10 km | 206.3M, 392k km2 | 1947.0M, 2016k km2 | 98.1% |

The FUA around a small centre can belong to another town (Surabaya +5.35M from Mojoagung's). The
10 km reach triples the growth elsewhere and swells Europe 2.5% for the same Lagos result. Small
centres by gap to a metro's centres (all regions): touching 60 (16.0M), up to 1 km 37 (8.4M),
1-2 km 20 (3.1M), 2-3 km 27 (4.9M), 3-5 km 66 (12.2M), 5-10 km 281 (48.2M).

## The plan after

517 planned rows (515 before): Europe 201.2M to 201.7M people (+0.3%), 377,019 to 377,278 km2;
elsewhere 1830.6M to 1866.6M (+2.0%), 1,788,381 to 1,822,790 km2 (+1.9%). 48 rows changed units,
Dusseldorf the only one in Europe (Wuppertal, 1.15M to 1.66M). New rows at the end: Duhok
(order 516, 1.77M) and Colachel (517, 1.19M), both under 1M before. Every earlier row kept its
order and id; the next 50 are unchanged, so no new picture. Largest overlap 0.008 km2.

Largest growers: Marhaura 1.25M to 4.13M, Maharajganj 2.80M to 5.13M, Mehsi 1.16M to 3.41M,
Gyanpur 1.12M to 3.29M (all on the Bihar and east Uttar Pradesh plain, where GHSL's centres chain
through dense villages), Zhangjiagang 1.26M to 3.00M (Jiangyin), Jakarta 35.04M to 37.55M,
Panyushi 7.08M to 8.41M (Huadu), Nantong 1.91M to 3.10M (Haimen), Ganzhou 1.40M to 2.45M,
Xi'an 6.31M to 7.31M (Xianyang), Lome 2.37M to 3.29M (Aflao across the Ghana border).

## Jakarta's split

Jakarta grew past 40M again with Cilegon and Cipanas, and the old split (grown from the most
populous unit group) then left DKI Jakarta alone as an 11M piece and named the 38M rest Bogor.
The first piece now grows from the group nearest the metro's densest square km: Jakarta 37.55M
(was 35.04M) and Tangerang 11.45M (was 10.95M), as before. Guangzhou's and Dhaka's pieces came
out as before (Dhaka and Comilla grew by satellites).

## Weak spot

On the Bihar plain the satellites roughly triple four small rows. Those rows were GHSL's
chained village centres already (notes/T-536.md); the gate (T-538) sees them as any other row.
