# T-440: T-380's parking rule in the everyday and regional layers

Rule: docs/demand.md 4.3 (Parking). Built 2026-10-15.

## What changed

- `everyday.rs` `pass`: per zone `Params::park_parts` once, then each pair's rail share is
  `rail_share_parked` (the commutes' two-logit mix) times `1 - walk`. Cordons arrive through
  `engine.base.cordon`. The old `park_cost_cap_min` and `park_jobs_per_km2_per_min` fields are gone
  (`realnet --set` takes `park_paid_min` and `park_free_d` instead).
- `pipeline/intercity.py` `park_by_cell` mirrors `park_parts` + `cordon_by_zone` (cordon rings
  copied from `kernel::CORDONS`); `sites()` and `market_zones.own_sites` both call it, so every
  zone's `site_park` is the mean over drivers. The regional layer reads that figure as before.
- `data/packs/intercity.json` was not regenerated (that reruns the joint gravity on the merged
  packs): `site_park` alone was replaced, each old site taking the new group in its 600 m square
  (258,013 sites; 48,840 had no group in the same square since the merge moved cell centres, and took
  the nearest, at most 806 m away). Job-weighted mean parking 7.3 to 11.0 minutes, highest site 43 to
  64 (Midtown with the Manhattan charge), sites over 20 minutes 79 to 220. A full `intercity.py`
  run would regroup the sites on the merged packs and renumber them against the commutes.
  The pre-change file is not kept in the tree.
- Market packs rebuilt: `market_zones.py` over all 26 registered zones (registry rows rewritten).

Before is the commit's snapshot build on 8800 (its total matches the smoke run before the change).
The everyday layer gains because the new rule charges the dense and mid-density centres more than the
old one (Downtown Brooklyn about 9 to 19 minutes, Midtown 43 to 59 with the congestion charge), the
same move T-372 made for commutes (rail share 17.3% to 20.6%); the commute figure is flat because
more everyday riders crowd the same trains. Nothing was tuned (no calibration now).
