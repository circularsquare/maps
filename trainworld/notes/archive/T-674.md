# T-674: airports on the map, and the airport bubbles that stay red (Anita's playtest, 2026-10-18)

Two things, the second a bug nobody has diagnosed yet.

## 1. Drop the airport bubble, keep its icon
"the airport bubble is confusing cuz its unclickable. lets actually just drop the airport bubble, but
keep the little icon." Airports are drawn as bubbles since T-653/eed8d58 (`airportMarks.ts`, the
plane badge on an airport's bubble, T-448). Remove the bubble itself (the circle and its count,
so nothing looks clickable that is not) and keep the plane icon at the airport's place, with the
hover it has now. The airport's trips still count in the totals and in a station's riders.

## 2. Destination bubbles stuck on "100% driving"
Anita's report: "i built a line to airport and noticed the destination bubbles all stayed red, and
they're like saying ~100% driving if i hover them. but if i actually click one it updates and says
like ~50% blue. but then if i click off and dont select any specific bubble, they return to being
red and saying 100% driving. it took a page refresh to fix." Destinations view, a line to an airport
(Haneda in the Tokyo pack, a new game). With a bubble selected the figures are right (the selection
query), with none selected they are the no-rail ones, and a reload fixed it.
- Reproduce first (`?new=1`, Tokyo, a line from a station to the airport station, Destinations on).
  Likely suspects: the unselected bubbles read a cached or "bare" figure for the zone (T-325, T-328's
  cache beside the save) that a later final solve does not replace; or the airport purpose's riders
  are left out of the unselected sums while the selection query includes them. Compare
  `window.twDemand` figures with and without a selection (`t586-trip-agrees.mjs` is the nearest
  existing check).
- A fix needs a check that fails before it: add it to `checks/` (a line to an airport, bubbles'
  unselected mode share against the selected sum for the same cell).

## Outcome (2026-10-19)
- Reproduced (`checks/t674-airport-modes.mjs`, before the fix): after a Hamamatsucho, Shinagawa,
  Haneda line the worker's airport legs at Haneda were 8.9% train, the airport's 228k bubble and its
  hover still 0% train / 99.9% drive; the destination bubble under it, selected, read 10.3% train.
- Cause: `airportMarks.ts` read each zone's airport mode split once per kind of day and kept it
  ("legs do not move with the network"), but the split it drew does move. Its bubble sat over the
  destination bubbles under All purposes with a hover radius as large as the bubble, so hovering
  there showed its stale split; selecting a destination bubble hid the airport layer, which is why
  a click "fixed" it until deselected.
- The destination bubbles themselves also read 0% train unselected and 10.3% selected right after
  the solve: that is T-156's catch-up (bubbles blend old into new over a game day of trips; paused,
  nothing moves; a selection is the current forecast at once). After one game day at top speed the
  unselected bubble matched the selection exactly, so no bug there; the check runs that day.
- Fix: the airports are read again with each demand result (kept per result, the old ones shown
  until the new arrive); the bubble is gone (stage 1), so only the plane's 12 px hover shows them.
  The plane's hover is the current forecast, not caught up like the bubble under it.

Files: `map/airportMarks.ts`, `map/bubbles.ts`, `map/commuterView.ts`, `game/demand.ts`, the demand
worker's bubble sums if the bug is there. Stage 1 (drop the bubble) can land alone; report the
diagnosis of stage 2 before a big change.
