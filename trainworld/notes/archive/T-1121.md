# T-1121 to T-1125: station boxes, the getting-off pops, crowds at the stop, transfer bubbles, trains at capacity

One agent, one tree (t1121), 2026-10-24. The rules are in docs/ui-map.md (Stations, Platform crowds,
Life at stations, Track display) and docs/track-and-service.md 6.6; this file keeps what was found
and measured.

## What the purple lines were (T-1121)

The platform edges of T-236 (Anita then: "show the actual platforms as like little blue lines"),
`PLATFORM_BLUE` #8085f0, drawn as the renderer's station layer 3: 15% of the white in from the rim
up to zoom 17, then moved in beside the two tracks once double track parts (T-658c), which is the
outside-to-inside migration she saw. They carried nothing a player needs beyond the box itself
(no number, no state), so they are gone; the crowd fill now reaches the rim, and close in starts
at the track pair's outer edge. The station inspector's complex diagram (ComplexMap.tsx) still
draws its own blue platform edges; that is a separate diagram and was left alone.

## Measured on the real save (T-007-real-nyc.save, 08:00 weekday, 34 St-Penn Station)

- Transfer pulses in the hour: 42,809, all with a walk of 1 s or more (739,600 riders); none at
  zero walk, since the save gives each line its own station. 41,880 bubbles drawn in the hour (the
  rest have a station outside the buffer or under one rider). Building them: 15 ms once a game hour.
- Frame cost at Penn Station, zoom 16.3, 40 frames: GPU 5.96 ms with bubbles against 5.68
  without; CPU 0.34 ms against 0.27 (one draw call; the vertex shader puts each bubble or culls it).
- A bubble's path runs between the two platform middles while its time is demand's walk between
  the two station nodes, so on long platforms the bubble walks faster than 1.2 m/s on screen
  (one at Penn: 305 local units in 113 s).
- Crowds at the stop: a platform's crowd goes from 12.6 to 0 the second its train stops (180 s
  before it leaves a first stop, the turnaround), 115 to 0 at another, and grows again only after
  the train leaves.
- Stretches at capacity: 127 stretches on 48 lines at 08:00.
