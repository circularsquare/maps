# T-217, T-218, T-219: complexes as one shape, a thicker packed-train edge, platforms that fill up

Done 2026-10-13. Design as built: docs/interface.md ("Stations on the map", "How full each train
is", "Platform crowds", "Network tooltips", "Display settings"), docs/track-and-service.md 6.6
("Room on a platform"); decisions in DECISIONS 2026-10-13.

## Where things are

- `map/renderer.ts`
  - `STATION_VS/FS`: `u_layer` 0 the dark shape, 1 the white inside the rim, 2 the crowd fill
    (each side's half from the middle out, `a_crowd` = the two sides' shares and left-behind
    flags). The draw runs, per pass (unselected, then selected), layer 0 for stations and
    bridges, layer 1 for both, then layer 2 for stations. Zoomed out `a_cplx` (centre offset,
    reach, part) makes the complex's lead draw one dot and hides the other members; `a_sel` is
    now two flags (as a platform, as a dot).
  - `BRIDGE_VS` (with `STATION_FS`): a link's bridge, ends from `NetworkBuffers.stationLinks`.
  - `SEL_VS/FS`, `drawGlow`: the selection's outline and glow, one quad over the selected
    shapes' union; the shapes (CPU twins, CSS px) in a 3-texel-a-shape RGBA32F texture.
  - `syncGroups` (complex dots from `DrawOptions.groups`), `syncSelection`, `drawnShapeOf`,
    `selectedShapes` (now with bridges, so the demand cut-out follows them), `dotStations`,
    `crowdShare` (for the tag), `updateCrowds` (shares of `platformRoom`).
  - T-218: the train shader's red edge 3.9 px.
- `map/stationGeometry.ts`: `platformLine`, `bridgeEnds`, `bridgeShape`, `BRIDGE_HALF_PX`,
  `enclosingCircle`.
- `map/network.ts`: `stationLength`, `stationLinkIdx`, `stationLinks` (built in the clock worker).
- `game/boarding.ts`: `platformRoom`, `PLATFORM_SIDE_WIDTH_M`, `PLATFORM_M2_PER_RIDER`.
- `game/complexView.ts`: `complexLeads` (each complex's busiest station); `main.ts` passes it
  as `groups`.
- `map/networkTips.ts`: the platform-full line, the dot line; `crowdStationAt` and
  `CROWD_REACH_PX` are gone (the crowd is inside the box), and `trackHover.ts` no longer calls it.
- `ui/SettingsTab.tsx`: one checkbox, "How full each platform is"; `display.platformCrowds`
  keeps its values ("dots" left in older settings counts as on).
