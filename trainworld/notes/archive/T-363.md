# T-363: read a region's OSM once

`pipeline/osm_features.py` reads a Geofabrik extract once into `<extract>.points.fgb` and
`.areas.fgb`; tier0.py (job places, schools), canada.py (osm_places, osm_caches: terminals,
medical, hospital buildings, parks) and places.py (tier 0 place list) read those through
`osm_features.read` with the same bbox, mask and OGR SQL filter they used to give GDAL.

## Two things that made a naive cache wrong

1. **GDAL hides a relation's member ways.** A closed way that is a member of a multipolygon
   relation is left out of a read that also reports that relation, even when the way has tags
   of its own (the Royal National Throat, Nose and Ear Hospital way inside UCL's campus relation;
   commons inside the Maidenhead and Cookham Commons relation). So a read's rows depend on its
   filter: a filter the relation fails reports the way. The first cache (one broad filtered
   read) lost 8 London parks, 2 medical sites and a school against the old per-city reads.
   Fix: closed ways (`osm_way_id IS NOT NULL`, which no relation passes) and relations in
   separate passes, a third unfiltered pass to learn which ways a relation hides (England: 816
   of the cached ways; 484 by a cached relation), and `member_of` on each; `read` drops a way
   when one of its relations is in that read's result. Membership is matched by ring point set.
   Relations with any land use are cached because osm_places' filter includes `landuse IS NOT
   NULL`, so such a relation hides its members there.
2. **pyarrow 21's `match_substring`** missed matches in a 20,000-row batch that it found in a
   short slice (135 of 2,504 Liechtenstein job points). The filters run in pandas instead.

GDAL's attribute filter is also slow on the OSM driver: the unfiltered passes take 4 minutes
each against 9-12 for the filtered ones.
