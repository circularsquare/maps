# T-373: park-and-ride stage 4 (rides in the regional layer)

Design: notes/T-295.md (stage 4). Rule as built: docs/regional.md 5.3.

## What was built (2026-10-15)

- `kernel::place_rides` became `kernel::access_options(zones, net, p, places, End)`, the one ride
  function for every layer: `End::Home` costs the cheaper of parking and `d_drop` at the station
  (T-371's rule), `End::Far` costs `d_drop` alone. Same `p_ride` 10, `d_drop` 10, 15 km, 6 lines.
- The market builds 2 km zones from its sites (`ride_zones`: true jobs, a site's 0.36 km² as land
  for the density speeds) and rides from each zone's homes-and-jobs centre; routes are not loaded
  for the market, so the road is 1.3x the straight line.
- Per site, `add_access` nests the walk (the mean of its weights at the trip's scale, the form the
  walk-only market had) with the zone's rides at `station_theta` 0.2. Without rides the weights
  are as before. Summing rides straight in at 0.025 would have made any extra option worth
  minutes: a 30-minute ride beside a 20-minute walk would have lowered access to -3 minutes.
- Commutes between zones: rides at the home end when the zone centres are 5 km apart or more
  (`CPair::ride`, else the walk-only list `access_c_walk`); the work end walks. Inter-region trips:
  Home rides at the origin, Far rides at the destination.
- Fixed on the way: the commutes' home stations were not in `want`, so a trip home to a station
  no work end used was dropped by `assign` (a debug assert only). They are added now.

## Values picked

The brief's 15 km for long trips too (the design had suggested 40 km): 40 km would serve far
more zones, and the pair count, already 3x, is what the solve pays for. For T-372 to revisit.

## Corridor test network (`research/intercity/corridor_net.py`, `zones_day --no-everyday`)

Commute legs a day between zones, by rail. T-276's figures had the old 15 km/h fast walk; real
lines carry about 3-5x those (notes/T-276.md).

| corridor | T-276 (fast walk) | before (6 km/h walk) | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Haven - New York | 1,278 | 521 | 2,870 |
| Boston - Providence | 1,302 | 639 | 3,752 |
| Baltimore - Washington | 1,080 | 420 | 5,165 |
| New Haven - Hartford | 876 | 386 | 1,795 |
| New York - Philadelphia | 599 | 240 | 3,462 |

Against T-276's figures that is 2.0-5.8x, so about 0.4-1.9x of real by the rough 3-5x rule:
Providence and Washington in range, New Haven lines under, New York - Philadelphia over (its
gravity is 0.56x LODES too, so the over is less than it looks). Inter-region trips by rail on this
network 3,460 to 72,962 a day (New York - Philadelphia 808 to 15,174 of 380k, 4%). Served market
pairs 8,250 to 270,936.
