### Why the two parked

- **Cardiff**: "3 plan units, 2 matched to OSM; 2 OSM units, 1 plan polygons": one of the three NUTS3
  units (TLL52-54) has no OSM match, so the outline is 1,636 km2 and the pack holds 559k of 1.10M
  people. A real fault in unit matching for a country with no module. Its top job cell (51.408,
  -3.242, near Llantrisant, not the centre; not looked into) is also over the 1% cap.
- **Belfast**: units all matched; only the proxy job cell at the centre is over the cap (1.28%
  against 1.0%). The cap was set on England and the Northeast; a small monocentric city with
  OSM-proxy jobs puts more in its core. Real, but a threshold question, not a broken pack.

Neither pack is in the registry; their pack files and res-9 copies stay on disk (18 files each).

## Retrospective

1. Backup, sim/ copy, row choice: 6 minutes. 2. Batch: 44 minutes unattended. 3. Checks and audit
of files written: 5 minutes (the two checks run together). Tokens: the orchestrator's measure.

- **The plan ran out of UK rows at 11.** The next batch is the rest of Europe; `plan --countries`
  prints the row counts, so say in the brief how many rows exist.
- **Reading the extract is still the largest phase** (Ireland 5.8 min of 8.0 in its region, Scotland
  4.0 of 7.8). Overlapping the next region's download and read with this one's gate (T-592) is the
  saving; more `--jobs` is not (two cities at a time did not make builds the long part).
- **`--jobs 6` buys little**: builds are 0.6-2.1 min each; the five Yorkshire/Midlands builds ran two
  at a time. 3 would have cost a few minutes more.
- **Countries with no module (Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, and all of the rest of Europe)
  rely on NUTS3-to-OSM matching** and job proxy lumps; 2 of 3 no-module rows here parked or came
  close (Glasgow shipped). Expect more parks on the rest of Europe from these two causes.
- **Gate warnings on top_zone_jobs_share** (3 of 6) are the same proxy-jobs lumpiness; not acted on.
- **A parked row keeps its 18 pack files** on disk; harmless, but a batch of 100 parks would pile
  up. Consider letting the runner delete a parked row's pack files after recording the reason.
- **Before the rest of Europe**: T-610 (unit matching) first, since a missing unit silently halves a
  pack on any country without a module.
