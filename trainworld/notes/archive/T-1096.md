# T-1096 The complex view's trains, smooth

Real save, Times Sq-42 St complex, 08:30, normal speed, headless Chrome 1400x900 (scripts were
throwaway; outputs in `artifacts/trainworld/t1096/measure-*.json`, `complex-*.png`).

| | trains redrawn a second | main-thread task ms a second, panel closed | panel open |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | 3.4 (SVG re-rendered when a 5 s bucket of the arrival time changed, checked every 150 ms) | 142 | 184 (+42) |
| SVG rects moved every frame (first try) | 60 | 157 | 485 to 524 (+330) |
| the same with the SVG on its own layer (`will-change`) | 60 | 147 | 299 (+150) |
| **canvas over the SVG (kept)** | 60 | 157 | 184 (+27) |

Moving SVG rects every frame repainted the panel, about 5 ms a frame; script time itself rose only
about 25 ms a second. The kept version draws the trains on a canvas over the map every frame the clock
moves; the clear SVG rects are their hovers, moved four times a second. The view also now shows trains
pulling out (60 game seconds, gathering speed), and keeps the same object while the same trains are
drawn, so Preact re-renders only when trains come or go or riders waiting change.

Side effect: the trains are drawn over the transfer curves (they were under them).
