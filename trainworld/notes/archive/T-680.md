# T-680: 25,000 people waiting at once on a new line's platform (Anita's playtest, 2026-10-18)

Anita, first: "after building and assigning stops for a new line, some of the stops immediately get
like 25k riders. this feels a bit unrealistic, it seems like they should sort of trickle in rather
than happening instantly?" Then, 2026-10-19, on an adoption ramp: "i dont think we want to ramp,
just want 24 hour count. but im not sure you understand what i'm describing. it's that people arrive
and theres like 25k people waiting at the station that came all at once."

So: **not** a ramp of a line's daily riders (decided against, DECISIONS 2026-10-19). The bug is the
**platform crowd**: right after a new line gets its stops, a station's waiting count jumps to about
25,000 in one step. Undiagnosed.

## Cause and fix (2026-10-19, done)
While a line has eras, `Service::account_start` begins its boarding account's day six hours before
the latest change. For a new line nothing ran in those six hours, but the account's cold run lets
the first train take everyone who arrived since the day's last departure a day earlier, which is
just before that start: six hours of riders. The hour slice drew the same, since the last event
before the hour wrapped round to that same departure six hours back. The 8800 build (last commit)
showed 68,824 on one Mumbai test-line platform the moment its trains started.

Fix, all in `sim/src/track/boarding.rs`: `simulate` finds the latest era whose pattern had no trains
(`RunAccount::open`, when it falls inside the account's day), and when its departures cross it the
queue empties and arrivals count from then. `hour` replaces any events before it with one at it
holding nobody, and counts the hour's arrivals from it; `tally` ("today", the past day) skips the
wrapped departures before it and leaves out who would have been waiting. The rest of the account is
unchanged. Rust test `a_new_lines_platforms_fill_from_when_its_trains_start` (19,000 before, now
empty), and `checks/t680-new-line-crowd.mjs` (FAIL on 8800 before, PASS after).

## Likely shape (as first written)
The boarding account (`sim/src/track/boarding.rs`, T-164; `game/boarding.ts`, `game/complexLive.ts`)
works a day at a time from the solve's riders per period. When a line is new, its first trains enter
gradually at terminals (T-206), so for a while riders arrive at the platform at the solved rate with
no train to take them. If the account starts the day (or the period) from its beginning instead of
from the edit's game time, the whole backlog since the period began lands on the platform at once.
Or the first solve's riders arrive before any train is due and pile up for an hour. Either way the
crowd should start empty at the moment the service starts and grow at the arrival rate, never
appear in one step.

## Method
Reproduce in a new game (`?new=1`): a line through a busy area, constructed, trains set; watch a
station's waiting count over the first game hour (the station inspector's waiting rows and the
platform hover). Find where the jump comes from. Fix so riders start arriving at the platform when
the stop starts being served (and the waiting count only grows at the arrival rate), with a check
in `checks/` that fails before the fix. Riders who would wait longer than the period because no train
comes yet should not appear at all (they took another mode). Report the cause before a large
change to the boarding account.
