# T-745, line bullets still off centre after T-705

Anita, 2026-10-19, on the a8f2ec6 build: "bullet symbols are still not centered on the bullet".

## Where bullets are drawn

Only two places, both through `ui/bullet.ts`: `Chip` (ui/widgets.tsx: Lines and Stations tabs, every
inspector, search, the trip explorer) and `lineBullet` (map/trackTagRows.ts: the track and station
hover tags, the resting-hover list of T-732). The map itself (WebGL, MapLibre layers) draws no line
symbols.

## Why T-705's bullets were off (all bullets, not some)

1. **Canvas metrics are whole pixels.** Chrome returns `actualBoundingBox*` rounded outward to
   whole pixels at the size measured: a 12 px Nunito "L" reads ascent 9 (the outline's is 8.46),
   left 0 and right 7. T-705 measured at 12 px, so every symbol's box was up to half a pixel wrong
   on each side, the same way for every symbol.
2. **The circle and the symbol snapped separately.** The circle was the span's CSS background,
   which Chrome snaps to whole device pixels; the SVG holding the text is a replaced element whose
   origin snaps on its own, and its text is not snapped sideways. In rows at fractional positions
   (the dock's rows are 20.3 px, the tags sit where the layout puts them) the two drifted apart by
   up to a device pixel, sideways mostly.
3. **Text baselines snap to whole device pixels**, so a baseline at y 14.25 was drawn at 14.

Font loading was not the cause: by the time the first bullet was measured Nunito had loaded, and
`loadingdone` cleared the cache. A measure taken while a face is still loading (`document.fonts
.check` false) is now never cached, and one `document.fonts.load` per font and symbol triggers the
re-measure. T-705's own "1 px" figure compared its placement with the layout box, not the pixels.

## The fix (ui/bullet.ts, style.css `.chip:has(> svg)`, widgets.tsx `useCssZoom`)

- Ink box measured at 240 px and scaled down.
- The circle (or pill) is a `<rect>` in the same SVG as the text, so both share one origin; the
  span's background and padding are off when it holds the SVG (kept for the unmeasured fallback).
- The baseline is rounded to a whole device pixel for `devicePixelRatio` times the CSS zoom the
  bullet is drawn at, and the rect moved by the same amount (under half a device pixel). `Chip`
  reads its zoom from `currentCSSZoom` after mounting (the UI scale until then); the map tags are
  not zoomed. A change of pixel ratio (browser zoom, another monitor) re-measures.

## Measurements (device px, ink centre against the drawn circle's centre, on the screenshot)

`checks/t745-bullets.mjs` on the real save, light and dark, 100% and 90% UI, ratio 1 and 2: every
group passes; worst 0.55 (Nunito's "S" at ratio 1, the rasteriser's own), means 0.0-0.2. Before
the fix the same places measured worst about 1.4 to 1.5 (against the layout box, an earlier
version of the check).
