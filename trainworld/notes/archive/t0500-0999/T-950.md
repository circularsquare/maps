# T-950: "Calculating demand" forever on Anita's Android phone

Report (2026-10-22): anita.garden on Android Chrome, card "Calculating demand" with no bar, hash
`#3.94/42.15/6.5`. Not reproduced on the phone itself; the mechanism is established in emulation.
Anita, later: the start menu appeared and she started a new game; the phone is a Pixel 10 (or
near); it hangs every time, not once. That rules out a one-off download failure. The Pixel 10's
Tensor G5 has an Imagination PowerVR GPU rather than the Mali of earlier Pixels, so a WebGL path
(a shader or extension the overlay needs) is the first suspect. The fix shipped to anita.garden
on 2026-10-22 (website e03d745) shows the error text.

## What the screen means

The card with no bar at zoom 3.94 needs `clock.held`, no failure, `demandIdle` false and
`loadProgress` null. Zoomed out past `ZONE_LOAD_ZOOM` (7.5) the view needs no pack, so a new game
with no stations is idle and released the moment its network arrives. `demandIdle` stays false
only while `gameLoadArmed()`: the game's network never reached the demand client. So the clock
worker never sent its first network (or main.ts never got as far as starting it).

The start menu's New game sheet has no city picked by default; its main button is "Start on the
map", which opens at zoom 1.8 over [20, 30]; a pinch toward Toulon gives her hash. (`?new=1`
opens on the home zone at zoom 10.5 instead, where a stuck clock worker shows a creeping bar.)

## Silent paths found

1. `clock.worker.ts` ran its init (`await init()` of the track WASM, storage, the first game) in an
   IIFE with no catch: any throw left nothing sent. Its later messages logged only
   `console.warn("clock worker: ...")`.
2. `clockClient` set only the debug status on the worker's `onerror` (script failed to load).
3. An uncaught error in main.ts's setup after `startLoadingGate()` (line ~103) and before
   `showStartMenu` (the overlay's `no WebGL2`, a shader that does not compile on a phone GPU)
   leaves the map, the card, no menu and no workers: the same screen.

Reproduced with `scratchpad/mobile/probe.mjs` (400x860, DPR 3, touch, Android UA) on the dev
server: blocking `wasm/track/sim_bg.wasm` after "Start on the map" gave exactly her card, forever.

## Ruled out

- Memory. Both WASM modules start at 1.1 MB. As a 4 GB phone (`deviceMemory` 4, 8 threads: one
  solving worker plus aux), a new game holds 6 MB at Var (open bare in aux) and 9 MB at a zoom 7.6
  Provence view; at 3.94 nothing loads at all (0 loads). Demand workers' init errors already
  post `failed` and show the screen.
- Touch: the menu and sheet work with taps in emulation, live site included.
- Android Chrome lacking worker or WASM features: the demand workers use the same toolchain and
  would have failed to the screen.

Which of the three paths hit her phone is unknown; the new screen now names the error.

## Fix

Paths 1-3 fail at once to "The game could not start" with the error; a watchdog backs them up
(DECISIONS 2026-10-22). Checked: track WASM blocked (immediate), its request hung with
`?quietMs=20000` (fires at 20 s), the overlay's WebGL2 refused (immediate, "no WebGL2"), a pack
download hung under `?new=1` ("Demand could not load", Play without demand offered).
