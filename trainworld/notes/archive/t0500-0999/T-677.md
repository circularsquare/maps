# T-677: a start menu, and a loading animation (Anita's playtest, 2026-10-18)

"actual menu when loading in: just show like 'new game', show all saves if there are any" and "cute
loading animation, asian style drawn bunny in a train".

## The menu
On opening the page the player sees a menu card before anything loads: **New game**, then the list
of saved games (name, last played, size of network as the saves panel shows them, `SavesPanel.tsx`),
the most recent first; a click on a save opens it. Nothing is chosen for the player: today the
autosave opens straight away. Decisions made here:
- The menu shows on every page load. URL switches that mean a choice already (`?new=1`, `?synth`,
  `?demandTest`, `?paused`, a save in the hash if there is one) skip it.
- With no saves the menu is just New game; a single click starts. (No account, no settings here.)
- While the menu is up nothing heavy loads: the clock and demand workers start after the pick.
  Check that startup marks (`checks/startup-timing.mjs`) do not get worse once a choice is made, and
  that T-328's cached demand result still shows at once when a save is picked.
- Same visual language as the loading card (`LoadingScreen.tsx`), light and dark.

## The animation
A small drawn bunny riding in a train, for the loading card and the demand loading spots that use
`Spinner.tsx`'s role. Anita: "asian style drawn", cute, in the game's pastel look; she is not an
artist and neither are we: draw it as inline SVG (a flat, rounded, few-colour bunny at a train
window, track passing under; the wheels turn and the bunny's ears bob, 2-4 frames or a CSS
keyframe loop; **no CSS transitions** elsewhere, but a loading animation is motion by design, as
the trains are). Draft it, put screenshots of the frames in the artifacts folder, and keep the file
small (under 10 kB) and easy to swap. Anita will say what to change; do not spend long on polish.
The existing spinners (T-654, "loading spinners") stay for small spots (panels loading).
