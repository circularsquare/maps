# T-888: build_city slow on archipelago coasts

## Where the time went

All of it in `osm_units.build`, the planned outline (stage 5), before the water mask or the cells.
The runner's own faulthandler dumps (`world_run/20261021-190410/{aan,vri,pik}.build.log`) point
there, and cProfile on aan (outline only, original code, today's Finland extract) gives 1,130 s of
the build's 1,343 s:

| Step | aan s | What |
|---|---|---|
| `landed` (64 relations) | 430 | sea cut from each admin relation: `buffer(0)` 248, `is_valid` of the clipped sea 83, difference 41, clip 58 |
| `match` | 249 | the unit against each relation; every intersection was computed twice (lines 113 and 119) |
| `_people` (828 parts) | 237 | one `ghsl_window` per outline piece, each unzipping the whole GHSL tile (180 s of zlib) and building two pyproj transformers (53 s) |
| `union_all` | 114 | 42 s of it the sea tiles joined into one shape (1.7M vertices, tens of thousands of islet holes) |
| sea read, cut_packs, rest | ~100 | |

The cost is the islets: the sea has one hole per islet, so every cut of it, and every shape cut by
it, carries tens of thousands of tiny rings (Ålands skärgård: 29,038 pieces; Åland: 33,509). Also
paid for nothing: relations repeated at two levels (Åland at admin levels 3 and 4, Mariehamn at 7
and 8 are byte-identical) were cut twice, and relations in the box that meet no unit (Manner-Suomi,
Varsinais-Suomi beside Åland) were cut too. vri's profile is the same shape at scale: 1,706
`landed` cuts and 1,898 `_people` reads (about 500 s of tile unzipping).

## The fix (osm_units.py only; patch `notes/T-888.diff`, `git apply` from the maps root)

Exact (output byte-identical):
1. `_people_many`: one GHSL read over all the candidate pieces, each piece summing the same pixels
   `ghsl_window` would have given it (same window arithmetic, same row-major order, so the same
   float sum). aan 237 s to 0.4 s.
2. `match` keeps the first intersection's area for the `inside` test.
3. `landed` and `dried` are memoised by the input's WKB (repeated relation shapes cut once).
4. Relations that intersect no plan unit are dropped before the sea is cut from them (they could
   never be near a unit in `match`).

Not exact, but nothing in the pack moves:
5. `without_islets`: sea holes (islets) under `MIN_PART_KM2` (0.05 km2) are filled, in each sea
   tile piece before the union and once more after it. Any outline piece from such an islet is
   under 0.05 km2 and the outline drops it anyway.

## Proof the output matches

All runs read the same Finland extract (Geofabrik, 2026-10-21) in a scratch copy of the pipeline;
the comparison is original against fixed on the same inputs, not against the shipped packs.

- Fixes 1-4: `aan` and `vri` `.outline.geojson` and `.units.geojson` byte-identical (sha256).
- All five, full `build_city` from each outline (aan, vri, pik): `.bin` and `.json` byte-identical
  in all three zones. The outline is the same shape: same part count and area, symmetric
  difference under 0.5 m2. Only the ring start points and a few collinear vertices differ.
- `.boundary.geojson` (the drawn outline) differs: tier0 simplifies the outline at 0.0003 deg, and
  Douglas-Peucker's result depends on where each ring starts, so the line moves within that
  tolerance (about 20-30 m). Symmetric difference 0.53 km2 of 1,205 (aan), 1.26 of 27,610 (vri),
  0.70 of 30,357 (pik); same part counts. Both versions are simplifications of the same outline.
- `.units.geojson` (read only by `latin_names`, for names) changes in two ways. Match scores
  move a little (vri FI1C1 0.873 to 0.879), since islets no longer count as land. And near ties
  between sets covering the same land flip: vri FI1C7 two L7 seutukunta (0.970) became their six
  L8 municipalities (0.971); pik FI196 16 L8 municipalities became their 3 L7 seutukunta, and
  FI19A three L7 seutukunta became Österbotten (L4). Every name is Latin either way.

Risk of fix 5: a match decision within about 1% of a 0.5 threshold, between sets that cover
*different* land, could flip and change an outline. None did in these three zones. Fixes 1-4 alone
carry no such risk, at about half the saving.
