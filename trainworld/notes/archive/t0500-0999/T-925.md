## The change

`pipeline/osm_units.py`: where OSM relations leave at least REST_MIN of a plan unit uncovered, a
NUTS-3 unit's own polygon no longer fills it. `gb_fill` takes the geoBoundaries gbOpen units lying
mostly (half their area) inside the NUTS unit, at the finest level of that country whose licence is
in `FILL_LICENCES` (CC0, public domain, ODbL, CC BY without NC/ND/SA, dl-de/by-2-0, OGL) and that
covers most of the unit; the fill is that union less the sea and less what OSM covers. NUTS still
decides which units a zone has and whether a fill is needed (attributes and a test, no shipped
geometry). Units file `source` then reads e.g. `geoBoundaries ESP ADM3 (CC BY 4.0)`.

Levels the fill can use for the 9 (licence from the gbOpen metadata, data/raw/worldplan/geoboundaries-gbOpen-ALL.json):

| Country | Level used | Licence | Left out |
|---|---|---|---|
| DE | ADM3 (Kreise, BKG) | dl-de/by-2-0 | |

| NL | ADM2 (gemeenten) | CC0 | |
| FI | ADM3 (kunnat, OSM-derived) | ODbL 1.0 | |

Dry outline builds before the run (scratch kit `dry.py`): lis and pot now match OSM fully (their

at all; ceu has no OSM relation and takes geoBoundaries ESP ADM3 Ceuta whole.
