# T-874: commute zone pairs with tiny flows dealt in chunks (2026-10-21)

Built from notes/T-869.md item 1, on the code after T-854 (car ownership out, rail bounds, home
parking).

## What it does

`kernel::mode_choice`: per origin zone, the destination zones within reach carrying under
`COMMUTE_CHUNK` (0.1) trips in the period are grouped by block of 8 x 8 zones (16 km), in zone
order within a block. A block's total is dealt in `ceil(total / 0.1)` draws, systematic sampling
by trips from a start fixed by `unit_hash(origin zone, block)` (the market's, T-780). A pair drawn
gets a factor (its draws' trips over its own) on its trips; the rest are skipped. The factor is per
zone pair, so every origin subzone keeps its own share of the block's trips exactly. Stability:
the deal reads only the pair's trips (gravity, subzone weights), never rail's costs, so crowding
rounds and timetable edits deal the same pairs; an edit that adds or drops a station changes the
trips of the zones whose cells join or leave subzones, which re-deals only the blocks holding
them (and an origin zone scaled as a whole keeps its deal, since the step scales with it).
`?perfOff=chunks` turns it off with the market's (`DemandApi::set_chunked`).

## Measured (native, one core, a weekday with three crowding rounds, `calib`-style harness)

Timings are noisy (the machine was shared): base runs 38.9-44.6 s on New York. Pairs are subzone
pair visits in free flow over the five periods (deterministic).

New York, real network (`nyc_real.json`, 111 lines), against every pair solved:

Block size barely matters for cost (0.01: 8 km -24%, 16 km -26%, 32 km -27%). The worst segment
moves 1-5% at every setting, on segments of 2,300-2,700 riders a day: the crowding rounds' stopping
rule (T-434) reacting to any small change, not the dealing.

Paris (`par_real.json`, 97 lines): every pair 22.4-22.8 s, chunks 0.1 18.7 s (-18%, pairs -42%),
stations worst 0.02% / 0.10%, segment worst 0.05%; 0.3: 17.7 s (-23%), stations 0.08% / 0.46%.

Stability, New York, one stop dropped from line 68 (station 215, served by it alone): stations more
than 15 km from it moved 0.0015% of their entries with every pair solved, 0.0019% with chunks at
0.1 (0.0018% at 0.3); the worst station the same 1.55% in all three.

In the browser (WASM, `?demandWorkers=1`, the real save loaded then a line skipping one stop):
commutes every pair (market chunked as always) load 71.0 s, edits 66.8-67.9 s; chunks 0.1 load
59.1 s, edits 54.3-54.6 s: -17% and -19%. With `?perfOff=chunks` (market too) 75.2 s and 72.7-73.6 s.

Why 0.1: at 0.1 no figure a player sees moves more than 0.1% (stations) or 0.05 points (home cell
rail share), half the pair visits go; 0.3 takes another 8 points of time but moves the worst small
station 0.4% and a cell 0.3 points. 0.3 is the next step if the solve needs it.

`TW_BLOCK`/`TW_CHUNKX` overrides; the browser timing script `wasm-time.mjs`. Both were scratch.
