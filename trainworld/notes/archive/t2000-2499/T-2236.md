# T-2236: crowding shown by load (2026-11-03, agent t2236)

Anita, 2026-11-03: "since we're oging above 100% capacity now we can drop the like passenger wait time as th metric
for crowding, and ujst use actual crowding aboe 100%".

## What each indicator measures

| Indicator | Measured before | Measures now |
|---|---|---|
| Map crowding mark (Traffic view: red edges and darkening; on side-by-side bands, the band's red dashes) | riders' average wait for a train with room at the stretch's first stop over the day, from 0.2 min, full strength at 8 min | the busiest hour's mean load of the trains leaving the stretch's first stop, fuller direction, over the rated load (160 a car); marked from 100.5%, half strength there, full at 150% |
| Its track hover line | "Average 1.5 minute wait for a train with room" | "Peak load 132% at 08:00" |
| Line panel stops diagram in the Traffic view (marks and stretch hover) | the map's wait | the map's load |
| Line panel crowding dot per demand level, and its hover | average wait for a train with room at platforms where trains come full (from 1 min a rider, a rider turned away counting 30 min); a strip of stretch waits; "6-10: 4/10 trains full" | busiest hour's load on the fullest stretch over the level's periods; strip of stretch loads; "Past 100% on 2 of 14 intervals"; "6-10: 118%" per period |
| Lines list train count colour (and its hover, the dot hover) | the period now's wait score, orange from 9 min, red from 15 | the period now's load, orange from 130%, red from 140% |
| Track at capacity (red band round the ribbon) | track use at `FULL_RHO` | unchanged: track, not riders |
| Trouble list: riders left behind, trains left full | riders left at a platform by trains at crush (3% of those who wanted a train), trains leaving at crush (25%) | unchanged: they are the 150% ceiling, a separate problem from crowding |
| Trouble list problem spots | capacity tags (merges, platforms, crossings) | unchanged: not rider crowding |
| Station panel "Riders left at platform" | riders left by a full train this hour | unchanged, its own indicator |
| Train gauge and inspector; line panel Average load | load against seats, rated and crush | unchanged, already load |
| Trip inspector "Waiting for a train with room", crowding minutes | the route's perceived costs | unchanged: demand's costs |

Riders left on platforms now happen only when trains are packed to 150%, so they stay as their own indicators
(the station panel row, the trouble list's two rows) and leave the map marks and the dots.

## How

- Rust (`sim/src/track/boarding.rs`): `RunAccount::hour_loads` (per stretch and hour, the mean riders aboard
  the trains leaving its first stop), `Boarding::stretch_hours` (fuller direction, over `LineSvc::rated`),
  `period_peaks`. The hour slice's tail keeps its shape (count, pairs, two arrays): the stretches at
  `CROWDED_FROM` 1.005 or more, their busiest hour's load share and that hour. `line_periods` gains a tail
  (per period and stretch, the busiest hour's load share); `window` puts the period now's peak in the slot
  the km-weighted wait had (0 since T-1255). `full_shares`, `stop_waits`, `FULL_WAIT_MIN_S` and the run
  account's `full` and `wait` are gone.
- App: `BoardHour.crowdStretches/crowdLoads/crowdHours`, `Renderer.fullStretch` returns `{ load, hour }`,
  the shader's `a_full.x` is the load share (`CROWD_LOAD_FROM` 1, `CROWD_LOAD_STRONG` 1.5), `CROWD_STOPS`
  by load, `crowdLevel` and `CROWD_GRADES` by load, `peakLoadText`.
- The Rust `crowding()` period numbers 9 and 10 (waits at busy platforms) are now unused by the app; kept for
  the layout.
