# T-2104: how a trip's base fare is shared between lines

Anita (2026-11-03): "i think for bus profitabiblity, splitting trip base fare evenl between lines is not insane tbh.
by km is also not so crazy but yeah. maybe a middle ground. i think people probably wont dig into how this is split so
i think some nontrivial function would be fine here. would also help with long distance profitability metrics i guess"

Built 2026-11-03, `baseTrips` in `app/src/game/money.ts` (income and `regionMoney.ts`'s region split both use it).
No sim or WASM change.

## The rule

The pool is what the base came to before: every boarding times rail trips over boardings, at its boarding stop's
fare scale. It is shared between the running lines half by boardings and half by passenger-km (both at the fare
scale), and no line takes more than one base a boarding; a line that would is held at one and the rest is shared
again by the same weights. Each line's base then goes over its periods by passenger-km, as before. Buses and trains
use the same rule. The network's fares do not change, only the split.

Why this one:
- **Half and half** is the plain midpoint of the two rules Anita named. A pure km share would put a 2 km leg of a
  15 km trip at an eighth of a base, an even one at a half; this gives about a third.
- **The cap**: a trip pays one base whatever it rides, so no rule that splits each trip's own base can give a line
  more than one a boarding. Without it the LIRR's main branches would take about 1.5 a boarding.
- **Network-wide, not per trip.** The main thread has each line's boardings and loads, not each trip's legs. Doing it
  per trip means carrying a second quantity through every assignment (`board_extra` in kernel.rs, the intercity and
  market passes, the round averaging) and out through the WASM interface and worker protocol, for a figure players
  rarely open. The lie: a long line with few transfers also gains base from short lines it never shares a trip
  with, and a short single-line trip pays its line less than a whole base. Today's even rule was network-wide
  in the same way (every line got 0.50 a boarding here whether its riders changed or not).
- Shares by km to a power under 1 would do much the same with a less obvious number; not taken.
