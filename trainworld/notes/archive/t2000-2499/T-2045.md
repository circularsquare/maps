# T-2045 The city in focus solved first and settled during a load, whatever the save's size

Anita, 2026-11-02, on T-1904's proposal: "i think the biggest win would be to like start and finish
the city in focus even if we are in the middle of a larger load. like ideally we can basically
guarantee a certain load time for the city in focus regardless of how big the save is."

## What was built

A **focus pool** (`workers/demandFocus.ts`; rules in docs/demand-runtime.md "The focus pool"): during a
game load, a second pool of demand workers of the main pool's shape solves the zone in view alone,
from its pack to its last round, while the main pool opens the rest and runs the whole solve. A zone
zoomed into mid-load moves to the front of the focus pool (the solve in hand there is superseded).
The whole solve is untouched; its final replaces everything and ends the pool.

T-1904's proposal (the zone's round 0 estimated inside the whole solve's round 0) is not built: the
pool gives the same first figures and the settled ones as well, without touching the whole solve's
round loop. A local solve inside the main pool was ruled out: the zone's populations are the whole
solve's own, so solving them alone mid-round loses that round's state, and a zoom mid-load would
have to interrupt the whole solve.

Pieces:
- Client (`demandClient.ts`): `startFocus`, `focusSoon`, `focusMessage`, `publishFocus`,
  `withFocus`, `withZone`, `endFocus`; questions routed by version in `ask`, `target`, `still`,
  `cellModes`, `cityCells`; the main pool's rough solve skipped while the pool runs.
- Worker: a solve of `only` zones sends `onlyLines` (the lines it gives figures) and steps no cluster
  ahead (T-402) of the others; `solve.keep` keeps it as solved (the zone answers questions after the
  pool moves on); `pause` holds a solving worker at its next population and its opening
  (`?perfTry=focusFirst` only).
- `focusZones` (game/demand.ts): a zone shown from the pool, true once settled; `viewUpdating` and the
  status tag read it.

## Decisions

- **Settled** is the zone's own solve through every round and the capacity fit, with nothing outside
  it: no regional market, no riders from other zones. It counts as final for the status tag and the
  clock cap; the loading card and the held clock still wait for the whole solve's final.
- **The lie**: the settled figures leave out the market's longer trips on the zone's lines and other
  zones' riders on shared lines (and the crowding both add). Measured at the whole solve's final
  (below): the zone's rail trips move 0.3-3.4%, its trips not at all.
- **CPU**: the two pools run at once. The pool is half the logical cores less one, so both fit; holding
  the main pool while the focus pool solves (`?perfTry=focusFirst`) made Tokyo settle 5% sooner and
  the load's final 37% later (94 s against 69 s), and the real save's final 169 s against 108 s.
- **Status tag**: "Trips here being recomputed…" while the zone's own solve runs (not the "N cities"
  form, though the whole solve is under way); nothing once settled. No new string.

- **Figures within about 3 s of the pack being open**: Tokyo's pack opened in the focus pool at
  3.85-3.90 s and its first round showed at 6.85 s (3.0 s after); Seoul's (downloaded again, opened
  in the focus pool) 1.4-1.7 s after the jump in all. Before, a zone jumped to after the whole solve
  had started had nothing until that solve's round 0 (T-1904: 38 s on this save with 2 workers).
- **Settled in a bounded time**: the zone's own solve on the pool's worker count, with nothing else
  in it, so its time does not grow with the save: Tokyo 28-32 s here on one worker (19 s coarse),
  Seoul 6-7 s, Busan under 1 s. The main pool shares the cores meanwhile; on this machine (the
  Indonesia build on 12-13 of 16 cores) holding it made Tokyo only 5% sooner.
- **The whole solve's final came sooner, not later** (69-77 s against 86-89 s; 103-108 against
  111-123 s): the main pool no longer stops its opening for a rough solve of the zone in view.
- **The lie, at the whole solve's final**: the zone's rail trips moved Tokyo -0.3%, Seoul -0.7 to
  -0.8%, Busan -3.4%, New York +0.3%; its trips 0.0% everywhere (the market's longer trips are not in
  a city's trips). A player sees the zone's figures change by that much once, when the card goes.

**Status tag.** The loading card stays until the whole solve's final, as before, and while it is up

card, settling, and the card's bar. The tag's rules for the focus pool apply if the card goes first
(a load whose card another rule ends) and are there for T-2049.

- The focus pool's own WebAssembly is small (Tokyo 139 MB on one solving worker and its aux, 177 MB
  on two); most of the 0.8-0.9 GB it adds on Tokyo Seoul Fukuoka Busan is its workers' fixed cost
  and the overlap: its peak comes at about 20 s, when the main pool starts the whole solve while the
  focus pool still runs. A second solving worker adds about 0.1 GB.
- On China the page's peak is at the whole solve's final, after the focus pool has ended, so it
  barely moves; the 7 GB is the main pool's, the same before.
- The pool was the main pool's size (five solving workers on Anita's machine): now at most two
  (`FOCUS_WORKERS`). One worker saves 0.1 GB and settles Tokyo 7-9 s later, so two stay.
