# T-108: Blueprint extensions preserve constructed endpoint service

Drawing an unused blueprint extension at a constructed terminus changed its stop position: service checked all attached ports, so the train moved from the dead-end platform's middle to the node. Running time and single-track section occupancy could change even though none of the blueprint was used by the running line.

Constructed running lines now classify terminal stop positions using constructed ports. Planned lines continue to use all planned ports, preserving their preview of intended station geometry. No geometry, construction-cost or save-format changes.

The focused native regression checks built single and double track with an unused double-track extension. It requires exactly unchanged stop offsets, free and capacity-adjusted profiles, round-trip times, fleet, delay, canonical capacity resources and rendered departures, including undo/redo. It also checks that a planned line along the extension previews the two-port station at its node, while the existing constructed line still stops at the platform middle.
