# T-1412 brainstorming: what I'd add or change (2026-10-26)

From the spec, demand-plain, the ui docs, TASKS (Someday too), your TODO's long-term list, the last
days of ARCHIVE, and a headless play of 8800: a new New York game with a Manhattan line, the panels,
then the real save. Screenshots are in `artifacts/trainworld/t1412/`. Sizes are agent time.
**Top 5 are marked [1] to [5].**

## Minute to minute

- **[1] Riders before you pay.** A planned line shows its estimated riders a day and fares a day
  before Construct, the way the fare panel now estimates a fare change (T-1300). Today a blueprint
  line counts for nothing until it is built (track-and-service.md 6.5), so the main decision of the
  game, where to spend billions, is made blind and checked after. A second solve with the blueprint
  counted as built, in the line panel and the Build tab's total. Days.
- **[2] A trouble list.** One list of what is wrong on the network: lines leaving riders on the
  platform, full trains, capacity tags, reversals (usually an accident), with a click that goes there.
  The real save has a dozen delay tags in Midtown alone ([21](artifacts/trainworld/t1412/21-real-traffic.png))
  and each line's alerts sit in its own panel; on a big network nobody opens 109 panels. A day.
- **Opening day.** When a line opens, its stations get a small ribbon for the first game day and its
  riders count up in its row, with T-1413's sounds. The payoff for a build is now a number changing
  somewhere. Hours.
- **Where people drive.** A view of the biggest car flows between 2 km zones as arcs, thickest where
  a train would win most. The bubbles say where people live and go; this says which pairs to join,
  and is how players of these games actually plan. Days.
- **One rider's trip.** Click a train's rider dots for one sampled trip: from, to, minutes by train
  against the car. A shortcut into the trip explorer; cute, and it explains the demand model by
  example. Hours to a day.

## Progress and goals

- **[3] Goals per city.** A short ladder in the Cities tab: first line, 10% share (the star, T-685),
  20%, 30%, 1M rides a day, a profitable week. Points unlock nothing today (SPEC 6), so after the
  first hour there is no next thing to aim for. Also the first step of campaign mode. A day.
- **Points: hide or give them a job.** "20 a trip plus 1 a passenger-km over the past 24 hours" is
  hard to feel. Once goals exist, keep points for the leaderboards only, out of the Cities table.
  Hours.
- **The world number.** Zoomed out the top bar reads "World 0.0032%"
  ([15](artifacts/trainworld/t1412/15-world-zoomed-out.png)), a share of a fixed 25 billion rides
  (T-1301). It reads as nothing happening. Train rides a day ("11.8M a day") would grow visibly.
  Hours. A change to your T-1301 pick, so yours to say.
- **[4] Build dates, then a timeline.** Stamp each piece of track and station with the game day it
  was built (saves keep no dates now), then: a playback of the network growing (your TODO), and
  train share per city over the whole game instead of Money's last 30 days. The dates come first
  and are cheap; playback is the fun part. Hours, then days.
- **A year's report.** On New Year in game, a card: riders, share change, best and worst line,
  money. Something to screenshot and share. Hours to a day.
- **Campaign mode** (your TODO): cities unlocked by goals, easy, medium and hard starts. After the
  goals ladder, with a design pass with you. Days.

## Looks or feels unfinished

- **[5] The first minute.** A first visit is a card with one button ([01](artifacts/trainworld/t1412/01-start-menu.png)),
  then an empty New York with "No lines yet" and the bubbles off ([03](artifacts/trainworld/t1412/03-new-game-empty.png)).
  Open a new game with Homes bubbles on (state, not text, so within your panels rule) and go
  straight to the new-game sheet when there are no saves. Hours.
- **Imported names.** Every real-network chip reads "S" and lines read "subway 4" (T-330); New
  Jersey stations come in capitals with "STATION" ("PORT IMPERIAL HBLR STATION",
  [21](artifacts/trainworld/t1412/21-real-traffic.png)). Tidy case and drop the suffix at import.
  Hours.
- **The line panel's Profit row** reads "$101M -$28.4M" (fares, then costs) with no net and no labels
  ([24](artifacts/trainworld/t1412/24-real-line.png)). Show the net, parts in the hover. Hours.
- **Sounds** are under way (T-1413); the game is otherwise silent but for them. Light ambient
  station noise when zoomed close could follow, off by default. Hours.

## Bigger features

- **A real network as a rival** (your TODO): start a city with its real network run by someone
  else, and win riders off it. Needs T-1305's shared corridors first. Days.
- **Buses** as a cheap, slow feeder line drawn on roads (your TODO; ferries are T-280): something to
  build when the cash is low, and the suburbs' walk problem (demand-plain "Why rail is hard to win
  in the suburbs") gets a player answer. Days, and a demand change, so a design for you first.
- **Sharing a game by link**, read only (M4, T-988 to T-990): leaderboard names could open the
  network behind them. Days.
- **Copy and paste blueprints between saves** (your TODO). A day.
- **Growth near good stations** (T-454, parked): homes and jobs creeping toward well-served stations
  over game years. The strongest sense of progress there is, and a demand change, so yours. Days.
- **Cut: flights you run and freight.** Both are their own games; I'd keep the effort on rail and
  on the five above.

## Why these five

The first two fix the loop itself: decide with a forecast, then see what needs fixing, on any size
of network. Goals and build dates give the hours after the first one a direction and a record. The
first minute is cheap and is where a new player decides whether to stay. All five reuse what is
built (T-1300's estimate, the line alerts, the Cities tab, the ledger), and none touches demand
rules.
