# T-1067: waiting floods (2026-10-23)

**Cause.** Not the change of service itself. Demand's crowding rounds price riders left behind
(extra wait capped at 60 minutes, three rounds averaged with the free-flow pass), but nothing holds
a line's riders to what its trains carry, so a busy corridor gets several times its capacity. The
boarding account then kept every rider on the platform until a train took them, and ran a second
day when anyone was still waiting at 03:00, so the queue grew all day, every day: the same
picture each game day ("clogs for days"). Raising service raised demand with it (437k to 1.36M a
day here), so the line stayed overloaded; the queue left over from the old service at the moment
of the change cleared within about three hours.

**Fix (boarding side only; demand unchanged).** In `simulate_carry`, as each train leaves a stop:
riders whom full trains have kept more than `GIVE_UP_S` (90 minutes; first built at 30, raised on
Anita's word as too aggressive for infrequent lines) past the first train they came for leave,
oldest first; then the platform side holds at most `WAITING_CAP` (5,000), the latest arrivals
turned away. Both are `RunAccount::gone`, taken out of the line's boardings (`tally`). The app's
drawn crowd stops at 5,000 between trains (`WAITING_CAP` in game/boarding.ts, `crowdAt` and the
renderer's copy). Fares still follow demand (T-1069).
