# T-1170: moving the draw anchor rebuilt every stroke

Measured with `checks/old/t1170-anchor.mjs` on the dev server, headless Chrome, one demand worker,
paused: a save imported, then the camera jumped at zoom 12 between cities far enough apart to move
the anchor; per jump the ms to the first frame drawn at the new anchor and the clock worker's
[state, buffers] ms for that state; then a track piece asked by the edit API the moment the camera
lands in a city (jump to drawn). Outputs: `artifacts/trainworld/t1170/`.

## Where the time went

An anchor move ran `forget(true)`, as a load does: the worker read every edge, node and line row
again (`stateRows.reset`), asked the track model for every edge's points and every line's samples
again and converted each point through lng/lat (`packLocal`), laid every stroke piece again
(`networkFromState` with nothing reusable), and posted the rows whole, so the main thread rebuilt
its station names, capacity markers and complexes from new row objects as well as uploading the
network (New York at 6x: names 263 ms and markers 118 ms over seven states). On Japan the worker's
part was 0.6-1.1 s of state and 0.6-3.7 s of buffers per move.

## What changed

- An anchor move keeps the rows and their posting (they hold no local units); only the drawn points
  and the network's posting are dropped (clock.worker.ts, the "anchor" message).
- The worker keeps every edge's and line's points as float64 mercator 0..1 (`edgeMerc`, `lineMerc`,
  geo.ts `toMerc01`), so a new anchor's points are a subtraction and a multiply each, bit for bit
  what `toLocal` gives, with no track model call.
- The stroke pieces in the network cache move by the anchor shift (network.ts `shiftNetCache`)
  instead of being laid again; station pieces are relative to their node and stay. The lie: a moved
  point is off its exact place by float32 rounding at its distance from the old anchor. With
  `?patch=shiftcheck` on Japan: up to 0.031-0.047 local units (3-5 cm) within 100 km of the new
  anchor, 0.125-0.25 anywhere; distances along strokes within 0.07. A move of more than 2^22 local
  units (about 3,000 km) and every 8th move lay everything exactly, so it never passes 12.5 cm near
  the view or adds up. `?patch=check` lays every move exactly (its T-1029 check compares with fresh
  builds).
