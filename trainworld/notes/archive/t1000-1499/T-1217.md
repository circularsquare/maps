# T-1217: a zone's bubble drawn blue at 0% rail (Flevoland)

**Cause.** Zoomed out, a zone's bubble is a red dot with a blue dot inside whose area is the train
share (T-901, `splitEdge` and the disc shader in `app/src/map/demandOverlay.ts`). T-901 gave the blue
dot a minimum: any train at all drew it at least 2 CSS px in radius, capped at 0.6 of the red's, and
any share under 0.05% was rounded up to 0.1%. Flevoland in Anita's game had a few rail trips (the
regional layer's pairs with Kop van Noord-Holland, which her Hoorn line serves), so its small
bubble (about 4-5 px) got a 2 px blue centre, a third or more of its area, while the hover's bars,
drawn by share, showed rail at 0%. The colour and the hover read the same figures; only the
minimum differed. Not stale data, not T-1047, not a different share.

**Repro** (scratchpad script, a new game, a 3-station line at Hoorn, zones opened by hand):
Amsterdam drew a blue centre for 192 rail trips of 6.5M (0.003%). Screens in
`artifacts/trainworld/t1217/`: `before-hoorn-ams.png` and `after-hoorn-ams.png`, likewise `-kop`
(0.36% rail: a speck after); `after-princeton.png`, `after-nara.png` (New York 0.008%, Kansai 0.03%:
no blue). Anita's own screenshot shows the Almere bubble red with a blue centre.

**Fix.** No minimum (Anita 2026-10-24: "we should not be setting a min size for blue"): the blue
radius is sqrt(share) times the red's, the share kept to 1/100,000, and a blue dot under half a pixel
tints its pixel by its size rather than by half. The decode in the shader no longer leans on a
0.001 offset (`floor((edge + 0.5) / 3)`), which the finer steps need. Walking and the other modes
had no minimum in the bubbles or the bars. The one size floor left is the unopened zone's white dot
(at least 2 px so its outline leaves some fill, T-976), which shows no mode.
