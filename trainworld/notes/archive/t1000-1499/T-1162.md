# T-1162 and T-1165: the first placement in a new city, and a bubble click in Guangzhou

Measured 2026-10-24 on a dev server (6126), headless Chrome (Playwright, own profile), 2 demand
workers. Scripts: the perf agent's scratchpad (`t1162.mjs`, `t1165.mjs`), not kept; outputs and
Chrome traces in `artifacts/trainworld/t1162/` and `t1165/` (the `.trace.json` files open in
DevTools' Performance panel).

## T-1165: a Homes bubble clicked in Guangzhou

Setup: a new game in Guangzhou, a line of five stations, demand final, Homes bubbles on, zoom 12.5,
four bubbles clicked one after the other (Escape between).

**The cause.** The commuters inspector's Inter-city trips list had a row for every place the
selection has trips with: about 1,750 places each way in China, 3,498 rows, each with four hover
texts formatted by `toLocaleString` (a new formatter per call). Drawing the panel was one 500-620 ms
main-thread task when the longer trips came in, and 220-270 ms again when the far end came in (the
inspector redrawn). The far end's own answer (`flows`, 2-5 ms in the worker) sat behind both. On the
way: `intercitySelectionAt` looked each place's zone up with `CITIES.find` (1,750 places x 4,900
zones, 45 ms a query), and the far end's `flows` query waited 100-150 ms in the worker behind the
market's selection sum (`icSel`, `selection_all_day` in WASM, 100-180 ms on both workers).

**Fixes.** The list shows each direction's 15 biggest places and one "other places" row summing
the rest, its hover the count (`ui/inspector/LongerTrips.tsx`, the station's list too); `exact`
and `plural` keep one `Intl.NumberFormat` each (`game/format.ts`); a place's zone from a map built
once (`demandClient.ts` `cityOfPlace`); a selection's longer-trips query waits for its far end's
answer, up to a second (`flowsPending`), so the destinations come first.

| click (ms after the press) | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| destinations drawn (far end summed and on the map) | 922-1,324 | 42-48 (the first click after the zone opened: 703) |
| longer trips panel and bubbles | (before the far end, 300-1,000) | 179-222 |
| main-thread long tasks | 504-623 + 222-274 ms | none (first click: 51-74 ms) |
| longest frame gap | 750-900 ms | 33 ms |

Left: `selection_all_day` itself, 100-180 ms a query on each solving worker in China (a sum over
every market place for the selection's squares): a Rust change, in notes/T-1156.md's list.

## T-1162: the first blueprint placement in a new city

Anita's report could not be reproduced as a one-second freeze. Tried (each with a trace of the
view, the pointer over the city, the first click, the piece placed, a station on it and Construct):
a new New York game going to Boston, Guangzhou (zoom 14 and zoom 9, where the realm is still hidden
and the placement shows it) and Foshan; the real New York save going to Tokyo and Guangzhou (both
zooms); a game started in Guangzhou; a game with a Guangzhou line and the Homes bubbles on going to
Foshan; the real-network snap on; a quick click 1.5 s after arriving. In every case the click to the
piece drawn was 21-38 ms in a new game and 118-165 ms with the real save, with no main-thread task
over 85 ms after the click, and no frame gap over 85 ms.

What was found on the way:
- **The page's first press** opened the AudioContext for the station sounds (`stationSound.ts`
  `arm`): 57-216 ms on the click itself, every session. Now the context opens at the first sound
  (after any press, as the browser allows); the first click's task is gone (66-216 ms to none).
- **Arriving in a city far from the last** moves the draw anchor, and the clock worker rebuilds the
  whole network's state and buffers (`sendState`, 356 ms in the worker on the real save) and the main
  thread takes it (63-82 ms). A click in that window waits for it. It grows with the network (T-1156:
  measured there), so on a big network this is the likeliest form of the freeze; T-1028 and T-1029
  are the fixes.
- **Chinese and Japanese labels**: MapLibre draws CJK glyphs in the page (`localIdeographFontFamily`,
  T-056), 150-260 ms of main thread the first time a city's labels come in. Not ours to split; a click
  then waits for it. Left as is.
- The station tool's hover and the first ghost were 23 ms at most.

If Anita sees it again, the useful facts are the city, the zoom, the network's size and whether the
clock was running; the check here is `t1162.mjs` (setups `new|real|start-<city>`, `ZOOM`, `SNAP`,
`LINE`, `BUBBLES`, `WPROF=clock.worker` for the clock worker's profile).
