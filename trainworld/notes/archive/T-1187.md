# T-1187: Penn Station-Emeryville snapped route refused again (done 2026-10-24)

Found by T-1135's run of `checks/t727-snap.mjs`: the snapped route from Penn Station to
Emeryville was refused with "Geom:ArcDoesNotFit" and "GroundOverWater"; every other route placed.

## Cause

Not the rail graph, the trace fit or T-817's design radii: the fitted corners (6,214 for 5,324 km)
fit cleanly, and a native trace on the same real line gives the same corners to the bit. The
fault was in `bridge::bridge_long`, which bridges a long route one stretch of water at a time,
each between the corners 4 km either side of it (a window). Two bugs, both there since T-727d, and
reached now because the US packs rebuilt on 2026-10-23 and 24 carry more water along the route
(more windows, more shore slivers); `bridge.rs`, `geom.rs` and `trace.rs` are unchanged since the
check last passed (58a75d4, 2026-10-22).

1. **ArcDoesNotFit (six corners).** Fitted alone, a window gives the corner next to each of its
   ends the whole end leg (an alignment's end vertex has no curve), where in the route that leg is
   shared with the route's own corner at the window's end. `bridge` then sets every radius it
   fitted, so that corner came back with a wider curve than the leg holds. With one such corner
   the whole route fails its fit, `stroke_pieces` cuts nothing, and the route goes in as one edge,
   which also reads every river on it as ground over water. Fix: the window's corners take the
   radii the whole route gives them before `bridge` sees them.
2. **GroundOverWater (five windows, Barr Lake by Denver and the Great Salt Lake among them).** `bridge`
   read the window's water every 12.5 m and missed shore slivers 5-13 m wide that the check of the
   finer samples (`held`, every 3.1 m) found, so the bridge was rejected and the window raised
   whole; where the window's legs were too long to hold a ramp clear of the water (5 corners over
   38 km on the plains by Denver) the raise did nothing and the route stayed on the ground. Fix:
   inside `bridge_long`, `bridge` reads the water as finely as `held` does (`bridge_in(.., fine)`).

Also: where even raising the window whole finds no leg for a ramp (water just past an end of the
route, as a click on the Allegheny bank at Pittsburgh station), the route is now refused as
"BridgeNoRoom" instead of left on the ground to fail as "GroundOverWater". The fallback is no
longer silent (`raised` returns None).

## Checks

- Native harness (scratch, removed): the captured request and the 42 zones' pack water masks
  through `add_pack_water`; preview refused before, accepted after, 6,179 pieces.
- `checks/t727-snap.mjs` on a dev server with the new WASM: every check passes, Penn
  Station-Emeryville 5,324.2 km, 6,214 corners, worst 24.8 m, $254B, placed and undone.
- Legs: Harrisburg-Pittsburgh (392 km), Toledo-Chicago (374 km) and Chicago-Denver (1,664 km)
  place. Pittsburgh station to Toledo is refused BridgeNoRoom: both clicks sit by a river (the
  Allegheny 350 m from the start, the Maumee at the end), which is the honest answer.
- New tests in `wasm/bridge_tests.rs`: `a_long_route_keeps_its_radii_beside_each_bridge`,
  `a_long_route_bridges_slivers_beside_a_river`, `a_long_route_from_a_river_bank_says_there_is_no_room`;
  each fails on the old `bridge.rs`.

## Left as it was

20 of the route's 59 windows still find no room for ramps and are raised whole, the T-755
behaviour (eight of them 310-590 km from Penn Station, in upstate New York).
