# T-1047: zones the player's track crosses are in play

Anita, 2026-10-23: "anywhere we've built track should also be fully loaded / active. not just stations."

## What "loaded / active" meant before

For a zone with a built station within reach (4.5 km of its footprint): its pack opened in every
demand worker and every solve covered it; it was in the game's opened list (so zoomed out it was a
bubble, not a T-976 dot); its realm was shown. A zone that track only passed through had none of
that: it was a dot zoomed out, its realm stayed dark when no station was in it, and it had figures
only while the view was on it (opened bare, T-325). Its demand was never different either way: with
no station in reach none of its people can board (T-149), so a solve gives it its no-rail figures.

## The rule now

`game/trackZones.ts` works out the zones each network state's track crosses (a point of the drawn
line every 1 km inside a zone's coarse outline). Built track: the zone joins the opened list, its
realm is shown, and demand opens it bare in the auxiliary worker in the background (one at a time,
nearest the view first, after the load's card, after any zone opening for the view), then lets it
close with its figures kept. Blueprint track (not switched off) shows its realms only, as blueprint
stations do. Crossed zones never join the solves.

## Measurements (dev server, 2 solving workers, headless Chrome)

Test save: three long lines built through the edit API in sandbox, 1,962 km of track, 9 stations,
30 edges: Tokyo - Nagoya - Osaka; Osaka - Okayama - Tokuyama; Beijing - Zhengzhou - Wuhan. Its
track crosses 43 zones; stations reach 8 (tyo ngo osa oky ymg bei zhe wuh), so 35 are track only
(szo shg hij, and 32 in Hebei, Henan and Hubei). Scripts: the session scratchpad (build-long.mjs,
measure.mjs, timing.mjs), not kept.

| | final | crossed zones with figures | solving workers | aux worker | market packs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (`?perfOff=track`) | 14.7 s, 18.6 s (two runs) | 0 of 35 | 442-444 MB each | 76-78 MB | 32 |
| after: crossed zones bare | 14.6 s, 17.5 s | 35 of 35, 1-3 s after the card | 441-445 MB | 84 MB | 32 |
| rejected: crossed zones in every worker and the solves | 33.2 s | 35 of 35 | 911 MB each | 113 MB | 60 |

Rail trips a day 11,977 in all three (the every-worker variant only adds the crossed zones' trips
to the network's total trips, 612M to 781M). With the default five solving workers the rejected
variant would cost about 2.3 GB more.

edges, 27,269 km): 73 ms from nothing, 8 ms per later state (each edge kept by its ends and length).
A first version tested every drawn point against whole outlines: 9.6 s, then 0.6 s.
