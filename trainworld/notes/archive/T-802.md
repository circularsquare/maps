# T-802: why the station faces looked angry

Anita 2026-10-21: "why are emojis angry, see in station". Faces: `map/stationLife.ts` (which face),
`map/faces.ts` (the drawing), `docs/ui-map.md` "Life at stations".

## What drew the red face

`boardedAt` gave the wait of the riders on the platform as the time since the previous train left
(the longest any rider has waited, about twice the average), plus the gap before that train when it
left even one rider behind. Red at 600 s or more. The crowd test and the long-wait test were one
rule, so the red face was both "crowded" (the sad drawing) and "long wait".

The drawing itself was sad by design (brows raised at the inner ends, round eyes, a frown, a tear)
but pink-red with a dark red rim; at 14 to 20 px the brows are two dark strokes and the colour is
what reads, and red is the angry colour.

## Counts before (one hour at 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 12, 15, 17, 18, 20, 22, weekday, every arrival at
every platform; script in the scratchpad, not kept)

| | arrivals | red | by headway |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real save (109 lines) | 241,777 | 62,963 (26%) | 10+ min: 62,024 of 65,257 red; 6-10 min: 846 of 91,724; 4-6 min: 93 of 38,176; under 4 min: 0 |
| One new line, Mumbai Western, 9 stops, 4 trains per direction | 1,424 | 360 (25%) | 6-10 min: 108 of 985 (all by the doubling, wait 1,002 s on a 504 s headway); 10+ min: 252 of 252 |

So a station with trains every 4 minutes was never red; the red came from real headways of 10
minutes or more (27% of the real save's arrivals, 15:00 on the small line was 82% red) and from the
doubling. Still, a 10 minute headway is a 5 minute average wait, which is yellow on the crowding
dot's scale (orange is 10 minutes, Anita's own numbers in the playtest of 2026-10-19), so red said
"bad" at what the rest of the game calls middling.

Live, downtown Manhattan view at zoom 14, 08:00 to 09:00: 756 arrivals drew faces, 76 red (10% of
faces were red; 9 of the 10 stations with any red had only red).

## Fix

- `boardedAt` returns the riders' average wait: newcomers half the time since the last train left,
  riders it left behind that plus half the gap before it. It also returns how many were left.
- Sad (blue): the train before left 20 or more behind (`CROWDED_LEFT`). Tired (drained yellow, heavy
  lids, flat mouth): average wait 10 minutes or more (`TIRED_WAIT_S`). Otherwise happy. Sad wins. No red.
- The sound's lower note plays for either unhappy mood, as it did for red.
- The Settings tooltip for Faces says so.

## Counts after (same hours)

| | arrivals | tired | sad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real save | 241,777 | 27,156 (11.2%) | 1,590 (0.7%) |
| Small line | 1,424 | 0 | 122 (8.6%; 43% at 08:00, the line's trains leave riders behind) |

Tired on the real save is all lines of 10+ minute headways (27,153 of 65,257 arrivals there). Live
view, downtown 08:00 to 09:00: 5 tired of 748 arrivals, no sad (red before: 76 of 756). The small line
at 08:00 in its first station views: 12 sad of 37.

The hour's first arrival at a platform has no earlier departure in the hour's data and counts as
happy (36,234 arrivals in the real save's eleven hours); it was the same before.
