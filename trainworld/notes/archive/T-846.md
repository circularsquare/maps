# T-846: smoke's "0% regional" on the real save

Smoke's real-save line counts the market's longer trips (`longer` purpose) as a share of all
boardings, rounded to a whole percent. It reads 0% because the real save's regional rail really is
small now (about 0.04% of boardings), not because a market pack fails to open or the figure is lost.
It changed on 2026-10-17 with T-636, not today. No fix.

## Cause

- Market packs are fine: the save opens and solves with `nyc`, `phl`, `pvd`, `nhv`, `hfd`, `nlc`
  and `lit` market packs (all within 30 km of a station or reached). `nyc` covers the whole metro
  (Long Island to Brookhaven, Newark, Jersey City, Yonkers), so LIRR, Metro-North and NJ Transit
  riders are local; regional riders are only those leaving the metro (New Haven, Litchfield,
  Philadelphia and the like), on a save with no intercity line.
- T-636 (10-17, 0a1c37e): the mode choice twice as sharp (0.05 to 0.10 a perceived minute, the
  market's longer trips and commutes between zones included), no rail constants, parking from 5.
  Where rail is slower than the car, a sharper choice cuts its share hard; the regional share went
  from about 1% to under 0.5%.
- T-718 (10-19): the regional layer's station pairs at their logsum; "regional rail falls a half to
  four fifths" (ARCHIVE), checked and kept in T-721/T-712 (the shortfall there was the plane's
  cost and the station's reach).

Both are deliberate model changes, so the low figure is the model as designed. Raising regional
rail would be a demand-model question (T-712's ideas), not a loading fix.
