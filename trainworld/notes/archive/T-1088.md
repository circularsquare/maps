# T-1088: T-1087's rule in the planner, US waves 3-4 replanned (2026-10-23)

The rule is in `world_plan.zone_groups` (docs/zones.md 2, "No ring zones" and "Commuter belts");
the belts are `world_plan.metro_belts`, from the ACS 2016-2020 county flows (`COMMUTE_FLOWS`).
`zones --countries` now computes without the plan lock and takes it only to re-read the plan and
splice in the country's planned rows (`splice`), refusing if any of them moved on meanwhile;
`--out <file>` writes a dry run instead.

## Against T-1087's dry-run logic

- Kept from the planner, which the dry run simplified: units under 100,000 by the longest border, the
  island and archipelago rules (Hawaii and Maui stay one zone), joins across water within 10 km.
- Dropped: the dry run's rescue of a small zone whose every join wraps by another region's neighbour.
  It put Kootenai with 27 Montana counties and Billings with 23 of Wyoming's; such a zone now joins
  anyway and the cut follows.
- Added: a cut's half whose every join wraps stays as it is (rejoining rebuilt the same ring every
  round, New Mexico's halves three times); a cut's halves count the zone's own water joins as holding
  them together (St. Mary's MD with the Northern Neck, across the Potomac).
- Fixed areas in the wrap test: every plan row of the country not being regrouped (metro rows, built
  and shipped zone rows; a pack's outline where one exists) and the country's packs from before the
  plan (the Northeast's 13). Their centre for a cut is their people centre.
