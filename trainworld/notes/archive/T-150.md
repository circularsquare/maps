## Delivered scope

NYC metro public-school attendance now combines the existing five-borough DOE
building data with the national NCES EDGE/CCD 2023-24 administrative school source
outside those boroughs. Added **3,199 schools / 1,849,674 enrolled pupils**. Preserved
**1,213 DOE buildings / 844,956 pupils** exactly. Combined enrollment is **2,694,630**;
100% of eligible NCES enrollment mapped into existing cells, with one 237.3 m snap.
The mixed count of DOE buildings and NCES schools is 4,412 records, not 4,412 distinct
physical buildings (the legacy metadata field remains `matched_buildings`).

The reusable `pipeline/education_sources.py` fetches cached official paginated data,
rejects duplicate NCES school IDs, filters operational status/virtual attendance and
missing enrollment, and accepts arbitrary city and exclusion polygons. It uses
MEMBER, excluding adult education. Distinct schools colocated at one site remain
distinct pupil counts; the five-borough polygon is excluded wholesale to prevent
national rows from duplicating DOE building totals. No invented gap-fill enrollment.

`pipeline/enrich_schools.py` retains its existing full-builder integration. It now
spreads child-home estimates throughout all 25 NYC metro counties, using full
five-digit county FIPS for fallback and the established CT state+tract join; gaps in
CT use metro share. Input child weight 3,873,639.621486; packed f32 cells sum in f64
to 3,873,639.622437 (under 0.001 difference). 1,261 blocks used fallback. Metadata's
f32 reduction reports 3,873,640.25, a sub-person rounding difference.

School source/year/coverage remains explicit in pack metadata and the detailed
audit (`data/work/nyc.schools.audit.json`). NCES query results cached under `data/raw`.
School arrays are replaced by compacting live arrays, removing orphaned bytes;
unchanged array contents are copied bit for bit. Pack remains 11,707,620 bytes.

The UI agent updated the purpose label to **School (metro public)** and the coverage
note. No runtime demand algorithm, purpose-array shape, worker enablement, attendance
factor, cutoff, or timing changed. NYC is still the only city with everyday/school
runtime enabled; this is reusable national-source infrastructure, not nationwide or
global playable attendance coverage.

## Research and limits

[Education-data research](../research/education-data.md) compares IPEDS, Wikidata,
ROR, ETER/EHESO and England's school register, with primary links and reuse caveats.
Wikidata offers a plausible semi-global enrollment+coordinate fallback under CC0,
but dates, count qualifiers and campus/system duplication must be handled. ROR is a
CC0 identity/location aid without enrollment, sometimes at city-level precision.
IPEDS is the strongest next US college layer: join directory and fall enrollment
by UNITID, subtract exclusively distance-education enrollment, and separate university
origins/timing from K12. University runtime is deliberately deferred to its own task.

Current public-school counts mix DOE's 2026-09-09 report with NCES's 2023-24 school
year. No private schools, universities, exact grade/catchment assignment, school
calendar, escorts, school-bus model, or student fares. Supplemental-virtual schools
use the general provisional attendance factor. Existing DOE missing/unmatched
buildings remain missing. Foreign-country importers and enabling further US city
packs remain follow-up work.
