# T-865: shift-drag to group-select demand bubbles for the trip explorer

Done 2026-10-21 (groupsel agent). Rules: docs/ui-demand-views.md "Bubble selections",
docs/ui-inspectors.md "Between groups"; calls in DECISIONS.md (T-865).

## Where it lives

- `map/commuterView.ts`: the Shift+drag box (a capture-phase mousedown on the map container turns
  MapLibre's `boxZoom` off before its own handler sees the press, since both listen on the canvas
  container and MapLibre registered first; back on at mouse up). `farIn` takes the far discs in a
  box; `farTrip(sel, idx[])` takes several far discs, and when either end has more than one bubble
  at the zoom on screen it fills `TripPlaces.group` (counts, names, up to 3 x 3 sample pairs).
- `game/trip.ts`: `TripPlaces.group`, `GROUP_SAMPLE_SIDE`, `averageTrips` (per period weighted
  means; routes merged by `routeLabel`, top 5, each keeping its own pair with the pair's own `from`
  and `to`); `TripPeriod.sampled` / `railSampled`.
- `map/tripView.ts` `explore`: after picking the purpose and kind from the representative pair,
  asks every sample with that purpose and averages; `build` draws a pair's walks from its own cells.
- `ui/inspector/TripInspector.tsx`: group sub line, averaged By train (Effective time, Busiest
  routes), the average named in the car hovers.
