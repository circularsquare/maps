## The model (as coded)

- **Memory** at n: the Memory row's reading (`liveBytes` at the running count n0) + (n - n0) x per worker:
  unsplit the largest solving worker's WASM + `MEM_WORKER` (0.15 GB); under `?split=1` `SPLIT_EXTRA` (0.2 GB).
  Checked on T-3373 A's settled rows: NYC +443/+502 MB a worker measured against 536 (WASM 382 + 154), all-China
  +1,428/+1,501 against 1,446. `LIVE_PER_EXTRA` (1.56 x WASM) would overshoot China by ~0.5 GB a worker. Under the
  split there is no whole-tab figure at two counts yet: needs private-bytes runs (memory doesn't depend on load,
  3 cores is enough).
- **Waits**: T(n) = T(n0) x r(n)/r(n0), r(n) = f + (1 - f) x busiest worker's share. Unsplit share from the real
  dealing (`periodsOfWorker`, `marketsOfWorkers`, `PERIOD_COST`, market 0.8): 1, .561, .387, .346, .226. Split: a
  fresh `Holding(n)` over the opened zones by trips, costliest first, plus the market: NYC 1, .527, .527, ...;
  all-China 1, .5, .4, .4, .4. Anchors: this game's own load (pick to card) and last edit; none, no figure (no
  seconds from `SOLVE_FIT`).
- **Fit** (scratchpad `coreslider/fit.mjs`, in-sample): unsplit f = 0.40: NYC A 1/2/3 within -5..+8%, China A
  within -9..+7%. Split f = 0.22: China stage 4 1/2/3/5 within -8..+4%, NYC 2 = 3 (95 s) exact. Edits f = 0.17
  from North America 296 -> 188 s (T-3868), one point, used for both modes; under the split unmeasured (stage 6 is
  being built). Known lie: `Holding.loads` counts the market even with intercity trips off.
