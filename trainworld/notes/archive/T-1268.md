# T-1268: changing onto the same line

Anita, 2026-10-25: a lollipop line in Hong Kong (out along a trunk from Tsuen Wan, round a loop
through Tuen Mun and Yuen Long, back along the trunk). A rider from one side of the loop to the
other, or to the trunk the long way round, might best change at the junction onto the same line.

## What it was

T-575's change nodes (`kernel::Search`) barred every departure of the line a rider arrived on, so a
change onto the same line never happened: a rider off a loop rode round it. A station whose only
line was the lollipop had no change node at all.

## What it is now

An arrival off route node `a` at station `v` is barred only the line's departures whose next stop
is the stop `a` came from (T-575's ride back) or the stop `a`'s train goes on to (staying on, in
effect, or swapping onto a twin train on the same track). Next and previous stops step past a
circle's stand at its own station, so a circle's train through its first stop is still "the train
goes on to". A line that passes `v` once has both its departures barred either way, so for every
ordinary line the change node is the same one as before (`WHOLE`); only a line passing a station
twice gets a change node per pair of stops. A walk from another station still bars the whole line.

Change nodes stay at most one per route node (per visit, the two directions share a pair), so the
heap bound holds. Costs are a normal change's (2 + wait + platform walk). Label-setting with
positive costs, so no cycles; the assignment and trip explorer follow change nodes' bits as before.

## Measured

- `a_lollipop_line_changes_onto_itself_at_its_junction` (kernel tests): trunk T0-T1-J, loop of 8
  stops, 12 tph, 2 min a stop. L1 to L8: 16.5 before (round the loop), 11.0 now (ride to J, change,
  one stop). As a 6 ending at J, L8 to T0: 22.5 before by the same reckoning, 13.0 now. Trips with
  a direct train are unchanged and board once; riders at T1 whose inbound trains are full still
  wait (23.25) rather than ride out to J and back in on the line's other visit (13).
- `a_circle_rides_the_short_way_through_its_first_stop`: its ring that is not a circle (trains
  turn back at stop 0) now pools the long way with a change at 0 onto the next run, 5 to 1 at
  9.5 instead of 10.5. Test updated.
- Real New York save (smoke, tree t1268): 13,014,633 rail trips a day, identical to master. None
  of its lines passes a station twice where it matters.
