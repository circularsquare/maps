## The choice (prepared 2026-10-17, nothing shipped)

The code is at today's colour. The change for each strength is one line in `modeMix`
(`game/palette.ts`), right after `if (all <= 0) return ...;`:

    train *= 1.5;   // weak
    train *= 2;     // mid
    train *= 3;     // strong

It counts each train trip as 1.5, 2 or 3 trips when blending the three mode colours. The blend is
rescaled to full brightness afterwards, so the factor only moves the hue. Nothing else changes: the
mode bars, hovers and sizes use the true counts. Today is `train *= 1`, so no line.

What a pure 30% train, 70% car bubble becomes (blend of train blue 30 and car red 70):

- today: pink red
- weak (1.5): a deeper, more magenta pink
- mid (2): magenta
- strong (3): purple, nearly even blue and red

A zone with no train at all stays red, and one with all train stays blue, at every strength.

Images, light theme (`../artifacts/trainworld/`): `t577-today-zoomedout.png`, `t577-weak-zoomedout.png`,
`t577-mid-zoomedout.png`, `t577-strong-zoomedout.png` (every zone bubble forced to 30% train and
70% car, not real data: no zone in the New York save has that share) and `t577-<name>-nyc.png`
(Manhattan and Hoboken cells close up from the real save, as it is).

Walking is green in a bubble's mix, and the bend multiplies only train, so it moves green-heavy cells
less than red-heavy ones.
