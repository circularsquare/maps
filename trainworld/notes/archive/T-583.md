# T-583: job lumps where a pack's signal is thin (with T-611)

2026-10-17. First the findings, then the rule that was built, then the results.

## What the lumps were

Blend in all three Nigerian packs: nres 0.2, osm 0.3, pop 0.5 (T-537). OSM is close to empty there,
and GHSL's non-residential layer is as well, so the top-cell failure comes from both parts and the
top-place failure from OSM alone.

The lumps are land-use polygons of ordinary size, not single places with big weights: with 100-500
units in all, one capped 1 km2 area (200 units) is 43% of the OSM part, which comes to 12.8% of the
jobs. Each OSM unit stands for 450-2,100 jobs (London and Toronto: 15-25).

## The rule (pipeline/tier0.py), for a pack whose jobs are the proxy alone

1. **A place cap.** In `signals`, each OSM place (an area's spread rows together, else one row) has
   its weight cut to at most `PLACE_JOBS_CAP` = 0.75% of all jobs, i.e. 0.0075 / osm blend of the
   pack's weight before capping (`osm_w0`, `osm_wcap` in the signals file). The OSM part is read
   against the weight before capping, so what the cap removes is not renormalised away.
2. **A cell cap.** In `job_shares(capped=True)`, a cell takes at most `CELL_JOBS_K` x sqrt(jobs /
   1000) = 500 x sqrt(thousand jobs) jobs through the nres and OSM parts.
3. What the two caps leave over goes to the population part, in proportion to people. The jobs source
   in the pack header ends with "N% of the jobs moved to population from places and cells over the
   caps" when it is 0.05% or more; the blend it names is still the one set. `osm_signal_line` applies
   the place cap too (when the signals file has `osm_w0`), so the gate's top place is the capped one.

Only `tier0.py` line `jobs = jobs_from(sig, J, job_blend, capped=True)` (the no-table case) uses it.
A module that spreads a table's jobs inside its units calls `jobs_from(sig, 1.0, ...)` unchanged, so
Toronto, London and every other table pack come out exactly as before. A signals file made before
the rule (no `osm_w0`) has no place cap, only the cell cap.

K = 500 is set from the real packs' densest cell over sqrt(thousand jobs): 100-575 across 38k to 18M
jobs (Birmingham 575, Liverpool 514, London 476, Tokyo 284, Belfast 283, Cardiff 290). The first
version capped a cell at 120 x the share of the mean cell; that is wrong because the cell count is
mostly empty outline (Belfast has 30,088 tier 0 cells and 8,325 pack cells) and it cut Belfast's
real centre by two thirds.

## The gate (checks/pack_gate.py)

`top_cell_jobs_share` over 1% is replaced, for proxy packs, by `top_cell_jobs_vs_size` (densest
cell's jobs over sqrt(thousand jobs)) failing over 600. Belfast's centre at 1.28% (6,229 jobs, 283
on this measure) is a plain city centre: real packs of 0.5-1.7M jobs reach 1.0-2.0% (Birmingham
1.68%, Liverpool 1.97%, Hartford 1.96%). The 1% was set when the only proxy packs were large
cities. Table packs keep their warn at 3%. `osm_top_place_jobs_share` over 1% stays.

## Results

Cap simulated on the cached signals (no rebuild) before the run: gla 205, lis 238, pot 194, lag 234,
mum 292, seo 147, bel 283 on the new measure, none moved by the rule (moved <= 0.1%); the Nigerian
packs 850, 2,641, 808 before and 501-512 after, with 16-24% of their jobs moved to population.
