# T-140 — NYC public-school attendance

Completed 2026-10-11. School attendance is the third NYC non-work purpose after errands and
social/leisure. Further facility/city coverage remains T-142.

## Inputs and coverage

- [DOE school point locations](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Education/School-Point-Locations/jfju-ynrr):
  cached 2024-08-28 shapefile, building identifiers and geographic coordinates.
- [Enrollment, capacity and utilization report](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Education/Enrollment-Capacity-And-Utilization-Reports/gkd7-3vk7):
  latest source date 2026-09-09. Deduplicate repeated building totals across organizations,
  require consistent totals, omit missing enrollment and unmatched positive buildings.
- [ACS 2023 B01001 age definitions](https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs5/groups/B01001.html)
  and the [official table-based summary file](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2023/table-based-SF/data/5YRData/acsdt5y2023-b01001.dat).
  Child weight is ages 5–17 plus 0.4 times under-five population, divided by tract population
  and applied to 2020 block residents. Only the five NYC borough counties contribute. Missing
  tract shares use county shares (430 blocks). Same land-area spread/water mask as other inputs.

Matched 1,213 buildings and 844,956 enrolled pupils, 97.8876% of positive source enrollment.
All matched buildings fall in existing H3 cells, without neighbor snapping. Child weights total
1,475,925.75. These are reported pupils at public-school buildings, not an exact K–12-only
population. No private/suburban school or university coverage is implied.

`pipeline/enrich_schools.py` appends optional `school_children` and `school_enrollment` f32
cell arrays and source/scope metadata, preserving existing arrays and H3 IDs. The NYC full
builder invokes it after gravity packing. Audit: `data/work/nyc.schools.audit.json`, including
individual matched buildings, omissions and spread checks. Read-only source downloads are
cached under `data/raw`.

## Model and presentation

Destination-constrained budget: enrollment × provisional 0.9 attendance factor on a typical
school day. Sixteen deterministic home samples per school cell use child weights and 2.5 km
zone-distance decay. School edges reverse the customer sampler's fixed endpoint, preserving
home-to-destination semantics. No observed student OD, grade/catchment assignment, school
calendar, escorts, school buses or student fares. Staff commuting remains Work.

Each outing has two independently routed legs, assigned to actual period service with shared
fares, loads and crowding. The existing 150 m cell-centre exclusion removes all modes without
replacement; exactly 150 m remains eligible. Existing 40–80 km local taper applies.

Outward shares: 94 / 4 / 1.5 / 0.4 / 0.1%; return: 1 / 82 / 15 / 1.8 / 0.2%, in
6–10 / 10–16 / 16–20 / 20–24 / 0–6. Afternoon dismissal mostly falls in 10–16, before the
evening commute peak. These schedules are provisional.

The worker exposes purpose names alongside selection arrays; `purpose_count()` supports
older packs with two everyday purposes and enriched packs with three. Each purpose retains
30 directional mode/period values plus 3n far-cell mode totals. Queries, bubbles and snapshots
sum every available purpose. The inspector adds **School (NYC public)** and an explicit
coverage/estimated-origin note, in both All modes and Train only scopes.
