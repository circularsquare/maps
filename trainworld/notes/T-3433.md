# T-3433 Kobe Airport demand offset (kobe, 2026-11-18)

Result: not a data bug. Nothing to rebuild.

Airport trips are placed by `pipeline/tier0.py airports()` -> `enrich_airports.terminals()` (OSM `aeroway=terminal`
footprints within 4 km of the OurAirports point, split by area) -> `place_sites()` / `cell_of()` (nearest pack cell,
limit SNAP_M 3 km). The OurAirports point is not used when terminals are mapped.

Kobe (RJBE), pack osa, read from data/packs (read only):
- OurAirports point 34.6328, 135.2240 (the airfield reference point, the coordinate in the report).
- OSM terminals: Terminal 1 building 34.6366, 135.2284 and Terminal 2 building 34.6377, 135.2256.
- Cells that hold airport_trips: 34.6378, 135.2277 (1,614/day) and 34.6369, 135.2246 (3,050/day). Each is about
  130-150 m from its terminal; 460 m and 650 m north-east of the OurAirports point.
- On 6195 (new Osaka game, Destinations, All purposes) the plane badge sits at 34.63697, 135.22459, on the terminal
  building. The red bubbles are on Terminal 1 and 2. What reads as "offset" is Positron's own airport icon and
  "Kobe Airport" label, drawn at the airfield point on the runway 470 m south (screenshot
  artifacts/trainworld/t3433_kobe.png).

Others (nearest airport_trips cell to the OurAirports point; they are all on terminal buildings, same rule):
KIX 712 m (Terminal 1 side; T2 cells at 1.4-1.6 km), Chubu 883 m, Haneda 420-480 m (three terminals), Hong Kong
242 m and 1.5 km, Incheon 1.4-2.1 km (T1, T2). The distance from the point is the terminal's, not a snap error: every
snap is under about 150 m because the islands have job cells under the terminals.

Only risk in the rule: an island airport with no pack cell within 3 km of its terminals gets "nothing placed" (a
WARNING in the run log), not a wrong place. Not seen for these six.
