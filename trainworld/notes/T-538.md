2026-10-16. `checks/pack_gate.py`, design in notes/T-535.md "Checks a pack must pass". Usage and
where it sits in a build: docs/adding-a-city.md 8; the code: docs/code-map.md.

## Checks and thresholds

Thresholds live in `THRESHOLDS` at the top of the script. FAIL parks the pack; WARN is listed in
the batch report. **A check FAILs only when it compares a pack with its own source**. Against
another source it only WARNs (the `_other` names), because the fleet showed the other source is
wrong as often as the pack: GHSL has Shizuoka at 2.0M against the census's 3.6M; TIGER's lakes sit
off OSM's.

## Moved from the design, and why

- **2 km zone over 5%**: 36 of 67 fail, all of them real city centres (London 7.7%, Boston 8.7%,
  Sendai 13%, New London 15% with Electric Boat and the casinos in LODES). Now FAIL over 20%, WARN over 10%.
- **Cell over 1% of jobs**: 22 fail. In the LODES and Japanese mesh packs these are real single
  employers (Yale New Haven 5.2% of its county). The 1% rule stays for tier 0 proxy jobs. An
  absolute 50,000 a cell applies to every pack, and catches the model's lumps whatever the
  pack's size.
- **Main piece 95% of people**: Nagasaki 0.88, Kagoshima 0.90, Okinawa 0.91, Kumamoto 0.94: real
  islands of whole prefectures. Zones skip it, and a city counts pieces within 20 km of its main
  one, so Hong Kong-like outlines are not parked.
- **OSM top place over 1%**: 23 fail, all with jobs from a table. The OSM signal there spreads a
  unit's jobs inside that unit, so the formula (OSM blend x the place's weight over the pack's)
  overstates it. The check now covers tier 0 proxy jobs only.
- **School rate floor 0.08**: Akita 0.077, Sorachi 0.070 (Japan's oldest prefectures). Now 0.06.
- **Pupils per school site** is read as the largest cell, which does not need the sites. It FAILs
  over 20,000 because Lagos's 155k and Mumbai's 55k are faults (below).

## The 67 packs (2026-10-16, after T-544)

62 ship, 5 park. Log `artifacts/trainworld/T-538-gate-all.log`, JSON per pack in
`artifacts/trainworld/pack_gate/`. The browser check passed on all 67 (about 25 s a pack, one
demand worker). 49 lines were built from OSM stations and 18 made up, mostly US counties and
small prefectures where stations are more than 4 km apart.

Parked, every one a real fault:

- **sha** 141,000 jobs in one cell (south-west Shanghai, 121.37 E 31.07 N; 2.8% of the OSM land signal in that cell), **mum**
  101,000 (Taloja, 4.2%), **seo** 65,000 (Namdong, Incheon, 4.4%): OSM land use rows cached before
  T-460's 1 km² cap. Task T-572.
- **ber** 62,000 jobs in one cell at Treptow; the 10th cell has 4,500. The weight is GHSL
  non-residential volume x height², from a few tall buildings. Task T-573.
- **lag** 155,000 pupils in one cell, **mum** 55,000: tier 0 sizes OSM school sites by the
  square root of site area with no cap, so a 10 km² polytechnic campus tagged `amenity=school`
  takes a city's share. Task T-574.

WARNs: four TIGER masks against OSM water (Litchfield 0.90, New Haven 0.93, Philadelphia 0.90,
Windham 0.83). The misses are named lakes such as Crystal Lake and West Branch Reservoir, where
TIGER's outline sits off OSM's. Shizuoka against GHSL 1.78: GHSL's 2020 100 m grid also has only
2.6M there, so GHSL undercounts. LODES single employers (New Haven, New London, Springfield,
Worcester). Six city-centre zones over 10%. Schools: Seoul 12,400 pupils in a cell; New York, São
Paulo and Shanghai under 7,000.

Two known issues are not caught. Shiga was re-enriched by T-548 before the final pass (largest
hospital 580 beds, cap 1,500; 201 on the old sizes, also a PASS). Lagos's Mumbai curve (T-545)
gives a mean commute of 8.7 km, inside 3-25: the gate bounds a curve, it does not choose one.

## Coverage on the existing packs

Water points ran on the 16 packs whose extract's water cache is still on disk. The rest SKIP:
their caches went after their batches (adding-a-city 11). The runner gates before clean up, so a
new pack always has its cache. Stations: 65 packs (Windham and Litchfield have none). Plan and FUA:
no existing pack has a plan row, and zones skip FUA. Other packs unchanged needs a snapshot taken
before a build, so the runner's job.
