# T-036: a faster commute pair loop and scatter

2026-10-15. `kernel::mode_choice` and the new `demand/lanes.rs`; WASM SIMD on for both modules
(`sim/.cargo/config.toml`).

## What changed

- An origin subzone first lists its pairs within the walk bound zone by zone (as before), then
  takes them eight at a time in `F8` lanes (two `F4`, so two independent chains run at once):
  logsum over the egress stations, drive, logit, and the spread of the rail trips onto egress
  stations, all in one pass (no `touched` list, no second read of each destination).
- Lanes run across pairs, not across a pair's egress stations, so every lane does the scalar
  expressions in their order; egress lists are padded to 6 with weight 0 (an exact +0). The yr
  sums keep the pairs' order lane by lane; rail trips by egress entry accumulate per destination
  in vectors (`acc_q`), the same adds in the same order. Results identical to the bit.
- Per origin zone, the decay row is filled only as far as its origins reach (`reach`).
- First try, four destinations of one zone per lane block: 31 ns a pair, since a zone visit
  holds 1.9 pairs on average (9.8M zone visits for 18.9M pairs a pass), so lanes sat half empty.
  Fixed-length scatter loops with pads cost more than the mispredicts they save (dropped).
