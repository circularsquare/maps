# T-276: zones and the intercity redesign

2026-10-13. Design: [docs/zones.md](../docs/zones.md). Anita's decisions: option 2 of
research/intercity-review.md (retune plus commutes between cities) inside a world of zones; Mercer
probably part of New York. Check outputs and logs are in `../artifacts/trainworld/t276-*`.

## Stages

1. **Mercer County in New York's pack.** `build_city.py` nyc gained 34021; rebuilt (95 minutes,
   nearly all of it Overpass answering 504 for the parks). 207,766 cells (202,330), 21.97M people,
   10.44M jobs, 6,407 gravity zones, 8.83M commutes. `read_pack.py nyc` OK. Hospital buildings for
   Mercer's hospitals came from the cached request, so they size by the pack's median beds.
2. **The market retuned** (docs/zones.md 5). NHTS 2017 trips by distance:
   `research/intercity/nhts_long.py` -> `nhts_long.json`; the fit `market_fit.py` -> `market_fit.json`
   (L went to infinity: a pure power 2.37; R 0.107; 17% leave the map). Outside world:
   `pipeline/outside.py` (Kontur r6, copied from religiondots' download into data/raw/kontur/).
3. **Commutes between packs** (docs/zones.md 4). `sim/src/bin/joint_gravity.rs`, run by
   `pipeline/intercity.py`; region-pair factors in `pipeline/borders.json`.
4. **Non-city zones and their bubbles.** Packs `wor`, `wnd`, `nlc`, `lit`; registry via
   `pipeline/zones.py`. The focus and nearby zones' bubbles were built by a sub-agent
   (app/src/game/zones.ts, map/zoneFocus.ts, commuterView.ts; DECISIONS 2026-10-13).
5. **The far-zoomed per-zone bubble**: intercityView.ts `renderCities`, local trips (the city view's,
   or the registry's `localTrips` for a zone not opened) plus the regional layer's.

The real network stops at New York's pack edge (T-007), so it carries almost no commutes between
packs. A test network of five commuter corridors across zone lines (`research/intercity/corridor_net.py`,
real stops, about today's run times and trains) gives these commute legs a day by rail: New Haven -
New York 1,278, Boston - Providence 1,302, Baltimore - Washington 1,080, New Haven - Hartford 876,
New York - Philadelphia 599 (none before T-276). Real lines carry several times that (MBTA
Providence Line about 19,000 a weekday, the whole line); the gap is park-and-ride, which the game
does not have (only stations within the fast walk serve a zone).

## With the four non-city zones (the shipped state)

`pipeline/zones.py` registered Worcester (`wor`, 851,808 people, 36,083 cells), Windham (`wnd`,
115,083), New London (`nlc`) and Litchfield (`lit`, 183,912) as market places 10-13. Windham and New
London's medical and parks steps failed on Overpass in the build and were rerun with
`enrich_medical.py` and `enrich_parks.py`; New London and Windham have no airport with scheduled
service. The joint gravity over 13 packs (25,593 zones, 993 s, max row error 1.6e-4): 19.41M
commuters, 1.51M (7.8%) across pack lines. Worcester - Boston 178,886 both ways, Worcester -
Springfield 35,041, Worcester - Providence 25,033; Litchfield keeps 17,026 of its 54,270 commuters,
Windham 13,765 of 34,304 (small zones send most of their commuters out). Market: 258,013 sites,
3,260 zones, 4.37M longer trips a day between zones and 0.76M leaving the map, 172,099 commute
pairs (1.50M commuters), 13.9 MB. Local trip legs a day with no rail (`localTrips`, for the
far-zoomed bubbles): Worcester 1.79M, New London 0.56M, Litchfield 0.34M, Windham 0.23M.

Review pairs with the 13 zones (pair_table.py): New York - Philadelphia 379,799 longer + 397,554
commute legs; New Haven - New York 93,363 + 244,729; New Haven - Hartford 24,980 + 249,283; Boston -
