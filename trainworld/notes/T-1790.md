# T-1790: the demand status and the speed cap after an edit

## What runs after an edit

1. The local solve (T-1733): the city populations the edit touched, solved through their rounds
   against everything else frozen. Its result is shown as a `local` cluster result.
2. An 8 s wait (`EDIT_SETTLE_MS`) for a further edit, then `whole`.
3. The whole solve: every dirty cluster in turn, market (longer trips) included, each through all
   its rounds; the solve's rounds all publish at once at the end, then the final.

The clock cap (T-1665) is `demandUpdating`, true from the edit to step 3's final.

## Why the status never passed 1/4, and why the cap outlived it

- `demandStep` counted the solve's published rounds (`publish`). After an edit (no barrier) the
  rounds only land together at the very end, so it sat at 0 of 4: "1/4" the whole time.
- The status showed only while `viewUpdating` (the zone in view still in `demandSolving`). The
  local result removes the touched zones from `demandSolving`, so the status went at the local
  result, while the cap held through the 8 s wait and the whole solve. On Anita's machine that is
  her "about 15 s" (8 s plus the whole solve).

## Fix

- After an edit the steps are the stages: "Updating demand 1/2…" during the local solve,
  "2/2…" from when every local result is shown to the final (wait included). With no local solve
  (none planned, `?perfOff=refine`, or a worker with none, which sends `whole` at once) it says
  "Updating demand…" with no count. Barrier solves keep counting rounds; the zone in view's
  cluster solved ahead now counts its own rounds too.
- The status shows while any solve runs (`demandUpdating`), not only while the zone in view is
  being solved; a zone already done or untouched keeps its split unfaded and says so in the hover.
- Every status hover ends "The clock runs at 4x at most until it's done." while capped.
- Demand that stopped (red alert) no longer caps the clock: nothing would ever lift it.
