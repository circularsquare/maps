# T-137 ? five more Northeast cities

Anita requested subagent-led city additions in batches of at most five, reusing established
sources with sensible extents and basic loading checks rather than separate calibration.
This batch adds Baltimore (`bal`, market 5), Providence (`pvd`, 6), New Haven (`nhv`, 7),
Hartford (`hfd`, 8) and Springfield (`spf`, 9). Earlier market IDs remain unchanged.

Footprints use the existing 2020 county geography, deliberately chosen for gameplay:
Baltimore includes Anne Arundel, Baltimore County/city, Carroll, Harford, Howard and Queen
Anne's. Providence includes all Rhode Island plus Bristol County MA. New Haven is New
Haven County; Hartford is Hartford, Middlesex and Tolland. Springfield includes Hampden,
Hampshire and Franklin (the broader Pioneer Valley). None overlap any other playable pack.
These are metro demand areas, not municipal limits or exact current statistical definitions.

All use the established Census 2020 blocks, LODES 2023 WAC/RAC (2022 fallback), ACS work-from-home
adjustment, 25 m TIGER water and existing zone gravity. No new city calibration or job relocation.
`pipeline/intercity.py` now consumes city IDs and place labels from the shared registry,
so future registered packs do not need another hardcoded market list. Mercer stays market 2.

All five builds and independent read-back checks pass; all playable counties are disjoint.
Build/read-back logs are
`T-137-<id>-build.log` / `T-137-<id>-check.log`; screenshots stay outside trainworld.

confirms the dynamic ten-market API (`T-137-market-check.json`). No Rust/model changes

`t137-browser.mjs` passes all 26 checks in an isolated Chrome session: all nine local
populations solve, all five new city navigation buttons load finite cells and visible selectable
homes, selected areas match their regional sites and have cross-city trips, and a constructed
6 km test line in each new city earns positive local riders (roughly 1.0?2.2k/day). New-city
station queries decode, the existing 109-line NYC save loads in its original frame and earns
