# T-3896: fastest train reads 856 km/h

Report: Bulest (Discord bug-reports, 2026-11-20, French UI): a 160 km/h double track stretch's hover in the Speed
view said "Train le plus rapide 856 km/h". Screenshot: notes/T-3896-report.png.

## Cause

The Speed view's train speed (`network.ts` `lineTrainSpeeds` / `speedAt`) reads each line's run profile out of
the phase tables at the window the profile meta gives (`first`, `count`). The clock worker lays the tables out
line by line (`StateRows.phases`, T-1540) and reads a line's table again only when the model lists the line as
changed (`take_changed_lines` / `peek_changed_lines`). The meta is read whole every state.

`timing::retime` (T-3346 stage 2, run after every edit that settles) clears and works out again the
train-by-train variants of every timed line (lines with passing waits or following). The variants are part of a
line's phase tables, but retime noted no line as changed. So an edit could change a timed line's tables (even
their length) without that line being listed: the page kept its old table, the layout went out of step with
the meta, and every line after it was read from the wrong place. A window that runs past its own profile into
the next one ends on that profile's first accelerating phases (s near 0); `speedAt` takes the last phase
starting before x and extrapolates v² + 2a(x - s) over the whole distance: 856 km/h is 237.8 m/s, 28 km past a
1.0 m/s² phase or 57 km past a 0.5 one, so any line of that length after the stale one can read it. Trains are
drawn from the same tables, so they would be misplaced too until the next whole read.

## How it was found

- Probes on the real NYC save, northeast, Tokyo day 77 and big240: no table out of step at load or after
  train count, headway, rows, fast and remove edits in the game (play.mjs, scratch `tables.js`, `over.js`).
- t1382-rows (the standing check of the line-by-line layout) runs on NYC, which has no timed lines. The same
  check on the Tokyo day 77 and big240 fixtures failed: Tokyo seed 5 (a node made flying: line 18's table
  changed in content), seed 12 (a station renamed: tables 4 phases longer than the model's, line 4 changed
  and unlisted), big240 seed 3 (a train count: 154 phases out). Snapshots around the read, the cost sums and
  the construct-all quote showed the edit itself changed the tables, not the trials.
- Reading the Speed view's way over the seed-12 layout: 262 km/h on line 18 (limit 160) against 160 from the
  whole read.
- In the game, the seed-12 edits replayed on Tokyo day 77 with the pre-fix WASM: tables laid out 11078 phases
  against 11007 counted, windows running from s 28.6 km back to 0 and ending on accelerating phases.

## Fix

- `timing::retime` keeps each line's variants from before it clears them and notes every line whose
  variants differ afterwards (`Service::note_changed`), the empty-key path too.
- Guard: `StateRows.phases(a, meta)` sums the meta's counts; tables laid out to another length are read whole
  (and logged as an error, so checks catch a new path).
- Test: `timing::tests::a_line_timed_otherwise_is_noted_changed` (fails without the fix).
- t1382-rows takes a `tokyo` setup (Tokyo day 77, which has timed lines; NYC has none), run on request:
  `node trainworld/checks/t1382-rows.mjs 12 10 tokyo`. Seeds 5, 9 and 12 fail on it without the fix.

## Checks

- t1382 tokyo 12x10 and nyc (default) PASS; Tokyo seeds 5, 9, 12 at 50 steps and big240 seed 3 (scratch
  copy with `SAVE=`) equal the model's whole read. (big240 seed 3 keeps 88% of rows, under t1382's 90% bar
  for NYC: the timed lines retime moves are now read again, as they must be.)
- In the game with the fix: the replay leaves the tables at the counted length; no guard error.
- agent_cargo test: 699 and 903 passed. Typecheck clean. Smoke PASS (29 checks).

## Shown where

- The impossible speed (262 km/h on a 160 line) came from the node harness reading the laid-out tables the
  Speed view's way. In the game the pre-fix replay put the tables out of step (11078 phases against 11007)
  but the hovered speeds still read at most 160 there (that edit likely did not rebuild the Speed buffers).
  856 itself was not reproduced; it fits the mechanism (28 to 57 km past an accelerating phase).
- `dirty_lines` (the other per-line list the page uses) only drops drawn line paths, which variants don't
  move: not affected.
