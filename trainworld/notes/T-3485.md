# T-3485: airport ground trips and rail

Agent airport, 2026-11-16. Investigation only; no code or data changed.

## What was measured

Each case is a fresh game (`?new=1`) on my own Vite, or the real New York save (T-007) for JFK and
Newark, one weekday solve before and after a rail link built with the edit API (12 trains an hour,
peak and midday, 6 at night; the default fare). Airport legs are the airport purpose's legs at the
terminal cells within 4 km of the airport (`cellModes('airport')`, both directions), plus, for
Istanbul, the regional layer's airport legs across the zone line (kind 4 of `part_summary`, read
from the workers' result messages, summed over the five periods). Script:
`scratchpad/airport/airport.mjs <lhr|hkg|ist|jfk|ewr|lhr2>` (not kept in the repo).

Links built:
- Heathrow: Liverpool Street, Tottenham Court Road, Paddington, Ealing Broadway, Hayes, T2/3, T5
  (Elizabeth-like). "+ Piccadilly": a second line King's Cross, Holborn, Piccadilly Circus, South
  Kensington, Hammersmith, Acton Town, Osterley, Hounslow West, Hatton Cross, T2/3.
- Hong Kong: Hong Kong, Kowloon, Tsing Yi, Airport (Airport Express-like).
- Istanbul: Gayrettepe, Kagithane, Kemerburgaz, Gokturk, Ihsaniye, Istanbul Airport (M11-like).
- JFK: AirTrain from Jamaica (Federal Circle, Terminal 4, Terminal 8), in the real save.
- Newark: AirTrain from the NJ Transit station (Terminals A and C), in the real save.

Hong Kong 21.8% to 21.6%; JFK 39.6% to 35.6%. A longer decay spreads trips over more of the city,
not more of them near the line.

## Causes

**1. The airport's hover and bubble miss trips across a zone line (Istanbul; plain bug).** Since
T-2896 (5) an airport whose zone is not its city's (Istanbul Airport in Arnavutkoy, Wuhan's in
Huangpi) sends the people outside its zone to it through the regional layer. Those trips are
solved and ride the player's line (Istanbul: 109.4k legs, 26.3k by rail with the M11-like line),
but nothing shows them as airport trips: the plane's hover and the Destinations bubble read the
zone's own everyday layer only (`cellModes('airport')`), and the selection's cross-zone blocks
leave airports out (`E_CROSS` in sim/src/demand/intercity/selection.rs and `CROSS_KINDS` in
app/src/workers/demandSums.ts are [0, 1, 2, 3, 5]). A player hovering Istanbul Airport sees
4,300 trips and 64 by train, which is issue 11's "a few thousand at most". T-3488 (Arnavutkoy into
Istanbul) fixes Istanbul alone. Fix for every airport: the airport's hover and its bubble add the
regional layer's airport legs ending at its zone, and kind 4 joins the selection's cross kinds.

**2. Airports near a border or a solve unit's edge lose ground trips (Hong Kong; plain bug).** The
pack keeps its GHSL share of an airport's outings and hands the rest (`outside_outings`) to the
neighbours, weighing the outside people at full weight; the draw over the neighbours then
multiplies them by the border factor and only reaches packs in the same unit, and the share
that does not land is dropped. At the 1,415 zones whose airports share a catchment with other zones,
1.86M of their 9.58M airport outings a day are outside the pack, and 0.60M of those (6% of all)
are dropped. The worst (outings a day lost, share of
the airport's total): Hong Kong 39.5k (54%), Luton 19.2k (77%), Chengdu Tianfu 12.8k (17%),
Zhuhai/Macau 10.7k (38%), Geneva 10.1k (42%), Singapore 9.9k (18%), Copenhagen 9.5k (21%),
EuroAirport Basel 8.8k (71%), Tel Aviv 8.1k (25%), Shenzhen 7.6k (8%), San Diego 6.5k (20%),
Luxembourg 3.3k (46%). Hong Kong's people across the Shenzhen border take 58% of HKG's
straight-line catchment; only 2.4k of their 41.8k outings land. Fix: weigh the outside people by
the same border factor (and only where the draw can reach) when the pack share is worked out, so
the pack keeps what its neighbours cannot take, still never over the published figure. Hong Kong
would go from ~66k to ~140k legs (0.45x to about 1x). Needs the airports step and cross_everyday
rerun for the affected packs (the next world rebuild).

**3. Mode choice does not hold airport rail down.** One line from the centre takes 17-23% of an
airport's trips (Heathrow, Hong Kong, Istanbul), the share real single services have (Airport
Express 23%, M11 at most ~20%). Riders near the line choose it strongly (61% within 3 km of
Charing Cross). Heathrow's real 42% needs the Tube network around the line, which a fresh game
does not have; with New York's real network the game is above the published shares (JFK 40% against
at most ~20%, Newark 31-39% against ~7%). No station access penalty, fare or time rule singles out
airports: they take the everyday rule. A rule for air passengers (luggage, taxis instead of
parking) would pull New York down further, so none is proposed.

**Not covered: trips from farther cities.** Issue 6 asks for airport trips from other cities
(Suzhou to Shanghai's airports, Seoul to Incheon). Ground trips past 40 km taper off and stop at
80 km; a city 100 km from a hub sends none. That is a new factor for Anita, not a bug.

## Proposed tasks (numbers above)

- Airport hover and bubble count trips from across the zone line (cause 1): sim selection's cross
  kinds and the app's `CROSS_KINDS` take kind 4; the airport hover adds the regional layer's
  airport legs. Istanbul's hover 4.3k -> ~114k legs. Plain bug, about 2-3 hours.
- Airport pack share weighed by the border factor (cause 2): pipeline airports step and
  cross_everyday, next rebuild; Hong Kong ~0.45x -> ~1x of its published ground trips, 0.6M
  outings a day back worldwide.
- For Anita: whether airports should draw ground trips from cities 80-150 km away (issue 6).
