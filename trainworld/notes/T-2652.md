# T-2652: leaf stations and single-line runs in the strategy search (2026-11-06)

Agent searchleaf, tree `.tw-trees/searchleaf`. Stage 1 of T-2642 (design and prototype: notes/T-2642.md).
Shipped (i'): leaf stations worked out after the queue, the route nodes at them stepped along the line. Not
shipped: (i''), the exit step (DECISIONS 2026-11-06).

## What changed

- `sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs`: `Search::new` finds the leaf stations once per graph (`leaf_ix`,
  `leaves`, their route nodes `lf_off`/`lf_rn`, `chain`); `Search::run` unchains the destination's route
  nodes for its own search, settles a chain route node through `chain_step` (its offer noted, the route nodes
  riding into it settled in turn while they and the one riding into them are at leaves), and after the queue
  `leaf_join` joins each leaf's offers sorted by the key the queue would have popped them with (cost bits,
  then the route node's id, or its boarding turn's id `n + r` where `rn_left > 0`), stopping at the first
  that comes after the station's own key; a reached leaf is settled last.
- `set_plain_search(bool)` (thread-local, read by `Search::new`): no leaves, the old search. Native only.
- Tests: `DestSearch::check_plain` (cfg(test)): with `TW_T2652_CHECK=1`, or the thread flag, every search is
  made again plain and compared: costs, next nodes, accepted departures, offers, change nodes to the bit, the
  settle order the same nodes and every node after its next. `kernel::tests::leaf_stations_leave_every_strategy_as_it_was`
  runs it on the synthetic network, a circle with and without its wrap, and two lines with a branch, each
  again with `rn_left` on every third route node, and compares the loads to the bit. `intercity/t2652.rs`:
  `t2652_search` (the interleaved search benchmark) and `t2652_ab` (the market's pass, every figure),
  `#[ignore]`d.
- `docs/demand-kernel.md` step 4: the leaf stations paragraph.

**No `?perfOff=` switch.** No app setting reaches the kernel's search without a new WASM call (farends'
`farChunks` added `set_far_chunks`), and the brief ruled out a WASM interface change.

## Measurements (busy machine, one core pinned; result lines in `../artifacts/trainworld/searchleaf/`)

**Japan searches** (`t2652_search`, `t2621/jp.net.json`, period 0; 8,476 destinations, two rounds, modes
taking turns every 64 destinations): plain 443.0G ticks, 547.4M pops; leaves 283.5G ticks, 250.5M pops:
**0.640 of the time, 1.56x**. Per destination 32,290 pops plain, 14,780 with leaves: over the pass's 8,394
destinations 271M -> **124.0M**. Graph: 9,253 stations, 7,483 leaves (80.9%); 20,640 route nodes, 14,966 at
leaves (72.5%). Every one of the 8,476 strategies the same (route costs, planned-wait entries, accepted
departures, next nodes, change nodes, the settle order as the same nodes).

The exit step, measured beside it in the same run: 0.623 of the time (1.60x), 219.0M pops (109.5M a round);
strategies the same, but a pass then moved 1-7 loads of 20,640 by one float rounding (worst 1.2e-7) and one
transfer count, against none with (i').

**Japan pass** (`t2652_ab`, one round, free flow then crowded at 3x): CPU plain 61.9 / 57.4 s, leaves 44.9 /
43.4 s (about 1.35x the whole pass). Every figure (all loads by kind for every population, rail, pair rail,
the far-zone table, transfers, fractions, corridors) the same to the bit but `Loads::pax_min` (one f64 total
summed in walk order): 7e-14 and 2.5e-14 relative.

**North America** (`t2606/na383.net.json`): 1,613 stations, 1,237 leaves (76.7%), 2,476 of 4,270 route
nodes at them. Searches 1.05x in ticks (three rounds; pops 6.7k -> 4.35k a search); the pass's CPU 2.07 s ->
1.84 s on average over three rounds of each (1.12x). All 1,572 strategies the same; every figure to the bit
but `pax_min` (up to 1.5e-13), free flow and crowded, every round. NA's searches are small (6.7k pops), so the
fixed per-search work (going over all 1,237 leaves after the queue) takes much of what the queue saves.

**Smoke in the tree** (WASM rebuilt, two cores): passes, real save 12,515,595 rail trips a day; the same tree
built with the plain search as default gives the same 12,515,595 (other trees record 12,515,594: the base,
not this change). A first run on a fully loaded machine timed out on the far new game and logged 126 console
errors; plain and a second leaf run both passed whole.

**Kernel and the rest of the tests' networks**: `cargo test --release --features demand --lib` with
`TW_T2652_CHECK=1` checked every search made by the suite (812 search objects on the tests' networks, every
destination of each): all the same. 540 passed.
