## Design

**What merges.** Demand already makes snapshot stations within 100 m one demand station ("places",
`merge_stations`, never two consecutive stops of one line). On top of that, a line's consecutive bus places
merge into one demand station while every two of them are within 300 m (between place centres) and every one is
served by the same lines: a run along one line, or along the lines that run together there. A place is eligible
when it is bus only (every stop there has a stretch of route, `st_bus`), is no BRT station, no tram stop, no
train station, its reverse stop is its own (T-3348) and its stops have no pass / board-only / alight-only mark
(T-4013). A place joins at most one run. Lines are taken in the network's order; a run starts only at a place no
earlier run took. A crossing line's place has other lines than its neighbours, so it never joins a run: every
line through a group stops at each of its places, the lines fold alike there, and a change at a group is at a
stop the lines share. No line is left with one stop (its groups are dissolved).

Why not every bus place within 300 m: on Detroit the 100 m merge has already joined the crossings (5,398 places
from 7,382 stops); a 300 m merge across lines adds only 7% more, and would give changes between lines 150-300 m
apart with no walk, where T-4079 deliberately has none. The gain is in consecutive stops along lines (median
spacing 282 m). Counts (demand stations / line stops; the snapshot's 7,573 and 9,798 stops):

| Rule | Detroit | Belgrade |
|---|---|---|
| 100 m places (today) | 5,398 / 7,573 | 2,146 / 9,798 |
| runs, any place | 3,625 / 5,353 | 1,666 / 6,963 |
| runs, same lines (built) | 3,897 / 5,714 | 1,783 / 7,546 |
| runs, single-line places only | 4,173 / 6,318 | 1,931 / 8,178 |

The first build took any place into a run; parallel lines through a group then stopped at different subsets of
its places and folded to different run times, and free-flow riders shifted between them (Belgrade: half the
lines by more than 6.5%). Same lines fixed that (below).

**Stable.** Groups are a pure function of the network as set: stop positions, the lines' stop order and the
line order. No ids. A reload gives the same groups as the edits did; an edit changes groups only along the
lines it touches. Nothing is saved. Each population computes them in its own frame (as the 100 m merge does);
a pair right at 300 m may group differently in two zones' frames, which nobody sees, since every figure goes
out on the real stops.

**Fold.** The demand line visits a group once: one route node per run (its consecutive stops collapsed; two
stops of one 100 m place in a row stay two, as today). A run's ride time is split half to the hop before and
half to the hop after (a rider boarding in the group boards on average mid-run). A group's platforms are at its
centre, so a change there walks nothing (the lines share its stops); walks to other stations are measured
between the real stops (shortest straight line, T-4079's 150 m and minute as before): measured between centres,
81 of Detroit's 118 walks between groups and their neighbours were lost. Per-stop and per-route-node inputs fold
to the runs: times and fare km (the same half split), span (the largest share), fare multipliers and zone flags
(the run's first), passed fare-zone stations (all the run's), crowding totals (seg: the segment leaving the run;
boardings and alightings: summed), transfer selections (any).

**Expand: every figure out is on the real stops.** Boardings, alightings, transfers in, the purpose parts and
the market's: split over the run's stops by each stop's catchment (population and jobs of the cells nearest that
stop among the run's, within 400 m; at least a quarter of the run's mean, so no stop reads empty; equal where a
population has no cells there, the market). Segment loads inside a run: rebuilt cumulatively from the split
boardings and alightings, so the load moves stop by stop between the run's in and out loads, with no jump and
no flat stretch. The crush share likewise. Fare extras: on the run's first node, so each line's total is exact.
Transfer pairs: both ends split by the runs' weights. A stop's riders (where they live and work): each cell's
riders shared among the group's stops by nearness (inverse square from 50 m), with that share of the walked and
rode figures and zones. The market's station
figures: scaled by the stop's share. The trip explorer: rides board at the run's stop nearest the trip's
start and leave at the one nearest its end.

**Not merged, not changed**: the network the player builds, draws and runs; the 100 m places the bus stop panel
shows (`busPlaces`); connectors (each stop's own 150 m, T-4079); rail, BRT and tram stations. A network with no
eligible run folds to nothing, so a rail-only save solves exactly as before.

**The lie**: riders reach a group anywhere along its stops' stretches and board at its middle; a rider whose
stop is at the run's end rides up to half the run (a minute or so) more or less than they would, and a ride
between two stops of one group (one or two stops, under 300 m) is no longer offered.

**Done when**: Detroit and Belgrade solve faster with less memory per worker (target: more than a third off);
bus riders by line within about 10% (most lines within 5%), rail trips within 1%; NYC's loads hash unchanged; a
clicked stop in a merged group shows its own riders and the line's load moves stop by stop.

## Every per-stop figure and how it is split

| Figure (where shown) | Source | Split |
|---|---|---|
| A stop's boardings, alightings, its lines' boardings there (stop and station panels, Stations tab, line stops strip) | route node `board`, `alight` (all three populations) | each run's figure by stop weight |
| Transfers at a stop (Transfers circles, the panel's transfers) | `transfers_in` | by stop weight |
| Origins and Total circles | boardings less transfers, boardings | follow the two above |
| Transfers view between lines of a complex | `transfers`, `transfers_day` triples | both ends by their runs' weights |
| A line's load along its stops (Traffic widths, line panel, crush) | `seg`, `load_of_crush` | out of a run as solved; inside it moved stop by stop with the split boardings |
| Purposes on a line (stacked bars) | everyday `part_loads`, market `commute_loads`, `lean_loads`, `everyday_loads`, `window_loads` | as loads, boardings and alightings |
| Riders waiting at a stop, buses leaving full (the clock's boarding account) | board, alight, arrive per route node | follow the boardings |
| Fare income by line | `fare_extra`, `base_extra` | by stop weight (each line's total exact) |
| A stop's riders: where they live and work, walked or rode, zones (station panel, Destinations) | `station_riders_day` | each cell's riders shared among the group's stops by nearness |
| A stop's longer trips (market station flows) | `station_flows_day` | the stop's share of its group |
| The trip explorer's stops, waits and rides (local and market) | route nodes in pairs and legs | boards at the run's stop nearest the rider, leaves at the one nearest where they go next |
| Connectors on the map, Change on foot list | the app's own geometry (`busPlaces`, 100 m; 150 m walks) | untouched |
| Wait at a stop in the explorer | the line's headway at the group | the same as the stop's |

real stops): branchriders (T-4064) works elsewhere in that file, on the change rules; settled (T-4093) is in
`stalls.rs`, not touched.

## Known residuals

- The trip explorer's access walk metres and ride minutes at a group are the group's (its centre, its half-run
  times), not the named stop's.
- No edit was run on a merged save; stability rests on the grouping being a pure function of the stops'
  positions and the lines' order (no ids), which a reload repeats.
- The T-4025 bench (`perf_bench.mjs`) was not used: its saves have no Detroit and it waits 600 s for a final, which
  Belgrade's load passes on this machine today.
