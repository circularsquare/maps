## Shared predicates (`app/src/map/visibility.ts`)

Four predicates, one per kind of thing, all covering hidden levels, hidden modes, and hidden blueprints (`main.ts` supplies `stationBuilt` and `nodeBuiltPorts` in `HiddenLookups`):
- `stationOff(id, built?)`
- `nodeOff(id, builtPorts?)`
- `edgeOff(e)`
- `lotOff(stationId, built?)`

`renderer.ts` uses `stationOff` in `hideStations` and `stationHidden` (falling back to live `stationOff` if `vis.v` changed before the next draw), `lotOff` in `lotHidden` / `lotAt` / `ParkingLots.setShown`, and skips `stationHidden(i)` in `selectedStationAt`.
