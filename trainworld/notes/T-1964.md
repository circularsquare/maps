# T-1964: stations across joints between pieces of track

Anita 2026-11-02: "its also annoying how we cant place stations that like cross between multiple
segments of track sometimes? seems to be finicky. ... could we have this be allowed?"

## What refused

The sweep (`sim/src/track/wasm/station_sweep_tests.rs`, ignored tests): a 200 m station asked of the
track model every 10 m (drawn network) or 100 m (real save) along every piece, each refusal keyed by
its reason and by the joints within half a platform and 2 m (walked through plain nodes, classified
as `unjoin_near` saw them). "Slid" is the nearest spot within half a platform that fits, tried only
for a refusal for room, as the station tool does.

- **Joints at a slight angle were never joined.** `free_joint` asked the joint to lie within 5 cm of
  the straight between the corners either side; pieces may meet within the heading tolerance
  (0.003 rad each way), so over a long leg a joint can sit a metre off. Those joints stayed,
  the platform reached them, and the answer was "The platform would reach the end of a piece of
  track" (or "Too close to the end of the track" within 5 m). The station tool's own guess
  (`joinThrough` in `map/stationSnap.ts`) joins them, so it never offered the slide either: a hard
  refusal at an invisible joint, the "finicky" case.
- A station clicked within 5 m of a joint that stays (built beside blueprint, single beside double)
  was refused as a split too near a node.
- A split on a ramp said "Too close to the end of the track, or on a ramp."; a join that moved a
  ramp under the platform added "Not enough room to change elevation".
- The page's join rule did not check high speed beside ordinary track (the worker does).

Real save (T-007-real-nyc.save, 44,978 samples): no plain joints at all (every node is a station or

the first 14,000 and was stopped there). Its refusals: another station's platform 6,150 (2,891 slide),
`CrossingOverlap` 2,050 and `Geom:RadiusTooSmall` 1,374 alone plus about 1,200 mixed (the
overlapping imported lines, T-691), flat crossings 499.

Browser (`checks/t1964-station-joints.mjs`): the station tool on every plain joint of two routes
drawn as the track tool draws them, 14 joints (10 blueprint, 3 built, 1 built beside blueprint): all
placed at the pointer, before and after (the check now also draws clicks a hair off straight ahead).
In the drawn sweep 3 of 116 joints were bent, 0.11 to 0.20 m off the straight, each where a click
almost straight ahead of a track end gave a straight piece (no corner) within the heading tolerance
of the end; those 3 joints gave the 58 refusals.
