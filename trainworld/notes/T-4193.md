# T-4193: bus zones' streets before the network on load (agent busload, 2026-11-24)

From T-4188 (the 13-minute open of Community save jwxidghd). Landed in tree busload; settlestop's T-4239 (the settle
stopping once lines hold nearly still) not in.

## The cause and the fix

A load put the network in the track model with no street speeds, settled it once, and only then fetched the street
speeds of the zones its bus routes meet (`masksForLoaded` -> `ensureStreets`). Each zone's `add_pack_streets` times
the buses in it again (`rework_buses`), and that runs `settle_train_counts` over every line: one whole settle per
bus zone. On jwxidghd (370 lines, 92 bus lines in 19 zones, a settle that never holds still so every one runs all 80
rounds) that was 20 settles, 762 of its 783 s.

Now `masksBeforeLoad` reads the bus routes' points from the save (`TrackApi.save_bus_points`, beside `save_points`:
no derive, the same points `bus_route` gives once loaded), works out their zones in the new-game frame as for the
masks, empties the model and fetches those zones' streets alongside the masks, waiting for them as the load did
before (T-2499). The load then times its buses on them in its one settle, and `masksForLoaded` finds nothing left
to add. It still catches a zone whose fetch failed (fetched again after the load, a settle each, as before).

Cousins checked by reading: every save reaches `openNetwork` converted to the new-game frame (clock.worker.ts), so
the other-frame early return is not met by players; a panic's reopen (clockPanic.ts) clears `streetsIn` and now takes
the same path (its fetches cached); streets landing for the view during the load go into the empty model or are
skipped, never into the last game's network.

## Figures (jwxidghd, before and after, in the game, trips calculated)

- Rail trips 17,201,856 -> 17,200,189 (-1,667, -0.0097%); all trips 1,137,276,325 both; bus riders 4,327,541 ->
  4,327,246 (-0.007%).
- Line rates right after the load: 355 of 370 lines identical to the bit, all 92 bus lines among them; 15 rail lines
  move, none over 1%: Commuter Semi-Express (Tsukin junkyu, 251) 1.698 -> 1.711 tph (0.77%), S-TRAIN (20) 0.46%, a
  local (Kakueki teisha, 16) 0.33%, then under 0.1%.
- Riders a day: 155 lines move, 5 over 1%, all in the Toyoko/Fukutoshin group T-4194 found circling: Toyoko Line
  Commuter Limited Express (18) 22,774 -> 21,828 (-4.2%), a local (16) 182,468 -> 178,219 (-2.3%), S-TRAIN (20)
  +2.1%, S-TRAIN (257) -1.9%, Toyoko Line F Liner (19) 261,962 -> 258,097 (-1.5%); then under 1%.
- The save's own rates (decoded, rows from slots 0, 3, 4): before and after alike, 336 of 370 lines end exactly at
  the save's (all 92 bus lines); the other 34 rail lines within 0.5% either way. Of the 15 lines that moved, 5 end
  nearer the save's rates and 10 a hair further; summed over them the distance falls (0.072 -> 0.044 tph).

Why they move: the old load settled without street speeds and then 19 more times, each from where the last stopped;
the new one settles once, from the save's rates, with them. Where the settle holds still both end at the same rates
(`streets_before_a_load_give_the_figures_of_streets_after_it`); where groups of lines circle without settling (Tokyo
trunks sharing full track, this save) each ends somewhere else on its circle. The worst network shape: many bus zones
over rail groups that circle. Neither end is truer than the other, so no DECISIONS entry.
