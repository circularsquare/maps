# T-102: delay indicators

Delay tags now use the station-name zoom cutoff (12) and breathing room, shared in
`map/labelLayout.ts`. A greedy placement pass keeps the greatest delay, then highest utilisation;
offscreen tags are hidden. Cached dimensions are measured together and reset after fonts load;
an unchanged camera skips placement. Tooltip clears when moving or hiding tags.

Validation: TypeScript and Vite production build pass. `notes/t102-check.mjs` loads the real New
York save: 80 resources, zero visible below zoom 12, 11 at zoom 12 and 13 at 12.5, no overlaps,
including an animated pan. The save has no busy junction, so a derived UI-only fixture checks
that a 2-minute junction delay wins against a colocated 10-second platform delay, its tooltip
explains the delay, clicking opens the junction inspector, and the display toggle restores tags.
