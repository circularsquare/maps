# T-3646: undo and redo put back the rates each edit moved

Agent fullrate, 2026-11-20. Tree `.tw-trees/fullrate`. Only `sim/src/track/world.rs` (and two tests in
`tests.rs`) changed.

## Goal, as stated

An undo of an edit gives every line's rates, trains, round trips, pacing and departures as they were before
the edit, to the bit; a redo gives what the edit gave. The load's cap lowering, if it came between, stays
(T-3528), on top of exactly that.

## Cause

Two, both found natively (`fullrate_probe`, scratch, on the shipped London and the T-3482 Tokyo save):

1. **An undo was a forward edit.** `undo` applied the inverse op and settled from the edited state. The settle
   is path dependent: held shares of full track keep the rate they came with (T-517, T-3655), lines outside the
   edit's sharing scope keep theirs to the bit (T-1863), a headway line switched in keeps its counts until some
   later settle steps it (T-752), and pacing sets keep their edges (T-3388). So the inverse does not retrace the
   edit. Reproduced natively with the T-3550 checker's sequence on London (hw 31 600/900/1200, count 4
   60/40/10, hw 17 200/300/1200, undo x3, redo x3): undo off by up to 20.84 s of round trip (line 125) and
   0.61 trains an hour (line 30), redo by up to 105.12 s (line 32) and 0.68 (line 28), the checker's figures.
2. **Removing a line left the lines it shared track with out of the settle.** The scope (T-1863) is the
   sharing closure on the capacity after the edit, where a removed line shares nothing. With trains, the
   rebuild named its neighbours anyway; with none (as a new line stands before it gets trains, or after its
   headway is undone) the scope was the line alone (seeds [150, 150], scope 1), so the lines it had cut kept
   their cut rates. Tokyo (ri1.save, T-3482's scene): line 150 removed and added back as the page does (no
   trains, fast, cars, headways 450/600/1800); undoing back to before the add left line 42 at 15.00 trains an
   hour at the peak instead of 23.12.

Why 15.00 with line 150 there but empty: line 42's peak bottleneck is a crossing whose users are [150, 42, 42],
at 100% with 150 running nothing. A line with no trains still makes a crossing that caps the lines it crosses
(for the orchestrator: likely a bug of its own, not touched here).

## Fix (world.rs)

- Each commit records, per line it moved, the line's settled state after it and before it (`LineRates`: weekday
  and weekend rates, pacing and what it was paced from, trains, phases and splits, its `settle_kept` entries),
  with the history entry (`undo` / `redo` are now `(Op, Rates)`). An undo or redo whose lines are all as its
  step left them (and `rest_solved` unchanged) puts the before state back (`put_rates`), rebuilds those lines'
  services, and does not settle; the departures are put back after the placing (`put_phases`). Otherwise it
  settles as any edit. The money refusals (`apply_priced`, `apply_unrecorded`, `step_paid`) and `trial_with`
  roll back the same way.
- The settle scope also takes the lines that shared a resource with what the edit changed before it
  (`was_sharing`), so removing a line (or moving it off their track) re-settles the lines it was cutting.

## Results

Native, `fullrate_probe` (scratch), agent profile:

- London (`data/packs/real/lon.save` network, 171 lines), the checker's sequence: every undo and redo equal to
  the state it returns to, every line (rates, round trips, trains, weekend rates) and the network's save bytes
  (`save::encode`, without the eras that grow with every edit). Master: undo up to 20.84 s and 0.61 trains an
  hour off, redo up to 105.12 s and 0.68.
- The same with weekends at 80% (`set_weekend_share_all(80)` first, so the weekend half is restored too):
  equal to the bit.
- Tokyo (ri1): the five edits of the add, undone to the load and redone, equal to the bit both ways; master
  stops at 15.00 for line 42 (and lines 43, 56, 128) after the undo.
- Removing a line with no trains (forward, no undo): line 42 back to 23.12 (master 15.00). Probe only: a
  corridor test could not show it (master passes it), since the crossing an empty line makes is what cuts
  line 42 there.
- Forward edits alone are unchanged: the dumps after 8 forward edits on London (both saves) and Tokyo are
  identical to master's, every line, rates and round trips at full precision.
- Undo and redo are faster: no settle (London 15-30 ms against master's 60-150 ms, but 95-145 ms for the
  undo of the 124-line headway edit, whose services are rebuilt).

In the page (tree WASM, my Vite on 6177, London `lon.save` after its first demand final so the load's cap
lowering came first; the T-3550 checker's `undo.js`): every undo and redo 0.000 s of round trip, no trains
needed or rates off, against the checker's 21 s and 105 s; export and import the same.

Edit times, native agent profile, master and this tree one after the other, two runs: edit_bench's 11 edits on
`lon_real.twt2` mean 61 / 74 ms (master) and 64 / 78 ms (tree); the checker's three edits 98-286 ms both; their
undos and redos 52-209 ms on master, 15-48 ms here but for the undo and redo of the 124-line headway edit
(97-147 ms).

Smoke on my Vite: 29 checks passed, the real save's demand final 12,930,959 rail trips a day (as master).

- An edit after an undo behaves as from the state before: on London `count 4 60 40 10; undo; hw 17 200 300
  1200` against `hw 17 200 300 1200` alone, every line identical (weekdays and with weekends at 80%); master

- The demand view is taken before the rates are put back, so the lines whose rates the undo moves are in the
  edit's `demand` list (asserted in the test).

Tests (`tests.rs`): `undo_and_redo_put_back_the_rates_each_edit_moved` (a new line, its trains, a headway

## The lie

None in what is shown: an undo now shows exactly what was there. The cost is memory: each history entry keeps
two states of every line its edit moved (a London headway edit moved 124 lines, at about 0.55 KB a state
about 140 KB an entry). Not measured in the page.

## Follow-up after the check (notes/T-3646-check.md)

The checker's miss (London, the first undo back to the load after waiting for the demand final: 13.6 s off,
8 lines left at the edited rates) was the load's cap lowering. `rest_solved` was false on every commit on
London (logged in the page). The page logged three edits off the history (18, 1 and 14 lines moved) arriving
after the first demand final, so during the checker's chain, underneath its edits; the undo then found other
rates than its edit left and settled instead of restoring.

- The cap lowering (`apply_off_history(.., true)`, from `set_train_counts_unrecorded`) now keeps its place on
  the undo stack (`Back { again }`). An undo takes back every such edit above its own entry exactly (newest
  first), undoes, and makes them again on top (T-3528: an undo takes back the player's edit, not the game's),
  except those setting a line the undone edit set, or all of them where the undone edit is more than line
  counts and headways. The redo takes back the ones made since its undo (the stack length it kept), redoes,
  and makes the whole list again on the state it was made on. The result merges all of it
  (`merge_results`), so demand hears about every line. They are never undone alone, and `can_undo` ignores
  them. A refused (money) undo or redo is taken back by the step the other way. The other edits off the
  history (a removed fare area's codes, a save's old names) are not kept: undo does not take them back
  (`new_stations_take_their_neighbours_fare_zone_and_a_removed_areas_codes_go` wants that), and an undo
  under one settles.
- So on the checker's London case, undoing back to the load gives the loaded game *with* the cap lowering on
  its lines, not the figures from before the lowering: those lines differ from the checker's S0 by the
  lowering itself, everything else is S0 to the bit.
- An edit that changes whether weekends are settled on their own (`rest_solved`) is now put back as well: the
  flag as before, and the weekend service worked out again at the next weekend settle, as after a load.
- Tests: `an_edit_off_the_history_stays_through_undo_and_redo` (edit, cap lowering, edit, then two undos
  equal to a world given only the cap lowering, to the bit, and two redos to the end; and an edit of the
  lowered line itself, whose undo gives the start) and
  `undo_puts_back_an_edit_that_set_weekends_apart` (an edit making weekends apart and one ending it, undone and
  redone to the bit). Both fail on master, as does the first test.
- Cash (native, London, money on, master and tree): 100,000 -> 78,510 for line 4's trains, unchanged by undo,
  redo, a line removal and its undo, in both builds. Undo has never refunded trains.

## Not covered

- An undo of the game's rows or hours (`Op::Rows`, `Op::Hours`) goes through the reslot, which works every
  line out again; it is not put back.
