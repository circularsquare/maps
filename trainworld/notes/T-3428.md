# T-3428: no demand at Shenzhen Bay Port and Tide Park (2026-11-16)

Report (feedback 1794824551-df3620d2, 2026-11-16): trips at the port and the park are 0, and the plot belongs
to neither Shenzhen nor Hong Kong SAR in the game.

## What is missing

About 6.0 km2 of land at the Houhai / Shekou reclamation (centre 113.934 E, 22.494 N, from 22.47 to 22.508 N,
east to the present shore at 113.95 E) is in no zone at all: no cells, so no homes, no jobs, no places, no
station catchment. Shenzhen Bay Port and the park lie in or on the edge of it. The nearest shz cell is 536 m
from the port. Measured: OSM coastline water polygons (data/raw/osmdata) minus the union of every shz, hon, dog,
zho, mcu and zhu unit (data/work/*.units.geojson). It is the only gap over 1.5 km2 near the Pearl River delta
packs besides two others of 3.2 km2 (113.808 E, 22.410 N, outside Shenzhen, Lantau side) and 1.0 km2.
Scripts and pictures: scratchpad shenzhen/ (gap.py, gap.png, units.png).

## Cause

shz is a plan row built by osm_units.py: each plan unit's polygon (geoBoundaries / OSM level-8 sub-districts
Yuehai and Zhaoshang) is clipped to land, holes are filled, then cut along other packs. The reclaimed land was
sea when those polygons were drawn, so they stop at the old shore. It is not a hole (it opens onto the sea, and
the fill covers only enclosed holes), and nothing adds land the polygons never covered. The code already
names the cousin: "a territory whole is Natural Earth's outline, which misses reclaimed land" (osm_units.py,
TERRITORY). Water mask is fine: the coastline polygons show this as land.

## Fix (made, 2026-11-16)

`osm_units.reclaimed` (called in `osm_units.build` after the plan-polygon fill, before the outline is formed): land within
3 km of the units that no unit covers, 0.1 to 50 km2, lying within 2.5 km of them and closed in by units and sea
(at most 10% of its edge on other land, so inland strips beside another country or pack stay out), joins the unit
it shares the longest edge with. Test: `pipeline/test_osm_units_reclaim.py` (3 cases). Rule in docs/world-run.md "A planned city".
Run on shz: +5.6 km2 outline; the same function on the new shz boundary finds 0 gaps.

## Gaps elsewhere

Scan of every pack boundary against the OSM sea (scratchpad shenzhen/scan3.py, using `osm_units.reclaimed`) is too slow to finish
in a session: about 10 to 80 s per coastal pack, 4,046 packs, and it stalls on some big boundaries. Partial result
(first ~30 packs only): Egypt's aai Suez area shows 6 to 21 km2 pieces beside the Suez Canal and lakes (these are inland
water, which the sea mask does not hold, so probably false), aak 1.4 km2 (82.93 E, 17.53 N, Kakinada, India), nothing else.
Known by method rather than scan: any pack whose units come from polygons older than a reclamation (Pearl River
delta, Shanghai Lingang, Tianjin Binhai, Hong Kong and Macau fills, Dubai, Singapore, Incheon, Osaka Bay) can have the
same gap. Next step: scan the coastal packs only with the sea clipped to a 3 km buffer first, add inland water
(`osm_water.inland`) to the mask, run on 3 cores for about 2 hours, and rebuild the rows it flags (about 20 minutes
and a join each; a Pearl River join took 68 minutes with a contended machine).
