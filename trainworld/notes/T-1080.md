- T-1080 [P2] A world run at home can fail extract reads on Windows' commit limit (74.5 GB here): each parallel GDAL reader in `osm_features` commits a 6 GB node index, so with a join, a big profile and browsers up, Wisconsin's and Nebraska's reads failed "Failed to open dataset" with 31 GB of RAM free (T-1078). Since T-1239 the index is sized to the extract (20 MB a MB of .pbf, 6 GB at most), so only big extracts still commit 6 GB a reader: check free commit before a read and wait, or read with fewer parallel readers when it is short.
