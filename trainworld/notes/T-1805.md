# T-1805: the placement lag Anita still feels (2026-10-29, agent t1805)

Anita (older 8800 build): the first blueprint segment joining constructed rail lags, later
segments do not; a branch off a blueprint lags too. Her TODO: constructing track and placing
blueprints should not recalc.

Measuring tools: `checks/t1805-placement.mjs [url] [save | fresh]` (headless, track tool, keep
placing on; per pick a run of three segments off a constructed edge mid-way, then three off the
middle of that run's blueprint; each click to the next ghost, the clock worker's ms, whether a
network snapshot went to demand and a solve started; `TIMELINE=1` every message both ways after a
click, `PROFILE=1` the main thread's self time). Natively `sim/src/bin/branch_bench.rs` with
`TW_ADD=1 TW_WHY=1` prints each placement's demand-dirty lines.

## Causes

1. **A branch off constructed track sent demand a new network** (every save). Splitting the
   built edge beside a station turned the station's heading round by pi (`node_heading` follows
   the node's first edge end); the clock worker's built-stations key (`stationsKey`, T-1368)
   read that as a station change and scheduled a snapshot, so demand solved the whole network
   again, with the clock capped and lines "constructing..." meanwhile. Fix: the key takes the
   heading as an axis (mod pi), as the complexes do.
2. **Blueprint crossing constructed track loaded the constructed lines** (`capacity.rs`
   `build_crossings`): a flat crossing made a resource for the lines on one edge even when no
   train runs on the other, and with two or more lines on that edge the queueing term (mix > 0)
   gave it a delay. A blueprint segment at grade across a shared line therefore moved that line's
   holds, the settle stepped every line on shared track, and 20-45 lines went to demand (shared
   New York: 7-27 lines a placement in the browser, 50-170 ms of boarding account and a solve).
   Fix: a crossing no running line uses the other way makes no resource.
3. **A branch splitting a constructed shared edge cut its track section** (`build_sections`):
   sections join only through two-port nodes, so the branch's new junction split the section
   there, re-timing the lines on it past demand's 5 s. Fix: a node joins sections when two of its
   ports carry running lines, whatever blueprint or unused constructed track hangs off it.
4. **The edit's settle sent every line it touched to demand**, however little it moved: on shared
   track a settle re-times neighbours by thousandths of a train an hour. Fix: a line the settle
   moved goes to demand only when its trains change, a rate moves by 0.01 tph
   (`DEMAND_TPH_EPS`) or a stop-to-stop time by 5 s; it is still redrawn.
5. **A branch off constructed track redid the boarding account** (`clock.worker.ts`): the junction
   it cuts in is paid for, and a paid route sent trips (`board` 17-54 ms on T-007, 100-200 ms on
   shared track), which bumped the account and set the panels re-asking line accounts (three
   ~40 ms queries ahead of the next preview). A route op now sends trips only when lines change for
   demand.
