# T-3593 and T-3594 (smallui)

## T-3593 Show all
- Settings' "Show levels" row: a small "Show all" button (btn text mini) right of the label, in a `.level-head` row
  fixed at 24 px. It is `visibility: hidden` (not removed) while no level is hidden, so the toggles keep their place
  (measured: row top and height identical with and without the button, 206.3 / 50.4 px).
- `showAllLevels()` in game/lineVisibility.ts clears `hiddenLevels`. Key `settingsTab.showAllLevels` (en + context only).
- Shots: artifacts/trainworld/review/t3593.png (none hidden above, +2 hidden below).

## T-3594 ring thickness
- `BulletText.stem` = ink width of a bold "I" in the page font at the bullet's font size (ui/bullet.ts); the outline
  ring is that. All shapes pass through `bulletDraw`, so map tags, panels, the schematic and the picker follow.
  ShapeIcon (12 px picker icons) measures "I" at 12 px.
- Values: 1.6 px at 12 and 16 px, 2.4 px at 30 px (was 2.8 at all sizes). A line group's border is 2 px at all sizes.
  So the ring is thinner than a group's border at panel and map sizes and 0.4 px thicker at 30 px; Anita's premise
  that it comes out slightly thicker holds only for the large bullet. Not floored; her call.
- Shot: artifacts/trainworld/review/t3594.png (3x, before/after rows at 12, 16, 30 px with a group pill each).
