## T-3798: sort by type

  The Stations tab is unchanged (no `beside` there, so the old markup).

## T-3423: groups in groups

### Save shape (advisor consulted before choosing)

Each line keeps its own group as before (`group`, `group_name`, `group_look`) and now also `LineData::outer`: the
groups its group sits in, nearest first, each with name and bullet. Chosen over a group table because a group
holding only groups has no line to carry its name otherwise, and the chain on the line keeps undo, copy and delete
working through the existing `Op::Line` with no new undo op. Cost: a group's name and bullet are repeated on every
line below it; `edit_group` rewrites them all in one edit.

Save: a new trailing block `LGN1` after `LFD1`, written only when some group sits in another: count, then per such
group its number, the outer group's number, and the outer group's name, bullet text and colour (as `LGL1`). Level 29
-> 30, `BLOCKS` 33 -> 34. Decoding walks each line's parents and stops at a missing or repeated one. Older saves load
unchanged (no block: nothing nested), and a save cut before `LGN1` loads the groups lines sit straight in.

### Rules (sim `wasm/lines.rs`)

- `set_group_members(lines, groups, group, parent)`: lines and groups straight into `group` (0: none); a group no
  line is in yet is made inside `parent`. Refused for a group put in itself or a group inside it.
  `set_line_group(lines, group)` is it for lines alone.
- After every group edit and line delete, `settle_groups`: a group with fewer than two members (lines straight in
  it plus groups straight under it) is dropped from every chain, so its one member moves up to the group around it.
  That is the "delete a group keeps its contents one level up" rule; there is no delete-group control, today or now.
- `new_line_group` looks at every level.

### Interaction (Lines tab)

Extended, not new: a group's row now drags exactly as a line's (it used to go only into gaps).
- Onto a group's row: join that group. Onto a line's row in a group: join that line's group. Onto a line's row at
  the dragged row's own level (both top level, or both straight in one group): a new group of the two there. That
  last case used to do nothing for two lines in one group; it is how a group gets made inside a group.
- Into a gap: the gap's group, as before (the gap under a group's last row counts as inside for its own members).
- Never into itself: dropping a group on a row inside it shows nothing.
- Indent: 24 px for the first level as before, 18 px more each level after. Closing a group hides everything in it.
- Leave group (line panel) now moves out one level, into the group around; the group view has the same "Part of
  [bullet] group" row for a group in another, with Leave group.
- A group's figures, bullet colour, hover, the group view's diagram, the schedule chart and the map highlight all
  use every line in it at any depth (`lineGroups` returns those). Name and bullet come from the group's own entry
  (`groupMeta`), not the oldest line's own group.
