# T-3321: double track back to single, and the track-count icons

Anita, 2026-11-12, on T-3296: "i guess maybe we shuold also allow rebuilding double track to single track, maybe
that direction can be free. the buttons to do double track or single track should be same as for placing new track,
just the little 1 ilne or 2 lines". Built by agent dbl2 in `.tw-trees/dbltrack` on top of T-3296 (not landed then).

## What it does

- The Select tool's bar shows the track tool's 1-line / 2-line icons (`TrackCountButtons` in `ui/widgets.tsx`, now
  also used by the track bar, which passes its quad button as a child) while the pick holds rail track that is not a
  crossover. The icon for what the pick's rail track is now is on; neither when it holds both.
- Blueprint: picking the other icon changes every piece that can, at once, free, one undo step
  (`TrackApi::set_edges_tracks`, which replaces T-3296's `set_edges_double`; worker op `edgesTracks`). The notice adds
  why pieces stayed when the track model names a reason (the edit's answer carries the issues when it skipped some).
- Built: the icon is a choice (`rebuildTracks`, 1 | 2 | null; picking the pick's own count drops it), Rebuild spends.
  `rebuild_pieces` / `rebuild_quote` take `tracks` 0 keep, 1 single, 2 double. Single is free: `rebuild_plan` returns
  a `priced` list leaving out pieces whose only change is the count going down, and stations on them are not charged.
  With a level or type change too, that change is paid at the single track's price.
- The quote query's answer now carries the refusal kinds after the quote (`rebuildQuoteAnswer` in the clock worker),
  and the Rebuild hover lists the `NoSingle:*` reasons under "N of M pieces can't change". Other refusal reasons are
  not added to the hover (unchanged behaviour for level and type).

## Refusal cases (`single_refusal` in `sim/src/track/wasm/rebuild.rs`, `Issue::NoSingle { edge, why }`)

| why | when | text |
|---|---|---|
| HighSpeed | high-speed track, unless Regular is picked in the same rebuild | toolBar.singleHsr (existing) |
| Tram | tram or street tram track | issues.singleTram |
| Crossover | a crossover edge at either end node | issues.singleCrossover |
| Flyover | a flying junction (`node_flying`, 3+ ends) at either end | issues.singleFlyover |
| OneWay | a single piece on the other side of an end node, joined (`join_away`) and running one way (`edge_way` != 0) | issues.singleOneWay |

Quad and passing-track refusals were dropped by T-3339 (notes/T-3339.md). Allowed on purpose: a two-way single piece joined to the track (a branch to a terminus). Its join stays stored,
ignored while the main is single (`join_applies` wants double on the other side) and counted again if the main is
made double again (tested). A crossover between a double piece's own two tracks does not exist in the model (a
crossover joins two separate pieces), so there is no "safe removal" case.

A piece refused for its count is now refused whole: it keeps its level and type too, also for T-3296's direction
(before, with a level picked, a one-way single piece took the level and stayed single, uncounted).

Running trains: no rule of its own. The rebuild's (`before_edit` / `settle`): lines keep running and the timetable
takes the shared `SingleTrack` section at once. Trains the lines then need are bought as in any rebuild.
