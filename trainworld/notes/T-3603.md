# T-3603: weekend headway edits flash a decimal (agent weekendedit, 2026-11-17)

## Cause

The weekends' headway cell (`WeekendCells`, ui/inspector/schedule.tsx) showed the sim's settled rate (`restTph`) only
when the count shown equalled the count the sim ran. An edit sends the new count at once (`pendingWeekend`), so for
the moments until the sim answered the count differed and the cell fell back to the round trip over the count
(`n * 3600 / trip`): 4.14, 5.52, 8.29, 16.57 min. The weekday row never does this: it shows the stored stepped headway.
A second decimal source, found by the check: after the sim answered, a headway line at a weekend percent showed the
settled rate (4 trains, 4.14 min) where the weekday row shows its stored 4 min.

## Fix (app only, no sim change)

`restTphShown` (game/world.ts) is the one read for a weekend row's rate, also behind `shownRestHeadway`. On a headway
line it is the stepped headway the weekends are asked to run: the hand-set one (`WeekendShown.headways`) or the weekday
headway at the percent (`weekendHeadwayAt`, mirror of sim `weekend_headway`, whole minutes from a minute up). Count
lines and a line with no headway keep the settled rate / count over round trip. Pending weekday edits that weekends
follow are covered because the weekday headway is read through `headwayShown` (pending included).

## Check

`checks/t3603-weekend-flash.mjs` (about 4 minutes, from the tree root, Vite on 6201): builds a 3-stop headway line,
opens the timetables window, samples the dialog text every animation frame (about 4,200 frames) while pressing weekend
headway steps (longer x3, shorter x5, all 3 levels), the weekend percent (5 steps) and weekday headway steps (all
levels). Result: no "N.NN min" text ever. Control on the unfixed code: FAILS with 4.14, 5.52, 8.29, 16.57 min seen.
Hand-set weekend counts have no control on a headway line (the steppers are headway), so only share and headway edits
are driven. Count lines show count over round trip by design, weekday and weekend alike (one decimal).

Typecheck clean. No sim change, so no cargo. Smoke: see the report. No changelog item: the weekend schedule is
unpublished (c094, T-3521).
