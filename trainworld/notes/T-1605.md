# T-1605: why a cold load's figure signature can differ between runs (2026-10-28)

## For Anita

- **Two separate causes on all-China, one fixed.**
- **The big one (fixed):** on a big load the browser sometimes refuses one of the hundreds of
  requests going out at once (`net::ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES`). City and market packs are tried
  again, but the routed drives file (`market-drives.bin`) was read as *missing* on any failure, so
  the whole session ran on the rule's drives instead of the routed ones: another market. On
  all-China that cut the market's rail 11% (1,016,054 to 902,183 a day) and its commutes between
  zones' rail 14%, moved every line (one line 638,621 to 621,137 riders a day, 12,405 line figures
  off by more than 1%) and stations (one 21,880 to 17,582 boardings), and moved corridors' rail by
  up to 43%. Rail in all 41,731,634 to 41,629,999 (-0.24%). **A player could see it**: the same
  save gave visibly different line and station figures from one load to the next, about 1 load
  in 12 here. Now a failed download of the drives is tried again (0.5, 1.5, 4, 10 s) and then
  fails like a market pack does; only a 404 still means "no drives file".
- **The small one (not pinned):** 1 of about 26 loads moved one line's commute figures by 0.0016
  riders a day (line 118, western Liaoning, stations 2330-2331, all in coarse zones). Nothing a
  player could see. The best lead is below.
- T-1354 (the corridor table depending on the order market packs arrive) is not the cause: on a
  cold load all 370 market packs reach each worker in one message, so the first composition has
  them all, and the corridor table was identical in every run except where the drives differed.
- Worker count is not it either: 1 and 3 solving workers gave the same signature (eeaf56d3).

**Pinning the big one.** The only request outside city packs that failed in cn-o-1 and in no
eeaf56d3 run was `packs/market-drives.bin` (and `market-rows.json`, which is retried). Market trips
and the corridor table's rows were equal, its car and plane shares (the table's key) and the
market's rail not: the drives' signature. `marketRule.ts` `fetchMarketDrives` caught the failure as
null ("file missing", every drive the rule's) and kept that promise for the session. With the fix
and the drives request made to fail twice (`DRIVES=abort2` in the scratch `sig.mjs`), both loads
retried and gave eeaf56d3.

**The small one, what is known.** One commute pair on line 118: +0.0016 boardings at station 2330,
+0.0016 alightings at 2331, the same 0.0016 on each segment between, in the work purpose only; city
and market figures equal. Every load closes about 40 reached zones at ~13 s and reopens them a
second later (the client's `sweep` running while the network's reach is not yet complete), which
set differs run to run (`reopen.py`); a reopened zone goes to the end of the worker's `cities` Map,
so `solveOrder` and the worker's history differ. Holding Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Foshan back 8 s
(their packs open last) did not change the signature, so plain solve order is not enough; the run
that differed has no open-order record (it ran before that was recorded).
