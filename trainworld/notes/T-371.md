# T-371: park-and-ride stage 2 (ride access in the local commute, true walk)

Design and Anita's decisions: notes/T-295.md. Rule as built: docs/demand.md 4.3 ("Walking to a
station", "Riding to a station"), SPEC 5.3.

## What was built (2026-10-15)

- **Walk** (`Params`): 6 km/h on 1.3x the straight line, linear (`w_walk` = `w_walk_far` = 1.5),
  `walk_cutoff_m` 3 km (a 3 km walk costs 59 of `pair_walk_max`'s 80).
- **Ride** (`kernel::place_rides`, `Subzones::ride`): per home zone (from its home-weighted
  centre), within `ride_max_m` 15 km, the cheapest station of each line, then the stations of the
  `ride_lines` 6 cheapest lines. Cost `p_ride` 10 + 1.33 x `drive_min_f` (zones' routed factor,
  both ends' density speeds) + min(parking at the station zone as a destination's, `d_drop` 10).
- **Offered** at the home end of commutes whose zone centres are 5 km or more apart
  (`ride_offered`, `ride_min_km`). Walk subzones carry their zone's rides after their walks
  (`acc_mid` splits the CSR); the work end, everyday purposes (`everyday.rs`) and the regional
  market use walks only.
- **Ride-only subzones**: homes beyond every walk, one subzone per 6 km square (3 x 3 zones) and
  region (`RIDE_GROUP`), placed in their most populous zone, which gives their distance decay;
  their trips keep each member zone's row total (`Subzones::origin_weight`). One per 2 km zone
  was the first version: the same rail (2.993M against 2.997M) but the browser day 39 s against
  29 s; grouping is the fallback notes/T-295.md named.
- **mode_choice**: a second logsum row per origin (walks and rides at the cheaper offset) for
  long commutes, its own Y row, both split back onto access entries. `trip.rs` `pair_choice`
  does the same (the reproduction test passes); the trip explorer's access minutes are a ride's
  drive minutes for a ride entry (shown as walking until T-374).
- **Counters**: `DemandApi::home_access_modes` (walk and ride boardings per station at the home
  end); `realnet` prints the day's ride share and writes `home_walk`, `home_ride` per period.

## Values picked (plausible starting values for T-372)

`p_ride` 10, `d_drop` 10 (notes/T-295.md's starting values); `ride_min_km` 5 (a shorter commute
cannot pay for a ride and a train against driving the whole way); `ride_max_m` 15 km and
`ride_lines` 6 (the design). `w_walk` 1.5 rather than the usual 2.0: with 2.0 the real network's
rail fell to 15.5% of commutes (ACS 20.3%), the boroughs 9-16 points under ACS, and LIRR, Metro-North
and NJ Transit all fell, since the work end's walk in Manhattan doubled in cost.

## Retrospective

About 1.5 hours wall. Worth it: realnet variants run four at once (one process each) settled the
walk weight in one round; the per-station ride-share script (scratchpad, not kept) read straight
from realnet's new `home_walk`/`home_ride`. Not worth it: the first plain `cargo test` ran without
`--features demand`, so it skipped every demand test.

## T-389: rides for everyday trips (2026-10-15)

`everyday.rs` `pass`: a pair's home end (pair.u) uses walks and rides when the pair's mean
distance is 5 km or more (`ride_min_km`; the commute's rule, on the pair's own distance since
everyday pairs are sampled cell to cell), on the trip out (access) and the trip home (egress);
the activity end walks. Ride-only homes (the 6 km groups) now take part as homes; a home's ride
stations join the strategy search's destinations. `app/src/game/walks.ts` shows walking minutes
at 6 km/h, the model's walk.
