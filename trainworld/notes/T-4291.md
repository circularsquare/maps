## What names a change (why the walk could go)

The walk over every edge and node (T-4130's net) found records whose numbers moved with no edit touching them. Field by
field, what moves each and what names it now:

- Edge numbers (`edges_info`: ends, tracks, built, length, cost, lowest limit, levels): the edge's own data. An edit or the
  model's working out of it (`Network::derive`: the touched edges and every edge at a touched node) lists it in
  `take_touched`. Prices worked out again for water or terrain: the model says every line changed, and the read walks.
- Node numbers: position, level, platform, built: own data (touched). Ports, built ports, heading, a junction's crossing
  geometry, unusable ends: from the edges at the node; `derive` lists every node at an edge it worked out again and at a
  run of single track whose way changed. A junction's crossing flag also follows the lines' moves through it (T-3745,
  `conflict_nodes`): named by a path entry or a move (two entries run on through the node) that came or went on a line read
  again, a line that starts or stops counting or running (T-4130's case), and new here, a line whose trains start or
  stop running back (one way, one way by track, a loop), which gives or takes its own way back's moves.
- The 15 lists the rows read whole (off, high speed, crossovers and partners, one way, joins, limits, ramps; stations off,
  named, unbound, one track, one platform, fare zones, upgrades and their upkeep): compared with the last read's as before;
  a changed entry's record is read, and for an edge its ends too.
- Names: `take_name_changes`. Platform lines: the ends of track whose lines changed, this read and the next (T-4113's carry).
- Lines: `lines_info` is still read whole (242 lines on Asia) and each line's 22 numbers compared.

No cause is left with no namer. The walk runs in `?patch=check` (`checkRows`: a fresh whole read beside every state) and
in checks/t1382-rows.mjs, which passed with T-4130's case cleared from far away on both Asia seeds.

## What changed

- workers/rowLog.ts (new): each read numbered, with the ids whose list rows changed or went and whether ids came, went or
  moved; a reader takes the union since its own last read (`since`), or none known after a whole read or off the ring (64).
- workers/stateRows.ts: `begin` asks `edges_info_of` and `nodes_info_of` for the touched records and syncs the regions by them
  alone (`Regions.syncTouched`); the lines' moves name records by moves as well as entries, and a line whose running back
  changes is read whole; `edges` and `nodes` read only the records in scope (the walk is `walkEdges` / `walkNodes` for a
  load, a repricing and `?patch=walk`), patch the lists by id (`patchList`, splices) and note the read's changes, the fold's
  included; `lines` keeps a line no edit moved with one typed compare, notes its changes and the bus stops' nodes after the
  model's (`putBusTail`), and commits the read; `edgeNow`, `nodeNow`, `lineNow`, `changes`, `changedSince`, `modelNodes`.
- workers/regions.ts: `syncTouched`, `endsOf`, `keysOf` and `takesAny` (a bus line's regions kept until a region splits or
  merges, `cells`).
- workers/clockPost.ts: `postState` sends the logged rows (`loggedDelta`) when the state holds the last read's lists;
  `?patch=check` compares what the page then holds with the state's lists (`checkLogged`).
- workers/clockMain.ts: ways back sampled again only for the lines that moved; `edgeDrawn` notes its changes
  (`NotedMap`) and each edge's points and speeds are kept beside the edges' list (`drawnLists`, `alignList`); the
  platforms and line ends by the read's changes, each with a whole build beside it under `?patch=check`.
- workers/platformLines.ts: `sync` and `setLines` by the changed edges and lines; `differs` for the check.
- map/terminalBays.ts: `terminalEnds` takes lookups by id (`EndLook`).
- workers/clockDemand.ts `complexOfNodes` (lines 706-725): the stations from the kept node rows, the model's walk beside it
  under `?patch=check`.
- workers/clockMoney.ts and sim wasm/money.rs: `fleet_needed`, every fleet's need in one call (each of the eight calls worked
  out every line's weekends, a pass over the whole network's track limits).
- checks/t1382-rows.mjs: its stand-in world for the page's grids gets an empty `save` (the page's world diff reads it since
  T-4284; the check crashed on master at its first step).

Lines touched in fixes18's files: clockDemand.ts `complexOfNodes` and the new `rowGeo` / `sameLine` above
`scheduleSnapshot` only. None in clockFollowUp.ts, clock.worker.ts, map/network.ts, lineBands.ts, buildingNet.ts,
renderer*.ts, world.rs or service.rs.

## Left, and why it cannot be made local here

- Inside the track model, whole passes on every state (about 15 ms in Node): the noted lines' stamps (7), the upkeep
  shares a line (`line_upkeep_periods`, 2), the upkeep and the fleet needs (2.5, twice a state), the 15 lists, the profile
  meta, costs, crossings and marks. Each needs the model to keep its sums per record and say what an edit moved
  (service.rs, world.rs: fixes18's files during this task). Filed as T-4299.
- The complexes and the names folded by them (about 7): `ComplexBuilder` numbers complexes by their first station in list
  order, the numbering every reader takes (the network's waiting rooms, line_related, the page), so any station change
  renumbers in a pass over every station. Local needs another numbering. Filed as T-4300.
- The drawn network's arrays in the post (about 12): the network makes every per-sample and per-station array anew each
  build, so the post compares and copies them whole, and sends the whole array when one line's samples move. Local needs
  the network to hand back an array it did not rebuild as the same object and per-line ranges for the rest, and the page
  to take them (map/network.ts, mergeNet): T-4153's and T-3444 stage 5's.
- The phase tables are laid out whole when one line's change (a copy of every table, posted whole): a per-line patch to
  the page, with the network's arrays above.
- The page still gets every id when a record comes or goes (a station click): the id list, and its own pass over it in
  `takeRows` (T-4284's).
