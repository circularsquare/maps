## 5. Local or AWS

- Light pass (B): all at home. Its inputs are on disk; it is CPU-light; AWS would add uploading 15 GB of packs and
  res-9 copies and fetching them back. If Overpass is slow for the campus outlines, a planet filter on AWS (osmium
  `tags-filter` for amenity=university,college, about 1 h on a spot machine, under US$1, a new launch kind in
  `tools/aws/`) is the fallback.
- Full builds (A, C): the read-bound part is where AWS pays (#11); the gate and the join stay at home (the join
  needs every pack, and a bigger machine does not help one unit's one-core tail).
