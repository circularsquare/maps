## Coverage method

Lists: Table of General Standard Chinese Characters (2013) from
[jaywcjlove/table-of-general-standard-chinese-characters](https://github.com/jaywcjlove/table-of-general-standard-chinese-characters)
`data/characters.txt` (8,105 characters in table order; the first 3,500 are the level 1 list, last one 矗, checked), GB2312's 6,763
hanzi decoded from Python's `gb2312` codec, and the 793 distinct hanzi that zh-Hans.json uses today. Measured against each
font's cmap with fontTools (`scripts` in the session scratchpad, not kept).

## Candidates

### Chill Round M (寒蝉半圆体, "Hanchan banyuanti")
- Link: https://github.com/Warren2060/ChillRound (release v1.805, 2026-01-05, `ChillRoundM_v1.805.zip`, 7 MB; actively updated).
- Based on Zen Maru Gothic, completed to the GB2312 set (adds about 225 glyphs); so it is the Zen Maru look the player liked, with the missing Simplified characters drawn in.
- Licence: SIL OFL 1.1, repo `LICENSE`: "(c) 2023 ChillType, with Reserved Font Name 'ChillRoundF' 'ChillRoundM'. This Font Software is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1." Self-hosting and redistribution are allowed. **Reserved Font Name: a subset or woff2 conversion is a modified version, so it must be published under another family name** (as Google does with its slices); keep the licence file next to it.
- Weights: one, Regular (400). No bold: use `font-synthesis-weight: none` as zh-TW does, or accept the browser's fake bold (it looked acceptable in the image but is the same risk as Huninn, T-2680).
- Size: TTF 6.5 MB. Subset to GB2312 + CJK punctuation + fullwidth + ASCII, woff2: **1.22 MB**. Sliced like Huninn: about 1.3-1.4 MB total, and a page loads only the slices its text uses (Huninn's 2.5 MB covers about 10,600 characters).
- Look: clearly heavier than Nunito 400 and than Zen Maru 400, round terminals. Reads closer to Nunito's bold-ish colour than YaHei does; the heaviness may want a lighter look at 12-14 px (check in game).
- Caveat: shared hanzi keep Zen Maru's Japanese glyph shapes in places (the author adjusted some; I did not audit every shape). A reader of Simplified would notice at characters such as 骨, 直, 角, 画, 户. I saw no obvious wrong shape in the game's strings.

### Chill Round F (寒蝉全圆体)
- Same repo, `ChillRoundF v3.0.ttf` (6.2 MB; in the repo tree, no release asset). Based on Kosugi Maru / justfont Fen Yuan; same OFL and reserved name. Coverage identical in practice. woff2 GB2312 subset 1.27 MB. Fully round (rounder than M). Not compared in the image; Regular only.

### Resource Han Rounded CN (资源圆体)
- Link: https://github.com/CyanoHao/Resource-Han-Rounded, release v1.910 (2022-04-23): `RHR-CFF2-CN-1.910.7z` (16 MB), one OTF, **67.5 MB** unpacked. Not updated since 2022.
- Derived from Source Han Sans; the CN build has Simplified regional glyph shapes (the right shapes for 骨, 直 and so on). Covers all 8,105 table characters.
- Licence: SIL OFL (repo has `LICENSE.md` and `OFL-License.txt`; README: "the fonts are licensed under SIL Open Font License while other parts are not"). I did not read the reserved-name line: read `OFL-License.txt` before shipping.
- Weights: variable, wght 200-900 plus a ROND axis (roundness, default 100). A real bold exists.
- Size: variable font cannot be used as is. I subset to the same GB2312 set and cut static instances (the fontTools instancer failed on its GPOS, so layout tables were dropped; Chinese needs none): woff2 **1.40 MB at 400, 1.49 MB at 700**; sliced, about 1.5-1.6 MB per weight.
- Look: nearly the same colour and size as YaHei or Source Han Sans, with softly rounded stroke ends; the roundness is subtle at 14-18 px and lighter than Chill Round M. Pairs with Nunito's weight well; less of the "Zen Maru feel".

### Others checked, not recommended
- [ZCOOL KuaiLe](https://fontsource.org/fonts/zcool-kuaile/about) and [ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou](https://fontsource.org/fonts/zcool-qingke-huangyou/about): OFL, Simplified, rounded but display faces (playful, uneven); not for UI text. Coverage not measured.
- [Maple Font](https://github.com/subframe7536/maple-font): rounded, OFL, Simplified, but monospaced code font.
- Nowar Rounded (https://github.com/nowar-fonts/Nowar-Rounded): World of Warcraft font packs built on Resource Han Rounded; nothing new.
- jf open-huninn / Chiron GoRound TC: Traditional only (Huninn lacks 975 of the level 1 list).
- Yuanti SC (macOS) and SimYou (Windows): system faces, not redistributable.
- Not found: a rounded Simplified face on Google Fonts proper.

## Recommendation

Use Chill Round M for zh-Hans, wired like Huninn (woff2 slices, `font-family` under a new name, e.g. "Chill Round M Web", after Nunito, then the system faces). It is the same design as the Zen Maru the player liked, covers the whole level 1 list and GB2312, and is the smallest. Test in game that 12-14 px text is not too heavy and look at the Japanese-shaped characters listed above. If those bother Anita, use Resource Han Rounded CN instead (Simplified shapes, real bold and lighter weights, about 0.2 MB larger per weight).

Rare characters outside GB2312 (937 of the 8,105) are missing in Chill Round M; the system face fills them via the fallback stack.

Downloads and subsets live in the session scratchpad `simpfont/` (not in the repo).
