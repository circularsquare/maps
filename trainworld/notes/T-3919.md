# T-3919 the market sees trams as commutes do

## The gap (confirmed)

`IntercityApi` (the market: longer trips, and the commutes and everyday outings across a zone line) had no
`set_tram_lines`, and `intercity/setup.rs set_network_in` built its `Service` with `tram: &[]`, `st_tram: vec![]`
and `cars: cars.to_vec()`. So it held a tram line at a train's cars (no 0.75 a section) and with no tram-only stops
(no 3-of-6 cap in `build_access`), the same gap T-3909 closed for the everyday layer.

## Who rides a tram from the market

Longer trips proper hardly ride a tram: they are not what is exposed. The market's other two parts are.

- Tram A, 7 stops Port Chester - Stamford, and Tram B, 8 stops Stamford - Bridgeport: all inside the New York zone
  (the hand-drawn Northeast pack reaches past Bridgeport). "longer" riders 2 and 8 a day of about 9,500 each.
- Tram C, 7 stops Bristol PA - Levittown - Fallsington - Morrisville - Trenton downtown - Trenton Transit Center -
  Hamilton NJ, across the Delaware, so from the Philadelphia zone into New York's (new NYC game on :6180, track tool
  type Tram, double, snaps off, constructed; New line "Tram C"; More trams twice at each level: 2 trams, 22.2 min).
  Saved as `scratchpad/tramlong/tramc.save`. "longer": 0 a day.

The purposes view (`twDemand.view().purposes`) moves the market's cross-zone commutes into "work" and its outings
into their purposes (`purposeParts`, T-276, T-1175), so the market's share is found by a temporary worker edit that
kept all of the market under "longer" (reverted): Tram C's local-layer riders were then 2,206 work + 932 school +
1,121 errands + 1,577 leisure + 77 medical + 117 attractions = 6,030, so the market carried 30,122 - 6,030 = about
24,090 of the line's 30,122 riders a day (80%). (That run's own "all" read 35,657, since "longer" takes its own day
factor in `demandSums.ts`; the subtraction is the figure.)

## The fix

- `IntercityApi::set_tram_lines` (intercity/exports.rs), a `tram_in` field (api.rs, pass.rs).
- `set_network_in` checks the flags with the others (`NetInputs`), sets `st_tram` with `tram_stops` and scales the
  tram lines' cars by `TRAM_CARS`, as `DemandApi::set_network` does. `set_access` builds the market's walks with
  `build_access` on `period_net(svc, 0)`, which carries `st_tram`, so the 3-of-6 cap follows.
- demand.worker.ts `setUpPops`: `set_tram_lines` for every population, unguarded, so a population type without it
  fails `npm run check` (seen with the old wasm: "Property 'set_tram_lines' does not exist on type 'IntercityApi'").
- Shared demand functions only called, not changed (`tram_stops`, `TRAM_CARS`).
- `sim/src/bin/demand_mem.rs` gives no population tram flags; left so.
- Test `longer_trips_see_the_tram_lines_as_commutes_do` (intercity/tests.rs): a tram from place 0 with six
  tram-only stops 250 m apart, one an hour, to place 1 120 km off. With the flags its cars are 3 (from 4), its six
  stops are tram stops, and place 0's cell reaches 3 of them (6 without); tram boardings (all 14 of its route nodes) 230 against 275 without.
