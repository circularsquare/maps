# T-4567 Riders walk farther to take an extra train leg

Agent walkleg, 2026-11-28, tree walkleg. Kind: trip figures, sim.

## The reports

- Discord bug-reports, 11-27 23:30 UTC, player arcades (message 1576092980425789562, one screenshot, Playa del Carmen): riders on an intercity trip don't take the red high-speed line at its nearby stop (Blvd Playa del Carmen) but walk to a farther station (25 Avenida Norte), ride the blue line one stop back and change. The explorer's main route draws the walk from the picked bubble, beside the high-speed stop, to 25 Avenida Norte.
- Feedback 1795718971-85dc2c97 (g205), 11-26, v0.4.7.1, French, one picture (Paris, Rue de la Chapelle and Rue Ordener): riders change on foot between two stations about 800 m to 1 km apart, past a closer station or a passage; "not there before".

## Cause (arcades)

An intercity trip (the market) picks its start station per 6 km zone, from the zone's access list (`intercity/api.rs` `access_list`): its 8 best-weighted stations, then up to 10 with the best station of each track component none of the 8 is on (T-1506). An inter-city station at a city's edge has fewer homes beside it than the feeder stops in the dense part, so it ranks below 8 of them. While nothing linked it to the feeders, the component slot kept it. Once a feeder line reaches it (as arcades' blue line does at Blvd Playa del Carmen), it is on the feeders' component, the slot no longer applies, and it drops out of its own zone's list. Every long trip from that zone then has to start at a feeder stop and change onto the inter-city line: the walk to a farther station and the extra leg. Bus stops count for the best 8 too, so a bus network crowds the list the same way.

Confirmed by a test before the fix: a zone of ten metro stops whose line runs on to the inter-city station lists the 8 metro stops only; the inter-city station has no slot.

Ruled out on the way (read, not changed):
- Boarding costs: the first boarding pays 2 min (none at a street tram stop, bus or ferry), a change 2 min instead (1 in a building); starting on the feeder saves at most 0-1 min. Not enough for a longer walk plus a leg.
- The planned first wait of inter-city trips favours the direct boarding, not the change.
- The station choice is soft on long trips (0.33 per minute to 12 km and over, softened by sqrt(60 / car minutes)), so some of a zone's riders always start at feeder stops; that is the documented rule (regional.md 5.x) and right for people who live by those stops.
- The explorer draws each of the zone's routes from the picked spot, so a feeder route used by people elsewhere in the 6 km zone looks like the picked bubble's. Left as is: with the fix the direct route is among them.
- A cousin noted, not changed: `start_walks` (T-3718) compares a start walk on the search's cost without the trip-end costs and the planned wait. No report points at it.

## Fix

`access_list` keeps, after the 8 best and the component slots, the best station of a line longer along its stops than any line the list already has (`train_reach`: per station, the longest line with trains stopping there; buses take no such slot), still at most 10. All six market lists (trips, work ends, commutes, outings) share it. DECISIONS 2026-11-28 records the lie (line length stands for how far a line goes). Docs: regional.md 5.2, demand-market.md, demand-worldwide.md.

Tests: `a_linked_inter_city_station_keeps_its_slot` (the case above: the station is listed and over 5% of the zone's trips start there), `access_lists_reserve_slots_for_unlinked_track` extended with the line rule.
