## What it is

- A gear icon button, last in the row with the eye and the city/world toggle (`ui/LinesGear.tsx`). It opens a
  popover under that row at its right edge (same look as the start menu's gear panel and the speed slider: bg, 1 px
  rule border); a click outside or Esc closes it (Esc stops there, so it does not also clear the selection).
- Sections: **Types** (a pressed icon button per line type the game's lines have: train, high speed, tram, bus,
  ferry, the Type column's icons and names), **Cities** (a box per city a line stops in, sorted by name, scrolling
  past about 8; the heading is a box that ticks or unticks them all, half lit while some are off), **Columns**
  (City, Riders, Money, Fleet, Type). Name always shows; Near is not listed (it only shows while sorted on).
- A section with fewer than two choices is left out. Choices come from every line in the game, so they hold still
  as the view moves.
- Stored per browser, localStorage `trainworld-lines-view` (`game/linesView.ts`): what is turned off. A choice turned
  off that this game doesn't offer (another game's city, a type from a skipped section) counts for nothing.

## Rules

- A line is listed when the scope and the filters both let it through. It passes the types when its type is on; it
  passes the cities when any stop (bus stops too) is in a ticked city, or no stop is in any city. A line with no
  stops and the selected line always pass (as the scope keeps them).
- Groups (advisor's call, DECISIONS 2026-11-19): a group is listed when any line in it, at any depth, passes; open,
  it shows only the members with a passing line. Its figures, Type icon, bullet and hovers stay every line's, as under
  the scope. The filter applies only when drawing rows, so the hand order and drags still see every line.
- The gear has a dot (accent, round, outside its top right corner) while the filters keep any line in the game off
  the list; its hover adds "3 lines not listed". Turned-off columns don't light it.
- A sort on a turned-off column goes to Name (an effect on the tab; at page load too, since the sort starts on
  Riders and isn't stored).
- The City column (whole world only) still needs two or more first-stop cities, counted among the lines shown.
- The action row: `.list-vis` now wraps whole controls (eye, scope, gear) instead of letting the scope toggle split
  in two; at 1400 px the default dock (302 px at 90% UI) already wrapped the eye and scope under New line with a long
  city name, and now does with "New York" too (the gear adds 31 px).
