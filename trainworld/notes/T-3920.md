# T-3920: bus stops placed 400 m apart (agent busstop400, 2026-11-21)

**Outcome: not reproduced for placed stops. No code change.** Every stop a player places counts for demand whatever
its spacing. The 31 stops on 60 km that busdesign saw (T-3378) is the Auto rule, which the frontier tool's 400 m bus
uses on purpose and which is also what a player gets from a new line and from the stepper's first step, "400 m".

## What a player can do

- **Manual** (T-2891, the Stops heading's Manual button): the player clicks stops along the route. Each one is a stop
  demand sees (`StopRule::Hand`, `hand_stops` in `sim/src/track/bus.rs`). The only floors: no two within 50 m along the
  road (`HAND_GAP_M`), and one within 200 m along the road of a station the route reaches becomes that station's stop.
- **Auto with a spacing**: the Stop spacing stepper (400 m, 600, 800 ... 100 km) or a typed distance (in km). 400 m or
  less is stored as 0, the automatic placing (`spacing_from`, T-2947): stations, ends, crossings, and about one demand
  stop every 2 km (`DEMAND_STOP_M`) on the 400 m street grid, the other street stops drawn only. Anything above 400 m is
  `StopRule::Spaced`: every stop is a demand stop, cover 400 m each way.

## In the game (new NYC game, tree Vite 6184, the player's path)

One bus line drawn with the Build tab's Bus tool, 5 clicks, Make line, Construct: 3.7 km of road down McDonald Avenue,

while the panel calls both "10 stops". Placed and typed spacings above 400 m agree with each other.

## Left as is (Anita's call)

Auto reads "400 m" and "10 stops" while demand uses about one stop every 2 km, worth about a fifth of the line's riders
here. Changing it (Auto's street stops as demand stops, or the panel saying what demand uses) is a demand rule change
and bus tuning, and reverses T-2947; buses are settled. Recommendation: leave the rule, since a player who wants
every stop counted can step to 600 m or place stops (T-2947 judged the two "nearly the same", timing and cover
matching at 400 m; riders were not compared then); if she wants it changed, the plain fix is to count every Auto street stop for demand (more demand stops on
every Auto line in every save).

The frontier tool (`tools/frontier.py`, `sim/src/bin/frontier.rs`, `set_bus_spacing` only above 400) is faithful to a
player's default line; its comment already says 400 m is the automatic placing. Not changed.
