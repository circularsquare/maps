## Why the prong is long

Each hop's target is `1000 m x (length / 1000 m)^0.3`, whatever its stations are. That evens hops: a 0.5 km hop gets
0.81 km, a 2 km hop 1.23 km. So a branch of many close plain stops (south Brooklyn's locals, 0.5-0.9 km apart) is drawn
at nearly a full hop per stop, while a core whose lines stop less often (an express, or a player's own sparse lines)
gets fewer hops for the same ground. In her picture Grand Central to Atlantic Av is about 146 px for 7.6 km and
Atlantic Av to Mermaid Av about 215 px for 12 km: the same px per km, no opening of the core at all. The right-hand
green line, about 8 stops, is stretched to match the yellow line beside it, which has about 12.

The real New York save does not show it as strongly: there the core is crowded with lines, and their spacing opens
Manhattan to about twice the prong's px per km (Atlantic-Coney 119 pitches for 11.9 km, Grand Central-Atlantic 145 for
7.6 km). Her game has far fewer lines. No save of her game is here, so the proxy is `few`: the real New York save with
only the 2, 4, 6, 7, A, D, E, F, J, L, N and Q (310 complexes). It reproduces her picture: the prong (109 pitches) is
longer than Grand Central to Atlantic Av (97).

## What changed

- **Local hops** (`layout.ts` `prep`, `LAYOUT.local` 0.4, `LOCAL_MIN` 3): a hop between two local stops (draw.ts
  `localStops`: one line stops there, or lines of one colour), not lonely, gets 0.4 of its target, at least 3 pitches.
  Tried 0.6 (min 4) and 0.5 (min 4): the prong moved 109 to 90 and 99 pitches; 0.4 (min 3) moved it to 83.
- **Outline edge** (`borders.ts`): closing 30 to 50 pitches, and on the region's edge the fitted border may pass up to
  10 pitches further out than the grid's edge (`fitOcti`'s `outer`), never nearer the lines than before. Closing 50 alone
  filled more bays but kept the staircase edges; the outward tolerance is what turns them into long runs. A convex or
  octagonal hull was not tried in code: on New York it would wrap the harbour, Long Island Sound and the empty corners
  between branches into one blob, losing the network's shape that Anita liked after T-2488.
- **City names** (`view.ts`, `map/basemap.ts` `placeNameInk`): the basemap's label colour, light `#6f6d6a`, dark
  `#97938e` (from `SHADES.*.label` mixed toward black 0.35 / white 0.3), same font (Zen Maru Gothic 400, as the
  basemap's Regular) and size as station names, halo kept.

700, Toronto 270 -> 275, `few` 257 -> 248.

Borders (`checks/t2464-borders.mjs` on the new layouts): every zone closed, every station on its own side, every run
octilinear on all four. Legs: New York 154 -> 96, Northeast 162 -> 104, Toronto 84 -> 50, Japan 2,158 -> 1,858. Japan
has 3 pills touched by a border (0 before); they come from the tighter local hops (4 with the old edge rules too), where
a border between two zones passes between local stops 3 pitches apart. Borders in Japan 2.2-2.5 s in Node (3.5 s before
on the old layout; noisy).

What did not move: in the dense saves the prong stays about as long, since crowding (the sides terms, bundles) rather
than the hop targets sets it there. Toronto's drawing reshuffles (a Long Branch streetcar run now drawn south), as any
target change does.
