# T-1147: cars a bit faster? Measured; speeds left as they are (2026-10-24)

Anita (2026-10-24), on rail running high against real cities (New York's stations about 2.4x
their counts, everyday rail 15.5% against NHTS's 5.8%): "i think we can just make cars a bit
faster? i think we have their speed a little low".

## Outcome

No speed change. Every published speed found puts the model's car at or above real speeds already,
and the speed-ups barely move the figures that run high: 20% faster everywhere takes New York's
rail 5.5% down, its station entries from x2.83 to x2.70 of the counts and its everyday rail from
15.5% to 14.7% (NHTS 5.8%), while Tokyo, already under its counts (x0.66), falls further to x0.60.
Speed is not what holds rail up. A speed-up would be tuning against the evidence, which the brief
ruled out ("grounded, not tuned"). If Anita wants one anyway, x1.1 everywhere is the mildest
(rail -3 to -4%, ceilings fine); the table below has the rest.

## The rule as it is

`Params` in `sim/src/demand/kernel.rs`, `drive_min_f`: 2 km at each end on local streets
(`road_town_kmh` 44 in open country), then up to 8 km on main roads (`road_far_kmh` 88), both
falling to `road_city_kmh` 16.5 as homes plus jobs per km² rise (half way at 5,000), and any
middle at `road_open_kmh` 110; the road is the routed length x 1.074 (`road_routed_scale`). The
market's car is 15 minutes plus 1.25x the great circle at 85 km/h (`intercity.rs` `drive_min`).

## What this says about rail running high

New York's everyday rail at 15.5% against NHTS's 5.8% and its stations at 2.8x do not come from a
slow car: the car is fast already and a 20% faster one closes about a twentieth of the gap. The
overshoot is New York and London only (Tokyo is under its counts), which points at something on
the rail side or in where everyday trips go (T-1092's trips into town raised New York's rail
12.5%) rather than a worldwide car speed. Not chased here.

## The market's car

Measured in T-279 (notes/T-279.md): on flat motorway corridors 85 km/h is slow against routed
drives, across mountains and round inlets 1.25x the line is too short; the median over 43 land
pairs is close. One speed for the world would trade one error for the other, so the market's car
is left for T-279's routed drives.
