# T-3482: a new line on full track gets its trains at the edit

Agents passage (cause area) and settle (cause and fix), 2026-11-18.

## Cause

**At the edit.** `settle_counts` (sim/src/track/world.rs) steps a level only when some line on it is off its
fleet and not *held*; a held line is one short of its fleet on full track (T-517), taken as settled because the
fill gave it its share. A new line has no share yet: it is added with no trains (rate 0), then given headways,
and on the Tokyo - Sendai peak its track is already filled by four metro lines (Shinjuku, Oedo, Namboku,
Tsukuba Express, at 15 each). Short on full track, it counted as held, the metros were settled, so the peak
level never stepped and the line kept its rate of 0 (26 trains bought, none running at the peak).

**Minutes later.** Not a timer and not demand feeding back (the riders handler only feeds the boarding account;
no bus lines, so street speeds settle nothing either). It is the load's cap lowering (`game/capLoad.ts`,
T-3165): once per load, at the first demand final, every hand-set count over its cap is lowered by an edit. On
tyo_sdj.save that edits lines 5, 64, 81 and 95 (21 -> 19, 35 -> 22, 96 -> 42, 47 -> 43 at the peak). Line 5
shares track, through a chain, with the four metro lines, so its edit's settle scope holds the new line and
steps the peak level: the new line gets 2.35 and the four 13.83, r5's figures exactly. Whether that first final
came before or after the line was built varied run to run (in my a2 run the lowering ran at 38-45 s, before the
line; in r5 it is inferred to have run at the 222 s final, from the timing and the exact figures, not logged).

Shown natively: on ri1.save, the four counts put back, line 150 removed and added again as the page does
(no trains, fast, 16 cars, headways 450/600/1800), then the four lowered: master gives 0 at the edit and
2.35 / 13.83 after line 5's lowering. One more in-scope edit on master (line 5 one train fewer) moves it again
to 4.59 / 12.71, where it then stays: 2.35 was a stop on the way.

## Fix (world.rs)

- **A line with no share yet is not held in the edit's first settle.** `TrackWorld::settle_edited`: the lines
  the edit changed (`lines` and `t.schedules` in `commit_with`), each with the levels where it ran 0 trains an
  hour before the edit (every level for a line the edit made or an undo brought back). In `settle_counts` such
  a line, short on full track, still steps its level, until its rate moves less than 0.01 trains an hour a
  round. The weekends' settle gets the same lines by their weekend rates (`settle_edited_rest`). Cleared before the trim (`settle_unpaced`), so the trim's settle and every later one treat it as any
  held line. Lines outside the edit's sharing closure are never stepped (T-1863), so T-1787 holds.
- **A switch to headways at a level with no trains** (T-849) follows the round trip and is trimmed as any
  headway line's (`switching` carries the levels that were empty). Before, the count from the round trip at
  rate 0 was frozen for the edit (26 at the Tokyo peak) and kept untrimmed, then sold back at the next edit.
  Levels with trains still keep them (T-752).

A first, wider version (every line the edit changed, whatever its rate) also fixed a held line's own count or
headway change, but cost London's shared save 36 -> 330 ms an edit (Paris 5 -> 38) and moved every shared
save's edit results; narrowed on the advisor's call.

## Follow-ups (not filed)

- **Held shares are path dependent.** Any edit that steps a full level re-shares the held lines on it from
  where they stand, so the same network can settle at different shares (master: 0, 2.35, 4.59 over three
  edits; the page after the fix: 4.59 -> 6.01 when line 5, beside them, loses one train). A fill from each
  fleet's own rate, not the damped step, would make the share depend on the fleets alone.

- **A held line given a new count or headway on full track keeps its rate** until a line beside it moves
  (master and fixed alike: London's busiest line keeps 8 trains at medium as its headway goes 540 -> 360 s).
  Stepping it costs London's shared save about 300 ms an edit.
- **A train can vanish at a change** (service.rs `hand_over`): a train dwelling at its first stop when its
  profile changes is redrawn standing there (its shift puts its draw at the change), and a train waiting at
  that terminal takes it as a departure, so one of the two disappears. Repro: the T-2429 fixture
  (`a_change_to_a_following_pair_on_full_track_moves_no_train`) with the express given its fair share (12.8)
  instead of the 8 it starts at: local 40 -> 3 at MIDDAY loses one express train at x = 100. A fix that passed
  every test (a shifted trip drawn before the change is never taken over): scratch `service.rs.fixed`.
- T-3483 (Tokyo - Osaka with both real networks below the line alone) may be this: its line also starts at
  Tokyo among the real network. Rerun its pair on this build.
