# T-1902: the trip explorer's numbers, its "walk too long", and "can't be worked out"

Probes (scratch scripts, not kept): a new Seoul game with one high-speed line Seoul Station -
Daejeon - Dongdaegu - Busan (then on to Fukuoka, Osaka, Nagoya, Tokyo), trains 4/3/2 an hour;
the real New York save; the old Japan test save (`artifacts/trainworld/t737-japan-trains.save`).
Outputs in `artifacts/trainworld/t1902/`.

## Why "train 400 min, car 502 min" read as only 24% train

Seoul - Busan, midday, before the fix (Anita saw 400 / 502 / 24%):

| | |
|---|---|
| inter-city trips | 3,391 |
| train | 26.9% |
| trips on area pairs the train can serve (a station in reach at both ends) | 41.2% |
| train's effective time, over those | 341 min |
| car's effective time shown, over all trips, money at a rider of average income | 502 min |
| car for the same served trips, money as the choice weighs it | 369 min |
| train's share of the served trips | 65% |

Three things made the panel misleading:

1. The train's effective time averages only the trips it can serve (41%); the car's averages all.
   The other 59% have no train at all.
2. The car's (and the plane's) shown effective time weighed money at a rider of average income's
   fare weight. The longer trips' choice weighs money at each income group's factor, which carries
   `LONG_TRIP_MONEY` (0.4) and leans to the richer groups who travel most, so the shown car was far
   dearer than the one the train is weighed against (502 against about 370 here).
3. Within the served trips the train's lead is spread: by the train's lead over the car, 63 trips
   over 2 hours ahead (100% train), 318 one to two hours (96%), 572 up to an hour (74%), 443 behind
   the car (27%). The longer trips' logit is damped (`mode_beta`: 0.1 x sqrt(60 / car minutes),
   0.035 a minute at 500), so half an hour ahead gives about 74%.

Tokyo - Seoul over the same line (Anita: train 1,100, plane 1,700, 1% train): train 947 min over
the 32% of trips it can serve, the plane shown at 1,679 but 895 for the same trips as the choice
weighs money; train 10.5% in all. Busan - Tokyo: 773 against 839, 47% served, 32.5% train.

The fix (display only, no demand rule touched): `market_trip` prices the head's car at the
travellers' mean weight on money (`Place::trip_w`), and adds slot 24, the car and plane together as
rail's choice weighs them (reference less ln(rest) over the scale), over the served pairs. The panel
shows "Car for these trips" (or Plane, or Car or plane) and "Trips the train can serve" under the
train's effective time; the train's share of the served trips is in the first one's hover.

After: Seoul - Busan reads train 360 min, "Car for these trips" 369, "Trips the train can serve"
41%, train 21% (By car 360, was 502); Tokyo - Seoul train 983, "Plane for these trips" 895, 32%
servable, train 10%; Tokyo - Osaka (Japan save) train 472, car for these trips 545, 77% train.
Screens: `artifacts/trainworld/t1902/trip-seoul-busan-z8.png`, `trip-seoul-tokyo-z8.png`,
`trip-tokyo-osaka-zA11.png`.

## "The walk from the nearest station to the end is too long" while the bar shows train

On the real New York save, cell pairs 2-25 km apart read as the trip tool reads them: 5 of 71
showed a raw train share in the headline while the representative trip offered none, but each was
under one whole trip, so the bar showed 0% train. Read as a bubble selection (every cell within
1.5 km of each end), 2 of 27 pairs counted whole train trips (2%, 0.06%) while the representative
(errands) trip had none: one walks too long, one every station pair walking further than walking
there. The headline counts the homes and places around the points (and deals a zone's commuters
over its cells by jobs); the cell's own trip gates on `pair_walk_max`, the walk rule and the walk
fade. Fix: `DemandApi.trip` reads such a trip's route with those three lifted (slot 46 flags it)
and the explorer shows it when the headline counts a whole train trip, under one line naming why
the exact points have none; both sampled pairs now show a route
(`artifacts/trainworld/t1902/lifted-nyc.png`). A pair with no route at all still says so.

## "Can't be worked out" between big cities

Not reproduced as "could not be worked out" (the workers' no-answer case) in the Seoul or Japan
games. What did fail: on the Japan save, the trip tool from Tokyo Station's cell to Osaka, Nagoya
or Sendai answered "One of the two places is outside the city's homes and jobs." The start cell
(Tokyo Station's cell: 0 longer trips to any place or area) has no longer trips of its own, so
`farEnd` found no far area within 6 km nor a far place within 60 km and gave up. It now falls back to the two whole
places (the zones the two points are in), the city-to-city market.
