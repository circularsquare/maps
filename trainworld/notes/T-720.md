# T-720: a walk to a station far too short (Brooklyn to Fulton Mall)

Anita, 2026-10-19, on 8800 (1b1170e), New York with her own A line (Fulton Mall, Concord St,
Thompson St): a midday shopping trip from a home in Cobble Hill / Carroll Gardens to SoHo says
"Walk to Fulton Mall 10 min", the dashed walk on the map 2.5-3 km, Google about 40 minutes.

## Cause: the access subzone's averaged walk (a rule, not a bug)

The model does not price a home cell's own walk. Station access is per **access subzone**: the
cells of one 2 km zone that share their nearest station (docs/demand-kernel.md 4.4 step 3,
`kernel::build_subzones_on`). The subzone has one walk per station: its perceived minutes are a
logsum over its cells (scale `beta` 0.10), its metres the cells' mean weighted by homes plus jobs
(or activities, in the everyday layer). Every home in the subzone rides as if it had that walk.
The explorer's "Walk to ... 10 min" is that mean distance in real minutes (`acc_t`), and its map
hover already says "the model's average for the homes near here".

With a dense network a subzone is small (the cells nearest one of many stations), so this is close.
With a short line, every cell of a zone whose nearest station is the same one is one subzone, and
it can span walks from 1 to 30 minutes. Reproduced natively on the New York pack with Anita's three
stations (a diagnostic test since removed; `artifacts/trainworld/t720/brooklyn_walk.log`):

| home, south-west of Fulton Mall | its own walk to Fulton Mall | subzone (same for all four rows) | priced walk, real / perceived |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 km | 13.0 min | 38 cells, own walks 1.0-29.4 min | commutes 9.4 / 10.1; everyday 10.6 / 11.6 |
| 1.5 km | 17.3 min | | |
| 2.0 km | 25.1 min | | |

The zone holds Downtown Brooklyn as well as Cobble Hill and Boerum Hill, so its jobs and shops sit
on the station and pull the mean in; the logsum leans further towards the near cells. A home
2.0-2.3 km out (the far side of that zone) is priced at 10.6 minutes against 25-30 of its own.
Homes 2.5 km and more out fall in the next zone, whose subzone is 3 cells at 34-38 minutes and is
right.

## How widespread, and what it does to ridership

Commutes, morning peak, free flow (`measure.log`). "Over twice" is the share of rail riders on
walk access, spread over their subzone's cells by homes (home end) or jobs (far end), whose own
walk to the station is more than twice the subzone's. "Rail" is rail trips against one subzone per
cell (`band_min` -1, the exact model).

| network | subzones | over twice, home end, real / perceived | rail vs exact |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York, the Fulton Mall line | 48 | 8.2% / 24.0% | **+20.7%** |
| New York, the real network's first 3 lines | 388 | 4.1% / 10.2% | +6.8% |
| London, the real network's first 3 lines | 165 | 1.7% / 8.7% | +14.6% |
| New York, real network (867 stations) | 4,450 | 2.1% / 5.5% | not run (too big exact) |
| London, real network | 4,155 | 2.2% / 6.3% | not run |

So on the full networks it is a few percent of riders, but on an early game's sparse network rail
is overstated by 7-21%, with the far cells' riders the ones that should not be there. That is the
stage a player is learning the game in. (T-090 measured subzones within 0.2% of exact, but on the
dense hand-made network.)

## Walk bands, the options measured

`Params::band_min` (T-090 had 5-minute bands and dropped them when it went to the logit): a
subzone is the cells of a zone that share their nearest station **and** whose walk to it falls in
the same band, so a 25-minute home is no longer averaged with a 2-minute one.

| network | no bands | 15 min | 10 min | 5 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fulton Mall line: rail vs exact / subzones | +20.7% / 48 | +6.8% / 71 | +4.4% / 81 | -2.2% / 118 |
| NY first 3 lines | +6.8% / 388 | +3.3% / 571 | +1.2% / 671 | +0.3% / 1,031 |
| London first 3 lines | +14.6% / 165 | +3.8% / 266 | +0.2% / 312 | +0.2% / 495 |
| NY real: over twice (home, perceived) / subzones | 5.5% / 4,450 | 3.7% / 6,797 | 2.0% / 7,888 | 0.5% / 11,611 |
| London real | 6.3% / 4,155 | 4.0% / 6,354 | 1.6% / 7,480 | 0.3% / 10,991 |

I recommended 10. **Anita chose 15** (2026-10-19), with the explorer pricing its own trip exactly.

## What was built

- `band_min` 15 by default (`Params::default`, so commutes and the everyday layer alike; the bench
  follows it).
- The trip explorer answers for its two cells (`DemandApi::cell_subzones`, trip.rs): each cell as a
  subzone of its own, as `band_min` -1 would build it (its own walk to its 6 nearest stations
  within the walk's bound, around water through `Routes::walk_to_cell`, which reads a station's
  kept search and never keeps one made for a single cell), the home's rides those of its subzone,
  the drive between the cells; the everyday answer on the cells' own distance and walk factor
  (`TripInputs::walk_factor_cells`). The subzones' answer rides along in head slots 37-40 (their
  walk, train and drive chances, their cheapest walk at the home end), and the inspector's hovers
  give it where it differs: the period rows ("Counted with the homes and places near here, as the
  map's bubbles count it: ...") and the first walk (the group's walk when a minute or more apart).
  The map's walk hover now says "from this home".
- Checks (`sim/src/demand/t720.rs`, synthetic city, one sparse line): every subzone's cells within
  one 15-minute band (fails with no bands); the explorer's walk is the home cell's own, and with
  one subzone per cell its chances equal the subzones' to 1e-4.

## Results with 15-minute bands

New York's real network (nyc_real_track.json, native, warm, other agents running):

| | no bands | 15 min |
|---|---|---|
| commutes: subzones | 4,450 | 6,797 |
| commutes: AM free-flow pass | 1.48-1.99 s | 2.75-2.81 s |
| commutes: rail trips, AM | 1,826,089 | 1,812,018 (-0.8%) |
| everyday: midday free-flow pass | 0.31 s | 0.28-0.30 s |
| everyday: rail trips, midday | 2,495,963 | 2,463,822 (-1.3%) |

The commute pass's pair loop grows with the subzone pairs (about 1.9x); the everyday pass is over
its samples and does not.

Anita's case (her three stations, shopping midday to Thompson St), homes along the ray south-west
of Fulton Mall every 200 m: the bubbles' group walk, the explorer's own walk, the bubbles' and the
explorer's train chance.

| from Fulton Mall | no bands: group walk / train | 15 min: group walk / train | explorer: own walk / train |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2 km | 10.6 / 76% | 6.9 / 77% | 1.0 / 86% |
| 1.0 km | 10.6 / 76% | 6.9 / 77% | 13.0 / 76% |
| 1.2 km | 10.6 / 76% | 20.6 / 62% | 17.3 / 71% |
| 2.0 km | 10.6 / 76% | 20.6 / 62% | 25.1 / 65% |
| 2.6 km | 35.6 / 16% | 35.6 / 18% | (a ride) / 24% |

(The explorer's chances past about 2 km mix in rides to the station on trips over 5 km.)

**Seams.** Along that ray the bubbles' train chance jumped once by 60 points (76% to 16%, at the
zone line about 2.5 km out) with no bands; with 15-minute bands the steps are 15, 44 and 18 points:
more seams, each smaller, the biggest a quarter lower. The 44-point step remains where the zone
line meets the 30-minute band. Softer, not gone: a seam is where a band or zone edge falls, and
only per-cell pricing removes them (5-minute bands would make them small at about 2.6x the
subzones).

## Not the cause, for the record

- Walk speed and detour: 6 km/h along 1.3x the straight line; `pack_walk --probe` finds no water
  in the way (2.0 km straight = 26 min, 2.7 km = 35 min; Google's 40 for 2.7 km).
- The explorer showed the model's number for the subzone, as its hover said.
