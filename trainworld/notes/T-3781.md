# T-3781: demand data not loading within 10 minutes

Agent demandload, 2026-11-19. Stage 1 read-only on the main tree; stage 2 in tree `.tw-trees/demandload` (master
7761956b), Vite 6189. The cause of the two reports is **not found**: the player's saves load normally. What landed
is a restart when a load is stuck and the diagnostics to name a stuck wait next time.
Also covers the Seville report (1795014711-19bd38fc), added by the orchestrator.

## What the failure is

`loadingScreen.demandTimeout` ("Demand figures did not load after {time}.", the small reason line under
`loadText.failed`) is the loading card's watchdog (`ui/LoadingScreen.tsx` `startWatchdog`, T-950), not a download
timer: the card up and visible for `QUIET_BAR_MS` = 10 min (3 min without a bar) with no `markAlive()` (no demand
worker message other than a query's answer, no pack bytes, no clock worker game or network message). Then
`loadCannotFinish` -> `DemandPool.fail()`, which stops demand for the session. The string stays as it is (Anita,
2026-11-19).

## What changed (tree)

- **Restart once when stuck** (`DemandPool.restartStuck`, `game/loading.ts` `loadStuck`, `LoadingScreen.tsx`): when
  the watchdog fires on a demand load, every demand worker is replaced (a planned restart, not counted as a panic),
  reopens what it held, and the newest network is solved again; the focus pool stops (its figures stay); a network
  held for the pause is let go. A second quiet spell shows the failure screen as before. `lastTrouble` carries the
  pool's diagnosis from the moment of the restart.
- **Pause hold during a load: dropped.** A network arriving after a half-loaded one had taken the hold, with
  "Recalculate trips while paused" off and the game paused, could wait for a clock the card never releases. T-3686's
  `midLoad` (2a5413b4) now keeps every mid-load network from demand, so the first one is the loaded game's and this
  can't arise; the change was taken out after the rebase rather than kept beside it.
- **Diagnostics** in the feedback report's demand line (`diagnosis()`): the status a failure replaced (`before`),
  the solve's line count, round 0's periods in while it is unpublished, each worker's last message kind and age and
  the barrier period it is in (a new `unit` message from the worker at each period's start, ended by its result),
  a network held while paused and for how long, the focus pool on or off, the restart's age; a focus pool stop sets
  `lastTrouble`.
- **Dev hook**: `?debugStall=i` (dev server only) makes worker i's first solve step wait forever
  (`debugPanic` "stall").

### What this says

- The cause **fits memory**: the two accounts with 1,600+ station, 7-13 zone games peak at 1,628-1,705 MB in an
  emulated tab, 270-340 MB above the shipped Tokyo start, inside the range where iOS kills a tab. A (262cb413's
  game) peaks below the Tokyo start; if that phone's limit is near 1.3 GB both would die. Not proven:
  this is Chrome on master, not Safari on v0.4.2-0.4.4 (Safari has no Oilpan garbage, the "rest" differs; v0.4.2-0.4.4
  had the old loading card, which cost Chrome about 190 MB more at the peak, T-3160, and nothing on Safari).
- Nothing warns before such a load on an iPhone: Safari reports no `deviceMemory`, so `budgetKnown` is false and
  `overBudget` never fires; `memoryRefusal`'s soft device check takes a phone as 4 GB and warns past 3 GB, and its
  one-worker estimate (1.8 GB of page, 0.49 of worker, plus 1.2 MB per million local trips held) is not expected to
  reach that on these games (not worked out per save), while iOS kills the tab at 1.4-2 GB.

### T-3933 (a load out of memory retries with fewer workers), for phones

A phone already runs the smallest pool: 1 solving worker and the aux worker (confirmed in every run above). A retry
with fewer workers can drop only the aux worker, 65-97 MB at the peak here (and the sums worker's 40-50 when it is up).
That is an upper bound: this harness starts a New York game first, and the aux worker already held 65 MB (41 WASM, 24
JS) before the import, which a player's own path would not. Dropping the solving worker too means no trips at all.

These browsers did load the same games at other times: 262cb413's browser held a day-1,240 game in its one slot, and
udc0fd's single writer kept saving its game between and after its three reports. So the kills look like loads at the
edge of the limit, where under 100 MB can decide it. A retry without the aux worker **could rescue some loads, but not
B or C against a 1.4 GB limit** (B 1,628-1,705 to about 1,540-1,610; C 1,653 to about 1,565). On these saves the
larger room is elsewhere: the clock worker (359 settled on B), the page (up to 268), the solving worker's fine zones (B
holds 10 fine), and the 430-800 of rest. On desktop (several solving workers) T-3933 has more room.
