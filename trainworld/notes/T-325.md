# T-325: zones no station reaches stay out of the solve

T-303 item 1 (notes/T-303.md), in three stages: a (this), b = T-336 (clicks), c = T-337 (spec).

## Stage a, done 2026-10-14

**What changed.** A zone no station reaches, viewed or on screen beside the view, opens **bare**:
its pack goes to the aux demand worker alone, which builds the commute and everyday layers, sets
them on an empty network and solves every period once (no rail, so cheap). No solving worker opens
it and no solve is started or waited for. The page gets, with the `opened` reply, each period's
city summary and purpose parts (as a solve's `result` carries them) and builds the zone's city
view from them (`bareView`, the arithmetic of `dayResult` and `dayDemand`). Bubbles come from a new
`bare` query: commuters by mode (`cell_modes_day` with no subzones) and the everyday legs once per
solving worker's periods (new Rust `cell_modes_mask_day`, `cell_modes_of_mask_day`), added up in
the page in `byMask` order as a solved city's are.

- Bare only once the reach is known (first network and footprints); at startup the viewed city
  still opens in every worker, so startup is as before.
- A station built within reach: the pack is downloaded again and opened in every worker; the solve
  waits for it as before (`solveNeed` = reached cities plus any opened in every worker). Its bare
  answers serve until a shown solve covers it.
- The startup and game-load clock hold also waits for the viewed zone to be open, bare or not.
- Files: `sim/src/demand/everyday.rs` (`modes_in`), `app/src/workers/demand.worker.ts` (`openBare`,
  `bare` query), `demandProtocol.ts`, `demandClient.ts` (`reachedSet`, `solveNeed`, `want`,
  `bareOpened`, `bareModes`, `bareView`, `addBareViews`, the load bar's `loaded`).

**Exactness.** `checks/t325-bare-check.mjs` runs the real worker in Node: two solving workers
(periods 0, 2, 4 and 1, 3) and the aux worker solve a Midtown line with the market and the zone
open; the aux worker also opens the zone bare. Equal to the bit: city info, every period's summary
and parts (rounds 0 and 2), every worker's everyday legs (weekday, weekend, each kind alone, a kind
the zone lacks) and the commuters by mode. Passed for Philadelphia and Toronto.
`checks/t149-worker-check.mjs` passes unchanged. Outputs in
`../artifacts/trainworld/notes-outputs/T-325-bare-check-<zone>.json`.
