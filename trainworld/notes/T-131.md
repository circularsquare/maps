# T-131 — city-pack boundaries at regional zooms

Implemented 2026-10-10. `map/cityBoundaries.ts` installs a GeoJSON source and dashed outline
for every city in the shared registry. The outlines use `data/packs/<id>.boundary.geojson`
unchanged, generated by `pipeline/build_city.py` from the dissolved county polygons that
define each playable demand pack. These are **metropolitan pack footprints**, not municipal
limits or inferred rectangles. Islands and disconnected pieces retain their actual polygons.

The neutral 1.5 px outlines sit below basemap text and the game's canvases. They are strongest
at zoom 8.5 and below, fade smoothly to zero at 10.5, and are hidden above that. They introduce
no selection or hit-testing targets. `startCityBoundaries(map)` reinstalls sources and layers
after `style.load`; its returned cleanup removes the listener and the installed sources/layers.

Vite's pack plugin serves GeoJSON with `application/geo+json` in development and copies each
registered boundary into production `dist/packs/`, alongside the existing JSON/binary packs.
Adding a city therefore uses the same registry and pipeline output as the other city content.

Validation:

- `npm run check --prefix trainworld/app` passed.
- Both generated FeatureCollections parse and contain valid closed polygon rings. NYC has
  25 counties, 19 MultiPolygon components and 4,157 vertices; Philadelphia has 11 counties,
  4 components and 1,765 vertices. Their source property identifies Census
  `cb_2020_us_county_500k`, dissolved.
- NYC's exact bounds are `[-75.195114,39.498537,-71.856214,42.080906]`; Philadelphia's are
  `[-76.233259,39.290417,-74.389708,40.608580]`. The outlines intentionally preserve these
  larger regional footprints, including pieces outside the initial two-city camera bounds.

Production Vite build copies both exact GeoJSON sources (95,186 and 40,558 bytes). Integrated
browser checks in `T-132-browser.json` load both sources, verify close-zoom hiding and reinstall
after a full style replacement. Regional screenshot inspected at
`../../artifacts/trainworld/T-131-regional.png`.
