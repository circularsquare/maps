# T-2986: both platforms at a line's end only with a terminal

Agent terminal2, 2026-11-10, tree `.tw-trees/terminal2`. Anita: players took the terminal to mean
"trains use both platforms", so make it so: with a terminal a line's end uses both platforms (and
the sidings at a through station), without one a single platform, one train at a time.

## What changed

- Sim (`sim/src/track/capacity.rs`): `dead_end_places` (both constructed platform tracks only at a
  station with a terminal, else 1) pools a dead end's turns (`turn_at`). A double-track station dead
  end without a terminal gets `Tag::OnePlatform`, wire kind 14, so the alert's fix is the terminal.
  A single-track dead end and a bare track end stay kind 3. Through stations unchanged (one platform
  for the turnaround without a terminal, platforms for the dwell and the sidings with one).
- Tests: `tests::terminals_at_dead_ends` gives the fixtures whose tests are about something else
  (spacing, merges, fleets, headway limits, transitions) terminals, so they keep the capacity they
  were written with; `true_dead_end_keeps_pooled_platform_turnaround_capacity` now tests both cases,
  undo, and single track.
- App: `terminalBays.ts` kind 3 (a dead end's one place, the arrival track's, held as a place with a
  group so the guard waits for it); `TrainGuard.placeLines` guards a line with places at its ends even
  alone on its track. `capacityKinds.ts` kind 14 ("terminusOne"), TroubleList and the line panel's
  spots treat it (and 9, 10, which fell through to "track") as at a station.
