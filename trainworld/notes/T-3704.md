# T-3704: bus and ferry fleet and headway changes did not recalculate

Player reports 2026-11-18 (Discord: PranCZ, Márcio; in-game feedback 1795022059-6d21d6e5, Nathanial).

## Cause

T-1863 (commit 51457285, 2026-11-16, shipped in v0.4.0) made an edit settle only the lines in its
`settle_scope`: the lines the edit rebuilt, recomputed or that share a capacity resource with them
(`TrackWorld::commit_with`, `sharing_closure`). A count or headway edit only reschedules a line
(`t.schedules`), and the scope reached a rescheduled line only through `svc.shared`, the users of
capacity resources. A rail line always has some (sections, platforms, terminus), so it was always in.
A bus or ferry line uses no track and has no resource, so it was never in scope: its counts changed
but `settle_counts` never stepped its rate (`line_tph`). With the rate stuck:

- the panel's headway (counts mode) stayed at the old rate's;
- demand saw no change (`demand_moved` false), so no new solve: trips and fares stayed;
- running cost reads the rate, so profit stayed;
- in Headway mode `trim_auto` took the stale rate as buses "waiting at the ends" and trimmed the
  fleet: a 4 min headway on a 39 min round trip got 6 buses instead of 10, so past some fleet size
  the headway stopped mattering ("the headway doesn't change at some point").

## Fix

`seeds` in `commit_with` include `t.schedules`, the edited lines themselves. Lines with resources
were already in through `svc.shared`, so only bus and ferry lines change. Tests:
`bus_tests.rs` `a_bus_lines_rate_follows_its_buses`, `a_bus_lines_rate_follows_its_headway` (both
fail without the fix). Doc: docs/track-and-service.md, the settle paragraph.

tph). The train line's rate stops at 15.00 from 4 trains: that is its terminus limit (4 min), the
intended cap (T-3165, red headway and held messages), not this bug.

## For T-3703 (bus and ferry schedules like trains)

- The bus and ferry panel (`BusInspector.tsx`) shows `<Schedule>` with no `limits` (no track, so
  `trainCap` is 1000 and nothing turns red), no `copy` (no schedule panel button), no `weekends`.
  The rail panel adds `ScheduleButtons` (LineInspector.tsx `ScheduleButtons`: the schedule panel
  and timetable chart buttons); the schedule panel (`SchedulePanel.tsx`) renders
  `<Schedule ... weekends />` plus `DefaultHeadways`.
- `DefaultHeadways` and `lineKind` (game/newHeadway.ts) return null for bus and ferry lines: new
  bus and ferry lines take headways from their tools (as seen: bus 480/900/1800 s, ferry 1200/1800/3600 s), not the
  per-kind defaults; adding them means a "bus" and "ferry" kind there.
- The sim's weekends (T-3521, `Weekend`, `weekend_counts_in`, `settle_weekend`) are not rail
  specific: a bus line has `LineData::weekend` like any line and the weekend edits run on it. A
  weekend-only edit seeds its scope from `t.weekend`, so it reaches a bus line; a weekday edit now
  does too.
- Bus and ferry lines are skipped by `pace_merges` (no merges) and by the client's cash pre-check
  for headways (`headwayTrains` returns null for a bus: the worker decides).
- Vehicle words come from `vehicleOf(st)` (bus, boat); the schedule strings take `{vehicle}`.
