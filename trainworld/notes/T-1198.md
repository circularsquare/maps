# T-1198: zones opened by looking are never released (2026-10-24)

Rules: docs/demand-runtime.md "Packs close when nobody needs them". The wider memory picture and
the proposals that follow from this: notes/T-1199.md.

## What was keeping the memory

Not the bare zones. Their packs in the auxiliary worker close as T-301 made them (the needed zones
plus two spare, 60 s), and the aux worker stayed at 28-29 MB through a 16-city tour. The page's
own JS heap grows about 1.5-2 MB a city visited (boundaries, footprints, place lists, outlines
kept for the page's life) and the GPU process about 8 MB a city (map tiles): small next to:

**The market, in every solving worker.** Selecting a zone (a city click, T-801) opens its market
pack in each solving worker, and every market pack opened or closed recomposes the market. A
composition is about 450 MB whatever its packs: its tables by pair of the world's 2,567 places
(`PairRule` 48 bytes, corridor index, corridor lists and sums: about 64 bytes a pair, 6.6 million
pairs). The old composition was freed only after the new one was built, and in a game with any
network a market part still held it, so each worker held two or three compositions, plus
fragmentation, and grew with each city clicked. WASM memory never shrinks, so none of it came back.

## What changed

`demand.worker.ts` `recompose`: the old composition is freed before the new one is composed (when
no part holds it), and a part composed of exactly the same packs serves again instead of composing
(`composedOf`, by the list of places). So a selected zone's market opened and later closed costs
one composition, not two, and its close composes nothing (0.5 s instead of 4.5-7.5 s a worker).
Figures are unchanged: `checks/t301-worker-check.mjs` passes (close, reopen, close during a solve,
the market closed and reopened, to the bit), and the probe below shows the market's figures equal.

## Measured

|---|---|---|

| a close of a selected zone's market | 4.4-7.4 s | 0.4-0.6 s |

After the change memory stops growing at about two compositions a solving worker; it does not
come back down, since WASM never shrinks. Giving it back needs either a smaller composition (Rust,
T-1204) or a worker started afresh (T-1205). Evidence: `../../artifacts/trainworld/t1198/`.
