# T-009 UI mock: notes

Done 2026-10-08. Static mock, not game code: `mock/index.html`, `mock.css`, `mock.js`, `data.js`.
Served at `localhost:8800/trainworld-mock/` (serve.py name `trainworld-mock`). Screenshots at
1440x900: `T-009-a.png` (set A, Zen Maru Gothic), `T-009-b.png` (set B, M PLUS Rounded 1c),
`T-009-c.png` (set C, Zen Maru Gothic).

## What is on the page

- Dock on the left (`clamp(304px, 24vw, 352px)`), full height, tabs build, lines, stations,
  money, city plus a menu button. The tab content sits on top; the inspector fills the rest and
  scrolls. Every tab has placeholder content; the lines tab is the worked one.
- Inspector for the selected line: letter chip, name, a colour swatch button (cycles colours in
  the mock, to show colour is settable; the game gets a picker), rename button, stops and length;
  schedule table with steppers for trains an hour at peak, shoulder, off-peak, the headway and
  trains needed per period, "Same all day"; round trip; running cost a day; riders a day,
  fullest train at peak, fare stepper, fares a day; the stops as a vertical strip in the line
  colour with transfer chips and boardings per stop. Steppers recompute headway, trains and
  running cost (service hours 6/8/6, $1,250 a train-hour, mock numbers).
- Top bar over the map: city name, the mode split (train first, then walking, driving, with the
  train share at the larger size), cash, day, clock, period name, pause and three speeds. The
  clock ticks one game minute per second at 1x and stops when paused or hidden.
- Build toolbar flush in the map's bottom-left corner: track and station tools, level -3 to +3,
  and the cost per km of the chosen level.
- Map: MapLibre 5.24 (unpkg; 6.x ships no UMD build) with OpenFreeMap Positron repainted: neutral
  land and water, faint grey labels, the basemap's own railways and road shields hidden. Five
  invented lines as GeoJSON, smoothed with a centripetal Catmull-Rom through the stations,
  station dots white with a dark ring (bigger at transfers), trains as 300 m round-capped capsules
  in a darker shade of the line colour with a white edge, as many as the peak needs. Click a line
  on the map or in the list to select it. Station names for the selected line are HTML markers in
  the UI font.
- Mock switcher in a plain grey strip along the bottom, outside the game: colour set and font.
  Remembered in localStorage; `?set=a|b|c&font=zen|mplus&paused=1` override it.

## Tokens

The UI has three tokens per set; everything else in the UI is `color-mix()` of them (muted text,
rules, hover shade, selection tint).

| Set | --bg | --text | --accent | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A cream | #fbf7f0 | #4b403a | #d9788e | warm off-white, cocoa text, sakura pink |
| B sky | #f6f9fb | #2f4255 | #4f8ac6 | cool white, slate text, cornflower blue |
| C matcha | #f6f6ec | #3d4535 | #7f9f4f | pale tea, moss text, matcha green |

The sets change the UI only. Map, lines, trains and stations are one fixed set in every variant
(Anita, mid-task: line colours are a normal saturated metro palette and player-settable; the UI's
softness must not reach the network drawing):

- Lines: red #d7263d, blue #1f6fd1, green #1a9a4a, orange #f08a00, purple #8a3fb8,
  yellow #f2c200, brown #8f5a2a, teal #0e9a9a. Chip letters white, dark on yellow.
- Network ink #2b2b2b for station rings and station names; basemap land #f2f2ef, water #cbd8df,
  parks #e3e8df, labels #a9abad.

The first draft used the T-002 spike's pastel line set and tinted the basemap per UI set; both
were dropped on that instruction.

## Style decisions

- Square buttons, 1 px borders, radius 0; button groups share borders; the "on" state is the
  accent border plus a light accent tint, not a solid fill. Icons are inline SVG line icons.
- Japanese 2010 web touches, kept small: section headings with a small accent square before them
  instead of uppercase labels; dotted rules between rows; folder-style tabs with a 3 px accent top
  edge on the active one.
- Font sizes 12, 14 and 18 px; weights 400 and 700. No shadows (except the text halo on map
  station names), no blur, no gradients, no cards, no transitions. MapLibre's own CSS fades
  markers in, so `.maplibregl-marker` and controls get `transition: none`.
- Fonts: both load from Google Fonts at 400 and 700. M PLUS Rounded 1c sets wider and looks a
  size larger at the same px; at 1280 wide its tab labels nearly touch. Zen Maru Gothic is the
  softer of the two. Not decided; Anita to pick.
- Copy follows her user-facing text rules: sentence case, no dash-joined phrases, "a day" rather
  than "/day".

## Checked

Headless Chrome (CDP driver, private profile) at 1440x900 and 1280x800: no overflow in the top
bar at either size (the split bar shrinks to ~190 px at 1280); the inspector scrolls at both
heights; every tab renders; a peak stepper click gives 21 an hour, 2.9 min, 26 trains; selecting
a line moves the station labels; no element has a transition or animation; no console errors.

## Known rough edges

- Basemap place labels collide with our station names (Positron's "New York" sits on City Hall).
  The game should either drop basemap place labels near stations or draw station names as a
  symbol layer that takes part in collision.
- The mode split numbers are not consistent with the riders shown (811k boardings a day would be
  a far smaller share of a 21.7M metro's trips); picked for the look.
