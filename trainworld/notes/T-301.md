# T-301: close packs nobody uses (2026-10-15)

Rules: docs/demand.md "Which cities are open"; the hysteresis in DECISIONS.md.

## What was built

- `demandClient.ts` `sweep` (after every `ensure` and on a timer): keeps the zones viewed, beside
  the view, reached or in the load in hand, and the market while one of those is its zone or a
  station is in its reach. Anything else open closes after 60 s unneeded, or at once beyond two
  such zones (least recently needed first). A bare copy of a zone the shown solve covers closes at
  once. A full close drops the zone from the solves' covered lists (no re-solve: no station reaches
  it, so no line figure depends on it); a closed bare zone's city figures stay shown (`bareKept`).
- `demand.worker.ts` `close`: frees the commute engine, everyday layer (or bare copy) or the market
  and its parts, takes them out of the kept networks' clusters; a pack the running solve covers
  closes when it ends, unless an `open` of it comes first. `?perfOff=close` turns it off.
- Found on the way: the brief's "every zone opens in every solving worker" was already untrue since
  T-325 (unreached zones open bare in the aux worker only); so the gain is in the aux worker, plus
  the market's ~45 MB a solving worker becoming reusable when nothing needs it.

## Measured

`checks/t301-pan.mjs`, own Vite 6101, 3 solving workers + aux, bubbles on, zoom 10.5: 13 Northeast
zones from Washington, on to Toronto, 75 s there, back, 75 s at Washington. Before is the same tree
without the change. WASM memory in MB, solving workers then aux.

| | peak | end | most packs open at once |
|---|---|---|---|
| New game, before | 45, 46, 46, **154** | same | 14 |
| New game, after | 45, 46, 46, **110** | same | 5 |
| Real save, before | 176, 176, 156, **133** | same | 14 |
| Real save, after | 176, 176, 156, **97** | same | 8 (3 reached + market + bare) |

- WASM memory never shrinks, so end equals peak: closing caps what the aux reaches (the needed
  zones plus two), it does not lower it. On the Northeast that is 30-45 MB of the aux; at hundreds
  of zones it is the difference between bounded and unbounded. Solving workers hold only reached
  packs, which never close, so they are unchanged; the market closed at Toronto on the new game but
  its space is only reused, not returned.
- Reopening (download from the local server, then opening): 1.4-2.3 s per zone, of which download
  1.0-1.7 s; on the pan back every zone was already reopened as a nearby zone before the view got
  there, so the wait on arrival was 0 ms before and after. With bubbles off a closed zone's view
  waits its reopening, about 1.5-2 s here.
- Real save final 7,596,985 rail trips a day before and after; `checks/t326-clusters.mjs` with
  `?perfOff=close` and without: all seven hashes identical (ny 58c891d0, both eeaccb6d, ..., overlap
  3ed52d2d). Its "network error" console lines (packs fetched by the page the save import reloads)
  appear with closing off too.
- `checks/t301-worker-check.mjs`: closing and reopening Philadelphia, the market, a close during a
  solve, a close cancelled by a reopen, a bare close and reopen: figures identical to the bit; a
  reopened bare pack fits in the freed space (aux 34 MB before and after).
