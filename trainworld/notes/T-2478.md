# T-2478: Vancouver stalled, failed after a reload, then worked (2026-11-04, agent loadwait)

Anita: "i tried reloading again afte rnot being able to load a stalled vancouver, and after reloading, it failed. then it
worked after a retry. is something wrong with vancouver tho?"

## Nothing is wrong with Vancouver

Loaded 17 times without a fault: new game at `?city=van` and a save with one line there (`van-line.save`, 4 stations,
Waterfront to New Westminster, in `artifacts/trainworld/loadwait/`), each cold (fresh profile) and warm (reloaded in
the same profile), on the tree's Vite with 2 and 3 workers and on 8800's build with 3. Every load ended with its final
in 2-7 s, with no console error, no failed or 4xx request and no retry. Its packs (`van`, rewritten 2026-11-04 13:58
with the other 2,761 zones) open; its cluster with a line is van, chw, kel, six plus their market packs (wht, abb), solved
in one pass. Logs: `artifacts/trainworld/loadwait/van-*.log`.

## What most likely happened: 8800 was rebuilt under the page, twice

8800 was rebuilt at 22:36 and again at 22:42 (`.tw-snap` reflog), and the note came in at 22:49. A build empties
`dist/` and copies every zone's pack files back in at its end (about 20 s for dist/packs at 22:45:41-22:46:03), so for a
while each pack file answers 404. Played back with the page's requests answered 404 (`van.mjs`, `FAULT=missing:<ms>`):

- **The stall.** With every pack file 404 for the first 20 s of a new game at Vancouver, the load never finished
  (240 s), and nothing said so: no card, no failure. The zone in view is found from its boundary file
  (`van.boundary.geojson`), and a boundary that failed once was kept as failed for the page's life (`cityReach.ts`
  `store`), so the view counted as dead space, `viewedZone` stayed null and no pack was asked for again, though the
  pack retries (T-484) themselves ran. The coarse outlines (`outlines.json`) were the same: one failed fetch, none for
  the page's life.
- **The failure after a reload.** A page whose worker script answers 404 stops with "Demand could not load" and
  "worker 2: undefined" (the error of a worker that did not download has no message); Try again reloads, which works
  once the build is done.
- A save with a station in the zone recovered from the same 25 s of 404s by itself (39 s instead of 6 s): a zone
  reached by a station counts as reached when its boundary fails, and its pack is tried again (T-484). A half-written
  `van.bin` is caught ("array ... runs past the end of the .bin") and opened again 10 s later (12 s instead of 6 s).

## Fixed

- A failed boundary is forgotten 10 s later (then 30 s, then every 60 s) and asked for again by the next placing of the
  view (`cityReach.ts` `FOOTPRINT_RETRY_MS`, `zones.ts`); meanwhile a place whose own boundary may be the failed one is
  placed by the zone centres, as before any boundary is in. Replayed: the same 20 s of 404s now ends with Vancouver's
  figures at 20.7 s.
- `outlines.json` is tried again 1, 4, 10, 30 and 60 s after a failure before the page goes on without it
  (`coarseOutlines.ts`), the page's copy and the clock worker's.
- A worker whose script did not download says so: "worker 2: its code could not be downloaded".

The rebuild itself emptying the folder 8800 serves is not the game's to fix: T-2483.
