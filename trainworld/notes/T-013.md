# T-013 notes: spreading big blocks over their area

**Done 2026-10-09.**

Before, each 2020 block's people and jobs went whole to the res-9 cell holding its internal point,
so a suburban block bigger than a cell left its neighbours empty. Now every block is spread over
the land its polygon covers. Code: `pipeline/spread.py`, called from `build_city.py`.

## How

- Block polygons: the 2020 redistricting (PL 94-171) TIGER release has the same 2020 blocks as
  TABBLOCK20 **one zip per county** (`TIGER2020PL/LAYER/TABBLOCK/2020/tl_2020_<county>_tabblock20.zip`),
  so the 25 counties come to 73 MB instead of 510 MB of state zips (New York state alone is
  326 MB). POP20 still comes from the state .dbf files already cached.
- Each block is rasterized on a 50 m grid aligned with the water mask (pixel centre inside the
  polygon, the scanline fill in `pipeline/water.py`). A pixel weighs its land share from the 25 m
  water mask (4 sub-pixels), so a block's people and jobs avoid the water inside it. Each pixel
  goes to the res-9 cell holding its centre (8.05M pixel centres through h3).
- Blocks that catch no pixel centre (977 of 184,440, all under ~50 m across) keep their internal
  point; 4 blocks whose pixels are all water are spread over them unweighted.
- Totals unchanged (population still equals the 2020 county counts exactly).
- Build time +50 s (block polygons ~30 s, h3 ~15 s).

## Numbers (New York, with the T-014 adjustments)

| | before (internal points) | after (spread) |
|---|---|---|
| cells | 74,920 | 202,337 |
| cells with people | 72,460 | 198,594 |
| cells with jobs | 55,910 | 190,612 |
| median cell | 125 people, 19 jobs | 15 people, 2 jobs |
| gravity zones | 5,710 | 6,154 |
| pack `.bin` | 2.17 MB raw, 0.97 MB gz | 6.85 MB raw, 3.80 MB gz (1.11 MB of it the T-030 water mask) |

Most new cells are sparse: 46,459 cells have under 5 people plus jobs and hold 0.4% of the
people. Biggest cells by people are a little lower (Yorkville 11,788 to 9,500) because Manhattan's
blocks now split across cell edges too.

**Demand kernel on the new pack** (`demand_bench --pack data/packs/nyc.json --no-periods --no-edit`,
native, one thread): recompute 508 ms against 452 ms before (+12%; access 8.0 ms, subzones 10.8 ms,
mode choice 223 ms over 6.6M subzone pairs), peak heap 18.7 MB against 12.4 MB. Rail share 24.8%
against 24.7%: the network results barely move.

`T-004.png` is redrawn from the current pack (the old one, with the empty gaps between suburban
internal points, is gone; the numbers above are the record).
