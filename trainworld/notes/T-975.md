# T-975 Regions: design for Anita

Design, 2026-10-22; stage 1 built 2026-10-23 (below the design). Her brief (TODO.md, 2026-10-22):
country-sized regions that can be shown or hidden; regions not built in start inactive, covered by
a grey darker than the other-city shade, drawn as one overlay and a button that loads the region.

## Anita's answers (2026-10-23), which override the design below where they differ

- The draft list is fine as drafted (China in the seven usual regions).
- A shown region need not be hideable. A region shown only by its button is **not kept**: a reload
  hides it again unless the game has built there. So there is no `shown` header field: a region
  shows when it has a station (blueprint or built), holds the start city, or was clicked this session.
- Player-facing words: never "region" for zones, and no "area". "City" in general ("Find a city"),
  "inter-city trips" for the longer trips. The new regions are named by their names (Japan, France,
  China north); the button need not say "region".
- Leaderboards will be global and per city, so nothing about regions is needed for them (stage 3's
  per-region boards are dropped).

## Summary

**Recommendation.** A region is a fixed, hand-edited list of countries and (for the big
countries) first-level units, about 30 regions for what is built today. Every zone gets its
region id in the registry. A region is **shown** when the game has a station in it, it is the
start city's region, or the player clicked its button (kept in the save). An inactive region
draws one dark fill over its land and one button with its name; its zones draw nothing (no
bubbles, labels or outlines) and load nothing for viewing at any zoom. Showing a region costs
almost nothing: zoomed out its bubbles come from the registry, and packs still load only when
the player zooms in, as now. **Regions change only what is drawn and loaded for viewing, never
demand**: solves, the market, commutes, money and points are the same whatever is shown.

**First step** (one task): the list, the registry field and region shapes, the overlay and
button, the four ways a region turns on, and inactive zones skipped for viewing. Per-region
outline files (a startup saving) and hiding a region again come after.

**Questions for you**
1. The draft list below: China in the seven usual regions or your five? Spain and Portugal
   together? Small countries grouped as drafted?
2. Hiding a shown region again: wanted? My default is yes for a region with no stations (a
   later stage); a region with stations stays shown, since its demand runs anyway.
3. The word "region" already means a zone in a few player-facing places ("Find a city or
   region" in the new game sheet, "inter-region trips" in hovers and the city inspector). My
   default: "Find a city or area" and "longer trips", so "region" means only the new thing.

## What a region is

**A hand list**, `app/src/game/regions.json` (hand-edited like `zone-names.json`; no pipeline
step writes it): each region an id, a name and its members, as country codes, or a country
with first-level unit names for a split country. Not derived from the plan: the plan's
`region` field is a priority rank ("uk", "europe", "world") and its `solve_region` is a
province, neither of which is country-sized.

**Every zone gets its region**: `pipeline/regions.py` (run by `world_run.py join`) writes a
`region` id on each registry row, from the row's country, or for a split country its
first-level unit (the plan rows' unit parents; China's province is already worked out for
`solve_region`). Rows with no plan row (the Northeast nine, Toronto, Japan's 45) need only the
country. About 12 bytes a zone. The same step writes `packs/regions.geojson`: each region's
land dissolved from geoBoundaries ADM0/ADM1, simplified to about 2 km, with a label point (the
point deepest inside the largest part). About 50 polygons, a few hundred KB, read at startup.

**Code word.** "region" is taken three times in the code (the save's per-zone ledgers,
`regions`; the plan's rank; `solve_region`), so the code calls these `realm` (registry field,
file, functions); players see "region".

## Built in, shown, and how a region turns on

- **Built in**: a station (blueprint or built) in one of the region's zones, or on its land in
  dead space (the region shape decides, so a station in Soria is in Spain and Portugal). Track
  alone does not count: it makes no demand.
- **Shown** = built in, or the start city's region, or opened by a click. The game works out
  built-in regions on each load; only the clicked ones are saved, in a new save header field
  `shown` (region ids). A save without it (every save now) shows its built-in regions and its
  start's region. An id no longer in the list is dropped.
- **What turns one on**: its button; placing a station in it (the station tool's click shows
  the region, then places as usual, nothing refused); a search pick of a zone in it; a new
  game's start city. A start on the world map shows no region: the world is the dark overlay
  with buttons.

## What an inactive region draws

- **One fill** over its land, the other-city shade's colour (#182536) at about 22% in the light
  theme (the outside shade is 7.5%) and black at about 60% in the dark one (35%), under labels
  and the network, taking no clicks. Values in `game/shadeTable.ts` to tune by eye.
- **One button** at its label point, in the map buttons' look: the region's name and an eye
  icon; hover "Click to show France". When
  zoomed in so the label point is off screen, the button sits inside the region's on-screen part,
  the placement the loading bars use (T-939). Buttons that would overlap give way to the region
  with more zones; at world zoom (below about 3) the buttons are the icon alone and the name is
  in the hover.
- **Nothing else**: none of its zones' bubbles, labels, coarse or exact outlines, loading greys or
  bars. Its zones are dead space for the view (`placeAt` skips them): the top bar shows no name
  and nothing loads for viewing. The player's own track and stations draw as always, and the
  real-network layer is unchanged.
- **A selection's far ends** (a selected zone's bubbles for the trips to each other zone) still
  draw over inactive regions: the player asked, and the trips are real.

**Showing a region** removes its fill and button in one step; its zones' zoomed-out bubbles come
from the registry's `localTrips` at once (no download), and zooming in loads them for viewing
exactly as today (above zoom 7.5, T-871). It does not download the region's packs: France's 86
packs are hundreds of MB, and nobody looks at all of them.

## Demand, the market and borders

No change, by construction. A zone that joins a solve is one a station reaches, so it is always
in a shown region. Commutes between zones and the market's longer trips run between reached
zones and to every other zone through the market rows, inactive regions included, so a French
line near Germany carries the same German commuters whether Germany is shown or not. A line
into Germany needs a German station, which shows Germany. Market packs open on the rules they
have now (reached, a station within 30 km of the zone's box, or selected). Leaderboard scores
(T-973) therefore never depend on what is shown.

## Saves

One optional header field, `shown`, read by the main thread and sent back whole when it changes,
as `opened` is. The unread `opened` list stays as it is.

## Performance (1,189 zones tonight, about 2,000 later)

- **Zoomed out**: today every market zone is a bubble, a hover target and a label candidate
  (1,189). With only the start region shown that drops to its zones (Japan 45, Germany 113).
- **Panning zoomed in** over Europe today loads each zone's pack, outline and water mask ahead
  (T-871 cut the long tasks to 0.4-4 s a pass); over an inactive region it loads nothing.
- **Startup**: `outlines.json` (every zone's coarse outline) is 3.1 MB, 1.2 MB gzipped, and grows
  with the zones. Split by region (`packs/outlines/<realm>.json`, fetched when a region is
  shown), startup takes only the shown regions' outlines plus `regions.geojson`. That is stage 2,
  so stage 1 stays small. The registry (694 KB, 79 KB gzipped) stays whole: search and the new
  game sheet need every zone.

1. **T-975 itself**: `regions.json`, `regions.py` (registry field and shapes), the overlay and

much of the clutter it targets is gone; doing T-975 first lets T-976 be judged on what is left.
T-974 (the start menu's map) is unaffected: the start menu keeps every zone's bubbles as a
picture of the world.

## Stage 1 as built (2026-10-23)

Names: everything is `realm` in code and files, since `pipeline/regions.py` (cells' states, T-162)
already exists: `app/src/game/realms.json` (the list), `pipeline/realms.py`, `data/packs/realms.geojson`,
registry field `realm`, `game/realms.ts`, `map/realmOverlay.ts`, `checks/t975-realms.mjs`.

- **List**: as drafted. US split at the Census West region (13 states west); Russia at the Urals by
  federal district (Ural, Siberian, Far Eastern east); small dependencies added to their realm (IM,
  JE, GG, GI, AX, FO). Countries are matched by Natural Earth's ISO_A2_EH (CYN, KOS, SOL, SAH fixed
  as world_plan's NE_FIX). geoBoundaries names Guangdong "Guangzhou Province" (`SOURCE_NAMES`).
- **realms.py**: about 93 s (82 s of it cutting China and Russia along their ADM1 at Natural Earth's
  10 m detail), 505 KB (167 KB gzipped) for master's 1,029 zones in 25 realms; 523 KB, 27 realms for
  the main tree's registry of tonight (China wave 1 and Taiwan in), no zone without a realm.
- **China zones by centre**: two zones' plan rows say `solve_region` henan but their centres lie in
  Hebei (wix, Wei) and Anhui (lnq, Linquan); realms.py goes by the centre (cn-north, cn-east).
- **App**: shown = realms with a station (land shape, else the station's zone via `cityWithin`) +
  the start zone's (`start` added to the clock worker's "opened" message; a save older than `start`
  uses the zone it opens on) + clicked this game (reset at each load). Before the first game's first
  snapshot everything shows (no dark flash while a save loads). File missing = feature off.
  Inactive zones: skipped by `zoneFocus` (view placement, nearby zones, no boundary fetched),
  `cityBoundaries` (coarse and exact outlines), `zoneLoading`, the overview (`intercityView`, unless
  a zone is selected), and the clock worker's masks ahead of the view (`skip` on the view message).
  A split country's zone without a registry `realm` is placed by the land (nearest vertex when its
  centre is off the simplified coast: Manhattan, Wenzhou).
- **Shades**: light #182536 at 22%, dark black at 45% (60% as designed hid the water in the dark
  theme). Buttons: `.btn.text` with the eye and the name; hover "Click to show its cities".
