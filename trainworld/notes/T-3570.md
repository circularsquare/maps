# T-3570, T-3571: real stations on the real world map, and new stations named after them

Agent realstations, 2026-11-17. Branch tw/realstations. Covers both tasks.

## Source and coverage

noritetsu's model stations, `noritetsu/dist/data/<cc>/stations.json` (one per region, the same 120 regions as the
real network's tiles). Each station: `n` local name, `e` English name, `x`/`y`, `l` line ids; `j: 1` marks a junction
point ("near Portage"), not a station, and is dropped (6,171 of them). 129,804 stations in all.

- Already published: every one of the 120 files answers 200 on R2 (`https://data.anita.garden/noritetsu/data/<cc>/stations.json`,
  CORS `*`, gzip on the wire). **No upload needed.** Biggest on the wire: ru 488 KB, de 390 KB, cn 309 KB, jp 263 KB,
  us 148 KB (raw 2.3, 1.6, 1.1, 1.0, 0.65 MB). The `logos` folder beside them is not a region.
- The noritetsu tiles carry no station layer (build_tiles.py dropped it), so the tiles were no option; nothing new
  was fetched or built.
- Names in Latin letters: 80,344 have a Latin local name, 33,531 more an English one; 15,929 have neither. Of those,
  Cyrillic transliteration covers most of ru (10,120), ua (1,556), by (984), kz (877, Kazakh letters beyond Russian's
  stay unshown), bg (339). Left with no Latin name: jp 202, cn 551, kp 205, gr 150, tn 138, and a few elsewhere.
- Tram stops are in it (Melbourne CBD, Vienna): 24,623 stations (19%) have only tram lines. Their kind is in the
  region's `lines.json` (per line `kind`), which the marks fetch too, so Passenger hides tram-only stops as it hides
  tram track; All shows them (screenshots `artifacts/trainworld/t3570/mel_z14_passenger.png`, `mel_z14_all.png`).
  lines.json is 5.5 MB gzipped over all regions; it is read only while the marks are on.

## The marks (T-3570)

Settings > Real world network > "Show stations", off by default. A ring per station, `realMain` round a land fill,
60% opaque in light and 70% in dark (`realStationOpacity` in shadeTable.ts), radius 1.4 px at zoom 10 up to 6 px at 18,
from zoom 10 (at 6 a view of Germany would be 12k dots). Shown whenever the lines are (setting not None, or while
snapping). One GeoJSON circle layer over the real lines, under the labels. Snapping is untouched.

Names: hover only, the ring's tag shows the station's name and nothing else (above the demand bubbles' hover,
under the airports'). Name in Latin letters as below, else the local name. No names drawn on the map: at any zoom
they would compete with the basemap's place names and the player's own station names.

## Naming (T-3571)

The first suggestion is the real station nearest the spot within **150 m**, if it has a Latin name and that name is
not in use; else naming is as before. Why 150 m: half a long platform from the station's point, and under the
distance to the nearest differently named station for almost every station. Nearest other-named station, measured
over each region's stations: p5 222 m in Japan, 168 m Germany, 232 m France, 323 m Britain, 419 m Korea, 81 m US (US
pairs are mostly two OSM points for one complex, "Times Square" beside "Times Square–42nd Street"; the nearest wins).
Under 150 m: 3% of Japan's stations, 4% of Germany's.

Script: the game's place names are Latin everywhere (places.py `latin_names`, the basemap's `name:latin`), so a real
name is the local name if in Latin letters, else the English one, else Cyrillic transliterated (the rule
pipeline/realauto.py `pick_name` uses for the real world networks). Tokyo gives "Shinjuku", Seoul "Seoul Station",
Moscow "Moscow Kursky railway station" (OSM's English). Melbourne's "Stop 3: Bourke Street Mall" loses its stop number.
`endsInStation` does not apply to real names ("Seoul Station" is its name).

- A click never waits on the download: the region's file is asked for when the station tool is picked and on each
  idle map with it in hand. The inspector's cycle button waits for it, and offers the real name first.
- Ferry ports never take a real station's name (newStationName's `real` false in ferryDraw.ts).
