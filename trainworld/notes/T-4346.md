# T-4346 — Move or close the first load's card

## Code trace before the change

- **First load flow**: `startLoadingGate()` (`app/src/ui/LoadingScreen.tsx`) sets `clock.setHeld(true)` at startup, and `holdForGameLoad()` (`app/src/game/loading.ts`) sets it on any whole-game load. `demandClient.ts` reports progress into `loadProgress` across three stages (`game/loadBarCore.ts` `stageInHand(parts)`):
  1. `"game"` (`STAGE_TEXT.game`, `"Loading"`): until `netIn` (the clock worker's first network reaches demand).
  2. `"data"` (`STAGE_TEXT.data`, `"Loading city data"`): until `job.dataAt` is set (all needed city and market packs downloaded and opened in workers, or downloaded for a cached load).
  3. `"demand"` (`STAGE_TEXT.demand`, `"Calculating trips"`): only trips remain to be calculated to the zone in view's final (`HOLD_ROUND`), at which point `checkHold()` calls `loadComplete()` and releases `clock.setHeld(false)`.
- **What was blocked while the card showed**:
  - **Clicks / tools**: `.loading-card-wrap` already had `pointer-events: none` (only `.load-bar` took hover), and `clockClient.ts` never blocked edits while `clock.held` was true. However, the card sat centred over the map covering the city, and `demandClient.ts` already handles edits submitted during `this.hold` (queueing/restarting the solve in the main pool and releasing `this.hold` when the edited network's final lands).
  - **The clock**: `clock.held` froze `clock.ticking` (`game/clock.ts:95`) until `loadComplete()`. `TopBar.tsx`'s `Clock()` dimmed the time and showed `topBar.clockHeld` ("The clock starts when demand has loaded"), while `Speed()` did not reflect `clock.held`.
  - **Top status / busy tags / zone bars**: `StatusTag.tsx`, `BusyTag.tsx`, and `map/zoneLoading.ts` all checked `loadingCardShown()` and hid themselves while the card was up. `startWatchdog()` in `LoadingScreen.tsx` also gated on `loadingCardShown()`.

## Changes made

- **`app/src/game/loading.ts`**:
  - Added `cardDismissed` signal and `dismissLoadingCard()`, reset on `startGameWait`, `holdForGameLoad`, `loadComplete`, `loadFailed`, and `continueWithoutDemand`.
  - Updated `endCardFull()` to skip flashing the card full for 400 ms when `cardDismissed` is true.
- **`app/src/ui/LoadingScreen.tsx`**:
  - Split `loadingHeld()` (clock held for initial load, whether card is open or closed) from `loadingCardShown()` (`loadingHeld() && !cardDismissed.value`).
  - `startWatchdog()` now uses `loadingHeld()`, so a stuck load still triggers the failure screen if the card was closed.
  - `LoadingCard()` enables the `x` button and header drag only once `up && !!world.value && load?.step === STAGE_TEXT.demand` (stage `"demand"`, after map, network, and packs are in).
  - Dragging uses pointer capture on `.loading-card-head`, positions a non-zoomed `.loading-card-pos` wrapper in viewport px (`position: fixed`), clamps inside `window.innerWidth`/`window.innerHeight` (and on resize), and resets `pos` on each new load (`gameLoadSeq` / `shown`).
- **`app/src/ui/StatusTag.tsx`**:
  - When `loadingCardShown()` is false and `loadProgress` is in `STAGE_TEXT.demand`, shows `recomputing()` (`"Calculating trips here…"` / `"Calculating trips in N cities…"`) with the existing thin `LoadBar` (`load.fraction`) and `statusTag.loadingTip` + `leftText(load.leftMs)` on hover.
- **`app/src/ui/TopBar.tsx`**:
  - `Speed()` now reads `clock.held.value`, dimming the play/speed buttons with `.held` and showing `topBar.clockHeld` ("The clock starts when demand has loaded") on hover in both modern and legacy layouts until the final lands.
- **`app/src/map/zoneLoading.ts`**:
  - Switched from `loadingCardShown()` to `loadingHeld()` so closing the first load's card does not pop a per-zone loading bar into the middle of the city.
- **`app/src/ui/style.css`**:
  - Added `.loading-card-wrap.dragged` (`z-index: 40`), `.loading-card-pos`, `.loading-card.movable`, grab/grabbing cursor on `.loading-card-head`, and `.loading-card-close`.
- **`docs/ui-panels.md`**:
  - Updated the Loading section to document the movable/dismissable card during `"Calculating trips"` and the dimmed play/speed buttons.
