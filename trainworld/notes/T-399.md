# T-399: one commute engine per city

Rule: docs/demand.md, the paragraph on openings ("A city's everyday layer is opened beside its
commutes").

## What changed

`EverydayApi::beside(commutes, header, bin)` opens a city's everyday layer from its commute
`DemandApi`; the worker (`demand.worker.ts`, solving and bare openings) uses it. The everyday
layer's engine is `DemandApi::with_weights`: the cells (`City`), zone grid, gravity (`Arc`s in
`DemandApi`) and routes (already an `Arc` in `Params`) are the commutes'; its own are the zones'
sums of residents and jobs and the weights its access and subzones are built on
(`kernel::build_subzones_on`). `EverydayApi::new` (checks, warm-up) opens a commute engine and
calls `beside`.

What stays twice: station access, subzones and the network's `Service` are built per layer on
every new layout. The subzones cannot be shared: the everyday layer's are weighted by residents and
jobs and the commutes' by commute weights, which moves centroids, the ride groups and which cells
count, so sharing them changes figures. Station access alone is the same for both (positions
only), but it is part of New York's whole 35-45 ms commute set-up on an edit and is dropped after
use (one u32 and one f32 per cell and nearest station), so keeping it to share would cost memory
for at most tens of ms; not done.

## Measured (native, counting allocator)

The T-398 probe (scratchpad `t399/probe.rs`, base tree from HEAD built beside it), one solving
worker, periods 0, 1, 4, free flow and three rounds, then a layout edit, a train-count edit and the
first network again:

| | New York peak | steady | everyday opening holds | nine cities peak | steady |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 197.6 MB | 166.1 MB | 30.9 MB | 350.4 MB | 315.2 MB |
| after | 187.8 MB | 156.3 MB | 21.2 MB | 317.6 MB | 282.4 MB |

About 10 MB a city of New York's size (the second engine was 12.3 MB: cells 6.0, gravity 1.7,
routes 4.3, walk tables 0.3; its own zone sums and weights stay), 33 MB with nine cities open.

Set-up, New York, eight networks alternating with a layout edit, three runs each way: first
network (commutes, everyday, market) 711 ms before and 702 after; the everyday layer's edit set-up
173 and 179 ms (spread 150-205 either way). Solves 23-25 s either way. Openings unchanged within
noise (the everyday opening is its samples).

Every output identical to the byte at all four steps, New York (120 MB dump) and nine cities
(469 MB), in two runs each way.
