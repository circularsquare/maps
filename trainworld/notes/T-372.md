# T-372 (with T-380's parking rule): park-and-ride fit

Rule as built: docs/demand.md 4.3 (Parking, Riding to a station). Design: notes/T-295.md, T-380.md.

## What changed (2026-10-15)

- **Parking** (`Params::park_parts`, `park_mean`, `rail_share_parked` in `sim/src/demand/kernel.rs`):
  T-380's rule on jobs per km² of land (the zone's res-9 cells x 0.1053 km², at most the square),
  the two-logit mix in `mode_choice`'s lanes and `trip::pair_choice` (bit-identical; a chunk with
  nothing to pay takes one exp), the mean over drivers at a ride's station end.
- **Cordons** (`kernel::CORDONS`, `cordon_by_zone`): a charging area per place rather than a
  registry figure per city (DECISIONS). Set in `DemandApi::new` from the pack's origin; a zone's
  charge is its jobs' share inside the area.
- **Merged cells** (T-044): `Zones::ncell` counts a res-8 cell as 7, so land (and the drive's
  density speeds, `Zones::density`) read as before the merge.
- **Fit**: `p_ride` 10 -> 15; `d_drop` 10 and `w_walk` 1.5 kept.
- **Not reached**: the everyday layer (`everyday.rs`, T-433's this round) has its own inline copy
  of the old rule, and the regional layer's car reads `site_park`, the old rule baked into the
  market pack by `pipeline/intercity.py`: both T-440.

Pack state: the fit ran on New York's pack as merged by T-044 (written 19:13, 121,233 cells); the
"before" run is on the same pack and matches T-371's shipped run on the unmerged one (2.996M against
2.997M rail, county error 4.38 against 4.34). `data/work/realnet/cell_county.u8` was rebuilt for
the merged pack (the old one, for 207,766 cells, is `cell_county.pre-1015.u8`).

## The "one station keeps ~30%" check

- **Home end, a small city** (`zones_day --no-everyday` on `research/intercity/corridor_net.py`,
  then with two feeder lines through each of New Haven and Providence stations; scratchpad
  `corridor_feeder.py`; artifacts/trainworld/t372_corridor*.txt): the lone station keeps nearly
  all its corridor riders. New Haven - New York commute legs 2,366 alone, 2,364 with feeders;
  Boston - Providence 3,233 and 3,164; inter-region rail New Haven - New York 2,637 and 2,596. The
  rides already reach the station from the whole city, and a feeder trip adds a change. Surveys
  say 85-90% (notes/T-295.md): the model is at the top of that.
- **Work end, a big city** (realnet on the real network with the subway and PATH removed,
  `nyc_cr_only.json`): rail commutes 3.567M to 1.030M, so New York's commuter rail terminals alone
  keep **29%** of the city's rail commutes (Anita's 30%); a suburban station keeps 38-94% of its
  riders (Hicksville 57%, Ronkonkoma 60%, White Plains 61%, Croton-Harmon 43%, Tarrytown 38%,
  Metropark 88%, New Brunswick 90%, Morristown 94%), since the work end walks only and Midtown's
  jobs beyond a walk of Penn Station and Grand Central are lost without the subway.
