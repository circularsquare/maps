# T-3891

Cause: LineTitle.tsx closes the name/colour editor on any pointerdown outside [data-line-edit]; the Construct button was outside, so the press closed the editor, the panel shifted, and the click was lost.
Fix: ConstructButton carries data-line-edit and keeps focus in the name box (mousedown preventDefault). Shared by bus, ferry, rail and tram panels, so all are fixed.
