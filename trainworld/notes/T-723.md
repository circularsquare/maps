# T-723: whole-country cover, Spain as the pilot (2026-10-20)

Anita approved whole-country cover with Spain first and countryside zones on a curve borrowed from
Japan's fitted zones (T-719 decisions 1 and 5). The orchestrator's calls on the rest of T-719's list
are in DECISIONS.md (2026-10-20). Planning note: notes/T-719.md.

## What was built

1. **Zone rows in the world plan** (`world_plan.py zones --countries ES`, `plan_zones` and
   `zone_groups`): japan_zones.py's method for any unit table. Free units (no plan row, no pack
   holding half their people, GHSL 2025 1 km), under 20 a km² left as dead space, under 100k merged
   smallest first into the longest-border zone of their NUTS-2 region or, for an island, the most
   populous zone of the region within 150 km (this reproduces "the province's largest island" for
   every Spanish island without a province table); a small unit with neither stays alone. Rows
   carry `kind` "zone" and go beside the metro rows; the plan's `zones` block records the rule, the
   dead units and the merges. A metro replan keeps zone rows and their extents.
2. **Runner and plan_config**: a zone row's config gets `kind` "zone", its name and the zone curve;
   register.py, the gate (pop_vs_fua and the main-piece check skipped for a zone) and the market
   already read `kind`, so nothing else changed.
3. **The zone curve**: `"curve": "borrowed_zone"`, `tier0.borrowed_curve(zones=True)`: T-314's
   rule over the 38 fitted Japanese zones only. d^-1.4 exp(-d/16 km), 0.3 points over each zone's
   own (the metros' rule gives d^-1.8 exp(-d/80 km)).
4. **Joint gravity once a batch**: `world_run.py` gates each region as before but solves the joint
   groups once, after the last region (`Run.go`), so a covered country's group is solved once
   instead of after each of its 18 regions. Each group's re-gate compares against a snapshot taken
   just before that group's solve (`Run.solve`).
5. **Run**: all 40 zones shipped; mad bar val sev mal stayed as they were (re-merged by the joint
   solve only, backups in data/work/pre-T723/ with tou bor lis pot and the registry). Registry 176
   zones, 630 KB. every-pack-opens PASSED on Spain's 45 zones (454 s); smoke PASSED (12 checks).

## Retrospective

- **The gate was 72% of the batch** (177 of 247 min), not the 18% T-719 measured. Most of it was
  the station check's live Overpass queries: every mirror answered 502 or 504, and the stack dump
  shows one fetch hung over 15 minutes. Builds were 1-3 minutes a zone, as estimated. Proposal
  T-770: the stations from the extract's caches. After that, a lighter browser check for zones
  (T-719 speedup 8) is the next gain.
- **The deferred join had two faults no small test reached.** A one-row pilot ran no group solve
  that re-gated, so a missing snapshot path showed only at the end of a four-hour run, and then a
  second fault (snapshot shared by groups) only with two groups. Proposal: the pre-flight (T-719
  speedup 2) runs two tiny rows in two joint groups through the whole flow in a scratch root.
- **A crashed re-gate parked a whole country.** The runner treats "no result" as a FAIL of every
  member and parks every new pack (T-773: treat it as a run fault, park only failing members).
  Recovery was cheap because the packs were built and gated: status back to `built`, `--no-browser`.
- **Waiting.** One background watch on the log (ending on the runner's exit or three parks) cost
  one wake-up for the four-hour run; it also stopped me reading a 500-line log of identical parks
  until the run ended.
- **Dead space.** 20 a km² leaves 9 of Spain's 50 provinces out, and four Castilian zones are
  islands in it (T-772). A country with a thinner interior would lose more.
- **Next country**: Portugal or Italy would go the same way (NUTS-3, option a). Germany's Kreise
  need option (b), growing units to a size, which `zone_groups` does not do yet.
