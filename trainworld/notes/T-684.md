## T-684, points
Anita: "introduce 'points'.... for tracking city performance? a point is similar to money, maybe
something like 20 points for a trip + 1 point per km travelled? we can use this for leaderboard,
achievements, maybe even unlocking other cities?" and "for demand coloring... maybe weight by points?"
and, in the long list, "brainstorm more ideas to get a sense of progress... gamification".
- Points per city, earned as riders ride: the boarding account already has each day's trips and
  km per line (`boarding.rs`; fares use the same), so points are a second ledger beside money, read
  per local day: trips x 20 + passenger-km x 1. Per city (a trip counts where it starts, as money's
  region ledger does, `regionMoney.ts`).
- Open: whether points are the only score (money is a resource; points a measure of public good: a
  short trip and a long one differ, a transfer is one trip or two?), whether they decay, and what a
  threshold unlocks (cities need a start rule first: modes in TASKS "Game modes wait").
- Demand colouring by points: a colour ramp on the destination bubbles by the points a cell would
  earn (long trips weigh more) as a third choice beside mode share. Cheap once points exist.
- Built 2026-10-19 (`game/points.ts`, the clock worker's settle; rules in docs/track-and-service.md
  6.4). Journeys come from the solve, not the boarding account: the account (`boarding.rs`) works
  from boardings per route node and platform transfers and never knows a journey, while the solve's
  rail trips per city (`citySummary`) and per regional corridor are one per OD pair choosing rail,
  however many boardings it takes. checks/t684-points.mjs (Mumbai, two lines with a 190 m walk at
  Dadar): 1,722,155 boardings, 200,244 changes, 1,521,911 rail trips, exactly boardings less
  changes; points a day 36.2M = 20 x 1,521,911 + 5,792,996 passenger-km; a whole hour's ledger
  points equal the hour's table. Approximations: a region's trips are exact a day but land by the
  hour of its boardings; passenger-km count where riders board (each boarding the line's average km
  that period), not strictly where the journey began, which differs only on lines crossing a zone
  edge; trips of a region none of whose stations has boardings are lost (riders who all board
  across a boundary). Points are not scaled by the 100x economy.

## T-685, city stamps
Anita: "give a city stamp at hitting 10% ridership? we can make a generic one per continent maybe and
then make custom ones for every big city. they should generally try to use like the same line weight
and style somehow... i am not an artist, i think we can maybe try drawing these as svg and applying
stamp sorta filter?"
- Trigger: a city's rail share (the top bar's goal number: the mode split) reaching 10% once; the
  stamp is kept in the save as earned (a passport, in the Cities tab: a grid of stamps, locked ones
  as outlines).
- Art: one SVG per stamp, drawn by an agent on a shared template: a circle or rounded frame with the
  city name in the game font, one landmark or line drawing in a fixed stroke width (say 2 px at 128 px)
  and one ink colour, then an SVG filter (turbulence plus displacement for an inked-edge look, a
  speckle mask) applied in CSS so all stamps share the look. Generic stamp per continent first (6);
  then custom ones for the biggest cities in batches (an agent can draw 20 a go; Anita reviews a sheet).
- Questions: 10% of what (all trips in the city, as the goal number, or commutes)? A bigger stamp at
  higher shares (25%, 50%)? Where do stamps show (Cities tab, a toast on earning)?

## T-686, sound and mood faces
Anita: "sounds, when very zoomed in and we can see stations, of trains arriving (popping sounds of
people getting on and off?)" and "maybe emoticons of people getting off if they're happy, or red
emoticons if people have been waiting too long".
- Sound: made in the browser with WebAudio (no files to licence): a soft chime for an arrival, small
  pops for people getting on and off, volume by zoom (silent below the zoom where platforms are
  drawn, loudest at street level) and by how many trains are in view (capped). Off by default? A
  Settings switch and volume; muted until the first click (browsers need one anyway).
- Faces: at the zoom where platforms are drawn, small faces rise from a platform as riders leave a
  train: a happy face by default, red when the platform's wait is long (the boarding account
  has each platform's crowd and wait, `complexLive.ts`). Count is sampled (a face per ~20 riders)
  so a busy station stays readable and cheap; a pooled sprite set, drawn in our overlay.
- Questions: sound on by default? Faces: only when riders have waited long, or the happy ones too?

definitely dont wanna show all this stuff (especially when at medium or far out zoom) but theres

public sources (the NIMBY Rails wiki and manual, devlogs, videos described in text, screenshots

crossovers, what its station building asks of the player; then what we could borrow and what would
make the default case harder. Sources only, no code; WebSearch is capped at 200 a session.
