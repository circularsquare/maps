## What the gap is

Rule in docs/ui-building.md ("The gap"), choice in DECISIONS.md 2026-11-02. In short: the two sides
are the track the line router reaches from the line's stops and from the station (`reach_from` in
`sim/src/track/wasm.rs`, the search that makes the station's marker "cut"); the gap runs from the
loose end of either side nearest the other side's track to that nearest point
(`sim/src/track/wasm/gap.rs`).

Why loose end to track: in the real save most lines are separate track that runs side by side with
others for miles (1 and 2/3 along 7th Ave), so the nearest points overall would land anywhere along
them. Loose end to loose end would miss a branch built up to a line but not joined.

## Checks

- `cargo test --lib gap_tests` (made-up track: end to end, a branch short of the main line, a stub far off).
- `node trainworld/checks/t1896-gap.mjs`: the same in Node, and on the real save 40 cut-off stations
  per line for all 109 lines: 4,360 asked, all with a gap, 0.1 ms each on average, slowest 0.6 ms.
- Headless on the real save (scratch script, 2026-11-02): a new line from Whitehall St-South Ferry,
  then a click on Fulton St (track of its own): the gap runs from the 1's South Ferry end to the
  nearest point of Fulton St's track, about 450 m; gone after 5 s; zoomed in on Fulton St the
  camera pans to show both ends at the same zoom. Pictures: `artifacts/trainworld/t1896/`.
