# T-4256: Port PR 30, Auto bus catchments stop at rail-linked stops

Ported Leni-Vienne's PR #30 ("Fix automatic bus catchments extending past rail-linked stops") from `circularsquare/trainsworld` against master (save level 40).

## What was ported

- `trainworld/sim/src/track/bus.rs` (`plan_by`): in automatic stop mode (`!spaced`), include rail-linked stops (`station.is_some()`) as boundary neighbors when computing each street stop's corridor `cover` midpoint, while still only assigning `cover` to non-station stops. Spaced/Hand mode is unchanged.
- `trainworld/docs/demand-kernel.md` ("Access and station-to-station times", item 2): updated the **Bus corridors** rule to state that on automatic lines a street stop's stretch ends halfway to each neighboring stop, including a station stop, while keeping master's `500 m (BUS_REACH_M; 300 until T-3856, 600 until T-4224)`.
- `trainworld/sim/src/track/wasm/bus_tests.rs`:
  - Updated `a_feeder_bus_stops_at_the_station_it_reaches_and_runs_at_its_speed` so the last street stop's corridor ends halfway to the linked rail station instead of at the end of the road.
  - Added PR #30's regression test `an_automatic_bus_terminal_does_not_cover_the_road_beyond_nearby_stations`, adapted for T-3924's 400 m Auto street-stop grid (`STREET_STOP_M`): uses 500 m station spacing (`step = 500.0`, route from `-500.0` to `9000.0`, 19 rail stations) so no intermediate street stops are inserted (`500 / 400 = 1.25 -> n = 1`), verifying the terminal's corridor ends at `-250.0` (halfway to the first station) instead of stretching 9.5 km past all 19 stations.

The moves make sense:
- **Bus 1 / Line 1** share all 10 rail-linked stops (Bus 1 has no street-only stops), so they are unchanged (-2 and -1 riders).
- **Bus 2** is an Auto bus line running alongside **Line 2** with both street stops and rail-linked stops. Previously, Bus 2's street stops stretched their catchments past Line 2's rail-linked stations, pulling in riders near those stations as artificially short walks. Bounding those catchments halfway to the rail-linked stops drops Bus 2 by 3,429 riders (-6.8%, mean ride 0.84 -> 0.89 km) and shifts +835 riders (+11.4%) onto parallel Line 2.
