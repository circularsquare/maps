2026-11-16 (agent doc). Read the registry's 723 Chinese zones against docs/zones.md "Names". 199 were already hand-named; the rest show the unit's name, which the rule allows.

Fixed in app/src/game/zone-names.json (worktree tw/doc, merge with the main tree's own edits):
- Same name on two zones: the smaller one now carries its province ("Taizhou, Jiangsu", "Fuyang, Zhejiang", "Datong, Qinghai", "Bozhou, Guizhou", "Taihe, Jiangxi", "Qidong, Hunan", "Yulin, Guangxi", "Suzhou, Anhui", "Pucheng, Fujian", "Fengcheng, Liaoning", "Yushu, Qinghai", "Binzhou, Shaanxi", "Wei County, Hebei/Henan").
- Two-county compounds got one name: Taixing, Wudi, Zhaoping, Xihe, Suizhou, Xunyang, Shanyang, "Li County", "Dali County".
- Swapped or wrong prefecture: Yaozhou/Qian County were named Binzhou/Tongchuan the wrong way round (yoz now Tongchuan, qax Binzhou, Shaanxi); Wanzhou was shown as Yunyang (now Wanzhou); Suzhou district of Jiuquan was Zhangye (now Jiuquan; Ganzhou district is Zhangye); Changzhou district of Wuzhou was shown Changzhou (now Wuzhou; Cenxi back to Cenxi); Kaiyang was "Bozhou District" (now Kaiyang); Ye County was "Jia County" (now Pingdingshan, Jia County is Jia); Chaling was "You County" (now Chaling); Hengyang County no longer clashes with Hengyang.
- One-syllable counties unnamed: Wei, Yu, Yi, Qi, Hua, Sui, Nan, Lin, You, Jia now end in "County"; Yun is Lincang.
After the edit no two zones of China (or China and elsewhere) show the same name. docs/zones.md "Names" has the province and "County" rules.
