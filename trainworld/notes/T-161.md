# T-161 and T-197: complexes as transfer points, and stations the player links

Anita (2026-10-12): "be able to create interchanges, linking stations into a complex even if they
wouldnt normally" (T-197) and "complexes and manual complexes should allow transfer yeah" (T-161).

## What was built

**Links in the track model** (T-197). `Network::links` (sim/src/track/net.rs): node pairs, smaller
id first; `link`, `unlink`, `is_station`, `links_live` (pairs whose two ends are stations now).
`TrackApi.link_stations`, `unlink_stations`, `station_links` (wasm.rs); `remove_station` drops the
station's links. Not in the undo history: a setting like fares, so no new `Op` and nothing for
undo to rebuild. Saved as an optional block after `LUI1`, `LNK1` (count, then pairs in the dense
ids), written only when there are live links, so a network without links encodes byte for byte as
before and older saves load with none; decoding drops a pair that names no station rather than
refusing the save. `convert_frame` copies them.

**Complexes take the links** (`game/complexes.ts` `buildComplexes(stations, links)`): a link
unions its two stations like the distance rule does. `complexView.ts` keys on them too. The clock
worker's state carries the links (`ClockState.links`, flat node pairs; `Save.links` on the main
thread as station id pairs).

**Demand** (T-161). The clock worker's snapshot gives each constructed station its complex
(`SnapshotStation.complex`): `buildComplexes` over constructed stations only, with the live links.
A blueprint platform carries nobody, so it bridges nothing for demand (it can for display). The
demand client packs it as `DemandNetwork.complex` (one number per station; a station without one is
its own), and the worker calls `set_complexes` on every population (`DemandApi`, `EverydayApi`,
`IntercityApi`) before `set_network`. `api::demand_groups` carries the snapshot complexes over to
the demand stations they were merged into (two complexes sharing a demand station join), giving
`Service::group` and `kernel::Network::st_group`. `kernel::build_graph` adds, for every route node,
a walk edge to every other demand station of its station's complex beyond the 300 m walk (those
within it have their edge already), costed like every walking transfer: `xfer_min(d) * w_xfer`,
d the centre-to-centre distance. A list of the wrong length means no complexes.

**Why walk edges, not one merged station.** Merging a complex into one demand station would put its
catchment at one mean point (a 1 km link would move both stations' riders to the middle), make
every change in it free (a 1 km link a teleport), and lose the platform-to-platform transfer
counts the complex map draws (T-176 keys them by route node, but a merged station's walks would
be gone). Edges keep each station's catchment, charge the walk, and the T-176 counting picks the
walks up with no change (`assign_with_transfers` already counts a route node's riders leaving to
any station other than the destination). The trip explorer shows the change as a walk leg
(trip.rs needs no change: a walk leg's minutes are its cost over `w_xfer`).

**Why no extra fixed penalty.** Every boarding after the first already pays `transfer_min` (5
perceived minutes) plus its wait, at one platform or after a walk. The walk adds distance x 1.3
detour at 1.2 m/s, weighted 1.39: 3 perceived minutes for 120 m, 12 for 470 m, 25 for 1 km. A
fixed extra for complex walks would make two platforms 120 m apart jump in cost the moment they
became a complex, and would differ from the 300 m walks for no reason.

**The 100 m merge and 300 m walks stay.** The merge is what a station is for access; the 300 m
walk covers stations near each other but not in one complex (Times Sq to the 8 Av platforms).
Both unchanged.

## How demand moved on the real New York save

`cargo test --release --features demand complexes_real_network -- --ignored --nocapture` (needs
`node trainworld/notes/t161-complexes.mjs` first: the T-007 save loaded in the track WASM, its
complexes from `game/complexes.ts`, written to `../artifacts/trainworld/t161/`). Commutes and the
everyday purposes, every period, free flow and three crowding rounds, with and without complexes.

**Nothing moves.** Rail trips (commutes 3,459,744, everyday 4,881,687), boardings (14,651,518 a
day), transfers (6,310,074, 1,570,838 walking between demand stations), every line's boardings and
every segment are identical to the last digit, as are the changes inside Penn Station (566,227 a
day), Grand Central (342,792) and Times Sq (466,507). The reason: the 100 m merge is single
linkage, so a chain of platforms is already one demand station. The save's 855 complexes are 873
demand stations; only 16 complexes span more than one, and their 20 pairs of demand stations are
at most 269 m apart, inside the 300 m walk. Penn Station's 24 platforms (centres spread 218 x 261 m)
are 2 demand stations, Grand Central's 16 are 3, Times Sq's 10 are 2. So the premise in T-146's
notes ("a long chained complex like Penn Station is several demand stations") was wrong for this
save: the merge, not the walk limit, holds it together, and has all along.

So T-161 matters for complex members more than 300 m apart: platforms end to end (two 400 m
platforms 30 m apart have centres 430 m apart), long chains a player builds, and every link.

## The link in the real app (browser check)

`notes/t197-browser.mjs` (Playwright, own profile in the scratchpad, own Vite on 6831 with HMR and
watching off; T-007 save, round 3). It selects a station, clicks "Link to another station",
clicks the other station on the map, waits for demand to solve again, reads the transfers across
the link and searches trips along the biggest change across it for the one whose riders use the
link most; then saves to a file, unlinks in the inspector, reads the same trip without the link,
loads the file (the link comes back) and unlinks. `PAIR=a,b` links given stations, `TAG=name`
names the outputs. No page errors, GL error 0 in both runs.

- **5 Av/42 St-Bryant Pk (the 7, in a complex with the B/D/F/M) and Grand Central (Metro-North),
  471 m apart** (`PAIR=286,1297 TAG=grandcentral`; the nearest members are 373 m apart, so no walk
  joined them before). Changes between the two sides: 0 a day before, 8,437 after (F to Harlem
  line 550, M to Harlem 449, F to New Haven 434, D to New Haven 379...). The complex becomes 23
  stations; its map draws the dashed link ("You linked 5 Av/42 St-Bryant Pk with Grand Central
  464 m away"); the changes across it are too small beside Grand Central's own (the 7 with the
  New Haven line 23k) to make the 8 curves. Trip explorer, Carroll St (F) to Fleetwood (Harlem
  line), night: with the link 74% of the train riders take the F to Bryant Park, walk 10.9 min to
  Grand Central and ride Metro-North, and the train's chance is 31.8%; without it they go by Jay
  St-MetroTech and the 4/5 and the chance is 29.4%. The 7 at 5 Av loses about 5k boardings a day
  (60k to 55k) to riders who now walk instead of riding it one stop. The save file holds the
  `LNK1` block and the link comes back on loading it. Results `../../artifacts/trainworld/notes-outputs/t197-browser-grandcentral.json`.
- **Lexington Av/53 St/51 St (the 6) and 5 Av/53 St (E/F), 440 m** (`PAIR=195,511
  TAG=lexington`, the busiest pair of small complexes the search finds): 0 changes across before,
  415 a day after, and the complex map draws two of them as curves (F at 5 Av to the 6 at
  Lexington Av, 120 and 107 a day). No searched trip uses it much: the existing Lexington Av/53 St
  passage already joins the E and the 6. Results `../../artifacts/trainworld/notes-outputs/t197-browser-lexington.json`.

## Known limits

- A blueprint station can bridge two constructed ones into one complex for display but not for
  demand. Constructing a station that serves no line sends no new snapshot, so a complex it
  completes reaches demand at the next service change.
- Linking is outside Ctrl+Z; undoing a station's removal does not bring its links back.
- The walk is centre to centre: for two long platforms the real walk can be shorter or longer.
- T-200 (walks start at route nodes, so a strategy can board and at once walk) applies to complex
  walks too; it is small and left to T-200.
- Linked stations are not drawn joined on the main map, only in the complex map (dashed) and by
  every member being drawn selected in complex view.
