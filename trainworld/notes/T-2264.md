Effective time is how long a trip feels in minutes. Fares and other expenses are converted into minutes.
Train trip effective time
- Walking to station: 1.5 min / min
- Waiting for train: depends on train frequency
- Riding train
- Boarding/disembarking train: 2 minutes each
- Transfer: 2 minutes, plus walking time and waiting for next vehicle
- Fare: conversion rate depends on average income in the country
Car trip effective time
- Driving: 1.33 min / min
- Parking: 7 minutes
- Parking prices: scales a lot with population density, up to max of 80 minutes
- Car ownership cost: high fixed cost, scales partially with trip length and average income in the country
Walking trip effective time is just 1.5 min / min
People take into account effective time of all modes, and decide between them with soft thresholds.
