## The causes, from the code

- An edit's answer waited for `commit_with` (world.rs) whole: the edit and its checks, then `before_edit` over every
  running train, the service rebuild, the settle, departures, retime, the undo record's two `line_rates_all`. Only
  the first part can refuse; the rest works out trains. editmeasure's table (notes/T-4267-measure.md): checks 5-15 ms,
  the rest 20-480 ms.
- In a run, the next click waited for the previous follow-up run whole in one block: any message but a preview, the
  delete question or an edit going ahead ran it first (clock.worker.ts `processMessage`), and demand's riders and
  transfers and the page's income, points and region shares come all the time while trips are calculated
  (0.4-2.5 s blocks, editmeasure).
- A station on constructed track was charged the re-pricing of the two pieces its split leaves (`built_after -
  built_before`, $0.001 to $220), so it counted as paid and its answer waited for its rows; the next click waited
  with it.

## What changed

- Rust (world.rs): `commit_with` split into `apply_checked` (the edit, its checks) and `services_after` (everything
  that works out trains), with what the services read first in `Ahead`; behaviour unchanged for every other edit.
  `commit_local` applies a station or a track delete and checks it; where `moves_no_train` holds (no running line
  uses the track, nodes or stations it touched, nor a bus line or way back it reached, no crossing made or removed) and
  it set no line's trains, rates, rows or twin (`sets_rates`, Review 4), it goes on the undo history and its services are owed (`Owed`); otherwise it is rolled back (as a trial is) and goes
  the whole way. `settle_owed` works them out for every owed edit at once; every other commit, undo, redo and
  `trial_with` runs it first; `reload` drops it. The first owed edit's undo record takes what the services moved
  (exact undo); later ones' records are marked `unknown` and settle as an edit does when undone.
- Rust (wasm/edits.rs): `run_answered` for `station_ops` (plain and free stations) and `delete_edge`; both charge
  no construction (`run_free`'s price) on either path. `settle_owed`, `owes`, `take_settled_demand` and
  `set_answer_first` (off: the whole path, for tests) exported. What owed services touched is noted as the edit's
  (touched rows, `service_moved`, merged into `last`).
- The station ghost's money check (`attempt`, dry) counts only trains too, as the click now does.
- Worker: the follow-up's first piece settles what is owed, or takes in what something else worked out of it, and
  forgets the drawn lines it moved (clockFollowUp.ts `settleOwed`, trips with the state where a line moved for demand); autosave waits while anything is owed. The page's money figures (income, points, region
  shares, a charge's split) and the clock pass a waiting follow-up; riders and transfers are kept in order until no
  follow-up waits (`keepUntilState`, `whenStateWent`).
- Tests: wasm/tests/answer_tests.rs (answered edits end as the whole path does, edit for edit, through undo and redo;
  the next edit works out what is owed; a station on a running line's track goes the whole way; a station on
  constructed track charges nothing). Eight tests that read services right after a station on track no line runs on
  now call `settle_owed` first (in scope, bus, keeps, changed, perdir and twin tests), as the worker does before its state.

## Review (Gemini's, before landing; answered by agent answerfix, 2026-11-25)

Gemini's review: artifacts/trainworld/gemini/t4272/review.md. Each finding checked against the code.

1. **Old game's riders and transfers applied to a new game: real, narrow.** Riders and transfers kept while a follow-up
   waits were put at the queue's tail when its state went. A new game, an import, a slot opened, a flip or a panic's
   rebuild that ran that follow-up first queued them after its own restart, and neither message carries a game, so
   the old game's riders went into the new model (master ran them before the restart, which clears them). Only within
   the follow-up's few hundred ms. Fixed: each kept message carries the game it came in and is dropped at its turn when
   that game is gone (clockFollowUp.ts `keepUntilState`, clock.worker.ts `whenStateWent`), which is what master's order
   came to. Within one game they run after the message that ran the follow-up; demand's results coming after an edit
   is the usual case, so that order is harmless.
2. **Drawn lines not forgotten after `settle_owed`: real.** The edit's `forget()` runs right after its apply, before its
   services; the lines the owed settle then moved (planned rail lines through `take_moved_lines`, bus lines through
   `dirty_lines`) were forgotten by nothing, so the state kept their old samples and left them out of `touched.paths`
   until the next edit: a planned line stayed drawn over track just deleted. Fixed: the follow-up's settle piece calls
   `forget()` after `settle_owed`, which puts what the services touched into the last edit's result (or makes one after
   a preview, which leaves none). Game run: the station on a planned line's track and the delete of track under it
   were each answered at once, and the next state had `touched.paths: [0]` with the line broken.
3. **What a refused edit worked out was lost: real, and wider.** Every world path that runs `settle_owed` first left its
   result in `owed_done`: an edit refused for money (never read), one refused for its problems (rows noted, demand
   dropped), an undo or redo with nothing to take, and a trial with services (the Build tab's quote, `blueprint_cost_items`).
   The follow-up's piece then saw `owes()` false and skipped the rows, trips and snapshot; a preview's `done(Ok)` would
   have taken it and thrown it away (previews clear `last`). Fixed in the one place every path reaches:
   `TrackApi::settle_owed` settles anything owed and then takes whatever `owed_done` holds, whoever worked it out; only a
   successful edit takes it into its own result, in `settle` (no longer in `done`, which previews call). Test
   `what_a_refused_edit_or_a_trial_worked_out_still_reaches_settle_owed` (a money refusal, the quote's trial).
4. **Twin lines read after `twin_ops`: real cause; the divergence a test found was small.** The answered edit's own apply
   runs `twin_ops` and `way_reroutes` (`apply_rerouted`), and `settle_owed` read `Ahead` and `line_rates_all` after
   them, where the whole path reads them before. With the new gate off, a station that gives a planned line its twin,
   then undo, ended unlike the whole path in the removed twin's departure phases (its freed slot); trains and rates
   matched only because the settle moved none of the parent's. Running lines were already covered (`lines_touched`
   holds `t.lines`). Fixed: an answered edit whose inverse shows it set a line's trains, headways, rates, rows or twin,
   or made or removed a line, goes the whole way (`sets_rates` in world.rs, and any `schedules` or `weekend` touch); a
   line moved through a new station keeps all of that and stays answered. Test `a_station_making_a_twin_goes_the_whole_way`
   (a count line and a headway line, through three undos).
5. **Rows sent before the settle: real, brief.** An answered edit with no patch (or no click) is answered with its world;
   when another edit went ahead, `standAside` sent rows built before its services. Fixed: those rows settle first. Its
   cousin, a save while services are owed (only a game left while an edit waits for its ground could), now settles
   first too (`saveBytes`, `saveParts`: `settleOwedNow`).

Gemini's note on `sendMoney` from `regionShares` and `chargeShares` while owed: not reachable as stale. Owed services move
only lines that run no trains, which have no running cost, and an edit reaching a running bus line goes the whole way.

**Found in the game run, not fixed here (for the orchestrator):** `?patch=check` flagged "patched rows differ from the
state's: nodes 3: line" after deleting the track between a planned line's middle and end stations. The answered
delete's patch is built before its services: `movedPaths` reads the line as still ok (`lines_info` is the service's),
and the end station's platform line is ordered by the last state's line counts, while the state after the settle has
the line broken. The click map holds that row only until the state (16 ms in the run). Fix choices: `movedPaths` gives
no patch while a noted line's services are owed (those edits then answer with their world, as on master), or the
owed services of planned lines are worked out before the patch.
