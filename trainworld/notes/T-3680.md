# T-3680 Community cards "Could not load this network"

Cause: each card asks the server for its drawing (`GET /trainworld/community/<id>/<rev>`), 12 at once per page. The
server's per-IP bucket for drawings is 60 at once, then 4 a second (shonei-server `registerTwCommunityRoutes`). Tab
switches and Shuffle fetch 12 new drawings each (and drawings of a page the player already left are still asked
for), so after about 5 pages the bucket is empty and the rest answer 429. The card took one failure as final
(`useDrawing` has no retry), which is why clicking a card afterwards loaded at once: the bucket had refilled.
Not a client timeout (patientFetch gives up after 120 s of silence).

Repro (live API from Vite 6152, `?acct=live`, GETs only; scratch `repro2.mjs`): Biggest, Random, then Shuffle x8 at
0.9 s apart.
- Before: from the 4th page on, 6-7 of 12 cards on "Could not load this network"; 45 responses of 429 in 10 clicks.
- After: 0 error cards at every step and after settling; 4 responses of 429 in total, each retried unseen.
- 5 plain tab clicks 6 s apart (`repro.mjs`): 0 failures before and after, so slow clicking never showed it.

Fix (app only, `game/community.ts` `loadDrawing`/`releaseDrawing`, `ui/Community.tsx` `useDrawing`): drawings are
asked for four at a time; a 429 (also a network error or 5xx) holds the queue for 1 s and requeues the drawing, up to
10 tries; a card that unmounts before its turn is dropped from the queue unasked. "Could not load this network" now
shows only after the tries run out or for a 4xx other than 429.
No string changes. Server limit untouched (a deploy waits for Anita).
