# T-572, T-573, T-574: the three faults that parked five packs

2026-10-16. The pack gate (notes/T-538.md) parked Shanghai, Mumbai, Seoul, Berlin and Lagos. Three
rules in `pipeline/tier0.py` fix them for every tier 0 build; the five packs were rebuilt and all
five ship. Measure script: `notes/t572_measure.py` (script removed 2026-10-24, in git history) (output `../artifacts/trainworld/T-573-measure.json`).
Logs: `../artifacts/trainworld/T-572-*.log`; gate results before in `T-572-gate-before/`, after in
`pack_gate/`. Backup of everything the rebuild overwrote: `data/work/pre-T572/`.

## T-573: the height weight, capped at 40 m

`HEIGHT_CAP_M` 40: the mean non-residential height in the GHSL term stops at 40 m, so above it a
cell's weight grows with its volume alone. Berlin's cell was the Treptowers at Elsenbrücke (52.4942,
13.4572): 70 m mean height on 13,500 m² of surface, (7.0)² = 48 times its volume, 5.8% of Berlin's
whole GHSL term from 0.15% of its volume; the second was the Tropical Islands hangar (30 m).

Measured as T-460 (jobs misplaced between 2 km zones; London's proxy against BRES spread by today's
proxy, Toronto's against the real pack's StatCan jobs, the res-9 copy; Berlin its own rule, Zensus

40 m is the best height cap on Toronto (0.26 points better) and leaves London unchanged (its
tallest cell is 35 m). A 1% share cap scored a little better on both, but it depends on how much
of a region the outline takes: a small pack's one real centre is several percent of its term, and
the Japanese prefecture zones' top cells are 4-36%. A height is the same everywhere. Heights over
40 m are rare (the 99.9th percentile is under 50 m in every pack but Seoul's 54 m); 19 of the 49
packs with tier 0 signals have a cell over it, the tallest Tokyo 84 m, Seoul 83, Berlin 70, Osaka
69, Mumbai 68 and Shanghai 65.

## T-574: one school site, and one cell's sites

`SCHOOL_SITE_MAX` 3,000 pupils a site (about a large secondary school) and `SCHOOL_CELL_MAX` 6,000
for the sites of one pack cell together, the excess to the other sites in proportion as T-387's
hospitals; pupils the sites cannot hold go to school where they live, by children
(`tier0.school_site_pupils`). Sizing by the square root of area is unchanged.

The first rebuild had the site cap alone, and Mumbai still failed: 24,723 pupils in one cell at
Vile Parle, eight school polygons of the Parle Tilak campus each at 3,000 (Dadar's IES campus 21,617
the same way). Hence the cell cap.

OSM maps few schools in Lagos and Mumbai (861 and 803 sites for 5.0M and 6.2M pupils; the median
site already took 5,200 and 7,100), so every site there is at the cap:

## The gate (`--browser`, each against a snapshot less the other four rebuilt packs)

All five ship. FAILs before: sha, mum, seo, ber top cell jobs; mum, lag school cell pupils. WARNs
after: school cell pupils (sha 5,200, mum 8,090, seo 6,000, lag 6,953; WARN is over 5,000); Seoul's
top cell share 0.12% is under the fleet's lowest (the range check). Other packs unchanged: 0 for
all five. Water points now run on Seoul and Berlin too (1.0), with fresh water caches.

## The rebuild

As docs/adding-a-city.md 4, 6 and 7: `build_city.py`, `routes.py` (`--extracts`, as each pack's
header named), `merge_cells.py`, `nocar.py`, `everyday_rates.py`, `register.py --market --trips`,
`routes.py --check` (all current); for the cell cap, `merge_cells.py --undo`, tier 0's schools step
(`education_osm`), and the same steps after it. Builds 65-280 s each; routes 1-5 minutes.

The tree's `sim/` stopped compiling partway (another task mid-edit in `demand/kernel.rs`), so Berlin's
walk stage, Lagos's routes and the steps after ran on a copy of the committed crate through
`TW_SIM_DIR`, which `market_zones.py`, `zones.py` and `pack_gate.py` now honour as `routes.py` and
`build_city.py` did.

Downloads, deleted once verified: Geofabrik South Korea and Brandenburg (extracts gone after
`routes.py`; their `.points/.areas/.water.fgb` and `.ways2.npz` deleted), Nigeria's extract for
routes (its new `.ways2.npz` deleted; the older Nigeria `.fgb` caches were there before and stay).
