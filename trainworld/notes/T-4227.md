# T-4227: weekend periods with trains but no services

Player report (11-22, v0.4.5, board g157, thread 1795339542-5daeeac8): "Weekend trains not running at
times when they have allocated trains. e.g. being allocated 4 trains for midday but not having any
services scheduled". No save or diagnostics download attached; 14 lines, Australian currency.

## Cause

Where a line's hours put two of its rows into one demand period (moved hours, T-3542 stage 2: midday
11-15 inside the 10-16 period, or the morning from 5), that period's slot runs the rows' time-weighted
rate and each row's piece of it is shared out by what each row's trains run (`Network::place_tph`).
On weekends the share-out read the **weekday** trains and headways (`trains_of`, `auto_of`, the
weekday rates and pacing), never the weekend ones. So:

- a row with no weekday trains in that period ran **no weekend departures** there, whatever its
  weekend count (the report's case: weekend midday 4, weekday midday 0);
- the row beside it ran the whole period's weekend rate inside its own hour (45 departures in one
  hour for a 2-train row in the probe);
- the schedule window's weekend headway for that row (sim `line_rows`, the same share-out) showed
  none, beside its 4 trains.

At the default hours no period is shared and weekends ran right. In 0.4.5 already (hours landed
11-19, 875b00701; 0.4.5 published 11-20).

Probe on master's sim through the WASM API (`TrackApi`, the calls the game makes; the line_api
fixture, 2 trains every row), departures of run 0 per local window:

| case | Tue midday | Sat midday |
|---|---|---|
| 5 rows, default hours, weekend midday 4 (weekday 2) | 49 | 75 |
| 5 rows, weekday midday 0, weekend 4 | 0 | 75 |
| 5 rows, weekday all 0, weekend midday 4 only | 0 | 75 |
| own 4 rows, weekday midday 0, weekend 4 | 0 | 75 |
| 3 rows, weekday none, weekend high 4 | 0 | 75 (and morning, afternoon) |
| weekend set first, then weekday midday 0 | 0 | 75 |
| headway line, midday none, weekend counts 4 / headway 15 min | 0 | 75 / 20 |
| share 50% of weekday midday 4 | 75 | 49 |
| **own hours 7-11-15-20, weekday midday 0, weekend 4 (Sat 11:30-14:30)** | 0 | **0** (10-11: 45, 15-16: 43) |
| game 4 rows, midday 9-16 (midday period not shared) | 0 | 45 |

## Conditions listed (a period with trains allocated and no services)

1. A period with no hours: not possible (every stretch 2 h at least, `cuts_ok`).
2. Weekend-only rows (weekday 0, weekend more): run, at the default hours (table).
3. A count rounded or capped away: a percent rounds counts up (never to 0 from 1 up); the panel's cap
   is one past what the track lets run, at least one step. Not met.
4. A line with weekend rows of its own: runs (table).
5. Not rebuilt after a change: a weekend-only edit settles the weekends; the order of edits does not
   matter (table). Not met.
6. Panel and departures from different sources: the window's weekend headway is `line_rows`, the
   departures the lattice; both read the same share-out, so both were wrong together under moved
   hours (fixed together). Separately, the board and the chart show the next 24 hours from now only,
   so on a weekday they show weekday departures: a row with weekday 0 looks empty there whatever its
   weekend count. Not a bug; T-3346's planned weekday/weekend tabs on the board cover it.
7. **Moved hours sharing a period between rows: the cause above.**
8. A superholiday's days (AU: Christmas) run the weekday trains on their weekend days, by design
   ("Long holiday travel days run the weekday trains").

## Fix

- `DayFleet` (net.rs): what a line runs on one kind of day as the share-out reads it: trains and
  headways per place, rates and pacing per slot. `Network::fleet` is the fields as they stand
  (weekdays, or what a weekend settle or placing swaps in); `Network::place_tph_in` and
  `running_borrowed` take a fleet; `place_tph` is unchanged for every weekday caller.
- `Network::weekend_places_by` (net/service.rs): the weekend counts per place, moved out of
  `TrackWorld::weekend_counts_in` (which now calls it with its track-clamped headway counts), so the
  settle and the departures read one rule.
- `Service::rest_fleet` / `fleet_on` (service.rs, beside `lattice`, away from the boarding and
  left-behind code): a line running weekend rates of its own shares a mixed slot's weekend rate by its
  weekend fleet; `lattice` (the weekend pieces in `hours_of`) and `line_rows` (the window's weekend
  headway) both use it, only on lines whose hours mix.
- The lie: a headway line at a weekend percent counts the trains its weekend headway takes over the
  round trip, not cut to what the track lets run (the settle's count); on full track the slot's rate
  is cut instead and shared out by those, as weekdays are.
- Weekdays are untouched to the bit (the weekday fleet is the one read before), and so is every
  line at the default hours.

Test: `hours_tests::t4227_a_shared_period_runs_each_rows_weekend_trains` (fails on master: Saturday's
midday has no departures). Both Rust suites pass (752 and 982).
