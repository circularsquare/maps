and one `join` a wave. Russia, Belarus and Ukraine stay held (Anita).

## The plan (zone rows, `world_plan.py zones`)

Calls (DECISIONS 2026-10-20): under 20 a km² dead space; no country modules; the zone curve; and,
from 22:15, **Anita's floor: zones of at least about 0.5M**, merged within NUTS-2 (or the country
when the region has no neighbour left), islands and enclaves that cannot join kept as they are.
The first plan (18:00, 507 rows: NUTS-3 as they are in most countries, 0.3M grown zones in DE BE NL
GB AT CH SI) built wave 1 before the floor came; its 184 built or gated rows keep their size.

**Code** (`world_plan.py`): `zone_groups(units, free, region, floor)`: smallest first until each
zone holds ZONE_FLOOR (500k); under 100k the longest border in the region; from 100k the smallest
neighbour keeping both within ZONE_CAP (1.5M), else the smallest (the longest border gave Bosnia one
zone of 1.39M: Sarajevo, Zenica, Mostar); with no neighbour left in the region, one of another
region (Osttirol, Ticino, Brčko); islands within 150 km under 100k, within 10 km above (the Isle of
Wight stays alone); what can join nothing stays. `plan_zones` regroups only `planned` zone rows and
keeps the rest (`kept` in the plan's `zones` record); loads a country no metro reached (not in
state.pkl) from NUTS or geoBoundaries and records its unit source; GISCO's Kosovo (`XK`) maps to
XKX; `zone_name` drops ", Kreisfreie Stadt", "Arr. " and the like, and a second zone of one name
takes its two largest units' names ("Leipzig, Nordsachsen"); `iso2s` no longer lets Northern
Cyprus's -99 overwrite CY; `make_id` skips Windows device names (con, prn, aux, nul). Dry runs on a
scratch copy of the plan (scratchpad t788 `dryzones.py`). Logs: `../artifacts/trainworld/t788-zones.log`
(first plan), `t788-zones-floor.log` (the floor).
