# T-4152: "Track has to leave a junction along the existing track" refusing sensible joins

Agent junctionmsg, 2026-11-23.

## The rule

`Issue::NodeHeading` (`net/validate.rs` `check_node`): at every node, each edge end must leave along the node's
heading or its reverse, within `HEADING_TOL` (0.003 rad, 0.17 degrees). It is real: a node is a point where trains
pass straight through; a kink there is a corner with no curve. It stays as it was.

## Why it misfired

A route drawn between two existing ends with no corner clicked between them gets a lead-in PI at each end
(`lead_in`), each aimed at the *other end's position*. An end the other lies almost straight ahead of (within
0.002 rad) gets no lead-in; the other end, turned a little, does, and that lead-in is the real next vertex. So the
straight from the first end ran to the other's lead-in, off its heading by more than 0.003 rad: refused as
NodeHeading. The more nearly in line the two tracks were, the likelier: exactly the joins a player expects to work.
Blueprint or built made no difference.

Sweep on master (scratch test, double track, A's end heading east, B's start ahead by a gap, offset sideways by dy,
its track turned by rot): 26 of 288 refused, every one NodeHeading, all with dy of 1 m or less and rot of 1-5
degrees (also 3-60 degrees at dy 0); both ways round. Lateral offsets of 5 m and more always joined. The other
refusals in the wider sweep were real (RadiusTooSmall on an S-bend 20 m long, or B behind A).

## Fix

`end_leads` (`wasm.rs`): with no corner between, an end that gets no lead-in toward the other end is aimed again
at the other's lead-in. Used by the drawing tool (`draw.rs` `route_op_as`), street tram ends (`tram_ends`) and
the node drag's lead-ins (`edits.rs`, the same pairing, practically unreachable there). After it: 0 of 288
refused (`wasm/t4152_tests.rs`, blueprint and built, both ways).

In game (port 6168, new NYC game, Bed-Stuy): double track west to east, a second one 850 m further on starting in
line and turned 5 degrees, then the join from the first's end to the second's start: placed, 4 nodes, 3 edges.
Screenshot: artifacts/trainworld/review/junctionmsg.png.

## Message

Where it can still fire (a shape that really kinks at a node), "junction" confused players at plain track ends. en.json `issues.nodeHeading`:
"Track has to leave a junction along the existing track." -> "Track has to line up with the track it joins."
Translations go stale through `sources/` as usual.
