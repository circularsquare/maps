# T-4079 Bus stop connections drawn and timed by kind

Anita, 11-23: "dense bus network auto-connections look kinda silly / web-like sometimes. could we have bus stops
have a smaller autoconnect radius? and allow making manual connections between bus stops and other stations."
Then: dashed for bus stop connections and BRT auto connections, the white connector for a built passage; bus
stops take no passage, BRT stations do; "lets actually make bus passages save no time, and raw connections lose
1 minute ... lets just say that building a passage (for bus stations) saves one minute."

Manual connections to a bus stop (her second ask) are split off to T-4083: a bus stop has no identity that
survives an edit or a save, which T-4080 (naming stops) needs too.

## What changed

- **Radius** (sim `demand/network.rs` `BUS_WALK_M` = 150, `Network::walk_reach_m`; app `BUS_STOP_WALK_M`): a
  walk with a bus stop (a demand station that is a bus stop only) at either end reaches 150 m; between stations
  300 m as before. Trip start walks read the same list. A complex still walks however far.
- **Minute** (`CONNECTION_MIN` = 1 real minute, `Network::connection_min`, in `build_graph`'s `walk_min`): a walk
  with no passage touching a bus stop or a BRT station (`upgrades::BRT_STATION` in the demand station's bits)
  takes a minute more. Passages unchanged (2 m/s, straight line). Train-to-train unchanged. In real minutes,
  inside the walk edge, so the walking rule (`walk_column`) sees it too and the perceived cost is 1.39 minutes.
- **Look**: the bus layer's white connectors (T-3890, places one place apart or a walk apart) are now the
  dashed transfer line, lit at full ink with the hovered or selected stop's place; the white connector machinery
  (`FLAG_LINK`, passes 3-4, `u_link*`) is gone. A BRT station's joins by the complex distance rule with no passage
  (`brtJoins`) leave the stations' white connector (zoomed out, `syncGroups`) and bridge (zoom 14+,
  `complexPassages` caller) and are dashed by the bus layer from zoom 13. A built passage stays white.
- **Passage at a bus stop**: picking a bus stop while placing a passage says "Bus stops can't have passages."
  and keeps picking. At a BRT station the passage button's hover has "Saves one minute on each transfer through
  it" as its third line.

## Picking the radius

Fixture: the real NYC save plus a dense bus grid over Park Slope / Prospect Heights (scratch `grid.js`): 13
north-south lines 250 m apart, 10 east-west lines 400 m apart, two diagonals, 25 lines, 412 stops, built.
`measure.js` groups stops and stations into demand's places (100 m chained) and counts, per radius, walks
between two places where either has no train station (the kernel's, at merged centres), the ones the map draws
(a line one has and the other has not, no bus line at both), and the train stations still reached by a bus-only
place, of the 30 that have one within 300 m.

| radius | walks | drawn | walks a place | stations reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 m | 260 | 61 | 1.58 | 30/30 |
| 250 m | 136 | 20 | 0.82 | 22/30 |
| 200 m | 42 | 8 | 0.25 | 9/30 |
| 175 m | 18 | 7 | 0.11 | 4/30 |
| 150 m | 16 | 7 | 0.10 | 3/30 |
| 125 m | 4 | 0 | 0.02 | 1/30 |
| 100 m | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0/30 |

(165 bus-only places, 117 train stations in the box. Nearest bus-only place to each of the 30 stations, m: 113
129 149 163 188 188 191 194 200 200 202 203 211 219 222 224 226 226 227 232 236 243 254 258 265 272 289 292 296
296.)

Picked **150 m**: the web between parallel lines a block or two apart is gone (walks 260 -> 16), while a stop
beside a station (within 150 m) still connects. The stations further off are mostly on a bus route's 200 m
station reach already, so the route stops at them (the stop is the station, no walk). 200 m kept the parallel
lines 200 m apart joined (Barcelona's two-block spacing) for little gain; 100 m is demand's merge, so every walk
would be gone.

## Riders, before and after

The real NYC save has no bus lines and no BRT station (109 rail lines), so nothing in it changes. Measured on the
grid fixture (scratch `grid.save`, `riders.js`), weekday, the same save loaded fresh on the old and the new WASM:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| bus riders a day (25 lines) | 505,718 | 550,703 (+8.9%) |
| transfers at bus stops | 71,358 | 80,831 |
| rail trips | 13,104,957 | 13,104,395 |
| Grid 22 (busiest, a diagonal) | 35,696 | 38,094 |
| Grid 15 (quietest) | 12,133 | 13,711 |

Every grid line gains (1-18%). Riders who walked between parallel lines a block apart now ride a crosstown
bus and change, hence more boardings and more transfers at stops; rail is flat (-0.004%).
