# T-3109 One click per job while stops are edited (and T-3110, T-3063)

Agent loopclick, tree `.tw-trees/loopclick`, 2026-11-10. Anita's words and screenshots: TASKS.md T-3109 line
(artifacts/trainworld/t3109-anita-*.png).

## What changed

- **Exact edits is gone** (T-3023/3024/3079/3080/3089 UI and sim). No toggle, no numbered list, no "+", no map
  numbers or empty circles. Sim: `LineData::manual`, `Network::line_manual`, `set_manual`, `line_manual`,
  `manual_folds`, `order_fault_line`, `drop_visit`, `manual_stops`, `edited_for`, `route_hops_turn` and
  `find_path_turn` removed (`route_hops`/`find_path_side` always cost a turn `REVERSAL_SEARCH_S`). The free turn at a
  listed stop was only for manual lines, so it went too: **T-3100 is moot**. `manual_tests.rs` removed;
  `save::tests::a_manual_out_and_back_folds_on_load` added.
- **Old saves**: the `LMN1` block is still read and no longer written. Each listed line whose stops and path read the
  same both ways folds into its open line (`save::fold_manual`, both ways, `hand` off); any other stays the normal
  line with those stops.
- **The stop list** uses `turnedLine` while stops are edited too (the "a hand-ordered line's rows stay one per pass
  while editing" rule is dropped), so a turned line never lists every visit. On Anita's Line 1: 18 rows for 28 stops.
  This also fixed **T-3110**: Line 1a beside Line 1 (and Line 1 beside Line 1a) draws the same parallel strip edited
  or not (`checks/t3109-loopclick.mjs`, t3109-line1-related-editing.png). The per-pass list was what drew the other
  line across every row.
- **Train/tram stop markers** (`map/stopMarkers.ts`, `map/tools.ts`): a click adds or removes a stop, nothing else.
  Removing takes every visit; a loop whose first stop goes closes again on its new first stop (as the list's cross,
  T-3050). Right click: tier close -> close the loop, open -> open it, turn -> `turnEnd`, unturn -> `openLoop`.
  Touch: a finger held 500 ms (8 px slop) on a marker is the right click; the tap after it is swallowed.
- **Bus routes loop back** (T-3063): in the route tool a right click on a point partway along joins the active end
  back to it and makes the line. Sent to the sim as a loop whose last legs are its first ones backward;
  `bus::loop_back_at` recognises it; `Poly::project_passes` / `on_route_at` put a station or hand stop on the way
  out on both passes. Editing the route folds it back (`BusDraw.begin`). Test
  `bus::tests::a_route_that_loops_back_stops_both_ways_on_its_way_out`.
- **Bus route tool clicks** (`map/busDraw.ts`), to match trains: a click on a point deletes it (the active end, or
  where a loop closes, still finishes); right click: other end -> close the loop, partway point -> loop back,
  where a loop closes -> open it, active end -> delete it (no loop meaning there). Holding a finger is the right click
  (it deleted before); a tap on a point is a click. Switching the active end by clicking the other end with two points
  is gone (that click now deletes it).

## Design calls (DECISIONS.md has the ones worth revisiting)

- Right click on a station with nothing to loop (not a stop, or a stop with no loop offer): no edit, tag "Can't loop
  back here" for 4 s, the station's name above it.
- A bus route loops back from its active end (the end being drawn), to the point clicked; a route drawn from its
  tail is turned round first. Two points or more must lie between (a loop of three legs or more).
- Bus stop editing (hand stops) has no right click: its loop lives in the route, edited in the route tool.

## Round 2: T-3118 (main tree, 2026-11-10)

- **Open anywhere.** Trains and trams: every stop of a loop line offers "Right click to open the loop" (in
  `StopMarkers`, no sim change: a stop of a closed line with no other loop tier). A circle that calls at each station
  once is opened at the stop clicked: the stretch leaving it goes, `[A, B, C, D, A]` at C is `[D, A, B, C]`
  (`setStops`); any other loop (a turned line) loses its way back (`openLoop`) from any of its stops. Bus routes and
  ferries: a ring opens at the point or port clicked the same way (`openAt`, it becomes the active end); a route that
  loops back stops looping (closing leg off) from a right click on any of its points.
- **Ferries** take the bus tool's gestures (`ferryDraw.ts`): click deletes a port (the active end or where a loop
  closes finishes), right click closes, loops back, opens or stops looping; a right click on the active end takes it
  out. Loop back is sent as the ports out, round and back again (`runPorts`: `[A, B, C, D, B, A]`); the sim already
  takes repeated ports on a loop, so no sim change. Not run in a check yet (the bus one is); the ferry close is
  (t2899). No hold on a ferry port (there was none; a tap places at once): the Loop button covers touch there.
- **Touch Loop button**: `loopTap` in `game/ui.ts`, a text button "Loop" in the bar at the bottom left on touch
  (stop editing of a train or tram line, bus and ferry tools with two points or more); pressed, the hint is "Tap a
  stop / point / port to loop there" and the next tap on one does the right click's work, then it goes off (also on
  any tool change). Checked in t3109-loopclick.mjs with `touchInput` forced on (Playwright's own click on the button
  timed out; a DOM click works, so the check uses that; real taps not tried on a phone).
