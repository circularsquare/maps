# T-610: a plan unit OSM does not match shrank a pack (2026-10-17)

## Cause

Cardiff's three ITL3 units are each two or three OSM districts: TLL52 Cardiff and Vale of
Glamorgan (OSM has Cardiff 148 km2 and Vale of Glamorgan 335), TLL53 Gwent Valleys (Caerphilly,
Torfaen, Blaenau Gwent), TLL54 Monmouthshire and Newport (Newport; Monmouthshire's relation is not
in Geofabrik's Wales extract, which cuts it at the English border). `osm_units.match` took the one
relation with the best overlap/union: Vale of Glamorgan alone scored 0.70 for TLL52 (so Cardiff
itself was never taken), Caerphilly 0.54 for TLL53, Newport 0.18 for TLL54 (unmatched, kept as its
own polygon). Outline 1,636 km2, 559k of 1.10M people. The gate caught it (pop_vs_plan 0.51), so
it was loud at the gate but silent in the build.

Not a naming fault: `match` has no names in it, and the units matched as the rule says.

## Fix (pipeline/osm_units.py)

1. A unit may take several relations: those of admin level 8 or coarser with at least half their
   area inside it, coarsest first, skipping one that is mostly under a relation already taken. The
   set is used when its overlap/union beats the best single relation's and reaches 0.5 (`MULTI_LEVEL`).
2. Whatever of a unit no taken relation covers keeps the plan's polygon when that is at least a tenth
   of the unit (`REST_MIN`; before, only a wholly unmatched unit, and only at half). Each such unit
   is printed in the build log with its share, so a fallback is visible.

Cardiff now: 5 OSM units (Vale, Cardiff, Caerphilly, Torfaen, Blaenau Gwent) and TLL54 from the plan
polygon; outline 2,010 km2 (the three units' own areas add to 2,010).
