## For Anita

- **What Shenyang waited on.** 24 s after your game's first final, the game cut its trip workers from 2 to 1 to stay
  inside its 8 GB memory target, and a worker left alone holds only part of the solved figures. So it recalculated the
  whole map on one worker (4-6 minutes on this save), and until that ended no worker could answer anything about a
  city: Shenyang's bubbles sat at "Loading data" with the bar near 90%. Nothing about Shenyang mattered: any city with
  stations would have waited, any time in those minutes.
- **Why the pool was cut.** The game sizes the pool twice: once when a save starts loading, from an estimate, and again
  after its first final, from what the workers really took. The first check left the aux worker (the one that draws
  the bubbles) out of the sum, the second counted it, and on your Asia save that was the difference between 2 workers
  and 1. Fixed: the first check now counts it too, so the load runs on the pool the second check keeps and nothing is
  recalculated after it.
- **"Use more cores" was on in that tab** (it started 5 trip workers; with it off it starts 3). With it off, your Asia
  save loads on 1 worker and this cut cannot happen, but the waits after edits below still do.
- **Sometimes fast, sometimes slow.** A city answers at once while the workers hold the figures on screen, and waits
  while they are recalculating something whole they cannot answer from: the cut's recalculation (this file), any
  edit's whole recalculation on a big joined-up save (filed as T-4265), and a load's own first calculation. Bigger
  cities are the ones with stations; a city with none is answered by the aux worker at once.

## Why every question waited

`shrink` sets `freshParts`, and `dispatch` turns it into `refreshing`: `target()` then sends every question to the new
solve's last round (`wait: true`), since the worker left holds the shown solve for its own periods only, and every answer
needs all five (`mask !== ALL_PERIODS`). `dispatch` also clears `pushed`, the shown solve's bubble sums. Shenyang's
bubbles (`cellModes` -> `subSums`) came back incomplete until v7's final; `bubblesWaiting` showed "Loading data..." with
`timeShare("bubbles")`, which creeps to its 95% cap (the bar near 90%).

## Why the pool was cut: the load-time check left out the aux worker

`capPool` (after a solve): the page outside the WASM (`outsideWasmBytes(first WASM, aux WASM)`, the T-3757 fit) + n x
(need + 150 MB JS) + the aux's need + 150 MB, against `memBudget` (8 GB). `capAhead` (before a save's packs open):
`outsideWasmBytes(perWorker, 0)` + n x perWorker, no aux anywhere. Her save, offline from the registry's local trips
(162 zones, 1,576 M trips):

| | per solving worker | aux | 2 workers | fits 8,192 MB? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `capAhead` before (estimate) | 1,830 MB | not counted | 7,949 MB | yes: 2 |
| `capPool` after v6 (measured) | 1,664 + 150 MB | 257 WASM, 330 need | 8,284 MB | no: 1 |
| `capAhead` now (estimate) | 1,830 MB | 273 MB | 8,676 MB (1 worker: 6,846) | no: 1 |

The trip estimate itself was close (1.64 GB of WASM against 1,505 MB measured); the aux worker, about 0.9 GB in
`capPool`'s sum (its WASM x 1.69 in the page, its need, its JS), was the whole gap.

## The fix

- `game/memoryCheck.ts`: `memoryNeed` also gives `auxGB`, the aux worker's WASM for the zones: 0.03 GB plus 0.15 MB
  per million local trips a day (her Asia save 257 MB at 1,576 M trips, New York 32-65 MB). Rough: in my run the
  same save's aux reached 769 MB during the load, so this errs low, and errs on the side of the old behaviour.
- `workers/demandClient.ts` (aside3's file; my lines only): `stationsAhead` passes `need.auxGB` (the `capAhead` call,
  about line 1214), and `capAhead` (about lines 3560-3570) applies `capPool`'s own test: the page outside the WASM from
  the estimate's WASM and the aux's, the aux's need and JS beside it, and the share as well when "Use more cores" is
  off (before, the share alone there and the budget alone with it on; `capPool` applies both).
- Figures: unchanged (the pool size changes no figure; the demand cache key holds the starting pool, so a cached result
  from a 2-worker load is not reused once). No NYC diff owed.
- docs/architecture.md (the pool paragraph): the after-final cut, its re-solve, and `capAhead` counting the aux.
