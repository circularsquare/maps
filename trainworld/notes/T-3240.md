Why: the ground curve weighs every destination by its people alone, so a Warsaw resident's trips
of 250 km go to every town at that distance per head, and Kraków, a big city, gets only its
headcount's share; and there is no tail under 300 km (only half at 500), so 250-500 km pairs get
little from either curve (T-1508's "valley"). In Europe the flat d^-0.7 tail also sends most of a
city's far trips abroad (Berlin: 16% of its departures abroad, 1% domestic over 500 km). The US
pairs are fitted, which is why NY - Washington is right; it hides the gap everywhere else.

## 3. The two cases

**Poland, "region crossing feels too expensive".** Nothing in the game charges for crossing a
region or realm: Poland is one realm, its zones are 44 places (NUTS-3 subregions), each a "region"
in the trip lists and the Money tab. Three things a player meets there:

1. *Few trips* (the table above): a Warsaw - Kraków line can carry at most 3,734 riders a weekday
   both ways, however good, about ten trainloads.
2. *Default fares*: Poland's scale is 6.5 (income at market rates^0.5), so a new game's fare is
   $1.40 base, $12.50 at 100 km, $0.11 a km after: Warsaw - Kraków (~290 rail km) $33.50, about
   PLN 127, and $49.60 (PLN 188) on a high-speed line at the 1.5 multiplier. By car $23.80 a person
   (316 road km x $0.15 / 2) plus parking. Real 2nd class (from memory, not checked): TLK about
   PLN 60-80, EIP up to about PLN 170. So the default is about EIP's top fare, twice a TLK ticket.
   At Poland's long-trip money weight (3.1 perceived minutes a dollar, middle income group) the gap
   to the car is 30 minutes on a normal line and 80 on a high-speed one: it costs riders, but a
   fast line still wins on time; it cannot lift the 3,734 cap.
3. *The Money tab by region*: running cost is shared by route km, fares by boardings, so a region
   a line only crosses shows a loss. Could read as "crossing is expensive".

Most likely (1), with (2) on high-speed lines (T-3239's multiplier).

**Hechi - Jinchengjiang.** These are one place in the game. The zone `jnj` (Jinchengjiang district
with Nandan, Huanjiang, Tian'e and Leye, 1.21M people) is labelled "Hechi" on the map
(zone-names.json) but listed as "Jinchengjiang region" in the long-trip lists, which read the
market's raw names. So a player may see "Hechi" and "Jinchengjiang" as a pair. Its trips, each
way a weekday: within itself (25 km+) 2,617; Yizhou zone (Hechi's other district and counties,
106 km between centres) 5,015; Baise 2,583; Liuzhou (182 km) 1,514; Nanning (238 km) 1,244.
The zone makes 0.050 departures a person (China's national income, 0.33 of the US's, to the 0.7).
Real: no station or city count found (Hechi's statistics bulletins are not online in reach). A
timetable bound: the Guiyang - Nanning high-speed line (opened 2023-08) runs up to 56 trains a day
and its six Guangxi stations sent 12,740 a day in its first month (chinanews 2023-10-01), and
Ctrip lists 35 trains a day Jinchengjiang - Nanning West. Rail alone Hechi - Nanning is likely a
few thousand a day each way against the game's 1,244 by every mode: low by perhaps 2-4x, the same
big-destination gap (Nanning is the capital). Hechi - Yizhou looks plausible.

The map also labels a town "Hechi" (Hechi town, 河池镇, OSM) about 38 km west of the
"Jinchengjiang" label (金城江区), both inside the zone `jnj`. A player who joined those two labels
built a line inside one zone: its riders are that zone's own local trips and its 25 km+ trips within
itself (2,617 each way a weekday for the whole zone), not inter-region ones. A small town 38 km
from the district seat would have few trips in reality too. Likeliest reading of the report.

Seen on the way (T-3242's screenshots): a zone's "Top destinations" are led by its neighbouring
zones through commutes and everyday outings across the zone line (Hechi: Yizhou 94k, Baise 55k,
Longlin 50k; Warsaw: Skierniewice 111k, Ostrołęka 95k, Siedlce 55k), so a big city 250 km away
never shows there; it reinforces the "big-city pairs are low" impression.

## 4. Proposal

The fix is where a city's long trips go, not how many there are. Options:

1. **A destination pull on the ground curve too**: weigh each destination by (people x its density
   pull)^θ, the tail's own `ta/a`, each origin's departures kept. Rough look from the rows
   (`pull.py`, abroad ignored), each way a weekday:

   | Pair | now | θ 0.5 | θ 1 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Warsaw - Kraków | 1,867 | 3,026 | 4,336 |
   | Warsaw - Tricity (tro) | 1,032 | 1,888 | 3,204 |
   | Warsaw - Łódź | 9,638 | 16,050 | 22,856 |
   | London - Manchester | 6,777 | 9,940 | 13,689 |
   | Hechi - Nanning | 1,244 | 1,843 | 2,839 |
   | Hechi - Yizhou | 5,015 | 3,554 | 2,064 |
   | NY - Boston (right now) | 26,584 | 35,920 | 48,864 |
   | NY - Washington (right now) | 20,197 | 25,482 | 33,409 |
   | NY - Philadelphia (already 3x high) | 199,290 | 222,073 | 237,104 |

   Big-city pairs 1.5-3x up, small towns' pairs down, but the US Northeast pairs that are right
   today overshoot, so the ground (0.107, -2.37) would need refitting on NHTS with it. A new
   constant: Anita's call. Even θ 1 leaves Warsaw - Kraków under real rail alone.
2. **T-1599's refit** (the tail's existing constants: onset, Rt, slope, density pull) against
   all-mode targets with rail counted, adding Warsaw - Kraków, Warsaw - Tricity, Berlin - Munich,
   Hamburg - Munich, Beijing - Shanghai to NHTS, MLIT and the NEC. No new constant; reaches
   300-700 km pairs, not Warsaw - Kraków at 253 km unless the onset moves under 250. Pipeline fit
   about 6 minutes, then every pack's market normalisers and the rows rebuilt (a world data
   rebuild). T-1508 found that the onset alone does not lift Germany's pairs.
3. **Both as one refit** (1 and 2 together, the ground's θ and the tail's density pull fitted on one
   target set). The fullest fix, a world rebuild, maybe 4-8 hours of agent time plus the rebuild.
4. **Leave it**: national totals are right; the cap only bites on players' flagship lines.

Recommendation: 3, as T-1599's scope widened, with done-when: national 100 km+ and 300 km+ per head
within 20% of NHTS and MLIT, NY - Boston/Washington within 30% of today, and Warsaw - Kraków,
Berlin - Munich and Beijing - Shanghai each at least the published rail figure by all modes.
Separately and cheap: the long-trip lists use the zones' shown names.

## 5. Round 2 (Anita 2026-11-11: question the income exponent, big cities get more pull, local factors allowed)

The Poland player's words: "they only want to travel to their own city's parts". Their zone's longer trips go
mostly to itself and its neighbours (Ostrołęka: 6% own zone, 32% other places under 100 km, 36% to the 8 big
cities), and the city panel's "Top destinations" are its neighbours through commutes and outings across the zone
line (Warsaw: Skierniewice 111k, Ostrołęka 95k, Siedlce 55k), which no long-trip change touches.

### 5.1 The income exponent (0.7), a crude fit

| Evidence | Exponent it implies |
|---|---|
| Eurostat tour_dem_ttw 2019, residents' overnight trips a head (domestic + abroad) against GDP per head PPP, 30 European countries | 0.96 (se 0.32); 1.32 without LU, IE, MT, CY. Spread is cultural (Finland 9.6, Estonia 6.7, Italy 1.1, Greece 0.6 a head) |
| China against the US, trips of 300 km and more (rail + air, rough) | about 0.6 |
| India against the US, 100 km and more (rail non-suburban + bus, rough) | about 0.9 |
| Japan (MLIT) against the US (NHTS), 100 km and more | 0.5-1.1 (MLIT leaves out trips within a prefecture) |

Fitted value about 0.8, spread 0.5-1.1: poorer countries do travel less, roughly in proportion to income^0.8.
0.7 is inside the spread; **keep it**. At 0.5 (`pullfit.py --eps 0.5`) China's 300 km+ rate goes to 0.0102 a
person a weekday (published rough 0.005-0.0065, already over), Poland's 100-300 km +11%; at 0.9 China 0.0042,
Poland -10%. The exponent moves national totals, not where trips go; it is not the Poland player's problem.

### 5.2 The pull: importance beyond headcount

**Measure.** A place's importance is the best Natural Earth 10m populated-places `scalerank` (0 most important,
a cartographer's rank of size, capital status and regional weight; public domain, global) among the points
inside its zone's outline (`rank_zones.py`, 2,421 of 3,982 places have one). Its expected rank for its people is
a line through every ranked place, `rank = 20.80 - 1.08 ln(people)`. The **excess** (expected minus actual,
clipped 0-3) is how much more important the place is than its headcount: Warsaw 2.7 (rank 2 at 3.1M people),
Gdańsk 2.3, Kraków 1.5, Berlin 3.1, Nanning 2.2; Poznań, Wrocław, Łódź, Katowice, Hechi 0 (as important as
their size).

**Rule.** A destination's attraction (ground and tail alike) is times `pull = exp(0.8 x excess)` (Warsaw 8.7,
Gdańsk 6.1, Kraków 3.3, Berlin 11.7 at the cap, Nanning 5.7), fading in over 50-150 km so nearby trips keep
their pattern; each origin's departures are kept (its trips renormalised over every destination). Residents of
the US, Japan, the UK and Korea see no pull (their pairs fit today): `market_pull.json`, 936 places pulled,
`off: [US, JP, GB, KR]`. Two constants (0.8, the cap 3) and one window; fitted on the pair table below.

**Before -> after** (`pullfit.py 0.8 3 --off US,JP,GB,KR --excess --phase 50,150`, rows only, place-centre
distances, both ways a weekday, all modes; targets rough all-mode figures from the table in section 2):

| Pair | Now | With the pull | Rough target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warsaw - Kraków | 3,734 | 14,243 | 25,000 |
| Warsaw - Tricity | 5,611 | 22,896 | 25,000 |
| Berlin - Munich | 2,868 | 18,145 | 22,000 |
| Hamburg - Munich | 954 | 5,501 | 10,000 |
| Beijing - Shanghai | 19,676 | 113,901 | 65-160k (rail + air) |
| Hechi - Nanning | 2,488 | 6,093 | 6,000 |

| Kraków - Katowice | 44,922 | 39,775 | |
| Hechi - Yizhou | 10,030 | 6,771 | |
| New York - Boston, New York - Washington | 53,168, 40,395 | unchanged | 53,000, 41,000 |
| London - Manchester, Tokyo - Osaka | 13,554, 146,216 | unchanged | |

National rates, a person a weekday, 100-300 km / 300 km+: US, Japan, UK unchanged (NHTS, MLIT hold); Germany
0.0265 / 0.0043 -> 0.0316 / 0.0067; Poland 0.0221 / 0.0024 -> 0.0232 / 0.0022; France 0.0228 / 0.0045 ->

could take a lower 0.8, a local factor).

**The Poland player's zones** (share of the zone's longer trips; before -> after): Ostrołęka to the 8 big
cities 36% -> 52% (Warsaw 23k -> 36k a weekday), Olsztyn 12% -> 37%, Kielce 21% -> 44%, Rzeszów 8% -> 21%,
Nowy Sącz 25% -> 27%. Own-zone and nearest-neighbour trips fall to make room.

**Lies and risks.** Natural Earth ranks remote centres high for map balance (Anchorage, Antofagasta, Darwin,
Leh reach the cap, 11x); a hand list of targeted cities is the alternative Anita suggested, and
`market_pull.json` can be edited by hand like zone-names.json. The rows-only scenario ignores zone-pair
distances inside near pairs and the world field's unzoned people (kept as their share of departures).

### 5.3 What is built, and what applies at the rebuild

Built (scratch, no game code): the measure, the fit and the config (`notes/T-3240/`: `rank_zones.py`,
`place_rank.json`, `make_pull.py` -> `market_pull.json`, `pullfit.py` the scenario). Not built, for the
implementing task:
1. `pipeline/market_zones.py`: each 6 km zone's two normalisers add the pull (over the world field, the pulled
   places' cells times their pull from 50-150 km); the pull table read from a pipeline config copied from
   `market_pull.json`. Every pack's `.market.json` normalisers change: a world rebuild of the market packs
   (normalisers only; no OSM or census step).
2. `sim/src/demand/intercity.rs` (`Rule::ground`/tail weights, `Market::directed`, the near-pair build) and
   `app/src/game/marketRule.ts` (`directed`, held by `checks/t1148-market-rule.mjs`): the destination's pull,
   faded in over 50-150 km; no pull for an origin in an `off` country. The registry's `market.rule` carries
   `pull_phase_km` and `pull_off`; each market row carries its place's `pull`.
3. `bin/market_pack.rs`: the rows rewritten (near trips change).
4. Docs: regional.md 5.1, demand-worldwide.md "The world market's long trips", the wiki's between-cities page,
   DECISIONS (Natural Earth as the measure, the four exempt countries).
Estimate: the code about 3-4 hours of agent time with its checks; then the rebuild.
