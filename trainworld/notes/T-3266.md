# T-3266 Terrain back to squares (agent hexterrain, 2026-11-12)

Anita: "hex terrain looks ugly, under the hexes we just see squares (i didnt realize the squares were coarser than the hexes would be, maybe lets revert to squares)".

## Cause

The class grid is 1/960 degree cells (about 115 m), but the data in it changes class almost only every 4th cell
(1/240 degree, about 460 m). Counted on the Tyrol block: class changes between neighbouring cells are 3.7x more
frequent across every 4th cell boundary than elsewhere, both ways. So the real resolution is 460 m squares; the 110 m
hexes of T-2890 were drawn finer than the data, each square showing as a patch of same-class hexes with a hex-edged
border. Hex width 1/768 degree is larger than a cell, so the hexes were never finer than the stored cells, only
finer than the data's squares.

## What changed

- Drawing: `map/terrainTint.ts` colours each image pixel by the grid cell under it (squares, 1/960 degree cells, so
  the 4x4 data squares show as squares). Image about one pixel per screen pixel, as before.
- Pricing: `Terrain::class_at` and `terrain_spans` / `terrain_blocks` read the cell the point is in again;
  `hex_centre`, `TERRAIN_HEX`, `TERRAIN_HEX_SHIFT` and the hex test deleted; the app's hex helpers deleted from
  `game/terrain.ts`. Prices are back to what they were before T-2890 (the same sampler).
- Check: `checks/t2890-hex.mjs` replaced by `checks/t3266-cells.mjs` (142 of 142 priced segments charge the classes of
  the cells under them; 4000 of 4000 image pixels show their cell's class at z13 and z15).
- Docs: ui-map.md class tint, track-and-service.md 6.4, code-map.md, wiki terrain page (both copies).
- DECISIONS line added. T-2890's reason against going back (two samplers on built track) does not apply: both
  drawing and pricing moved together. Built track reprices at next load, no cash moves, as T-2890 did.

## Quotes before and after (blueprint cost, same seeded routes, one fresh new game each)

60 random routes (400, 1000, 3000 m, drawn at level 0) in each of three boxes; Tyrol hills, Lake Garda shore, Ligurian
coast. Per route the change in `money.blueprintCost`:

| Area | Routes | Total hex, US$M | Total cells, US$M | Change | Identical routes | Largest single route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tyrol | 59 | 11,778.3 | 11,760.7 | -0.15% | 41 | +6.0% |
| Garda | 60 | 15,419.4 | 15,451.0 | +0.20% | 46 | +10.1% |
| Ligurian coast | 60 | 14,865.5 | 14,832.4 | -0.22% | 39 | -11.5% |

Median route change is 0; totals move under 0.25%; a route that skirts a class border moves up to about 11 percent
either way. No systematic jump.
