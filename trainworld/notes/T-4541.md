# T-4541: the game froze for minutes after a big selection

Her 11-27, 22:15: "im unable to load any other saves, and when i place blueprints it places them as white straight
lines with no curving. actually it just caught up and loaded the save, but yeah it was stuck for like 2 minutes".
Diagnostics: `trainsworld-diagnostics (7).json` (build mwzp0635, 2 h session, 3,090 stations, 272 lines, 199 zones).

## What happened

From 6752 s into the session to the download at 7215 s, the clock worker (the one that holds the game) did little
but answer `doubleUp` questions: 940 of them logged as slow, 0.1-4.8 s each, about 2 a second, nonstop. Every other
message to it waits in the same queue, in order (`clock.worker.ts` `onmessage`: one promise chain):

- **Placing track**: her route edits at 6797, 6814 and 6819 s were started by the clock 34, 61 and 59 s after the
  click. When the first was posted, 62 `doubleUp` questions sent earlier were still queued ahead of it. Until the
  clock answers, the track tool draws its stand-in ghost (`toolParts.ts` `standInPts`, T-4143: a straight line,
  or a plain arc from a heading) and keeps it as the placed segment's stand-in (T-4208, T-4210). Those were the
  white straight lines.
- **Loading another save**: the slot switch posts `leave` (save the game in hand first) into the same queue
  (`clockClient.ts` ~1517), so it waited behind the ~60 queued questions, about 1 s each. That was the 2 minutes.
  It went through once they drained.
- Side effects of the worker never getting a free moment: heartbeat gaps of up to 93 s (queued messages run back to
  back as one chain of microtasks), water-mask fetches for 14 zones timing out (7088-7164 s), and the tag stuck on
  "Downloading terrain... 90%" from 6880 s. No worker restart. The page heap was 2.6 of 4.2 GB at the download.

## Why so many questions

`PickDoubleUp` (ui/Blueprint.tsx, T-1680, from 10-28) prices Double up for the selection bar: one `doubleUp`
question per run of double track in the pick, one after another, asked again whenever the pick or the world changes.
`useLatestAnswer` (ui/useClockQuery.ts, 10-24) dropped an old answer, but nothing stopped the old ask's loop: it went
on sending its remaining runs. The Select tool's drag box rewrites the pick every frame (map/blueprintBox.ts ~404), so
a drag across many runs starts a new full loop each time the runs inside the box change, and all of them run side by
side. Each has one question in the queue at a time, so about 60 loops meant about 60 queued questions ahead of
anything she did next. On her network each run's price is up to five trial edits (`preview_double_up` ->
`double_up_op`: both sides, whole and trimmed, then one more trial), 0.2-4.8 s. On a small network the same loops
finish in milliseconds, which is why nobody saw it before.

None of tonight's changes caused it (T-4444, T-4449, T-4504, T-4506/T-4534, T-4433, T-4501, T-4502, T-4508, T-4510).
The patch re-sends (T-4506, T-4534) change the tools' `clickWorld`, not `world`, so they don't restart the ask.
The code is a month old. What was new was a big drag box on a save this size.

## Fix

- `useLatestAnswer` hands its ask a `live()` check that turns false once the answer is no longer wanted (its deps
  changed, or the panel unmounted). Only `PickDoubleUp` asks more than one question; the other callers are unchanged.
- `PickDoubleUp` waits until the pick has been still for 300 ms (`DOUBLE_UP_REST_MS`), then checks `live()` before
  each run's question. A drag box asks once, after it stops, and taking another tool stops the pricing after the one
  question already sent. At most one `doubleUp` question is queued at a time, so an edit, a preview or a save
  switch waits behind at most one run's price (up to about 5 s on her save, usually under 2).

## Checks

Typecheck clean. One game run on :6149 (new NYC game, six separate 2-piece lines of built double track, Select tool,
real mouse drags):
- Plain: 15 `doubleUp` asks over 4 pick changes (the old code: 18). On a small network the old loops finished
  before the next change, so this alone shows little.
- Each `doubleUp` held 400 ms before reaching the worker, as a slow clock: a box rested mid-drag and then grown sent
  8 asks (the superseded 3-run ask stopped after 2, the in-between picks none, the final 6). The old code would have
  started loops of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 runs side by side: 20. The full box and then the track tool taken 0.8 s after
  release sent 2 asks and stopped (old: 15, still going after the switch). The bar's hover still reads "Double up to
  quad track on 6 runs for $1.23B (blueprint)".

## Left over

- T-4542: each run's price is still 0.2-4.8 s of the clock on her save, asked whenever a pick holds double track and
  again after every edit. Ways out: price on the button's hover only, keep prices per run until the track changes, or
  one trial per side in `preview_double_up`.
- Cousin, not filed: the clock worker runs queued messages back to back with no task boundary between them, so a
  long queue also starves its heartbeat, fetches and timers (the water-mask timeouts above). With this fix the queue
  no longer fills with slow questions; worth a look if a heartbeat gap shows up in diagnostics without one.
