## What the download holds now (`?diagnostics=1`, button "Download diagnostics", strings unchanged)

One JSON file, format 2: `current` and up to three `previous` sessions (last 800 events each), `demandNow` (the pool at download
time: each worker's WASM MB against what it needs, zones and market packs open, levels, last 8 passes) and `save` (the save as it is
at download, base64; `node trainworld/tools/diag_report.mjs <file> --extract-save out.save`). All times are epoch ms.

Log shares, so one busy kind cannot push out another: demand 4000 events, edits and clock 2000, the rest 600. At the 1-3 passes and
a few dozen zone events an edit makes, that is the last 30-60 minutes of editing. Frequent calls are counted in `counts` (n and slowest
ms) and not logged: the clock's "today", "linesPeriods", "lineHeld" queries and the page taking "state" and "trips". Only queries over
100 ms and receives over 30 ms are logged (slow-query, slow-receive). A big log is written to IndexedDB every third second.

Demand events (workers/demandTrace.ts, a sampler four times a second reading the pool's own timings; nothing per trip):
- `dm-network` each network the clock sent, `changed` false when nothing demand reads changed (a train count): no pass follows.
- `dm-waiting` a network waiting for packs (the zones it lacks); `dm-pass` a pass sent: version, kind (edit, whole, rough), why
  (edit, zone opened, after a worker restart, load, rough), zones, market packs, arrived and sent times (arrived to sent is the queue wait).
- per pass: `dm-estimate`, `dm-early-lines`, `dm-local-end`, `dm-local-shown` (first lines shown), `dm-whole-start`, `dm-held` (a newer
  network held behind it), `dm-stopped` (a newer network stopped its whole solve, with how long it ran), `dm-final` (calculating trips done,
  workers' WASM MB).
- `dm-zone` asked, downloaded, open, closed (bare: opened by panning, in the aux worker); `dm-market-packs` counts per tick;
  `dm-level` fine or coarse changes; `dm-worker-restart` (worker, planned for memory or panic or code download, why);
  `dm-state` each change of: calculating trips, step, edits settling, held behind a final, network waiting, workers ready, solving workers,
  workers being replaced, shown version and final.
Edit events as before (edit-click, clock-post, apply-start/end, edited, clock-answer, build-state, receives).

Hooks in others' files (all one line): workers/demandClient.ts, `startDemand` (`if (tracing()) startDemandTrace(c)`, which wraps
`submitNetwork` on the instance) and `workerDied` (`demandNote("worker-restart", ...)`), plus the two imports. freezeTrace.ts, the recorder worker, the check and diag_report are mine.

The clock's settle (T-4194, tree settle) is not in this tree: its agent adds `freezeCheckpoint("settle", { rounds, ms })` where the settle ends
(any event named settle* is kept in the edit share and printed under its edit by diag_report). Not done here.

## Reading it

`node trainworld/tools/diag_report.mjs <file.json> [--session N] [--events] [--extract-save out.save]`: per edit, seconds from the click
and percent of the whole wait for: network reached demand (clock, settle, page), pass sent (queue or packs), local stage, first lines shown,
whole solve, stopped, calculating trips done; the zones, levels, holds and restarts inside that wait; then the passes table, the
calculating-trips on/off spans, restarts, zones, stalls, counts.
