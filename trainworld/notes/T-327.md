# T-327: clicks during the startup solve (T-303 item 4)

Built as notes/T-303.md item 4 (a), with one addition the code needed (the round barrier below).
Rules now: docs/demand.md "The pool and the bubbles' chain".

## What changed

- `demand.worker.ts`: a question marked `wait` (station, flowRail, purposes, icSel, icStation,
  transfers, trip) about a round or version the worker has not reached is kept and answered the
  moment it reaches it, before that round's last `result`. `flows` (the far end spread) reads only
  the sums it is handed, so it now answers at any round of the version, like `cellModes`.
- `demandClient.ts` `target` / `viaRound`: while the running solve's estimates are shown, a cached
  result of the same network is, or nothing is shown yet, questions go to the round after the
  published one (round 0 before any) with `wait`. The answer is tagged with the shown result's
  version, so the views take it as before. The next round's answer is asked for at once and kept
  per question, so when the view asks again at a publish the answer is already there and matches
  the shown round.
- **Round barrier** (`go`, `barrier` on the solve message). Not in T-303's design, which assumed
  five workers of one period each. Since T-381 the default pool is three workers with periods
  2, 2, 1; the one-period worker ran up to two rounds ahead, so "the round after the published one"
  was often already behind it and the click fell through to the final (11-12 s waits measured).
  With the barrier a worker starts round k only once every worker has round k-1 (the client sends
  `go` as it publishes); the fast worker waits at the boundary holding its round. Figures identical;
  the final lands about 0.7 s later (32.0-32.9 s to 33.0-33.6 s here), since each round now waits
  for its slowest worker rather than each worker's total. Only solves where clicks are answered by
  round get it (estimates shown, or a held game load); solves after an edit do not.
- `map/demandViews.ts`: the station effect asks even before any result is shown, so a station
  picked during the free-flow stage has its figures at the first estimate.
- `?perfOff=ahead` turns all of it off (the before runs below).

Choices left open by T-303 and made here: the cached-result case is included (the station panel
shows a round's figures beside the cached final ones until the fresh final, at most a crowding
round apart); stations before any result are included; the barrier only on startup-like solves.

## Measured

Real save, headless Chrome 1400x900, own Vite on 6127, 3 solving workers (the default here),
station 12's complex (24 demand stations), selection set from script; time from the selection to
"Live nearby" figures in the inspector. Script and outputs:
`%TEMP%/.../scratchpad/t327/measure.mjs` (one-off, not kept). Solve: first estimate 14.2-15.1 s,
final 32-33.6 s after navigation. Other agents were building on the machine.

| Clicked at | Before (`perfOff=ahead`) | After, no cache | After, cached result shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 s | 27.4, 27.7 s | 9.9, 9.3, 9.6 s | 9.5, 9.4 s |
| 10 s | 22.2, 22.8 s | 4.4, 4.6 s | 4.5, 4.6, 4.2 s |
| 15 s | 17.1, 17.0 s | 5.8, 5.6 s | 5.8, 5.8, 5.6 s |

- Before, every click waited for the final. After, a click waits for the next round boundary: at
  5 s that is the free-flow stage's end (the first estimate, about 10 s away), later about one
  crowding stage (5.5-6 s with two periods a worker), so 4-6 s. The "about 2 s" of T-303 assumed
  five one-period workers (3 s stages); with the default three workers a stage is twice that.
- Bubble clicks (checks/t303-measure.mjs clicks mode, far end drawn): clicked at 15.5 s, 5.8 s;
  at 20.3 s, 6.0 s (before: until the final, about 17 s and 12 s). Without the barrier the second
  was 12.4 s.
- Final figures unchanged in every run: 7,597,261 rail trips a day; smoke check "demand final"
  7.60 M, 44% / 2% / the rest, PASS.

## Not done

- A worker answers only at its own boundaries, so the wait floor is one stage; a shorter wait
  needs shorter stages (more workers, "Use more cores") or items 1 and 3 of T-303.
- After an edit clicks still wait for the final, as T-303 recommended.
