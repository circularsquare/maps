## Cause

1. **One point per campus (a rule, everywhere).** tier 0 puts each OSM campus's students in the
   one cell under its outline's representative point (`tier0.education_osm`; hand packs use one
   IPEDS or `ca_campuses.csv` point too). For UBC that point is at the south end, 2.2 km from the
   bus exchange, so a station where the B-Line loop or the planned SkyTrain terminus is gets few of
   the 47,000 student legs on foot. This is what a player would see as "UBC is dead": the trips
   exist, but in one bubble in the wrong place.
2. **Jobs: a data hole for Canada's planned rows.** Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary and Edmonton use
   tier 0's job proxy; Toronto alone uses the census's workers by tract of work (98-10-0498-01),
   which covers every tract in Canada and is already cached in `data/raw/ca/`. A low-rise campus
   set in green space gets about a third of its real jobs from the proxy.
3. **Students: a source hole.** UBC's Vancouver campus item in Wikidata has no count, so it is
   sized by area: 45,400 against 59,150.
4. **Not a rule that undercounts universities everywhere.** UCLA, U of T and SFU come out near their
   real enrolment (below). The attendance factor 0.6 is marked provisional in the docs; UBC's own
   daytime estimate, 48,950 FTE students for 59,150 enrolled (0.83), hints it is low for a
   commuter campus, but one campus's FTE figure is not attendance.

With 2 and 3 fixed the estimate reaches 107,000-115,000 of 152,400; the rest is the attendance
factor and what the game leaves out on purpose (visitors, deliveries, trucks).

So the one-point rule bites wherever a big campus's outline puts its representative point off to
one side (UBC, UNAM), and the student count can be wrong either way: too low where the campus item
has no count (UBC), far too high where one campus carries the whole institution's count (UNAM, 2-3
times CU's real students, on top of its other campuses sized by area).

## Side notes

- The shared Wikidata cache `data/raw/tier0/wikidata_students.json` has lost 1,465 ids that packs
  were built with (UCLA, USC, UNAM among them; 746 of them have counts on Wikidata today): several
  builds write the whole file at once and the last writer drops the others' new entries. The packs
  are right (built in memory); a rebuild just queries them again. Harmless unless Wikidata is down.
- The data register's Canada cells said census jobs by tract and the Ontario university files were
  used; that was Toronto only. Corrected.
