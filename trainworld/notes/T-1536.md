# T-1536: service levels High 6-20, Medium 20-24, Low 0-6

Anita's decision (2026-10-27, after notes/T-1523.md): three levels, High 6-20, Medium 20-24, Low
0-6, made by remapping `PERIOD_LEVEL` onto the five existing demand periods. No new periods, no
change to any share table.

## What changed

- `PERIOD_LEVEL` is `[HIGH, HIGH, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW]` (sim/src/track/params.rs) and the copy in
  `app/src/game/periods.ts` has midday as "high". The demand client's start-up check compares the
  two tables.
- `service.rs`: departures are laid out per stretch of one level (`level_stretches`: consecutive
  periods at the same level), not per period. Before, every period began its own lattice at its
  start, which was fine while neighbouring periods had different levels. With three High periods in
  a row, a lattice restarting at 10:00 and 16:00 left a gap of up to 1.5 headways there on lines
  whose period is not a whole number of headways. Now the High lattice runs 6-20 with one headway.
- Tests that took midday as Medium now take it as High (`demand/api/tests.rs`, `demand/everyday/tests.rs`,
  `track/tests.rs`, `track/boarding/tests.rs`, `track/transition_tests.rs`, `track/wasm/t1071_tests.rs`).
  `checks/t1071-train-count.mjs` edited the Medium count at 11:15; it edits High now.
- Docs: demand-timing.md 4.2, demand-runtime.md (worker dealing, re-solve), track-and-service.md
  (scheduling, departures), ui-inspectors.md (the hours hover and the dot).

## What was checked and needed nothing

Everything below reads the period table, so it follows the remap:

- The level hours in the line panel and bus panel (`levelTimes` from `levelHours`) now read
  "High demand: 06:00 to 20:00"; the top bar's "until" joins neighbouring periods of one level.
- The crowding dot and its per-period hover (`CrowdDots`): High lists three periods, 6-10, 10-16
  and 16-20.
- Running cost by hour (`clock.worker.ts` `LEVEL_HOURS`, `costHour`), the money tables and
  `moneyTiming.ts`, the boarding account (`PERIOD_HOURS` only), day kinds and holidays (period
  share tables only; no level), the cache key (the app build and the network's reads).
- Old saves: a line's three counts are unchanged on load (109 of 109 lines the same before and
  after); each count runs at its new hours, so midday runs the High count.

## Unit-test changes worth knowing

- `a_game_saved_while_trains_change_over_loads_the_same` compares trips of a loaded game with the
  live one to 0.5 s (was 0.01 s for the draw): the settled rates differ by a few thousandths of a
  second a departure, and the High lattice now counts departures from 6:00, so by midday the sum
  is about 0.16 s.
- `riders_come_and_wait_...`, `the_line_summary_...`: midday has the peak's trains now, so the
  longest waits in the spill-over are shorter and the queue clears by 10:30; their lower bounds
  were lowered to match (900 s and 1,200 s).
