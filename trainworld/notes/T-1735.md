# T-1735: the market's pairs round an edit re-solved with the local solve (T-1650 stage 3)

Design: notes/T-1650.md section 2 ("Optional, for the one visible lie") and stage T-1735; built on
T-1733's local solve (notes/T-1733.md). The rule as built: docs/demand-runtime.md, "The market's
pairs in a window".

## The mechanism

**Rust** (`sim/src/demand/intercity.rs`; WASM interface: `IntercityApi.set_window`, `window_zones`,
`zones_at`, `window_loads`, `solve_crowded` added).

- **The window.** `set_window(stations, zones, minutes, share, floor)` before `set_network`: the
  zones with a marked station in an access list, the zones given, then the zones whose nearest
  station is within `minutes` (perceived, multi-source search on the midday graph at free flow,
  `rail_minutes`), nearest first, while the window's stations stay within `share` of the stations
  the market searches or `floor` stations, whichever is more (the worker: 9%, 800). The pairs set up
  (`set_served`) are those with an end in the window (the same sampled pairs as the whole market's).
- **The pass in a window** (`pass_window` with `win`). Each pair goes under its end in the window
  (`under`), so only the window's stations are searched. A pair ending outside (a longer trip
  starting in the window; a commute or outing whose home alone is in it, `swapped`) is priced on the
  route costs the other way: from its far stations to its home ones, with its own access lists (a
  first try mirrored the pair the other way and lost 22% of the longer trips: the home end's access
  has rides, the far end's only walks). A leg ending outside is assigned as the leg the other way
  and turned round onto the same lines (`mirror_into`: a segment onto the segment between the same
  stops the other way, boardings onto alightings). A window of every zone is the pass to the bit
  (test `a_window_keeps_its_pairs_trips`).
- `window_loads(q, zones)`: one window pass on what a population holds solved (its network and last
  crowding), in blocks: all, commutes, each everyday purpose. `solve_crowded(q, total)`: a period in
  one pass at the crowding `total` gives.

**Worker** (`workers/demand.worker.ts`).

- **Which edits.** The edit is diffed against the network the market part last solved on
  (`marketNet`, `editedStations`: stops added or dropped, a hop's run time past 1%; trains an hour
  do not count, since a train-count edit moves the market by under 1% on lines (T-1650) and a city
  line's knock-on trains on whole far lines opened a window for China's metro edit), giving the
  changed stations of both networks and the longest run between a
  line's first and last changed stops. A run under 20 minutes (`WINDOW_EDIT_MIN`: a city line)
  leaves the market frozen, as T-1733; the window's reach is the run, at most 180 minutes
  (`WINDOW_REACH_MIN`), so the station budget (`WINDOW_SHARE`, `WINDOW_FLOOR`) bounds it first.
- **The solve.** A population beside the market part (`beside`), set on the new network with the
  window; the touched cities solve their rounds as in T-1733 (the window's pairs' old loads stand in
  for their new ones); then the window's pairs in one pass at the crowding of the cities' last round,
  and every population, the window's too, held to the trains' room on the totals with the window's
  pairs' old loads (the old part's `window_loads`) swapped for their new ones. The result's loads are
  the frozen market's less the old plus the new, each purpose to its own (`delta` in `figures`).
  The window population is freed after the local result; nothing carries to the whole solve.
- A shared period (T-1607, the market on another worker) keeps the market frozen in that period.
- `?perfOff=window` turns it off.

## The lies (until the whole solve lands)

- Pairs with both ends outside the window stay frozen, on their old routes.
- The window's pairs are one pass at the last round's crowding, not the rounds' average; their old
  loads are one pass at the old last crowding (so a node can come out below zero: clamped).
- A leg ending outside the window rides the other leg's lines the other way; a pair starting in the
  window is priced on the route costs towards its home.
- The market's own figures (corridors, the top bar's network totals) stay as last solved; the
  transfers of turned-round legs are not counted.
