# T-3186 long ferry routes find no path (agent ferrypath, 2026-11-11)

Anita: "long ferry routes dont work sometimes, cant fidn a path". The tool says "No water path found" for every
refusal (`no-water-path`, `too-long`, tiles), so the cause is not visible from the message.

## Reproduction

`checks/t3186-ferry-long.mjs` routes 34 cases in Node on the real tiles (ports snapped at a reach of 800 m, as the
tool does at wide zooms): Puget Sound, Firth of Forth, Tokyo Bay to Oshima, Georgia Strait, Hong Kong to Macau,
Helsinki to Tallinn, Hanko and Turku, Stockholm archipelago, Seto Inland Sea, Bergen fjords, Wellington to Picton,
Michigan, the Channel, Piraeus to the Cyclades, and others, 25 km to 416 km. With the code as it was (HEAD):

- **Over about 100 km, `too-long` though under the 150 km limit** (Kobe to Takamatsu 111 km, Michigan 135 km,
  Stockholm to Mariehamn 135 km). The coarse plan read its tiles at zoom 12, 11 or 10 and gave up where even
  zoom 10 took over 80 tiles. A leg's box is the two ports plus 35% of their distance each side, so a long or
  diagonal leg passes 80 tiles at zoom 10 well under 150 km. The second try (a wider box) hit the same wall and
  its `too-long` hid the first try's answer.
- **A coarse raster with nothing sailable near an end** (Firth of Forth to Dundee passed only because the wide
  box happened to work; Michigan, the Georgia Strait and others needed the wider ends once the wide box was
  gone). The coarse cell is 50-130 m and a cell counts only if its centre is 5 m from land, so a harbour or
  inlet narrower than a cell has none within the fine box's 700 m, and the plan cannot start.
- **A berth in a pond or stream.** A click by a pond (Oshima) or a stream (Picton) snapped to it: nearest water
  with 6 m of room. That water has no way out, so every route from it is `no-water-path` (its water reached
  about 900 to 2000 cells of 2.5 m). Nothing said so at the click.

Not a cause: the fine ends not meeting the coarse middle (the hand-over worked on every case that got that far),
bridges filled across a channel, tiles not loading (the worker's fetch is fine; a failed tile is `tiles`).

## Fix (app/src/ferry/route.ts)

1. The coarse tile zoom goes down to 8 (was 10), and `MAX_LEG_M` is 400 km (was 150): Stockholm to Helsinki
   (416 km by water) routes in 1 s, Piraeus to Santorini in 0.5 s. Over 400 km stays `too-long`.
2. A leg that fails coarse is tried with wider, coarser end rasters: 700 m at 2.5 m, then 3 km at 5 m, then 9 km
   at 12.5 m (`END_RUNGS`). Each retry is 1 to 2 s at worst (Firth of Forth to Dundee 2.6 s, Michigan 2.7 s).
3. `snapPort` only gives a berth in water a boat can get out of (`openWater`): a body of sailable cells touching
   the snap raster's edge with 20,000 m2 or 15 m of room, or wholly inside it with 150,000 m2. A click by only a
   pond or stream is `far-from-water`; a click by a stream and a harbour goes to the harbour.

Costs: none on the usual leg (rung 1, same as before); `openWater` is one flood over the snap raster (about 600
cells across), unnoticeable.
