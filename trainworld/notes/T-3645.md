# T-3645 A train vanishes at a change of train count

Cause (as T-3482's agent found): in `hand_over` (sim/src/track/service.rs), a train dwelling at its first stop when
its line's profile changes is redrawn standing there (its shift sets its draw at the change). A train waiting at that
terminal took that redrawn trip as a departure, so one of the two disappeared.

Fix: a trip with `draw != dep` that an era at its change drawn (`e.drawn` holds (run, dep), `dep < e.until`) is never
offered as a departure to be taken over. Ported from settle's service.rs.fixed (only this hunk; the rest of that file
is T-3474 work).

Test: `a_change_with_a_train_dwelling_at_its_first_stop_loses_none` (transition_tests.rs): the T-2429 fixture with the
express at 12.8/h; failed before ("at the edit, gone ... x: 100"), passes after.
