# T-3382 Minutes per city in twstats

Anita 2026-11-13: "we dont have a way of seeing what cities people are actually building in".

## Call: which city a minute belongs to

The focused city, `activeCity` (game/cities.ts): the zone whose boundary holds the view centre (zones.ts `placeAt`), kept
at the last zone over dead space. It is already what the top bar and the market follow, so it is the city being played.

Sampled with the existing play-time ping (once a minute, game/playtime.ts), not a new request. The "15-minute
buckets" of T-3508 are the server's ring of seconds, a different thing; the ping rate is untouched. The ping gains
`city` (a zone id, a few bytes).

Server (shonei-server 5bf10c7): each credited gap (20-75 s) adds its seconds to the city of the ping that ends it, in
the day's `cities` map (seconds). Bounded like `starts`: 400 cities a day, the rest `other`, one small map per UTC day,
nothing per play, so nothing new for check_resources.py (playtime.json grows by under ~10 KB a day at the cap).
No new personal data: a zone id, no ids tied to it. Old pages send no city and add to none; a malformed city is dropped.
`/trainworld/stats` days gain `cities: [{city, minutes}]` (top 10, or all with `?starts=all`).

Not done on purpose: per-play city history, and play before the deploy (no city; the Starts list still has it).
Each ping credits the whole gap to the city it ends in, so a switch is off by under a minute.

## Check

Local server (port 8093, scratch dirs, SHONEI_FEEDBACK_THREADS=1 so stats answers) took 15 pings from three players, one
wandering New York to Boston; twstats on a static server showed Boston, London, Tokyo, New York in minutes.
Screenshot: artifacts/trainworld/t3382-twstats.png. `go test .` passes (new TestTwPingCountsMinutesPerCity); `npm run check` passes.
Not run: a real game in a browser sending the ping (the field is one line in playtime.ts).
Without SHONEI_FEEDBACK_THREADS the stats route panics on a nil `s.fb` (older bug, off in tests, on in production).
