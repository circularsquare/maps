# T-432: Japan stage 2b, Fukuoka, Sapporo, Sendai and Hiroshima

2026-10-15. Four regional metro packs built by `pipeline/tier0.py` with Japan's inputs from
`pipeline/japan.py`, exactly as Greater Tokyo's (notes/T-420.md): census 500 m mesh population,
economic census 500 m mesh jobs scaled per municipality to the census's workers by place of work,
a curve fitted on the census's municipality flows (`notes/t420_curve.py <id> T-432`), the census's
at-home workers plus a telework share, KSJ P29 schools, names by `latin_names` and UniDic romaji.
Each is registered (`market: -1`, `utcOffset` 9), in the world market and merged (T-044). Japan
stage 2a (Keihanshin, Chukyo) was T-431, built at the same time. Logs and images:
`../artifacts/trainworld/T-432-*`.

No outline has a hole. **Otaru joins Sapporo**: 15.2% of its 43,796 resident workers (6,639) work
in Ishikari (2020 census table 4), over a 10% threshold. By the same rule Nanporo (36%), Naganuma
(17%), Abira (12%) and Iwamizawa (11.9%) in Sorachi and Iburi would join too. They are left to
stage 3's Sorachi and Iburi zones, since the brief named only Otaru; their commutes cross packs
through the joint gravity.

**japan.py change** (backward compatible; Tokyo's caches and outputs unchanged): a pack's
`prefectures` may name units smaller than a prefecture: a Hokkaido subprefecture
(`japan.SUBPREFECTURES`, by N03's `N03_002` name, e.g. `"01-ishikari"`) or one municipality
(`"01-01203"`, Otaru). N03, the flows table and P29 are read for the unit's prefecture and
filtered: the flows to the unit's municipalities of work. The outline picture's aspect now follows
its own latitude.

**tier0.py change**: where no university campus in the boundary has a Wikidata student count
(Fukuoka's 73 campuses), the sites are sized by area alone before the national scaling. Before,
the median density was 0 and the pack got no students. Every existing tier 0 pack has at least one
counted institution (London 19, Paris 58, Shanghai 6, Mumbai 1, Berlin 37, Tokyo 24), so none
would change if rebuilt.

## Retrospective

About 2 hours 45 minutes of wall time, 6.3M weighted tokens so far (`checks/token_usage.py
--timeline`, `../artifacts/trainworld/T-432-tokens.txt`), so about 1.6M a pack. Research/japan.md
estimated 4-6M for six metros in one batch.

| Part | Wall | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| Brief, docs, japan.py read; OSM reads and fetches started; japan.py units, four configs | 10 min | 0.9M |
| Otaru decision, curves, Sapporo and Fukuoka built; water points; the university fix | 35 min | 1.0M |
| Fukuoka rebuilt, registry, market, browser checks; the stalled Sendai fetch | 25 min | 1.9M |
| Stop and report on the processes stopped; Sendai's curve with its downloads (61 minutes) | 65 min | 0.4M |
| Hiroshima and Sendai built, merged, registered, checked; docs, notes, clean-up | 30 min | 2.1M |

Five wakes after a long idle cost about 1.4M (22%), each re-reading the whole context. Big tool
outputs were most of the rest: one registry read of 800 lines, two directory listings and a
sha256 dump (together roughly 1M across the following steps).

Not worth its cost: reading registry.json directly (a 3,000-line file); listing directories with
`ls`; the stalled N03 download (two hung fetches, about 50 minutes of machine time and a stop to
report); a Fukuoka rebuild for the university fix, which the build's log could have caught on the
first pack.

Proposals:
1. **japan.py downloads through curl, or with a read timeout per chunk**, since KSJ's server stalls
   a Python request without closing it (`england._get`'s 600 s timeout is per read). Small.
2. **`read_pack.py` checks universities**: fail on 0 students where the header has campuses
   (Fukuoka would have shown it on the first build).
3. **The register, zone boxes and market step as one script** for a batch (`register.py`-style:
   append the registry line by pack id, run `zone_boxes.py`, then `market_zones.py <ids>`), so no
   agent reads registry.json.
4. **A batch of packs costs about 1.5M a pack with one module** once the module exists, and about
   1M without the wakes: fetch, curve and build each pack in one background chain and wait once
   (T-360's rule), instead of a wait per pack and step.
5. **Stage 3** (38 zones): T-424's station lookup from OSM would save hand-placed test lines; a
   zone needs no browser line check beyond the opening one.
