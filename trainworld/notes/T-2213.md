# T-2213: islands I1, land parts in the packs

Design: notes/T-2207.md (sections 2, 3, 6). Agent t2213, 2026-11-03.

## What it is

- `routes.py`: the land parts step. The pack's water at 50 m (as the walk raster's, before its bridges), every
  water pixel under an OSM way some car may use made land (DRIVE_CLASSES, plus service ways, tracks and private
  roads: read_ways flag 4), islets under 0.1 km2 off the grid's edge made water, 8-connected land. A part is a piece
  of land holding cells, except that all land reaching the grid's edge on one world landmass is one part (joined
  beyond the edge: a first pass split Kolkata's Hooghly banks, Assam's Brahmaputra banks and Sorel-Tracy's St
  Lawrence shores, whose bridges are outside their grids). A cell's part is the land under its centre or the
  nearest. `part` (u16 per cell) and a header block `parts` (per part: people, jobs, cells, open, world landmass,
  lon and lat of its busiest cell). A full routes run makes them before the drive tables, and the snap joins each
  cell and zone to roads on its own part (where its part has any). `--stage parts` makes only the parts, for a
  finished pack, merged or not (`write_parts`: as nocar.py does, or merged again where a merged cell would span
  two parts).
- `merge_cells.py`: `part` is an integer per-cell array kept equal within a merged cell; the bank key (zone, walk
  part) gains the part, so no merged cell spans two parts.
- `parts_run.py`: the worldwide build, extract by extract (download threads ahead of reader processes; each
  download in data/raw/osm_parts/, deleted once read), the per-pack pixels in data/work/parts/px/ (10 MB, kept so
  the parts can be made again with no download: `--redo`), summaries in data/work/parts/<id>.json.
- `market_zones.py`: zone files carry `site_part`, `zone_parts` (each 6 km zone's [part, share]) and the pack's
  `parts` table (`--parts` adds them to files as they are; a full build too).

## Runs

- Batch 1, dry: the 158 packs served by the 14 test packs' 35 extracts (about 9 GB of downloads), 16 minutes on 4
  readers and 5 downloads.
- Worldwide, written, 2026-11-03: `python pipeline/parts_run.py --all --workers 4 --downloads 5` (log
  `artifacts/trainworld/t2213/world.log`): 3,138 packs (all with routes; 2 have none), 318 extracts read (67 GB of
  downloads, a few from kept .ways2.npz; `asia/sea` swapped for `asia/vietnam` for qie), 1 h 42 min, no failures.
  Then the packs again with the open parts folded (`--all --redo --workers 4`, `world2.log`, 11 minutes): 3,098
  take `part` as merged, 40 merged again; over the two passes 972 packs were merged again (a few cells more
  each: a merged cell no longer spans two parts, even a 1-person islet's). 901 zone files have sites a little off
  their merged cells now (they move at their next market_zones build; their part fields are right).
- Backups: `data/work/pre-T2213/` (10.3 GB: packs, res-9 copies, stamps, zone files as they were before T-2213).
  Delete once the orchestrator has checked and committed.
- Disk: downloads peaked at about 10 GB in data/raw/osm_parts (deleted); packs and res-9 copies grew by the `part`
  arrays.
