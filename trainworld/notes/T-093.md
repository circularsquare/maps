## What moves

- A node moves while every edge meeting there is blueprint and its station, if any, is not
  constructed (`TrackApi::moved`; refused with `NodeInUse` otherwise, worded "Constructed track
  cannot be moved").
- **Heading rule.** A node with two or more edge ends (a junction, a station on through track, a
  plain node where a route continued) keeps its heading, so every end still leaves along it (SPEC
  6.2) and lines keep the side they pass through. For each edge there, seen from the node:
  - two or more PIs, or one PI and a far end that is a track end: the first PI (it holds the
    heading) moves by the same offset as the node; the others stay;
  - no PI, or one PI that also holds the far node's heading: a lead-in PI is added on the node's
    heading towards the next vertex (`lead_in_along`, as drawing does), and if the edge had no PI
    and the far node has two or more ends, one on the far node's heading too. The next drag moves
    the added PI, so they do not pile up (tested).
- A track end (one edge end) has no heading of its own: it moves, its edge's PIs stay and the
  first leg swings round to it.
- Set radii that no longer fit their legs go back to auto (`relax_radii`). T-079 edges are
  converted first (their clicks become auto PIs, notes/T-092.md).
- One undoable edit (`Op::Batch` of the node and its edges). Lines keep stops and paths; their
  times change (the test checks a longer run to a moved terminus, the app a 184 to 195 s stop).

## In the app (`map/nodeDrag.ts`)

- Select tool only. The press is caught on the map container in the capture phase, before
  MapLibre: over a movable node (within 9 px) it turns off the map's drag-pan for that gesture; a
  press that does not move 3 px is an ordinary click (MapLibre still sees it and selects). Over a
  constructed node the map pans as usual, unless that node is selected (a station, a junction, or
  an end of the selected track): then the drag is caught and the hint says it cannot move.
- While dragging, `previewNode` asks the worker (`preview_move_node`, the same one-in-flight queue
  as the drawing preview) and gets every edge at the node, drawn as one preview in the level's
  colour (red when refused), with each curve's radius and speed and the reason by the cursor.
  Squares of a selected stretch hide during the drag (they would be stale); the node shows as a
  ring. Let go: `moveNode`. Escape: nothing changes.
- The move cursor shows over a node that can be dragged.
