# T-2459: a selected zone in another cluster goes ahead during a load (2026-11-05, agent loadfix)

## The change

During a game load the main pool solves the first ranked zone's cluster alone first (T-402), the zone in view's
(T-393), then the rest together; the rest show only at their final. A zone selected in the city view in another
cluster (an island, a country beside a big one) therefore waited for the whole solve: all of Japan, Okinawa
selected with Tokyo in view, 18 minutes (T-2476).

Now, when a solve with the barrier is sent while the focus pool (T-2045) has the zone in view in hand (being
solved, or shown, for the same network), the solve says `focused` and the worker ranks the city view's zone
first, the zone in view second (`demand.worker.ts`, the `firsts` order). The zone in view's figures come from the
focus pool meanwhile, through its last round. The loading card and the held clock, which went at the zone in
view's cluster's final (T-421), then go at the focus pool's last round of the zone in view when the cluster
ahead is not the zone in view's (`viewHasFinal`). `?perfOff=focusAhead` gives the old order.

Why the hold counts the focus pool's final here: the reverse case (Okinawa in view, Tokyo selected) would
otherwise hold the clock until the whole solve, 18 minutes, where it went at Okinawa's cluster's final in
seconds. The focus pool's figures leave out the market's longer trips and other zones' riders on shared lines
(0.3-3.4% of the zone's rail trips, T-2045); the whole solve's final replaces them as always.

## T-2484 (the focus pool takes the selected zone next): not built

On the Tokyo save with this change, the focus pool's last round of Tokyo came at 25.4 s and Seoul's figures at
27.4 s, from the main pool's round 0 of their shared cluster. A focus solve of Seoul could start only once Tokyo's
ends, and its own round 0 takes seconds, so it would show Seoul no sooner than the main pool already does; the
"about 15 s" T-2476 estimated assumed the second zone's round 0 alone, without the wait for the first.
