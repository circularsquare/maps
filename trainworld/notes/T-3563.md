# T-3563 Trams path like trains in every way

Anita, 2026-11-17: "yes trams hould use passing loops the way rail does. trams should be identical to trains in
pathing in i think like all ways?"

## As built (agent tramloops, 2026-11-17, tree `.tw-trees/tramloops`)

- `Network::no_passing` (`sim/src/track/net.rs`) drops its tram clause (T-3352): on single track only a one-track
  station (the 1-line icon) is a stop with no passing loop, for rail and tram alike. A tram stop placed as a passing
  station (the 2-line icon, the default as for rail) now cuts the single track in two and its trams meet there, as
  T-3540 already drew it. Its only caller is `capacity::build_sections`.
- Test `trams_pass_and_turn_at_a_stop_as_trains_do` (`wasm/onetrack_tests.rs`): the same single-track layout as rail
  and as tram, the middle stop a passing station or not, a line through it and one turning at it, same speed limit,
  dwell, turnaround and cars: identical capacity resources and users, identical single-track holds.
- Check `checks/t3563-tram-loops.mjs`: four made-up 12 km single-track lines, stops every 3 km, tram or rail, the
  three middle stops loop or no loop, all held to 60 km/h.

## Every tram difference found, and what became of it

Removed:
- A tram stop on single track never a passing point (`no_passing`, T-3352). The only pathing rule that differed.

Kept, as deliberate tram properties that are not pathing:
- Section, junction and platform holds (60 s + v/0.6, 45 s, dwell + 60 s; T-3125, Anita 2026-11-10). Signalling by
  sight, her figures; taking rail's would cut tram capacity, which the brief did not ask for.
- A tram yields to a train at a flat crossing (T-3032): crossing rules.
- Speed caps (60 street, 80 off-street), 18 m curves, platform 20-80 m, cars 1-4 of 20 m, the tram fleet, dwell 15 s
  and turnaround 120 s for a new line: vehicle and track properties.
- No high-speed trains on a tram line (`Network::set_line`, `fast`): vehicle.
- Tram and rail never join at a node (`TrackFamily`), no flyovers built for tram junctions (`ramps_over` false,
  T-3170 rail only): track building, street running.
- Street snap and the tram-only real-network snap, `tram_ends`: drawing tools.
- Demand: 0.75 of a car's riders per tram car, no boarding cost, at most 3 tram-only stops per cell
  (`access::TRAM_SLOTS`): demand.
- A tram stop's flat price and upkeep (`station_flat`, `station_upkeep`): the same with or without a loop, as a rail
  station on single track is (both counted as one platform track), so nothing to change there.

Found, left for Anita (each is a new thing players could build, with a price or drawing to settle):
- A tram stop's station panel offers no terminal and no passing tracks (`StationUpgrades.tsx`, the filter on
  `s.tram`); the sim takes both, and the line panel's "add passing tracks" button already adds them at tram stops.
  So a tram line turning at a stop holds one place (15 an hour at most), where a rail terminal holds two (30).
  Recommended: offer both at tram stops, the terminal priced as 1 km of the stop's own track kind rather than rail's.
- No crossovers on tram track (`Network` edge check, T-3352 "neither has a crossover"): trams cannot cross to the
  other track to turn back short. Recommended: allow, priced as tram track.
