# T-2638: load bar against the clock

Anita (2026-11-07): "the initial loading bar (and i thikn a lot of loading bars) finish the first "30%" pretty fast and the
last few percents usualyl take longer. i think we should just recalibrate to have the first 30% be like 15% or something.
front load it so it feels less bad when its slow at the end". And 2026-11-06: "About 6 min left" at 3% for a ~3 min load.

## Measured (agent loadbar, own Vite, TW_CORES=3, fresh browser profile, so no remembered pace)

The card's bar at each tenth of the load's time (from the import or the New game click to the card going).

| load | | 10% | 20% | 30% | 40% | 50% | 60% | 70% | 80% | 90% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| North America import, 150 s | before | 29 | 29 | 45 | 60 | 68 | 76 | 83 | 90 | 97 |
| | after (130 s) | 14 | 14 | 29 | 41 | 50 | 60 | 68 | 82 | 93 |
| New York real save import, 101 s | before | 34 | 42 | 52 | 60 | 66 | 75 | 84 | 89 | 95 |
| | after (114 s) | 21 | 28 | 40 | 47 | 56 | 64 | 72 | 83 | 92 |
| New Tokyo game, 4.5 s | before | 8 | 9 | 9 | 10 | 10 | 52 | 79 | 86 | 91 |

Stage time against bar share before, North America: game 20% of the time and 18% of the bar, data 19% and 37%, solve 61%
and 45%. New York: game 16% and 20%, data 3% and 3%, solve 81% and 77%. So the data stage took twice its share and the
solve fell short; the solve model (`solveModelMs` times the browser's pace) underestimates a first load.

## Change

Every load bar (the card's, "Loading data", a zone's) shows the model's fraction to the power 1.7
(`shownShare` in game/loadBarCore.ts): fitting the before rows gives 1.5-2.0 (median about 1.8); 1.7 puts a model 30%
at 13%, Anita's "like 15%". The model itself, keepPlace and the time left are unchanged. checks/t2552-load-bar.mjs holds
the two measured rows: shown bar within 12 points of the time from 30% to 90% (worst 5 and 4).

The time left read sensibly on these loads (North America: "About 2 min" at 20% with 120 s to go, "About 1 min" at
40% with 90 s; New York: "About 1 min" with 80 s to go), so it was left alone; the 6 min at 3% came from a
remembered pace in Anita's browser, which these fresh profiles do not have.

Scripts: the measuring and analysis scripts were in the agent's scratchpad; data in
artifacts/trainworld/loadbar/t2638-*.json.
