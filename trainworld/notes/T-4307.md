## For Anita

- An edit's quick figures no longer stay up after a newer edit whose own quick calculation is turned down (a fare
  change, too little time left, memory): they go at once and the figures before the edit show, as with no quick
  calculation. On New York a fare change made while the "2"'s quick figures showed put them back to the main
  calculation's 2.9 s later; before, they stayed until the fare change's whole calculation ended (about 2 minutes).
- Money, the score, achievements and stars never read quick figures now. While quick figures show, a line's riders
  move but its income, profit and the score wait for the main calculation.
- An edit whose quick calculation is turned down gets the zone in view's early figures again (T-2605), as before
  T-4220; and zooming into a zone during a long calculation gets its detailed figures even after a quick calculation
  ran.
- No player text changed. No changelog item: stages 6-7 are unpublished (c009 covers them).

## The reasoning

A side job's figures go on screen over the main pool's (`sideOver`) and must leave when they stop standing for the
player's network. What retired them: the main pool's final of their network or newer, a later side job's figures, or a
newer network's plan finding the edited zone as on screen (`sideFresh`). The first two take a whole solve on a big game
when no side job runs for the newer network; the third needs the plan to be asked. So the cause of finding 1 is that
nothing acted on "the newest network's side job is decided and shows nothing". That same missing state is behind
finding 2's second half (T-2605's edit refine was removed, and nothing replaced it when a side job is refused). One
place now records it, `asideNothing(svc)`; finding 2's first half and 3-6 are separate.

The rule kept: what is shown is the main pool's figures, with at most one side job's over them, of a network that is
the newest or whose newest successor still has a side job coming (the quiet second, one in hand, a snapshot or time
left awaited). Nothing kept (money, the boarding account, points, achievements, stars, the cache, a save) reads them.

## The findings

1. **Real.** Stale quick figures over a newer network. Fixed at the cause: every outcome of a side decision that shows
   nothing for the newest network calls `asideNothing`: the page refusals (off, device, split, pool, snapshot, zones,
   fares, memory, slower), the plan's and the later ones (its own refusals, nothing, time, level, memory, slower), the
   pool ending or an error, a result not shown while its network is still the newest, and a solve whose workers were
   never asked for a snapshot (memory short at dispatch: before, the decision waited for it forever, silently). It drops
   an older network's figures (`dropOverlay`, the screen only: `dropSide`'s `coveredAt`/`asideSeq` reset stays a
   game load's, else a late side result could sit over a main final of its own network) and marks the network for
   T-2605. The review's second case (the same zone edited again, refused after the plan) is the same rule.
   Kept while a decision is pending, as the main pool keeps older figures until new ones come. Not covered, bounded by
   the main pool's own solve of the newer network: a network not wanted for a side job at all (`quickHold`, or sent with
   a local stage: retired by its local result in seconds; a barrier solve of an edit that opened a zone: retired at its
   final, and such a network nearly always still has the edit); an undo made while paused with "Recalculate trips while
   paused" off never reaches demand until the game runs (`pauseHolds`), as for any figures.
2. **Real, both parts.** (a) `refineAside` returned while `apool` lived, and the pool is kept idle to the whole final:
   the zone-in-view refine was blocked for the rest of the solve. Now it waits only while a side job is in hand or in its
   quiet second, and ends an idle side pool for itself; it is called again at the end of every decision and side job.
   (b) T-2605's edit trigger is back for a network whose side job showed nothing (`asideNone`, and no side figures of
   it), so a refused side job leaves the wait as before T-4220. Gated on that, it never starts a second before an
   accepted side job (which would end it). Left: a side job starting still ends a running refine (one pool at a time,
   stage 6's call); the edit refine now starts after the side decision, about 1 s later than before T-4220 where the
   side job is refused. A zone both refined and fresh under side figures: the side figures' zone view and lines win
   (same network, with the market); the bubbles are the refine's.
3. **Real.** `demandByDay` (shown) fed `incomeByDay`, the boarding account's riders, region shares and stars
   (`clockClient.ts`), `lineCover`, `fullPoints`, `scoreSettled`, `demandFigures` and the profit and ready checks
   (`achievements.ts`), `lineShares`. They now read `mainDemandByDay`, written with `demandByDay` in one `batch` by the
   `days` setter (the main pool's figures alone). Whole chains converted (achievements' ready check compares the view
   its profit question read; `lineCover` mixes income with the view's trains). Marking the overlay `final: false` was
   not used: the riders effect never tests `final`, and `dispatch` reads `demandView.final` for `showEstimates`, so the
   next solve would turn into a barrier solve. Display readers (`demandView`, `cityDemandViews`, the map, the guide,
   the edit note, which settles only at a main final) stay on `demandByDay`. `checks/t2266-fare-lag.mjs` writes both
   signals now (its income probe would have quietly stopped moving); `t2793-figure-dips.mjs` writes `demandByDay` for a
   display case and is unaffected.
4. **Real in part.** A side worker that never answers kept `asideJob` set (no side job, no refine) until a game load
   or a pool restart: `sideCovered` skipped ending the pool while a job was in hand. Now it ends it once the main pool
   shows a final of the job in hand's network or newer, when its figures could never be shown, so a hung or slow job
   is bounded by today's wait. No separate timer: a deadline at the main pool's expected time would also cut jobs that
   could still win when the main pool runs late. Cancelling a job when a newer network comes: left. The worker already
   goes stale at a yield when a newer job lands in `pending`, but the page sends one job at a time by stage 6's design
   ("edits made meanwhile share the next one"); the cost is the rest of one side job (6-28 s on New York), never past
   the main pool's result.
5. **Real.** `asideBroken` was cleared by every whole snapshot, so a worker that ran out of memory on its zones started
   and failed at every whole solve. Now `ASIDE_FAILS` (2) failures in a game turn side jobs off until another game.
6. **Real, display only** once 3 is fixed. A side job's views were made for the kinds of day of that moment; a
   superholiday coming near or going redid only the main days. Now `refreshDays` drops the side figures when the kinds
   change (before its own early returns). Remaking them was the alternative; the case is rare and dropping is safe.

The review's "checked, no finding" list: agreed.
