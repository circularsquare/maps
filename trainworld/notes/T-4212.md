## What ends the card (demandClient.ts), for whoever reads her diagnostics

`checkHold`: `holdDone` (the solve's HOLD_ROUND, or the zone in view's cluster solved ahead, or the focus pool's figures
for the zone in view), `cacheDone` (a kept result shown), `settleIdle` (`idle()`: nothing needed, i.e. no station
reach and no zone in view, or every needed pack "broken" after failed downloads, T-484). The card also hides while
`demandIdle` is true (`ensure`: nothing needed). A load that ends through `settleIdle` or `demandIdle` with packs that
failed to download would show exactly "loaded, 0 riders"; zone failures then show on the zones (zoneFailures).
