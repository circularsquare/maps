# T-402: per-cluster finals in the startup and zone-opening solves

Rules: docs/demand.md "Clusters" (the paragraph on solving after an edit) and "The pool".

## What changed

- Worker: a solve with T-327's barrier whose dirty clusters are two or more, the zone in view's
  among them, runs in two groups: that cluster alone through every round (each round sent as a
  `result` with `cluster`, the round in `summary[0]`), then the other clusters together, round by
  round, as before. `plan.ahead` names the first group's cities. Each group is set up when its turn
  comes; populations keep `solveOrder` within a cluster.
- Barrier: `go` carries the group; a worker waits on (version, group, round). The aux worker (no
  periods, never sent `go`) no longer waits at the barrier at all, so it sets up every group.
- Questions: the worker tells the group a question means by its zone's cluster. A zone in a later
  group waits for that group's round; one in a finished group is answered from its held state
  (T-392's path). Station, market, transfers and subzone answers read a cluster only while it is in
  the group being solved or held, never a cluster not yet set up on the network.
- Page: cluster results are collected per round; the first group's rounds send `go` (group 0) and
  are shown where the solve's estimates are: a view of the cluster alone while nothing else is
  shown, then at its final the T-392 overlay and `cityVersion`. `target` counts that cluster's own
  rounds for its zone (`shown` on the target, and `aheadP` cleaned per zone). A load whose zones are
  all in the first group counts as solved at that group's round.
- Unchanged: a game load's card and clock still wait for the whole solve's final (HOLD_ROUND).
