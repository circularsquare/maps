# T-1464: buses, stage B1

Design: [T-1445](T-1445.md) (approved 2026-10-26: free drawn at 1.2, one fare curve, flat 18 km/h,
bus-only trips count as rail). This note holds the interfaces and the measurement.

## The sim (`sim/src/track/bus.rs`, `wasm/bus.rs`)

- A bus line is an ordinary line (same id space) whose `LineData.bus` is `Some(BusRoute { points,
  built })`; its `stops` and `path` stay empty in the network. `Network::line_bus`, `is_bus`,
  `bus_lines`, `bus_road_m`.
- Its stops are derived by the service (`bus::plan`), never saved: train stations within 200 m of
  the route, both ends (an end within 200 m of a station merges into it), a stop where it crosses
  another bus route, and one about every 2 km between, snapped to the 400 m street grid. A
  constructed route stops only at constructed stations. Stops that are no station get ids
  `bus::STOP_ID_BASE (1 << 30) | line << 10 | index`. Every point of the route belongs to its
  nearest stop that is no station (`cover`, ground metres).
- `LineSvc.bus: Option<Plan>`; its runs have no path (`segs`, `nodes`, `stop_idx` empty), `stop_s`
  in road metres, a flat-speed profile (`bus::profile`), dwell only at stations, no capacity holds.
  `Service::position` walks the drawn route. Capacity and the boarding account's platform queues
  skip bus lines; `LineSvc::crush()` is 80 for a bus.
- Every edit touching a station or a line rebuilds every bus line's service (`TrackWorld::commit`).
- Money: fleet kind 2 = buses (`fleet_bus`, `buses_needed`), `BUS_PRICE` 0.6, `RUN_COST_BUS_KM` 3,
  `UPKEEP_BUS_KM` 30 real (3k a game day), `BUS_ROUTE_COST_PER_KM` 1.0 on Construct
  (`built_cost`). Blueprint rows: kind 10 (bus route, road m), kind 11 (buses). Removing or
  redrawing a constructed route is final.
- Save: trailing `LBS1` block (index, built, points); a network without buses encodes as before.
- WASM: `set_bus_line(id, xy, hw_high, hw_mid, hw_low, dwell, turn, colour, name)` (headways),
  `set_bus_route`, `is_bus`, `bus_lines`, `bus_road_m`, `bus_route` ([built, x, y...]),
  `bus_stops` (8 a stop: id, x, y, road s, kind, station or -1, cover g0, g1), `bus_catches`,
  `bus_street` (x, y), `bus_quote(line, xy)`, `construct_with_buses`, `line_stops` (a bus line's
  derived stops).

## Demand (`demand/kernel.rs`, `api.rs`)

- `set_bus(catch_n, catch_xy)` before `set_network` (`set_bus_ll` for the market): per stop its
  stretch of route as points. `Network::catches`, `st_bus` (a station that is a bus stop only).
- `build_access`: bus-only stations are left out of the plain scatter; each cell within 400 m of a
  stretch gets that stop at its distance to the route + 100 m, at most 2 (`BUS_SLOTS`), after its
  6 train stations (`Access::w` = 8 with buses, else 6; `Access::of`).
- Subzones: key gains the cell's first bus stop; walk entries K trains + up to 2 buses (`KE`).
- The trip explorer's cell walks add `bus_walks`.

## The app

- Snapshot: a running bus line's stops are `line_stops`; its stops that are no station join the
  snapshot's stations with `bus: true`; `SnapshotLine.catches` per stop; `cars` 0.5 (80 at crush).
  `DemandNetwork.catchN/catchXY` carried through `demandClusters` and set in `demand.worker`.
- State: `ClockLine.bus: ClockBus` (route, roadM, stops with kind and station, street stops, lng/lat
  forms); bus stops that are no station are `ClockNode` rows with `bus: true` (platform 0, ports 0)
  so every stop has a node; `LineStats.bus`, `TrackNode.bus` on the main thread.
- Ops: `addBusLine { route, name, colour }` (starts at 8/10/20-minute headways), `busRoute`,
  `construct { buses }`. Query `busQuote { line, route }`.
- Money state: `fleetBus`, `busesNeeded`, `busPrice`, `runCostBusKm`, `upkeepBusKm`, `busRouteKm`,
  `upkeep.buses`, `upkeep.busKm`. Save header `fleetBus`.

## The gate: demand time with 30 buses

`cargo test --release --features demand t1464_bus_cost -- --ignored --nocapture`: New York's real
network (`nyc_real_track.json`) through the commute layer, five periods, free flow and three
crowding rounds, then with 30 feeders of 6 km (4 stops each), two cores:

| run | without | with 30 buses | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| first | 33.0 s | 43.5 s | x1.32 |
| warmed | 36.8 s | 46.9 s | x1.27 |

Under the third the design allows; rail trips +0.4%, subzones 6,693 -> 6,987.
