# T-078 one access mode, a fast walk; data for the demand views

Done 2026-10-09 by the demand agent (batch 5). Getting to and from a station is now one mode at
both ends of a trip: a walk at about bicycle speed and reach. The drive/bus "other leg" at the home
end is gone, with everything that existed only for it. Its constants were set for game feel and
checked roughly against the real New York network; the per-city rail constant went from 1.5 to 0.
Recompute is 15-28% faster. The demand workers can now answer the queries the demand views
(T-084) need: commuters by mode per cell at both ends, and a station's catchment and where its
riders go.

## The fast walk

| | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| speed | 4.17 m/s (15 km/h) along a path 1.3x the straight line, so 11.5 km/h as the crow flies | a relaxed bike; Anita's "bicycle speed and willingness" |
| reach | 2.5 km straight line (13 real minutes) | a station draws from a circle the player can see and plan around: a few stations wide in the centre, a small town's worth in the suburbs, never 20 km |
| weight | 2.0 perceived minutes a minute (walking was 1.39) | each km costs about 10.4 perceived minutes, so a station's draw visibly fades towards the edge of its circle: a home 2.5 km out has about a quarter of the odds of one beside the station, and a station 1 km further than another gets 12% of its riders |
| transfer walk | on foot: 1.2 m/s, weight 1.39 (`xfer_mps`, `w_xfer`) | changing platforms is not a bike ride |
| station logit | 0.2 per perceived minute, unchanged | |
| rail constant | 0 (was 1.5) | rail and driving judged on perceived time and cost alone; the fast walk makes rail cheaper at both ends, and 0 puts the real network back near ACS |

Speed and weight only act through their product (perceived minutes per km), so "15 km/h at weight
2" and "10 km/h at weight 1.33" give the same demand; the reach is the one hard edge. Constants:
`Params::walk_*`, `w_walk` in `sim/src/demand/kernel.rs`, `ASC_RAIL` in `api.rs`.

## Recompute time

WASM in Node 22 (the app's demand module, `wasm-pack --profile wasm`, nodejs target), one
`DemandApi` solving all five periods, free flow plus three crowding rounds each, the same machine,
runs interleaved before/after (the machine was shared, so absolute numbers swing 20-30% between
batches; the ratios held in every batch):

| network | pairs a period | per period stage | set_network | a day (5 periods x 4 stages) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hand-made, 434 stations, before | 6.59M | 340-440 ms | 26-38 ms | 7.2-7.7 s |
| hand-made, after | **4.14M** | **230-290 ms** | 20-28 ms | **5.1-5.4 s** (-28%) |
| real, 867 stations, before | 20.0M | 1.08-1.72 s | 33-35 ms | 26.2-27.2 s |
| real, after | **13.6M** | **0.94-1.65 s** | 17-38 ms | **22.4-24.4 s** (-15%) |
| lone line, before | 12k | 1 ms | 10-17 ms | 23-37 ms |
| lone line, after | 2.3k | under 1 ms | 4-9 ms | 7-15 ms |

Fewer pairs, but each costs more: a 2.5 km reach gives a subzone up to 6 stations where 1.5 km
gave 2-3, and the egress sum runs over them; plus the bookkeeping for the views below (measured
inside the noise). Subzones: 7,400 before on the hand-made network (6,000 of them reachable only
by the other leg), 2,056 after; on the real network 3,702.

In the app (Chrome, three workers, `?demandTest=1`, four resubmits): the mode split lands
**494-552 ms** after the network is posted (T-026: 720-830 ms) and the third crowding round
**1.88-1.98 s** after (SPEC estimated 2.8 s). Per period stage 207-287 ms.

## Data for the demand views (T-084)

Asked for when a view needs it, not pushed with every solve. The types are in
`app/src/game/demand.ts`; the main thread calls the functions there, `workers/demandClient.ts`
asks the workers and merges, `workers/demand.worker.ts` answers between periods (never inside a
solve), the Rust is in `DemandApi` (`sim/src/demand/api.rs`, "Demand views").

```ts
cityCells(): Promise<CityCells | null>        // static: ask once
cellModes(): Promise<CellModes | null>        // for the solve demandView shows; cached per version
stationRiders(id: string, top = 20): Promise<StationRiders | null>

interface CityCells { n; xy: Float32Array /* 2 per cell, pack metres */; h3: BigUint64Array;
  zone: Uint32Array; zoneM: number; zoneXY: Float32Array /* centre per zone */ }
interface CellModes { version; homeRail; homeWalk; homeDrive; workRail; workWalk; workDrive /* Float32Array per cell */ }
interface StationRiders { version; stationId;
  home: CellRiders /* living in each cell, using it at the home end: the catchment */;
  work: CellRiders /* working in each cell, using it at the work end */;
  homeRidersWorkIn: ZoneRiders; workRidersLiveIn: ZoneRiders /* top zones, most first */ }
interface CellRiders { cells: Uint32Array; riders: Float32Array; total: number }   // most first
interface ZoneRiders { zones: Uint32Array; riders: Float32Array }
```

- **Units: commuters a day**, one person making a trip to work and one home. Home-end rail over
  all cells = the day's rail trips / 2. Every per-cell array is in pack cell order (`CityCells`);
  `game/coords.ts` turns pack metres into lng/lat, or use the H3 ids.
- **Walk** is the whole trip on foot (the mode split's walking), not the walk to a station. Drive
  is everything else (car, bus, taxi).
- **Null** when there is no solve yet, or when the workers have already moved on to a newer
  network than the one `demandView` shows (they keep only the newest). Ask again when
  `demandView` changes; a view keeps showing its last answer meanwhile.
- **Stations within 100 m are one demand station** (SPEC 4.4), so a side-by-side pair answers the
  same.
- **How the destinations are worked out**: a station's home riders are split over the stations
  they ride to (the period's station-to-station rail trips), and each of those stations' arriving
  riders over the zones they walk to. That assumes riders arriving at a station spread the same
  whatever station they came from; exact at the zone totals, a little blurred per pair.
- **Sizes and times** (New York pack, hand-made network, Chrome, three workers): `cityCells` 3 ms,
  2.6 MB once (xy 1.6 MB, h3 1.6 MB as BigUint64, zone 0.8 MB); `cellModes` 11 ms, one 4.9 MB
  transfer (six Float32Arrays over one buffer), then served from cache; `stationRiders` 3-6 ms, a
  few kB (the busiest station: 58 home cells, 59 work cells, 20 zones each way). Natively on the
  real network: `cell_modes` 2-3 ms, `station_riders` 0.6-1 ms. Each worker's sub-results add
  4 x subzones floats (60 kB on the real network) per query; the S x S station table it keeps per
  period for the destinations is 3 MB a period on 867 stations.
- Also added: `DemandView.peakHourFactor` per period (the busiest hour against the period's mean
  hour), which T-087 asked for.
- Debug: `window.twDemand.cellModes()`, `.stationRiders(id)`, `.cityCells()` with `?debug=1`.

Messages (`workers/demandProtocol.ts`): `cells`, `subSums` (every worker: rail by subzone over its
periods), `cellModes` (worker 0: expands the summed subzone results to cells), `station` (every
worker: its periods' part, packed `[n_home, n_work, cells.., riders.., cells.., riders.., zones..,
zones..]`). Each carries an id; the subzone and station queries carry the solve's version and a
bit mask of the periods included, and the client checks all five are there.

## Code

- `sim/src/demand/kernel.rs`: `Params` lost the other leg (`other_*`) and gained `xfer_mps`,
  `w_xfer`; `Subzones` lost `oth_*` and `n_other_only`, and cells with no station within reach
  are in no subzone (they keep road and walk); it keeps the member cells (`mem_off`, `mem`).
  `mode_choice` takes one access list for both ends and also returns rail trips by home and work

  `Gravity::walk_trips_by_zone`.

  `zone_xy`. Test `views_add_up`.

  existing exports unchanged.

## Accuracy of subzones

`demand_bench --pack data/packs/nyc.json --exact` (hand-made network, morning peak, free flow):
one subzone per cell (25,688 cells within reach of a station, 627M pairs, 23 s native) against
the 2,056 subzones (4.1M pairs, 185 ms): rail -1.8%, station entries 5.1% and exits 6.1% off (sum
of absolute differences), segment loads 2.2%. T-020's figures with the other leg were -2.1%,
5.4%, 5.5%, 2.3%: the wider reach did not cost accuracy. Where riders come from (distance from
home to the boarding station, exact): 0-0.5 km 16%, 0.5-1 km 30%, 1-1.5 km 25%, 1.5-2 km 16%,
2-2.5 km 13%.
