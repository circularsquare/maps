# T-2759 nyc.bin fetched twice (agent c2, 2026-11-16)

Not a duplicate fetch of the same bytes. On a cold ?new=1 start the pool's whole-file download of packs/nyc.bin (7+ MB) is followed by Range requests for other parts of the same file: bytes=2224584-3336779 (1,112,196 bytes, the 1.11 MB of T-2758) is the water mask (clock.worker.ts fetchWater, water_row..water_x), plus six smaller ranges for street data (fetchStreets: pop, jobs, zone, zone_gx, zone_gy, zone_cells9) and walks.ts. They are read by the clock worker for the track model; it cannot wait for the whole file. fetchRange uses no-store (T-1635, T-2499), so the browser cannot answer them from the whole download. Saving those bytes would mean the page handing its downloaded pack to the clock worker; a design change, not a bug. Suggest closing.
