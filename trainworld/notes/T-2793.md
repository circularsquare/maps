# T-2793: fares falling while demand is recalculated

Anita: "fares going to 0 while recalculating (someitmes for periods even when its not saying recalculating... on top) is still an issue".

## Interrupted implementation, reviewed 2026-11-08

The uncommitted changes are in `game/money.ts`, `game/today.ts`, `workers/clock.worker.ts`, `workers/demandClient.ts` and `perfFlags.ts`.

- Money uses `lineLoadsFor` to carry a line's previous segment loads onto its edited stops while its new riders are solved. If a cached result cannot be carried, the last income stands.
- The clock's `today()` keeps its last complete readout while the boarding account is being recomputed across kinds of day. Previously the new account for today could be read without yesterday's share of the past 24 hours. The page also retains the last count for a running line temporarily absent from that readout.
- A just-opened zone's intermediate rounds fill its city view alone; existing network-wide line figures stay final. A rough one-zone solve cannot replace an existing line with that zone's riders alone. `?perfOff=keepFigures` restores the earlier demand publishing route for diagnosis.

This review found no definite new correctness blocker. These changes preserve figures temporarily; they do not change the demand model or the final pricing rules.
