## Found and fixed, 2026-11-09 (agent dips)

**The case the earlier replays missed: the zone in view refined aside (T-2605) while a long line's stops changed.** Solve in hand after an edit, the camera moves into a zone (level goes fine) or the edit touches the zone in view: a focus pool solves that zone alone. `publishRefine` put its figures in for lines "the shown result has none for, new or with their stops changed". A line with changed stops does have figures (carried onto the new stops, c18d2fa1), and here they were replaced by that zone's riders alone. Then `refineOver` put the same figures back over every later result covering the zone coarse or of an older network, the edit's local result and the whole solve's final included. The local result clears the line's pending mark, so the hold (T-2773) ended and the 2% figure was shown and paid, unmarked. The same replacement applied to any `fshown` zone through `withZone`.

Repeatable with `node trainworld/checks/t2793-figure-dips.mjs <save> <url> lodEdit` (drop a stop on the longest line, then jump the view to four of its stops at zoom 11.5; exits 1 if any line falls by more than half). On master 161fd866, Tokyo save, Hikari (1,068 km):

## Interrupted implementation, reviewed 2026-11-08

The uncommitted changes are in `game/money.ts`, `game/today.ts`, `workers/clock.worker.ts`, `workers/demandClient.ts` and `perfFlags.ts`.

- Money uses `lineLoadsFor` to carry a line's previous segment loads onto its edited stops while its new riders are solved. If a cached result cannot be carried, the last income stands.
- The clock's `today()` keeps its last complete readout while the boarding account is being recomputed across kinds of day. Previously the new account for today could be read without yesterday's share of the past 24 hours. The page also retains the last count for a running line temporarily absent from that readout.
- A just-opened zone's intermediate rounds fill its city view alone; existing network-wide line figures stay final. A rough one-zone solve cannot replace an existing line with that zone's riders alone. `?perfOff=keepFigures` restores the earlier demand publishing route for diagnosis.

This review found no definite new correctness blocker. These changes preserve figures temporarily; they do not change the demand model or the final pricing rules.
