# T-3653 time controls as slower and faster (timectl)

Top bar: pause, slower, speed figure, faster. Slower and faster step along SLIDER_STOPS (20 stops) from the chosen
speed and are disabled at the ends; comma and period do the same from the keyboard (main.ts keydown, no repeat).
`clock.stepSpeed(dir)` does the step; it resumes a paused clock like the old buttons did. The figure is a button
(40 px wide, tabular) with the double-click picker; it dims while the demand cap holds the clock. The guide's
"play" step now pulses the pause button (it resumes). Strings: topBar.slower/faster, help.speedKeys added in all 12
languages (translated by hand, merged with l10n.mjs); topBar.normalSpeed and topBar.speed removed.
Checked headless (nyc): 4 commas reach "real", 24 periods run real, 4real, 0.5x ... 100x and hold at 100x; 24 commas
come back down and hold at real. Speed group width is 108.9 px at every stop at 1400 and 390 px; no horizontal scroll at 390.
