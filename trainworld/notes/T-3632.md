## Group 1 in detail

The sentence "No transit route serves both ends in this period" (`marketTrip.noRail`, why "noRail") comes from four
places, and a player can't tell them apart:

1. **No trains that period.** A line with 0 trains at Low runs nothing at night; the period has no route. Seen in my
   Milan run (night only). Francesco's line has Low = 0, but his picture is midday.
2. **The sim finds no rail for any zone pair** (`market_trip.rs`, `rail_all <= 0`): no pair is `solvable` (each end
   needs a station in its access lists, a car or plane to compare with, at least `min_trips`, and the two ends'
   stations on one track component), or the period's skims join none of the access stations.
3. **No market part answered that period**, and the worker fills in "no rail" itself (`demand.worker.ts`, the
   `if (!best)` after the parts loop in `icTrip`; its comment says this is meant: "with no part, an answer with no
   rail"). The same happens for every period when a worker has no market part at all but the market exists
   (`mcs.length === 0 && intercity && live`). An untested lead from reading the code: nobody has seen it give a
   daytime "no route" on a served pair.
4. **Rail above zero but no route listed** (`!main` in `MarketTrip.tsx`), T-1500's old shape, fixed 10-26 by always
   keeping the biggest route.

Both pictures show the day with some transit (`MarketTrip` sums the periods), so some periods had rail and the one
on screen didn't. In 1794788348 commutes are 11% transit over the day, yet the evening peak has nothing, not even
commuters; that fits a missing answer (3) or a dead period better than a real zero, but neither is shown.

Repro (scratch, `noroute/nyphl.mjs`, my Vite on 6196, one core; 1 demand worker, and a second run asking for 4 by

### 2. Lines missing from the real networks (new; the commonest local "no route" found)

A sweep of 300 random commutes around Osaka on the Kansai real network (`local.js`, 244 inside the city, 5
periods): 89% offered, 4% offered by the lifted walk rule, 2.9% the walk limit ("No transit trip found"), 2.0%
`noRoute` ("No transit route runs between the stations near the two ends"), 0.8% no station near the end, 0.7%
slower than walking. Every `noRoute` pair, all day, ends near Hirakata and Katano. Control: Osaka Station to beside
Hirakatashi station is `noRoute` in every period; Hirakata to Kisaichi (both on the Katano line) is offered; Kyoto
Station to Uji is offered (Uji has JR stations too).

Cause: the network has **no Keihan Main Line**. Its branches, the Uji line (Chushojima - Uji) and the Katano line
(Kisaichi - Hirakatashi), are in it and touch nothing, since their junction stations are on the main line. The
shipped saves are built by `pipeline/realauto.py` from route relations in noritetsu's OSM extract (Japan,
2026-09-29), and OSM has no passenger route (`route=train`) on Keihan's main line: its 655 track ways (116 km) carry
only an infrastructure relation, `route=railway` 1921059 "Keihan Main Line・Ōto Line" (Overpass, today). So the
extract is not stale; the gap is OSM's.

The same check over the Japan extract (named passenger-looking track, 5 km or more, under 10% of it under any
passenger route, in a box around each Japanese real-network city):
- Kansai: Keihan Main Line 116 km, JR Wakayama Line 95, JR Kakogawa Line 50, Keihan Ishiyama-Sakamoto 28, Keihan
  Keishin 15, Keihan Nakanoshima 6.
- Nagoya: JR Kansai Main Line 90 km, Yoro Railway 58, Ise Railway 38. Checked in `real/ngo.save`: no Kansai Main
  Line, but Kintetsu's Nagoya line serves Kuwana and Yokkaichi, so no place is cut off that I found.
- Fukuoka, Sapporo, Hiroshima: none. Tokyo and Sendai rows (Chichibu Railway, freight branches) were not checked
  against their saves.
