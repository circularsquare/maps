## Group 1 in detail

The sentence "No transit route serves both ends in this period" (`marketTrip.noRail`, why "noRail") comes from four
places, and a player can't tell them apart:

1. **No trains that period.** A line with 0 trains at Low runs nothing at night; the period has no route. Seen in my
   Milan run (night only). Francesco's line has Low = 0, but his picture is midday.
2. **The sim finds no rail for any zone pair** (`market_trip.rs`, `rail_all <= 0`): no pair is `solvable` (each end
   needs a station in its access lists, a car or plane to compare with, at least `min_trips`, and the two ends'
   stations on one track component), or the period's skims join none of the access stations.
3. **No market part answered that period**, and the worker fills in "no rail" itself (`demand.worker.ts`, the
   `if (!best)` after the parts loop in `icTrip`; its comment says this is meant: "with no part, an answer with no
   rail"). The same happens for every period when a worker has no market part at all but the market exists
   (`mcs.length === 0 && intercity && live`). An untested lead from reading the code: nobody has seen it give a
   daytime "no route" on a served pair.
4. **Rail above zero but no route listed** (`!main` in `MarketTrip.tsx`), T-1500's old shape, fixed 10-26 by always
   keeping the biggest route.

Both pictures show the day with some transit (`MarketTrip` sums the periods), so some periods had rail and the one
on screen didn't. In 1794788348 commutes are 11% transit over the day, yet the evening peak has nothing, not even
commuters; that fits a missing answer (3) or a dead period better than a real zero, but neither is shown.

Repro (scratch, `noroute/nyphl.mjs`, my Vite on 6196, one core; 1 demand worker, and a second run asking for 4 by
URL, not confirmed, which gave the same figures): New York - Philadelphia
with 3 New York and 2 Philadelphia feeders: all 5 periods rail 153-2,352 trips, 8 routes each, both ways.
Milan - Bologna, 3-stop high-speed line with 4/2/0 trains and 3 + 2 metro lines: periods 0-3 rail 189-407, 8
routes; night "noRail" (0 trains). Neither reproduces a daytime "no route".
