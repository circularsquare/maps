## The change

`workers/demandClient.ts`: `cacheDone()`. Once a game load's network is in and it is the cached result's network
(the cache is keyed by that exact network, packs, build and flags), with the zone in view holding figures, the
hold ends: the card's load ends unremembered, the clock runs. `cacheSolve` keeps what the hold gave the re-solve of the
same network: the barrier (it runs and publishes as under the card) and no level-of-detail changes until its final.
Any new network clears it, so an edit is an ordinary edit. `?perfOff=cacheCard` is the old card.
