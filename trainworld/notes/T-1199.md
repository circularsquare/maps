# T-1199: memory on mid-size worlds (2026-10-24)

Goal (Anita): a game under about 8 GB, less where the device has less. Her Paris and Shanghai save
uses about 6 GB. With T-1198 (the clicked-cities growth), notes/T-1198.md.

## For Anita

- Most of the memory is one table: the market (longer trips between zones) keeps a row for every
  pair of the world's 2,567 places, about 450 MB, and each of the three solving workers has its
  own copy. Clicking a city you have not built makes a second copy in each (another 1.2-1.3 GB).
  Before today it kept growing with every click; now it stops at the second copy.
- The fix that gives most of it back is a Rust change (T-1204): keep only the pairs that touch a
  place that is open. About 1.2 GB less on this save at load and 2.4 GB after a click, with every
  figure the same. It waits for the data join to finish.
- Huge networks (all of China) need more than that: a coarser model for zones when very many are
  built (T-1206). That changes figures a little, so it is your call; options below.

## Built (no figure changes)

T-1198's `recompose` in `demand.worker.ts`: the old composition freed before composing, and a part
made of the same packs reused. 435 MB less a solving worker once a clicked city's market has opened
and closed (1,845 to 1,410 MB in the Node probe with a network; 905 to 475 with none), and the
close costs 0.5 s instead of 4.5-7.5 s a worker. Nothing else cheap was left on the app side: the
market's size is set in Rust, and the rest is either fixed (worker heaps, MapLibre) or small.

## Proposed cuts, by GB saved on this save (3 workers)

1. **T-1204, market tables only for open places (Rust).** `pp`, `corridor_of`, `corridors`,
   `c_corridor` and `corridor_trips` are sized by every pair of places; as far as a read of
   `intercity.rs` shows, the market looks up pairs with an open place at one end (its zones'
   `place`), the rest coming from T-777's rows; the task confirms that first (line 2692's `pr(a, b)`
   and the `near` loop at 1306 are the ones to check). Indexed by open place x every place
   (k x 2,567, k the places with a pack: 9-12 here, about 200 for China), a composition drops from
   about 450 MB to tens of MB. **Saves about 1.2 GB at load and 2.4 GB after a click** (2 and 4 GB
   with five workers), and a composition's 4.5-7.5 s a worker. Cost: about a day of Rust, checked
   to the bit against the current composition (t301-worker-check, t326 hashes). Figures unchanged.
2. **Fewer solving workers on big games** (app). Each solving worker holds a full copy of the
   market and every reached zone; two instead of three saves one worker (about 0.7 GB here today,
   0.3 GB after T-1204; about 2.5 GB on the China network) at about 25% slower solves (T-361's
   table). A rule by measured memory (each worker's WASM after the first solve, times the count,
   against a budget of about 6 GB, or half that where `deviceMemory` is under 8) would cap only
   the games that need it. Part of T-1205. Figures unchanged (worker count changes no number).
3. **T-1205, give back a worker's high-water mark** (app). WASM never shrinks, so after a peak
   (a clicked city's market, an edit's set-up) the worker keeps it. Starting a solving worker afresh
   when idle and well above its need returns it: about 0.4 GB a worker today after a click, 50-100
   MB after T-1204. Cost: medium (the client reopens the worker's packs and re-solves, or serves
   from the kept result while it does), overlapping the loading agent's teardown code.
4. **T-1206, a coarser model on huge networks (changes figures; Anita decides).** On the China
   network most of the 2.4-2.6 GB a worker is zones. Options, cheapest first:
   - a. One everyday destination sample per 32 trips instead of 8 (T-397) once more than about 30
     zones are reached: the samples and their pair indices (most of a zone's everyday memory) shrink
     about four times; figures move by sampling noise. Perhaps 0.8-1.2 GB a worker on China.
   - b. Zones far from every other reached zone's market (not the view: figures must not change
     with panning) solved on T-852's 4 km blocks instead of 2 km. Coarser local trips there.
   - c. Reached zones with few riders (say under 1% of the network's) solved for the morning and
     evening peaks only, the other periods scaled from them. Fewer periods held.
   Each needs a check that the network's totals stay within a few per cent.
5. Small: T-361 (one engine per zone, 20-40 MB a big zone a worker), T-876 (about 12 MB a worker),
   T-777 (rows by country), an LRU for the page's per-zone caches (about 2 MB a city visited).
   Not worth doing for memory alone.

With 1 the Paris and Shanghai save is about 2.4 GB at load and stays there; a China-sized network
then needs 2 or 4 to fit 8 GB.
