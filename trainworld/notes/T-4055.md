  PVD,PHL gives nyc,pvd,phl. Both orders give the same figures within each history (table above). Zone/pack open order
  changes nothing.
- B (creation order): no effect either. Separate edits: 68,341 in both orders. One batch: 63,996 in both orders.
- The batchcheck14 "order" result was really batch against separate. Its PHL-then-PVD "separate" run most likely had the second

## Cause, as far as the evidence goes

The first (batch) solve ends as final (version 2, round 3) with figures that differ from the true ones by 6% for the PHL line,
and the next solve of any kind replaces them. A fresh load of the saved game gives the true values. So it is a solve
that was computed on a market composition (open packs) older than the one the page then holds, and nothing re-solves
when the packs finished arriving. Zones open together in one edit: PHL and PVD packs arrive during/after the first solve; the
separate path has the first solve finish before the second pack opens, and the pack opening forces a new solve.
Not found: which pack the stale solve lacks, or the exact line in demandClient/demand.worker that declares final without
re-solving (candidates: openQueued/"open" handling in demand.worker.ts, `solveAgain`/`flush` in demandClient.ts). Not tested:
a debug read of each worker's composition at the final.

## What a fix would take

Small to medium, probably 1-2 hours: log pack opens and solve starts per worker for the batch case (add a debug read of
composedOf key and open pack ids to the result message), find which open landed after the solve's market was composed,
and make a pack open that completes after a solve began mark the solve stale, so a new solve runs and the final waits for it
(or the final only counts a result whose composition key equals the page's last pack set). Then a standing check: batch
vs separate give the same PHL figure. The reach: any edit that reaches two new zones at once, or one whose packs arrive during
its solve; the player sees up to 6% too few riders on lines in the newly reached zones until their next edit. Real-save loads
are not affected (one composition).
