## #16, douglaszickuhr: branch lines

- What: a branch is an ordinary line with `LineData::branch = head`. After every edit it copies the head's name,
  symbol, colour, cars, limit, dwell, group (a `branch_ops` pass beside `twin_ops`). Shift + click a station off the
  line makes one: the sim tries every stop as the junction and both sides as the trunk, keeps routes with no reversal,
  takes the shortest hop. Stop edits on the head reach branches; remove route promotes the next branch; save block
  `LBR1`. The line panel lists routes as rows; figures (riders, money, trains) fold into the head by station; branches
  are left out of lists. 55 files, +1,422/-112, 29 new Rust tests, a plain-node check. Service, capacity, spacing,
  boarding and demand are untouched: they see separate lines as today.
- Where it stands: you commented on it 2026-11-16 19:55 ("hesitant ... line groups are intended to solve this problem
  with no additional code required"). He answered 21:33 that groups cover naming and colour, and what he missed was
  (1) making the branch (copy line, re-edit stops, find the side that does not reverse) and (2) trunk edits having
  to be made on every line of the group. He offers to cut it to a Shift + click that makes a plain line in the same
  group (no relation, no save block, no folds or band changes), and optionally trunk stop edits reaching the group's
  lines. He has not been answered since. No task on our side; line groups inside groups is T-3423 (unrelated).
- Applies: 3 conflicts, each one hunk. save.rs (the BLOCKS array and LEVEL), clock.worker.ts (his `wearShape` split
  against our T-3420 hidden-lines edit in `newLineShape`), today.ts (his `branchesToo` against the twin upkeep line).
  All mechanical. Save level: his 19 collides with maps' 19 (ferry route block); on maps it would be 20, and a
  merged #16 is the third thing wanting a new level since the export's 18, so levels are no longer yours to promise.
- Risk: large and spread out (55 files: today.ts, lineBands.ts, stopMarkers, search, achievements, stationLabels,
  twin.rs, the inspector). A new relation between lines that every line list, figure and band has to know about;
  his own limitations list five gaps (turn_end, open_loop, order_stops and sort_stops not carried; trunk stop removal
  on a branch only; a branch's trunk routed fresh; the diagram draws a second stroke). A second way to build
  branches next to line groups, which you meant to be the way. It also adds a thing to build and a gesture (a
  change to what players can build), so it waits for your word under CLAUDE.md.
- Recommendation: **her call; I'd leave the PR as built**. The part that answers a real gap is small: Shift + click
  makes a copy line in the same group with the right junction and no reversal, and trunk stops reaching group
  siblings. If you want either, reply to his question and ask for that cut-down PR; it would cost no save level if
  he does it as he describes. Credit him if any of it is used.

## #22, krosk93: level focus on the map

- What: a row in the map's top-left controls (the DemandSelector box): seven buttons for levels -3 to +3 and "All
  levels". Click toggles a level, double click shows only that one, at least one level always stays on. Levels not in
  focus draw at 30% (not hidden): track, lines, stations and their names, ridership circles, stop markers, trains,
  grade marks, one-way chevrons; the basemap never dims. While the track or station tool is in hand, nodes and track on
  unfocused levels are not snap or join targets, so track can be drawn over or under other track without catching it
  (ramps count as focused if either end is). Capacity tags, bus and ferry ribbons, station buildings and car parks
  stay full strength. The mask is a per-browser display setting (`display.levelFocus`), no save change. +461/-75, 29
  files. Added data: a per-sample height (`sampleLevel`) from the clock worker for trains, and `Service::position3`.
- Strings (6 in en, plus ca): "Level focus", "Choose levels to highlight", "{name} ({label})", the tip "{name}
  ({label}) / At least one level stays highlighted | Click to dim|highlight / Double click to highlight only this
  level", "All levels", "Highlight every level". The tip carries two interaction instructions for ordinary buttons,
  which our hover rules drop ("No interaction instructions for ordinary controls"); the "Level focus" group label and
  the intro would need rewording to our voice in any case.
- Applies: 6 files conflict, renderer.ts the largest (6 hunks) next to rendererShaders.ts (2), gradeMarks.ts (2),
  stationCircles.ts, tools.ts, ui.ts (1 each). Maps' renderer changed with T-3430 (per-layer multisample buffers) and
  the stroke work; the shader additions (a `u_level_focus` int uniform, `a_level` on station instances, a levels
  texture for trains) are small and portable. No Rust beyond the two level-carrying functions.
- Risk: it adds a vertex attribute and a per-sample texture to the stations and trains (a little more memory, on
  the P1 memory line, T-3160), changes which nodes the tools snap to, and adds a setting row to the already
  crowded map controls. It is a new feature, a new setting and a change to snapping, so it waits for your word. The
  author tested a new game and the real NYC save headless, with no console errors; there are no unit tests.
- Recommendation: **her call**. If yes: take with changes (our strings, the two tip instructions dropped, memory
  measured on London, the maps renderer port by hand), about 3-5 h. If no: thank him; a design question you may want
  to give him is whether "dim the other levels" or "hide the other levels" is the idea (see the TODO about
  settings clutter).
