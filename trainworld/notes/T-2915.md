## What is already the same

One schedule table (`Schedule` in schedule.tsx, T-2373): three demand levels, a count or a headway per level,
the same steppers, hovers, cash refusal, 1 minute to 4 hours. One departures rule (`Service::run_deps`), one
hand-over at the ends, one fleet rule (owned, needed, spare, shared within the mode), one Average load basis
(T-2373, Anita), one fare curve, one Copy line rule (the copy has no vehicles). Dwell and turnaround are set
when a line is made and no mode lets the player change them.

## Recommended, by how much a player notices against the work

1. **Riders by purpose on bus and ferry panels** (row 4). Pass `PurposeSplit` as `extra` beside Changing to
   trains. An hour or two. Wait for the Purpose bubble colour and hover follow-ups filed 2026-11-09 so it is
   done once.
2. **Crowding dots for boats, then buses** (row 2). Boats: make `LineSvc::rated()` 320 for a boat (T-2460)
   and pass `crowd` in BusInspector. Small, sim plus app, one check on a ferry save. Buses: the same call,
   but rated equals crush, so the dot stops at yellow; still tells the player which level is full. Without
   it the schedule gives no hint whether to add buses or boats. Half a day for both.
3. **Riders left at platform for ferries** (row 3) and **boardings today per port** (row 13). The account
   already keeps a port's crowd. Small, app only. Bus stays without (no platform).
4. **One stop-time format and north-up order** (rows 7, 8): the rail form everywhere. Small, app only. Do
   it inside or right after T-2895 since that rewrites the rail list.
5. **Schedule copy and paste on bus and ferry** (row 6): headways or counts only; a paste across modes
   leaves out cars and kind. Small. The timetable chart stays rail only unless she asks.
6. **A short round trip block for bus and ferry** (row 5): running, standing at stops, turning at the ends.
   Small to medium: `RoundTrip`'s dwell sum assumes a dwell at every middle stop, and a bus's street stands
   sit inside its running time, so it needs its own split from the sim.
7. **One delete rule** (row 12): ask only when the removal cannot be undone (a built bus route); a train
   line then goes at once like a ferry line. Small. An agent's call unless she likes the question on trains.
8. **One list component and one set of gestures for stops** (row 10), after T-2895 and T-2891 land, not
   before: dot click removes, drag reorders, the same rule for whether an edit mode is needed. Ferry's
   `FerryPorts` already has the drag; T-2895's design reads it. Medium. Until then, both agents should copy
   the ferry gesture and not invent a third.
9. **Defaults in one place** (row 22): each mode's new-line headways, dwell and turnaround as sim constants
   the app reads. Small, no player effect.

## Leave different

Rows 9, 11, 14, 16 to 21. Dwell and turnaround differ for real reasons and no mode edits them. Capacity is
track's. Bus demand rules and fares are Anita's and "buses are settled" (CLAUDE.md, 2026-10-27). Prices follow
her simple-rule decision. Row 15 (a bus's rated load equal to its crush) is hers too; changing it moves bus
demand.
