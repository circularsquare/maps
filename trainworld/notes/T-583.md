GHSL nres is concentrated the same way.

## The rule proposed (not built)

Each building part of the proxy carries at most a fixed number of jobs per unit of its signal.
When a pack's signal is thin, the jobs it cannot carry go to the population part:
`b_part_eff = min(b_part, K_part x S_part / J)`, for OSM (units) and nres (height-weighted volume).
Toronto and London should be untouched if K sits above their ratios (then misplaced is unchanged
exactly). This matches tier 0's everyday rule (completeness by places a resident) and gives a
shop or a land unit a believable job count. Expected on the scratch packs with K_osm about 30: Aba's
top place ~6,000 jobs (0.86%, near the 1% gate), Aboh 0.2%, Owerri 0.15%.

On its own it does not bound a small pack's share: a 1 km² area always gets K x 200 jobs, more
than 1% of a pack with under ~600k jobs. If the gate's 1% should hold everywhere, add a cap on one
place's (and one cell's) share of jobs through the building parts at about 0.5%, again with the
excess going to population. That is the brief's first option, measured in the pack's jobs.
