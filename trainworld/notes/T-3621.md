# T-3621 Cairo scored as a hard start (2026-11-18, agent cairo)

## Cause

The start's centre landed in the desert. `start_difficulty.centre()` takes the OSM city or town named "Cairo" in the
pack's own place list nearest the registry centre within 40 km. The only such place in `cai.places.json` is an OSM
place=city *area* (a governorate-sized outline) whose box centre is 31.5645, 30.0346: 37.0 km east of the registry
centre, past New Cairo. Its 10 km circle holds 53,336 of the pack's people (the sim's own count for the start was
53,955), against 7,964,153 round the registry centre (31.1816, 30.0141, Giza side) and 10,972,292 round the real Cairo
node (Tahrir, 31.2357, 30.0444). So the score of 439 was the desert's.

The real Cairo node (place=city, capital=yes, population 9,120,350) is missing from `cai.places.json` because it sits
0.25 km outside the pack's footprint (`cai.boundary.geojson`, a 13-part MultiPolygon whose two big parts meet along the
Nile; the node is in that gap), and places.py drops points outside the footprint. The pack's cells are fine: 55,936
people within 1 km of Tahrir, 3.2M within 5 km.

Not the cause: no other registry start is within 40 km of Cairo (no metro split over packs; every cell within 10 km of
Tahrir is in `cai`), and the pack's demand is ordinary (legs per person below).

Data side, for the orchestrator (not fixed here): the Tahrir node outside the footprint means a search or a station
name near central Cairo can't use "Cairo" from that node, and the stray area centre is a place named Cairo, kind city,
population 0, in the desert (placeList names stations within 6 km of a city of no population after it). Whether the
in-game search for "Cairo" lands on the stray point was not checked.

## Same symptom elsewhere

Swept all 462 metro starts (scratch `sweep2.py`): people of the start's own pack within 2 km and 10 km of the chosen
centre and of the registry centre. Cairo is alone: 652 people within 2 km of its chosen centre, the next lowest is
Oklahoma City's 11,284 (an unmoved centre in a sprawling city). As a share of the registry circle's people within 10 km,
Cairo had 0.7%; the next lowest are Ramallah, Alexandria and Istanbul at 68-71% (their OSM city node is the old centre,
a few km off the registry centre: the rule working as meant), then nothing under 80%.

## Fix

`centre()` now skips a name match whose 10 km circle holds under half (CENTRE_MIN_PEOPLE 0.5) the people of the
registry centre's circle (the start's own pack's cells), trying the next nearest match, else the registry centre. A pack
that can't be read keeps the nearest match. Dry run: only Cairo's centre changes (4,134 starts), to the registry centre.
docs/zones.md "Start tiers" says so.

(cai.json 2026-11-12 09:32). `suc` is gone from the registry since T-3619 (the night run's T-3511 work).

rank). Totals easy 124, medium 603 -> 601, hard 3,408 -> 3,409. Other tier changes, all scores unchanged and sitting
on the old cuts: Wenzhou easy -> medium (12,147, it was the old easy cut; T-3619 had just moved it up), João Pessoa
medium -> hard (3,980, the old medium cut), zones Catania (3,990) and Dashiqiao (3,997) medium -> hard. No other score
moved by over 2%.
