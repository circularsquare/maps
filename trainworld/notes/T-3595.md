## Her break: Caracas Line 2, an extension that ends in a station

Her saves: `artifacts/trainworld/t3595-caracas-day45-earlier.save` (16:14:01) and
`t3595-caracas-sanmartin-broken.save` (16:14:29). Both already have the extension to San Martín (station 146, at the
end of the new track: node 146 has one edge) and both have Line 2 broken ("Track this line ran on is gone"). They
differ only in Line 2's stops (the later one drops San Martín). So the earlier one is not a before.

Repro on `artifacts/trainworld/t3541-caracas.save`, on master (T-3541 in), played through play.mjs: tram double drawn
300 m on from the loose end just past Avenida Arismendi (node 144), a station placed at the new track's far end,
Construct blueprints. Line 2: running, 1h08m round trip -> broken, "Track this line ran on is gone". The same with
the station 60% along the new track (bare track beyond it) stays running (1h08m -> 1h09m; the return run turns at
the new end, T-3541's fix). That is her "only some of the time".

On her broken save, setting Line 2's stops again on master: any stop list that takes in Pasaje Calzadilla I with a
stop before it stays broken (her original Este 18 list too, with no stop on the extension at all); San Martin >
Arismendi > Loyola runs.

## Cause

Line 2's return run (derived on every edit, `route_hops` through the stops reversed) can reach Pasaje Calzadilla I,
on the one-way leg that runs toward El Silencio, only by lapping, and the lap turns back at the bare track end
(node 144) just past Arismendi: a train may turn back only at a stop or a bare end (`bare_end`: no station). Built
track past 144 makes it no end. If the new track ends in bare track, the turn moves there (T-3541). If it ends at a
station (not a bare end, and not a stop the run is at), there is nowhere left to turn: no way back, the line is
broken. Nothing about the extension itself is wrong; the line was only running because of a turn at a loose end.

## The onewaystops tree (T-3565, stopped) fixes her Line 2

Served from a scratch copy of `.tw-trees/onewaystops/trainworld/app` (its wasm built 15:48, before its last
`legs.rs` change; the copy's node_modules and data were junctions, since removed: don't repeat that, point a Vite at
the tree read only instead): her broken save loads with Line 2 running (Arismendi > Loyola > Calzadilla I > Este 12 >
El Silencio, 19m16s, Calzadilla I served one way); every stop list above runs; the t3541 repro (station at the far
end, constructed) stays running, 20m04s, the stops put in order on load. Its leg stop rule means the return run no
longer needs a turn past Arismendi.

So her Line 2 is an acceptance case for T-3565: on the t3541 save, extend past Arismendi to a station at the track's
end, construct, and Line 2 runs.

## The rule, fixed (agent onewaystops2, with T-3565, 2026-11-17)

Decision (maps-1c): a train may turn back at any track end, station or not. Built in `.tw-trees/onewaystops2`:
`Network::turn_end` (one constructed end, station or not) in `find_path_side` and wasm `reach_from`;
`stop_reversals` (reversals that must fall on a stop) leaves out every track end, and `build_run` tells a turn at a
stop (turnaround stood at the stop) from a free turn (`Run::ends`) by the stop match. Detail: notes/T-3565.md
"T-3595 (stage 2)". The headshunt Rust test runs (not ignored) and passes; the check below passes; the return run
runs out to the new station and back without stopping, as at a bare end.
