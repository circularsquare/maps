## The timeline (base, perf_bench run, 5 cores, 3 solving workers + the helper; s from the import)

perf_bench imports the save over a finished new NYC game, so the page's own start (boot, 7.3 s here) comes before it.
No demand result is kept for this network in this build (a first load after an update is the same), so the loading
card holds the clock until the trips are final: everything below is "the game loading" for her.

| s | what |
|---|---|
| 0.2 | the save decoded; its stations sent ahead, so the demand side asks for its packs |
| 0.8-3 | 126 zone packs and 278 market packs asked and downloaded (439 MB from the local server; R2 and her line add to this, the browser cache takes it off a second load) |
| 1-51 | each zone opened in every worker, one after another (each worker 35-45 s of opening); markets composed at 47.7 |

The three biggest pieces of the 215.6 s:
1. **The trips solve itself**, 50.8 to 215.6 s: **165 s, 76%**. 36 period passes over 6 clusters, 321 s of the
   workers' period time summed, setup 17 s.
2. **Opening the packs in every worker**, 1 to 51 s: **50 s, 23%** (before the solve can start). Every solving
   worker and the helper open all 126 zones (commutes plus the everyday layer, 0.1-4 s a zone, tyo 2.9 s), then the
   market (9-12 s a worker in the dev probe; T-4199's area).
3. **The map**, 0 to 24 s: **24 s, 11%**, alongside piece 2, so it delays what she sees, not the trips. The clock
   worker's steps (dev probe, 4 cores, the demand workers busy beside it): water masks for 218 zones 9.0 s, the network
   into the track model 4.9 s, street speeds for 9 zones (the buses) 8.1 s, one zone's mask the first pass missed and
   the network loaded a second time 3.0 s, terrain 1.7 s, then 10.2 s from "network open" to the first full state.

mostly fine (a smaller game, or a phone) it does more.

## What changed: no closing and reopening while a save loads in a running game

A save opened while another game runs (perf_bench's import; a slot or file opened from the game) sends its stations
ahead, so its packs start opening before its network arrives. Meanwhile the pool's sweep (`demandClient.ts`
`keepSet`/`sweep`) still went by the last game's reach, found the new save's opened zones spare, closed all but two
(KEEP_SPARE), and opened them again once the network came: 23 zones twice on this save (dev probe: 11 zones closed at
19 s and reopened at 32 s in every worker; the reopening was 10-14 s of each solving worker and 26 s of the helper).
Now `keepSet` keeps the save's prefetched zones and markets while `ahead` has not arrived.

A load from the start menu with a fresh page was not affected (startup is "loading" then, and the sweep waits). So this
is not her "initial load"; it is a load from inside a game, and every perf_bench measurement (the bench's import is that
path, and NYC sometimes reopened a zone too: 64.5 MB down against 62.9).

Figures the same: rail trips identical on both saves (82,603,971; 12,672,465).

## What is left (proposals, nothing built)

- **The solve (76%)** is the load. Figures-same speedups of the period passes are the demand performance line's long
  work. The quickest way to make her wait shorter is the kept result: a load of the same network in the same build
  already shows its kept figures and lets her play once the map and the zone in view are in (T-328, T-1539, T-2560); a
  new build drops them. Showing the last build's kept figures as a stand-in while the new ones calculate (the card going
  at about the map's time, trips calculating after) is a call for her (old figures shown for a few minutes after an
  update). About 3-4 hours.
- **Opening the packs (23%)**: every worker opens every zone, one after another. Next step is a native profile of
  `DemandApi::at_level` and `EverydayApi::beside_with` on the big zones (tyo, sha, seo, osa) to see whether a part
  of the open can move to the pipeline. About 2 hours to profile and propose.
- **The map (24 s)**: masks and street speeds are fetched before the network opens; one zone's mask the first pass
  misses makes the network load twice (3 s, `clockGround.ts` `masksBeforeLoad` takes the save's points, the second
  pass the drawn curves); 10 s between "network open" and the first state are in `clock.worker.ts` (tree stopclick's
  file today). About 2 hours.
