# T-4381 — No cap on buses per hour in a bus lane

Dropped the 100 buses/hour per-way cap on BRT lanes (`LANE_BUSES_H` / `bus::lanes::Room`) and its UI row/alerts (`BusInspector` "Busiest lane" row and full-lane alert, `SchedulePanel` BRT lane limits), keeping shared-lane cost/upkeep splitting (`bus::lanes::Lanes`), speed floor, in-town pricing, BRT stations, and busways untouched.

## What changed

- **Sim (`sim/src/track/`)**:
  - `bus/lanes.rs`: removed `LANE_BUSES_H`, `LaneWay`, `Room`, `steps_of`, `Lanes::build_of`, and `Lanes::sharers_way`; kept `Lanes`, `town_steps`, `marks`, and `sharers` for shared-lane cost/upkeep.
  - `capacity.rs`: removed `LANE_RES`, `LANE_KIND`, and the `brt_room` loops in `line_limits_lone` and `fill`.
  - `net.rs`, `net/store.rs`: removed `Network::brt_room` cache field and method.
  - `world.rs`: removed `fit_lanes` and its calls in `settle_unpaced` and `settle_weekend`, and removed `brt_room` / `brt_order(true)` from `sharing_closure`.
  - `wasm/service.rs`: removed `lane_load`, `lane_held`, `bus_lane_load`, and `LANE_RES`/`LANE_KIND` handling in `limit_place`, `line_held`, and `line_row_held`.
  - `wasm/bus_tests.rs`: replaced `a_full_brt_lane_cuts_its_lines_buses` and `a_full_brt_lane_cuts_weekend_buses_too` with `brt_lane_has_no_bus_cap` and `brt_lane_has_no_weekend_bus_cap`.
- **App (`app/src/`)**:
  - `game/capacityKinds.ts`: removed `LANE_KIND` and `KINDS[LANE_KIND]`.
  - `workers/protocol.ts`, `workers/clockDemand.ts`: removed `laneLoad` from `ClockBus` and `a.bus_lane_load(id)` calls.
  - `ui/inspector/BusInspector.tsx`: removed `LaneRow`, `useLaneHeld`, `LaneHeld`, and `limits` on `Schedule`.
  - `ui/inspector/SchedulePanel.tsx`: removed `lineHeld` query in `BusBody`.
  - `i18n/`: removed `busInspector.lane`, `busInspector.laneLoad`, `busInspector.laneFullTip`, `busInspector.laneHeld`, and `capacityKinds.laneTitle` from `context.json`, all 13 `messages/*.json`, and all 12 `sources/*.json`.
- **Docs, SPEC, Wiki**:
  - Updated `SPEC.md` section 6, `docs/track-and-service.md` (removed "Lane room"), `docs/ui-inspectors.md` (bus line panel), and `wiki/brt.md` + `wiki/read/brt.md`.

## Effect on existing saves

Removing the cap does not change trip or rider figures on saves where no BRT lane exceeded 100 buses an hour (`fit_lanes` was a no-op when no way exceeded `LANE_BUSES_H`, and `brt_room`/`line_limits` only drove the cap and UI).
