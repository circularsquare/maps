# T-3753 Cross-border trips about 1% off the fare curve

Agent xborder, 2026-11-19, tree xborder (Vite 6145). App only; no sim change.

## Cause

A city population's curve extra (and base extra) came back in its own country's dollars, while income
charges the base and rate at the stop's scale. T-3748 converted only the market's extras (curve dollars) to the
stop's scale per route node; the cities' were added as they came (`sumAll` in the demand worker). So a Detroit
resident boarding in Windsor paid the base and rate at Canada's 0.784 but the extra at the US's 1. The checker's
guess was right.

Measured on the checker's fixture (new Detroit game, line "Cross", 2 stops, 36.95 km US to Canada, bent curve
[0,1],[10,3],[30,4],[100,4.5]), each population's figures logged from the worker:
- Only Detroit's populations (commute and everyday) and the market ride it; no Windsor city population.
- Detroit's extra at the Windsor route node: -21.8285 / 5.02903 riders = -4.3405 a rider, the curve's extra at
  scale 1 (curve 4.0496, first stretch 1 + 0.2 x 36.95 = 8.390).
- City riders boarding in Windsor over the day: 10.168; x 4.3405 x (1 - 0.7845) = 9.51, exactly the gap
  (hand 963.69, game 954.18). Riders reconcile (254.57 market + 13.15 city = 267.73), so no second cause.

## Fix

One rule, owned by `countryFareScales(country, fares)` (game/fareDefault.ts): a country's base and rate
multiples of the curve as set (its scale, or its rounded default's ratios, T-1662).
- `stopFareScales` (game/money.ts) is that function on the stop's country (income, `packService`'s `fareS` and
  `baseS`).
- The demand worker keeps each city population's own multiples (`popScales`, from the same function in
  `setUpPops`), and `sumAll` charges every population's extras at the leaving stop's multiple over the
  population's own (the market's own is 1). A stop whose multiple equals the population's (compared as f32, as
  `fareS` holds them) adds the value as it is, so one-country games are unchanged to the bit.
- Absent `fareS` (every stop at 1) still converts a population whose own multiple is not 1.
- The transfer fare is inside the cities' `fare_extra`, so it is now charged at the scale of the stop where the
  rider changes.
- Docs: `docs/track-and-service.md` "Fares and the ledgers" (the rule, and the two lies left); wiki `fares.md`
  ("A trip pays the scale of the country it starts in, ..." -> "Each part of it pays the scale of the country it leaves from: the base fare at the stop where it boards, each stretch at the stop where that stretch starts.").
  DECISIONS 2026-11-19.

## Not done

The base extra on a line's later stretches pays the stop each leaves, where "the boarding stop's" would need the
sim to carry each trip's boarding scale into `board_base`. Only on a line with its own multiple whose trips ride
past a border stop; zero on a two-stop line. Named in DECISIONS.

## Strings

None. No changelog item: about 1% on lines crossing a border between countries of different fare scales, too
small for a player to notice.

Advisor called once before the fix: confirmed the cause on the numbers; said to convert by construction from one
function, compare multiples as f32 for bit-exactness, treat absent `fareS` as all 1, and not go into Rust for the
base extra.
