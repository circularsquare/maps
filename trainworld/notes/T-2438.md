# T-2438: the network diagram zoomed far out, city names only

Anita (2026-11-04): "also for schematic map, when zoomed far out, we should not show station names, just city names."
Rule: docs/ui-diagram.md "City names". Code: `app/src/diagram/cityNames.ts` (placed in `workers/diagram.worker.ts` after
the borders, painted from `diagram/view.ts`), `names.ts` (the faint levels now start at 1.47 px). Check:
`checks/t2438-city-names.mjs`.

## What it does

- Below a pitch of 1.47 px (names level k = -9) no station names; each block has its name, at levels from 0.16 to
  1.24 px. From 1.47 px up the station names as before (the faint big-station levels 1.47 to 3.5 px, then the rest).
- A block's name: the zone holding most of its stations (by the borders' zone per station), ties to a city zone, as
  the map shows it. The page sends each zone's name and width with its outline. A name a bigger block has
  is not shown again.
- Placed biggest block first, at the one of up to twelve places round its box (above, below, beside, inside a corner)
  covering the least line, never over another city name.

## Choices

- **The switch at 1.47 px**, not at the first level proper (4.2 px, where the faint levels started): at 3.5 px the real
  New York save is 2,400 px across, so the one name "New York" above it would be off screen with nothing else named.
  At 1.47 px New York is about 1,000 px across, a screen; Tokyo's block 914 px. Below the old faint levels (0.62 px)
  nothing was named at all; Japan whole on a screen is about 0.45 px, now named by cities.
- **Plurality, not the biggest zone by trips, nor a city zone first**: Japan's zones are prefecture-sized, and Gunma,
  Tochigi and Nagano are plain zones, not city zones, so either rule named Gunma's block "Tokyo" (Tokyo held 50 of its
  175 stations, Gunma 109). The zone with most of the block's stations is the place it is.
- **One name once**: Japan's zones hold several blocks (Nagano five, Tokyo, Nagasaki and Shizuoka three); naming each
  "Nagano" reads as an error, so only the biggest shows it. The smaller ones go unnamed (T-2447 for naming them by
  place).
- Same font, size, ink and halo as the station names, at full opacity.
