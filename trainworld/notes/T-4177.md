## If you want the access gone on AWS's side too (optional, yours or your friend's)

This PC can no longer reach the account, but the user `anita` and its key still exist there. To delete them:

1. Open https://console.aws.amazon.com
2. Sign in to account 429583250612 as IAM user `anita` (or your friend signs in as herself)
3. Type IAM in the search bar at the top
4. Click IAM
5. Click Users in the left menu
6. Click anita
7. Click the Security credentials tab
8. Under Access keys, click Actions on the one key
9. Click Deactivate, then Deactivate again to confirm
10. Click Actions on the same key
11. Click Delete, paste the key id it shows, click Delete
12. Under Console sign-in, click Manage console access
13. Click Disable, then Apply (this signs you out if you are `anita`)
14. Your friend, signed in as herself: IAM, Users, tick anita
15. Click Delete, type anita, click Delete

Steps 8-11 delete the key and steps 12-13 the password; 14-15 the user and its two policies. None can be undone,
but your friend can make a new user at any time.
