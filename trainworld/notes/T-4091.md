# T-4091: undoing a bus reroute and its stops

Outcome (11-28, busundo): on the current build an undo puts every stop of a rerouted bus line back exactly. No code
change; one Rust test pins it. The sighting that filed this task does not reproduce, and its cause was not found.

## The sighting

The T-4069 checker (notes/checkbns.md, case 3) ran on the busnosnap tree before T-4069 landed (9e216718c, 11-23 09:33),
on the real NYC save: after undoing a busRoute reroute the route was back to 2 points, but station 105's stop was at
-73.99385, not -73.99374 (about 9 m). Not compared with master. Its scripts went with its scratchpad, so how it read the
route and the stops is unknown.

## Why the code can't do that

- `set_bus_route` (sim/src/track/wasm/bus.rs) edits a copy of the line's data: placed, kept and Ctrl-placed stops are
  projected onto the new route, then one `Op::Line`.
- `Network::set_line` (net/edit.rs) hands back the whole old `LineData` as the inverse; `line_data` clones the
  `BusRoute` (points, kinds, hand, hand_kept, exact, first_off, spacing, BRT flags). The undo applies exactly that.
- Stops are never stored. `build_bus` (service.rs) works them out again from the route on every rebuild
  (`bus::plan_route`); a station's stop sits where the route passes nearest the station, and `Poly` is pure geometry.
  An undo rebuilds the line: `Touch.lines` holds it, `lines_touched` keeps it, `update_scoped` always rebuilds.
- The page: bus lines get no stamp (wasm/touched.rs `live_stamp`), so `take_changed_lines` always lists them, and
  stateRows.ts re-reads a listed bus line's whole row, route and stops from one `busRow` call. Before T-4113 (11-23
  14:18, so also in the T-4069 tree) bus rows were never kept at all. Neither side can pair the old route with the
  bent route's stops.
- Ruled out on the way: a constructed route (its reroute is final and clears the undo history), the T-4214 map patch
  (rail paths only), the drawn-ahead route (T-4147, landed after T-4069).

## Checks

- Rust: `track::wasm::bus_tests::undoing_a_redraw_puts_the_stops_back_where_they_were`. Rail stations 1.7 km apart, a
  Manual bus line 90 m beside them with stops at both stations, one between and one Ctrl-placed 130 m from a station.
  Reroutes with a 4 m and a 60 m bend each move the station stops; undo gives the stops and route back bit for bit,
  redo the bent stops, a second undo the first ones. Both suites pass (836 and 1073 tests).
- Game run: a new sandbox NYC game on the tree's build, the same fixture made with the bus tool's ops
  (`tw.client.edit`), a 60 m bend, then the real Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+Z. The page's stops (longitude, latitude to
  6 places):
