# T-292: Mumbai from tier 0 with sub-district census totals and a curve fitted to B-28

2026-10-15. Mumbai (`mum`) built by `pipeline/tier0.py` with India inputs from
`pipeline/india.py`: Census 2011 population by sub-district grown to 2020 by each district's
2001-2011 growth (the `population` hook), tier 0's jobs proxy with half its weight on population
(the new `job_blend` key), work from home at B-28's "no travel" share, and both curve parameters
fitted to Census 2011 table B-28's distance bands (the new `tier0.fit_curve_bands`). Registered,
`market: -1`, `utcOffset` 5. Place names in Latin script where OSM has one.

Scripts: `pipeline/india.py` (units, outline, population hook, B-28 bands), `checks/city-browser.mjs
mum`. Logs and images in `../artifacts/trainworld/T-292-*`: `features.log`, `water-read.log`,
`mum-outline.png`, `mum-build1-units.log` (first build, every taluka scaled on its own),
`mum-build.log`, `mum-check.log`, `mum-water.png`, `mum-pack.png`, `mum-places.log`,
`mum-routes.log`, `mum-browser.json`/`.log`, `packs-before.sha256`/`packs-after.sha256`,
`tokens.txt`.

## Footprint

The Mumbai Metropolitan Region as whole units: Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban districts and 15
talukas, Thane, Kalyan, Bhiwandi, Ulhasnagar, Ambarnath, Murbad and Shahapur (Thane district),
Vasai and Palghar (Palghar district, part of Thane district in 2011), Panvel, Uran, Karjat,
Khalapur, Pen and Alibag (Raigad). MMR proper takes only parts of Shahapur, Murbad, Palghar,
Karjat, Pen and Alibag; whole units widen it (24.2M people in 2011 against MMR's 22.8M). About
9,100 km2, 8 land parts (the island city's two, Elephanta and other islets), no holes
(`T-292-mum-outline.png`). Thane Creek, the harbour and Vasai Creek lie between units, outside
the outline. Box 72.65-73.80 E, 18.49-19.87 N. No other pack nearby.

**Unit polygons**: OSM boundary relations from the Geofabrik western zone extract, Mumbai's two
districts at admin_level 5 and the talukas at 6, all 17 present and valid; clipping by the OSM
coastline changed no area (OSM's taluka lines already follow the shore). SHRUG's PC11 polygons
were not used.

## Decisions (DECISIONS.md 2026-10-15)

- **Population grown by district, not GHSL unscaled.** GHSL's total in the outline (27.49M) is
  2.9% under the grown census total (28.30M), so either total would do; scaling keeps the census's
  split: GHSL has Mumbai City +14% and Mumbai Suburban +9% over the grown figures (the dense-core
  excess London and Shanghai showed) and Thane -10%, Vasai -10%, Palghar -14%, Panvel -13%
  (fast-growing suburbs; GHSL's +14% on 2011 is near-uniform). Carried-forward growth may run
  high for Thane district (+32% in nine years); there is no 2021 census to check it.
- **Kalyan, Bhiwandi, Ulhasnagar and Ambarnath scaled as one** (`india.GROUPS`): on the first build
  (`mum-build1-units.log`) GHSL in OSM's Ulhasnagar was 2.56 times its 2011 census population,
  Bhiwandi's 0.55 and Kalyan's 0.70, while the four together are +1.8%. OSM's lines between them do
  not follow the 2011 talukas; checking against SHRUG's polygons would have needed the
  non-commercial file, and the sum settles it.
- **Jobs blend population 0.5** (tier 0: 0.2), by judgement for informal and home-based work.
- **Work from home 20.1%**: B-28's 'no travel' share of other workers in the four districts.
- **B-28 as road distance**: the census asks the worker the distance to the place of work, so the
  band edges (1.5, 5.5, 10.5, 20.5, 30.5, 50.5 km, between reported whole km) are divided by 1.3.
  `routes.py` later measured the near zone pairs' median drive factor at 1.62; not refitted (T-314).
  'Distance not stated' (3.6%) left out; the 51+ km band is kept though some of it works outside.
- Test line: the Western line, Churchgate to Andheri, 16 stations through the centre (Dadar).

## Curve: B-28 bands (`mum-build.log`)

6.98M travelling other workers in the four districts. Band shares:

| | 0-1 | 2-5 | 6-10 | 11-20 | 21-30 | 31-50 | 51+ km | KL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B-28 | 0.217 | 0.246 | 0.196 | 0.130 | 0.082 | 0.086 | 0.043 | |
| **d^-1.65 exp(-d/32.7 km)** (fitted) | 0.189 | 0.308 | 0.150 | 0.147 | 0.083 | 0.075 | 0.049 | 0.0170 |
| d^-1.4 exp(-d/17.3 km) (best length at tier 0's power) | 0.162 | 0.306 | 0.166 | 0.166 | 0.089 | 0.073 | 0.039 | 0.0233 |

The fit is flat between powers 1.6 and 1.75 (KL 0.0170-0.0176) and worse fast below 1.2 or above
2. The 2-5 km band is always over-filled and 0-1 under: 2 km zones put every neighbour pair at 2
or 2.8 km, so the two shortest bands are not separable at this grain. Model mean 10.0 km, median
4.5 km (straight-line). Thane district's 13.2% at 31-50 km (commutes into Mumbai) is the
distinctive feature; the model gives 7.5% overall against 8.6%.

never prints ("wrote"); searching the A-2 workbook in three PDF readers before finding one that

Proposals:
1. **A country module prints GHSL against each unit's census total before the first build**
   (`india.py mum` could have done it from the GHSL tiles): broken unit lines show at once.
2. **Band fits should take the pack's own road factor**: `routes.py` runs last, so either run a
   drive-factor sample before the build or refit after routes (T-314).
3. **India**: every district has B-28 and the PCA on the nada catalog; walking catalog ids
   around a known one (6700-6719 for Maharashtra's PCA) finds a state's files in one call.
4. **Wait strings**: `routes.py` ends with "routes written"; the playbook could name each
   script's last line so background waits match it.
5. **Brief**: right and complete; the SHRUG note saved a licence question.
