# T-277: Toronto, the first city outside the US

2026-10-13. Anita: "maybe we can try adding some other countries. we can maybe start with
toronto." An exception to the pause on city additions, to test that the design carries to another
country. It followed docs/adding-a-city.md end to end for the first time and rewrote it.

## Extent

The Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area, by whole census divisions (Canada's county): Toronto
3520, Peel 3521, York 3519, Durham 3518, Halton 3524, Hamilton 3525. 28 census subdivisions
(municipalities), 7,281,694 people (2021). Cheaper than the CMA, whose edge cuts census divisions
(it takes parts of Simcoe and Dufferin and leaves out Burlington and Oshawa); whole divisions are
also docs/zones.md's unit for Canada. The pack stops 40 km short of the Niagara River, so one
region (ON), border factor 1, and no national border factor needed yet.

## Sources (pipeline/canada.py; the US input each replaces in brackets)

- Population (census blocks): Statistics Canada 98-10-0015-01, population by dissemination area
  (DA, 400-700 people), https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/tbl/csv/98100015-eng.zip. Polygons: Esri
  Canada's copy of the DA boundary file (StatCan's own boundary downloads, www12, answer a
  Cloudflare check), https://services.arcgis.com/wjcPoefzjpzCgffS/arcgis/rest/services/DisseminationAreas/FeatureServer/0.
  Census tracts: a public ArcGIS Online copy of lct_000b21a_e,
  https://services1.arcgis.com/ZozYr1WfCwexXgcn/arcgis/rest/services/lct_000b21a_e/FeatureServer/0.
  Municipalities (cartographic, clipped at the shore), lakes and rivers, DA ages: Esri Canada's
  CensusSubdivision, Water_Bodies_in_Canada_2 and Canadian_Age_and_Gender_2021 services.
- Jobs (LODES WAC): 98-10-0498-01, usual place of work by census tract of work and employment
  income band. The census was taken in May 2021, when 35.6% of the Toronto CMA's workers worked
  at home and count at home. Those jobs are put back at workplaces by income band: each band's
  CMA ratio of at-home to usual-place workers, applied to each work tract's workers in that band
  (at-home workers earned more, so office tracts get more back). "No fixed workplace" workers
  (98-10-0480-01, 337k in the Toronto CMA) are added in proportion. Within a tract, jobs go to its
  DAs 80% by OSM job places (shops, amenities, offices, crafts, and commercial or industrial land
  at one place per 5,000 m²) and 20% by population. The 66 DAs outside every tract (34k people,
  rural Durham) take their municipality's workplace count from 98-10-0459-01 less its tracts'.
- Home end (LODES RAC x ACS): commuters by tract of residence (98-10-0480-01) plus the at-home
  workers who commute again, to DAs by population.
- Work from home (ACS 2019-2023): the Labour Force Survey's May 2024 figure, 24.7% of the
  Toronto CMA's employed mostly at home
  (https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/240826/dq240826a-eng.htm); so 30.6% of the
  CMA's 2021 at-home workers commute again, at both ends. The same 24.7% is used for the Hamilton
  and Oshawa CMAs (28.3% and 27.3% at home in 2021).
- Everyday destinations (LODES sectors): OSM shops and services (errands, 39,674 places) and
  restaurants, bars, venues and hotels (leisure, 23,583), counted per DA, read from the Geofabrik
  Ontario extract (1.03 GB). Scaled to 15% and 8% of jobs; only relative draw matters.
- Gravity curve (fitted on LODES OD for the US): fitted here on the census commuting flows between
  the 28 municipalities, 98-10-0459-01 (below).
- Institutions (PL 94-171 P5): none taken off; Canada publishes collective dwellings only for
  larger areas.
- Water (TIGER): StatCan lakes and rivers, plus everything outside Ontario's cartographic
  municipalities and the US states (Census cb_2020_us_state_500k).
- Schools (NCES): Ontario's school information file 2023-24 (every publicly funded school, public
  and Catholic, English and French, with enrolment and point),
  https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/school-information-and-student-demographics. 2,099 schools,
  1,042,085 pupils, all matched to cells. Children from DA ages.
- Universities and colleges (IPEDS): Council of Ontario Universities Table 3, fall 2022-23
  headcounts (https://ontariosuniversities.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Table-3-Fall-Term-Headcounts-by-Institution-and-Level-of-Study.pdf),
  and Ontario's college enrolment file, fall 2024-25 full-time headcount by campus
  (https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/college-enrolment), at campus points in
  pipeline/ca_campuses.csv. 36 campuses, 396,238 students. University of Toronto split over its
  three campuses by its own 2023-24 figures. Private partner campuses of colleges left out
  (about 16,000 students; addresses not checked).
- Airports (FAA, DB1B): StatCan 23-10-0253-01 enplaned passengers 2024: Pearson 22.8M, Billy
  Bishop 1.0M, Hamilton 0.15M. No national connecting share: Pearson 0.75 by judgement, the
  others 1. Pack share from Ontario municipalities' and US counties' populations. 48,127 outings a
  day (Pearson 45,134).
- Medical and parks (OSM): the existing scripts, their OSM read from the Geofabrik extract
  (`canada.osm_caches`), because the public Overpass servers answered 504 all afternoon: 3,393
  medical places, 5,654 parks.
- Place list: places.py, 1,012 places.

## The commute fit

`notes/t277_fit.py`: the game's gravity on the pack's 2 km zones, scored on the 2021 census flows
between the 28 municipalities (1.77M commuters with both ends inside; 59.2% work in their own
municipality). Grid over pow 0.4-1.5 and km 10-120 (`../artifacts/trainworld/T-277-fit.log`):

| curve | commutes misplaced between municipality pairs | own municipality (census 59.2%) | mean trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| US, d^-0.8 exp(-d/30) | 15.1% | 46.4% | 16.6 km |
| **Toronto, d^-1.2 exp(-d/20)** | **5.6%** | 60.4% | 11.0 km |
| d^-1 exp(-d/15) | 5.9% | 59.6% | 11.0 km |
| d^-1.5 exp(-d/45) | 5.9% | 60.8% | 11.6 km |

The good fits lie on one ridge with a mean trip of about 11 km. Peel to Toronto: census 15.8% and
20.7% (Brampton, Mississauga), model 17.9% and 20.2%. The caveat: May 2021's commuters were the
ones still going to work in a lockdown, more local than a normal year's office commuters, so the
fitted curve is probably somewhat too steep. The pre-pandemic 2016 flows (98-400-X2016325) are on
StatCan's blocked server. Since T-277 the game takes each pack's shipped curve at opening
(`DemandApi::new`); before, a pack whose curve differed from the US default was re-solved with
the US curve.

## Pack

75,442 cells (nearly every one with people: DAs tile the land, so rural cells get thin
population), 2,299 zones, 3,406,496 jobs, 2,529,867 home-end and 2,587,229 work-end commuters,
water 26.7% of the extent. `.bin` 5.68 MB (after every purpose). Build: the cells about 10
minutes (OSM extract read 5 min), then `canada.py tor --finish` (gravity, purposes, checks)
about 22 minutes, 17 of them reading parks and medical places out of the 1 GB extract.

Checks: `read_pack.py tor` OK (gravity worst row 6.5e-8, column 8.4e-5; ten water test places
right); `look_pack.py`, `look_water.py` images `../artifacts/trainworld/T-277-tor-pack.png`,
`T-277-tor-water.png` (harbour, islands, Humber, Hamilton Harbour, Lake Simcoe all right).
Population equals the census divisions' published total exactly. Jobs: 3.41M against the census's
1.89M usual-place workers in the same municipalities (+80% by construction: at-home and no-fixed
workers added). City of Toronto 1.38M jobs; within 3 km of King and Bay 358k (266k commuters),
which is probably still under the pre-pandemic count (the City's employment survey had roughly
600k downtown in 2019, from memory, not checked) while about right for today's commuting.

`every_pack_everyday` (native): Toronto opens in 296 ms; errands 0.299 a resident, social 0.200,
school 0.128 (US 0.115-0.138), university 0.033 (US 0.021-0.078), airport 0.007, medical 0.040;
school walk share 20.6%.

New Haven rebuilt into a scratch folder with the T-277 code: all 21 rebuilt arrays and the
totals, gravity and regions blocks bit-identical to the shipped pack (US paths unchanged).

## In the game

Registered in `app/src/game/registry.json` as `tor` with `market: -1` (outside the regional market;
the city bubbles, the worker's longer-trip selection, `intercity.py` and `outside.py` skip it).
Added only after the pack, boundary and water mask existed. Node checks with ten packs:
`t066-worker-check.mjs` (now asserts `registry.cities.length`) passes, 118.6M local trip legs a
day, Toronto's everyday 10.3M; `t149-worker-check.mjs` passes (loads bit-identical with every pack
open, 571 MiB WASM memory, every pack at init 9.2 s). `npm run check` passes.

`notes/t277-browser.mjs` (own Vite on 6127, watching off, isolated Chrome profile): all 7 checks
pass, `../artifacts/trainworld/T-277-browser.json`.
- Jump to Toronto: the pack opens 1.0 s after the pan (download 43 ms, open 1,016 ms in the
  workers), Toronto becomes the active city, its boundary layer draws (`T-277-tor-homes.png`).
- Ground track across Toronto Harbour refused (`GroundOverWater`), a tunnel accepted.
- A Yonge subway, Union to Eglinton (10 stations, 7 km, 10 trains an hour at the peak): 201,148
  riders a day (work 71k, shopping 58k, social 33k, school and university 30k, medical 8k, airport
  0.6k); purposes add up to 1e-8. Toronto's rail share 1.3% of local trips (`T-277-tor-yonge.png`).
- Renderer precision (T-127): Toronto is 543 km from the origin; Float32 rounding at most 1.6 cm on
  the ground. No re-anchoring needed.
- No page errors.

`notes/t277-network.mjs`, a rough subway (Yonge, Union to Eglinton, 12 stations; Bloor-Danforth,
Jane to Main Street, 24 stations; 20 trains an hour; the starting $6B buys no more): Yonge 293k
riders a day, Bloor-Danforth 325k; 474k rail trips a day, 3.1% of Toronto's 15.5M local trip legs;
2.7% of commute legs. Against the real system before the pandemic (Line 2 about 500k a weekday,
Line 1 about 700k with its northern and University sections, about 1.2M linked subway trips; GTHA
census transit commute share about 20-25%, all of buses, streetcars and GO included) this is about
60-65% of the real lines' riders with no feeder buses, no GO, the University side and Finch
missing, and a quarter of workers at home. Plausible. `T-277-network.json`, `T-277-tor-network.png`.

Routed walking and driving (T-275's `routes.py tor`, 210 s): walk raster 26.6% barrier with
22,710 walkable bridges, drive factors median 1.38 (24.5% of near zone pairs over 1.5); the pack
grows to 6.15 MB (2.35 MB gzipped); `routes.py --check tor` current. The browser check rerun on it
passes the same (Yonge 199,983 riders a day; its airport riders fall from 567 to none with routed
driving, not looked into).
