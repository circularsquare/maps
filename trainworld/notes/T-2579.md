# T-2579: a cache hit's re-solve ends at the cold figures on the Tokyo save

Agent tokyocache, 2026-11-06, for maps-93. Tree `tokyocache` (master 53c1c698), Vite 6177, headless Chrome pinned to
the 3 cores the ledger granted, `demandWorkers=2`. Script `tc.mjs` (kept in `artifacts/trainworld/tokyocache/` with
its log `tc.log` and one JSON per run): a fresh profile with a New York game, the Tokyo save opened as a file
(`importGame`, the player's path), its final and cache write awaited, then reloads into the slot with the camera on
Shinjuku; per load the solves (zones, markets, coarse zones), the cache reads and their misses, the stored entries,
the packs and markets posted to the workers, and the final signatures (the view, lines and stations, as cachehit's

## The cause

The import's solve covered New York. Open a file reloads the page with the file pending; the page starts on the
last game's camera (New York), and its zone's pack downloads before the save's network says what its stations
reach (`fetchCity` "undecided", docs/demand-runtime.md "At startup"), so it opens in every worker. Under the load's
hold nothing closes (`sweep` waits for the hold), and `solveNeed` counts every zone open in every worker, so the
load's solve waited for New York and solved it: the view's trips a day held New York's 68,242,895 (979,241,645
against 910,998,750; rail trips, every Tokyo zone's figures and the market's the same). At the final the hold ended,
the sweep closed New York and trimmed it from the solve's zones, and the cache was written keyed by the 20 Tokyo
zones with figures holding New York's trips.

So a reload hit that entry (`b0588350`), the re-solve, with no New York open, ended at `955d35a5`, and wrote it. A
reload that started on another game's camera (cachehit's second run) opened that zone the same way, so its need
had a zone the entry did not ("zones"), and its cold solve counted the zone again (`8105bd29`, the import's).

| run (`tc.log`) | import final | reload 0: cache / final | reload 1 | reload 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base1 (master) | b0588350 | hit b0588350 / 955d35a5 | hit 955d35a5 / 955d35a5 | hit, same |
| base2 (master) | b0588350 | hit b0588350 / 955d35a5 | no cache: 955d35a5 | |

The cold figures of the Tokyo save are `955d35a5` (a reload into the slot with the cache off); only a load that
opened a stray zone differs.

## The fix (`workers/demandClient.ts`)

- `closeStrays`: a zone opened in every worker while the reach was unknown (`unknownReach`, set in `fetchCity`) that
  the network, once its reach is known, neither reaches nor views closes in a game load's hold, before the load's
  solve (`flush`). `?perfOff=strays` is the old path.
- The cache keys a result by the zones and markets the solve was sent with (`Solve.sent`), not the list `closeFull`
  trims afterwards: a solve whose figures hold a zone closed while it ran is keyed with that zone, so a load without
  it misses rather than showing figures it would not solve to.
