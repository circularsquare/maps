# T-3735: weekend departures placed from the weekend service (agent weekenddep, 2026-11-20)

From T-3584 (notes/T-3584.md "Not done") and T-3521's "Lies": weekend departures were laid out with the weekday
spare time (worked out at the weekday rate and headway, whatever the weekend ran), the weekday split and the
weekday phases folded into the weekend headway.

## What changed

- `Service::lattice` (service.rs): a line whose weekend rates differ from its weekday ones
  (`Network::runs_own_weekend`) lays out its weekend departures on its weekend headway (`Network::weekend_auto`,
  moved from TrackWorld), its weekend pacing (`Network::line_pace_rest`, moved from `TrackWorld::pace_rest`),
  and weekend split and phases of its own (`Network::line_phase_rest`, `line_split_rest`), the moved-hours pieces'
  weekend row too (`spare_paced`). Every other line goes the old way, to the bit.
- `TrackWorld::place_weekend` (world.rs): after each edit's weekday placement, the edit's lines (with its
  weekend-only lines, `t.weekend`) that run their own weekends are placed by `spacing::place` and
  `pin_twin_phases` with the weekend rates, headways, counts, pacing, phases and splits swapped into the weekday
  fields (as `settle_weekend` swaps them) and `rest_solved` off for the duration. Every other line keeps a copy of
  its weekday phase and split, put back after the placing too (the spacing may move a neighbour's split). Cleared
  when no line runs its own weekends. On load, placed once for every line where the save carried none.
- Save block `LWP1` (save.rs, level 32), after `LRA1`, written only with `LWE1`'s weekend rates: the slots mask,
  then the lines whose weekend phase, split or pacing differ from their weekday ones. Every other save is byte
  for byte as before.
- Rolled-back edits put the weekend phases back too (`phases`/`set_phases`); `seed_slots` carries the three new
  vectors; the boarding key hashes the weekend phases and splits of a line running its own weekends.

## The call (DECISIONS 2026-11-20)

The round trip stays the weekday service's: weekend trains run their slot's weekday timetable
(`lattice_deps_local` gives `lev % SLOTS`, `prof_of(run, lev, var)`), so taking `svc_rest`'s run lengths would put
the reverse departures out of step with the trains drawn. Measured with a scratch probe (`svc_rest` against `svc`,
every running line, both runs, every worked slot) on T-007-real-nyc.save: 0.0 s apart on all 109 lines at 70%
and at 200%. Drawing weekend trains on weekend timetables would mean weekend profiles through the renderer, the
boarding account and the timetable: not done.

Only lines with their own weekend rates are placed: placing all would turn `rest_on` on for lines with none of
their own, and timing (timing.rs) leaves a pair with mixed `rest_on` untimed, which moves weekday trains.
