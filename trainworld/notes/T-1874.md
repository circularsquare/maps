# T-1874: more, uneven sample homes for everyday trips (2026-10-30)

Anita on T-1866: "maybe do like at most 100 homes + at least 30 trips per home? and can randomly
vary their sizes so it feels a bit less rigid."

Built in `sim/src/demand`: `lod.rs` `Sampling::site` (fine: one per 30 trips, rounded down, 2 to
100, for school and university; coarse unchanged), `everyday.rs` `spread` (uniform 0.5-1.5 from
`cell_seed`: the fixed cell's H3 index, purpose, salt, and the sample's slot) and `norm` (per
purpose and live cell, drawn samples over their spread sum), applied in `ends`, which now returns
the sample's share of its cell's trips instead of the cell's sample count.
