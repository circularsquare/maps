# T-3231: two lines drawn over each other at a one-place end

Agent termdraw, 2026-11-17, tree `.tw-trees/termdraw` (branch tw/termdraw), Vite 6191. Not landed: the
orchestrator lands it after the v0.4.0 publish. Tree changes: `app/src/map/trainGuard.ts` (`holdForPlaces`
and a small `standNear`), `checks/t2018-trail.mjs` (allowance widened, below), `docs/ui-map.md` (the place
rule's paragraph), changelog c136.

## Cause

t1965's layout: 8 km of double track, a local and an express, both ending at the same two dead ends
without a terminal, so each end has one place (`kind` 3, T-2986) shared by both lines. A diag that logged
every train within 900 m of an end each frame (scratch `diag1965.mjs`) showed how the express ended up on
the local's platform, all in `holdForPlaces`:

1. **Held short of the crossover, then in one long frame later.** "Standing at its last stop" was decided
   from the timetable and the lag after the frame's decay. Held at 330 m with 23 s of timetable left, a
   frame over about 17.7 game s (960x) made it "standing", so neither held nor holding. Seen with a 16-18 s
   frame at 960x. (A second case looked like a 106 game s gap between two frames; not checked further.)
2. **Following another into the one place, right behind it.** The place counted as taken only once a
   train stood there or was leaving. The express, kept 30 m behind the local by the following rule, is
   past the hold point (230 m before the stop) before the local stands, so it was "already over the
   crossover" and went on; and the following rule leaves out a train standing at an end
   (`f.bays[..+2] >= 0`), so nothing kept it off the local. Not a fast-clock effect.
3. **Two waiting at one hold point.** The following rule runs before the place rule and keeps a train
   behind where the one ahead would be unheld; once the place rule pulls the one ahead back to its hold
   point, the one behind can end up on the same spot (seen as the one express's two trips stacked).

What holds trains at t2980's T: not the guard's place rule (its log stays empty on master). The capacity
timetables each arrival to stand at its own hold, 320 m out there (`capacity.rs` `turn_at`,
`stand - max(platform, train) - HOLD_MARGIN_M`), 10 m past the guard's hold point (330 m); the queue
behind is the following rule. The earlier attempt (old fix 2, `artifacts/trainworld/t3231/full-fix.diff`)
broke t2980 by holding trains at 330 m when their timetable stood them at 320 m: each then carried the
timetable's whole wait as lag, lags reached the 20-minute cap and capped trains were let onto the occupied
platform (77-79 pairs; my own version without the fix below: 5 pairs, all with a capped train).

## Fix (`holdForPlaces`)

- A train counts as standing at its end only if its timetable says so and it was drawn there last frame
  (or is first seen this frame). Hole 1.
- Trains due at a place are looked at nearest the end first (as drawn last frame). One past its hold
  point this frame is "going" in: a train due at the same place behind it is held at its hold point as if
  the place were taken, or, if already past its own hold point, kept its length and 30 m behind the back of
  the one ahead (standing or going). Hole 2. Trains still far out hold no one (unlike old fix 2).
- One waiting at its hold point keeps a train behind it due at the same place its length and 30 m back,
  without holding it otherwise. Hole 3.
- The hold point is the timetable's own stand for the place where that lies past the guard's, with the
  front still short of the platform (`standNear`), so a train waiting there per its timetable is not
  pulled back and charged its wait. (The gap between the two grows with train length, so the window is
  bounded by the platform, not a fixed distance.)

## What is left

Every remaining frame involves the express's timetable not being drawable (T-3271): since T-2419 the sim
times the express at a 907 s round trip against the local's 1,885 s on plain double track with nowhere to
pass. Held properly behind the local at the ends now, its lag climbs to its round trip and then the cap:

- **at the cap**: let through by design.
- **lag past the round trip**: the train's next trip on the same run starts before this one has
  finished, so one express is drawn twice. The guard carries lateness to a train's next trip only while
  that trip is guarded (started); a trip not started yet does not pass it on (the T-3202 side bug's
  family). This is also t2018's remaining halts.
- **one frame in some 1+1 runs** (the same game moment, 150 s after an hour boundary): the express's next
  trip starts at the west end's place with ~200 s of carried lag while the local is still leaving it. In
  a frame dump (scratch `diag1965b.mjs`) no express was drawn within 900 m of the west end in the frames
  before, so its arriving trip was most likely dropped from the new hour's trips (its timetable end had
  passed) and its carried lag stopped growing. Not proven.

Master's t1965 failures were the place rule; the fix's are the express being later than the guard can
draw. t1965-follow will pass reliably only with T-3271 (the sim's following loss) or T-3264 plan B's
fade valve. t2980 and t1965 do not want opposite things: t2980 is unchanged.
