# T-4431 A station's owed settle costs its lines' whole length (agent settle, 2026-11-28)

## For Anita

- Most of what a station on a running trunk costs afterwards is the trunk's own work, not loops left over the whole save:
  the passing plans over the stretch the lines share, the timetables of the lines timed together, and the lines' profiles.
  A new stop moves the slower line's times at every point after it, so every plan through it really changes; and placing
  the station splits an edge, which moves everything past it along the track by a few mm, so nothing past the station is
  the same to the bit and no exact memo can keep it. "Costs the stretch around it" is not reachable exactly.
- Changed here, exact: the weekend service now uses the weekdays' passing plans where they are the same (one plan for both,
  its worked-out outcomes kept by rates). Before, placing the weekend departures (which runs on the weekday service at
  weekend rates) worked out again every weekend outcome of the rebuilt plans that the weekend copy and its settle had just
  worked out in their own copy of each plan. From stage 4's breakdown that is most of the weekend placing's 12-16 ms
  natively; not measured here.
- The goal (under 40 ms natively) is not met by this. What is left, by size, and where it is filed: the weekend copy's
  loops over every line (T-4445), the trains noted around the settle (T-4446), the plans' sweeps (T-4590, moves figures
  slightly, so the shift goes to you first), the rebuilt lines' profiles (T-4591), and splitting the settle so a later click
  waits for part of it (T-4432).

## What a later click waits behind, from the code

`settle_owed` -> `services_after` for the owed station: `update_scoped` (weekdays) -> `mirror_weekend` (the same on the
weekend service) -> `settle_train_counts` (weekday rounds, then a weekend round) -> `place_departures` -> `place_weekend` ->
`retime`. Shares on the trunk click (Node, after T-4444, T-4449, T-4504): both capacity updates 21-26 ms, nearly all of it
`Plan::best` (66 calls a click, about 0.4 ms each); the lines' profiles 14-16 and rebuilds 4-5 over both services; retime
13-19; `place_weekend` 12-20; `before_edit` + `note_changes` 12; settle rounds 4.

What of it is the whole length, and whether a player could see the difference if it were cut:

1. **Capacity resources along the whole paths** (sections, platforms, junctions, crossings, following losses): T-4444 timed
   their rebuild at about 4 ms of both updates; the rest is solving them, which is the plans' outcomes. Far from the station
   they come out the same but for the few mm the split moves every offset past it, and the stop indices past it (one more
   stop). Keeping them would be a lie of a few mm per station, adding up until a reload, for about 4 ms. Not worth it.
2. **The plans' outcomes** (`Plan::best`): a plan is one faster run behind one slower run over the stretch they share; its
   sweep runs a cycle of trains through every point of it. On the trunk that stretch is the trunk, the new stop is inside
   it, and every point after it moves. Each kind of day works them out at its own rates (weekdays and weekends differ on
   lines with weekends of their own). What can come off without a lie came off in T-4504; the rest is fewer phases or a
   shorter warm-up (87% of a sweep's trains on Asia's long stretches), which moves figures: T-4590.
   Duplicate found: `place_weekend` places weekend departures on the weekday service, so it asked the weekday plans for
   weekend outcomes, which the weekend service's own copies of the same plans already had (worked out in
   `mirror_weekend` and the weekend settle). Fixed here (below).
3. **Retime**: the redone cluster is the three lines timed together; their trains meet at every passing station along the
   trunk, and the new stop moves when the slower trains reach every one after it. T-4449 took 48 to 13-19 ms; what is left
   there is the cluster's own work.
4. **Profiles and rebuilds** of the three lines, both services: the rebuild lays every hop of each run again
   (`profile::hops`), and each slot's profile plans its holds again, though past the two hops around the new stop every
   hop and hold is the same, moved a few mm. Reusable within rounding (a hop laid from its start and shifted, as T-4449 lays
   them now): T-4591.

## What changed (sim/src/track; none of shift6's files)

- `overtake.rs`: `Plan::absorb` takes over the outcomes an equal plan (`same_as`) worked out; `MEMO` 8 -> 16 outcomes a
  plan, room for a settle's last rates on both kinds of day. Memory: the two services now hold one plan where they held
  two, so the most outcomes kept is the same as before (16 a plan pair).
- `capacity.rs`: `Capacity::update` lists the plans of the resources it built again (`fresh`), and takes an equal plan
  from `donor` before its own dropped one (absorbing that one's outcomes).
- `world.rs` `mirror_weekend`: the weekend service's update gets the weekdays' `fresh` plans as its `donor`.
- Exact: an outcome depends only on the plan and the two lines' rates (T-4444), so which equal plan holds it cannot move a
  figure; the plans' contents, the resources and every rate are as before.
