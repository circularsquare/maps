# T-351: a new game opens the viewed zone bare

Code: `app/src/workers/demandClient.ts` (`knownReach`, `want`, `fetchCity`'s `undecided`
mode, `holdDone`, `prefetchViewed`, `NEW_GAME`); `app/src/perfFlags.ts` `?perfOff=bareStart`
gives the old behaviour for A/B runs. Spec: docs/demand.md "Bare zones" (the startup
and first-station bullets), docs/architecture.md 7, docs/zones.md 6, docs/ui-panels.md "Loading".

## What changed

- **Startup.** The viewed zone's pack is fetched at once as before, now `undecided`: once it has
  downloaded it opens bare (aux worker) if the network's reach is known by then and leaves the
  zone out, else in every worker as before. With `?new=1` the reach is known before the network
  arrives (no stations), so the viewed zone is fetched bare from the start. The solving workers
  open only the market and solve it (a solve covering no zone, about 30 ms).
- **The hold** (`holdDone`): the held network's solve has published `HOLD_ROUND` and the viewed
  zone is open, in every worker or bare. A bare zone's figures are final as they come, so a bare
  start's card and clock go when they arrive. A bare start's solve is not remembered as the next
  load's solve-time estimate (`LAST_SOLVE`); the loading bar expects 0.6 s for it.
- **The first station** (`prefetchViewed`): in a game with no station, picking the track, station
  or blueprint tool (outside a load's card) opens the bare viewed zone in every solving worker too,
  without a solve (`prefetched` keeps it out of `solveNeed`). The next solve covers it, since the
  workers solve every zone they hold. Without this, a first line's solve waited 2.4 s for the
  pack to be fetched again and opened in five workers.
- **Saves** are unchanged in practice: the real New York save's network arrives about 2.1 s after
  navigation and its viewed pack has downloaded by 0.65 s, so the pack goes to every worker as
  before. Waiting for the reach would make a save whose stations are elsewhere start bare, but
  cost every save that reaches its viewed zone about 1.4 s; not done.
