## What the panel shows today

Above the cards: **Save to a file** (downloads the playing game as a .save), **Load a file** (opens a
.save as a new game), **New game** (the new game sheet), **Save a copy** (opens a name row with Save
and Cancel; the copy becomes the game played). Under them: "These saves can be lost if the disk fills
up." while the browser refuses to keep its storage, and the folder copies of T-1214: **Save on this
computer** (pick a folder; every game is written there as a .save at each save), then "Saved to
X/trainworld at 14:05" with **Stop**, or **Save to X again** on each new visit, because Chrome wants
the folder allowed again ("save to trainworld" was this button).

Playing card: picture, name (double click renames), Playing, Sandbox, a cloud mark when signed in,
when saved, day and cities, lines, stations, cash; "Saved to your account 9:41 PM" or "Not on your
account yet" under the facts; "Saved 9:41 PM" after a save by hand; **Save**, **Rename**. Under it
the browser's two backups (5+ minutes apart) and, signed in, the account's two (30+ minutes apart,
with a cloud), each with **Restore as a copy**. Other cards: **Open**, **Rename**, **Delete** (asked:
Delete saved game / Keep it), "Open on another device", the crossed cloud (a button that adds a
browser-only game to the account). Account-only cards: **Open** (downloads), **Delete** (asked).
Callouts: autosave off here (another tab or device), a save error.

Start menu: the account icon, **New game**, one button per saved game and per account-only game.
Account panel (T-972, T-988): games and space on the account, the sign-in question (Add / Don't add),
the sign-out question (Keep games / Remove games).

So a signed-in player on Chrome can face 5 buttons above the cards, then per playing card 2 buttons
and up to 4 Restore as a copy, three kinds of copy outside the game (file, folder, account) and two
lists of backups.

## The new panel

- **Head**: "Saved games", then one account icon and Close. Signed out, a crossed cloud (hover "Kept
  in this browser only. Sign in to keep your games on your account."; a click opens Sign in); signed
  in, a cloud (hover "Signed in as anita"; a click opens the account panel). Where accounts are off
  (a localhost page) no icon, unless the browser refuses to keep storage, where the crossed cloud
  says "These saves can be lost if the disk fills up."
- **Top row, two buttons**: **New game** (accent) and **Open a file** (hover "Open a .save file as a
  new saved game").
- **Playing card**: picture, name with a pencil (hover "Rename"; double click still works),
  Playing, facts as now. Signed in, the account state is a small cloud after "Autosaved just now":
  cloud, hover "On your account, saved 3:31 PM"; crossed cloud in the warn colour, hover "Not on
  your account yet" plus the reason. Buttons, bottom right: three icons, then **Save**:
  - copy, hover "Make a copy": makes the copy at once, named "<game> copy", and it becomes the
    Playing card with its name box open and selected (Enter or leaving keeps it, Escape keeps the
    default name). No separate name row.
  - download, hover "Download as a file": what Save to a file did.
  - clock, hover "Earlier versions": opens the list under the card (lit while open, remembered for
    the visit). Each row "Today at 2:58 PM" (with "· Day 26" where the backup has a day) and **Open
    as a copy**, which does what Restore as a copy does.
- **Other cards**: download and bin (hover "Delete", asked first as now) icons, then **Open**.
  "Open on another device" and the crossed-cloud add button stay as they are.
- **Account-only cards**: bin and **Open**, as now, the cloud's hover "On your account. Open downloads
  it here."
- Callouts (autosave off, save errors) and the delete questions stay. Start menu and account panel
  are unchanged.

A first-time player who never signs in sees: the title, the crossed cloud, New game, Open a file, and
one card with Save and three icons (mock A).

## One place for backups

- **The game is on the account** (signed in, tied, the account answered): the list shows the account's
  two backups. They also keep the copy another device wrote over, which the browser never sees.
- **Otherwise** (signed out, a game kept only in this browser, or the account not reachable): the
  browser's two.
- **The browser keeps writing its own two** for every game, as now (they cost nothing and need no
  network). Signed in they are hidden, and they come back in the list when the account cannot be
  reached or after signing out. So the history is kept twice, and only one list is shown.

## Dropped or moved

- Save to a file: the download icon, on every card that is in this browser (an unopened game's
  bytes are read from storage, as the account upload already does).
- Load a file: renamed Open a file.
- Save a copy: the copy icon; the name row goes.
- Rename and Delete buttons: pencil and bin icons.
- Restore as a copy: Open as a copy, folded under Earlier versions.
- "Saved to your account ..." and "Not on your account yet" lines: the cloud after Autosaved, with
  the same text in its hover.
- "Saved 9:41 PM" beside Save after a save by hand: dropped; the facts line already says "Saved just
  now" then (it reads "Saved", not "Autosaved", after a save by hand).
- "These saves can be lost if the disk fills up.": the head icon's hover.
- **Folder copies (Save on this computer, Save to X again, Stop): dropped** (question 1). Signing in
  now covers a browser losing its storage, and Download covers a copy outside the browser; the
  folder needed allowing again on every visit, which is what read as confusing.

## Migration

Nothing in a save, a slot, a backup or the account changes; this is the panel only. Both kinds of
backup keep being written as now. If folder copies go: the stored folder handle is forgotten at the
first start of the new build and the clock worker stops writing there; the .save files already in
the folder stay where they are, and each opens with Open a file.

## Build size

About half a day, one Opus agent (UI, trainworld's rule): `SavesPanel.tsx` (the head icon, two top
buttons, icon rows, the versions toggle, copy then rename, the one-list rule, about 150 lines

docs/ui-panels.md "Saved games" rewritten; `checks/t988-cloud.mjs` steps that click "Save a copy",
"Load a file" or "Restore as a copy" renamed. No server change.
