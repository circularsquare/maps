# T-884 The loading bar, again

Anita (2026-10-21): "the loading bar when entering game still spends like >50% of its time in the last
90% of the bar filling. ive brought this up several times in the past and we never seem to actually fix
it". Read as the last 10%: the bar near full for most of the wait.

Method: headless Chrome, a temporary profile per run, the card's `.load-fill` width read every 100 ms,
entering through the start menu (a save imported into the profile's slot first, then the slot's card
clicked), also `?continue=1`; "record" = what the browser remembered of the last load; "cold" = HTTP
cache cleared (8800 sends no-store, so every 8800 load is cold anyway). Dev server on 6175 unless
noted; the machine was shared with other agents. Shares are of the card's time, weighted by time (a
blocked main thread holds the fill it froze at). Scripts were in the scratchpad; the standing check
is `checks/t884-loadbar.mjs`.

## What the measurements say

On the real New York save the bar was already even: above 90% for 9-11% of the wait on every path
(menu or continue, record or not, warm or cold, dev or the 8800 build), which is what a bar moving at a
steady pace gives. So the "last 10%" was not what the earlier fixes' save showed. It shows on bigger,
many-zone games, which no check loaded:

- **Japan (t737-japan.save, 10,320 stops):** the bar reached 83% two seconds in, then the page froze
  for 99 s with the bar at 83%, then sat between 79% and 87% for the last 50 s. Fill 0/0/0/65/76 at
  10/30/50/70/90% of a 162 s wait in time terms (the first samples show 83% because the freeze held
  the frame): in her words, most of the time in the last part of the bar. Above 90% was only 1%, so
  T-793's measure would have passed it.
- **Her world save (world-map-day73, 143 lines, Toronto to Tokyo):** the bar reached 27%, fell back to
  0, the page froze 10-15 s, the opening step stood at 46-48% for about 20 s; 94-116 s in all.

Why:

1. **Before the game's network reached the demand pool, the bar ran a load of its own** for the zone
   under the camera (New York's pack, the market, a solve guessed from a record): that small load
   filled fast, to 27-83%.
2. **The main thread then froze** while the game was taken in: `regionMoney.ts` places every station
   (`lineShares`, T-531) and every built track edge's midpoint (`upkeepShares`, T-867, new today) in a
   zone, `placeAt` walking the zones' outlines, uncached for edges, and again on every money update
   and every batch of zone boundaries arriving. With Japan's prefecture coastlines that was 68 s of
   `distanceTo` and 30 s of `cityNear`/`zonesTouching` in one load. A frozen page cannot move a bar.
3. **Then the bar jumped:** back to 0 (T-840's restart for a new game, which also fired for the
   pick's own game), or, in Japan, up to 79%, because the frozen time counted as download time.
4. **Nothing remembered fit:** a game whose view is solved ahead (T-402) never stored its solve time
   (its release record waited for a solve that a later re-solve replaced), and an empty game's solve
   (0 stops, the market alone) was stored and stood in for every zone without a record.

Why earlier fixes missed it: T-212, T-347, T-793 measured and tuned on the real New York save (one
cluster, no freeze); T-793's target was the share above 90%, which a bar frozen at 83% passes; and the
freeze is in money code, not loading code.

## What changed

- `workers/demandClient.ts`: the bar is the time since the pick against that time plus the time still
  expected (`jobProgress`), not a weighted share of steps. Still expected: downloads at their rate,
  opening at its rate once a quarter is in, the solve from `solveStage`'s new `rem` (a late round goes
  on a further 15% of its time, the rounds after at its pace). A game's wait is remembered by slot
  (`anitabuilder-load-wait`, `rememberWait`), trusted until the solve shows its pace and bounded to
  0.7-1.5 times the model after. The pick and its game are one wait (`gameWait` in game/loading.ts):
  the pre-network load stays in the bar and is not restarted. A 0-stop solve is not remembered, and a
  solve released at its view's final is remembered at once.
- `game/zones.ts`: `placeAt` remembers its answers (for good once every nearby boundary is in, else
  until more arrive), and boundary arrivals re-place everything at most every 250 ms.
- `game/loading.ts`, `main.ts`, `workers/clockClient.ts`: the wait's start and slot key.
