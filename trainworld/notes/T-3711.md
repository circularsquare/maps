## What it is

- `LineData::ones: Vec<(station, side)>` (`Network::line_ones`): the stops the player made one way, each with the
  port side trains arrive by where they stop (2 while not yet fixed: set on a line with no route). Saved in a new
  trailing block `LOS1` (per line: index, then per stop its place in the line's stops and the side), save LEVEL
  21 -> 22. Filtered to the line's stops in `set_line`; a bus or ferry line has none.
- The sim treats such a stop between the ends of an open line as a leg (`Network::legs(stops, ones)`, with
  `one_stop_side` / `can_one_stop`): routing (`route_legs`, `leg_run`), stopping (`match_run` stops only where the run
  arrives by the side), the way back (`back_run_of`), half dots (`line_stop_ways`). Every function that took a line's
  stops without the line now takes `ones` too (compiler-checked: route, route_hops, match_run, way_stops,
  fwd_stops_of, back_run_of, back_path_of, route_memo, best_slot, straighten, stop_spread, legs.rs). Loops, twins,
  link edits' plain routes and tests pass `&[]` or the line's own.
- For ordering it stays a skeleton stop (its place from clicks, as any stop); `legs::place` matches the skeleton
  with `match_run` so a run passing it is fine, and `take_legs` gets each run's skeleton without the one-way stops the
  other run serves (else the way back had to reach the north platform and the south one went to the end).
- **The way** (`settle_ones`, lines.rs): of its two sides, the one whose line turns back fewer times over both runs;
  on a tie the side it had, or for one just set, the way the line goes there in its stop list (run 0's arrival; at
  the first stop, the way it leaves). Weighed again on every stop edit (add, set, shift, drop, end, stop at a passed
  station, order by hand, sort); a side kept leaves the edit's own path (a drop keeps its path). A drop or end that
  leaves such a stop on the line routes again (`dropped`), since the forward path may only pass it.
  - Why not just "the way the line goes there" (the brief's wording): tested, it fails two of the four click orders.
    Given [P, N, S, Q], the zigzag line stores [S, P, N, Q] (S at an end), whose run 0 passes N eastward, the same way
    as S: forced so, the line still zigzags. Clicked N first on [P, Q], its way is eastward; then adding S (also
    eastward, right-hand) zigzags. Reversals decide both right; the tie keeps "the way the line goes".
- `TrackApi::one_way_stop(id, station)` (edit op `oneWayStop`): a station not on the line goes in where `add_stop`
  puts it and is made one way; a stop not one way is made one way; one made so is served both ways again. A one-way
  station or one-track platform is one way already: plain add, or nothing. `TrackApi::line_one_stops` feeds
  `LineStats.oneStops` (for the hover rows).
- App: `tools.ts` passes Shift on a station or marker click (`toggleStop(station, oneWay)`); `stopMarkers.ts` hover
  rows; a shift-click on a stop keeps its row and marker (no add/remove guess).

## Decision (for DECISIONS.md, main tree; this agent may not edit outside its tree)

- 2026-11-18 (agent oneway, T-3711; Anita: "force a one way stop in the way that makes sense"): a shift-clicked
  one-way stop is served the way whose line turns back fewer times over both runs; on a tie the way it had, or for a
  new one the way the line goes there in its stop list; every stop edit weighs it again, so a later stop can flip it.
  "The way the line goes there" alone failed two of four click orders on the Caracas layout. notes/T-3711.md.
