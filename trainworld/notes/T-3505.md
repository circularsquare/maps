# T-3505: demand bubbles with 0 trips

Agent bubfold, 2026-11-17. Anita: a bubble in rural Ontario hovered "0 trips from here".

## Where the 0-trip bubbles come from

The pack cells themselves are tiny, not T-3503's new counting. In thin country a census block is
spread over the cells it covers by land area, so a farm cell holds 0.05 people and 0.02 jobs.
`merge_cells.py`'s floor (T-850b: a cell under 50 trip ends a weekday joins the nearest cell of
its zone part at or over 50) works inside a 2 km gravity zone, and these cells are each the only
cell of their 2 km zone (Sol Plaatjie: 9,966 cells under 1 person and 1 job, every one alone in
its zone part; the pack has 15,731 zones for 18,629 cells). They cannot cross the zone line,
since each zone's gravity factors (`zone_row`, `zone_col`, `zone_home`, `zone_work`) come from the
joint solve over every pack.

Counted from the packs (pop + jobs under 1; scratchpad `bubfold/coarse.py`; the two rows are
different regions, see stage 2):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| without T-852's 4 km blocks (`zone_size`) | 3,205 | 16.8M | 758k (4.5%) | 746k of 5.8M (12.8%) |
| with them | 934 | 6.4M | 45k (0.7%) | 41k of 1.37M (3.0%) |

On the real New York save (with its lines, so with T-3503's regional legs) they are few under
All purposes: 225 of 54,239 home bubbles at res 9 and 492 of 54,237 destination bubbles (959
with `perfOff=regional`: the regional legs lift some small cells over 1). Under Medical, T-3503
does add them: 2,179 of 6,298 destination bubbles under 1 (13 without the regional legs), its
outings across zone lines spread thinly over cells by attraction. So both causes exist; the pack
cells are the big one, and the fold below covers both.

So they are many bubbles holding almost no trips, except under a narrow purpose in thin
country, where dropping them would lose a few percent: hence a fold, not a cut.

## Stage 1 (built): no bubble under 1 trip a day

`foldSmall` in `map/bubbles.ts`, applied per level in the bubble worker's `build`: a group under 1
leg goes into the nearest group of at least 1 (a grid search by centre), which keeps its place;
with none, into the busiest, dropped only if the whole set is still under 1. Display only, like
T-909's far-end grouping; demand and every figure are untouched.

- Every level adds up to the zone's legs (the T-3503 sum rule holds to the leg).
- The level carries the fold (`BubbleLevel.fold`, pairs of group indices). Clicking or box-selecting
  a bubble selects the folded groups' cells too (`selectGroups`), so the selection's count, its far
  end and its inspector agree with the hover.
- The selection's own bubbles (`drawPick`) fold among themselves the same way, so zooming in on a
  selection brings back no specks.
- The smoothed colour and the Purpose colouring take the same fold by group index (the purpose
  build is made unfolded and folded on the main thread with the bubbles' fold).
- Far ends are untouched: T-909 already deals them into bubbles of at least 10.

Measured after (rural Barrie, Grey and Bruce, zoom 13.5; scratchpad `bubfold/verify.mjs`):

- Homes, Destinations and Homes under Medical: no bubble under 1 at any level (smallest 1.0003),
  every level summing to the zone's legs within 0.03 of 2.72M (float rounding); res 9 folds
  1,674 / 2,874 / 6,388 groups, res 6 folds 61 / 94 / 225; none dropped.
- A bubble that took one group: hover "25 trips per day from here", click selects 2 cells with
  24.69 legs, the inspector says 25 local trip legs and "One area", one outlined bubble.
- `foldSmall` against brute force on made-up sets (3,000 random groups, one host with far
  outliers, a line of points, none at 1, all tiny): every fold goes to the true nearest group of
  at least 1, totals kept; 3,000 groups in 9 ms.
- Sol Plaatjie (outside Kimberley, zoom 12.5): the same at every level for Homes, Destinations,
  Homes under Medical and Homes coloured by Purpose (smoothed colour kept on every level; each
  disc's purpose parts add up to its legs); a bubble that took 5 groups: hover 11 trips, click
  selects 6 cells with 10.65 legs, one outlined bubble. Whole build in the bubble worker 75-147 ms.
- How far trips move (centre of the folded group to the bubble it joined, Sol Plaatjie, res 9):
  all purposes Homes p50 2.7 km, p95 8.0 km, max 25 km; Destinations p50 3.5, p95 11, max 33 km;
  Medical Homes p50 8.7, p95 31, max 70 km (hospitals' patients are few per farm, so the next
  bubble of a whole trip is far). This is the lie: in the thinnest country a farm's fraction of a
  trip shows at the next hamlet, and selecting that hamlet takes the farms with it. If it ever
  looks wrong, a cap (fold only within, say, 10 km, drop the rest) costs exact totals.
- Screenshots: artifacts/trainworld/t3505_brr_hover.png, t3505_brr_selected.png,
  t3505_slj_selected.png.

A bug found on the way: the nearest search started from the query's own square even outside the
grid, so a selection's one host with a far small group searched about 10^9 rings and hung the
page; it now starts from the nearest square inside the grid.

## Stage 2: folding the sites themselves (design, not built)

Not at pack load: a tiny cell is its 2 km zone's only cell, so folding it into a neighbour moves
weight across gravity zones whose balancing factors were solved jointly offline; the zone's
commutes would change. It needs the pipeline.

The gain is small. `demand_mem --parts` (no market, rounds 0) per solving worker:

| Pack | Cells | Zones | Commutes + everyday heap | Zones holding only tiny cells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barrie (brr) | 17,754 | 8,949 | 13.9 + 7.7 = 21.6 MB | 1,396 (16%) |
| Sol Plaatjie (slj) | 18,629 | 15,731 | 15.5 + 8.2 = 23.7 MB | 9,793 (62%) |

The two packs differ by 6,782 zones and 875 cells for 2.1 MB, about 0.3 KB a zone (a rough figure
from two packs). Dropping the tiny-only zones would save about 0.4 MB a worker on Barrie, about
3 MB on Sol Plaatjie (the worst pack), and nothing visible on the real New York save (`nyc` has
146 such cells of 54,258 and 81 of 6,407 zones). The heap is mostly per-period graphs and routes
that do not scale with these zones.

What already exists: T-852's 4 km blocks (a 2x2 block of 2 km zones each under 1,000 trip ends
becomes one zone, decided 2026-10-21 for new packs and for existing countries "at their next
routes rebuild"), inside which the floor rule can join cells. Packs with blocks have 0.7% tiny
cells against 4.5% in the 3,205 without (different regions, so not like for like; a pack without
`zone_size` may also have run `--coarse` and found no block, since the array is written only when
one forms; thin packs like Barrie and Sol Plaatjie clearly never ran it). Simulated read-only on
the merged packs (scratchpad `bubfold/blocksim.py`: blocks by `zone_gx // 2`, `zone_gy // 2` and
region, then T-850b's floor per block part):

| Pack | Cells | Zones | Cells under 1 person + job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barrie | 17,754 -> 15,326 | 8,949 -> 3,855 | 2,244 (12.6%) -> 1,041 (6.8%) |
| Sol Plaatjie | 18,629 -> 11,172 | 15,731 -> 7,968 | 9,796 (52.6%) -> 3,444 (30.8%) |
| North Bay | 6,120 -> 4,079 | 4,671 -> 2,086 | 2,405 (39.3%) -> 1,004 (24.6%) |

So blocks about halve both, saving perhaps 1.5-2.5 MB a worker on these packs at 0.3 KB a zone.
Recommendation: no new pack rule; pass `--coarse 1000` on the next rebuild of thin regions, as
decided (T-3519, filed). A further rule (a 2 km zone under N trip ends joining a neighbour before
the gravity) would need the joint gravity re-solved and is not worth a few MB.
