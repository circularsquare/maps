# T-4114 Edits show at the click, stage 1: stop clicks, new line, delete (agent lineclick, 2026-11-23)

**Summary for Anita**
- Is it expected: yes, on a big game. Every one of the three waits grows with the save. On your Asia save (2,647 stations) a stop click's line path took 1.5-3.5 s to redraw, deleting a line 1.1-1.4 s and the New line button 0.9-1.1 s. On the New York save (1,756 stations) the same took 0.8-1.9 s, 0.25-0.4 s and 0.3-0.4 s.
- Where it went: the track model applying the click (0.1-0.5 s on Asia, 0.01-0.04 s on New York), a 0.5 s pause after the last click so quick clicks share one redraw, then the worker rebuilding the whole drawn network (about 1 s on Asia) and the page taking it (0.3-1 s on Asia).
- Now the line's path changes as soon as the click is applied, before the network is rebuilt. On Asia that is 0.02-0.18 s after the click instead of 1.5-3.5 s (about 95% off that wait), on New York 0.02-0.07 s instead of 0.8-1.9 s. Taking out a line's first or last stop is drawn in the same frame as the click. Adding past an end, or a new line's second stop, shows once the worker has applied the click: 0.1-0.2 s on Asia, 0.4 s while it was still building the previous click's network.
- Deleting a line takes it off the map at the click (from the panel's Delete line), instead of after 1.1-1.4 s on Asia.
- Stop clicks no longer wait for each other. Each is sent at once by station, and clicks that queue up behind a slow one are applied back to back, with one panel update for all of them.
- Creating a line: the New line button itself is unchanged (0.9-1.1 s on Asia). Its second stop is a stop click, so its path now shows in 0.1 s instead of 1.7-2.8 s.
- Once the worker's network arrives, the map is the same as before this change. A stop click by station gives the same stops and path as the old edits (checked).
- Advisor called twice. The first call, on the design, changed four things: answers owed through the follow-up's waiting list rather than a task yield in the edit, plain clicks still counted for undo and queued edits, the order (insertion paths first), and a refusal test. The second, a review, found two bugs, both fixed: a guess made from a network older than the world, and a refusal that dropped an earlier click's path. It also asked for the checks below.

## How it was measured

T-4097's probes (`editspeed\inst.js`, `helpers.js`, copied to scratch `lineclick\probe\`), with `r.lineAhead` stamped too. A game kept open with `checks/play.mjs` on 2 claimed cores, clock paused. Stop clicks by pointer in Edit stops on line 17 (Shinjuku, Asia) and line 2 ("3", New York). Bursts were three `tools.toggleStop` calls 350 ms apart, the same path as a click minus the marker pick. The new line used the Lines tab's New line button, then two station clicks. The delete used `edit({ op: "removeLine" })`, which is what the panel's button calls; the button also hides the line at the click, which the probe skips. Script `probe\p_cases.js`, summary `probe\sum.mjs`, runs under `probe\runs\` (asia-master*, nyc-master*, asia-after*, nyc-after). Master was measured on this tree before the change, and the burst baseline with the change stashed.

- "Network": click to the renderer's network with the edit set on the page. This is master's only moment the path changes.
- "Path set": click to the line's new strokes set on the page (`lineAhead`). The next frame draws them.
- Saves: Asia t3600-asianita-1852 (221 lines, 2,647 stations), the real New York save (109 lines, 1,756 stations).
- The machine was at the core cap (14 of 14) throughout. The first edit after a game load is left out (its network take was 4-7.6 s in both builds).
- The headless browser sometimes went 1-2 s without a frame while its main thread was idle: MapLibre had a frame queued that did not run. This hit master and the change alike, and was worst on the first edits after a load. So times are "set on the page", with the first draw where it came promptly.

## 2. What was built

- **The worker says what a line now draws** (`clock.worker.ts`, a `linePath` message). It goes right after a stop edit or a line delete is applied, before the rows state. The message holds the edges of the line's plan path and its return run, or null for a deleted line. It covers the line tool's clicks and the line panel's stop edits (set, drop, end, order, sort, turn, open loop, stop passed). It skips bus and ferry lines, which have no strokes.
- **The map draws it until the network catches up** (`map/lineAhead.ts`, `map/aheadShow.ts`, `renderer.ts`).
  - The line's own network strokes are not drawn (`u_hide_lines` in the stroke shader). A second stroke set is drawn instead: the line's own segments on the edges it keeps, with their bands, and the track's segments in its colour on the edges it gained, centred (the network places its band there). The stroke cache sees the change through `uploads`.
  - A deleted line has its colour's alpha set to 0, as a line the player hid, so its trains go too.
  - The worker's word is dropped by the next network set, which holds the edit, since messages arrive in order. A guess made at the click is kept through networks until the worker's word replaces it, or for 15 s at most.
  - A refused edit drops it (`dropAhead`): the line is back as it was, with the knock and notice as now.
- **Guesses at the click**, for what the page can tell:
  - A click that takes out the line's first or last stop drops the strokes of that end stretch at once (`edgesWithoutEnd`, from the strokes' stretch numbers). This needs no other click of the line out, no loop, and a line not partly built.
  - Delete line in the line panel hides the line at once.
  - Everything else waits for the worker's word, 0.1-0.2 s on Asia: a station added, a middle stop out where the worker may reroute.
  - The stop circle already changes at the click (T-3159). No extra pending style was added, since the path follows within about a tenth of a second.
- **Stop clicks by station** (`toggleStop`, `clockEdits.ts`). The worker takes the station out (every visit; a loop re-closes, T-3050) when it is a stop then, else puts it in as `insertStop`. The rules are the same as the page used.
  - Plain clicks are sent at once (`sendStopEdit` in `game/world.ts`), still counted in `stopsQueued` and the queue's promise. So undo, loop clicks, shift-clicks and the line panel's queued stop edits still wait for them.
  - A plain click made behind a queued edit keeps its place in the queue.
- **One rows state for a burst** (`rowsSoon`). A stop click's rows go out a task later, through the message queue, so clicks already queued are applied first; the newest click's rows replace the earlier ones. Their answers go with that rows state through the held follow-up's waiting list (`Waiting.worldSent`), as T-3803's rows did. The follow-up, network and its 0.5 s hold are unchanged.
- Strings: none added or changed.
