# T-3805 Trip routes and gap flash drift on big screens

Tree `.tw-trees/bigscreen`, agent bigscreen, 2026-11-19. Copies the T-3802 rings fix (notes/T-3802.md).

## Change
`map/tripView.ts` `draw` and `map/gapFlash.ts` `draw` scaled their 2D context by `map.getPixelRatio()`. Both now
scale by `canvas.width / max(1, overlay.cssW)`, read after `fitSparse` has sized the canvas (as `realStations.ts` does).
`GapFlash` keeps its `overlay` (`private overlay`). No in-game strings. The WebGL overlays are untouched (positions
come from clip space).

## Proof
Scratch probe (scratchpad `bigscreen/probe.mjs`): new NYC game, `map.setPixelRatio(6)` in a 1098 css px map (canvas
clamps to 4096, k = 3.73), a trip selected between two points and a `GapFlash` shown between the same points. Bounding
box of the drawn pixels / k against `map.project` of the ends (each ring reaches 9.1 px past its centre):
- before: got [692.7, 466.7, 1097.7, 822.7] (css px), want [374.2, 257.7, 723.8, 565.3]: off by 1.6x from the corner
- after: got [365.1, 248.5, 732.6, 574.2], want the same: exact to 9.1 px each side
Screenshots `artifacts/trainworld/t3805-before.png`, `t3805-after.png`. The dpr-swap case (no resize) shares the
formula and was not reproduced headless (as T-3802). Typecheck clean. No smoke.
