# T-761: the regional market on a whole-country cluster (exact, 2026-10-19)

Left by T-756 (notes/T-756.md "The market on the whole country"): on Japan's whole network (one
cluster, 10,320 stations) the market's part alone ran a worker out of WASM memory. Built option 1 of
that note, the exact one (orchestrator's call, 2026-10-19), and the same treatment for the other big
tables on the market's path.

## What the market held, Japan's whole network

Measured natively (`t761_market_bench` in `sim/src/demand/intercity/tests.rs`, on the network
`checks/t756-reach.mjs capture` recorded, made plain by `checks/t761-plain-net.mjs`): 9,114 served 6 km zones, 12.4 million zone pairs, 97,307
commute pairs, 8,502 destination stations.

| Table | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| route costs `r`, S^2 f32 | 426 MB a pass | the same (the logsums need every pair) |
| strategies, every destination | 1,083 MB a pass | 256 MB kept, the rest searched again |
| longer trips' OD, S^2 f32 | 426 MB a pass | the same (52 million of 106 million pairs carry trips) |
| commutes' OD, S^2 f32 | 426 MB a pass | 1.6 million pairs by destination, about 13 MB |
| far-zone table `st`, S x 2 x served zones f32 | 752 MB a period, and one more a pass | 59.7 million entries: 239 MB a period, a 120 MB pattern once |
| zone pairs, 40 B each | 497 MB, peaking near 1.2 GB while grown by doubling | 497 MB, made at its size |

## Built

- `kernel.rs`: `DestSearch` (one destination's strategy search, and its column of `r`; `skims_extend`
  runs on it), `DestStrategy` / `Strategy`, `DestAssign` (the assignment one destination at a time;
  `assign_inner` runs on it, so both are one code path), `strategy_bytes`, `skims_cached`.
- `intercity.rs`: `pass` streams (`pass_streamed`) when the market's strategies would pass 256 MB
  (`MARKET_STRATEGY_BYTES`) and no population on the worker holds skims of the graph: sweep 1
  searches every wanted destination keeping its column of `r` and the first 256 MB of strategies;
  `demand` (the logsums, both ODs, pairs, corridors, `st`); `r` dropped; `assigned` walks each
  destination's columns (longer trips plus commutes, and commutes alone) along its kept or freshly
  searched strategy. The cities' cached skims are cleared first (the market's part is last in its
  cluster's `solveOrder`). `pass_on` (one table) goes through the same `assigned`.
- Commutes' OD as `SparseOd` (stable sort of the additions, summed in their order: the dense table's
  sums to the bit). `st` as values over `StPattern` (CSR of the entries a station's zones can fill,
  u16 columns when they fit), looked up from a per-row cursor (`find_from`). `set_served` counts the
  pairs first (a bit per zone pair, their trips), then makes the table once.
- `rail_logsum` takes `r` and its side; the egress weights' logs once a pair (22.5 s against 25.2 s
  of the native `demand` on the whole country).
- Test `a_streamed_pass_is_the_pass_to_the_bit` (Tokyo and Gunma packs, two lines changing at Tokyo:
  longer trips, commutes and transfers): every load, figure, `st` and transfer of `pass_streamed`
  equal to `pass_on`'s to the bit, keeping nothing, three strategies, or all.

## Measurements

`checks/t756-reach.mjs solve <label> reach`, the real worker in Node; before = the same tree before
T-761 (`t761nyc0`, `t761jp70`), after = `t761nyc`, `t761jp7`, `t761jp`. Outputs in
`../artifacts/trainworld/t756/`.

| Network | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| New York real save, 3 workers | 37.5 s, 163-181 MB | 38.6 s, 165-176 MB; all 458 figures identical |
| Japan's seven cities, 3 workers | 311 s, 1,387-1,528 MB | 346 s (beside another run), 992-1,078 MB; all 2,622 figures identical |
| Japan whole country, 2 workers | out of memory | 1,267 s, 3,199-3,427 MB |

Whole country, the market's pass in WASM: 82-86 s a period (43 s in the night period). Natively
60 s: a sweep of 8,502 searches 17-18 s (the second sweep searches the 76% not kept again), the
logsums 22.5 s. So the streaming costs about a quarter more than one table would (which cannot fit),
not double. Threads are not available inside a worker (no shared WASM memory). Market alone in one
worker (`market.mjs`, periods 0, 1, 4): 3,078 MB with only the strategies streamed, 2,654 MB with the
sparse commutes and far-zone table.

In the browser (`DEMAND_STRICT=1 node trainworld/checks/t737-japan.mjs`, 2 solving workers, my own
Vite): PASS, demand's final at 1,150 s, workers 3,409 and 3,181 MB; 5.3 ms of main thread a frame,
0.3% of frames over 50 ms (`../artifacts/trainworld/t737/japan-t761b.json`; a first run ended the
same way, failing only on a basemap style's DNS lookup). The check now waits 35 minutes for the
final. Smoke passes.

On 2 workers each holds three periods: the market's part is most of the 21 minutes (4 passes x 3
periods x about 85 s); the cities' part about 5 minutes.
