# T-3245: what rides along with the next world rebuild (2026-11-11, agent rbqueue)

Survey, read-only: no pipeline run, nothing under data/ written. Speed is T-3244's (rbspeed); this list is what
should go into the rebuild, what is missing, and what to check after. Hours are agent hours, from the tasks' own
estimates where they have one.

"The rebuild" here means every shipped plan row (3,919 rows; plus 137 planned, 9 held, 1 parked, `world_run.py
status` 2026-11-11) built again from fresh Geofabrik extracts, then every country's join. Hand-built packs (nyc phl
bos wdc bal pvd nhv spf, tor, lon, par, sao, the Japan metros) are not plan rows and are not rebuilt by it.

## In short

- **Why a full rebuild at all**: T-3214 (campus students spread over the campus outline) is the one landed fix that
  needs the extracts read again. Everything else on this list works from caches or at the joins. If the full read is
  judged too costly, a light pass (education step + airports step + joins, no re-read) takes everything here except
  T-3214, T-2510 and the OSM attractions harvest; T-3226 puts that light pass at about 2 h on 8 cores for the
  education step.
- **Must go in** (a regret to rebuild without): T-3214/T-3216/T-3226 (landed), T-2896 items 1-4 (airport figures,
  about 3.5 h), T-3240's long-trip refit (longdist, 4-8 h, already approved), T-3218 (attendance 0.8, under 1 h),
  T-2112 (run seams.py before the joins, 0.5 h), T-2627 (the join's market step will hit its 1800 s limit, 0.5 h),
  the gate served from a frozen app/ or a no-WASM-landing rule (T-2298), and baselines taken before (1 h).
- **Her calls**: airport trips across zone lines (T-2896 item 5); GHSL epoch 2020 -> 2025 in the packs; whether
  the 137 planned metro rows in unbuilt countries build in the same run; an overnight run at up to 12 cores;
  T-765 (EU work at home); three ideas from her TODO (beach towns' long trips, school data where players are, Japan
  airports).
- **Hours before the rebuild could start**: must-haves about 7 h of agent time, gated by T-3240 (4-8 h, in progress),
  so about 4-8 h wall with two or three agents in parallel. The "should" items add about 20 h, parallel to about a day.
  Her calls add up to about 5 h if all yes.

## C. After the run (checks, and what a rebuild breaks)

Checks, in order:
1. `world_run.py status`: shipped and parked counts with causes (world-run.md rules); `stale.py` (no market pack
   names a freed place); `checks/build_inputs.py` (no FLAG); smoke 22 of 22.
2. `t2213_packs_load.py after.json --against before.json`: every pack loads, legs a day moved where expected.
   `t2213_islands.py` and `t2213_interior.py` (fresh OSM can move roads and bridges).
3. `split_zones.py` (T-3033: 37 -> 0 material), `state_gaps.py` (T-2726), `zone_origins.py`, `ghsl_blobs.py`,
   `admin_totals_check.py`.
4. Demand: T-636 ceilings (A at least 85%, B at least 50%), `t2088-ridership.mjs` seven cities and jpn7 (T-2088,
   T-2093: information only), the real save's rail trips against A12, `t1148-market-rule.mjs` (after A7),
   `t1177-cross-everyday.mjs` with `IDS=gua,fos` (T-1233).
5. Each fix's own done-when: T-3213's (UBC students over the campus, the bus exchange among the busiest cells,
   peninsula jobs 10,000+, over 100,000 a weekday), T-2896's airport table again (China nationwide from about 0.45),
   T-3240's pairs (national 100 km+ and 300 km+ per head within 20% of NHTS and MLIT, NY - Boston/Washington within
   30% of today, Warsaw - Krakow, Berlin - Munich, Beijing - Shanghai at least the published rail figure). T-3218
   kept or reverted on the campus figures.
6. `nation-people.mjs`: paste the new table into achievements.ts if the zones' people moved.

What a rebuild usually breaks or touches:
- **Saves**: no replan, so zone ids stay; if T-3033 or T-2726 takes an id out, `zone_successors.py` and smoke's
  t1053-zone-ids. Packs are not in saves, so no save level change.
- **Real saves**: their settled rates are service, not demand; check London's and New York's load time is as
  before (86-92 s, T-3233).
- **Leaderboards**: points come from the game and are sent while it is played, so a game's points move to the new
  demand only when its owner plays it again; old shares keep old points. Tell her.
- **Achievements**: rail-share thresholds per country (her KR/TW 10%, CN 2%) were set on today's demand.
- **Publish**: every release uploads all packs (about 1 GB) to a new R2 version anyway, so no extra cost;
  terrain-world is untouched. `--verify --page`, then old versions only through `r2_purge.py` (11-09, 09b, 09c due).
  A changelog line in updates.ts for the demand changes (her words or a draft for her). Credits if sources change
  (CAAC, OSM attractions in; nocar's out).
- **Docs**: the data register's extract dates and the cells A3, T-3215, T-2725, T-2106 change; DECISIONS for the
  airport rule (A4) and attendance (A8); demand-worldwide.md for A7 and A8; demand-plain.md for A7.
- **Disk**: 72 GB free on C:, packs 5.5 GB (the pre-T### backup doubles that); extracts are fetched and deleted a
  region at a time.
