# T-4243: speedometer on a train's panel

A player asked (tw-feedback 11-24); Anita's design 11-24: a round dial, the rim in the game's speed colours (red slow,
purple very fast), a needle turning around a centre circle, the speed in the middle in the speed's colour.

## What was built

- `app/src/ui/inspector/Speedometer.tsx`, shown in `TrainInspector.tsx` under Next stop, right-aligned, while
  the train is on its way (not on the "finished its trip" panel). Buses, trams and boats get it too: one ramp.
- 68 px wide, its rim 3 px thick (Anita 11-24, round two: 60% of the first 112 px dial, the rim 40% of its 8 px); 270
  degree rim with the gap at the bottom, the unit sitting in the gap. Rim: a CSS conic gradient of `SPEED_STOPS` (sRGB
  blend, as `speedColour` blends), so the rim under the needle is `speedColour(speed)`. Figure 14 px bold in a 32 px
  centre circle ("320" fits with 3 px each side), unit 12 px muted, needle 2 px. Figure colour
  `legibleOn(speedColour(v), dark)` (the yellow at 60 km/h is darkened a little on the light shade).
- Scale: linear, 0 to 320 km/h (her round two: "closer to linear"; the first version gave each colour stop an equal
  step). A metro at 80 sits at 25%, 110 at 34%. DECISIONS (Map and UI) 2026-11-24.
- Round two also removed the panel's "Along the line" row ("1.2 of 5.4 km"), with `format.distanceOf`, which nothing
  else used, and both strings in every language.
- Speed: `network.ts` `profileV` (the phase `profileS` uses, v + a t, none before leaving or after the last stop)
  and `profileTop` (hover). `profileS` and `profileV` share `phaseAt`; `profileS` unchanged.
- `format.ts` `speedParts` (figure and unit apart); `speed()` now builds on it, same output.

## Reading before running: what could go wrong

1. Frame cost. The panel re-renders once a game minute; the dial has its own requestAnimationFrame loop while the clock
   ticks (stops when paused, hidden or held; `clock.onChange` restarts it). Per frame: a check that the trip buffer is
   the same, one binary search in the profile's phases, and a DOM write only when the needle moves a quarter degree or
   the figure changes (none at cruise or standing). Not on the overlay's `afterDraw`: setting that switches off the
   overlay's still-camera throttle (overlay.ts `scheduleNext`), which would cost map frames.
2. The guard (T-1965) draws a held train later and later in its run: the timetable speed would show 50 km/h on a train
   standing behind another. The dial reads the guard's lag at each guard update (`guard.lastTime` changes) and takes
   the train's speed times (1 - the lag's rate): 0 while held, up to 1.3x catching up (`LAG_DECAY`, now exported).
   When the guard stops looking (trains not drawn) for 4 of its steps the rate drops to 0.
3. The trip buffer is replaced each hour and after edits: the trip's index is found again when `r.trips` or the epoch
   changes, never scanned per frame.
4. A train standing at the terminal for its next departure (drawn past its trip, T-206): 0.
5. Units and shade: the loop's effect re-runs on either; the panel's own re-renders (an edit while paused) redraw it.
6. Found on the way, fixed: `pick.ts` returned a clicked train's selection with the guard's lag added to its
   departure, while every reader (this panel, the follow button, the hover, hand-over, the selected train's highlight
   uniform) finds the trip by the timetable departure and adds the lag itself. A train clicked while held or catching
   up matched no trip: On board stuck on "Calculating trips", Follow stopping at once, no highlight. The selection now
   keeps the timetable departure. Not reproduced: at the moment probed no trip on the real NYC save had a lag (the
   guard runs only near the view), so its reach is unknown; no changelog item for it.

## Strings

- Dial hover: "Top speed 160 km/h" (`trainInspector.topSpeed`: "Top speed {speed}"), the fastest on its way to its
  last stop. New, for review.
- On the dial: the figure and "km/h" or "mph" (units as the game already writes them, reused).
- Removed: "Along the line" (`trainInspector.along`) and its value "{part} of {whole}" (`format.distanceOf`).
