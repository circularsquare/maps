## Where the time goes (a temporary stopwatch inside `networkFromState` and `lineBands`)

| step | worker `buffers` | in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| delete a built station, master | 650 to 2200 ms | `lineBands` 600 to 2100 ms: every one of the 109 lines worked out again |
| place a station on built track, master | 200 to 900 ms | `lineBands` 130 to 750: `besideSamples` 60 to 350, 8 to 15 lines worked out again, 50 to 380 |

  `nPhaseLines` still says 109 after the fix; it is cheap.

## T-2768 fix (done): `map/lineBands.ts`

`bands()` kept a memo per line (T-2238) but threw all of it away whenever any pair rule (`rel`, the network-wide
left/right order of two lines' tracks) differed from the last call. Deleting a station on line 0 removes or adds three
pair keys (0,3 0,4 0,25), which cleared every line. Now a line and a ribbon entry record the pair keys they read
(`rels`, `relDeps`, `Shape.rels`) and only those that read a changed, new or gone key are worked out again.
Lines redone on the delete: 109 to 14 (13 by edges they read, 1 by path).

## T-2767 (not fixed)
