# T-1465: buses, stage B2, routes along roads

Spec: [T-1445](T-1445.md) "Stage 2". Rules as built are in docs/ui-building.md ("Drawing a bus line")
and docs/track-and-service.md 6.7; this note holds the choices the spec left open and the numbers.

## Choices the spec left open

- **Road classes**: OpenMapTiles `transportation.class` trunk, primary, secondary, tertiary, minor,
  busway. Motorways, service ways, tracks, paths, rail and ferries are out (T-1445's list). No
  preference between classes: the shortest path by length, so "bigger roads a little cheaper" from
  T-1445 is not built.
- **A click within 100 m of a road lands on it**, even when its leg then stays straight (so a point
  never sits between two legs that disagree). Farther than that it stays where it was clicked.
- **Straight legs keep the stage 1 factor, 1.2.** Only a leg that follows roads counts at 1.0.
- **3 times** is measured between the two points after they have landed on their roads, the road path
  from one landing to the other.
- **Tiles**: fetched by `busRoads.ts` from the basemap's own tile server (the URL template from the
  style's source), zoom 14 whatever the map shows, only the `transportation` layer read, by a reader
  in `roadTiles.ts` (the map library's own is private; no new dependency). A leg waits for the tiles
  under its box plus a margin of a quarter of its length (600 m at least); while they load it is
  straight and the quote is asked again when they land. A leg over 100 tiles stays straight.
- **Tile edges**: each tile is cut to its own square, and two road ends on the shared edge within 3
  grid units (2 m) are one node.
- **Route in the sim**: points with two bits each (`K_CORNER`, `K_ROAD`); the app keeps the player's
  corners in the route so Edit route can reopen it. Save block `LBS2`; `LBS1` loads as all straight.
  Up to 8,000 points. The demand corridor of each bus stop is thinned to within 15 m.
- **Redrawing** opens with the stored corners and the stretches between them as stored; only legs
  the player adds are searched.
- **A 3.8 times detour is refused**: Twin Peaks Boulevard to Castro Street (1.3 km straight, 4.9 km by
  road) stays straight, as the spec says.

## Numbers (headless Chrome, `checks/t1465-bus-roads.mjs`, real mouse moves)

- Pointer move in the bus tool, main thread: median 0.4-0.6 ms, p95 under 1 ms, slowest 3.7 ms
  (Manhattan 3 clicks, 66 moves; San Francisco and Lisbon alike). The search itself: 0.5-1 ms a leg
  (Node: a 6 km Manhattan leg 1.2-3 ms, over 24 tiles and 11,500 nodes). A* from the first landing
  to the pointer, with the straight line as the guide, so only a thin band of roads is visited.
- Adding a tile to the graph: about 3 ms (76 ms for 24 tiles), done when the tile lands, not in a
  pointer move. A Manhattan zoom 14 tile is about 690 KB decoded from the wire, 30 ms to read the
  first time (10 ms after); the pointer ghost is straight until its tiles are in.
- Memory: the graph holds at most 220 tiles and is rebuilt from the kept decoded tiles (500) past it.
- Sim: `crossings` compares runs of 16 segments by their boxes first; a bus is placed from the
  plan's cumulative road metres (no per-frame work over the points).

## Not done

- A cue for a straight leg (all legs are drawn alike).
- Money by region (`regionMoney.ts`) still shares a route's road km by the chords between stops,
  which is rougher on a winding route.
- The 30-bus demand gate (notes/T-1464.md) was not run again: its feeders are straight, and the
  corridor points are thinned.
