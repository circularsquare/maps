# T-014 notes: head-office lumps in LODES jobs

**Done 2026-10-09.** Anita accepted the home-care rule.

LODES places a job at the establishment its employer reports to unemployment insurance, so an
employer that reports many sites (or work done in clients' homes) under one office puts all of
it on one block. `pipeline/lumps.py` lists candidates (`python pipeline/lumps.py nyc 40` scores
cells against the mean of rings 1-2; `LUMPS_BLOCKS=1 ... 70` lists the biggest blocks with their
sector mix). Since T-013 spreads a block over its polygon, the block list is the better tool.
The fixes are in `build_city.py` (`CITIES["nyc"]["job_adjustments"]`, `adjust_jobs`), applied to
the block table before spreading; totals unchanged. **341k jobs moved (3.3%).**

## Fixed

1. **Northwell Health at North Shore University Hospital, Manhasset** (block 360593018002016,
   41,521 jobs, 98% health care, 86% earning over $3,333 a month: a hospital-type workforce, not
   home care). NSUH has about 7,000 staff (Wikipedia; another source says over 10,000) and 806
   beds, against 12,810 jobs at LIJ's campus (about 1,000 beds with Cohen Children's and Zucker
   Hillside) and 14,664 at Montefiore's Moses campus. Northwell's big hospitals report on their own
   blocks (Lenox Hill 7.6k, Staten Island University 7.2k, Southside 3.2k, Huntington 2.1k,
   Plainview 2.0k, Mather 1.7k within 600 m), while several smaller ones show almost nothing
   (Glen Cove 13, Syosset 160, Valley Stream 170, Peconic Bay 110, Phelps 480, Northern
   Westchester 620), and the sites together come to roughly Northwell's ~85,000 employees of
   2023. So the block holds NSUH plus the rest of the system (ambulatory sites, physician
   practices, labs, offices, the small hospitals). **Rule: keep 10,000 on the block** (NSUH's
   ~7,000 and the Feinstein Institutes on the same campus, taking the higher staff figure), **move
   31,521 over the population of the six counties where Northwell ran hospitals in 2023** (Nassau,
   Suffolk, Queens, New York, Richmond, Westchester): outpatient and practice staff work where
   patients live. No public list of Northwell's ~900 sites with staff counts was found, so no
   finer split.
2. **JetBlue's head office, 27-01 Queens Plaza North, Long Island City** (block 360810033011005,
   11,872 jobs, 87% transport). The building holds about 1,300 staff (2021 reporting); an office
   cannot hold ~10,300 transport jobs, and an airline's NAICS code puts its office staff in
   transport too. **Rule: keep 1,300 transport jobs and every other sector on the block, move
   9,012 to JFK's terminal-area block (360810716001015)**, JetBlue's main crew base (Terminal 5).
3. **Home care and personal-assistance agencies** (109 blocks, 300,188 jobs). Among the 249 blocks
   with 1,000+ jobs and 80%+ in health care and social assistance, the share earning over $3,333 a
   month splits cleanly: hospitals at 60-100%, and a second group at 35% or below, with only 6
   blocks between 35% and 60%. The low group is agency offices whose aides work in clients'
   homes (no hospital is anywhere near their size at most of them; the largest, 19,287 jobs in
   Bushwick, is 400 m from Wyckoff Heights, a ~350-bed hospital). The employers could not be
   named from public sources in the time spent; the earnings profile is the evidence. **Rule:
   blocks with 1,000+ jobs, 80%+ health care, and at most 35% high earners: their health care jobs
   go over the population of the block's own county.** By county: Brooklyn 56 blocks 172,042 jobs
   (mostly Borough Park and around), Queens 24 / 68,833, Bronx 10 / 21,457, Manhattan 5 / 15,870,
   Rockland 4 / 8,896, Nassau 3 / 4,531, Suffolk 2 / 2,848, Hudson NJ 2 / 2,072, Bergen 1 / 1,486,
   Orange NY 1 / 1,159, Morris 1 / 994. Smaller agencies (under 1,000 jobs) stay where they are.

## Looked at and left (real, or not provable)

- Midtown's biggest blocks, Columbia (31,197, education; may include some medical-centre staff
  from 168th St), NYU and NYU Langone, Mount Sinai, Montefiore, NewYork-Presbyterian, Citigroup
  in Tribeca, Merck Rahway (7,105 manufacturing), Rutgers New Brunswick (12,660 education),
  Hackensack, Morristown Medical Center (14,729; Atlantic Health may book some system staff
  there, not checked), NYU Langone Long Island in Mineola, St Francis Roslyn, Vassar Brothers:
  real places of work.
- JFK, Newark and LaGuardia: real.
- **Unsure, listed:** Suffolk County government in Hauppauge (11,489 public admin) and Nassau's
  in Mineola (13,137): county employees (police precincts, county offices) are spread over the
  county, but no source splits them. The Staten Island west shore block (14,778 transport, the
  Matrix Global Logistics Park with Amazon's JFK8) may be partly real. An Edison block (6,745,
  76% professional) looks like an IT staffing firm whose consultants work at client sites. A
  Yonkers block of 3,925 education jobs and Elizabeth's 4,207 look like school districts reporting
  at their central office, a pattern that probably repeats across the region's districts (NYC's
  own Department of Education was not checked). A block near Penn Station with 34,958 jobs, 75%
  information, is in Midtown and left alone.
