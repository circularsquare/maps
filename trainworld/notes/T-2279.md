## The rule (on since T-2335, 2026-11-04: the dot's fill; its ring is the peak)

Each stretch (interval between two stops) has its load: the busiest hour's load over the level's
periods, the fuller way, as a share of the rated load (what the hover's strip shows). The dot's load is

    100% + ( mean over every stretch of max(0, load - 100%)^3 ) ^ (1/3)

a power mean of the part past 100%, power 3 (`CROWD_MEAN_POWER` in `app/src/game/today.ts`). Every
stretch counts once, whatever its length. When all stretches are equally full it is their load (today's
peak rule); when only a share `s` of them are past 100%, all at the same load, it is `100% + (load - 100%)
x s^(1/3)`. So one stretch at crush (150%) among 20 grades 118% (yellow), among 12 121%, among 5 129%
(orange); half the line at 140% grades 132% (orange); the whole line at 140% is red. The hover keeps the
peak ("Peak load 148%") and the strip; only the dot's fill (and the Lines list's orange and red trains
count) moves. The switch it was built behind (`CROWD_MEAN_ON`, `?crowdMean=`) went with T-2335.
