# T-2531 (and T-2508): did loading the zone in view's bubbles regress?

Agent bubbleload, 2026-11-05, for maps-17.

## Verdict

**No build made it slower.** On Anita's save, builds 827, 956 and master load the zone in view's bubbles the same
way and at about the same times; 956 and master are faster than 827 on a reload, since their reload finds the demand
cache (827's missed every time). What she sees is old slow paths that her save, now 117 lines and 81 zones in the
solve (a whole solve is 3-4 minutes on 3 cores), makes long enough to notice, plus one bug 956 has and master lost:

1. **Opening a save** (any load while another result is on show): the zone's figures come from the focus pool in
   30-60 s, its bubbles only with the main solve's round 0, 125-200 s. That is T-2508, a bug in `commuterView.ts`;
   meanwhile the bubbles drawn are the game before's. Fixed in the patch below: bubbles 1-11 s after the figures.
2. **A bubble clicked while a reload is solving** (T-2523), two causes:
   - on 956 (what 8800 serves) and 827, a click while the zone is shown from the focus pool **never** gets its far
     end (3 of 3 runs on 827, 2 of 2 on 956 on the real save, where a reload misses the cache): `ask` sends the `flows` question, its sums
     transferred, to both focus workers, and the second post throws ("An ArrayBuffer is detached"). Master has one
     focus worker since T-2495 (a19c9e08), so it got through by accident; still latent with `?focusWorkers=2`.
     Fixed in the patch.
   - after a cache hit the far end comes only with the re-solve's final, 156-202 s, with no note. The patch asks
     again while a solve runs (72-99 s); the rest is T-2533.
3. **After an edit**: the zone's figures update at 36-54 s (the edit's local result) but its bubbles only at the
   whole re-solve's final, 150-230 s, in every build. Not fixed here: T-2532.

## T-2508: why the bubbles wait on an import

The view shown through the load is the game before's final (version 1), with the focus pool's NYC figures put into
it at each of its rounds (`withZone`: new view objects, the same version). The bubbles on show were built for
version 1, and `commuterView.ts` `isCurrent` holds bubbles current for any view of the same version while
`demandUpdating` is set (`(s.dv === dv || updating)`), so no `cellModes` question goes out from the import until
the main pool's round 0 (a new version). It is neither of focusmem's guesses: the sums are pushed, and the focus
worker answers in 0.1-9 s once asked; nobody asks. A reload that misses the cache shows nothing first, so there the
bubbles come 2-11 s after the figures in every build.

Under it, an ordering bug: `publishFocus` set `demandByDay` before `fshown`, so the bubbles' effect, which runs
inside that assignment, still saw no focus figures for the zone and waited a round (the fix's first try: bubbles
at the focus pool's round 1, 156 s, figures 110 s).

## T-2523: why a click waits, or never ends

- **Never** (827, 956): with the zone shown from the focus pool, `flowsAt` asks `flowRail` there (answered at its
  next round), then sends the summing `flows` question to worker `[0]` with its sums transferred. `ask` maps any
  list of solving workers to all of the focus pool's (`pool.workers.slice(0, pool.n)`), two on 956; the second
  `postMessage` throws on the detached buffer, the promise never settles, and the far end never comes, not even
  after the final (one page error per round the click was asked again).
- **Minutes after a cache hit** (956, master): the cached result is shown as version 0, which no worker holds, and
  the same network is solved again (T-328), sent about 25 s after the cache shows. A click before that asks
  `flowRail` of version 0, every worker answers empty at once, and `refreshPick` leaves a null far end for the next
  demand result: with a cached result on show, the re-solve's final. Questions about the re-solve are answered at
  its round 0 already (T-327), so asking again while a solve runs brings the far end then.

## Edits (T-2532)

The edit's local result (T-1733) puts the zone's new figures into the last final's view at 36-54 s, but no worker
keeps legs for it: the one `cellModes` the fix sends then (its source changed to the edit's version) comes back null
(the local cluster's `subSums` have no copy), and the bubbles' retry (`again`) runs only while no bubbles are shown.
So the bubbles wait for the whole re-solve's final. Filed as T-2532 (a worker change: keep and push the local
result's bubble sums).

## Proposed fix (T-2508, and two parts of T-2523)

`artifacts/trainworld/bubbleload/t2508-t2523-fix.patch`, against master 7355b831 (`git apply --check` passes on the
main tree; typechecks; the same code as the runs, which were on c50034b8, rebased over T-2371's tidy). Three files,
no new player text:

- `workers/demandClient.ts`:
  - `legsKey(zone)`, beside `cellModes`: the solve `cellModes` reads the zone's legs from, `f<version>` (`F` added
    once settled) for a zone shown from the focus pool, `c<version>` for a cluster's final (`cityVersion`), else
    `v<view version>`; added to `setDemandQueries`.
  - `publishFocus`: `this.fshown.set(...)` before `demandByDay.value = days` (the days worked out first).
  - `publishCluster`: a final's `cityVersion` set before its figures go out (`settle()`).
  - `ask`: `which` becomes `only?: number[]`; with the focus pool, one solving worker named stays one
    (`[Math.min(only[0], pool.n - 1)]`).
- `game/demand.ts`: `legsKey` on `DemandQueries` and an exported `legsKey(city?)`.
- `map/commuterView.ts`:
  - `ZoneShown` gets `src` (the `legsKey` at the ask); `isCurrent(s, dv, end, purpose, updating, zone?)` reads
    `(s.dv === dv || (updating && s.src === legsKey(zone)))`; every place building a `ZoneShown` carries `src` (the
    focused zone's ask, the stand-in end, the entry kept on a focus change, nearby zones).
  - `refreshPick`: a null far end while `demandUpdating` is asked again after 1, 2, 4, 8, 8... s (`farTries`, reset
    for a new selection) instead of waiting for the next demand result.

Cost: one or two more `cellModes` questions and bubble builds per load (the focus pool's final, a cluster's final,
the pool retiring), after a cache hit one rebuild of the same bubbles at the zone's cluster final, and a running
solve asked for a far end every 8 s at most.

After landing: a DECISIONS line (the bubbles follow the source of the zone's figures during a load), and
`docs/demand-runtime.md` if it describes when the bubbles ask; the orchestrator runs smoke.
