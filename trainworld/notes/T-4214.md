# T-4214 Quick edit sequences go through the model in order (agent editseq, 2026-11-24)

**Summary for Anita**
- Your three sequences all have one cause: the game's worker applies every edit at once and in order, but the map you
  click on only learns about an edit when the whole network has been rebuilt (0.1-1.5 s on big saves, longer while
  trips are calculated). Until then the tools aim at the old map. What we draw at the click is only a picture: no tool
  can click on it.
- The station-then-delete bug: a station splits the track into two pieces, and the piece before the station keeps the
  old piece's number. The map still has the unsplit piece, so your delete names the old number, and the worker deletes
  the piece before the station, the opposite side of your click. A delete clicked right after a station can also reach
  the worker before the station does (the station looks up its name first), and then the whole track goes and the
  station is refused or put somewhere else.
- Not a new bug since v0.4.5: the same code was there. Drawing the station at the click makes you click on sooner, so
  you meet it more.
- The general fix, in five stages, about 20-28 hours: edits leave in the order you click; the worker works out what
  each click meant against the map as it is when the edit is applied; the map you click on is patched right after each
  edit instead of after the full rebuild; the track tool chains segments without waiting; and the at-click drawings
  become that same patched map. Nothing is built yet; the design waits for you.
- Gemini reviewed it (artifacts/trainworld/gemini/T-4214-answer.md); its main points are taken in below.

## How each sequence goes today (from the code; not reproduced, her 11-24)

The worker applies every edit to the track model when it reaches it, in arrival order (clock.worker.ts, `apply(msg.op)`
after any ground-data waits). Rows, the drawn network, trips and boarding are the follow-up, which trails: 0.1-1.5 s,
held while the pointer moves (T-2404, 220 ms of rest, at most 5 s), longer while trips calculate. So the model is
never behind. The page is: every tool turns a click into ids and spots from `world` (rows) and `renderer.net` (the
drawn network), which change only when a follow-up lands. The stand-ins drawn at the click (T-4145 to T-4208) are
pictures; no tool hits them.

1. **Track, then delete it quickly.** `deletePick` (map/toolDelete.ts) reads `renderer.net` and `world.edges`; the new
   segment is a stand-in (`addPlaced`), so nothing is under the pointer until its network lands. Same in v0.4.5.
2. **Track, then a station on it quickly.** The station tool snaps on the drawn network (`tk.snap`); over a stand-in it
   finds open ground. In v0.4.5 that click said "stations go on track only"; on master (T-3999) it starts the two-click
   station on open ground. Same wait, a more confusing outcome.
3. **Station, then delete the track past it (data bug).** Two ways:
   - **Old id.** A station mid-edge splits edge E: the half towards E's a end keeps id E, the half past the station gets a
     new id (sim net/paths.rs `split_plan`, world.rs `Op::Split`; checked with a Rust test, since removed). The page
     still has the unsplit E, so a delete past the station sends `removeEdge E`: the worker removes the half before the
     station, trimmed to keep half the platform (links.rs `station_trim_ops`), and keeps the half clicked. The page hides
     the whole drawn E meanwhile (stationAhead.ts `trackGoneShow`, then the word's `hideEdges` from the same id). A
     second station clicked past the first names E with a point past the split: it projects onto E's end, is refused
     as `SplitPoint`, which the station tool treats as a want of room and retries spots along the old E
     (`firstAccepted`, `roomSnap`): the station lands somewhere else on the first half.
   - **Send order is not click order.** `stationClick` awaits the name (`nameFor` -> `newStationName`: place lists, real
     station names) before it sends; `removeThing` sends a blueprint delete at once. The delete can reach the worker
     first: all of E goes, then the station names a dead edge and is refused or tried elsewhere.
   - **Regression check** against v0.4.5 (b0899150e): `edit()`, `ClockClient.edit`, the worker's queue (`goesAhead`,
     `standAside`, `passesHeld`), `deletePick` and `removeThing`'s send are the same in substance; the split rule is
     older. Not a code regression; the at-click station (T-4146) invites the quick delete.

Other ways the old view or the order goes wrong (found by the advisor and Gemini, checked):
- Track clicks while one is out are dropped, not queued (`segmentClick` returns while `placing`).
- Removing track past a station whose station is at the b end deletes E itself and keeps the stub under a new id
  (links.rs `station_trim_ops`, the `(None, Some(sb))` arm). Station removal joins two pieces under one id.
- Ids are reused at once: `alloc_edge` and `alloc_node` pop the free list (net/store.rs), so a stale id can name a
  new, unrelated piece instead of a gone one.
- Other awaits before a send: the track tool's real and street routes, `heldLanded` (up to 3 s), undo waiting for stop
  clicks (`afterStopEdits`), BRT stations waiting for roads.
- The delete question (`describeRemovable`, `stationTrim`, `lineEffects`) is built from the old rows too.

## The design (for her before building)

**Rule: each edit names what the player aimed at, as seen at a known version of the map, and the worker works out what
that means against the model when it applies the edit. The page's map is patched right after each edit, so the window
in which it is behind is the apply time (tens of ms on a small game, 0.06-0.47 s on the Asia save), not the network
rebuild.** No second track model on the page (memory, and the apply would block the page).

| stage | what | hours |
|---|---|---|
| A. Send order is click order | Every tool click posts its edit at once. A station is sent with the name the hover already worked out, or with none and named by a follow-up that is no undo step. The track tool's route lookups, `heldLanded` and undo's wait stop letting later clicks overtake. Every edit carries the game stamp, so a panic or load drops edits made before it. | 3-4 |
| B. Stale names resolved in the worker | The page stamps each edit with its base: the newest edit whose word it has taken into its map. The worker keeps, per applied edit, the id changes it made (split E into E and E2 with where, deleted, joined, trimmed to a new stub; undo and redo as their own entries) back to the oldest base still out. An edit naming edge E at point P from base b: E untouched since b, E; E split since, its live descendant under P, on E's level; E gone or its id reused, refused with its own notice and no room retries. Nodes the same. Ids not reused straight away (a FIFO free list) as a safety net. | 5-7 |
| C. The map patched right after each edit | With the word, the rows and drawn points of the edges and nodes the edit touched (`take_touched` per edit, T-4113; `drawnEdgeNow`). The page patches `world` and a pick layer (edge points, the snap grid) at once; the delete, station and track tools pick on it; the full network still trails for the look. This is what makes her sequences 1 and 2 instant and gives B its base. It is T-4097 section 4 parts A and B (stream D of T-4144) seen from the tools' side: build it as that. | 6-9 |
| D. Track runs chain without waiting | Drop the `placing` lock; the next segment starts from "the end of edit seq N", which the worker resolves; a refused segment refuses the rest of its run. With T-4210 (ghostspeed, map/tools.ts). | 3-4 |
| E. At-click drawings are the patched map | Stations and track drawn at the click from the stand-in's checked geometry, then from C's records; the per-kind guesses for track and stations (stationAhead.ts, pieceAhead.ts) go. Line paths and panel figures keep their words. | 4-6 |

Order: A and B first (they end the wrong deletions and misplaced stations), then C (the waits in sequences 1 and 2),
then D and E. Total 21-30 h; past tasks of this size (T-4145, 3-4 h guessed, landed in about that) suggest the guesses
are fair.

**Risks**
- Undo and redo restore old ids: B's record has them as entries in order, so walking it from a base stays right.
- Refusals mid-sequence: an edit naming something a refused edit was to make is refused, one notice for the chain,
  never sent to a neighbour.
- Saves: unchanged; bases, seq names and the id record are for the session only. A panic, load or new game clears them.
- Big edits (Construct all, a rebuild of many pieces) touch thousands of records: C sends only up to a cap and the page
  waits for the network past it.
- The delete question keeps using the old rows until C lands.
- Any place still building an op from the page's view without a point or base: B's rule lists every op that names
  track, nodes or stations; the dev build warns on an edit that names an id with no base.

## Waiting clicks (agent waitclick, 2026-11-24)

Option B of notes/T-4214-ship-options.md, built in tree waitclick. Her three sequences now go right on a new game.

**Rule.** A click on the map with a tool (a tap, the touch bar's confirm) made while an edit sent before it is not yet in
the network the tools pick on waits, and is carried out at the same map spot once every such edit's network is set. The
tool's tag shows the spinner after WAIT_MS (0.3 s): in its first row, or alone by the click when the tag is hidden (not
for the bus, ferry and building tools, which draw their own tags).

- **Edits out** (game/pending.ts `editsOut`): every edit sent (`pendingSent`) but stop clicks. Gone when refused, when
  its word says no network follows (`noNet`), or when a network that arrived after its word is set (main.ts
  `pendingNetIn`, after `setNetwork` and both grid carries; a microtask later, so a draw-anchor move in the same state
  is done first). A word-less ok answer counts as the word. Cleared on a load or a worker restart (`pendingClear`, which
  drops the waiting clicks too). An entry older than 30 s no longer counts (a warning).
- **Worker**: fares, fare reference, borrow and repay now send `noNet` (`plainWord(seq, noNet)`); a fare-area edit
  that tidies stations does not (a state follows), nor link and building edits.
- **Clicks being carried out** (`Tools.clicksOut`, `carrying`): a station click until `stationClick` settles (name,
  tries), a delete until `removeThing` settles (its question, or its answer), the touch confirm, and the T-2404 wait of
  a click near a held segment. The track tool's `placing` plays that part for segments.
- **Exemptions**: with a track run going (`start` set), the run's own segments and its Backspace undo (tagged by
  `asRun`, numbered per run) hold no click; its next click waits only while `placing` (it was dropped before). Stop
  clicks are never out, so line-tool bursts are as before; any other edit out holds line clicks too.
- **Order**: waiting clicks are a list, carried out one by one; each waits for the edits the one before sent. A double
  click's second click while its first waits marks it, and is carried out right after it (detail 2: ends the run, T-445).
  Each carried-out click is `move(e)` then `clickNow(e)` (or `tapNow`) at the spot projected again, with Shift, Ctrl,
  Meta and the click count.
- **The network wanted now**: the first waiting click posts `release` once (T-2404's hold for the pointer's rest),
  unless only its run's own segments are out. The T-648 trips hold (up to 250 ms, normally a few ms) is left alone: taking
  a state before its trips would draw the old trips on new run profiles for a frame.
- **Tool switches** the player makes while clicks wait (`pickTool`: toolbar and keys; New line, Edit stops, bus and
  ferry Draw and Edit route, the building outline, fare painting; `Tools.exit`: Done, Cancel, Enter, a right click) go in
  the same list behind them (game/ui.ts `inClickOrder`), so each click goes with the tool it was made with and a click
  made after the switch with the new tool. The toolbar shows the switch when its turn comes. An exit the game makes
  itself (the line being edited is gone) goes at once (`exitNow`); clicks of another tool are then dropped. (check10
  found a waiting station dropped by a switch to delete; fixed this way.)
- **Dropped**: when the spot has left the screen at its turn, on Escape or a load.
  After 5 s at the head of the list a click is carried out on the map as it is, and the edits out stop holding (a
  warning): the worst case is a click as it went before.
- **Undo and redo** wait for waiting clicks, clicks being carried out and `placing` (game/world.ts `afterClicks`, 10 s
  at most). Backspace in a run does nothing while a click waits; its undo is sent before `placing` is set.
- **Game stamp**: every edit carries the page's game stamp (ClockClient.edit), so one made before a load, a side switch
  or a panic's rebuild is refused without a notice.

**Not covered** (the later stages): a panel button or a node drag acting on something picked before a quick edit
changed it (its edit still counts as out, so the next map click waits for it); the bus, ferry and building tools' own
awaits before they send; the wait itself (stage C patches the clickable map so it is short); piece numbers reused at
once (stage B).
