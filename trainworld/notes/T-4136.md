# T-4136 The track ghost lags behind the cursor (agent ghostlag, 2026-11-23)

## What changed

- **The ghost is at the pointer every frame.** While the answer for the pointer's own spot is on its way, the page draws the
  last checked answer bent so its end is at the pointer (`Tools.bend`, `drawGhost` in map/tools.ts): every point turned and
  scaled about the start; from a start with a heading (on track, or on from the last segment) the change is eased in by
  distance from the start (smoothstep), so the ghost still leaves along the track and the part near the start stays as
  checked; from a free start the segment is straight and moves whole. Curve labels, the curve circle and the tag move with
  it and keep the checked answer's figures. When the answer for the pointer's spot lands it replaces the bent one.
- **Every answer for the run is shown**, not only the newest request's (the T-830 gate dropped any answer the pointer had
  moved on from; under steady movement from a station on the Asia save 90% of answers were dropped and the ghost froze).
  The click still takes only the answer for its own spot (T-830), and a placed segment's stand-in is the checked answer
  for the click, never a bent one.
- **The ghost no longer redraws the network.** The ghost, the placed stand-ins, their marks and one-way chevrons are drawn
  over the strokes' cached layer (`ghostPass` in map/rendererDraw.ts) and their GL uploads no longer count as a change
  for that cache (`uncached` in map/renderer.ts). Before, every ghost update redrew every track and line stroke (one
  cache redraw per frame on NYC, 118-127 of 118-127 draws while moving; 2-28 ms of main-thread time each on Asia).
- Unchanged: what can be built, what it costs, a refused spot (red once its answer lands), one preview in flight with the
  newest waiting (T-1039), previews ahead of an edit's follow-up (T-1407, T-2404).

## Step 1: where the lag was (master, before the change)

Method: play.mjs on my Vite (2 claimed cores, machine at the ledger's cap throughout), the track tool, a pointer driven by CDP
every 16 ms in a straight line 450 px over 2.5 s (never waiting for the page), from open ground ("free") and from a
station on a running line ("station": Asia node 71 Ryogoku area, NYC Times Sq node 13), snap switches off, clock paused.
Per run: the drawn ghost's distance from the pointer's spot in every animation frame (the nearest point of what
`setPreview` was last given), the share of pointer moves drawn at their spot before the next move, and per preview the
page-to-worker, worker queue, worker work and back legs (worker timestamps added for the measurement, removed after).
Scripts and raw results: scratchpad ghostlag/ (p2-*.js, r2-*.json).

(The 4x run throttles the page's thread only; Chrome does not slow workers, so the preview's own time is not a phone's.
The Asia "station" frames above are frames with a ghost drawn; with 90% of answers dropped, most frames showed an old one.)

Where the time went, as a share of a preview's wait (pointer move to its answer on the page), Asia from a station, idle:
the track model's preview work 21 of 31 ms (about 70%); the worker's queue behind other messages 0 ms (0%: one preview in
flight, previews already jump ahead of follow-ups); page to worker and back 0-6 ms at p50 (the rest: the page's own
handling and the wait for the in-flight preview, p90 up to 20 ms). Retime is not in it: a profile of the station case
(named WASM build) has 1,209 ms in `preview_route` over 77 previews, all in `route_op`'s sketch and unsketch
(`apply_and_derive` 921 ms: `Network::derive` 825, `check_line`/`check_route` 405, `back_run_of` 244, `find_path_side` 218,
`back_lines` 200, `fit_capped` 175) and `commit_with` 171 (`total_and_built_cost` 72); no `timing::retime`. A preview
from a station spikes to 100-1,064 ms on some routes (first measurements, north-east of Ryogoku).

So the causes, biggest first:
1. **Stale answers dropped** (T-830 gate): with previews slower than the pointer's 16 ms, nearly every answer was for a spot
   the pointer had left, so it was thrown away. From a station on Asia 90% were; the ghost froze 40-86 px (p50) and up
   to 309 px behind and caught up only when the pointer stopped.
2. **The preview's own cost** (track model: derive and line checks of the lines through the start; 5-25 ms, spikes to 1 s).
3. **The page's draw**: each ghost update redrew the whole cached network.
4. Not a cause: the worker's queue (0 ms), stale answers drawn after newer ones (answers come in order).

## After (this change), same runs

The ghost's end is at the pointer in every frame drawn: the trailing distance is 100% gone (Asia from a station, p50 40-86 px
to 0, p90 171-265 px to 0). A pointer move is drawn on the next frame, 5 ms after it at p50 (9-13 ms p90) on Asia and NYC
idle. The NYC maxima of 30-46 px are the checked shape for the pointer's own spot ending on the track it joins, not lag.

**Still slow: frames on Asia while trips calculate**, 3-18 frames/s against 54-56 with the pointer moving and no track
started, before and after alike (before: 6-29). Switching off in turn the ghost's GL work, the map repaint it asks for, and
its tag and labels did not bring frames back; with no start (no previews) they come back. So in my runs the cost is the
clock worker running previews back to back (one core while the pointer moves) beside the trip workers on 2 claimed cores
of a machine at its cap: CPU contention, which I could not separate from this machine's load. On NYC frames held 30-38/s.
If it shows on her machine, pacing the previews (at most one a frame, or every 50 ms) would halve the worker's duty;
filed nothing.
