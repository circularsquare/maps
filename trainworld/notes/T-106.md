# T-106: narrow top bar

Container queries on the main map column replace the viewport-width rule. Secondary walking/
driving labels, day and demand disappear as space tightens; the full values remain in hover
titles. Below 520 px the header has two rows; below 300 px it has three. Cash, time, train share,
the split bar and all four playback buttons remain visible. No overlap or horizontal scrolling.

TypeScript and production build pass. `t106-check.mjs` imports the real NYC network and checks
visible child rectangles for containment and overlap at viewport widths 1600, 1280, 1024, 880,
688, 600 and 500 (map widths 1248 down to 196 px). A dock enlarged to 850 px at a fixed 1280 px
viewport correctly produces a compact 430 px map header. No browser errors.
