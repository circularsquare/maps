# T-1178: continuous metros split by a zone line

## For Anita

- **Recommendation: keep the zones, and take the damping off across these lines only.** A short
  list of zone pairs, made by one rule from GHSL population, gets a commute factor of 1 (what
  Tokyo and Yokohama get inside one pack); every other line keeps 0.5. With T-1163's everyday fix
  (being built now) this gives the same trips as merging the two zones would.
- **Which lines.** The rule: at least 10 km of the line has 1,500+ people a km² on both sides
  (GHSL's density for an urban centre), and the built-up area it runs through holds at least 0.2M
  people on each side. 19 pairs pass, all in China and one in Taiwan: Guangzhou-Foshan (38 km of
  line through city), Shenzhen-Dongguan (32 km), Guangzhou-Dongguan, Foshan-Zhongshan,
  Zhuhai-Zhongshan, Suzhou-Wujiang, Hefei, and a dozen prefecture cities whose built-up core is cut
  between two countryside zones (Nanyang, Guilin, Shangrao and others). None in Europe, the US or
  Japan: their lines run through suburbs below that density (Baltimore-Washington, New York-New
  Haven and Tokyo-Tochigi have no such stretch at all; the Ruhr and Rhine lines have 3-4 km).
  Shanghai-Suzhou just misses: 12 km of line, but only 0.11M of Shanghai's built-up area is on
  Kunshan's side.
- **Numbers, Guangzhou-Foshan** (share of trips arriving at Foshan's places within 3 km of the line
  that come from Guangzhou): 8.6% now; 26.1% with the everyday fix and 0.5; 31.5% with the
  everyday fix and 1, which is also what one merged zone gives. Shenzhen-Dongguan (Dongguan side):
  8.5%, 26.2%, 31.5%. Commuters across Guangzhou-Foshan: 0.65M a day now, 0.78M at 0.5, 1.33M at 1.
- **Why not merge.** Merging fixes one pair but the rule finds chains: merging every passing pair
  makes the Pearl River Delta one 48.5M zone of 66,550 cells, bigger than Tokyo (the biggest pack,
  61,521 cells), and is the 40M pack you split by prefecture on 2026-10-22. The small Chinese pairs
  would merge into 2.6-4.2M zones, over China's 2.5M zone cap. Each merge is also a pack rebuild,
  a zone id removed and a joint solve rerun, and one zone name over two cities. The pair list costs
  a few lines in the joint solve and nothing at runtime.
- **Your call.** Whether to do this at all (it is a new rule in demand: a list of pairs with no
  damping). I'd do it, with the 0.2M rule. Lowering it to 0.1M adds 19 pairs (38 in all),
  Shanghai-Suzhou, Bristol and San Juan among them.

## 1. Which pairs: the test

`../artifacts/trainworld/t1178/seams.py` (read-only, 3 minutes on one core). For every registered
pack (1,749): its boundary in Mollweide and the GHS-POP 2025 1 km pixels of 300+ people inside it
(1.35M pixels; 27 claimed by two packs, the first kept). Urban centres as GHSL's degree of
urbanisation draws them, without its gap filling: pixels of 1,500+ people, joined edge to edge,
in clusters of 50,000+. Per pair of packs:

- **km of line through city**: pairs of neighbouring pixels, one in each pack, both 1,500+ and in
  one urban centre (each pair about 1 km of line); also at 5,000+ on both sides;
- **people on each side** of the urban centre the line runs through;
- **GHS-FUA 2015**: a functional urban area holding 15%+ of its people on each side (a commuting
  area split by the line; a looser test, kept as a column in `seams.json`).

The GHSL urban-centre database itself would not do as a test: its centres chain whole regions (one
Guangzhou-Foshan-Dongguan-Zhongshan centre of 25.6M here, and the Pearl River Delta at 43M in
T-535), so "one centre spans both" says nothing about where the line falls. A test on the line
itself does. The FUA test is too loose: it splits nearly every county town's commuting area
somewhere (227 pairs, 142 of them with under 3 km of built-up line and 102 with none).

Outputs: `seams.json` (every pair touching at 300+ people, 2,224), `seams_3km.txt` (179 pairs
with 3+ km of line at 1,500+), `rule_10km_0.2M.txt` and `rule_5km_0.1M.txt`.

## 2. The two options

### What each gives

Measured with T-1163's method (`border.py`, a copy of `notes/t1163_border.py` that re-balances the
commutes to any factor; zone grain, read-only). Arrivals a day at the zones within 3 km of the line;
share from the other side:

| Line, destination side | Now (no everyday across, 0.4) | Everyday fix, 0.5 (T-1175) | Everyday fix, 1 = one zone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guangzhou - Foshan, Foshan side | 8.6% | 26.1% | 31.5% |
| Guangzhou - Foshan, Guangzhou side | 4.5% | 18.9% | 22.0% |
| Shenzhen - Dongguan, Dongguan side | 8.5% | 26.2% | 31.5% |
| Shenzhen - Dongguan, Shenzhen side | 4.6% | 17.1% | 20.3% |

Commuters across the line a day: Guangzhou-Foshan 0.65M now, 0.78M at 0.5, 1.33M at 1;
Shenzhen-Dongguan 0.59M, 0.71M, 1.22M. Outputs: `border_gua_fos_to05.txt`,
`border_shz_dog_to05.txt`; the factor-1 column is T-1163's (`../t1163/out_*.txt`).

At zone grain, one merged zone and two zones at factor 1 give the same trips: inside a Chinese pack
the commute gravity has no border factor (each tier 0 pack is one region, factor 1), and T-1175's
everyday draw across a line uses the same rule as inside a zone. What differs is where the
cross-line part is solved:

- **One zone (a)**: everything at cell grain in the local layer, walking included.
- **No damping (b)**: commutes across the line in the regional layer at 6 km zones, rail against
  the car, as every commute between zones is now; everyday trips across it in T-1176's third pair
  list, walking first. The coarser grain matters for short trips: 8.5% of Guangzhou's commuters into
  Foshan travel under 5 km (31k of 360k now), and those cannot walk in the regional commute's
  choice. Half of everyday trips across are under 5 km, which T-1175 handles with its walking share.

### What (a) costs: merging into one zone

- **Chains.** The pairs link up: merging all 19 makes Guangzhou + Dongguan + Shenzhen + Boluo +
  Foshan + Zhongshan + Zhuhai one zone of 48.5M people and 66,550 cells (Danyang + Gaoyou + Jiangdu
  another, 6.0M). Today's biggest packs: Tokyo 61,521 cells (39.8M), New York 54,441, Seoul 41,007,
  Shanghai 35,339. The world plan caps a pack at 40M (T-535), and the Pearl River Delta was split
  into prefecture rows on 2026-10-22 (T-897, DECISIONS) because the 40M version was the game's
  biggest pack by far and slow to build. Merging only the pairs that do not chain (Guangzhou-Foshan
  alone: 21.5M, 31,331 cells) breaks the rule for its neighbours, since Guangzhou-Dongguan and
  Foshan-Zhongshan pass it too.
- **The zone cap.** The 11 pairs of two countryside zones would merge into zones of 2.6-4.2M,
  over China's and Taiwan's 2.5M cap (`world_plan.ZONE_RULES`); a regroup of those provinces' zones would be
  needed instead.
- **Rebuild.** A plan edit merging the rows, a full pack build (large packs take up to an hour or
  more, T-535), the province's joint solve again, market rows, zone aliases and names, and a zone id
  removed from the registry (adding-a-city.md, "Zones removed", T-1053); Anita's saves that reached
  the removed zone lose it.
- **Runtime.** A bigger pack opens whole: viewing Foshan downloads and solves Guangzhou too
  (4.1 MB of bin instead of 1.8 MB). The everyday draw runs per origin zone over the pack's zones,
  so its work grows with zones squared: 2,342² against 1,331² + 1,011², about twice.
- **Interface.** One zone with one name ("Guangzhou", Foshan an alias that search finds); the top
  bar says Guangzhou over Foshan; zoomed out, one bubble for both; the city inspector's totals
  cover both. Foshan stays a market place (ids are never renumbered) but stops being a zone the
  player can pick, which reads oddly for a city of 6M people in the pack.

### What (b) costs: a pair list at factor 1

- `pipeline/borders.json` gains a list of pack pairs with no damping (or a rule a pipeline step
  turns into one); `joint_gravity.rs` gives those pairs factor 1. Each tier 0 pack is its own
  region in the solve ("pack gua"), so a pack pair is one region pair: a few lines where the region
  table is filled. No pair in the list is a US or Japanese multi-region pack, so the state and
  prefecture rules are untouched.
- The affected joint solves rerun: Guangdong, Jiangsu, Henan, Jiangxi, Guangxi, Hebei, Hubei, Anhui
  and Taiwan. T-1175's data already needs a rerun of every solve group at the next rebuild, so this
  adds nothing if it lands first.
- Nothing at runtime: the commute pairs across these lines grow (Guangzhou-Foshan's 0.65M to 1.33M
  commuters on the same 6 km pairs), not the number of pairs.
- Docs: zones.md 4 (border factor), demand-worldwide.md (the rule's entry), DECISIONS.

## 3. Recommendation

**(b)**: no damping across a line that runs through one continuous built-up area, by the rule in
section 1, from GHS-POP 2025 at 1 km (global, already on disk for the world plan). It gives the
merged zone's trips without its chains, cap breaks or rebuilds, keeps every zone a place the player
can find, and leaves the fitted 0.5 in force wherever two separate metros meet. Japan's census fit
is untouched, since no Japanese pair passes.
