## The rule

At a change (an arrival off a line at that line's own station, `Search::ban`) two bars beyond the line's own:

- **back**: no departure of any line whose next stop is the station the rider came from (`pst` of the arrival).
  The same-line rule (T-575) for every line. Any line stopping at both has a departure at the earlier
  station going the rider's way, so the only thing riding back bought was a fresh roll of that line's wait.
- **even**: no departure whose previous hop was in the same evenly spaced class (`Graph::rn_even`, T-4050) as
  the hop the rider rode in on. That train runs right behind theirs on the same track, so they could have
  boarded it where they got on.

Walks to another station get neither (`own` false). Both are precomputed per arrival (`Search::xbar`, NONE where no
other line at the station meets them), so stations where nothing applies take the old path. The change node's
key is now `Bar { line, ban, back, even }`; `Search::bars` replaces `barred` in the four places that test a
departure against a change node.
