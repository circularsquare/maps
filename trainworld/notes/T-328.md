# T-328: show the last final demand result at once on reload

T-303 item 5, built in one stage. Design in docs/architecture.md 11 "Demand cache"; the lookup
choice in DECISIONS.md 2026-10-14. Code: `app/src/workers/demandCache.ts`, the cache part of
`workers/demandClient.ts`, IndexedDB helpers in `workers/saveFile.ts`. Check:
`checks/t328-cache-check.mjs`.

## What it does

- Each final result of the current solve is written to IndexedDB beside its slot,
  `demand:<slot>:<sha256 of the network>`, one per slot (a deleted slot takes it along). Contents:
  every kind of day's network view (lines with loads, board, alight; stations; purposes), each
  solved zone's split figures, the market's trips, and the viewed zone's weekday bubbles of every
  purpose when they were on show. Real New York save: 7,261,466 bytes, 4.9 MB of it the bubbles
  (typed arrays plus 8 bytes per other number). Writing takes under 20 ms of main thread.
- Key: the network as demand reads it (ids replaced by places, complexes renumbered, since a load
  renumbers ids), the SHA-256 of every pack the solve covered and of the market (hashed as they
  download, no extra request), the build (`__TW_BUILD__`, set by vite.config.ts at each build and
  dev-server start), the worker count and `?perfOff`. The zones must equal the new solve's.
- On a whole-game load with a match: shown as a final view under version 0; the clock runs; the
  hold, loading bar and zone grey go; the status reads "Updating demand" (its hover: "These figures
  are from the last time this game was open. They are being worked out again."; the split's hover
  ends "(from the last time this game was open)"). The fresh solve's estimates are not shown; its
  final replaces the cache in one step. Queries about version 0 get nulls from the workers, so
  clicks and other purposes' or days' bubbles wait for the fresh final.
- Not written by test pages, `?new=1`, or a tab without the slot (`saveSlotsState.writable`).
- The bubbles' sums are now added in worker order, not arrival order: before, the same result's
  bubbles differed in the last bit from load to load, so cached and fresh could not be compared.
- Also removed T-345's dead `baseSplit` and `cityBaseSplits`.

## Measured

`checks/t328-cache-check.mjs` on my own Vite (port 6151), headless Chrome, the real save in the
profile's autosave slot, weekday shown, Destinations bubbles on. Times in ms after navigation.

| | network drawn | clock runs | first figures | bubbles | fresh final |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (3 loads, bubbles off) | 1680-2093 | 9997-11023 | same, an estimate | | 19563-20663 |
| after, no cache yet (2 loads) | 2383-2514 | 11123-11226 | same, an estimate | 11621 | 20533-20819 |
| after, cache (5 loads) | 1673-2058 | 1789-2185 | same, final | 3673-3713 | 19547-20677 |

With the cache the figures show 0.1 s after the network (IndexedDB read and checks 51-128 ms), the
clock runs at once, and the bubbles show once the zone's cells are open in the aux worker. Views:
`0:3:true` then `1:3:true`, nothing between.

Results: the signature of every figure (lines, stations, purposes, split, zone views, market;
210,691 numbers) is `b67db952` on every load before and after, and the cached view's is the same,
bit for bit; the cached bubbles equal the fresh ones (`eb5181bd`) on every load.

Stale (`MODE=stale`): a tampered build (miss "build"), a tampered market hash (miss "pack market")
and a changed fare (another network hash, miss "none") each showed no cache and solved as before.
Not tested in a browser: a load that renumbers ids (a session with deletions, then a reload); the
place-based key covers it by construction.
