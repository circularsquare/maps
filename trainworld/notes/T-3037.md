# T-3037: hidden achievements

Rules are in docs/ui-panels.md "Achievements" (Hidden firsts). Code: `game/achievements.ts` (`CORRIDORS`,
`NATIONS`, `corridorFigures`, the share loop in `demandFigures`), the view hides unearned ones, and
`STEPS` counts a hidden one only once earned. Check: `node trainworld/checks/t3037-hidden-achievements.mjs
http://localhost:6161/ [--real]`.

## Choices

- **Service**: lines are joined into one network where they share a station or a complex (the game's own
  transfer places, `complexView`); bus lines and lines without trains do not count. A corridor needs a built
  station at each end on that network.
- **Places** are zones (`par`, `ist`, `nyc`, `snf`, `san`, `bue`, `cap`, `cai`, `ade`, `daw`, `xia`) by
  `stationZone`; Hokkaido and Shikoku by position inside Japan (rules in the code's comments); western Europe a
  country list (GB IE FR BE NL LU DE CH AT LI MC ES PT IT AD); eastern Russia at or east of 104E (Irkutsk).
- **Share**: the top bar's percent is a zone's train rides over its rides; the world's is train rides over a
  fixed 25 billion (3 rides a person, T-1301). A region's share here is the same fixed-denominator kind: train
  rides a day in its built zones over 3 rides a person times its people (zone populations summed,
  `checks/nation-people.mjs`). Europe's zones go by `continentOf` on the zone's centre.
- **Thresholds** (measured with the real saves, `--real`): the NYC save carries 1.235% of the US's rides
  (12.1M a day), the Tokyo save 3.13% of Japan's and 5.14% of Korea's (Seoul and Busan, 7.9M a day). Japan 10
  and Europe 5 are Anita's. A country's own is about twice what one strong metro reaches, so it takes several
  cities: US 2, Spain 3, Britain 4, Italy 3, Germany 4, Korea 8, Taiwan 5, China 1 (about 42M a day), Indonesia
  2, Mexico 2, Brazil 2, Australia 4, Turkey 2. The countries are those with the most play (twstats).
  Countries other than these two saves' are guesses until someone plays them; retune in `NATIONS`.
- **Names**: Anita's for the first six except Euro Trains, which she called terrible: Grand Tour (the old
  European tour; others considered: Interrail, Rail Europe). Japan Railways, British Rail, Bundesbahn (West
  Germany's old name, not Deutsche Bahn) and Ferrovie (Italian for railways) are nods; the rest are "Rail
  {country}". Extra corridors: Trans-Andean, Cape to Cairo, The Ghan, Silk Road (Beijing to Lhasa and London to
  Edinburgh had no clear zone to name, or were too easy).
