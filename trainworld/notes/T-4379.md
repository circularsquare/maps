# T-4379 Crossing refusal wording

Player (ja, 11-27): "heights differ by 1 but I can't cross". Cause: `find_crossings` (sim/src/track/net/derive.rs) marks a crossing Bad when the height difference AT the crossing point is under one level (8 m), so a ramp still climbing there refuses even though the two ends are a level apart. The old text ("unless they are a full level apart") read as if the levels were meant. The refusal carries no lengths (Issue::Crossing has only edges and position), so the text says the rule plainly with the floor ramp length (160 m, 100 m tram; same as rampTooShort).

Changed (en, then all 12 languages, merged with l10n.mjs, i18n.mjs passes, tsc passes):
- issues.crossing: "Tracks cannot cross on a ramp unless they are a full level apart." -> "One track is still on its ramp where they cross, so they are less than a level apart. Cross past the end of the ramp. Each level needs at least {ramp} of ramp." (issues.ts now passes ramp)
- gradeMarks.badCross (hover on the red crossing mark): "Tracks cross on a ramp less than a level apart" -> "One track is still on its ramp here"

Family checked, unchanged: issues.rampTooShort (already says the needed length), platformNotLevel, platformCrossing, splitRamp, crossingOverlap: none say the confusing thing.
