# T-041 notes: water mask from OpenStreetMap

Code: `pipeline/osm_water.py` (the source, a CLI, water-only packs). How to use it for a new city:
docs/adding-a-city.md, "Water outside the US". Comparison script: `notes/t041_compare.py`.

## What existed before

- **Toronto** (`canada.py`, T-277): StatCan lakes and rivers (Esri Canada's ArcGIS copy) plus
  everything outside Ontario's cartographic census subdivisions and the US states. Canadian only.
- **Tier 0** (`tier0.py`, T-287): no water of its own; the config passes a `water_source` callable
  to `water.build_mask` and raised "OSM water is T-041" without one.
- Both go through `water.build_mask(source=...)`, so OSM water is one more `source`; the
  rasterizer, the 2-pixel opening, the encoding and the checks are shared and unchanged.

## Sources

- **Sea**: osmdata.openstreetmap.de `water-polygons-split-4326.zip` (906 MB, one zip for the world;
  no per-region download exists). Kept zipped in `data/raw/osmdata/`; GDAL reads the shapefile
  inside it with a box filter in 9-13 s per city, so no extraction or per-world cut is needed.
- **Inland**: Geofabrik extracts read with GDAL's OSM driver (multipolygons layer, with
  `waterway` added to the closed-way polygon keys so old closed riverbank and dock ways count).
  Kept: `natural=water`, `landuse=reservoir`, `waterway=riverbank|dock|canal`. Dropped:
  `intermittent=yes`, `seasonal=yes`, `covered=yes`, any `tunnel`, `location=underground`,
  `landuse=basin` alone (mostly dry detention basins). Cached per extract as
  `data/raw/osm/<extract>.water.fgb` (FlatGeobuf with a spatial index).

## New York: OSM against TIGER on the same grid

The OSM mask built on exactly the nyc pack's water grid (11,410 x 11,647 pixels of 25 m, same
2-pixel opening), extracts NY, NJ, CT, PA, MA, RI. The shipped nyc mask was not touched; the OSM
one is `data/work/osm_water/nyc_osm.json/.bin`. Picture: `../artifacts/trainworld/T-041-nyc-compare.png`
(blue both, red TIGER only, green OSM only).

- **All 18 T-030 test places right** (read_pack's `WATER_TESTS["nyc"]` on the OSM mask).
- Water: TIGER 40.22% of the grid, OSM 40.32%.
- **Disagree on 0.56% of all pixels** (TIGER water where OSM has land 0.23%, the reverse 0.33%);
  within 1 km of TIGER's shoreline 1.48%.

| Place (box) | TIGER km² | OSM km² | Pixels that disagree |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hudson, Battery to Tappan Zee | 115.2 | 116.4 | 0.70% |
| East River and Harlem River | 43.2 | 43.8 | 0.65% |
| Upper Bay and Kill van Kull | 41.9 | 42.4 | 1.19% |
| Lower Bay and Raritan Bay | 316.6 | 316.1 | 1.14% |
| Newark Bay and the Arthur Kill | 80.3 | 82.4 | 1.42% |
| Jamaica Bay | 73.1 | 78.6 | 4.41% |
| Long Island Sound (west) | 436.7 | 438.6 | 0.88% |
| Great South Bay | 496.3 | 498.4 | 1.44% |

Where they differ (biggest patches, 0.7-3.3 km² each): OSM draws Jamaica Bay's marsh islands and
some Meadowlands marsh (Kearny) as water, and the slips between Manhattan's and Brooklyn's piers;
TIGER has a strip of beach as sea along Long Beach and Sandy Hook (shoreline drawn at a different
tide line), and a few upstate lakes (the biggest, 3.3 km² at -74.53, 41.53, probably tagged
wetland or intermittent in OSM); OSM has 2.6 km² of water at -75.10, 40.73 (probably Merrill
Creek Reservoir, NJ) that TIGER does not. These patch readings are from their positions only, not
checked one by one. Elsewhere the differences are single-pixel shoreline noise and
small ponds near the 50 m cut. For the game (track over water, walks round water) the two are
interchangeable.

## London

`osm_water.py mask lon --extracts europe/united-kingdom/england --bbox -0.9,51.1,0.75,51.9
--origin -0.1246,51.5073` (Greater London plus about 25 km round it; origin Charing Cross):
4,729 x 3,719 pixels of 25 m, 31,560 runs (0.14 MB), water 3.1%, 17 s with the caches. The sea
polygon reaches up the estuary only at the east edge; the tidal Thames is OSM river area.
All 14 test places right (`WATER_TESTS["lon"]`: the Thames at Waterloo Bridge, Greenwich and
Gravesend, the Royal Victoria and Royal Albert Docks, Queen Mary, King George V and William
Girling Reservoirs; land at Trafalgar Square, Buckingham Palace, Mudchute, Heathrow, Stratford,
Greenwich Observatory). Picture: `../artifacts/trainworld/T-041-london.png` (close-ups: central
London, the Isle of Dogs and Royal Docks, the Staines reservoirs, the Lee Valley reservoirs).

For T-288: the mask is `data/work/osm_water/lon.json/.bin` (a preview in the pack format; the
pack's own mask is built over its boundary by tier 0). Set the config's
`"water_source": osm_water.source(["europe/united-kingdom/england"])`; the England water cache
and the sea zip are in place, so it takes seconds. The England `.osm.pbf` (1.7 GB) is kept in
`data/raw/osm/` for T-288's other OSM reads (routes, places, parks); delete it after those.
