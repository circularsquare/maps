## What the first runs found, and the rule changes they made

- **Russia came out at ADM1** (oblasts: Krasnoyarsk Krai whole for Krasnoyarsk), because
  geoBoundaries' ADM2 has no units in Moscow and Saint Petersburg and so failed the coverage test.
  Fixed by rule, not case: a level need cover only half the country's people to be chosen, and a
  first-level unit it misses a tenth of takes the finest level that covers it. Russia is now ADM2
  (Moscow 17.7M in 31 units, not the oblast whole); ARE, BHR and LBY stay ADM1 (no finer level).
- **Hong Kong and Macau have no geoBoundaries**: the territory whole from Natural Earth.
- **Extracts**: preferring single-country children was not enough (multi-country extracts and the
  continents were chosen beside Russia); the rule now takes, at each level, the covering child or
  greedy set of children with the least total area.
- **Splits** left slivers under 1M (Guangzhou's); a piece under 1M now joins a touching piece it
  fits in, else is dropped and listed.
- **Names**: Isfahan came in Arabic script from the UCDB; a non-Latin name now falls back to the
  merged centres' and FUAs' names, then the largest unit's.

## Counts (the plan as written)

515 planned metros: 11 in the UK, 74 in the rest of Europe (Russia's 16 among them), 430
elsewhere; the first world row is Guangzhou (order 86). Seven rows are split pieces (Guangzhou
three, Jakarta two, Dhaka two). Unit levels: geoBoundaries ADM2 in 62 countries, ADM3 in 37, ADM4
in 4, ADM1 in 3 (ARE, BHR, LBY); NUTS-3 in 22; ITL3; Hong Kong and Macau whole. Largest overlap
between any two planned extents, or one and an existing pack: 0.008 km2 (boundary rounding). The
next-50 picture: `../artifacts/trainworld/t536-next50.png`. The 28 dropped metros
are mostly the existing packs' own (Paris, Tokyo, Mumbai...) and four that fall just under 1M
when snapped (Bristol 0.97M, Krasnodar 0.98M, Gdansk 0.76M, Colachel 0.98M).

## Known weak spots, left for the gate (T-538) or stage 5

- Big units in thin countries make big extents: Dammam (Saudi governorates) spans 11 x 12
  degrees, Johor Bahru, Kuala Lumpur and Oslo several. The gate's 40,000 km2 area check and
  T-535's "drop the outermost units" fallback deal with these.
- GHSL's merged centres carry odd names for their region: Hajipur (Patna's region), Kozhikode
  (Kerala's coast), Comilla (Dhaka's second piece). Names are GHSL's or geoBoundaries', in Latin
  script as they come (diacritics kept: São Luís, Đà Nẵng).
- A split piece first kept the metro's name only if a unit held the densest pixel; Buenos Aires's
  falls on the shore outside every unit and came out "La Matanza". The piece nearest that pixel
  now keeps the name.
- geoBoundaries' Chinese county names run together with their suffix (Panyushi, Zengchengshi).
- The plan's China units are geoBoundaries ADM3 (1:4M-derived per the register); the China
  module's OSM admin_level 6 is finer in outline; stage 5 can map one to the other.
