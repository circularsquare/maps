# T-3238: Tokyo saves hang at "Calculating demand" since v0.3

Agent savehang, 2026-11-11. The player's save had not arrived; the cause below is from a reproduction.

## Cause

Passing tracks (T-2231, `track/overtake.rs`). At a passing station a slower train waits for every
faster train that would catch it before the next passing point, capped only by its own headway minus
the spacing. When the express is frequent enough that the next one always catches the local, the
local waits out nearly its whole headway at every such station, so its round trip is
`B + m * (headway - s)` for m such stations. A count line's rate must satisfy
`rate * round_trip = trains * 3600`; with the round trip growing with 1/rate there is no solution once
m >= trains (two or three trains, five passing stations). The settle then lowers the rate every
round (we saw it halve each round: 0.027, 0.0136, 0.0068 ... 0.0002 per hour), and each round's
plans sweep one cycle of departures train by train, where the cycle and warm-up grow with the slower
headway: at 0.0034/h the sweep held 38,409 trains, 24 phases a plan, recomputed every settle round.
Cost roughly doubles per round, so one settle never ends in practice. In the browser that is the
clock worker stuck inside `load`, the world never posted, and the watchdog's
`loadingScreen.simTimeout` after the 10 minutes it allows while a load bar is up.

It matches the report: a rapid sharing a local's track (the plan pairs), a local with few trains at
some demand level, passing tracks at its stops. An edit that sets this up can itself hang, and a save
whose rates were left low by earlier edits hangs on load: on load the settle steps every line off its
fleet.

Not new in v0.3 (passing tracks shipped 2026-11-03, before v0.2.1); the player's network reached it
after day 60. Live (3a442a6e) and master behave alike: overtake.rs is the same in both.

## Evidence

- Fuzz harness (scratchpad `savehang/fuzz`, outside the repo): real Tokyo network, random player-like
  edits (rapids over a line's every 2nd/3rd stop, removals, new lines, counts, headways, "Add passing
  tracks"), after each a save reloaded under a 20 s limit. Seeds 11 and 12 hung inside a
  "passing tracks" edit within about 100 steps, on master and on live.
- `artifacts/trainworld/t3238/repro-load-hang.track` (track bytes, Mercator frame; feed it to
  `edit_bench` or a `TrackApi::load`): live code does not finish loading it (killed after 120 s);
  with the fix it loads in 0.8 s natively. Made from seed 12's state before step 80 plus that step's
  passing edit.
- New test `a_rare_local_behind_a_frequent_express_settles` (overtake/tests.rs): the Shinkansen line,
  passing tracks B-F, express 24 trains, local 2. Fixed: settles in 0.04 s, local 0.90/h, two trains
  over a 134 min round trip. Live code: still running after 5 minutes.

## Fix (overtake.rs)

1. A slower train waits at most one faster headway plus the lead past its own time (beside the old
   own-headway cap). After that the next faster train would catch it just the same, so it goes and the
   faster one follows it (its loss). Round trip is then bounded by the faster line's headway: a fixed
   point exists for any fleet.
2. `HEADWAY_MAX_S` = 4 h: plans timetable a rarer line as every 4 hours, so a rate near zero for any
   reason cannot make a sweep unbounded.

Rates move only where a local used to wait for more than one express headway at a stop, which was
the runaway case. Saves left with a collapsed rate recover on load (seed 11's line at 0.005/h came
back over 0.4/h).

## Found on the way (not fixed)

Real Tokyo line 99 at the low level loses about 16% of its rate on every edit, whatever the edit:
0.95/h to 0.005/h over 55 edits, round trip unchanged, `line_pace` growing 22,000 s to 143,000 s.
That is T-2973's even share at a full merge: `even = 3600 k / sum(line_tph)` reads rates the pacing
itself lowered, so it feeds back. With the fix it no longer hangs anything, but the line's service
fades away. Probably the same area as T-3235.

## Timeout and message

`LoadingScreen.tsx` watchdog: 3 minutes with no bar, 10 with one ("The train simulation did not
answer after 10 minutes."), then the failure screen with "Try again" and "Back to start". The clock
worker cannot be interrupted while it is inside WASM, so a usable error is what there is; no text
changed.
