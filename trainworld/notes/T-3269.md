# T-3269: a flag beside a player's name

Anita's TODO: "in community / leaderboards, let people add a flag next to their name, countries or cities or random
other assortment maybe too". 2026-11-12: "sure can do flags by names too. i guess via account settings." Agent `flags`.

## What it is

- **Account panel**, signed in, when `/noritetsu/config` says `trainworldFlags`: a Flag row under the played time. The
  box shows the flag and its name ("No flag" by default). Focused, it lists No flag, the game's own few, then every
  country by its name in the player's language (Intl, as the leaderboards' country boards); typing filters (start of
  any word, or the code). A pick saves at once (`PUT /trainworld/flag`) and marks the boards stale so they refetch.
- **Leaderboard rows** and **Community cards** show the round flag, 14 px, before the name; its name in the hover.
- **Server** (shonei-server e98ae76): `flag` on the profile in `players.json`; codes only from the embedded
  `trainworld_flags.json`; board rows, `me`, Community rows (list, card, mine) and `/trainworld/me` carry it. A hidden
  name (T-3227) or one the filter blanks sends no flag; the owner still sees their own. A code a later list drops is
  sent nowhere public. Tests: `trainworld_flags_test.go` (3), route added to the rate-limit list; full `go test` passes.

## The list (`app/src/game/flags.json`)

- 249 ISO 3166-1 countries and territories plus `xk` (Kosovo, which the game has). Not just the 86 countries with
  cities: a flag says where the player is from.
- More, in this order: Train, Earth, Pride, Transgender, European Union, United Nations, Pirate, Checkered, Amsterdam,
  Chicago, Washington, D.C.
- Left out: NATO, the Olympic rings (IOC trademark), Klingon (trademark), regional, historical and disputed-state flags
  that circle-flags also has. Easy to add one: an entry in flags.json, rerun the tool, deploy the server.

## Pictures

- [circle-flags](https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags) 2.8.3, MIT: round SVGs, all 261 used come to 173 KB, one
  file each in `app/public/flags/`, loaded only where shown (the picker loads all when opened). Its LICENSE.md is copied
  beside them. Emoji were out: Windows draws flag emoji as two letters.
- Train, Amsterdam and Chicago are drawn by `tools/flags.mjs` in circle-flags' style (its palette, circle mask);
  Amsterdam and Chicago are simple public domain designs, Washington is circle-flags' `us-dc`.
- Rebuild: `npm pack circle-flags@2.8.3`, untar, then `node trainworld/tools/flags.mjs <package folder>` from the maps
  root. It rewrites `public/flags/` and shonei-server's `trainworld_flags.json`.
