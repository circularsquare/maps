# T-3269, T-3279: a flag after a player's name

Anita's TODO: "in community / leaderboards, let people add a flag next to their name, countries or cities or random
other assortment maybe too". 2026-11-12: "sure can do flags by names too. i guess via account settings." Agent `flags`
built it (T-3269). Her review the same day (T-3279, agent `flags2`): "lets maybe have it like citycircles quiz where we
just show an empty flag rectangle outline and then our display name ... i dont like how prominent "Flag [box] No Flag"
is. should be smaller, cuter"; add NYC, Tokyo, Osaka, maybe all prefectures; a bigger picker that may reach past the
window; countries first with the player's own on top, then other places, then the non-place flags "as easter egg";
in Community and on the leaderboards the flag after the name, smaller after a small grey name.

## What it is

- **Account panel**, signed in, when `/noritetsu/config` says `trainworldFlags`: under the played time, the flag (an
  empty 4:3 outline, 20 x 15 px, while there is none) then the leaderboard display name, as City Circles' quiz has it
  (`website/assets/citycircles.html`, `#win-flag-btn`). Its hover: the flag's name, then "Flag shown after your name on
  the leaderboards and in Community".
- **The picker**: the flag opens it, fixed over the page inside the dialog (so it is not clipped by the panel), 300 px
  wide, up to 480 px tall, under the flag or above when there is more room. Order: No flag (only while one is picked),
  the probable country, every country, other places, the game's own few; a 10 px gap between groups, no rules. Each
  group by name in the player's language (Intl collator); places have their country in grey at the right. Typing
  filters (start of any word of the name or of a place's country, or the code: "japan" gives Japan and its 47
  prefectures); arrows and Enter pick; Esc or a click outside closes it, not the panel. The search takes focus only with
  a mouse (a phone keyboard would cover the list). A pick saves at once (`PUT /trainworld/flag`) and marks the boards
  stale.
- **Probable country**: the browser's time zone through `game/tzCountry.json` (553 zones and links from the venv's
  tzdata 2025.2 `zone.tab` and `tzdata.zi`), loaded only when the picker opens; else the region of the first browser
  language that has one. Time zone first because many people anywhere keep en-US.
- **Leaderboard rows** show the flag after the name, 16 x 12; **Community cards** 12 x 9 after the small grey name, or
  16 x 12 after a bold name (shares from before titles). Its name in the hover. A long name is cut with an ellipsis,
  the flag never (`Named` in `ui/Flag.tsx`).
- **Server** (shonei-server e98ae76, deployed; list 2 in the T-3279 commit, not deployed yet): `flag` on the profile in
  `players.json`; codes only from the embedded `trainworld_flags.json`; board rows, `me`, Community rows and
  `/trainworld/me` carry it. A hidden name (T-3227) or one the filter blanks sends no flag; the owner still sees their
  own. Config `trainworldFlagSet: 2` names the list version it accepts.
- **A game ahead of the server**: entries in `flags.json` carry `set` (2 for New York City and the prefectures); the
  picker leaves out any code whose `set` is above config's `trainworldFlagSet` (1 when absent). So against the deployed
  e98ae76 server the picker shows no prefectures and no New York City, and nothing can be refused. Showing a flag
  never depends on it: a code the page knows is drawn wherever the server sends it.

## The list (`app/src/game/flags.json`)

- `countries`: 249 ISO 3166-1 countries and territories plus `xk` (Kosovo, which the game has).
- `places`: the EU; Amsterdam, Chicago, Washington, D.C. (game strings); New York City (`nyc`, string `flags.nyc`);
  Japan's 47 prefectures (`jp-01` to `jp-47`, ISO 3166-2). Tokyo is Tokyo Metropolis's flag, Osaka the prefecture's;
  no city flags of their own, so no name is listed twice. Prefecture names: romanised without macrons, as the game
  writes Japanese places (Hyogo, Kochi, Oita), in every language but Japanese and Chinese, which get the Wikidata labels
  (東京都, 大阪府, 兵库县); the same rule as `i18n/places.ts` for cities. One gap filled by hand: Tochigi's zh-TW (栃木縣).
- `more`, the easter-egg end: Train, Earth, Pride, Transgender, the UN, Pirate, Checkered.
- Left out: NATO, the Olympic rings (IOC trademark), Klingon (trademark), historical and disputed-state flags; England and Scotland were not asked for; Spain's, Portugal's, Indonesia's and France's regions are T-3328 (notes/T-3328.md). To add one: an entry in flags.json with the next
  `set`, rerun the tool, bump `trainworldFlagSet` in shonei-server's `noritetsu.go`, deploy the server.

## Pictures (`app/public/flags/`, 309 files, 1.7 MB, each loaded only where shown)

- Countries, `eu`, `un`: [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) 7.6.0, MIT, its 4x3 SVGs as they are;
  `LICENSE-flag-icons.txt` beside them. Serbia's is the largest (181 KB).
- Prefectures: Wikimedia Commons, the file each prefecture's Wikidata item names as its flag (P41), recorded per entry
  in flags.json (`commons`). Commons marks 45 of them public domain (Japanese prefectural insignia, simple designs).
  Hiroshima's file carries no licence at all and Kagawa's vector is CC BY-SA 3.0 (the design itself is a simple public
  emblem), so those two are drawn by the tool from the published construction instead (Hiroshima a crescent, Kagawa
  its arcs). Kagawa's Commons page notes that the prefecture asks for notice before its emblem is used; that is a
  usage rule, not copyright, and a player flag beside a name is the kind of citizen use such rules allow in practice.
  Commons flags are 3:2 or so; the tool sets them to 640 x 480 with `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"`, cropping a
  little off each side.
- Earth and Pirate: [circle-flags](https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags) 2.8.3, MIT, with the circle mask taken
  off and the ground widened to 4:3; `LICENSE-circle-flags.md` beside them.
- Drawn by `tools/flags.mjs` (public domain designs, or the game's own): Train, Pride, Transgender, Checkered,
  Amsterdam, Chicago, Washington, D.C., New York City (its seal simplified to a blue ring round a disc), Hiroshima,
  Kagawa.
- Rebuild, from the maps root: `npm pack flag-icons@7.6.0` and `npm pack circle-flags@2.8.3`, untar each, then
  `node trainworld/tools/flags.mjs <flag-icons package> <circle-flags package> <commons folder>`. Commons files missing
  from the folder are fetched (Commons rate-limits: the tool waits and retries). It rewrites `public/flags/`,
  `game/tzCountry.json` and shonei-server's `trainworld_flags.json`.
