## Why the bar was full, from the code

1. **The load in hand waited for an edit's whole re-solve.** `jobNeedsSolve` (workers/demandClient.ts) made a mid-game
   load of a city a solve must cover end only at that solve's round 0. Since T-4051 (11-24) a city about to open with no
   figures opens bare first, and one she had looked at has bare figures already, so the solve that takes it in is not a
   barrier solve: an edit's, publishing only its final. Every newer network re-points the job (`dispatch`), so with steady
   editing it could last as long as the edits did. Its bar: the edit's units, bounded, so it ran to 99%.
2. **The status tag's bar carried across a final.** T-3868/T-4277 keep one place for "the running solve's wait" through a
   held edit (a break under 1 s is the same wait). v17's final, then v23 (a quick local solve: its units bound it, and the
   wait counted from v17's start, so the share went to ~98%), then v24 with no break at all: the bar never goes back, so
   v24's whole solve sat at the top of the bar for its minutes, whatever the tag's words (calculating, "Loading data…" for
   bubbles, a click's wait).
3. **The time left counted down to 0 between units.** Reproduced on the smallest case (below): a whole solve's worker,
   between units with blind units left (`work`: not busy, blind 63), said 259 ms left, bounded, and sent nothing for 1.7 s
   (a set-up or the market between units, which no unit times). The page counted 259 ms down to 0, the share went to 1,
   and the tag's bar sat at 99% for 1.3 s until the final at 1.85 s. `editLeftOf` treated a unit in hand past its time as
   late (a floor of a fifth of its time so far, no bound) but not the wait for the next unit.
4. **A zone's bar said "Loading city data" while it waited on the calculation.** `zoneProgress` labelled an open zone
   "data" unless a barrier solve was running (`st`), so after an edit an open zone with no figures, or the click waiting on
   it ("Loading Foshan…" in the tag), said loading while only the trips were left.

## Changes

- `workers/demandClient.ts` `jobNeedsSolve`: outside a game's load, a city with figures on the map is loaded once its packs
  are open; only a city with none waits for the solve (a barrier solve then, which publishes its rounds). The wait after is
  the status tag's "Calculating trips after your change…" with its own time left.
- `workers/demandClient.ts` `zoneProgress`: a zone open where the solves need it (every worker) says "Calculating trips"
  ("Loading city data" only before), with the running solve's time left from its units where that solve covers it.
- `ui/StatusTag.tsx`: the solve bar's wait ends when `demandBehindFrom` goes to null (a final covers every edit made); a
  solve still running then starts its own bar from empty. A held edit's solve keeps the wait (its edit is still behind).
- `game/editLeft.ts` `editLeftOf`: a worker with units still to run and none in hand, idle longer than what it has left
  (plus the page's gap after a local stage), is late: a fifth of the idle so far is added and the bound lapses, as for a
  unit in hand past its time. The bar holds instead of filling.
- Diagnostics (small): `workers/demandTrace.ts` logs `dm-load` (the load in hand: cities, items, the solve it waits for
  against the newest, packs in, game load or not, stage, bar in tens) and `dm-tag` (the status tag's words and bar, the
  loading card's, each zone bar's zone, tenth and stage), each on change; the download's `demandNow` has both as of the
  download. `tools/diag_report.mjs` prints them as "LOAD IN HAND AND WHAT THE TAG SAID" and under AT DOWNLOAD. What was
  missing for this report: the tag's words and bar and the job, which the file had no trace of.
- Docs: docs/ui-panels.md (a city loading later, when its load ends; the bar's bound lapsing on a late next unit; the tag's
  wait ending at a final that covers every edit).
