# T-230: waiting riders and train fullness disagreed

Anita (2026-10-13), on her line G2 (21 stops, 18.7 km, 5-car trains, 8 / 4 / 2 trains, 165k riders
a day): Greenpoint Av "To Pennsylvania Plaza 523, 601 left behind, longest wait 51m00", while the
line panel said "Fullest train at high demand 91%". "why doesnt it just take the extra people?
this is something that would confuse people." Then: "it seems like we should fix the demand
forecast's average train to instead actually show something closer to the per train account."

## What happened

The two figures came from different models:

- **The panel's 91%** was demand's forecast (`fullestPct`): on the line's fullest stretch, the
  busiest hour's riders (the period's mean times 1.4) shared evenly among the trains then, against
  their crush. For G2: 5.7 trains an hour each way of 800 at crush is 4,530 an hour, so 91% is
  about 4,120 an hour, 730 a train.
- **The platform** is the boarding account (T-164): each train's own riders. Its riders arrive
  fastest in the middle of each peak (1.8 times the period's mean at 08:00 and 18:00, against the
  1.4 demand judges by), so around the middle of the peak about 1.29 times the busiest hour's
  average want each train: about 940 for 800 places. Those trains leave full and the queue grows
  until the rate falls; with a train every 10.6 min a rider can wait several trains, hence 601
  left behind and 51 minutes. So the trains were in fact full on that stretch; only the forecast's
  average had room.

Her save is in her browser, so G2 itself was not rerun. The same thing on the real New York save
(`notes/t230-real.mjs`, `../../artifacts/trainworld/notes-outputs/t230-real.json`): of the 93 lines the forecast puts under 100%, 28 leave
riders behind in the account, every one with full trains: the D at 99% has 17 trains leaving full
(08:05-08:35, 17:45-19:07) and 6,100 left behind; the L at 87% has 13 (17:51-18:34) and 4,300;
the 4 at 90%, 16 and 3,300; Metro-North New Haven at 87%, 8 trains and 6,000 left, waits up to 97
min. A G2-like line in an empty new game (`notes/t230-browser.mjs`, Church Av to Penn Station)
gets 480k-725k riders and is overloaded all day, so it is no copy of hers; it showed that a queue
that never clears gives waits of a day or more (now shown as "More than a day").

## What changed (Anita's steer: show the account, drop the forecast average)

- Line inspector: "Fullest train at high demand" (the forecast) is gone. A new "Trains today"
  section, from the account (`TrackApi.line_account`, `Boarding::line_summary`): fullest train
  ("Packed full" or "N% full"), trains leaving full, when (spans of full trains under 30 minutes
  apart, at the stop where each is fullest), riders left on the platform a day, longest wait.
  Asked again after each edit and each hour of the account (`game/boarding.ts` `accountVersion`).
  Screenshot `../artifacts/trainworld/T-230-real-line.png` (the D), `T-230-line-18.png` and
  `T-230-station-18.png` (the new-game line).
- Train gauge: a train with no account (its line's stops just changed, until demand's next result)
  is drawn without a gauge instead of demand's busiest-hour or average train; its tag says
  "Calculating demand" instead of "About N riders on a train in the busiest hour".
- Train inspector: "Typical train now" (demand's period average) is now "Typical train this hour",
  the mean of the same line and direction's trains drawn this hour on the same stretch.
- `fullestPct` stays in the demand result (not shown).
