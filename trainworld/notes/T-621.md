## What it adds to T-509's fit

   trip as $10 over a driver's car trips a day (NHTS: about 3, so $3.33, about 10 perceived
   minutes at 3 a dollar). One worldwide figure in dollars, as fares are. T-551 measured it at
   +1.1 points of rail in New York and +1.7 in London before any refit.
2. **The walk weight against 1** (DECISIONS 2026-10-16): the fit runs twice, with the walk's 1.5
   and with 1; Anita picks, knowing her wish is 1 for clarity. (Waits are already plain minutes,
   T-585.)
3. **Walking fitted, not held**: `asc_walk` and `everyday_asc_walk` (held since T-558 to keep
   walking where it was) enter the fit on the travel surveys' walk shares: NHTS (US), NTS
   (England), EMP (France), Japan's person-trip surveys. Per country where a survey exists, a
   worldwide value elsewhere (as everyday rates do, T-450).
4. **Refit** with them: `asc_rail`, `everyday_asc_rail`, `everyday_walk_d0_km`, `other_asc`, and
   Japan's +0.5 rechecked. Leave one city out; Toronto's hold-out; every pack on the test cross
   (`notes/t509s7.py`); smoke.

Not in it: the long trips (T-528, a corridor fit of its own), crowding (`crowd_alpha`), bikes to
stations (T-529).
