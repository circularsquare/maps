The second pass of T-509's fit (notes/T-509.md sections 1-15), on the same harness (`calib.py run`,
`notes/t509s56/fit.py`, cached runs) and the same cities: New York (half weight), London, Paris,
Seoul, Tokyo, Sendai fitted, Toronto held out. It does not need the world's cities: the constants
live in the sim, so packs built later take them with no rebuild.

## What it adds to T-509's fit

1. **Car ownership cost** (T-620, DECISIONS 2026-10-16 T-551): about $10 a day, charged per car
   trip as $10 over a driver's car trips a day (NHTS: about 3, so $3.33, about 10 perceived
   minutes at 3 a dollar). One worldwide figure in dollars, as fares are. T-551 measured it at
   +1.1 points of rail in New York and +1.7 in London before any refit.
2. **The walk weight against 1** (DECISIONS 2026-10-16): the fit runs twice, with the walk's 1.5
   and with 1; Anita picks, knowing her wish is 1 for clarity. (Waits are already plain minutes,
   T-585.)
3. **Walking fitted, not held**: `asc_walk` and `everyday_asc_walk` (held since T-558 to keep
   walking where it was) enter the fit on the travel surveys' walk shares: NHTS (US), NTS
   (England), EMP (France), Japan's person-trip surveys. Per country where a survey exists, a
   worldwide value elsewhere (as everyday rates do, T-450).
4. **Refit** with them: `asc_rail`, `everyday_asc_rail`, `other_asc`, the walking set
   (`walk_trip_w`, `asc_walk`, `everyday_asc_walk`; T-558 replaced `everyday_walk_d0_km`), and
   Japan's +0.5 rechecked. The search starts from the shipped values (`asc_rail` 0.4,
   `everyday_asc_rail` -1.1), not `fit.py`'s pre-fit 0 and 0. Leave one city out; Toronto's hold-out; every pack on the test cross
   (`notes/t509s7.py`); smoke.

Not in it: the long trips (T-528, a corridor fit of its own), crowding (`crowd_alpha`), bikes to

## Stage 1 (2026-10-17)

**Built.** `Params::car_own_usd_day` (default 0) and `car_trips_day` (default 3), both in
`set_extra` (so `calib.py run <city> --set car_own_usd_day=10` works). `Params::car_own_min()` is
`usd / trips x FARE_MIN_PER_USD` perceived minutes; it sits in `park_parts`' fixed part, so every
destination-car site gets it: the commutes' gravity, the zones' choice with no rail, the pair
loop's lanes, the everyday layer, the trip explorer. Not charged to: homes without a car
(`nocar_choice` has no parking), a drive to a station (`access_options` takes `park_mean` less
the ownership cost; it is the whole-trip car's cost), the long trips. The regional layer's
commutes between zones add it to `CPair::car` through `commute_params()`, which is
`Params::default()`, so it takes the default at stage 3 and not a `--set`. At 0 the sums are
unchanged to the bit (real save 10,406,013). The explorer's head grew to 33 numbers (slot 32 is
the ownership minutes; slot 11, parking, leaves it out); a "Car ownership" row shows under By car
when it is non-zero.

**Reading it.**
- Today's values moved the fitted six from 5.15 to 6.20 since stage 6/7 (T-585 plain waits, T-587,
  rebuilt packs). Tokyo gets better (1.17 to 0.95: more rail, level x0.78 to x0.88); New York,
  London, Paris and Seoul get worse by 0.1 to 0.5, all by more rail (station level up 0.1 to 0.3).
- $10 a day with nothing else refitted raises rail everywhere: commute rail +1.8 points in New
  York (T-551 measured +1.1), +2.6 London (T-551: +1.7), +4.8 Paris, +3.0 Seoul, +3.2 Tokyo, +3.0
  Sendai, +2.1 Toronto; everyday rail +1.1 to +3.2. Only Tokyo's score improves (0.95 to 0.76,
  its areas part 0.47 to 0.28, as the +1.5 `asc_rail` it wants alone). The others get worse by
  0.08 to 0.5 because they already overshoot; the refit has to take `asc_rail` back down by about
  as much as ownership adds (stage 2). Paris moves most (+4.8 points).
- Differences from T-551's +1.1 and +1.7 are probably the base (T-551 ran before T-585) and what
  was counted (commute rail share of commuters over its areas); not chased.
