# T-885 (with T-881): the trip explorer in every combination of ends

Done 2026-10-21 (groupsel agent). Rules: docs/ui-inspectors.md "Between zones"; calls in DECISIONS.md.

## The ends

- **L** a local bubble of the focused zone; **LG** a group of them (Shift+drag, T-865)
- **P** an inter-region bubble as a whole place: a zone's bubble in the city view (zoomed out to
  8.5) or a selection's far place (zoom 9.5 and out); **A** a 6 km area (a selection's far end up
  close); **IG** a group of P or A (a Shift box over them)
- **T** a trip tool point

## Every pair, and what it shows

| Ends | How | Shows | Before |
|---|---|---|---|
| L, LG to L, LG (same zone) | select, then click a far bubble or Shift box over far bubbles | local explorer; a group averaged over up to 3 x 3 cell pairs | T-208, T-865 |
| L, LG to P or A | select, then click an inter-region bubble | market between the cells and the place or area | T-866 |
| L, LG to IG | select, then Shift box over inter-region bubbles | market, each far end asked and merged (`mergeMarketPeriods`) | new (T-881) |
| P to P | click a city in the city view, then another | market between the two places, every 6 km area pair | new |
| P to IG | city selected, Shift box over other cities | market, merged | new |
| P to L, LG (another zone) | city selected, zoom in past 8.5, click a bubble or Shift box | market between the cells and that city | new |
| A as a first end | none | a 6 km area is drawn only as a selection's far end, so it is never picked first; its pairs are reached from the other end | not offered |
| IG as a first end | none | a selection is one place; a box over cities with none selected is not a selection | not offered |
| T to T (both in the zone) | trip tool | local explorer | T-195 |
| T in to T out | trip tool | market, start's cell to the far 6 km area (else place) nearest the end | T-866 |
| T out to T in | trip tool | market, end's cell as destinations, the far area nearest the start | new |
| T out to T out | trip tool | market between the zones' places whose centres are nearest the ends (within 60 km) | new |
| T to L, LG, P | none | the trip tool takes points only; a point on the bubble is the same trip | n/a |

Place to place is the market's areas weighted (every 6 km area pair between the places, each by its
trips, `IntercityApi::market_trip_between`), not one trip between the two centres: the market
decides these trips area pair by area pair, so a centre-to-centre trip would be one sample of it,
and the headline would not add up to the bubbles. Two 6 km areas would be centre to centre in
effect, but no gesture picks an area first.

## Where it lives

- `sim/src/demand/intercity/market_trip.rs`: `market_trip_between` (near place or 6 km zone, every
  zone weighted 1), the body shared as `market_trip_w`, `zones_of`.
- `workers/demand.worker.ts` `icTrip` and `demandClient.ts` `marketTrip`: an optional `near` end.
- `game/trip.ts`: `MarketEnd`, `mergeMarketPeriods`, `marketGroup`; `MarketShown.fars/near/end`;
  `TripPlaces.fars`; `Selection` trip `near` (game/types.ts).
- `map/intercityView.ts`: city to city click, `placeTrip`, `interBox` (the Shift box's
  inter-region bubbles); a selected city's view only at the overview zoom (`cityMode`).
- `map/commuterView.ts`: `cityTrip` (a city selected, then local bubbles), the far box tries
  `interBox` first, the city view takes a Shift box, the hover hint.
- `map/tripView.ts`: `exploreMarket` over several far ends and a near place; `farEnd` for both
  trip tool cases. `ui/inspector/TripInspector.tsx` and `MarketTrip.tsx`: the names.

## Known limits

- A merged far group's routes are drawn ending at the first far end's centre.
- The city view's pair hover counts commutes between zones too; the explorer counts longer trips
  only (its hover says so).
- A city selected and then a local bubble in the same place selects the bubble, since the market
  has no trips within one place.
