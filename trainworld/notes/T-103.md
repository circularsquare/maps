# T-103: close-up train cars and crowding colours

The renderer reads actual car counts from worker-built `NetworkBuffers.lineCars`, transferred
with the other arrays. Old synthetic fixtures default to eight cars. At zoom 15.5 and above,
one instanced train draw emits six vertices per car, with each independently sampling its
20 m place along the route and its own stroke offset. Rounded rectangular bodies have visible
gaps; windows and the leading cab appear once car length exceeds 18 screen pixels. At terminals
the consist centre clamps far enough inside the route to keep every car on track. Further out
the existing capsule stays, with actual consist length replacing the fixed 160 m assumption.

Gauge position is measured across the full consist, including gaps, and reverses with the train.
Empty colour mixes 18% white (formerly 60%); filled colour is line RGB times 0.48 (formerly 0.62).
Seat/crush scale, busiest-hour basis and red overload rim are unchanged. Close-up picking checks
every car chord rather than only the analytic centre. No per-train frame work added to CPU.

Validation: TypeScript and Vite production build pass. `notes/t103-check.mjs` loads the real
New York network and checks all 109 line car counts (2 to 13 cars). An isolated eight-car train
on a 90 m radius curve renders distinct cars; front/middle/rear clicks all select the train.
Zoom 15.49 is compact, 15.5 and 18 are detailed. Both directions and unknown/empty/half/full/
overloaded gauges produce no WebGL errors or page errors. Screenshots visually reviewed:
`../T-103-cars.png` and `../T-103-network.png`. Results: `T-103-check.json`.
