# T-3901 Bus speeds shown while placing a waypoint

Her ask (11-20): the speed tag, as when placing track, centred on each bus stretch at one speed, in the colour of its stretch.

## What was built

- Sim: `Pace::speed_runs` (sim/src/track/bus.rs) cuts the previewed route into stretches of one moving speed (whole km/h) from the
  same `Pace::along` the line's service uses: `street_bus_kmh(car speed there, trunk)`, i.e. 85% of the car's street speed with the
  30 km/h floor, and 75 km/h (88 x 0.85) on trunk roads and motorways. `bus_quote` appends the runs after the street stops
  (count, then start m, end m, km/h, trunk flag); `busQuoteLL` passes them on. WASM rebuilt with `agent_cargo.py wasm`.
- App: `BusDraw.speedTags` (map/busDraw.ts) places them with the track tool's `CurveLabelPool` (map/trackTagRows.ts: same
  `pi-label` element, `chip`, `speed()` text). `CurveLabel` gained `colour` and `centred`; `.pi-label.centred` in style.css.
- Colour: the bus road hint's own (`busRoadColour` in basemap.ts): fast road blue (#3a4fb0 light, #a6bcff dark), other roads its
  teal. Her "light blue and dark blue" read as these two, since the preview line itself is one colour (the line's).
- Rule: only while a point is being placed (pointer leg, no drag), from the last quote's route. A stretch needs 64 px on the screen
  (about a tag's width); longer stretches win where two tags would overlap (labels sorted by length, `placeCurveLabels` drops later
  ones within 64 x 22 px). Tag on the stretch's middle by ground metres.
- The speed shown is the street moving speed. A hop between stops runs faster over its middle on open road (main roads, 110 km/h car
  open highway), which the sim's hop time counts; the tag does not.
