# T-326: separate clusters solve separately (T-303 item 3)

## Stages

notes/T-303.md item 3 names three: (a) the worker solves per cluster on the existing message, bit
check, timing; (b) only dirty clusters re-solve, published per cluster; (c) the viewed or selected
cluster first, spec. (a) alone gains nothing, since every cluster would still be solved, so stage 1
here is (a) plus the part of (b) that lives in the worker alone:

1. **Stage 1 (this note, done):** each demand worker splits the network into clusters, solves each
   on its own sub-network, and keeps a cluster's result when its sub-network is unchanged. The
   client is untouched except for `?perfOff=clusters`: it still publishes one result per solve,
   which after an edit now costs only the touched clusters' solving.
2. **Stage 2 (T-392, done; section below):** per-cluster publishing and answers after an edit; a
   click on a cluster not being solved answered at once; the status tag for the zone in view.
3. **Stage 3 (T-393, done with T-392):** the zone in view's cluster solved first. Startup and a
   zone just opened (solves with the round barrier): T-402, notes/T-402.md.

## Design (rules: docs/demand.md "Clusters")

- `workers/demandClusters.ts` `splitClusters`: union-find over stations, the job's cities and one
  market node. Joins: a line's stops; a complex; stations within 1 km; a city to stations within
  30 km of its cells' bounding box; the market to stations within 200 km of a market zone's centre.
  Each cluster gets its own network (stations in snapshot order, lines in snapshot order, complexes
  renumbered by first station) and maps for stations and route nodes, plus a 64-bit hash `sig` of
  everything its result depends on (sub-network, cities, market, rounds, fare, frame).
- Choices T-303 left open, made here:
  - Reach is judged generously, by bounding box and fixed margins, not by the kernel's own access
    search: too wide only merges clusters (time), too narrow would change figures. The market's
    reach comes from the registry's zone centres, not the market pack, so the auxiliary worker
    (which holds no market) splits exactly as the solving workers do.
  - Clusters are recomputed for every network, never stored, so a later link (T-362's worldwide
    market reaching Toronto and New York stations) merges two clusters with no other change. T-362
    must give its market a reach rule in `splitClusters` (today: the Northeast's zone centres).
  - Kept results live in the populations themselves (`held`: population -> sig of the last
    finished solve); nothing is copied. A superseded solve leaves its dirty populations unheld.
  - A station outside a city's cluster answers `station` and `icStation` as a population on the
    whole network did for a station it cannot reach (zeros, the zones listed: Rust
    `IntercityApi::no_station_flows`). `trip` answers' route nodes are mapped back.
  - Lines and stations no population reaches belong to no cluster (zero figures, as before).

## Stages 2 and 3 (T-392, T-393)

Rules: docs/demand.md "Clusters" (second paragraph).

- **Worker.** After an edit (a solve without T-327's round barrier) the dirty clusters are solved
  one after another, the zone in view's first (`first` on the solve message: T-393, which fell out
  of the ordering), each to its last round, its network set up only when its turn comes. Each but
  the last is sent at once as a `result` carrying `cluster` (cities, market, lines; arrays still
  the whole network's). Then the solve's results for every round are the final figures, worked out
  once: after an edit only the final is shown, and T-324's new lines take round 0's, which are now
  the final ones. Solves with the barrier (startup, a zone just opened) step all clusters round by
  round together as before.
- **Answers.** The worker keeps its last six networks' clusters; a question about any of them is
  answered at once while the asked zone's cluster holds that network solved to its last round
  (`held`), whatever is being solved. That covers clicks on the shown result during an edit's solve
  in another cluster, and on a cluster already published.
- **Page.** A cluster result goes into the shown views (lines, their stations, purposes, its zones'
  views, the market's if in it); the network-wide totals wait for the final. `cityVersion` sends
  that zone's questions and bubbles to the solve it came from. `plan` (the clusters a solve works on)
  feeds `demandSolving`; `viewUpdating` (status tag, the top bar's "before your last change") is
  true only while the zone in view is still being solved.
- **Found on the way:** the order cities were added up in followed the order packs happened to open
  (`cities` map insertion), so the same network could give figures differing in the last bits from
  one load to another (seen as a hash mismatch between two runs). The worker now adds up in the
  registry's order.
- With the barrier (startup, a zone just opened) the viewed cluster's final now lands on its own
  too: T-402, notes/T-402.md.

### The startup regression after 2710f82

Smoke's demand final went from 35 s to 43-44 s with T-392. A/B of the real save's startup solve
(`scratchpad/t326/startup.mjs`, worktrees of b59da8c and 2710f82, back to back): b59da8c 30.1-34.8
s, 2710f82 38.0-42.5 s, almost all in round 0 (periods 0-2 +4-5 s each) and set-up (+0.4-0.9 s).
2710f82's worker with b59da8c's client was as slow; b59da8c's worker with 2710f82's client as fast
as b59da8c; 2710f82 with only the registry-order sort taken out was as fast. So the cause is the
order the populations are solved in (registry nyc, nhv, lit against pack-open order), not the
figures (identical either way): it changes how the worker's shared caches are reused (T-405).
Fix: figures are still added up in registry order, but populations are set up and solved in
pack-open order again (`solveOrder`). Clean A/B with the same WASM (fb63fa8's) everywhere:
b59da8c 29.3, 29.1 s; fb63fa8 34.0, 35.0 s; fixed 29.6, 29.7 s. (Each city's everyday layer
straight after its commutes was in between, 33.4-34.9 s.) Also fixed: a solve without the barrier
sent its final results twice (the round loop ran again after the once-only results); t066 and t149
worker checks pass, smoke's demand final 7,597,261 in 35 s, a Toronto edit's final 1.6 s (2.3 s
before), and t326-clusters' seven hashes unchanged.
