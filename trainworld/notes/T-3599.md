# T-3599: time left in the calculating trips hover

Anita, 2026-11-17: "could we have it so that hovering over the "Calculating trips after your change...." says like
estimated 5 minutes left or whatever estimated time is?" (big Asia save, an edit took about 5 minutes).

## What the hover says now

The status tag's hover after an edit gets one line:

- "Calculating trips after your change…" (the clock capped, or a click waiting): the time to the edit's final.
- "Trips here being recomputed…" / "Trips in N cities being recomputed…" during an edit's solve: the time to the
  zone in view's figures (its local result if the edit touched it, else its cluster's whole result).

The line is "About N min left" (N rounded, 1 or more), "Less than a minute left" under 60 s, or "Time left not
known yet" before every solving worker has sent its plan, or while units with no timed passes are left and none
like them has ended. Its other lines ("Time capped at 4x while updating demand") stay below. Shot of the real
hover: `artifacts/trainworld/t3599/nyc7-hover-light.png`.

T-3601 (c5319e6d, merged into this work) keeps "Calculating trips after your change…" up to an edit's whole final;
before it, a train-count edit's tag went once the zone in view's local result was in and the whole solve (2-2.5 min
on the Asia save) ran with no tag at all. That tag takes the time to the final, so the hover now covers the whole
wait (checked after the merge, below).

## How it is worked out

`app/src/game/editLeft.ts` (pure), fed by each solving worker's `work` message (`demand.worker.ts` `Work`).

- **Units.** An edit's solve already counted its work in units (T-2729): a period of a local solve, a round of a
  period of a cluster solved whole. Each unit now also carries an expected time, planned up front: the sum of its
  populations' passes in that round of that period the last time this worker solved them whole (`passByRound`,
  kept beside T-3512's `passMs`, which sums the rounds). The zone in view's split free flow (`splitRound`, T-1543)
  is a unit too: before it was, a worker sat 10-43 s outside any unit on the real save.
- **Pace.** Units run off their passes' time: a local solve at about a tenth (its crowding starts from the last
  solve's), a whole solve at 0.9-1.2, and after a load a period's passes can be off by 3-10x for that period alone.
  So the time left is each stage's expected time times its pace in this solve, the whole solve's period by period
  (`ranQ`/`expQ`), each leaning on the one above it as if that had run one unit (`PACE_WEIGHT_UNITS`): the last
  edit's pace (kept within 0.8-1.25 for the whole solve, where 0.91-1.20 was measured, since the passes it ran on have been replaced) under the
  worker's, the worker's under each period's.
- **No pass.** A unit whose populations are mostly without a pass (under half) has no expected time: it is timed
  by its size (populations) at this solve's ms a population, from such units once one has ended. Before any unit
  of a solve with those has ended, the hover says "Time left not known yet". After one load of the Asia save 211 of
  213 populations had a pass; after another (same save, same build) 1 of 213, only the market (cause not found;
  the load's level-of-detail refine replacing the city populations is a guess).
- **Outside units.** The page waits 8 s after the local results show before it lets the whole solve start
  (`EDIT_SETTLE_MS`), then the worker sets up: the gap, 8.6-9 s measured, is added while a local stage is still
  running. After the last unit the figures go to the page and it works out the views: the tail, 1.4-2 s on the
  real save, 5-18 s on the Asia save. Both are remembered from the last edit (first guesses 9 s and 5 s).
- **The solve's time** is the slowest worker's: workers run their periods side by side, and the solve ends with
  the last.
- **Which wait.** On "Calculating trips after your change…" the time to the edit's final; on "Trips here being
  recomputed…" the time to the zone in view's figures: the local stage up to its cluster when the edit touched it,
  else its cluster's whole solve (on the real save, one cluster: the whole solve).
- **Smoothing.** The shown time counts down with the clock and takes a new estimate as the loading card's does
  (`nextLeft`, T-1654): a lower one at once, a higher one only past 1.4 times the count plus 10 s, else the count
  slows to half speed. Shown in whole minutes, so it moves at most once a minute in the middle of a wait.

## Measured against the real wait

`checks/t3599-time-left.mjs`, 3 solving workers (as a 12-thread machine gets), the clock at 10x, edits through the
line panel and Ctrl+Z as a player makes them; another agent's timing runs shared the machine. Real save
(`T-007-real-nyc.save`, one cluster of 4 zones, no local stage): a line near Times Square halved, undo, line C off,
undo. Asia save (`artifacts/trainworld/reports/t3385-asia.save`, Anita's Japan, Korea and China, 147 zones, a
cluster of 106): the Japan-Korea line at Fukuoka off, undo, Busan's Line 1 off, undo. Time left as the estimate
had it (s) against the real time left, at 30%, 50% and 70% of the wait; mean error over every tenth past 20% with
over 20 s left (`t3599-replay.mjs`). Final code: runs `nyc7` and `asia6`.

Every NYC wait is under a minute after its first seconds, so its hover said "Less than a minute left" throughout,
right each time. On the Asia save the hover read "About 2 min left" for the first 40 s of the 130 s waits, "About 1
min left" to about 60 s left, then "Less than a minute left": at no tenth past 20% more than a third off in the
final run (one earlier run: "About 1 min left" at 103 s left, the whole minute rounding 87 s down).

The weak case is the first edit after a load, when the passes are missing (1 of 213 populations had one after
four of six loads): "Time left not known yet" for the first 70 s of 241 s (30 s before the solve even started,
then a first local unit of 35 s with no timed pass; 46 s and 58 s in an earlier run) and again from 110 s to 133 s
(the whole solve's first unit), then under by a third (62 s against 97 s at 60%), from the tail
guess (5 s, really 15 s) and the rounds' 0.75 decay. Every later edit has every population's passes from the edit
before.

**After merging T-3601** (run `asia7`, tag up to every edit's final): the Japan-Korea line off (first after a load;
209 of 213 with a pass) 114 s, estimate 50 / 39 / 27 s against 80 / 57 / 34 s at 30/50/70%; undo 126 s, 102 / 60 /
30 against 88 / 63 / 38; Busan's Line 1 halved 131 s, 93 / 75 / 46 against 92 / 65 / 39. Mean error 18%, none past
a third; the live hover twice rounded across a minute ("About 2 min left" at 88 s, "About 1 min left" at 92 s).

Changes of the hover, every one, in the final runs (`nyc7`, `asia6`): never a step up. Asia undo: "Time left not
known yet" for 0.5 s, "About 2 min left" at 127 s left, "About 1 min left" at 91 s, "Less than a minute left" at
53 s; real save: "Less than a minute left" from 1-3 s in.

**A second edit during the first's solve** (real save, line S halved, line C halved 20 s later; scratch script):
the second waits for the first's final (T-1734, filed as T-3616) and is solved after it, 203 s in all. With the
running solve's time doubled while an edit waits (`editHeld`), the hover went "Less than a minute left", then at
the second edit "About 2 min left" (178 s really left: a minute under), then at the second solve's start "About 2
min left" (92 s left), "About 1 min left" at 63 s, "Less than a minute left" at 47 s. Before the doubling it said
"Less than a minute left" to the first solve's end, then started again. A 0.5 s "Time left not known yet" at the
second solve's start is gone (the count carries over).

Things tried: a flat mean unit time (the T-2729 bar's) ran 186 s against 85 s at the start on NYC, since the local
stage's units look like whole-solve ones; scaling the passes known up to the whole cluster made one market pass
stand for 213 populations (2,000 s a unit against 15 s); a pace leaning on 20 s of prior ms adapted too slowly when
the passes were 10x off. The pace's weight (0.5 to 5 units) moved the mean error by 1 point.

## Re-checking on another save (T-3600's)

    node trainworld/checks/t3599-time-left.mjs http://localhost:<port>/ <path to save> "<lng>,<lat>,off" undo

from the maps root of a tree, on its own Vite, `TW_CORES_WANT=4` for 3 solving workers. Each edit moves the camera
to the point, sets the trains of the line with the stop nearest it to none (`off`) or halves them (`half`); `undo`
undoes the last. It prints, at each tenth of each wait, the hover against the time really left, and writes the
trace to `artifacts/trainworld/t3599/<save name>.json` (`NAME=` to rename). A different estimate can be tried on a
trace without solving again: `node trainworld/checks/t3599-replay.mjs <name>.json`.
