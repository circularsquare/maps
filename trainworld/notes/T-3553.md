# T-3553 more accurate shoreline than the 12 m margin

Measurement only; nothing landed. Anita, 2026-11-17, after testing: track by water "mostly feels okay", so only
cheap wins are on the table.

## Method

Truth is the water the basemap draws: OpenFreeMap (OpenMapTiles) z14 `water` polygons, not tunnels, minus `pier`
areas (the style paints piers in the land colour), rasterised at 1 m in each pack's own frame. Each option is
evaluated on the same 1 m grid. "Kept" water is the drawn water of the features a 25 m mask keeps after its 50 m
opening, so dropping water under 50 m wide (by design) is not counted as an error.

Per window and option: the area of game water on drawn land, and of game land on kept drawn water, each divided by
the shore length (m/m: the mean width of the misplaced band), and the depth of the wrong pixels from the drawn edge
(median/p99/max, m; these are conditional on a pixel being wrong, so read them beside m/m). Narrow rivers: 1,500
random points in drawn water; local width = shortest of 18 crossings through the point; one straight crossing at a
random angle (kept under 3x the width), sampled every 12.5 m from a random start as `water_spans` does.
"Registered" = a sample inside the crossing reads water; "wet" = wet samples x 12.5 m / drawn crossing length.

Windows: Sydney Bondi to Maroubra (syd, OSM water, 14.7 km of shore); Berlin Spree through Mitte (ber, OSM, 28.4
km); New York East River south, East River north, Harlem River, Newtown Creek (nyc, TIGER 2020, 19-35 km each).
Narrow rivers: Newtown Creek (NYC, 40-80 m over most of it) and the Spree (50-70 m through Mitte).

Scripts (scratch, not kept): measure.py, mvt.py (tile decoder), summary.py, cost.py, plot.py in the shoreline
agent's scratchpad.

Findings:
- **Reading between cell centres is not a real accuracy gain.** At threshold 0.5 it trims the false-water band about
  20% (2.4 -> 1.8-1.9 m/m, p99 13-14 -> 10 m); thresholds 0.75-0.8 behave like a 6-9 m margin. Margin size and
  threshold all move along one trade-off: less game water on land means more game land on water. No option without
  new data improves both sides.
- **The 25 m cells are nearly the whole error on OSM packs**: Sydney's and Berlin's masks score the same as a fresh
  25 m rasterisation of the basemap (2.35 vs 2.43 and 3.56 vs 3.47 m/m in Sydney; Berlin equal), so source age adds
  little. Real gains need finer data: 12.5 m cells halve
  the error, 5 m cells take it to 3 m, a shore distance byte to about 8-9 m p99 both ways.
- **TIGER is the biggest error in the game**: NYC's mask sits up to 36-54 m onto drawn land and 116-178 m into drawn
  water (Brooklyn piers and slips, Battery Park and Hunters Point landfill, Harlem River banks), against 17 and 25 m
  on OSM packs. 9 packs use TIGER water: nyc, phl, bos, wdc, bal, pvd, nhv, spf, wor. The NYC "drawn water at 25 m"
  row is a ceiling for switching them to OSM: it has the basemap's piers removed, and `pipeline/osm_water.py` does
  not cut piers.
- **Basemap z12 tiles miss piers**: against z14, z12 false water reaches p99 21-32 m in the East River windows.
  A runtime-tile design would need z14 tiles near piers.

## Costs

- **Cheap (no data change)**: margin 6 m is a constant; the between-cell read is about 20 lines in `RunMask`
  (cost.rs) plus the test. A between-cell read takes 4 cell reads (2 row searches) against today's 5 with the margin;
  lookups run per 12.5 m sample on edits and drawing previews only, never in the demand solve. Either is about 1 h
  with tests, the WASM build and the T-007 real-save check. `lineProfile.ts` reads the rows itself (no margin today).
- **Masks load whole per pack**, on demand (`fetchWater` in clock.worker.ts: when the view is within 20 km of a pack
  or track within 2 km), not in chunks inside a pack. Today 567 MB over 4,139 packs; NYC 1.1 MB.
- **Shore distance byte in packs**: NYC 1.1 -> about 9 MB in 8x8-cell tiles (7.6x over a 250-pack sample: 567 MB ->
  about 4.3 GB), less in a sparse layout; every pack's water rebuilt from re-downloaded Geofabrik extracts (the
  caches are deleted after each run): roughly 10-20 h of machine time and 4 h of agent time, plus a per-area split
  so a player loads only the shore detail where they build.
- **Finer run masks**: 2x bytes at 12.5 m, 5x at 5 m, same full rebuild; 5 m breaks the u16 column limit on wide
  packs (vlp is 34,199 columns at 25 m).
- **Basemap tiles at runtime**: no pack change, but water would follow the weekly basemap version, and `reload()`
  re-prices every built edge when water arrives (T-3524 measured 182 of 1,647 NYC edges moving); native tests and the
  realsave bin have no tiles. A design call, about 6 h.
- **OSM instead of TIGER for the 9 packs**: about 2 h of agent time plus the state extracts, water arrays replaced in
  place; needs pier cutting in osm_water.py first.

## Recommendation

Nothing has to change: the 12 m margin does what T-3524 wanted. If one cheap step is wanted, read the mask between
cell centres with threshold 0.8 in place of the 12 m margin (about 1 h): narrow rivers are charged for 69-75% of
their width instead of 55-60% and 30-50 m water registers a little more often, game land on drawn water drops by
about a third (12.4 -> 8.5 m/m), while game water on drawn land rises from 0.01-0.10 to 0.14-0.24 m/m (worst case
6-10 m from the coast, as now 7-9 m). The real gain on her list would be OSM water for the 9 TIGER packs.
