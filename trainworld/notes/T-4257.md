# T-4257 bus routes take the quickest road

Her 11-25: "it shouldnt be 1.4x cuz we changed the ratio, but shouldnt we still make it take the fastest route rather than the shortest?" Credit: xoaninc, PR 32 (weights trunk roads in the bus search; this takes its idea, with the speeds from the sim instead of a flat 1.4).

## What changed
- sim: `bus::road_speeds()` (bus.rs) gives a plain bus's moving speed on streets, trunk roads and motorways, and busways, from the same functions `Pace::speed_runs` uses, at the car's 44 km/h street (what the sim takes with no street grid): 37.4, 52.4 (x1.4, `BUS_TRUNK_MAX_X`), 60 (`BUSWAY_KMH`). Exposed as the free wasm function `bus_road_speeds`.
- app: `map/busSpeeds.ts` reads it once (the track wasm the ghost already loads). `BusRoads.need` waits for it (a leg is not ready before), then `RoadGraph.setBusSpeeds`. `RoadGraph.path` (the bus search only) costs an edge as length x street/edge speed: trunk and motorway edges 0.714, busway edges 0.623 (busways now carry kind 4, weight `BUSWAY` 1.25; the short stubs joining a busway to a road stay plain). The A* guide is scaled by the smallest factor, so it stays a lower bound. A leg's reported `m` is road metres (tracked beside the cost), so quotes and stops are unchanged.
- The detour cap (3x straight + 1 km) now compares road time, so a route on fast roads can run up to about 1.6x longer than the old cap. Hop and snap penalties stay street metres.

## Brief vs code
The brief says the search keeps a turn penalty. The bus search (`RoadGraph.path`) has none; the turn cost belongs to the tram/rail search (`turnPath`, `TURN_M`), which is unchanged. So nothing to carry over.

## The lie
Real sim speeds depend on the place's density and the line's floor (BRT). The tool does not know either, so it weighs roads at the 44 km/h reference. In a dense centre a busway is 2.4x a street (not 1.6x) and the trunk ratio is 1.4 everywhere (the cap), so only the busway factor is off, and low.

## Saves
Saved bus routes keep their drawn points (they are not searched on load), so no existing line moves and no riders change on load. Only legs drawn or moved after this build take the new roads. NYC save: no bus line moves.
