# T-3718: trips can start with a walk to a station

Agent walkstart, 2026-11-18. Anita, 2026-11-18: "let trips start with walk". Background: notes/T-3689.md.

## Rule

A rail trip may start with the same walk it may end with: from its station to another within the walking-transfer
limit (300 m, `xfer_walk_m`) or anywhere in its station complex, costed as a walking transfer (straight line x 1.3 at
1.2 m/s, weighted 1.39; a passage at 2 m/s), then the walk to the platform of the train taken. No change minutes and
no transfer fare: it is the first boarding. Nobody changing trains takes it (a change's walks are the route node
walks they already had), no walk follows another, and a walk to the trip's own destination is no rail trip.

## How it is represented

Outside the graph, as an origin-only step after each destination's strategy search (advisor's call, agreed):
`Search::start_walks` picks, for each station, the station nearby whose boarding pool (`dist` less its first
boarding's change minutes) plus the walk is lower than its own, by the plain cost (no fare curve or base multiplier,
so a search that writes no route costs picks the same). The result is a sparse list per destination,
`DestStrategy::start` / `Skims::start` / `Strategy::start` (station, station walked to), like T-1430's `entry`.
Read by trip start:
- route cost column (`run_by`): the station walked to's cost (its fare curve km and base multiplier included) plus
  the walk; the inter-city planned-wait cost (`entry`) the same way;
- real minutes for the walking rule (`skims_extend`, `start_walk_min`);
- the assignment: `DestAssign::walk` (kernel, inter-city) and `assign_lanes` (everyday) move the riders to the
  station walked to before walking the strategy (`Strategy::fold_start`): boardings and fare extras land there, no
  change is counted; the entry stays at the trip's station, as an exit after an end walk is counted at the
  station walked to;
- inter-city fare km (`fare_col`, `Strategy::start_column`);
- the trip explorer's routes (`trip::routes`): a walk leg first, the platform walk joining it.

Lie, small: `cap_loads` cuts first boardings at the station walked to while the entry is at the trip's station, so
a stalled line's entry cut can miss a few walkers.

## Would a player see anything wrong?

The trip explorer lists a route from a station the trip walks away from as "walk to <station>" then the ride, the
same row a change's walk uses; the access row still names the trip's own station. Station entries stay at the
trip's station, boardings are at the station walked to (as exits and alightings already split after an end walk).
A start walk from a station with no line of its own sends -1 as the leg's first route node (guarded in
`Leg::numbers`; the app's walk row shows only where it goes). Nothing else shows.
