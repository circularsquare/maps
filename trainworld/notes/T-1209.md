# T-1209: a clock state's effects on the main thread

Done 2026-10-26 (agent t1209). Follows notes/T-1200.md (item 2) and T-1208's handover.

## What changed

- **`game/money.ts`**: `moneyTerms`, the fares, economy and upkeep rates out of `moneyState`, the same
  object while they are the same (an hour's money message and every state's money left the sums
  alone only in name: each redid income for three days, the region shares and the upkeep shares).
  `moneyWorld`, the world as the figures read it (each line's id, number, stops, status and stop
  positions, each station's place and built flag): the last world while those are the same, so a
  train-count or other state that leaves them redoes nothing. `incomeByDay` keeps a day's figures
  while its view, the network, the terms and the zone boundaries are as before, and returns the
  same array when nothing changed, so the clock client's effects (and their `JSON.stringify` keys)
  do not run. `lineGeo` / `scalesOf`: each line's stretch km, stop zones and fare scales, built once
  per network and zone boundaries, shared by income, points and region shares. The per-segment
  reduces became loops (same order of additions, so the same doubles). `incomeByDay` now follows
  `zonesLoaded` as the points and shares already did (before, it caught up at the next money
  message).
- **`game/points.ts`**: `linePoints` redoes a day only when its view, zone views or corridors change
  (or the network, or zone boundaries); `dayPoints` on `lineGeo` with loops.
- **`game/regionMoney.ts`**: `lineShares` memoised on the weekday's view, `moneyWorld`,
  `moneyTerms` and zone boundaries; `upkeepShares` keeps each edge midpoint's zone (`midZone`, per
  edge id while its ends and the boundaries stay) instead of `placeAt` for every edge on every
  state, and returns the last shares while they come out the same.
- **`workers/clockClient.ts`**: a kind of day's riders are not posted again for the view they were
  last posted from (cleared on a load, with the income, shares and points).
- **`game/cities.ts`**: `cityWithin` keeps its answers by exact (lon, lat), dropped when the coarse
  outlines arrive or past 50,000 entries; the realms (per station) and the rail countries (per built
  node) asked it the same points.
