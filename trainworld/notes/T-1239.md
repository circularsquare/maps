## 1. metro_remnants: a remnant stays in a smaller city's FUA

Step 2 (gravity) sent Mission to Vancouver (73% of its travel) though Mission is in Abbotsford's
census metropolitan area. GHSL's urban centre for Abbotsford (110,000) stops 9 km short of Mission
(the Fraser), so the built-up area alone does not hold it; GHSL's functional urban area (GHS-FUA
2015, the urban centre and its commuting zone, already a planner input) does. Rule (`city_fuas`,
`metro_remnants(..., fuas)`): a unit with half its people in the FUA of a city, the biggest urban
centre inside that FUA at CITY_POP (100,000) or more, never joins by gravity a row that does not
hold that centre. Cities under 100,000 do not count, as in the two-cities rule, so Saint-Jérôme
(urban centre 53,000, in Montreal's CMA) and Chambly (in Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu's FUA, 77,000) still
join Montreal.

Existing plans: a dry run of Korea and Taiwan gives every row the same (23 and 9 rows: ids, units,
people, status, order). The step runs only for metro rows still `planned`, so China (metro rows
shipped) and any built country cannot move; the US's zones take belts from its commuting table, not
remnants.

**What it changes in Canada** (dry run `can.json`/`can.log` in the kit, 46 zones, median 0.32M, 33
under 0.5M, 823 units dead with 1.0M people, as T-1191's): Mission, Matsqui Main 2 and Upper Sumas 6
stay out of Vancouver (2.79M to 2.75M). The Fraser Valley is still two zones, now for another reason:
Squamish joins Vancouver (Squamish-Lillooet D, 73%, with Squamish inside it), so Whistler (60% to
Vancouver, but not touching the row as it stood) joins the Fraser Valley zone, whose arm north round
Vancouver's row makes Abbotsford's join wrap; the ring cut splits evenly by angle: Abbotsford 0.17M
(7 units) and Chilliwack 0.19M (Chilliwack, Mission, Whistler, Hope, Kent, Pemberton). Tried and
dropped: the gravity step repeated, touching the row as grown with partners judged once

to 4.81M (370 joins after the first pass), T-1191's run-on again. Filed T-1375 (a ring cut that sheds
a thin arm before halving). Kept as is: both zones are Fraser Valley towns and Mission is with them.
