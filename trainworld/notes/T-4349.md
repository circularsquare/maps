# T-4349: a ramp's speed limit shown over a tighter curve's (agent slopespeed, 2026-11-25)

Anita, 11-25: "if i have a tight curve thats limited by curvature to 80kph but its limited by slope to like
150kph itll show 150, i think."

## Cause: the labels only; trains were right

Where each limit is combined, from the code:

- **Trains and times**: `geom::limits` takes, per stretch, the lowest of the curve (capped at the track's cap)
  and a ramp's limit (a ramp stretch is emitted only where `vr < v`). `service::build_run` (the trains'
  profile), derive's `edge_time` (`geom::run_time`), routing and fleets all read it. Right before and after.
- **Speed colours** (ghost, Speed display, built track): `render_speeds`, from `geom::limits`. Right.
- **Track panel "Slowest curve"** (`edges_info` vmin) and the line panel's stop-time hover
  (`line_stretch_speeds`, "A ramp limits trains to ..."): from `edge_limits`. Right.
- **The speed labels** (the chips on the map) were wrong in two ways:
  1. `preview_ramp_labels` gave one label per ramp slower than the track's cap, at the ramp's middle,
     whether or not a tighter curve sat on it. The app listed ramp labels before curve labels, and
     `placeCurveLabels` drops a label within 64 by 22 px of one already placed, so the ramp's figure won
     wherever the two were close on screen: her 80 km/h curve showed the ramp's 150.
  2. A curve's label (`vertex_kmh`: preview rows, `edge_pis` for a selected stretch) was the curve's own limit,
     never a slower ramp's, so a gentle curve on a short steep ramp showed the curve's figure, and with no
     ramp label at all when dragging a node, in the blueprint box or on a selected stretch not being dragged.

## Fix

- `geom::ramp_stretches`: the stretches of `limits` where a ramp is the lowest limit, a run at one speed
  joined across pieces, 10 m or longer (`RAMP_STRETCH_MIN_M`). `preview_out` labels each at its middle
  (none for a ramp under a tighter curve). The 10 m came from the game run: a tangent arc of a hair over 90
  degrees is two corners whose arcs a sub-millimetre straight parts, and that straight sits at the ramp's
  limit (160 under an 80 curve), which labelled it. Trains are unaffected (no train can reach it there).
- `vertex_kmh` takes the lowest of curve, cap and a ramp at the curve's middle (`geom::ramp_limits`); every
  caller passes the fit's `old` (preview: the scope's; node drag: the piece's; flyover legs: false, as their
  colours; `edge_pis` now fits in the piece's own ramps, `fit_edge`, and its `old`).
- App: `slowestFirst` (trackTagRows.ts) orders a tool's labels slowest first, so where two would overlap the
  slower shows. Used by the drawing tool, reshaping a stretch (dragging and not), node drags and the blueprint
  box. (The bus tool keeps its own order, longest stretch first.)
- `realsave --slow` counted only curves and caps; it now uses `edge_limits`.
- Docs: track-and-service.md (Levels: what the preview labels), ui-building.md (Curve labels).

No sim figures change: `limits`, `run_time` and every train path are as before (`ramp_limits` keeps the old
filter), so no NYC figure diff was needed (budget row: App logic).
