# T-3540, T-3564: stations on single track, tram stop width

Anita, 2026-11-17: "currently one-track stations (no passing loop) on trams look the exact same as two-track
stations on one-track line (passing loop). we should draw the passing loop. and if its a one track, we should let
the platform be smaller and skewed toward one side ... by default on the outside ... tram platform boxes shoudl be
a little narrower than train paltform boxes in general. visually. lets say 30% less wide."
Then: "the one-track tram station getting smaller platform to one side is not tram specific ... i think it should
apply even if we have a passing track. the passing track should be on the inside". Then (T-3564), on seeing it:
"oh oh i see. yes drawing can go back for this case."

## Where it ended

- A station on single track that splits round a passing loop (T-3212's split, tram stops included since T-3540)
  draws a platform each side, as on double track (flag 16), each way's trains stopping on their own track.
- A station on single track that does not split (one-track stop, one-way single track, single track joining one
  track of a double at the station) draws one platform: the one way station's bar and bulge (ST_ONE), the bar
  centred on the track (ST_ONE_MID), filled as its fuller side (ST_SINGLE, in STATION_VS), its waiting room on the
  bulge side joined to the bar (facilityPlace.ts; one way stations on double track too).
- The side (`map/stationSide.ts`): (1) the side of the double a single leaves (its `join`, followed through plain
  nodes up to 3 km), so a split double's two one-way legs face away from each other; (2) else on one-way track the
  running side; (3) else south-east of the track (south-west keeping left). Not checked in a left-hand game.
- Tram stop boxes and one-platform bulges are 0.7 of a station's width (`TRAM_BOX`, STATION_VS and the CPU twin).
  The placement ghost (tools.ts) still draws rail width; not changed.

## Lie

The sim ran every tram stop on single track as one with no loop (`Network::no_passing`, T-3352) when T-3540 landed;
T-3563 (tramloops) is making trams use the loops.
