### T-4239: weekend headways and `settle_kept_rest` (real)

The weekend settle (`settle_weekend`, world.rs:1302) swaps `settle_kept_rest` in as `settle_kept` and the weekends'
headways into `line_auto` (`swap_in_weekend`, :1249), so `running_trains` reads the weekend headway. A level is kept
off its fleet when its entry matches `(line, slot, rate, round trip, kept_key)` (:1494). `kept_key` is the train count
(plus other slots' rates where hours mix places), never the headway. T-4239 drops entries when the headway changes
(:2323-2338), but compares only `Ahead.keys`, the weekdays' `auto_at` (:2276, :2330), for both lists.

So a weekend-only edit that moves the weekend headway and leaves the weekend count alone (`set_line_weekend_headways`,
`set_line_row_weekend`, or `set_line_weekend_share` on a headway line, whose weekend headways follow the share) finds
no weekday change, the stale weekend entry stays, and the weekend settle (services_after's `!t.weekend.is_empty()`
branch, :2451) holds the level at its old rate. Same count is the usual case for these lines: kept entries are for
lines circling on full shared track, where `weekend_counts_in` clamps the count to the track's limit
(`clamp_auto`). The player sees the weekend trains an hour (and weekend figures) not follow the headway set, and the
save keeps the entry, so a reload does too. The cousins are covered already: count changes (in `kept_key`), pacing
(`pace_merges` drops entries, :1067, which during the weekend settle is the weekend list), hours moving (`mix_moved`).

The other direction is too eager: a weekday headway change drops a line's weekend entries even where its weekends run
headways of their own (both lists are filtered by the one `gone` list). Harmless, but the fix below separates them.

T-4239 is unpublished (changelog c018), so no player met this; no changelog item.

### T-4249: station-only edits on a way-back line's run 1 (not real)

The finding rests on `der.edges` being empty for a station edit. It isn't: `set_station` calls `touch_node`
(stations.rs:133), and `derive` re-derives every edge at a touched node's ports and returns them in `der.edges`
(derive.rs:33). A run 1 that stops at or passes the station has a segment on one of those edges, so `back_moved`'s
last test (world.rs:2544) is true and the line is rebuilt (platform lengths, `running`, passing waits with it).
Construct and unconstruct, platform length and passing track all go through `set_station`. A change in which
station run 1 stops at (the paired opposite-side stop) is caught by the run comparisons above it, which work the runs
out from the net as it now is. The T-4249 + T-4149 interaction rests on this finding and goes with it.

### T-4153: a step of no length at the splice's end node (real, not visible; fixed)

`smoothKnots` gives two alike pieces (same recipe, values within 1e-6) a step of no length at the middle of their gap.
A whole sweep passes such a step at a node exactly at its place before taking the node's value (`hi <= m`,
`sweepKnots`), so node e1 reads the piece after it. `spliceOf` took into the sweep only steps starting before node
e1 (`sml[k1] < S[e1]`), so a no-length step exactly at S[e1] was left out and entry e1 - 1 ended on the piece before.
The difference is under the 1e-6 alike tolerance in shift, width and share, with the same recipe, so nobody sees it,
but `?patch=check` (t4153-net.mjs) compares bands bit for bit and would report it.

Fix, lineBands.ts:1997: `sml[k1] <= S[e1]`, so steps starting at the node are swept too. Nothing else moves: a step of
length starting exactly at S[e1] adds nothing there (it starts only past its first place, `lo < m`, and its curve is
0 there), its knot at S[e1] falls outside entry e1 - 1 (`entryKnots` takes knots before `m1 - 1e-3`, :2042), and the
sweep stops after node e1. The T-4153 + T-4262 interaction goes with it. T-4153 is unpublished (c002): no changelog item.

### T-4262: `putLane` and `changedRows` (gone)

T-4274 rewrote it: `putLane` is `putTrain("lane")`, which writes through `putSlots` with `packLane` (defaults 1, 0, 1e9)
on every write, and `differs` takes a source shorter than a key's slot, or present on one side only, as changed
(trainSlots.ts:267, :270). `changedRows` no longer reads the lane table.
