# T-3241: rebuild a stretch between two stations to another level

Players via Anita, 2026-11-11: the rebuild tool would not take the one piece between two stations (her
screenshot: a French player's 4.3 km piece between Roissy-en-Brie and Ozoir-la-Ferriere, to -2) to another
level. Issue shown: "The platform would be on a ramp" (`PlatformNotLevel`).

## Cause

`plan_edges` (sim/src/track/wasm/rebuild.rs, shared by the rebuild tool and the blueprint level buttons) moves a
node only when all its track is picked, and a constructed station only when it is picked too. So with just the
middle piece picked, both stations stayed at 0 and the piece ramped down from each of them inside itself. A
ramp is centred between two vertices of different levels: with a corner near the station the ramp starts at the
station, on the platform, so the piece was refused. A piece with no corners has no level of its own and was
refused outright (its ends could not move).

## Rule now (Anita's decision, details mine; DECISIONS.md 2026-11-11)

- **Every station on the picked track takes the new level, picked or not**, and is charged as rebuild charges a
  station (built new at the new level). Before, a station left out of the pick refused the pieces at it; that
  case now just moves (two Rust tests changed to say so).
- **A station at an end of the pick ramps on the track beyond it**, outside the pick (`beyond`, `ramp_in`):
  - The ramp is 200 m a level (4%, `RAMP_PER_LEVEL`) and starts `PAST_PLATFORM` = 2 m past the platform's end
    (half the platform at a through station). So -2 from ground with a 160 m platform: the change reaches
    80 + 2 + 400 = 482 m into the next piece; +1: 282 m.
  - It is held either by two new vertices with no turn on a straight (as `bridge.rs` places a bridge's ramps;
    the corners either side get their fitted radius set so the shape stays), or by setting the next piece's
    corners up to some corner to the new level (the ramp then sits centred on the leg after). All candidates
    are fitted; the one whose change reaches least far wins, the shape checked to within 0.25 m and 0.05 m of
    length. On high-speed track the new vertices get the 320 km/h design radius: an auto radius would leave a
    1 cm curve of 1,796 m from the millimetre grid, a 160 km/h limit (the Rust test caught it).
  - Where the next piece is too short, it and the joint after it go to the new level whole and the ramp runs on,
    joint after joint, up to `BEYOND_PIECES` = 8 pieces.
  - Track that runs on to another station that also moves, or back into the pick, moves whole: no ramp down
    and back up between two moved stations. A short stub to a loose end moves whole with its end. A station at
    a dead end (a terminus) just moves.
  - **No room**: a junction, a station that stays, constructed track in a blueprint pick, a crossover, or 8
    pieces without room. The station stays, and the picked piece is planned as before (it may still ramp inside
    itself where its corners allow, which is Anita's other option, "rebuild just the segment in the middle with
    ramps"); if it is refused, the issue is `Geom:RampTooShort` ("Not enough room to change level. Each level up
    or down needs 200 m of ramp.") instead of the platform one. A problem the trial finds on the track beyond (a
    crossing, ground over water) blames the picked piece at that station, and is the one reported if it stays.
  - Not when the track type changes family (rail to tram): those stretches already move whole.
- **Price and display**: the track beyond is charged as built new over the stretch that changes only
  (`stretch_price`: its track over that stretch, `track_cost_t` on a shifted copy, and its road crossings), not
  the whole piece. The quote lists it after the changed ids as `edge, from node, metres` triples (header
  unchanged). The app adds it to the hover's length and the question's ("Rebuild 5.3 km of built track and 2
  built stations ..."), and draws it in the pick's band on the map, cut to the metres that change
  (`blueprintBox.ts` `stretchOf`). No new or changed strings.
- **Shared stations**: a station has at most two track ends, so every line through it runs through the moved
  station and over the ramp; lines that only use the track beyond keep running, deeper at that stop. Nothing
  else special.
- Unchanged: picks with no station at their ends, and ends mid-track (a joint with unpicked track), behave as
  before (the T-3087 tests pass as they were).

## Not done

A ramp is never laid on a curve: `bridge.rs` cuts an arc into arcs of its own radius to hold a ramp there, and
`ramp_in` could borrow `slice` for it. Where the track beyond leaves the station on a curve, the ramp waits for
the next straight (in the browser check, 650 m past the station instead of 482 m). Worth doing only if players
build stations on curves often.

## Known wart

Rebuilding the same stretch again (say -2 then +1) leaves the first ramp's two no-turn vertices in the piece
beyond; the second ramp uses them if that is shortest. Harmless (no turn, no speed limit); the inspector shows
them as corners.
