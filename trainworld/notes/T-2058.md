## 1. Catalogue

Scratch `diag-page.js` (agent scratch t2058) walks every line's band profile and lists each sideways
move (consecutive knots changing the same way), with its cause, length, and how steep it looks at each
zoom: the angle of a shift of d line widths over L metres, `atan(d * linePx(z) / (L / metresPerPx(z)))`.
A move of 15 degrees or more is a visible kink. The track's own turn over the move is recorded too.

Tokyo save (65 lines, 380 shared edges): 578 moves; kinks 97 / 56 / 42 at zoom 13 / 14 / 15, 55 of
the z14 ones on straight track. Out-and-back bumps within 250 m: real 641, Tokyo 72.

**The one cause under all of them: ground-fixed ease lengths for screen-fixed shifts.** A band's shift
is in line widths (pixels on screen), its ease in metres: 30 m at a node (`BAND_RAMP_M`), 80 m along
the track (`CORRIDOR_EASE_M`). A line width is 46 m of ground at zoom 13 and 15 m at zoom 15, so a
one-slot change (0.8 line widths) over 30 m is a 50-60 degree jog at zoom 13, and over 80 m about 25
degrees. Every rule that changes a shift therefore shows as a jog:
- **a track comes beside or leaves** (corridors, T-1848): the ribbon re-centres, so every line in it
  steps, including lines whose own track runs straight on (Newark, `before-real-newark-z14.png`: three
  steps on straight track at stations where tracks join the bundle);
- **order changes**: on the real save each line is its own track 4 m from the next; tracks within 6 m
  are ordered by line number, wider by their place on the ground, so a spacing crossing 6 m, or a
  track joining, reshuffles bands; the yellow line at Kew Gardens jumps across nine bands in 80 m
  (`before-real-kew-z15.png`);
- **a line joins or leaves**: the others close up over 30 m at the node (`before-real-queens-z15.png`,
  the notch at Woodside where the Port Washington lines leave);
- **the ribbon's middle follows the tracks' spacing**: small (0.24 line widths) but many, wobbles
  where tracks splay round platforms;
- **station boxes**: through a box the bands are squeezed to its width (`u_cap`), a pinch at each box
  edge (Kew, Jamaica); unchanged by the prototype;
- **zoom**: bands fold into one stroke at about zoom 10 and close onto the real tracks from about
  16.5. Both happen across zoom, not along the track, so they are not jogs; left as they are.

## 2. Ideas

A. **Long S-curves, added together** (prototyped, `bandsSmooth`). Every change of shift becomes a
   smoothstep 250 m long for a ribbon of two lines, 50 m more per line beyond, at most 800 m; changes
   closer than that are summed into one curve, so short runs no longer make steps. All lines of a
   ribbon take the same length at the same place, so they move together. Gives: no kinks at zoom
   14-15, few at 13. Costs: lines still drift sideways where nothing turns, only slowly (a close-up
   over 300 m); order changes become long diagonal crossings, still crossings. Cheap (lineBands only).
B. **Anchored ribbons**: solve each ribbon's offset from its track over the network (least squares)
   so lines going on together through a node keep their band and the line that turns off takes the
   change; ribbons may sit off-centre on their track. Gives: no drift for through lines, the
   real-world look where a branch peels off a straight trunk. Costs: a two-pass build (collect, solve,
   then knots), a few hundred lines, a weight rule for who is "going straight". On top of A.
C. **One order per line everywhere** (LOOM-style): a global rank, from the parting rule summed over
   the network, used in every ribbon instead of each ribbon's ground order. Gives: bands swap only
   where tracks really cross, so Kew's jump and most of the real save's 643 order changes go. Costs:
   bands may sit on the far side of their own track along a corridor and swap at its end. Needs A.
D. **Shift in the shader in screen space**: give each stroke vertex its metres along the line and
   the line's steps in a texture, and ease each over a length in pixels, so a change is the same
   slope at every zoom (A is exact near zoom 13 and lazier at 15-16). Costs: an attribute, a texture,
   strokes cut finer near changes. A refinement of A, not needed unless zoom 16 drifts look slow.
E. **No shifting at all**: one stroke per shared track, the lines as colour stripes along it (a dash
   per line in turn). Never jogs; a line is harder to follow along a busy trunk. Not prototyped.

My pick: A now (it is the bulk of the fix), then C, then B if drifts still show.

## 3. Prototype: `?perfTry=bandsSmooth`

`map/lineBands.ts` `setBandMode("smooth")`, set in the clock worker from the init message
(`bandMode`, `bandTry()` in perfFlags.ts); `smoothKnots` replaces the knot building per line: each
run's pieces are steps (each piece one value), each step a smoothstep sampled at 9 knots, centred on
the gap, or at a node between ribbons of different sizes starting at the node on the side with fewer
lines (stage 4's rule). Trains, picking and the Traffic view read the same knots (`sampleBand`
unchanged); the Traffic view's recipe per knot is its piece's, so a Traffic ease mixes two recipes
linearly between knots rather than exactly (fine for looking).

| | kinks z13 | z14 | z15 | bumps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| real save, now / smooth | 873 / 337 | 539 / 35 | 243 / 0 | 641 / 169 |
| Tokyo, now / smooth | 97 / 13 | 56 / 0 | 42 / 0 | 72 / 26 |

(The count cuts a summed curve into pieces at each change of direction, so zoom 11-12 counts go up;
at those zooms a move is 1-2 px.)

Seen: Jamaica's 17-line ribbon flows round without steps (`pair-real-jamaica-z14.png`); Newark's three
steps are gone, the remaining crossings there are order changes (C); the Woodside notch is a smooth
split (`pair-real-queens-z15.png`); Ueno's sidestep on the Tokyo save is a long gentle bend
(`pair-tokyo-ueno-z15.png`); Kew's yellow line still crosses the ribbon, now as a long slant.
At Tamachi a track leaving the corridor now drifts away over 250 m with a gap opening.

## 4. Muted lines (T-1873's alternative)

`?perfTry=muteThin`: with a line or group picked, the other lines are drawn solid at 45% of their
width (casing as before); `muteThinPale`: the same, their colour mixed halfway to the casing colour.
Trains and buses keep the 55% dimming. Pictures: `mute-over-queens-z13.png`,
`mute-over-midtown-z14.png`, `mute-bands-jamaica-z14.png` (now, muteThin, muteThinPale side by side).
Both remove the worm; the pale one keeps the picked line the only strong colour.
