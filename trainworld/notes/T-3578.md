# T-3578 A line's fare multiple scales the base too (with T-3581, undo keeps it)

Agent farebase, 2026-11-17, tree `.tw-trees/farebase` (branch tw/farebase). Not archived: a fresh checker tries
to break it first (repro at the bottom).

## The rule

Anita, 2026-11-17: "i'd rather scale the base too actually, like if a person's ride uses that line at all it gets
charged the higher base. i guess if there are lots of legs, we use the max of them."

A trip pays the base times the largest own multiple (`Fares.lines`) among the lines it rides (1.00 when none is
set), plus the part above the base as before (times each line's kind multiplier times its own, by km). Only the
own multiple scales the base; the high-speed, bus and ferry multipliers still scale only the part above it.

## Where the max is taken (advisor consulted before choosing)

Per station pair, in the strategy search: `DestSearch::run_by` (sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs) adds
`base * (M - 1)` to the pair's route cost, beside the curve's extra, where `M` comes from `trip_base_mul`, a
backward pass over the strategy like `trip_km`: a route node is max(own multiple, the rest), a station or change
node the frequency-weighted mean of its departures, 0 at the destination. Every reader of the route costs then
sees it with no change of its own: the everyday and city mode choice (SIMD and scalar), the station-pair choice,
the intercity pass and market, the commutes, the explain panel's pair costs, the planned first wait (`entry`).
Not at the `p.fare_min` sites: those see station pairs, not legs, and a strategy splits riders by frequency, so
there is no one leg list there. Not in the search's edge costs: a max is not additive along a path, and below 1 the
term is negative, which the heap cannot take.

The lie: exact on any single route. Where riders split between lines after riding a line with a multiple, the
pair takes max(that line's, mean of the branches' largest), a little under the mean of each rider's largest.
Choosing between routes inside a strategy does not see the base term (as with the curve's extra); choosing
between station pairs and modes does.

Other paths:
- income-group weighing in the market (`IntercityApi::fare_col`, `FareX::dist`) adds the same term, so groups
  weigh the scaled base;
- the explain panel: each leg of a route carries the route's base multiple (the largest own multiple of its lines;
  `LEG_LEN` 14, `TRIP_LAYOUT`), and the panel's fare is the trip's base times it (`baseFareOf` in game/trip.ts,
  the trip and market panels and their base fare hovers); a leg's fare is its distance fare only, never below 0;
- the ferry fan's composed routes (everyday.rs) add two strategies' costs, so a trip over a fan pays both legs'
  base terms rather than their max (rare; left).

## Income (redone after the check, notes/T-3578-check.md)

The first build put each trip's `base * (M - 1)` into the lines' fare extra by km while the base pool (T-2104) is
shared by boardings and passenger-km network-wide; with every line at 0x, 33 lines went below 0 (line 1
-$17.75M a day). Now:

- the sim keeps the base's part apart: `Loads::rn_base_x` (`board_base`, by plain km), exported as `base_extra(q)`
  (US$, plain `fare_x_usd`, no transfer fares) on all three engines, summed per line as `LineDemand.baseX`;
  `fare_extra` / `fareX` are the curve's extra and transfer fares only, as before T-3578;
- `baseTrips` (game/money.ts, read by money.ts, regionMoney.ts and stretchMoney.ts): when any line's solved fares
  carry an own multiple off 1, the pool becomes max(0, pool + the network's `baseX` in base fares of each line's
  curve), and each line's two weights and its cap (bases a boarding) are times its own multiple. So the network's
  base fares are what demand charged, a line at 0x takes no base, and no share or cap can go below 0. With no own
  multiple the old code runs untouched (bit-identical).

The lie: which line's multiple set a trip's base is not traced; the pool's network-wide shares stand in, as they
already do for the base itself. A free line earns nothing (no base, no distance fare); the bases its changing
riders still pay go to the other lines they ride.

At 1.00 (no own multiple off 1) `rn_base_mul` is empty, every new sim branch is skipped, `rn_base_x` stays 0 and
`baseTrips` takes its old path.

## Plumbing

- sim: `Graph::rn_base_mul` and `base_min` (in `SkimKey` and mem), `put_fares` takes the base multipliers and
  fills `rn_km` for them on a straight curve; `set_base_mul` and `base_extra` on DemandApi, EverydayApi,
  IntercityApi; `Loads::rn_base_x` through the lanes, MSA averaging, stalls, the market's mirror and mem.
- app: `DemandNetwork.baseW` (demandClient, only when an own multiple is off 1), sliced per cluster with
  `fareW` and in the cluster signature, sent with `set_base_mul` in the demand worker; the result's `baseX`
  through demandSums (days, weekend scaling, per line, the packed views) to `LineDemand.baseX`.
- T-3581 (clock.worker.ts): `tidyLineFares` keeps a deleted line's multiple in hand (`goneLineFares`, not saved)
  and gives it back when an undo brings the line back, run on a removeLine, undo or redo edit so that edit's own
  state carries it (before, only the next network for demand tidied, after the state had gone); a new line (add,
  bus, ferry, copy) taking a deleted line's id starts at 1.00x. Cleared on load and new game.
