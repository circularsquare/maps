# T-3628: Tokyo and Kansai fail to load on iPad (2026-11-17, agent ipadload)

Read-only investigation. Cause not found; narrowed to data downloads failing on the device, not memory.

## The message

`statusTag.placeFailed` "{name} could not load" (ja 「{name}を読み込めませんでした」; the player's 「関西(東京)」 means
"Kansai or Tokyo"). Shown by `ui/StatusTag.tsx` when `zoneFailures` (game/loading.ts) has the zone in view. Only
`DemandClient.unreachable()` (workers/demandClient.ts) sets it, from three paths:

1. a city pack's download failed after its retries (`fetchCity` catch: fetch error, non-ok status, or a throw in
   the `.then`, such as an allocation failure copying the bytes);
2. a market pack or the market rows/drives failed to download (`fetchMarket` catch);
3. a worker could not open a pack with a non-panic error (`openRejected`; the first is retried, a second is final
   and never retried, so the tag stays for good).

Retries: 0.5/1.5/4 s per download, then the zone is tried again after 10, 30, 60, 60 ... s while still needed;
`recovered()` clears the tag on the next good download. The reason (`why`) goes only in the tag's hover
(`demandClient.zoneFailed`), and not in the feedback report's `diagnosis()`, so neither report says which path.

A worker running out of memory does not land here: a Rust allocation failure aborts (sim demand/wasm.rs
`last_panic` "out of memory"), which is a WebAssembly.RuntimeError, `isPanic`, so the worker is reported dead and
replaced ("workers ... replaced N"), and the pool stopping shows "Demand could not load" instead.

## What the two reports show

- 17240a38 (iPad, "Chrome 157", so WebKit): `workers 2, replaced 0`, demand solve final, no last trouble;
  clock's last error `water mask for osa not loaded: TypeError: Load failed`.
- e4db8ec6 ("Safari 27.2", device not said): `workers 2, replaced 0`, final; clock's last error
  `world water mask not loaded: TypeError: Load failed`.

`TypeError: Load failed` is WebKit's text for a rejected fetch (network error, or a blocked or CORS-failed load);
Chrome's is "Failed to fetch". `workers 2` is one solving worker plus the aux: the small-device path, so
`mobileDevice()` caught the iPad (it already treats a Macintosh UA with touch as a tablet). No worker died.

"Can't lay track" follows from the water fetch: Construct waits up to 20 s for world.water and the masks of the
zones under the blueprint (`groundForBlueprint`, clock.worker.ts) and refuses with TerrainMissing when one is
missing. So both symptoms are data downloads from data.anita.garden failing, in two different threads (demand
on the page, water in the clock worker).

## Ruled out or weakened

- Memory as the cause: no worker panicked or was replaced in either report; the small-device pool was in use.
- Pack size: tyo.bin 6.4 MB (13th of 4,138), osa.bin 4.2 MB, tyo.market.json 2.2 MB, world.water 0.6 MB. Several
  packs are bigger (mic 11.7, css 10.7). Nothing points to Tokyo or Kansai beyond them being what Japanese players
  open first; neither player says smaller cities work on the iPad.
- Server CORS (2026-11-18 prefix, from here): GET and Range GET answer `access-control-allow-origin: *` on cache
  hit and miss, with or without Origin; the Range preflight allows `range`. Not tested on the older prefix the
  reports used, nor from a Japanese Cloudflare edge.
- A header and .bin mismatched inside one prefix: deploy.py refuses an existing version except `--resume`.
  Unlikely.
- Fetch errors alone are not iPad-only: desktop Chrome reports carry `water mask for mpu/lck/ung not loaded:
  TypeError: Failed to fetch` and `yal ... TimeoutError`. Other Safari reports (18.5, 18.6, 26, 26.2, 26.6) show no
  such error. A bulk listing of the inbox hit the server's 429 rate limit partway, so that sample is incomplete.

## Not known

Why WebKit fetches keep failing long enough to outlast the retries. Candidates, none checked: iOS suspending or
killing the networking process (backgrounding, sleep, memory pressure elsewhere), a content blocker or DNS
blocker (common in Japan), a Cloudflare edge answer without CORS headers. Playwright WebKit on Windows does not use
iOS networking, so it was not tried.

## Smallest next step (diagnostic, not a cure)

- Add each zone failure's `why` (zone id and reason) and the clock's failed ground keys to the feedback report's
  `diagnosis()` line, so the next report says download vs would-not-open and which file.
- Check the status tag's hover is reachable by touch; if not, a tap could show it.
- If the reason turns out to be a fetch error that persists, a retry on `visibilitychange` / `online` for both
  demand packs and ground (and a sooner first broken-retry) would cover the backgrounding case.
