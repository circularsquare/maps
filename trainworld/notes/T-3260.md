## T-3262 track count reset by the track type

Cause: `setTrackType` (game/ui.ts) cleared `singleTrack` on High speed and Tram and nothing put it back, and
`setSingleTrack` on High speed called `setTrackType(2)`. Quad was already kept.

Fix: `countPicked` (a signal in game/ui.ts) holds the count the player picked; High speed and Tram show single as
double and Regular puts single back; `build.tracks` saves the picked count. Single on High speed is refused (the
spec forbids single high-speed track, T-3126): the type stays, the notice says "High-speed track can't be single
track. Pick Regular first." Double has its own setter (`setDoubleTrack`) so pressing it on High speed forgets a
remembered single.

## T-3263 road snap ghost lag and clicks

Measured on Vite, headless, one core (`scratchpad/buildfix/lag.mjs`): a pointer move every 8 ms for 3 s.

| case | ghost updates / median gap / longest gap |
|---|---|
| new game, Manhattan 2 km, roads | 134 / 21 ms / 83 ms |
| new game, ordinary track | 276 / 9 ms / 116 ms |
| Tokyo 9.5 km leg, roads | 68 / 19 ms / 623 ms, main-thread search 79 ms (16,698 states), long task 143 ms |
| real save, clock running, roads | 68 / 26 ms / 233 ms |
| real save, clock running, ordinary | 4 / 264 ms / 1,357 ms |

So at this machine's speed the road snap is no slower than ordinary drawing. The mechanism that makes it laggy
when the track model is slow: every pointer move dropped the spot being fitted (router worker) or priced (clock
worker), so a fitted ghost and its tag landed only once the pointer rested for search + fit + price. The ordinary
ghost queues the newest ask behind the one in flight and keeps updating. With every ghost answer held 150 ms (a
slow track model, a big save): fitted ghosts while the pointer moved for 2 s, **0 before, 5-7 after**.

Fix (`map/streetSnap.ts`): a spot in the fit or price phase finishes and is shown; the newest pointer position is
kept and asked for next. A click asks for its own spot at once (as before).

Not reproduced: clicks not going through. Every click in the repros placed track or was refused for a stated reason
(a tight curve, a crossing on a ramp), and a corner chain round Midtown placed every segment with double track.
With quad track, before T-3261, every chained road segment after the first was refused ("The curve is too tight",
the express pair swinging out next to a road corner at the click), which may be what she saw.

Left alone: the road search runs on the main thread (`BusRoads.tramLeg`), about 1-2 ms for 2 km and 80 ms for a
9.5 km leg in Tokyo; moving it to a worker is the next step if long legs still feel slow.

## T-3261 quad merging to two on snapped routes

Cause: T-423's carry-on (both pairs continuing from a loose quad end, ending apart on open ground) was gated on
`through && pis.is_empty()`; the real-network and road snaps send corners, so their quad routes swung the express
pair out from the start node and merged it back into the end node, a pinch to two at every click. Fix
(`sim/src/track/wasm/draw.rs`): the gate is `quad && route_tangent` (the drawing tool's routes, corners or not);
`express_verts` already offsets every corner between own ends. Quad ending on existing track (a node, or the middle
of a piece) still merges there, per the spec. Before: `t3261-before-roads-ghost.png` (the second quad road segment
red, "The curve is too tight"); after: `check-d-quad-corner.png` (both pairs through the joint).

## T-3260 Ctrl draws straight

Keyboard map: Ctrl was already the track tool's "snap to nothing" (T-1887), "draw by hand" with Snap to roads and
"flip the street snap" with Tram; Ctrl+Z/Y/S/K/F are untouched (key events with Ctrl and a letter). Straight goes
on the same key (DECISIONS.md). `map/tools.ts`: `Placed.dir` (the start's leaving direction: the end heading of the
segment just placed, else the first leg of an ordinary ghost from a start on track); `straight()` moves an
open-ground hover or click onto the line through the start along `dir` while Ctrl is held and no route is snapped.
From open ground every segment is straight already. Ramps are unchanged; no junction, so no flyover.
