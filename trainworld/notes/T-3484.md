## 1. What the code does (confirmed)

- hon is its own country, `HK` (pack header and market row; inc 0.893); Shenzhen is shz, `CN` (17.57M, inc 0.326); Macau
  is mcu, `MO`. borders.json has no `CN|HK` or `CN|MO`, so commutes and everyday outings across both lines take
  `national_default` 0.05 (the gua-cn-guangdong unit's joint_report lists `MO-pack mcu` - `CN-pack ...` at 0.05; hon is
  held fixed in that unit, its own unit hon-hk).
- Regional and long trips (market, `intercity.rs` `border_factor`): 0.26 x origin income^1.5, so 0.219 from Hong Kong
  and 0.048 from Shenzhen; a pair's trips each way are the mean of the two directed values (`market.rs` line ~204).
- Units: joint commutes are commuters (people a weekday, home to work; each makes 2 crossings); cross_everyday pairs are
  outings (each there and back, 2 crossings); market-rows `near` is trips a day each way (1 crossing each).

## 4. Game against real, by kind (Hong Kong - Shenzhen)

| Kind | Game | Real | Game / real |
|---|---|---|---|
| All crossings, weekday | about 595,000 | 542,000 (2025) to 606,000 (last 12 months) at the Shenzhen land ports, plus part of the high-speed line's 78,000-87,000 | about 1.0 |
| Commuters (people) | 71,321 | about 42,000 (2017: 17,300 living in Hong Kong, all mainland; 24,900 living in Shenzhen) | 1.7 |
| - Hong Kong home, Shenzhen work | 49,729 (85,236 to all mainland) | under 17,300 | about 3 (5 to all mainland) |
| - Shenzhen home, Hong Kong work | 21,592 | 24,900 | 0.9 |
| School (outings, both ways) | 2,957 | 28,280 students (53,000 school crossings) | about 0.1 |
| Everything else (everyday less school and university, plus the market) | about 439,000 crossings | about 400,000 (2025 weekday less work, business and school at the 2017 survey's shares) | about 1.1 |

So the 0.05 is not why the corridor looks empty: the game's total across the line is within about 10% of the
Immigration Department's weekday count. What is off is the mix: commutes run the wrong way (the game sends Hong Kong's
workers to Shenzhen's many jobs; in life far more Shenzhen residents work in Hong Kong than the reverse), and the
cross-boundary students (Hong Kong children living in Shenzhen) are mostly missing. The bulk of the game's crossings are
market trips, which reach rail only through the regional layer between zones a station serves.

## 5. What the factor should be

- **Keep CN|HK at 0.05** (an explicit pair entry with this note's check would stop a later "fix"). A symmetric raise to
  MY|SG's 0.5 would multiply the commutes and everyday outings roughly tenfold where the cross share is small (on the
  order of 0.7M commuters and 0.8M outing crossings), putting the line at about 4x the published count.
- To fit both commute directions with one symmetric factor is impossible: matching the total needs about 0.03, matching
  Shenzhen to Hong Kong about 0.05-0.06, Hong Kong to Shenzhen about 0.015-0.02. The general fix is a **commute border
  factor that depends on direction**, higher toward the richer side (workers cross toward higher pay). That is a new
  complicating factor: Anita's call. It would also fix the two cases below.
- School alone could take a pair factor (about 0.5 for school outings from Shenzhen homes to Hong Kong schools gives the
  right order), but that is a per-kind, per-pair rule for one policy quirk; I would leave it.

## 6. Side effects and cousins

- **Macau - Zhuhai** takes the same default and is a different story: the game makes about 146,000 crossings a weekday
  (commuters 23,475 people = 46,950, of them 15,494 Macau residents working in Zhuhai and 7,981 the other way; everyday
  outings 49,262 = 98,524; market 277 each way), against roughly 400,000-470,000 at the Zhuhai land ports, a third or
  more of them mainland tourists from further away (long trips in the game). So the local part is about 2x low, and the
  commute direction is reversed again (Macau's foreign workforce lives largely in Zhuhai; not checked against DSEC).
  A symmetric CN|MO raise would inflate the wrong direction too; the directional factor fits it.
- **MY|SG 0.5** shows the same fault: the game sends 171,067 Singapore residents to work in Johor Bahru and 173,716 the
  other way (market-rows.json). The published 250,000-400,000 is Johor to Singapore only; Singapore to Johor commuting is
  small. T-1738's check counted the pair's total, not its direction.
