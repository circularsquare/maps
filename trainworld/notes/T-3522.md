# T-3522 bus boarding cost 6 to 4 perceived minutes

Anita, 2026-11-17: drop it from 6 to 4; no ceiling tests (rail is not touched); measure a save or two.

## Change
One constant: `BUS_BOARD_MIN` in `sim/src/demand/kernel/access.rs` (6.0 to 4.0). It feeds `Graph::rn_bus`
and `rn_left`; nothing in the app, pipeline or trip explorer holds its own copy (they read the graph). Tests use the
constant, so none pinned 6. Docs changed: SPEC.md, docs/demand-kernel.md, docs/demand-plain.md (bus by walk
and bus "about 24" is now "about 22"), docs/demand-worldwide.md. The wiki states no bus figure.

## Measurement
`checks/t1880-bus-feeders.mjs` on the real save (T-007-real-nyc.save, which has no bus lines: baseline all == rail,
identical before and after), one bus line added at a time, weekday, game dollars a day. master = main tree Vite
(6 min), after = worktree Vite (4 min). Raw: artifacts/trainworld/t3522/{master,after}.json.

Bus riders +7 to +14%. Rail trips of the whole save are 12.68M a day; the buses add 60 to 300 more rail trips with
the cheaper boarding (feeders), under 0.003% of the total, so the rail share (rail trips of rail plus car) does not
move visibly. The Throgs Neck bus goes from losing money to breaking even.
