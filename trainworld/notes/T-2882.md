# T-2882: two Sydney spots a bus could not cross

Read from the z14 tiles. Bus-class end to next road gap, levels, seams and the motorway rule were not the cause at either spot.

**Glebe (The Crescent and Nelson Street).** Nelson Street's north end joins The Crescent through a 23 m `class=service` piece with no `service=` tag. Buses could not use service roads, so the 177 m leg took 518 m. Fixed by the link rule: a plain service road (no `service=` subtype, no `access`, ground level) at most 60 m long with each end on a street is drivable and not snappable. The leg is now 191 m.

**College Street and Oxford Street.** The eastward minor road ends about 24 m short of Oxford Street. In the tile the only lines across that gap are footways. There is no road to follow, so this is a data limit. The OSM query for the spot failed (502), so its exact tags are not confirmed. Short alleys as links do not change the leg (484 m). Every service road as a bus road gives 135 m, through the block rather than across the gap.

Bridging such gaps by distance is not safe: of the road dead ends in one central tile, 30 of 61 (Sydney), 31 of 43 (Seoul) and 48 of 65 (London) have another street within 30 m. Those ends were counted and not read one by one; a street closed to cars at its end would look the same to such a rule.
