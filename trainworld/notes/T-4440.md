# T-4440: The first load's card shrank and can't be moved or closed

Her 11-27, on 8800 (snapshot 797e9310a, which has T-4346): "when we made the load bunny train animation changes to make it
movable/dismissable we seem to have made it a lot smaller horizontally / less margin, which i dont want. also it doesnt
actually seem to be movable or dismissable. theres no x in the corner. and i cant drag it to the side."

## Why the card shrank (a bug, fixed)

- T-4346 (85df2231a) put the card inside `.loading-card-pos`, the unzoomed box a drag places in window px. The card's
  `min-width: min(260px, 100%)` then took its 100% from that box, whose own width is shrink-to-fit around the card. That
  is a cyclic percentage, so the 260 px floor never applied and the card hugged its widest line. Before T-4346 the card
  was the grid item itself and 100% was the map column's width.
- "Less margin" is the same cause: only the 20 px side padding was left between the text and the edge. The vertical
  layout never changed.
- Fix (`ui/style.css`): the floor moved to the wrapper, `min-width: min(calc(260px * var(--ui, 1)), 100%)` and
  `max-width: 100%`, and the card fills it. Measured at 1400x900 (UI scale 0.9): master 162 px, fixed 234 px
  (260 x 0.9, as before T-4346), and still 234 px while dragged.

## Why she saw no x and couldn't drag (the stage gate, working as built)

- `LoadingCard` (`ui/LoadingScreen.tsx`): `canDismiss = up && world.value && load.step === STAGE_TEXT.demand`. The
  x and the drag exist only in the card's third stage, "Calculating trips". In "Loading" and "Loading city data" the
  card takes no input (a press pans the map). The gate was the T-4346 agent's choice: her 11-25 ask named no stage
  ("having the initial load panel ... be draggable to the side or dismissable with an x").
- Stage times measured (own Vite 6119, 2 cores, 1 demand worker, card polled every 40 ms):
  - New NYC game, three loads: "Loading" 2-7 s, "Loading city data" 6-16 s, then the card closes. It never showed
    "Calculating trips": a new game has no lines, so its trips are ready as soon as its data is.
  - A 22-line Tokyo player save (`checks/fixtures/t3302-tokyo-day60.save`) opened as a file: "Loading" 1-2 s,
    "Loading city data" 6-12 s, then "Calculating trips" with the x until the end (14-43 s).
  - A load that ends at a cached result (T-328, T-2574) spends `CACHE_SHOW_MS` = 1.5 s in "Calculating trips".
- So in a new game the x never shows, and in a reload whose result is cached it shows for 1.5 s. Not widened (brief):
  her call. If she wants it earlier, dropping the stage test from `canDismiss` covers "Loading city data" (the top tag
  already has "Loading data" for that stage, `StatusTag.tsx`); the first "Loading" stage, before the network is in,
  would need a look.

## Two smaller gaps, fixed

- Only the header (the bunny and the stage name) dragged. A press on the bar or the row under it was taken by the card
  and did nothing (master: moved 0, 0). Now the whole card drags, except the x. The header div is gone, so the markup
  is the old card plus the x.
- The x was a 28x22 box (`.btn.icon`'s width beat `.loading-card-close`'s) in the muted grey. It is now 22x22 in the
  text colour, like the guide's x. Its box sits 6 px from the top and right edges, and the glyph is level with the
  bunny's roof, about 44 px clear of it.
