# T-567: history across sessions

The money ledgers (network and per region, from T-531) were already in the save header, so a reload
kept the money history; T-565 adds `rides` to every entry and the same header carries it. Nothing else
in the UI is a history chart. Entries are rounded on save (money to US$1, rides to a tenth).

Size on the real save (T-007-real-nyc.save, 75.3 KB gzipped with no history), synthetic entries with
real-looking magnitudes, `T-567-size.mjs` (argument `1` rounds as the game does):

| History | full precision | rounded as saved |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days + 48 hours, one region | +6.6 KB | +3.9 KB |
| 60 days + 48 hours, one region | +8.7 KB | +5.3 KB |
| 60 days + 48 hours, three regions | +15.9 KB | +9.4 KB |
| 60 days + 48 hours, six regions | +25.5 KB | +14.9 KB |

"One region" is the network ledger plus one region's. The ledger keeps 30 days (`LEDGER_DAYS`);
60 days is a figure for raising it. An Osaka test game (9 days, 2 regions) saves 1,386 bytes in all.
