# T-3509: trips within one zone count in its figures

Agent tripswithin, 2026-11-18, tree .tw-trees/tripswithin (branch tw/tripswithin). Anita, 2026-11-18:
"yes, all trips within should count".

## What was missing

The market (regional layer) has trips between two 6 km zones of one zone: the corridor table's
diagonal row (`from === to`). Nothing counted them in a zone figure or a bubble, though they ride the
player's lines. On the real New York save, average day, the diagonal holds:

- longer trips (25 km and more apart): 2,254,748 legs, 150,727 by rail;
- long commutes (T-1000's 50-150 km set share) whose job is in the home's own zone: 133,415 legs,
  45,740 by rail. The joint gravity puts these in the market only (zones.md "Long commutes are set"),
  so they were in no figure either and count too ("all trips within").

(T-3503's 1.93M was a weekday figure from the market rows; the 2.25M here is the save's average day.)

## The rule (no new one)

- **Figure**: a zone's Trips within = its local legs + the diagonal's legs (longer + commutes); rail
  the local rail + the diagonal's; walking and No route the local ones; the rest driving (the plane's
  share of the diagonal is 0 in New York and London and would count as driving, as in the bubbles).
  So its train share, its goals (average day), the top bar's goal number, the Cities tab's share and
  rides, the city view and the zoomed-out bubble (L + R, L now with the diagonal) all read it.
- **Bubbles**: the diagonal's legs as the zone's trips with other places already count (T-3503): a
  longer trip under Homes where it starts and under Destinations where it ends (All purposes only), a
  commute at the home and at the job (and under Work). So Homes = L' + R_leaving, Destinations =
  L' + R_arriving, with L' the new Trips within. Advisor consulted; it agreed no new rule is needed.
- **Before the first line** there is no market, so the figure and bubbles are local only, as T-3503.
