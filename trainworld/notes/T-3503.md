# T-3503: demand bubbles include trips between cities

Agent bubbles, 2026-11-16. Stage 1: design, nothing built. Builds on T-3500 (`airLegs`,
`airport_legs_day`, `airportAcross`, `selectionSquares`' `air`), read in the airhover tree.

## What the zone figures count today

- **Street zoom** (above 8.5): `cellModes` is the zone's own local layer only (commutes x 2 plus the
  everyday layer's kept share). Homes adds up to the zone's local trip legs `L`, Destinations to `L`
  too, Total to `2L` (both ends of every local leg are in the zone).
- **Zoomed out** (8.5 and below) and the city inspector: `L` plus `R`, the corridor table's legs with
  an end in the zone (`intercityView.ts` `totalsOf`/`pairs`, `CityInspector` "To other places"). The
  table (`Market::corridors_of`) holds every pair of places that have a zone, open or not: longer
  trips both ways (2t a pair) and commutes between places (2 legs a commuter; the rows' `commute`
  numbers are commuters). It leaves out three parts of the regional layer:
  1. **Everyday outings across a zone line** (T-1175, T-2896 airports): in the market only, never in a
     zone's figure. Arnavutkoy's 109k airport legs from across its line (T-3485) are in no zone's
     total, so its plane says 111k (T-3500) while its zone figure has none of them.
  2. **Longer trips within one place** (the corridor's diagonal, 6 km zones of one zone 25 km+ apart):
     New York 1.93M legs a day (2.8% of its 68.8M local), London 0.60M, Philadelphia 0.25M; median
     0.13% of local.
  3. **Elsewhere** (trips with places that have no zone): New York up to 1.15M each way.

## The rule

A regional leg has one end in the zone, a local leg both. So, with `R_home` + `R_dest` = `R`:

- **Homes** adds up to `L + R_home`: the regional legs leaving the zone's homes, the way a selection's
  far end counts them (`IntercityApi::selection`): longer trips starting here, commutes of the
  people living here to other places, their everyday outings across the line.
- **Destinations** adds up to `L + R_dest`: longer trips arriving, commutes of people working here,
  outings coming here (airports' at their terminals).
- **Total** adds up to `2L + R`.
- **Zoomed out** the zone bubble stays `L + R` under either button, so Homes and Destinations step by
  about `R / 2` at zoom 8.5 (New York about 1.4%, invisible; a small zone beside a big one about
  10%). That is the honest form of "add up to the zone's local plus intercity totals": per end, each
  leg counted at the end that is in the zone. The literal alternative (every regional leg in both
  Homes and Destinations, so each sums to `L + R`) puts arriving visitors on the homes and breaks a
  selected bubble's far end adding up to its own count, so I recommend against it.
- `R` gains part 1 (everyday outings across, both ends), so the overview, its hover's inter-city
  line and "To other places" (a walking part added to its bar) agree with the bubbles. Parts 2 and
  3 stay out of both bubbles and figures; part 2 is T-3509, filed (it needs a call on whether it counts in
  "Trips within", which the goals read).
- Under the purpose filter each purpose takes its own regional part: Work the commutes between
  places, School, Errands, Leisure, Medical and Airports their outings across the line (Airports is
  T-3500's, folded into the one query), Attractions none (they do not cross); longer trips only under
  All purposes. The Purpose colouring then puts commutes across in work, outings in their groups and
  longer trips in "everything else" without change (`attachPurp` takes other = total - work - errands).
- Always on, no setting. Before the game has a line no regional market is composed (T-3500), so a
  fresh game's bubbles and overview are both local only until the first line, as the overview is now.

## Where the trips come from and how they are spread

The selection's own arithmetic, so a clicked bubble's added trips equal its far end's regional part:

- **Squares and weights**, static per zone pack: the worker that holds the zone (the aux worker for a
  zone open bare, as `bareSquares`) gives each cell its 600 m square and its share of the square by
  `cell_commuters` at each end (`selectionSquares` with every cell picked; a square with no weight
  at an end shares evenly over its cells so nothing is lost). Kept client side per zone and frame.
- **Per square legs** (new `IntercityApi::zone_legs_day(market, squares, day, kinds)`): each square's
  site, its 6 km zone `z`, and the site's share of `z` by people (`pop / zz.pop`), attraction
  (`jobs / zz.w_attr`) and jobs (`job_w`) as `selection` weighs them. `z`'s legs per category,
  all modes: longer trips with other places' zones (`zone_row`, same place dropped) and with places
  not open (`zone_place_eachway`), per period by `share_out` and the rush-day lean; commutes from
  `c_home` / `c_work`; outings from `e_home` / `e_act` by kind. Airports at the destination end are
  spread over the cells by their own airport legs as T-3500 does (its terminals weighting), not by
  attraction. Answer: per square, home end legs, rail, walking, then the same at the destination end.
- **Rail** per round from each market part (`zone_legs_rail_day`), from the solved pairs' `pair_rail`,
  `c_frac` and `e_frac` only (no `zone_row` walk), as `selection_rail_day`'s.
- **Client**: per cell, square legs x the cell's share, added to blocks 0-2 (home) and 3-5
  (destination): rail, walking, driving = legs - rail - walking (car and plane both; regional trips
  have no No route part). One wrapper after `cellModes`, `purposeModes` and `bareModes`, for the
  focused and nearby zones alike; `airportAcross` goes into it.

## Cost

- `zone_row` walks every open zone per 6 km zone: New York's pack has 795, against a few thousand
  open zones in a Northeast game, so 1-3M pair evaluations. Measured first thing in stage 2 on the real
  save; the all-mode legs depend on the market composition and the day only, so they are worked out
  once per composition and day in each solving worker and kept (a few floats per 6 km zone: New
  York about 795 x 12 x 8 bytes, 76 KB), not per round. Rail per round walks the solved pairs only.
- Messages: the squares (about a third of the cells) times 6 f64 per worker per ask; New York about
  1.5 MB, short-lived, smaller than `purposeModes`' 8 floats a cell from every worker.
- No flicker: sizes come from the kept all-mode legs, so they do not wait on a round; a round's rail
  arrives with the bubbles as now (asked with them, `BY_ROUND`), and until a zone's rail answer is
  in, its regional rail counts as driving (colour only).
- Overview part 1: a per-place array of outings across a line (legs at either end, rail, walking)
  in each period's market result beside `elsewhere`, a weekend scaling them by errands' factor as
  `marketPartsOn` does; 3 floats a place.

## What else reads the bubbles' legs (each changes with them)

- The bubble selection inspector's totals (`commuterView` from the cell legs) take the regional part;
  its purpose rows and pie are local only, so they gain each purpose's regional part from the
  selection's own `icSel` answer (already asked for the far end) and longer trips a row of their own.
- The station tool's nearby legs tag (`tools.ts` `askLegs`) takes them too, which is right: regional
  riders walk to stations the same way.
- The cached bubbles beside a save (T-328) are stored with the regional part, so a reload shows them
  at once; `t328-cache-check` and `t325-bare-check` compare bubbles to the bit and are run.
- Airport and attraction marks read `cellModes("airport")` / `("attractions")`: airports keep
  T-3500's figure through the shared query (not added twice), attractions none.

## What a player would see (expected; all modes, weekday, measured in stage 2)

From the market rows with no solve (scratchpad `bubbles/regshare.py`): the regional legs with other
places are a median 4% of a zone's local legs, so most zones' bubbles grow a few percent, the most
along a zone line facing a bigger neighbour.

| Zone | Local legs | Regional legs with other places (longer + commutes) | Homes or Destinations grows by about |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York (real save) | 68.8M | 0.93M + 0.53M, plus far places with zones | 1-2% |
| Philadelphia | 18.8M | 0.74M + 0.58M | 3-4% |
| Mettmann (beside Dusseldorf) | 0.97M | 87k + 206k | 15% |
| Lake County (Illinois) | 1.82M | 163k + 288k | 12% |
| Arnavutkoy (Istanbul Airport) | 0.73M | 47k + 83k, plus 109k airport outings | Destinations about 25%, most on the airport |

Plus each zone's everyday outings across its lines (not in the rows; measured in stage 2). The bubble
over Istanbul Airport under All purposes goes from about 4k to about 111k, as its plane's hover.
