# T-1654: the load card's time left (2026-10-28)

Own Vite on 6131, headless Chrome, 2 demand workers (4-core claim), save imported into a slot then picked from the
start menu; the card sampled every second. JSON per load in `../artifacts/trainworld/t1654/` (`fin-*` the final
code, `base-*` before). Scratch scripts (measure, analyze, replay over a trace) were in the agent's scratchpad.

## What was wrong

"Taking a bit longer" showed when the last round ran past its expected length (nothing expected left), or when the
count fell far behind a raised estimate. The Japan loads here did not show it (1291-1374 s, rounds 361 / 330 / 303 /
294 s, so later rounds are 0.9 of the first, not the guessed 0.5), but a slower last round does.
A first load's estimate was 0.4-0.65 of the actual until round 1 had landed.

## What changed

- `solveStage` moved to game/loadBar.ts. A cluster solved ahead keeps no landing times, so jobProgress
  now notes when each round's first period lands (`job.firstLand`, to 0.1 s).
- A round's length is its first landing times the waves' ratio (previous later round's, else waves - 0.4); the rounds after it are
  the mean of the later rounds so far with it. A round past that: as long again per wave landed, 0.7 of it for the last wave.
- `late` and `countBehind` are removed; `nextLeft` jumps up over 1.4x, else slows to half speed; text "About N min left".
