# T-3421: high-speed auto-ramps over water at 160, not 320 (agent bugs2, 2026-11-16)

Not reproduced; no fix. Tried in sim tests (wasm/bridge_tests.rs, kept as checks):
- high-speed, target 320, free end across a river, straight and curved, several widths: every piece keeps 320
  (`a_high_speed_bridge_keeps_its_design_speed`).
- a traced real high-speed line (7,500 m curve) across water in the world frame, three water layouts: stays above 300
  (`a_traced_high_speed_line_over_water_keeps_its_speed`).
So the bridge/ramp itself does not cap speed (grades never change speed; track kind cap is 320).
What does give 160: (1) the Build tab's target max speed at 160 (switching to high-speed track raises it to 320, and
switching off restores the old value, ui.ts designBeforeHsr; a player who set the target after, or loads a save/setting with
design 160 and hsr on, draws 160 curves); (2) clicked corners over 160 are not set to the design radius (draw.rs: "the corners
made here, not the clicked ones"), so a route with clicked corners (real snap, street snap) keeps auto radius capped at
1,796 m = 160 km/h, but only a real-line trace sets wide radii; (3) a bridge on a corner whose radius is auto.
Next: ask the reporter for the track inspector's limit reason on the slow stretch (why 0..3: trains, ordinary track,
curves, crossover) and whether the track was drawn with snap on. If "curves", it is (1) or (2); then the fix is a
proposal for her: set design radius on clicked corners at design above 160 (changes curves players get).
