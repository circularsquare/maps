# T-2410 A new line skipped the local solve (2026-11-04, agent solveend)

Built in tree t1734 with T-1734 (`demandClient.ts` `serviceFromSnapshot`, `keyOf`, `setAlone`;
`?perfOff=alone` restores the old network).

## Cause

The line itself was never the problem. A player (and `buildLine` in checks/lib.mjs) builds the
stations first and the line on them a moment later. On China the clock worker's snapshots of the two
came apart (v5 stations, v6 the line, about 2 s); on New York the same happened with a 3 s pause
between them. The stations-only network differs from the last solve in the cluster's network (its
stations, and the market part's view, which is the whole cluster), but in no city population's view
(stations on no line are in no line's span): `editOf` found nothing fresh, so the cluster went
straight to its whole solve, which sets every population on the new network. Before T-1734 the line's
network then cancelled that whole solve and found every population set on v5 and unsolved, so nothing
was frozen: whole solve, status step 0/1, "constructing..." to the end (T-2386's China case). With
T-1734 alone the line waits for the stations' whole solve to land, then gets its local solve on top.

T-2386's New York "new line" (Midtown, -73.98, 40.75) was in fact no line: its east end falls on
the East River, the line never ran, and what was measured was the stations' whole solve.

## Fix

A station no line stops at changes no figure, so demand leaves it out of its network: `serviceFromSnapshot`
keeps only stations some line stops at (and passages between them), and puts the rest in `alone`,
outside the key (`keyOf`). Building stations then changes no key and starts no solve; their positions
still reach zones (`nearOf`), so a zone's packs open ahead of the line as before (`setAlone` updates
the network in hand and works its reach out again). The line then comes as one edit against the last
solve and is solved locally.
