# T-3777: Nagoya's JR Tokaido Main Line reversals (agent realjapan)

Cause: OSM's two JR Tokaido Main Line relations (Tokyo-Kobe, route master 11680904) list their stop members out of line order (Tokyo, Mishima, Bentenjima, Araimachi, Futagawa, Nishi-Kozakai ... Atami, Mikawa-Miya ...): 2,700 and 3,300 km of hops for 560 km of track. The auto build trusts member order, so the line ran back and forth (12 reversals on 2 lines).
Fix (pipeline/realauto.py `along_order`, called in `cut`): a relation whose member order is 2.5 times longer than its stops sorted along its own chained ways gets its stops sorted along the ways (only when 90% of stops lie within 300 m of the chain). Threshold 1.5 also reordered Beijing, Chengdu, Guangzhou, Istanbul, Taipei relations that were fine or arguable (Guangzhou gained a reversal, Istanbul T1 got worse), so 2.5.
Result: Nagoya 12 reversals on 2 lines -> 0. Reordered relations at 2.5, over the 34 auto cities: Nagoya 2, Osaka 2, no others. Osaka 12 -> 8 reversals (165 -> 163 lines, two duplicates merged). Reshipped ngo and osa only; the rest unchanged (extract identical).
Remaining after: Fukuoka 11 reversals on 8 lines (other cause, not member order: relations between 1x and 2.5x ratio; not investigated), Hiroshima 1, Tokyo 3, Beijing S1 1, Osaka 8, Sapporo 0, Sendai 0.
Note: the Tokaido relations have no Nagoya station stop, so the line skips Nagoya station itself (OSM data).
Count script: scratchpad revcount.mjs (counts lines with partway reversals from lineStats after load).
Run: REALNET_OUT=<scratch dir>, REALSAVE_EXE=<scratch realsave>; `realauto.py build ngo osa`, `ship ngo osa`.
