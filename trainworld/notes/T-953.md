# T-953: the map's WebGL on other devices and browsers

After T-952 (the Pixel 10's PowerVR compiler rejected an array constructor), an audit of every
shader and GL call in `app/src/map/` (renderer.ts, gradeMarks.ts, demandOverlay.ts, overlay.ts) and
the MapLibre custom layer, against the usual mobile, Safari and Firefox pitfalls. Line numbers are
as of 2026-10-22, after the fixes.

### 1. Context loss (fixed)

Before: neither of our canvases called `preventDefault` on `webglcontextlost`, so the browser never
restored them; MapLibre restores its own style but drops custom layers, so `tw-camera` was gone and
the overlay never saw the camera again; and realNetwork.ts's `style.load` handler re-added its
pmtiles sources, which the restored style already had: an uncaught "Source tw-real-us already
exists". After a loss and restore in headless Chrome the map was blank apart from the DOM labels
(`artifacts/trainworld/t953-before-d3d11-bubbles-restored.png`). A game had to be reloaded.

Now: both canvases take the loss; on `webglcontextrestored` the renderer's `restore()` compiles its
programs again and replays what it was last given (the network, demand layers, full track, preview,
highlight; trips re-upload from `tripsBy` on the next draw), GradeMarks forgets its old buffers in
`init`, and the demand canvas makes every disc and line set again from the data each keeps. Sets
made while the context is lost are kept as data only. The overlay skips drawing while lost (the
clock still ticks), `tw-camera` is added again on every `style.load`, and realNetwork skips layers
the style already has.

Checked with `WEBGL_lose_context` on all three canvases: after restore the map, network, trains,
stations and bubbles are pixel-identical to before the loss in Chrome D3D11 at dpr 2 (z15, z17,
bubbles views: 0 px differ) and render in WebKit too. MapLibre's own warning that the custom layer
"cannot be restored" still prints; it is expected.

### 2. mediump in STROKE_FS (fixed)

`v_cap` carries device px along the segment (distance from each end), interpolated across the quad.
At fp16 (10-bit mantissa) values from 2048 to 4096 are 2 px apart and anything over 65504 is
infinity. The real save's longest line segment is 134 m (track is sampled), at most about 3,700
device px at zoom 20 on a dpr 3 phone, and near an end the interpolated value is small and precise,
so the caps are off by under a pixel: no visible harm today. A single straight stroke over roughly
10-30 km zoomed right in (an unsampled preview, say) would overflow and turn its round caps square.
highp is guaranteed in WebGL2 fragment shaders and costs nothing here, so STROKE_FS is highp now.
PROBE_FS stays mediump: it writes a constant.

Could not reproduce the fp16 case on desktop: Chrome's `--emulate-shader-precision` changes nothing
now, and ANGLE's Vulkan back end reports mediump as fp16 (15/15/10) but the AMD driver runs it at
fp32 (mediump and highp builds pixel-identical).

### 3. MSAA on the overlay (T-955)

The network canvas asks for `antialias: true` and a stencil: 4x multisampled colour plus
depth-stencil, at dpr 3 on a 1080x2400 phone about 80 MB before the resolve buffer, beside
MapLibre's and the demand canvas's own. Every shader already antialiases analytically
(`clamp(0.5 - d, 0.0, 1.0)` on distances, quads a pixel larger than the shape), so MSAA buys little.
On phones it is also the likeliest trigger of a lost context (memory) and of Safari's per-tab limit.

### 4. Varyings (headroom only)

The station and bridge programs (STATION_VS/BRIDGE_VS to STATION_FS) carry v_p, v_size, v_dot,
v_alpha, v_crowd, v_scr, v_lane, v_hot and seven flat vec4s of the platform's centre line
(PLATFORM_POINTS 13): 12 vec4 slots packed by the GLSL ES 3.00 rules, 15 if a driver packs nothing.
WebGL2 guarantees 15. Fits, with no room for more points or varyings without packing the four
flat floats into one vec4. Every other program uses 4 or fewer. A comment at `QV` says so.

### 5. WebKit keeps the bubbles (T-956)

In Playwright's WebKit (the Windows build, `webkit-2272`) switching Homes off leaves the bubbles
drawn, even after the camera moves; Chrome clears them. Not the context-loss code (it happens before
any loss) and not a frame that only clears (`scratchpad/webgl/clearonly.mjs`: WebKit presents those
fine). The demand canvas is being drawn with the bubbles on, so something upstream of
`setBubblesVisible` differs. Screenshots `artifacts/trainworld/t953-wk2-*.png`.

### 6. Calls into MapLibre during a loss (T-957)

MapLibre sets `map.style = null` from `webglcontextlost` until restore. Effects that call
`map.setLayoutProperty`/`setPaintProperty` then throw (seen: realNetwork.ts's effect when the
display changes). Rare in play, since the tab is usually hidden while lost.

## Checked and fine

- **Array constructors**: none left (T-952's `qUnpack`). `polyFrame` takes `vec2 q[13]` as a
  parameter, which is a by-value array copy; it compiles on every back end tried, and the Pixel 10
  runs it since T-952.
- **Indexing and loops**: local arrays only by loop index; uniform arrays indexed dynamically
  (`u_level_colours[...]`, `u_hot_st[k]`) are allowed in GLSL ES 3.00; SEL_FS's loop bounded by
  `u_count` is allowed in ES 3.00; TRAIN_VS's binary search is a constant 18-step loop with `break`.
- **Integer arithmetic**: every `%` and `/` is on non-negative values (profiles, texel indices,
  vertex ids, colour ids). Fragment ints are mediump by default; the ones that need range are
  declared `highp int`, and uniforms shared between stages (`u_halo`, `u_layer`, `u_pass`, `u_ring`,
  `u_dpr`) have matching precision.
- **Limits against WebGL2 minimums**: vertex attributes at most 10 (16 guaranteed); vertex uniform
  vectors about 40 in STATION_VS (`u_hot_st[16]` takes 16) of 256; fragment about 20 in CUT_FS of
  224; vertex samplers 10 in TRAIN_VS of 16; texture units up to 11 of 32 combined. Textures are 2048
  wide (1024 for per-line ones) and grow in height: the real save uses 45 rows of samples and 42 of
  phases, so a network would need about 45 times the real save's track to pass 2048 rows (no check
  in `texture()` today; a P3 thought, not filed).
- **Float textures**: R32F, RG32F and RGBA32F are sampled with `texelFetch` and NEAREST filtering
  only, which WebGL2 core supports everywhere; nothing renders to a float target and nothing filters
  one linearly, so neither EXT_color_buffer_float nor OES_texture_float_linear is needed.
- **Safari**: no `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` (the debug panel shows "no timer"), no
  `navigator.deviceMemory` (demandClient's `poolSize` already caps that at 2 workers). Demand final
  took 118 s in WebKit against 34 s in Chrome; not graphics.
- **No WebGL2**: Overlay throws "no WebGL2" before `setupDone`, so T-950's screen shows it. Chrome no
  longer falls back to SwiftShader for blocklisted GPUs, so those land here.
- **Firefox**: Playwright's Firefox is not installed (about 90 MB; not installed without asking).
  Firefox validates GLSL with ANGLE's translator as Chrome does, so shader acceptance should match.

## Not covered

Real phones. Desktop GPUs run mediump at full precision and report generous limits, so an fp16
artefact or a driver compiler bug like T-952's shows only on a device. Worth a look on Anita's Pixel
10 and an iPhone: zoomed right in on a station (caps and platform shapes), and switching apps for a
few minutes and back (context loss).
