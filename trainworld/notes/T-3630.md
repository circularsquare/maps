## Intercity trips, a setting on every device (orchestrator's call from her answer; DECISIONS.md 2026-11-18)

- `display.intercityTrips` replaces the phones-only `mobileIntercity`: on by default on a computer, off on a phone or
  tablet. A phone's stored choice carries over; on a computer the stored value is ignored (every computer had the
  old default, off, stored).
- The box decides whether a game opened from the menu has intercity trips; the pick no longer turns them back on
  (`keepMarket` is gone). "Load without intercity trips" (the memory warning) and "Play without intercity trips"
  (memory failure, mid-load or mid-game) untick it; the old tab flag `tw-no-intercity` is gone. The warning's
  "Load without" button hides while the box is unticked.
- Off still means no demand cache (`cacheOn` reads `marketOff()` at the client's start, after the pick). The cache
  would not have served a full result to a no-market game anyway: `tryCache` compares the market packs it holds.
- Help's "There are no intercity riders" shows whenever the setting is off, on any device.
