# T-066 — NYC everyday outings and purpose inspection

2026-10-11, Codex. First slice implemented; proposal/research is T-138.

NYC's 25-county pack generates home-based shopping/errands and social/leisure outings from
all residents. Retail/other-service employment (CNS07+CNS19) attracts errands. Social
destinations mix independently normalized food/recreation jobs (CNS17+CNS18) and residents,
50/50. Employees are relative draw, not customer capacities. Sector jobs retain original
LODES block locations and use the established block land-area spreading. Cached inputs suffice.
`enrich_everyday.py` appends weights without altering commuting; full pack builds also emit them.

Budgets are provisionally 0.3 errands and 0.2 social outings/person/day, each with two legs.
Origin-constrained exponential destination gravity uses 3/6 km scales and 2 km attraction bins,
with 1.04 km same-bin distance. Sixteen deterministic stratified destination samples per origin
cell/purpose integrate that distribution, choosing actual res-9 cells by attraction weight.
This bounded quadrature approximates continuous demand, not individual travel diaries or exact
street addresses. It is stable across service edits/reloads; destination cells can have sampling
noise. Coarse attraction bins particularly limit short-distance distribution interpretation.

Anita superseded T-138's retention of short walks, then lowered the initial 300 m cutoff to
150 m (T-141): **below 150 m represented cell-centre distance, the outing disappears from all
modes and totals without replacement**. Exactly 150 m
remains eligible. Cell grain limits real within-cell precision. Existing commuting is unchanged.
Local weights use the complement of regional demand's smooth 40–80 km onset; cross-pack
everyday travel remains outside this first slice.

After access rebuilds, edges aggregate into purpose-specific subzone pairs with weighted length
and walking share. Outward and return choices use their own directed routes, the period's fares
and frozen combined crowding. MSA retains both rail probabilities for actual cell/period queries.
New riders join line/station totals and fare income. City mode splits include both local layers;
other cities remain commute-only. Source-neutral attractor array names allow different national
inputs later; runtime enables this rollout only in NYC.

Local bubbles count trip legs at home/activity pairs, outward and return together. Commuter
counts are doubled before adding everyday travel; density doubles to preserve commute-only
bubble area. Selected bubbles show Work, Shopping and errands, Social and leisure counts/shares
with All modes / Train only scope. Separate To destination / Return home charts show actual
period departures per hour. Tooltips include counts, intensity and direction shares. Purpose
totals reconcile to the bubble; everyday far-cell totals reconcile with no cut, while the old
commute 1% cut remains. Longer trips stay separate. Station catchments still count commuters.

## Validation

- All native tests: 104 pass, six existing ignored. New tests cover the 149/150 m cutoff,
  all-mode exclusion, conservation, purpose/period/cell/boarding totals, shared crowding,
  period-specific service and asymmetric outward/return costs.
- Production WASM fixture checks period-pool additivity for modes/purposes, work timing versus
  commuter cell counts, and fare-sensitive everyday rail choice.
- Real NYC: 202,337 cells; 6.508M errand and 4.340M social outings, 21.695M new legs/day after
  exclusions. Mean lengths 5.813/10.538 km versus commute gravity's 18.727 km. No-service
  walking fractions 12.82%/5.73%. These are initial model results, not fitted survey estimates.
  A small central line gets 202,258 new rail legs/day with the checked service/fare. Its selected
  destination's modes match purpose/period counts. `../../artifacts/trainworld/notes-outputs/T-066-wasm-check.json` and script.
- Actual worker module: all nine packs plus regional demand, five periods, one shared crowding
  round. Combined summaries reconcile; NYC snapshot modes match purpose queries; Philadelphia
  returns Work only. About 255 MiB WASM memory for this worker/line. `../../artifacts/trainworld/notes-outputs/T-066-worker-check.json`
  and script; Node adapters supply message transport/WASM fetch, without UI automation.
- NYC H3, population, jobs and both commute arrays remain byte-for-byte equal to the
  pre-enrichment cells pack. Save and track formats unchanged. Type check and build pass.
- CUA reports no enabled browser/app surfaces. Live visual QA of the inspector is unavailable;
  worker checks are not visual verification.
- T-141: lowered cutoff to 150 m. The 149/150 m native boundary checks, production WASM
  checks, nine-city worker checks and rebuild pass. NYC totals above remain identical to the
  300 m run: this existing cell-endpoint sample has no retained outings in the 150–300 m band.

No commute-share target was imposed. School/facility attendance, tourists, activity chains,
cross-pack outings, empirical fitting and other cities are future work (T-140).
