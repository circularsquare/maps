# T-2794: a phone's game, memory and the tutorial

Anita, 2026-11-08: "mobile crashing seems to be common! ideally we try to work on a way to geta t least tutorial finishing on most phones without crashing".

Player feedback (`shonei-server/tools/feedback.py`, the last 3 days) has no report about phones, so there is no first-hand evidence of what kills them. Everything here is measured on desktop Chrome pretending to be a phone: Pixel 7 size, touch, DPR 3, Android user agent, on a scratch production build (the dev server's own weight is several hundred MB and misleads). **No real phone was run.** iOS Safari's engine, a phone's GPU memory and a real phone's kill threshold are not measured.

## Figures (`checks/t2794-mobile-memory.mjs`, MB)

Renderer = Chrome's private bytes for the page's process, which holds the page, every worker and the WASM. That is the figure compared with the brief's 700 MB. The GPU process is reported apart: on desktop it holds different things from a phone's, and a phone's GPU memory is shared with everything else. Runs vary by 50-100 MB, so ranges are shown.

| stage | before | after | after, Chrome's low-end mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| start menu, renderer | 555-595 | 333-361 | 276 |
| game up, renderer | 949-1035 | 694-806 | 546-556 |
| demand final, renderer | 942-1047 | 733-840 | 647-664 |
| settled after camera moves, renderer | 800-805 | 614-658 | 597-603 |
| peak during the load, renderer | 1031-1148 | 992-1121 | 876 (no line built) |
| peak, all Chrome processes | 1589-1676 | 1436-1567 | 1288 |
| page JS heap | 165-168 | 22-29 | 22-29 |
| demand workers' WASM (aux, solver) | 33, 65 | 33, 65 | 33, 65 |
| **first line built, clock running** | not measured | not measured | **1010-1018, WASM 184 + 65** |

"Low-end mode" is `--enable-low-end-device-mode` (`LOWEND=1`), whose collector is less lazy; a phone's browser behaves nearer to that than desktop Chrome does. There is no before figure in that mode.

Where the before went, by Chrome's own accounting at demand final (`DUMP=1`): V8 main heap 177, partition allocator 245 (array buffers 115, Blink buffers 132), malloc 118, worker heaps 63, WebGL 80, canvases 60. A sampling heap profile put 105 of the page's 150 MB of JS at the start menu in two lines: `boundaryGeometry.ts` (`OutlineEdges`' segment keys, 54) and `coarseOutlines.ts` (every zone's decoded polygons, 51).

## What changed (all gated on `smallDevice()`: a phone or tablet, or 4 GB or less)

- Map and overlay canvases, and the tutorial's ripple canvas, capped at pixel ratio 2 (`cappedPixelRatio`, deviceMemory.ts; mapView.ts, lite.ts, ripple.ts).
- The world's coarse outlines are kept as the file's deltas in typed arrays and decoded a zone at a time (`CompactOutlines`, coarseOutlines.ts). Their shared-border keys are held only for zones near the view, and none zoomed out past `OUTLINE_MIN_ZOOM` (cityBoundaries.ts). So a phone draws no coarse zone outlines at world zoom.
- The realm shade's worker is started for each cut, fetches the file itself, is ended with its answer, and waits for the load to end (shade.worker.ts, realmOverlay.ts).
- The demand cache is off, so no cache-write worker and no copies for it (demandClient.ts). A phone reloading a save solves again.
- Not gated, same results: the clock worker keeps the outlines as typed arrays (`readOutlinesForPlaces`).

Lost graphics: `watchGraphics` (game/loading.ts), on the map's and the overlay's canvases: a context still lost after 6 s pauses the game and shows the notice with Reload page (DemandStopped.tsx). One restored within the wait shows nothing. Out-of-memory text already existed (`isMemoryFailure`, T-2545, T-757); `checks/t2794-lost-context.mjs` confirms both.

Tried and dropped: `markets: []` on a small device in `nearOf` changed nothing (`market:nyc` still opens by another route), so it was taken back out. `?lod=coarse` did not move the first line's figure either.

The tutorial's crowding step could not be ticked by touch: a tap on the 7 px dot landed on the line panel's resize strip beside it (`.pane-resize`, z-index 4). On a coarse pointer the dot now sits above the strip with a larger touch area (style.css); the phone walk ticks it. This one is not a memory problem and would have stopped the tutorial on phones anyway.

## T-2802: the first line

A first line built took the renderer to about 1,010 MB (low-end mode), the solving worker's WASM from 33 to 184 MB as `market:nyc` opened. Two routes open a market: `marketZones` (a zone reached, a station within its riders' reach, a selection) and `stationsAhead`'s prefetch. Both are now off on a small device (demandClient.ts), so a phone opens no market at all and downloads neither the market rows nor the drives.

| first line built, clock running 45 s (renderer MB) | market open | no market |
|---|---|---|
| low-end mode, after | 1010-1018 | 639-652 |
| low-end mode, peak of the whole run | 1044-1052 | 741-779 |
| normal mode, after | not measured | 678 |
| normal mode, peak of the whole run | not measured | 1074 |
| WASM (the two demand workers) | 184, 65 | 65, 65 |

The phone walk still ticks every step; its last, the 1% share, reads 0.5/1% on the short test line after 60 s, a real number that a longer line would pass.

Anita asked that a phone says so: `ui/NoMarketNote.tsx`, once per game load at the bottom of the map, taps pass through the words, close button, gone after 45 s. It shows on every `smallDevice()`, so a desktop of 4 GB or less reads "on phones" too.

## What is not done

- The normal-mode peak (about 1,070) is transient, during the load, with Chrome's lazy collector; low-end mode's is 741-779. Still at 700+: array buffers about 106 and Blink buffers about 120, not attributed; the clock worker parsing all of outlines.json during the load is a likely part, and sharing the page's parse with it would need a change to the clock's start order.
- A phone has no market trips, so its figures differ from desktop's for the same network. The real New York save (109 lines) on the emulated phone: 12,175,750 rail trips a day against smoke's 12,521,264 on desktop (2.8% fewer), commutes 32% of boardings against 39%, regional none.
- With no market open, `dayResult` in demandSums.ts made the day's totals NaN (`undefined * factor`), so rail trips and the tutorial's share went NaN. Fixed there; it was already reachable on desktop when a market pack failed.
- The tutorial's last step (1% train share) was not reached in the emulated walk in 60 s of waiting, on a short test line; every step before it ticks. `t2123-tutorial.mjs` stops before it on desktop too.
