# T-1816 Transfer pulses after panning away (qwpulse, 2026-11-18)

Real bug. `platformTransfers` (and `transfers`) asked the demand workers by `activeCity`, which follows the map. A zone outside the network's solve answers mask 0, so `platformTransfers()` returned null (pulses missing; the clock client retried only on a new view).

Probe on T-125-northeast.save (solve covers nyc, phl, nhv, hfd, lck): at the start 12,037 pairs; panned to Boston (activeCity bos, not in the solve) null; every zone in the solve answers the same pairs (checked nyc, phl, nhv, hfd, lck), bos answers nothing. Panned to Philadelphia 12,051 pairs.

Fix: `transferParts` in workers/demandClient.ts asks zone `solve.cities[0]` when the zone in view is not in the solve. After it, panned to Boston: 12,037 pairs, also after a headway edit made while there.
