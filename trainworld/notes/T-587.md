# T-587: commute walking at a finer distance

Task line (before the T-588 tidy): commutes priced walking (T-558) at the 2 km zone pair's centre
distance, so a real 1 km commute across a zone line counted as about 2 km and walked too little,
while everyday trips use their own samples' distance. Give commutes a finer walk distance, keep
the gravity's 2 km zones for everything else, refit the commute walking constant so total
commute walking stays put, measure walking by distance band. Done 2026-10-17.

## As built

- `kernel::walk_points`: each zone's homes and jobs as six cells (`Params::walk_samples`), where
  the zone's cumulative commute weight in pack order crosses 1/12, 3/12 ... 11/12.
  `cell_walk_m`: the cells' centre distance, a cell to itself 0.52 of its square's side.
- `Gravity::set_walk_fine` (after `no_rail_ctx`, in `DemandApi::new` and `refresh_walk`): for
  zone pairs within 3 squares each way (`WALK_FINE_R`), the 36 cell pairs' mean shares walking
  and taking other with no rail (`wf_s`), and the walk cost for the choice with rail (`wf_c`): the
  one cost whose share against a driver parking free is the cell pairs' mean. `NoRail::split` and
  `walk_cost_at` read them (`fine_at`), so the no-rail totals, the pair loop and the trip
  explorer agree; the drive stays at zone centres. `walk_samples` 0 (`--set`) gives the old model.
- `asc_walk` 2.86 -> 2.80, `everyday_asc_walk` 0.35 -> 0.41 (everyday stays 3.21).
- `DemandApi::commute_walk_bands` (in `calib`'s output as `commute_walk_bands`): commutes with no
  rail within 3 squares by cell-pair distance, trips and walkers.

A first try priced each pair at the logsum of its cell pairs' costs. It was wrong for a fixed
spread of trips: the pair walked like its shortest cell pair (up to 230% of a band's trips in the
check). The mean of shares is what each commuter choosing alone adds up to.

Real save (`checks/smoke.mjs`, own Vite on 6110): demand final 10,413,069 -> 10,406,013 rail trips
a day (-0.07%), 51% commutes, 1% regional; 35 s -> 34 s to the final result (no slowdown).

builds the table at open, then drops it). The table costs 16 bytes for each near zone pair (at
most 49 a zone).
