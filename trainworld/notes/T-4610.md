Read from code, nothing run.
- No zone selected (overview): a bubble's size and label are total(place) (game/corridorFigures.ts marketPairs) plus the
  zone's local trips. marketPairs already adds each place's everyday legs (IntercityDemand.everyday, one total per place
  from IntercityApi.everyday_place_legs, demand.worker.ts ~2097). These bubbles and their hover line already count
  everyday trips; nothing to change.
- A zone selected: another zone's bubble is pair(own, c) = zoneRowOf (long trips plus commutes for the pair; rows from
  a worker, once per selected zone). Everyday trips are not in it: the market holds them only as one total per place
  (everyday_place_legs adds each outing to both its places), with no partner. This is the 774k against the hover's 944k.
  The hover gets the real sum by asking the workers for that one pair (askMarketTrip, T-4606).

   One extra call per selected zone, asked with its rows. Needs sim/ and a WASM build, outside an App-logic budget.
