# T-3580 Traffic view draws double track twice as thick

Cause: the line strokes' shader (`rendererShaders.ts`, vertex, `tk`) scales every stroke by the track's width
factor, 0.71 on single and 1.24 on double track (`SINGLE_WIDTH`, `DOUBLE_WIDTH`), blended to 1 as double track opens
into its two tracks. The factor is meant for track and for lines in the Line view; in the Traffic view it multiplied
the riders' width (`a_wmul`), so double track was 1.75 times single at equal riders.

Fix (tree `.tw-trees/trafficwidth`, branch tw/trafficwidth, one file): `tk` is 1 for line strokes when
`u_band_traffic == 1` (the Traffic view), so a line's width is the riders' width alone. Track, the Line view, the
other track colourings and the normal view are untouched. Changelog item c134 (schematic, fix).

Measure: new game NYC, one line A-B-D, A-B double track, B-D single (3 km each). Traffic view, drawn thickness of
the line's colour in a pixel column a quarter of the way along each stretch; riders' width multiple
(`renderer.linesWmul`) west 3.81, east 4.28 (ratio 1.12). Script: scratchpad `width.mjs`.

After, single/double is 1.0 to 1.16 (the riders' ratio is 1.12, plus pixel rounding and the band gap); before, the
double stretch was wider although it carried fewer riders. Sheet: artifacts/trainworld/review/t3580.png.

Left alone: close in (about zoom 14 and up), where double track's two tracks part, the Traffic view's line stays at
least the pair's width behind them (Anita, 2026-10-19, comment in the shader's lane branch). That floor is the
tracks' outer edges, not a thickness for riders; if she wants it gone too, it is the `max(core * wmul, ...)` in the
lane_mode 1 branch and the `grow` term.
