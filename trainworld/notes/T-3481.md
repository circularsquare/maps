# T-3481: long straight lines across the map (Tokyo report)

Status 2026-11-16 (agent lines2): cause found and fixed. Same bug as T-3150 (San Francisco, 2026-11-11).

## Cause

Zoomed out, the connectors between a complex's dots (T-313, renderer.ts `syncGroups`, BRIDGE_VS) take
their half width in local units from `u_bridge`, which the draw worked out as the connector's px width over
"px per local unit" measured **at the game's first station** (`n.stations[0]`). On the flat map that is
the same everywhere, so it never mattered. On the globe (Settings, zoom up to 12) it is not:

- With the first station behind the globe (globe blend over 0.2), `mercToCss` gives NaN, so `u_bridge`
  is NaN. Headless Chrome (ANGLE d3d11) then drops the connectors; other GPUs draw NaN boxes as huge ones.
- Near the blend's end (zoom about 11.8-12) it is finite but 14-135 times too small, so every connector
  is drawn 14-135 times too wide: a box as long as the connector and thousands of px wide, i.e. a long
  straight bar across the map, square-ended, through the connector, at right angles to it, its thickness
  the connector's length. The selection glow (worked out on the CPU from the true ends) stays short,
  which is why the glow in the screenshot covers only a short piece of the bars.

The player's save (Bangkok, Tokyo, New York) has its first station in Bangkok, so viewing Tokyo or New
York on the globe shows it. Every join in the save is real (218 connectors, the longest 905 m; 120 link
bridges, longest 905 m; the same after visiting each city at several zooms). Anita plays on the flat
map, which is why she did not see it; nothing builds up in the save. T-3150's "deleted a couple station
groups and it went away" fits too: fewer connectors, fewer bars.

Measured on the player's save, globe on (px per local unit, at the first station / at the view's middle):
Tokyo z11 NaN / 0.0262; Tokyo z11.85 0.00343 / 0.0472; New York z11.85 0.000348 / 0.0472.
Screenshot of the bars, before the fix: `artifacts/trainworld/t3481/globe-nyc-z11.85.png`; after:
`fixed-globe-nyc-z11.85.png`.

## Fix

- renderer.ts `draw`: the view's middle in local units (gl.ts `viewCentreMerc`, solved from the flat
  camera matrix, which the globe shares) is where on-screen sizes are measured: the connectors' width and
  `boxWhite` (the track's width cap through boxes and the walkers' size, measured at station 0 too; only
  wrong there with a pitched view or a first station at another latitude, since boxes are flat-map only).
- clock.worker.ts: the `?patch=check` and `?patch=shiftcheck` scratch builds get `buildingLL`, so a game
  with a station building no longer reports a false T-1029 difference.

## Check

`node trainworld/checks/t3481-globe-connectors.mjs http://localhost:<port>/`: the real New York save
(all stations in New York), connectors' `u_bridge` on the globe against the flat map at New York, Tokyo
and Bangkok, zooms 10.5, 11.5, 11.85. Before the fix 6 of 9 fail (NaN, or 30-93 times the flat width);
after, all equal. The player's save under `?patch=check`, visiting all three cities: no check errors.

## Earlier dead ends (agent lines, 40 minutes)

Scripted edit runs on San Francisco and Tokyo (`artifacts/trainworld/t3481/fuzz.mjs`, `realfuzz.mjs`,
`real-load.mjs`) never reproduced it: they ran on the flat map, and in single-city games station 0 is
near the view. Reading the complex builder, `withBuildingLinks` and the passages found no far join, which
the data check above confirms.
