# T-695: placing a station looks right on the first frame (2026-10-19)

Anita (playtest-2026-10-19.md): over overlapping tracks the ghost showed on the other track for a frame
before correcting, and near a track's end it showed off the end before snapping.

## What changed
- `map/stationSnap.ts`: `layoutIssues` (the refusals a station would meet, from the track's layout: a
  station or junction at a node, the platform's room to the next station, junction or end with a drawn
  route's joints joined through as `unjoin_near` does, a flat crossing under the platform) and
  `likelyFirst` (`firstAccepted` answered at once from it).
- `map/tools.ts`: the ghost shows the layout's guess on the first frame; the worker's trial confirms
  or replaces it; a refused spot stays where the guess put it. The worker's last answer per track is
  kept (16 px, same network and platform) and refused tracks are not asked again there. Where 7 or more
  candidates are under the pointer and the worker has not confirmed the guess, the ghost waits (stays
  if it was within 10 px, else none). The click tries the ghost's spot first.
- Draw order (`network.ts`, `renderer.ts`, `pick.ts`): built track sorts before blueprint track; when
  there is blueprint, track and lines are drawn in two passes (built track and its lines, then blueprint
  track and the lines planned on it), and picking follows the same stacking.

## Measurements (real save, `checks/t671-ghost-hover.mjs`, zoom 15.5)
The first frame before T-695 was the first candidate under the pointer. On 80 overlapping pairs
(spread over the 2064 found) the worker's spot differed from it in 14-17 (about 20%); on 112-336 hovers
near the ends of new blueprint, in about 18%. After: the ghost changed after its first frame in 1-2 of
80 overlaps and 0-1 of the end hovers; 21 of 80 overlap hovers waited for the worker (at most ~230 ms,
all in bundles of 7+ tracks).

The guess alone (no waiting, no memory of answers) was wrong in 9 of 80 overlaps, all but one in
bundles of 7+ candidates. The worker refuses those for what the page cannot see: `CrossingOverlap` and
`Geom:RadiusTooSmall` on the real save's imported lines that run on top of each other (T-691), and
these are not even constant along an edge, so remembering them per edge made things worse.
