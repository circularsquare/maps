# T-3495 Snap to any real rail that is drawn

## What the layer draws and what the snap follows
- Settings > Real world network: Passenger draws subway, rail and light rail that a line runs over (or that is flagged x). All adds, from the region tiles: track no line runs over (n), trams, monorail, funicular, narrow gauge, heritage; and from the extra archives (T-3464) freight and abandoned.
- The snap (map/realSnap.ts) takes the nearest rendered feature of the tw-real-* layers within 12 px, then asks the router worker for a route over the region's graph (pipeline/realgraph.py, data/packs/realgraph/<cc>.rgr). While the snap is on, realNetwork.ts draws only the Passenger filter, so what is drawn is what the graph holds. There was no drawn-but-unsnappable case while snapping; the gap was that turning the snap on hid everything All adds.
- Checked: ten views at zoom 11 (London, Tokyo, Chicago, Berlin, Kaohsiung, Sydney, Mumbai, Basel, Istanbul, Amman), every drawn Passenger sample point (about 40,000) has a graph chain within 25 m. Ring and covers() find all of them. checks/t3495-snap-survey.mjs + t3495-graph-compare.py.
- Zoom: nothing is drawn below 6 (MIN_ZOOM), so nothing is snappable there; no mismatch.
- Tile edges: no gap seen (features are queried across tiles; a drawn piece is snapped from the part in view).
- Regions with tiles but no graph: jo and mu. Amman has only narrow gauge, so nothing is drawn while snapping; mu has no rail kinds the graph keeps.

## Fixed (easy)
Unused rail, subway and light rail (n): pipeline/realgraph.py keeps every way of those three kinds that the tiles draw (rank 0 or 1, no service tag) instead of only the ones a line runs over; map/realNetwork.ts draws them while snapping under All (Passenger keeps drawing used track only). The graph's kind and rank are as for used track, so the router, the reach drawing and the fit are unchanged.
- Check (checks/t3495-snap-route.mjs, GRAPHS=dir serves rebuilt graphs): hover a point 60-300 px along a drawn feature after clicking its start; routes found. Chicago unused rail 5/5 with the rebuilt us graph, 1/5 with today's; London unused 2/2 either way; used rail, subway and light rail identical to today (same route lengths, London rail 5/6 and subway 5/6 both before and after, Chicago 6/6 and 6/6).
- Graph size: gb 2.14 -> 2.26 MB; us 2.72 -> 7.41 MB (the US has a lot of freight line with no passenger service). 44 MB for the 118 files now; the world would be about 70 MB or more. Rebuild took 10 s (gb) and 35 s (us) at 2 cores, so about 30 minutes for all 118 regions. The rebuild is not done: run from the maps root, `venv\Scripts\python.exe trainworld/pipeline/realgraph.py <every cc in data/packs/realgraph/index.json>`, then check one region with --check, then ship graphs and app together (the published page names its data version, so they cannot get out of step on the published game; on 8800 the app change shows unsnappable unused track until the graphs are rebuilt there).
- Download cost on the published game: a graph is read for a region when the track tool first snaps there; the US file is 4.7 MB more. If that is too much, keep n track out of the US only, or filter n track below rank 1 (r1 and n: 260 of Chicago's 783 points).
