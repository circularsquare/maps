# T-3510: a bubble selection's purpose rows with its trips with other places

Agent bubfold, 2026-11-17. Follows T-3503 (notes/T-3503.md): since then a selection's total counts
its regional trips at the end in the zone, but its purpose rows, pie and timing charts counted the
local trips alone.

## What was there

- The rows come from the demand workers' `purposes` answer to the selection (`Flows.purposes`):
  the local trips by purpose, period, mode and direction.
- The total comes from the cell legs (`cellModes`), local plus regional.
- Each purpose's own cell legs (`cellModes(purpose)`) already hold that purpose's regional part:
  Work the commutes across a zone line (`REG_COMMUTES`), an everyday purpose its outings across
  one (its kind's bit), Airports with T-3500's trips at the terminals. Longer trips belong to no
  purpose (`cellModes("longer")` is empty); they are only in all purposes' legs.
- Measured on a fresh New York game (no regional market yet), 66 Midtown cells: every purpose's
  local rows equal its cell legs to the leg (Errands 200,528 both, Work 112,649 against 112,651),
  and all purposes' legs equal the rows' sum plus nothing (620,803 against 620,801). The workers'
  Work rows split rail slightly differently from the cell legs (destinations: 1,124,960 against
  1,117,221 by rail), which the new rows take from the cell legs.

## The rule (`map/purposeRows.ts`, called from `commuterView.ts` `regionalRows`)

- Each shown purpose's row takes its cell legs over the selection, by mode (No route with
  driving, as the rows always counted it): the local rows are scaled per mode to that figure, so
  the regional part takes the periods and directions of the purpose's local trips. The model times
  a purpose's regional trips by the same period tables as its local ones, so the shape is close;
  the lie is the rail share per period, which crowding moves a little differently.
- A mode with regional trips but no local ones takes the purpose's own all-mode timing, else the
  selection's.
- **Inter-city trips** (`Longer`, shown as the existing "Inter-city trips" in its existing grey):
  all purposes' legs minus every purpose's, per mode, at `longerShare(day, q)` (the market's own
  departure shares), all toward the destination. Shown when at least half a leg.
- So the rows add up to the selection's total under All purposes, by mode, rail included. Under a
  purpose filter the total counts that purpose and the rows still every purpose, as before.
- The rows come a moment after the far end (8 cell-leg queries, cached per result); the rows
  already shown for the same selection stay meanwhile. If the purposes' legs cannot all be had
  for one result after five tries 1.5 s apart, the local rows are shown.
- Only on a final result with no solve under way. Mid-solve each purpose's regional part is the
  last answer kept for its kinds (`lastReg`, demandClient.ts) or none, and all purposes' entry is
  kept more often (the bubbles ask for it), so all minus every purpose could hold commutes and
  outings across zone lines and read as inter-city trips. A selection made mid-solve shows the
  local rows (or the rows it had), and the result's final asks again. Seen in passing: on the real
  save, the first round of a re-solve answered all purposes' legs local only (68,528,648 Homes
  against 70,268,456 settled) where no last answer was kept yet; the bubbles had one kept.
