# T-921: deal commute pairs under 1 person a day in chunks (agents commutechunks, commutechunks2, 2026-11-24)

Ready to land from the tree `.tw-trees/commutechunks` (uncommitted, master merged at 8522f24f). Default 1 a day, so no
shift beyond her limit; the dial and its numbers are below for her.

## The change

- `COMMUTE_CHUNK_DAY` (sim/src/demand/kernel/mode.rs, 1.0): the one number to tune. In every period, commute zone pairs
  with fewer than this many people a day (`x period_share` trips in the period) are dealt their block's trips in chunks
  of about that size; the comment says what raising and lowering it does. `Params::commute_chunk_day` carries it;
  `mode_choice_with` deals pairs under `max(commute_chunk, commute_chunk_day x period_share)`. 0 gives back T-874's
  0.1 trips a period; `commute_chunk` 0 (`?perfOff=chunks`, tests) still solves every pair. Coarse zones keep their
  1 trip a period up to about 1.4 a day (peak share 0.72), then their peak periods are dealt coarser too.
- `demand_mem --set commute_chunk_day=2,chunk_salt=3` tries a value natively without a rebuild.
- `perf_bench --saves` also takes any `<name>` of artifacts/trainworld/perfbench/saves/<name>.save.
- Docs: demand-kernel.md (pair loop), code-map.md. DECISIONS (Demand) 2026-11-24 replaces T-909's 0.1 entry.
- Tests: `agent_cargo.py test` 738 and 967 passed.

## The in-game mystery

The first agent's head dist (`perfbench/dist/chunks-head`, built 21:02 from the tree) did not deal at 1 a day: its New
York rail trips equal the base's to the trip (12,672,465), and so does a fresh build with the number at 0. Most likely
it was built before the dealing was switched on in the tree; nothing in the dist can say now. A clean A/B on one build
with only the number changed (play.mjs, then perf_bench dists t921-d0f / t921-d1f) moves New York in game
12,672,465 -> 12,672,459 rail trips a day. So the chunking reaches the game.

## Found on the way: master's production build does not start (fixed in this tree)

`clock.worker.ts` imports `clockWordLines` before `clockWord`, and the two imported each other (`lineEdges`). Vite
dev is fine; the production bundle evaluates `clockWord`'s `KINDS` before `clockWordLines` exists: "The train
simulation could not start (Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'lineWord'))", every perf_bench run fails at
boot. Since 2171510df (T-4147 stage 1). Fix here: `lineEdges` moved into clockWordLines.ts, clockWord imports it from
there (no cycle; typecheck clean). The dists t921-d0f/d05f/d2f are t921-d1f with the other value's demand WASM copied
in (the builds are byte-identical: d1 and d1f's demand WASM have the same hash).

## Sparse save: western Montana

`perfbench/saves/t921-montana-4lines.save`: a new Missoula County (msu) game, 4 lines built by play.mjs `edits` (the
route, addStation, constructAll, addLine ops the tools send): Hamilton-Missoula-Polson-Kalispell-Whitefish,
Missoula-Butte-Bozeman, Helena-Butte, and a 5-stop Missoula town line. 22 stations, 6,849 rail trips a day, every station

elsewhere. Worth a profile of a sparse load (not filed: no id).

## For Anita

1 a day stays as the default: it moves riders by a few trips in millions and saves about 7% of New York's calculation
natively, a few percent in game. It does not speed up sparse areas: there the trips calculation takes under a second
and the wait is loading the zone. Raising it to 2 a day changed nothing measurable either.
