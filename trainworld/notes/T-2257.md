# T-2257: Rikers Island, a false land part

Agent t2257, 2026-11-03. Land parts: notes/T-2213.md, docs/demand-mode.md "Land parts".

## Causes

1. **Rikers.** The Rikers Island Bridge (Francis R. Buono Bridge, OSM way 11836206) is `highway=unclassified`,
   `access=no`, `bus=yes`: closed to the public, open to the Q100 bus and the jail's own traffic. T-2213's rule
   dropped every way with `access=no` or `motor_vehicle=no` whatever exceptions it carried, so the bridge joined
   nothing and Rikers (27 people, 7,081 jobs at res 9) was New York's part 1. Not the mask, the extract or the
   50 m drawing: the bridge is in the New York extract and is drawn whole once let through.
2. **Stale way caches.** parts_run.py took a kept `data/raw/osm/<extract>.ways2.npz` over a download, and the 9
   kept (England, Ontario, India western zone, Ile-de-France, Shanghai, Georgia, Rhode Island, DC, Bhutan) were
   written before T-2213, with no flag 4: their packs' parts had no service ways, tracks or private roads. Found
   by the scan below (8 packs changed with no new-rule way involved).

## Rule changes

- `routes.way_flags` (read_ways' per-way rule, now one function, also used by the scan): a road closed to motor
  traffic still joins land when some road vehicle may use it, i.e. any of motor_vehicle, motorcar, bus, psv, hgv,
  goods, delivery, taxi, minibus, coach, tourist_bus, share_taxi, hov, agricultural, forestry is set to anything
  but `no` (`CAR_EXCEPTIONS`, `car_may_use`). Emergency, foot, bicycle and motorcycle exceptions do not count, and
  an emergency access way (`service=emergency_access`) closed to the public takes no exceptions: a first draft
  joined Suomenlinna to Helsinki through its service tunnel (`access=no`, `motor_vehicle=permit`). Flag 1 (the
  drive network) is unchanged; `vehicle=no` still does not close a way.
- `routes.WAYS_RULE` (2257) is stored in each `.ways2.npz`; read_ways reads an extract again when its cache has
  another, and parts_run.py uses a kept cache only when it matches (else the kept .osm.pbf, else a download).
- parts_run.py: `--backup <folder>` names the backup folder (default pre-T2213); `write_pixels` split out of
  `extract_job` for the scan.

## The scan

`checks/t2257_links.py`: every pack with two parts or more (1,249), its 230 extracts read again (fresh
downloads, or a kept .osm.pbf); the current rule's pixels per pack and the link ways near it (bridges, tunnels,
embankments, fords and causeways of any highway or railway, and roads closed to motor traffic). Per pack: the
parts by parts_run's pixels and by the new ones (`changed`), and on the new parts each link way that crosses
water, drawn with its ends carried 60 m on, joins the land it touches; a part joined to another by a car way
(a near miss of the 50 m drawing), only by paths, only by rail, or only by a road being built or proposed.

Run 2026-11-03, 4 readers, 1 h 40 min (`artifacts/trainworld/t2257/world.log`), then Finland again under the
final rule (`finland.log`). Changed: anr, bhc, bhm, grt, lon, nyc, ota, sth, tor (Rikers by the new rule, the
rest by the stale caches). Car joins found: none, so no bridge is lost to the 50 m drawing.

Final report after the rerun (`report.log`, `links.json`): 1,248 packs, 0 changed; of the 1,006 parts other than
the first with 1,000+ people or jobs that do not reach the grid's edge, 0 joined by car, 14 only by paths (den:
Nusa Lembongan to Ceningan, `motorcar=no`; vag: two Gothenburg archipelago islands, `motor_vehicle=no`; ven,
kom, sui, bwa, fej footbridges; pti boardwalks; ste a `motorcar=no` track bridge), 3 only by rail (hya, slx), 18
only by roads, bridges or tunnels under construction or proposed (ili Guimaras, cop Bornholm, ktr, keg, lag,
nnh, gap...), 971 with no fixed link in OSM. Every line is in `report.log`. The 72 parts with 1,000+ jobs and under half as
many people (Phu Quoc, Koh Samui, Gulangyu, Curtis Island's LNG plants, Rottnest, Flotta, Revithoussa...): none
has a car link. Two small islands in a Dalian bay (waf parts 1 and 2, 4,260 and
2,124 jobs, no road mapped on them) are real islands in the mask with jobs the jobs layer put there.
