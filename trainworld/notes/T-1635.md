## 2026-11-16 (agent b4)
Not reproduced here (needs the live host's immutable cache headers on R2). Change made on the likeliest cause: Chrome cannot answer a whole-file request from a cache entry holding only ranges. The Range reads (`workers/rangeFetch.ts`, the street speeds; `diagram.worker.ts`) now use `cache: "no-store"`, so no partial entries are left for the demand worker's whole-file fetch to hit. They are small and read once per game load. To confirm on the published page: load a game and look for `ERR_CACHE_OPERATION_NOT_SUPPORTED` in the console. If it still shows, the whole-file fetch in demandClient (`patientFetch`, line ~1570) wants `cache: "reload"` instead.
