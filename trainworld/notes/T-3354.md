# T-3354 one-track platform jumps sides when dragged north-south (bug-a, 2026-11-16)

Not reproduced; left open, no code changed.
The seam is real: StationData.one is stored against a fixed axis (net.rs one_for_travel: 1 eastward, 2 westward,
north/south where due N-S), so a node whose heading turned through due N-S with node_one unchanged would serve the
other physical track. But in the sim a station node's heading cannot turn that way by a drag: move_node keeps a
two-end node's heading (its lead-in PIs move with it; checked in a test, heading changed by 2e-6 rad when the station
was moved 300 m sideways), and set_edge_pis on one side of the node is refused with NodeHeading (both edges must leave
along the node heading). A heading turn of about half a circle (a renumbered end) is handled by the axis.
Idea if it is ever seen: in Network::derive, after derive_node, flip node_one when the heading moved less than 90
degrees across the seam and the station was not set in that touch. Tried, test passed with and without it (no
reproduction), so not kept.
