# T-1739: attractions with few Wikidata figures (2026-10-29)

Run: `venv/Scripts/python.exe trainworld/pipeline/attractions_json.py [--fetch] [--report]` from the maps root.

## What was tried (fit on the 1,693 places with a figure; error is ten-fold leave-out, in ln visitors)

| model | error |
|---|---|
| 10,000 per sitelink (old) | 1.15-1.23 |
| sitelinks, kind | 1.00 |
| + people within 25 km | 0.93 |
| + country, shrunk (built, then switched off: `COUNTRY_EFFECT`) | 0.86-0.88 |
| per-language-edition weights (non-negative fit, 118 editions) | 1.6, worse than the old rate |
| Huff-style share of the catchment's outings | 1.4, worse |
| UNESCO flag (P757) | coefficient -0.2: places with a figure and a listing sit slightly under the rest, so no floor |

People within 25 km beat 50, 100 and 200 km. A country's effect is mostly its mix: China's figures are theme parks, so
the ride-park, animal-park and museum kinds are fitted as controls and left out of the estimate (a ride park would
otherwise get 7 times, and abandoned ones with it). Open scenic places keep their own effect (0.6, from 101 lakes,
islands, hills, parks with a figure).

With the country term (China x3.4, Korea x2.4, Taiwan x3.0, Italy x0.7) West Lake was 6.1M, Lingyin 1.5M, Hangzhou
zones +53% both, real save 12,659,647. Set `COUNTRY_EFFECT = True` to bring it back; it is Anita's call.

Estimated rows in both files, new over old (median): China 3.6, Egypt 3.4, Korea 3.4, India 2.8, Japan 2.8, US 2.9, UK 1.9,
France 1.9, the world 2.15. So without the country term the small Chinese temples and mosques still rise 3.6 times
(0.1-0.4M to 0.3-1.3M, the largest 1-2M), no more than elsewhere; the rise is the fit's level against the old
10,000 per edition, helped by China's dense cities.

Still short of what Anita knows: Kiyomizu-dera about 5M, West Lake tens of millions. Wikidata's figures come from
counted, ticketed or huge places, so a free temple or lake is not in what the fit learns from. Closing it needs a
source of visits for open places (page views, say), not another term.

Restricted places: Zhongnanhai carries presidential palace, headquarters, official residence and Chinese garden.
Presidential palaces and headquarters without a figure are left out (20 places, Zhongnanhai, the Blue House, Quirinal
among them); "official residence" is not used, since it also tags open royal palaces (Hofburg, Royal Palace of Madrid,
Holyrood, the Kremlin). A place with a figure stays whatever its class.

Known odd rows: abandoned parks with few editions are sized as parks; destroyed or ancient sites (Titanic, Troy,
Byzantium) are still listed. In the 100 m merge a place with a figure outranks an estimated neighbour of any size
(Topkapi Palace's 1.9M had been swallowed by one; 1,171 rows with a figure became 1,273).

`checks/t263-attractions.mjs`: the Tokyo National Museum hover fails the same way on the old file (a station card
covers the mark in the screenshot); the other six pass.

The scenic rule: an item that is none of KINDS or DIRECT but has a class under "tourist attraction" and, as its own
class, a lake, hill, mountain, island, river, beach, reservoir, protected area or national park. A scenic byway is tagged
too and is a road, so it needs the nature class itself.
