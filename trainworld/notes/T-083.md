# T-083 riders on board

Done 2026-10-09 by the gameplay agent (batch 5). `T-batch5-train.png`.

- Train inspector (`ui/inspector/others.tsx`, `OnBoard`): the segment the train is on (from its
  position along its run and the line's `stopS`), the period now (`game/periods.ts`), and
  `LineDemand.loads[period][segment]` (riders over the whole period; run 1's segments follow run
  0's) divided by the trains the line runs in the period (trains an hour at the period's demand
  level times its hours). So it is an average train of the period; the line panel's "fullest
  train" is the busiest hour, about 1.15-1.5 times more.
- Shown against seats and crush: 44 and 160 per 20 m car, a copy of `SEATS_PER_CAR` and
  `CRUSH_PER_CAR` in `sim/src/demand/api.rs` (the demand agent's file; read only). A bar with a
  tick at the seats, and "Empty", "Seats free", "Standing" or "Over full".
- Before demand has solved, or while a newer network is being solved for a changed line, the row
  says riders are worked out after the next demand update.
- No new export was needed from `game/demand.ts`. If the demand side ever exposes the busiest-hour
  factor per period (`peak_hour_factor`), the inspector could show the busiest train too.
