v0.2 of my train game is out, and it has trams. i hadn't posted about the earlier updates, so this is everything new since the first version.

**trams**
- trams are a new track kind. draw them by hand, or snap them to streets and they follow the road
- they get their own stops, cars and lines, and their own prices. street track costs less to keep up than track on its own ground
- trams give way to trains at flat crossings
- the street guide shows from zoom 11, and trams turn at 18 m radius with no u-turns

**lines and routing**
- a line can loop back at its end: click the stop before a loop of track and the line turns round it, at either end
- in the line panel a looped line gets one row per station, with the loop drawn as a bend beside the stops
- a line can have its own speed limit
- one-way loops for buses and boats (new ones start one way), and arrows on the map show which way they run
- double track dead ends use both platforms only if you build a terminal

**buses**
- bus lines that cross can share a stop
- bus stops sit on the route, with a dashed line to the nearby station
- a bus fare multiple in the money tab, like the ferry one
- bus and ferry loop lines

**map and places**
- one speed color scale for trains, trams, buses and boats, dark red at 1 km/h up to purple at 320
- new places: morocco, algeria, tunisia, egypt, israel and palestine
- new zealand is one place now
- the grey outside the zones shows at every zoom
- the network diagram is back on the m key, with trams drawn on it

**interface**
- some new hidden achievements. i won't say which
- a color blind option for the demand bubbles
- after four deletes in a row you get a hint about the select tool
- toasts stay up for 10 seconds
- big money numbers fit in every language now

**languages**
- the game is in 11 languages now (english, spanish, french, german, portuguese, turkish, russian, japanese, korean and both chinese scripts). the new text is first-pass translations, so tell me what reads wrong
- (check) the six languages added on 9 november were already in the live build, so they are not new here; delete this line if you would rather not say so
