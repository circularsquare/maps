v0.2 of my train game is out, and it has trams. i hadn't posted about any of the earlier updates, so this is everything new since launch.

## v0.2

**trams**
- trams are a new track kind. draw them by hand, or snap them to streets and they follow the road
- they get their own stops, cars and lines, and their own prices. street track costs less to keep up than track on its own ground
- trams give way to trains at flat crossings
- the street guide shows from zoom 11, and trams turn at 18 m radius with no u-turns

**lines and routing**
- a line can loop back at its end: click the stop before a loop of track and the line turns round it, at either end
- in the line panel a looped line gets one row per station, with the loop drawn as a bend beside the stops
- a line can have its own speed limit
- bus and ferry loop lines, one way or both ways, with arrows on the map showing which way they run
- double track dead ends use both platforms only if you build a terminal

**buses**
- bus stops sit on the route, with a dashed line to the nearby station
- a bus fare multiple in the money tab, like the ferry one

**map and places**
- one speed color scale for trains, trams, buses and boats, dark red at 1 km/h up to purple at 320
- new places: morocco, algeria, tunisia, egypt, israel and palestine
- new zealand is one place now
- the grey outside the zones shows at every zoom
- the network diagram is back on the m key, with trams drawn on it

**interface**
- some new hidden achievements. i won't say which
- a color blind option for the demand bubbles
- after four deletes in a row you get a hint about the select tool
- toasts stay up for 10 seconds
- big money numbers fit in every language now

## the quiet update before it (9 november)

**languages**
- portuguese, turkish, russian, german, french and korean. that makes 11 with english, spanish, japanese and both chinese scripts. these are first-pass translations, so tell me what reads wrong
- simplified chinese uses a rounded font

**buses and boats**
- bus stops by hand: a bus line's stop spacing is Auto or Manual, and in Manual you click every stop between the two ends
- buses and boats got the train panel's crowding dots, riders by purpose, round trip and schedule copy
- buses are timed from the road's speed, run at highway speed on motorways, and mind crowding from 85% full
- the roads buses can use are tinted while you place a route, and buses can use short service roads that link two streets
- esc finishes a new bus or ferry line
- bus and boat sounds

**map and interface**
- bubbles can be colored by trip purpose, and their hovers show the purpose split
- track and stations are priced by the hills they cross, from a finer terrain map
- train stop rows drag into an order you keep
- the schematic has a line width slider and a pause
- blueprint stations are blue, quad track is back, and the issues list names buses and boats and suggests fixes
- deleting an account needs your password or a fresh google sign-in

## v0.1.1
