# T-3515 Tram snap refuses turns at Zurich junctions

Branch tw/tramsnap (tree `.tw-trees/tramsnap`). Found in T-3069 (notes/T-3069.md, "Routes across junctions").

## Causes

Three separate things, none of them the 60 degree turn rule itself.

1. **Zurich: the graph had no track between Bahnhofplatz and the lines north-west of the Hauptbahnhof.**
   The Bahnhofquai tram stop is being rebuilt (OSM: the two tracks there are `railway=construction`,
   `construction=tram`, `opening_date=2026-12-13`; noritetsu's extract keeps construction track only where a
   route relation runs over it). Trams run meanwhile on a temporary track along the river, mapped as
   `railway=tram service=siding` (ways 217408201, 217408198, 278182062) and in no route relation, and
   `realgraph.py` dropped every tram `service=*` way no route uses: it never even reached `gap_fillers`. So the
   only way from Wiedikon or Bahnhofplatz to Limmatplatz and Werdholzli was up Stampfenbachstrasse to the
   Bucheggplatz loop and back down Schaffhauserstrasse (11.2 km for a 3 km gap), over the same chains both ways,
   which the track model refuses ("The new track would run on top of existing track", CrossingOverlap).
   With tram sidings admitted the same pair routes 6.2 km over the Bahnhofquai track (Python copy of the router).
2. **Melbourne (and any tram city with tight single-track corners): curves thinned into chords.** The graph
   thinned every chain at 3 m (Douglas-Peucker). A 15 m street-corner curve at 3 m tolerance keeps one vertex every
   ~19 m, so where a curve is split by a node (two ways meeting mid-curve, a crossing) the heading taken 10 m into
   each side is a long chord, and the turn reads over 60 degrees (Lygon St into Elgin St: 63; line 82's corners in
   Footscray). Rebuilt at 0.5 m for tram chains the same junctions read 30-40 degrees.
3. **"Placeable but ignores the click" (Zurich's first candidate, 603 m, $34.6M): a checker artifact.** The
   T-3069 sweep took points with y > 100 px; that candidate's end (8.539081, 47.388907) was at page y 182, under
   the track bar (y 118-187). Mouse moves over the bar still reach MapLibre (the ghost and its price follow the
   pointer under the bar), clicks do not. Its third candidate ended at y 178 under the bar's track count button in
   this run and failed the same way. With points under a panel or bar left out, every placeable route placed
   (6 of 6 on the current graphs).

The turn rule was never the problem: on OSM tram route relations (see "Ground truth") the router follows every
real service in Zurich, Amsterdam and Milan already, and every one in Melbourne once corners keep their shape.

## Ground truth: the router against real tram services

`routecheck.py` (scratchpad): for every OSM `route=tram` relation in a box, points 500 m apart along its own track
(every 400 m), routed by a Python copy of `realRoute.worker.ts`'s search (same costs, families, headings and
60 degree rule; checked against the game on the T-3069 Zurich pair: 11,158 m against the game's 11,163 m) with a
6 m snap radius. A pair counts as failed when the route is over 1.5 x 500 + 100 m. Pairs that "fail" at about
1,500 m are one chain at the other-family cost (3 x 500: the nearest start candidate was on rail), not turns.

| Region | Pairs | Old graphs: real failures | New graphs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zurich (ch) | 698 | 0 | 0 |
| Melbourne (au) | 1,549 | 9 (Lygon/Elgin corner, line 11, six on line 82) | 0 |
| Amsterdam (nl) | 587 | 0 | 0 |
| Milan (it) | 707 | 0 | 0 |

With the game's own snap radius (about 40 m at zoom 16) the Lygon corner routes on the old graphs too (the other
track's corner reads under 60), but line 82's single track fails at 7 of 362 pairs.

Random pairs of tram points 150-600 m apart are no measure: most real junctions lack most turns (a 4-way crossing of
double track has a few curves), and the "fixes" a looser turn rule made were turns through diamond crossings
(61-72 degrees at Bellevue's crossings), which trams cannot take.

## Change (tree tw/tramsnap)

- `pipeline/realgraph.py`: tram chains thinned at 0.5 m (`THIN_TRAM_M`), others at 3 m as before; tram ways with
  `service=siding|diversion|spur` that no line runs over go to `spare`, so `gap_fillers` admits them on its T-816
  terms (joining drawn track at two points or more, at most 3 km a group). Yards stay out. Both are tram only, so
  train track (which never follows tram) routes exactly as before.
- `checks/t3515-tram-junctions.mjs` (new): eight service turns in Zurich (2), Melbourne (3), Amsterdam (2) and
  Milan (2) snapped, routed under a length cap, accepted and placed; then the Zurich sweep, every placeable ghost

  waits for it as it waits for the street snap.
- `docs/ui-building.md` "Snapping to the real network": the two rules.
- No app or sim change.

## Options not taken

- **Out-and-back routes.** The router can still return a route that runs out to a terminal loop and back over the
  same track (the old Zurich case), which the track model then refuses. Options: (a) leave it: the ghost shows the
  detour and the model's refusal says why; (b) the router answers "nothing joins these" when its route uses a
  chain twice (cheap, but misses out-on-one-track, back-on-the-other, which the fit also lays on top of itself);
  (c) a search that forbids reusing track (costlier state). A behaviour call; I'd leave it (a) until a player
  meets it with the graphs fixed.
- **Construction track that opens soon.** Zurich's Bahnhofquai tracks come back as `railway=tram` with the next
  OSM refresh after 2026-12-13; keeping `railway=construction` tram track with an `opening_date` would need
  noritetsu's extract to keep that tag. Not done.
- **Light rail corners.** Light rail (followed by both track types) is still thinned at 3 m, so a downtown light
  rail corner (Portland's MAX, say) could read over 60 degrees too. Changing it changes train track's routes over
  light rail; it wants T-3069's London/Chicago/Tokyo comparison rerun first.
- **Ghost under the track bar.** The ghost and its price follow the pointer under the track bar while a click
  there goes to the bar. Stopping mouse moves at the bar would fix it; left, as it misleads only a check.
