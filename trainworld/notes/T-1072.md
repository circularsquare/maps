# T-1072 trains keep their place on the ground when a line's end stops change (bug-b, 2026-11-16)

Cause: `Service::before_edit` kept each running train as `s`, metres from the start of its run, and
`note_changes` looked `s` up again in the new profile. A stop added past an end (or the first stop removed)
moves a run's start, so the other direction's trains jumped by the length added or taken.

Fix (sim/src/track/service.rs): `InFlight::at` keeps the path entry the train is on, which time that entry comes
up in the run, and the metres into it (`ground_at`); `note_changes` finds that entry in the new run
(`offset_at`) and uses its offset. Falls back to the old `s` when the new path lacks the entry; a train standing
at the last stop keeps its old handling.

Test `a_stop_added_at_an_end_moves_no_train` (wasm/t1071_tests.rs): 2-stop line on 12 km of track, stop added at
the far end; without the fix the run-1 train moved 6,000 m, with it none (30 m allowed). cargo test (573 + 760
demand) pass, smoke passed 28 checks. Not checked in the browser by eye.
