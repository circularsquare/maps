# T-1543 The first free-flow round split over the workers

Follow-up to T-1538 (notes/T-1538.md). Tree t1543. Spec: docs/demand-runtime.md "One period in
parts".

## What changed

The rough solve's round 0 (a cold load) and the zone in view's preview after an edit (a new line's
first figures) were each worker's own first period solved, the others estimated from it. Now, with
two or more solving workers, every solving worker solves the same period (the morning peak) for a
part of its origins, estimates every other period from that part, and sends a result per period
marked `part: [k, n]`; the page adds a period's parts up (`addParts`, demandSums.ts) and goes on as
with any result. Then each worker drops the periods it does not hold and solves its own as before,
so later rounds and finals do not change.

- Sim: `DemandApi::set_part(k, n)` / `EverydayApi::set_part`, `forget_period(q)`;
  `kernel::mode_choice_part` deals the origin zones round-robin; the everyday pass deals its pairs
  the same way. A period remembers its part (`Period::part`): the trips and the walking with no rail
  are the first part's, `summary` gives no crowding figures for a part, `kind_totals`,
  `mode_totals` and the everyday bubbles (`modes_in`: a sample counts in the part of its pair, a
  sample with no pair in the first) follow the same rule.
- Worker (`splitRound` in demand.worker.ts): parts only when no period of the cluster is kept here;
  the market is solved whole in the worker's own periods only and its figures go in those periods'
  parts; a rough round's bubble sums go out over all five periods, and the worker's own round 0
  then sends no second set (the page must get one kind from every worker). The worker's own round-0
  results still go out afterwards: the page takes them only for a period whose parts did not all
  come (a worker that kept a period sends none) and ignores results for a round already shown.
- `fareRaw` on a part: the four sums the local fare reach comes from, added on the page and the
  reach worked out again.
- `?perfOff=parts` turns it off.

## Do the parts add up?

`origin_parts_add_up_to_the_period` (cargo test, small zone) and `origin_parts_on_new_york`
(ignored; New York's pack on its real network): the whole period against the parts' sum, every
figure a result carries, as the largest gap over the figure's largest value.

| figure | solved period | estimated periods |
|---|---|---|
| loads (seg, board, alight), load of crush, purposes' loads | 1.1e-6 (float order) | the same |
| commute transfers (`xferIn`'s) | under 1e-6 | under 1e-6 |
| everyday transfers | under 1e-6 | 2-4e-4 |
| summaries (trips, rail, walk, pairs, fare sense), kinds | under 1e-6 | under 1.1e-5 |
| bubbles: commute sums, everyday legs per cell | 2.3e-6, 3.4e-6 | |

The everyday layer's estimate scales transfers and fare extras by the period's rail over the
first period's rail (`EverydayApi::estimate`), and each part's ratio is its own pairs', so these
are near the whole's estimate, not equal; the weekend blocks of `xferIn` weigh the everyday
transfers by each part's purposes the same way. All of it is replaced by the solve within seconds.
The market is never split (one worker's figures per period), so it is exact. The test also has each
part's copy solve the period whole afterwards: to the bit a fresh solve's.

In the browser, the real save's final after a cold load and its final after a Staten Island line,
every figure (t328's signature plus the stations' transfers, and the bubbles' legs), are the same
hash with and without `perfOff=parts` (daebcddc and 97bc68b1; scratch script, fresh browsers).
Worker memory peaks are unchanged (solve peak 291 / 291 / 277 MB both ways: the whole solve's).

## Measured

Native, New York one period's free flow: whole 2.5 s commutes + 0.46 s everyday; a third of the
origins 1.41-1.46 s + 0.22-0.23 s. Each part still finds every station's strategies (about 0.8 s),
which mode choice needs for all destinations, so a part is a little over half the whole, not a third.
