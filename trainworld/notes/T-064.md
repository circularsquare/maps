## How it works

- **Track model** (`sim/src/track/wasm.rs`): `edge_pis(edge)` (each PI with its set radius, level,
  the radius the fit uses and the curve's speed), `preview_edge(edge, pis)` (a trial edit with the
  same output as the drawing preview), `set_edge_pis(edge, pis)` (one undoable blueprint edit; ends
  and tracks kept; constructed edges refused with "Constructed track cannot be moved").
- **Map** (`map/edgeEdit.ts`, `EdgeEditor`): with the select tool and a blueprint stretch
  selected, each PI is a draggable square (DOM, like the drawing tool's markers, moved on every
  MapLibre frame). Dragging asks for a preview on every pointer move through the drawing tool's
  one-in-flight queue (`ClockClient.previewEdge`); the preview strokes and the radius labels
  follow; a refused shape shows its reason by the square and the stroke turns red. Letting go
  sends `edgePis`; a refused edit says why above the toolbar and the squares go back.
- **Inspector** (track inspector, blueprint only): one row per PI with its speed and a radius
  stepper (100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400, 500, 600, 800, 1,000, 1,200, 1,500 m, then auto; from
  auto, minus goes to the next value below the radius in use), and the stretch's level
  (+3 to -3) for every PI at once.

Lines over the stretch keep their path and get new times; nothing wakes demand until the track
is constructed (SPEC 6.5).
