# T-4028: China's populations out of date? (chinapop, 2026-11-22)

Read-only. Only edit: China's population cell in docs/data-register.md (one sentence added).

## What the game uses
Every China pack (metros and zones, e.g. sha, shz, chg, hag, hef built 2026-10-24 to 11-16) states the same
population source in its pack metadata: 7th national census, 2020-11-01, by township (ASPECT 100 m grid,
Ju et al. 2025, figshare 27323106), spread inside townships by GHSL built volume (E2020), T-1150 / T-1185.
Shanghai is further held to its census bulletin's district totals. Nothing newer is used anywhere in China.

## Comparison (prefecture resident totals)
Game pack sums cover the metro extent, not the whole prefecture (Chengdu pack 16.4M vs prefecture 20.9M), so
the comparable thing is the growth since the census, which the game lacks.

provincial/city bulletins as quoted by Yicai, Thepaper, Jiefang Daily, GMW. Resident-population series are
revised between census and yearbooks, so treat gaps as about +-1 point.

## Feedback
`feedback.py --hours 72` and `--hours 720`: no message says China's population is out of date. China
mentions are about rail/air demand (11-21 "improve Chinese station flows", 11-13 mainland airports too low),
the Shenzhen Bay port zone with 0 demand (11-16), ferries. The complaint Anita quotes did not reach the
inbox in that window (maybe Discord or elsewhere).

## Verdict
Gap is small at city level: 2-7% over four years, the fastest being Hefei, Hangzhou, Changsha. Local gaps
could be larger inside the cities that grew by new districts (Hangzhou Qiantang/Linping, Hefei Binhu,
Chengdu Tianfu/Jianyang, Shenzhen Guangming/Pingshan); no district-level 2024 figures were gathered.
Likely cause of the complaint: 2020 figures are 6 years old only in the sense of 4-5 years of 1% growth;
a player seeing "Hefei 9.4M" vs 10M news headlines would notice. Not a data error.

## Proposed fix (not built)
Method any census can feed: a per-prefecture (or per-district where published) growth factor
`newest resident total / census total`, stored in a small CSV beside the source table
(`pipeline/pop_update.csv`: unit id, year, total, source URL), applied as a scaling of township counts within
each unit in `china.plan_hooks` (and the same hook for any other country whose census is older than ~5 years).
Then rebuild China packs and re-run gates.
- CSV for the ~300 prefectures from the 2024 provincial bulletins/ yearbooks: 4-6 agent hours (Chinese
  bulletins, some prefectures only in PDF; verify each against the bulletin, not aggregators).
- Hook plus tests: 2-3 hours.
- Rebuild of 721 China rows and joint solves: mostly compute (T-897: 3-8 hours a China solve), about 2-3
  agent hours of babysitting; every china pack changes so shipped saves/benchmarks shift by a few percent.
- Total roughly 8-12 agent hours. A cheap version: scale only the ~20 fastest-growing metros: 2-3 hours.
Recommendation: low priority; gap is under 7%. Worth doing only if scaling is wanted for all countries
(the same method fits Japan, India, etc.), or as the cheap top-20 version.
