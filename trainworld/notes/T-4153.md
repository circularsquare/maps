## Stage 1: the spikes on station, copy and delete edits

### Where the network's time went (Node, the worker's own code on the Asia save)

`checks/t4153-net.mjs`'s way of running the worker in Node (`stateSteps` from workers/clockMain.ts on the track
model) with a lap per part of `networkSteps`. Node runs about 2-3x faster than the worker in the page while trips are
calculated. Medians of a few runs, ms:

| part | no edit | station placed | line copied | line removed | station removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| network, whole | 300-370 | 330-370 | 500-1080 | 360-530 | 530-950 |
| of it: the bands (`lineBands`) | 20-30 | 20-40 | 180-470 | 170-330 | 200-250 |
| of it: lines drawn again | 11-16 | 12-23 | 30-390 | 20-250 | 18-160 |
| of it: station buildings | 7-14 | 40-50 | 7-22 | 12-16 | 50-200 |

What made the spikes:
- **The bands' order of lines on each shared piece of track** (`lineBands`, T-1799) was worked out again for all
  3,892 shared pieces on every edit that moved any line, walking both lines' paths for each pair. Half the bands' time.
- **The bands' per-piece fingerprint** was a text made for each of about 5,000 pieces on every call.
- **The station buildings' layouts** were kept by a key holding station indices; a station placed or removed moves every
  index after it, so every building's inside was laid out again (40-200 ms).

### What changed

- `map/lineBands.ts`: each shared piece keeps its order with what it was worked out from (its lines, their first entry
  on it, their paths, and every piece the comparisons read). An order is worked out again only when one of its lines
  or paths changed, or a piece it read changed row or points (found through a reverse index, so the check costs the
  changed pieces, not the reads). A copy of a long line now redoes 18-90 orders, a station removed on two long lines
  about 90-280, instead of 3,892. The fingerprint is a record per piece, changed in place.
- `map/lineBands.ts`, `map/corridors.ts`: what the bands and the corridors keep between calls is now one state per
  network cache (`NetCache.bands`). Before, the check build from scratch (`?patch=check`) shared it with the kept
  build, so its fresh points threw the memo away on every check build, and the bands' memo was never tested against
  a truly fresh build. Now it is, byte for byte.
- `map/buildingNet.ts`: building layouts kept by station ids; a kept one gets its station indices moved to where those
  stations are now.
- `map/network.ts`: work counts per build (`netWork`: pieces of track, lines and stations drawn again) and laps
  per part (`netLaps`), for this task's measuring.

After (Node, same edits): station removed 290-490 (bands 60-140, buildings 8-13), line copied 350-660 (bands 70-300,
the larger when the copy shares track with 10 lines), line removed 300-370. The rest of every build is the floor below.

### Stage 2, begun: the floor

Same-answer changes to parts every build pays for, whatever the edit:
- **The fastest train per piece of track** (`network.ts`, T-1271): a line is looked at again only when its row, its
  profile meta or a piece of its path changed (pointer compares, no arrays made), and a piece's fastest train is
  folded again only on the pieces of lines whose speeds changed, kept in `NetCache.trains`. 30-50 down to 5-15 ms.
- **One-way chevrons' distance to the nearest stop** (`oneWayMarks.ts`): a grid of the stops instead of a scan of
  every stop for every mark; the same distance (checked against the scan on the Asia save, 930 marks, 0 differ).
  18-22 down to 4-8 ms.
- **The bands with no rail line changed** (`lineBands.ts`): the same line rows and the same points of every edge they
  use give the kept bands, without laying out every path and making the paths' text. 20-30 down to about 10 ms.
- **One-way legs' shifts at junctions** (`legShifts`): the ends laid out only at nodes such a leg ends at. 15-30 down
  to 1-2 ms.
- **The stations' ways**: each node's own edges sorted instead of one sort of every edge (the same order); node places
  kept as numbers. A few ms each.

## Stage 2: the floor (agent editnetwork2, 2026-11-24)

### What changed (map/network.ts unless named)

- **The edges compared by identity** (`edgeDiff`): the rows are kept while unchanged (workers/stateRows.ts), so a build
  tells changed edges from the last build's by index (or by id when edges were added or removed), and an edit's own edges,
  read again into new arrays of the same numbers, count as moved only.
- **What the edges alone give** (`EdgeSide`: each edge's row by id; per node its edges, counts of single and double
  track ends, joins; one-way legs' shifts) is kept while no edge changed and patched at the end nodes of those that did
  (`patchSide`). The per-node counts, single-track splits' taper test, the stations' ways and the junction places read it.
- **The pieces of track** are worked out again only for changed edges, the edges at their end nodes, and those whose
  single-track split or leg shift changed (`edgePieceDirty`); with none, the last build's pieces, points, drawing order
  and ramp marks are used as they are (`loopSame`), and the maps are copied only once something in them changes.
- **Lines**: a line whose row is the one its piece was made for is kept without comparing its path, stops or edges; its
  samples' lengths, its speeds' path check and the path table's lengths are read from what was kept when its edges are
  unchanged; a line's phase tables are compared where they lie instead of copied out; the count of lines on each edge is
  kept per line row and the last counts kept when equal.
- **Stations**: the stations' ways are sorted again only at nodes whose edges or line counts changed (and share the
  node's own list when already in order); a single-track station's outside, the passing tracks per station, each line's
  turn-backs and the single-lead crossovers are kept with what they were made from; station places come from the node's
  last edge, once per node; the station, junction and id lists are kept while the nodes are.
- **lineBands.ts**: the kept bands are used when the rail lines' ids and paths are the same (a station edit makes new rows
  with the same paths), and the edges' rows come from the network instead of a map made per call.
- **buildingNet.ts**: the edges' boxes are kept per network points map. **facilityPlace.ts**: the edges are not walked while
  the network's points are the same map. **terminalBays.ts**: `singleSplitEnds` takes each node's edges from the caller.
- A fastest-train change is put into the kept pieces as the build finishes instead of copying the map.

## Stage 3: band orders kept through a changed path (agent bandorders, 2026-11-24)

### What changed

- `map/lineBands.ts`, the orders: a kept order now holds, per pair of its lines, the rule's answer and the step each of its two
  walks ended at (where the lines part, or a path ends), and as its reads only the edges where those walks ended (the two
  edges there and the one before, whose rows and points `turn` reads). The edges walked through together are no longer
  reads: equal entries step to the same edge and node whatever the rows say. When a line's path changes, its old and new
  paths are compared once (alike but for one stretch, `pathDiff`), and each kept pair is tested exactly (`walkNow`):
  - neither line's changed stretch within the walk: the same walk;
  - both lines changed alike inside the stretch they walk together (the same steps, the same new entries, each on an edge
    that exists), short of the walk's last two steps: the same answer, its end moved by what the stretch grew;
  - anything else (a line parts at the change, the change is at the walk's end or the step before, only one line changed,
    a line's entry on the edge moved): that pair is walked again. The order is then sorted from the pairs' answers, as
    before.
  - A walk that passed two entries differing but read alike (both ways along an edge from a node to itself) is never kept;
    lines on an edge that is gone (its row gone, its path still naming it) have every pair walked again.
- `map/corridors.ts`: past 64 changed edges every edge's samples were worked out again (a line removed with its own track
  of 100+ edges: 500-700 ms of 820-850). Now each edge is tested against every change (inside the box of all of them
  first), and all are worked out again only past 4 million tests (`MANY_TESTS`).
- `map/corridors.ts`, a bug found on the way (on master too): the kept samples could list a sample's tracks in another
  order than a build from scratch (the same tracks), as the edges came in the order the lines name them, which moves when
  a line is removed or put back, and edges new since the last call came last. The ribbons are walked from those records,
  so the bands came out a little different: on Asia, removing line 201, undoing it, then removing line 4 drew six lines'
  bands differently from a full build (`?patch=check`: "the network built from the cache differs"). The edges are now
  taken in id order, and the kept ones laid out in it again after an edit.
- The bands' whole check (`?patch=check`, T-2238) now works the corridors out from scratch too; it shared the kept ones,
  so a slip in them was in both sides and only the network check saw it.
- `checks/t4153-net.mjs` prints the pairs kept on edges where their lines' paths changed, so a run shows the new test used.

## Stage 4: lines made again entry by entry (agent cutstretch, 2026-11-24)

### What changed (map/network.ts unless named)

- **A line made again copies its entries' strokes** from its last strokes where an entry is drawn as it was: the same
  piece of track (`keptEdge`), the same flags, drawn in a band or not alike, and, in a band, the same knots bit for bit
  (`sameKnots`; the recipes' numbers are read only as `bandPoints` reads them: none, or two in a row differing). The
  copy (`StrokeBuilder.copy`) works the stretch and the distances along out again as `add` does, including the
  float32 step back `add` takes when an entry's last point is near the one before (`LineEntry.bits`: popped, band's
  width zeroed, banded). Old and new paths are matched as alike but for one stretch (`alikeEntries`).
  - Before the cut every entry is copied. After it, a line alone on its track (all of New York) copies every entry
    too; a line in bands does not, as the cut moves the knots after it by 1e-6 to 1e-5 (the cm rounding of knot places
    in path metres), which moves its points in float32. Measured on Asia (`scratch cutstretch/probe3.mjs`): station on
    line 33's trunk, 661 of 909 entries copied, the 247 after the cut all differ; lines 112 and 69 likewise.
  - A stop change keeps the same entries, so the whole line is copied with its stretches renumbered.
- **A line's samples made again copy the samples** on the entries before the first unlike the last ones' (the same
  entry, piece of track, stroke and band knots; `alikeSamples`). After the cut they are worked out, as the run's
  metres there move.
- `laneAlong` keeps each piece's distances along (by its drawn points) and bisects them instead of summing the piece
  twice per sample; the same answer.
- **Fastest trains** (`lineTrainSpeeds`): each run's phase found by stepping from the last found (`phaseFinder`),
  the same phase the bisection finds while the phases are in order (checked per line; out of order it bisects).
- `bandPoints` writes into kept working arrays (`bpScratch`) instead of growing four number arrays per entry; a new
  line builder is given room for the line's last stroke count at once.
- A kept line whose band was renumbered only (any cut renumbers the recipes of every line after it) is known by its
  profile in its own numbering (`LineBands.own`, a new array only when its bands are worked out again) instead of
  comparing the whole profile.
- **lineBands.ts**: the second "same bands" test (every line's path written into one text) is gone: the first test
  (rows and points by identity, then paths) already decides the same cases. The Traffic table copies a line's records
  from the last table where its recipes are numbered alike (`LineMemo.tab`). The per-edge record pass and `lowest`
  read an edge's lines in place instead of making an array per edge.
- `checks/t4153-net.mjs`: a new edit (a station on track a long line shares, partway along), counts of entries and
  samples copied, and `--self-test=strokes` (entries copied whatever their knots; a station on the longest line's
  shared track must fail the check, and does).

### Found on the way

### Where the cost was, and what changed

Read from the code first: an edit's build still went over every station (their arrays, flags and outside), every
building against every station and every piece of track, every edge for the facilities' grid, every station's
single-track split walk, every leg's shift, every kept band order along a cut line (an array set per edge), and every
corridor edge twice. Each now starts from what the edit changed (the edges `edgeDiff` names, the nodes and lines whose
rows changed) and redoes only what can read those; the rest is the last finished build's.

- **Stations** (map/network.ts): the stations whose numbers can differ are worked out (`stDirty`: nodes whose row
  changed or came or went, the nodes of changed edges and pieces made again, nodes whose ways were sorted again, whose
  lines through them or stops changed, whose split changed, and single-track stations whose way out to a join read an
  edge or node that changed, now recorded with the outside); the rest are copied from the last build's arrays
  (`NetCache.stArr`, a run of them at a time when a station came or went). The lines on each edge and the lines stopping
  at each node are patched from the lines whose rows changed (`countsOf`), and with them `lined` and the stations' ways.
  stations.loop 9.4 -> 1.6-1.9, stations.ways 8 -> 3.3-3.5.
- **Node rows compared by identity** (`nodeChanges`): each node id gets a slot of its own (`NetCache.nodeSlots`; her
  save's node ids are far apart, so an index array by id took 1.3 s to make in a first version).
- **Single-track splits** (map/terminalBays.ts `splitsWhole`, `patchSplits`): each station's part kept with the nodes
  and edges its walks read; only stations whose walks read a changed node or edge are walked again, and the ends of the
  edges they name laid out again in node order. **Leg shifts** (`patchLegShifts`): only legs with an end at a node
  whose edges changed. pre.counts+splits 11 -> 6.1-6.5.
- **Station buildings** (map/buildingNet.ts): the stations near a building found through a grid, and the edges' boxes
  kept with the network's cache by id (`EdgeBoxes`, `NetCache.bldBoxes`) and patched from the changed edges; a
  building's tracks are taken in edge id order (the layout reads them as a set). buildings 8.7 -> 2.7.
- **Facilities** (map/facilityPlace.ts): the grid's edges patched from the changed edges when it was made from the map
  they are relative to (as the bands'). 8.7 -> 3.0-3.5.
- **Band orders** (map/lineBands.ts): an edge along a cut line whose lines are the same and every pair's walk holds is
  changed in place (no arrays made): orders 9.4 -> 4.7-5.3.
- **Corridors** (map/corridors.ts): the kept edges' boxes in a grid of cells with the most local units a metre of any of
  them kept (`BoxIx`), so an edit's call finds the edges near what changed without two passes over every edge; made at
  the end of a whole call, so the first edit does not wait for it. corridors 6.2 -> 1.7-1.8, first click 29 -> 4.
- **Lining up ribbon changes** (`alignChanges`): a long group by a grid of its places; and `pointOn` finds its segment
  by halves. align spikes 5-10 -> 4-7 (most of what is left is `nearM`'s point lookups).
- **Turn-backs**: a line's calls the same as kept are the kept list, and the strokes are the last build's when every
  list is. **Pieces of track**: with rows changed the points map is laid out once, the drawing order kept when equal,
  ramps gathered without an iterator per piece.
