# T-4153 Rebuild only the touched part of the drawn network (agent editnetwork, 2026-11-23)

Anita, 11-23: "this is the entire issue. this is what we should fix", not left to the track rework.

## Stage 1: the spikes on station, copy and delete edits

### Where the network's time went (Node, the worker's own code on the Asia save)

`checks/t4153-net.mjs`'s way of running the worker in Node (`stateSteps` from workers/clockMain.ts on the track
model) with a lap per part of `networkSteps`. Node runs about 2-3x faster than the worker in the page while trips are
calculated. Medians of a few runs, ms:

| part | no edit | station placed | line copied | line removed | station removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| network, whole | 300-370 | 330-370 | 500-1080 | 360-530 | 530-950 |
| of it: the bands (`lineBands`) | 20-30 | 20-40 | 180-470 | 170-330 | 200-250 |
| of it: lines drawn again | 11-16 | 12-23 | 30-390 | 20-250 | 18-160 |
| of it: station buildings | 7-14 | 40-50 | 7-22 | 12-16 | 50-200 |

What made the spikes:
- **The bands' order of lines on each shared piece of track** (`lineBands`, T-1799) was worked out again for all
  3,892 shared pieces on every edit that moved any line, walking both lines' paths for each pair. Half the bands' time.
- **The bands' per-piece fingerprint** was a text made for each of about 5,000 pieces on every call.
- **The station buildings' layouts** were kept by a key holding station indices; a station placed or removed moves every
  index after it, so every building's inside was laid out again (40-200 ms).

### What changed

- `map/lineBands.ts`: each shared piece keeps its order with what it was worked out from (its lines, their first entry
  on it, their paths, and every piece the comparisons read). An order is worked out again only when one of its lines
  or paths changed, or a piece it read changed row or points (found through a reverse index, so the check costs the
  changed pieces, not the reads). A copy of a long line now redoes 18-90 orders, a station removed on two long lines
  about 90-280, instead of 3,892. The fingerprint is a record per piece, changed in place.
- `map/lineBands.ts`, `map/corridors.ts`: what the bands and the corridors keep between calls is now one state per
  network cache (`NetCache.bands`). Before, the check build from scratch (`?patch=check`) shared it with the kept
  build, so its fresh points threw the memo away on every check build, and the bands' memo was never tested against
  a truly fresh build. Now it is, byte for byte.
- `map/buildingNet.ts`: building layouts kept by station ids; a kept one gets its station indices moved to where those
  stations are now.
- `map/network.ts`: work counts per build (`netWork`: pieces of track, lines and stations drawn again) and laps
  per part (`netLaps`), for this task's measuring.

After (Node, same edits): station removed 290-490 (bands 60-140, buildings 8-13), line copied 350-660 (bands 70-300,
the larger when the copy shares track with 10 lines), line removed 300-370. The rest of every build is the floor below.

### Stage 2, begun: the floor

Same-answer changes to parts every build pays for, whatever the edit:
- **The fastest train per piece of track** (`network.ts`, T-1271): a line is looked at again only when its row, its
  profile meta or a piece of its path changed (pointer compares, no arrays made), and a piece's fastest train is
  folded again only on the pieces of lines whose speeds changed, kept in `NetCache.trains`. 30-50 down to 5-15 ms.
- **One-way chevrons' distance to the nearest stop** (`oneWayMarks.ts`): a grid of the stops instead of a scan of
  every stop for every mark; the same distance (checked against the scan on the Asia save, 930 marks, 0 differ).
  18-22 down to 4-8 ms.
- **The bands with no rail line changed** (`lineBands.ts`): the same line rows and the same points of every edge they
  use give the kept bands, without laying out every path and making the paths' text. 20-30 down to about 10 ms.
- **One-way legs' shifts at junctions** (`legShifts`): the ends laid out only at nodes such a leg ends at. 15-30 down
  to 1-2 ms.
- **The stations' ways**: each node's own edges sorted instead of one sort of every edge (the same order); node places
  kept as numbers. A few ms each.

## Stage 2: the floor (agent editnetwork2, 2026-11-24)

### What changed (map/network.ts unless named)

- **The edges compared by identity** (`edgeDiff`): the rows are kept while unchanged (workers/stateRows.ts), so a build
  tells changed edges from the last build's by index (or by id when edges were added or removed), and an edit's own edges,
  read again into new arrays of the same numbers, count as moved only.
- **What the edges alone give** (`EdgeSide`: each edge's row by id; per node its edges, counts of single and double
  track ends, joins; one-way legs' shifts) is kept while no edge changed and patched at the end nodes of those that did
  (`patchSide`). The per-node counts, single-track splits' taper test, the stations' ways and the junction places read it.
- **The pieces of track** are worked out again only for changed edges, the edges at their end nodes, and those whose
  single-track split or leg shift changed (`edgePieceDirty`); with none, the last build's pieces, points, drawing order
  and ramp marks are used as they are (`loopSame`), and the maps are copied only once something in them changes.
- **Lines**: a line whose row is the one its piece was made for is kept without comparing its path, stops or edges; its
  samples' lengths, its speeds' path check and the path table's lengths are read from what was kept when its edges are
  unchanged; a line's phase tables are compared where they lie instead of copied out; the count of lines on each edge is
  kept per line row and the last counts kept when equal.
- **Stations**: the stations' ways are sorted again only at nodes whose edges or line counts changed (and share the
  node's own list when already in order); a single-track station's outside, the passing tracks per station, each line's
  turn-backs and the single-lead crossovers are kept with what they were made from; station places come from the node's
  last edge, once per node; the station, junction and id lists are kept while the nodes are.
- **lineBands.ts**: the kept bands are used when the rail lines' ids and paths are the same (a station edit makes new rows
  with the same paths), and the edges' rows come from the network instead of a map made per call.
- **buildingNet.ts**: the edges' boxes are kept per network points map. **facilityPlace.ts**: the edges are not walked while
  the network's points are the same map. **terminalBays.ts**: `singleSplitEnds` takes each node's edges from the caller.
- A fastest-train change is put into the kept pieces as the build finishes instead of copying the map.

### What is left

- **A station on a line's track, a copied or removed line**: the bands. Cutting an edge in two changes the paths of the lines
  on it, and every kept order whose walk read that edge (all along a shared trunk) is worked out again: 220-490 orders,
  85-340 ms in Node. Most of those walks only pass through the cut edge with both lines, which cannot change the order; an
  exact test for that (a walk that passes through, not one that parts there or turns at its end) is the next step.
  Tried: keeping an order when its walk read nothing near the changed part of a path; no help, as the walks do read it.
- **complexOfNodes** (workers/clockDemand.ts, 7-15 ms in Node) is counted in the page's network build time and worked out
  again on every edit.
- The rest is about twenty parts of 1-8 ms each (the stations' flags, one-way chevrons' nearest stop, the station links,
  the sample arrays written whole, the passing samples). The sample arrays (about 10 MB on Asia) are made and written
  every build and mostly thrown away as the same as last posted; reusing the posted copies needs a hook in
  workers/clockPost.ts.
