# T-2018 An express behind a local never halts mid-route

Anita, 2026-11-02: "the express seems to be stopping kinda randomly at like 4 waypoints ... it
should either wait at the station before ..., or just travel at a slower speed matching the avg
speed of the train in front (prob fine), or just literally trail behind the train in front, staying
close behind (ideal). but not stop randomly". Agent t2018.

## Cause

T-1924 baked an express's following loss into its timetable as up to 4 stands a stretch
(`FOLLOW_WAITS_MAX`, at least 90 s each), outside the stations the local stops at. Those stands are
in the run profile, so the express was drawn standing there whether or not a local was in front.

## What changed

- `capacity::holds_for`: a following loss is one spread hold over the stretch (`Hold::span`; the
  Follow resource's one owner carries the stretch's start in `at` and end in `occ`).
- `profile::run_with`: a spread hold lowers the speed over its span, one cap for every hop in it,
  bisected (to 0.05 s) so the run takes exactly the loss longer; below 0.5 m/s the rest waits at
  the stop before the span. Run times, holds, trains, demand: the same totals; only where along the
  stretch the time is lost moves (an express stopping between the ends now loses it in proportion
  to running time rather than at the 4 places).
- Drawn: the express runs slower than the local between stations and closes on it while it stands
  at a station; there the display guard (T-1965) holds it 30 m behind, so it trails.

ends). "Before" is the last commit's build on 8800. Halts: frames an express has not moved since
the last, away from its own stops, with the local ahead of it moving.
