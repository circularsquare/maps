# T-3731 Stop edits lost on large saves

Agent stoplost, 2026-11-18, tree `stoplost`.

## Cause

`map/tools.ts`: each stop click on the map is drawn at once (`StopMarkers.guessClick`, T-3159; the panel row by
`guessRow`) and queued, each sent once the one before is answered (T-2766: it reads the stops that one left). The
queued click (`toggle`) started with `if (session !== this.lineSession || tool.peek() !== "line") return;` and read
the line from `lineDraft`. Ending stop editing (Done, Escape, Enter, another tool or line) bumped `lineSession` and
cleared `lineDraft`, so every click still waiting returned silently; `settleClick` then put the hidden marker back,
and editing again showed the stops as before. Only the click in flight at the exit landed. No refusal, epoch or lease
was involved: the worker queues edits in order and saves whatever it was sent.

## Reproduced (before)

`checks/play.mjs` with the probe `scratchpad/stoplost/probe.js` (kept in the agent's scratch folder): select the
longest rail line, the panel's Edit stops, click 5-8 of its inner stops' markers on the map, leave at once, wait until
the stops stop changing.

- Real NYC save, 8 clicks 20 ms apart, Escape: 1 of 8 lost (clicks answered in about 150-300 ms, so only the last was
  waiting).
- `artifacts/trainworld/t3362/na476.save` (158 lines): clicks answered in 1.2-3.8 s while its first trips were
  calculated, about 0.3 s after. Control (each click waited for): 0 lost.
- na476 with a slow sim stood in for (200 `stopTiers` reads queued on the clock worker behind the first click,
  about 5 s of its time), 8 clicks, Done button: 7 shown taken out, 1 landed by the exit, 2 in all; **6 lost**.

## Fix

- `game/world.ts`: the stop click queue (`queueStopEdit`, `stopsQueued`, `afterStopEdits`). An undo or redo waits for
  it (it takes back the last click shown). Closing the tab while a click is out does not ask: a "Leave site?"
  prompt was tried and dropped (Anita, 2026-11-18: "its a bit weird to ask "Leave site?" for this if we dont do that
  for other cases"). Every wait gives up once no click has been answered for 10 s
  (`STOPS_GIVE_UP_MS`, as clockClient's `stopOut`), so a worker that never answers holds nothing for ever.
- `map/tools.ts`: a click takes its line and `gamesLoaded` at the click and is sent however stop editing ended; only a
  game loaded since (ids renumbered) or a line gone drops it. `lineDraft` is updated only while that line is still
  being edited. `newLine` (T-1899: train type by track, colour re-pick) is cleared after the clicks still out are
  sent, so a new line's late clicks keep its rules. The busy tag shows "Saving stops" while clicks are out and no
  stop editing is on.
- `map/stopMarkers.ts` and `ui/inspector/stopRowGuess.ts`: the click's drawn guess (marker, panel row) is keyed by
  line and station, so line A's clicks still out are never drawn on line B's markers or rows.
- `workers/clockClient.ts`: a save (`manageSaveSlot`), leaving the game (`leave`: save and quit, opening another game,
  deleting it) and a reload's save (`saveBeforeReload`) wait for the clicks.
- docs/architecture.md, the T-2766 paragraph.

## After

Same probe, na476 with the stand-in slow sim, 5-8 clicks: 0 lost for Done, Escape, Enter, another tool, another
line's Edit stops and the panel closed (which keeps stop editing on). Real NYC save, Escape and Done (with and without
the stand-in): 0 lost. The tag showed "Saving stops" during the drain (screenshot
`artifacts/trainworld/t3731-saving-stops.png`; it is hidden while the loading card is up, as every busy tag).
Undo pressed straight after Done with 6 clicks out: the 6 landed, then the undo took back the last one. Five clicks,
Done, five undos: the stops came back one at a time. Ctrl+S straight after Done: the save was posted after the last
stop edit. Save and quit to main menu with 5 clicks out (`scratchpad/stoplost/leave.js`, the leave recorded, not
sent): five `setStops`, then `leave`. A new line (created, 5 clicks queued, left at once): all 5 applied, `newLine`
kept until sent, then cleared. Another line's Edit stops mid-drain: its markers at the first line's clicked
stations read "cut" during and after the drain (no borrowed guess).

One click in the na476 runs (the 6th, on line 76, before and after the fix alike) did nothing: its marker never
showed the change ("on" before the exit), no notice, and the line lost exactly the other 7. A missed click (likely the
hover tag by the pointer over the next marker), not a lost edit: what the map showed was applied.

No standing check: the probe needs the stand-in slow worker to show the loss with power; it stays in the scratch
folder (`probe.js`, globals at its top).

## Not changed (for a later task)

The line panel's own stop edits: `stopEdit` and `moveStop` ignore a second click while one is out (module flag
`stopEditing`; nothing is drawn for it, so it does not look applied), and `setStops`/`removeStop`/`takeOut` build the
list from the rendered `l.stops`, so two quick dot clicks can send the second from stale stops. Not the reported
path; worth folding into the same queue (computing the op when it is sent).

Bus lines edited manually (`map/busStops.ts`) send each click at once and were not affected.
