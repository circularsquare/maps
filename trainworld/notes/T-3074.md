# T-3074: the new-game picker's inter-city row

## What was wrong

The picker's hover (overview bubbles, `app/src/map/intercityView.ts` `tag`) wrote the inter-city row
from `totals`, which is an all-zero array until the market's first result (`intercityDemand`) lands.
A start on the world map has no result yet, so every city read "0 inter-city trips per day". The
"within" row is fine: it comes from the registry's `localTrips`.

## Fix (done)

With no market result the inter-city row and its mode bar are left out of the hover. Once a result
lands the row shows as before (a real 0 for a city with no pairs still shows). Other users of the
figure: `CityInspector.tsx` already waits on `market` ("Calculating"), the "between" and selected-city
lines only exist with a result (`own` is cleared without one). Nothing else needed the change.

## Precompute (not done, needs a sim tool)

What the row shows once a game runs is `pairs(corridors).totals[zone]`: the zone place's longer trips
and commute legs with every other place of the market, times the day's scales
(`longerScale`, `commuteScale`, from the solve's `corridor_shares(q)`), minus by-rail adjustments,
plus the rail share. The corridor table (`corridor_rows_*` in `sim/src/demand/intercity/exports.rs`)
is made by the WASM from the market packs (hundreds of `data/packs/*.market.json`, 12.7M corridors
for the world), and the scales exist only after a period is solved. So a precomputed figure needs:

1. a new tool in `sim/` (off limits to this agent) that loads every market pack and sums, per
   registry zone place, `longer` and commute legs of the corridor table, i.e. `totalsOf` in
   intercityView.ts, before scales;
2. the scale for the picker's day (`longerScale`, `commuteScale`), taken from one solved world run;
3. the sums written beside `localTrips` as `interTrips` in `app/src/game/registry.json` from
   `pipeline/zones.py` `local_trips()` (that is the path `localTrips` follows), then the hover uses
   `c.interTrips` while `pairsIn` is null.

Without a solve the by-rail part is 0 anyway, so the figure is "no rail" all-modes trips, which is
what the game shows before any line is built. The scales and the 12.7M-corridor load make it longer
than 15 minutes with no existing bin to reuse, so only the leave-it-out fix is in.
