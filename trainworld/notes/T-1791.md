# T-1791 Line groups (design for Anita)

Her ask (TODO, 2026-10-29): run different kinds of service on one line without it falling apart
into unrelated lines; group lines by dropping one on another in the Lines tab; a copy stays in
its source's group; a group view with one combined diagram where a click on a line's dot at a
station takes that stop out, and a ghost where it passes lets you add one; the Lines tab shows a
group as one row that opens to its lines. The dim bullet for a line with no trains is T-1792.

## What the player sees and does

- **Making a group.** In the Lines tab, drag a line's row onto another row. They become one
  group row. Dropping onto a group row adds the line to that group. Copy line on any train line
  puts the copy in the source's group (making a group of two if the source had none) and opens
  the group view, so "copy the 4, take stops off" is two steps.
- **Leaving a group.** Drag a line out of an open group row and drop it anywhere outside the
  group. The line panel also has a "Leave group" icon (for touch, where dragging is fiddly). A
  group left with one line stops being a group. Deleting a line takes it out of its group.
- **Undo** takes back a join, a leave or a copy's joining as one step each.
- **The Lines tab.** A group is one row: the chips of its lines, then the name of its oldest line
  (the lowest number, so the 4 and not "4 copy"), and the group's totals: riders, money and trains
  summed over its lines. A chevron at the row's start opens it to show each line indented, with
  its own figures, sorted by the tab's sort. The group row sorts among the lines by its totals;
  Near uses its nearest stop; City its oldest line's. A group shows when any of its lines is in
  the viewed place, and then shows all of them. Open or closed is kept while the page is open.
  Riders summed over lines count a rider who changes between two lines of the group twice; the
  hover says "Riders on each line, added up". A click on the group row opens the group view; a
  click on an indented line opens that line's panel, as now. Double click fits the map to all of
  the group's stops.
- **The group view** (a panel like the line panel). Title: the chips and the oldest line's name.
  Under it, one plain row per line (chip, name, its trains now) as links to each line's panel,
  and Copy line per row. Then the combined diagram. No times, no Past 24 hours, no schedule, no
  round trip. Boardings only if they fit: left out of stage 2, a later stage if wanted.
- **The combined diagram.** Stations are rows, top to bottom; each line is a column, side by
  side, in its line's colour, with its bullet at the top. On each row a column shows one of:
  - a **stop**: a dot (the white pill of the related-lines drawing, one per column). Hover "4
    stops at 14 St-Union Sq. Click to remove the stop". Click takes the stop out.
  - **passing**: the column's bar runs through with no mark. Hover shows a ghost dot (white,
    40%) and "4 passes 23 St. Click to add a stop". Click adds it.
  - **not there**: the column has a gap; nothing to hover. The line's path does not come here.
  A line running no trains at the level in force is drawn at 40%, its bullet dimmed, hover "No
  trains at high demand" (the same rule T-1792 sets everywhere, so whichever lands second reuses
  the first's function). While the pointer is on a column, the map draws that line full and the
  group's other lines at 30%, as stop editing does.
- **Short turns.** Copy the 4, then click the dots at one end, one by one, to take them out. The
  line's path shortens with them: a line's path only runs between its first and last stops, so
  the trains turn at the new end. Shift-click on a stop takes out every stop past it toward the
  nearer end ("Shift-click to end the line here"), so a short turn is one click. Turning at a
  through station is limited to 15 trains an hour on its platform (track-and-service 6.3), and
  the existing capacity alert says so when the short turn runs more.

## What a group is in the model

**Recommended: only a grouping, for the player's eyes.** Each line keeps its own trains,
schedule, stops, path, fleet and figures, and the sim runs them exactly as now. A group is a
number on each line (0 for none), saved with the line. "Different kinds of service on the same
line" is then a group of lines: the local, the express, the short turn.

The other way, a group the sim knows (one fleet shared out, one timetable spacing the services
evenly on their shared stretch), changes departures, the hand-over at the ends, fleet counting
and the save, and every line figure would need a group version. It is much bigger and none of
her asks need it. If evenly spaced services on the shared stretch matter later, that can be a
P3 on top (offset one line's departures by half a headway), without merging the lines.

Where it lives: a `group: u32` on the sim's `LineData`, saved in a new trailing `LGR1` block of
the track save (line index and group, only for grouped lines; a save without it loads every
line ungrouped, as other trailing blocks do). Putting it on the line data in the sim, rather than
in the save's JSON header, gives three things for free: `copy_line` clones the line data, so a
copy keeps its source's group; undo works through the existing ops; and deleting a line takes
its group with it. The sim reads it for nothing else. A group change must not count as a service
edit: no demand re-solve, no "constructing...", like a colour change. The group's number is
arbitrary; the app names it by its oldest line.

## The combined diagram's layout

- **Rows**: start from the group's longest line (most stops) as the spine, in its stop order,
  with the stations it passes between them. Each other line is matched to the spine by station
  complex (as related lines do, within 400 m), and turned round if it runs the other way. Its own
  stations not on the spine go in where it leaves the spine: between the two spine rows it leaves
  and rejoins at, or after the spine's last row it shares for a branch at an end (the 2 and 5 in
  the Bronx), or before its first.
- **Rows for stations no line stops at** (every line passes): shown, muted, so a stop can be
  added there. Stations no line's path reaches are not rows.
- **Columns**: one per line, equal width, in the order the lines are listed (oldest first), each
  drawn only over the rows on its path, with a gap and a short curve where a branch line leaves
  the rows and comes back. A loop shows once round as the line panel does.
- Up to 6 lines fit at 14 px a column in the narrow dock; past that the panel scrolls sideways
  in the diagram only. A group past 6 is rare; no cap.

## What exists to reuse, and what is new

Exists:
- `TrackApi.insert_stop` and `set_stops` (sim/src/track/wasm/lines.rs): adding a stop at a
  station on the line's path keeps the path (stop tier 1). Enough for the ghost click.
- `TrackApi.passed_stations`: the stations a line passes, by gap. Enough for "passing" cells.
- `TrackApi.copy_line`: copies the line data, so it copies the group once the field is there.
- `Op::Batch`: one undo for a join that changes several lines.
- `game/relatedLayout.ts` and `ui/inspector/RelatedStops.tsx`: the pill, bar and turn drawing,
  complex matching of rows, hovers. The group view's layout is new (equal columns, no spine
  line in the middle) but draws with the same pieces and constants (BAR, LINE, GAP, pills).
- The line panel's stop list and `setStops`, the Lines tab's rows and sort.

New:
- Sim: the `group` field, the `LGR1` block, `set_line_group(lines, group)` as one batch, the
  group in the lines' data the app reads. This changes the WASM interface, so that stage runs in
  its own worktree.
- Sim: **`drop_stop(line, i)`**, taking a stop out and keeping the path. Today the panel's remove
  sends `set_stops` without the stop, which finds the route again: taking a local's middle stop
  out can move it onto faster express track between its neighbours. In the group view a removal
  should never move the line, so the path stays and only past an end is it cut back to the new
  end stop. Does not exist yet; small.
- App: drag and drop on Lines tab rows (pointer events; no drag library), the group row and its
  totals, the group view panel, `game/groupLayout.ts` with a Node check like t1168-layout.mjs.

## Stages (one agent each)

1. **Groups in the model and the Lines tab** (medium; Opus, sim interface plus list design;
   own worktree). The field, `LGR1`, `set_line_group`, copy joining the group, drag a row onto a
   row, drag out, Leave group in the line panel, the group row with chevron, totals and sorting.
   Clicking a group row selects its oldest line for now (the group view is stage 2).
   *Done when*: dragging the 5 onto the 4 on the real save gives one row "4" with both chips and
   riders, money and trains equal to the two lines' sums; it opens to both lines with their own
   figures; Copy line on the 4 puts "4 copy" in the group; dragging out and Leave group both
   ungroup, and a group of one disappears; undo takes back each of these; save and reload keeps
   the groups and the real save loads ungrouped; no group edit shows "constructing..." or starts
   a demand solve; typecheck, `cargo test` and smoke pass.
2. **The group view, read only** (medium-large; Opus, layout calls on branches and order). The
   panel, `groupLayout.ts` with its check, stops, passing and gaps, branches, dimmed lines with
   no trains, the map highlight on hover. Copy line opens it.
3. **Editing in the diagram** (medium; Sonnet, the design is settled by then). `drop_stop` in the
   sim, click a dot to remove, ghost and click to add, Shift-click to end here, refusals shown as
   the stop markers show them.
4. **Boardings in the diagram** if Anita wants them after playing (small).

## Decisions for Anita (each with my pick)

1. Model: a grouping only, each line still its own service, or a shared timetable? **Grouping
   only.**
2. Copy line always groups the copy with its source and opens the group view, even when the
   source had no group? **Yes**: copying to make an unrelated line is rarer, and drag out undoes it.
3. The group's name: its oldest line's, or a name of its own you can type? **Oldest line's**,
   no new name box.
4. Taking a stop out in the group view keeps the line's track (never moves it onto faster
   track). Should the line panel's own remove button do the same? **Yes for the group view;
   leave the line panel as it is** unless you've seen a remove move a line.
5. Rows for stations every line of the group passes and none stops at: shown so you can add a
   stop there, or left out to keep it short? **Shown**, muted.
