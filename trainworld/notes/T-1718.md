# T-1718: are bus money params reasonable?

**Summary.** Buses lose money by design, and they are not worse off than real city buses: the game's
feeders cover 17-84% of their costs in the real save (NYC Transit buses about 20%, London about 68%).
What feels wrong is the contrast with rail: the real save's 109 rail lines take in 2.5 times what they
cost. Rail running costs sit at about a quarter of real ones and buses' at about two thirds of real, so
in the game a bus costs 7.5 times a rail car per place-km, against about 3-4 times in life.
If Anita wants buses to break even more often, lowering `RUN_COST_BUS_KM` from 9 to 4-6 is the one
lever (proposals below). Nothing was changed.

## How it was measured

`artifacts/trainworld/t1715/money-before.json` (new New York game: one 4 km rail line across Queens and
four constructed bus lines) and `money-real.json` (the real save `T-007-real-nyc.save`, which has no
buses, plus six 3.2 km bus lines through stations in Brooklyn and Manhattan). Weekday figures, demand
final, `?demandWorkers=1`. Fares are the income module's per-line figures (`incomeByDay`), running
cost tph x 2 x km x per-km x economy over the day (as `runningToday`), upkeep `LineStats.upkeep`.
Probe scripts: the tbus scratchpad (`bus.mjs`, `real.mjs`), not kept.

Parameters (sim/src/track/params.rs, app/src/game/fareCurve.ts): economy 100x; bus US$9 a bus-km,
upkeep US$30 a route-km a day, US$0.6M a bus, US$1M a route-km; rail US$4 a train-km plus US$2 a car-km
(160 a car at crush, a bus 80), upkeep US$300 a route-km and US$500 a station a day. Default New York
fare US$1.00 plus 8 cents a km, one curve for bus and rail.

Costs per unit: a bus-km is US$9.1 real all in (upkeep is about 1% of it), US$144 a bus-hour at the
16 km/h the lines averaged. The real save's rail is US$24 a train-km, US$2.6 a car-km.

A bus breaks even at about 10 riders a bus-km at the game's fare (8 at New York's new-game fare of
about US$1.15 a ride, 10.5 at the real save's US$0.86, lower because the save's riders change more and
the base is shared out over boardings). The measured lines run 1.8 to 25: only a busy Manhattan avenue
clears it easily, a feeder into a station sits near it, and crosstown or outer lines are far under.

Bus fares a rider are lower in the real save (US$0.86 against rail's US$1.51) for two reasons that are
the design, not a slip: bus rides are short (8 cents a km on 2 km), and every boarding's base is
scaled by trips over boardings, so a feeder rider who changes to a train pays most of the base on the
train's line.

So real city buses mostly do not pay their way, and NYC's are among the worst. The game's buses
cover more of their costs than NYC's do, with about 40% of the real fare (US$1.15 a ride against
US$2.90) and about half the real cost a bus-hour.

## Against the game's own rail

Real NYC Transit: a subway car-km costs roughly the same as a bus-km or a little less (around
US$10 against US$15), and a car holds 2 to 3 times what a bus does, so a bus costs about 3-4 times a car
per place-km. In the game, a car-km is US$2.4 (US$2 plus a tenth of a 10-car train's US$4) and a bus-km
US$9, 3.75 times; per place-km (160 against 80) a bus costs 7.5 times a car. Rail's running costs were
set "under real figures so fares can cover it at 100x" (params.rs); bus running cost was raised from
3 to 9 in T-1576 to match NTD's per bus-hour figures, with no such discount. That one asymmetry is why
a rail network is a money machine and buses beside it look like a loss.

## Proposals (Anita's call; nothing changed)

1. **Keep US$9** (the honest option): buses stay a subsidised feeder, as in most cities; busy avenue
   lines profit, feeders about break even, crosstown and outer lines lose. Effect: none.
2. **US$6 a bus-km** (two thirds): the measured recoveries go up 1.5 times: the new game's feeder 1.01
   to 1.5, crosstown 0.36 to 0.54, Flatbush 0.23 to 0.35; the real save's six lines 0.17-0.83 to
   0.25-1.25. Break-even falls to about 7 riders a bus-km. Still well under rail's 2.5.
3. **US$4 a bus-km** (rail's discount on real costs): recoveries up 2.25 times: feeder 2.3,
   crosstown 0.81, Flatbush 0.52; real-save lines 0.38-1.9. Break-even about 4.5 riders a bus-km: most
   feeders profit, as rail lines do; a bus is then 3.3 times a car per place-km, close to life. This
   undoes most of T-1576's raise, which Anita approved, so it is her decision to reverse.
4. Not proposed: a separate bus fare (T-1445 chose one curve so bus to train is a free change), or a
   lower upkeep (1% of a bus line's costs, no effect).

My pick would be 2 or 3 if bus lines should feel like something a player builds for money; 1 if they
are a service the rail pays for. Changing it is one constant (`RUN_COST_BUS_KM`, params.rs) and its
pinned copy in money_params; the Money tab's rule text follows.
