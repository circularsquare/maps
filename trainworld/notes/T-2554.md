throwaway script in the agent's scratchpad.

**Proposed (a separate small task, if Anita agrees):**
1. `routes.py`: stop writing `drive_near_f.npy` (one line); with the after-join deletion, 6 GB that stays gone.
2. `world_run`: delete `<id>.cells.*` and `walk_zone.u8*` when a row ships, as it already deletes extracts; 3.8 GB that stays gone.
3. The t196/t142b caches as `.json.gz`: `canada.osm_caches` and the Overpass cache helpers write and read gzip
   (falling back to plain `.json` for old files), `hospital_beds.py` reads both, `world_run.ROW_CACHES` globs both;
   then a one-off gzip of the existing files. About 7 GB.

Sum: 102.4 GB this morning, 91.4 now, about 76.5 after the after-join list, about 66 once pre-T2302 goes,
about 59 with the three proposals.
