# T-1366: demand workers grow back (2026-11-16, agent pool)

## What a player meets

Switching games reloads the page (T-1196), so a small game after all-China already starts with a fresh pool sized by
its own save: Amsterdam got 3 workers, 8.8 s to its final, before this task. What stays in a tab is T-3072's
remembered cap after a worker ran out of memory (sessionStorage); left as it is. Within one page the pool was cut
(`capAhead`, `capPool`) and never grew back: a game whose reach or levels later need less, and every in-page load
(checks' `importSave`).

## The rule (as built, `workers/demandClient.ts`)

- `memCheck` (once the pool is quiet, as for a restart) asks `growTo`: up to the pool's size at the start
  (`poolN`, and `poolSize`'s remembered cap), the most solving workers that fit `GROW_ROOM` = 95% of both of
  `capPool`'s limits (17.5% of the budget for the solving workers, the budget for the page), each counted at the
  most any solving worker holds (its WASM) or needs; a worker being restarted in the same check counts its need.
- No growth within `GROW_GAP_MS` = 2 minutes of the pool's last change; each cut after a growth doubles that wait.
- `grow` is `shrink` in reverse: new workers start before the aux worker (which moves up), open what the pool
  holds through the replacement path (`reopen`), periods are dealt again, and the network is solved once more
  with the figures shown until its final. `?perfOff=grow` turns it off.
- Found on the way: `sweep` returned without a timer when called in a load's hold or while a worker restarted, so
  a still page (paused, no pan) never closed what an earlier game left: after all-China, Amsterdam's workers kept
  China's 370 markets open (need 752 MB instead of 296) and its figures counted them (total trips 848-919 M
  instead of 756.6 M; rail 201,423 instead of 201,426). It now tries again after 5 s.

## Measured

Headless Chrome on my Vite (6175), `?new=1&paused=1&demandWorkers=auto`, saves imported with `importSave`. Before
on 4 pinned cores, after on 3 (the ledger had 3 free), so times are only roughly comparable. MB: Windows private
bytes of the profile's Chrome. Script and JSON: scratchpad `pool/measure.mjs`, `run-*.json`.

| Run | Workers | Load to final | Edit solves | Settled MB | Figures |

| all-China then Amsterdam, same page, before | 1 throughout | 13.4 s | 0.8, 0.8; later 0.8, 0.2, 0.2 | 3,327 | rail 201,423; China's markets counted |
| same, after | 1 at the load, 3 from 70 s after its final | 16.3 s | 1.3, 0.9; after growing 0.2-0.3 | 3,834-3,845 | rail 201,426, trips, every line and the split equal the fresh page |

| NYC, after | 2, never grew in 3 minutes | 72.2 s | 1.2, 1.2 | 2,810 | every line equal |

- NYC needs 435 MB a worker: 3 x (435 + 150) = 1,755 MB is over the 1,362 MB growth room, so it stays at 2.
- Amsterdam grew to 3 at 298 MB a worker (3 x 448 = 1,344 of 1,362 MB); a 10% margin would have given 2.
- The aux worker kept 289 MB of WASM from China (69 MB needed, under the restart's 384 MB threshold), and the
  page's own heap stays larger than a fresh page's, so the same-page run settles about 1.3 GB above the reload.
