# T-1391: related lines, round 2 (Anita's playtest, 2026-10-26)

T-1168 built the related-lines diagram (notes/T-1168.md). Anita: "show related lines looks great", then
these, in her words where it matters (screenshots were of a Shanghai game: line 6 purple meeting the
orange line between Dongfang Rd and N Nanquan Rd; a line 2 diagram with a red dashed interline box):

1. **Lines that run parallel and end at a shared station.** Purple line 6 should run parallel between
   Dongfang Rd and N Nanquan Rd and terminate at N Nanquan, not cross.
2. **A line that interlines for 2 stops** is drawn as two perpendicular crossings; it should be beside.
   "There's a lot of edge cases, sorry."
3. **Curving off takes more horizontal space than needed** (the 4). Put the other line's transfer
   bullet on the first and last stations of the stretch they share, so every point where a passenger
   might transfer is marked. For expresses, also mark the points before and after stretches where
   their stops differ. These parallel-line bullets are lower priority and show after the bullets of
   lines that cross perpendicularly.
4. **A parallel line that keeps going after this line terminates** is drawn continuing straight past,
   not curving off. And the other way: a parallel line that stops mid-way just stops, no curve.
5. **Parallel and interlined lines in the same thin style as the perpendicular (crossing) lines.**
6. **The toggle icon** is small and confusing: a cross with a circle station in the middle, a little
   bigger.
7. **A line that crosses itself** shows itself as an available transfer at that station, even with
   related lines off.
8. **Loop lines:** every station once (not the first station twice); the main line continues past the
   ends and curves, suggesting it meets round somewhere.
9. Nice to have, not necessary: keep related lines showing while stops are being edited.
10. **Drop the times** (like "22m37s") from a stop row when the name, time and transfer bullets do not
    all fit: they matter least.

pair with a bow, a two-stop interline, going on past the terminus, one station twice, beside a loop
across its seam), `checks/t1391-related.mjs` (a new game with all the cases, Anita's Shanghai one

(`t1168-layout.mjs` now expects the J and Z beside the 6X for their two rows, and the drawing within
40% at the 280 px dock rather than two columns a side).

Per point:

1. Line 6 beside the orange line from Dongfang Rd to N Nanquan Rd, ending there: the "under three
   rows is crossings" rule is now "one row", and the two-meeting rule (no short-run allowance) skips
   pairs neither line stops between (`related.rs`).
2. A two-stop interline: a column, from the same rule.
3. The gutters and their bullets are gone. Side lines' bullets sit in the rows after the crossing
   lines' (`LayoutRow.marks`): first and last rows both stop at, and rows both stop at next to a row
   only one does. Turns are short (5 px corner, 9 px past the outermost column), with their own
   hover ("2 goes on to Yuyuan Garden").
4. Where the other line goes on past the selected line's first or last row, its column goes straight
   on 13 px; where it ends, the column ends at the stop.
5. Columns 3 px, as the crossing bars, 7 px apart (own track 3 px further out).
6. Toggle: two lines crossing at a station, 16 px in a 22 px button.
7. `selfTransfer` in LineInspector: the line's own bullet (and the bigger dot) where it stops at a
   complex again (not the next or previous stop, not a loop's seam), both views; its bar's hover
   "Hook stops at Union Sq twice".
8. Loops (when not editing stops): the last row (the first again) is hidden; the plain bar and the
   diagram's spine go on past both ends and curve right. A line beside the loop across its seam:
   Rust starts its second group at the seam's first pass, and the layout runs the first group's
   column on past the last row (`foldLoop`).
9. Related lines stay while stops are edited; the passed-stations rows go between the drawing's rows
   (rows measured by `li[data-r]`).
10. `useTightTimes` (RelatedStops.tsx): a row whose `.grow` overflows gets `data-tight`, which hides
    its time; measured after each render and on resize; both views.
