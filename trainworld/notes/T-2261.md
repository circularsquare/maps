# T-2261: the diagram graph no longer depends on the camera anchor (agent sc, 2026-11-16)

Cause: the clock worker's graph was built from `n.edgePts`, float32 local units from the draw anchor, and `kAtY` read the current anchor. Another anchor gave other float32 roundings and another k, so another sample of the chaotic layout (notes/T-2255.md).

Fix: `postDiagram` (clock.worker.ts `diagramFrame`) rebuilds each edge's points from the kept float64 mercator points (`edgeMerc`) relative to an origin of the network's own: the middle of the points' box on a 1 degree grid (a lng, lat integer), and passes that anchor to `diagramGraph` (`at`, used by `kAtY`). `graph.origin` holds the lng, lat; view.ts `nearest` uses it instead of the page's anchor (it used the page's anchor against the worker's mid, wrong after an anchor move). Converted points are cached per edge while the mercator array and origin are the same, so the arc cache in graph.ts still hits. An edge with no kept mercator points falls back to its local points shifted by the anchor.

Check: `checks/t2261-graph-anchor.mjs` opens the real New York save, builds the diagram, sends the camera to San Francisco and back to Connecticut (the anchor moves twice) and builds again: 854 complexes, graph places hash equal (2030076593 both, origin -73,41). About 80 s.

Not checked: that the old code differs in this test (the old code's anchor was at New York's home point on the first build and at -73,41 after, so it should).
