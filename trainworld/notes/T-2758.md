# T-2758 First-visit check of the live site (agent firstvisit, 2026-11-07)

Measurement only. Live site https://anita.garden/trainsworld/ (data from data.anita.garden/trainworld/2026-11-07b/),
loaded at about 18:46-18:50 UTC on 2026-11-07. Seven loads in all; nothing in the app or data changed.

## Method

- Script: `scratchpad/firstvisit/fv.mjs` (outside the repo; kept in the agent's scratch folder). Playwright
  through `checks/lib.mjs` launchChrome, a fresh temp `--user-data-dir` for every run, Chrome pinned to 2 cores
  (ledger claim `firstvisit`, 2 cores, released at exit) and `?demandWorkers=1`. Real Chrome with d3d11 GL.
- Network: a raw CDP client on `--remote-debugging-port` attaches to the page and to every worker it spawns
  (Target.setAutoAttach, flatten) and sets `Network.emulateNetworkConditions` on each, so the demand worker's
  pack downloads are throttled and counted too (a page-level session alone misses them).
  Profiles: unthrottled; broadband 20 Mbit down / 5 up, 40 ms; slow 4G 5 Mbit down / 1 up, 150 ms.
  Bytes are `encodedDataLength` of finished requests, cache hits not counted.
- The URL carries `?debug=1` so the page exposes `window.twDemand` / `window.twViews` for the ready test (it also
  draws a small debug panel at top right of the screenshots). Nothing else differs from a plain visit.
- Steps: load, wait for the start menu with the city buttons (a fresh profile opens on the new game sheet), click the
  city, click Start. Clock is from the page's `goto` for the menu and from the Start click for the rest.
  "Bubbles" is `twViews.overlay.discsDrawn > 0` (Homes bubbles on, as a new game sets them). "Ready" is bubbles drawn
  and the status tag and loading card both gone. A stall is 15 s with the same stage, bar percent, tag text and
  bubble count; none happened.
- Caveat: Chrome throttles each CDP session on its own, so page and worker could in principle each use the full rate.
  Measured: slow 4G moved 19 MB in 36 s (0.53 MB/s against a 0.625 MB/s cap), broadband 17.8 MB in 12.6 s, so the
  caps held closely enough.
- Caveat: Chrome on 2 cores with 1 demand worker is a slow machine; a real visitor with 4+ cores and 3 workers will
  see the compute parts shorter, the download parts the same.

## What a first-time player sees on slow 4G

Menu after 5.5 s. After Start: a loading card with the train, "Loading", 0-7% for about 12 s (the app code and
the city pack), then "Loading city data" 32% to 88% with "About 1 min left" counting down to "20 s left", the bar
moving at least every few seconds (no stall over 15 s in any run). At 34 s the card closes and the status tag says
"Loading data..." for about 2 s, then the Homes bubbles draw. Shots: `slow4g-tyo-loading.png` (card at 6%),
`slow4g-tyo-play.png` (bubbles drawn, tutorial open).

## Problems

Nothing a first-time player would take for broken. Nothing over 60 s on any profile (worst: 40 s on slow 4G).
No page errors; no failed requests in the throttled runs; no console errors in the throttled and warm runs.

Smaller things:

1. Two console errors in the two unthrottled runs only: 404 on font glyph ranges
   `fonts/Zen Maru Gothic Regular,Noto Sans Regular/5120-5375.pbf` and `5376-5631.pbf`. These are the glyph ranges
   glyphs.py leaves out on purpose (smoke ignores them, MapLibre draws the codepoints locally); harmless, but a
   visitor who opens the console sees two red lines. They did not show on the throttled runs (the requests were
   likely not made before the capture ended).
2. The "About 1 min left" at 32% on slow 4G was longer than the real remainder (22 s to the card closing); the
   estimate is pessimistic by about 2x. Not wrong enough to matter.
3. The biggest cost is two downloads, `<city>.bin` and `outlines.json` (11-12 MB of 18-20); on slow 4G they are the
   25-35 s. `outlines.json` is the same for every city and compresses to 4.56 MB on the wire (check whether
   Cloudflare serves it with brotli; encodedDataLength 4.56 MB for what is probably a larger JSON suggests it
   does, but the content-encoding header was not recorded). A city's second `nyc.bin` request (1.11 MB, 24 s on slow
   4G, in the NYC slow run only) is a second fetch of the same file, likely a range or a second worker.
4. Debug panel and tutorial: the unthrottled "play" shots carry `?debug=1` chrome (top right); a real visitor
   does not see it.

Not measured: Safari/Firefox, mobile GPUs and phone CPUs (a phone's demand workers will be slower than this machine's
pinned 2 cores or not; unknown), iOS memory, the language pickers, and what happens past the first minute of play.
Data lives behind Cloudflare; many simultaneous visitors were not tested (the site is public, a handful of loads only).
