# T-3903 trams reach demand as trams

Confirmed in game (new NYC game, one 5-stop tram line, 2 trams): on master the demand worker's views dropped
`tram`, so `set_tram_lines` saw none (no 0.75 car per section, no tram-only stops). Tram A: 173,261 riders/day on
master, 165,968 with the fix (-4.2%), rail trips/day 173,315 -> 166,025.

Real NYC save (111 lines, no trams): rail trips/day 12,664,388 on master and on the tree, identical.

Fix: demandClusters.ts `SLICED` (name -> station | line | stop) and `slicedOf` copy ups, fz, ferry, tram, pierLines,
gradeStops to cluster networks and reach views; `passagesOf` keeps the pairs. Route-node fields (hopKm, fareW,
baseW, fzDist, fzPass*, span), `back` and the bus stretches keep their helpers. `tram` joined the signatures beside
`ferry`. A mismatched full-network array is logged by `slicedOf` and left out.
Rust: `NetInputs::check` (demand/api.rs), called from `DemandApi::set_network` and the intercity `set_network_in`:
a non-empty complexes, upgrades, bus, tram, ferry, grade, back or span array of the wrong length is an Err (the same
path as "network arrays disagree"), test `a_per_line_input_of_the_wrong_length_is_an_error`.
Check: checks/t3903-view-fields.mjs fails for a DemandNetwork field a view lacks or holds at the wrong length, and
for a protocol field it has no shape for.
