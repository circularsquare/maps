# T-4283: drop `settle_kept_rest` on weekend headway changes

Agent fixweekend, 2026-11-25, tree `fixweekend` (`tw/fixweekend`). Follow-up to T-4282's review of T-4239.

## Cause

In `trainworld/sim/src/track/world.rs`, `services_after` dropped held settle entries (`settle_kept` and
`settle_kept_rest`) when a line's headway changed, but compared only `Ahead.keys` (weekdays' `auto_at`) for both
lists. A weekend-only headway or share change that kept the weekend train count left `settle_kept_rest` in place, so
`settle_weekend` held the line at its old weekend rate (and saves kept the stale entry).

## Change (`trainworld/sim/src/track/world.rs`)

- `struct Ahead`: added `keys_rest: Vec<Option<[Option<u32>; SLOTS]>>` beside `keys`.
- `fn rest_auto(net: &Network, l: usize) -> [Option<u32>; SLOTS]`: maps `net.weekend_auto(l)` across the line's slots
  (matching what `swap_in_weekend` puts in `line_auto`).
- `TrackWorld::ahead`: fills `keys_rest` for each ok line via `rest_auto`.
- `TrackWorld::services_after`: splits the headway-change drop into two lists — `gone` drops `settle_kept` by weekday
  `auto_at`, and `gone_rest` drops `settle_kept_rest` by `rest_auto`.
- `circle_tests::a_kept_weekend_line_given_another_headway_runs_it`: sets a headway line's weekend headway to 620 s,
  records its `settle_kept_rest` entry, then changes the weekend headway to 640 s with the same weekend train count,
  asserting the `settle_kept_rest` entry is dropped, the weekend rate updates to `3600 / 640` tph, and the weekday
  rate stays 6.0 tph.
