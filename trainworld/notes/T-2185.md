## What was wrong at a merge

Two lines joining one track at a Y: the shared track towards the main line is one resource per direction, and its
trains wait for it at its entry, the merge node, standing on the two branches. But its dot sat midway along the
section's first edge (whichever key sorted first), anywhere along the trunk, often off screen and on top of the
other direction's dot at the same midpoint. So the trains stood at the merge and the mark was somewhere else.

Now (`capacity::build_sections`): a double-track section whose users come onto it from two or more distinct edges at
one node (a line turning back there, or starting there, joins nothing) has `Resource::node` = that node and its point
there; `wire_kind` 12, "Merge" (`game/capacityKinds.ts`). Same utilisation and wait as before; only where it is drawn
and what it is called change. The way out from the main line (lines leaving together, parting at the Y) stays plain
shared track.

## Threshold

The same as every dot: 75% for the map, 90% (or full) for the trouble list. At 75% a two-line merge's wait is about
30-40 s a train (Kingman, mix 0.5, 107-137 s a train on 80-160 km/h track), about what braking to a stand and
starting again costs, which is where the timetable's hold turns from a slower approach into trains standing at the
merge (`profile.rs`, a hold becomes a stop once it is longer than the stop's loss). Below it trains slow a little
and rarely stand, so nothing to see; a separate threshold for merges would make one kind of dot mean something else.
