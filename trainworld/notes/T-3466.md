# T-3466: changelog icons by area, groups

Anita's ask (2026-11-16): an icon per changelog item by area instead of the dot, items sorted by area, bug fix text a
little lighter, the same on the board, retroactive. Then: small fixes go under a group item ("track bug fixes"), and a
fix that belongs to one area takes that area's icon (the grey bug only for fixes of no area).

## What's where

- Areas, in the order the game sorts by: track (blue track, #2456e0 / dark #8aa6ff, the station circle's blue),
  demand (the demand-bubble control's purple dot), scheduling (green Marey chart), fares (green money, `--good`),
  schematic (yellow folded map, `--yellow`; also map display and zone names), community (orange person, `--warn`;
  accounts, sharing, feedback), performance (computer: white screen with a #9a9a9a edge on light, #f2f2f2 on dark),
  other (a plain muted dot: languages, achievements, controls), bug (grey bug: a fix that fits no area).
  Within an area, fixes come after the rest. App: `ui/updates.ts` (`AREAS`, `changes: [key, area, "fix"?]`, `sub` for a
  group's items), `ui/Changelog.tsx`, icons `foldMap`, `bug`, `computer` in `ui/widgets.tsx`, CSS `.cl-area`.
- next.json items gain `area`, `fix`, `parent` (a group's id), `group`. `changelog.py add --area A [--fix] [--under id]`
  (area required), `group "text" --area A [--fix]`, `set id [--area] [--fix|--no-fix] [--under id|none]`. Publish sorts
  by area, writes a group with its visible items as `sub`, leaves out a hidden group (and its items) and a group with
  nothing visible, and refuses an item with no area.
- Board: an icon button before each item opens a menu: the nine areas, "Bug fix (lighter text)", "Under" a group;
  "Add group" by the heading (a new group starts "new group", area bug, fix, its text selected to type over); "Delete
  this group" on an empty group. Groups bold, their items indented under them, fixes lighter. serve.py POST takes
  `area`, `fix`, `parent`, and `{op: "group"}` / `{op: "delete"}`.

## Pages (T-3468)

One page per x.y version (`v0.3` holds v0.3 to v0.3.5), picked with a Segmented row under the title, the newest open
each time it opens; switching scrolls to the top. Only the page shown is rendered, and the Changelog renders nothing
while closed (before, its whole list was built on every render of the start menu and Settings). Measured with a test
v0.3.6 of 57 items: 108 list items on the v0.3 page, 24 on v0.2, a page switch about 22 ms to the second frame.

## Roadmap (T-3467)

On the board's Changelog tab, above the next update: "Roadmap" with one column per planned version (0.4, 0.5 to
start, until she saves). Each column: its name (click to edit), items as cards (click to edit, saved 1.2 s after
typing stops and on blur), a grip to drag a card within or between columns (a blue line marks where it lands), an
x on hover to delete a card or a version (a version with items asks first), and "Add an item" (Enter). "Add version"
adds the next number after the last ("0.6" after "0.5"). Saved whole to `trainworld/changelog/roadmap.json` through
serve.py `/twboard/_roadmap` (the scratch pad's pattern: PUT with the version read, 409 reloads the newer file; refused
unless it parses as JSON). Not in the game. Checked on a throwaway serve.py (8853): add, reorder within and between
columns, add a version, edit, delete, reload keeps it; no console errors.

## Calls

- No accessible labels: the icons are `aria-hidden` (the text says what the item is), so no new strings.
- "other" is an area Anita didn't list: some items fit none of hers (languages, achievements, game speed, zoom buttons,
  follow a train, saves menu). They keep a plain dot, sorted before the bug fixes.
- Sub-items of a group inherit its lightness and show no icon of their own.
- Retroactive counts, the 92 published items in updates.ts: track 31, other 10, demand 9, community 9, bug 9,
  schematic 7, fares 7, performance 6, scheduling 4; 15 are fixes (track 2, fares 2, demand 1, performance 1, bug 9).
  The 54 items in next.json, plus 3 groups (track bug fixes 14 items, train bug fixes 3, money bug fixes 3): track 21,
  schematic 8, demand 7, scheduling 6, fares 5, bug 4, community 3, performance 2, other 1.
