# T-096 hotkeys and a delete tool

Done 2026-10-09 by the gameplay agent (batch 7). Covers T-088. Screenshots in the project root:
`T-096-tools.png` (the Build tab's tools with their keys), `T-096-delete-hover.png` (the delete
tool over blueprint track: the stretch drawn red, "Remove 2.1 km of track" by the cursor),
`T-096-confirm.png` (the question above the bottom bar for constructed track),
`T-096-inspector-ask.png` (the same question from the station inspector's Remove station).

## Keys (main.ts keydown, in this order)

- Esc with a question waiting: Keep (the question goes, nothing changes).
- Esc while dragging a node or a corner: it goes back (T-093, T-064).
- 1, 2, 3 (no modifiers, not repeated): draw track, station, delete (`TOOL_KEYS` in ui/tabs.tsx),
  opening the Build tab; the key of the tool already on does nothing.
- `tools.key`: Esc drops a half-drawn route, else leaves the tool (track, station, delete, line);
  Enter and Backspace as before.
- Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, then Esc clears the selection, then Space pauses.
- Everything is skipped while typing in a field.

The buttons show their key as a small muted digit after the label, and in the title ("Draw track
(1)", "Select (Esc)"). Four buttons with digits did not fit the dock's default width, so they read
Select, Track 1, Station 2, Delete 3, a little tighter than other buttons.

## Delete

- `Tool` has "delete" (game/ui.ts). In map/tools.ts, `deletePick`: a station within 10 px, else a
  flyover (a junction with one) within 10 px, else the track under the pointer (the drawing tool's
  snap; at a plain node, the nearest stretch). Hover draws a stretch red through the preview layer
  and rings a station or flyover; the tag by the cursor says "Remove 2.1 km of track", "Remove W
  42nd St station", "Remove the constructed flyover".
- game/remove.ts, `removeThing`: blueprint is removed at once (`removeEdge`, `removeStation`,
  `flying: false`), undoable; constructed sets `ask` ("Remove 2.1 km of constructed track?
  Nothing is refunded.", Remove / Keep), shown by ui/Toolbar.tsx in place of the hint; Remove runs
  the same edit, final and unrefunded as before (SPEC 6.4). The selection clears if it was the
  thing removed; a line stopping at a station still refuses its removal with the usual reason.
- The track and station inspectors' Remove buttons call `removeThing` too.
