# T-1163: short trips across a zone line

## For Anita

- **Why.** Everyday trips (errands, visits, school, university, medical) never leave the home's
  own zone: the draw runs over the home pack's zones only. The only short trips that cross a line
  are commutes, and between two packs of one country those are damped to 0.4 (outside the US;
  a US line inside one state counts 1). The market's longer trips are not the cause: they start
  at 25 km for every pair, inside a zone or across a line alike.
- **How big.** At Foshan's places within 3 km of Guangzhou, 8.6% of the trips arriving come from
  Guangzhou now, all of them commutes. With the same rules and no line it would be 31.5%. Fixing
  the everyday trips alone gives 24.6%; raising the commute factor to 1 alone gives 14.9%. The
  other lines measured look the same (table below). A place right on the line still draws most
  from its own side, because errands and visits are mostly within a kilometre or two, so about
  70/30 is what the rules give, close to what you expected.
- **Not every pair you named has a line.** Haarlem, Newark, Cambridge, Kawasaki, Yokohama and
  Paris's inner suburbs are all inside their big city's zone. The lines that cut through one
  built-up area are mostly in China (city prefectures as zones), Germany's Ruhr and Rhine, and
  where US metros meet.
- **Proposed fix.** Everyday trips that cross a zone line become a third kind of trip in the
  regional layer, beside commutes between zones and longer trips. They are worked out offline with
  the same rule as inside a zone, over the home's zone and its neighbours' zones together; the
  home zone's own trips shrink by the share that now goes across, so nobody makes more trips. They
  are solved the way commutes between zones are (rail or car), with walking added, since half of
  them are under 5 km. No two packs need to be open together. About three stages of work (below).
- **Your call.** Whether the commute factor between two zones of one country (0.4, your T-584
  choice, from Japan's census fit) should rise toward 1 as well. I'd keep 0.4: it is the one
  fitted value, and the everyday fix does most of the work.

## What was measured

`notes/t1163_border.py A B [more] [--near KM] [--factor F] [--clip KM]` reads the packs (read
only) and works at the 2 km zones, as the sim's everyday draw does (zone attraction x
exp(-d / scale), normalised per origin zone, 80% local and 20% central at 6 km with the density
weight, T-1092; school and university drawn from the site; rates from each pack's `everyday`
block). Commutes use each pack's shipped factors: within a pack `row x col x f(d)`, across packs the
same times the border factor; "at 1" re-balanced over the listed packs with their flows to other
packs held. Check: the formula gives 651k commuters a day between Guangzhou and Foshan, the shipped
joint file 669k (the difference is the 1% of long commutes).

By trip length, Guangzhou residents' arrivals there now are 7k at 0-2 km and 22k at 2-5 km (all
commutes), against 72k and 123k with no line; Foshan's own residents bring 527k and 464k now (381k
and 325k with no line, the rest going to Guangzhou). Zones right on the line (within 2 km) come
out the same: 8.7% now, 32.9% with no line.

Across the whole Guangzhou - Foshan line, the rule with no line sends 0.93M outings a day across
(1.9M trip legs; errands 518k, social 293k, medical 38k, school 67k, university 17k), 4.9% of the
two zones' errands, against 0.67M commuters crossing now. The other lines: Shenzhen - Dongguan
0.73M (commuters now 0.59M), Shanghai - Suzhou 0.17M (0.27M), Dusseldorf - Ruhr 0.11M (0.10M),
Baltimore - Washington 0.10M (0.45M), Tokyo - Tochigi 0.05M (0.05M), New York - New Haven 0.06M
(0.09M). In zones.md 3 these trips were left out as "small"; near a line through one built-up area
they are about as many as the commutes.

How far they go: Guangzhou residents' errands into Foshan mostly end close to the line (80% within
3 km of it); Foshan residents' errands into Guangzhou go deeper (36% within 3 km, 33% 5-10 km in),
because a fifth of errands and visits go to dense centres at the 6 km scale and Guangzhou's centre
is the region's (174k of Foshan's 402k errands into Guangzhou are 10-25 km long). Those are the
long everyday trips the Guangfo metro carries.

## Which rules make the gap

- **Everyday trips stay in the home's pack** (demand-sources.md 4.1, `everyday.rs`: the draw's cdf
  runs over the pack's zones). The main cause: 0% from the neighbour in every everyday purpose,
  against 22-40% with no line.
- **The border factor between packs** (`pipeline/borders.json` `pack_line_default` 0.4; T-584).
  Second: commutes from the neighbour 27.6% at 0.4, 48% at 1 (Foshan side). In the US a pack line
  inside one state already has 1 (joint_gravity.rs: a pair in one region takes 1), so the gap there
  is everyday only.
- **The market's onset** (25-50 km) is not a cause: within a country it treats a pair inside a zone
  and across a line alike. Nor could it carry these trips: its ground rate is 0.107 x s departures a
  resident (about 0.05 in China) against 0.83 everyday outings, and its weight is flat under 25 km.
- **T-1000's set share** (1% to 50-150 km) does not touch short trips.

## Options

**A. Everyday draws over neighbouring zones' places in the local layer (a halo).** Each pack would
carry copies of its neighbours' cells near its line as destinations only. Exact at cell grain, but:
a 5 km halo adds 35% to Guangzhou's cells, 47% to Foshan's, 30% to Shenzhen's (6% New York, 4% Tokyo,
15% Paris, 28% Amsterdam, 12% Washington); and it would still miss Foshan's trips into Guangzhou's
centre (42% of them end more than 5 km in). Worse, a halo cell is another zone's place: its
arrivals would have to be added into the neighbour's bubbles, selections and trip explorer, which
all assume a cell belongs to one pack. Not recommended.

**B. Everyday trips across a line in the regional layer (recommended).** As commutes between zones
are now (zones.md 4):
1. Offline, per solve group (the same groups as `joint_gravity`): for every origin 2 km zone and
   purpose, the draw over its own pack's zones and the neighbouring packs' zones within the local
   layer's reach, normalised over all of them. Two outputs: each pack zone's kept share by purpose
   (the part of its outings that stays in its pack; a small array written into each pack), and the
   cross part by 6 km zone pair and purpose, with each pair's walking share with no rail worked out
   at cell grain, into the market pack beside the commute list. Social's three parts need a weight
   that compares across packs (each part scaled to the pack's people rather than to a share of 1).
   A national border takes a factor as commutes do (Shenzhen touches Hong Kong).
2. Sim: the everyday layer multiplies each origin's budget by its zone's kept share (in the pack,
   so bare zones get it too). The regional layer gets a third pair list, solved where a station
   serves both 6 km zones as commutes are, at each purpose's everyday timing, rail against the car
   on the ratio curve with walking first, rail taking from walkers by its share as the local layer
   does. Loads join each purpose's.
3. App: the far-end lists and purpose filters already show regional trips by place; the trip
   explorer between two zones needs a walking row for short pairs. Docs: zones.md 3 and 4,
   regional.md 5.1, demand-sources.md, demand-plain.md, demand-worldwide.md.

Cost of B: Guangzhou - Foshan's cross outings fall into 6,282 pairs of 6 km zones at 1 a day or more
(99.5-99.8% of them), beside the 19,349 commute pairs the shipped file has between the two (the
Guangdong file holds 554k in all): the regional commute pairs near such a line grow by about a
third, so that part of a solve by about as much; the local layers are most of a solve, so the whole
grows by a few percent near a dense line (estimated from the pair counts, not timed in the worker).
Memory about 12 bytes a pair, under 0.1 MB for this line. No pack opens for another. The real New
York save: New York's lines with its neighbours carry about 0.1M of these outings a day (New Haven
0.06M), 0.4% of New York's 25.9M; at the real save's 15% everyday rail share at most, under 0.1% of
its 11.7M rail trips, and less since they are suburban edge trips.

**C. Commute factor 0.4 to 1 between packs of one country.** One number in `borders.json` and the
joint solves rerun (Japan's alone is about 25 minutes). Doubles the commuters across each such line
(Guangzhou - Foshan 0.67M to 1.33M) and takes the Foshan side from 8.6% to 14.9%. Japan's census fit
said 0.4 (at 1 the model sent 1.9 times the census), so this is a call for Anita; with B built, C
adds 24.6% to 31.5%.
