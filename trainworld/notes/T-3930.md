# T-3930 Planned line shows the wrong train cost after a headway switch

Result: not reproducible by the player's path; no code change, no changelog item, no strings.

Run (11-21, own Vite on 6191, new NYC game, `play.mjs`): drew 1.7 km of track and two stations, a line kept planned
(Construct for $485M: track and stations $335M, trains $150M for 12 trains). Clicked Headway: the panel shows 12
trains at 0.75 min (the headway is worked out from the 12 trains). Typed a 5 min (300 s) headway: the trains become 2
(round trip 8m48s) and the Construct button reads $360M, hover "Track and stations $335M, trains $25.0M". Pressed
Construct: cash fell by $360.3M. Panel, quote and charge agree.

Why the Rust test differs: `set_auto_headway` on a count line (`(None, Some(t))`) keeps the line's trains (T-752)
while setting `auto` to 300 s, so the test reaches "12 trains with a 300 s headway", and `line_train_cost` reads
those 12 (`trains.max() * cars`). The panel's Headway switch cannot do that: it sets the headway from the trains
(0.75 min), and each typed headway then retunes the trains (`_` branch, `auto_trains`). So it is a test-helper state only.
