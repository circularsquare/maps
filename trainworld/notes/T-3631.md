# T-3631: new station names in the player's script

Agent nativenames, 2026-11-18. Rule: docs/track-and-service.md "In the player's script"; code `app/src/i18n/nativeNames.ts`,
used by `map/stationNames.ts` (streets, tile places, `placeName`), `game/placeList.ts` (`stationPlaceName`, `aka`) and
`map/realStations.ts` (`realStationName`). Bus route end names (`BusInspector.nameEnd`) follow through `placeName`.

## Was it a regression?

No. Station names from the place list began 2026-10-13 (722d2e4e); the JP and CN packs came 2026-10-15 already showing
Latin names (T-291 9da1431c, T-420/T-426 f9cc50ac), and the basemap street names were always `name:latin` first. What
was new is T-3571 (2026-11-17): a station on a real one takes its name first, in Latin letters, so it showed more.
Zone and city names have been in Japanese and Chinese since T-2681/T-3318, which made the Latin station names stand out.

## Data

Native aliases are there for all four countries (share of places with one: JP 100% Han, 7% kana; CN 100% Han;
TW 100% Han; KR 100% Hangul). None missing for a whole country. The first alias is OSM's own `name` where the
pipeline swapped in a Latin one; later ones are alt/short/old/official names (old ones like 崇明县 never win).
Mixed aliases ("香港 Hong Kong", "台北縣 (Taipei County)", Tibetan beside Han) give their readable part.

Suffix histograms (scratch `suffix.py`): JP neighbourhoods are 71% 丁目 blocks (stripped at any length: 桜三丁目 to 桜);
JP 町 on quarters and neighbourhoods is part of the name (大手町, kept); CN quarters are 83% 社区, suburbs 72% 街道
(both stripped, matching the Latin names, which drop them); 小区, 园区, 开发区, 新区, 村 kept; KR 동 stripped on
quarters/suburbs/neighbourhoods (역삼1동 to 역삼, as stations are named), 가 and 리 kept, 신도시 and 지구 kept.

## For Anita

A Japanese player in mainland China gets the Simplified names as OSM writes them (云南中路, 张家楼, 涞亭, 浦东 where
Japanese would write 浦東). Han can't be told apart by script; converting Simplified to Japanese forms would need a
character table. The alternative is Latin (Pudong) for Japanese players in mainland China only. Kept as is.
