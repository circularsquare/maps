# T-3233: London's real network settles ten times slower (lonslow, 2026-11-11)

Status: done. The settle stops early on an exact repeat; real networks give every dead-end station a
terminal and ship in the Mercator frame with their settled rates and pacing (`LPC1`); a load with saved
pacing keeps it; all seven real saves reshipped (London on its real levels too).

## When and why (bisect)

`realsave --shared` "load + derive", London, over `trainworld/sim` in a worktree; inputs the shipped
corridors (`artifacts/trainworld/t1305-shared/`).

| Commit | London |
|---|---|
| c68db5c4 (10-29, London shipped) | 9.9 s |
| 4e504211 (11-06) .. 677380aa (11-10 02:54) | 11.0-11.8 s |
| 3abe175f, aaa4c1bf (after 9752cd03) | 31-33 s |
| 08435565 (11-10 08:53) and later | over 50 s |
| 72e30ce0 | 146 s |

- **9752cd03 (T-2973, count lines paced through a merge)**: a full settle runs again after each pace
  change, up to `PACE_ROUNDS` = 3. The pacing never settles on these networks: each round flips lines in
  and out of step (London about 240 changes a round, New York about 40), so all three always run.
- **08435565 (T-2986, a dead end without a terminal turns on one platform)**: realsave gave no station
  an upgrade, so every real terminus turned one train at a time (Tokyo's busy lines capped at exactly
  15 an hour, one holding 166 trains for 7.5 an hour). London's round trips then jumped as holds came
  and went, and each paced settle circled to its 80-round cap (one line +-0.10 trains, above
  `SWING_NEAR`, so T-1283's catch never fired).

In the game it was worse: real saves shipped in the legacy frame, the Mercator conversion on load drops
settled rates, so every start settled in full (London 42.8 s natively, about 3x that in the browser).

## What changed

- `track/world.rs`: a settle whose rates, round trips and swing state equal those of 1-4 rounds before
  runs only the rounds that end it where the 80-round cap would, then stops; the swing counter stops at
  `SWING_TURNS` (the only value read). Output byte-identical (all seven cities checked against the old
  realsave); realsave London 146 -> 106 s on the old inputs. Catches exact repeats only.
- `bin/realsave.rs`: every dead-end station gets a terminal; the network is converted to the Mercator
  frame before it is settled and saved, so the save is exactly what the game runs; `_real_track.json`,
  `_holds.json` and the printed positions stay in the pack's local frame.
- `track/save.rs`: `LPC1` (save level 16), written with `LRT1` when any line is paced: the count lines'
  pacing. `Network::pace_from_save`; `TrackWorld::reload` with it settles without pacing again. Old saves
  (no `LPC1`) load and pace as before.
- Seven saves rebuilt into `artifacts/trainworld/t3233-realnet/` (six from T-1773's staged inputs,
  London's corridors rebuilt there with levels) and shipped with `realnet.py ship`.
