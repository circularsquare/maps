### Areas Checked That Came Up Clean
- **Double-charging on cash**: Every `edit()` passes `pendingSent` once (one entry per `seq`); `pendingWord` overwrites `e.show.cost` rather than accumulating it; `pendingTaken` clears `e.money` on the first money/cash/state message arriving after `e.armed`.
- **Behaviour while paused & staged service states**: `overlay.invalidate()` ([overlay.ts](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/app/src/map/overlay.ts#L263-L275)) schedules a one-shot `requestAnimationFrame` even when `clock.ticking === false`, so ahead guesses, words, refusals, and settled networks render immediately while paused. Staged service states (`effectiveAt !== undefined`) carry `noNet: true`, clear panel/money immediately, and flush before any subsequent `setNetwork()`.
- **`StrokeCache` invalidation when `buildAhead()` runs**: `buildAhead()` increments `this.uploads` ([renderer.ts](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/app/src/map/renderer.ts#L846)), which is included in `cache.stale(...)` ([rendererDraw.ts](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/app/src/map/rendererDraw.ts#L96)).
- **Boundedness of other caches**: `pending.ts` `sentAt` is capped at 256 entries; `lineBands.ts` `orders.memo` prunes unvisited edges and compacts `orders.readBy` at `3 * live + 1024`; `corridors.ts` prunes removed edges from `made` and `seenBy` and swaps `cache`; `network.ts` `stationIndexes` and `lineAhead.ts` `sigCache` use `WeakMap`.
- **Undo across anchor moves & rapid undo after a refused edit**: `undoGuess` checks `now.anchor?.version !== l.was.anchor?.version` ([pending.ts](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/app/src/game/pending.ts#L263)); `after` / `refusedLast` ([clock.worker.ts](file:///C:/Users/anita/projects/maps/trainworld/app/src/workers/clock.worker.ts#L993-L997)) prevents undoing an earlier accepted edit when an in-flight edit was refused.
