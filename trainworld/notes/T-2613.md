# T-2613 terrain downloads, T-2614 a failed cloud save (agent terrainnet, 2026-11-06)

Anita's words: "sometimes my internet is bad and terrain isnt loading. we should show a "downloading
terrain" indicator like the "calculating trips". (we download this from online, right?) also what happens
if we place track / contsruct without knowing what terrain should be? is that a way to sorta cheat" and
"also what happens if we fail to save to cloud?"

## Where terrain comes from

Two different things are called terrain:

- **The hill shading** (Settings > Show > Terrain, off by default) is a picture only. Its tiles come from
  the Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data (`s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod`), always from the internet.
  Nothing is priced from it.
- **The terrain classes** that price track (flat, hills, mountains) are the game's own files,
  `terrain/<zone>.terrain` beside the packs: on the published game from R2 (online), on 8800 or a dev
  server from this machine. The clock worker fetches a zone's file when the view comes near the zone or
  track is drawn there, along with the zone's water mask (from its pack) and, once, `world.water` (the
  sea between zones). Road crossings come from the basemap's road tiles (OpenFreeMap), under track only.
  Average terrain file 140 KB, largest 5 MB (4209 files).

## What happened before this task

- Nothing on screen said terrain was downloading or had failed. A failed fetch was tried again only
  when the next view move or edit needed that zone.
- **Previews never waited for terrain**: track drawn before the file landed was quoted as flat ground
  and re-quoted when it landed. That part is fine.
- **Construct waited for the terrain and road tiles under the blueprint, but went ahead when the
  download failed.** A failed fetch resolved as "nothing", so Construct then charged the flat price.
  On a bad connection, mountain track (0.3 + 3 = 3.3x at ground level, 247.5 US$M a km against 22.5 on
  flat ground) could be built for the flat price. Road crossings worked the same way (a failed road tile
  was zero crossings). So yes, it was a way to cheat, by accident or on purpose (block the requests).
- A failed water mask left the ground as land: a blueprint drawn then could be ground-level track over
  water, priced without the water surcharge, and Construct built it. The world's water the same for the
  open sea between zones.
- Built track is never charged again: when terrain landed after a construct, the built value changed
  but nothing was billed. Only Construct (`construct`, `constructAll`, `constructLine`) charges for
  track; every edit of built track is refused (checked in `set_edge_level`, `set_edges_level`,
  `set_edges_hsr`, node moves), so Construct is the one door to close.
- No download had a time limit: a stalled connection could hold Construct (and every edit queued behind
  it in the clock worker) for minutes.

## What happens now (T-2613)

- The clock worker counts its terrain, water-mask and world-water downloads in flight and failed, and
  tells the page (`ground` message). The page also counts the hill shading's tile downloads.
- The status tag (the demand status's tag on the map's top row) says **"Downloading terrain…"** with
  the spinner once any of these has been on its way 1.5 s, and **"Terrain could not load"** (no
  spinner) while one failed, hover "Check your internet connection. It is tried again within 30 s."
  It comes after a city's "Loading data" and before the bubble and trip cases, and also shows after
  demand stopped. Gone when nothing is downloading or failed.
- A failed download still needed by the view or the blueprint is fetched again 2 s later, doubling to
  30 s; one no longer needed is dropped until something needs it. Each download gives up after 60 s.
- **Construct needs all the ground under the blueprint**: the terrain, the water masks, the world's
  water and the road tiles. It waits for them; if one could not be had it is refused with
  "The terrain here did not load. Try again." (or "The roads here did not load. Try again.", the bus
  tool's existing words), nothing built or paid. A pack head answering 404 counts as no mask, as a
  terrain 404 counts as no terrain, so a zone without the file is never blocked.

blueprint quoted flat, Construct refused with the notice; terrain back but 6 s per file, "Downloading
terrain…" during the retry, the tag going, the blueprint re-quoted on the mountain (262.5 US$M a km with

## A failed cloud save before this task (T-2614)

- Every save is written to the browser first (IndexedDB), then sent to the account. A failed send
  never touches the browser's copy, so **nothing built is lost**; the change counts as "changed here"
  until a send goes through.
- The failure was written to the slot's record and shown only inside the saved games panel: a crossed
  cloud after "Autosaved", hover "Not on your account yet. The server could not be reached."
- It was sent again at the next autosave (each minute while the game runs and something changed), when
  the page is hidden, at the next page load, or after the browser's `online` event (which only
  re-claimed the game, so a claimed game that failed a send was not resent then). A paused game with
  nothing changing was not retried until the page was hidden or reloaded.

## What happens now (T-2614)

- A failed send of the game in hand is tried again every minute by the clock worker's tick, paused or
  not, and at the browser's `online` event.
- When the playing game's latest change has failed to reach the account for 2 minutes, the yellow dot
  beside the search glass (the "Saved only in this browser" dot) shows with the hover
  "Not on your account yet!" and the reason, then "This game is saved in this browser." and, where a
  retry can help (offline, server not reached), "Click to try again." A click saves by hand, which
  sends it at once. The dot goes within 5 s of a send going through. Account full or game too big:
  the same dot, the reason saying what to do, no click line.
