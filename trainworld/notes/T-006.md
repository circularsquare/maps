## Model and fit

The game's own doubly constrained gravity on the pack's 6,154 zones (T-042 commuters: 8.61M),
solved in numpy (dense float32 decay matrix, Furness to 1e-3, 2-3 s per solve), scored on:

- **length**: share of trips in the wrong 2 km band (half the sum of absolute band differences,
  0-120 km);
- **county pairs**: correlation of log shares over the 25 x 25 county pairs, share of trips in the
  wrong county pair, and the own-county share.

Fitted by minimising length + county share misplaced: a grid over exp(-d/b) (b 6-14 km) and
d^-a exp(-d/b) (a 0.5-1.3, b 15-70 km), then Nelder-Mead in each family (~60 solves, 3 minutes).
All results in `data/work/nyc.fit_decay.json`.

| decay | mean km | trips misplaced by length | county pairs log r | county share misplaced | own county (LODES 42.8%) | within 4/10/20/40 km |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| exp(-d/9 km), before | 15.3 | 15.0% | 0.828 | 18.5% | 38.9% | 8.9 / 36.3 / 73.6 / 95.7% |
| best exp, 9.0-10 km | 15.3-16.5 | 14.7-15.0% | 0.83-0.84 | 18.5-18.9% | 36-39% | |
| **d^-1 exp(-d/29 km)** | **18.4** | **2.9%** | **0.881** | **15.5%** | 39.0% | 15.8 / 40.0 / 67.5 / 88.3% |
| LODES | 19.6 | | | | 42.8% | 14.2 / 39.1 / 66.7 / 86.9% |

**No exponential fits**: its best (about 9-10 km, the current value) still puts 15% of trips in
the wrong band, too few short ones and far too few long ones (100x too few at 100 km). The
two-parameter form (the "combined" or Tanner function) fits the whole curve: the power term
gives the steep fall over the first few km, the exponential the long tail. There is a ridge
(a 1.0 with b 29 km, a 1.3 with b 45-70 km and a 0.8 with b 20 km score within half a point of
each other); the optimum was a = 1.01, b = 29.2, rounded to 1 and 29. The mean comes out 18.4 km
against LODES 19.6 km (6% short; within 10 km 40.0% against 39.1%).

County flows improve too (log r 0.828 to 0.881) but are where the rest of the error is: the
model now slightly overstates the smallest county pairs (far-apart counties) and understates
own-county commuting (39.0% against 42.8%). Population as the home weight (not resident workers)
and the missing local labour markets of outer counties are the likely causes; not chased here.

## Effect on the game (New York, hand-made network, morning peak, free flow + one crowding round)

Gravity solve 2.4 s (28 Furness iterations, was 61); city open unchanged (1.9 ms). Commutes in
the morning peak 3.87M (4.58M before T-042). Rail 922,722 = 23.8% of them (24.8% before T-042 and
this fit); other leg 33.6% of rail; recompute 520 ms native.
