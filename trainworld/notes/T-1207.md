# T-1207: panning on a slow CPU

Done 2026-10-26 (agent t1207). Rules now in docs/architecture.md "While the camera moves"; the
Settings switch in docs/ui-panels.md "Units and performance".

## What changed

Stage 1, for everyone (`map/moving.ts`: `MOVING_PLACE_MS` = 100, `PlaceThrottle`, `PanPlane`):

- The overlay keeps `moving` from MapLibre's movestart to moveend (also on the renderer and in
  `game/ui.ts` `cameraMoving`), and repaints once on moveend so every placer places in full.
- Station names: a full placement at most every 100 ms while moving; between, the name plane follows
  a flat pan as before, now past its 128 px margin too; through a zoom the names hold still until the
  next placement.
- Capacity tags, attraction marks, zone loading bars: their marks moved into a plane (`.cap-plane`,
  `.mark-plane`) that a flat pan translates; a full placement at most every 100 ms while moving.
  Capacity tags used to re-place every tag on every frame (80-86 ms a frame at 6x in T-1200's trace).
- Platform crowds worked out at most every 100 ms while moving (`renderer.ts`).
- Station sounds and faces sample every 250 ms instead of 125 while moving (`stationLife.ts`).
- Lines tab: the live counts (`game/today.ts`, four answers a second, each re-rendering three cells
  in all 109 rows) wait while the camera moves; the tab reads the view's centre only when sorted by
  Near (`listScope.ts` `scopedLists(..., near)`), so a moveend no longer re-renders it.
- Transfer bubbles built straight into typed arrays (two passes) instead of a growing number array
  copied three times.
- The audio context opens when the page is next idle after the first click or key ends, with no
  button held and the camera still, and only with Sound on (`audioOut.ts`); before, at the first
  sound, which came mid-drag (444 ms at 4x).
- `?perfOff=moving` (measurement only) turns stage 1's waiting off, for A/B.

Stage 2, the lighter map (`map/lite.ts`, `basemap.ts` `setBasemapLite`): hides `building`,
`landuse_residential`, `highway_minor`, `highway_path`, the three aeroway layers, `waterway`,
`waterway_line_label`, `airport`, `highway-name-major`, `label_other`, `label_village`,
`water_name_line_label` and the hillshade (46 visible layers to 32), and caps the pixel ratio at 1.
Settings "Lighter map": Auto (default) / On / Off; its hover adds "On now" when Auto has switched.
Auto: median gap between MapLibre frames drawn while moving, last 40 such gaps (gaps over 250 ms and
frames while the clock is held for a load left out), over 40 ms; then on until reload.

## Measurements

Harness: `artifacts/trainworld/t1207/` logs (scratch script `pan6.mjs`, after `checks/t714-pan.mjs`):
real save imported in a new game, demand to its final, then CPU throttled 6x; per scene a jump, 4 s,
then two drags each way (8 px per 16 ms) at Manhattan z12, z14 and Brooklyn z13, and a wheel zoom
11-15. fps from requestAnimationFrame, long-task share of the scene's time. Old behaviour is the same
build with `?perfOff=moving`, interleaved run by run.

The machine was heavily shared (other agents' builds and pages): the same build's fps moved 8-25
between runs, so fps differences are within noise. Our own code's cost is the figure that held:

| 6x, profiled (2 rounds, 4 scenes each) | ours (src + Preact) ms a frame | MapLibre ms a frame |
|---|---|---|
| old (`perfOff=moving`) | 7.7-18.8, mean 13.7 | 10-23 |
| stage 1 | 4.3-13.8, mean 9.0 (-35%) | 13-30 |
| stage 1 + lighter map | 3.8-12.5, mean 8.2 | 8.6-22.6 (round 1 about a third under stage 1, round 2 not) |

Preact alone went from 2.3-5.3 s a scene to about 0.5 s (the Lines tab's counts). What is left of
ours is the overlay's WebGL draw, the crowds' sums at 10 a second, station life, labels.

Unprofiled fps / long-task share, 6x, three interleaved rounds (all four scenes):

| | round 1 | round 2 | round 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| old | 14.3 / 65% | 16.5 / 56% | 8.4 / 76% |
| stage 1 | 14.5 / 76% | 20.7 / 62% | 15.8 / 65% |
| lighter map | 17.0 / 69% | 14.7 / 70% | 17.0 / 61% |

At 1x the same harness pans at 56 fps, no long tasks, and Auto stays off; at 6x Auto had switched on
by the end of the run. Headless Chrome runs at pixel ratio 1, so the ratio cap's GPU saving (for a
2x or 3x screen) is not measured here.
