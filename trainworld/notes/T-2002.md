# T-2002, T-2299, T-2001: three preflight additions (agent qwpre, 2026-11-18)

All in `checks/country_preflight.py` (flags 9, 10 and the off-town flag of `docs/adding-a-country.md` 3) and
`world_plan.GB_SPLIT_FAR`. Proof: runs on the live plan, `world_plan.json`, read only; logs in qwpre's scratch.

## T-2002 FLAG phantom

Rule: a unit with a 1 km GHS-POP pixel of at least 20,000 people whose eight neighbours average under 0.2 of it
(a spike), lying in no GHS-UCDB R2024A urban centre, or in one whose `GC_UCB_YOB_2025` (first year counted urban)
is 2020 or later.

What did not work: the brief's rule (pixel over 20,000 and no urban centre) flags 0 Cambodian rows, because UCDB
draws a centre round every Cambodian phantom (Trapeang Prasat's pixel is in a 241,000-person centre). Spike,
centre size, pixel against centre mean and pixel against centre total do not separate them from Turkish and
Philippine towns that really have a lone 25,000-50,000 pixel (Giresun, Rize, Tarsus: 15 Turkish rows flagged by
the spike test alone). The year the centre first counted as urban does: Cambodia's four rows read 2020 or 2025,
Turkish towns 1975-2015 (Valencia PH 2010).

Results (live plan):

| Country | flagged rows | what they are |
|---|---|---|
| KHM | 4 of 21: tpe, anv, phm, slk | exactly T-2000's four held zones |
| IDN | 6 | yai Yalimo, puc Puncak Jaya (Mulia's 86,000 pixel), toi (Nduga): the Papua phantoms of T-1738, held or shipped high |
| THA, MYS, NZL, KOR | 0 | quiet |
| TUR 6, PHL 3, VNM 2 | rows | pixels in no centre at all, neighbours 300-2,000 each: Palandoken (27,693, neighbours 283), Rodriguez PH (68,513); not proved phantom or real without a census |

A flag does not prove a phantom: compare with a census (admin_totals) before holding a row.

## T-2299 FLAG off town, FLAG cut name

Off town: (a) no pixel within 3 km of the origin holding a tenth of the row's densest, or (b) a pixel of 20,000+
within 3 km of the origin, in none of the urban centres of a row that has some (a centre counts for a row when a
quarter of it lies in the row). Test: Turkey's plan with kon and pld on their origins of 2026-11-04 (git
11405b29: Konya 33.0464 37.7156, Palandoken 41.1203 39.8042; scratch `turold.json`): both flagged. Both origins
sat on GHSL phantom pixels (Karatay 20,330, Palandoken 27,693), which is why (b) is a hot pixel test and not a
distance to the nearest centre: the first version (any origin over 3 km from every centre in its row) flagged 24
rows in Indonesia, 17 in Russia, 14 in Vietnam, 9 in the Philippines, origins at real small towns of 3,000-6,000.
Live plan, rows flagged: IDN 4 (Banjar, Tolikara, Sukabumi, Serang), RUS 1 (Sarov, 85,075 in a closed city), VNM 1
(Thuan Chau), TUR 0, PHL 0, KHM 0, THA 0, MYS 0, NZL 0, KOR 0. Branch (a) fired on no live row. The picture code
crashed on KOR (a LineString part among the neighbours' packs); guarded.

Cut name: not cut in `world_plan.py`. geoBoundaries' RUS ADM2 names pile up at 26 characters (137 of 2,327; 59 at
25, 13 at 27); Canada ADM3 and Cambodia ADM3 end with "*" at 30 and 20. Unit names cannot be fixed from the file,
so a FLAG: a row named or led by a name of a length where the file has at least 20 names, twice the next shorter
length and eight times the next longer one. Russia: 16 rows (the pls row's lead is the Arkhangelsk one); 2 of the 16 read as whole names (Karabudakhkentsky
District, Mikhaylovka), 0 flagged in KHM, IDN, TUR, PHL, VNM, THA, MYS, NZL, KOR.

## T-2001 GB_SPLIT_FAR

Far-flung part test as Malaysia's (`far_parts`, clusters over 50 km): Portugal ADM3 115 of 2,905 features (41
repeated names; Feteira on the mainland and two Azores islands, 0.1-4,900 people far), Sierra Leone ADM3 2 of 165
(Koya 18,500 people 152 km away, Maforki), Indonesia ADM2 30 of 519, all islands (Natuna, Bintan, Sumenep, Raja
Ampat) except "Danau" (the lake feature, 4,366 people 2,400 km apart). Portugal and Sierra Leone added to
`GB_SPLIT_FAR`; Indonesia stays whole as T-1738 decided (the far_towns rule keeps its islands). PRT ADM2 and SLE
ADM1, ADM2 and ADM4 have no far clusters, so the per-country switch splits nothing else. Only replans see it
(Portugal's plan is on NUTS3).
