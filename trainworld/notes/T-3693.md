# T-3693 Show which mode you're building

Anita's old TODO: "make more obvious when ur building single track or double or quad, and when ur looking at it.
also maybe make it more obvious when ur looking at a bus line or tram or train or hsr". Her answer 2026-11-18:
"yeah they should have slightly different halo colors i guess, maybe some more indicators too if u have ideas".

## The three looks (her reference)

Sheet: `artifacts/trainworld/review/t3693-mode-looks.png` (Now, A, B, C; 1:1 crops at zoom 14). Fixture rows
added to the real save on western Staten Island: single, double, quad, high speed double and tram double lines,
a bus line, bare double and bare quad track; building each kind, hovering each row, six selected, and the real E
selected on Queens Boulevard. Probes in the scratchpad `modelook/` (fixture.js, fix2.js, capture.js, sheet.py).

- A, halo only: aura and outline in the mode's colour, wider for more tracks.
- B, halo + words: A's colours at one width, plus "High speed" / "Double track" with their icons by the pointer
  and first in the hover tag (strings reused, none new).
- C, halo + a ring a track: rings round the ghost and a selected or hovered line or piece, one a track; a mark
  of 1, 2 or 4 strokes by the pointer.

The orchestrator picked C (2026-11-18); Anita can swap by letter. A and B were prototypes behind `?modelook=` and
are gone; rebuilding one means redoing it from this description.

## What landed (C)

- Colours (`MODE_HALO_COLOURS`, palette.ts, both themes): regular #0a819d (the track tool's own), high speed
  #5a5fc8, tram #c0603a, bus #a09e2d (the bus tool's own). Not checked against T-2267's method; tram's orange
  sits near the passage, building and delete auras, high speed's indigo near the station's. Hers to judge.
- The track tool's aura and descriptor square take the track type's colour; a mark above right of the pointer
  (`modeMarkSvg`, map/modeTag.ts) draws the count buttons' icon in it.
- Stroke shader `u_halo` passes: rings 1.5 px wide and 1.5 px apart, outermost first, before the selected passes
  and before a hovered redraw (u_only_sel 2), and outside the ghost's outline. Ring count per stroke: double from
  `a_lane`, times two where the stroke carries `F_QUAD`.
- `F_QUAD` (network.ts): `map/quadPairs.ts` finds quad's other pair by drawn points (a parallel double edge about
  8 m off at the middle, grid of segment samples), in the clock worker's stroke build; set on both pairs' track
  strokes and the line strokes over them, and on the ghost when Quad track is picked. So a line shows two rings on
  its double parts and four only where it runs on quad (fixes the prototype's whole-line flag).
- A selected or hovered quad piece lights its other pair too (`u_twin`, `map/quadTwin.ts` on the page).
- Bus lines: one ring in the bus colour (busLayer.ts). Ferry lines take it too. The bus and ferry tools are
  unchanged.
- Hover halos show with plain selecting only, as hover lighting does (T-730).
- Docs: ui-building.md "Click modes", the track type and count bullet.
