# T-3690: "Too close to the end of a piece of track" (agent endgap, 2026-11-18)

## Why the 5 m exists

`Network::split_plan` (sim/src/track/net.rs) refuses a split within 5 m of either end of the piece it splits, or on a
ramp (+-1 m), as `Issue::SplitPoint`. The rule is in the first commit (a75adfd0, 2026-10-09; notes T-008 4.1, the
split that keeps a piece's shape) and has no DECISIONS line. What it guards: no sliver piece and no new PI a hair from a
node (the split puts a PI each side of the node inside a curve; the snap to a piece boundary within 2 m is there for
the same reason). Experiment: with the limit at 1 m, the two sim suites fail only the two tests that assert the
refusal itself (`add_point_to_blueprint`, `a_station_near_a_bare_end_goes_on_the_end_and_a_junction_still_refuses`);
nothing else depends on it. Relaxing it was still not taken: it would let players build pieces under 5 m, which the
brief rules out, and every caller (add point, crossovers, flyovers, `end_station_shift`, links.rs's "5 + 2") reads it.

## Where players met it

Measured on a fixture built by the player's path (play.mjs, sandbox New York, single track: a bare end, a plain joint, a
3-end junction, a single/double joint, the double's bare end): presses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 m from each node along the
track, as a track start, a track end and a station, at zoom 19 (0.23 m a pixel, so the track tool's 12 px node snap
reaches 2.7 m). Stations were never refused (T-1964/T-3412 and the station tool's room snap already move them). Every
track start or end 2-4 m from any of the nodes was refused, 48 of 80 track presses. At zoom 18 and below the node
snap's 12 px is over 5 m (0.45 m a pixel at 18, worked out, not run), so it is a zoomed-in refusal. Scripts: scratchpad endgap/base.js, attempts.tpl.js, run.sh.

## Built: the route end slides

`split_room` (sim/src/track/wasm/draw.rs), called in `route_op_as` after `branch_end` and before the first split is
noted, so the ghost, the click and the edit API agree: a route end on track within `SPLIT_ROOM` (5 m, now a const in
net.rs) of the piece's end goes
- on a plain joint (two ends, opposite sides, no station): the joint becomes the junction, as a click on it does;
- on a bare end when the route's next point lies past it (carrying on from the end);
- else 5.1 m in from the node: next to a junction (no four-end node made by the slide), a hairpin joint, a bare end the
  route heads back from. A piece under 10.2 m is left to refuse.
Ramps stay refused (T-2268). Add point and crossovers are unchanged (crossovers already use an 8 m joint snap).

After: 0 of 80 track presses refused for room (all drawn as single track; double track and flyovers take the same
end, which lands only where a click already could, so they were not rerun). Ramps were not pressed: they are the same
message and untouched here (stations on a ramp: T-2268; a branch off a ramp has no task). The only refusals left in the fixture, before and after alike, are a
route *ending* 1 m from a bare end (the node snap takes the end, and the route would turn back on itself: "The curve
is too tight"), which is not this rule.

Test: `a_route_end_near_a_piece_end_goes_on_the_joint_or_slides_in` (wasm/tests.rs). Sim suites 642 + 836 pass; smoke passes (30 PASS) on the tree's Vite.
