# T-4265: a city's bubbles answer from the figures on screen while a whole solve runs (agent shownanswer, 2026-11-25)

Tree shownanswer (master 7eb5fa9e1). Background: notes/T-4263.md (where it was found), notes/T-4220.md (the snapshot).

## For Anita

- **What changed.** After an edit whose trips are calculated for the whole map (a fare change, an edit to a long
  line, or the quiet refresh after you stop editing), a city you move to shows
  its bubbles in seconds, from the figures on screen, instead of "Loading data" until the calculation ends. On your
  Asia save ("Use more cores" off, 1 worker): Nanjing's bubbles were drawn within 6 s of moving there; the
  calculation ended 364 s after the edit.
- **The lie.** Until the calculation ends those bubbles are the figures from before the edit, as every line and
  number on the map is meanwhile, and for a city not looked at since the game loaded they leave out the trips with
  other places (other zones, longer trips): 5.5% of Nanjing's trip legs. At the end they change to the new figures.
- **Memory, yours to judge**: the page now keeps every city's bubble figures through that calculation, 36 MB on
  your Asia save; before, it let them go at the edit and got new ones at the end.
- **Still waiting meanwhile**: a station's riders, a bubble selection's far end, a city's trips by length, and the
  bubbles of one purpose (School, Shopping and so on). They read what the workers hold per pair of places, which no
  store keeps through the calculation (below).

## Following the player's action

Focusing a city asks for its bubbles: `cellModes` adds every solving worker's bubble sums of the solve the zone shows
(per subzone, plus the everyday legs per cell), the aux worker maps them onto cells (`cell_modes_day`, which needs
the network's subzones), then `withRegional` adds the market's trips with other places (`zoneSquares`, `regLegs`,
`airLegs` and the airport purpose's legs, all from solving workers). After an edit the map shows the old final
(`demandView` keeps its version; a local result only moves its touched zones' `cityVersion`), so every one of
these is a question about the old version. What each found:

1. **The sums.** The page had every zone's sums of that final (`pushed`, sent unasked at its last round), and threw
   them away at the next `dispatch` (`pushed.clear()`, also in the rough solve's). The workers drop theirs at a job's
   start (`sumsByRound.clear()`) and their state at each cluster's set-up, so the `subSums` question came back with
   mask 0 (seen in the run: 37 ms, mask 0) and `cellModes` returned null; the bubbles asked again every 1-8 s
   until the final.
2. **The day.** Off-screen zones' sums at a final were the weekday's only, but the bubbles ask for the day shown, the
   average by default (her save showed day 6, a holiday's): so even kept, they would miss for any city not near the
   view at the final. Those weekday copies were read by nothing but a weekday view and T-1609's refine.
3. **The subzones.** The aux worker answers `cellModes` only for the version in hand (`sameNet`), and it sets every
   cluster on the new network at once; it had no way to say that a zone's subzones are still those of the final shown.
4. **The market.** `regLegs` and `airLegs` read the market parts' state, gone too, and each question waits behind a
   solving worker's pass first (seconds on Asia), several in a row.
5. **A level change** (the zone in view goes fine after 2 s, or an edited or nearby zone): `lodApply` dropped the
   page's cells of the zone and `cityCells` waited for the zone to reopen in every worker, which a busy solving worker
   does only after its solve: coming back to such a zone waited for the whole solve too, though the aux worker, whose
   cells they are, never reopens it (it holds every zone fine).

## Why not T-4220's snapshot

`ShownStore` holds each population's route-node loads, summaries and trails, what `figures` and a side job read.
None of the bubbles' inputs: no subzone sums, no everyday legs per cell, no market legs per square, no subzones. The
page already receives the first two as `sums` at each final, so the fix is their lifetime rather than a copy of them
in the snapshot (a second transfer at every whole solve's start, tens of MB on Asia). The store is also empty when
`snapOn` is off (small devices, short memory) or past its 120 MB cap. The panels that still wait read per-pair state
(a station's riders by origin, a selection's flows, trips by length per pair), which neither store holds; keeping it
is T-4220's stash-and-restore that its design priced at 12-18 h.

## The changes

- `demandClient.ts`: `pushed` keeps, past a dispatch, each zone's sums of the final it shows (`keepShownSums`;
  `shownOf` = `cityVersion` else the view's version), dropped when a newer final of the zone is shown (the solve's
  final, a cluster's final), on a game load and in `fail`; the solve in hand's own rounds as before (T-2485). Late
  off-screen sums of the final shown are taken after the next dispatch. Kept parts are whole when their masks hold
  every period once (`everyPeriodOnce`), not by counting senders, since the pool may change size. `gone(id, version)`:
  the solve in hand, newer, solves the zone (`Solve.solving`, from the lead's `plan`); then `regionalInto` uses the
  last answer (`lastReg`) or none and asks no solving worker, `zoneSq` asks the aux first, and the airport part is
  left out. A relevel keeps the zone's cells (only its squares go), and `cityCells` asks the aux of a releveling zone
  at once (not on a phone, where the one solving worker is the aux too and does reopen).
- `demand.worker.ts`: `keepSums` sends a zone off screen the day shown (the weekday as well for the zones on screen);
  T-1609's kept sums are by day. `layoutSpan`: per commute population, the first and last version it was set on since
  its layout last changed (`layoutAt` in `setUpPops`); `cellModes` also answers a version in that span.
- `sim/src/demand/api.rs`: `DemandApi::layout_kept()`, whether the last `set_network` kept the layout
  (`Service::same_layout`, which is what keeps station access and subzones), so the aux errs to "different" exactly
  where Rust rebuilds them. A version it never set up inside the span is assumed the same (the aux sets up every
  solve at once; not seen otherwise).
- Figures: the solves are untouched. Bubbles after a final are the same numbers: the copy is made by the same calls
  (`sub_sums_day`, `everydayModes`) on the same state the live answer read.
- Memory: the kept sums are the final's own, held until the next dispatch before; now until the zone's next final, so
  the page holds them through the whole solve, when the workers are at their peak. On Asia (1 solving worker) 36.2 MB
  for 126 zones through the whole solve, 36.8 MB after it (127 entries, the old final's dropped). Each solving worker
  sends its own parts, so with "Use more cores" on they would be that times the workers; a kept final's parts are now
  added into one at the dispatch (`keepShownSums`, in `cellModes`'s order, so the same bits), one worker's worth
  whatever the pool. That merge came after the run: typecheck only.
- Subzones through a station edit elsewhere: `packService` numbers a station of no complex past every complex, so any
  station added renumbers them, but Rust's `group` is the grouping (`demand_groups`), not the numbers. Rust test
  `layout_kept_through_renumbered_complexes`: the same network with its complex numbers moved keeps the layout, run
  times changed keep it, a station moved does not.
