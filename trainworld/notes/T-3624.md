# T-3624: driving to school counts twice (2026-11-18, agent schoolcar)

Anita's pad, 2026-11-18: "someone recommends that peopel going to school should use transit more. if easy, i think
thatd be nice to do. kinda like the wealth split, but slightly different i guess." Her call the same day: driving to
school costs about twice what it costs for other purposes (pupils are driven, they don't drive). One factor, nothing
else.

## The rule

`everyday::SCHOOL_CAR_MUL` = 2.0, read through `everyday::car_mul(kind)` (1.0 for every other kind). School and
university are separate everyday kinds (2 and 3), so university keeps 1.0. The factor multiplies the car's whole
perceived cost of a school trip (drive minutes, ownership, parking at both ends, any cordon) and the paid-space
extra, before anything reads them, so the logit's damping scale (`mode_beta`), walking against the car, the ratio
curve against rail and the two-land-parts bound all see the doubled car. Multiplying by 1.0 is exact, so other
purposes are bit for bit what they were (shown below).

Where it applies:

| place | what |
|---|---|
| `everyday.rs` the pass (the subzone-pair choice) | `road` and `paid` times the pair kind's factor |
| `everyday.rs` `no_rail` | the sample's walking share with no rail (feeds the walk totals and what rail takes of walkers) |
| `trip.rs` `everyday_choice` and the explain's no-station branch | the trip explorer, through `TripInputs::car_mul`; its road cost (`h[12]`) is the doubled one |
| `intercity/pass.rs` the outings across a zone line | `ep.car * car_mul(k)` in `mode_beta`, the walk logit and `ratio_share`; the band softening keeps the plain car (a trip-length measure) |
| `cross.rs` `no_rail` (`bin/cross_everyday.rs`, offline) | the outings' walking share with no rail, written into the market files; the shipped files keep 1x until the next world run |

Not touched: commutes (`kernel/mode.rs`, the market's cpairs), the longer trips between cities (`zone_car`, `alt`),
airports, the drive to a station (`p_ride`), and `intercity/selection.rs` (it reads `e_frac`, which the pass
sets). `market_trip.rs` and `setup.rs` untouched too.

App: the trip explorer's car "Effective time" hover sums drive + parking + ownership; for a school trip the
doubled total no longer matched, so the hover adds a term `driven by a parent` (the road cost minus the three
parts; shown only when over 0.05 minutes, so only for school). New string `tripText.termDrivenByParent`.

## The intercity part

calib leaves the regional market off, so the outings across zone lines have their own test:
`intercity::tests::school_outings_weigh_the_car_twice` solves the made-up world with one outing pair as errands and
again as school, a medical pair beside it: in every period the school pair's rail share is at least the errand's
(more where rail is offered), and the medical pair's share is the same to the bit. The shipped market files' walk
shares for these outings were made with 1x and stay so until the next world run (`cross_everyday` now writes 2x).
