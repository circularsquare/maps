# T-3622: transit and driving value money differently in Malaysia (2026-11-17, vot)

## What the player saw

The local trip explorer (a trip inside one city), the two "Effective time" hovers, with Singapore dollars chosen as
the display currency (a global setting; nothing to do with Johor Bahru being next to Singapore):

- By transit: "Walk 4.3 × 1.5 + wait 2.2 + ride 5.6 + fare $2.46 × 8.86 = 36 min"
- By car: "Drive 24 × 1.33 + parking 10.3 + ownership $5.63 × 2.31 = 55 min"

Reproduced by playing (scratch `market.mjs`, `repro.mjs` in vot's scratchpad; a two-station line, 4 trains):

| Start | Transit (min per S$) | Car (min per S$) |
|---|---|---|
| Johor Bahru | 8.86 | 2.31 |
| Kuala Lumpur | 8.86 | 2.31 |
| Singapore | 3.23 | 2.31 |

Johor Bahru takes Malaysia's weight, as it should (no country mix-up).

## Why they differ: a recorded rule, and a recorded oddity

- A fare dollar weighs 4.4 x (90,000 / GDP per head)^0.5 minutes by the trip's country (T-1277, T-2670;
  docs/demand-worldwide.md "Value of time and fare sensitivity"). Malaysia's GDP per head is $13,125:
  11.52 min per US$, / 1.3 S$ per US$ = 8.86.
- The car's money in the local layers (ownership, parking money, a drive to a station) stays at 3 min per US$ in
  every country (`FARE_MIN_PER_USD` in the sim, `CAR_MIN_PER_USD` in the app): 3 / 1.3 = 2.31.
- So the hover is right: demand itself weighs the two that way. It is not a display bug.
- But it was left, not chosen: T-1277 "left at 3 minutes a dollar for now. Scaling it too would be consistent but
  would move every poorer city's mode split, so it wants its own measured step"; T-2668 "a separate oddity, not
  proposed here"; demand-worldwide.md "their car's money should scale too".

How far apart they are (fare weight over the car's 3): US 1.47, Singapore 1.40, UK 1.83, Japan 2.33, China and
Mexico 3.7, Malaysia 3.84, India 8.3, Nigeria 12.7. Every country differs, the US too. Making them equal is a demand
change: the car's ownership money would weigh more almost everywhere (3.8x in Malaysia), driving loses ground most
in poorer countries, and the ceiling tests (notes/T-636.md) need a rerun. One way that keeps the US where it is:
the car's money at each country's fare weight, with the ownership dollars ($2.00 a trip, $0.13 a km) divided by
1.47 so a US trip's car costs the same minutes. Not measured; waits for Anita.

Between cities (the regional market) the two already match: Kuala Lumpur to Seremban shows the train's fare at
3.55 min per S$ and the car's running cost at 3.58 (the car's is the mean over three income groups).

## A hover bug found on the way (fixed)

In the market trip (between cities), when the trip starts in a country other than the start city's, the train's
effective time hover wrote the fare in the start country's prices at the start country's weight, so it disagreed
with the Fare row beside it. Singapore start, Johor Bahru to Singapore:

- before: Fare row $1.63; hover "... + fare $4.52 × 1.28 + ..." (car "running $4.10 × 3.58")
- after: hover "... + fare $1.63 × 3.55 + ..." (same 239 min total)

Fix: `trainEquation` in app/src/ui/inspector/MarketTrip.tsx takes the trip's fare scale and writes the fare term
times it, its weight over it. Display only; perceived minutes unchanged. Changelog c014 (fares, fix).
