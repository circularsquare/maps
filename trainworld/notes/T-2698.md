# T-2698: start cameras on the city

Agent bug-f, 2026-11-16. Moved the `center` field (the start camera, also the Cities list jump and the market place centre) of the 124 metros whose registry centre was over 5 km from the nearest same-named OSM place, onto that point (`centre` in artifacts/trainworld/t2690/scores.json, kind "city"). Zones untouched. `origin` (the pack frame) is not changed: the pack is built around it. Largest moves: Owerri 35 km, Kochi 33, Oklahoma City 31, Zhangjiagang 30, Virginia Beach 29, Hajipur 29.

`pipeline/register.py` writes center and origin from one point, so re-registering a metro resets its center to the old spot.
