### A typical trip, term by term (trip explorer, high demand, perceived minutes)

Station 19 to station 23, 5 stops, 2.1 km. Each mode alone:

| term | tram | bus |
|---|---|---|
| walk to stop / from stop | 2.2 / 2.8 | 2.2 / 2.8 |
| wait (half the headway) | 2.1 | 0.36 |
| ride | 6.15 | 8.71 |
| fare, per-km part (base 11.06 both) | 1.87 | 1.88 |
| boarding cost | 0 (street stop) | 0 |
| crowding | 0 | 0 (fullest bus 50% of rated, minded from 85%) |
| sum of the parts that differ | 8.25 | 9.07 |

The tram's 2.6 minutes of faster ride is mostly spent on 1.75 minutes more waiting. Under T-4041 (riders take
whatever reaches their stop first, 25% the first that comes) the explorer splits this trip tram 48%, bus 41% (the
rest on neighbouring stops). By trip length at high demand:
- 3 stops (6 to 22, 1.2 km): tram 5.74, bus 5.40: bus 67%, tram 29%.
- 5 stops (19 to 23): tram 48%, bus 41%.
- 8 stops (11 to 1): tram 11.8, bus 14.2: the bus can never arrive first, tram takes all of it.
At medium demand (bus every 2.9 min, tram every 9) the 5-stop trip goes bus 55%, tram 34%, and even the 8-stop
trip gives the bus 42%.

So the split follows from the two headways: a bus every 43 s beats a 4-minute tram on every trip shorter than about
5 stops, and in the 10 off-peak hours the bus runs 2.3-3x as often as the tram. Crowding is applied to buses (the
fullest bus is at 50% at the 1-minute headway, so it never bites; at a 4-minute headway the fullest bus reaches
105% and is held to its room).

## Every way the model treats a bus and a tram differently (code, today)

- Wait and ride: none; same rules (T-4041 first-arrival pool at a shared stop).
- Boarding cost: street tram stop 0, off-street tram stop or tram at a rail station 2 + 2 perceived min, bus 0
  (T-4041, T-3856). A tram drawn without Snap to streets is off-street and pays 4 min a trip; T-4041 measured that
  as 21-22 points of a tram's share beside a bus with its own stops. Not a factor on her HK save.
- Walk: a bus-only stop is reached from its corridor (straight distance to the route + 100 m, within 600 m,
  BUS_REACH_M; T-4056 put it back from 1 km), at most 2 bus stops a cell; tram stops are walked to straight, at most
  3 tram-only stops among a cell's 6. Not a factor where they share stations.
- Fare: bus per-km part 0.8x by default (Bus multiple). Her HK save's bus fare comes out the same as the tram's.
- Crowding: bus minded from 45 aboard (85% of 53, BUS_CROWD_START), train or tram from 70% of rated; both capped
  at crush (bus 80). Applied in every round.
- Speed: bus 85% of the car's street speed, never under 25 km/h moving, 28 s a stop; tram up to 60 km/h (street) or
  75, 30 s stand plus braking. In a dense centre the tram is much faster (Amsterdam 1.9x, HK 1.45x).

## Options (trials on her HK save; none built)

1. Leave it. The split is what the T-4041 rule says for these headways; at equal headways the tram carries 3x.
   No cost.
2. Rail feels nicer than a bus: a bus minute counts more than a tram or train minute (a "rail bonus"; T-2921 notes
   list studies, Dresden weak but consistent, Yap 0.6x, Stockholm none). A complicating factor, her call. Trial
   (bus hop times scaled in the demand network only; this also slows the bus slightly in the walking rule):
   - 1.2x: Bus 1 86,270, tram 89,093 (as saved: 105,092 / 75,621); rail trips 228,619 (-3.2%). At equal headways
     37,272 / 128,617.
   - 1.5x: Bus 1 64,629, tram 104,112; rail trips 218,580 (-7.5%); Bus 2 51,117 to 45,255.
   Calculation time: one multiply per hop when the network is built, no extra work in the solve (not benched).
3. Limit how often a bus can run on one street (say every 2 min, 30 an hour, about what one stop's kerb handles
   without bunching). A change to what players can build, her call. Trial at 2 min in high demand: Bus 1 71,803,
   tram 102,750, rail trips -2.6%. No solve cost.
4. Earlier crowding on buses (her guess): would change nothing on this save. The fullest bus at the 1-minute headway
   is at 50% of its load, under even a train's 70% start. No solve cost (the rounds run already).

perf_bench not run: no option adds work to the trip calculation.
