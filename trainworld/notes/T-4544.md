# T-4544: moves per line beside the lattice (stage 2 of 18, T-4511)

Agent shift2, 2026-11-28, tree shift2. Sim only; nothing a player sees, no save change, no strings, no changelog item.

## What is built
- `track/moves.rs`: `Move { run, at, secs, kind }` (kind: Own, With, Gap, Auto), `Moves = Arc<[Move]>` sorted by `at`, `secs()` (the lookup), `words()` (what hashes read), `sorted()`.
- **The match** (`moves::secs`): a departure of a run takes the move of that run nearest its unmoved game time, within half its headway (strict); two moves for one departure leave the nearer; a move no departure is within half a headway of waits (not applied). It reads only the departure and the list, so every window agrees. Seconds are clamped to +-1800 (`MAX_MOVE_S`). Stage 5 replaces `at` with the key plus `lead` and the clamp with the range.
- `Service.moves`: one `Moves` per line (`moves_of`; `set_moves` sorts and notes the line changed). `Lattice` stays `Copy` and unchanged.
- **Applied**: `run_deps_as` adds the seconds to `draw` (so `end` follows) and keeps `dep` unmoved: the min-gap cut, the table's key, the variant place and the boarding key all read the unmoved time. `lattice_deps_moved` and `lattice_deps_local_moved` (widen the window by 1800 s, move, cut to the window) feed retime (`day_deps`, `around`, through `Built.moves`) and `span::stop_deps` / `line_span` (arrival masks). With no moves they call the old functions, so the output is the old one to the bit.
- **Keys**: `Lattice::timing_key(&moves)` now returns `TimingKey { lat, moves }` (`LineSvc::timed`, `Held`); `timing::cluster_key`, `span::span_key` and `Boarding::key` (current and every era's) hash `moves::words`, which adds nothing for no moves.
- **Eras**: `Era.moves` is the moves the line had before the edit (`Before.moves`); a loaded era has none (as its variants).
- Tests (`moves_tests.rs`): one departure moves by its seconds with dep, level and var kept, its neighbours and the other direction equal to before; two moves for one departure leave the nearer; a move for another day moves nothing; four windows agree; a moved time leaves or enters a window; no moves again equals before; an era keeps its moves.

## Left for later stages
- **`hand_over` and `Trip`** read `end` = moved draw + length, so a moved train is ready earlier or later than its unmoved stand: stage 3 adds `Trip.moved` and measures from unmoved times (rule 1). Until then nothing sets moves in a game.
- **Trips are chosen by unmoved `dep` in [a, b)**: a train moved over a window's edge shows by its `dep`. Readers of drawn times (board, chart) take a margin of the range (about 6 min) or filter on `draw`: stage 13.
- **`set_moves` makes no era** when the pattern is unchanged, and has no edit lead: stage 3's `Op::MoveTrain` does that (`before_edit` and `note_changes` around it).
- **`stop_pats` (span/gaps.rs) ignores moves** (the round method): stage 9. Kinds of day: `weekday()` makes retime read every move on the weekday reference; the calendar split is stage 7.
- Nothing saves moves (stage 8). `words()` is a hash input, not a save format.
