# T-4606 Zone pair hover counts everyday trips

Cause: the zoomed-out hover on a zone other than the selected one took its figure from the market's pair row
(`zoneRowOf`: long trips plus commutes, both ways). Everyday trips across a zone line are not in that row (only
a per-place total, `IntercityDemand.everyday`), but the trip explorer asks the workers for the pair
(`market_trip_between`) and adds them. Two totals for one pair.

Fix: `game/trip.ts marketKinds(periods, ways)` is the one sum (long x ways + commutes + everyday, with rail and
walking); MarketTrip.tsx and the hover (`map/intercityView.ts pairTag`) both use it, and the hover asks the same
`askMarketTrip` the explorer's click does (client-cached per round), showing "Calculating trips" until it lands, then
refreshing the tag (`demandHover.refreshHover`). If the workers cannot answer it keeps the old two-kind figure.

Run (nyc sandbox, 3-station line, final done): hover New York to Philadelphia 944k trips per day = 465k intercity +
309k commutes + 170k everyday; the explorer on click: 944k, 465k, 309k, 170k. Picture:
artifacts/trainworld/T-4606-hover-and-explorer.png.

Not changed, listed: the bubble's size and its label ("Philadelphia . 774k trips") still count long trips plus
commutes only; the city panel "To other places" already adds everyday trips (per place total); the "Grounded"
achievement counts long trips plus commutes, no everyday trips (explainer 11-28); the bus-only "no station"
shortcut in the explorer is T-4604's. Hovering many zones asks the workers once each per solve round.
