# T-897: all of China as whole-country zones (plan, 2026-10-22)

A plan for Anita, not a build. Measured today on a scratch copy of the world plan (nothing in
`pipeline/`, `data/` or the plan changed); scripts in the session scratchpad `china\` folder
(`dryzones_cn.py`, `osm_check.py`, `costs.py`, `batches.py`), throwaways.

## In plain words

- **Zones.** On Anita's 0.5M floor, mainland China comes to **about 1,200 countryside zones** beside
  the plan's **80 metro rows** (plus Hong Kong and Zhuhai-Macau) and the built Shanghai pack: about
  1,280 packs, more than the whole of Europe so far (757 registered). A 1M floor would make it about
  **650 zones**. Zones are counties (geoBoundaries ADM3) merged within their province.
- **Sources.** Everything needed is open. Population is the strong part (the 2020 census by county
  and the ASPECT 100 m grid built on township counts). Jobs by district exist only for a few cities,
  so the tier 0 proxy, scaled by province from the 5th economic census, is the honest choice. No
  commuting flows exist; zones take the zone curve and metros the published mean commute. OSM is
  good for roads, rail, water, places and admin lines and almost empty for shops and services.
- **Cost.** About 30-45 hours of machine time at 12 cores (3-4 nights), twice that at 6; agent
  tokens about 30-60M weighted (3-6% of a week); about 25-30 GB of disk at the peak (50.8 GB free).
- **Five things the dry run found** that must be fixed before any China run (section 5).
- **Recommendation:** fix those, then a pilot province (Jiangsu: 8 metros, 51 zones, one 80 MB
  extract, no dead space), then the rest in provincial waves with one joint solve at the end.

**Units.** The plan's unit level for China is geoBoundaries ADM3 (2,852 counties, gbOpen of Dec

about 330, median about 3.5M) are far over the 1.5M cap, and townships are too fine and not in

high: at county grain China's countryside is many units the size of a European zone.

**Shanghai (sha): kept as it is.** It is the whole of Shanghai municipality (16 districts, census

### Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan (Anita's call)

The data treats them three ways at once: geoBoundaries' China file includes Hong Kong, Macau and
Taiwan (as "Taiwan Province", with pre-2010 county names in pinyin: Taibeixian, Gaoxiongshi);
Natural Earth and ISO have HKG, MAC and TWN as their own; Geofabrik has Hong Kong and Macau under
`asia/china/` and Taiwan as `asia/taiwan`. The dry run therefore planned **Taiwan twice** (16 zones
from CHN's units, 14 from TWN's) and a 1,199-person Macau sliver zone (São Francisco Xavier, Cotai).

Recommendation, which is hers to decide:
- **Hong Kong and Macau in**, each as its own place named "Hong Kong" and "Macau" (Macau joined to
  Zhuhai's pack as the plan has it, or its own small zone). The game shows place names, not
  countries, so nothing needs saying about sovereignty; their own sources (HK census by district,
  their own currencies and border crossings with Shenzhen and Zhuhai) can wait.
- **Taiwan in, from the TWN units only**: 4 metro rows (tai 10.4M, tac 4.0M, kao 2.9M, taa 1.3M)
  and 14 zones (5.1M), named by today's names (Taipei, New Taipei, Taichung, Kaohsiung, Tainan,
  Hsinchu...; Taiwan's own romanisation, not mainland pinyin). CHN's units on Taiwan are dropped.
  Kinmen and Matsu (beside Fujian's coast) and Penghu would be tiny island zones (0.05M, 0.01M,
  0.07M). They must not join a Fujian zone; I'd keep them as their own small zones.
- **Disputed borders**: geoBoundaries' Cona County runs south to 26.9 N, over Arunachal Pradesh
  (India's), so the dry run gave Cona 0.92M people, most of them Arunachal's, and an India extract.
  Aksai Chin likely sits in Hotan's units the same way (dead space, so it did not show). China's
  units should be clipped to the line each side holds on the ground (OSM's admin_level 2 or Natural
  Earth's de facto country polygons), so no zone claims land another country's zones cover.
- **Names in the UI**: Latin only (her rule), English exonyms where English has one (Beijing,
  Guangzhou, Inner Mongolia, Tibet, Xinjiang) and pinyin otherwise. No zone name says "Province",
  "Special Administrative Region" or "Republic", so the game takes no side in its labels.

## 3. Runner fit

- **Country module or tier 0 alone.** The plan's 80 metro rows already carry `archetype C, module
  china`, but `pipeline/china.py` serves only Shanghai (its district table is Shanghai's). The zones
  take `D, module null` (T-779's rule). To use the census, china.py needs a general population hook:
  ASPECT's grid summed into each pack's cells (any Chinese pack, metro or zone), plus the province
  jobs shares. About the size of a city task (2-4M tokens), and it would also serve the metros. It is
  optional: tier 0 alone works, at GHSL's errors (+20-50% in old city centres).
- **Geofabrik extracts.** China is 34 province extracts under `asia/china/` (1.73 GB together,
  Guangdong 174 MB the largest, Macau 3 MB), plus `asia/taiwan` (328 MB); `china-latest` (1.61 GB)
  is not needed. The plan already assigns each row its province extract (Henan 105 rows, Shandong
  100, Hebei 90, Sichuan 81, Hunan 80...), so each province is one region of one run. Reading:
  Germany's 4.4 GB read in 54 minutes, so all of China about 20-25 minutes.
- **Units and the planner.** `world_plan.py zones` crashed on China's units (GEOS "unable to assign
  free hole to a shell" in `zone_groups`'s union of 500 m simplified geometries): needs
  `shapely.make_valid` on the simplified units (my scratch patch). It also needs Taiwan's units
  dropped from CHN, the disputed edges clipped, and a rule for the Macau sliver.
- **Dead space.** The 20-a-km² rule leaves 239 counties and **20.9M people** out: Xinjiang 7.7M
  (50 counties, among them oasis cities in huge counties: Altay, Artux, Kargilik, Hotan County,
  Keriya), Inner Mongolia 4.1M, Tibet 2.9M, Qinghai 2.0M (Golmud, Delingha), Sichuan 1.7M (Kangding,
  Garzê), Gansu 1.1M (Dunhuang, Yumen). Europe's dead space was thin farmland; here it is towns of
  50-300k in counties the size of Belgium. I'd change the rule for counties over, say, 5,000 km² to
  the density of their settled land (GHSL cells with people), so oasis towns become zones and the
  empty land between stays empty.
- **Names.** geoBoundaries' unit names are pinyin with tone-free diacritics ("Samzhubzê District",
  "Nêdong District"), and its ADM1 calls Guangdong "Guangzhou Province". `zone_name` would leave
  "County", "District", "City" and "Autonomous County" in. Docs/zones.md "Names": a merged zone shows
  its biggest city; at 1,200 zones that wants a script (Wikidata P442 and OSM `name:en` for the lead
  unit's seat) rather than T-847's hand list, then a look by an agent.
- **Time zone**: one, Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8) for every mainland row; Hong Kong and Macau the same
  offset. Xinjiang's unofficial local time is ignored, as the official clock is what timetables use.
- **Joint commute group.** Every Chinese pack within 50 km of another chains into one group, the
  largest yet (about 1,280 packs against Germany's 110 and Japan's 45). Joins so far ran 0.12-0.4
  minutes a pack (wave 4: 185 packs in 23 minutes at 6 cores), so one China solve is about 3-8 hours
  at 12 cores, and the runner re-solves the whole country's unit at every join. Two changes: join
  once at the end (or after the pilot and at the end), and let a country this size solve by province
  with its neighbours held fixed, as T-786 does by country. The solve's memory at 1,280 packs is not
  known; worth measuring on the pilot.
- **Market (intercity) packs**: today's zones have a median 0.57 MB `.bin` and 0.18 MB
  `.market.json`; China's zones hold more people, so perhaps 1-2 MB and 0.3 MB each: 1.5-3 GB of
  packs in all. Startup files grow too: the registry (0.6 KB an entry, +0.8 MB) and
  `data/packs/outlines.json` (2.4 MB for about 760 zones, so +4 MB).
- **Worker memory** is the real limit. A countryside zone costs about 4 MB per solving worker (T-850,
  1.4-1.6 KB per 2 km zone plus cells; T-852's mixed grid saves opening time, little memory), and
  every reached zone sits in every solving worker. A Beijing to Guangzhou high-speed line (2,300 km)
  reaches perhaps 40 zones and 6-8 metros: 40 x 4-6 MB x 5 workers is 0.8-1.2 GB before the metros
  and market packs (gua alone would be well over 100 MB a worker). A national network is several
  times that. At a 1M floor the zone part halves; cutting gua matters too.

## 4. Cost

**Machine time.** Wall per row in recent runs, reads, gates and parks included: 2.6-2.9 minutes at
4 cores (Germany 86 rows in 223 minutes, Romania and Moldova 28 in 82), 6-12 at 2 cores (central
Europe 41 rows in 252 minutes, the Nordic run 22 in 271 with archipelago builds). Rows' own steps
over 1,341 rows: median 1.3 minutes, 2.0 at 0.6-1M people. So, with three runs of 4 cores:

| Part | 12 cores (overnight) | 6 cores (day) |
|---|---|---|
| 1,198 zones at 0.9-1 minute a row | 18-20 h | 36-40 h |
| 80 metros (367M people; about a minute per million people, gua alone an hour or more) | 3-5 h | 6-10 h |
| Downloads and reads (1.73 GB) | under 1 h | about 1 h |
| Joint solve, once | 3-8 h | 6-16 h |
| Reruns of parked rows (Europe's waves parked 5-20% once) | +20-30% | +20-30% |
| **All** | **about 30-45 h, 3-4 nights** | **about 60-80 h** |

At a 1M floor the zones' part roughly halves (650 rows, though each builds a little longer): about
20-30 hours at 12 cores. CPU today: another agent's merge holds cores, so the pilot at 4.

**Agent tokens** (weighted, notes/city-costs.md). Tooling first: planner fixes (make_valid, Taiwan,
disputed edges, dead-space rule, Macau), the PRD recut, the province join unit: 3-6M. A china.py
population hook (ASPECT) and province jobs: 2-4M, optional. Batch supervision: Europe's T-788 ran
360 rows in four waves with one long-lived agent and fixes along the way; at about 2-4M a wave of
100-150 rows, China's 8-10 waves are 20-40M. Names for 1,200 zones by script and a look: 2-4M.
**About 30-55M in all**, 3-6% of a week at 9-14M a percent.

**Disk.** Today 50.8 GB free; `data/work` holds about 14 MB a pack (10.6 GB for 757), `data/packs`
about 1.25 MB. China: `data/work` 15-20 GB, packs 2-3 GB, GHSL tiles for China about 1 GB (kept as
for every city), ASPECT about 1-2 GB if used, a region's extract and caches at most 1-2 GB at once
(deleted after), join backups 2-3 GB (keep the last). **About 25-30 GB at the peak**; clear
`data/work/pre-T788/` first.

## 5. Risks and recommendation

Found by the dry run, to fix before any China run (each a small pipeline task):
1. `zone_groups` crashes on China's unit geometry (make_valid).
2. Taiwan planned twice (CHN's "Taiwan Province" and TWN); Macau's sliver zone; hon and zhu's
   double-counted plan figures.
3. Cona County holds Arunachal Pradesh: clip China's units to the de facto border, and check Aksai
   Chin and the other borders the same way.
4. Dead space drops 20.9M people, oasis cities among them: settled-land density for big counties.
5. One country-wide joint group of about 1,280 packs: join once, by province.

Other risks: worker memory on long lines across many zones (section 3); gua as one 40M pack; rural
roads thin in OSM (Henan); zone names at scale; the first runs with T-852's `--coarse 1000` grid on
dense Chinese countryside (2 km zones in Henan hold far more people than Europe's, so fewer blocks
will coarsen).

**Stages.**
1. Planner fixes (list above) and the PRD recut; re-plan CHN and TWN; check the zone map.
2. **Pilot: Jiangsu**, 8 metros and 51 zones (or 30 at 1M), one 80 MB extract, no dead space, beside
   built Shanghai, on `--coarse 1000`; join alone with sha. Measure build times, the joint solve's
   time and memory, a long line's worker memory (Shanghai to Nanjing to Xuzhou), drive factors.
   Guangdong is the harder pilot (gua, Hong Kong, Macau) and better second.
3. Waves by province, east first (Zhejiang, Anhui, Shandong, Henan, Hebei with Beijing and Tianjin;
   then the centre; then the north-east, south-west and west), two or three runs at once, one join
   at the end.
4. Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau when she has decided.
5. A China module (ASPECT population, province jobs, CAAC airports) whenever she wants the metros
   tailored; the zones work without it.

## Decisions for Anita

1. **Zone size for China**: the 0.5M floor (about 1,200 zones, median 0.79M) or a higher one for
   China (1M: about 650 zones, median 1.47M, half the machine time and worker memory)? I'd take 1M:
   China's counties are already the size of a European zone, and a national network reaches many.
2. **Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan**: in or out, and Taiwan from its own units with its own names? I'd
   include all three, Taiwan from TWN's units, no country labels anywhere.
3. **Disputed land**: clip China's (and India's, Pakistan's) units to the de facto line? I'd do it.
4. **Dead space in the west**: keep the 20-a-km² rule (20.9M people out, oasis cities among them)
   or judge big counties by their settled land? I'd change it.
5. **The Pearl River Delta**: cut gua (39.9M) into prefecture rows? I'd cut it.
6. **A China population module (ASPECT)** with this task, or tier 0 alone first? I'd do tier 0 first
   and the module after the pilot, if the pilot's centres look over-full.
7. **Machine time**: three or four overnight runs at 12 cores.

## Build (T-897 stages, from 2026-10-22)

Anita's decisions (2026-10-22, DECISIONS.md): floor 1M, cap 2.5M; Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan their own
countries; de facto borders; no density dead space in China; Guangzhou split by prefecture; pilot our
choice (Jiangsu); no exclaves (zones contiguous, by land or a short water gap). Scratch scripts: session
scratchpad `china2\` (recut.py, names_cn.py, wd_fetch.py, setup_sim.py, backup.py, setbuilt.py).

### Stage 1: planner fixes (`world_plan.py`, `intercity.py`)

- **De facto lines** (`de_facto`): every geoBoundaries unit (`gb_units`, so the plan and
  osm_units' outlines alike) loses the land Natural Earth gives another country; a unit mostly in
