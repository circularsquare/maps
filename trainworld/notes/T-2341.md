## Retrospective
- Wall times: desk plan (countries2) about 30 min; patch, retire and market heal 6 min; run 1 44 min (all parked); rerun 21 min;
  join 49 min; terrain and drives 1 min; checks 15 min.
- Two runs, not one: sum and krw were built while the old jak pack still held their units (a changed metro row must be
  unregistered before the rows that take its units build: retire.py did this for T-1189, jak_patch.py's step list did
  not say so); jak and sra parked on the gate's browser wait, broken for every country since T-2365 (13:42) changed the
  top bar's hover. Proposed for the playbook (section 4): when a hand patch moves units from a built row to new rows,
  unregister the built row (`world_run.Run({}).unregister([id])`) before the run.
- Worth less than its cost: nothing here; the gate's regex is a check that broke silently with an app change
  (T-2298's family): a gate whose browser check fails on every row of a run should stop the run as a fault, not park.
