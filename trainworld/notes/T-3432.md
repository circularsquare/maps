What the screenshot says: the trip explorer, New York to Philadelphia, 944k trips a weekday, 112 km apart. The day's inter-city split is
99% car, 1% transit (so rail carries something somewhere), but the panel "By transit, Inter-city, evening peak" says "No transit route
serves both ends in this period". That text is marketTrip.noRail with why = 2 (NO_RAIL): in `market_trip_w`
(sim/src/demand/intercity/market_trip.rs) `rail_all` is 0, meaning for that period no zone pair of the two places had an offered rail mix:
`solvable(a, b)` (intercity/setup.rs) false for every pair, or `rail_mix_x` summed to 0. `solvable` needs both zones served by a station
(`served_at`), a car or plane to compete with, the pair's trips at least min_trips, and the two zones' stations in one track component
(`served_comp` shares an id). A mixed-up component id (two lines that cross without a shared station) or a period in which the lines
have no trains would both give this text.
