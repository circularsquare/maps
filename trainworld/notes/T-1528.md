Built in tree t1528. The rules as built are in docs/track-and-service.md 6.1 ("Shared by a
complex", "How they are drawn") and docs/ui-inspectors.md (the upgrade row); this note is the
design and why.

## 1. How a lot is drawn

- True to size on the ground, like the base map under it: a bay is a row of 2.5 x 5 m spaces, a
  6 m aisle and another row (16 m). One bay per station with park and ride, at most three. As long
  as the longest platform with park and ride, 80-300 m, cut to whole spaces. A single 200 m
  station's lot holds 160 spaces; Grand Central's (16 stations, the 300 m cap, 3 bays) 720.
- A straight rectangle along one platform of the complex, at its middle direction, outside every
  box (its near edge at least 20 m from the platform's centre line, past a box at zoom 14 where the
  box is about 32 m wide on the ground; at zoom 15 and in, where boxes are 16 m, that leaves a
  gap of about 12 m).
- Drawn by the network renderer first, under the bus lines, track, stations and trains
  (`map/parkingLots.ts`): a surface a little off the land's colour, a hairline outline, the
  spaces' side lines across each row and a line where two bays' rows meet back to back, in a
  slightly darker tone. Light: surface #e7e3dc, lines #c4beb4 on land #f3eee5. Dark: #39352f and
  #625b52 on #292520 (the first dark pass, #34302b, all but vanished at zoom 15).
- Zooms: from zoom 14 (where boxes start), a faint outlined strip; the spaces' lines fade in
  between 2.5 and 4.5 px apart, about zoom 16 at New York's latitude, so they never turn into a
  grey hatch; bays' head lines from 4-8 px. Below zoom 14 nothing is drawn; the P after the
  station's name stays as the sign at every zoom.
- Blueprint (none of its stations built): 0.6 opaque; every one switched off: 0.3, as boxes are.
- Hover: "Park and ride", the station, "Shared by n stations" when more than one has it. A click
  selects that station, so its complex opens with the button to remove it.

## 2. Placement

In the clock worker with the rest of the network's buffers (`map/facilityPlace.ts`, replacing
T-1416's `annexSide.ts`), complexes in node order, each placed against what earlier ones placed.

- Waiting room: still an annex joined to a box (Anita wanted it on the box, T-1416; it is inside the
  station). One per complex: of the stations with one and both sides of each, the spot with the
  fewest other platforms (2) and tracks (1) within reach, as T-1416 scored it, plus car parks
  already placed (2) and the name label's patch (1). Ties go to the outside of the bend.
- Car park, after the room: candidates nearest first, over the near edge's distance 20, 30, 42,
  56, 72 m, then each platform of the complex (park and ride ones first, longest first), then the
  side away from the label first, then along the platform at its middle, a quarter and a half of it
  either way. A candidate is bad by 3 for each platform centre line within 18 m (half a zoom 14
  box plus a little), 2 for each track within 6 m, 2 for the room or another lot within 4 m, 1 for
  meeting the label's patch. The first at 0 wins.
- The label: station names are DOM labels placed below the station where its track runs east-west
  and to its right otherwise (stationLabels.ts), so the patch is about 180 x 50 m below or
  150 x 50 m to the right of the complex's middle, by its first station's way: about a name's size
  at zoom 16. Zoomed further in, a name covers less ground and the patch is generous; further out,
  more, but the lot is then a faint strip the name sits over anyway (names are above the canvas).
- When nothing fits at full size: one bay, then 0.6 of the length (at least 60 m); when nothing
  fits at all, the candidate with the least in the way. It is still drawn there, under the track
  and boxes, so the player sees the facility exists and can hover or click it.
- Long straight track is one segment between far vertices, so a segment is tried a cell (40 m)
  apart along its length for nearness, not just at its ends (the first version missed tracks
  passing a station between two far points; the check's one-side case caught it).

## 3. Complex-wide facilities

- Storage: each stays a bit on each station (`StationData::upgrades`), the only thing demand reads.
  No new save field, no sim or wasm change: `set_station_upgrade` already takes many nodes in one
  edit and charges what changed on built stations.
- The complex view's upgrade row now has park and ride, the waiting room and the passage (the
  terminal stays per station: it is track at one platform). A button is on when every station has
  it. A click builds it at every station that lacks it (US$20M or 5M each, so n times one station's
  price for a complex that has none) or, when all have it, removes it from all (no refund, as
  before). A complex where only some have it shows as not built; its hover says "2 of 3 stations
  have one" and the click builds it at the rest, at their price only.
- Drawn once: whatever number of its stations have it, a complex draws one lot and one room.
- The station view keeps its own buttons for one station, as before.
- When the complex changes:
  - a station joins (built near, or linked by a passage): it has no facility until the player
    builds it there; the complex button shows the partial state and offers the rest. No automatic
    charge, since a player placing a station would not expect a bill for a car park.
  - a station leaves or is removed: its bit goes with it; the others keep theirs; the lot stays
    while any station has one, a bay narrower once fewer than three do.
  - a complex splits (a passage removed, a station moved): each part keeps the stations it had and
    their bits; each part with one draws its own lot and room, placed again.

## 4. Saves

Nothing to migrate: the bits are where they were (station node flags bits 4-6). An old save's
per-station park and rides draw as the complex's one lot, its waiting rooms as one room per complex.
Effects are unchanged, since they were always per station.

## 5. Demand

No change. Park and ride's 10 perceived minutes off a ride to or from the station and the waiting
room's 0.8 stay per station, read from the same bits; a complex-wide build is the same as building
each station's. The lot's size and its spaces count are drawing only.
