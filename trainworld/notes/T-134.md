# T-134 — Boston and Washington, DC

Both new cities reuse the established US pipeline with broad metro footprints. No new trip
model or city calibration. Pack generation and totals: [T-134-packs.md](T-134-packs.md).
Expanded regional endpoint model and native checks: [T-134-market.md](T-134-market.md).

The shared city registry includes both cities, their pack origins and their stable market IDs
(Boston 3, Washington 4; NYC 0, Philadelphia 1 and Mercer 2 retained). The existing workers,
water-mask loading, boundary layer and city navigation consume the registry. Place labels are
now registered too, and the JS longer-trip decoder scales its headers with the number of
markets. Inspectors show every represented place, and Whole region includes the new cities.

Acceptance is intentionally limited to valid packs and working gameplay using trusted sources.
`t134-browser.mjs` checks city navigation, local bubbles, regional selection matching, a running
local line in each new city, station queries and loading the existing NYC save. Results:
`T-134-browser.json`. Captures: `../../artifacts/trainworld/T-134-bos.png` and
`../../artifacts/trainworld/T-134-wdc.png`.

Validation: 101 native tests pass, including the new five-market routing/flow conservation
regression. The expanded real pack loads with finite results and positive rail on the existing
corridor fixture. TypeScript, demand WASM compilation and production Vite build pass; all four
cities' JSON/binary/boundary assets and the regional pack are shipped. Browser checks exercise
both new local populations and their longer-trip endpoints, earn local train riders in Boston
and Washington, decode station queries, and load the old NYC save with no browser exceptions.
