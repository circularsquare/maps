# T-2722: the arrival masks' segments made when needed

After T-2707 (notes/T-2707.md) the masks' segments were 185 of 340 MB of the boarding account
natively on all of Japan with three kinds of day. Done 2026-11-07, agent boardmem, main tree.

## Changes

- `span::Mask` holds pieces, not segments: a plain segment (start, share), or a whole tolerance ramp
  (start, step, count, the departure it leads to). A ramp's segment `j` starts at `t0 + j * h` with
  share `within(d - start - h / 2)`, the same expressions `stop_mask` made it with, so they come out
  the same to the bit. `stop_mask` still builds the segment list (phi reads it) and keeps a ramp as one
  piece only when every step of it became its own segment; in test builds it checks the pieces give
  the list back bit for bit.
- Positions are (piece, segment in it). `Mask::find`, `seg`, `segs_from` (start, end, share, mark).
- The running sums (`Masked::pre`) sit at every piece's first segment and every 8th of a ramp
  (`span::MARK_EVERY`), not every 8th segment: a departure's `cum` lands in the plain segment just
  before it, so it adds nothing up, and the ramp's logarithms are taken only where a time falls inside a
  ramp. `with_shared` works the shares out once for its two passes.
