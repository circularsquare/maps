## Re-run
`python trainworld/tools/split_track.py --ref master` from the tree, after rebasing. It reads the unsplit net.rs and wasm/tests.rs from git at --ref and rewrites the split files. Methods go to the group of the latest cut point before them (NET_CUTS), so new upstream methods land beside their neighbours; NET_EXTRA pulls single methods into stations.rs. Tests likewise (TEST_CUTS); helpers used from two areas stay in tests.rs. It refuses a ref that is already split. Hand edits to split files are lost on re-run, so re-run before landing.
