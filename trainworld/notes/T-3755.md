# T-3755 national tertiary figures, new Caribbean countries (agent tertcar, 2026-11-19)

Added to `pipeline/tier0.py` NATIONAL_TERTIARY; taken up at each country's next rebuild (no rebuild, no packs touched).
Share is students over the World Bank population of the same year.

- Trinidad and Tobago: 20,889, 2025, UNESCO UIS indicator 25053 (public API api.uis.unesco.org), share 0.0153. Ministry of
  Education 2019/20 digest (Tables 133, 134) has 28,280 in public tertiary institutions (UWI 16,126, UTT 6,354,
  COSTAATT 5,800) plus 16,193 estimated private: higher, older, and the private part is an estimate. UIS picked per brief.
- Cayman Islands: 1,423, 2023, UIS 25053, share 0.0195.
- Bahamas: about 7,666, 2022: University of The Bahamas about 5,000 (Board of Trustees statement, Feb 2022; UB's own later
  reports say 4,575 / 4,235 / 5,200 for other years) plus BTVI fall 2022 2,666 (Tribune 2023-10-19). No national count and
  no UIS since 1995. Share 0.0193.
- Dominica: about 1,500, 2023, Dominica State College's own page. No UIS (last 1999-2005 only for post-secondary), no
  ministry digest found. Excludes UWI Open Campus and offshore medical schools. Share 0.0226.
- Haiti: nothing usable. UIS stops in the 1990s, no MENFP/DESRS total found; only undated guides saying 100,000-180,000 and
  institution-level drops. Left to the existing fallback (campuses unscaled).
- World Bank SE.TER.ENRL is null for all five, 2010-2025.
