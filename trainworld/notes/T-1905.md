## The pattern

A city-wide total is shared by a weight, and the weight is lumpy or thin where the place is odd:

- the residents' outings over the listed places within 25 km: few listed, each takes a big share (Okazaki, Nagoya
  Castle 10.8M against 2.2M, USS Iowa 11M, Christ the Redeemer 25M). Against the 1,273 Wikidata figures the budget ran
  x0.70 where a place's share was under 2% and x3.2 (90th percentile x11.7) over 30%;
- a city's social outings over leisure jobs: a theme park's, airport's or stadium's staff are a lump of "leisure jobs"
  that pulls social trips (Disneyland), on top of the attraction's own visits;
- a block's jobs over its land: a big block (an airport, an industrial estate) makes a field of equal bubbles (JFK).

## Proposed (not built: demand rules or packs)

1. **An attraction's staff draw no social outings** (T-1932). Within 1 km of an attraction, leisure jobs above 2,000 a
   cell, up to the attraction's visitors a year / 1,000, are taken out of the social weight (its visits come through the
   attraction purpose). Emulated on the packs: Disneyland's 1.2 km 645k -> 375k trips (social 428k -> 156k), within 2x
   of ~200k; the Met's 1 km unchanged (no cell over 2,000; without the threshold it wiped every Upper East Side
   restaurant's pull); LA's leisure jobs -3.2%, New York's -1.1%. In the sim (`beside_with` already has the attractions),
   no interface change.
2. **Airport jobs on the terminals** (T-1933): a block's jobs over an airport go to its airport_trips cells, so JFK is
   one cluster. Pipeline, US packs rebuilt.
3. **Parking cap 50 -> 40 perceived minutes** (T-1934). `park_paid_cap_min` in sim kernel.rs, mirrored in
   pipeline/intercity.py (`PARK_PAID_CAP_MIN`, baked into the market packs). 15 ln(d / 3000) reaches 50 at 84,000 jobs/km²
   and 40 at 43,000. Zones at the cap, jobs-weighted mean paid parking:

   | | cap 50 | cap 40 | cap 35 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | New York | 3 zones (Midtown 171k jobs/km², Lower Manhattan 117k; 12% of jobs), 14.0 min | 6 zones (18%), 12.4 | 9 (20%), 11.5 |
   | Tokyo | 2 (Marunouchi 110k; 4%), 12.8 | 8 (11%), 12.1 | 12 (15%), 11.5 |
   | London | 3 (the City 145k; 18%), 17.4 | 7 (28%), 15.2 | 7 (28%), 13.8 |
   | Osaka | 0, 7.8 | 2 (7%), 7.5 | 2 (7%), 7.2 |

   Midtown, the City and Marunouchi each lose 10 perceived minutes a car trip. Needs the ceiling tests rerun (A sat at
   84-86% in T-636).
4. **Stadiums**: no data in the attractions build (stadiums, arenas and sports venues are in `NOT`); their pull is
   their leisure jobs' social outings, local (1 km decay). Wider pull = T-263 stage 3 (capacity x events a year as
   attractions, drawn at the attractions' 10 km halving).

## Answered, no change

- Attractions' intercity pull: visits are drawn inside the zone (half from homes, half from leisure jobs standing for
  hotels, halving every 10 km); between zones only the superholiday lean (T-1374) uses them.
- Industrial jobs: US jobs are LODES counts by block, spread over the block's land, so a big industrial block is a
  wide even spread, not extra jobs. Tier 0 countries use the proxy, which demand-worldwide.md already says overdraws
  industrial land (Brampton +40%).
