## T-2574: the bar

On a cache hit the card went at the bar's place (10-39%) with "About 1-5 min left": the bar's stages were the full
load's (the slot's last full load remembered; a cache-hit load is never remembered). Now:

- once a cached result is expected (`expectCache`: the read found an entry whose zones, markets, format, build,
  workers and flags match, only its packs' digests still to come), the stages are the game, the packs' download and
  the result's showing (`CACHE_SHOW_MS` 1.5 s once the network and packs are in); `keepPlace` keeps the bar where it
  is and runs it from there. A read that then misses (`log.miss`, `dropEarly`) goes back to the full load's shares;
- when the card goes at the cache (`cacheDone`), `endCardFull` keeps it 0.4 s with the bar full and no time left.

| path | bar before the hit is known | then |
|---|---|---|
| first reload after an import (the ahead read misses, T-2552) | 10-39%, "About 1-5 min left" (the network, then the read, within a second) | 100%, no time left, 0.3-1.6 s, gone |
| a later reload (the ahead read hits 3-12 s in) | climbs to 94-99%, "A few seconds left" / "About 10 s left" while the network is worked out | 100%, gone |
| a cache miss | as before: the card goes from 98-99% (`checks/t2552-load-bar.mjs` PASS) | |

Left as is: before the network on a first reload nothing says the cache will hit, so the bar sits at the game stage's
share of a full load until the read, then jumps to full.

## T-2533: answering the zone in view

Before: the cache's result is version 0, which no worker holds; `applyCache` ended the focus pool, so every question
waited for the re-solve's round 0 (the main pool's packs open first: 30-140 s after the card).

Now (`workers/demandClient.ts`):

- `applyCache` hides the focus pool's figures (`hideFocus`) and keeps the pool; `startFocus` starts it under a shown cache too;
- the focus pack downloads as soon as the save's stations are known to reach the zone in view (`focusSoon` from
  `stationsAhead`), not at the network (all loads);
- with the cache shown the focus solve stops at round 0 (no `go`): its later rounds only took the cores from the main
  pool (2 cores, North America: the main solve was sent 30-55 s later and its final 30-140 s later with them);
- `target` sends the zone in view's questions to that round 0 (`focusAsked`); a question asked before the focus solve
  is sent waits for it (`focusComing`, woken by `wakeFocusAsks`);
- `retireFocus` ends the pool once the main pool's solve of the cached network is sent and the focus round 0 has
  answered what was asked of it (1.5 s after its last answer, for a far end's second question), or at once if that
  solve already has a round of the zone.
