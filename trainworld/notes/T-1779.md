# T-1779, T-1780, T-1781: track placement on real saves (2026-10-29)

Anita's playtest: branching off a line froze 1-5 s with the ghost stuck near the line ("curve is too
tight"), on the New York save we built (not on her hand-made Tokyo); placing a blueprint took ~150 ms
to the next ghost; curving in beside track took tight curves.

## T-1779: the freeze

Cause: every preview is a trial edit (`TrackWorld::trial`), and the trial was a full commit: after
applying and checking the track it rebuilt the services of the lines on the touched track and ran
`settle_train_counts` over every line, then did it all again rolling back. A branch splits a line's
track, so its line was rebuilt. On the shared-track New York (`artifacts/trainworld/t1305-shared/
nyc_real.save`) that settle never converges (every edge `sep`, many lines sharing track), 0.3-0.9 s
each way, so 1-2 s a preview natively and 1-5 s in WASM. The page keeps one preview in flight
and the newest waiting (not a queue), so the ghost showed the last answer it had, from near the start,
and its "too tight" was that early position's. Positions near other lines were worse only because
they split lines with bigger settles.

Fix: `trial` checks the track and measures cost and crossings without touching the services
(`commit_with(.., service: false)`); no preview caller read them. Same accept and refuse results.

Native (`cargo run --release --bin branch_bench -- <track bytes>`, 1440 previews, 60 edges):

| save | before mean / p95 / max | after |
|---|---|---|
| shared NYC | 135 / 1303 / 1990 ms | 0.5 / 1.0 / 5.3 ms |
| real NYC (`data/packs/real/nyc.save`) | 0.7 / 1.7 / 20 ms | 0.4 / 1.0 / 3.3 ms |

Browser (`checks/t1779-branch-ghost.mjs`, shared NYC, 7 branches, 30 moves out to 400 px):
before mean ask 508 ms, max 4959 ms, ghost behind the stopped pointer 2461 ms mean (4947 max);
after mean ask 7 ms, max 178 ms (one, the first ask of a run), behind 0 ms (2 max).

## T-1780: the click

Clock worker after a blueprint click on real NYC (the `edited` answer's ms): apply 6-10, state
5-16, buffers 27-40, post 8-17, and `board` 54-62 (`sendTrips`: the boarding account's hour twice,
built for every line, plus the split line's account redone because its round trip moved 1e-10 s).

Fix: a `route` edit that charges nothing and changes no line for demand sends no trips (the drawn
trains and loads stand), and the boarding key hashes times to the microsecond, so a joint cut into a
line keeps its account. Real NYC: worker total 102-153 -> 53-70 ms; click to next ghost 255 -> 156 ms
mean (placed ones).

Not done (two follow-ups for the orchestrator to file):
- the state build and post (state + buffers + post, ~45 ms in the worker) and the main thread's
  handling of the new state (~40 ms) still sit between the click and the next ghost. Under 20 ms
  needs the next preview answered before the state is built, or the state built in less: a design
  change (the tool checks its start node against the world, so the state has to arrive first).
- on the shared-track save every service edit settles 0.3-2.9 s (`edit_bench`: headway edits
  0.4-2.9 s, counts 0.24-1.2 s), so a branch that splits a built line still takes 1.3-1.8 s to place
  there. The settle circles: round trips of lines away from the edit move ~11 s each time it runs.

## T-1787: the settle on shared track (agent t1787, state at hand-back)

Built (tree t1779, tests pass, smoke passes on 6199):
- **The edit's settle leaves a line within 0.0001 trains of its fleet alone** (the first stage's
  freeze, now at the settle's own tolerance; `settle_counts(full: false)`).
- **A save carries the settled rates** (`LRT1` block after `LBS2`, `save.rs`): each line with trains,
  its three f32 rates to the bit, then the circling lines the settle kept (`Network::settle_kept`,
  moved there from `TrackWorld`). A load with the block settles with the edit's settle and changes
  nothing; a save without it (all saves so far, or one changed to another frame by `convert_frame`)
  settles in full (`full: true`, HEAD's algorithm to the bit: every line stepped and probed).
  `Network::rates_settled` says the rates are worth writing (a settle has run or a block was read);
  `rates_from_save` picks the load's settle. This is what fixed the reload test the fast freeze
  failed: a tenth-of-a-train-an-hour rate in the old line record restarted the settle.
- **The edit's probe from the capacity solve alone** (`Service::probe_trips`): the 1% probe's
  round-trip rise is read as the change in each line's hold delays (a hold adds its delay to the
  run, by construction in `profile::run_with`), on a clone of the capacity state, so profiles are
  worked out once a round, not twice. Same fixed point; only the step's slope estimate differs.
  Loads keep the profiled probe.

Bit equality (`lines_info` compared, `cmpdump.py`): every load (the four real saves and six shared)
identical to HEAD; every save-and-reload after the edits identical to what was saved; smoke's T-007
figure 12,758,872 rail trips, HEAD's exactly (the first stage's 9 trips are gone, since loads now
run HEAD's settle). After the edits: `data/packs/real` nyc, tyo, par identical; the shared saves and
T-007 differ by under 0.0001 tph on settled lines, more only on lines held at full track, where the
rate is the fill's share and depends on the path (T-007 line 1 rush hour 0.069 tph, 6.5 s of round
trip; New York shared line 35 0.03 tph). Accepted: the same lines differed under the first stage.

Left (the target was well under 100 ms p95): New York's headway edits on its busiest line (two or
three settles of ~15 rounds, 150-400 ms) and Paris placements' tail (p95 237 ms: Paris lines carry
~9 holds a run and level, a profile ~2-3 ms a line, 13-33 lines re-profiled a round).

Tried and dropped:
- held lines (short at full track) left out after the first round: New York edits mean 69 ms,
  max 240, placements p95 99; but `a_change_on_full_track_moves_no_train` fails, a held line's
  share then depends on when it stopped stepping (with its last want kept for the fill, a train
  vanishes at the edit instead). The jitter is real: each round the held lines' fill shares move a
  little and keep their neighbours (New York 12, 14) just over 0.0001 for a dozen rounds.
- rescheduling only the lines whose rates moved in each pass: exact, no gain (the cost is profiles).
- binary-searching a hop's speed-limit breakpoints in `profile::intervals`: exact, no gain (a run
  has ~170, a hop ~25; `hop_time` itself, ~4 us, ~8.5 calls a hold in the crawl bisection, is it).

## T-1781: curving in beside track

Cause: a route coming in to the line beside track turned onto it where it had gone along the run as
far as the start is from it, a 45 degree turn whose auto radius is limited by that short leg (about
500 m from 150 m off).

Fix (`beside.rs`): 17 turn-in places from there out to 90% of the way, each sized by the route's own
fit with the line's corners as they are; the widest wins (up to the design radius, then the nearest),
skipping any whose straight in crosses the run or whose fit fails; the old place if none. Test
`coming_in_beside_takes_the_roundest_turn` (over 1000 m from 150 m off). The fit leaves out a node
start's lead-in, so a start on a track end is sized slightly generously.
