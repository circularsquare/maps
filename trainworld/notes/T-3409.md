## 1. Does building far away change a city's schematic?

It depends on how far away and whether a line joins it. Measured on the real New York save (`T-007-real-nyc.save`, 854
complexes, 109 lines), Vite 6186, light theme, water off. The hashes are of what the layout worker posts (places, pills,
ribbons, line paths, names, borders), as in T-3360.

- **A line far away and not joined (more than 500 km from every station, T-3448's regions):** no effect. It is its own
  region on its own sheet, laid out after the one in view; New York is the same to the bit.
- **A line within 500 km** (Philadelphia here), even with no track between: the two join one region and one sheet
  (`regionGroups`), so the city's field, cells and room change. Manhattan came out more crowded (pills over lines at
  Penn Station and Grand Central).
- **A line that connects to the city** and runs far: one region whatever the distance. Past 600 km across, the sheet
  switches to its country settings (cells scale with the whole map, the cut, the turn pull), so the city is redrawn at
  a different turn and spacing (125 St 31% nearer Times Sq, Atlantic Av 40% farther). T-3453 (finer grid near dense
  cities) is the fix for this one if she wants it.
- **Track with no line, far away**: changes the drawing, a small bug. With one region the clock worker builds the graph
  from all track (`postDiagram` -> `diagramFrame`), and the frame's origin is the middle of every track's box (rounded
  to a degree). Track in Chicago moves New York's origin from about -74 to -81, the float32 places round differently,
  and the layout drifts (Times Sq to Union Sq 69.4 -> 76.6 pitches). With two regions or more each is built from its own
  lines' track (T-3448), which is why the Chicago line case is identical. Fix: build a lone region from its own lines'
  track too, as the several-region branch does (about half an hour; it may move today's drawing of saves with unused
  track once). Not filed (the brief says file no tasks).

**Speed.** A far region does not slow the city in view: the region nearest the camera is laid out first and shown, the
others after (the 4-station Chicago region took 4-5 s more, mostly its sheet reading the ground). A region whose graph
is unchanged is not laid out again, so an edit in Chicago does not re-solve New York. Two caveats: the layout worker
does one region at a time, so an edit in New York made while Chicago is being laid out waits for it; and a joined or
connected region is a bigger solve. The timings I took (New York shown 15 to 40 s after M, same input) were too noisy to
compare cases.

**Picture and shares.** A far region is placed beside the others 24 pitches off, so it adds its own size to the saved
picture: here 2,395 -> 2,472 pitches wide, the same names level (4.2 px). A big far network (another whole city) would
push the picture to a smaller level. Shares drop name levels only when the file passes the server's size limit.

**On this save, "one city" is the whole network**: 1,754 of its stations are in the New York zone (the hand-drawn
Northeast pack extent) and 2 in New Haven. A city schematic matters for games with several cities.

## 2. Ways to get a schematic of chosen cities

Common to all: "a city" is the zone a station is in (`stationZone`, the Cities tab's rows, as fares by city, T-3766),
so countryside zones can be picked too; a station in no zone (dead space) is in no chosen city. The whole network stays
the default, keeping her 2026-11-16 call that the schematic draws everything.

**A. Hide the rest (paint time).** A button on the schematic's top row opens a list of the cities with stations, each a
checkbox, "All cities" first. Lines with no stop in a chosen city and stations outside them are not drawn (the mode
buttons' mechanism, T-3360), and the view and saved picture frame the chosen cities. Instant, no new layout. A line
that leaves is drawn whole (the mask works per line), and the city keeps the shape its neighbours gave it, so it does
not fix part 1. About 1.5 hours. Risk: low; looks unfinished where long lines run out of the frame.

**B. Lay out the chosen cities alone (my pick).** The same list on the top row. The clock worker builds the graph from
the chosen cities' stations only: each line cut to its runs inside them, ending at its last station inside with a short
stub (2 pitches) the way it goes on (a "to Philadelphia" label later if wanted). Chosen cities more than 500 km apart
are separate regions as now. The city is drawn as if nothing else were built, which answers part 1 directly. Kept
per game in the save's header JSON (zone ids are per game; no save level, as fares by city), default All cities. The
whole network's drawing is kept, so going back is instant; each new choice is one layout (seconds; up to about 20 s on
New York). Saved picture follows the choice. Share: I'd keep "Share network" the whole network (the Community card
shows the network), which costs one whole layout when sharing while a choice is on; else a share sends what is shown
(`sharer` returns the drawing shown today). About 3-4 hours. Risk: a line cut into two runs needs both picked by one
click and hover (the drawing keys lines by id); the stub is new drawing work.

**C. "Schematic of this city" from the city view.** A button in the city view (inspector, beside home and go-to) opens
the schematic of that one city, laid out alone as in B; closing it or pressing M again goes back to the whole network.
One city at a time, nothing saved, no list to build. About 2-2.5 hours. Risk: no sets of cities, so the second half of
her ask is not met; harder to find than a top-row button.

Hours checked against T-3360 (buses and ferries on the schematic, about 1.7 hours of agent time).

**What was built**

- `diagram/cut.ts` `cutLines`: each line cut to its runs of two stops or more inside the chosen cities (stops matched along
  the path by complex), each run a line of its own with the line's id, `from` (its first stop's index in the line) and
  `cutA`/`cutB` (the way the track goes on, measured 3 km along it or to the next stop). Stations outside are made no
  station (a copy of `complexOfNodes()`), so nothing passed or beside outside enters the graph. `busRuns` does the same
  for bus and ferry lines (their route cut between the run's ends), used by the clock worker and by the page's thin lines.
- Clock worker: `postDiagram` cuts when cities are chosen and builds the graphs from the runs and their own track only
  (`diagramGraphs`, the old body, now taking the network, the stations' complexes and the kept points); `lineRegion` and
  the lone-bus `byId` are keyed by `runKey` (id, or id/from), since two runs share an id. Header field `schematic`
  (zone ids), loaded, reset on a new game, sent to the page as `diagramCities`; the page's `diagramCut` message carries
  the cities and the station nodes and bus stops in them. `diagram` takes `whole: true` for a share.
- bareframe (T-3806) interplay: I did not touch the `if (groups.length > 1)` line or its comment; my nearest changed line
  is two lines above it. A trial `git merge-file` of my clock.worker.ts over bdd8ae88 merges with no conflict. With it, a
  cut lone region goes through the per-region branch, whose extra track comes from the cut track only, so the frame stays
  the chosen lines' own track.
- Page (view.ts): `syncCut` sends the inside stations and stops (by `stationZone`, as fares by city) before the diagram
  opens and again when the cities, the world or the zones' outlines change; a load resends. The whole network's region
  sigs are kept (`keepSigs`) and their layouts not dropped while cities are chosen, so All cities again is instant. A
  share with cities chosen asks for the whole network's graphs too and answers from the kept layout, else lays them out
  after what is shown (`wholePlan`, `shareReady`). Thin buses and ferries are cut with `busRuns`, stubs added after the fit.
- Drawing: `stubEnd` adds 3 pitches past a run's cut end, straight on unless the line goes back (over 100 degrees), before
  the pills; `BESIDE_CUT` 1.05 makes a cut line beside an axis cost a bit more (see Findings). `lineTag` counts stops from
  `from`. regionSig hashes `from` and the stubs.
- UI (`ui/DiagramButton.tsx` `CityChoice`): the cities icon button after the mode buttons, pressed while cities are
  chosen; a list under it, "All cities" then each zone with a built station or a stop, by name, tick boxes. Esc and a
  click outside close it (Esc stops there, so it no longer also closes the diagram). Shown when there are two zones or
  more, or a choice.

**Findings**

- Pre-existing, not changed: where parallel lines end at one station together, an axis along them ties with one across
  (a line beside the axis costs its distance, the pill across costs its length), and the tie goes to the horizontal axis:
  a dot on one line with the others beside it. Weighting every line beside an axis 1.05 changed New York's whole drawing
  (pills 8843257604ea, ribbons and names too), so I kept the weight to a cut line at the station its stub leaves from.
  Worth its own look if Anita wants terminating bundles drawn with a pill across.
- A bus stop's id can equal a station's (world.ts skips such a stop), so the page sends stations and bus stops as two
  lists and tests a bus stop at a station by its station.
- Which zone a far station is in depends on the zones' outlines being loaded (`placeAt` falls back to the nearest
  centre); the page sends the stations again when outlines arrive, so a choice can redraw once after the map has been
  near there.

**Not done / limits**

- A line with a single stop inside the chosen cities is not drawn (no hop to lay out); its station shows if another line
  stops there.
- No "to Philadelphia" label on a stub.
- An edit made while the diagram is up and cities are chosen may lay out twice (the clock worker posts with the old inside
  stations, then the page sends the new ones).
