# T-3265: S-Bahn and other commuter rail in the real networks (agent sbahn, 2026-11-12)

Anita's TODO: "some people complained about s-bahn tracks not being included in the real world tracks".

## What players most likely saw

**The real network layer leaves out Berlin's and Hamburg's S-Bahn track.** OSM maps both as
`railway=light_rail` (with `usage=main`), and the game's real network layer (`app/src/map/realNetwork.ts`,
`SHOWN`) draws only `subway` and `rail` track from noritetsu's tiles; the track tool's snapping graph
(`pipeline/realgraph.py`, `KIND`) keeps the same two. So anywhere in the world a player sees the main lines
and the U-Bahn faintly under the map, but the S-Bahn beside them is missing, and the track tool cannot snap
to it. noritetsu's own line model has the S-Bahn (all of S41's, S7's and S2's ways are in its ways.json) and
its tiles carry the track as `k=light_rail`: only the game's filter drops it.

`railway=light_rail` with `usage` main or branch, km of track by region (no service track):

|---|---|---|

| United States | 519 | DART 269, Sacramento, MAX, TRAX, St Louis, NJ Transit light rail |

All light rail (any usage): Germany another 1,428 km, the US 2,459 km, Britain 407 km (DLR, Metrolink), Spain 242.
Munich's, Vienna's, Zurich's and most other S-Bahns are `railway=rail` and are drawn already.

## The fix (realauto.py, realnet.py)

- `realauto.cut`: a `route=light_rail` relation is **heavy** when over half its ways are `railway=rail` or
  `usage` main or branch (not tagged unelectrified), it has at least 5 stops inside the pack, and they are at
  least 1 km apart on average. A heavy route runs commuter trains (`OSM_TRAIN_M` 200 m, 10 cars) at commuter
  speed (`OSM_KMH` 40), through a `lines` list the extract writes and realnet's `osm` adapter now reads after
  the source's own. It keeps the light-rail kind, so its own track and pairs (a commuter line would be laid on
  the main-line track beside it: the graph has no S-Bahn track, and the router takes a chain within 120 m of
  the shape) and light rail's default frequency (10 / 6 / 3 an hour, near the S-Bahn's 10-minute service).
- Over the 30 cities only Berlin's 15 S-Bahn lines are heavy (S15, 3 stops, is not). The stop count keeps out
  Hong Kong's and Shanghai Pudong's airport people movers, the electrified test Chongqing's Bishan SkyShuttle
  (all three tagged usage=main). Would also catch, in cities not built: Hamburg's S-Bahn and Copenhagen's
  S-tog (right), and Dallas's DART and some US light rail tagged usage=main (wrong: their trains are 3 cars).
- The extract also records every route it dropped, by reason (`dropped_routes`), for the per-city check.

## Proposed: S-Bahn in the real network layer and the snapping graph (new task)

`app/src/map/realNetwork.ts` `SHOWN` takes `k` in subway and rail; `pipeline/realgraph.py` `KIND` the same. Add
`light_rail` to both, as "other rail" (drawn like rank 1 rail, kind 0 in the graph so the router treats it as
main-line family). noritetsu's tiles carry no `usage`, so the layer cannot tell S-Bahn from tram-like light
rail: all light rail would be drawn (Germany's Stadtbahn, US light rail, the DLR, Manchester's Metrolink),
which matches "rail the player can build on". Trams stay out. Then rebuild all 118 graphs (about 5 minutes,
`realgraph.py`, writes data/packs/realgraph, not during a join) and Berlin's real network once more (its
S-Bahn then routes on its own track: 40% of Berlin's km is off the graph today, nearly all S-Bahn and trams).
About an hour of agent time. It changes what the layer shows worldwide, so it may want Anita's word.
