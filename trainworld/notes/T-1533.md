# T-1533 The zone in view shows its demand within about 5 seconds

Anita, 2026-10-26: "for the city that we're looking at ... we should be prioritizing this because we
are looking at it. but in some cases it can take like >30s."

## Method

Headless Chrome on my Vite, a fresh profile per run, one browser at a time. The time is from the
moment the zone becomes the zone in view (the start menu's pick, the camera's jump, the line built,
the bubbles turned on) until its bubbles are drawn (zoomed in its own; zoomed out to 8.5 or less the
one bubble per zone). Samples every 250 ms of `twDemand`, the overlay's bubble counts, the solve log
(`client.log`), the packs' loads (`client.start.loads`) and the queries (`client.qlog`). The machine
was busy throughout (10-12 cores claimed by other agents against a cap of 10): read the ratios, and
expect Anita's machine to be about twice as quick (T-1479 measured the real save's first round at
21 s there; here 17-53 s). The sampler is scratch; the standing check is
`checks/t1533-view-first.mjs` (below). JSON per run in `artifacts/trainworld/t1533/`.

Two smaller causes found on the way: the bubbles chased the rounds (each round published while the
last answer was still being built threw that answer away, so a zone stepping through rounds every
1-2 s stayed bare until the last), and a weekend's sums, which only the live state holds, came back
empty for every round a worker had passed (the China save shows a weekend), so the bubbles retried
with a growing wait until the solve ended (9 s after the figures).

## What changed

All in the app's demand scheduling (`workers/demandClient.ts`, `workers/demand.worker.ts`,
`workers/demandProtocol.ts`), the bubbles (`map/commuterView.ts`) and the blank map
(`game/loading.ts`); no sim change. Spec: docs/demand-runtime.md "The zone in view first".

- **The map shows at the zone in view's first figures**, a cached result, a rough solve's or an
  estimate, behind the card; the card and the held clock still wait for the final (T-347 stands).
  `viewFigures` in game/loading.ts, reset at each whole-game load (`gameLoadSeq`).
- **Download**: the zone in view's pack first, at high fetch priority, ahead of the markets.
- **Open**: city packs open one at a time, market packs together after them; the page sends a game's
  market packs only once its city packs are in (composing China's 370 held a worker for a minute);
  the zone in view's pack goes to the front of each worker's queue (`open.first`, and a `first`
  message when the view moves onto a zone whose pack is already queued); the aux worker opens one
  pack at a time so it answers the bubbles in between.
- **Rough solve** (`solve.only`): while a whole solve waits for packs, the zone in view, once open in
  every worker and with no figures yet (or with lines new since its figures), is solved alone with
  no market. It starts ahead of the opens waiting, which wait for its free-flow round; its rounds
  show as estimates, never final (no card release, no cache write, no stars), and the whole solve
  replaces them. A cached result shown cancels it (a rough solve of no zone). With figures already
  shown it feeds only the new lines (T-324's path). `?perfOff=rough` turns it off.
- **After an edit** the zone in view's cluster sends its free-flow round at once, for the new lines'
  first figures, instead of after its last round.
- **Bubbles**: an answer overtaken by a newer round still shows while the zone has none; a weekend's
  sums and one purpose's legs are asked of the next round with `wait` (answered as the worker reaches
  it, T-327) rather than of a round the workers have passed.

## Where 5 s is not met, and why

- **The real New York save with no cached result** (a first load, or after a pack or build change):
  the rough solve is New York alone, which is most of the network (1,756 stops); its free-flow round
  is 12-17 s here, about half that on Anita's machine. Only a cheaper first answer would do (one
  period, or the coarse level T-1206 describes, measured 10-20% shorter for New York): proposed as a new task.
  The rough solve also holds the other four zones' packs back until its free-flow round, so the
  final comes a few seconds later than it would (here 86 s against 82-132 s before, within noise).
- **A new line in a city with a big network** (the real save): the edit's solve must set up New
  York's 1,756-stop network before its free-flow round. Same remedy as above.
- **The China save's zoomed-out load**: with no zone in view nothing can go first; the cached result
  shows when the clock worker's network arrives and the cache is read (17-42 s here: T-1156's network
  build), and cold it is the whole load (375 s here, its round 0). A cache readable before the network (the slot's last result) would help: proposed as a new task.
- On this machine every time above is roughly doubled by the other agents' load.

## The standing check

`BASE=http://localhost:<port>/ node trainworld/checks/t1533-view-first.mjs` (about 12 minutes; CHINA=1
adds the Wuhan jump, 3 more): real save cold and warm, the bubbles off and on, a line on the real
save, five jumps on the world save, a line in a new game. Prints each time against 5 s; the real
save's cold load and its new line are reported as known misses and do not fail it.
