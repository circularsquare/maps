# T-3374 Inter-city trips near city borders (Philadelphia - New York)

## For Anita

- Reproduced exactly in a new New York sandbox game: a high-speed line Philadelphia 30th St -
  Trenton - New York Penn and a 3-stop local line across the Delaware at Trenton. The Trip panel
  for Philadelphia - New York reads like the player's: 465k inter-city trips, 309k commute legs,
  170k everyday legs, "By transit, Evening peak", main route the local line two stops at Trenton
  with 46 min "To" and 31 min "From" (player: 44 and 29), effective time 265 min against the car's
  125 (player: 271 and 128).
- The route shown is a real route, but not an inter-city one. The panel's routes pooled three kinds
  of rider: inter-city trips, commutes between the two zones, and everyday outings between them.
  New York's zone takes in Trenton and Philadelphia's takes in Bucks County across the river, so
  "commutes between Philadelphia and New York" are mostly 5 km hops over the Delaware. Those all
  ride one station pair and outnumber the inter-city riders, who spread over several high-speed
  station pairs. The panel then drew that hop from Philadelphia's centre to Manhattan's, which
  reads as "walk to Trenton, ride two stops, walk to New York". This is your "near city borders".
- The high-speed line does carry riders (about 10k a day in the repro), commuters between
  Philadelphia and Trenton included. Inter-city rail share is small (under 1%) because the trip by
  rail costs about 190 felt minutes even for the people who take it (about 60 to reach 30th St,
  33 riding, 7.5 waiting, 59 for the 1.5x fare, 27 from Penn), against 125 by car. Fare at 1.0x
  only moves it to about 1%. That is the demand model working as written; changing it is your call.
- $7 by car is the rule: US running cost $0.1037 a road km (gasoline at 8.5 L/100 km plus tolls),
  about 140 km of road, split between two people in the car. Not a bug; the hover says it is split.
- Fix built in the paths worktree (display only): the By transit block's routes are the inter-city
  riders' whenever any inter-city trip goes by train, as the effective time and "Car on the same
  trips" rows beside it already were. Only with no inter-city rider at all does it fall back to the
  commutes' and outings' routes. No new strings.
