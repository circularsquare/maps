# T-2475: why expected line profit ticked up and down

Anita (2026-11-04): "its a bit weird how expected line profit ticks up and down continuously over time. is it cuz of
holidays / weekends changing?"

## The answer

Yes, weekends and holidays. The line panel's Profit (and the Lines tab's Money) was the past 24 hours'
fares, minus running cost and upkeep. On a weekday after a weekday that window holds two copies of the
same kind of day, so it stays flat. On a Saturday the window slides from Friday's hours into Saturday's,
hour by hour, and Saturday's riders come at different times of day (fewer at the morning rush, more at
midday), so the figure fell and rose again through the day. The same on Sunday, and on Monday, whose
window still holds the rest of Sunday. So three days a week, plus each holiday and the day after it, it
moved the whole day. Running cost and upkeep never moved, and demand was not involved: no demand result
landed in the run below.

Measured on a three-station Manhattan line in a new New York game at 64x, eight game days, sampled four
times a wall second (scratch script, artifacts/trainworld/uismall2/t2475-samples.json):

| Day | Past 24 hours profit through the day |
|---|---|
| Tue to Fri | $39.5M all day |
| Sat | $39.5M, $35.9M at 09:00, $39.8M at 15:00, $37.9M at 21:00 |
| Sun | $38.0M sliding to $32.7M |
| Mon | $31.8M, $37.0M at 09:00, $34.1M at 15:00, $38.7M at 21:00 |

## What changed

It was noise a player can't act on (a weekly cycle in a figure the hover calls "Expected profit today"),
so it is now what the hover says: the expected profit for a whole day of the line's local day now. The
clock worker's `today` answer carries each line's fares for every hour of its local day on that day's
kind (`lineDaysAt`, `dayFares` in game/today.ts); running cost over any 24 hours was already the same
for every 24. Riders, boardings and the rest stay counts of the past 24 hours.

The same run with it:

| Day | Expected profit today |
|---|---|
| Mon to Fri | $39.5M all day |
| Sat | $38.0M all day |
| Sun | $31.8M all day |

It changes when the line's day turns, when riders are worked out again and when the network changes.
The first day of a line shows the full day at once instead of climbing from zero.
