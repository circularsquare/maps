# T-2524 (and T-2523): what a big save's load is doing while its bar sits still

Agent loadnotes, 2026-11-05, for maps-17.

## Verdict

**Nothing stalls on Anita's save; the bar's model does.** From the network's arrival to the final, both solving
workers are 90-100% busy in WASM (CPU profiles, 10 ms samples), the page's main thread is 80-90% idle, and garbage
collection is under 5% of the main thread and nothing in the workers (WASM memory is not collected). The longest
quiet between two worker messages is about 30 s (the whole solve's setup plus its first period). What looks like a
stall is the bar running ahead of the work and then crawling:

1. **A file import** (opening a save while another game is up): the bar is at 70% before the network is in and at
   97.4% when the whole solve starts, then creeps 97.4 to 99% over the solve's 140 s. The likely cause (read from
   the code, not traced): before the network `jobProgress` takes the remembered wait of `gameWait().key`, and
   `sameSize(rec, null)` is true with the stops unknown, so a record that is not this game's (here, most likely the
   profile's own small New York game: the key is the active slot when the clock worker says "loaded",
   `clockClient.ts` `holdForGameLoad`) sets the stage shares; the bar keeps its place when the model replaces them
   (`job.anchor`), so data and solve share the last 30%, and the data stage, moving by real bytes and packs, fills
   nearly all of it.
2. **Zones outside the load's own bar** (here the 76 zones of the save beyond the view's; their bars show only once
   the card is down, so on this save mostly for a zone opened later or one whose cluster went ahead): their share
   is download and opening only, the opening expected from the pack's size (`OPEN_MS_PER_MB`), but each worker
   opens 81 packs one at a time. They reach 99% within seconds of their download and sit there for about 90 s,
   through the opening queue and the solve's round 0, with no step of their own left to show.
3. **The last round** (a reload, where the remembered wait makes the bar otherwise good): round 3's first period
   lands 0.5 s after the round starts (period 1 has converged and is quick), the wave model takes that as the
   round's pace, and the card says 99% and "A few seconds left" for the round's 22-25 s.

Smaller, real costs, none a stall:

- **Unequal periods**: with 2 workers, worker 0 holds periods 0, 2 and 4, worker 1 periods 1 and 3; worker 1 waits
  for worker 0's third period 8-9 s in rounds 0, 1 and 3 and 2 s in round 2, about 27 s idle in a 143 s solve.
- **Opening**: each solving worker spends about 35 s opening the save's 81 packs before the whole solve can go;
  then `set_network` takes 15 s per worker before the first period.
- The focus pool solves New York alone meanwhile (rounds at 27, 43, 54 and 68 s), in its own worker.

## What changed (T-2523, T-2524)

- `game/loading.ts`: `markStep`/`lastStep` (a load's last step: pack bytes, a pack opened, any worker message but a
  query's answer, the focus pool's too), `STALL_MS` 90 s (three times the longest quiet seen, `?stallMs=N`),
  `zoneSteps` (each loading zone's stage and time left), `solveLeft` (a running whole solve's time left), and
  `loadLines` (a load's hover lines: stage, then the time left counted down, or "No progress for N min").
- `workers/demandClient.ts` `zoneProgress`: fills `zoneSteps` and `solveLeft` (the solve stage the T-421 bars
  already used, smoothed with `nextLeft`); a zone whose pack is open and which the running solve covers says
  "Calculating demand" with the solve's time left, so a bar sitting at 99% says what it waits on.
- `map/zoneLoading.ts`: the bars' hovers from `loadLines`, written again each second while a bar is up.
- `map/commuterView.ts`: `farWaiting` (the bubbles' loading mark's condition, now shared).
- `ui/StatusTag.tsx`: a click that waits (a bubble's far end, a trip, a loading city in the overview) puts the
  status on what it waits for, with the time left or the stall in its hover. While the loading card is up the
  card says it and the status shows nothing, as before.
