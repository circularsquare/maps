## Work

**T-2016.** On the real save the Stations list's bullets were already 2 px apart (measured 2.00 at
UI scale 1, 1.99 at 0.8 and 1.2), so the gaps Anita saw came from the other rows of bullets:
- the two-row bullets (`stop-chips2`, tall rows of the related lines view and of a group's names)
  were a grid whose columns took the wider of the two bullets above each other: gaps of 2, 2.97,
  3.27, 3.41, 5.94, 6 and 9.25 px in the 6's related view. Now each row is its own flex row
  (`TwoRows` in LineInspector.tsx): 2 px everywhere at 0.8, 1 and 1.2;
- bullets that sat straight in a row with a wider gap: the line panel's stop rows (6 px, the `li`
  gap), a train's next stops (3 px), and the station panel, junction moves, a trip's route, the
  closest stations and search rows (their rows' gaps). All now in a `.bullets` span, 2 px;
- a group diagram's end bullets side by side were spaced at a fixed 18 px pitch and placed by
  `left: x - 8`, as if every bullet were a 16 px circle, so a pill ran 7 px past its slot into the
  next bullet. `groupLayout` takes each bullet's width (`chipW`, from `bulletText`) and lays a run
  2 px apart round its mean; the bullet is centred on its x. Measured "6 (2.00) 6X".
Bullet sizes are unchanged: a one-character bullet 16 px (W 21.3, M 18.4), "6X" 23.3 px, all 16 high.
