## Looks (judged on screenshots)
- Nagoya: Tokaido trunk, Meitetsu, Kintetsu, Aichi Loop, Linimo-area lines and subway lines H, M, T, S, ST all there, on land. Line names are OSM refs (H, M, T) as for the other auto cities.
- Fukuoka: JR Kagoshima/Nippo/Nagasaki, Nishitetsu (T, A?), subway (K, N, H), Kokura monorail, Heisei Chikuho present. One straight vertical line south of Chikushino (a hop with no track on the graph) and JR lines with Japanese names left (OSM has no name:en). Holds 33% is the highest of any auto city (dense JR/Nishitetsu trunk into Hakata): a finding, not fixed.
- Sapporo: Namboku, Toho, Tozai, Gakuentoshi/Sasshō, streetcar, airport rapid. Small network, matches reality (JR Hakodate local patterns thin).
- Hiroshima: Hiroden trams (many routes), Astram, JR Sanyo/Kure/Geibi, Skyrail. A long straight east-west line near Fuchu (Sanyo, off graph), over land.
- No line crosses the sea in any of them. Missing by comparison with the real network: no Shinkansen (filtered by design); Nagoya's Meitetsu/Kintetsu are fewer patterns than reality; Hiroshima's Hiroden numbers are OSM routes.

## Left out, and why
- Kyoto, Kobe: inside Osaka's pack (box 134.2-136.3 E), already in osa.save.
- Kitakyushu: inside Fukuoka's pack (Kokura lines, Kitakyushu monorail present).
- Okayama, Shizuoka/Hamamatsu, Niigata, Kumamoto, Kagoshima, Matsuyama, Nagasaki etc.: only prefecture-sized zone packs exist, whose rail is JR regional lines plus one small tram or private line; not worth a start. Okinawa (Naha monorail) is a pack box mostly sea.
