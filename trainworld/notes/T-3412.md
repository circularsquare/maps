# T-3412: "too close to end of track" (agent bugs2, 2026-11-16)

What it guards: Issue::SplitPoint (net.rs `split_plan`): a station, junction or branch is placed by splitting a piece of track;
the split needs 5 m from either end of the piece (and not on a ramp, +-1 m) so no sliver piece or hair-thin PI is made. Pieces
end at every clicked corner, junction and station, not only at the end of the line, so players hit it mid-line, near a joint.
It is also what the placement sweeps count as a "room" refusal.

Done: the refusal now says that. issues.splitPoint:
"Too close to the end of the track, or on a ramp." -> "Too close to the end of a piece of track, or on a ramp. A station, junction or branch needs 5 m of track on each side, and pieces meet at corners, junctions and stations."
