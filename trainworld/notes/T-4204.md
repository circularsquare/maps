by play.mjs `edits` overstate level-change waits; use the page's `edit()` for anything about levels.

## What makes the wait

1. **Every edit's final is one whole solve of the big cluster, 165-300 s here.** The local stage shows the edited lines in
   5-11 s; "calculating trips" stays up until the whole solve lands. 91-95% of a lone edit's wait.
2. **A second edit inside that window waits a whole solve again.** In the first 40% of the running whole solve
   (RESTART_SHARE, T-3925) it is stopped and the new solve has no local stage (the populations it was solving are no
   longer held), so even the edited line waits the full solve; after 40% the edit is held until the final (22-226 s seen),
   then gets its own local stage and whole solve. Editing every minute or two on this save, nearly every edit lands here.
3. **Edits that reach new zones never get a local stage** (new market packs: the market part is not intact, T-1733), so a
   new intercity line shows nothing until the whole solve: 310 s for E2.
4. **A solving worker restarted for memory reruns the whole solve of an unchanged network** (about 30 s reopening plus
   4-5 minutes), and resets the local stage for the next edit. This is the closest match to "queued after a whole world
   rebuild". Seen once in 40 minutes here; worker 1 was close to its own.
5. Zones opening while a whole solve runs open between its units (20-35 s each): new zones in 3-6 s, zones reopening at
   another level 81-280 s (sce, hua, zhe, kai). A network that needs them waits for them.
6. The clock's settle (T-4194): 0.5-12 s of an edit (setStops on the 16-stop line 243: 4.5 and 12 s), 0.3-5% of these waits.
   Not the cause here.

None of these is a needless rebuild that a fix could drop with every figure kept: the memory restart's re-solve is the
one solve of an unchanged network, but it is the replacement worker's only way to hold solved state again, and its fresh
market parts can move figures. So nothing was built; options below.

## Options (for her pick; hours of agent time)

- **No re-solve after a memory restart while trips are final** (point 4): keep the shown figures and let the next edit's
  solve rebuild the worker; or restart only workers whose WASM is far over (raise MEM_FREE for big saves). Saves the 4-5
  minute "calculating trips" with no change; costs memory (here 533 MB) or a next edit with no local stage. 1-2 h, a
  figures check on a big save.
- **A local stage after a restart** (point 2): the stopped whole solve keeps the populations it had not yet started as
  held, so the restarted edit still shows its lines in seconds. Demand worker (T-1733, T-3925 area); 3-5 h.
- **A local stage for edits reaching new zones** (point 3): solve the new zones and touched cities against the old market
  frozen first. Design call (the market part changes); 3-5 h.
- **The whole solve itself** (point 1) is the big one: 165-300 s for 140 zones on 2 workers. A third solving worker when
  memory allows, or T-3373 B's split; any of these is a solve-speed task.
