## Near the limit or past it

### 1. Account cap, 2,000 (52%, about 1.5 to 2 days)

Change in `shonei-server/noritetsu.go` and a deploy (`.\deploy\deploy.ps1`, needs Anita's say):
- `MaxNoriAccounts` 2000 to 10000 and the alert (the 1000 constant beside it) to 5000.
  Cost on the box: the deploy notes measure 16 MB of memory for 2,000 accounts in the worst case, so 10,000 is
  about 80 MB of the 1.1 GB free. The accounts file is 118 KB now and is rewritten on each change; at 10,000 it is
  about 600 KB, fine.
- `MaxNoriPasswordAccounts` 1500 to 5000 (it counts a quarter of sign-ups).
- The disk stays safe: 0.2 MB of games an account makes 2 GB at 10,000 accounts, which is the 2 GB alert line of
  the 4 GB games cap. Raise the games cap in `trainworld_games.go` together with this if it grows.
- Do it before Nov 12, because every sign-up past 2,000 gets a 503 and the game's sign-in looks broken.

### 2. Sign-up rate guards (70% at peak, and refusing a few already)

- The global 100 sign-ups an hour already peaked at 70. If the game doubles again the evening peak gets 429s.
  Raise it to 300 an hour (the constant is in `noritetsu.go`, 20 at once stays).
- 5 registers an hour per IP refused 29 attempts since Nov 8. Japanese mobile carriers share addresses, so this
  will keep happening. Raising it to 10 an hour is cheap; the global guard still covers floods.

### 3. Resend (over the daily figure, about 3,500 a month against 3,000)

The 117 mails on Nov 9 passed the 100-a-day figure in our notes and the log still shows no failure, so either
Resend does not enforce it as written or the account is on a different plan. I can't see the account from here.
Anita can: https://resend.com/emails (Sent, Bounced, Failed counts) and https://resend.com/settings/billing (usage and plan).
What the mail is: almost all of it is "Confirm your noritetsu recovery email", sent when someone adds a recovery
email to a username and password account (254 of those accounts). Options:
- Resend Pro, $20 a month, 50,000 mails a month and no daily cap. Simplest.
- Or send the confirm mail only when the player asks for it, not at sign-up. That cuts it by most of the volume
  but it is a server and page change.
- Mail failures are only a log line (`[mail] send to ... failed`) and nothing is queued, so a failure would be silent.
  Grep it: `ssh root@5.161.112.237 "journalctl -u shonei --since today | grep -c 'failed'"`.

### 4. R2 (storage already above the free 10 GB, operations unknown)

Not a stop. Above the free tier R2 bills storage at $0.015 per GB a month: 25.8 GiB is about $0.24 a month, and a
rebuild folder kept as an old version (there are 2026-11-07c to 2026-11-09c prefixes under `trainworld/`) adds 1 GB each.
Delete old prefixes after a release (`rclone purge r2:anitamaps/trainworld/<old>`). The number I can't read is
requests: a London load made 19 requests to the data host and the clock worker does Range reads, so 14,000 new
browsers a day could be 0.3 to 1 million reads a day. Class B is 10 million a month free, then $0.36 per million:
at 30 million a month that is $7. Cloudflare's cache in front of data.anita.garden (immutable, one-year max-age)
does not count hits as R2 operations. Anita can read the real figure: Cloudflare dashboard, R2 Overview, bucket
`anitamaps`, Metrics ("Class A operations" and "Class B operations", last 30 days), and Billing, Usage. If it
runs high, nothing needs doing but it is worth knowing the bill.

### 5. GitHub Pages size (58% of 1 GB)

Not driven by players. The site is about 580 MB; biggest are `assets/` 332 MB, `nycriders` 101 MB,
`ancestrydots` 70 MB, `trainsworld` 43 MB. Each game publish replaces assets and keeps the previous set
(T-2962), so it stays about 43 MB. A large map copied into the repo could take it past 1 GB; move big files to
R2 as nycriders trains already were. Bandwidth: Cloudflare caches the page and assets (4 hour max-age), so GitHub's
100 GB soft limit is the origin side only. A new visitor downloads about 5 MB compressed of scripts and wasm plus
the 10.6 MB market-rows.json, which is roughly 5 TB a month if it were all uncached at 12k new browsers a day (my estimate), so
the Cloudflare cache is what keeps this safe. If the site is ever taken off the Cloudflare proxy (grey cloud), the
100 GB limit would be hit within days. Check on GitHub: Settings, Pages shows nothing for bandwidth; GitHub emails
the owner at the limit. Cloudflare dashboard, anita.garden, Analytics, "Cached bandwidth" shows how much it saves.

### 6. The Hetzner box (comfortable, watch RAM)

CPU is idle (load 0.3 of 2), disk 12%. RAM is the one to watch: 775 MB of 1.9 GB used, no swap. Memory grows
with accounts (about 8 KB each worst case), not with players. Dashboard: https://console.hetzner.cloud, project,
server, Graphs (CPU, network). An upgrade to CPX21 (3 vCPU, 4 GB) is a resize of a few minutes with a reboot.
