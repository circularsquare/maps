# T-4240 Stations on her Asia save take seconds to show up (agent stationwait, 2026-11-24)

**For Anita.** While trips are calculating, every edit waited for the boarding account (each train's load and
platform crowd) to be worked out again for every line on the map, because new riders had just come in. On the Asia
save that was 4.8 of a station's 6.1 s. Now an edit waits only for the lines it changed; the other lines keep their
last loads for the few seconds until the background work reaches them, as they already did before the edit. The
same wait hit track, Construct, stop edits and train counts made while trips calculate.

## The fix: an edit works out only the accounts it made stale

- `sim/src/track/boarding.rs`: each `LineAccount` keeps its service key (`base`) beside the full key (service plus the
  riders' generation). `ensure_service_some` works out only accounts whose line's service changed, leaving those out
  of date for new riders alone (and lines with riders but no account yet, which is what new riders for a kind of day
  leave) to `ensure_some`; it has its own cursor. `hour_kept` is `hour` with that, reading the other accounts as
  they are: their service is the one the hour's trips come from, so their loads line up.
- `sim/src/track/wasm.rs`: `boarding_step_service`, `boarding_hour_kept`.
- `app/src/workers/clockDemand.ts`: an edit's steps before its state (`editSteps`) are `boarding_step_service`;
  every trips slice takes `boarding_hour_kept` (the other callers already send loads only once `accountStale` is
  false, so for them it is the whole account's hour); a service edit's loads follow (T-3655 `loadsLater`) only while
  lines it changed are left, and still set the steps going. The steps after new riders (`stepBoarding`) are as
  before, and their last one sends both hours with the new loads.
- Docs: track-and-service.md 6.6 (the account's rule), architecture.md (T-3655's line). DECISIONS (Saves and
  performance): the lie. Changelog: c002 reworded "stations, track and deleting lines show up faster on big
  networks, even while trips are calculating".
