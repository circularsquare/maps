# T-816: snap to the real network near the Eurostar line

Anita, 2026-10-21: "snap to rail doesnt really work near eurostar, seems like the line is quite
disjointed". Scripts in the session scratchpad (`t816/`: `repro.mjs`, `hops.mjs`, `router.py`, a
Python copy of the worker's search, `islands.py`, `dropped.py`); pictures
`../artifacts/trainworld/t816-*.png`.

## What was wrong

**Station to station worked before the fix.** St Pancras-Ashford 89.9 km (by Stratford and
Ebbsfleet on HS1), Ashford-Calais-Fréthun 75.0 km through the tunnel, Fréthun-Lille Europe 100.6 km and
Lille-Paris Nord 225.1 km on the LGV, London-Paris in one go 490.6 km; 111 hops of 4 km at zoom 12
and 42 of 12 km at zoom 10 along that line all found the line (`t816-before-*.png`). So the
trouble is not the station clicks but two things around them.

1. **Gaps in the graph where no line runs.** `realgraph.py` keeps only the ways the layer draws:
   rail or subway that some line in noritetsu's `ways.json` runs over. Short pieces no line was
   assigned to are left out of both the layer and the graph, so the drawn line has holes and the
   graph is cut there. On the Eurostar route: Eurotunnel's Track A and Track C at the Folkestone
   portal (0.25-0.55 km each), one track of HS1 at Ebbsfleet (0.42 km), HS1 pieces at St Pancras,
   and on the French side the raccordements (Annet, Vémars, Ronchin). The route usually found the
   other track, but a click on the cut track, or a pair of clicks either side of a cut, went round.
   Worse elsewhere: in the shipped jp file Hokkaido was its own piece and Shin-Aomori to
   Shin-Hakodate was 2,195 km by the graph (the Seikan line cut by three pieces of 104, 443 and
   669 m); jp's largest piece held only 61% of its junctions.
2. **Loops instead of turning back.** The search refuses turns over 60 degrees, so a click on the
   line beside the one meant (the Folkestone-Ashford line at Cheriton beside the tunnel approach, a
   track of a four-track line that only joins the other way) is reached by going round a loop
   somewhere: Cheriton to a point in the tunnel 16 km away came out at 569 km, Stratford to Camden
   Road 10 km at 383 km. Of 80 random pairs of points 3-25 km apart on rail within 1.5 km of the
   Eurostar route, 41 came out over 2.5 times the straight distance, most by hundreds of km.

## The fix

- **Gap fillers** (`pipeline/realgraph.py`, `gap_fillers`): ways the layer leaves out, except
  yards, sidings and spurs and industrial, military, tourist or test track, are kept aside. Those
  joined to one another through nodes off the drawn track form a group; a group goes into the graph
  whole when it meets drawn track at two nodes or more and is at most 3 km long (`SPARE_KM`). They
  are not drawn, so a click never starts on one, but routes pass through them. Rebuilt jp us gb fr
  de (2026-10-21; same format, 2.0-3.7 MB):

  | region | fillers | km | pieces before | after | largest piece before | after |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | jp | 2,870 | 334 | 262 | 124 | 61% | 86% |
  | us | 5,741 | 686 | 282 | 151 | 83% | 85% |
  | gb | 4,329 | 466 | 73 | 20 | 95% | 98% |
  | fr | 5,832 | 907 | 112 | 52 | 97% | 98% |
  | de | 9,226 | 1,101 | 300 | 110 | 95% | 97% |

  Shin-Aomori to Shin-Hakodate 2,195 to 158 km by the graph, Sapporo joined to the rest of Japan.
  Most fillers are crossovers between the two tracks of a line (two drawn nodes each).
- **Detour cap** (`workers/realRoute.worker.ts`, `DETOUR`, `DETOUR_M`): a route longer than twice
  the clicks' distance apart plus 20 km is refused like one that does not exist ("No real track
  joins these points"); the search stops once its cost passes three times that (the other family
  costs up to three times its length). The player then clicks the line meant instead of getting a
  loop. Station-to-station routes are 1.1-1.4 times the straight distance, so they are far inside it.

## Other long tunnels and borders

- Seikan: cut in the shipped file, joined now (above).
- Gotthard and the Øresund bridge: no graph files yet (T-727e); built into scratch with and without
  fillers, Erstfeld-Biasca (73 km) and København H-Malmö C (50 km) were joined either way, dk and se
  meeting on the bridge by OSM node.
- The Channel Tunnel border itself was never the cut: gb and fr join at the two running tunnels'
  shared nodes.

## Results after

- The four legs unchanged: 89.9, 75.0, 100.6 and 225.1 km, all fitted (`t816-after-*.png`).
- London-Paris in one go is now 451 km, by Boulogne and Amiens rather than 491 km by the LGV and
  Lille: a filler joined the classic line, and the router takes the shortest. Real track, but not
  the Eurostar's; a click at Lille keeps it on the LGV. New York-Emeryville likewise 5,400 to 5,324 km.
- Cheriton to the tunnel (the 569 km loop) is now refused. Of the 80 random pairs, 44 placed (all
  under 2.5 times the straight distance), 35 refused by the cap, 1 had no ring at the first click.
  Before: 41 of 80 over 2.5 times, up to 1,707 km for 16 km; with fillers alone, 37, mostly 50-380 km.
- Tokyo-London's refusal searches 379k states in 254 ms (125k before: more of Japan is joined).
- `checks/t727-snap.mjs` all pass (Tokyo-Shin-Osaka 514.7 km, Penn Station-Emeryville 5,324 km);
  `npm run check` and smoke pass on a dev server.

## Open

  shorter classic one (London-Paris by Amiens). Weighing high-speed chains (flag 8) a little
  cheaper would keep intercity snaps on the LGV; not done, as it changes which line a snap picks.
