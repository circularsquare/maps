# T-1823: can a player lose or wreck their save?

Anita, 2026-10-29: "before we release we should try to make sure its not possible to mess up saves...
what are some ways this could happen?" A review of the code as of c0be9c39, no code changed.
Read: `workers/saveFile.ts`, `workers/clock.worker.ts` (autosave, load, slots, panic recovery),
`workers/cloud.ts`, `sim/src/track/save.rs`, `game/remove.ts`, `game/saveSafety.ts`, the saves panel
and start menu docs, shonei-server's `docs/trainworld-api.md` and `deploy/README.md` (backups).

## What protects saves today

- Every slot write is one IndexedDB transaction: a crash mid-write leaves the old save whole.
- A game is never written over with another game: new game, Open a file, Open as a copy each take a
  new slot (T-743).
- A save that will not load is left as it was (and copied to a hidden `unreadable-<time>`); the new
  game gets its own slot. A save from a retired format, or for another world, is refused with a reason.
- One tab writes a slot (Web Lock); one device writes an account game (claim, 409 "elsewhere").
  The other tab or device says "Autosave is off here..." and must make a copy before leaving.
- Two backups per slot in the browser (at least 5 min apart) and two per game on the account (at
  least 30 min apart); Earlier versions opens either as a copy.
- Deleting a slot, a line or built track asks first. Line deletes and track removal can be undone
  in the session. There is no delete-all; Remove on sign-out deletes only slots whose latest
  version is on the account. Deleting the account asks first and keeps the browser's games.
- Saves are small (New York's real network 78 KB, the real NYC save 75 KB), far under the 4 MB cap.
- The server writes the new save, then its index through a temp file and rename. Hetzner keeps
  a daily image of the whole server for 7 days.
- Tested here (own Vite, headless): a blueprint edit, which does not trigger an immediate save, was
  still there after the tab closed 0.3 s and 3 s later (the hidden-page flush works, on a small
  network).

## Ways to lose work, most worrying first

| # | What happens | How likely | How bad | What protects it now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mistake noticed late**: deletes a line, removes track, switches to sandbox, then plays on or reloads | medium | the game as it was more than ~10 min ago | undo (this session only, cleared by a reload or by building); browser backups reach back only 5-10 min of play, account backups about 30-60 min |
| 2 | **Deletes a game by mistake** (bin, then Delete save) | low-medium | the whole game, everywhere | the question only. Its browser backups go with it, the account copy and backups go at once, other devices delete their copies at their next load. Only Anita could restore it, from a whole-disk Hetzner image (7 days) |
| 3 | **Browser storage wiped**: Clear browsing data, a cleaner app, a full disk (it happened 2026-10-24), a private window closed, Safari and every iOS browser deleting a site's storage after 7 days of use without a visit | medium, signed out | every game in that browser at once | signed in: the account copy (nothing lost but the last ~2 min). Signed out: nothing. `persist()` is asked, but Chrome refuses it for a site that is new to the player; a crossed cloud icon is the only warning |
| 4 | **An older build opens a newer save**: a tab left open across a release, Anita's 8800 and the live site on one account, a rollback | medium for Anita, low-medium for players | newer features quietly dropped: groups, hand order, group bullets, header fields like `loan` | none. `decode` in save.rs stops at the first block it does not know and never checks for leftover bytes; unknown header fields are not carried; the next autosave writes the shorter save and uploads it. `SAVE_FORMAT` is still 1, so "from a newer version" never fires |
| 5 | **The device that gave way**: a game opened on device B while A still has changes not yet uploaded | low | A's last few minutes | by design (notes/T-988.md): A's slot is replaced from the account at its next load, with no copy of what A had |
| 6 | **Second tab closed**: playing in a tab without the lock, then closing it | low | that tab's changes | a top-bar notice only; no "leave page?" prompt |
| 7 | **Storage fills, or is cleared while the page is open** | low | changes since the failure, if the player ignores the notice | a notice and Download. Demand caches (several MB per slot, rebuildable) are never freed to make room; a cleared database keeps failing until reload because the open connection is reused |
| 8 | **Browser crash or power cut** | low | up to 60 s of unpaid edits (paid ones save at once) | the one-minute autosave |
| 9 | **A panic** in the simulation | low | at worst a half-applied edit saved | the model is rebuilt from the last edit, or from the live one when it can still be read (code read, not seen) |
| 10 | **A save that fails to load** | low | nothing is overwritten | kept aside, but the `unreadable-` copy is invisible: the player cannot download it to send to us |
| 11 | **Files** (Download, Open a file) | low | as row 4 for files from a newer build | Open a file always takes a new slot; a non-save is refused |

Not risks as far as the code shows: a crash mid-write (atomic), Open or New game replacing a saved
game, deleting the game being played (refused), a removed zone (successor table), autosave writing over
a save that failed to load (a new slot is used), two tabs both saving, the server's index (temp + rename;
an unreadable index refuses writes instead of being overwritten).

## Fixes, in order

Each is one agent. Sizes: XS under an hour, S a few hours, M about a day.

1. **Backups that reach further back, and one before any overwrite** (S, Sonnet). Keep browser
   backups at roughly 10 min, 1 h, 1 day and 1 week old (about 100 KB each), and write one before
   anything replaces a slot from elsewhere: the account copy taken in (`takeInto`, including after
   giving way), a forced claim, a frame conversion. Done when: Earlier versions lists up to four
   spread-out versions (checked with `?backupMs=`), and a slot replaced from the account shows its
   old version there.
2. **Never save a game the build did not fully read** (S, Opus: a quiet wrong call loses data).
   `decode` reports bytes left after the last block it knows; the header gets a `level` number raised
   with every new block or header field (a Rust test fails if a block is added without raising it).
   A game with unread data or a higher level opens with autosave and upload off and says "This game
   was saved by a newer version. Reload the page to update." Download still works. Done when: a
   save with an extra block, and one with a higher `level`, both open, show the notice, and leave the
   slot and the account copy byte for byte as they were.
3. **"Leave page?" when changes cannot be saved** (XS, Sonnet). A `beforeunload` prompt while the
   game has changes and autosave is off (another tab, another device, storage failure, test page
   after an edit). Done when: closing such a tab prompts; closing a normal tab does not.
4. **Recently deleted, in the browser** (S, Sonnet). A deleted slot moves to `deleted:<slot>` with
   its backups for 30 days, listed under the cards with Open as a copy. A tombstone from the account
   moves other devices' copies there too, instead of deleting them. Done when: delete, reload,
   restore gives the same game; entries older than 30 days are gone at start.
5. **Recently deleted, on the account** (S, shonei-server agent, then a small page follow-up). DELETE
   keeps the save under its tombstone for 30 days (counted in the 64 MB), with a route to restore it
   as a new game. The privacy page's "7 days" wording changes to match. Done when: a deleted game
   restores from another device within 30 days.
6. **Storage full or cleared while open** (S, Sonnet). On a failed write: drop demand caches and
   retry once; if the database connection was closed, open it again and write the game into it.
   Done when: a check that fills the quota (or deletes the database under a running game) ends with
   the game saved and no notice left.
7. **Account backups that reach further back** (S, shonei-server agent). Add a 1-day and a 1-week
   backup to the two 30-minute ones. Done when: the games list shows them and Earlier versions
   restores each.
8. **Unreadable copies visible** (XS, Sonnet). List `unreadable-<time>` as a card saying it could not
   be opened, with Download and Delete. Done when: a corrupted slot leaves such a card.
9. **A nudge for games only in this browser** (S, needs Anita's call first). After an hour of play
   in a game kept only here, the crossed cloud on its card turns the warn colour (hover: games here
   can be lost if the browser clears its storage; sign in or download). This would be the only
   protection against row 3 for players who never sign in, and the only one on Safari and iOS.

Not proposed: a confirm on delete-all (there is no delete-all), refusing to autosave over a save
that failed to load (already done), atomic writes (already atomic).

A standing check, `checks/t1823-save-safety.mjs`, can grow with fixes 1-4, 6 and 8: each fix adds
its own case.
