# T-158: filter the map's demand to one purpose

Anita: "be able to filter to just one demand segment and see only that segment on the map?"
Done 2026-10-12. Spec: docs/interface.md 8 "Purpose filter", docs/demand.md 4.4 "Loads by
purpose", SPEC 8 summary.

## What the sim already had

- Populations were already separate in each demand worker: one `DemandApi` per city (work
  commutes), the NYC `EverydayApi` (four purposes: shopping and errands, social and leisure,
  school, university), and the `IntercityApi` regional market (longer trips, no purposes of its
  own). The worker summed their `seg`, `board`, `alight` into one vector per period.
- Inside `EverydayApi::pass` the four purposes were mixed into one station-pair OD matrix and
  assigned once, so line loads per everyday purpose did not exist.
- Selection queries already had everything by purpose: `EverydayApi::selection` returns each
  purpose's timing and far-end cells, `purpose_timing` the commute's.
- City bubbles (`cellModes`) had commutes and the everyday sum only.

## What changed

Rust (`sim/src/demand/everyday.rs`):
- `pass` assigns purpose by purpose. Pairs are stored purpose by purpose (edges are kind-major),
  so one s x s matrix is filled, assigned and cleared at each change of purpose; the flush adds
  into the purpose's `Loads`, so it would still be right if pairs were ever interleaved. The
  total is the sum of the purposes (what crowding reads); `Period.parts` keeps seg, rn_board,
  rn_alight per purpose, MSA-averaged with the same round weights as the total.
- `part_loads(q)`: per purpose, three blocks (seg, board, alight) per route node.
- `cell_modes_of(kind)`: `cell_modes` restricted to one purpose's edge range.
- Tests: `purpose_parts_add_up_to_loads_and_cell_modes` (fixture, free flow and a crowding round),
  `everyday_real_network` (ignored; the real pack and real network, timings and sums).

Worker and client:
- `demand.worker.ts` `purposeParts`: work (all city populations), the everyday purposes, longer
  (regional), packed per key; sent as the result's optional `parts`. New query `purposeModes`.
- `demandClient.ts`: `purposeDemand` builds `DemandView.purposes` (per purpose: lines' loads and
  boardings, stations' boardings and alightings, laid out as the totals). `purposeModes(p)`:
  work from the commute sums alone (`cellModes(false)`), an everyday purpose summed from the
  solving workers, longer trips zeros. `flows` adds `byPurpose` (work's far end as the commute
  answer, each everyday purpose's from its own far-cell blocks).
- `game/purposes.ts`: keys, names, colours (the pie's, moved here), `EVERYDAY_KEYS`.
- `game/ui.ts` `purposeFilter` signal, not persisted.
- Map: `demandLayers.ts` widths and station circles (and complex circles) from the purpose's
  numbers, gauges from the total; `trackHover.ts` count and a third line; `commuterView.ts`
  bubbles, hover words, selection near and far; `intercityView.ts` hidden under a local purpose;
  `demandViews.ts` station catchment hidden unless all or work.
- UI: `DemandSelector.tsx` dropdown; notes in the commuter and station inspectors.

## Choices (DECISIONS 2026-10-12)

- Control: a native select at the end of the None/Homes/Destinations bar, not in settings. It is
  a view choice made while looking at the map, like Homes/Destinations; settings is for things
  set once. A swatch in the purpose colour while filtered.
- Six choices plus All: Longer trips counts as a segment, since it loads lines too.
- Not remembered across page loads.
- Train fill, crowding and line/station inspectors stay all-purpose.
- Station catchment discs (commuters) and longer-trip bubbles hide under purposes they do not
  contain, rather than show a different segment.

## Cost

Native, real NYC pack and real network (867 stations, 3,512 route nodes, 816,662 everyday
pairs), one period: assignment purpose by purpose 98-101 ms against 32-33 ms for one summed
assignment, in an everyday pass of about 500 ms. About +65 ms per period and stage, roughly 4%
of a combined stage. No extra matrix. Kept purpose loads 170 kB a period. Result messages
+250 kB each.
