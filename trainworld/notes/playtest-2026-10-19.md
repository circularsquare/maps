# Anita's playtest of 2026-10-19 (the 9e466a4 build), filed as T-692 to T-701

Her words in quotes; decisions made in filing them in plain text. Each task's agent reads its section.
Liked as built: the loading screen, the Profit row, round line caps, airports, the light colour scheme,
the real network, the station sounds (with changes below).

## T-692, the line panel layout
- "the column header 'trains on line' is too long lets just say 'trains'. also the demand column can get
  too long and spread onto 2 rows. this generally shouldnt be possible. lets have all of these enforced
  only one row high..." Every cell of the schedule table stays one line (shorter labels, nowrap,
  ellipsis with the full text in a hover where cut).
- "i think the dividers between riders, rider bar, profit, trains leaving full, riders left are perhaps
  not necessary and take up extra space. lets just have one divider before the stops list. and one
  divider before the past 24 hours label. dividers between sections, not within sections."
- "the crowding indicator is red even when people are only waiting 2.2 minutes on avg for a train? i
  think like ~3 min should be yellow and like ~10min should be orange and ~15min should be red and 20+
  dark red, since its pretty achievable to get 20+min." The dot's colour (T-673's `crowdColour`) goes by
  the level's average wait for a train with room: green under 3 minutes, then a smooth ramp through
  yellow at 3, orange at 10, red at 15, dark red from 20. The hover keeps its text.
- "can we get rid of the express copy feature? its not really worth the space it takes up given that we
  can just use normal copy line". Remove the button and its code path (keep Copy line).
- Dark theme: "the elevation selector in dark mode looks a bit weird cuz the black surrounding box of the
  selected setting blends in with the black background, lets try a whiteish surrounding box" (Build
  tab's Elevation chips; the selected chip's outline light in dark theme).

## T-694, points as a measure, not a running total
"i was envisioning points as a static thing, not a thing that goes up over time as you leave the game
running. so like points is avgd over the past 24h or so, and updates every hour or so. it is a measure of
how good your network is." So a city's points = the points earned over the past 24 game hours (20 a
trip plus 1 a passenger-km, as now), recomputed once a game hour; no running total shown or saved as a
score. The Cities column and the top bar hover show that figure. Stars stay as built.

## T-695, placing stations feels instant
- "when tracks overlap we still are not consistently picking the track that makes sense to place on. it
  seems to select the other one and then correct a frame later sometimes? we should make this instant.
  also i think we should just draw blueprinted tracks over nonblueprinted ones to help make this easier /
  match with visuals."
- "the snapping to track end doesnt seem to be super responsive, like we can momentarily put the station
  off the end but then it snaps to correct. we should make this feel more clean"
The ghost must never show a spot that is later corrected: decide the order and the likely-accepted spot
on the main thread (the station-nearby and blueprint tests, the end-of-track test from geometry) so the
first frame is right, and use the worker's trial only to confirm or refuse; while waiting, keep the
previous ghost rather than show a wrong one. Blueprint track drawn above built track.

## T-696, station sounds and faces
"station sounds are nice but i think they are a bit too consistent, we should vary the note by the line.
so like each line gets a random note. also currently the sound is 2 notes long, lets reduce that to one
note. also the sound should be slightly different if the station is crowded. and the emote should be sad."
One note per arrival, the note fixed per line (from the line id, on a pleasant scale); a crowded platform
(the same test as the red face) plays a slightly different, lower or softer, sound; the crowded face is a
sad face (check the current red one reads sad, not angry).

## T-700, Shanghai's sharp walking divide
"shanghai's ridership has a bit of a weird pattern. theres like a sharp divide between on the left people
are walking ~25% of the time and on the right people are walking ~0% of the time. its so sharp that i feel
like maybe the bubbles that are getting no walking are having their walking computed wrong? they won't walk
to a place like 5 minutes away." Her screenshot: Huangpu near Nine-Zigzag Bridge and Middle Henan Rd, the
pink bubbles east (towards the river) against grey-violet ones west. Undiagnosed.

## T-701, driving speeds (a question)
"we should work on getting rough estimates of actual driving speed on actual road network. how hard would
this be? dont wanna give up substantial performance but am wondering". Drives inside a pack already follow
OSM roads (T-275, `routes.rs`); answer what speeds they use now, what the trip explorer's "speeds of the
places at each end" is, and what road-class speeds (or congestion by time of day) would cost in pack size
and solve time. A short written answer for Anita first.
