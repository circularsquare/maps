# T-2084: one generic `admin_totals` reader, and the cut-unit fix (agent t2084, 2026-11-03)

Code: `pipeline/admin_totals.py` (the reader, CONFIGS for ID and PH), `pipeline/tier0.py` (`scale_to_units`,
`ghsl_people`, `moll_window` split out of `ghsl_window`), `checks/admin_totals_check.py`. Nothing built or
registered; no pack touched. Downloads: `data/raw/admin_totals/ID/` (USCB `indonesia.gdb.zip` 40 MB and
`indonesia_uscb_202405.xlsx`, plain curl from HDX, CC BY 4.0). The Philippine gdb is read in place from
`religiondots/data/raw/ph/philippines_uscb.gdb.zip` (the config's `mirror`; else fetched to
`data/raw/admin_totals/PH/`). GHSL per unit cached in `data/work/admin_totals/{ID,PH}.ghsl_e2020.csv`.

## 1. The bug

The old hook set the cells inside each unit (by cell centre, inside the pack) to the unit's whole census total.
A unit the pack's outline cuts got all its people in the part inside. England never showed it: its outline is
whole LADs and its hook took only LADs whose representative point is inside.

- Made-up case (check 1): a unit of census 70,000, GHSL 40,000 over the unit, half of them inside the pack:
  old rule 70,000 inside, now 35,000; a unit wholly inside keeps its 30,000 exactly.
- Real packs, with every unit touching the outline handed to the hook (what a generic reader does): see the
  "old rule" column below. yai (Yalimo with Mamberamo Tengah): GHSL 480,001, the census by the new rule
  164,698 (Yalimo 105,139 and Mamberamo Tengah 51,010 of its 51,719, the rest slivers), the old rule
  1,078,518: Jayawijaya's 277,923, Tolikara's 244,345 and Yahukimo's 361,776 people poured into the 1% or
  less of each that the outline takes.

## 2. The fix (tier0.scale_to_units)

Each unit's cells (by centre) take `total x share`, share = GHS-POP people in those cells (`pop_ghs`) / GHS-POP
people over the whole unit (`ghsl_people`: the unit polygons rasterized on the 100 m Mollweide grid by pixel
centre in tile-aligned blocks, rasterio, so each block decodes one tile). That is the brief's "GHSL's people in
its part times census / GHSL over the whole unit". A unit with no GHSL people outside the outline (pixel centres,
`ghsl_people(..., inside=boundary)`, at least 99.9% inside) takes its total exactly; a unit GHSL puts no one in
counts as in or out by its representative point.

Tried first: share = pixel centres inside the outline / the whole unit. It under-counted coastal units the pack
holds whole: the outline's shore clip is tighter than USCB's coast, and tier0 gathers pixels within about 0.4 km
of the outline into inside cells, so Jolo showed 85% inside where the cells hold 88%. Both measures agree that
pti's cells hold only 80-90% of GHSL's people of Jolo, Bongao and Siasi (Pandami 54%): stilt villages and
coastal pixels whose cells lie outside the clipped outline, lost before the hook as after (T-2099).
Printed: a line per unit when the pack has 50 or fewer, or the unit is cut or flagged; FLAG when census is over
2x GHSL either way (rule 3 of "The general fix"); a summary line with the people of units holding no cell (lost).
The pack's population source line names the config's source (`hook(...).source`).

## 3. The reader

One entry per country in `CONFIGS`, no code:

    "ID": {"source": "...",
           "polygons": {"file", "url", "mirror" (optional), "layer", "code", "name"},
           "totals": {"file", "url", "sheet" + "skiprows" (xlsx) or "layer" (gdb) or a .csv, "code", "pop"},
           "parent": r"^(\d\d)\d\d$",   # one group: a unit code's parent code
           "national": "0", "no_total": ["1288"], "mode": "factor"}

- `check <ISO2>`: codes unique on both sides, every polygon a total (but `no_total`), every unit row of the
  totals (codes the parent regex matches) a polygon, units summed by parent equal the parent rows exactly, and
  the national row.
- `ghsl <ISO2>`: GHSL people per unit over the whole polygon (ID 514 units in about a minute, PH 1,634).
- `dry <ISO2> [ids]`: per built pack, from `data/work/<id>.tier0_signals.npz` and `<id>.outline.geojson`:
  GHSL's people today, the census's after scaling, the old rule's, units, units cut (2-98% inside), FLAGs.
- Mode "shares" (old counts, India 2011 next): each unit's total = GHSL over its `hold` parent (default
  `parent`) x its census share of the parent (`held_shares`); needs the GHSL table.
- `plan_hooks(row)` gives `admin_totals: hook(row["iso2"])`, so a country with no module takes
  `"module": "admin_totals"` in its zone rule (and an ARCHETYPE row, C) when T-2038, T-2076 switch it on.

Configs:
- **ID**: USCB ADM2 layer of `indonesia.gdb.zip` (515 polygons, `NSO_CODE` = BPS 4-digit), totals from the xlsx
  sheet Age-Sex (`NSO_CODE`, `BTOTL`, row 1 the header in words). Danau Toba (1288) has no total. Join: 514 units,
  34 provinces exact, 275,773,774 (the Long Form, mid-2022).
- **PH**: USCB ADM3 layer (1,634 municipalities and cities), totals the gdb's `PH_AGE_SEX` table on `GEO_MATCH`;
  116 tabulation provinces exact, 109,033,245. Municipalities, not barangays: Manila's barangays are often under a
  0.1 km² cell, and a unit with no cell centre loses its total (DECISIONS).
