# T-4401: lines drawn straight across the map after an edit

## Reports

- T-4400 (internal, 11-27): at zoom 12 a stroke ran from one line's last point to another's first, in a new NYC game
  built by quick edits; gone with the stroke LOD off.
- pugl on Discord, 11-28 (Firefox on Windows, "fairly high end pc", a Poland save of about 950 stations): a Medal clip.
  They box-select 11 pieces near Zywiec, press single track, confirm "Rebuild", then zoom out. From the first zoom-out,
  lines run in straight strokes between unrelated places all over Poland, at every zoom they visit; one frame mid-zoom
  (22:24 in the clip) draws everything right, the next is broken again. Frame: artifacts/trainworld/review/linespray.png.

## Cause

`mergeNet` (map/network.ts) wrote a network's new segments into the arrays it shares with the last network as soon as
the state arrived (clockClient `takeNet`). The state is then held for its trips (T-648, up to 250 ms) or staged until a
train count takes effect (T-1071), and all that time the renderer still draws the last network and reads its arrays,
which already hold the next network's segments, moved where segments were added or removed. A zoom band's simplified
copy made in that window (`workBand`: the last network's significance over the moved segments) joins unrelated points,
and since T-4274 that copy and the significance are carried through the edit, so the strokes stay. The mid-zoom frame
that drew right is the full set, drawn while the next band was being made. Picking (`net.lines.band`), the gathered
instances and the culling boxes read the same arrays in that window.

## Fix

The write waits until the renderer takes the network (`netIn` at the top of `NetworkRenderer.setNetwork`); a network
merged on top of one not yet drawn writes that one first, each write once. `checks/t1381-strokes.mjs` has a case that
fails on the old behaviour.

## Checks (11-28)

Paris sandbox with `?patch=check`, three lines, `tw.client.takeState` delayed 3 s to widen the window, a rebuild to
single track whose patch moves segments, then a zoom to a band not made yet during the window. Before: straight
strokes across the map, kept after the state was taken, and "T-4274 check: the simplified copy for zoom 12 carried
through the edit differs from a whole one". After: drawn right, no check errors. (With the delay alone, before the fix,
"T-4314 check: the trains' fill kept per line row differs" also showed; it went with the fix in the same run.)
