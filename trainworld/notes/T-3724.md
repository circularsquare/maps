# T-3724 "No transit route" on a pair a line joins (railnone, 2026-11-18)

## For Anita, in short

Found and fixed. Between two big networks (seen on New York and Philadelphia, on a player's own save) riders
spread over dozens of station pairs. The panel lists routes from the biggest pair down, but asked the biggest pair
for a route carrying nearly all of that pair's riders whenever the pair held just over 0.5% of them; a pair whose
riders split between two trains then gave nothing, the trip had riders and no route, and the panel fell back to
the "no transit route" sentence. Now a period with any rail riders always lists its busiest route. The demand
workers and the market split between them are not involved.

It accounts for both inbox reports of a line with "no route" (New York's confirmed on his save; Milan's read from
his picture, below). How much of the Discord complaint it is, I can't tell; the rest of case 3 is the true cases
T-3632 now words apart (no train at night, islands with no road or flight, no station in reach).

## How it was found

- **The player's own save.** boblovespi's New York game (rev 412, 11-18, read-only copy from the server to my
  scratch folder; 10 cities, 90 lines), loaded with the default 3 solving workers (play.mjs `--cores 4`; markets
  dealt [[0],[1,4],[2,3]], T-1607 sharing on). New York to Philadelphia, place to place: periods 0-2 and 4 list a
  route; period 3 (evening) has 4,759 longer trips by rail (13,082 with commutes and outings), `why` 0, 40 station
  pairs, **0 routes**. MarketTrip.tsx's `!main` branch then prints `noRailText("")`, the noRail sentence.
- **His report fits this branch only.** His picture's heading reads "Inter-city, evening peak" beside commute and
  everyday rows; since T-3374 (11-13, before his 11-15 build) that label needs `t.rail > 0` (else the period name
  alone). So his period had rail and no route: every rail = 0 cause, the worker stand-ins (6, 7) included, is ruled
  out. His panel also showed the car's minutes and "112 km apart", which a stand-in answer has not.
- **Not the workers.** The same save with `?perfOff=share` (markets with their cities) gives the same period 3: 0
  routes. Every icTrip reply carried the sim's own code; the masks were disjoint. Milan - Bologna rebuilt (3-stop line
  4/2/0, two metro lines, one Bologna line) and loaded from a save with 3 workers: all day periods route, with sharing
  and after an edit.
- **Cause** (`market_trip.rs`, from T-1500, 10-26): pairs walked from the biggest down while over `PAIR_MIN` (0.005),
  each with `min = ROUTE_MIN / share` of the pair's riders, the biggest always. Here the biggest pair held 0.0062 of
  the rail riders, so a route had to carry 0.005 / 0.0062 = 81% of it; its riders split over two departures, no
  route passed, and the next pair (0.005-) ended the loop. Periods whose biggest pair is under 0.005 are walked at
  `ROUTE_MIN` and do list one (with 99.9% of riders on routes not listed). The band is wider than 0.005-0.01: any
  pair whose best route holds under 0.005 / share of it fails (a 4-way split just under 0.02). The loop and its bar
  are T-1500's (10-26), which fixed the case of every pair under 0.005 and missed this one: never fully fixed,
  rather than fixed and broken again.

## How often (the player's save, every ordered pair of its 10 open cities, place to place, 5 periods)

Before: 8 of 373 periods with rail riders had no route (2%): nyc>phl evening, phl>pvd morning peak and evening peak,
bos>nyc night, bos>phl evening, pvd>nyc morning peak and midday, pvd>phl evening. A selection (a click on a bubble)
is a different mix of zones and lands in the band by chance as well; the reports came from the busiest corridors.

## Fix

`pick_routes` in `market_trip.rs`: the same rule, and when it gathers no route the pairs are walked again in order
at a floor of 0.01% of the pair and the first pair's biggest route is kept. Nothing changes where a route was listed
(the extra walk runs only when the list is empty). No demand figure changes (the save's totals the same to the
trip). Test: `rail_riders_always_list_a_route` (the thin two-way split, a four-way split just under 0.02, a first
pair that yields nothing, and the old listing unchanged). `agent_cargo.py test`: 644 and 840 pass.

After, same save and sweep: 0 of 373 periods with rail riders lack a route. New York to Philadelphia, evening:
"To Trenton 22 min, Northeast Regional to North Philadelphia 24 min, change, SEPTA N to S Penn Square 11 min".
Screenshot: `artifacts/trainworld/t3724-nyc-phl-evening.png`.

The route listed carries about 0.2% of the riders (the rest spread over pairs too small to list) and starts at
Trenton, as in the other periods on his network.
