# T-1200: responsiveness on a weak machine

Measured 2026-10-24 (responsiveness agent) on the dev server (6148), headless Chrome 1400x900 with
GL, one demand worker, the CPU slowed with CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` at 4x and 6x as a
phone stand-in, and once unthrottled. The machine was shared (a data join and several agents), so
absolute times are inflated; compare within a column. Logs, the per-phase JSON and the traces'
summaries (`trace-summaries.txt`: each long task over 100 ms with the app functions inside it) are in
`artifacts/trainworld/t1200/`; the traces themselves were deleted for disk space. Scripts were scratch
(`session.mjs`, `analyze.mjs` after `checks/t348-analyze.mjs`, `tabprobe.mjs`, `shots.mjs`).

## The session

The real New York save imported into a new game, then: 45 s after the network is drawn, on to
demand's final; drags at zoom 12 and 14 and a wheel zoom in and out; jumps to Boston, Philadelphia
and Brooklyn with a drag in each (the draw anchor moves); clicks on four stations, three pieces of
track and, with Homes on, three bubbles; every dock tab and the saves panel; four pieces of track
with the mouse on open ground, two stations, Construct (C), a new line (N), its trains raised three
times, three undos; a busy real line's trains raised three times. Anita's world save
(world-map-day73, 143 lines, Toronto to Tokyo) ran the load, pan and city jumps at 4x (its clicks
phase was lost to a trace read failure). Each action records the long tasks overlapping it and the
Event Timing entries (press to next paint); a CDP trace per phase gives each long task's functions.

## The busy tag

`game/busy.ts`, `ui/BusyTag.tsx`, `workers/clockClient.ts` `arrive`. A clock state expected to take
over 150 ms on this device is held one painted frame with the tag up ("Drawing the network" for a
load or a redraw at a new anchor, "Updating the network" for an edit's state); every message after
it waits behind it, in order, and the tag goes when the state has been taken. The time per edge and
node is learnt per kind from each state taken (half the last, half the new), with a guess per kind
before the first (`STATE_MS_GUESS`). New York at 6x: the tag showed for the three anchor moves
(500-800 ms) and for nine of the edit states (100-560 ms). At 1x, before the load got its own kind,
it showed for the anchor moves for 55-70 ms (the load's slow first state had taught the redraw kind
a slow rate); with the load apart, three anchor moves at 1x show no tag (New York is 3,403 edges and
nodes). The rate follows the device, not a sudden change: after 1x moves, a move with the CPU
switched to 6x mid-page showed none (a test artefact; a phone is slow throughout). Screenshots:
`artifacts/trainworld/t1200/t1200-busy-light.png` and `-dark.png` (the tag after "All purposes").

Not covered yet, each with its task: demand's publish (the status tag already says "Updating
demand" then), a selection's sums, the pan frame. `runBusy` in game/busy.ts wraps any other work.

## For Anita

What makes the game laggy on a slow machine, worst first:

1. **Panning and zooming** is the frame itself: on a phone-speed CPU the map draws about 16 times a
   second instead of 60. About two thirds of each frame is the basemap (roads, water, place names),
   a quarter our own station names, platform crowds and marks. A lighter mode when frames run slow
   is filed (T-1207).
2. **Every change to the network** (each piece of track, station, line, and arriving in another
   city, which redraws the network) holds the page for a fraction of a second while the game
   recounts money, points and zones for the whole network; up to 1.4 s when a save opens. Filed to
   recount only what changed (T-1209, with T-1170 for arriving in a city).
3. **Demand's results** freeze the page about half a second each time a forecast round lands,
   several times after every change. Filed to add them up off the main thread (T-1208).
4. **Clicking a station or a bubble** spends a quarter to half a second adding up its longer trips
   (T-1210).
5. **Long lists**: the Stations tab took over 2 s to open on New York at phone speed; fixed now
   (0.7 s, the first 80 stations, more as you scroll). The Lines tab and the saves panel take about
   0.4-0.5 s (T-1211).
6. Smaller: building after an edit (the click grids rebuilt), Chinese and Japanese place names on
   arrival, the sound starting up once.

Where a stall cannot be avoided yet, a small tag on the map's top row now says "Drawing the
network" or "Updating the network" while it lasts; a fast machine rarely sees it.
