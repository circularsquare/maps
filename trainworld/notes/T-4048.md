# T-4048: remove passages and station connections

Agent passx, 2026-11-24. Built in tree passx; not committed.

## What a player sees

While placing a passage (the station panel's Passage icon), from zoom 14, every join the map draws in a complex has
a small cross button: a passage's bridge, a bridge the distance rule draws, a BRT station's dashed join. Buttons sit
at the join's middle, else moved along it (0.35, 0.65, 0.2, 0.8, 0.1, 0.9) or one button-width to either side of
its middle; none overlaps another, a station name (`StationLabels.shownBoxes`) or a capacity dot
(`CapacityMarkers.shownBoxes`, added). One that fits nowhere is left out (a bigger sideways step was tried and
dropped: at zoom 15 the buttons floated 36 px off their joins and read as loose crosses). Hover: the delete tool's
station ring, 2 px of the accent (#d9788e in pink). No hover tag (the cross on a join in the passage tool says it).
A click removes the join at once and picking goes on.

- A passage's button removes the passage (`separate` op, nothing refunded). Notice "Passage between A and B removed." (existing string).
- A distance-rule join's button splits the complex at that join (`cutPairs` in game/links.ts): the members on the
  clicked station's side (through the other drawn joins, passages, buildings and touching boxes) are kept apart from
  the rest: every pair the rule links across (`nearEnough`) goes into `Network::apart`. One click, the bridge goes
  and no other appears. Removing only the clicked pair was the first idea; in a meshed complex (three platforms
  side by side) another bridge would appear at once, which reads as the removal failing.
- Both are free and one Ctrl+Z step each. The station panel's Remove in "Passages" is the passage's map button
  (`unlink` calls `removeJoin` with the joins the map draws, `drawnJoins`), so a passage between stations the rule
  also joins splits them there too (DECISIONS 2026-11-24). Making a passage stays outside the history (it is paid).

## Model

- `Network::apart` (track model): station node pairs the distance rule does not join. Links and buildings still
  join. Saved in a trailing `LAP1` block after T-4080's `LBK1`, level 41 (`SAVE_LEVEL` 41 in saveFile.ts). Live-filtered like links.
- `Op::Link` and `Op::Apart` (world.rs): set one pair, inverse the old state. `TrackApi::separate(a, b, apart)`
  runs them as one Batch through `run` (undoable). `Op::Station { data: None }` now takes the station's links and
  pairs with it inside the same edit (`Network::take_pairs`), so an undo restores them and a node id used again
  starts clean (this also fixes the same stale-link risk for passages).
- Every copy of the complex rule takes the pairs: `buildComplexes` and `ComplexBuilder` (a pair change reworks
  both ends' complexes, like a link), `complexView` (`complexes`, `dotGroups`), the clock worker's
  `complexesOf` (`apartNow`), the state's `complexKey`, twin.rs `complex_of`.
- Demand: the snapshot carries `apart` (constructed pairs) to `DemandApi::set_apart` (and Everyday, Intercity).
  `merge_stations` never merges a pair, not even through a third station near both (the 100 m demand
  station); `build_graph` gives a pair no 300 m walk, but the complex loop gives it one when the pair is in one complex
  again (a passage, a building, a third station).
  The cache key (`canonical`) and the cluster signature include it.
- The lie (DECISIONS): riders could walk the street between separated stations; stations on either side of the
  split that the rule did not link (100-300 m apart) still walk to each other.

## For T-4083 (bus stop connections)

The button acts on whatever pair a join names: `removeJoin(a, b, joins)` sends one `separate` edit. T-4083's
connections join the same way: list them in `JoinButtons.refresh` (ends in local units), and let `separate`
also drop a kept stop's connection (an `Op` like `Op::Link` on the kept stop's list). Removing an automatic bus
walk (the "kept apart list on the same kept stops" of notes/T-4080-design.md) would put kept-stop ends into
`Network::apart` and the demand `apart` input; nothing else changes. Today bus stops have no lasting id, so their
dashed walk connectors get no button.

## Edge cases left

- A station placed later can join two separated sides again (its own joins are new connections, with buttons).
  network.ts `complexPassages` then picks bridges by gap and may draw a separated pair as the complex's bridge
  (not touched: cutstretch's file). Clicking it cuts again, which is still right.
- A BRT station's dashed joins come from `brtJoins`' own tree; a cut through one uses the drawn joins of both
  kinds, so a rare BRT complex can lose more pairs than drawn. Not seen.
- Below zoom 14 (dots and connectors) there are no buttons.

## Review fixes (passfix, 2026-11-24)

Review: passreview's notes plus Gemini's (artifacts/trainworld/gemini/t4048-review.md). Merged master (T-4080's
`LBK1` at level 40), so this tree takes level 41: `LAP1` after `LBK1` in `BLOCKS`, encode and decode.

- **F1** (search.rs, the complex loop): the cause was the loop inferring "the 300 m walk is there already" from the
  distance (`d2 <= cut2`), true only while the grid loop skips no pair; the grid now skips separated pairs, so a pair
  separated and then joined into one complex again (a passage, a building, a third station) had no walk at all. The
  loop now asks `near[a]` itself: any two of a complex walk once, whatever filter the grid loop has. The bus-walk
  pairs skip separated pairs too. Without separated pairs the edges are the same as before (the grid loop and the
  bus walks give exactly the pairs within `cut2`). Test `separated_stations_take_no_walk` (third station, passage).
- **F2** (api.rs `merge_stations`): single linkage merged A and B through a station or bus stop within 100 m of
  both. Now each merged group carries its members' separated partners and refuses a union that would take one. The
  station between goes with whichever it meets first. Test `separated_stations_are_never_merged`.
- **F4** (world.rs `step`): a passage removed (undo step "link on") and paid for again off the history left the step
  with nothing to do. `drop_done_steps` drops undo steps that only set links and pairs already as they are, so the
  undo takes the edit before; `step_is_service` looks past them too. Only links change off the history and only by
  being added, so redo steps never go stale. Test `t4048_an_undo_passes_a_removal_a_new_passage_undid`.
- **F5** (world.rs `apply_rerouted`): any player edit that touches a separated station (a move, a platform change)
  and leaves the pair out of joining range (`Network::near_enough`, the twin's copy of the rule) lets the pair go in
  the same edit, so its undo keeps it. Done where the edit is made, not in `moved_by`, so every way of moving is
  covered. Test `t4048_a_move_out_of_range_lets_a_separated_pair_go`.
- **F6** (joinButtons.ts): the cut is given every join drawn (`all`), those of hidden stations too; buttons only
  for visible ones, BRT joins included; what is hidden (`stationHid`) is part of the refresh key. Covered by the
  typecheck only (no check drives the buttons).
- **F7** (demandClusters.ts): every optional part of the cluster signature is hashed after its name, so two parts
  of one shape (passages, separated pairs) never read as each other; `station_apart` comes sorted, so the order of
  separations no longer changes the cache keys. The signature is in memory only. Typecheck only.
