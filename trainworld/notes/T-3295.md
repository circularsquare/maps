# T-3295: changing between two stops of one line

Player report via Anita (TODO, 2026-11-12): a line with two stops near each other on the ground but
far apart along the line (a near self-intersection); "the stations won't connect", and a passage
built between them gets nobody changing there, though it would help some trips.

## Cause

The route search (`sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs`, `Search::ban`) gave a rider who walked off a
line to another station a change node barring that whole line there (T-575's guard, kept whole for
walks by T-1268: "A walk from another station still bars the whole line"). The walk edge existed
(stations within 300 m, or any two of one complex, a passage walking at 2 m/s), but at the far stop
the line itself was barred, so nobody could ride the line to A, walk to B and ride the same line on
from B. Changes onto other lines across the passage were never affected.

## Fix

`Search::walk_ban`: after a walk off route node `a` to another stop of the same line, barred are the
line's departures whose next stop is the stop walked from or behind it along the way the rider rode
(on a circle, the nearer way round); the rest are open. Each route node's place along its line is
kept per search (`Search::at`). The change node is keyed by the barred departures' next stops (at most
two, else the whole line, as before).

What stays barred: walking to the next stop or the last one to ride back (gyaku-nori by walking),
riding again over track already ridden. What opens: across the bend, ride on from B, or back round
the bend from the far side to a stop in the loop (only when that beats riding round, by cost).

## Measured

Rust tests (`kernel/tests.rs`): `a_line_bent_back_near_itself_is_changed_onto_across_the_bend`
(P to T across a 200 m gap 18.0 perceived minutes against 32.5 round the bend, both ways; to a stop
in the bend from the far side 21.0 against 23.5; through a passage 600 m apart, which is past the
walking limit, too; nobody rides round), `a_walk_off_a_line_never_rides_it_back` (a walk to the last
stop to ride back past full trains stays barred).

Sandbox scene (scratchpad `bend.mjs`, Brooklyn, one line P-A-L1-E-L3-B-T, A and B 256 m apart and
about 8 km apart along the line, 5-minute trains, weekday final, one demand worker):

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| rail trips a day | 61,300 | 61,383 |
| boardings on the line | 61,300 | 62,299 |
| changes at A / B | 0 / 0 | 676 / 240 |
| bend segments (run 0) | 8,436-8,691 | 8,276-8,531 |

Small, because the two arms beyond A and B are short (3 stops each side of the bend in all).

Real save (T-007, weekday final): 12,581,682 rail trips, 24,397,163 boardings and 11,807,282
changes before and after, every line and station the same: no line there bends back near itself
where it pays.
