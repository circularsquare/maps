# T-418: drawing track like NIMBY Rails (design)

Anita, 2026-10-15: try "the track goes through every clicked point" and "the next click never
changes what is already placed", with two clicks per segment by default, and the faint circle of
curvature NIMBY shows. Stage 1 built 2026-10-15 as below (section 8); stages 2 and 3 are T-422, T-423.

## 1. How drawing works now

Each click is a corner (a PI): the track runs straight towards it and cuts the corner with a
curve, so the track does not pass through the clicked point. The curve's radius depends on both
legs next to it, so the next click can still tighten the previous curve (T-092 limits it to that).
The route is committed only at the end (double click, Enter, or a click on track), as one piece per
corner (T-244).

## 2. The NIMBY rule here

- **Each segment is one circular arc** from the last placed point to the click, leaving along the
  track's direction there (a tangent arc). This is how NIMBY's tool behaves as I understand it
  (research/nimby-rails.md says nothing about the drawing tool). For a click d metres away at an
  angle a off the heading, the radius is d / (2 sin a) and the track turns by 2a. Example: 500 m
  away, 30 degrees off: a 500 m curve, 84 km/h.
- **Why a tangent arc and not straight-then-arc**: of all single-curve shapes that leave along the
  heading and pass through the click, the tangent arc has the largest radius, so the highest speed.
  We have no transition curves, so nothing else to fit. Today a two-click route from a track end
  already leaves along the heading, but as a tighter curve then a straight (the lead-in PI sits
  0.3 of the way): 397 m instead of 500 m in the example above.
- **Very gentle clicks**: a tangent arc over 1,796 m (the 160 km/h radius cap) becomes straight,
  1,796 m curve, straight, still through the click and leaving the click in the same direction.
- **Too tight** (under 100 m: a click close by and well off to the side) or **behind** (more than
  about 85 degrees off the heading): the ghost is red with the reason, and the click is refused.
  No straight-plus-arc fallback in stage 1: the shape would jump as the cursor crosses the limit.
- **First segment from a free point**: straight. **From a track end**: leaves along that track.
  **From track or a junction**: leaves along the track, in whichever direction is nearer the click
  (today's lead-in rule).
- **Ending on track or a node**: the segment must also join that track like a turnout, so it keeps
  today's shape (a curve at each end, straight between). It is the one segment that is not a single
  arc.
- **Levels**: each click takes the selected level; a segment that changes level needs room for its
  ramp (200 m per level) on its second half, or it is refused.

## 3. Two clicks per segment, and continuing

Default: click the start, click the end, and the segment is blueprint at once (one undo step).
To carry on, click the end just placed (a track end, so the next segment leaves along it). That
needs no new rule: starting on a track end already works.

For drawing many segments in a row:
- **A. Hold Shift while clicking**: each click places a segment and starts the next from its end.
- B. Hold Ctrl or Alt instead (Ctrl-click is a right click on a Mac).
- C. A setting in the Build tab ("one segment" or "keep going").

T-410's shift-click ("place this corner anyway") exists because a route still being drawn can be
fixed by the clicks after it. In the new mode each segment is final when placed, so an unbuildable
one could never become buildable; the edit refuses it anyway. Shift-anyway has nothing to do in the
new mode, so **I recommend A**: Shift chains in the new mode and keeps its T-410 meaning in the old
one. Backspace while chaining takes back the last segment (the same as Ctrl+Z); Escape stops.

## 4. The faint circle

The full circle that the segment's curve is part of: centre and radius of the arc in the hover
preview, drawn in fixed grey ink at about 35% like the station rings (an SVG layer in
`map/tools.ts`), red when the curve is too tight. The map is conformal, so a circle on the ground is
a circle on screen. Only while drawing; placed track shows none.

## 5. What changes in the code

- **The track model needs nothing new.** A segment from a track end is an edge with one PI: on the
  start's heading, d / (2 cos a) along it, auto radius. Both its legs are end legs, which the auto
  radius may use whole, and they are equal, so the auto radius is exactly the tangent arc. Segment
  ends are plain joints (two edge ends, no station), like T-244's: the joint lies on the line between
  the PIs either side, so `free_joint` treats it as now (a dragged corner moves the joint with it, a
  station merges the pieces next to it).
- **sim, `wasm.rs`**: a flag on `route_op` so the start's lead-in PI sits at the tangent-arc distance
  instead of 0.3 of the way, the arc centre added to the preview's answer, and the route edit's answer
  carrying its end node, so chaining can start from it. About 40 lines with tests.
- **app, `map/tools.ts`**: the mode switch; the second click commits; Shift chains; the circle;
  the old T-410 click check skipped in the new mode. The hover preview is today's with no corners.
- **Reshaping**: unchanged. Dragging a segment's corner later works as now; the curve then uses the
  shorter leg, so it becomes curve-then-straight but stays valid. A NIMBY-style "drag the clicked
  point" is the existing node drag (T-093): a joint keeps its heading and both corners next to it
  move with it. No new edit rules in stage 1.
- **Saves**: unchanged (edges with PIs and nodes, as now).
- **Checks**: Rust tests that a tangent segment is one arc of radius d / (2 sin a) ending at the
  click on heading 2a, the refusals, a station placed across a joint, a corner drag moving a joint;
  `t145-check` (the preview contract) for the new fields; a short Playwright run drawing a chain on a
  new game.

Two known gaps:
- **Stations across several short segments**: a station merges only one joint on each side
  (`unjoin_near`), so a 400 m platform over three 100 m segments would be refused. The fix is to merge
  joints repeatedly until the platform has room.
- **Quad track**: each segment would get its own express pair with its own swing-outs and junctions
  at every joint. In stage 1 quad stays on the old drawing; a later stage continues the express pair
  from the previous segment's.

## 6. Effort and risk

Not hard: the geometry fits the existing model, and most of the work is in the tool.
- **Stage 1** (about half a day of agent work): the new mode beside the old behind a Build-tab
  setting, with tangent arcs, two clicks, Shift to chain, the circle, the tests above, and the
  multi-joint station merge. Quad uses the old mode. Low risk: the old mode is untouched.
- **Stage 2** (an hour or two, after you have tried it): make the new mode the default, decide
  whether the old one stays, and update track-and-service.md 6.1 and ui-building.md.
- **Stage 3, if wanted**: quad in the new mode; a straight-then-arc fallback for clicks too tight for
  a tangent arc.
Main risk: more, shorter edges in a network (one per segment instead of one per corner), so more
joints, which only the station merge cares about.

## 7. Decisions for Anita

1. **Shape of a segment**: a tangent arc (one curve through the click, the fastest), or curve then
   straight, as two-click routes from a track end are today. I'd take the tangent arc.
2. **A click too tight for a 100 m curve**: refused with a red ghost and circle, or bent as a
   straight then a 100 m curve that still reaches it. I'd refuse, as NIMBY does as far as I know.
3. **Continuing**: Shift held to chain segments, with T-410's shift-click kept only in the old
   mode. I'd do that; the alternatives are Ctrl or Alt, or a Build-tab setting.
4. **The old mode after you have tried both**: retire it or keep it as a setting. I'd keep it until
   quad works in the new mode, then retire it.

## 9. Stage 3, quad through points (T-423, 2026-10-15)

- **The design**: a through-points quad segment ending on open ground leaves two loose ends (the
  express pair's 8 m to the left), not the fork of Corners. A segment starting on a track end that
  has another loose end 4-12 m beside it, level and facing the same way (`quad_beside`), carries
  both pairs on: the drawn pair from the end clicked as the tangent arc, the other from the end
  beside, every PI offset concentric (`express_verts`'s `own_a`/`own_b`, with `side` for an end on
  the right). So a chain has plain joints on both pairs, no swing-outs and no junctions, and
  clicking either pair's loose end works. Ending on track or a node, both pairs meet there as
  before; a segment to a loose quad end whose partner is on the matching side ends on both.
- **Not one arc**: a quad segment from other track (a node, a junction, plain double track) still
  swings out over 150 m, which needs a straight, so it keeps the Corners lead-in shape (curve then
  straight) and shows no circle. A concentric express inside the turn needs the drawn pair's curve
  at 108 m; the circle drawn is the drawn pair's, so a 104 m circle can show red.
- **Twin stations**: `twin_at` puts the twin on the other pair's loose end (within 8 m) or joint
  (within 1 m) when level with one, since a split needs room from a node. This also gives a
  station on a quad loose end its twin (T-340's refusal is about Corners' shared end node).
- **The ghost**: the preview polyline jumps from the drawn pair's end to the express pair's; that
  step's speed is -1 and `strokesFromPreview` (map/network.ts) breaks the stroke there.
- Test `quad_drawn_through_points_carries_both_pairs_on` (four-segment chain, concentric radii,
  8 m everywhere, four loose ends, twin stations across a joint and on the loose ends, a crossover,
  a segment from the express pair's loose end the other way, one ending on plain track as one
  four-end junction, the ghost's break and circle).
- Browser run (scratch script, new game, Queens): a Shift chain of four quad segments (8 edges,
  4 loose ends, no junction), a two-click segment from the loose end, the ghost close up, a twin
  station across the first joint and another on the fourth segment, a crossover on the third, all
  constructed, a line from the first station's local platform to the second's express one taking
  the crossover, its train on it at 08:30. Screenshots ../artifacts/trainworld/T-423-*.png.
- Corners can retire as far as quad goes: nothing in quad needs it now.
