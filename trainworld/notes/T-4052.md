# T-4052: riders waiting at bus stops (stage 1 agent buswait, stage 2 agent buscount, 2026-11-24)

Status: both stages in. A bus stop holds at most 200 waiting (stage 1), and riders left behind are counted once each
(stage 2). The issues list's "<line> crowding" row and the line panel's "Riders left at the stop" now read about
half what they did on an overloaded bus line. Follow-up options at the end are the orchestrator's call.

## What the player sees

Bus lines have no drawn platform crowd and no "Waiting now" rows (`Boarding::hour` skips them, T-1464). The only
waiting figure is the issues list's "<line> crowding" row (person icon) and the line panel's "Riders left at the
stop", both the past 24 hours of the boarding account (`Boarding::past_day`, `tally`), shown with the denominator
"wanted" = riders carried + left behind (app: `TroubleList.tsx`, `schedule.tsx`).

## Why bus numbers ran bigger than stations'

1. **The stop held a platform's 5,000** (fixed in stage 1). As each vehicle leaves, a stop side holds what it and the
   next 30 minutes' vehicles take (`room x (1 + floor(1800 / gap))`), at most `WAITING_CAP` = 5,000 (a 300 m platform
   side). That cap binds a long train at about two trainloads; an 80-rider bus never reaches it, so a bus stop held up
   to 15 busloads at a 2-minute headway. Now a bus stop holds what a bus's length of sidewalk holds by the same area
   rule (12 m x 5 m / 0.3 m^2 = 200), a BRT station its 40 m platform's (667); stations and ports keep 5,000
   (`LineSvc::waiting_cap`).
2. **The count took in riders turned away** (fixed in stage 2). It summed, per departure, `min(arrived since the last
   vehicle, queue after it)`, where "arrived" included those turned away from a full stop. At a full stop that counted
   the queue's older riders again at every vehicle they missed, and the turned away as left behind: up to (queue
   after / room) per rider carried, 1.5 at a bus stop, about 1.08 at a 10-car platform.
3. **The rest is a real day's total.** An overloaded bus line in a dense city really leaves thousands behind a day;
   the row shows that 24-hour total as a bare person count.

## Stage 2: each rider left behind counted once

- The rule: a departure's riders left behind are those who joined the queue since the vehicle before and do not get
  on this one, `min(arrived - turned away, queue after)` (the queue boards oldest first, so the queue left is the
  latest to join). Each rider is counted once, at the first vehicle to leave them, however many more they miss;
  riders turned away never joined (they go another way, docs/track-and-service.md 6.6).
- Where: `simulate_carry` (sim/src/track/boarding.rs) works it out per departure and stop; `Queues` keeps it as a
  third number beside `q_after` and `gone` (`Queues::left`), only where a queue is (a left count needs a queue
  after, so the kept set is unchanged); `tally` (the past 24 hours: issues row and line panel) sums it, and
  `left_h` (the line summary's day, the period crowding, a platform's hour) is the same numbers by hour. The window
  needs it per departure: by hour would split the window's edge hour and a new line's opening hour wrongly.
- Not changed: trips, fares, boardings, the stage 1 caps, demand (its own `kernel::left_behind` estimate has no stop
  cap, so nobody is turned away there and its `min(arrived, queue after)` already counts each rider once).
- Test `riders_left_at_a_bus_stop_count_once_each` (sim/src/track/wasm/bus_tests.rs): a bus every 2 minutes takes 50
  at its first stop, leaving room for 30 at its second, where 2,700 an hour come all morning; the stop holds 200, so
  a rider who joins waits through about six buses and nearly two in three who come are turned away. Left behind
  3,747 a day against 3,622 who boarded there in the morning (the 125 more: the queue still waiting as the morning
  ends); turned away 6,981, not among them; the old count gave about 3x. The day's count by hour and the past 24
  hours' agree. `queues_read_back_to_the_bit` reads the third number back. Both suites pass (752 + 982).

## Follow-ups (not done)

- **The denominator.** The row's tip and the line panel show "left/wanted" with wanted = riders carried + left
  behind. Riders left behind mostly board a later vehicle, so they are counted twice in "wanted" (true before too
  wherever nobody was turned away, as on most rail). The riders who came to the stops (`arrivals`, those turned away
  included) would be the honest "wanted": `tally` can sum them per departure into the window block's unused slot
  (`d[e + 6]`, 0 since T-2236), no layout change. It moves the row's 3% threshold a little.
- **Presentation**: the row shows a 24-hour total beside a person icon, which reads as people waiting now. Options:
  the left/wanted share on the row, or the most waiting at one stop (200 at most at a bus stop).
- Demand's queue (`kernel::capacity_fit`) has no stop cap, for stations (5,000) or bus stops (200): adding one is a
  demand rule change.
