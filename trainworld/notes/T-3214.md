# T-3214: campus students over the campus, not one cell

From T-3213 option A (notes/T-3213.md). Agent ubc, 2026-11-11. Pipeline only; no pack in data/packs
changed.

## The rule

`tier0.education_osm`: a university or college OSM maps as an outline has its students spread over
the pack cells under it (`campus_spread`): the outline sampled at H3 res 11 centres (res 10 or 9
for outlines over 10 or 70 km²), each sample's res-9 cell (or its merged res-8 parent) taking its
share, half by area and half by the res-9 cell's GHSL non-residential volume
(`CAMPUS_BUILT_SHARE` 0.5, from the pack's `tier0_signals.npz`; area alone when it has none).
Samples outside the pack are dropped; an outline with no sample in the pack, a site too small for
a res-11 centre, and a point stay in their one cell as before. Each site's total is unchanged.

`osm_education`'s cache gains a `wkt` column (universities' and colleges' outlines, simplified to
about 3 m). A cache made before it reads with `wkt` empty, so those packs keep the old placement
until the cache is made again. `python pipeline/tier0.py <id> --education` runs schools and
universities alone (through `merge_cells.py <ids> --through "tier0 {id} --education"` on a
merged pack).

on disk, under the shared lock `wikidata-cache`, written whole (`shared_lock.write_atomic`).

## What picks it up

Every registered tier 0 pack with a campus outline: 3,344 of them, holding 177.3M of their 178.2M
students. Hand packs (IPEDS, Ontario's points) are unchanged. A pack takes it when its
`osm_education_<id>` cache is made again, which needs its extract's features cache
(`osm_features`), and no extract is kept on disk now (nine area caches are: Tianjin, Shanghai,
Heilongjiang, Bhutan, India western zone and four more).

- A world rebuild of a row deletes the cache, so rebuilt rows get it with nothing more.
- Without a rebuild: delete the caches, read each region's extracts again (the download and GDAL
  read of a world run), `merge_cells.py <ids> --through "tier0 {id} --education"` (seconds a
  pack), then the cross-zone kept shares (`cross_everyday`) for the groups touched. The reads
  are most of it, roughly a world run's read phase. Overpass outlines matched into the existing
  caches, as the proof did, would skip the downloads: one query a pack, a few hours in all, at the
  cost of matching live OSM to the extract's sites (47 of 48 at Vancouver).
