# T-3655: a line on full track keeps its old rate after a new count

Agent fullrate, 2026-11-20 (cause, two failed fixes) and 2026-11-21 (the fix, `sim/src/track/world.rs`
`reshare_full`; rule in docs/track-and-service.md "Capacity limits in the clock worker"; DECISIONS 2026-11-21).
Part 2, the edit time, agent fullspeed 2026-11-21: "Answer before the loads" at the end (rule in docs/architecture.md
"A service edit's loads can follow").

## The fix

An edit that gives a line another headway, or a count line other trains, at a level (`settle_reshare`; a
headway line is compared by its headway alone, since an edit setting it sends its trains anew; T-3482's levels
with no trains before keep their own path) re-shares full track in that edit's first settle, before the
ordinary steps:

1. **Seeds**: those lines at those levels, on full track (`on_full_track`, T-517's held test) or with a fleet
   whose rate is over what the track lets them run (`line_limits`). The second case: a count lowered from a
   state where the track was not full yet (T-3646's off-history edit re-made with another state's rates: without
   it the lowering was stepped and landed on 19.2 against 17.3 trains an hour, failing
   `an_edit_off_the_history_stays_through_undo_and_redo`).
2. **Group**: the seeds, every line on full track (a limiting resource at `FULL_RHO`) with them, and with those
   in turn. Without the closure a line next to a held one kept its rate and capped the share (London line 44 at
   480 s: 4.23 going up, 4.52 coming down, held back by line 38 at the crossings it shares with seven lines).
3. **Fill**: each group line wants its fleet's rate over its round trip as it stands; one `Capacity::fill` with
   only the group in `want`, the rest keeping their rates; one service update.
4. **Passes** (up to 8): a line now off its fleet and not held, and any line on track the share filled, joins;
   the fill is made again. An uncut line wants the rate its fleet runs at the round trip that rate gives, by the
   secant of its last two passes, or halfway between them where its shortfall turned (plain repeats swung with a
   factor of about -0.5 and never got under 0.0001 trains).
5. **End**: a line over its fleet is taken under it (to the most it ran under it, or 0.001 trains under), up to
   3 times (only shortens round trips, mostly); a line up to 0.05 trains under its fleet is kept there in
   `settle_kept` as T-1283 keeps a swinging line, or up to 0.5 trains where its shortfall turned 3 times within
   0.2% of its rate (a round trip that jumps as a hold comes and goes). Without this a few thousandths of a
   train made the ordinary steps step the whole level, held lines too: 109 rounds (753 ms) for 600 -> 420 s,
   409 rounds (2 s) for a neighbour's count. Anything else still goes to the ordinary steps.

Why not the notes' plain idea (group-only `want`, one fill): it stopped lines coming off held from the fill's
cut, but left 10-26 rounds of ordinary steps (100-250 ms), and more plain passes made the result depend on the
path (1.7 trains an hour on line 23 after 600 -> 360 -> 600 s).

## Cause (2026-11-20)

A headway edit runs three settles (`auto_counts` rounds, then the trim's). In all three line 44 was short of its
fleet on a crossing at 100% shared with line 25, so it was held (T-517), nothing at the level was active and the
level was not stepped; `trim_auto` then dropped the trains waiting at the ends. A count edit the same. A level
stepped only when some line on it was not held, and then the damped steps raced. Tried then and dropped: a
re-share with every line of the level in `want` (lines beside it came off held, 80-round swings, 0.8-3.8 s).

## Left

- A weekend-only edit (`Op::Weekend`) is not re-shared: its branch sets only `settle_edited_rest`, so a
  weekend count on full track keeps its old weekend rate, as before.
- A weekday edit that moves a line's weekend counts at a level where its weekday fleet did not change is not
  re-shared on weekends there.
- Held shares elsewhere stay path dependent (T-3482 follow-up 1), and the first edit after a load re-forms the
  ones its share reaches.

## Faster passes (fullrate2, 2026-11-21, advisor consulted twice)

Three changes in `reshare_full`, plus one latent fix:
1. **Secant across a far turn**: where a line's shortfall turned between two passes, it aimed halfway between them.
   From a load the last rate was the stale held one (line 162 at level 0: 0.26 tph against a fleet's 3.16), so it
   bisected from a huge bracket (+2.8, +1.4, +0.7, +0.34, +0.17 trains, never closing in 8 passes; the ordinary steps
   then took the whole level, 43 rounds, 277 ms). It now takes the secant root inside the bracket where the two rates
   are more than half the higher apart (`RESHARE_STALE`), halfway otherwise. The secant on every turn broke
   `whole_line_counts_include_congestion_and_odd_fleets` (two lines on one track ended 0.007 trains under their
   fleets after 8 passes, kept; 12 passes did not fix it), so it is kept to that stale case. Not every stale case:
   on the bare `twt2` (no cap edits first) the first count edit and the count after `clear` still fall to the
   ordinary steps (55 rounds, 240-265 ms natively, 356 ms apply in the browser for the count after `clear`).
2. **Join tolerance** `RESHARE_CLOSE`: after pass 0 a line joins another fill only if it is off its fleet by more
   than 0.1% of it (at most `RESHARE_NEAR`, 0.05 trains). Passes 3-8 were chasing a single line 0.0001-0.003 trains
   off. The lie: a line may end up to 0.1% of its fleet under it (kept, as T-1283's) where before it was 0.0001 trains;
   a line over its fleet is still taken under it exactly.
3. **Moved-only update**: each pass's `update_scoped` gets only the lines whose rates the fill moved, not all 171
   (`cap.update` re-solves only resources they use). Save dumps bitwise the same as updating every line, with and
   without weekends at 80%, over cap edits, counts, switches and undo.
4. `put_rates` sorts `settle_kept` (both lists): an undo put the restored lines' entries at the end, so save bytes
   differed in order only. Latent since T-3646; surfaced because more lines are kept now.
