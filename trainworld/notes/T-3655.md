# T-3655: a line on full track keeps its old rate after a new count (not fixed)

Agent fullrate, 2026-11-20. Cause traced, two fixes tried and dropped; nothing of this is in the tree. The
patch of the attempt (both variants' last state, with T-3646's work) is in the agent's scratchpad
(`fullrate/attempt_t3655_and_t3646.patch`); the parts below are enough to redo it.

## Cause (traced natively, London shared save `data/work/realnet/lon_real.twt2`, busiest line 44)

edit_bench's sequence on master: headway 300/600/1200, then medium 540, 480, 420, 360, then counts 20/10..13/4.
Line 44 at medium stays at 3.99 trains an hour and 8 trains through every headway (and through the counts
10 to 13); a trace of `settle_counts` (line 44, slot 3) shows why:

- A headway edit runs three settles (`auto_counts` rounds, then the trim's). In all three line 44 is short of
  its fleet (4.45 trains at 540, 9.98 at 360) on a crossing at 100% shared with line 25 (`res Crossing users
  [44, 25, 25]`), so it is *held* (T-517), nothing at the level is active, and the level is not stepped.
- `trim_auto` then drops the trains that wait at the ends: back to 8. So the new headway moves nothing.
- A count edit (10 -> 13 at medium) is the same: held, nothing steps, the rate stays.
- A level does step when some line on it is not held (a count lowered below its rate): `count 44 20 10 4`
  stepped the peak for 41 rounds (363 ms) and left line 44 at 2.20 against 9.71 before, line 25 taking the room:
  the damped steps race, and whichever steps keeps the track.

Master's stepping edits on this save run to 41-59 rounds (8 ms a round): the 50 ms goal is a convergence
problem of the damped step, not only of scope.

## Tried

Both variants made the edited levels (levels where the edit changed a line's trains or headway, besides
T-3482's levels with no trains before) take a share at once, as follows. The edited line, and every held line
on a full resource it uses at that level, is set to what its fleet runs over its round trip as it stands
(`running_trains * 3600 / trip`, undamped), and the level's fill then cuts them together, so the share follows
the fleets rather than the rates they came with. Only where the level is not stepping anyway.

1. **Re-share, then the ordinary steps.** Count edits were monotone and modest: line 44 at medium 1.80, 1.97,
   2.14, 2.31 trains an hour for 10, 11, 12, 13 trains, 83-137 ms an edit (master 6-8 ms, no change). Headway
   edits ran the wrong way and slowly: 3.68, 3.04, 1.66, 1.97, 1.66 as the headway went 600 to 360 s, 0.8-1.4 s
   an edit. After the first re-share, lines 6, 23, 30, 50 and others came off held (the fill cut them, or freed
   their track), the ordinary stepping started, and line 44 flipped between about 3.2 and 4.2 trains an hour
   to the round cap (159-170 rounds traced an edit).
2. **The same, with the re-shared lines left out of the ordinary steps** (only the fill moves them): worse,
   2.7-3.8 s an edit, headway results still not monotone (1.66, 1.66, 2.31, 1.66).

## Next attempt (advisor's pointer, not tried)

The fill after the re-share takes every line of the level as `want`; `Capacity::fill` keeps the rates of lines
left out of `want` and only lets them take room, so passing only the re-shared group would keep the cut inside
it and leave the other lines settled (no lines coming off held, no stepping). Then measure: the counts were
already within reach of the 50 ms goal (83-137 ms on the agent profile with the stepping included).

Also worth knowing: a headway line's count is the headway's (`auto_counts`) during the settle's rounds and only
trimmed after, so its fleet there is the headway's own rate; a count line's is its trains over its round trip.
