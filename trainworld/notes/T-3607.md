# T-3607 One-way chevrons by distance, bus arrows further out

Tree `.tw-trees/chevrons` (branch tw/chevrons). Not landed.

## What changed
- `map/oneWayMarks.ts`: new `oneWayTrackRuns`. One-way track edges are chained into runs (a run goes on through a node
  with exactly one one-way edge in and one out, restarts elsewhere). Marks sit at the middle of each 100 m step of the
  run's distance, carried across edge boundaries, so no piece forces one. The mark number `m` is the run's, so the
  shader's halving (`CHEVRON_GAP_PX` 300, zoom 13) thins along the run. `network.ts` calls it for all one-way edges.
  Train-line chevrons (`trainChevrons`) already counted distance along the whole line; unchanged. The route ghost still
  uses the per-edge `oneWayTrackChevrons`.
- Bus arrows: `ARROW_ZOOM` 13 to 12. Spacing rule (`ARROW_SPREAD` 1.25, 150 px gap, halving) is by distance along the
  whole route already and just thins further at zoom 12 (45 arrows in the loop, drawn every 8th at zoom 12).
- Bus arrow size: `ARROW_BARB_PX` 4 to 2.5, `ARROW_BARB_SHARE` 1.1 to 0.5 (busLayer.ts). The arrow's width, tip to tip
  across the line, was 3.2 line widths; it is now 2.0 (line width plus half a line each side, 2.5 px minimum). The arrow
  is a filled triangle, so "thickness" is its width and length. Bus line 5.07 px at zoom 12, 6.24 at zoom 13 (device px).
