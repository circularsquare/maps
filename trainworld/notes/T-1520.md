# T-1520: the market's income groups made cheap (2026-10-28)

**What changed** (`sim/src/demand/intercity.rs`, `market_trip.rs`):
- One logsum per zone pair instead of one per income group (`RailMix`, `rail_mix_cols`,
  `rail_mix_x`). It is taken at the groups' trip-weighted mean fare factor and the scale (`soften`)
  of the mean group's car. Each group's rail cost is the logsum moved to first order:
  `c + (d_g - d_mean) x E[fare] + (th_g - th_mean) / th_mean x (E[C] - c)`, where E[] is over the
  station pairs' shares and d logsum / d th = (E[C] - logsum) / th. The loads go over the station
  pairs once, in the mean's shares, with all groups' rail trips. A place with one group gives the
  same figures to the bit.
- The pass fills the fare km column (`fare_col`) only for stations that the longer trips read at
  their work end. Commutes and outings read no km while no fare factor is set. This part is exact.
- The trip explorer uses the same mix, so it matches the pass.
