# T-3881: a cold load's bar sticks after a cached load

Agent loadcard, 2026-11-20. Found by loadleft (notes/T-3876.md): the load card's stage shares come from the slot's
remembered stage times (`rememberWait`/`lastWait`, `was` in `jobProgress`). A load that ran no solve of its own
(a cached result's card, zones needing none) remembered a demand stage of seconds, so the next cold load gave its solve
a sliver of the bar: 83-88% when the solve starts, then 98% for minutes.

## Change
- `demandClient.ts`: a load remembers its stage times (and the pace) only when it measured a solve of its own
  (`job.solveRounds`).
- `loadBarCore.ts`: the storage key is `anitabuilder-load-wait-v2`, so records written before this (possibly with the
  seconds-long demand stage) are never read.
- Typecheck passes. No smoke. No strings, no changelog item (the bar only stops sticking).

## Proof (na-day-491, 3 cores, Vite 6181, own Chrome profile; traces in the scratchpad `loadcard/`)
I could not make a cached load write a slot record in this tree (the cached card's job ends without remembering; the
poisoned record's exact writer is not pinned down), so the poisoned record was seeded under the slot's key:
`{stops: 2553, stages: {game: 8000, data: 20000, demand: 4000}}`, then a cold load (cache key changed by a perfOff flag
before; a Vite restart after).
- Before (`trace_before.jsonl`): bar 83% at 97-137 s waiting for the network, 88% when the solve starts (138 s), then
  90% at 158, 93% at 218, 96% at 298, 98% at 398, done at 416.
- After (`trace.jsonl`): 52% when the solve starts (91 s), 60% at 111, 74% at 172, 86% at 252, 96% at 332, 98% at 352,
  done at 357. Even, 0.15%/s. The old record (old key) is ignored.
