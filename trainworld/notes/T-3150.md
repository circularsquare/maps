# T-3150: long straight rays at a far zoom (player report, San Francisco)

Status 2026-11-11 (agent rays, about 2 hours): not reproduced. Nothing changed in the app.

## What the screenshot shows

`artifacts/trainworld/t3150-player-rays.png`, zoom about 10, dark theme. About 45 station dots on
the peninsula, drawn in the right places. **No track and no line strokes at all** between them,
where a healthy game at that zoom shows both (compare `artifacts/trainworld/t3150-repro1.png`).
Thin light lines cross the whole view; the faint ones fan in towards a point off screen to the
west-south-west (in the Pacific off Pacifica). A new SF game's draw anchor is (-122, 38), north-east
of downtown; the fan's point is about where each station's local position doubled would land
(downtown reflected through itself away from the anchor), i.e. a far end of `S + S`. That is a
rough reading of a 519 px Discord image, not a measurement.

So the stations' own buffers look right, and what is wrong is drawn data that joins two points:
the strokes (track, lines, their zoomed-out LOD copies) or the connectors between dots.

## Tried

- `?new=1&city=snf`, sandbox: two parallel lines 70 m apart (auto passages form), a player link,
  line B removed, its track and stations removed, quad track in its place with stations and a
  line, undo and redo. Then two random fuzz runs (130 edits in all): lines double and quad,
  links on and off, removeStation, removePieces, removeLine, doubleUp, station buildings added
  and removed, train counts, undo, redo, views far away (anchor moves) and at zooms 9-13, paused
  and running. After every edit, checked: connector ends (`renderer.connEnds`), bridge ends
  (`net.stationLinks`), station middles, the track and line `a_seg` read back from the GPU
  (inside the network's box, and byte-equal to `net.track/lines.seg`), and the LOD bands' segments.
  `?patch=check` on the first script. No ray, no difference. Scripts: the session scratchpad
  `rays/repro1.mjs`, `rays/fuzz.mjs`, `rays/fuzz2.mjs` (fuzz2 is the useful one: a ray checker
  over every drawn pair of points).
- `undo`/`redo` posted as `client.edit({op:'undo'})` answered ok:false every time in these runs, so
  undo was not really exercised.

## Ruled out by reading

- Connectors (`renderer.ts syncGroups`) and bridges are built from the current network's station
  indices only; ids map through `stationIds`, missing ones are dropped. `complexPassages`' cache hit
  needs the same member ids, pieces and positions.
- Patched networks: `mergeNet` and `setNetwork` both check the base serial and old counts.
- Floors (station buildings) are not drawn below zoom 13; transfer bubbles are dots.

## Open suspects, untested

1. `strokePost.ts` patch mode writes the next network's new segments into the arrays the drawn
   network still views ("the last network is not to be read once its successor is merged"). The
   renderer reads `net.track`/`net.lines` after that in two places: a state staged for a train
   count (T-1071, `renderer.stage`) applied after a later state was merged, and the LOD work
   (`pickStrokes`, `workLod`, `workBand`), which runs in 8 ms slices across frames on `src`.
   Fuzzing did not hit it.
2. Undo of a delete that took passages and complexes with it (not exercised, see above).

## Same code in v0.2.1?

chevrons and the refused-pieces outline, and network.ts' changes are lanes and single track
(none in the station, passage or connector code). Whatever it is, master likely has it too.
