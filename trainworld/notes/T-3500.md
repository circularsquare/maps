# T-3500: airport hover misses trips from across the zone line

Agent airhover, 2026-11-16. Found by T-3485 (notes/T-3485.md, cause 1).

## What changed

- sim `E_CROSS` and app `CROSS_KINDS` take kind 4 (airport): a bubble selection's far-zone blocks
  now list airport trips across zone lines. The home end weighs them by people like every kind; the
  airport end by the share of the zone's terminals selected (`selection`'s new `air` input, four
  numbers per 600 m square holding terminals: x, y, their airport legs, of those selected; the
  worker's `selectionSquares` builds it from the everyday layer's airport legs at the activity end).
  With no `air` given (tests, `selection_flows_day`) the airport end holds none.
- New `IntercityApi::airport_legs_day(market, end, xy, w, day, mask, rail)`: the kind-4 pairs'
  legs, rail legs and walking legs of each point's 6 km zone (end 0 homes, 1 airports), times the
  point's weight over its zone's points'. Same formula as the selection (`e_legs`, shared).
- Worker query `airLegs` (as `icSel`: all modes from the full market over the worker's periods,
  rail from each market part), and `DemandClient.airportAcross`: `purposeModes("airport")` adds the
  answer to the cells with airport legs (home blocks 0-2 at homes, activity blocks 3-5 at
  terminals; driving is legs minus rail minus walking), for the focused zone, nearby zones and bare
  zones alike. Cells' market positions: world -> pack frame -> `cityToMarket` (most zones' sites
  are in their own pack frame, not the market frame).
- Solved figures: none read `E_CROSS`; it only shapes the selection's answer. Measured identical.
