# T-3695: transit split by mode in the mode bars

Agent modesplit, 2026-11-20. Anita: "break down transit into train/bus/ferry/tram/hsr ... in the RGB bar on top and
in the cities views, like anywhere we show a RGB split really. but not in the actual demand bubbles" and "this
breakdown should be toggleable. default off."

## What it does

Settings, Demand bubbles, under Colors: "Show transit by mode", off by default. Off, nothing changes (the bar's
DOM is the old one). On, the transit part of these bars becomes ferry, tram, train, high speed, bus (bar order,
teal to blueish purple):

- the top bar, for a city and for the World (zoomed out: the built cities' splits weighted by their transit trips);
  its hover's Trip count strip splits too, and a line under the table names the kinds ("Train 15.3%, High speed
  2.3%", shares of all trips; a kind under 0.05% is in the bar, not named);
- the Cities tab: each city's bar and the All cities row, and their hovers (the same line under the sentence);
- the city view: the Trips within bar and the To other places bar, each kind its own part with the usual part hover
  ("Train: 10,849,442 trips within New York per day, 15%"); its Transit share row's hover; the all cities view's;
- zoomed far out, a city's hover bars (within, and to and from other places);
- the trip explorer's bar (each segment's hover "High speed: 100% of the 14 trips per day between the two places");
  a Red Hook bus + F trip to Midtown came out Train 87%, Bus 13%, High speed 0.4% (a one-stop hop on a line set to
  high speed in one route), about the bus leg's 1.2 km against the F's 9 km;
- the market trip explorer's main bar (`MarketTrip.tsx`, the same `tripTransit` over its routes). Coded and
  typechecked, not seen in a screenshot: a trip between two zones with rail was not found in the probe save.

Left at three parts (no data by kind): the demand bubbles (by design), a bubble's or selection's hover bar and the
selection inspector, the far-zoom hover between two zones, airport and attraction hovers, the market trip
explorer's pair, commute and outing rows, the city view's trips-by-length graph, and the hovers' Distance strip/row
(no km by kind).

Under the 2-color and Color blind bubble schemes the hover's "Transit" square and Distance strip keep the scheme's
own transit colour (#4d4dff, #0072b2) beside the five fixed ones; #0072b2 reads near the ferry teal
(t3695-topbar-colorblind-light.png). Left for Anita.

## Where the split comes from

`game/transitModes.ts`, on the page, from figures it already has: each line's boardings per stop on the day shown
(`DemandView.lines`, and `purposes.longer` for the market's riders). A zone's split is the boardings at its stops
(`stationZone`, bus stops included) by the line's kind (`lineKind`: ferry, bus, tram, fast = high speed, rail =
train). Trips within: all boardings minus the longer trips'; to other places: the longer trips'. Memoised per day's
lines map and world version. No worker, WASM or demand change, so trip numbers and trip calculation time are the
same.

By leg count (a boarding is a leg), not length: assignment is by strategy per destination, so no trip's legs are
known; riders x km by kind is a share of distance, where a few long high-speed rides would swamp a bar that counts
trips. Known lies: a trip changing between two lines of one kind counts twice for that kind; a station just outside
the zone counts for the zone it stands in; the market's within-place trips (T-3509) take the local mix. A zone
with transit trips but no boardings at its own stops keeps one transit part.

The trip explorer knows its routes: each listed route by its share of the riders, each ride in it by its
station-to-station straight-line length (Anita's "proportionally by length").

A worker-side exact per-city split (each city population's own `board(q)` by kind, as extra citySummary slots) was
considered and dropped (advisor): it would have to stay right through parts, sides, window solves and estimates in
`figures()`, and weekend days scale only listed slots.
