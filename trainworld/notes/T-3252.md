# T-3252 and T-1863: line rates after edits (bug-d, 2026-11-16)

Both fixed in sim/src/track/world.rs. Repro harness (outside the repo): real Tokyo track
(savehang/tyo_real.track), the T-3238 fuzz with PROBE_LINE=99.

## T-3252: line 99 loses rate on every edit

Fuzz seed 11 reproduces it: line 99 at the low level 0.95/h falls to 0.0088/h over about 50 edits (pace
at the low level 6,300 s growing to 340,000 s). Cause in `pace_merges`: the even share 3600 k / sum(tph)
reads rates the pacing already lowered, and a count line left at its own headway (or cut by the full
track) runs less than 3600/h, so the share grows every settle. Fix: the even headway e is solved from
sum over the count lines of min(3600/max(h, e), the line's rate now if it is not paced) = sum of their
rates now. A line not paced yet counts at what it runs; the share is then the same on every edit. Seed 11
now stays at 0.719/h (what the full track carries); seeds 15 and 16 stable.

## T-1863: settle moves lines that share no track

Native: a no-op train-count edit on line 0 or 9 of the real Tokyo save changed lines 26, 27, 28, 99, 103,
105 (all within 1e-4 trains of their fleets but not at it; the settle steps every unconverged line of a
level the edit makes active). Fix: an edit's settle steps only lines that share a capacity resource,
directly or through other lines, with a line the edit touched, rebuilt, recomputed or shared
(`TrackWorld::settle_scope`, `sharing_closure`). Loads and bulk settles are unchanged. After: 0 lines
changed for edits on lines 0, 9, 18, 36...; edits on 27 and 99 change only 27/28 and 26/99/103/105,
the lines on the same track.
