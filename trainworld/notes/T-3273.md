# T-3273: a parent's count shared over its campuses in the world

Agent parentsplit, 2026-11-12. Pipeline only: `pipeline/campus_pools.py` (new), `tier0.py`
(`university_sizes`, `institution_of`, `not_campus`, `wikidata_labels`, NOT_CAMPUS split into
OPEN_UNIVERSITY + the rest), `wikidata_prefill.py`, tests `pipeline/test_campus_pools.py`. Nothing in
data/packs or data/work/res9 written; the light pass on the changed packs is the orchestrator's.
Rule: docs/demand-worldwide.md "Universities", DECISIONS 2026-11-12 (T-3273).

## What was wrong

T-3216's parent rule split a parent's count only over its campuses inside one pack, so a parent
with campuses in several packs placed its whole count in each, and a branch whose parent is a
system took the system's count. Over the 3,919 light-pass packs, reproduced on cached inputs: 168
institutions placed past their own world count, 4.8M students beyond it (the Texas A&M System's
122,247 on Galveston and three more packs, Indiana University's on IU Indianapolis, Q739627 in 10
packs).

## The rule now

1. The world table (`campus_pools.py build`, `data/raw/tier0/campus/pools.json`, about a minute):
   every university and college in the campus bands, its institution (own count, else smallest
   counted parent, else a counted institution whose Wikidata label its name holds), each
   institution's count less its counted members' (parents of counted ids are now cached too), and
   each campus's students (`allot`: by area, every campus but the main one capped at the curve
   1,872 x ha^0.31, the rest on the main; main = its own tagged site, else its biggest).
2. A pack takes, per institution, the count less what the table gives its campuses outside the
   pack, split over its sites the same way, then T-3226's cut and caps as before.
3. Name joins: labels of two words and 12 characters, or 4 CJK characters, at word boundaries, held
   by one institution only, and held by the names of fewer than 2 other Wikidata ids that aren't its
   members (drops "Fachhochschule", 国際大学, École polytechnique, and "Texas A&M University", which
   its sister universities' names hold); the site within 500 km of the institution's tagged sites.
   2,675 sites worldwide join this way (sampled 120: nearly all right; a wrong one is a different
   institution with a near name, e.g. Université d'Alger 3 joins Alger's).
4. Left out: open, distance, online universities and study centres by name in the main languages
   and short names (IGNOU, NOUN, AIOU, UNED, UPBJJ, 空中大學, 开放大学, 广播电视大学, 放送大学,
   방송통신대, المفتوحة, پیام نور, Fernhochschule, ...); every site of an institution whose Wikidata
   label matches that (13 institutions: IGNOU, NOUN, Universitas Terbuka, FernUni Hagen, UOC, AIOU,
   Arab Open University, Open University of Tanzania, ...; P31 doesn't mark them, NOUN is a plain
   "university"); university farms and forests (實驗林, 林場, 農場, "University Farm", arboretum).
5. Item 3 of the brief (Quemoy's Jinsha campus) is the name join: it joins 國立金門大學's count.
