## Build (ttzoom, 2026-11-19; tree tw/ttzoom, not landed)

**What changed** (app only; no sim, no saves):
- `ui/inspector/TrainTable.tsx`: the wheel zooms the table and chart about the pointer (1.25x a 100 px notch, one
  step a frame), from the whole day across to 15 minutes; over the station names it scrolls the rows; sideways
  wheel, Shift-wheel and a mouse drag pan (touch pans natively; no pinch). Opening zoom: see "After the second
  advisor call". Columns stand exactly at their train's first time (the old push is gone).
  `pickLevels` picks the drawn columns per zoom level (`GAPS` = 10 s x 1.41^n): each level keeps the coarser level's
  set and fills gaps left to right, so the set depends only on the zoom, never the scroll, and zooming in only adds.
  Every bullet is drawn in the view (a rimmed Chip, `Chip rim`), the drawn columns' bullets after them (in front).
  Hover: the head row picks the bullet with the nearest centre within 9 px (`onHeadMove`); `Lit` draws the veil, the
  bullet, the column and bg covers (`.tt-cover`) over drawn columns within a column's width. Per frame only box
  positions change: each column's cells (`Cells`) and each line's bullet are one cached vnode reused, so Preact bails
  at them. The zoom sets scale and scroll in one render and the scroll is set, never read back (`setX`).
- `ui/inspector/MareyChart.tsx`: only `MareyBody` remains (the dock's `MareyPanel`, `MareyButton`, `mareyOpen`, its
  own zoom and `defaultSpan` are gone). It takes `win` (the table's day; the same `lineTrips` question as the table,
  so one answer) and `view` (`t0`, `k`). Trains are drawn on a canvas: each profile a `Path2D` in trip seconds,
  placed with `addPath(path, DOMMatrix([k,0,0,1,x,0]))` and stroked in batches per look; only trips in view. Hover by
  `isPointInStroke` (9 px) in draw order (own front, then pass dots, then the rest); the tag through the canvas's
  `data-tip` (tip.ts watches it). Ticks, labels, veil and now line stay SVG. Dark line colours are mixed toward white
  in JS (`inkOf`), as the old CSS did.
- `SchedulePanel.tsx` unchanged; the other lines toggle is restyled in CSS (T-3819: 28 px, right after the switch).
  `LineInspector.tsx` (button gone), `Dock.tsx` (panel gone), `widgets.tsx` (`Chip rim`, `BulletRim`), `style.css`.
- Checks updated: `t3346-schedule-panel.mjs` reads the chart's runs from `window.__mareyRuns` (debug) instead of
  `use.run`; `t3543-default-headways.mjs` wants the schedule button alone by the heading.

**The Marey question.** The schedule panel already drew the same `MareyBody` under the timetable, with the paths,
other lines, flat junction rules, pass dots and hovers, so nothing the chart showed is lost. What goes is the
dock panel itself: a chart over the map, beside the dock, that followed the selected line as the game ran
(non-modal). Removed as her words said.

**For T-3818 (related lines).** `Src.rank` and `Col.rank` exist (0 for the axis line and a group's lines). Give a
related line's sources rank 1 in `sourcesOf`; `pickLevels` already fills rank 1 only in the gaps rank 0 leaves at
each level (and drops a coarser rank-1 column that crowds a rank-0 one), so a related line never takes the line's
place. Bullet offset (2 px lower) and time opacity (60%, less when deprioritized) hang off `c.rank` in the bullet
`style` and `Column` class. The chart already draws other lines (`otherDrawn`).

**After the second advisor call**: the opening zoom is the coarsest level drawing at least 95% of the day's
columns (no closer than an hour across); the old `scaleOf` opening drew about half (143 of 291 on the L) once
columns stopped being pushed. A drag let go with the button up elsewhere is dropped (`onMove` checks `buttons`), a
press on the scrollbars scrolls natively, the table and chart don't select text, and the panel's click-outside close
needs the press to start outside (a drag let go over the backdrop closed the panel). The chart draws each trip
from its phases' starts alone below 0.05 px a second. The other lines toggle's pressed tint now shows.
Probed (play.mjs, scratch probe2.js): a 200 px drag pans 222 px at UI scale 0.9 and 167 px at 1.2 (CSS px), no
selection, no stuck drag after a release outside; moving over a chart path lights its column and sets the tag
("Leaves 14 St/8 Av 07:55 / Reaches Canarsie-Rockaway Pkwy 08:29"), at UI scale 1.2 too.

**Performance** (headless Chrome, `?debug`, NYC real save's L, 291 departures a way, after its trips were
calculated, 3-8 of 14 cores claimed by others):
- Zoom sweeps (25 notches 30 ms apart, out and in): frames median 16.7 ms, p90 33-50 ms, p99 86-131 ms. Not smooth
  at every frame: about half the zoom frames run long (long tasks ~90 ms each, mostly compositor commit and
  layerization of the changed table and chart, and GPU raster).
- Our own work per zoom step (wheel frame through render, canvas and table layout, `window.__zoomMs`): median 6-15
  ms, p90 16-19 ms. Canvas draw alone: median 0.8 ms.
- Comparators in the same session: a hover sweep across the columns (the same area repainted, no zoom): median
  16.7, p90 17.1, p99 38 ms; panning by wheel: p90 17-18 ms. So the zoom frame itself is the slow part, not the
  environment.
- On the 2,647-station save (notes/T-3801.md, Line 1a, 264 a way, a shared line and pass dots), our step median
  7-20 ms; its frames are dominated by the page-wide layerization (~25 ms a painted frame there whatever the table
  shows) and the game's own work while trips calculate.
- No `lineTrips` (or any clock) message is posted while zooming or panning (counted at `Worker.postMessage`).
- What was tried: SVG trip layer replaced by the canvas (the biggest win, about 35-50 ms a step), one render per
  zoom step with the scroll set not read back, cached cell and bullet vnodes, culling to 1.5 views, coarse paths
  zoomed out. Not tried: rendering the bullet row to a canvas, stretching a composited snapshot during a gesture.
- Scratch tools: scratchpad ttzoom/ (open.js, open2.js, zoom.js, modes.sh, trace3.js, probe2.js).
