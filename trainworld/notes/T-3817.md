## Build (ttzoom, 2026-11-19; tree tw/ttzoom, not landed)

**What changed** (app only; no sim, no saves):
- `ui/inspector/TrainTable.tsx`: the wheel zooms the table and chart about the pointer (1.25x a 100 px notch, one
  step a frame), from the whole day across to 15 minutes; over the station names it scrolls the rows; sideways
  wheel, Shift-wheel and a mouse drag pan (touch pans natively; no pinch). Opening zoom: see "After the second
  advisor call". Columns stand exactly at their train's first time (the old push is gone).
  `pickLevels` picks the drawn columns per zoom level (`GAPS` = 10 s x 1.41^n): each level keeps the coarser level's
  set and fills gaps left to right, so the set depends only on the zoom, never the scroll, and zooming in only adds.
  Every bullet is drawn in the view (a rimmed Chip, `Chip rim`), the drawn columns' bullets after them (in front).
  Hover: the head row picks the bullet with the nearest centre within 9 px (`onHeadMove`); `Lit` draws the veil, the
  bullet, the column and bg covers (`.tt-cover`) over drawn columns within a column's width. Per frame only box
  positions change: each column's cells (`Cells`) and each line's bullet are one cached vnode reused, so Preact bails
  at them. The zoom sets scale and scroll in one render and the scroll is set, never read back (`setX`).
- `ui/inspector/MareyChart.tsx`: only `MareyBody` remains (the dock's `MareyPanel`, `MareyButton`, `mareyOpen`, its
  own zoom and `defaultSpan` are gone). It takes `win` (the table's day; the same `lineTrips` question as the table,
  so one answer) and `view` (`t0`, `k`). Trains are drawn on a canvas: each profile a `Path2D` in trip seconds,
  placed with `addPath(path, DOMMatrix([k,0,0,1,x,0]))` and stroked in batches per look; only trips in view. Hover by
  `isPointInStroke` (9 px) in draw order (own front, then pass dots, then the rest); the tag through the canvas's
  `data-tip` (tip.ts watches it). Ticks, labels, veil and now line stay SVG. Dark line colours are mixed toward white
  in JS (`inkOf`), as the old CSS did.
- `SchedulePanel.tsx` unchanged; the other lines toggle is restyled in CSS (T-3819: 28 px, right after the switch).
  `LineInspector.tsx` (button gone), `Dock.tsx` (panel gone), `widgets.tsx` (`Chip rim`, `BulletRim`), `style.css`.
- Checks updated: `t3346-schedule-panel.mjs` reads the chart's runs from `window.__mareyRuns` (debug) instead of
  `use.run`; `t3543-default-headways.mjs` wants the schedule button alone by the heading.

**The Marey question.** The schedule panel already drew the same `MareyBody` under the timetable, with the paths,
other lines, flat junction rules, pass dots and hovers, so nothing the chart showed is lost. What goes is the

each level (and drops a coarser rank-1 column that crowds a rank-0 one), so a related line never takes the line's
place. Bullet offset (2 px lower) and time opacity (60%, less when deprioritized) hang off `c.rank` in the bullet
`style` and `Column` class. The chart already draws other lines (`otherDrawn`).

**After the second advisor call**: the opening zoom is the coarsest level drawing at least 95% of the day's
columns (no closer than an hour across); the old `scaleOf` opening drew about half (143 of 291 on the L) once

**Performance** (headless Chrome, `?debug`, NYC real save's L, 291 departures a way, after its trips were
calculated, 3-8 of 14 cores claimed by others):
- Zoom sweeps (25 notches 30 ms apart, out and in): frames median 16.7 ms, p90 33-50 ms, p99 86-131 ms. Not smooth

- Our own work per zoom step (wheel frame through render, canvas and table layout, `window.__zoomMs`): median 6-15
  ms, p90 16-19 ms. Canvas draw alone: median 0.8 ms.
- Comparators in the same session: a hover sweep across the columns (the same area repainted, no zoom): median

  environment.
- On the 2,647-station save (notes/T-3801.md, Line 1a, 264 a way, a shared line and pass dots), our step median
  7-20 ms; its frames are dominated by the page-wide layerization (~25 ms a painted frame there whatever the table
  shows) and the game's own work while trips calculate.
- No `lineTrips` (or any clock) message is posted while zooming or panning (counted at `Worker.postMessage`).
- What was tried: SVG trip layer replaced by the canvas (the biggest win, about 35-50 ms a step), one render per
  zoom step with the scroll set not read back, cached cell and bullet vnodes, culling to 1.5 views, coarse paths

## Related lines (ttrelated, T-3818)

Tree `tw/ttrelated`, not landed; app only (no sim, no saves). Advisor called once before building (it caught the
station-id problem's consequences: toggle hidden, chart empty, opening zoom and table blanking; all handled below).

**What "related" means.** Three candidates were looked at on the real NYC save:
- Lines sharing two stations by id (the group table's old rule): none at all here, since express and local stop at
  different stations of one complex (the 2 and the 3 share 0 station ids, 22 complexes).
- Lines sharing two complexes: far too many (the 2 meets 12 lines, the R 22), mostly lines crossing it twice.
- The line panel's related lines, `lineRelated` groups of kind beside (T-1168): the 2 gets 1, 3, 4, 5; the W gets N,
  Q, R; the 7 gets 7X and the S. Used this. Not its own quad twin, not a group's own lines (they stay rank 0).
- The chart's other lines (shared track, `sharedStretches`) and grade passes: none on this save (every line has its
  own track), so the toggle never showed on a line here before; it now shows whenever the line has related lines.
- "Deprioritized": no player setting dims a line (grepped ui.ts), so it is read as the table's existing fades: the
  past fade (0.5) gives 0.6 x 0.5 = 0.3, and a lit related column keeps its times at 0.6 (background solid: the
  fade is on the time spans, not the column). DECISIONS 2026-11-20.

**What changed:**
- `TrainTable.tsx`: `useRelated(l, st, links, skip)` asks `lineRelated`, keeps beside groups, and per related line
  gives `rowOf` (its stop -> the table line's stop, from meetings where both stop), `fill` (rows inside a beside
  span, the only rows "|" may fill, so a line that leaves and comes back gets no bars between) and `stretches` for
  the chart (stop to stop where both stop, mapped linearly; `Shared.m`). It adds those stretches to `links.shared`
  for lines with no shared track, so the chart draws them as other lines under the toggle. `sourcesOf` gives them
  rank 1 (and now matches a group's lines by `stopKey`, the station's complex, which the group table needed on this
  save too). `useColumns` keeps trips per line, so the toggle asks only for the related lines and the table stays
  up meanwhile. `openingScale` counts rank 0 only, so the opening zoom is the same with or without them;
  `lineKey`/`placedFor` don't include them, so the toggle keeps zoom and scroll. Bullets: related 2 px lower
  (`REL_DROP`), order related back, own back, related drawn, own drawn; related back bullets are kept one per 3 px
  of the day (`REL_THIN`, binned by the day's px so a pan never changes which; 684 -> 253 spans zoomed out on the
  W). Related lines are running ones only, and a related line whose trips don't come back is left out (only the
  line's own must answer before the table shows). Head hover: nearest centre, a related
  one counted 2 px further so the line's own win a tie; the head's `data-tip` names another line's bullet where
  its name differs from its letters (LIRR "Port Washington Branch"; NYC "W" gets none, as the tag would repeat the
  bullet). HEAD 2 px taller while related lines show.
- `SchedulePanel.tsx`: line and group bodies call `useRelated`; `any` includes related lines.
- `MareyChart.tsx`/`mareyShare.ts`: a stretch maps by `m` when it has one.
- `style.css`: `.tt-col.rel span { opacity: 0.6 }`.
- `docs/ui-panels.md` Schedule panel: a "Related lines" bullet; the group's complex matching.

**Performance** (headless, `?debug`, W, 25-notch sweeps, alternating toggle on/off twice, ledger full at 14 of 14):
- Zooming in: frames median 16.7, p90 16.8 both ways; our step median 6-9 ms on, 3 off.
- Zooming out to the whole day: frames median 16.7 both ways; p90 33 ms on against 17 off, p99 67 against 33; our
  step median 13-15 ms on against 7 off. About half the extra is the chart (render with canvas 4.3 ms against 0.9;
  ~250 more runs), the rest the bullet row (426 spans against 134).
- The F (11 related lines: 234 own columns, 1,697 related a way), the heavy case: zooming in, frames median 16.7
  both ways, p90 17-20 on against 17; step median 10-11 ms against 3. Zooming out to the whole day: frames median
  16.7 both ways, p90 83 ms on against 67 off (the off case is already long there under load), step median 21-23 ms
  against 8 (chart render with canvas 6.6 ms median, p90 18). Thinning related back bullets to one per 3 px took the
  step from about 24 to 22 ms, so most of the rest is the chart's extra runs and the table's re-layout.
- No `lineTrips` posts while zooming. Not tried: a canvas bullet row; a chart layer cached across zoom steps.

**Drag to pan** (Anita 11-20 via the orchestrator: "could we also allow like dragging the marey diagram or the
timetable to pan it?"): already in c68df3ef, and 8800's served bundle (`.tw-snap`, index-BSeqjyDz.js) has the

change. Gaps: a touch swipe pans the table (native scroll) but not the chart (`onDown` skips touch and the chart is
no scroller); at the whole-day zoom there is nothing to pan. Why she didn't find it is not established.

no NYC subway bullet gets one. To always name it, drop `b.name !== b.letter` in `onHeadMove`.

**Strings**: none added or changed. The hover tag shows a line's own name (data, not a new string). The toggle's
"Show other lines" / "Hide other lines" now also covers the table's related lines (unchanged text, reused).
