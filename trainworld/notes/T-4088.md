## The shape of it, and two follow-ups worth a task

- Session scratch is the biggest and fastest grower (78 GB): agents copy trees and cargo targets into scratch, and
  nothing sweeps finished sessions. A daily sweep of session folders untouched for 2 days (her standing rule), with the
  junction check from delete.py, would keep it small. Could live in tools/ops/disk.py as `--sweep` and run from watch.py.
- Each tree is a full checkout of maps/ (1.4 GB, of which trainworld/ is about 0.2 GB). A sparse checkout in
  agent-tree.mjs (trainworld/ plus the root files) would save about 1.2 GB a tree, 10-15 GB with today's 10-14 trees.

## The alert

- `trainworld/tools/ops/disk.py`: levels low under 30 GB free, urgent under 10 GB; `disk.py` prints the line,
  `--notify` chimes (tools/ping.py, her quiet chime) and shows a Windows notification ("C: is getting full", "N GB free.
  Builds fail at 0."). `--warn-gb`/`--urgent-gb` fake the thresholds.
- `board/watch.py` checks every 5 minutes and prints one "disk:" line per level crossing (low, urgent, and "fine again"
  once back 2 GB above 30), chiming and notifying only as it falls. `--disk-warn-gb`/`--disk-urgent-gb` for tests.
- `tools/ops/check_resources.py`: a "home / drive C:" row flagged LOW or URGENT by free space.
- docs/ops-checks.md and docs/orchestrator.md say so.
- Only runs while an orchestrator's watch runs. Outside sessions nothing checks; a Windows scheduled task running
  `disk.py --notify` hourly would cover that (not made: it changes her system).
