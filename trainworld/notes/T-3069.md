# T-3069 Trams that snap to real rail

Folds in T-2929 (trams stage 3's real snap) and open-source issue 9 (snap to light rail such as Kaohsiung's
circular line, which OSM tags light_rail), and T-3495's "Left, with cost" item 1 (trams, monorail, funicular,
narrow gauge and heritage in the graph). Branch tw/trams.

## What changed
- Graph (pipeline/realgraph.py): version 2. The kind byte is the true kind: 0 rail, 1 light rail, 2 subway, 3 tram,
  4 monorail, 5 funicular, 6 narrow gauge, 7 heritage (OSM `preserved`); 8 and up free for freight and abandoned
  (T-3464). Every way of those kinds at rank 0 or 1 is kept, used or not, as T-3495 did for rail. Flags unchanged
  (all eight bits were taken). pipeline/realroute.py (real network saves) keeps to kinds 0-2, so the saves route as before.
- One table, app/src/game/realFollow.ts, says what each track type follows and each kind's family:
  train track: rail and light rail (one family), subway, monorail, funicular, narrow gauge, heritage; never tram.
  Tram track: tram and light rail (one family), rail, subway, funicular, narrow gauge, heritage; never monorail.
  The router (workers/realRoute.worker.ts) drops kinds not followed from candidates, the search and the reach; the
  other-family cost (3x) applies between families. realGraph.ts reads versions 1 and 2 (a version 1 file has only 0
  and 2, which the table reads the same).
- Tram fit: a route asked with mode "tram" is fitted with `trace_kind(line, tol, 3)` in the game's frame at 10 m,
  25 m if that fails, and laid as off-street tram track (realReq already sent tram: true). No Rust change.
- Layer (map/realNetwork.ts): while snapping it draws what the track type follows. Train: All = every kind but tram;
  Passenger unchanged. Tram: All = every kind but monorail; Passenger = passenger track plus every tram line.
- Switches (game/ui.ts, ui/ToolBar.tsx): tram track has its own real snap switch (`snapRealTram`, saved as
  `build.tramSnap`, off by default), first in the tram row before Snap to streets; the two exclude each other, and
  with it on the beside switch shows and is off. With the tram real snap on, Ctrl does not bring the street snap in
  (tools.ts streetSwitch). `snapMode` (null, "rail", "tram") drives the router and the layer.
- Icons (ui/widgets.tsx): one family. The same magnet (two pole tips apart from the body) top left of every snap
  icon, over what it snaps to on one sweep: a line (real network), a road with a centre line (roads), a street
  turning a tight corner (streets, new), two tracks one dashed (beside). Screenshot:
  artifacts/trainworld/t3069_snap_icons.png (rail row above, tram row below).
