# T-4075 Trams drawn off their track

Agent trams, 2026-11-24. Tree .tw-trees/trams.

## Cause (measured)

A train's place along its line comes from `samples`: run 0 of each line resampled every 50 m
(`run_polyline`, `SAMPLE_STEP`), and the train shader's `lineAt` took the straight between two samples. The track
itself is drawn from each edge's pieces at a 5 cm chord tolerance (`render_chainages`). On a curve the straight
between two samples cuts inside the track by up to h^2/8R (50 m on an 18 m tram curve: more than 10 m). Not a
lane shift, lag or offset: the sample points themselves sit on the track (0.08 m), the error is all between them.

Distance from the drawn place (centre line, before the own-track shift) to the line's drawn track, every metre:

| save | before: max, share over 1 m | after: max, share over 1 m |
|---|---|---|
| Nottingham, street-snapped tram with a tight corner (built by the player's path) | 13.0 m, 37% | 0.12 m, 0 |
| Anita's Amsterdam save (t3099), trams + metro | 13.4 m, 6.4% | 0.64 m, 0 (67 places over 0.5 m: street corners at the finest cut) |
| Anita's Amsterdam save, 2 bus lines against their route | 12.5 m, 19% | 0.47 m, 0 |
| real NYC save, 109 lines, 4,499 km | 13.7 m, 1.8% | 0.29 m, 0 |

A per-line finer step was ruled out: every NYC line has some tight curve, so 0.25 m would need 446k samples (5x the
90k), each carrying a dozen per-sample arrays and textures.

## Fix

- `sim/src/track/service/bends.rs` (new): per 50 m interval, if the track at its quarters strays more than `tol`
  (ground m) from the straight, the interval is cut into 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 equal parts (the fewest within `tol` at
  32 fine points) and its inner points returned. Unit tests: a straight has none; an 18 m half turn goes from
  over 10 m off to under 0.25 m; a Mercator-scaled plane keeps ground tolerance; a right-angle corner is cut finest.
  `TrackApi::run_bends` (wasm/service.rs) runs it on `svc.position`, so rail, tram, bus and ferry alike.
- Clock worker (`workers/clockMain.ts`): `run_bends` per line with `run_polyline`, kept per line as offsets from
  the straight (local units, no anchor move changes them); per state `sampleBend` (per sample: first point x 32 +
  count) and `bends` (dx, dy). `ClockRender` (protocol.ts) and `SAME_FIELDS` (clockPost.ts) carry them.
- `map/network.ts`: NetworkBuffers `sampleBend`, `bends`; `bendAt` adds the offset in the CPU `lineAt` (picking,
  follow, tags, the guard). `map/rendererShaders.ts`: the same in the GLSL `lineAt` (POS_GLSL: the train shader and
  the probe). Two textures (`bendAt` R32F, `bends` RG32F), bound on units 5 and 14 in `bindTrainTextures`.
  `map/pick.ts`: line blocks take in the bend points.
- Close up each car now bends along the circle through its own ends and middle, as a whole pill did already: a
  20 m car on an 18 m tram corner was a straight chord 3 m inside the curve (`TRAIN_VS`, `!u_detail` dropped).
- Cost on NYC: 15,212 of 90,134 intervals bent, 24,750 points: 0.36 MB per-sample codes + 0.2 MB points per copy
  (worker, posted copy, main, GPU).

base runs, 96% in the head's; other agents' builds and checks ran meanwhile). The one head tab peak of 2722 MB did not
repeat (2438). Full record: artifacts/trainworld/perfbench/compare-2026-11-241016.json.
