# T-1091: the commute curve flat inside 2 km outside the US (built 2026-10-23)

Option (a) of notes/T-1090.md section 7: f(d) = f(max(d, 2 km)) on every curve but the US one, so
a job in the home square (its own pair 1.04 km) weighs as one in the next square. One rule
(`kernel::flat_near_km`: none on d^-0.8 exp(-d/30), the LODES curve; `FLAT_NEAR_KM` 2 on any
other). The shipped packs on the US curve are exactly the 164 US packs; no other pack has it.

## As built

- `kernel::Curve` {km, pow, flat_km} is the one place the commute decay is evaluated
  (`decay_table`, `decay_tables`, `decay_ftab` take it); `Params::decay_flat_km`,
  `PackGravity::flat_km`, header `gravity.decay_flat_km` (absent: 0, so every pack solved before
  stays as solved). `DemandApi::new` copies it from the pack; `city_gravity` uses shipped factors
  only when it matches, else solves (`kernel::gravity`, same curve).
- `pack_gravity`: `--decay-flat-km D`, default the rule for the curve given. So `build_city.py`,
  `tier0.py`, `coarse_zones.py`, `regions.py` and every other caller take the rule with no change.
  New `--keep-zones`: re-solve a finished pack alone on its own zones (blocks, merged cells, routes
  kept; its curve and border factor unless given), for packs that join nothing. `with_gravity` now
  writes `zone_cells9` back when the gravity carries it (a merged pack re-solved on its zones, or
  joined, keeps its land; before, a write dropped it).
- `joint_gravity`: every solved pack takes the rule (`--flat-km D` for trials), its own table and
  its cross-pack table from it, and writes it; a fixed pack keeps its own.
- Readers: `read_pack.check_gravity` (the gate), `road_speed.py`, `county_flows.py`
  (both removed 2026-10-24, in git history), `notes/t1000lib.py`. `checks/ceiling/t1056_ceiling.rs` updated for the new `decay_tables`.
- With blocks: a block's pairs are the mean over their squares of the flat curve (its own pair
  3/4 at f(2 km), 1/4 at f(2.83 km)). The pull cap is unchanged: measured against the pack's own
  pulls under the same curve (no pack held anything in the runs below).
- Test `kernel::tests::flat_near_end` (squares, blocks, rule, solve, pack round trip,
  `city_gravity` re-solves for a different flat end); `pack::tests::gravity_roundtrip` extended.

## Applying it to built countries (the fix batch)

- **Joined packs** (1,485 of the 1,503 non-US): rerun the country's join (`world_run.py`'s join /
  `intercity.py --solve ... --name ...` or `intercity.py --group auto <ids>`), with this crate. No
  header edit, no rebuild: the join writes `decay_flat_km` 2 into each pack it solves. Same reruns
  as T-1000's.
- **The 18 non-US packs that join nothing** (sao, tor, kyp, mll, eiv, mea, tee, mum, hou, seo, mlt,
  lag, trd, reu, ret, mos, vor, sai; all merged): `pack_gravity data/packs/<id>.json
  data/packs/<id>.json --keep-zones` each (seconds), then `read_pack.py <id>`. Back up first.
- US packs: nothing (the rule gives them none; New York's re-solve is byte-identical).
