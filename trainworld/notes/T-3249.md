# T-3249 satellite map mode: survey (agent sat, 2026-11-11)

Anita, 2026-11-11: "lots of people have requested satellite map mode. is that something we could do? how hard would it
be? just like a base map? ideally something that works well / is not misaligned for china".

Survey only; no app code changed. Scratch test page and screenshot script: scratchpad `sat/index.html`, `sat/shoot.mjs`
(MapLibre 4.7.1, page served by Playwright routing at http://localhost:7799, no server started). Screenshots in
`artifacts/trainworld/t3249/`; the summary one is `for-anita-satellite-free-vs-paid.png`.

## Short answer

Yes, as a base map. Technically small (a raster layer under the vector basemap, ~5-6 h agent time). Alignment in
China is not a problem with any global imagery source: every one tested lines up with OSM, so with our track, in
Shanghai, Beijing and Chengdu. The real constraints are **cost at our traffic** (paid imagery runs thousands of
dollars a month) and **loading inside China** (the cheapest paid source is blocked there).

## Traffic the costs are based on

The brief said "a few hundred players"; the play stats say much more. `shonei-server/tools/trainworld_plays.py
--no-names --days 4` (playtime pings, read 2026-11-12 ~02:30 UTC):

| day | plays | browsers | minutes with a game open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 10 | 39,565 | 21,662 | 931,614 |
| Nov 11 | 37,024 | 14,482 | 1,371,418 (22,860 h) |

Top starts Nov 11: Tokyo 9,230, "map" 5,756, Osaka 2,058, New York 1,832 (Tokyo + Osaka + New York = 35% of plays).

Model (all assumptions, stated so they can be changed):
- 22,860 open hours a day = ~686k a month. Minutes count while a game page is open, idle included; take 40% as hours
  with the map moving = 274k.
- Satellite mode is opt-in: take 20% of that = **55k satellite hours a month**.
- New tiles fetched per satellite hour, after the browser cache (Esri sends max-age 86400, Stadia 21600, EOX 604800):
  a fresh 1920x1080 screen is ~45 tiles of 256 px or ~15 of 512 px; players build in one area and revisit it, so
  take ~35 fresh screens an hour = **1,500 tiles of 256 px (~500 of 512 px)**. Range 500-5,000.
- Central: **82M tiles of 256 px = 27M of 512 px a month today**. Low (10%, 500/h) ~14M; high (30%, 5,000/h) ~410M.
  10x traffic: x10.

Tile weights measured: Esri 256 px 15-21 KB, GSI 17 KB, USGS 29 KB, EOX 4-9 KB, Stadia 512 px 88-128 KB.

## China

- **Alignment**: tested at z15 with OSM roads and rail from OpenFreeMap over the imagery: Esri, Stadia and EOX all
  line up in Shanghai (Shanghai station's platforms), Beijing (Beijing South's oval) and Chengdu East, as well as
  Tokyo and Osaka. No offset. The GCJ-02 shift is only in Chinese providers' maps (Amap, Tencent; Baidu its own
  BD-09) and in Google's road map of China (its imagery is not shifted). Those providers shift their imagery to match
  their roads, so their apps look consistent; ours would look just as consistent, since the game, OSM and the imagery
  are all WGS84. A Chinese player comparing coordinates with Amap would see the usual 300-600 m difference; on the map
  itself nothing is misaligned.
- **Loading in China** (viewdns.info DNS test from 5 mainland cities; DNS only, can't see IP or SNI blocking):
  OpenFreeMap, EOX, MapTiler and data.anita.garden answer correctly; Stadia gets a fake answer (blocked); Mapbox and
  Esri's ibasemaps-api get other CloudFront addresses (probably GeoDNS); server.arcgisonline.com could not be tested;
  Google is blocked by address whatever DNS says. A real check needs a player in China.
- Satellite helps China in particular: OSM is thinner there than Amap, and imagery shows stations and lines OSM lacks.

## How it fits the game

- **Layer**: one raster source and layer in `map/basemap.ts`'s `restyle`, right above `background`; while on, hide
  the land cover, landuse, park, water and building fills, the hillshade and `water_over_shade` (imagery has its own
  relief), keep motorways and main roads as faint white lines (~25-35%) or hide them, hide minor roads. Labels:
  place names light with a dark halo (the no-halo rule, T-1692, can't hold over imagery). Boundaries faint white.
  Raster sources take `bounds`, so a patchwork (GSI over Japan, USGS over the US, Sentinel-2 elsewhere) is three
  sources stacked, each fetching only inside its box.
- **Readability**: dim the imagery a little (`raster-saturation` ~-0.3, `raster-brightness-max` ~0.8) so track and
  stations read first; the Base map slider maps onto that dimming. Our network is drawn on the overlay canvas above
  everything, unchanged. Map ink (unopened dots, real network colours) uses the dark theme's set while satellite is
  on, whatever the theme; panels keep following Light/Dark.
- **Toggle**: Settings > Map, a segmented "Map | Satellite" beside Flat / Globe. A quick button beside the Terrain
  mountain button can come later if players ask.
- **Vector tiles still load**: labels need them, and `busRoads.ts` / `roadFeed.ts` read z14 road tiles for bus
  routes and crossing prices, so satellite adds to the vector traffic rather than replacing it.
- **Performance**: raster tiles are cheap to draw (one textured quad each), cheaper than the vector fills; the cost
  is download (~0.8 MB per fresh screen at 256 px, ~1.6 MB with Stadia's 512 px) and GPU memory for decoded tiles
  (256 KB / 1 MB each). Phones on the lighter map: keep `maxTileCacheSize` modest.
- **Pictures**: save cards, Community thumbnails and Save picture are drawn from the network alone (clock worker,
  `ui/Community.tsx` canvas, the schematic), never from the basemap, so they are unaffected and no imagery terms
  apply to them.
- **Attribution**: each source's credit in the map corner and in Credits.

## Recommendation

1. **Build it on free sources first** (~5-6 h agent time: layer and paint set ~2 h, setting + strings in 13 languages
   ~1 h, the three bounded sources and credits ~1 h, checks and screenshots in Tokyo, New York, Shanghai ~1-2 h).
   GSI for Japan (sharp to z18), USGS NAIP for the US (to z16), EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless 2024 everywhere else,
   China included (aligned, loads there, sharp to ~z13 and soft at z15). Cost $0 a month today and at 10x. Sharp
   where 35% of plays start. Worth an email to EOX about volume (their hosted service states no limits); if they
   object, self-host Sentinel-2 on R2 later (a data run; ~$10-30 a month in reads today, near zero behind the
   Cloudflare cache rule T-3243 proposes).
2. **Sharp imagery everywhere is a money call for Anita.** Cheapest is Stadia: ~$1,500 a month at the central guess
   today, ~$16k at 10x; or its $250 plan with a hard cap (~6M tiles, about 4,000 satellite hours a month) that falls
   back to Sentinel-2 when spent. Stadia is blocked in China, so Chinese players would stay on Sentinel-2. Esri is
   the paid option that might load in China (unverified) and is aligned there, at ~2.5x Stadia's price.
   Adding a paid source on top of (1): +1-2 h (key or domain setup is hers; the budget fallback).
3. Not recommended: Google (blocked in China, most expensive), MapTiler and Mapbox (pricier than Stadia, no China
   gain proven), Esri's keyless endpoint (licence unclear at our size), Tianditu (Chinese developer account, unknown
   quotas), any Chinese provider (shifted and their terms forbid it).
