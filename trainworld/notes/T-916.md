# T-916: a stronger penalty on driving to a station (2026-10-22)

Anita: "almost everyone going to the city center of a largeish city is parking and riding, even
those coming from like >30min drive". Built: the drive to a station counts `ride_drive_f` 2 x
`w_drive`, 2.66 a minute instead of 1.33 (`Params::ride_w`, `kernel::access_options`). Nothing else
changed in the ride's cost.

## Method

A private copy of the crate (session scratchpad) with a bin, `t916`: the real network
(`data/work/realnet/<city>_real_track.json`) on the city's pack, the morning peak solved, then the
trip explorer's own answer (`DemandApi::trip`, commutes) from one home per 4 km square (its most
populous cell, weighted by the square's commuters) to two points in the core (New York: Times
Square and about 1.2 km south east; London: Charing Cross and the City). Each home is placed in a
band by the explorer's drive minutes to the core; rail is split by whether the access entry is a
drive (`PAIR_LEN` slot 12). Variants by env in the copy: the drive's weight, `p_ride`, `d_drop`, and
station parking not capped by the drop-off.

Note the drive minutes are to the core's own cell, through its slow streets: in New York the
15 minute band is Manhattan and the inner Bronx and Brooklyn; 45 minutes is roughly 15-25 km out.

## Before (the model as it was)

Shares of commutes into the core, % (drive the whole way / drive to a station then train / walk
then train / walk the whole way):

| Home's drive to the core | New York | London |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min | 0.5 / 1.0 / 86.2 / 12.3 | 0.2 / 0.0 / 93.5 / 6.3 |
| 30 min | 1.2 / 0.6 / 98.1 / 0.1 | 0.4 / 0.3 / 99.3 / 0.1 |
| 45 min | 1.6 / 17.7 / 80.4 / 0.2 | 1.3 / 15.2 / 83.5 / 0 |
| 60 min | 4.2 / 52.0 / 43.8 / 0 | 1.9 / 37.8 / 60.4 / 0 |
| 65-90 min | 10.2 / 72.7 / 17.1 / 0 | 3.3 / 45.5 / 51.2 / 0 |

So from 60 minutes out about half drive to a station, and from further out most do. Hardly anyone
drives the whole way: the core's parking (up to 50 perceived minutes) settles that, and it is
untouched here. The drives to a station themselves are mostly short: of New York's riders who drive
to a station, 6% drive under 5 minutes, 45% 5-10, 29% 10-15, 16% 15-20, 4% 20-30 (London 11 / 56 /
24 / 8 / 1). Under 1% of the morning's ride riders come from homes beyond 30 minutes' drive from
their station: the access bound (`pair_walk_max` 80) already cut those.

## Variants

Drive to a station then train, % of commutes into the core at 45 / 60 / 65-90 minutes; morning
rail trips in the whole pack; the share of rail riders who drive to their station.

| Variant | New York | NY rail, rides | London | London rail, rides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 17.7 / 52.0 / 72.7 | 1.815M, 16.6% | 15.2 / 37.8 / 45.5 | 1.665M, 9.0% |
| drive 2.0 a minute | 10.4 / 44.7 / 65.5 | 1.787M, 13.2% | 11.1 / 30.6 / 39.1 | 1.657M, 6.7% |
| **drive 2.66 a minute (built)** | 6.4 / 38.0 / 55.5 | 1.763M, 10.5% | 8.0 / 24.7 / 31.9 | 1.651M, 5.0% |
| drive 4.0 a minute | 3.1 / 24.4 / 33.0 | 1.716M, 6.1% | 3.9 / 14.2 / 17.9 | 1.640M, 2.7% |
| start 25 (was 15) | 8.0 / 37.5 / 58.6 | 1.780M, 11.2% | 7.2 / 24.2 / 32.9 | 1.654M, 4.8% |
| start 30 | 5.8 / 33.0 / 52.1 | 1.767M, 9.5% | 5.1 / 19.8 / 28.9 | 1.651M, 3.7% |
| start 40 | 4.1 / 25.7 / 36.2 | 1.740M, 6.9% | 2.8 / 13.7 / 23.2 | 1.644M, 2.4% |
| station parking not capped by the drop-off | 16.4 / 51.9 / 72.7 | 1.815M, 16.5% | as before | as before |
| drop-off 20 (was 10) | the same as uncapped | | as before | |

- **The drop-off and the cap do nothing here.** Suburban station parking is 7 (free, under the
  drop-off's 10); the cap matters only at stations in dense centres, where few drive to.
- **A larger start constant hits short drives hardest**, the opposite of the ask: at 25 the riders
  driving under 5 minutes fall from 6% to 1% of ride riders and those driving 15-30 minutes rise
  from 20% to 26%.
- **A heavier drive keeps the short drives and takes the long ones.** At 2.66: ride riders from
  New York's homes by their drive to the station, about 18k / 136k / 87k / 48k / 12k a morning
  (under 5, 5-10, 10-15, 15-20, 20-30 minutes) become 13k / 89k / 50k / 26k / 6k; London 16k / 84k
  / 36k / 12k / 1.5k become 8k / 51k / 17k / 6k / 0.
- Who stops driving to a station mostly walks to one instead (60 minute band, New York: walk then
  train 44% to 56%); driving the whole way gains a little (4.2% to 6.4%), since the core's parking
  still decides that.
- With the access bound unchanged, a drive of about 15-20 minutes now fills the 80 perceived
  minutes for both ends (a 10 km suburban drive costs about 64), so longer ones go entirely. That
  is the bound acting as a ceiling on the drive; it was about 30 minutes before.

## Checks

- Anita's ceiling tests do not change: A and B give each end a 3 minute walk, no ride (the
  harness in notes/T-854 has no ride term). Last measured A New York 86.0, London 85.6; B 78.8,
  78.5.
- `api::tests::flows_add_up` checked that homes 6.5 km from the test lines reach rail by driving;
  at the new weight that drive passes the access bound, so the test lifts the bound.
