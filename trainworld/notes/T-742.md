# T-742: fares at $0 after importing a save

## Cause (a real game bug, not the check)

The clock ran on the last game's demand after a load. Timeline on 2d4b95c (own Vite 6165,
`?debug=1&new=1&paused=1`, the real save imported about 0.6 s after the demand client appeared, as
t488 does):

| t (s) | what |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | the clock worker's "loaded": `holdForGameLoad` holds the clock and arms the next hold |
| 3.4 | the blank game's solve (v1) answers; its own hold (`{version: 1}`) is done, so `checkHold` calls `loadComplete` and **the clock is released** |
| 3.8 | the save's network reaches `submitNetwork`, which takes the armed hold (`{}`), too late |
| 14 | v2 dispatched; the view shown is still v1's (final, no income) |
| 21 | v2 round 0 published: the first income for the save's lines reaches the clock worker |
| 36 | v2 final; `loadComplete` again (the clock was already running) |

From 3.4 s to 21 s the clock could run with zero income (the worker clears income at every load and
waits for the main thread's), so every hour settled then was booked with running cost and no fares.
t488 waits for `!clock.held`, which was already false, then ran the clock at a game day per ~4 s:
the rest of day 1 and all of day 2 went by before income arrived. A player loading a file or slot
while the previous game's demand is still on its hold (the first seconds after opening the game)
loses the fares for however long that takes, about 20-40 wall seconds of play. A load after the
previous hold ended was not affected. T-867's upkeep and T-872's holiday grouping play no part;
the run during T-867 that had day 2's fares had the import land on the other side of the race.

## Fix

`game/loading.ts` `loadComplete`: does nothing while a whole-game load is armed (`holdArmed`), i.e.
a later game loaded and the demand client has not yet taken its hold. The later game's own final
releases the clock as before. No change to the check: its wait for `!clock.held` is now right.
