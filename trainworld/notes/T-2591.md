# T-2591: land drawn as sea on the rubber sheet (agent njwater, 2026-11-06)

New York's whole view drew a big water area in the top left (and another in the top right) that is land.

## Cause

Two things, both about land the network has no station on:

1. **The rule "a land mass with no station over 2,000 km2 is sea"** (T-2567, made to drop Korea's and China's coast in all
   Japan). In New York the sheet's ground raster has two such masses, cut off from the stations' land by rivers in the
   masks: Pennsylvania past the Delaware (6,037 km2, the top-left water) and eastern Connecticut and Rhode Island past
   the Thames (2,086 km2 in the checks' masks, 5,358 km2 in the page's; the top-right water). Both were drawn as sea.
2. **The page loaded too few masks.** view.ts sent the masks of the zones touching the stations' box, but the sheet
   reads ground out to that box plus its margin (the larger of 8 km and 8% of the diagonal). Past the loaded masks the
   ground's far grid guesses, filling from the nearest known cell, and known cells are mostly water (masks' wide water;
   masked land is not "known" there). In New York the page had no masks east of -71.78, so a 10 km strip of Rhode Island
   was guessed sea. The checks used a 0.3-degree margin and did not see this.

## What changed

- `sheet.ts`: the raster keeps each row's masked spans (`covStart`, `covA`, `covB`); `massCoasts(R)` gives each land
  mass's coast and its **cut**: masked land cells beside unmasked cells the ground calls water, the mask's straight
  edge against guessed sea. `sheetPadM` (the margin, now one function) and `sheetZoneBox` (the stations' box widened by
  it, and a quarter more for the frame's scale).
- `sheetLines.ts`: `cutLand(C)` replaces the 2,000 km2 rule. A land mass with no station is drawn as sea when its cut
  is at least half its width (the square root of its area, `CUT_WIDTH = 0.5`). `info.cutKm2` reports the area dropped.
- `view.ts`: the sheet's zones are those touching `sheetZoneBox(zf.box)`. The flag-off water's zones are unchanged.
- `checks/t2580-sheet-water.mjs`: the page's masks (via `sheetZoneBox`), a `landSea` check, and a `far` case.

## The check (`landSea`)

Samples on a lattice every half of rho over the sheet's raster, rho = max(1 km, 8 raster cells) (1 km New York,
10 km all Japan). A sample counts when it and its 5 x 5 neighbours at rho/2 are all masked and all land or all water
(away from coasts and the masks' edge). Its place is carried through the carried map and looked up in the drawn sea
(the fitted rings, filled on the drawn grid). Land of a mass `cutLand` drops is only counted. Wrong samples are grouped
(4 neighbours on the lattice); fails when the biggest group of land drawn as sea, or sea as land, is over 25 rho^2
(25 km2 New York, 2,600 km2 Japan).

| | land / water samples | biggest land-as-sea group | biggest sea-as-land | cut land drawn as sea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New York, old rule | 119,708 / 86,464 | **3,644 km2 (FAIL)**, 19,223 samples wrong | 1 km2 | |
| New York, now | 119,708 / 86,464 | 2 km2 (62 samples) | 1 km2 | 0 |
| far (New York plus a line to Binghamton), now | 243,473 / 92,534 | 3 km2 | 2 km2 | 0 |
| all Japan, now | 10,228 / 16,980 | 51 km2 (4 samples) | 26 km2 | 569,611 km2 (Korea, China) |

`far`: New York's graph with a line added from its station nearest (-74.9, 40.9) to Binghamton (-75.92, 42.1), a
station every 4 km (43 stations), to stretch the sheet's box well past New York's zones.
