# T-4395: a game load ending after the first round

Agent loadround0, 2026-11-27, tree `loadround0` (master 916e0fbff), Vite 6155. Stage 1 only: the hold is Anita's on
purpose and round 0 sits far from the final on the lines that move most, so nothing was built. No code changed.

## For Anita

Question: should a game load's card go, and its clock start, at the first round of trips (round 0) instead of the last?

- Time: on the real New York save with one solving worker, the card goes 198 s after the file is opened today. At
  round 0 it would go at 101 s: about 49% off the wait, 97 of 198 s. A big save's one-worker load would lose about half
  as well (later rounds run 0.3-0.8 of round 0's time, T-4343).
- What the player would play on meanwhile: round 0's riders are 2.7% high in all (rail trips a day 13.01 M against
  12.67 M), but a line at a time they are far off. Of the 82 lines with 20,000+ riders a day, 44 move more than 5%
  between round 0 and the final and 18 move more than 10%. The most: HBLR (line 96) reads 276,817 at round 0 and
  164,466 at the final (+68%), PATH (34) 432,155 against 302,326 (+43%), MNBN (99) +37%, PATH (33) 125k against 166k
  (-25%), NJCL (83) +17%; the big subway lines D, L and 4 read 6-9% high. Each line's figure then changes again at
  rounds 1 and 2 (PATH 34: 432k, 263k, 282k, 302k).
- Money: fares are paid on whatever figures are shown, so the clock would earn on round 0's (weekday fares 3.3% high
  in all, 44-78% on HBLR, PATH 34 and MNBN). Cash and the Money tab's daily fares and train rides are saved. Over New
  York's 97 s that is about 0.5% of one day's fares extra at 4x, 2% at 16x and 11% at 100x (the slider's top, where the
  97 s are almost 7 game days), so the cash is barely off; the per-line charts are visibly off on those lines for the
  days it covers.
- Not affected: stars and goals (only final figures award them), the stored result that makes a reload instant (only
  a final is stored), and an edit's figures (an edit already keeps the last final until its own final).

Recommendation: keep the hold. The figures that would be in play are 17-68% off on the lines that move most (PATH,
HBLR, MNBN, NJCL) and 6-9% on the busiest subway lines, and the calls since T-172 (T-347, T-421, T-2560, and T-232's
"only final results replace final results" after an edit) chose settled figures over earlier ones. If she wants the time back anyway, the version I would
build ends the card at round 0, keeps the clock at 4x or slower until the final (the existing cap and its "Calculating
trips here…" tag), and is about 4-6 h.

## Why a game load holds for the last round

- T-172 (2026-10-12) had the clock wait only for the first estimate (round 0).
- T-347 (2026-10-14, Anita) reversed it: keep the loading card and the held clock until the fresh solve's final round.
  Her reason then: "we're doing it and it's still loading demand and like lagging / low fps / unresponsive". The
  entry said to revisit when T-348 (the lag) landed. `HOLD_ROUND = ROUNDS` in workers/demandClient.ts is that rule.
- T-421 (2026-10-15) let the card go at the zone in view's final instead of the whole solve's.
- T-2560 (2026-11-05, the current DECISIONS entry, marked Anita): "A loaded game holds its card and clock until the
  zone in view's final solve (Anita, T-421), not the first estimate (T-172)." Only a stored result of that very
  network releases it early, because those figures are the final (notes/T-2560.md).

So after T-348 fixed the lag the hold was kept twice. Nothing written down gives a newer reason, but the one that
follows from the code is the one measured below: figures before the final are not the final.

## What reads the figures between round 0 and the final

- Shown already, behind the card: the card takes no clicks, so a player can open a line under it today and see each
  round's figures land (`showEstimates` on a game load's solve).
- Fares: `incomeByDay` (game/money.ts) is worked out from each shown view and posted to the clock worker
  (clockClient.ts "income"), which pays it hour by hour while the clock runs: cash and the ledgers (fares, costs, net,
  train rides; every day and the last 48 hours) are in the save.
- Riders for the boarding account (clockClient.ts "riders"): each train's load and each platform's crowd; the trains
  would run full or empty on round 0's riders until the next round.
- Not: stars and goals (goalRules.ts skips any view that is not final), the demand cache (written at a final only,
  `writeCache`), the income kept for lines being worked out again (`linesPending`, edits only).
- After an edit the last final stays shown until the new final (T-232). A load has no last final, so "refine in the
  background as after an edit" would in fact show each round in turn.
