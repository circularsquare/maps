## Result: same answers, search 10% faster, solve about 2%; 15% not reached

Built (`sim/src/demand/kernel/search.rs` only, uncommitted):

- `settle_order` places a node at once when nothing it sends riders to is left to place: between walks, a
  station or change node no line joined late (it sends riders only to nodes settled before it); anywhere, a
  route node whose next is placed. `late_at` gives each node its first entry in `late`, so the walk no longer
  binary-searches `late` for every departure, and next nodes already placed are not pushed. The order comes out
  the same node for node. `enter` calls 4.54 million -> 1.21 million per all-destinations search; the walk 252
  -> about 100 ms.
- `late_fix` skips the recompute at a node made before whose late lines were all left out on a loop (74k of
  505k); `drop_late` lowers a change node's `n` so it stays right.

## Why 15% is out of reach without a change in answers

**The floor** (`floor.sh`, interleaved): with no late lines at all (scratch toggle `T4054_NOLATE`) the solve is
49.6, 50.7 s (+23% over before T-4041; peak 418.2 MB), against 57.9, 57.8 s with them (the same instrumented
build). So late joins are about 7.7 s of the 17 s (19 of the 42 points), and even free late joins leave +23%.
T-4041's notes put the rule without late lines at +13%; the cause of that gap is not established. Roughly, the
solve moves about 14 ms per ms of the all-destinations search; 15% (about 46.9 s) would be a search of about
870 ms (the 435 ms before T-4041 was never measured on this machine: T-4041's ratio).

Where the 1,504 ms go (instrumented runs; timers cost about 10%):

  and shares about 240 (431k recomputes, 333k of them pools of 3+ lines), `Shares::finish`'s resort 50.
- Pools of 3+ lines about 600 ms (measured by calling each twice, `T4054_DOUBLE`): 1.41 million calls, 553k at
  joins, 342k at settle, 333k late recomputes, 184k in `entry_costs`; spread evenly over sizes 3 to 14. Split on
  1.41 million dumped pools (`T4054_DUMP`, returning early): setup and its divisions 13%, sorts 8%, the

- The rest of the queue (lists, `note_shares`, entry costs) roughly 270 ms over before T-4041.
