## Shifting speedups (Anita 11-23: "yeah! we can land both")

Saving is of the all-destinations search's cycles (median of 3 interleaved rounds); shift on the real save
(native `demand_mem`, every population and period).

1. **No late joins at change nodes** (built). A line offered after a change node left the queue no longer
   joins it (stations keep theirs). Search -9 to -11.5% (`late_fix` -37%), about 3-4% of the solve. Rail
   trips 13,138,238 -> 13,138,512 (+0.002%); 0.075% of boardings move; most: NJT Newark Light Rail patterns
   +3.3%, +2.8%, -1.2%, MNBN +1.8%, NEC +1.3%. Worst shape: a feeder meets a trunk at a change station; on the
   trunk an express every 20 min and a local every 5 min reach the same stop, the local's ride longer than
   the express's by more than half the express headway and less than all of it (express 10 min, local 22).
   Riders who changed there all wait for the express; those who start there still split.
2. **Pool integrals on at most two Gauss points** (built; were up to six). Exact up to three lines that can be
   first, nearly for more. Search -7 to -8%, about 2.5% of the solve. Rail trips +641 (+0.005%); 0.056% of
   boardings move; most: LIRR West Hempstead +0.9%, Long Beach +0.8%, Port Jefferson +0.6%. Worst shape: one
   platform with 4 or more lines to the same stop, similar rides, mixed headways (5/10/15/30 min).
   - 2b, at most three points: search -3.5%, 37 boardings move in all. Superseded by 2.
3. Both together (the tree): loads hash b50feb79be67f500, rail trips 13,139,169 (+931, +0.007%); 0.116% of
   boardings move; most: NLR +3.2%, +2.8%, -1.2%, MNBN +1.9%, MNBNP +1.7%, NEC +1.5%.

Measured and dropped: pools capped at 4 lines (boardings -1.1%, single lines -16 to -28%) or 3 (-2.0%,
up to -44%); joins costed on 1 or 2 points with the exact cost at the settle (search only -2 to -3%).
