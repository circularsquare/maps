### Step 1: entry_costs (built, same answers)

`entry_costs` is for inter-city trips (`Search::entry`, `EntryDeps`): per station, the pool of every line that can be first at the planned waits, kept only where it costs less than the station's plain cost by more than 1e-4. Where every line at a station has a headway of 15 minutes or less (planned spread = plain spread) and none is left behind (`rn_span` 0), the planned pool is the plain pool: never kept. The station list where that does not hold is made once per graph (`Search::entry_st`; 58% of the stations visited per destination are off it on the real NYC network), and a station on it whose offered lines all have equal spreads is skipped too. Test builds check every skipped station against the full computation (`check_not_kept`): both suites pass with `TW_T2652_CHECK=1` (733 + 962), and over all 873 destinations of period 0 no skipped station would have been kept.
(Computing the cost first and the shares only where kept was tried and dropped: 294,123 of the 308,073 stations left are kept, so it only added work.)
