# T-3730 local trip panel listing no route (localroute, 2026-11-18)

Checked on the real save (T-007-real-nyc.save), the trip explorer's `askTrip` on random point pairs (500 m to 25 km
apart, commutes and three everyday purposes), 3,000 pairs, 11,429 periods with station pairs: every one lists a route
(0 cases of "No route." with rail riders). The most station pairs seen was 66; the case needs about 160 pairs for the
biggest to sit just over 0.5%, which a local trip's cells do not reach. So no player case found.

Change anyway, it is small: `trip.rs` (local) now uses `pick_routes` from `intercity/market_trip.rs` (made
pub(crate)), so a trip whose biggest pair is under 0.5% or whose routes split under the bar keeps its busiest
pair's biggest route. Local `PAIR_MIN`/`ROUTE_MIN`/`MAX_ROUTES` removed (the shared ones have the same values).

Proof: a read-only query, nothing in the solve changed. Real save after the change: trips/day 830,740,105, rail
12,538,045, per-line trips identical; a 407-pair sweep's pair counts and routes (hashed) identical to before.
Test `demand::trip::tests::rail_riders_always_list_a_route`; `agent_cargo.py test` 644 and 841 pass; smoke passed.
No changelog item (no real case found).
