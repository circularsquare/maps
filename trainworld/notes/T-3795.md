# T-3795: schedule window periods row layout and lag (agent periodsrow, tree tw/periodsrow, 2026-11-19)

Anita, 2026-11-19 playtest: "shouldn't 'set for this line' and 'periods 3/4/5' be on the same line? and on hover of
'set for this line' lets say 'By default, periods are set globally. Click to set just for this line' also 'same as
game' should be on same line as 'This line...' also it feels quite laggy to set the number of periods, takes a while
to respond."

## What changed

- Layout (`ui/inspector/SchedulePanel.tsx` `Periods`, `ui/style.css` `.sp-periods`): the grid has three columns,
  label | 3/4/5 | button. "Set for this line" sits at the end of the Periods row; once the line has its own, the
  "This line" row carries "Same as game" at its end. Nothing else in the window moves.
- Hover on "Set for this line": "By default, periods are set globally. Click to set just for this line" (her words,
  new key `schedulePanel.setForLineTip`; other locales fall back to English, as they already do for this panel).
- Lag (`workers/clock.worker.ts`, the edit handler): a `setPeriods` edit (the game's or a line's) sends a rows-only
  state (`sendRows`, T-2766's) right after the sim applies it. The full state, trips and boarding follow as before,
  and the edit's answer still comes after the full state. The follow-up is not held (no stop-click hold).
  `setHours` has the same follow-up but was left out: not measured, and the hours box shows what was typed anyway.
- docs/ui-panels.md "Schedule panel": where the two buttons sit, the hover, and that rows show first.

## Where the time went (before), New York real save, 109 lines, 3 cores, paused clock

A game period click, settled (demand final before each click), click to the window showing the new rows:
1403 and 1326 ms. Per line ("This line" 3/4/5): 1744 and 1778 ms. Back to back (2.3 s apart): 1565, 2443,
4614, 2743, 4387 ms.

Split of a clean click (worker timings, temporary probe since removed):
- sim apply (`set_game_periods`, Rust): about 260 ms
- the boarding account redone for every line, 109 steps of `boarding_step(3)`: about 500 ms of work, plus about
  500 ms of gaps between the steps (each step is its own `setTimeout(0)` task, clamped to about 4.6 ms)
- full state, trips, boarding hour: about 100 ms; then the page holds the state for its trips (T-648)

The window needs only the rows, so everything after apply was waiting on work it doesn't use.
Why the rows go first and nothing breaks: the rows state (`state.rows`) is T-2766's, already used by stop clicks:
the page takes the world at once and leaves the renderer's network and the demand layers for the full state
(`worldIsRows`). The rows state and the full state have their own versions, so the window's reads (`linePeriods`,
`lineHeld`) ask again after the full one. Those reads also make the worker run the follow-up at once (any message
that is not a preview does), so the boarding steps' gaps go too.

## After

Same save. Each click after `wait.final()` (the probe's own "settled" test let some clicks start while demand was
still calculating trips, which is where the slow ones below came from): 388, 404, 427, 292 ms, sim apply 217-301 ms.
Earlier series, some clicks during a recalculation: game 416, 621, 443 ms; per line 413, 847 ms; back to back with
the clock running 836, 1018, 400, 382, 649 ms (apply 254-860 ms). In every case the rows state lands at sim apply +
30 to 120 ms. The full state now lands 50-150 ms after the rows; the answer 0.7-1.9 s after the click. Ledger: 9 of
14 cores claimed for the before runs, 10-13 for the after ones.

Frame by frame after a click (scratch `probe_frames.js`): the table changes once, from the old rows straight to the
new rows with their final trains and headways; the dots stay up throughout (the crowd read is by period, not by row,
so an older answer is never indexed against the wrong row count).

Fit (`probe_fit.js`, `.sp-periods` scrollWidth against clientWidth): no overflow at 1400 px, at 390 px, and in the
narrow 360 px panel (a rail window given the bus window's `narrow` class; the real save has no bus line). The
button keeps at least 32 px from the 3/4/5 in all three.

Undo and redo of a period change work (rows and periods back each time) but are not on the rows-first path: 2.9-3.5 s
back to back on the real save.

Small game (new New York, two short lines): before 17, 55, 28 ms; after 34, 10, 10 ms.

Rows and trains stay right: on the small game 3 -> 4 -> 5 -> 3, each line's trains per row came out [4,4,3] ->
[4,4,4,3] -> [4,4,4,4,3] -> [4,4,3], and the only edits the page sent were the three setPeriods. The window's
table matched (scratch `probe_rows.js`). Save and undo are unchanged (the edit itself is the same op).
