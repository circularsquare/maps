# T-4548: timed partners move with a moved train (stage 6 of 18, T-4511)

Agent shift6, 2026-11-28, tree shift6. Sim only; nothing a player sees yet (the app's arrows are stage 14), no save change, no strings, no changelog item.
A game with no moves runs as before: the only new work in `retime` is writing down pairs.

## What is built
- **Pairs, written by retime** (`moves::Pair { timed, other }`, `Train { line, run, lev, place }`; `Service::pairs`, by the line whose train is timed;
  dropped with the line's variants in `timing::clear`, so a cluster that stands keeps its pairs). A train is named by its place in the day
  (`Dep`'s level and place), which no move, rate or retime changes.
  - `following` and `lead_in` (`note_follow`): a faster train that `follow_one` gave a stand or loss is paired with the slower train ahead of it
    (the `slow` entry it was timed behind).
  - `passing_waits` (`note_waits`): a slower train that waits at a station is paired with the faster train whose pass set the time it leaves
    (`train_waits`' last raising pass; the earlier ones are behind it).
  - Weekday only (`NO_PLACE` trains are left out): weekends are T-4549.
- **Partners** (`Service::partners(train)`): the other side of every pair the train is in, where the timed side still has a variant at its place
  (`floor` may have dropped it). Direct only: the express of two locals pairs with each; a move of one local takes the express and not the other local.
  `train_at` (key to place) and `place_key` (place to key) turn a train into its place and back, so a partner is found on the day of the anchor.
- **The step** (`TrackWorld::move_op`, move_train.rs `group`): the train and its partners each get their `move_range`; the step is the tightest of the
  group's, in seconds more than each is moved now (so a partner already moved on its own keeps its difference). Partners are stored as `With` moves on their
  lines (a partner that already had an `Own` move keeps `Own`); one line is the same `Op::MoveTrain` as before, several are an `Op::Batch` of them, undone as
  one. A refusal names the tightest member's reason. Back at 0 on the clicked train takes each partner back by the same seconds, so a partner moved only
  with it goes back to 0 (Reset is that step; "Reset N moved trains" is stage 14). `move_holds` already holds rows with `With` moves.
- **A bug found on the way** (stage 3): `line_occ` (rule 2's gap from the line's own trains) counted a following loss's users, whose `occ` is where the stretch
  ends (an offset, 1,000 s or more), so any line timed behind another (an express) had a gap of that and no room to move. It skips following losses now.
  Before this no express on the 180 km test line could move at 10 min headway.

## Tests (move_train/tests.rs, 2 new)
- an express with nowhere to pass, timed behind its local (30/20 min): every local with a partner (9) moves its express by the same seconds in one edit,
  the express keeps its waits to within 1 s, undone to the bit; walking to the end stops at the group's tightest range with the express always at the
  local's seconds; a step back to 0 puts both lines' moves away and the timetables back to the bit.
- passing tracks at B to F (10/10): an express two locals wait for; moving the first local moves that express only (the local line has one move, its own);
  the express's moves are all `With` at the local's seconds.

## Limits and lies
- A partner moved later has that much less stand at its far end, and retime fits its waits into what is left (T-4546). Where its waits already fill its
  room (the test line at 30/10: the express's loss is at its cap), the partner keeps its place behind the local but runs 30 s less wait; at 30/20 and
  10/10 it keeps them to the second. Not made a refusal: the group's range is on each slot's own timetable, as T-4511 says.
- Pairs are written for the weekday reference day only; a weekend train has none (T-4549).
- Pairs cost a few words per timed train in memory (a pair is 24 bytes); none on a network with nothing timed.
