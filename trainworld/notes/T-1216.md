# T-1216: a bubble click in a new Tokyo game

Measured 2026-10-24 on a dev server (6151), headless Chrome (Playwright, own profile), 3 solving
workers and the aux worker, the machine shared with the data join and other agents. Setup: `?new=1`
(a map start; also through the start menu's New game, the same figures), Destinations on, the view
over the Tokyo region at zoom 10 for 4 s, then Tokyo Disneyland at zoom 13, the click as soon as its
bubble exists (Tokyo is one zone, `tyo`, opened bare 1.4-2.9 s after its download). Times are ms
after the press. Script: the agent's scratchpad (`t1216.mjs`, not kept); outputs
`artifacts/trainworld/m-bubble-*.json` and `.png`.

## Where the time goes

| step | first click in the zone | the same click again |
|---|---|---|
| selection outlined | 25-37 | 9-14 |
| far end (aux worker `bareFlows` 31-131 ms, summed 2-52 ms) drawn | 271-376 (1,058 under the Attractions filter, the aux worker busy with that purpose's bubbles) | 172-197 |
| zone's market pack (`market:tyo`) asked for | 2-5 | (open) |
| downloaded | 63-262 | |
| opened in every solving worker | 9,489-15,096 (each worker 5,167-13,449 ms composing) | |
| new solve with the market, its view out | +1,200-1,500 after the open | |
| longer trips landed | 11,208-17,168 | 411-461 |

The longer trips (`icSel`) asked at about 270 ms wait in the solving workers' queues behind the
market's open and come back empty (the version moved on); they are asked again when the new view is
out and take 200-300 ms. A plain bubble (Shinjuku) behaves the same: 271 ms to the far end, 14.5 s
to the longer trips. The Attractions purpose's wide draw is not the cost: 4,985-6,595 far cells,
summed in 2-52 ms. Main thread: one 125-190 ms task at the press (the inspector and the selection's
discs), 50-65 ms when the far end lands, nothing long after.

**The cause** of the 20 s: a bubble selection opens its zone's market pack in every solving worker
(T-396, T-801: never for viewing alone), and composing the market is slow. In a new game no market
is open, so the first selection in a zone pays the whole composition before its longer trips can be
answered (and the inspector's longer trips spin all that time). Composing is
`MarketPacks::market()` (sim/src/demand/intercity.rs `build_for`): in Node, one composition with
Tokyo's pack is 4.3-5.3 s (also three at once, so not contention between workers), 70% of it the self
time of one function (the WASM has no names, so which part is not certain). The likeliest part is
its loop over pairs of places: 2,567 x 2,567 `PairRule`s (border factors by country name, airport
pairs, crossings), which depend on the places alone, not on the packs open, and are built again at
every composition. In the browser the same work took 5-13 s a worker. Filed as T-1220.

App-side, nothing big remains: the market starts downloading within 5 ms of the press, and opening
markets ahead for viewing would undo T-1198's memory fix (450 MB a worker per composition). The far
end, the part drawn on the map, is already 0.3 s.

## The loading mark

From the press until the far end is drawn, the selected bubbles' outline turns as the panels'
spinner does (`DemandOverlay.setLoading`, `commuterView.ts`); docs in ui-demand-views.md "Loading
mark". On at 31 ms; first built to stay until the longer trips landed (off at 15,183 ms), then cut
to the far end on the orchestrator's call, since the inspector's spinner covers the longer trips.
Screenshots: `artifacts/trainworld/m-bubble-disney-first-mark0.png` to `mark2.png`.
