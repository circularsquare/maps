# T-2919 train, bus and ferry panels and crowding made alike

Built 2026-11-09 from the review in notes/T-2915.md, on Anita's word: "lets do all 7 fixes. / sorry i think bus
crowding actually can be like train crowding. i dont see a reason for it not to be. ferries too. i think itd be
nice to have them directly comparable. / lets just say buses are unsettled."

## What changed

1. Riders by purpose on bus and ferry panels (`PurposeSplit` beside Changing to trains).
2. Crowding as a train's. A vehicle's rated load is two thirds of its crush for all three:

   | | seats | crowding minded from (70%) | rated (100%) | crush (150%) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | train car | 44 | 112 | 160 | 240 |
   | bus (a third of a car; was half a car, full at 80) | 14.7 (was 22) | 37.3 (was 56) | 53.3 (was 80) | 80 |
   | boat (two cars) | 88 | 224 | 320 | 480 |

   Demand: `network::BUS_CARS` 0.5 to 1/3, a bus's crush no longer its rated load, so the kernel's crowding
   weights and the wait for room run on a bus as on a train. Boats were already two cars in demand. Track sim:
   `LineSvc::rated()` is crush x 160 / 240 for every vehicle (closes T-2460), so the hourly stretch loads the
   dots read are a share of 53 for a bus and 320 for a boat. App: `BUS_CARS` 1/3 and one `capacity()` rule; the
   schedule's crowding dots and the Lines tab's coloured vehicles figure now show for bus and ferry lines; the
   bus gauge has its 100% tick; the drawn bus fills and rims as a train.
3. Riders left at platform and boardings today per port on ferry panels (a port keeps a crowd; a street
   stop does not, so buses have neither).
4. Stop times as the train panel's ("12m30s" after the name, from the stop on top) and its north-up order,
   on bus stops and ferry ports; the port list's drag works on the shown order. The bus and ferry panel's
   sections are in the train panel's order: schedule, the day's figures, stops, round trip.
5. Schedule copy and paste on bus and ferry lines. Train length and kind are carried only from a train line
   to a train line; any other pair carries the vehicles or headways, and the hovers say only that.
6. The round trip block on bus and ferry panels: running, dwell at stops, turning at the ends. A bus's
   dwell is the line's 30 s at each train station between its ends plus 20 s at each other stop, both ways
   (worked out in the app from the stops; the sim's short-hop cap on a street stand, 40% of the hop, is not
   mirrored, so a very short hop can read a few seconds high).
7. Delete line asks only where it cannot be undone: a constructed bus route. A train line now goes at once
   as a ferry line does.

New lines: bus 8 / 15 / 30 minutes (was 8 / 10 / 20), ferry 20 / 30 / none and trains 0 / 0 / 0 unchanged,
all with their dwell and turnaround in `app/src/game/newLine.ts` (`NEW_LINE`); the sim's unused
`ferry::FERRY_*` copies are gone. Prices unchanged.
