cluster, asked at the solve's send by `lengthsAhead`, answered with round 0), but the city view never asked for

zone with a bar showed "Calculating demand" in place of everything under its first rows, the graph included,

**Fix** (`ui/inspector/CityInspector.tsx`): a zone still loading that has figures of its own shows its Trips
within and its graph; only To other places and the top destinations wait, under "Calculating demand". A zone with
no figures yet shows "Calculating demand" alone, as before. No new string.

## T-2495: the focus pool's memory during a load

Measured the T-2472/T-2485 way: headless Chrome on this tree's Vite, a fresh profile, `?debug=1&new=1&paused=1`,
the real save (`T-007-real-nyc.save`) imported, Windows private bytes of every Chrome process of the profile summed
every 2 s from the import to the final (`mem.mjs`), Chrome pinned to the cores the ledger granted (4 for 2 demand
workers, 5 for 3). "Figures": the zone in view's (New York's) first figures with rail, from the import. Late in the
night the machine was otherwise quiet (Europe's runs had ended).

### Where the memory went (before)

Per target every 2 s (`measure.mjs` with `SAMPLE=1`, CDP `Runtime.getHeapUsage` and the workers' WASM reports):
the load held 3.7-4.0 GB from about 25 s to the final, with two kinds of bump:

- **The focus pool, the whole load.** On this save it was barely ahead of the main pool: each of its rounds came
  1-3 s before the main pool's same round (focus 21/50/73/96 s, main 48/70/92/108 s), yet its three workers
  (WASM 185 + 179 + 33 MB, plus each worker's own JS, isolate and compiled code) stayed to the final.
- **The corridor table's copies in the main solving workers** (25-60 s): 157-228 MB of ArrayBuffers in each,
  gone only at the worker's next garbage collection. `tableColumns` took four whole columns from the WASM (pairs,
  trips, commute legs, modes, 47 MB each on this save) through the binding's getters, which copy; the worker then
  ran its solve in the WASM, allocating nothing in JS, so nothing collected them.

### Built

| Cut | Where | `?perfOff=` for the old way |
|---|---|---|
| One solving worker in the focus pool (`FOCUS_WORKERS` 2 -> 1) | `demandFocus.ts` | `?focusWorkers=2` |
| No aux worker of its own: its aux questions (cells, cell modes) go to its solving worker, answered between populations (`auxAt`) | `demandFocus.ts`, `demandClient.ts` `ask` | `focusAux` |
| The pool's workers end once the main pool's own estimates are the view and, for every zone it solves or shows, it is no further on (or has settled the zone); its figures stay where it is ahead or settled (`withFocus`), questions go to the main pool's next round (`retireFocus`) | `demandClient.ts` | `focusEnd` |
| The table's columns read in place in the WASM's memory and freed there, one at a time, no JS copy (`inPlace`, raw exports from `init()`) | `demand.worker.ts` `tableColumns` | `inPlace` |
| Questions still waiting on a focus pool that ends are answered empty (they waited for ever; the memory poll's `pendingQueries.size` check would then never pass) | `demandClient.ts` `stopFocusPool` | |

On the real save the pool now ends at the main pool's round 0 (41-44 s of an 88 s load): the focus pool's round 0
is shown, the main pool's round 0 is as far on.
