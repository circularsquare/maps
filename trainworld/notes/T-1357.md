## What changed

- **`map/stationLabels.ts`**: when a state brings more than 200 stations (`DEFER_NAMES`) named unlike
  every label drawn, and 200 more stations than labels (a game's first network, an import), the build
  (complexes, label groups, the DOM elements, the shadow source) runs in a task after the paint
  (`afterPaint`, game/busy.ts). States meanwhile hand it their world; it builds from the latest. Edits
  never wait.
- **`game/trackZones.ts`**: unchanged from T-1348 (field-compared edge cache). Putting `followTrack` off
  past the paint was tried and dropped, below.

## followTrack put off: dropped

Deferred the same way (more than 200 edges not worked out), the figures stayed byte-identical but the
screenshots at rest did not: at zoom 14 the waiting riders drawn on the platforms of Penn Station and
Grand Central sat in other slots (repeatable, two runs alike). The zones it finds open crossed zones for
demand (`addOpened`, demandClient's `openCrossed`), so a frame's delay changes demand's intermediate rounds
and with them the riders the clock worker holds on platforms, though not the final figures. Its own
cost, sampling every edge's drawn line each km, stays in the state (about 90 ms at 6x on New York);
cutting it exactly is T-1358.
