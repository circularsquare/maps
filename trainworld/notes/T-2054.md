## The terrain question: drives only

Since T-2901 stage 3 the game reads terrain only from the world's blocks: the clock worker fetches `index.bin` and
`<la>_<lo>.terrain` from `TERRAIN_BASE` (`game/dataBase.ts`, `terrain-world/`; `workers/clock.worker.ts` terrain section),
and nothing in `app/src` or `sim/` builds a `packs/terrain/<id>.terrain` URL. The blocks are every block with a non-empty
GEDTM30 tile (19,341 files in `data/terrain-world-v2/`), so a zone anywhere on land 65 S to 85 N is covered with no step.
Left of the zone terrain, all for T-2901 stage 4: Vite's `/packs/terrain` middleware, the build's copy of
`data/terrain/*.terrain` and its "zones have no terrain file" warning (a warning only; it will fire for each new zone until
stage 4), `zone_terrain.py`'s zone build, and the files. The gate, `deploy.py` and `freeze.py` do not read zone terrain.

So the task shrank to drives, plus two cousins found on the way.

  market step that did not fail: `market_drives.py build --jobs N` as a step (one thread each, `DRIVES_S` 3600 s,
