# T-3845 Ferry waypoints placed in drawing order

Anita, 11-20: people cannot even place two ports when the whole leg is too long or complicated to search, so the
waypoint must come between the ports: port A, shift click, then click port B. Tag: "Place port", over water "Shift
click to place waypoint".

## What changed (app/src/map/ferryDraw.ts)

- `tail`: the waypoints placed after the last port of a new route (or of an edit with fewer than two ports). The
  route search includes them (`runPoints`), so each shift click searches only the leg from the last point; the next
  port takes the tail as its leg's `ways` entry. `allWays()`/`legWays(k)` treat the tail as leg `ways.length`, so
  dots, removal and drags work on it.
- Edit route with both ends keeps the least-added-distance insert (`appending()` false).
- Make line drops a tail (a waypoint after the last port leads nowhere). Backspace and a right click on empty
  ground take the tail back one at a time; taking back the last port of a new route leaves its leg's waypoints as
  the tail, so back steps undo in the order placed.
- A shift click with no port is refused; on land refused as before; a looped line refuses (loop closed).
- Hover tag: over water with a port placed (the port probe's "on-water" answer, the same test the old red "Place
  ports on land by the water" used) says "Shift click to place waypoint"; with no port the red line stays.
