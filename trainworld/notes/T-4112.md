# T-4112: a station on a blueprint refused for water at a ramp far away

Agent stationwater, 2026-11-23, tree `.tw-trees/stationwater` (branch tw/stationwater from tw/track 851776ff).

## Cause

A ramp is laid centred between its two vertices (geom.rs `vertical`), and `split_plan` (net/paths.rs) moves any
vertex its cut changes. Cut inside an arc, the arc's PI becomes one PI per part, each at the middle of its own part
of the arc. A bridge's ramp is often held by the PI of a wide curve (`bridge::one_ramp` tries the alignment's own
vertices first), so a station placed anywhere on that curve slid the ramp along: 225 m toward the water for a station
5 km away, in the probe below. The ground-level track the ramp left then crossed the water, and the split was refused
with "Track at ground level cannot cross water". The pieces were not checked against water they did not have before;
the ramp itself had moved. The cut also gave the PIs of the arc it cut the arc's level, wrong when the arc held the
ramp: a spurious ramp down and up again after the station. T-622 already held a ramp that lies on one straight; one on or
touching a curve was not held.

## Fix

`split_plan_in` compares each part's height profile with the piece's own over that stretch
(`geom::profile_between`, `geom::same_profile`, ends within 1 m) and, where a ramp moved, lays the part's ramps
again where they were with `bridge::hold_ramps`: a ramp still in place keeps its own two vertices (which also keeps a
ramp its neighbours squeeze short, at the floor length, as it was); one that moved is held by a vertex either side of
it, the arc cut into arcs of the same radius exactly as a bridge's ramps are (`build`, `slice`); with no ramp in the
part every vertex takes its one level. `build` now takes the levels as a function, so ramps between any levels are
held, not just the bridge's 0 and 1. `settle` has an `along` switch: the bridge keeps its same-chainage check; the
hold asks only that the new alignment lie within 0.25 m of the old, since in the world frame an arc's chainage runs at
one scale over the whole arc and a long arc cut in several puts the same chainage up to about 0.4 m further along (the
same-chainage check refused every hold on an 8 km radius curve). Where nothing can hold the ramp, the part is left as
before (the ramp moves, the edit is checked as before), so nothing that could be built before is refused now.

It is in the split primitive, so every split holds its ramps: a station, a branch drawn off the middle of a piece,
crossings, flyovers, add point.

## Refusal wording

No string changed. The refusal for a station on a ramp stays "The platform would be on a ramp." (PlatformNotLevel),
and a tram stop standing on water at ground level stays "A station at ground level cannot stand in water."
(StationGroundOverWater). After the fix a station is no longer refused for water it does not stand on in any probe
case, so no rewording was needed.

## Master

`split_plan_in`'s geometry on master is the same as on tw/track (only the EdgeData fields differ), so master has the
same bug. Tried once on 8800 (master, new NYC game, the same route edit, then the station tool by pointer at W 37th St and
9th Ave): refused "Track at ground level cannot cross water. Pick a viaduct or tunnel."; a station on the same
piece in Jersey City placed. Screenshot `artifacts/trainworld/t4112-master-refused.png`.

Port: the three hunks (geom.rs two functions, bridge.rs `hold_ramps` and the `build`/`settle` parameters,
paths.rs the hold after the radius easing) apply nearly as they are; bridge.rs differs from master by 7 lines elsewhere.
The standing test needs master's `add_station` (the same kind 2 split). About 45 minutes with a test run.
