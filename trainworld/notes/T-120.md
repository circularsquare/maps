# T-120: longer-trip bubbles drawn like commuter bubbles

Anita: "Elsewhere in the New York region" for starting points within the region is confusing, those
should be visualised normally; the yellow bubble style goes: size proportional to people, colour red
or blue by train usage, everyone not on a train red.

## What changed

Rust (`sim/src/demand/intercity.rs`):
- `selection_flows` returns, after the old 25 numbers (matched share, 8 per market), 4 numbers per far
  zone (starts all, starts rail, ends all, ends rail) and then every zone's own x and y.
- `station_flows` returns the old 18 numbers (per market, now derived from the zone rows), then rail
  riders starting and ending per zone, then the zones' x and y.
- `Period::st` is now one f32 per station, direction and zone (was 18 f64 per station by market). The
  pass does two adds per term instead of six. Memory: stations x 2 x zones x 4 bytes a period (the
  real pack has about 1,050 zones with sites, so 875 stations is 7 MB a period).
- Zone x, y are the zones' own, identical on every worker, so the client does not add them up (the
  per-zone all*x, all*y the task asked for are not needed for that reason).
- Tests: zone sums equal market sums for both directions, rail <= all per zone, positions are the
  zones', a half share halves every zone, station zone rows add up to the market rows. 87 native
  tests pass.

App:
- `demandClient.ts` `sumIntercity` adds everything but the closing x, y block (`intercityZoneXY`);
  `game/demand.ts` `PlaceFlows.zones: ZoneTrips[]` (x, y in pack metres as the places, all, rail),
  biggest first, zero zones dropped.
- `map/intercityView.ts`: one disc per far zone with at least `ZONE_FLOOR` (0.5) trips a day, at most
  `ZONE_CAP` (400, the smallest dropped), at the zone's position. Area proportional to trips, the
  biggest 0.6 of a 6 km zone (as the commuter far end sizes against its hexagon), so the bubbles
  scale with the map; layer "inter" now takes the commuter size slider. Colour
  `modeMix(rail, 0, all - rail)`, `BUBBLE_ALPHA`, white edge. Biggest drawn first. Hover lines
  "N longer trips a day to here" / "M by train" (from here for Jobs; a station: "N riders a day on
  longer trips to here"), under the commuter discs in finder order. No name tags.
- Deleted: `INTERCITY_*` palette entries, `INTER_PX`, `DemandOverlay.interScale`, `.inter-tag` CSS.
  Inspector swatches use the train blue.
- `intercityPlaceName`: the own region's row is "Other New York region places" (and "Other
  Philadelphia region places").

## Sizes: a judgement call

The fixed 44 px of T-118 is gone. With zones the bubbles are 6 km apart, so a geographic size does
not make specks or fill the screen: the biggest is about 2.6 km in radius (23 px at zoom 10, 6 px at
zoom 8) and the slider scales it. Zoomed right out, small zones are specks, as the commuter far end
is.

Counts (real save plus the Philadelphia check line): Times Square homes 1,054 zones with trips, 865
of them at least 0.5 (so the cap of 400 applies, dropping everything under about 1 trip a day);
Times Square jobs 923; Philadelphia 3 km homes 569; Bridgeport station 26. Totals are unchanged from
T-118 (Times Square 8.9k starting, 62 by train; 16.5k ending, 179 by train).
