# T-1177: the app's side of the everyday pairs across a zone line (and T-1229, the sim's)

Done 2026-10-24 in tree t1229, both halves. The rule is docs/zones.md 4a (last bullets) and
ui-inspectors.md "Between zones". What was built, by place:

- **Sim** (`sim/src/demand/intercity.rs`, `intercity/market_trip.rs`): `part_summary` has four
  numbers per everyday kind after its first eight (legs, rail legs, walking legs, walking with no
  rail); `selection` has a block per kind of `E_CROSS` (errands, social, school, university,
  medical) per far zone, three numbers a side (legs, rail legs, walkers: the all-mode part from the
  market alone, `selection_rail_day` adds rail and takes the walkers rail takes, negative);
  `market_trip`'s head is 22 numbers, 19 to 21 the pairs' legs, rail and walking, their rail riders
  in the station pairs and routes (`market_trip_between` counts both directions). New market fields
  `e_home`, `e_act` (CSR by home and by activity zone), `e_base`; `IntercityApi.epair_of`. Test
  `everyday_pairs_by_purpose_in_selections_and_the_explorer`; `cargo test --features demand` 339.
- **App**: `workers/marketParts.ts` (`marketPartsOn`: the weekend scaling by each purpose's own
  factor; before this `dayResult` asked for a factor named "errands", which is no purpose key and
  took work's), `demandClient` (`intercitySelectionAt` reads the blocks into `byPurpose` and the all
  lists with a `walk`; `intercityZones` is `(len - 3 - 12P) / 41`), `demand.worker.ts`
  (`marketFigures` adds the per-purpose figures over parts, walkers fixed up per purpose),
  `game/trip.ts` (`MarketPeriod.everyday`), `ui/inspector/MarketTrip.tsx` (Everyday trips),
  `LongerTrips.tsx` (walking column), `map/intercityView.ts` (the far end under a purpose filter).
- **Checks**: `checks/t1177-cross-everyday.mjs` (the worker, made-up pairs Boston to Providence
  written in memory, `t1177-data.mjs`), `t1177-cross-everyday-page.mjs` (a new game, a Playwright
  route; screenshots in artifacts/trainworld/t1177/). On the page the average day shows 1.063,
  1.173 and 0.739 of a weekday for errands, social and school, which is each purpose's own weekend
  factor working.
