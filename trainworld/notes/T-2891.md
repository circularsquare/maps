# T-2891 bus stops by hand

Built 2026-11-09 from the shape agreed in T-2895's notes. Anita: "if in manual mode we click the stops,
as if it were a train. if clears out all the auto stops and u have to click all of them except the start
and end points."

What it is:

- `BusRoute::hand: Option<Vec<[f64; 2]>>`, points on the route between its ends (`bus.rs`, `StopRule`). Some
  means By hand; spacing is then 0. `plan_by` is the one place stops are decided: Auto, Spaced or Hand.
- Hand stops are points, not road metres, so a redraw can move them onto the new route (those within 200 m
  stay). At each plan a point takes the station within 200 m along the road, else it is a plain stop.
- Timing and cover are a spaced line's (`spaced_profile`, 400 m each way), so a long hop runs fast.
- Editing is the train editor's state (`tool = "line"`, `lineDraft`), with `map/busStops.ts` taking the clicks
  while the line is by hand: same marks, tag, S key and Enter. Not a new tool.

Calls made here:

- Choosing By hand starts empty every time; going back to it after leaving does not bring the old stops.
  Undo does. Anita asked for cleared stops, and the old ones may have been on another route by then.
- The ends cannot be removed; a click near one does nothing. 50 m is the least gap between two placed stops.
- Stations in reach show as the hollow marker, and a stop anywhere within 200 m of a station goes to it (the
  brief); a plain stop within 200 m is not possible, as for the automatic stops.
- Saves: `LBH1`, level 9. No block when no line is by hand, so a save with none is unchanged.
- T-2919's schedule work (tree schedunify) moves other rows of the bus panel but not the stepper or the stop
  rows' data, so this change does not touch what it needs; both touch `BusInspector.tsx`, in separate places.
