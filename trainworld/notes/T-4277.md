## Fixed

- **T-4264 #1, the status tag's bar going back during one solve: real, and easy to meet.** `placeFor(kind)` started the
  tag's place from empty whenever the tag's wording changed (`solve`, `data`, `bubbles`, `click`), though in all four
  the bar is the running solve's share (`solveShare`). Panning during a whole solve to a city whose bubbles are a moment
  away ("Loading data…"), or a click that waits, and back, reset the bar twice: above half it fell to the raw share
  (an anchored bar) or to at most half (a bound that had lapsed, as in the last seconds after the last unit). Cause:
  T-4264 folded the old `solvePlace` (held through the wait, reset at its end) into the per-kind place. Fix
  (ui/StatusTag.tsx): a bar on the running solve's share has one place, `solvePlace`, through the whole wait whatever
  the tag says, reset when the wait has been over a second or a newer edit restarts the solve; the per-kind place is for
  guesses only. A cousin covered by the same change: a wait on the solve, then a zone's own bar (its download) for a
  few seconds, then the solve again no longer starts the solve's bar from empty.
- **T-4264 #2, the hover's time left kept past its wait: real in code, hardly reachable.** `editShown` was cleared only
  when `demandWork` turned null. Every solving worker sends its units at planning, a few seconds into a solve, so a
  solve that ends with `demandWork` still null is one cut before its workers planned. Fixed anyway in one line beside
  #1: the count goes when the wait ends, with `solvingSince` and the bar.
- **T-4265 #1 (part), the airports' trips across the zone line left out of the bubbles during a whole solve: real.** For a
  zone the solve in hand solves again, `withRegional` skipped the airport part (`gone`), so a city drawn before the edit
  showed its airport terminals' bubbles smaller until the final, against DECISIONS T-4265 (figures from before the
  edit). Fix (workers/demandClient.ts): `airportAcross` keeps what it added per zone and day (`lastAir`, 256 zones at
  most, a few terminal cells each), and while the zone is solved again that is added, as `lastReg` is for the rest of
  the trips with other places. A zone not drawn since the load still has neither (the lie as written).
- **T-4265 #2, a final's late bubble sums held a copy per solving worker: real, memory only.** Off-screen zones' sums of
  a final come after its result; when the next solve went at that final (a held edit's, sent at once) they arrived
  after `keepShownSums` and stayed one part per solving worker through the whole next solve. Fix: the sums handler adds
  a final's parts into one as soon as they are whole (`mergeSums`, the same additions in the same order as
  `cellModes`, so the same bubbles to the bit). With one solving worker (her Asia save) nothing changes.

## Not real, or left, with the reason

- **T-4263: no finding**, agreed. The minor note (`capAhead` counts the aux's estimate as a need, about 40 MB more
  than `capPool` on her Asia save: 273 MB x 1.12 + 64 + 150 = 520 MB against 330 + 150) errs to the old behaviour and is
  inside the estimate's own miss (the aux reached 769 MB in T-4263's run).
- **T-4265 #1 (rest), `lastReg` older than the final shown: real, left.** A zone not drawn during the final shown keeps
  the regional answer of the last final it was drawn on. Leaving that part out instead would be the bigger error (all
  of it, 5.5% of Nanjing's legs, against what one solve moved of it); refreshing every zone's at each final is the cost
  T-4265 left open. Within DECISIONS T-4265's lie (figures from before the edit).
- **T-4266 #1, `pathStart` not checked in `movedPaths`: not real.** The patch's lines reach only the tools' world
  (`clickWorld`, clockClient.ts `layPatch` -> `toWorld`), which never reads `pathStart`; the drawn split between
  running and planned (map/network.ts 994, 2218) is built from full states only. clockPatch.ts is answerfirst's; not
  touched.
- **T-4266 #2, `writeCache` racing past its sequence check: not real.** From the check (`seq !== this.cacheSeq`) to the
  post to the cache worker nothing awaits (`writeStored` copies the days in one go and posts), so a write past the check
  is posted before any newer one, the worker writes them in order, and their answers set `cachedHash` and the rest in
  that order: the fields say what the slot holds. A game switch: the old write goes to the old slot (taken at its
  start) and its fields describe that write; the next game's first write compares its own hash.
- **Interaction T-4265 + T-4266, the cache keeping bubbles worked out while the next solve went: real, older than both,
  left.** A held edit's solve is sent at the final; the final's bubbles may then take the trips with other places from
  the last answers (before T-4265 the same race got an empty answer from a busy worker and the same fallback). They are
  the bubbles shown, and at the next load they show until that load's own final.
- **Interaction T-4265 + T-4220, page memory during a whole solve: not a bug.** The kept sums (36 MB on Asia) and the
  snapshot (at most 120 MB, off on small devices) are on the page; `capPool` counts the page as measured where it can
  (`pageOther`), else by the T-3757 fit, whose error is larger than this. Worth a look if T-4220's later stages keep more.
- **Interaction T-4265 + T-4264, the fallback path showing the bar's reset:** the same cause as T-4264 #1, fixed there.

## T-4220 stage 3: for stage 4 (dev only today: `twDemand.client.asidePlan()` with ?debug=1, no player reaches it)

- **#1, `planAside` sets the worker's frame: not real.** The frame is the game's (from its save): every network of one
  game has the same one, so a side plan between a solve's populations sets the value it already has.
- **#2, clusters with nothing fresh skip the fare and cluster checks: real in code.** A fare edit moves no city's view,
  so every cluster is skipped and the plan says "nothing to solve" instead of "fares"; the same for a cluster joined or
  split with no view past `sameView`'s tolerances. Stage 4 should run the fare check and the cluster check over every
  cluster before skipping the ones with nothing to solve, or it may show the snapshot as the new network's figures.
- **#3a, `FocusPool.aside` notes a generation sent without its snapshot: real in code, unreachable** (the one caller
  always sends it). Set `asideGen` only when `shown` went.
- **#3b, `ShownStore.merge` counts an ignored snapshot as a new generation:** a needless resend of the snapshot to the
  focus worker once stage 4 sends it per edit; bump `gen` only when the store changed.
- **#3c, `forAside` answers once one solving worker has reported:** with more than one solving worker the snapshot may
  lack the others' periods; stage 4 should wait for every period once (as `everyPeriodOnce` does for the bubbles).
