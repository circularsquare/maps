# T-871: panning across Europe

Anita, 2026-10-21: "panning around europe is still pretty laggy, as we constantly load zones (perhaps
we dont load zones unless we actually are somewhat close in zoom-in? ...)".

## What "load" was on a pan
A `?new=1` game, Homes on, real mouse drags (400 px each) London, Paris, Brussels, Amsterdam,
Hamburg, Berlin, Prague, Vienna, Munich, Zurich, Milan. Per pan end, before:
- the viewed zone's pack opened bare in the aux worker, at every zoom (14 opens at z5.5, 96 at z9.5);
- every zone whose box was in view (from zoom 5) fetched its exact boundary and had its outside
  shade built (310 boundary files in the first z5.5 pass);
- the clock worker fetched the water masks of zones near the view;
- and the main cost: every exact outline that arrived, and every change of focus, rebuilt the
  outline layer from every outline fetched so far and sent it all to MapLibre's worker
  (`edges.lines` plus `setData`): 0.5-1.4 s tasks, growing with the pan;
- the overview's place labels (one per zone, about 300) were each measured on every map move,
  a forced layout per label.

The bare opens themselves cost little main thread; they cost downloads and the aux worker's time.

## Changes
- `ZONE_LOAD_ZOOM` 7.5 (`game/zones.ts`), one level out from `OVERVIEW_ZOOM`; `zonesLoadForView`
  set by `map/zoneFocus.ts`. At or below it: no viewed zone opens (`demandClient` `viewedSet`), no
  exact outline is fetched for the view (`cityBoundaries` `OUTLINE_MIN_ZOOM`, coarse ones stand),
  no water mask is fetched ahead (`main.ts`). Reached zones and their markets are untouched.
- The outline layer holds only zones within a view's width of the screen (`OutlineEdges.lines`
  `only`), redrawn when that set changes.
- `intercityView` `placeLabels`: labels past the cap of 8 or far off screen are hidden unmeasured.
- `?perfOff=zoneZoom` brings back the first three for A/B (not the label change).
- Unloading: not added. T-301 already closes a viewed-only zone a minute after it leaves the view,
  or at once past two spare; the aux worker's WASM peaked at 35-39 MB over the whole pan.
