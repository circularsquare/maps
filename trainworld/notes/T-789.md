# T-789: the freeze after a stop edit (2026-10-20)

Anita, on 8800 (d8592be): a new normal game in Paris, a short line, stops added: the whole page
freezes for a while after each stop.

## Cause

The main thread, in `demandClient.ts` `dayDemand`: the market's corridors were every pair of its
places, `(0,0) .. (P-1,P-1)`, whatever their trips. With France, Spain, Italy, Portugal and Greece
covered the registry has 775 places at d8592be (300,700 pairs) and 1,001-1,011 in the working tree
while the Europe batch runs (501,501 pairs); at 74ae679 it had 217 (23,653). Each publish summed
them over 5 periods for 3 kinds of day, with a `slice` and a string key per pair, twice an edit (the
first estimate's new lines and the final), each period's arrays came as plain `number[]` (2 M
numbers a message), `dayResult` copied them per weekend day, the cache cloned 500,000 objects into
IndexedDB, and the city bubbles kept a P x P x 3 matrix. Only zone places have market rows, so the
pairs with any trips are Z(Z+1)/2 (166,743 of 501,501 with 577 zones); the rest were zeros.

Not Paris: New York on the same build froze the same (longest task 4.6 s). Not the packs: a stop
edit in Paris solves the Paris zone with 3 market packs (par, ois, eue; Grevena: gre and 4); no
zone opens. Not the workers: their solve of an edit is 0.45-0.6 s.

| 8800, d8592be (300,700 pairs) | 3,400 ms | about 7 s (3.5 + 2.7 + 0.6) |

| dev after, Grevena (GR countryside) | 0 ms | none |

The steps of the fix, longest task per edit: sparse corridors and typed arrays 180-230 ms; the
weekend factors carried instead of copies, no corridors for the early estimate or a cluster without
the market 106-178; the rest kept by key and only railed pairs summed 52-56; the cache written in a
worker 0 (no long task in either edit). CPU profile self time per edit, before: `dayDemand` 8.1-8.4 s,
`writeDemandCache` 0.8 s, message decoding 0.8 s, GC 0.7-0.8 s.

## Fix

- Worker (`demand.worker.ts` `keptCorridors`): only pairs with longer trips or commutes go out
  (the same pairs in every period and network), as `Float64Array`s transferred, with a key of all
  but the rail (FNV over from, to, trips, commute legs) and the pairs with rail as (index, rail,
  commute rail).
- Client (`demandClient.ts` `sumCorridors`, `corridorSums`): a day's sums of everything but the rail
  kept by the five periods' keys and the day's factors (up to 6 kept); a result with known keys
  works out only its railed pairs; weekend days carry their factors (`corridorDay`) instead of
  scaled copies; the first estimate's new lines and a cluster solved ahead without the market skip
  the corridors. `IntercityDemand.corridors` is columns (`Corridors`), read by intercityView,
  points.ts and CityInspector; the city bubbles keep zone places only (Z x Z, not P x P).
- The cache write in `cacheWrite.worker.ts`, the corridors copied a day per task and transferred
  (`CACHE_FORMAT` 2: older caches are not shown).
- Same figures: the kept sums against the full sum, every column of every pair, weekday and both
  weekend days, the real result and one with rail made up on 2% of the pairs: identical to the bit
  (`repro.mjs` "bits"). t409-worker-check PASS (market parts to the bit), smoke PASS,
  t648-stop-edit on the real save 76 ms to world, 118 to the frame (T-648's after: 70-76, 112-130).
  t328-cache-check: the cached view equal to the fresh final on the first reload; later reloads
  failed "fresh finals agree" because the Europe batch rewrote market-rows.json and registry.json
  between runs (22:46 and 22:48 local), changing the market under it.

## Zones and lag (Anita's question)

Before the fix the number of places was the whole freeze: its work was linear in place pairs,
quadratic in places, so the 136 to 397 zones of the last two days (217 to 775 places) made it
about 13 times longer at d8592be and 21 times in the working tree. After it, what still grows with
the zone count Z:

- Each period's result message carries Z(Z+1)/2 pairs (5.3 MB of corridors, 2.7 MB of commutes) and
  the P x P mode shares (4 MB), transferred: decoding takes 5-13 ms a message on the main thread, 16-17
  messages an edit, each its own task. T-790 (sent only when they change).
- The first result after the market changes (startup) sums every pair once: about 15-25 ms a kind
  of day, 45-75 ms in one task.
- The city bubbles' Z x Z matrix: 8 ms to build a result, 5 ms a step while catching up.
- In the workers the market's pass over its zone pairs (50-75 ms a period here), and the Rust
  `corridor_summary` still builds every place pair per period before the worker drops the empty ones.
- Opening a zone: a stop edit opens none. Pack opens measured: Paris zone 1.5 s, Grevena 1.3 s,
  market packs 0.3-1.0 s each; 220-230 MB a solving worker.

Merging small zones: 584 zones now, 273 under 0.5M people (Italy 68, Germany 53, France 43, Greece
31) and 444 under 1M. Pooled within each country to at least 0.5M, about 479 zones, two thirds of
today's pairs; to at least 1M, about 335 zones, a third. After the fix that would save a third or
two thirds of the per-message decoding and the market pass, nothing a player would see as a
freeze; it is not needed for performance today. The pairs grow with the square of the zone count,
so at 2,000-3,000 zones worldwide (12-26 times today's pairs) the message size (T-790) is what to
fix first.

74ae679 was not run in a worktree: the profile put the whole freeze in the per-pair loops, so its
217 places (23,653 pairs, a 21st of the working tree's) are taken as about 0.5 s of work an edit in
tasks of up to about 250 ms, worked out from the pair counts, not measured.
