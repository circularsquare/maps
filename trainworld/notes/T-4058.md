**3. A bus and a street tram on one street** (8 tram stops 700 m apart in Nassau; the bus drawn along the same

End to end the bus gets nothing: it arrives after the tram's worst case. That is the rule doing what it says;

## What looks off, and what I'd do

1. **Riders change onto a line they could have boarded where they were** (setup 4). On branches sharing a trunk
   they take the frequent line to the split and change to their own branch (10% at equal service, 43% at 5/20,
   57% at 4/60), and from the last trunk station some ride the frequent line one stop back to catch their branch
   there (44% at 4/60). It comes from every line's wait being independent at every station: the model treats the
   wait for South at the next station as a fresh roll, so riding there gives a second chance. In the game the
   frequent branch's Riders and trunk loads are too high, the rare branch's too low, and the split station shows
   changes nobody would make. Her words in T-4050 were riders past a split "waiting for their own branch".
   **Proposal for the search (T-4054's file, not built):** two bars at a change node, both local to the node:
   (a) riders off a line don't change onto a line whose next stop is the station they just came from (the
   same-line rule we already have, for any line); (b) riders off a line don't change onto a line that was one
   evenly spaced service with it (T-4050's class) on the stop they just rode, since that train is right behind
   them. Neither touches the real save (its lines don't share stations). An express then local on track with no
   passing (setup 1, 0 to 8: 6.5% at 10/30, 52% at 30/10) has the same flaw but the express's ride is faster on
   paper, so it would stay; fine to leave.
2. **The first-train quarter is missing inside an evenly spaced service** (setup 7's fare test). Proposal: let
   a quarter of the riders inside a T-4050 service take whichever line comes first (by trains an hour among the
   lines that can still make it), as they do elsewhere. That softens the all-or-nothing step to 75/25. Small,
   same file; her softening applies everywhere.
3. **Trip explorer fails right after a change** (seen in setup 7's first solve, also in T-4050's notes):
   "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'nodeGlobal')" in the demand worker. Cause found: the question
   is about the last network (version 3) while the worker has moved on (version 4) and has meanwhile reopened the
   zone at another level, so `cities.get(city)` is a new population the old network's cluster has no view of.
   The fix is to answer an older version from that cluster's own population for the city (or answer "not yet"),
   which touches how the worker keys its populations, so filed rather than fixed here (T-4063).
4. Not riding, seen on the way: a line list in a paused game says "calculating trips…" on every line forever
   once the trips are in (the Riders column waits for a day's count while the clock has not run); the 20 km/h
   limit on a street tram (setup 3).
