# T-466: merging the sparse children of busy parents

2026-10-16. Anita: "in general we should be trying to merge more sparse cells"; small edge stations
moving is acceptable. Rule: docs/demand.md 4.1. Code: `pipeline/merge_cells.py` (`merge`,
`COMPONENTS`), `read_pack.py`, and in the sim `PackGravity::cells9` read from `zone_cells9`
(`kernel::zones_from_pack`). Backups of every pack, market file and the registry before the
re-merge: `data/work/pre-T466/`.

## The rule

T-044's rule (a res-8 parent under 2,500 trip ends a weekday merges, children within one 2 km zone
together) is unchanged. In a parent at or over 2,500, the children under 1,250 trip ends each
(`CHILD`, half the threshold) that touch each other and share a zone merge into one cell: one cell
per connected group of 2-6 children (the centre child touches all six, the ring runs 4-6-2-3-1-5-4
in H3 digits). The merged cell takes the res-9 index of its busiest child (so ids stay unique and
the res-8 bubble grouping still finds its parent), sits at the group's trip-end-weighted centre and
sums every weight; a group whose centre leaves the zone square or falls on water stays unmerged, as
before. The busy children stay at res 9.

**Land.** A merged cell's index no longer says how many res-9 cells it stands for, and the sim's
zone land (parking density and drive speed, T-372: res-9 cells x 0.1053 km²) counted a res-8 index
as 7 and a res-9 index as 1. The merged pack now carries `zone_cells9` (each zone's res-9 cells, from
the res-9 pack) and the sim uses it when present. This is also exact where T-044's count was not: a
merged parent counted 7 even when 2 of its children hold people (the other cells are not in any pack),
so sparse zones had up to several times their land. Over Tokyo's zones the old count was a median
1.38 times the res-9 count (1.06 weighted by people and jobs); 1,793 of 4,792 zones were over 1.5x.

The new rule itself barely moves riders: at 1,250 rail moves 0.01-0.14% and 0.4-2.9% of busy
stations pass 5%, under T-044's 1k merge (1.2-5.7%). The land fix moves more (rail +0.1-0.3%); its
worst stations are not where land changed (Tokyo's -58% is a grid stop 3.7 km from the origin whose
zones' land is equal in A and B): on the grid, parallel stops close together trade riders on small
global changes. The memory saving is small (peak -3 to -9 MB), the cells -18 to -27%. 357 (the
"never above a merged parent" choice) merges only 3-5% of cells; 1,250 was taken for Anita's "merge
more", and its merged groups (at most 6 x 1,250 trip ends) stay inside one res-8 footprint.

Why sparse cells are left: in Tokyo's res-9 pack 10,790 children under 357 sit in busy parents;
2,555 of them are the only sparse child of their parent in their zone, of which 888 touch a sparse
child of another parent and 918 a merged parent. Merging across parents (or into a neighbouring
merged parent) would take those; not built.

**T-455's Chichibu box** (139.0-139.15 E, 35.95-36.05 N) in Tokyo's pack: 514 cells before (151
merged parents, 363 at a res-9 index), 351 after (151 and 200, the 200 including the merged sibling
groups); res-9-index cells under 300 trip ends 128 -> 67.
