# T-585: waits as plain minutes

Anita, 2026-10-16 (DECISIONS): a wait is half the headway in plain minutes, no 1.37 for the first
5 minutes and no 0.4 after; no refit. Done 2026-10-17.

## What changed

- `kernel::board_cost` is `headway / 2 + transfer_min`; `Params::w_wait`, `w_home_wait` and
  `platform_wait_cap_min` are gone (and `w_wait` from `set_extra`; no script used it).
- The extra wait for full trains (`rn_left`) joins the strategy search at 1 a minute (it was at
  `w_wait`), in `kernel.rs` and the trip explorer's legs (`trip.rs`).
- "Other"'s fixed 8-minute wait (`other_wait`) is 8 perceived minutes, not 11.
- The trip explorer's "counts as" sum (`app/src/game/trip.ts` `routeTerms`) takes its weights from
  the worker's own perceived minutes, so its wait row now shows no weight; only the hover's last
  sentence in `TripInspector.tsx` changed ("Walks count for more than a minute on board ...").

Where the old weight sat: equal at a half headway of 8.1 minutes (a 16-minute headway). Below
that a wait got cheaper (5 minutes: 6.85 -> 5), above it dearer (15 minutes: 10.85 -> 15), so
frequent metros gain a little and sparse commuter lines lose.

since the fit. Rail falls in both: the dearer long waits on commuter lines and the cheaper "other"
outweigh the cheaper short waits.
