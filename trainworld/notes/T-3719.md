# T-3719: switching to sandbox lowers some lines' train counts

Closed as intended: the sandbox switch changes no line. The drop is T-3165's load cap lowering
(`game/capLoad.ts`), which lands when the loaded game's trips are first calculated (`gameReady`, about
75-95 s after opening `T-007-real-nyc.save` on 3 cores). T-3703's checker took its "before" snapshots soon
after a load and its first one after the sandbox switch past that point, so the two coincided.

## Evidence (scratch probes in the sandcount scratch folder, real save, port 6186)

- No switch at all: the same three lines drop to the same values on their own (watch.js: 77-78 s
  after load).
- The only edits sent after the load are capLoad's (watch2.js, `tw.client.edit` patched):
  `setTrainCount` line 8 (7) to [17,13,5], line 20 (S) to [2,2,1], line 23 (L) to [19,17,5], all
  `unrecorded: true`, between 94.3 and 95.9 s, with `gameReady` turning true at 94.5 s.
- Sandbox alone: switched right after load (before the lowering) and again on a game past it, then
  waited for the final; 0 of 109 lines changed either time (mode read back as "sandbox").
- What runs is unchanged: of the three lines only `trainsNeeded` moved. Line 7 runs 15 trains an
  hour at high (the track's limit) over a 4016 s round trip, 16.7 trains, so T-3165's cap (the whole
  trains the track runs plus one) is 17; the other 6 of the save's 23 stood at the ends, paid for.
- The rule is written down: DECISIONS 2026-11-11 (T-3165: lowered once when a game loads) and
  2026-11-17 (T-3528: off the undo history, and why it waits for the calculated trips).

## Versions

On master and in v0.4.1: capLoad.ts and T-3526/T-3528 (aa6ad2cd, 85accc38) are ancestors of the v0.4.1
publish commit 3ce746f9; the only later change near it (capacity.rs `live_ports`, T-3474) is about
lines running part of their plan, not these lines.

## Reach

Any save holding a count set manually above the cap: saves from before T-3165, or a line whose cap fell
after a track edit (kept until the next load, by design). Once per such load; the lowered counts are an
ordinary accepted edit, so a slot keeps them after its next save, and only a file opened afresh (like
the real save, always opened from disk) shows the drop every time. New NYC real-network games
(`data/packs/real/nyc.save`) are not affected: loading it sent no lowering edits.

The only oddity a player could meet: the panel shows the old count for the minute or so before the
trips are first calculated, then the lowered one, with nothing to say why. Not changed here; T-3528
chose to wait for the calculated figures.
