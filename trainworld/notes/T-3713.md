# T-3713: route ending near a bare end refused as too tight (agent rampend, 2026-11-18)

Repro by pointer (scratchpad rampend/t13.js): a bare end (the +1 track's end), route from 70 m beside and 70 m back along the
track, released at 999 or 1001 m (the node snap takes the end at zoom 18-19). "The curve is too tight. Curves need a radius
of at least 25 m." Coming from the open side (beyond the end) it joins fine.

Cause: the snap makes the end the node, so the route must arrive heading out of the end's open side, i.e. turn back on itself
from the track's side.

Built: `route_op` (draw.rs) checks the route with `sketch`; when it fails and the last end is an existing bare end, it is
laid out again with `bare_slide`, where `back_on_bare` turns that node end into a point 5.1 m in along the track (when the
route's approach lies on the track's side), so the route branches off just short of the end, as a click there does (T-3690's
slide). The retry is kept only if it is valid; else the route as first drawn (and its refusal) stays. Only the route's last end:
a start from a bare end is the player's own pick and keeps T-418's refusal of a click behind it (a_too_tight_or_backward_tangent_click_is_refused).

Limit: a second route into the same bare end after one branch already sits 5.1 m in is still refused (the stub is under 10.2 m).

Tests: `a_route_ending_beside_a_bare_end_from_the_tracks_side_branches_in`; sim suites 648 + 845 pass; t3162-branch-hit 53 PASS;
i18n check, tsc clean. Screenshots: artifacts/trainworld/t3713_after_placed.png (the 102 m route ending as a junction 5 m
short of the end; ghost: t3713_ghost_joins.png).
