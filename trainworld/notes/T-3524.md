# T-3524 track near the shore counted as over water

Cause: the pack mask is 25 m cells sampled at the cell centre, so its edge sits up to ~15 m off the drawn coast; every point test (refusal, price, ramps, station, tram) reads the same mask through `net.water_mask`.

Measured (Sydney open coast, pack `syd` against OSM sea polygons, 24.7k samples across the shore): game water but drawn land: median 3 m, p99 12 m, max 15 m deep; the other way up to 22 m. With a 12 m margin (centre plus four probes): residual false water 29 of 24.7k at 10 m / 13 at 12.5 m, max 6 m. Pack water sources: Hamburg and Sydney OSM, New York TIGER 2020 (not measured against the basemap). Sydney Harbour's inner water comes from the NSW extract (not cached), so only the open coast was measured.

Fix: `RunMask.shore` = `WATER_MARGIN` 12 m (params.rs), packs only; the world's degree-celled mask 0. Probes off the raster do not veto. Test `shore_margin_keeps_the_bank_dry_and_the_channel_wet` (cost.rs): 50 m channel straight and diagonal still wet, raster edge not a shore.

Real save T-007-real-nyc.save with the NYC pack mask (temporary test, removed): 182 of 1647 edges get less water; wet metres 83,639 -> 67,998 (-19%, 15.6 km); sum of edge costs -2,933 and built cost 736,726 -> 733,794 (-0.4%) in the game's units.

Not changed: app/src/workers/lineProfile.ts reads the pack water rows itself (profile chart), without the margin.
