# T-1004: the United States as whole-country zones (design, 2026-10-23)

Anita (2026-10-23): "The US next, after China: plan it as China was, whole states as zones beside the
metros already built. Design first." Her standing rule for a whole country: the plan goes ahead on its
recommended options and the open questions are listed after. A design, not a build: measured on a dry
run in memory (`notes/t1004/`: `dry_us.py` makes the units and their cache `dry_state.pkl` beside
itself, `dry_us2.py` the metros and zones, `tables.py`, `waves.py`, `draw.py`; outputs in
`artifacts/trainworld/t1004/`; nothing in `pipeline/`, `data/` or the plan changed). It assumes T-1005's wrap guard (in
`zone_groups`, used by the dry run) and T-1000's pull cap with its 1% add-back are in before the
first US join. Picture: `artifacts/trainworld/t1004-zones.png` (lower 48).

## In plain words

- **Zones.** **45 new metro rows** and **about 196 countryside zones**, beside the 13 US packs already
  built (New York, Philadelphia, Boston, Washington, Baltimore, Providence, New Haven, Hartford,
  Springfield and four Connecticut and Massachusetts county zones). Metros are the GHSL commuting
  areas over 1M, as in Europe, made of whole counties: Los Angeles 15.6M, Chicago 8.9M, Dallas,
  Houston, Atlanta, Miami ... down to Honolulu 1.0M. Countryside zones are counties grown **within
  their state** to at least **0.5M** (cap 1.5M; median 0.79M), every county in one: no dead space,
  no exclaves. Small states come out as one zone each (Montana, Wyoming, the Dakotas, Vermont, New
  Hampshire), which is the "whole states as zones" reading.
- **Sources.** The tier 0 pipeline (OSM, GHSL, the runner), with a small new `usa` module that swaps in
  what the US has and the built US packs already use: **2020 census blocks** for people, **LODES** for
  jobs and their sectors, the **US commute curve** and its **state line factor**, Census county lines.
  Schools, universities, airports and hospitals stay tier 0 (OSM, Wikidata) for now.
- **The built US packs stay as they are.** Only their joint commute solve changes: the Northeast leaves
  its old separate solve and joins the general one, so new neighbours can join it (task T-1014).
- **Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico in; the four small territories out.** Alaska as one zone along its
  road and rail belt (Anchorage to Fairbanks), the bush dead space; Hawaii as Honolulu plus two island
  zones; Puerto Rico as six zones of its own country. Guam, the Northern Marianas, American Samoa and
  the US Virgin Islands (each under 0.2M) wait for their own regions of the world.
- **Waves.** A pilot (Georgia and Florida, 22 rows), then the South (63 rows), the Midwest (66), the
  West (51) and last the Northeast's neighbours with Puerto Rico (39). About **241 rows**, **10-14 hours
  of machine time** in all at 10-14 cores (5-6 sittings), about 20-35M agent tokens, about 15 GB of
  downloads (OSM 12.2 GB, deleted region by region) and 4-5 GB kept.
- **Before wave 1**: the `usa` module and the planner's US rules (T-1015, T-1016). Before wave 4: the
  Northeast's move (T-1014). The rest is ready.

### Units and regions

- **Units: Census 2020 counties** (`data/raw/cb_2020_us_county_500k.zip`, on disk, public domain),
  GEOID as the unit id, the state as parent. Not geoBoundaries' USA ADM2 (TIGER 2018, names only):
  the GEOIDs are what blocks, LODES and the built packs' county lists key on, and 2020 counties are
  the ones the 2020 blocks nest in (Connecticut's old counties; LODES 8 uses them too). Puerto Rico's
  78 municipios from the same file, as country PRI.
- **Merge region: the state.** Zones never cross a state line except by the fallbacks above.
- **People in the planner: GHSL 2025** (346.4M over the counties and territories, against the 2020
  census's 331.4M for the states and DC: five years of growth and GHSL's spread). Only the plan's
  grouping uses it; the packs count census blocks.

**U** used by the 13 built US packs, **N** usable, **-** none.

Why the module rather than tier 0 alone (China did tier 0 first): the US sources are national, open,
already coded (`build_city.blocks_for_state`, `jobs_for_state`, `group_quarters`, `wfh`) and clearly
better than GHSL and the jobs proxy; the new packs join one solve with 13 LODES packs, so the same
jobs source on both sides of a pack line keeps the balance honest; and a later swap would rebuild every
US row. LODES OD is a check, not an input (county pairs, notes/T-198.md), as for the built packs.

  own frames (`market_zones.py`, as every other zone), gives them `solve_region` "northeast"
  (`intercity.SOLVE_REGION_PACKS`), retires T-580's park rule and the Northeast branch of
  `solve_unit`, and solves them once with `joint_gravity --packs` alone (nothing new beside them yet)
  to show the change: their factors and commutes move a little (the solve is the same gravity with
  Anderson mixing to 1e-8), and the real save's demand final with them (report the figure; T-479's
  stale sites go away with the sites). Their region keys are already state FIPS codes, which is what
  the new packs will carry, so state lines between old and new packs take 0.23 and lines inside a state
  1.0 (`joint_gravity`'s region table).

  Seams are held fixed as China's provinces were; T-1000's cap bounds the huge western zones' factors

- **Realms** (`app/src/game/realms.json`, by hand): split "US east" and "US west" into the Census

- **Time zones**: from each row's origin (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska, Hawaii,

  panhandle, west Texas, the Dakotas, Idaho) runs on its origin's.
- **Borders abroad**: Canada 0.05 (`borders.json`), Mexico the 0.05 default; Buffalo may join Toronto's

- **Names**: US counties already have English names. A one-county zone keeps the county's name ("Kern

## 7. Risks (China's, and the US's own)

1. **The Northeast's move** (T-1014) touches the only packs with real saves on them; its demand shift
   is the one Anita will notice. Measure the real save before and after.
2. **Enclaves**: Virginia's independent cities (Charlottesville, Winchester, Lynchburg...) sit inside
   or between counties. As units under 100,000 they join the county round them by the longest
   border, and outlines come from TIGER counties (no OSM filled holes, so no hen/hes repeat).
3. **The join's balance**: huge western zones (Montana, rural Nevada, Wyoming) held fixed against
   their neighbours stalled Xinjiang (T-998); T-1000's cap should bound them. If a division still
   stalls, `SOLVE_ALONE` as Xinjiang.
4. **Parked rows**: drive factors in the Appalachians, Ozarks and Rockies; long mean commutes in the
   big zones (relax with reasons, as China); browser flakes (rerun alone). T-997 (a parked unit
   breaking later units' fixed lists and the market step) is still open and bit China's wave 4: worth
   fixing before the first US join.
5. **Big metros**: Los Angeles at 15.6M and about 65,000 km² (San Bernardino's desert) and Chicago at 8.9M;
