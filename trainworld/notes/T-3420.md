# T-3420: hide or show lines one by one and by track level

Built 2026-11-16 by agent visibility, to Anita's scratch pad design (2026-11-16). Rules: docs/ui-inspectors.md "Hiding
lines", docs/ui-panels.md "Settings" (Show levels). Decision: DECISIONS.md 2026-11-16.

## As built

- Lines tab (after Anita's review on 8800, 2026-11-16): the new bus and ferry line buttons are gone from the actions
  row (the Build tab's Bus and Ferry buttons and U and O stay). The eye button turns hide mode on: a small checkbox per
  row (`.vis-check`, 12 device px against Settings' 14; the inner box an inset ring, since a centred ::after drew
  blurred at the rows' fractional positions; rows kept at 24 px). The eye again shows every line (no Show all button),
  so the save holds hidden lines only while hide mode is on; a load with hidden lines opens hide mode. Hidden rows'
  bullet and name are faded at 40%. The first version (eye icons per row, an always-laid-out Show all) is replaced.
- Settings > Show: "Show levels" over the seven level buttons (`LevelToggles` in ui/widgets.tsx), all lit; a click
  hides or shows the level. Under them "Buses and ferries" (`hideBuses`, page session): main.ts adds every bus and
  ferry line to the hidden set, so the bus layer, the trains and the picks drop them. Lit buttons drop the track tool's dark outline (seven lit ones read as one black frame).
- State: game/lineVisibility.ts (signals; hidden lines go to the clock worker as `hideLines` and come back as
  `hiddenLines` on a load; the header's `hidden`, alive lines only; a new line or a copy taking a deleted line's id is
  shown). main.ts turns it into map/visibility.ts `hidden` (line numbers with twins, level bits, station and node
  levels).
- Drawing: the line colour texture's alpha is 0 for a hidden line; the stroke shader drops line strokes with alpha 0 and
  track and line strokes whose two ends are on hidden levels, and fades a ramp's alpha toward a hidden level; the
  train shader drops a hidden line's trains and a car whose sample is on a hidden level (a per-sample level texture,
  `slevel`, built only while a level is hidden); stations on a hidden level get `a_sel` -1; grade marks on a hidden
  level are not drawn; bus and ferry lines are left out of busLayer's build. The stroke cache key carries `hidden.v`.
- Picks: `pickSkip` (per stroke set) in topSeg, so every topSeg user passes over hidden strokes; stations
  (`stationAt`), junctions, grade marks and a hidden line's trains are skipped too. Trains on a hidden level are not
  skipped by the pick (only drawn), a small gap.
