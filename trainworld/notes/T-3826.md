# T-3825 and T-3826 Schematic camera limits, cities by region, adding a neighbour

Agent schemcity, 2026-11-19/20, tree `.tw-trees/schemcity`, Vite 6209. Anita, 11-20, after trying the cities list
(T-3409) for the first time: the drawing was off screen so it looked broken; a half-view zoom-out limit; a divider
before the cities button; the "Drawing diagram" bar on a cities change; a region choice (then, through the
orchestrator: "the regions should expand into dropdowns ... if part of a region is selected the checkbox ... semifilled
/ minus sign"); hover a neighbouring city and click to add it. Rules now in docs/ui-diagram.md (camera paragraph and
"Chosen cities").

## Why it was off screen

`compose()` keeps the camera on the region it is on by finding the old drawing's node nearest the view's middle in the
new drawing. A cut layout is a new sheet: with Philadelphia ticked from a view of New York, the old node is in no new
part, so `cx, cy` stay in the old drawing's units, wherever that falls in the new one; and even when found, the same
station sits somewhere else in the cut drawing.

## What changed (all page side: `diagram/view.ts`, `ui/DiagramButton.tsx`, `ui/style.css`, i18n)

- **Pan limit** (`clampView`, run in `draw()`, so every pan, zoom, compose, resize and check camera call): the view's
  middle is kept inside the drawn box (`d.box`), then the station nearest the middle is kept at least 40 px inside the
  view (the box alone was not enough closer in: at pitch 7 a box corner can be empty). At the zoom-out limit all of the
  box shows.
- **Zoom-out limit**: `MIN_SHARE` 0.1 -> 0.5 (the larger side of the drawn box at least half the view's matching side).
  `place()` applies it too, so a small network now opens larger: a 3-station, 4 km line in a new game opens at a pitch
  of 16.2 px (was 7). A drawing that fits the view on opening is centred whole (it opened on the station nearest the
  map's centre, cut off at the view's edge).
- **Ghosts only on the new drawing**: none while an older drawing stands in for the choice's (else Philadelphia's own
  stations in the whole drawing gave a spot for Philadelphia); the hovered one is dropped on every compose; a click
  matches it by city, so a compose between press and release does not turn the add into a plain click.
- **Fit on a cities change**: `syncCut` notes a change of the cities part of its key (not a load's or an edit's new
  stations) as `choice = "asked"`; the next graphs from the clock worker turn it to `"laying"` and arm `fitChoice`;
  `compose()` fits (`fitView`: 0.9 of the view, no closer than the opening pitch of 7 px or the zoom-out limit) whenever
  what it shows is only the new plan's regions (not the old drawing standing in), until all are drawn or the player
  pans or zooms. The fit takes in the add spots below.
- **Indicator**: while `choice` is set, every region of the new plan counts until laid out (the T-3812 let-off for an
  older drawing covering its stations does not apply), and the bar shows at 0 from the click until the graphs come.
  Closing the diagram clears it.
- **Divider**: `<i class="diagram-rule">`, 1 px of `--rule`, 16 px tall (14 px at this --ui), 2 px margin each side
  plus the row's 8 px gap; only when both the mode buttons and the cities button show.
- **Cities by region**: realms (`game/realms.ts`, the regions the map shows and hides as one, "US northeast"), by name;
  each row a chevron (`btn icon mini chevron`, as the Lines tab's groups; aria-label the realm's name, no hover), a box
  and the name. Box: ticked when all its listed cities are chosen, a minus (accent, on the tint) when some are; ticking
  it adds all, unticking removes all. Opened, its cities (16 px further in, boxes under the region's name). A list of
  one region opens on it (`opened` holds the regions flipped from their start). Cities in no realm follow, bare.
  "All cities" stays first: an empty choice is the whole network, stations in no zone included, which every city ticked
  is not.
- **Adding a neighbour** (`ghostsOf`, `similarity`, `ghostAt`, paint at the end of `paintNow`): with cities chosen and
  the cut drawing shown, each unchosen city (zone) with a built station or bus/ferry stop within `REGION_JOIN_KM` (500
  km, so a click joins the same sheet) of a drawn station gets a spot. From the drawn station nearest any of the city's
  stations: the ground offset to the city's stations' middle, turned by a turn/scale/mirror fit of that region's ground
  places to its drawn ones (least squares, mirror tried both ways); placed as far as the fit's scale puts it but no
  further than the box grown by 6% of its larger side plus 3 pitches, then stepped out until that far from every drawn
  station; two spots nearer than that pushed apart. Hover over nothing drawn within 40 px of a spot: the realm buttons'
  circle (16 px, white, #8c8c8c rim; dark #8a857f, #141517 rim) at 0.85 alpha, cursor pointer, tag "Click to add
  Philadelphia". A press that began with the spot showing and ends on it, without a drag, adds the city; a tap (no hover
  first) does the usual click. Spots are drawn on the view only, never the picture or a share. `twDiagram.ghosts()` for
  checks (page px, and `raw`: how far past the box the fit alone would put it, in box sides).

## Calls made

- Realms as the regions: the only top-level grouping the game has (the map's dark regions with a button each); no new
  grouping, no new names.
- 500 km for a neighbour: the regions' join distance, so an added city lays out on the same sheet as the chosen ones.
- Spots pulled in to just past the box: on New York the fit alone puts Philadelphia 0.2 box sides out, which the pan
  limit allows but not at every zoom; a real-distance spot for a small chosen city (Philadelphia alone, New York 130 km
  off) would be many box widths away and unreachable.
