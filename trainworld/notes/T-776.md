# T-776: the registry split (2026-10-20)

## The split

`app/src/game/registry.json` had 176 zones in 630 KB on disk (208 KB compact). 175 KB of the
compact 208 was `marketRow` (the world market's row per zone: `air` 67 KB, `near` 31 KB, `commute`
14 KB, the sums); everything else is what the app reads before a zone opens. Only the demand
workers read the rows, and only to make `MarketPacks` once a market pack opens. The registry was
bundled four times (the main chunk and the clock, demand and realRoute workers).

- **Index** (`registry.json`, bundled): every field but `marketRow`, plus `country` from the row.
  106 KB on disk, 55 KB compact (cities 40 KB, `market.places` 15 KB): about 310 bytes a zone.
- **Rows** (`market-rows.json`): {zone id: row} in registry order, one row a line. 154 KB, about
  0.9 KB a zone; a hashed asset in a build (`new URL(..., import.meta.url)` in demandClient.ts), so
  vite.config.ts needs no copy rule and the 8800 snapshot ships its commit's rows.
- **Why one rows file, not one per zone or country**: the market needs every place's row at once
  (pairs from served places to every other place, each place's departures to places with no zone),
  so per-zone files would all be fetched together anyway. A split that loads only nearby countries
  needs the Rust market to work from partial rows: T-777.

## Measurements

Production builds in the scratchpad, `vite preview` on 6179, headless Chrome, fresh profile per run,
nine runs before, six after, in alternating rounds; medians. Menu: navigation to the start menu's New
game button. New game: the click on Start to New York's zone open (bare). Real save: import to New
York's pack and market pack open in every worker, and to demand final. A first version fetched the
rows as soon as a zone started to open for the solves (a new game's viewed zone too, before its
reach is known): a new game's zone opened about 0.2 s later (median 2.18 s in six runs), so the rows
now download beside the first market pack, which is several times their size.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| main chunk | 1,866 KB | 1,720 KB |
| demand / clock / realRoute workers | 272 / 302 / 251 KB | 127 / 156 / 105 KB |
| JS loaded by the start menu | 2,318 KB | 2,176 KB |
| market-rows.json (fetched once a zone starts to open) | - | 154 KB |
| start menu | 1.18 s | 1.19 s |
| New game, Start to zone open | 1.93 s | 1.97 s |
| real save, import to market open | 3.27 s | 3.27 s |
| real save, import to demand final | 35.6 s | 35.9 s |

Spread within a side: about 0.1 s on the menu, 0.2 s on the new game, 0.15 s on the market open,
0.9 s on the final, so the two sides are the same.

Rail trips a day on the real save: 9,672,887 in every run before and after (the market's input is
the same JSON in the same order).

At 176 zones the saving (145 KB of JS per bundle) is below the timing noise; what it buys is the
growth: at Europe's ~1,000 zones the bundled index is about 0.3 MB instead of about 3 MB per bundle,
and the rows (about 0.9 MB) load off the start path.
