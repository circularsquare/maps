# Angola (taxonomy/ao2024.py). RGPH 2024, mother tongue (língua materna), ages 2 and over.
# Parents and languages other fragments already define are repeated with the same label and no
# colour, so their owners can retune them (br.txt moved Portuguese while this was being
# written) and a relabel still shows up as a clash, not a dangle. Kimbundu, Umbundu, Kongo, Kwanyama and Lingala were
# defined bare by fi.txt and pl.txt (a few immigrants each); Angola is where they are big, so
# they get their colours here (build.py lets a later fragment colour a node defined bare).
#
# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog/languages.csv): every language
# below is Atlantic-Congo (atla1278) there, with no finer levels in that file. The groups are
# the conventional Guthrie ones: Ngangela (Glottolog Nyemba, nyem1238) under zm.txt's
# Chokwe-Lunda group (K.10-20), with Luvale and Mbunda; Nyaneka (nyan1305) and Nkhumbi
# (nkhu1238) as the Nyaneka-Nkhumbi group (R.10). Fiote, the Kongo of Cabinda (Woyo, Vili,
# Yombe), is a language node beside Kongo rather than under it, because Kongo is a language
# node other countries draw and making it a group would wash theirs out.
#
# Colours, for the neighbours on the Angolan ground:
#   Umbundu red (the central plateau: Huambo, Bié, Benguela); Kimbundu magenta to its north
#   (Malanje, Cuanza Norte, Bengo, Luanda's hinterland); Kikongo green further north (Uíge,
#   Zaire), with Fiote a pale yellow-green in Cabinda; all three apart from Portuguese, which
#   br.txt makes a blue-violet and which is the first language of most townspeople everywhere;
#   Kwanyama orange in Cunene, next to Namibia's Oshiwambo orange (the same people across the
#   border); Nyaneka a light gold and Nkhumbi a dark green between Nyaneka and Kwanyama (Huíla,
#   Cunene); Ngangela a teal in the south-east, near zm.txt's Mbunda, which INE counts inside
#   it; Lingala a light cyan (Uíge, Lunda Norte, Luanda), apart from Kikongo's green and
#   Chokwe's pale yellow. Chokwe and Luvale keep zm.txt's colours.
