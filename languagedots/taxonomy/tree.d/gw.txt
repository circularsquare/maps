# Guinea-Bissau (taxonomy/gw2009.py). RGPH 2009, principal ethnic language ("dialecto") of
# Guinean nationals, national table, placed by região through each etnia's answers.
# Parents and languages other fragments already define are repeated with the same label and no
# colour: Balanta, Manjak, Mankanya, the Jola group (sn), Fula, Mandinka, Nalu, Susu, Soninke.
#
# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog): everything new is Atlantic
# (atla1278), flat under it as sn.txt and gn.txt do: Papel (pape1239; Glottolog's
# Manjaku-Mankanya-Pepel group, whose other two sn.txt already put flat), Biafada (biaf1240),
# Mansoanka (mans1259), the Bijagó languages (kamo1256, bidy1244; one census answer), and the
# Balanta Mané (no code of their own; a sibling of Balanta). Ejamat (ejam1238, "Felupe") goes
# under sn.txt's Jola group, where Glottolog puts it (Her-Ejamat, inside Jola).
#
# "Sem dialecto" gets a leaf under `other`: no ethnic language named as principal, which the
# census files Kriol, Portuguese and foreign answers under alike (taxonomy/gw2009.py).
#
# Colours, for neighbours on the ground. Bissau and Biombo: Papel purple beside Balanta's light
# cyan, Manjak's dark teal, Mankanya's pale mint. Oio and Cacheu: Balanta Mané rose and
# Mansoanka pale yellow beside Balanta, Mandinka green, Manjak, Felupe (Jola blue). Quinara and
# Tombali: Biafada ochre beside Balanta, Fula red, Nalu (gn's generated mauve). Bijagós: Bijagó
# orange-brown, an archipelago of its own.
