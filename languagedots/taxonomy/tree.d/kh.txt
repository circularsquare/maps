# Colours. Khmer is nearly everyone and pale pink, so each minority is set off from it:
#   * Chamic in the Austronesian blues. Cham (the Mekong and Tonle Sap belt, beside Khmer) a
#     clear teal; Jarai (Ratanak Kiri, beside Tampuan and Kreung) a lighter sky blue.
#   * Bahnaric violet, apart from Khmer's pink and Vietnamese's purple by hue and lightness.
#     Tampuan, the biggest in Ratanak Kiri, a deep violet; Kreung and Brao (its neighbours to
#     the west and north) a light lavender and a mid blue-violet; Bunong (Mondul Kiri) a rose.
#   * Kuy a red (Preah Vihear, Kampong Thom, Stung Treng: Khmer and Lao country).
#   * Pearic a warm orange-pink, scattered in the Cardamoms and Kampong Speu.
