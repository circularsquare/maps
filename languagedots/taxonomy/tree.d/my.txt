# Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog, classification paths) for the new nodes:
#   * North Borneo (nort3253) holds the Sabahan languages (Dusunic: Kadazan Dusun cent2100,
#     Rungus rung1259, Sabah Bisaya saba1267; Paitanic: Upper Kinabatangan uppe1427, the Orang
#     Sungai's languages; Murutic: Tagal Murut taga1273; Ida'an idaa1241) and the Sarawak ones
#     (North Sarawakan: Kelabit kela1258, Lundayeh lund1271 with Lun Bawang lunb1237 as its
#     dialect, Kenyah, Penan pena1271, Berawan bera1264, Kiput, Narom, Miri, Sa'ban; Central
#     Sarawak: Melanau mela1255, Kajang kaja1255, Ukit, Bukitan, Sihan; Kayan bara1370). One
#     group, as a reader would expect "the languages of Sabah and Sarawak's interior" to be,
#     with the census's groups as flat leaves; Glottolog's five middle levels are not drawn.
#   * Bidayuh (bida1240) is Land Dayak (land1261), outside North Borneo: a leaf of Austronesian.
#   * Bajau: Sabah's Bajau speak West Coast Bajau (west2560) and the Sama languages (cent2092,
#     sout2918), Sama-Bajaw (sama1302): one leaf, "Bajau", under ph.txt's Sama-Bajaw group,
#     since the census does not say which.
#   * Suluk speak Tausug (taus1251), Iranun Iranun (iran1262), Balabak Molbog (molb1237): all
#     Greater Central Philippine, so leaves of the existing Philippine group.
#   * Brunei Malay (brun1242) and Kedayan (keda1250, a dialect of it in Glottolog, but a census
#     group of its own in both Sabah and Sarawak) are Malayic leaves.
#   * Aslian (asli1243): the Orang Asli's Senoi (Semai sema1266, Temiar temi1246, Jah Hut...) and
#     Negrito (Jahai, Batek, Kensiu: North Aslian) peoples speak Aslian languages. The census
#     names peoples, not languages, so both sit on the group, unnamed.
#
