# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog), "families as most readers know
# them" (AGENT_BRIEF §3):
#   * Grassfields (Glottolog's Wide Grassfields, under Southern Bantoid), a group of its own
#     under Bantoid, with Bamileke (Glottolog's Bamileke, inside Mbam-Nkam) as a subgroup:
#     Ghomala' (Bandjoun; Glottolog lists it only as the parent of its Ngemba dialect ngem1253),
#     Fe'fe' fefe1239 (Bafang; Nufi), Medumba medu1238 (Bangangte), Yemba yemb1246 (Dschang),
#     Ngiemboon ngie1241 (Batcham), Ngombale ngom1271 (Babadjou), Ngwe ngwe1238 (Nweh; the
#     Bangwa of Lebialem). "Mbouda" is a card label naming the Bamboutos town, whose chiefdoms
#     speak Ngombale, Ngiemboon and Mengaka: a leaf, as the card printed it.
#     The rest of Grassfields: Bamun bamu1253, Mungaka mung1266 (Bali Nyonga), Bafanji
#     bafa1249 (Mbam-Nkam Nun); Ngemba ngem1255 and Mankon (its dialect mank1253, a card label
#     of its own), Bafut; Limbum limb1268, Yamba yamb1251 (Nkambe); Lamnso' lamn1239, Kom
#     komc1235, Oku okuu1243, Aghem aghe1239, Mmen mmen1238 (Ring); Meta' meta1238, Moghamo
#     (mogh1246, a Meta' dialect the survey names apart), Ngie ngie1242, Ngwo ngwo1241, Oshie
#     (Ngie-Oshie), Mundani mund1327 (Momo); Befang (nucl1410, Menchum).
#   * Other Bantoid, flat under Bantoid as ng.txt has Ejagham: Kenyang keny1279 (Mamfe),
#     Esimbi esim1238 (Tivoid), Tikar tika1246 (Northern Bantoid), Ncane ncan1245 and Noni
#     (Beboid).
#   * Bantu (zone A), flat under Bantu beside us.txt's Ewondo, Basaa, Duala and Fang: Eton
#     eton1253, Bene (an Ewondo variety; the survey names it apart), Bulu bulu1251, Ntumu (a
#     Fang variety), Mvele (Bamvele, bamv1238), Batanga bata1285, Akoose akoo1248, Mokpwe
#     mokp1239 (Bakweri), Oroko orok1266, Bakundu (Lundu-Balong; not under this name in
#     Glottolog), Mbo, Bakoko bako1249, Abo (Bankon), Bafia bafi1243, Tunen tune1261 (Banen),
#     Yambassa (Glottolog's Yambassa A.60 group, as respondents named it), Nomaande noma1260
#     (Lemande), Makaa maka1304, Koonzime koon1245, Bajwe'e, Pol pomo1271. cf.txt's Kako reused.
#   * Chadic: Mafa mafa1239, Kapsiki (Psikye psik1239; Nigeria's Kamwe kamw1239 is Glottolog's
#     other Higic language, kept apart), Massa (Masana masa1322), Guiziga (North and South
#     Giziga, one answer), Kotoko (Glottolog's Kotoko-Buduma languages, one answer), Wandala
#     wand1278 (Mandara), Musgum musg1254, Guidar gida1247, Jimi jimi1254, Daba nucl1683, Gavar
#     gava1241, Hina, Mada mada1293, Podoko (Parkwa park1239), Zulgo (zulg1242), Lele.
#   * Adamawa, cf.txt's group (Glottolog's Cameroun-Ubangian): Tupuri tupu1244, Mundang
#     mund1325, Dii diii1241, Fali (North and South Fali, Glottolog's "Adamawa Fali" under
#     unclassified Volta-Congo; Adamawa as most readers know it), Mubako (the speech of the Bali
#     chiefdoms Bali Gashu, Balikumbat and Bali Gham, a Samba Leko variety; ng.txt's Chamba is
#     not split into a group to hold it, so a sibling).
#   * Cameroonian Pidgin, beside Nigerian Pidgin under English-based creoles.
#
# Colours. Bantu's and Chadic's generated slots are crowded (sources/cd.md §6), so every new
# leaf there is hand-coloured off the generated grid (chroma 0.20 or 0.08 against Bantu's 0.14;
# 0.18 or 0.06 against Chadic's 0.12; Adamawa 0.21 or 0.09 against 0.15; Bantoid 0.19 or 0.07),
# and no node outside Cameroon moves (checked against a build without this fragment). Grassfields
# and Bamileke are new groups whose members are generated around them. South: Ewondo red, Eton
# pale pink, Bulu deep orange, Basaa olive, Bafia orange. North: Fulfulde (us.txt's brick), Mafa
# gold, Kapsiki pale green, Massa green, Tupuri violet, Mundang deep blue, Guiziga yellow.
# West and North-West: Bamileke violets, the rest of Grassfields blues.
