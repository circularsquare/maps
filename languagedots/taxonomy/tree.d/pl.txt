# Poland, NSP 2021, language used at home (taxonomy/pl2021.py). GUS prints about 350 labels,
# most of them a few dozen people answering from a list of the world's languages. Checked against
# Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog/languages.csv): Silesian (sile1253), Kashubian (kash1274), Upper
# and Lower Sorbian (uppe1395, lowe1385) are West Slavic; Lemko (lemk1239) is a Rusyn dialect in
# Glottolog, but GUS prints it apart from Ruthenian ("ruski") and Ukrainian, so it is a sibling of
# Rusyn, not a child (a child would make Rusyn a group, drawn washed out). Wymysorys (wymy1235),
# the Germanic language of Wilamowice, is Continental West Germanic. Hupa (hupa1240) is
# Athabaskan; Kanuri is Saharan (Nilo-Saharan as most readers know it, AGENT_BRIEF §3); Hiri Motu
# is a pidgin and sits under Creoles; Esperanto (espe1235) is an "artificial language" and sits
# under `other`. Caddoan (cadd1255), Muskogean (musk1252), Tungusic (tung1282) and Abkhaz-Adyge
# (abkh1242) are new roots, each holding a few dozen people in Poland.
#
# The Polish dialects GUS prints (gwara cieszyńska, góralska, kociewska, wielkopolska, kurpiowska)
# are leaves beside Polish under West Slavic: drawn as named, not merged into Polish.
#
# Colours: Silesian (Upper Silesia, Opole) and Kashubian (Pomerania) sit beside Polish's dark teal
# and German's blue, so Silesian is a light yellow-green and Kashubian a pale cyan. Lemko (Lower
# Silesia's copper belt and the Beskid Niski) is a darker green than Ukrainian's yellow-green.
# Podlachian (Hajnówka, Bielsk Podlaski) sits beside Belarusian's green and Polish, so it is a mid blue.
