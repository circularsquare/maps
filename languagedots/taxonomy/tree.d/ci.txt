# Côte d'Ivoire (RGPH 2021, the national language spoken most). Nodes for taxonomy/ci2021.py.
#
# Only the 13 languages Tableau 4.20 names by région are drawn; the other 82 labels the census
# coded are printed nationally only and sit together on `nigercongo` per région.
#
# Middle levels are the conventional ones, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog), which
# makes Kru (krua1234) and Mande (mand1469) families of their own and Senufo (senu1239) and
# Kulango (kula1277) branches of Atlantic-Congo outside Gur; here, as most readers know them,
# Kru and Mande sit under Niger-Congo (as au.txt and ca.txt already have them) and Senufo, Lobi
# and Kulango under Gur.
#   * Kwa: Baoulé (baou1238), Anyi (anyi1245; the census's Agni), Attié (atti1239; Akyé),
#     Abbey (abee1242; Glottolog's Abé). Kwa's existing leaves (Akan, Nzema, Ga...) are flat
#     under `nigercongo.kwa`, so these are too, without a Tano/Bia level.
#   * Mande: Dioula is pl.txt's `nigercongo.mande.dyula`, Yacouba ou Dan is au.txt's
#     `nigercongo.mande.dan`; new: Maninka (the census's "Malinké ou Malinka") and Guro.
#   * Kru: Bété (a cluster: Gagnoa, Guiberoua, Daloa Bété) and Guéré (Glottolog's Wè Northern,
#     Southern and Western), each one census answer, so one leaf each.
#   * Gur: Senufo (one answer for the cluster; Tagbana, Djimini, Niarafolo, Nafana and Palaka are
#     printed apart nationally), Lobi, Kulango.
#
# Colours (AGENT_BRIEF §3): neighbours on the ground told apart, inside each family's part of the
# wheel. North: Senoufo orange, Dioula green, Malinké dark teal. North-east: Lobi brick, Koulango
# peach. East and south-east: Agni dark mustard, Attié yellow-olive, Abbey pink, Baoulé golden.
# West and centre-west: Dan leaf green, Guéré pale lime, Bété dark olive, Gouro pale cyan.
# Checked: every pair of named languages at 2% or more in a shared région is at least 0.079
# apart in OKLab (closest: Bété/Agni, Attié/Dan, both minor shares in Abidjan and the south).
# Dyula and Dan already existed (pl.txt, au.txt) without colours; they are tiny there.
