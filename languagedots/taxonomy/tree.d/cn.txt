# (taxonomy/cn2020.py, countries/cn.py). Nodes other fragments already define are repeated with
# identical labels (and colours where one gives them), so China does not depend on them.
#
# Han (everyone on Chinese) are drawn on their county's main dialect group, below the Sinitic line;
# the census itself does not split Mandarin, Cantonese, Wu, Min, Hakka...
#
# New groups, each because a nationality speaks several languages that no source splits:
#   * Loloish (Glottolog lolo1267, the Ngwi branch): the Yi nationality's Nuosu, Nasu, Nisu,
#     Sani, Azhe... are drawn on the group, unnamed. Lisu, Lahu, Hani and Jino are its leaves
#     (Glottolog lisu1250, lahu1253, hani1248, youl1235). Poland's `sinotibetan.yi` "Yi (Nuosu)"
#     is one Yi language and stays where it is.
#   * Qiangic (qian1263): Qiang (qian1264), Pumi (nort2723/sout2729), and unnamed, the Qiangic
#     and rGyalrongic languages of Sichuan's Tibetans (Gyalrong, Ergong, Ersu, Minyak, Zhaba...),
#     filed here as Chinese linguistics conventionally does.
#   * Hmongic (hmon1337): the Miao nationality's three Hmongic languages (Xong, Hmu, and the
#     Chuanqiandian cluster) unnamed on the group; Bunu (buna1273), spoken by Yao, and She
#     (shee1238) as leaves. `hmongmien.hmong` (Hmong of Laos and Thailand) stays a sibling.
# Kra-Dai, Mongolic, Turkic and Tungusic members are flat leaves, as `kradai.zhuang` and
# `tungusic.manchu` already are. Gelao is filed under Kra-Dai as Chinese and most Western
# linguistics do (Glottolog lists gela1261 under a bookkeeping node). Tshangla (tsha1245), spoken
# by most of China's Monpa nationality, is its own Sino-Tibetan branch in Glottolog, so a leaf
# under the root. Tsat (Utsul) is the Chamic language of Sanya's Hui, a leaf under Austronesian.
