# Central African Republic (RGPH03 2003, language commonly spoken). Nodes for taxonomy/cf2003.py.
#
# Families checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog). Middle levels are the conventional
# ones, which here mostly are Glottolog's own:
#   * Ubangian (Glottolog `Ubangi`, uban1244): Banda (Bandaic), Ngbandi (Ngbandic: Yakoma,
#     Sango Riverain, Ngbandjiri), Zande-Nzakara (Zandic), Ngbaka-Mba (Mundu-Baka, whose river
#     branch holds Ngbaka Ma'bo, Gbanziri, Monzombo and Kpala), Sere (Sereic). Kpatili is a
#     Glottolog bookkeeping entry, so it sits on Ubangian directly, where Ethnologue puts it.
#   * Gbaya-Manza-Ngbaka (gbay1279) is its own branch of North Volta-Congo in Glottolog and in
#     Monino's work, and Ubangian in Greenberg's; a group of its own here, under Niger-Congo.
#     It holds Mandja (Glottolog `Manza`), Ngbaka-Manza, Ngbaka Minagende, Ali, Bofi, Gbanu,
#     Suma, Bokoto, and the Gbaya dialect names the census prints (Kara is Glottolog's Gbaya
#     Kara and Lay its Lai, both Northwest Gbaya; Buli, Bokare, Budigiri, Gbaguiri, Gbadok,
#     Tongo, Bouar, Boda and Mboundjia are not in Glottolog and sit here because the census
#     files them in its Gbaya block, between Gbaya and Mandjia).
#   * Adamawa, for the Mbum languages (Glottolog `Mbumic`): Mbum, Kare, Pana, Tale (Glottolog
#     a dialect of Kare; the census prints it apart).
#   * Central Sudanic (cent2225) under Nilo-Saharan: Sara, Ngam, Yulu, Binga, Gula, Birri, and
#     Kresh (Glottolog's Kresh-Aja family; Central Sudanic in the conventional account). Barar is
#     not in Glottolog; the census files it in its Sara block, and Central Sudanic is the
#     likeliest home for it. Runga is Maban (maba1274), a group of its own.
#   * Bantu: Mpiemo, Kako (the census's "Kaka", 97% in Mambere-Kadei, where Kako is spoken),
#     Pomo, Mbati (the census's "Issongo"), Bonzio (a Mbati dialect in Glottolog, printed apart),
#     Bomitaba, Babango, Aka, Bobangi, Kari, Bodo.
#   * Fula: the census prints Fulfulde, Peulh, Mbororo and Fulata as four answers, so four nodes,
#     siblings under Atlantic (Fula is us.txt's leaf and stays one).
#
# Sango is ca.txt's `nigercongo.sango`, directly under Niger-Congo; it belongs inside Ngbandi
# with Yakoma, but moving it would change Canada's node, so it is reused where it is and only
# given a colour here (ca.txt sets none).
#
# Colours (AGENT_BRIEF §3): neighbours on the ground told apart. West (Ouham-Pende): Fula (us.txt's
# brick), Gbaya green, the Mbum languages blue-violet, Sara cyan. Centre: Gbaya green, Mandja
# dark teal, Banda pink, Sango pale yellow. East: Banda pinks, Yakoma amber, Nzakara and Zande
# violets. North-east: Runga blue, Arabic (tree.txt's pale green), Yulu pale blue, Sara cyan.
