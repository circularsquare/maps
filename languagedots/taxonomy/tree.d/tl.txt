# Timor-Leste, Census 2015, mother tongue (taxonomy/tl2015.py). Parents and languages other
# fragments already define are repeated identically, so Timor-Leste builds on its own.
#
# Two new groups, one per family as most readers know them (AGENT_BRIEF §3):
#   * `austronesian.timoric`, "Timoric": every Austronesian language of Timor the census names.
#     Glottolog splits them between Timor-Babar (timo1265: the Tetun pair, Galoli, Waima'a,
#     Naueti, Kairui-Midiki, Habun, Idaté, Lakalei, Baikeno, Maku'a, and the Wetar languages of
#     Atauro) and a Western Timor branch (west2945: Mambae, Kemak, Tukudede); "Timoric" is the
#     name Wikipedia and Hull use for the lot, and the map gains nothing from a second level.
#   * `papuan.timor_alor_pantar`, "Timor-Alor-Pantar" (Glottolog timo1261): Bunak, Fataluku,
#     Makasae, Makalero, Sa'ani. Under au.txt's `papuan` root, as sb.txt put the Central Solomons.
# The Tetun pair are new leaves inside Timoric, beside uk.txt's bare `austronesian.tetun`
# ("Tetun", used by uk, au and pl for an answer that does not say which Tetun): giving that node
# children would turn it into a group, and their named answers would then draw as unnamed.
#
# Every census label is a leaf, including Hull's two cover terms the census lists beside their
# members (Idalaka for Idaté-Lakalei, Kawaimina for Kairui-Midiki, Waima'a and Naueti) and the
# Atauro names (Atauran, Adabe, Dadu'a, Rahesuk, Raklungu, Resuk); taxonomy/tl2015.py says why.
# Adabe is a Glottolog bookkeeping entry (book1242), spoken on Atauro; it sits in Timoric with
# the island's other names rather than on a root of its own.
#
# Colours. Drawn by municipality, so the languages of one municipality mix and those are the
# pairs to tell apart. Tetun Prasa is everywhere and is a pale cream that sits behind the rest
# (a pale blue first, and it was English's near-white). Mambai (Aileu, Ainaro, Ermera, Liquica,
# Manufahi) a mid green; Kemak (Ermera, Bobonaro) a violet; Tokodede (Liquica) a pink; Bunak
# (Bobonaro, Covalima, Ainaro) a brick red; Tetun Terik (Covalima, Manufahi, Manatuto, Viqueque)
# a teal; Baikenu (Oecusse) a dark steel blue, clear of br.txt's Portuguese; Makasae (Baucau,
# Viqueque, Lautem) a dark orange beside Waima'a lavender and Midiki slate blue in Baucau, Naueti
# magenta and Kairui pale lilac in Viqueque, Fataluku light lime, Makalero olive green and Sa'ani
# dusty pink in Lautem; Galoli (Manatuto) a light orange beside Idaté's rose, Dadu'a's olive
# yellow (Mambai's green otherwise) and Habun; Lakalei (Manufahi) generated. The Atauro names
# sit close to some mainland colours, but their dots are on the island and the others are not.
