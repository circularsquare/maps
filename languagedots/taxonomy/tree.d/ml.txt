# Parents and languages other fragments already define are repeated with the same label and no
# colour. Bambara (ca.txt, fi.txt), Soninke (ca.txt), Fula (cf.txt, fi.txt, us.txt) and Mòoré
# were defined bare by countries that draw a few; Bambara, Soninke and Fula are coloured here,
# where they are big.
#
# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog), "families as most readers know
# them" (AGENT_BRIEF §3):
#   * Songhay (song1307) is its own family in Glottolog; here it is a group under Nilo-Saharan,
#     Greenberg's classification and the one most readers know. The census's one Songhay answer
#     is a leaf under it, so another country can name Zarma or Koyra Chiini beside it.
#   * Dogon (dogo1299) is its own family in Glottolog; here a group under Niger-Congo, with the
#     census's one Dogon answer as a leaf, for the same reason.
#   * Bomu (bomu1247, Bwamu) and Mamara (mama1271, Senufo) are Atlantic-Congo in Glottolog and Gur
#     by the usual classification, as ci.txt puts Senufo. Mamara sits beside `senufo`, not under
#     it, because `senufo` is a leaf Côte d'Ivoire draws on.
#   * Khassonké (xaas1235), Konabéré (nort2819, Northern Bobo Madaré), Marka (mark1256, the
#     census's Dafing), Duungooma (duun1242, the census's Samogo) and Bozo (bozo1252) are Mande,
#     flat under `nigercongo.mande` as Bambara, Maninka and Soninke already are.
#   * Hassaniyya (hass1238) is Arabic; it sits beside `arabic` (a leaf many countries use), as
#     `judeo_arabic` does. Tamasheq (tama1365) under Berber.
#
# Colours, for neighbours on the Malian ground:
#   South-west (Kayes, Kita, Nioro): Bambara an olive yellow (a light gold until 2026-10-05,
#   when Anita found it too close to Mooré's gold, 0.020; now 0.100, and greener, towards Mande), Maninka ci.txt's dark teal, Soninke
#   a light green, Fula a strong red, Khassonké a steel blue, Hassaniya a mid teal (also Nara).
#   South (Sikasso, Koutiala, San): Senufo ci.txt's orange, Mamara a dark olive, Bomu a light
#   apricot, Samogo a mid green, Marka a darker green.
#   Centre (Mopti, Bandiagara, Douentza): Dogon magenta, Bozo seafoam, Fula red, Bambara yellow,
#   Songhay blue.
#   North (Tombouctou, Gao, Kidal, Ménaka, Taoudenni): Songhay blue, Tamasheq amber, Arabic the
#   existing mint, Hassaniya mid teal.
