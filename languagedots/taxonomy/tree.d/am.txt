# Armenia, 2011 census mother tongue (taxonomy/am2011.py). Every node but one already exists in
# another fragment; this file repeats them, and their parents outside tree.txt, identically so
# Armenia does not depend on another country's fragment being in the build.
#
# One new node, Yezidi. Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog/languages.csv) has no Yezidi language: the
# speech of Armenia's Yezidis and Kurds is "Erevan Kurmanji" (erev1241), a dialect of Northern
# Kurdish (nort2641). The census prints Yezidi (`Եզդիերեն`, Armstat's English "Yezidian") apart
# from Kurdish, and in Armenia the two names follow the two communities' identities, so Yezidi
# is a sibling of Kurdish under Iranian, not its child (a child would turn Kurdish into a group,
# drawn as "language not named"). No glottocode: none names Yezidi alone.
#
# Colours, looked at for where these languages meet: Yezidi villages on the slopes of Aragats and
# the Ararat plain (Aragatsotn, Armavir, Ararat) among Armenian's light teal; Kurdish, much
# smaller, in the same villages; Assyrian villages (Verin Dvin, Arzni, Dimitrov) in Ararat and
# Kotayk; Russian (green) in Yerevan and the Molokan villages of Lori. Yezidi is hand-picked a
# saturated orange so it stands off Armenian's teal and off Kurdish's generated sand.
