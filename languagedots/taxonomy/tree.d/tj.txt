# Tajikistan, 2010 census native language (taxonomy/tj2010.py).
#
# The Uzbek tribal groups' own languages (Lakai, Kongrat, Durmen, Katagan, Yuz, Ming, Kesamir,
# Semiz): the census counts these groups as nationalities apart from Uzbeks, and each one's "own
# language" as an answer apart from Uzbek (tj2010.py says why). Glottolog (values.csv) has nothing
# below Northern Uzbek (nort2690) for any of them, so they are leaves beside `turkic.uzbek`, which
# cannot take children without turning into a group drawn as "language not named". The Lakai and
# Kongrat dialects are Kipchak Uzbek.
#
# "Afghan": the census's own language of the Afghans, which names no language (Dari, Pashto, or
# Parya, the Indo-Aryan speech of the Hisor valley's self-styled Afghans); a named leaf under
# `other`, as Azerbaijan's "Jewish".
#
# Colours. The tribal dialects live in Khatlon and the Districts of Republican Subordination beside
# Uzbek's pink (0.74 0.15 345) and Tajik's sand. Hand-picked in a magenta band next to Uzbek, so
# they read as Uzbek's neighbours but apart from it and from each other: Lakai (60,392) deeper and
# darker, Kongrat (37,831) pale; the three under 300 people are left generated.
