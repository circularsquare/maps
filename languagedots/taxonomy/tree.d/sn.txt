# Senegal (taxonomy/sn2023.py). RGPH-5 2023, the language each person aged 3+ speaks most often
# ("première langue la plus souvent parlée"), by région.
# Parents and languages other fragments already define are repeated with the same label and no
# colour. Wolof (bf, fi, us) and Mandinka (au) were defined bare by countries that draw a few;
# they are coloured here, where they are big. Fula, Soninke and Hassaniya keep ml.txt's colours.
#
# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog), "families as most readers know
# them" (AGENT_BRIEF §3). Everything new is Atlantic or Mande:
#   * Serer (sere1260 cluster), Wolof, Fula: flat under Atlantic, as Wolof and Fula already are.
#   * Jola: a group, with the census's Joola answer (the Jola cluster: Fonyi, Kasa, Karon, ...;
#     Glottolog jola1264) and Bayot (bayo1262) as leaves. Bayot is its own branch in Glottolog
#     and a Jola language in the usual classification (Sapir; Ethnologue's "Bak, Jola").
#   * Balanta (bala1300), Manjak (mand1419), Mankanya (mank1251): flat under Atlantic (the Bak
#     languages beside Jola; Glottolog's Joola-Manjaku and Balanta).
#   * Tenda: a group for Bassari (the census's Oniyan, bass1258), Bedik (Mënik, bedi1235) and
#     Wamey (Konyagi, the census's Womey, wame1240), Glottolog's Bassari-Bedik-Bapen and Wamey.
#   * Jaad-Badyara (the census's Kanjad, bady1239) and Bainouk-Gunyuño (Guñuun): flat under
#     Atlantic. Jaad is sometimes put with Tenda; left out of the group rather than claim it.
#   * Yalunka (the census's Jalunga, yalu1240): Mande (Susu-Yalunka), flat as Mandinka is.
#   * Tourka (Sénégal): printed among Senegal's national languages, 1,484 people, mostly in
#     Kaolack and Fatick, and not identified with any language (sources/sn.md); a node of its own
#     under `other`, as et.txt does for its unidentified names.
#
# Colours, for neighbours on the Senegalese ground:
#   North and centre (Louga, Saint-Louis, Matam, Kaffrine, Kaolack, Diourbel, Thiès, Fatick):
#   Wolof golden yellow, Fula ml.txt's strong red, Serer purple; Hassaniya ml.txt's teal (Saint-
#   Louis). South (Ziguinchor, Sédhiou, Kolda): Jola blue, Mandinka mid green, Balanta cyan,
#   Manjak a darker teal, Mankanya pale mint, Fula red, Wolof yellow. East (Tambacounda,
#   Kédougou): Fula red, Mandinka green, Soninke ml.txt's light green (lighter than Mandinka),
#   Yalunka dark olive, the Tenda languages generated around a pink-violet, away from the brown
#   of `africa_other` (31.5% of Kédougou).
