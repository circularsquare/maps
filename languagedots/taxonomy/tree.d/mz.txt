# Mozambique (taxonomy/mz2017.py). IV RGPH 2017, mother tongue (língua materna), aged 5 and over.
# Parents and languages other fragments already define are repeated with the same label and no
# colour, so their owners can retune them; Yao (pl.txt) and Shona (us.txt, bw.txt) were defined
# bare by countries that draw a few; Yao, and Mwani (ca.txt, label kept as "Mwani"), are coloured
# here, where they are big.
#
# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog/values.csv, classification):
#   * Makhuwa (P.30): Glottolog's Makua node (maku1247) holds Makua-Lomwe (Makhuwa, Lomwe),
#     Koti-Nathembo and Chuwaboic (Chuwabo, Lolo). One group here, conventional Guthrie P.30.
#   * Nyanja-Sena (N.30-40, zm.txt's group): Glottolog's Sena-Nyanja (sena1271) holds Nyanja,
#     Sena, Nyungwe and Barwe (Barwe is Senaic there, though Guthrie numbered it S.17).
#   * Shona (S.10, shon1250) holds Ndau, Manyika and Tewe. Shona itself is a leaf other countries
#     draw on, so the three hang from Bantu beside it, as bw.txt's Kalanga does.
#   * Tswa-Ronga (S.50, za.txt's group): Tsonga (Changana), Tswa, Ronga.
#   * Chopi (S.60, chop1242): Chopi and Gitonga, a new group.
#   * Yao and Makonde are Glottolog's Ruvuma (ruvu1234); Mwani is Sabaki, beside Swahili. All
#     three hang from Bantu, as Yao and Swahili already do.
#
# Colours, for the neighbours on the Mozambican ground:
#   The north: Emakhuwa a strong yellow (Nampula, Niassa, Cabo Delgado, 5.8 million); Yao a dark
#   teal beside it in Niassa, apart from Nyanja's magenta on the lake shore; Makonde crimson and
#   Mwani a sky blue on the Cabo Delgado coast, with Swahili (us.txt's orange) between them; Koti a
#   red on Nampula's coast at Angoche.
#   Zambézia: Lomwe green, Chuwabo cyan, Lolo a pale green, all apart from the yellow Makhuwa and
#   the dark purple Sena.
#   Tete, Manica and Sofala: Nyanja (zm.txt's magenta), Sena a dark purple, Nyungwe a light lilac,
#   Barwe a pale pink; the Shona varieties warm (Ndau orange, Tewe a light amber, Manyika brick
#   red) so they read apart from the Nyanja-Sena violets they border.
#   The south: Changana is za.txt's teal Xitsonga; Tswa a deeper blue-teal (Inhambane); Ronga a
#   pale mint (Maputo); Chopi coral and Gitonga a light orange on the Inhambane coast, warm
#   against the teals. Portuguese is br.txt's blue-violet and is in every town.
