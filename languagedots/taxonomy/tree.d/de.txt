# surveyed area (Westfalen-Lippe) is Westphalian, so NRW's share goes on nl's Westphalian node
# (Glottolog Westphalic, west2356); the other Laender mix Northern Low Saxon, Eastphalian and
# East Low German, so they get one leaf under the Low German group (a named label must not sit
# on the group). Light teal, away from German's mid blue across the whole north.
# North and Saterland Frisian (Glottolog nort2626, sate1242) are siblings of `frisian`, not its
# children: `frisian` is a leaf West Frisian draws in nl and elsewhere, and children would make it
