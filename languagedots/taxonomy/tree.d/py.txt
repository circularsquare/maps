# Paraguay, Censo 2002, household language (taxonomy/py2002.py). Families from Glottolog
# (data/raw/glottolog): Lengua-Mascoy (leng1261) is a new root, called Enlhet-Enenlhet as the
# languages' own scholarship now does: Enlhet Norte, Enxet Sur, Sanapana, Angaite, Toba-Enenlhet
# (the census's "Toba", the Toba-Maskoy people) and Guana of Paraguay (guan1268, NOT the Arawakan
# Guana/Terena of Brazil). Ybytoso (Ebitoso) and Tomaraho are Glottolog dialects of Chamacoco
# (cham1315, Zamucoan); the census names both, so each is a leaf under a new Chamacoco group
# beside Ayoreo. Ache (ache1246) is Tupian; set under Tupi-Guarani, its conventional subgroup.
# Nodes other fragments define are repeated only as parents or reused leaves, with the identical
# label and no colour.
#
# Colours, for the Chaco, where these sit beside Nivacle's blue, Qom's blue-violet, Ayoreo's
# magenta and the Mennonite colonies' German blue: Enlhet-Enenlhet takes the greens nothing in
# the Chaco uses, Enlhet Norte (the largest, around the colonies) a clear green, Enxet Sur
# (Presidente Hayes, by the river) yellow-green, the small ones paler or darker steps. Chamacoco
# is pink and red, beside Ayoreo's magenta in Alto Paraguay. Ache is a purple: its communities
# sit in Canindeyu, Caazapa and Alto Parana among Guarani, Mbya, Ava Guarani and Portuguese, and
# every orange or yellow was taken. Four colours owned by ar.txt and br.txt were moved for
