# Malawi (taxonomy/mw2018.py). 2018 PHC tribe by district, read as home language through
# Afrobarometer (sources/mw_census.py). Parents and languages other fragments define are repeated
# bare (their colours are theirs): Chewa, Nyanja, Ngoni (Nyanja variety), Kunda and Chikunda,
# Tumbuka and Senga (zm.txt), Yao and Nyungwe (mz.txt), Nyakyusa and Ndali (tz.txt), the
# Mambwe-Nyiha languages (zm.txt, tz.txt). Lambya, defined bare by zm.txt, is coloured here.
#
# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog/values.csv, classification):
#   * Malawi Lomwe (mala1256) and Malawi Sena (mala1475) are languages of their own in Glottolog,
#     beside Mozambique Lomwe and Sena, so they get nodes of their own under mz.txt's groups.
#   * Tonga (Nyasa) tong1321 is in Glottolog's Tumbuka group (tumb1252), so under zm.txt's
#     Tumbuka (N.20), not with Zambia's Botatwe Tonga.
#   * Chingoni: Glottolog's Ngoni (Nyanja) ngon1270 is zm.txt's `nyanja_sena.ngoni`; Ngoni
#     (Tumbuka) ngon1272, the Mzimba Ngoni's speech, is new here under Tumbuka.
#   * Mang'anja (mang1391) is a dialect of Nyanja: under Nyanja-Sena.
#   * Nkhonde is Glottolog's Ngonde (ngon1268), a dialect of Nyakyusa-Ngonde; Sukwa (sukw1242)
#     a dialect of Ndali. Both hang from Bantu beside tz.txt's Nyakyusa and Ndali, as tz.txt
#     hangs those.
#   * Kokola (koko1267, the survey's "Khokhola") is Glottolog's Lolo-Kokola, Chuwaboic, under
#     mz.txt's Makhuwa (P.30) group as Lolo is.
#
# Colours, for the neighbours on the Malawian ground: Chichewa is zm.txt's pale pink and covers
# the centre; Mang'anja a deeper rose in the Shire valley beside a dark Malawi Sena purple
# (Chikwawa, Nsanje); Malawi Lomwe green (Mulanje, Thyolo, Phalombe) apart from mz.txt's
# Mozambique Lomwe across the border by lightness; Yao is mz.txt's dark teal (Mangochi, Machinga);
# Tumbuka zm.txt's blue in the north, with Tonga a lighter cyan on the Nkhata Bay shore and
# northern Ngoni a pale lavender in Mzimba; Chitipa's Lambya brown-orange, Sukwa olive and
# Ndali (tz.txt) pale green; Nkhonde a magenta near tz.txt's Nyakyusa in Karonga.
