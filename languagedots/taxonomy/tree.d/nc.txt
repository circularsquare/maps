# New Caledonia, 2019 census (taxonomy/nc2019.py): the Kanak language(s) each person aged 15+
# speaks. ISEE prints 29 answers as "langues kanak"; every one gets a node (spec 3.1).
#
# `austronesian.oceanic.kanak` is an AREAL group, as `austronesian.oceanic.vanuatu` is: it holds
# the census's "speaks a Kanak language without saying which" (10,668+ people), so it must hold
# what the census files as Kanak. Under it, three Glottolog groups (data/raw/glottolog) and one
# outlier:
#   loyalty    Loyalty Islands (loya1239): Drehu, Nengone, Iaai
#   northern   Northern New Caledonian (nort3325): Extreme Northern and Mid-Northern
#   southern   Southern New Caledonian (sout3313): Mid-Southern and Extreme Southern
#   west_uvean Fagauvea, a Polynesian outlier on Ouvea (west2516, Nuclear Polynesian), filed by
#              ISEE among the Loyalty Islands' languages
# Glottolog joins loyalty and mainland as New Caledonian (newc1243); the extra "Mainland" level
# is left out for a shallower legend. Tayo, which ISEE also counts as Kanak, is a French-based
# creole (Glottolog tayo1238, under Macro-French) and sits under `creole.french_based`; the
# unnamed remainder therefore sits on a node that leaves it out, by at most Tayo's 1.6% of the
# named mentions (taxonomy/nc2019.py).
#
# Two census labels are dialect areas rather than single languages, each kept as one leaf as
# printed: "Dialectes de Voh-Kone" (Glottolog's Voh-Kone group: Haeke, Haveke, Hmwaveke, Bwatoo,
# Vamale, Waamwang) and "Dialectes de l'extreme sud" (Numee, Glottolog nume1242, whose varieties
# include Kwenyii of the Ile des Pins).
#
# COLOUR: the group sits in Oceanic's green-to-blue band; the big languages are hand-picked so
# that ground neighbours differ (Iaai | Fagauvea on Ouvea; Paici | Cemuhi | Ajie down the east
# coast; Xaracuu | Xaragure at Canala-Thio) and all stay clear of French's pink, which covers
# most of the map.
