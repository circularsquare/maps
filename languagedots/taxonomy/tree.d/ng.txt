# Middle levels, checked against Glottolog (data/raw/glottolog), "families as most readers know
# them" (AGENT_BRIEF §3):
#   * Volta-Niger, flat as us.txt has Yoruba and Igbo: Yoruboid (Igala igal1242, Ibaji ibaj1238
#     an Igala dialect the survey codes apart), Nupoid (Nupe nupe1254, Gbagyi gbag1258, Ebira
#     ebir1243, Bassa-Nge, Ganagana, Gade gade1242; Glottolog's "Ebira-Nupoid" under
#     Benue-Congo), Idomoid (Idoma idom1241, Agatu agat1236, Alago alag1242, Yala yala1263,
#     Etulo etul1245, Igede iged1239, Yace yace1238), Igboid (Ika ikaa1238, Ukwuani ukwu1241,
#     Ikwere ikwe1242, Ekpeye ekpe1253, Ogba ogba1241; Ohafia, an Igbo dialect the survey codes
#     apart). Edoid as us.txt's group: Urhobo urho1239, Isoko isok1239, Esan esan1238, Yekhee
#     yekh1238, Okpela (ivbi1241), Okpe okpe1250, Epie epie1238, Degema dege1246, Emai-Iuleha-Ora
#     emai1241.
#   * Ijoid (ijoi1239, a family of its own in Glottolog; Niger-Congo as most readers know it):
#     Izon izon1238, Kalabari kala1381, Kirike (Okrika) kiri1254, Nembe, Ibani iban1261.
#   * Cross River (Glottolog's Delta Cross): Ibibio ibib1240, Efik efik1245, Anaang anaa1238,
#     Oro oroo1241, Obolo obol1243 (Lower Cross); Ogoni, Khana khan1278, Eleme elem1253
#     (Ogonoid); Abua abua1244, Ogbia ogbi1239 (Central Delta); Yakurr, Cross River Mbembe
#     cros1244 (Upper Cross). Poland's `ibibio_efik` (a census label naming both) stays apart.
#   * Plateau (Benue-Congo Plateau): Tarok taro1263, Berom bero1242, Tyap tyap1238, Jju
#     jjuu1238, Gyong (Kagoma), Ganang (Aten) gana1270, Hyam hyam1245, Adara (kada1284), Eggon
#     eggo1239, Mada mada1282, Ninzo ninz1246, Numana numa1252, Irigwe irig1241, Izere
#     izer1241, Migili, Koro.
#   * Kainji: Kambari, Bassa (Bassa-Kontagora bass1259), Kurama kura1249, Laru laru1238, Lela
#     (C'lela, Dakarkari).
#   * Jukunoid: Jukun (Jukun Takum juku1254 and the other Jukun varieties), Kuteb kute1248,
#     Jibu jibu1239, Tigon tigo1236, Etkywan (Ichen) etky1238.
#   * Bantoid (Glottolog's Southern and Northern Bantoid, not Bantu): Ejagham ejag1239, Jarawa
#     (Jar, jarr1236), Mambila, Ndoola ndoo1241, Bekwarra bekw1241, Bette-Bendi bete1262.
#     Tiv and Ekajuk stay flat where pl.txt put them.
#   * Adamawa, cf.txt's group: Mumuye nucl1240, Chamba (Samba Leko samb1305), Dza (Jenjo)
#     dzaa1238, Yungur, Waja waja1259 and Tula tula1252. Glottolog files Waja and Tula under
#     Gur (Tula-Longuda); conventionally they are Adamawa (Waja group), which is how most
#     readers know them.
#   * Gur: Bariba (Baatonum baat1238). Mande: Busa busa1253.
#   * Chadic: Ngas ngas1240, Tangale nucl1696, Bura-Pabir bura1292, Marghi marg1265, Karekare
#     kare1348, Bade bade1248, Kamwe kamw1239, Mwaghavul mwag1236 (Mupun is its dialect),
#     Gwandara gwan1268, Tera tera1251, Bole nucl1695, Bachama baca1246, Zaar (Saya saya1246),
#     Goemai goem1240, Kibaku (Chibok), Huba (Kilba) huba1236, Glavda glav1244, Piapung.
#   * Shuwa Arabic: Chadian Arabic chad1249, under Arabic. Zarma: bf.txt's node.
#   * Nigerian Pidgin nige1257 under English-based creoles, as Krio.
#
# Colours, for neighbours on the Nigerian ground. North: Hausa pale lime, Fula red, Kanuri
# lavender, the minor Chadic languages olive around the Chadic group. South-west: Yoruba salmon.
# South-east: Igbo orange, Cross River pinks and magentas. Delta: Ijoid teals, Edoid reds and
# roses, Pidgin green. Middle Belt: Plateau blues, Jukunoid purple, Kainji brown, Bantoid pale
# blue, Tiv yellow, Nupe yellow-orange, Igala deep red, Idoma rose.
