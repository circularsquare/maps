# Colours, for neighbours on the Beninese ground. The Gbe south is a patchwork: Fon (Zou,
# Atlantique) a warm yellow, Gun (Ouémé, Porto-Novo) green, Aja (Couffo) orange, Kotafon
# (Mono) yellow-green, Toli (Avrankou) brick, Weme (Ouémé valley) teal, Maxi (Collines) olive,
# Ayizo (Allada) pale lime, Saxwe pale gold, Gen (Grand-Popo) deep red, Xwla pale green. The
# Yoruboid east (Plateau, Collines) runs red to pink: Ede Nago red, Idaasha rose, Ifè salmon.
# The north-west: Ditammari magenta, Waama pale yellow-green, Biali brown, Nateni pale pink,
# Yom green, Lukpa olive, beside Bariba's yellow and Fula's red.
