### jp, Japan
- Searched: stat.go.jp 2020 census outline and questionnaire (`/english/data/kokusei/2020/pdf/outline.pdf`, `qre.pdf`).
- 19 items (15 person, 4 household). Person items: sex, birth, relationship, marital status,
  nationality, residence duration, residence 5 years ago, education, activity, industry,
  occupation, employment status. Nationality has 12 named countries plus other. No language,
  no ethnicity. Ainu and Ryukyuan are not counted anywhere official.

### eg, Egypt
- Searched: ERF data portal catalog 248 (2017 census scope), CAPMAS censusinfo (host does not
  resolve, as in the sweep), a search for a 1960 language table.
- 2017 scope: age, sex, education, occupation, marital status, mobility, religion, nationality,
  disability. No language. The 1960s "99,000 Nubians" figure turns up only in secondary
  sources; no census table located. Not pursued further (would be a 1960 national figure at best).

### kr, South Korea
- Searched: census.go.kr survey items page (2020).
- Complete count: name, sex, birth, relationship, nationality, entry date. 20% sample: migration,
  birthplace, care, commuting, work, marriage, fertility. No language or ethnicity item.

### ye, Yemen
- Searched: web for a 2004 language item; IHSN catalog 229 and its DDI export.
- The DDI's 441 variables are labelled only by code (Q_101...), with no question text or value
  labels, so it neither confirms nor rules out a language item. Nothing anywhere mentions one;
  Mehri and Soqotri have no census figures in any secondary source. Left ruling; the 2004
  questionnaire PDF itself was not found.

### mg, Madagascar (status changed to free)
- Searched: INSTAT RGPH-3 publications; read the thematic report
  "Competences linguistiques et scolarisation a Madagascar" (Feb/Oct 2021),
  https://www.instat.mg/documents/upload/main/INSTAT-RGPH3_CompetencesLinguistiquesetScolarisation_Fev-2021.pdf
- Question: ability to speak Malagasy, French, English, other languages, asked of everyone aged 3+,
  several answers allowed (ability, not first language).
- Table 2.2 (report p.18): by region (22 regions), population 3+ and the % able to speak each.
  Malagasy 99.9% in every region shown; French from 4.9% (Melaky) to 55.7% (Analamanga);
  English 1.1% to 23.8%; other 0.2% to 2.3% (Analamanga). National: French 23.6%, English 8.2%,
  other 0.6%. Percentages only, so counts = % x population 3+.
- Under AGENT_BRIEF §2 (multi-answer, and learned second languages folded onto the main
  language), French and English fold into Malagasy. "Autres langues" is unnamed and may also be
  mostly learned languages, so the builder should decide whether to share it in (on `other`) or
  fold it too. Either way the map is near-uniform Malagasy at region grain. The report says the
  18 ethnic dialects are not measured. A literacy-by-language table (Table 2.15) also exists,
  not useful.
- Buildable, low value; whoever takes it should weigh that.

### so, Somalia

### rw, Rwanda
- Searched: NISR RPHC5 2022 reports and microdata catalog 109 (variable search is JS-rendered,
  returned nothing), RPHC5 main indicators.
- The 2022 question is literacy by language (Kinyarwanda, English, French, Swahili), 15+, several
  allowed; tables by province and district. Literacy is not one of §2's buildable questions.
  The 1991/2002 "foreign language spoken" item is IPUMS-only (gated, IPUMS account blocked).

### bi, Burundi

  English / other), for 10+. Literacy, not buildable. 2024 RGPHAE: only preliminary results and
  an agriculture module are online; questionnaire not posted. Worth a re-check when final
  results appear.

### ht, Haiti
- Searched: web for the 2003 RGPH form and a 5th RGPH (IHSI, ECLAC, rgph-haiti.ht).
- 2003 asked only "can he/she read and write" (5+); no language spoken, though the form was in
  French and Kreyol. The 5th RGPH was never carried out; IHSI publishes 2024 disaggregated
  estimates instead.

### jo, Jordan
- Searched: 2015 census questionnaire coverage; news on the next census (Petra, Jordan Times).
- 2015 stands as the sweep found (nationality, no language). The 7th census is in the field
  1-31 October 2026, mixed field and administrative records; questionnaire not published.
  Re-check when results appear (2027).

### do, Dominican Republic
- Searched: X CNPV 2022 questionnaire (acento.com.do excerpt, es.wikipedia), ONE general report
  `https://www.one.gob.do/media/atbcumc0/informe-general-xcnpv-completo.pdf` (403 bot wall,
  read via Wayback 20260512041645, 641 pp.).
- Full-text search of the report: no "idioma", "lengua", "creol" anywhere. The census asked racial

### cu, Cuba
- Searched: 2012 census coverage; status of the next census (OnCuba, Diario de Cuba).
- 2012 asked skin colour (blanco / negro / mestizo o mulato), no language. The census planned
  for 2022 is still postponed; a pre-census register began May 2025, no enumeration date.

### pt, Portugal
- Searched: INE ICOT 2023 (Inquerito as Condicoes de Vida, Origens e Trajetorias), destaque of
  22 Dec 2023 (`ine.pt ... DESTAQUESdest_boui=625453018`).
- Censos 2021 has no language item. ICOT is a sample survey of ages 18-74 asking language at home
  until 15 and now: 486,400 spoke a non-Portuguese language at home until 15, 661,700 speak
  Portuguese plus another now; published as broad groups (other European, PALOP languages),
  national only. A survey, not a census or register, so not buildable under the brief; noted
  as a lead if Anita ever allows survey sources.
