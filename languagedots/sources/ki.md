# Kiribati (ki): record

Drawn 2026-10-05 (session edd42a8c-mono3). No home-language question: all 119,438 people on
Gilbertese, 24 islands, 2020 census populations, rows `derived`. Haiti model (`sources/ht.md`).
Placed on religiondots' Kontur hexes (read-only). 119 dots.

Files: `sources/ki_pop.py`, `taxonomy/ki2020.py`, `taxonomy/tree.d/ki.txt` (bare repeats),
`countries/ki.py`, `data/normalized/ki.csv`.

## Sources

- 2020 census questionnaire (religiondots' `data/raw/ki/census_report_2020.pdf`, p140): D9 "Does
  X speak English at home?" (yes/no) and literacy; no Kiribati/other language item, and no table
  of D9 by island was found in the report. A yes/no on English could not place a first language
  anyway: English-at-home households in Kiribati are bilingual, not English-first.
- Populations: 2020 island profiles (religiondots' `data/raw/ki/island_profile_2020.xlsx`), one
  sheet per island, row "Population (Census)". The 24 islands sum to 119,438, the census total in
  the report's Table G-1; every sheet's All Kiribati column agrees. 2020 rather than
  religiondots' 2015 because only population is needed here.
