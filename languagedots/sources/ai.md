## Source

The Statistics Department publishes no country-of-birth table for 2001, 2011 or 2022, and
Anguilla has no REDATAM base (redatam.org's Caribbean list checked). Its population workbook
has Table 1.01.1.6, 2001 population by district, citizenship (8 groups) and age (3 blocks):
district rows sum to each block total, blocks to 11,430 (asserted). Table 1.01.1.9 (2001)
counts second languages spoken: 91% speak one language only.

## How

Anguillian (8,300) and Kittitian (475) citizens on the Leeward creole (`antiguan`, anti1245
lists AI); US (515) and UK (260) on English; Dominican Republic (331) Spanish; Jamaica (245)
Jamaican Creole; Other Caribbean (905) on the `creole` group node (an unnamed mix, drawn as
language not named); Other (399) on `other`. National: religiondots' layer is one unit.

## Calls someone might reverse

- Citizenship, not birthplace: naturalised immigrants count as Anguillian (creole).
- 2001 vintage (11,430; the 2011 count was 13,572, 2022 about 15,800).
- US citizens on English rather than the US home mix (St Kitts's convention).
