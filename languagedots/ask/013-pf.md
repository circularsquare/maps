## The decision I already took

French Polynesia is drawn from the 2012 census: five language groups (French, Polynesian, Asian,
European, other) for the five subdivisions, because that is the newest table with any geography
that is open. The Polynesian languages show as one washed-out group ("language not named"),
though nationally they are 82% Tahitian, 9% Marquesan, 5% Paumotu, 4% Austral, 1% Mangarevan.
2017 and 2022 commune tables are not public (the 2017 pivot tool is gone; 2022 has only
five-class maps). Details in sources/pf.md.

## What reversing it would cost

Nothing to undo. With a 2022 table of "langue la plus couramment parlée en famille" (detailed
labels) by commune or commune associée, an agent rewrites sources/pf_rp2012.py and
taxonomy/pf2012.py as 2022 versions (an hour or two); the placement layer is already per commune
associée.

## What I need from Anita

"langue la plus couramment parlée en famille", detailed nomenclature, population 15+, by commune
