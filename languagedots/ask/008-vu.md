# Vanuatu: draw the 29,448 not asked their first language as Bislama?

status: closed
country: vu
summary: Census asked first language only of people who speak an indigenous language. The 10.9% who cannot (a third of Luganville) are left undrawn; drawing them as derived Bislama is a one-line switch.

## The decision I already took

Vanuatu's 2020 census asked "first language learnt to speak" (E10) only of people who said at E9
that they can speak an indigenous language. Table 6.16 covers 239,839 of the 269,287 aged 3+ in
private households. The other 29,448 (10.9%) are drawn nowhere and named in `gap`. They are
concentrated in the towns: 32% of Luganville's 3+ population, 22% of Port Vila's, and 15-28% of
a few councils near the towns (Eratap, Erakor, Malorua, South East Santo, East Malo, Central
Malekula). Among the asked, Port Vila is already 30% Bislama-first, so leaving the rest out makes
the towns look smaller and more vernacular than they are. `note_public` says so.

The alternative is the spec 3.5 pattern: draw the census's own complement on the country's main
language, here Bislama, as `tier="derived"`. These people speak no indigenous language, so their
first language is almost all Bislama, English or French, but the table does not say which, and
English and French are surely a bigger share of them than of the asked (expatriates, French-
and English-medium town families). I did not apply it because 3.5 was ruled for indigenous-only
censuses, and this is a different skip; a proxy is yours to allow.

## What reversing it would cost

One line: `DRAW_NOT_ASKED = True` in `countries/vu.py`, then `python scatter.py --country vu`
and the build tail. The rows are already in `data/normalized/vu.csv`.
