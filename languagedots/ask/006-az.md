# Azerbaijan: national-only mother tongue, or a nationality model by rayon

status: closed
country: az
summary: The 2019 census prints mother tongue only for the whole country, so Azerbaijan is drawn spread evenly. A model placing each nationality's census language split on 2009 nationality by rayon is built and switched off. Allow it?

## The decision I already took

The 2019 census (Volume B, Table 30) prints mother tongue crossed with nationality, but only for
the whole country (plus urban and rural). No volume prints it for a rayon, and the 2009 language
volume is not online. So Azerbaijan is drawn at national grain, as the brief says for coarse
tables: one unit, 9,951,409 people, every language spread over the country by Kontur population.
Lezgian dots land in Lankaran and Talysh dots in Gusar; `grain` and `note_public` say the dots
show how many, not where.

## What reversing it would cost

The alternative is built and switched off: `sources/az_model.py` writes
`data/normalized/az_model.csv`, and `MODEL = True` in `countries/az.py` draws it (tested: 66
rayons, 9,943,958 people, every row `modelled`). Then `how`, `grain` and `note_public` need a
rewrite (about ten lines) and an az re-scatter (under a minute).

The model: each nationality's 2019 count placed on its 2009 census distribution by rayon (the same
transcription religiondots' Azerbaijan uses), Azerbaijanis as each rayon's remainder, and each
nationality split over mother tongues by its own national split in Table 30. It fits your proxy
rule: the part that does not match (Lezgins naming Azerbaijani, 24.6%; Talysh, 50.5%) is a
published number per nationality. Its weakness is that the split is flat across places, so Gusar
comes out 56% Lezgian when its rural Lezgins almost surely name Lezgian more than Baku's do. A
finer version could use the urban/rural split both tables have.

What it shows that the national map cannot: Gusar 56% Lezgian; Zagatala 19% Avar and 9% Tsakhur;
Balakan 24% Avar; Gakh 12% Georgian; Astara 14% Talysh; Saatly 11% Turkish. Lerik comes out
almost all Azerbaijani, because the 2009 census counted only 2,281 Talysh there out of 74,522.

## What I need from Anita

Whether to switch the nationality model on (a proxy, so yours to allow), or keep the national
grain.

## Ruling (2026-10-05)

Anita 2026-10-05: yes, switch on the nationality-by-rayon model (MODEL = True), rewrite how/grain/note, re-scatter.
