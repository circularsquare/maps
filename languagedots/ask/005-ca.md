# Four more North American roots (Canada): Iroquoian, Salishan, Wakashan, Tsimshianic

status: closed
country: ca
summary: Canada adds roots iroquoian, salishan, wakashan, tsimshianic, families as roots like ask 003; Haida and Ktunaxa under br's isolate root; Michif under Algic per Glottolog. Built; follows whatever 003 decides.

## The decision I already took

Canada's 2021 census names about 70 Indigenous languages, in StatCan's own families. Most fit
the roots us.txt added (Algic, Na-Dene, Siouan, Inuit-Yupik-Aleut). Four families had no node,
so `taxonomy/tree.d/ca.txt` adds them as roots, the same way ask 003 did for the US, each a
Glottolog family:

- `iroquoian` (Mohawk, Oneida, Cayuga; 355 people)
- `salishan` (Halkomelem, Secwepemctsin, Syilx, Nlaka'pamux and four more; 1,095)
- `wakashan` (Kwak'wala, Nuu-chah-nulth, Heiltsuk, Haisla; 770)
- `tsimshianic` (Gitxsan, Nisga'a, Tsimshian; 1,035)

Also: Haida (45) and Ktunaxa (30) go under Brazil's `isolate` root (Glottolog calls Kutenai an
isolate; it has Haida as a two-language family, and the conventional reading is followed).
Michif (485) goes under Algic, where Glottolog puts Heritage Michif, rather than in a mixed-
language or creole node. "Indigenous languages, n.i.e./n.o.s." (240) go on us.txt's
`americas_other`.

## What reversing it would cost

If 003 is decided for an areal `americas` root, these four roots move under it: an edit to
the four families' lines in `tree.d/ca.txt` and the string prefixes in `taxonomy/ca2021.py`
(about 25 lines), then `taxonomy/build.py` and a Canada re-scatter (about 3 minutes).
