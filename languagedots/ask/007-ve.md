# Venezuela: the 2011 language answers are not published by place; draw a model, the 2001 census, or nothing?

status: closed
country: ve
summary: INE's 2011 census asked indigenous people which languages they speak but serves only pueblo by parroquia; language is national and per-state shares. Venezuela is undrawn. Allow a model on pueblo plus INE's state speaker shares?

## The decision I already took

Venezuela is not drawn. The 2011 census asked indigenous people "Que idioma(s) habla?" (their
pueblo's language, Spanish, another), but INE publishes the answers only as national shares
(10.2% speak only their pueblo's language, 54.1% both, 33.1% Spanish only) and as one speaker
share per state for eight states (Amazonas 75.2%, Anzoategui 17.1%, Apure 75.8%, Bolivar 87.3%,
Delta Amacuro 89.4%, Monagas 33.3%, Sucre 2.5%, Zulia 67.5%). Nothing gives speakers per pueblo,
not even nationally. INE's REDATAM server serves the full 2011 person file but without the
language questions; a second deployment lists them in its dictionary and its data files are
missing on the server.

What is on disk and checked: the 2011 pueblo of every indigenous person per parroquia (724,592
people, 1,128 parroquias, `data/normalized/ve.csv`), equal cell for cell to INE's published
pueblo-by-state table. That is identity, not language, so drawing it as language is a proxy.

## What reversing it would cost

Three ways to draw it, all needing your yes:

- **A, a model on 2011 (my pick).** Each pueblo's people in each parroquia, with the language
  implied by the pueblo, scaled within each state so speakers match INE's share for that state
  (the other 2.6% of indigenous people take the national rate). Pueblos whose language Glottolog
  marks extinct or moribund are drawn as Spanish, not as speakers: Kumanagoto 20,876, Anu 20,814
  (Paraujano, moribund), Chaima 13,217, Bare 5,044, Waikeri 1,985, Gayon 1,033 and five small
  ones, 63,631 people. Without that rule a flat state rate would invent about 26,000 speakers
  of dead languages, most of them Anu in Zulia. Weakness: the share is flat across the living-language pueblos of
  a state (Zulia's Wayuu and Yukpa get the same rate). Every row `modelled`. About half a day:
  taxonomy for ~45 pueblos, parroquia hexes, the entry.
- **B, the 2001 census, measured.** Its open REDATAM base has pueblo x "speaks an indigenous
  language" per parroquia, but only for the 327,998 indigenous people counted on the general
  questionnaire. The 178,343 counted by the separate 2001 census of indigenous communities, who
  are most of Amazonas and Delta Amacuro, are coded "No indigena" in that base and cannot be
  picked out, so they would be drawn as Spanish speakers. I would not use it.
- **C, leave Venezuela undrawn** until INE serves the 2011 language variables.

## What I need from Anita

Which of A, B or C. A is a proxy (identity standing in for language, with published per-state
shares to weight by), so it is yours to allow.

## Ruling (2026-10-05)

Anita 2026-10-05: option A (model on 2011), because it names more languages.
