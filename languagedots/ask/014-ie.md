# Ireland: draw Irish only for the 72k daily speakers outside education?

status: closed
country: ie
summary: Census asks no first language. Irish is drawn for the 71,968 who speak it daily outside school (CSO headline); the 1.87M who can speak Irish are drawn as English. One switch reverses it.

## The decision I already took

Census 2022 asks no first-language question. It asks whether a person speaks a language other
than English or Irish at home (751,507 named one; drawn by Small Area and county), and
separately whether they can speak Irish and how often. Irish is drawn only for the 71,968 who
speak it daily outside the education system, CSO's own headline figure for living use. The
1.87M who say they can speak Irish are drawn as English, under the brief's rule that a language
learned at school is not a home language (§2, El Salvador and Ecuador). Irish is also a real first
language in the Gaeltacht, which is why the rule says to flag it rather than settle it alone.

## What reversing it would cost

Nothing to build: `IRISH_DAILY` in `sources/ie_census.py` switches it, then a re-scatter. A middle
reading (daily plus weekly speakers, about 187k) is also a one-line change.

## What I need from Anita

Keep daily-outside-education (as now), widen to weekly, or draw every Irish speaker.
