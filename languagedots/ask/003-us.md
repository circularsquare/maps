# New roots for North America, Asia and Africa (United States)

status: closed
country: us
summary: The US adds roots Na-Dene, Algic, Siouan, Uto-Aztecan, Eskimo-Aleut, Uralic, Turkic, Mongolic, Koreanic, Japonic, Kra-Dai, Hmong-Mien, Austronesian, Niger-Congo, and americas_other for two cross-family Native remainders. Built; colours provisional.

## The decision I already took

The ACS microdata names 125 languages and groups, from every continent, so
`taxonomy/tree.d/us.txt` adds 15 roots, one per family, each checked against Glottolog:
`nadene` (Athabaskan-Eyak-Tlingit: Navajo, Apache), `algic` (Ojibwe), `siouan` (Dakota),
`utoaztecan` (the census's own "Uto-Aztecan languages", Hopi to Comanche), `eskimoaleut` (labelled
"Inuit-Yupik-Aleut (Eskimo-Aleut)"), `uralic`, `turkic`, `mongolic`, `koreanic`, `japonic`,
`kradai`, `hmongmien`, `austronesian`, `nigercongo`. Families as roots, as Brazil did (ask 002).

Two judgement calls inside that:

- **Niger-Congo is the conventional grouping, not Glottolog's.** Glottolog has Atlantic-Congo
  and Mande as separate families; the census's own code is "Other Niger-Congo languages" and
  holds both, so a narrowest node needs Niger-Congo. Mande sits under it.
- **`americas_other`, "Other indigenous languages of the Americas".** Two census codes hold
  languages from many families: "Other Native North American languages" (79,000 people: Cree,
  Cheyenne, Salish, Tlingit, the Alaskan Athabaskan languages, Cherokee) and "Other Central and
  South American languages" (44,000: K'iche', Mam, Q'anjob'al, Zapotec, Mixtec, Quechua). No
  family node holds either, and `other` would draw them grey as "Other or unclassified". So they
  get a root of their own, outside the families. An areal root `americas` with the families
  under it would be tidier (the remainder would then be "Indigenous Americas, language not
  named"), but it would break Brazil's families-as-roots. Mexico will need the same node for
  "lengua indígena no especificada".

Also added under existing roots, not new families: Romance, Slavic, Baltic, Celtic, Hellenic,
Albanian, Armenian and two Germanic subgroups (Continental West Germanic, North Germanic) under
Indo-European. The Germanic subgroups exist because `indoeuropean.germanic` has a fixed
near-white GROUP colour in build.py (English's), so German or Yiddish generated under it would
have looked like English.

## What reversing it would cost

Renaming or merging roots is an edit to `tree.d/us.txt` and the node strings in
`taxonomy/us2024.py` (125 lines), then `taxonomy/build.py` and a re-scatter of the US (about
four minutes). Colours are one line each in the fragment.

## What I need from Anita

Whether families as roots is right for North America too, and whether `americas_other` as a
separate "other indigenous" root is acceptable, or you would rather have an areal `americas`
root over every American family (which would also restructure Brazil).

## Ruling (2026-10-04)

Anita 2026-10-04: yes, North American families as roots, and americas_other is good. Keep other-indigenous remainders separate from other non-indigenous remainders wherever the census allows.
