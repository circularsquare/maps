# Cyprus: draw the north as Turkish from the 2011 TRNC census?

status: closed
country: cy
summary: The 2021 census covers the south only, so the north is drawn empty, as in religiondots. The 2011 northern census (286k people, no language question) could be drawn as Turkish by village; that is a proxy, yours to allow.

## The decision I already took

Cyprus is drawn from the Republic's 2021 census, which enumerated only the area the Republic
controls: 912,270 people in five districts. The north (Keryneia district and the northern parts
of Lefkosia, Ammochostos and Larnaka, about 3,400 of the island's 9,250 km²) is drawn empty, as
religiondots draws it, and the view frames the whole island so the blank shows. `gap` and
`note_public` say so. So the island's second language community appears only as the 1,551
people in the south who gave Turkish.

## What reversing it would cost

Small. The north's only census, 2011 (about 290,000 people), has no language question as far
as the coverage sweep found. Drawing it would mean putting its population, by village or by
district, on Turkish: a proxy that sets counts, which the brief says is yours to allow. It would
also be wrong at the edges: the Greek Cypriots of the Karpasia villages, the Maronites of
Kormakitis, and the English-speaking residents around Keryneia would all be drawn as Turkish
unless a second source splits them. Building it is a geography job (TRNC village boundaries or
the GISCO LAUs religiondots already left out, plus the 2011 tables) of maybe one session.

## What I need from Anita

Yes or no to drawing the north from the 2011 northern census as Turkish (with any named
exceptions), marked as derived and said so in the note. No means nothing changes.


## Ruling (2026-10-05)

Anita 2026-10-05: yes, draw the north as Turkish from the 2011 TRNC census.
