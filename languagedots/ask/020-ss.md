# South Sudan: download the 2025 phone survey, which asks household language in all ten states?

status: open
country: ss
summary: Drawn from the 2015 survey's tribe question, 7 of 10 states. The World Bank's 2025 phone survey asks the household's primary language in every state and county but needs your Microdata Library login.

## The decision I already took

South Sudan is drawn from the High Frequency South Sudan Survey that religiondots already holds
(your download for its ask 042): the household head's tribe, read as language, wave 1 (2015) for
six states and wave 2's towns (2016) for Warrap. Jonglei, Unity and Upper Nile, 5.0 million
people (38%) and most of the Nuer, Shilluk and Murle, were never sampled and are left empty
(sources/ss.md).

## What reversing it would cost

Nothing to reverse. The better source exists: the World Bank's *South Sudan High Frequency Phone
Survey* 2025, waves 1 and 2 (Microdata Library catalog 8589 and 8590, IDNO SSD_2025_HFPS-W1_v01_M
and -W2_v01_M), about 6,000 adults by phone across all 10 states and 3 administrative areas,
with state (b1_4), county (b1_5) and "What is the primary language spoken in your household?"
(b1_7). It is a real language question, nationwide, and would fill the three empty states. It
leans to phone owners (MICS frame plus random dialling), so I would check it against the 2015
tribe shares in the seven states before replacing anything. A rebuild is a new
`sources/ss_hfps.py` and a re-scatter: a few hours.

Optional, same login: the *High Frequency Survey 2012-2014 panel* (catalog 2576), Juba, Wau,
Rumbek and Malakal, asks the language people use with family and friends. It is the only check
on how many town households of each group still speak the group's language (the retention
question AGENT_BRIEF section 2 asks for).
