# Sudan: surveys put 98% on Arabic; allow an outside estimate for its minority languages?

status: closed
country: sd
summary: Every open survey interviewed in Arabic and finds Fur, Beja, Nubian, Masalit, Zaghawa at a tenth or less of their size and no Nuba languages. Drawn as surveyed; a homeland-placed speaker estimate would change counts.

## The decision I already took

Sudan is drawn from the only open sources with a home-language question: Afrobarometer R6-R9
and Arab Barometer III, pooled by state (sources/sd_afro.py, sources/sd.md). They put 98.4% of
Sudanese on Sudanese Arabic. Non-Arabic answers are 1.6%: Beja 0.4%, Fur 0.25%, Nubian 0.23%,
Fula 0.16%, Zaghawa 0.07%, Masalit 0.05%, and nothing at all from the Nuba Mountains.

That is far too low. Every Afrobarometer interview in R6-R7 was in Arabic, and all but 16 of
3,000 in R8-R9 (the rest English); only R9 used any non-Arab interviewers (Beja and Nubian, in
the east). The 1955-56 census's national figures (51% Arabic of the whole Sudan, with the South
30% of the people) put non-Arabic home languages at about 30% of what is now Sudan, roughly
twenty times what the surveys find. Red Sea, where Beja are the majority, comes out 9% Beja;
Northern state 2% Nubian; North Darfur 2% Fur. Algeria's fix (draw the ethnic-group question
instead) fails here: Arab Barometer VII's only ethnic answers are "Arab" and "Other", and it puts
Red Sea at 5% non-Arab and Sennar at 63%. Nothing else open was found: CLEAR Global's Sudan
layer is Afrobarometer R9 itself; the MSNAs ask no language; MICS 2014 needs a registration;
the 1955-56 census volumes are search-only on HathiTrust.

## What reversing it would cost

Drawing the minority languages near their real size needs per-language speaker estimates
(Ethnologue's figures as Wikipedia and Joshua Project carry them, or the 1955-56 census's
province tables if someone fetches them) placed on each language's homeland states, with
Arabic taking the rest. That changes the counts, so it is a proxy the brief leaves to you. It
would be a new `sources/sd_estimate.py`, a few dozen language rows assigned to states by hand,
and a re-scatter; half a session.

## What I need from Anita

Either keep Sudan as surveyed (the note says plainly that minorities are undercounted), or allow
an outside speaker estimate for this country, and say which (Ethnologue-type figures via
Wikipedia/Joshua Project, or wait for the 1955-56 census tables, which would need you to get the
volumes, HathiTrust record 001886402).
