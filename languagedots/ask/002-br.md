# New top-level families for South America (Brazil)

status: closed
country: br
summary: Brazil adds 24 roots: IBGE's families (Tupian, Macro-Jê, Arawakan, Cariban, Panoan...), Aymaran, Quechuan, Araucanian, Language isolates, Creoles, No established classification; plus Romance under Indo-European. Colours provisional; build.py sibling-colour flag.

## The decision I already took

Brazil's 310 indigenous-language labels hang on IBGE's own classification (Rodrigues' troncos
Tupi and Macro-Jê, then 16 families outside the troncos), so `taxonomy/tree.d/br.txt` adds 24
roots: `tupian`, `macroje`, `arawakan`, `cariban`, `yanomaman`, `panoan`, `tucanoan`, `arawan`,
`katukinan`, `nambikwaran`, `chapacuran`, `nadahup`, `muran`, `guaicuruan`, `chiquitano`,
`jabutian`, `boran`, `witotoan`, `aymaran`, `quechuan`, `araucanian` (migrants' languages in
the census), and three non-family roots: `isolate` ("Language isolates": Tikuna, Trumai, Warao...),
`creole` (`creole.french_based`: the Amapá Kheuól and Patuá) and `unclassified` ("No
established classification": IBGE's 41 "línguas sem classificação atual determinada", mostly
revitalised languages of the Northeast such as Tupinambá and Pankararú). I did not hang those
41 under `other`, which would have turned `other` into a group node and changed how Nepal's and
Pakistan's "Others" draw. Portuguese is `indoeuropean.romance.portuguese`, with a new Romance
group, which Spanish, French and Italian will share.

Colours: each family a hue (Tupian gold 70, Macro-Jê red 15, Arawakan green 145, Cariban lime
110, Yanomaman violet 295, Panoan teal 175, Tucanoan cyan 200, isolates magenta 330...), and
every member coloured explicitly in the fragment, stepped over lightness and hue by size, then
checked for close pairs among languages that share a município. Romance is a pale steel blue
(0.78 0.11 230) so the Portuguese background stays quiet beside the indigenous colours.

A flag for whoever owns `taxonomy/build.py`: its generator gives every uncoloured sibling the
SAME colour. `colour()` recomputes `free` (siblings not yet in `lch`) inside the loop, and each
sibling is added to `lch` as it is coloured, so every one of them finds itself at index 0 and
takes STEPS[1]. India hid this because its big languages are all in HAND. I worked around it by
colouring every Brazilian node explicitly; other agents will hit it.

(Supervisor, 2026-10-04: fixed. `colour()` now takes each sibling's place among those left
uncoloured by hand, fixed before generation starts. Nothing left to decide on this part.)

## What reversing it would cost

Renaming or merging roots is an edit to `tree.d/br.txt` and the node strings in
`taxonomy/br2022.py` (generated, 310 lines), then `taxonomy/build.py` and a re-scatter of
Brazil (a minute). Colours are one line each in the fragment.
