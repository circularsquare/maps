# Indonesia: name the 42.6M 'other regional language' remainder from the province ethnicity table?

status: closed
country: id
summary: BPS names only eight languages by province; 20% of Indonesia (most of Bali, Lombok, Aceh, North Sulawesi, Maluku, NTT) is drawn as unnamed regional language. Same publication's province x 31 ethnic groups table could share it out, as a proxy.

## The decision I already took

Indonesia is drawn from the 2010 census's language-at-home tables, which BPS published by
province for only eight languages (Javanese, Indonesian, Sundanese, Malay, Madurese,
Minangkabau, Banjar, Buginese). Every other regional language, 42.6M people or 20% of the
country, is drawn on a new areal node `indonesia_other`, "Other regional languages of
Indonesia", washed out as a remainder. That is most people in North Sulawesi (98%), Maluku,
North Maluku and West Nusa Tenggara (93%), Bali (84%), Aceh (79%) and East Nusa Tenggara (75%).
The national table (L4.1) names what is in it (Balinese 3.37M, Batak 3.32M, Cirebon 3.09M,
Sasak 2.69M, Acehnese 2.55M, Betawi, the trade Malays, Makassar ...) but not by province, so
nothing is guessed out of it. The 756,035 who ticked "foreign language" are on `other`.

The same BPS publication has Table L2.6, province x 31 ethnic groups (Aceh, Batak, Nias, Bali,
Sasak, Makassar, Minahasa, "suku asal NTT", "suku asal Papua" ...). It could share each
province's remainder across the groups that have no language column of their own, in proportion
to their numbers there, drawn `tier="derived"`. That reads ethnicity as language, which is
wrong in known ways: Betawi and Batak households in Jakarta or Medan often use Indonesian, and
the trade Malays (Ambon, Manado) have no ethnic group of that name. I did not apply it, because
a proxy is yours to allow.

## What reversing it would cost

About an hour: parse L2.6 (two pages, already downloaded), a crosswalk from ethnic group to
language node, new nodes for perhaps fifteen languages or areal groups, rescatter. The measured
eight languages would not change.

## What I need from Anita

Yes (share the remainder by ethnicity, derived, said so in `note_public`), or no (keep it
unnamed, as now). No finer source exists in the open: sensus.bps.go.id has only "ability to
speak Indonesian" for language, IPUMS's 2010 sample (gated anyway) codes language as Indonesian /
regional / foreign according to the coverage sweep, and the 2020 long form asked only that three-way split. `sources/id.md` has the search.

## Ruling (2026-10-05)

Anita 2026-10-05: yes, share the remainder out by the province ethnicity table (the multiplication is fine), tier derived/modelled, said in how and note_public.
