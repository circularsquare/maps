Walking time

Time on foot

Click anywhere to set the start. Each colour is how long it takes to walk there. A day is 8 hours of walking.

Speeds come from the Malaria Atlas Project walking-only friction surface (Weiss et al., Nature Medicine 2020, CC BY 4.0), which sets a pace for every square kilometre from roads, land cover and slope. It was made to measure how far people live from health care. The whole world is worked out on cells of about 10 km, and the area in view on cells of 3 km and then 1 km as you zoom in.

Seas and lakes cannot be crossed. Ponds under 5 km² are waded at 1 km/h. Danger, weather, borders and rest are not counted. Basemap from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap, drawn with MapLibre GL.
