# T-002 working notes: view refine, stage 1

2026-10-14, subagent. Design as built is in docs/resolution.md; this is the working record.

## Tiles

- `python tools/build_tiles.py` (plain python: the maps venv has no rasterio). 45 s, one 600-row
  strip (~104 MB float32) in memory at a time.
- 5 degree tiles: 43200/600 = 72, 17400/600 = 29; L3 14400/200 = 72, 5800/200 = 29. Other tile
  sizes that divide both grids exist (300/100) but 5 degrees keeps the count near 1100 per level.
- L1 compresses poorly (117.5 MB gzipped, ~3x on land): speed varies pixel to pixel.

## Small water (open, TODO.md, T-013)

MAP gives exactly 0.06 to every water pixel, seas and lakes alike (Pacific, Black Sea, Balaton,
Lake Geneva, Lake Victoria all 0.06; no nodata in the file). In a 1.1 x 1.6 degree box round
Cluj, 2.3% of pixels are 0.06: 394 patches, 291 of them a single pixel, inside 5 km/h land. They
look like satellite land-cover water (ponds, reservoirs), often 1 wide and 2-3 tall. At L10 and L3
the 25% land rule averages them away; at L1 they are impassable specks, drawn dark. Paths go round
them at little cost, so times barely change; it is mostly a look question, and a model question
(should a pond block walking?). Not changed here.

## Page and worker

- Canvas sources instead of image sources: MapLibre 4.7.1 only re-reads a canvas while it
  "plays"; `upload()` plays it until one frame has drawn with tiles present, then sets
  `_playing = false` (internal; pinned version). `animate: false` on add.
- Holes: coarser canvas cleared where the finer window is, rounded inwards. With linear
  resampling the hole edge blends half a coarse texel into the window, giving a faint line (T-014).
- Messages: page -> worker `init`, `run {lng, lat}`, `view {west, east, north, south, zoom}`;
  worker -> page `ready`, `none`, `coarse`, `win {level}`, `clear {levels}`, each with the
  originId of the click it belongs to. Old-origin windows are dropped by the page.
- Two counters in the worker: clickGen (cancels the L10 solve and windows) and viewGen (cancels
  windows only). A view arriving during an L10 solve is stored and used when it finishes.
- `window.solveLog` on the page holds every solve's timings, for probes.
- The start: the click point if its L10 cell is land, else the snapped L10 cell's centre (the
  marker follows). Window solves snap it to the nearest land cell within ~10 km.
