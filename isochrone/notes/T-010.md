## Rules

All on our surface (`walk_today.tif`). Blend: time with roads / time without =
1 - e + e x lc / 5 (lc = `lc_speed.tif`), road time 5 km/h x terrain factor. A water cell with a
walkable bridge is 5 km/h x terrain factor (except `share2_nobridge`, which made no difference).

| Rule | e, or what |
|---|---|
| map | MAP's L1 tiles, the page today |
| our | no roads |
| any | MAP's rule on our surface: a cell with a road at max(off-road, 5 x terrain factor) |
| shareA | min(1, A s) |
| sqrt | sqrt(s) |
| satK | 1 - (1 - s)^K |
| exact | the blend per 100 m cell from LC100 classes, road cells at 5 km/h |

## What it says

- **share3 is picked**: the lowest mean difference (4%) and band share (.07), no tile over 8%,
  ratio 0.92-1.04. share2 fits New York best but is 14% off in Mures; saturating forms (sat3,
  sat4) and sqrt sit between. One factor for every tile, not tuned per region.
- **Why `any` does so well on our surface when MAP's version of the same rule is 20-50% off.**
  `why_any.py`: MAP and our `any` put nearly the same cells on roads (MAP has 68-76% of
  Romanian land cells at 5 km/h, we see a road in 64-78%), but MAP keeps every road pixel at a
  flat 5 km/h, while the 100 m level, and so our `any`, walk a road at 5 km/h times the cell's
  terrain factor, 0.68-0.85 on Romanian road cells. A path along a chain of road cells is then
  15-30% slower in our version. So in the hills most of MAP's gap to 100 m is terrain on roads,
  not the whole-pixel rule; in flat New York (terrain factor 0.9-0.98) the two are close. The
  whole-pixel rule still makes `any` 3-8% too fast everywhere (ratio 1.03-1.08), since one track
  through a cell lifts the whole cell; share3 removes that bias (0.92-1.04).
- **share3 is below the off-road floor in Romania** (4-8% against 9-15%): the factor partly
  absorbs the land cover mosaic, which is fine for the page but means it should not be tuned
  further on these tiles.
- **Bridges** at 1 km changed nothing measurable here (`share2_nobridge` is the same to 0.1%):
  starts inside a tile rarely need a 1 km water cell's bridge. They stay in the rule for the
  long crossings (Verrazzano, the Bosphorus).
- **exact** (the blend per 100 m cell) equals share1: a cell-average blend of any kind misses
  that a walker keeps to the roads. The factor 3 is what joins them up.
