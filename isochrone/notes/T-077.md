# T-077 Corridors no longer hold up the finer windows

Done 2026-11-05. Rules: `docs/corridors.md` (top, Near-equal land parents, Passes). Code:
`js/session.js` (`countCorridors`, `refine`), `js/corridors.js` (`links`, `reparent`, `moveTime`),
`checks/ktrips.mjs`.

All timings are on a shared machine running other projects' jobs (12-13 of 12 cores claimed for much
of the session), so they swing 2-3x from run to run; node figures are interleaved A/B runs in one
process, best of 8-10, with the median after the slash.

## 1. Order

`refine()` now solves and paints every window first and counts corridors after: L10, then L3, L1,
L0.1, coarse first (a window's count needs the coarser counts and the L10 total). The worker yields
between levels; a newer click or view stops the rest, and the next refine counts what is left
(`corrSent` per level). A click no longer counts L10 before refining either.

Time from a click at zoom 8 to the L1 paint:
- node, the worker's session.js with a fake `self` (Tamanrasset): before, L10 and L3 corridors
  (200-450 ms of counting) ran between the L10 paint and the L3 solve; L10 paint to L1 paint 650-1140
  ms over 4 runs (median ~950). After, 630 ms in the one quiet run (the other three were hit by load:
  1.5-2.8 s, all of it solves).
- page, after (headless, `worker` messages timed on the page clock, 3 runs each): Cluj L1 paint at
  991 / 1101 / 1317 ms after the click, 440-590 ms after the L10 paint; Tamanrasset 1212 / 988 / 1086
  ms. Corridors then arrive 200-350 ms after the L1 paint, all three levels.
- There is no page "before" (the page serves one copy of the code); the T-056 page figures (L3 Sahara
  count 848 ms, L1 Cluj 282) were all on the path to the L1 paint.

## A bug found on the way

Since T-067 a walking start's land node can have an earlier neighbour (smaller weight) whose time is
past `maxHours` (slower but safer): 597 such links at Cluj L10, 1,611 at Tamanrasset. `reparent`
checked `t[u] <= maxHours` on canoe starts only, so these nodes, not in the reached list, were queued
anyway with stale `par` and `cnt`, and left `nch` dirty for the next count (it underflowed at node 0).
The counts then depended on what was counted before: the same L10 start gave different lines in two
harnesses, and windows drew long straight stray lines (Cluj L1: 37 lines, most of them grid-straight,
against 46 fanned once fixed; `maps/artifacts/isochrone/t077_lines_cluj_L1.png`, first panel). The
check is now made on every start.

## 3. Hazard in slack

Slack is now `w[u] + move time + K x move hazard - w[v]`, the move costed exactly as js/search.js
costs a land move (`len = stepKm x f`, mean of `inv` and of `hq` over the same 2 or 4 cells); the
SPREAD and NEAR budgets stay shares of the node's time. A changed parent's link time (for the line
speeds) is the move's own time, recomputed (`moveTime`), not read back from the slack.

Effect, L10 walking, area-weighted over reached land (`hzprobe.mjs` in the session scratchpad):

| Start | slack | parents changed | tree route time over the search's | weight over (share of time) | cells losing > 0.5 survival points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tamanrasset | time (T-056) | 41.8% | 0.55% | 1.52% | 47.8% |
| Tamanrasset | weight | 38.7% | 0.73% | 0.88% | 0.21% |
| Greenland east | time (T-056) | 44.8% | -0.95% | 13.0% | 89.0% |
| Greenland east | weight | 16.8% | 0.13% | 0.83% | 7.1% |

With time slack a faster but more dangerous neighbour counted as having no slack at all, so on the
ice the counted routes ran through the ice where the search went round it. Lines: Tamanrasset L10
455 to 434, L3 297 to 293; Greenland L10 392 to 424 (the fan of strands west of the ice thins).
Survival on the drawn trunks is now the search's within the 1% budget.

## 2. Speed

Profile of the T-056 count, L3 Tamanrasset window (1.01 M reached nodes): shares 113-128 ms, spread
65-88, choose 64-80, the rest (reach, flux, sum) ~30. Without candidates the same passes take ~90
in all, as before T-056. So the cost is the candidate work, ~2 of them per node, and almost every
node has near-equal ones in the desert (1,007,062 of 1,010,399).

What was done:
- Parents and candidate links in one pass in cell order; the search's parent always gets share 63
  without costing it; the share word carries the sum, the move and an "inner" bit, so later passes
  read one word a node. Byte-identical lines to the fixed T-056 code, but only ~15% faster.
- Interleaving flow and excess in one array: no gain (so not kept).
- Lazy shares (kept): the first pass only records which candidates are reached and earlier (for the
  leaves-first order); the slacks and shares are worked out when a node is reached in the spread,
  and only if its spread trips are at least LOW = 2% of the drawing threshold. Smaller nodes pass
  everything to the search's parent and keep it. At L3 Tamanrasset 18% of nodes spread; at L10 60%.
  With LOW = 0 this is byte-identical to the full computation.

Count times, node, interleaved (best / median, ms):

| Case | pre-T-056 | T-056 | now |
|---|---|---|---|
| L10 Tamanrasset | 17-23 / 25-49 | 82 / 112 | 84 / 129 (other runs 47-75) |
| L3 Tamanrasset window | 73-109 / 93-179 | 418 / 459 | 273 / 397 |
| L10 Cluj | 32 / 43 | 144 / 252 | 133-148 / 201-205 |
| L1 Cluj window (zoom 8) | 20-30 / 34 | 92 / 109 | 47 / 61-84 |
| page, after: L10 / L3 / L1 at zoom 8 | | | Cluj 160-210 / 53-63 / 76-84, Tamanrasset 78-132 / 45-91 / 92-124 |

So the windows are 1.5-2x faster than T-056 and L10 no faster: 1.6-3x the pre-T-056 times in
windows and 4-5x at L10, short of the ~1.5x target. What is left is the bookkeeping for the order (every node's reachable earlier
neighbours have to be known before the spread, so a node is spread only after all that may pass it
trips) and three ordered passes (spread, choose, sum) where pre-T-056 had one. Ways on, not tried:
- the search's settle order (it pops nodes in weight order, a ready topological order with no
  bookkeeping), recorded by js/search.js: one Int32 write per pop;
- nodes renumbered in reach order, so the ordered passes touch denser arrays;
- dropping `dtl` (a third M-sized array, written for every node) and working the changed link time
  out in `edgeSpeeds` for kept edges only.
Since corridors now come after every paint, their cost only delays the lines, not the map.
