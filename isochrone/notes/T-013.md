# T-013 notes: small water walkable

2026-10-26, agent. The rule is in docs/resolution.md ("Small water"); code in `tools/smallwater.py`.

## Choices

- **8-connectivity.** A diagonal channel (water pixels touching at corners only) stays one body, so
  a river or strait is not split into small patches that would become land. Cost: a few ponds
  joined to a neighbour by a corner stay water.
- **Strips.** Both builds read 600-row strips. `SmallWater` labels each strip alone, then merges
  labels that touch across a strip edge and across the 180 degree seam (graph of touching pairs,
  `scipy.sparse.csgraph.connected_components`), sums area per merged patch, and the builds relabel
  each strip as they go and look the patch up. One scan is ~40 s and ~1 GB at most; the whole
  world is never in memory. `build_grid.py` read 1000-row strips before and now reads 600, to
  share the strips.
- **Area** of a pixel: sphere, R 6371.0088 km, 1/120 degree wide, from the sines of its edge
  latitudes.
- A converted pixel keeps its 0.06, so at L1 it is 1.0 km/h (stored 40). At L3 and L10 it joins
  the mean friction of its cell like any land pixel, and counts toward the quarter-of-pixels land
  rule.

## Effect on times (from Cluj, hours on foot, L10)

L10 times go up 3-8%. Ponds are now averaged into the cells at 1 km/h, where before they were
dropped from the mean (cells then took the speed of their land pixels). At L1 a pond is walked at
1 km/h, so a path that crossed one at 4 km/h before and went round it now costs the same or less;
two L1 points 20 km from Cluj are unchanged (4.264 h, 4.119 h). The DECISIONS line "paths barely
change" holds at L1, not at L10 and L3.
