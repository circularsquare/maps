# T-085 Canoe water and river steps on our land mask

2026-11-06. Since T-028 the speed levels' land/water is our own surface (LC100, docs/resolution.md);
canoe water (`tools/canoe/water.py`) and the river steps (`rivers.py build`) were still MAP's. Both
read the speed levels (data/tiles l1/l3, data/grid.bin.gz) already, so rebuilding them is the fix;
no code change was needed for that. Rule now in docs/canoe.md 1 and 4.

## Commands

From `isochrone/`, plain `python`:

    python tools/canoe/water.py           # 485 s, ~6 GB; log data/raw/rivers_work/water.log
    python tools/canoe/rivers.py build    # 201 s, ~8 GB; log data/raw/rivers_work/build.log

New options for an A/B: `water.py --tiles DIR --grid FILE --out DIR` and `rivers.py build --tiles
DIR --grid FILE --out DIR` build the canoe data from other speed levels into another folder. The
MAP-mask set was rebuilt from `data/tiles_map2020/` that way (into the session scratchpad,
deleted after); it reproduced the old river step counts exactly (1,020,208 / 3,642,528 /
11,102,266).

The third row is by design: an L1 land pixel NE's ocean touches gets a sea canoe node (the
straits the land mask closes), and an L3 or L10 land cell with any sea or lake pixel gets one. It
grew at L3 and L10 because LC100's coast is tighter than MAP's MODIS one: more coarse land cells
hold some sea. A walkable bridge (road byte on a water cell) is land on the page and water in the
canoe data, as intended (a land node over the water).

river water 2.27 M px before the sea grows into it, 1.89 M after / 1.35 M, 0.47 M (LC100 has
fewer wide-river and lagoon pixels as water apart from the sea); coast pixels 5.88 M / 3.96 M.

## One rule changed: lake steps (`rivers.py`)

The first holes check found 15 unreached water nodes in the Sundarbans L1 box. They were river
fragments cut off at both ends: a step half in a one-pixel HydroLAKES lake was dropped because both
its cells were water, but one end was river water, which has no grid edges, so nothing joined the
inflow to the outflow. Now a step is dropped in a lake only when both its cells are sea or lake
water (`kind` 1 or 3+), read from the canoe water just built. It keeps 4 more steps at L10, 109 at
L3 and 6,534 at L1, and leaves every check trip unchanged; the Sundarbans box went from 15
unreached nodes to 4.

The 4 left: two one-pixel HydroLAKES ponds inside river water with no land beside them, and the two
river-water cells whose only nearer neighbour is one of those ponds (T-036's depth rule enters a
depth-1 cell only from a canoe node). Listed in docs/canoe.md 8. A fix would be in `js/canoe.js`
(treat a lake cell with no open-water or land neighbour as river water off the line) or in
`water.py` (call such pond pixels river water).
