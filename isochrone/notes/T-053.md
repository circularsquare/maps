## The run

    python -u isochrone/tools/roads/world.py --jobs 1

started detached from the maps root (PowerShell `Start-Process`, PID **25032**), claimed as
`python cores.py claim 2 iso-roads --pid 25032 "T-053 world roads"`.

- **Log**: `isochrone/data/roads/world.log` (stderr in `world.err`); one line per download, read
  and finished extract, with GB done and hours to go. Each extract's own read log is in
  `data/roads/logs/<name>.log`.
- **Alive?** `Get-Process -Id 25032` (the driver) and the newest line of `world.log`; a read
  shows as a `python ... build.py extract <name>` child. Finished extracts: `data/roads/done.txt`
  (513 lines when the pass is complete, including liechtenstein and andorra from the test).
  Failures: `data/roads/failed.txt` (absent if none).
- **Expected**: 511 extracts, 84.5 GB; ~5 min a GB of reading on one core, so **~7-8 h**
  (downloads overlap the reading). At most two extracts on disk; each is deleted as soon as it is
  read.
- **Disk guard**: before each download, if C: would have under 6 GB free afterwards, no more are
  started, the read in progress finishes, and the driver exits with code 3 ("stopped for disk
  space" in the log). 52 GB were free at the start.
- **Restart** (after a stop, a crash or a reboot): free space if needed, then the same command,
  from the maps root, and claim again with the new PID:

      python -u isochrone/tools/roads/world.py --jobs 1 > isochrone/data/roads/world.log 2> isochrone/data/roads/world.err

  Extracts in `done.txt` are skipped; an extract that was half read is read again (its tiles are
  OR-merged, so that is harmless). A `.osm.pbf` or `.part` left in `data/raw/osm/` is checked by
  md5 or downloaded again. A `.lock` left in `data/roads/bits/` by a killed read is broken after
  10 min. With two cores granted, `--jobs 2` reads two at once (~5 h for the whole pass).
