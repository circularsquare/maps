# T-091: corridor parents by directed cost

`js/corridors.js` re-chooses a land node's parent among near-equal ones (T-056, T-077) by the
weight lost going through each candidate, and gives a re-chosen edge the move's own time, which
sets its speed class. Both costed the move with the cells' symmetric speed. Since T-011 the
search walks by each cell's E/W/S/N hours per km (`L.sd`), and since T-039 L0.1 adds the slope
(`L.zs`), so the corridors could take a parent that is not near-equal at all and read a downhill
stretch at the uphill speed.

## Change

- `js/corridors.js` `move()`: one costing of a land move, used for every candidate's slack and
  for a re-chosen edge's time: directed by `L.sd` where the level has it, sloped at L0.1, carried
  at `cfg.carry` on a canoe start, hazard the cells' own; false where the search does not make the
  move (a knight move past water, too steep). The old `moveTime` and the inline copy in
  `reparent` are gone. Its tables (`stepKm16`, `wx16`, `iStep16` per level) are built the way
  search.js builds `stepKm`, `dirWeights` and `slopeSteps`.
- The search inlines its move cost in both loops and exports none of it, so there was no function
  to call. The guard against drift is a check instead: `checks/corridors.mjs` now costs every
  reached land node's own move (the search's) with `corridors.moveWeight` and requires it to give
  the node's weight exactly (2e-6 relative). `power.mjs` (below): with `L.sd` hidden, i.e. the old
  symmetric cost, the same test finds 149,148 of Brig's 301,031 L10 moves off (0 directed).

No data rebuild; the search, the time map and the hover route are unchanged.

- L10 to L1: 5-11% of walking parents change, under 1% on canoe starts (most of their trips are
  paddled; river and sea edges were never re-chosen). Line km by class moves by a few percent
  between neighbouring classes (Brig L10: 3.5-4.5 km/h 54,287 -> 55,576 km, 4.5-5.5 48,877 ->
  45,963).
- L0.1 changes most. The symmetric cost left out the slope and the height factor (above ~1000 m
  every move costs more), so a candidate's move looked cheaper than the search's own: its slack
  came out at or below 0, it took a full share and added no excess, and the 1% route limit
  (NEAR) never bit. With the true cost slack is real and adds up along re-chosen links: Brig
  re-chosen 48k -> 14k. Re-chosen edges also read slower: Kathmandu L0.1 line km at 4.5-5.5 km/h
  314 -> 14, at 3.5-4.5 420 -> 709; Brig at 2.5-3.5 83 -> 262 (the climbs south and north of the
  Rhone now red-orange, the valley floor still orange-yellow).
- The cost of that at L0.1: a few more straight runs. Share of line km in single straight
  segments of 1 km or more (`straight.py`): Brig 1.0% -> 3.3%, Kathmandu 20.0% -> 27.1% (2 km or
  more: 5.7% -> 7.5%). Kathmandu's flat city had spokes before too (the due-north one); due south
  and due east are now straight for 5-10 km. The old smoothing there came from the wrong cost, so
  it is not restored here; if Anita finds L0.1 too spoky, NEAR/SPREAD per level is the knob
  (a follow-up, not filed).
- Drift: 0 off at every start and level, L0.1 slope included (worst 6e-8 relative, float32).
