# T-047 — where the fine level goes: measurements

2026-10-26. Scripts in `notes/T-047/`: `tiles.py` (the sample), `glo90_fetch.py`, `sample.py`
(speed grids per tile), `compare.mjs` (the page's own search on both grids), `world.py` (what
every 1 degree land tile holds, from the 1 km layers, ~5 min), `rule.py`, `counts.py`.
`compare.json` is the comparison's output. GLO-90 tiles were fetched for the sample and deleted.

## Method

42 tiles of 1 x 1 degree: 9 cities, the 6 other built tiles, farmland, desert, ice, tundra,
taiga, rainforest, deltas, wetland, mountains, coasts (`tiles.py`). In each, five starts (the
centre and the quarter points, snapped to land), each solved inside the tile only at L1 (120 x
120) and at L0.1 (1200 x 1200). Every 100 m cell is compared with the L1 cell it lies in, as
the page paints it.

- **band**: share of cells reached at both levels whose colour band differs. Bands double, so
  this is the share of the map that looks different.
- **only**: share of land cells reached at one level only (water that cuts off or joins land).
- **gain** = band + only, with GLO-90 terrain.

Pairs (one row each in `compare.json`):

| Pair | 1 km | 100 m |
|---|---|---|
| plain | our surface (`walk_today.tif`, no roads) | LC100 classes x the L1 terrain factor |
| glo | same | LC100 classes x MAP's elevation and slope factors from GLO-90 at 90 m, grade capped at 10% on urban cells |
| road | MAP's L1 tiles, what the page shows | stage 1 tiles with OSM roads (built tiles only) |
| road_glo | same | stage 1 tiles with roads and GLO-90 terrain |

## What it says

- **Uniform land gains little**: sand seas, ice, central Australia, taiga, dry farmland on
  flat ground, lake tundra where lakes do not cut the way (Yamal, Quebec): gain 0.02-0.10.
- **Mountains gain most from the 90 m terrain**, not from land cover: the glo band is 2-6 times
  the plain band (Cusco .09 to .55, Everest .31 to .58). The 1 km slope smooths the hillsides.
- **Water that cuts land apart** (deltas, Mumbai's creeks, lake-riddled tundra, New York's
  rivers and bridges) changes which places are reached at all: "only" .16-.69.
- **Cities in themselves gain little** where they are one block of urban land (London .05,
  Trenton .07, Tokyo .14): urban land is 5 km/h at both levels. They gain where water or
  hills run through them (New York, Mumbai, Lagos, Cluj).
- **Mixed land cover gains ~.10-.25 in its own right** (Pampa, Ukraine, Lagos, Sudd): the 100 m
  search picks the faster patches the 1 km mean blurs. GLO-90 reads forest canopy and dunes as
  slopes (Congo .04 to .11, Sahara .02 to .20); the dunes are real, the canopy is not.
- **The biggest difference in farmland is MAP's roads, not resolution.** Against the page as it
  is, rural 100 m times are 18-52% longer: MAP puts a whole 1 km pixel at 5 km/h when a road
  crosses it, while at 100 m a walker keeps to the roads or crosses fields at 2.5 km/h. This is
  a 1 km problem with a 1 km fix (roads as a share of each cell in our own surface, T-010 and
  T-028), and until then the edge of an L0.1 window outside a town is seeded early (T-051).
