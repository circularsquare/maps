## Claims checked

- **Hydraulic geometry** W = 7.2 Q^0.5: confirmed by two papers citing Moody and Troutman 2002
  (NOAA repository paper on channel dimension estimation; Lin et al. 2020, GRL, quoting a = 7.2,
  b = 0.5). D = 0.27 Q^0.39: one secondary source (the same NOAA paper, text garbled); not seen
  in the original. These are bankfull relations, fitted on 10^2-10^5 m³/s; using them with mean
  flow and below 100 m³/s is an extrapolation. Effect on the design is small: v = 0.51 Q^0.11
  only moves 0.4-1.2 m/s, against p = 1.4 m/s. Sources:
  https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/68731/noaa_68731_DS1.pdf ,
  https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2019GL086405 ,
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70024149 (abstract, no coefficients).
- **HydroLAKES `Lake_type`** exists with 1/2/3 as above (dbf read).
- **Asia's share** of paddleable length at Q >= 1 (26%) matches research/canoe.md.

## Hand-worked examples (docs/canoe.md 3)

- Brest (48.39 N 4.49 W) to A Coruña (43.37 N 8.40 W): 635 km great circle; midpoint ~255 km from
  the Spanish coast, ~390 km from the French. Coast route ~1,150 km (Brest-Bayonne ~680, Bayonne-
  A Coruña ~480, by eye). Offshore ~119 paddling h x 3 real h x 1e-3 to 3e-3 = 0.36-1.07.
  Coast: 230 h x 2e-5 = 0.005. Switch K = 103 h / (0.36 to 1.07) = 100-290 h.
- Taiwan (Su'ao) to Yonaguni: 111 km.
