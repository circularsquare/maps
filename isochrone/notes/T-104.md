# T-104 working notes: corridor jog at window corners

2026-11-07, subagent. Rule as built: docs/corridors.md, end of "Windows".

## Cause

Not `clipOut` alone. Three offsets add up where a coarser line meets a finer window:

1. The coarser line is cut at the window rectangle (exact), but the finer window's own lines start at
   the node that took the flux: at least 2 cells in and up to one coarse cell sideways. Cluj L1 z8,
   cut (24.9706, 48.25) against the L1 line's first point (24.9875, 48.2208): 0.017 and 0.029 degrees,
   about 5 px at zoom 8.
2. Where the window's routes do not go back to the start, the worker draws the coarser line in
   pieces (`coarserLines`). They are cut from the coarser level's raw line, not its smoothed one, and
   the page smooths them at the finer scale. The smoothed coarse line is up to 0.7 coarse cell away
   from the raw one (Douglas-Peucker tolerance), so the piece sits off the cut end (a small double
   line at the edge) and the finer line starts beside the piece's far end, not on it.
3. Lines were never joined: a piece end, a cut end and a tree line start were three loose ends.

Ends within 5 fine cells were not enough for L0.1 under L1 (offsets up to 10 cells, a whole coarse
cell: the flux goes to the nearest node within `ceil(coarse/fine) + 1 + EDGE` cells), so the reach is
the larger of 5 own cells and 1.5 coarse cells.

## Fix

`joinCuts` / `pairUp` / `leanEnd` in index.html, `np` from `corridors.lines` through session.js.
Ends are paired nearest first, moved onto the target, and the move is tapered along the line. Nothing
cached is changed (`corr[level].sm` stays the plain smoothing; the join is redone per draw, ~1 ms).
The join alone left the piece start running along its raw route beside the smoothed cut line (a
hair-thin double line and a small S-kink, visible at zoom 10 and over): see T-105 below.

## T-105: pieces cut from the smoothed line

Worker change is small: `coarserLines` also pushes each piece's line index in the coarser level
(`src`, a Uint32Array, posted with `np`). The page's `carve` projects the piece's first and last raw
points onto that line's smoothed points (`base[level]`, built each draw, pieces of pieces carved
from their own parent) and takes the part between. No cache holds it, so a recounted coarser level
cannot leave a stale piece. Ends more than a coarse cell from the line fall back to the raw piece.
After it the cut and the piece are one line; the only trace is a small dark dot where two
semi-transparent line caps overlap. Before crops are the T-104 `_after` files; after crops are
`t104_cluj_l1_z8x10.5_t105after.png`, `t104_cluj_l3_z6x8_t105after.png` and
`t104_brig_l0.1_z11_t105after.png`. Smoke 18/18, corridors 10/10.
