# T-014 working notes: window edges

2026-11-07, subagent. The rule as built is docs/resolution.md, "The view refine" 9.

## What made the line

Shots taken by settling a view (windows solved), stopping new views (`sendView` replaced), then
jumping to a window corner at the same zoom. Measured in the page from the old hole rectangles:

- **Both levels at once along the edge.** The hole was rounded inwards to whole coarse texels,
  so a strip up to one coarse texel wide showed the coarser and the finer level stacked: alpha
  190 over 190 reads as 239, a brighter line. North and south edges always (texel rows never
  land on the 0.25 degree window edges): 2.3-2.6 px at L10 under L3 at zoom 6, 0.8-1.0 px at L3
  under L1 at zoom 7-8, 0.7-4.9 px at L1 under L0.1 at zoom 11. West and east edges land on
  texel columns in theory, but a window edge of 7.6000000000000005 gave a whole L1 texel
  (6 px at zoom 11) at Brig.
- **Canvases not on the same grid.** MapLibre places a canvas source by its corners rounded to
  whole 1/8192 units of one tile (`ImageSource.setCoordinates`, read back from `tileID` and
  `tileCoords` in 5.24.0): up to ~0.02 degree for the whole-world L10 canvas, ~0.003 for an L3
  window. So hole and window miss each other by a fraction of a texel either way, which made a
  1 px dark gap on the L3 window's west edge at zoom 6 (Cluj, 10.75 E).
- Not antialiasing (nearest resampling), not a band boundary.

## The fix

Per-texel alpha in the page (`fade`, `refresh`, `drawnAt` in index.html): the coarser level
fades out over 3 of its cells inside the window edge as 1 - (1 - a)^(1 - s), and each texel of a
finer level takes (a - B) / (1 - B) against the texel actually drawn under it (B the coarser
levels' alpha together), using the corners as MapLibre rounded them. The levels then add up to
the painted alpha everywhere but sub-pixel slivers where a finer texel straddles two coarser
ones. Lining the canvases up to one pixel grid instead was not possible: coarse texel rows are
linear in Mercator and never land on the 0.25 degree window edges.

Residual: faint 1 px lines at coarser texel boundaries inside the fade band, where a screen pixel
falls on one side of a coarser texel edge and the finer texel's centre on the other: at most 2-4%
in brightness (sum of RGB within 5 of 317 on Black Sea water, 204-210 against 212 at the L3 corner)
against 22% for the old line (274 -> 335 at Brig).

Time on the main thread when a window arrives (`window.fadeLog`, headless Chrome, loaded machine):
L10 1-3 ms, L3 10-19 ms, L1 24-36 ms (12 under an L0.1 window), L0.1 42 ms; a level whose
finer window alone changed 2-7 ms. Kept low by: skipping coarser levels already gone everywhere
under a level, clearing a hole's inside with clearRect, reusing a value along runs of texels over
the same coarser texel, and redoing only the hole when only the window over it changed.

Corridors: unchanged by this (lines still cut at the window rectangle). At the L1 corner by Cluj
the L3 line and the L1 line meet with a ~6 px jog at zoom 8, the same before and after
(`t014_cluj_l1_z8_before_corridor.png`, `..._after_corridor.png`): line geometry, not painting.

Hover: unchanged, reads the finest window under the pointer (smoke "hover reads L0.1" passes).
