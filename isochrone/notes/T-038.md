## What the screenshots show

- **Manhattan**: the Hudson, the East River and the reservoir in Central Park are water; Queens
  and Brooklyn are reached over the bridges in under 1.5 h from Midtown (hover in Brooklyn
  Heights 1 h 26 min); New Jersey only by the George Washington Bridge, 2-4 h (the tunnels are
  left out). At L1 (MAP) the Hudson is land and New Jersey is 1-2 h.
- **Cluj**: the town at 100 m, ponds walked round; the Somes is narrower than a cell and stays
  land, as at L1.

## Zoom numbers

T-030's zoom figures used 256 px tiles; MapLibre's tiles are 512 px, so every zoom is one lower:
the 1.5 M cap is reached at about zoom 9.4 on 1400 x 900 and 10.2 on 2560 x 1440 (New York's
latitude), where a 100 m cell is about 1 px. docs/fine.md has the corrected numbers.
