## 1. Road grade cap

**Rule**: road speed = 5 km/h x height factor x slope factor of min(cell slope, 10% grade), off-road
ground keeps the full slope. The road byte blends as before:
`1 - e + e x min(1, off-road speed / road speed)`. Bridges take the road speed. Build-side only;
the speed tiles are byte for byte the same, only `r1/`, `r3/` and `grid_roads.bin.gz` change.

**Why 10%**: the 100 m level's street cap (docs/fine.md), and about the steepest grade of
mountain pass roads. The 1 km GMTED slope cannot tell a road's grade, so the cap is a design
number; the winding detour a graded road takes is not counted, which argues against a lower cap.
How much of the road network it touches (1 km cells with a walkable way, slope over 5.7 degrees,
`roadslope.py` in the session scratchpad): Alps 58% (median slope 8.6 degrees), Himalaya 59%,
Andes round Cusco 60%, Cluj 20%, Iowa 0%. One cap for paths and roads alike: mountain paths are
steeper than 10% but switchback too.

**Build**: `python isochrone/tools/build_tiles.py 3` (116 s), `swap tiles_t028`.

## 2. The hazard band on our land

`tools/hazard/build.py`: `dist` and `tiles` take land, and the coarse levels' time weights, from
the page's L1 speed tiles (`speed_strip`) instead of MAP's surface with its own small water. Both
had to change: a land pixel with no distance reads as 2000 km from water, the worst thirst.
`HAZARD_OUT` builds beside the served band.

    python isochrone/tools/hazard/build.py dist                       # 1.5 min, 3 GB
    HAZARD_OUT=hazard_next python isochrone/tools/hazard/build.py tiles   # 5 min, 5.1 GB

then `data/hazard` renamed to `data/hazard_map2020` (MAP-land band, kept to compare and roll back)
and `data/hazard_next` to `data/hazard`. `dist_g2.npy` was overwritten (MAP's land version is not
kept; `dist` remakes it in 1.5 min). The band: L10 1.05 MB, L3 1115 tiles 9.0 MB, L1 1135 tiles
28.5 MB (MAP land: 1.1, 9.3, 29.3 MB).

**Mismatch with our land**, which T-028 measured (cells that became land and had no hazard code
because MAP called them water): L1 1.93 M before, 1.08 M now, and these are now cells whose
computed rate is 0 (mild country), not masked ones; next to a code over 160 (1% an hour): 245
before, 0 now. L10 1,722 -> 158. The canoe water had meanwhile been rebuilt on our mask by the
T-079 agent: our water on canoe land is 0 cells at every level (was 1.69 M at L1).

**ktrips walking trips** (hazard off is unchanged by construction): Greenland east-Kangerlussuaq
