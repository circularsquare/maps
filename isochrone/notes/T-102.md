# T-102: canoe level agreement after T-100

2026-11-07. Outcome: **no change needed, `cfg.access` stays 0.5**, but the best value has moved up:
0.75 to 1.0 now fits better. The canoe at L10 is 3% faster against L1 than in T-092 (L1/L10 1.04 ->
1.07). The whole move is in river and delta starts, which is where T-100's main-river count for
coarse steps acts.

## How

T-092's harness, restored from git (5469ab95) into `notes/T-102/` and kept there for re-measuring
after canoe data changes: `lib.mjs`, `measure.mjs`, `cmp.mjs`, `excess.mjs`, plus T-088's
`regions.mjs` (the 25 starts; it used to be imported from `../T-088/`) and `bydist.mjs` (L1/L10 by
trip distance). Only the import path in `measure.mjs` changed. Same measurement as T-092: 25
starts, destinations 20-300 km on L1 land, hazard on, L10 the world and L3 and L1 a box round the
start, each solved from the start alone, served data. About 1 minute a run on one core.

    OUT=<scratch>/ TAG=now05 node measure.mjs canoe          (ACC=0.75 TAG=now075 ... for the knob)
    OUT=<scratch>/ TAG=now node measure.mjs walk
    OUT=<scratch>/ node cmp.mjs canoe now05 now075 ...
    OUT=<scratch>/ node excess.mjs now now05 now075 ...      (walk tag first)

Walking on the served tiles gives T-092's "now" column to the hundredth (all 1.02 (0.94-1.19), mean
abs ln 0.115), so walking is unchanged and the harness is the same measurement.

## Where the move comes from

- The starts on big rivers moved: Coari +0.22, Kisangani +0.14, Khulna +0.12, Lagos +0.07; forest
  0.94 -> 1.06. The starts with no big river (Jyvaskyla, New York, Utrecht, Sogndal) moved by 0.01
  or less, and walking did not move. So the shift is the river data, not something else in the
  harness.
- That is what T-100's main-river count does: coarse river steps no longer paddle every twig, so L10
  river trips got faster (Kisangani to Kinshasa 30.8 -> 20.8 days, notes/T-100.md) while L1 changed
  little. T-092's Coari and Kisangani were the slow ones (0.86, 0.93); they are now at 1.07-1.08, like
  the rest.
- Not proven by a before/after run: `data/canoe_pre_t100/` is gone, so the pre-T-100 canoe could not be
  re-run through today's harness. The evidence is the pattern above plus T-100's own trip table.
- By trip distance (canoe L1/L10 median): Kisangani 1.29 at 10 km, 1.15-1.19 at 20-80 km, 1.06 at
  160-300; Coari 0.68 at 10 km, 1.0-1.25 at 20-80, 1.06-1.09 beyond; all starts 1.07-1.11 from 10 to
  80 km and 1.05-1.06 beyond. Short trips are slow at L10 everywhere (walking too: 0.57 at 5 km),
  which is the coarse start cell, not rivers.
