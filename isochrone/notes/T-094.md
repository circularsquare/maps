# T-094: ktrips on directed walking

`checks/ktrips.mjs` now calls `setDirs` on the L10 level with `grid_dir.bin.gz` and, with roads on,
`grid_dir_roads.bin.gz`, as index.html and js/session.js do. `SYMMETRIC=1` skips it (the old
behaviour, for an A/B); `REV=1` walks each walking trip the other way.

Walking trips, before (symmetric bytes) -> after (directed), hours:

| Trip | hazard off | K=300 | K=1000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tamanrasset-Agadez | 216 -> 202 | 222 -> 221 | 224 -> 227 |
| Tamanrasset-In Salah | 191 -> 186 | 193 -> 187 | 193 -> 187 |
| Cairo-Khartoum | 456 -> 438 | 457 -> 438 | 462 -> 442 |
| Greenland east-Kangerlussuaq | 408 -> 407 | 607 -> 608 | 658 -> 621 |
| Leh-Manali | 119 -> 118 | 120 -> 121 | 124 -> 123 |

Survival moves by under 3 points except Greenland at K=1000 (77.6% -> 75.0%).

Canoe trips (all eight, at -1, 300, 1000) are identical in time, survival and route: the canoe
layer does not read the direction planes, and the carried stretches are 0-9 h.

Reverse check (hazard off, symmetric -> directed): symmetric walking gives the same time both ways
(Tamanrasset-Agadez 216 h each way); directed differs, Tamanrasset-Agadez 202 h out, 204 h back;
Leh-Manali 118 h, back 116 h; Cairo-Khartoum 438 h, back 437 h.

Why the walks moved: directed walking is about 3-6% faster on flat desert (Cairo-Khartoum -4%
both ways, Tamanrasset-Agadez -6%), matching T-011's L1/L10 shift from 0.91 to 1.02. The largest
move, Greenland K=1000 -5.6%, is the hazard-weighted route changing across the ice (hazard off is
unchanged, 408 -> 407 h). Nothing moved for a reason that needs chasing.

No doc or spec quotes these figures as a reference; earlier notes (T-042, T-067, T-077, T-084)
record them on the symmetric bytes and stay as history.
