## For the hover (T-024)

Per node: `L.res.hz[node]` is the cumulative hazard along the chosen route; survival is
`exp(-hz)`. Weight is `L.res.w`, time `L.res.t`. `js/route.js` already reads it. Nodes past the
stop rule (survival under 1%) are reached but not expanded, so a hover can show survival under 1%
at the edge of the reached area.

## Constanta to Cyprus over Anatolia

T-024 saw the route from Constanta to Cyprus carry over Anatolia. It does so with hazard off too,
so it is not hazard: by the Bosporus, the Sakarya and Porsuk, the lake district and down to
Antalya it is 297 h (37 days; 126 h sea, 9 lake, 34 river, ~128 carried incl. getting in and
out), against ~1,700 km and ~340 h round by the Dardanelles and the Aegean. Carrying is twice
today's walking time, and today's walking surface has roads, so the land part is fast. In 10,000
BC (no roads, T-010) it should flip.

It exposed a cost bug, fixed: getting into a neighbour's canoe node cost half the step at paddling
speed even when that node sat on a land cell (a river node), so a path could hop river cell to
river cell, get out, get in, faster than carrying. Constanta to Izmir went over Thrace that way
(45 h of transfers, 0 carried); now it paddles the Dardanelles. A transfer to or from a canoe
node on a land cell now costs the whole step carried (docs/canoe.md 2).
