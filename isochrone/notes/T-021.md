# T-021 notes: canoe data, lakes and sea

2026-10-26, agent. Rules: docs/canoe.md 1 and 4. Tools in `tools/canoe/` (code-map).

## Rebuild

From `isochrone/`, plain `python`:

1. `python tools/canoe/water.py [--cache DIR]`: ~11 min, peak ~6 GB. Needs `data/tiles/` and
   `data/grid.bin.gz` (rerun after build_tiles/build_grid), NE ocean and HydroLAKES under
   `maps/data/`.
2. `python tools/canoe/sea_fetch.py drifter` (~110 MB of ERDDAP .nc, 30 s; deleted after use)
   and `python tools/canoe/sea_fetch.py era5 4` (streams 4.1 GB in ~7 min, keeps
   `data/raw/era5_summer/clim.npz`, 8.6 MB), then `python tools/canoe/sea_fetch.py era5ice 4`
   (T-033: sea ice alone for August and February, one hour every 49 h, 873 chunks, 0.34 GB in
   72 s, keeps `clim_ice.npz`; sea.py takes ice from it).
3. `python tools/canoe/sea.py`: seconds.
4. `python tools/canoe/water_plots.py`: PNGs `maps/artifacts/isochrone/t021_*.png`, strait and
   link-symmetry checks.

## Byte layouts (for T-022)

All files are gzip. Inside, whole bands one after another; each band row-major, row 0 north,
col 0 west. uint8 unless said.

| File | Grid | Bands in order | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `water.bin.gz` | 4320 x 1740 (L10, 1/12°, 180W 85N) | kind, shore, links | 3 x 7,516,800 |
| `l3/<tr>_<tc>.bin.gz` | 200 x 200 (1/40°) | kind, shore, links | 3 x 40,000 |
| `l1/<tr>_<tc>.bin.gz` | 600 x 600 (1/120°) | kind, shore | 2 x 360,000 |
| `sea.bin.gz` | 1440 x 580 (0.25°, 180W 85N) | u int8, v int8, hs, ice, sst int8 | 5 x 835,200 |

- Tile names and numbering as `data/tiles/` (tile index `tr * 72 + tc`, row 0 at 85N, col 0 at
  180W); `data/canoe/index.json` holds `levels["10"]` (file), `levels["3"]` and `levels["1"]`
  (path, tile size, `tiles` list) and `sea` (file, west, north, step, width, height, bands).
- **kind**: 0 land, 1 sea, 2 river water, 3+ lake with Hs = (kind - 3) x 0.05 m. On a cell
  whose speed is > 0 (land), kind 1 or 3+ means a canoe node besides the land node.
- **shore**: 0 land; 1-20 km; 21-78 = 20 + 10 x (code - 20) km. Decode `c <= 20 ? c : 20 + 10 *
  (c - 20)`.
- **links**: bit k = neighbour (dc, dr): 0 (1,0), 1 (-1,0), 2 (0,1), 3 (0,-1), 4 (1,1), 5 (1,-1),
  6 (-1,1), 7 (-1,-1); the order of `js/search.js`'s first 8 moves. Wraps at 180°. Only cells
  with a canoe node have bits, and only towards cells with one. Knight moves have no bit: the
  worker decides them from the four cells (docs/canoe.md 2).
- **sea**: u, v = value x 0.02 m/s (east, north); hs = value x 0.05 m; ice = %; sst = °C.
  Cell (r, c) centre: lat 84.875 - 0.25 r, lon -179.875 + 0.25 c. Look up a point at L1/L3/L10
  by floor((85 - lat) / 0.25), floor((lon + 180) / 0.25).
- Missing tiles: all sea, `shore` from the next coarser level. The tile sets equal the speed
  tiles' (L1 1141, L3 1130).

## Choices

- **Water mask from the built tiles**, not the tif: L1 water = speed tile 0 (T-013's small water
  is already land), L3/L10 water = speed 0. Canoe water cannot disagree with walking.
- **Sea fringe.** NE 10 m and MAP coasts differ by a pixel or two: 2.27 M L1 pixels were
  neither NE ocean nor lake; growing sea 8 pixels through that water left 1.89 M (big rivers,
  lakes HydroLAKES lacks). Rio de la Plata, Norway's fjords, the Sundarbans channels and the
  Baltic come out sea; the Amazon at Manaus river water (`t021_kinds.png`).
- **Straits MAP walks over.** MAP's 1 km surface has the Bosporus as land along its whole
  length and the Dardanelles cut at ~26.4E (`t021_links_bosporus.png`). First build: both NOT
  joined at L10 and L3. Fix: an L1 land pixel touched by NE's ocean polygon (all-touched
  rasterising, so the 1-pixel pinch at 41.05N stays continuous) gets kind 1 and shore 1, keeping
  its land node: 2.94 M such pixels worldwide, a ~1 km fringe along most coasts. Walking is
  unchanged. Side effect: the canoe can sit on the outermost land pixel of a coast; NE 10 m does
  not cut isthmuses (Corinth stays closed).
- **Coarse lake code** = the largest lake code in the cell (largest lake, the safe side).
- **Shore distances**: kd-tree of the 5.88 M coast pixels (land with a water 8-neighbour) on the
  unit sphere; exact for water pixels whose L10-interpolated distance is under 40 km (query
  bounded at 30 km), else bilinear from exact L10-centre distances (1-Lipschitz: error under
  ~7 km, against 10 km steps).
- **ERA5 sample**: 1991-2020, one hour every 73 h (hour of day cycles), kept if in JJA or DJF:
  1,803 wave hours, 601 ice and SST hours. Waves are ERA5's 0.5° field on the 0.25° grid.
- **Ice** is the August (north) / February (south) mean, not the summer mean (T-033).
- **Currents**: monthly drifter means over the summer months present; 1.6% of cells fall back to
  the annual mean; the rest without drifter data (land and some coastal cells) are 0.

## Checks

- Straits (`water_plots.py straits`, a flood over links inside a box): Bosporus, Dardanelles,
  Messina, Øresund joined at L10 and L3. Corinth isthmus not joined at L10 or L3 (no cell holds
  both gulfs), so it is not the exception docs/canoe.md expected; doc updated.
- L10 link symmetry: 0 mismatches.
- Gulf Stream, JJA: 1.33 m/s max within 1° of 36N 73W (the core sits just north-west of it;
  the 36N 73W cell itself 0.56). `t021_gulfstream.png`, `t021_currents.png`. Max anywhere 2.15.
- Summer Hs: open-sea mean 1.68 m; Mediterranean 0.67 m; 50-60S (DJF) 2.85 m; Somali jet in
  JJA ~3.5 m by eye. `t021_hs.png`.
- Ice, first build (JJA mean): the 15% edge lay between NSIDC's June and July median edges;
  Hudson Bay 37%, Baffin Bay 66%, Kara 49%, Beaufort 76%, Barents 6%, Bering Strait 8%. That
  closed Hudson Bay and the whole Arctic coast, so T-033 moved ice to the least-ice month.
- Ice now (August north, February south; `t021_ice.png`): Hudson Bay 2%, Baffin Bay (72N 65W)
  26%, Kara (74N 70E) 15%, Beaufort (72N 140W) 58%, Barents 0%, Bering Strait 1%, Gulf of
  Bothnia 0%. ERA5's August 15% edge follows NSIDC's August median edge: the Siberian and
  Alaskan coasts open, ice from ~75-77N off Siberia, Hudson Bay open, the Canadian Archipelago
  and northern Baffin Bay partly closed. Inside our grid (to 60S) there is almost no February
  ice.
- Sizes against the design: water.bin.gz 1.1 MB (est. 2), L3 4.1 MB (est. 5), L1 15.0 MB (est.
  20), sea 0.6 MB (est. 3).
- Counts: L10 5.37 M sea, 56 k lake, 1.2 k river-water cells; L3 59.2 M sea, 296 k lake, 30 k
  river.
