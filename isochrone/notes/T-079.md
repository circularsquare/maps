# T-079 Desert wadis paddled

Anita, 2026-11-05: a canoe start at Riyadh paddled across Arabia toward the Gulf at 8.2 km/h.
Rule now in `docs/canoe.md` 2 and `docs/hazard.md` 2.1; code `tools/hotdesert.py`.

## Cause

WaterGAP (RiverATLAS `dis_m3_pyr`) gives Arabia's wadis big mean flows. Wadi ad-Dawasir: 36 m³/s at
43.9E, jumping to 292 m³/s at 44.5E inside the desert, then falling to 72 at its end at 49.3E 23.3N
(766 km of Q >= 1). 8.2 km/h downstream is v = 3.2 km/h, Q about 160: the lower Dawasir (Q 105-195,
48-49E). Wadi ar-Rimah near Buraydah has Q 259. Before the fix the Riyadh start's corridors showed
"paddled, 7.9 km/h" on the Dawasir at 44.0E 20.8N.

T-042's hazard test (in hot desert, the reach's own driest month `dis_m3_pmn` >= 0.1) does not fix
it: the upper Dawasir has a driest month of 98 m³/s, Rimah 10, Cooper Creek 16, the Diamantina 18.
WaterGAP's driest month is as invented as its mean there. On the Q >= 1 network that test would drop
109,600 km in hot desert and keep the upper Dawasir, while cutting holes in real rivers where the
driest month reads 0 for a few reaches (Niger 8 km near Lake Debo, Draa 98 km, Darling 40 km).

Hot desert (the hazard's mask: CHELSA rain < 250 mm, warmer of the January and July highs >= 25 C)
holds 272,900 km of the 9.0 M km of Q >= 1 reaches (a reach counts when half its length is in it).

## Rule

In hot desert a reach does not use its own discharge. It carries only the water that comes in from
upstream reaches outside the desert (their mean and driest-month flow), walked down the network:

- scaled by Q / (sum of the upstream Q) wherever WaterGAP's own mean flow falls (its losses, never
  its gains);
- losing 3 cm a day over the wetted width W = 7.2 Q^0.5 m on every km (evaporation and seepage):
  sqrt(Q) falls by 0.00125 per km. A 1.5 m³/s stream sinks below 1 within ~180 km; the Nile loses
  about 14% more by it from Khartoum to the sea;
- a river while that is >= 1 m³/s mean and >= 0.1 m³/s in the driest month; its speed comes from
  the carried-in Q.

Outside hot desert nothing changes: every Q >= 1 reach runs, seasonal ones too (the paddler picks
the season). The land hazard reads the same flag (`rivers_work/desert.npz`) for its drinking water,
so both layers agree.

Tried first: the share of flow from outside the desert (>= 0.5) dropped the Niger at Gao (0.39:
WaterGAP adds in-desert flow to it) and the Tarim. Carried-in flow >= 1 without a loss kept a
1.5 m³/s Asir stream (Wadi Bisha) running 600 km into the Rub al Khali under the Dawasir's 292 m³/s;
the loss term ends it after 89 km.

## Rivers checked (traced downstream)

Kept whole: Nile from Khartoum (3,185 km), Niger from Mopti (2,943), Euphrates from Raqqa, Tigris
from Mosul, Indus from Sukkur, Colorado from Needles, Gila, Rio Grande from El Paso, Amu Darya from
Turkmenabat, Syr Darya from Kyzylorda, Orange from Upington, Helmand, Senegal, Murghab, Kunene,
Shebelle; Okavango from Mohembo and the Murray from Mildura are outside hot desert.

Dropped: Wadi ad-Dawasir and Wadi Bisha (1,242 of 1,331 km; the top 89 km of the Asir stream stay),
Wadi ar-Rimah (all 101 km), the Kuiseb, the Hoggar and Air wadis, the lower Draa past 8.3W (384 km;
it reaches the Atlantic only in rare floods), the lower Mojave (70 km).

Edge cases, decided:
- **Cooper Creek and the Diamantina stay.** They are fed from Queensland outside the mask, where
  WaterGAP says the Thomson never runs dry (driest month 8 m³/s at Longreach), so by our data they
  are seasonal rivers from outside the desert, like the Senegal. In fact they flood most summers and
  stop for months. Dropping them needs a source on flow permanence: Messager et al. 2021 (GIRES,
  non-perennial probability per HydroRIVERS reach, CC BY 4.0) would do it, inside hot desert only.
- **The lower Tarim drops** (688 km below 85.3E): the Kunlun and Tian Shan inflow has a driest month
  of 0.1-0.7 m³/s in WaterGAP (no glacier melt), and the desert loss eats it. Today that matches
  the river (dry below its reservoirs since the 1970s); for 10,000 BC it is wrong. The Hotan and
  Keriya also drop (WaterGAP gives them ~1 m³/s at the mountain front; the Hotan is ~140).
- **The Darling has a 34 km gap at Menindee**: reaches where WaterGAP's driest month is 0 just above
  the mask edge. At L10 Bourke to Wentworth still paddles through (16.3 days against 16.1), other
  reaches sharing those cells; the L1 window may show a short carry there.
