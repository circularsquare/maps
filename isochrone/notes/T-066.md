The rule and the rebuild commands: `docs/hazard.md`. Code: `tools/hazard/fetch.py`, `build.py`.

## Run (2026-11-05)

- Ice outlines: RGI 6.0 from OGGM's mirror timed out for ~15 minutes, then came back; `ice` and
  `dist` were rerun with RGI and NE together. GIMP is NSIDC (Earthdata login), not used.

  `index.json`; 39.7 MB in all.

## Bug found and fixed on the way

The first `tiles` run counted small water (< 5 km², land in the speed tiles) as land, but `dist`
had not, so those pixels kept the grid's "no distance" fill (65,535 km) and read as lethal: a
cell by Verkhoyansk at 0.13 an hour, and Greenland's straight crossing went from 1% to unreached.
`dist` now uses the speed tiles' land too, and `tiles` logs any land pixel with no distance (none
in the final run).
