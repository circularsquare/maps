# East and Southeast Asia, the rest: survey (2026-10-08)

Countries surveyed on 2026-10-08 that need no build of their own. The ones that do have their
own `<cc>_sources.md`: ph, mm, kh, la, mn, kp (kp waits on Anita's call); the Canary Islands
are in `canaries_survey.md`.

- **Brunei (bn)**: no railway. Its only lines were short industrial ones (the oilfield railway
  around Seria and Badas) that closed decades ago; no passenger rail, no people mover. Nothing to build. (Geofabrik ships
  it inside malaysia-singapore-brunei, which my_register.py already clips.)
- **Macau (mo)**: already built, inside cn. cn_sources.md: "Macau is in cn: it is inside the
  extract and inside China's boundary, and its light rail (氹仔線, 橫琴線, 石排灣線) is built as OSM
  lines". The Macau LRT Taipa line, the Seac Pai Van line and the Hengqin extension (opened
  Dec 2024) are therefore on the map; the East Line is under construction (not open). No
  separate region needed; if a mo region is ever wanted, it would be a clip of cn's data, as
  hk is.
