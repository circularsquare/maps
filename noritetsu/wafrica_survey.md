# West and Central Africa survey (2026-10-08): countries with no passenger service

Research only. Countries surveyed with service have their own `<cc>_sources.md`: `ng_`,
`gh_`, `sn_`, `bf_`, `cm_`, `ga_`, `cg_`, `cd_`, `ao_sources.md`. All nine were built on
2026-10-08 by `wafrica_register.py` (lists in `wafrica_lines.py`); each sources file has a
"Build" section, and `handoff_notes/wafrica_build.md` the totals and shared-file diffs.

- **Côte d'Ivoire (ci)**: no scheduled passenger train. Sitarail's Abidjan – Ouagadougou
  passenger train has not entered Côte d'Ivoire since the 2020 suspension (Sitarail's own
  Nov 2023 statement: no passenger traffic on Ivorian territory without the Ivorian
  authority's approval; en.wikipedia "Rail transport in Burkina Faso": "As of June 2024,
  Sitarail does not operate a passenger train to Abidjan"; nothing newer found). The Abidjan
  metro line 1 is under construction. Freight only. Re-check if Sitarail announces the
  Abidjan service again (fratmat.info, Sitarail communiqués).
