# East and Southern Africa: countries with no scheduled passenger rail (survey 2026-10-08)

Countries the eafrica survey checked and found no scheduled passenger train running at least
about weekly. Nothing to build; the ones with service have their own `<cc>_sources.md` (ke, tz,
et, dj, ug, zm, zw, mz, mw, mg, mu).

- **Botswana (bw)**: BR Express (Lobatse - Gaborone - Francistown) suspended since 2020 and still suspended in 2026 (seat61.com/Botswana.htm; Botswana Railways' timetable PDF is the pre-2020 one). Only Rovos Rail's occasional cruises cross. Geofabrik `africa/botswana` 83 MB if it ever returns.
- **Namibia (na)**: TransNamib StarLine passenger trains suspended early 2021, never resumed (seat61.com/Namibia.htm); Desert Express gone. Geofabrik `africa/namibia` 52 MB.
- **Eswatini (sz)**: no passenger service of its own; only Rovos Rail's rare Durban cruise passes. Geofabrik `africa/swaziland` 29.4 MB.
- **Libya (ly)**: no operating railway at all (the Italian-era lines closed in 1965; the Sirte - Ras Lanuf and coastal projects never opened). Geofabrik `africa/libya` 73 MB.
- **Sudan (sd)**: no confirmed weekly scheduled service. In the war since April 2023, Port Sudan - Atbara is reported running again ("the first line since the conflict", Sudan Tribune; frequency not stated) and the ministry reopened Khartoum - Wad Madani on 20 July 2026 (Sudan Tribune, 403 to our fetch: sudantribune.com/?p=316420 for Anita's browser), but no timetable or frequency was found; sinfin.net's "a few times a week" wording describes the pre-war network. Revisit when a timetable appears. Geofabrik `africa/sudan` 195 MB.
