# Middle East: countries with no regular passenger train (survey 2026-10-08)

The countries in the 2026-10-08 Middle East survey that have no scheduled passenger rail at
least about weekly, so get no `<cc>_sources.md`. The ones that do: `il_sources.md`,
`sa_sources.md`, `ae_sources.md`, `qa_sources.md`, `iq_sources.md`, `jo_sources.md`.

- **Lebanon (lb)**: no trains since the civil war (the last CEL service, Beirut - Jounieh,
  stopped in the mid-1990s); the track is mostly built over. Nothing to build.
- **Kuwait (kw)**: no railway. The Kuwait section of the GCC railway and the Kuwait Metro are
  plans; nothing under passenger service.
- **Bahrain (bh)**: no railway. The Bahrain Metro (phase 1) is at tender stage; the King
  Hamad Causeway rail link to Saudi Arabia is not built.
- **Oman (om)**: no railway in service. Hafeet Rail (Sohar - Al Ain, with Etihad Rail) is
  under construction and is planned as freight first. Overpass (2026-10-08) finds one
  railway=rail way in the country and no stations.
- **Palestine (ps)**: no passenger railway in the West Bank or Gaza (Gaza's line closed in
  the 1940s-70s; the Hejaz branch to Nablus is long gone). Two Israeli lines touch the
  territory: the Tel Aviv - Jerusalem high-speed line crosses the 1949 armistice line for a
  few km near Mevo Horon (Latrun), and Jerusalem's light rail runs through East Jerusalem.
  Both are in `il_sources.md` as questions for Anita; there is nothing to build as `ps`.
- **Syria (sy)**: no confirmed regular passenger service as of October 2026. CFS's network
  is being repaired for freight (Latakia - Aleppo grain trains from 25 January 2026,
  Latakia - Adra from 19 May 2026, Tartus - Talkalkh rehabilitation; Enab Baladi, Arab
  News). Passenger news since December 2024 is test runs and specials: an Aleppo - Hama test
  run on 6 August 2025 (en.wikipedia "Rail transport in Syria"), and special trains to the
  Damascus International Fair from 29 August 2025 (SANA). The Latakia - Tartus commuter
  train that ran until about 2024 has a 2022 fare table (Enab Baladi) but nothing found
  showing it runs now; Enab Baladi's article pages answer 403 to fetches. OSM (Overpass,
  2026-10-08, `data/raw/sy/survey/osm_routes_x.json`) has two Syrian passenger relations,
  14906342 Damascus - Al Hamah - Jdeidet al Wadi (the Hejaz railway's Barada valley
  suburban line) and 19291097 Aleppo - Ath-Thawra (CFS), and CFS line relations (Aleppo -
  Deir ez-Zur and others); no current timetable backs either service. Recheck in 2027, or
  if Anita has news of a timetable; the Latakia - Tartus and Damascus Hejaz lines would be
  the first to come back. Geofabrik `asia/syria-latest.osm.pbf`, 78 MB.
